### Preface

Looking at the small beginnings of our church, many peo- ple have asked me the secret of our growth.However, there is nothing secret about this old principle of frugality. It’s been used by successful people for ages!As stewards, God expects us to be faithful with the little He gives us. And I have come to realize that prosperity is inevitable when we wisely maximise profits and minimise losses.In this book, I will share with you some of the principles the Lord taught me to apply in my church, ministry and in my personal life. There is no need to be envious of successful people, you can be successful too!One cardinal feature of very intelligent people is that they have child-like minds, and are open to a wide variety of new ideas and possibilities. This makes them very creative and progressive. I think that most Christians need an attitude of being open and ready to learn.If you are seeking to increase in any area of your life, business, ministry, then this book will help to point you in the right direction.
