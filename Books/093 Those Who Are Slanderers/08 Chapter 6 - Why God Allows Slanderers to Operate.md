### Chapter 6\

Why God Allows Slanderers to Operate\
----------------------------------------------------------

The beast was given a mouth to utter proud words and blasphemies and to exercise its authority for forty-two months. It opened its mouth to blaspheme God, and to slander his name and his dwelling place and those who live in heaven. It was given power to wage war against God’s holy people and to conquer them. And it was given authority over every tribe, people, language and nation. All inhabitants of the earth will worship the beast-all whose names have not been written in the Lamb’s book of life, the Lamb who was slain from the creation of the world.... “If anyone is to go into captivity, into captivity they will go. If anyone is to be killed with the sword, with the sword they will be killed.” This calls for patient endurance and faithfulness on the part of God’s people.

Revelation 13:5-8,10 (NIV)

1\. God allows you to be slandered because He Himself suffers slander and blasphemy from godless men and beasts.

And HE OPENED HIS MOUTH IN BLASPHEMY AGAINST GOD, to blaspheme his name, and his tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven.

Revelation 13:6

If Almighty God suffers slander, then who are you to complain about being slandered by evil men? God’s house, His tabernacle, His name and all those in heaven are slandered regularly by the slanderer! You must understand that being slandered is part of being a godly person.

2\. God allows you to be slandered because it is part of the war.

And he opened his mouth in blasphemy against God, TO BLASPHEME his name, and his tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven. AND IT WAS GIVEN UNTO HIM TO MAKE WAR WITH THE SAINTS, and to overcome them: and power was given him over all kindreds, and tongues, and nations.

Revelation 13:6-7

We are in a real war. There are real haters and murderers out there in the realm of the spirit. God teaches our hands to war. He shows us how to fight the slander and the blasphemies of wicked and ungodly men.

The key to fighting slander is to not have the honour of men. If you are immune to the praises of men, you will also be immune to the slander and malicious talk of men. Remove yourself from every platform where you receive honour from men. When you do not belong to certain groups, you will be unaffected by the opinions of those groups, and you will also not mind what blasphemies and slander they hear.

3\. God allows men to slander you because it is only for a season.

And there was given unto him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies; AND POWER WAS GIVEN UNTO HIM TO CONTINUE FORTY AND TWO MONTHS. And he opened his mouth in blasphemy against God, to blaspheme his name, and his tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven.

Revelation 13:5-6

There are certain things in the Kingdom which are allowed but are also time bound. Blasphemers and slanderers can only operate within a season. The slander cannot and will not go on forever! These evil powers have an expiry date on them. In the scripture above, we see that the season of allowed slander was forty-two months.

I know thy works, and tribulation, and poverty, (but thou art rich) and I know the blasphemy of them which say they are Jews, and are not, but are the synagogue of Satan. Fear none of those things which thou shalt suffer: behold, the devil shall cast some of you into prison, that ye may be tried; AND YE SHALL HAVE TRIBULATION TEN DAYS: be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee a crown of life.

Revelation 2:9-10

In the scripture above we see that the church in Smyrna was taken through a tribulation. It was also a timed tribulation. It was to last only ten days! After ten days, God would not allow it to continue.

Take heart in your sufferings and tests. God is not dead! God is “wild”\*! God is very conscious of all that is happening. He will respond! He will react! He will right the wrongs and fight back at satan and his agents.

All the tests you are going through have an important spiritual effect on your life. The powerful effects of slander and tribulation in humbling and purifying God’s servant are worth far more than the “peace” you would have otherwise enjoyed.

Whether it is forty-two months or ten days, remember that every power has an expiry date. There will be a time when the powers of the slanderer will come to an end!

4\. God allows men to slander you to work patience in you.

My brethren, count it all joy when ye fall into divers temptations; Knowing this, that the trying of your faith worketh patience. But let patience have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, wanting nothing.

James 1:2-4

When you are slandered by wicked men, you must consider the provocation as something God allows. Count it all joy because the temptation and provocation of slander are working out patience and endurance in your character.

Your character is definitely modified after you have been tested, tried and tempted. An untested aircraft is different from one that has been tested. You would not want to sit on a plane that has not been tested. Allow God to take you through various tests, so that you become a valuable and usable vessel.

One day a sister told me she did not like being talked about at all. “I hate such things”, she said. I told her that she would have to resign from Christianity then, because in the church we have some of the most ardent slanderers ever. The devil loves to use Christians to slander because slander is more powerful when it comes from someone seemingly righteous. The slander you will experience is even more evil and more deadly if it comes from a high authority in the church.

I once met a pastor who had been slandered by a disgruntled former employee. When he described the insults and slander he had endured from this son of his, I perceived that he had become a much more mature Christian because of it. I loved him more and could relate to him better.

Slander will harden you! Being spoken about maliciously will toughen you up. Once you are unaffected by damaging and slanderous speech, it means you are unaffected by the praise of men. Who is man? What does man’s opinion matter? It is what God thinks about you that matters.

Who art thou that judgest another man’s servant? to his own master he standeth or falleth. Yea, he shall be holden up: for God can make him stand.

Romans 14:4

5\. God does not stop temptations but allows them to happen to solidify your faith.

And the Lord said, Simon, Simon, behold, Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat: BUT I HAVE PRAYED FOR THEE, THAT THY FAITH FAIL NOT: and when thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren.

Luke 22:31-32

Why did Jesus not pray that the temptation of Peter would not come on? Indeed, Jesus did not pray that Peter would not be tempted. Jesus prayed that Peter would have the right mind and heart of faith AFTER the temptation.

It is only the temptation that could knock out Peter’s attitude of arrogance and overconfidence. Jesus preferred that Apostle Peter should be humbled by the temptation so that he could be reformed. Peter needed to reform his thoughts and beliefs about himself and his ministry.

Jesus did not prevent the test, the trial and the temptation. He preferred for Peter to be tested. Indeed, slander will do something good for you and your personality.

Slander has a humbling effect on you. When you read about what is being said about you and what people think about you, you will realize that you are nothing. You will give up trying to have a great reputation. People will speak about you as though you are an object that does not have life or feelings. The insults will flow from the lowest of men! Liars will feel free to speak abominations and blasphemies just at the mention of your name!

Men, women, boys and girls alike will write and speak great malicious words which people will believe wholly. Many simple people will believe all the wicked things that they read and hear about you. You will stand before men and women and wonder what they think about you. You will see confusion and uncertainty in the eyes of many people. These things are indeed humbling.

6\. Slander is a door to higher levels in the ministry.

Confirming the souls of the disciples, and exhorting them to continue in the faith, and that we must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God.

Acts 14:22

Tribulation is pressure, affliction, anguish and persecution! Persecution is inflicted by slander and by malicious and damaging speakers and writers. Slander causes an unbearable burden and oppression. Many ministers are utterly and profoundly distressed by the slander perpetrated against them. Paul said that we would enter the kingdom through tribulation. That includes the tribulation of slander. Amazingly, troubles are the springboard that lead you to higher levels. Slanderous, bad stories about you will eventually lead to your promotion.

Those whom God has drawn to you will never be affected by any slander about you. It is only those who are not a part of your life and ministry who will be put off by any rumours about you. People who love you will never leave you because of a bad story whether it is true or not. Those who love you cannot be driven away from you because of a bad story. If anyone leaves me just because of a bad story or rumour about me, I would prefer for that person to stay away forever. There have been rumours about me since I began in the ministry. Ministry is always practised in the midst of bad stories, rumours and lies.

Peter the apostle was slandered and accused of robbing the grave of Jesus to make people think that Jesus did not actually rise from the dead. The Roman soldiers spread the story that Peter had stolen the body of Jesus. Peter was forced to start his ministry amid such slander.

And when they were assembled with the elders, and had taken counsel, they gave large money unto the soldiers, Saying, Say ye, His disciples came by night, and stole him away while we slept.

Matthew 28:12-13

Just as Peter went forth into his ministry amid slander, many great evangelists have received worldwide renown through the slander and bad press they received. These troubles and tribulations they experienced were actually a door to higher levels and larger crowds.

Many of my detractors and slanderers have worked to increase me and take me higher in ministry. If you have spiritual discernment, you will notice that you go higher with every trouble that you go through.
