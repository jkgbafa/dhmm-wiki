### Chapter 1\

What is Slander?\
--------------------------------------

Brothers and sisters, do not slander one another. Anyone who speaks against a brother or sister or judges them speaks against the law and judges it.

James 4:11 (NIV)

1\. To slander someone is to attack the character of a person who is not present.

2\. To slander someone is to attempt to discredit a person, by direct accusations or innuendos.

3\. To slander someone is to attempt to belittle a person.

4\. To slander someone is to speak in such a way so as to shame and dishonour a person.

5\. To slander is to lower the estimation of someone in the eyes of ordinary people.

6\. To slander is to cast somebody in a bad light.

7\. To slander someone is to depreciate a person’s reputation in the eyes of the public.

8\. To slander someone is to attempt to lower the value of a person to his followers and admirers.

9\. To slander someone is to portray the person as less impressive or important than previously thought.

10\. To slander someone is to circulate malicious and injurious stories about the person.

11\. To slander someone is to make a malicious statement, designed to injure the reputation of the person.

12\. To slander someone is to make a damaging or derogatory remark about a person.

13\. To slander someone is to cause an unjustified injury to their good reputation.

14\. To slander someone is to speak unfavourably about a person when they are not present.

15\. To slander someone is to represent someone incorrectly, falsely, improperly and unsatisfactorily.
