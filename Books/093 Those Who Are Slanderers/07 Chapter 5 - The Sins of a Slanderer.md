### Chapter 5\

The Sins of a Slanderer\
---------------------------------------------

All of them are stubbornly rebellious, GOING ABOUT AS A SLANDERER; They are bronze and iron. THEY ARE, ALL OF THEM, CORRUPT.

Jeremiah 6:28 (NASB)

THOU SHALT NOT GO UP AND DOWN AS A TALEBEARER among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I am the Lord.

Leviticus 19:16

1\. The sin of breaking the law: A slanderer breaks the law of God.

YOU SHALL NOT GO ABOUT AS A SLANDERER AMONG YOUR PEOPLE; and you are not to jeopardize the life of your neighbor. I am the Lord.

Leviticus 19:16 (NASB)

The law explicitly teaches that no one should go about as a slanderer. A slanderer breaks the laws of God by what he does!

A slanderer is an evil sinner. In the law of Moses, there are severe warnings against being a slanderer. Indeed, it is a sin to write or publish stories, spread rumours and information about someone in such a way as to belittle and destroy the person. All who fear God must be afraid of becoming the spreaders of stories, rumours, half-truths or outright lies.

2\. The sin of damaging another life: A slanderer damages lives.

Do not go about spreading SLANDER among your people. DO NOT DO ANYTHING THAT ENDANGERS YOUR NEIGHBOR’S LIFE. I am the LORD.

Leviticus 19:16 (NIV)

There is a severe warning for those who are talebearers! In the scripture above we see that slander can endanger someone’s life. Do not be someone who bears tales, questions, rumours, unsubstantiated stories and unverifiable information that are damaging to people’s lives.

I remember a news item I read about a man who was taking his mother-in-law to the hospital when she died in the car, enroute to the hospital. A rumour spread in the village that the man driving the car had killed a woman and was secretly going to bury her. This man’s car was quickly surrounded by the villagers because of this false story. He was hurled out of the car and beaten to death by the crowd. Indeed, an innocent man was murdered because of this unfortunate, fake story.

3\. The sin of campaigning against God’s servant: A slanderer goes up and down.

THOU SHALT NOT GO UP AND DOWN AS A TALEBEARER among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I am the Lord.

Leviticus 19:16

Going on a campaign to spread stories is what the Bible calls “going up and down” to destroy someone. A slanderer campaigns actively to destroy someone. He goes up, then he goes down on his wicked mission. He calls one person and then another, trying to get people to join his malicious mission.

He goes from person to person seeking to convince them to stop believing in someone. It is a sin to be an active campaigner, a spreader and publisher of texts, WhatsApp messages, Facebook posts of lies, scandalous stories and insults on social media to smear the reputation of God’s children.

...Touch not mine anointed and do my prophets no harm.

Psalm 105:15

Many pastors who leave churches as disgruntled employees become slanderers and follow this wicked pattern of slandering.

4\. The sin of revealing secrets: A slanderer reveals secrets.

ONE WHO GOES ABOUT AS A SLANDERER REVEALS SECRETS, But one who is trustworthy conceals a matter.

Proverbs 11:13 (NASB)

You may justify the revealing of people’s secrets for many reasons. However, you must remember that a slanderer is a devil and an accuser. There can never be a day when it is a good thing to be a devil!

You may have thought that it is a good person who goes about revealing the scandals and secret sins of others. After all, he is spreading the truth and enabling people to have facts and truth in their hands. However, it is not so! It is evil men who go around spreading secret sins and scandals of others. A slanderer is a revealer of secrets. Read it for yourself: “ONE WHO GOES ABOUT AS A SLANDERER REVEALS SECRETS, But one who is trustworthy conceals a matter” (Proverbs 11:13 (NASB)).

God sees slandering as evil and you must also see it as evil. . Slandering is evil because you are just spreading the failings of a fellow brother, who is as weak a person as you are.

People who fall into sin are to be helped to get up and walk again. People who fall into scandal and outrageous sin are to be given a helping hand to walk again and not to be pushed further underground, so they never rise again. Remember the important scripture: “Rejoice not against me O my enemy. When I fall, I shall arise again.” How can a person rise again if you keep spreading his failings to everyone?

Rejoice not against me, O mine enemy: when I fall, I shall arise; when I sit in darkness, the Lord shall be a light unto me.

Micah 7:8

A slanderer is someone who reveals secrets in order to destroy someone’s reputation. There are many secrets that when revealed will destroy a person’s reputation. A slanderer is someone who intentionally reveals secrets and hidden things to cause a collapse and general destruction of a church.

Beware of people who seem to have some deep information that no one else has. Beware of people who are inquisitive and seek to know things that do not concern them. They are only seeking to know secrets that they can divulge later as part of their slandering campaign.

5\. The sin of separating families and friends: A slanderer separates friends.

A perverse person spreads strife, and A SLANDERER SEPARATES CLOSE FRIENDS.

Proverbs 16:28 (NASB)

A slanderer speaks in such a way as to cause division and separation. Wherever a slanderer has lived and operated, there is strife, division and confusion. A slanderer is someone who separates the sheep from their shepherd. Through slanderers, many of your loyal church members will fall away. They will no longer be part of your fold because of the slanderous stories they have heard.

A slanderer will spread stories in a church in such a way that will cause stirring and unhappiness in the church; everyone will be unsure whether the stories they have heard are true or not. Where there are slanderers, everyone distrusts everyone! The slanderer succeeds in separating the sheep from their shepherd. A slanderer is indeed a devil in our midst.

6\. The sin of rebellion: A slanderer is a rebellious person.

All of them are STUBBORNLY REBELLIOUS, GOING ABOUT AS A SLANDERER; They are bronze and iron. They are, all of them, corrupt.

Jeremiah 6:28 (NASB)

A slanderer is usually a stubborn and rebellious person who listens to no one. Rebellion is defined as a resistance to authority. When a person rebels and hates authority, he seeks a way to destroy the person he has rebelled against. He has no other way to express his hatred for his former pastor or leader but to slander and speak maliciously about him.

I remember one rebellious slanderer who left our church. He was advised by many top leaders in the body of Christ to stop slandering me; but he would not listen to anyone. He went on to spread false stories about me to the extent that some people who had invited me to speak in their churches cancelled their invitations because of this fellow’s slanderous posts.

Watch out for rebellious leavers and disgruntled employees. They are often rebellious men with no hope for restitution. Such people will be your worst slanderers. But God will give you the victory over all slanderers! A slanderer is a devil, and he will be destroyed in the end.

7\. The sin of spreading fear in the church: A slanderer spreads fear.

I was a reproach among all mine enemies, but especially among my neighbours, and a fear to mine acquaintance: they that did see me without fled from me. I am forgotten as a dead man out of mind: I am like a broken vessel. FOR I HAVE HEARD THE SLANDER OF MANY: FEAR WAS ON EVERY SIDE: while they took counsel together against me, they devised to take away my life. But I trusted in thee, O Lord: I said, Thou art my God.

Psalm 31:11-14

As the scripture above says, slanderers spread fear through their stories. Through a slanderer’s stories, fear and panic spread in the church. Many people feel their church is collapsing because of the words of a slanderer. They feel their church is doomed because of the fear and panic that runs through it like wildfire.

Fear is an evil spirit. God has not given us the spirit of fear. God is not the one spreading fear. God gives a spirit of power, love and a sound mind.

For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.

2 Timothy 1:7

Many faithful followers are alarmed by the false and misleading stories of slanderers. Slanderers are so happy to announce the fallout from their stories and slander. They say, “Oh, have you heard that brother so and so has left the church? Mrs Ssokeyloo has also left the church and now attends Goobleygob Ministries. Wow! I hear everyone is leaving! Everyone is leaving! Many people are not happy with what is going on!” Indeed, a slanderer alarms the congregation and spreads fear everywhere.

But do not worry, dear friend, God will cause you to trample over satan and his demonized slanderers. It is only a matter of time before you will hear of those slanderers burning in the very fuel they had planned to use to burn down your church.

8\. The sin of combining leadership with slander: Some church leaders are slanderers.

You rebuke the arrogant, who are accursed, those who stray from your commands. Remove from me their scorn and contempt, for I keep your statutes. THOUGH RULERS SIT TOGETHER AND SLANDER ME, your servant will meditate on your decrees. Your statutes are my delight; they are my counselors.

Psalm 119:21-24 (NIV)

Rulers and elders of churches also sit down and slander people. Slander from a church leader can be particularly devastating. The authority of pastors gives credence to the stories they are peddling. When a pastor or bishop is a gossip, it is particularly dangerous. There are pastors who come alive whenever there is a story going around about someone involved in a scandal. Gossip is their food! They love to perch on a high pedestal and chat with other “birds of gossip”. For such people all their chatting is fault-finding, sarcastic remarks, accusations and unrighteous judgment.

Whilst some church leaders are also slanderers, others are not. I am always alerted in my spirit when I speak with a slanderer. Oh, how they love to give inside information about what is happening and what they have recently heard.

Some churches are filled with gossip groups who discuss various emerging scandals and rumours. The church is full of people who gossip and find fault with everything. That is why it is important to teach against gossiping, accusations and fault-finding.

Satan is the ultimate slanderer and he and his cohorts sit on the shoulders of top church leaders and spread rumours, slander and accusations. Remember that it is difficult for people to forget stories once they have heard them. Once stories are spread, people do not forget them.

May the judgment of God visit all fault finders, gossips, accusers and unrighteous judges within the church!

May a slanderer not endure on the earth; May evil hunt a violent person violently.

Psalm 140:11 (NASB)

9\. The sin of pride: Slanderers are cut off because of their pride.

WHOSO PRIVILY SLANDERETH HIS NEIGHBOUR, HIM WILL I CUT OFF: HIM THAT HATH AN HIGH LOOK AND A PROUD HEART WILL NOT I SUFFER. Mine eyes shall be upon the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me: he that walketh in a perfect way, he shall serve me.

Psalm 101:5-6

A slanderer has a high look and a proud heart! This is what the scripture says. Pride and overconfidence are the reasons why slanderers embark on their wicked campaigns. These proud individuals have secret meetings and make phone calls to spread tales, stories and rumours. All such proud acts are noted by God. Their high looks will be cut off.

God promises death to slanderers! Be careful, those who slander have a death sentence looming over their heads. Do not be close to a slanderer, lest you partake of his judgments. Wickedness will not prevail on the earth. God will allow the blasphemy for a season and a reason! After a season, an end will come to all slanderers, and they will be cut off!
