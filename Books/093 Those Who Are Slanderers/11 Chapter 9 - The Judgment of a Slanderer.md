### Chapter 9\

The Judgment of a Slanderer\
-------------------------------------------------

MAY A SLANDERER NOT ENDURE ON THE EARTH; May evil hunt a violent person violently.

Psalm 140:11 (NASB)

1\. A slanderer will be cursed!

DO NOT SLANDER A SLAVE TO HIS MASTER, OR HE WILL CURSE YOU and you will be found guilty.

Proverbs 30:10 (NASB)

Even slaves who are slandered, release curses against the slanderer. A slanderer will be cursed over and over by the ones he slanders.

Whether he hears the curses being spoken or not, the slanderer is being cursed continually by the people he has slandered! O slanderer, do not worry about whether you see or hear anyone cursing you; you are being cursed day and night! Even slaves who are slandered release curses against their slanderer.

I remember one slanderer who sent a message to the person he was slandering. He said “Stop cursing people! It is not biblical!”

He said this because he had watched his victim issuing curses against those who slandered him. “Stop the curses,” he cried. “ It is wrong to curse.”

However, curses continued pouring over his head from different quarters. I heard of several groups and individuals who continually cursed this fellow. They cursed him privately and they cursed him as a group. And indeed, he was truly cursed.

Later someone spoke to the slanderer and told him, “It was spiritual insanity to have attacked that church like you did.” This accursed, miserable slanderer developed crises in every field of his life and encountered the sword of the Lord.

The words and actions of a slanderer are so painful that he is due a curse! Never forget that even slaves who are slandered release curses against their slanderer. All who spread stories and information intending to harm someone should know that curses will abound in their direction!

2\. A slanderer will not endure on the earth.

MAY A SLANDERER NOT ENDURE ON THE EARTH; May evil hunt a violent person violently.

Psalm 140:11 (NASB)

Slanderers are destined to have their stay on earth cut short. Evil will hunt a slanderer. Slanderers must watch over their shoulders constantly. They will not endure on the earth. This is why many who choose to slander others as a profession do so at great risk to their own lives. “Professional slanderers” can become some of the most hated people in society. Many people detest slanderers because they damage people’s lives irrevocably.

It is almost impossible to change people’s minds, once the slanderer has published preposterous stories and rumours based on half-truths and difficult-to-explain realities! Those slandered are left with the impossible task of having to clear their image and reputation.

3\. A slanderer will not be allowed to remain in the church.

LORD, WHO MAY RESIDE IN YOUR TENT? WHO MAY SETTLE ON YOUR HOLY HILL? One who walks with integrity, practices righteousness, And speaks truth in his heart. HE DOES NOT SLANDER WITH HIS TONGUE, Nor do evil to his neighbor, Nor bring shame on his friend;

Psalm 15:1-3 (NASB)

Do not allow slanderers to stay on your leadership team! Do not allow slanderers in your board meetings! He who slanders others to you will slander you to others. It is just a matter of time! Psalm 15 describes people who will be allowed to stay in the house of God and those who will be allowed to remain as members. Once you have slandered the brethren, you can no longer be allowed to stay and dwell amongst us!

Lord, who may be allowed to stay in Your holy tent or reside in Your holy hill?

No slanderer can be allowed to settle in amongst us! Drive away slanderers! Let them go! Do not feel sorry for them! You are better off without a slanderer! To be a slanderer, is to be a devil! How can you accept a living, human, breathing devil into your midst?

Do not receive people who come with stories and rumours against their former church. Do not accept people who are filled with unverifiable, wicked and slanderous stories about their last employer.

Read the psalm carefully; Lord, who may reside in Your tent? Who may settle on Your holy hill? In other words; “Who can become a member of your church?”

Certainly, you should not receive a slanderer into your midst! Some people receive slanderers into their fold because they seem to be bringing news of the downfall of their competitors and brothers. Do not be fooled by this very common trick. The downfall or difficulty of your brother does not mean that you will do well.

4\. Evil will recoil on the slanderer.

LET EVIL RECOIL ON THOSE WHO SLANDER ME; in your faithfulness destroy them.

Psalm 54:5 (NIV)

Recoiling evil! Frightening! Evil will recoil on those who slander me! Evil will boomerang to the sender! Evil will boomerang to the speaker and writer of slanderous, damaging and defamatory articles!

A boomerang is a piece of wood that is so designed that when it is thrown it returns to the thrower. A slanderer will have the evil that he throws out return to him in a fantastic way.

A boomerang is something that does injury to the originator! The originator of the slander will have a thousand times more evil returning to himself.

I remember people who sat down at meetings, devising slander and saying with glee, “This story is electrifying. This will send shock waves!” They devised evil with the intent to destroy me. But there are scriptures and curses for all who devise such things. Evil will boomerang to them in shocking ways! Keep watching!

Evil will recoil and rebound against slanderers. This is what the Bible teaches. Every evil work of a slanderer will spring back against him with force and energy. If you sow the wind, you will reap the whirlwind (Hosea 8:7)!

Did Germany and Japan expect to bomb Europe, England and America and not be bombed in a more terrible and fantastic way? Indeed, by the end of the Second World War, both Germany and Japan were virtually flattened by bombs. They had sown the wind and reaped the whirlwind!

The evil they had unleashed on Britain and America, in the bombing of Britain and in the bombing of Pearl Harbour, boomeranged back to them a thousand times over. This is what the Bible teaches as recoiling evil! It is indeed frightening!
