### Chapter 2\

Who is a Slanderer?\
-----------------------------------------

YOU SHALL NOT GO ABOUT AS A SLANDERER AMONG YOUR PEOPLE; and you are not to jeopardize the life of your neighbor. I am the Lord.

Leviticus 19:16 (NASB)

1\. A slanderer is someone who acts on behalf of satan to attack God’s servants by intentionally spreading as much slime and scandalous information about them as possible. This is done to lower people’s estimation of God’s servants, and therefore reduce the scope of their ministry.

2\. A slanderer is a bitter personality who campaigns to lower the value and admiration that others have of God’s servant.

3\. A slanderer is a demon-possessed and extremely bitter personality who seeks to destroy someone he hates, by informing the public about confidential things, tales, secrets, stories and rumours that he feels will damage the person he hates.

4\. A slanderer is a jealous person who hates the success and leadership of another and who goes about intentionally sending malicious information to spread rumours, scandals, and unverifiable stories about people, who otherwise have a good reputation in the church.

5\. A slanderer is a disgruntled former employee who seeks to destroy his former place of employment by destroying its good image.

6\. A slanderer is a preacher who, instead of preaching and testifying about Jesus, spends his time maliciously sharing shameful and unfortunate news about a fellow Christian. A slanderer’s aim is not to win souls, but to inform the church of the mistakes of others.

7\. A slanderer has the aim of destroying the ministry of a servant of God who has a good and fruitful ministry, by spreading WhatsApp messages, messages on social media, text messages, stories, scandals, and questions about the life, character and ministry of God’s servant.

8\. A slanderer is a campaigner who goes around from person to person, to convince them that the servant of God they honour, and respect is an evil and unacceptable personality.

9\. A slanderer is a pastor who seeks to destroy and lower the reputation of the ministry he once belonged to.

10\. A slanderer is a low performer who seeks to justify to the public why he lost his job, by spreading false stories about his former employer.

11\. A slanderer is a smear campaigner. He goes around from house to house with his vitriolic stories and information. A slanderer is a campaigner, and he will call person after person diligently, to give information he believes will damage the ministry of another.

12\. A slanderer is someone who has lost all power to harm another minister, so he resorts to spreading lies, part truths, unverifiable stories and suspicions about God’s servants. The slanderer hopes that all he says will stick to and damage God’s servants.

13\. A slanderer is someone who hopes to portray a church or ministry as less acceptable, less commendable, less important and less impressive than people generally think it is.

14\. A slanderer is someone who writes, publishes, spreads and circulates malicious and injurious stories about another person. A slanderer is someone who attacks the character of a person.

15\. A slanderer is someone who casts a servant of God in a bad light. A slanderer is someone who attempts to belittle ministers of the gospel.

16\. A slanderer is satan personified. Satan is an accuser. A slanderer is an accuser. A slanderer is someone who attempts to discredit and damage a person, by directly accusing him of many things. A slanderer will speak many outrageous and preposterous accusations.

17\. A slanderer is someone who attempts to discredit and damage God’s honoured and anointed servants by speaking and writing against them, using insinuations and innuendos.

18\. A slanderer is a spreader of shame. A slanderer is someone who writes or speaks in such a way that shames and dishonours another person.

19\. A slanderer is someone who misrepresents another person to the general public. A slanderer writes and speaks incorrectly, falsely, improperly and unsatisfactorily in order to achieve his aim of hurting the person he slanders.

20\. A slanderer is someone whose aim is to lower the estimation of God’s chosen servant and to depreciate the Lord’s anointed in the eyes of the public.

21\. A slanderer is someone who releases, writes or publishes derogatory and malicious information designed to injure the reputation of a person. A slanderer is someone who speaks unfavourably about a person who is honoured in society, to make people dislike or disapprove of the person.
