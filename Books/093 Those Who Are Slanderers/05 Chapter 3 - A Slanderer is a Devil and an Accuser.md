### Chapter 3\

A Slanderer is a Devil and an Accuser\
-----------------------------------------------------------

Even so must their wives be grave, not SLANDERERS, sober, faithful in all things.

1 Timothy 3:11

And the great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called THE DEVIL, and Satan, which deceiveth the whole world: he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him.

Revelation 12:9

The aged women likewise, that they be in behaviour as becometh holiness, not false ACCUSERS, not given to much wine, teachers of good things;

Titus 2:3

The words SLANDERER, DEVIL, and ACCUSER are used interchangeably!

In the scriptures above, the words “accuser”, “devil” and “slanderer” are all translated from the same Greek word, DIABOLOS! This means that the words “slanderer”, “accuser” and “devil” are interchangeable.

The closest you will ever get to experiencing the devil physically is to encounter a slanderer. A slanderer is a person who goes around intentionally and energetically from person to person, spreading rumours, stories, messages and information to whip up disaffection against a leader.

I have encountered this kind of person a few times in my ministry. One time, there was a man who spent his energy, time and money going around from house to house to stir up hatred against our church. He continued this until all the neighbours of our church disliked us and became filled with hatred for us. Our neighbours even went on a demonstration against the church. This man surely went about as a slanderer to turn the hearts of the people against us.

Remember the scripture:

YOU SHALL NOT GO ABOUT AS A SLANDERER AMONG YOUR PEOPLE; and you are not to jeopardize the life of your neighbor. I am the Lord.

Leviticus 19:16 (NASB)

I once encountered a person who went around from person to person giving slanderous stories and unverifiable information about ministers in our church. By the time this fellow had finished spreading his wicked stories, there was confusion and uncertainty everywhere in the church. If you ever encounter someone who follows the Leviticus 19:16 description of a slanderer, and who has time and energy to go up and down, from person to person, with information and scandalous stories, you have encountered a slanderer!

These slanderers claim that they love the church and are fighting to prevent the church from falling into sin and decay. It is interesting how slanderers see themselves as righteous men who are exposing sin and correcting unrighteousness.

A slanderer’s scandalous rumours ensure that some people are damaged forever and are unable to ever serve the Lord again. It is difficult to get bad stories out of the minds of people no matter what you do.

Watch out for people who make phone calls, send messages, and make contact with people they do not even normally speak to, just to spread stories and give information against God’s servant!

YOU SHALL NOT GO ABOUT AS A SLANDERER AMONG YOUR PEOPLE; and you are not to jeopardize the life of your neighbor. I am the Lord.

Leviticus 19:16 (NASB)

It is that movement and energy of a slanderer, the moving up and down from person to person, the intent to defame, the intent to defile and to damage, that defines a slanderer. A slanderer is a living, moving, active human devil in your midst!

When a slanderer is sent against your ministry, it means a living, vicious devil has been sent to attack you. Some think that when the devil comes against you, he will come in the form of a red dragon with wings. Indeed, that old dragon will appear to you in the form of a slanderer. When a slanderer is sent against your ministry, it means a potent accuser has been unleashed against you. When satan visits your ministry, you will experience him in the form of a slandering personality and an accusatory person.

The effect of accusations and slander cannot be overemphasized. You will probably not understand it until you have experienced it! The scripture teaches that your entire ministry will be jeopardized because of a slanderer! Slanderers put the ministry into jeopardy.

You shall not go about as a slanderer among your people; and YOU ARE NOT TO JEOPARDIZE THE LIFE OF YOUR NEIGHBOR. I am the Lord.

Leviticus 19:16 (NASB)

To be in jeopardy means to be in a hazardous or risky situation. To be in jeopardy means you are exposed to loss, harm, peril, danger or death. A slanderer causes your entire ministry to be in danger of loss and harm.
