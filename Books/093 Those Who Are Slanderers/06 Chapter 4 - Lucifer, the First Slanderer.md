### Chapter 4\

Lucifer, the First Slanderer\
--------------------------------------------------

Thou art the anointed cherub that covereth; and I have set thee so: thou wast upon the holy mountain of God; thou hast walked up and down in the midst of the stones of fire. Thou wast perfect in thy ways from the day that thou wast created, till iniquity was found in thee. BY THE MULTITUDE OF THY MERCHANDISE they have filled the midst of thee with violence, and thou hast sinned: therefore I will cast thee as profane out of the mountain of God: and I will destroy thee, O covering cherub, from the midst of the stones of fire.

Ezekiel 28:14-16

Thou hast defiled thy sanctuaries by the multitude of thine iniquities, BY THE INIQUITY OF THY TRAFFICK; therefore will I bring forth a fire from the midst of thee, it shall devour thee, and I will bring thee to ashes upon the earth in the sight of all them that behold thee.

Ezekiel 28:18

Thou shalt not go up and down as a TALEBEARER among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I am the LORD.

Leviticus 19:16

Lucifer was the first slanderer. The behavioural pattern of a slanderer was first seen in none other than Lucifer who became satan. Every slanderer since then has followed the example that Lucifer set when he fell from grace.

Below, you will see the connection between the merchandise of Lucifer and the trading of a talebearer.

Lucifer’s Merchandise: Hebrew word: rekûllâh (Strong’s number H7404, Feminine passive participle of H7402) trade (as peddled) {merchandise} traffic.

Talebearer: Hebrew word: râkîyl (Strong’s number H7400; From H7402) a scandal monger (as travelling about): {slander} carry {tales} talebearer, A trafficker, trader.

The Hebrew words used for Lucifer’s merchandise are similar to words used for the word “talebearer”. A talebearer is a trader, a scandal monger! Lucifer trades in tales, scandals, stories, whispers and gossip. A talebearer is also a trader in scandalous stories and gossip.

1\. LUCIFER THE SLANDERER: A MAN OF PERFECTION.

Thou wast perfect in thy ways from the day that thou wast created, till iniquity was found in thee.

Ezekiel 28:15

Lucifer the slanderer presents himself as a good and righteous person. The slanderer will say, “You need me now more than ever. I am your friend.” “I have information that is vital for you. What I am telling you will save you and help you.”

The perfect disguise for a slanderer is apparent perfection. He seems to be a good person with a good heart. He gives the impression that he just wants the church to be pure and do the right thing. But he is full of vice and his hatred is directed at God’s servants! With smooth words and great blasphemies, he desecrates God’s house with his slime!

Lucifer used to be perfect! “Thou wast perfect in thy ways from the day that thou wast created, till iniquity was found in thee” (Ezekiel 28:15).

Watch out for seemingly perfect men who present a good front, but are busy spreading gossip and finding fault with everything.

2\. LUCIFER THE SLANDERER: FULL OF ENERGY AND MOVEMENT.

Thou art the anointed cherub that covereth; and I have set thee so: thou wast upon the holy mountain of God; THOU HAST WALKED UP AND DOWN in the midst of the stones of fire.

Ezekiel 28:14

Lucifer walked up and down amidst the stones of fire. He was full of energy and movement. He could not sit still for a moment. He had to see someone, he had to make many calls. He had to make contact with as many people as possible and spread his stories.

This is the pattern we see in Lucifer. “Lucifer” has a burden to move around energetically, to gossip, to find fault and to accuse. It is as though the slanderer has no other work to do than to spread bad and scandalous stories.

Initially, Lucifer moved up and down in the midst of the stones of fire in a good way. Today, he is still moving up and down, but to perform wickedness.

Always notice the unexplained energy of a slanderer when he makes phone calls and composes texts with malicious and damaging stories that he sends around the world.

When you encounter someone who is filled with passion to spread slander and rumour, you have encountered the devil in human form. That is the return of Lucifer; pacing up and down with his evil trade.

3\. LUCIFER THE SLANDERER: THE UNRIGHTEOUS TRADER.

By the multitude of your iniquities, IN THE UNRIGHTEOUSNESS OF YOUR TRADE You profaned your sanctuaries. Therefore I have brought fire from the midst of you; It has consumed you, And I have turned you to ashes on the earth In the eyes of all who see you.

Ezekiel 28:18 (NASB95)

Lucifer was a trader. He traded in tales, stories, slander, rumours, lies and defamation. This was his sin! The sin of his trade! What was his trade? The trade of scandal, rumour and gossip! Lucifer trades in rumour, fault finding, malice and defamatory tales.

Just like the talebearer described in Leviticus, Lucifer traded in scandal. The Hebrew word translated “talebearer” is the same root word translated as “traffick” or trade in Ezekiel 28.

Thou hast defiled thy sanctuaries by the multitude of thine iniquities, by THE INIQUITY OF THY TRAFFICK; therefore will I bring forth a fire from the midst of thee, it shall devour thee, and I will bring thee to ashes upon the earth in the sight of all them that behold thee.

Ezekiel 28:18

In Leviticus, the word “talebearer” or “slander” is the word RAKIYL which means a scandal monger; a trader in scandal, rumour, an informer and a slanderer!

In Ezekiel, the word “trade”, or “traffic” is the Hebrew word RAKAL, which means a trafficker or trader who goes about dubiously.

So, we see here that Lucifer had a trade and his trade was to spread evil stories. The scripture describes it as “the iniquity of thy traffick.” In other words, the sin of your dubious trade.

Lucifer was a trader in slander, scandal, rumour, stories, tales, gossip, libel, lies and defamation! That is why satan is called “the slanderer”!

Thou shalt not go up and down as a talebearer \[slanderer\] among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I am the Lord.

Leviticus 19:16

4\. LUCIFER THE SLANDERER: DESTROYED BY A FIRE FROM WITHIN.

Thou hast defiled thy sanctuaries by the multitude of thine iniquities, by the iniquity of thy traffick; THEREFORE WILL I BRING FORTH A FIRE FROM THE MIDST OF THEE, it shall devour thee, and I will bring thee to ashes upon the earth in the sight of all them that behold thee.

Ezekiel 28:18

Slanderers are destroyed by a fire that comes from within. The words of a slanderer lead to his own damnation and destruction. This is what the Bible describes as a fire from within. Slanderers destroy themselves because with time, people see through their actions and are able to tell that the slanderer is a devil in human form.
