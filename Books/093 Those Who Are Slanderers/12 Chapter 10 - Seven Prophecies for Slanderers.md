### Chapter 10\

Seven Prophecies for Slanderers\
-----------------------------------------------------

Thou hast heard their reproach, O Lord, and all their imaginations against me; THE LIPS OF THOSE that rose up against me, and their device against me all the day. Behold their sitting down, and their rising up; I AM THEIR MUSICK. RENDER UNTO THEM A RECOMPENSE, O LORD, according to the work of their hands. GIVE THEM SORROW OF HEART, THY CURSE UNTO THEM. PERSECUTE AND DESTROY THEM in anger from under the heavens of the Lord.

Lamentations 3:61-66

Jeremiah prayed and prophesied against the people who insulted him from morning to evening. He prophetically prayed about those who reproached him. It is time to deploy all spiritual weapons and truths against the slanderer. Jeremiah asked the curses of the Lord to be released against all his slanderers. A fight against the slanderer is a fight against the devil himself. Make no mistakes about that.

Remember that a slanderer is an accuser and a living devil. The word diabolos is translated as “devil,” “accuser” or “slanderer”. Therefore, you must fight the slanderer with equal strength and determination as you would fight the devil. Release all prayers and declarations just as the prophet Jeremiah did.

Seven Prophecies

1\. O slanderer, bearer of wicked stories and messages that corrupt and offend the house of the Lord! The sword of the Lord is flying in the realm of the spirit and will locate you no matter how far away you are. For you thought you were safe in your high tower, far away in a distant land. The sword of the Lord shall travel far and pierce your heart, O slanderer. The sword of the Lord shall enter into the house of the slanderer and scatter and destroy whatever is in the dwelling place of the slanderer.

For this is the day of the Lord GOD of hosts, a day of vengeance, that he may avenge him of his adversaries: and the sword shall devour, and it shall be satiate and made drunk with their blood: for the Lord GOD of hosts hath a sacrifice in the north country by the river Euphrates.

Jeremiah 46:10

2\. O slanderer! You shall go forward in your obstinacy and meet a great rock. For no one could counsel you! For no one could guide you anymore! No one could reason with you! No one could love you anymore! Therefore, thou shalt encounter a rock! Thou shalt encounter a hard block! Thou shalt encounter a concrete wall! For it shall be a supernatural rock, the rock of the Lord. The rock shall stop you and shall stop your slander! The rock shall be harder than your stubborn forehead! The rock shall break your head in pieces, O slanderer. For your destiny is to be suddenly destroyed by what is harder and stronger than thee. So shall it be!

The trouble they make for others backfires on them. The violence they plan falls on their own heads.

Psalm 7:16 (NLT)

3\. O slanderer, you shall be beaten with many stripes. Your wounds shall cover you. For every lie that you have told, you shall receive a stripe. For every falsehood you have peddled, you shall be stricken. For your multiple adulteries, you shall endure shame, defeat and poverty forever. O slanderer, you shall have many stripes. O slanderer, those curses that were meant for others shall be yours forever. For you said, I shall lie and deceive and shame God’s servant, but you shall be ashamed a hundredfold and your everlasting confusion shall never be forgotten! It shall come to pass upon the slanderer.

But the LORD is with me as a mighty terrible one: therefore my persecutors shall stumble, and they shall not prevail: they shall be greatly ashamed; for they shall not prosper: their everlasting confusion shall never be forgotten.

Jeremiah 20:11

4\. O slanderer! Thou shalt perish in the fire. For the Lord shall consume thee with a fire! You and all of your stubborn wickedness shall be consumed in a fire. For you said in your heart; I shall lie and steal and defraud the church of God. For your extortion and your blackmail, you shall be consumed in the fire that is determined against you and all that is yours. Your house, your dwelling and all your labours shall be consumed in a great fire. You shall not be able to stand in the fire of judgment!

Behold, all they that were incensed against thee shall be ashamed and confounded: they shall be as nothing; and they that strive with thee shall perish. Thou shalt seek them, and shalt not find them, even them that contended with thee: they that war against thee shall be as nothing, and as a thing of nought.

Isaiah 41:11-12

5\. O slanderer, you threatened and attacked the church. You should not have touched the church! For you have practised your bewitchments on many others but you should not have touched the church. It is hard to kick against the pricks! From now on, you shall perceive what it means to say “It is hard to kick against the pricks.” Fire! Fire! Fire! It is yours from the Lord! Many are they who lament and speak of your treachery but it shall be the fire of the Lord that will consume you, with your smooth and deceptive speeches! You cannot be restored. You cannot recant because your doom and destruction are determined by the Lord!

How long will ye imagine mischief against a man? ye shall be slain all of you: as a bowing wall shall ye be, and as a tottering fence. They only consult to cast him down from his excellency: they delight in lies: they bless with their mouth, but they curse inwardly. Selah.

Psalm 62:3-4

6\. O slanderer! You have caused devastation in the house of the Lord. You sowed confusion in the church of God. You never knew the effect of your defamations and libels. Darkness descends upon you now. O slanderer, this is a darkness that you cannot shake off.

I see the dust! I see ashes! Dust thou art and to dust you shall return. From ashes to ashes the slanderer shall return. From earth to earth and he shall be gone forever! These words shall follow fast behind the slanderer, for they will be pronounced over the slanderer.

Associate yourselves, O ye people, and ye shall be broken in pieces; and give ear, all ye of far countries: gird yourselves, and ye shall be broken in pieces; gird yourselves, and ye shall be broken in pieces. Take counsel together, and it shall come to nought; speak the word, and it shall not stand: for God is with us.

Isaiah 8:9-10

7\. O slanderer! Thou art a fig tree from which no one shall ever eat fruits again. From henceforth and forever more, no one shall desire your fruits anymore! Yea, your days of bearing fruit are over. No man eat fruit of thee from henceforth and forever more! Your ministry is over. Your life is over! Your fruits are not wanted anymore!

O slanderer, you shall encounter that which shall overcome thee. For you have said in your heart, there is none that can ever control me or counsel me. Henceforth you shall see that which overpowers and that which overcomes! Remember O slanderer, My church is the apple of My eye! You pierced the eye of the Lord when you attacked His anointed servants with your slander.

Therefore, you shall fall and you shall slip and you shall roll and you shall drop and you shall wallow in your own slime and in your own vomit and in your slander.

O slanderer, All that see thee shall stand in awe and say “Is this the one who caused the house of the Lord to tremble?”

And I will bless those who bless you \[who confer prosperity or happiness upon you\] and curse him who curses or uses insolent language toward you; in you will all the families and kindred of the earth be blessed \[and by you they will bless themselves\].

Genesis 12:3 (AMPC)
