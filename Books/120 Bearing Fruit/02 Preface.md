# Preface

I have always wanted to be useful to the Lord. Many years ago, I prayed a prayer from the bottom of my heart, and asked the Lord to make me fruitful. I am very grateful to the Lord for saving my life. If it had not been for the Lord, I cannot imagine where I would be.It is out of gratitude to the Lord that I desire to be fruitful. I think that this should be the desire of every sincere Christian. But bearing fruit is not as simple as it seems.In this book, I begin to share some vital truths about how God wants you to be fruitful.What are God’s expectations of me? God wants me to bear fruit after my own kind. There is nothing more difficult than trying to do something the wrong way. I have come to see that no matter how hard and how long you keep doing the wrong things, it still does not get you anywhere. Come with me on this journey as I share this important message about why you should bear fruit after your own kind.
