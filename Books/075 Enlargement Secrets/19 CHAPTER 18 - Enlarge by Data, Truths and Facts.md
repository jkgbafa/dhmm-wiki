### CHAPTER 18\

Enlarge by Data, Truths and Facts\
=======================================================

And Joshua rose up early in the morning, and NUMBERED THE PEOPLE, and went up, he and the elders of Israel, before the people to Ai. And all the people, even the people of war that were with him, went up, and drew nigh, and came before the city, and pitched on the north side of Ai: now there was a valley between them and Ai. And he took about FIVE THOUSAND MEN, and set them to lie in ambush between Beth-el and Ai, on the west side of the city.

Joshua 8:10-12

The Joshua generation are into data, information, statistics and facts instead of hearsay, rumours, gossip, stories, tale bearing, lies and deception.

Joshua knew how many people he had because he took the time to number the people. He set aside five thousand men for a particular purpose. There was no ambiguity or uncertainty about Joshua’s decisions. He knew exactly how many soldiers he had at his disposal. He waged his war based on data, truths and facts.

The spirit of data is the Spirit of truth. Truth is of God. Lies and deception is of the devil. Satan wants to deceive us with lies and deception. Satan wants us to base our ministries on a deception. Pastors who command crowds and money are often deceived about how far the gospel has reached. There is so much work to be done and very little has been accomplished

The Laodiceans based their ministry on a deception. Every ministry that is based on deception is doomed.

Because thou sayest, I am rich, and increased with goods, and have need of nothing; and knowest not that thou art wretched, and miserable, and poor, and blind, and naked: I counsel thee to buy of me gold tried in the fire, that thou mayest be rich; and white raiment, that thou mayest be clothed, and that the shame of thy nakedness do not appear; and anoint thine eyes with eyesalve, that thou mayest see.

Revelation 3:17-18

What were the deceptions of the Laodiceans? The Laodiceans thought they were rich but actually they were poor. They thought they were clothed but actually they were naked. They thought they could see but actually they were blind. This is the pitiful state of ministry that is based on a deception. Many ministries are based on self-deception. Most of us think that we are something that we are not. It is because many ministers believe that they have reached the world that they branch off into motivational speaking.

Many ministers are doing business instead of giving themselves to the ministry. This is because they are deceived about the extent of work that has to be done.

Why are so many preachers doing business instead of giving themselves wholly to the ministry? In our pitiful state, we must rather turn to the Lord and seek Him. We must seek the power of God and the anointing of the Holy Spirit.

Data and statistics tell us truths that are not easy to see in other ways. How can you know you are enlarged if you did not know what size you were before? Many people are oblivious to the realities of the state of the church. Even though the church will be the biggest thing in the world by the end of the age, the church has actually been on a decline for some time. Probably, the greatest reason for this decline is the loss of the focus of the church on the Holy Spirit and evangelism.

I have heard many people say that the church is increasing and growing, but I do not see the church growing everywhere. The data, statistics and facts on the ground do not show much growth.

Many souls have been left to find salvation for themselves. They are left to struggle with drugs, murder, addictions, alcohol, cigarettes, atheism, prostitution, fornication, homosexuality, secularism, pornography, violence, hatred, wickedness and darkness. Many people are subjected to false religions because the knowledge of Christ does not come to them through us.

In many nations, churches are reducing and emptying out! How many modern churches are conducting evangelistic crusades? How many modern churches are into door-to-door, person-to-person and soul-to-soul evangelism? How many churches are actually putting up new buildings? Most of the big cathedrals in the world are old and archaic buildings. Where are the new cathedrals?

If you actually count the people in your church, you will realise how little you have done. It will spur you on to work harder. Whenever I analyse how much I have done, I realise how little has been accomplished. There are seven billion people in the world. If you have published two thousand books, what impact will it make? God is calling you to make an impact in the world. God is calling you to be a saviour of the world.

Beginning from now, you will be spurred on by data, by truth and by realities! You are delivered from the deceptions of the Laodiceans. You and the members of your church will be listed among the army of the Lord that fights and never holds back. The spiritual giant in you is emerging to make an impact. I release you as a blessing to the world!

The happenings in your ministry all through the coming season will compel people to desire to serve God. From henceforth, your ministry will know no bounds. Deception and lukewarmness is a thing of the past! I declare a turnaround has taken place in your life and ministry.

When the Lord TURNED AGAIN the captivity of Zion, we were like them that dream. Then was our mouth filled with laughter, and our tongue with singing: then said they among the heathen, The Lord hath done great things for them. The Lord hath done great things for us; whereof we are glad. TURN AGAIN our captivity, O Lord, as the streams in the south.

Psalm 126:1-4
