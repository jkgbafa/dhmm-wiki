### CHAPTER 26\

Enlargement Is by The Guidance of the Spirit\
==================================================================

Now Jericho was straitly shut up because of the children of Israel: none went out, and none came in. And the Lord said unto Joshua, See, I have given into thine hand Jericho, and the king thereof, and the mighty men of valour. And ye shall compass the city, all ye men of war, and go round about the city once. Thus shalt thou do six days.

And seven priests shall bear before the ark seven trumpets of rams’ horns: and the seventh day ye shall compass the city seven times, and the priests shall blow with the trumpets. AND IT SHALL COME TO PASS, THAT WHEN THEY MAKE A LONG BLAST WITH THE RAM’S HORN, AND WHEN YE HEAR THE SOUND OF THE TRUMPET, ALL THE PEOPLE SHALL SHOUT WITH A GREAT SHOUT; AND THE WALL OF THE CITY SHALL FALL DOWN FLAT, AND THE PEOPLE SHALL ASCEND UP EVERY MAN STRAIGHT BEFORE HIM.

And Joshua the son of Nun called the priests, and said unto them, take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests bear seven trumpets of rams’ horns before the ark of the LORD. And he said unto the people, pass on, and compass the city, and let him that is armed pass on before the ark of the Lord.

Joshua 6:1-7

Expansion, enlargement, growth and improvement are possible when you receive supernatural guidance from the Lord. Beginning from today, your ministry will experience supernatural enlargement as you follow supernatural guidance. From now onwards you will lose confidence in human ideas and place your trust in God’s guidance. Every day will be a day of supernatural increase and unusual expansion. Your level is changing from glory to glory through the secrets in this book.

Joshua could never have defeated Jericho if he had used natural ideas. Jericho was a powerful city, completely surrounded by walls. The nation of Israel stood no chance against this ancient citadel. God spoke a word to Joshua and he believed it. “Take your choir, your singers and your musicians and march around the city for seven days. Then begin to shout and the walls will fall down.” Joshua did exactly that and experienced his very first taste of divine enlargement and expansion.

Do you want to experience enlargement? Do you want to expand the way Joshua and Caleb expanded into The Promised Land? Beginning from today, you will begin to enlarge into the will of God!

The Joshua generation is the generation that enlarges by supernatural guidance. We are nothing and we know nothing! What hope does a cow have, if it is walking by itself from Accra to Abidjan? Will it get there? Will it not be killed eaten long before it finishes that journey of five hundred kilometres? Indeed, we are the sheep of His hand. We have no hope of getting to our destination unless we are guided by the mighty Holy Spirit.

Divine guidance is the master key to enlargement. Joshua’s first step to enlargement was to follow divine and unusual guidance to possess Jericho. You must do what the Holy Spirit says. You must not attempt to trust your own ideas. You must not attempt to build God’s house with the world’s methods.

For more than thirty years the Holy Spirit has constantly given me unusual instructions. Each of these instructions has led to a great expansion and enlargement of the ministry. In 1991 the Holy Spirit led me to “give myself wholly” to ministry. I was like Joshua, standing before Jericho. None went in and none came out! There was simply no advancement or improvement in my life and ministry. But the Holy Spirit said to me, “Give thyself wholly to this classroom church filled with students and little children.”

There did not seem to be much hope in this little group of students. But as I gave myself wholly to the ministry, the little group of students grew into a mega church. Without supernatural guidance you will never become what God intends for you to become.

When the Holy Spirit spoke to me to write a book, I never imagined what would become of my book ministry. Most Christian authors are Americans. Non-Americans do not stand much of a chance in worldwide Christian publishing. However, following God has given me the greatest opportunity to bear fruits.

I expanded into evangelism when the Holy Spirit led me to begin crusades. You can hear from God better when you hear how he led others. That is why it is important to hear the testimonies and stories of other ministers and how they have been led by the Holy Spirit. I felt the Holy Spirit leading me to go outside our church. He said to me, “Your ministry must not only remain within your church. Your ministry must affect many other churches outside your denomination.” By following this guidance, I began to have conferences with pastors.

I once had a supernatural vision. I saw somebody encouraging me to have crusades and to pray for the sick. The Holy Spirit was directing me and leading me to hold supernatural Healing Jesus campaigns all over Africa.

Follow the Holy Spirit and you will come to a place of great expansion in the ministry.
