### CHAPTER 11\

Enlarge through Victory over King Debir\
=============================================================

Now it came to pass, when Adonizedek king of Jerusalem had heard how Joshua had taken Ai, and had utterly destroyed it; as he had done to Jericho and her king, so he had done to Ai and her king; and how the inhabitants of Gibeon had made peace with Israel, and were among them; That they feared greatly, because Gibeon was a great city, as one of the royal cities, and because it was greater than Ai, and all the men thereof were mighty.

Wherefore Adonizedek king of Jerusalem sent unto Hoham king of Hebron, and unto Piram king of Jarmuth, and unto Japhia king of Lachish, and unto DEBIR king of Eglon, saying, Come up unto me, and help me, that we may smite Gibeon: for it hath made peace with Joshua and with the children of Israel.

Joshua 10:1-4

The Joshua generation are a group that fight and conquer all five demonic powers that are out to prevent enlargement, expansion, development, growth and improvement. The Joshua generation will conquer King DEBIR.

King Debir is the next demonic power you need to fight in order to enlarge your ministry. Enlarge your ministry by conquering the demonic power of King DEBIR. King DEBIR will fight you because he is fighting God’s purpose.

DEBIR means pastureless. That is a demonic power that causes you to have no pasture to feed on. No pasture! No pastor! No teacher! It is a spirit of emptiness. It is a spirit of pride and emptiness that disconnects you from every fellowship that you belong to. The Lord is your Shepherd and He always wants to bring you to green pastures. The Lord wants you to have a pasture! Satan is trying to do the opposite. He is trying to make you pastureless. Satan wants you to be pastureless!

Your pasture is where you feed. Satan wants you to be pastureless. He wants you to have no teacher, no pastor and no feeder. Enlargement only comes when you have a pasture. Enlargement in your ministry will start by God revealing a pasture, a shepherd and a teacher to you. You must overcome the power of DEBIR that seeks to make you a man who is pastureless.

Then shall he give the rain of thy seed, that thou shalt sow the ground withal; and bread of the increase of the earth, and it shall be fat and plenteous: in that day shall thy cattle feed in large pastures.

Isaiah 30:23

A pasture is a collection of food for sheep. A pasture is vast amounts of food that is needed by the sheep. Without this collection, you will be pastureless.

I had been a Christian for so many years. I was serving the Lord and working in the church. But when the season of enlargement came, God revealed Kenneth Hagin to me. Suddenly, I was no longer pastureless. I had a whole collection of preaching material and a whole collection of books to read. Right before me were green pastures with amazing juicy green grass. As I fed from this pasture that God had revealed to me, I began to grow bigger in the spirit and I began to give birth to many sheep. I enlarged and expanded in the ministry because I overcame the condition of being pastureless. Indeed, I overcame the demonic King of Debir who stood to make me pastureless.

Four Types of Pastures You Can Have

1. The Bible is your biggest pasture. It is a collection of sixty-six books. You can feed on this collection for your whole life. When you begin to understand the Bible, you have discovered the most amazing pasture of your life. You are no longer pastureless. You have overcome the demonic power of Debir.

2\. A series of books is a pasture.

The PASTURES are clothed with flocks; the valleys also are covered over with corn; they shout for joy, they also sing.

Psalm 65:13

Most authors have a series of books. It is that series of books that makes up the pasture. Always remember that the pasture is a vast collection of materials that feed you. When God blesses you with an author, make sure you get all the books and read all of them because there are amazing secrets hidden in all of them. Have you ever wondered why sheep roam all over the field? They are searching for the secret meals that make all the difference.

3\. A series of audio messages is a pasture.

The demonic power of Debir is trying to keep you away from this large collection. The “Machaneh1” is an amazing collection of audio messages that will change your ministry forever.

4\. A series of video messages is a pasture.

Rise up today and get that series of video messages which is a pasture for you. This collection of videos of sermons will become an amazing pasture that will change your life. The demonic powers are constantly seeking to make you pastureless. You will begin to go higher as you feed on these amazing pastures God has for you.

That thou mayest say to the prisoners, Go forth; to them that are in darkness, Shew yourselves. They shall feed in the ways, and their PASTURES shall be in all high places.

Isaiah 49:9
