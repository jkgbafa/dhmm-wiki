### CHAPTER 7\

Enlarge through Victory over King Adonizedek\
==================================================================

Now it came to pass, when ADONIZEDEK king of Jerusalem had heard how Joshua had taken Ai, and had utterly destroyed it; as he had done to Jericho and her king, so he had done to Ai and her king; and how the inhabitants of Gibeon had made peace with Israel, and were among them; they feared greatly, because Gibeon was a great city, as one of the royal cities, and because it was greater than Ai, and all the men thereof were mighty.

Wherefore ADONIZEDEK king of Jerusalem sent unto Hoham king of Hebron, and unto Piram king of Jarmuth, and unto Japhia king of Lachish, and unto Debir king of Eglon, saying, Come up unto me, and help me, that we may smite Gibeon: for it hath made peace with Joshua and with the children of Israel.

Joshua 10:1-4

Joshua entered into enlargement by fighting five demonic powers and princes that came against him. Five demonic kings attacked Joshua at the outset of his enlargement campaign. In this chapter, we will look at the first demonic king that you must encounter and overcome in your quest to achieve enlargement and growth in the ministry.

First of all, to enlarge the ministry, you need to fight the power of King Adonizedek. Adonizedek represents a demonic king who came against the people of God. Whatever fights the people of God is fighting God’s purpose. Adonizedek means, “My lord is righteous.”

This speaks of a demonic form of righteousness. Adonizedek is the prince of self-righteousness. Adonizedek is the prince of self-deception. He is the prince of pride. Adonizedek is the prince of unrighteous judgment. Demonic righteousness is self-righteousness and unrighteous judgment. All forms of righteousness that do not come from the blood of Jesus are demonic. No one is good enough and no one will pass the test if placed under a microscope.

As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one:

Romans 3:10

This demonic form of righteousness is what possessed the Pharisees when Jesus came to this earth.

Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment.

John 7:24

Self-righteousness and unrighteous judgment are the demonic powers that prevented the whole of Israel from receiving the Messiah. They are demonic power that cut off an entire nation from its salvation. Adonizedek, is therefore, a major power that will keep you from the purposes of God.

Many good people do not enter the ministry. Many good people are morally upright. They do not quarrel, they do not smoke, they do not drink, they do not commit fornication, they have stable marriages, they have nice children, they have nice anniversaries, they give nice speeches, they are icons of the society, people speak well of them, they pay school fees for other people, they visit orphanages and they are pleasant people. At their birthday parties, people give speeches about how good they are and how wonderful and kind they have been through the years. Unfortunately, such nice people would not often go on an evangelistic crusade to win souls or become missionaries who plant churches in the world. The Great Commission, the lost and dying souls of this world, are often not the concern of these wonderful ideal Christians. In all your goodness, can you be described as someone who has hazarded his life for the gospel?

It seemed good unto us, being assembled with one accord, to send chosen men unto you with our beloved Barnabas and Paul, Men that have hazarded their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Acts 15:25-26

Do not under-estimate the power of self-righteousness and unrighteous judgment. Self-righteousness tells you that there is nothing lacking and nothing that you need to do. Self-righteousness deceives you. Adonizedek is the prince of self-deception. He is the prince of pride. That is why he met Joshua in the beginning of his campaign to enlarge the kingdom of God. Satan does not want you to do anything to enlarge the kingdom of God. Adonizedek is the power that meets you and says, “Go back, you are good enough! We do not need extremists and radical Christians. Just be normal. Stay at home and go to church for two hours every week.”

To illustrate the power of self-righteousness and unrighteous judgment to prevent believers from enlarging into the will of God, I want us to look at a parable that Jesus told.

And he spake this parable unto certain which trusted in themselves that they were righteous, and despised others: Two men went up into the temple to pray; the one a Pharisee, and the other a publican. The Pharisee stood and prayed thus with himself, God, I thank thee, that I am not as other men are, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, or even as this publican. I fast twice in the week, I give tithes of all that I possess. And the publican, standing afar off, would not lift up so much as his eyes unto heaven, but smote upon his breast, saying, God be merciful to me a sinner. I tell you, this man went down to his house justified rather than the other: for every one that exalteth himself shall be abased; and he that humbleth himself shall be exalted.

Luke 18:9-14

From this story, we see the power of Adonizedek, a demonic form of righteousness. We see its characteristics. Notice five pillars of self-righteousness that are revealed by Jesus’ parable.

Five Pillars of Self-Righteousness

1. A person with this form of self-righteousness is self-deceived and makes a wrong assessment of himself. Self-righteous people are self-deceived. Notice the man said, I thank God I am not as other men are. Jesus spoke this parable about certain people who trusted in themselves, that they were righteous, and despised others. To trust in yourself reveals a high level of deception! Most Christians have made the wrong assessment of themselves and feel that they are okay to live happily ever after without working for God.

2. A person with this form of self-righteousness makes erroneous comparisons with others. The Bible warns us against comparing ourselves with others (2 Corinthians 10:12). Paul said he would not dare to make any comparisons. It is not wise to compare because few things are equal. When a man falls into temptation and another does not, there are often factors that make things different. When you overcome a temptation, you may actually score less than someone who fell for the same temptation. There may indeed be factors which helped you to overcome. Stop comparing your church with churches that are not doing well. Stop comparing yourself with people who have fallen in sin. Many ministries are not doing the right things today. Nations which used to be the epicentre of Christianity have fallen away from true Christianity. It is a big mistake to compare yourself with those who are not walking in the will of God.

For we dare not make ourselves of the number, or compare ourselves with some that commend themselves: but they measuring themselves by themselves, and comparing themselves among themselves, are not wise.

2 Corinthians 10:12

3. A person with this form of self-righteousness has a list of things that he does not do. Notice this man said he was not an extortioner, he was not unjust, he was not an adulterer and he was not like the publican who was standing there. People who suffer from this demonic form of unrighteous judgment use the things they have not done as the greatest indication of their righteousness. Someone said, “I don’t harm anyone. I don’t steal. I don’t cheat. I do commit fornication but I do not sleep with people’s husbands.” This is an unfortunate way of assessing yourself. Self-righteousness and unrighteous judgment are grievous mistakes! Most of us have not done certain things because we have not been exposed to them, and not because we are righteous. Demonic self-righteousness will keep you from the purposes of God. It is nice that you have not committed certain sins before, but what have you done for the Lord?

4. A person with this form of self-righteousness has a list of rules that he keeps. Demonic righteousness is self-righteousness and unrighteous judgment. As they keep the rules that have been created, they develop a confidence in themselves as being good people. This self-righteousness is a set of rules that people keep to. Notice how this Pharisee was quick to mention his fasting and his giving. I fast twice in a week and I give tithes of all that I possess. Thank God for things you do. But there are many things that you are supposed to do that you are probably not doing.

5. A person with this form of self-righteousness is proud. It is only a person who is proud who would trust in himself and despise others. This man was full of pride and looked down on other people. Self-righteousness is synonymous with smugness, complacency, conceit, haughtiness, self-satisfaction, which develops into pretentiousness and full-blown hypocrisy. Self-righteousness is a fountain of pride.

Today, you must decide not to trust in yourself. You must trust in the righteousness that comes from believing and obeying God. Obey God and move into your realm of enlargement. Do not let your past achievements deceive you. Do not let your morality deceive you. Do not let the things you have not done minister pride to you. You are worse than the worst sinner. Lift up your head and listen to the voice of God. Follow his commandment and trust yourself to obeying and pleasing God. As you believe and obey, it is counted to you as righteousness.

For what saith the scripture? Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness.

Romans 4:3
