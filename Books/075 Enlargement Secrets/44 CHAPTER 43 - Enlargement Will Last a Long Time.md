### CHAPTER 43\

Enlargement Will Last a Long Time\
=======================================================

So Joshua took all that land, the hills, and all the south country, and all the land of Goshen, and the valley, and the plain, and the mountain of Israel, and the valley of the same; Even from the mount Halak, that goeth up to Seir, even unto Baalgad in the valley of Lebanon under mount Hermon: and all their kings he took, and smote them, and slew them. JOSHUA MADE WAR A LONG TIME WITH ALL THOSE KINGS.

Joshua 11:16-18

Expansion will take place for a long time to come. God is a God of enlargement and expansion. Get ready for a long fight! There is no substantial ministry that does not involve your whole life. Joshua made war for a long time. The younger, more eager generation, who serve God from their youth upwards, will serve God for a long time. When you begin to serve God from your youth, you will fight a good fight for many years. You will fight for a long time.

Expansion is not a nine-day wonder. Those who seek to enlarge do so for over a long period of time. They make war for a long time. They plant churches for a long time. They work for God for a long time. They have crusades for a long time. They enlarge and grow for many years.

Keys to Lasting Long in Ministry

1\. Honour your father and have a long-lasting ministry.

Honour thy father and mother; (which is the first commandment with promise;) That it may be well with thee, and thou mayest LIVE LONG on the earth.

Ephesians 6:2-3

Honouring your father is important because it activates a blessing that makes you last long in the ministry. Honouring your father makes you avoid the mistakes your father made.

2\. Honour your mother and have a long-lasting ministry.

Honour thy father and mother; (which is the first commandment with promise;) That it may be well with thee, and thou mayest LIVE LONG on the earth.

Ephesians 6:2-3

Honouring your mother is important. Honouring your mother equally activates a blessing that is important for your life and ministry. Honouring your mother causes you to sow a seed of faithfulness and love that you can expect to reap in years to come. The reward for honouring your father and your mother is long life.

3\. Get wisdom and have a long-lasting ministry.

Happy is the man that findeth WISDOM, and the man that getteth understanding… LENGTH OF DAYS IS IN HER RIGHT HAND; and in her left hand riches and honour.

Proverbs 3:13, 16

Through wisdom, you will be enabled to live long and last long in the ministry. People live much longer in places like Japan and Switzerland. Life expectancy in these countries is over eighty years. In these countries, a lot of science, technology and wisdom is used. Unfortunately, in other parts of the world, where less wisdom is applied, life expectancy is much shorter.

4\. Obey commandments and have a long-lasting ministry.

My son, FORGET NOT MY LAW; but let thine heart keep my commandments: For length of days, and long life, and peace, shall they add to thee.

Proverbs 3:1-2

Obedience is the master key to experiencing the blessings of God in your life. Almost all the curses in the Bible come from disobeying God. Curses arise in the ministry when we walk in disobedience. Adam and Eve would have been alive today if they had not lived in disobedience. The curse and overwhelming desolation that has engulfed the human race came through disobedience. Obeying commandments is always the reason for surviving for a long time and receiving many blessings.
