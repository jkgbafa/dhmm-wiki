### CHAPTER 42\

Enlarge by Switching from the Supernatural to the Natural\
===============================================================================

And the children of Israel encamped in Gilgal, and kept the passover on the fourteenth day of the month at even in the plains of Jericho. And they did eat of the old corn of the land on the morrow after the passover, unleavened cakes, and parched corn in the selfsame day. And the manna ceased on the morrow after they had eaten of the old corn of the land; NEITHER HAD THE CHILDREN OF ISRAEL MANNA ANY MORE; BUT THEY DID EAT OF THE FRUIT OF THE LAND OF CANAAN THAT YEAR.

Joshua 5:10-12

Follow the example of the Joshua generation who were able to switch from the supernatural to the natural when they needed to. Enlargement requires the ability to switch from the natural to the supernatural and do it well.

The children of Israel were instructed to stop using manna and eat the fruit of the land. They were to till the land like everyone else and become farmers. They were used to eating the supernatural supplies that came down from heaven. They had done this for forty years. Their switch to the life of normal farmers was seamless because they were an obedient generation.

It is important to excel in the supernatural as well as in natural things. Many spiritual people are unable to properly switch to do natural things. It is important to pray! It is important to wait on God! It is important to fast! It is important to worship. These are all supernatural things which God expects us to do. However, when God instructs you to do the natural, it is important to do it well.

Five Natural Things that You Must Excel in

1\. Leadership

So the LORD said to Moses, “Take Joshua son of Nun, a man in whom is the spirit of leadership, and lay your hand on him.

Numbers 27:18 (NIV)

Ministers of the gospel must be good at leadership. If you put me at the helm of a ship carrying a thousand containers, I would not know how to turn the ship left or right. I would not know how to enter a harbour and I would not know how to dock the ship.

I would also not know how to unload the ship. I would be hopeless and helpless at the helm of affairs. You can only expect chaos on the ship from someone like me who has no idea on how to properly guide a ship. This is how some ministers are when they are placed at the helm of affairs. They have no idea how to steer the affairs of the ship. They cannot take the ship forward or backward. They cannot unload the cargo of the ship. They cannot receive new containers and consignments. Many churches are aimless ships with incompetent captains. The pastor knows all about prayer, fasting and visitation but he has not got any clue about how to lead and steer the affairs of the ship on the high seas.

2\. Administration

And God hath set some in the church, first apostles, secondarily prophets, thirdly teachers, after that miracles, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, diversities of tongues.

1 Corinthians 12:28

Ministers of the gospel must fast, pray and worship but after that fasting, they must have a good administration for their church. Having reliable data, statistics, facts and figures is key to having a successful church. Many spiritual people are unable to do proper administrative work. A good administration is key to the survival of a successful ministry. Yonggi Cho, pastor of the largest church in the world, gave four important keys to church growth. One of these keys was the key of a good administration.

3\. Medical care

Drink no longer water, but use a little wine for thy stomach’s sake and thine often infirmities.

1 Timothy 5:23

Ministers of the gospel must take medical care seriously. Someone had a programme where he advertised a campaign against “devils, drugs and doctors”. Though he was a powerful man of God, he did not believe in the work of medical doctors. It is important that you allow the Holy Spirit to lead you to the right medical responses when you experience demonic attacks on your life. Many people are dying before their time because they do not believe in any of the medical advice that is available today.

4\. Legal work

Do everything you can to help Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their way and see that they have everything they need.

Titus 3:13 (NIV)

God will bless you as you do your legal work properly. Many churches have suffered because they did not do their legal work properly. It is almost as though spirituality sets aside common sense and everything that is natural. There are churches that do not have a proper plan of succession. Only a small fraction of people have proper Wills. God will deliver you from many evil spirits if you perform these mundane tasks properly. Follow the example of the Israelites who were able to switch from supernatural manna to corn.

5\. Finances

For this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting, and ordain elders in every city, as I had appointed thee:

Titus 1:5

Ensure that your finances are in order even though you are praying for God to supply your needs according to His riches. Decide to live without debts. Decide to live within your means. Decide to save money rather than to raise money. Decide to celebrate less and achieve more. Decide to give more and be liberal. Decide to waste less so you can have more surpluses.
