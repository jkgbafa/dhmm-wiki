### CHAPTER 4\

Enlargement Guided by Recent History\
==========================================================

And they answered Joshua, saying, All that thou commandest us we will do, and whithersoever thou sendest us, we will go. According as we hearkened unto Moses in all things, so will we hearken unto thee: only the Lord thy God be with thee, as he was with Moses. WHOSOEVER HE BE THAT DOTH REBEL AGAINST THY COMMANDMENT, AND WILL NOT HEARKEN UNTO THY WORDS IN ALL THAT THOU COMMANDEST HIM, HE SHALL BE PUT TO DEATH: only be strong and of a good courage.

Joshua 1:16-18

What has happened to the older generation is very important to the younger generation. It is important to know what happened to the older brothers and older uncles. What happened to them could easily happen to you. The new generation must learn from the mistakes of their fathers.

Enlargement will come to you if you will remember the mistakes of your fathers and mothers, uncles and aunties and avoid them. You will have an enlarged marriage if you learn from the mistakes of the marriage of your parents. You will have an enlarged ministry if you learn from what happened in your parents’ ministry.

Whenever something happens right before your eyes, you must consider it as a vision. Consider it as a direct warning from God for your life. God is speaking to you through an event that is taking place in someone else’s ministry.

For I tell you, that many prophets and kings have desired to see those things which ye see, and have not seen them; and to hear those things which ye hear, and have not heard them.

Luke 10:24

I want you to notice the scripture above. Jesus Christ was pointing out to His disciples that many prophets and kings would have liked to see and hear the things they had seen and heard. When something happens right before your eyes, it is a privilege God is giving you. Indeed, the physical things that are happening and that you are seeing and noting, are prophetic directions from God. Prophets and kings would like to see some of the things God has allowed you to see.

Joshua’s generation learnt from the murmuring, grumbling, complaining and disloyalty of the older generation. They realised how murmuring, grumbling, complaining and being disloyal had resulted in older ones missing The Promised Land completely.

Joshua and his team had no intentions on losing out on the enlargement that was coming up. They intended to steer as far from the rebellious ways of their fathers as possible.

In a remarkable statement they declared at the onset of their ministry that anyone who brings the slightest sniff of disloyalty and rebellion against authority would be eliminated with lightning speed and strength.

Enlargement will come to you if you will remember the mistakes of your fathers and mothers, uncles and aunties and avoid them. As you enlarge into your calling, you must remember all that has happened in the past. What has happened in the past happened as an example for you. History is not just a collection of stories. History is a set of lessons you must learn from!

The older generation of the children of Israel had rebelled against God. They refused to obey Him time and time again. They complained, they murmured and they grumbled incessantly and God counted the number of times they grumbled against Him. After they had grumbled against Him ten times, God told them that He had counted the number of times that they had grumbled against Him. Ten is the number of completeness. When you have ten, you do not need any more. That is why the Ten Commandments are all the commandments we need. Ten summarizes all that you will ever need. Murmuring and grumbling ten times was more than enough for God. That is what made God destroy them. God destroyed a whole generation because of their grumbling, murmuring and discontented attitude.

Because all those men which have seen my glory, and my miracles, which I did in Egypt and in the wilderness, AND HAVE TEMPTED ME NOW THESE TEN TIMES, and have not hearkened to my voice; Surely they shall not see the land which I sware unto their fathers, neither shall any of them that provoked me see it:

Numbers 14:22-23

When someone tells you about something that has happened, do not think you are just being told some gossip and some rumours. God is giving you information that is supposed to guide you. Your future success depends on how you handle the history that you are privileged to know. Receive wisdom from history and move into your realm of enlargement.
