### CHAPTER 14\

Enlarge with Equal Amounts of Spirituality and Wisdom\
===========================================================================

So Joshua arose, and all the people of war, to go up against Ai: and Joshua chose out thirty thousand mighty men of valour, and sent them away by night. And he commanded them, saying, Behold, ye shall lie in wait against the city, even behind the city: go not very far from the city, but be ye all ready: And I, and all the people that are with me, will approach unto the city: and it shall come to pass, when they come out against us, as at the first, that we will flee before them, (For they will come out after us) till we have drawn them from the city; for they will say, They flee before us, as at the first: therefore we will flee before them. Then ye shall rise up from the ambush, and seize upon the city: for the Lord your God will deliver it into your hand. And it shall be, when ye have taken the city, that ye shall set the city on fire: according to the commandment of the Lord shall ye do. See, I have commanded you.

Joshua 8:3-8

The Joshua generation takes supernatural, spiritual and natural aspects of life equally seriously. After the defeat at Ai, the children of Israel came out with a strategy. They decided to use the strategy of an ambush. They did not just deal with the accursed situation in a spiritual way. Dealing with the curse was the spiritual way of fighting the war. The children of Israel now needed a good wisdom strategy to help them win the war.

Enlargement involves strategy! Strategy is the art of using a series of wisdom steps to outwit your enemy. Enlargement involves dealing decisively and extremely with spiritual matters. Joshua and his team respected the importance of curses. They dealt with the accursed person and burnt the entire family of the disobedient person who had brought the curse into their camp. That is an extreme way of expunging the accursed thing from their midst.

And Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan the son of Zerah, and the silver, and the garment, and the wedge of gold, and his sons, and his daughters, and his oxen, and his asses, and his sheep, and his tent, and all that he had: and they brought them unto the valley of Achor. And Joshua said, Why hast thou troubled us? the LORD shall trouble thee this day. And ALL ISRAEL STONED HIM WITH STONES, AND BURNED THEM WITH FIRE, AFTER THEY HAD STONED THEM WITH STONES. And they raised over him a great heap of stones unto this day. So the LORD turned from the fierceness of his anger. Wherefore the name of that place was called, The valley of Achor, unto this day.

Joshua 7:24-26

After dealing with the curse, Joshua and his team developed an extremely good wisdom strategy for dealing with the enemy. Their war strategy was so good that it is used today to teach military tactics.

First of all, the wisdom of the children of Israel involved every one who could fight in this war.

Secondly, the children of Israel used a strategy to deceive their enemy. They drew them out of their city and burnt down the homes of their enemies. All this was the strong application of wisdom. Extreme spirituality with extreme wisdom and strategy!

The Joshua generation taught one of the greatest lessons of war. Don’t be drawn away from your work, your city, your calling by deceptive attacks. Deception will draw you away from God’s calling. Then your destiny will be burnt down to ashes.

All forms of enlargement involve both the natural and the supernatural. You need to deal with the curse because it is a reality. But you also need a strategy that will make you wiser and stronger than your enemy. Many Christians are either too natural or too supernatural. Wisdom will make you strong, not weak.

Wisdom strengtheneth the wise more than ten mighty men which are in the city.

Ecclesiastes 7:19

You Must Have Equal Amounts of Spirituality and Wisdom

1. Have equal amounts of spirituality and wisdom when you are doing academic exams. You must pray about your exams. You must even fast about your exams. After that, you must study as though your prayers will not count.

2. Have equal amounts of spirituality and wisdom when you are building the church. If you want to build the church, you must pray with all your heart and fast. You must wait on God. You must go to the prayer gardens and the wilderness to pray. It is important that you are as spiritual as possible. But after this, you must have a wise design for your church building. Many designs cannot be completed because they are too expensive and impractical. You must strategize and think through what you are building as though you do not even know about prayer.

3. Have equal amounts of spirituality and wisdom when you are growing the church. Church growth is another important area which involves high levels of spirituality and high levels of strategizing. You must pray for hours every day. You must wait on God. You must pray in the night. You must lie down in the presence of God. You must listen to messages and watch all the videos. You must read all the books. This is so important! After this, however, you must think and use strategy as though you have never prayed before. You must use maps, data, analysis, computers, buses and any other technology you can lay hands on to help you to build the church.

4. Have equal amounts of spirituality and wisdom when you are choosing a wife or a husband. When you are looking for a wife, you must wait on God. You must fast and pray. God will hear all your prayers. But that is not enough. You need to apply wisdom! You need to find out the temperament of the person you are getting married to. You must do certain medical check-ups to see whether you are really compatible. You must find out more things about the person you are praying about. Is the person mysterious? Is it a strange man or a strange woman? What exactly are you dealing with? Are your backgrounds similar or are you laying the foundation for incompatibility? Are you going to be an ill-married couple?
