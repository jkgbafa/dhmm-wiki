### CHAPTER 6\

Enlargement Is Born of the Spirit\
=======================================================

And it came to pass, when all the people were clean passed over Jordan, that the LORD spake unto Joshua, saying, Take you twelve men out of the people, out of every tribe a man, And command ye them, saying, Take you hence out of the midst of Jordan, out of the place where the priests’ feet stood firm, twelve stones, and ye shall carry them over with you, and leave them in the lodging place, where ye shall lodge this night.

Joshua 4:1-3

The Joshua generation who experienced enlargement from God were a super spiritual generation of young men. It is only by being super spiritual that you can experience enlargement and accomplish great things for God.

The children of Israel took twelve stones out of the midst of the Jordan river and took them to their lodging place. Enlargement started by Joshua obeying the Lord and taking the stones out of the river. The Jordan River speaks of the Holy Spirit. The stones that were taken out of the Jordan River tell us that Joshua and his team were a deeply spiritual group taking things only from the Holy Spirit. They were not interested in things that came from the secular world.

Anyone who attempts great things for God must do so by being deeply spiritual. Many pastors are not spiritual and that is why they cannot enlarge into the will of God. The Joshua generation was not just a group of young people who were just excited about sports and music. Many people feel that when you are doing a programme for young people, you must have a lot of sports, dancing and music. Churches hold youth camps and instead of taking the stones out of the Jordan river, they take the stones out of the soccer and basketball fields. Their principle is, “Give the children some sports! Keep them active and busy so that they will not have energy to live a sinful life.” The power to overcome sin is not found in making people exhausted through sports and other activities. The power to overcome sin is found in the cross. The power of God is released into the lives of young people through the preaching of the cross.

There is a generation of young people who will rise by being spiritual. Enlargement comes as you develop your spirit man. There are three parts to every man: the spirit, the soul and the flesh. If you develop the fleshly part of your life, you will become carnal. If you develop the soul part of you, you will become soulish. If you develop the spirit part of you, you will become spiritual. Spirituality is all about developing the spiritual parts of you.

1. Emphasize on the soul and become soulish:

Enlargement will not come by developing the soul. Your soul has to do with the mind, the will and the emotions.

People who emphasize on the soul give themselves to education and philosophy. Such people become great thinkers and logicians. They are theorists, academics and “truth” seekers. People who emphasize on the soul also become emotional. They find reasons for every emotional high and low. They give themselves to raising or lowering the emotion of the moment. Indeed, such people give themselves to music, love songs, love feelings and love poems. They constantly muse about relationships and desire relationships with the opposite sex. They are always teaching or exploring on what will make the relationship between a girl and a boy work better.

2. Emphasize on the flesh and become fleshly:

Enlargement will not come by developing the flesh. Perhaps, a stronger body and a healthier life will come by developing the flesh.

People who develop the flesh become body builders. They give themselves to health walks and health talks. They give themselves to the gym, to athletics, to exercise and to everything that will make them healthy and strong. Such people are constantly on one diet or another. Today, they eat eggs without egg yolk and on another occasion they have Macdonald’s hamburgers with ice-cold water. Such people are always drinking diet coke and watching their weight. Those who emphasize on the flesh spend a lot of time on make-up and also doing their hair. You cannot achieve much enlargement in God by becoming fleshly. The flesh dominates Christians whose lives are filled with these fleshly activities.

3. Emphasize on the spirit and become spiritual:

Enlargement will come to those who become spiritual and pick stones out of the Jordan River. It is time to pick your gifts out of the Holy Spirit.

People who give themselves to the Spirit become spiritual. Give yourself to the spirit world by giving yourself to the word of God and to prayer.

That he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, TO BE STRENGTHENED with might by his Spirit IN THE INNER MAN;

Ephesians 3:16

You become spiritual by developing the inner man - the hidden spirit within you, with the word of God. You become spiritual by becoming stronger in the inner man. Instead of emphasizing on the outward man, you must emphasize on the inner man. Your spirit feeds on the word of God.

As you feed on the word of God through audio and video messages, your spirit grows bigger. As your spirit grows bigger, it dominates your life. As you can see from the scripture below, the spirit man can actually grow.

As newborn babes, desire the sincere milk of the word, that ye may GROW thereby:

1 Peter 2:2

You become spiritual by developing the inner man and praying in tongues.

But ye, beloved, building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost,

Jude 1:20
