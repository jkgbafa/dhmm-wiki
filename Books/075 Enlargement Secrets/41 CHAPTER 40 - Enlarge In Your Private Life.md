### CHAPTER 40\

Enlarge In Your Private Life\
==================================================

At that time the Lord said unto Joshua, Make thee sharp knives, and circumcise again the children of Israel the second time. AND JOSHUA MADE HIM SHARP KNIVES, AND CIRCUMCISED THE CHILDREN OF ISRAEL at the hill of the foreskins.

Joshua 5:2-3

Enlargement is possible when you are prepared to work on the hidden aspects of your life. Expansion in the public eye is because you have expanded in the private realm. Circumcision is a painful private operation on the private parts. After circumcision the private parts are re-shaped and re-formatted. When you perform a circumcision on anything, it changes its shape and its look.

The Joshua generation are ready to have their private lives painfully re-shaped and re-fashioned so that they can be obedient to the Lord. All those who want to experience expansion and growth are ready to endure private pain and humiliation for the sake of the ministry.

Circumcision takes place on the private parts. You will never know if a man is circumcised or not, unless he tells you. Similarly, enlargement is possible when you are prepared to work on the hidden aspects of your life and ministry. People who do not work on the hidden parts of their lives only live to regret it. What is on the inside always comes out on the outside. Circumcision is an operation that simply brings improvement.

There are three private aspects of your life that you need to work on. There are three areas of your private life that need improvement.

1\. Circumcise your giving: Do a private operation on your private giving.

Take heed that ye do not your alms before men, to be seen of them: otherwise ye have no reward of your Father which is in heaven.

Matthew 6:1

Your giving should not be accompanied with boasting. Giving is not something you do to show off. Giving is something you do to show your love to God and to His house. Jesus said we should learn to give privately and reap publicly. If you work on your private giving you will see God honouring you in public.

2\. Circumcise your prayer: Do a private operation on your private prayer life. You will experience enlargement.

But thou, when thou prayest, enter into thy closet, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret; and thy Father which seeth in secret shall reward thee openly.

Matthew 6:6

You must overhaul your prayer life. You must be able to pray for hours on your own. There is no point in growing up without being able to pray for several hours in a day. If you want to be a minister, you must be able to pray for several hours in a day. Learn to pray for three hours! Learn to pray for seven hours! Learn to pray for twelve hours! God will reward you openly.

3\. Circumcise your fasting: Do a private operation on your fasting life. You will experience enlargement.

But thou, when thou fastest, anoint thine head, and wash thy face; That thou appear not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret: and thy Father, which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly.

Matthew 6:17-18

Learn to fast without anyone knowing that you are fasting. Learn to live a fasted life where you can fast for days without being under pressure to eat much. When you work on your fasting life privately, you will experience great enlargement.
