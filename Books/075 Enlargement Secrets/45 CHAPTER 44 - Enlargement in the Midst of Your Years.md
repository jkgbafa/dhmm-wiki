### CHAPTER 44\

Enlargement in the Midst of Your Years\
============================================================

Now Joshua was old and stricken in years; and the Lord said unto him, Thou art old and stricken in years, and there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed …. Now therefore divide this land for an inheritance unto the nine tribes, and the half tribe of Manasseh,

Joshua 13:1, 7

It is possible to enlarge and expand endlessly. You will enlarge endlessly in the way that Joshua and Caleb did. Beginning from now, you will get stronger and stronger according to the word of God! Instead of getting weaker and weaker, you will be declared stronger and stronger. Your path is shining brighter and brighter according to Proverbs 4:18. Joshua grew stronger and more purposeful as he served the Lord. This will also be your story! You will be called an old man with a young vision.

Your strength will be renewed every day! You will experience a change of levels: from glory to glory! These things will begin now and continue in your life for years to come. I declare you exempted from premature and unexpected death according to Psalm 91! Instead of losing all that you have, you will be taking delivery of loads of blessings. You will enter into great expansion in the midst of your years.

O LORD, I have heard thy speech, and was afraid: O LORD, REVIVE THY WORK IN THE MIDST OF THE YEARS, in the midst of the years make known; in wrath remember mercy.

Habakkuk 3:2

The Joshua generation will be old and stricken in years, but filled with the burden of God’s work. Joshua was an old man and yet he was burdened with the fact that there was much land to be possessed. The Joshua generation grew old and still had the burden of doing God’s will.

They shall still bring forth fruit in old age; they shall be fat and flourishing;

Psalm 92:14

Many ministries have metamorphosed into businesses as they got older. Some have changed into secular motivational institutions. Churches have accepted the doctrine and spirit of Sodom and Gomorrah. Ministers of the gospel who wield authority in large churches are in same marriages. Others are living in every kind of deception, duality and immorality. If the world wants to do their thing, they are entitled to carry on and they shall bear the consequences of it before God.

A person with the spirit of Joshua and the spirit of enlargement is not destined to diminish in his brightness and light. “Arise and shine, your light has come”! (Isaiah 60:1). The Bible does not say, “Arise and shine, your light has gone.” You will never be one of those whose light diminishes as you get older.

You must keep on enlarging and spreading the word of God into your old age. You are ordained to increase in the level of your brightness.

But the path of the just is as the shining light, that SHINETH MORE AND MORE unto the perfect day.

Proverbs 4:18

Expect God to be increasing the level of glory in your life. A change in the levels of glory in your life will be your testimony from now until your last day on earth. You will not start in glory and end in shame! You will become like Joshua who, according to scripture, was burdened even more with God’s work as an old man. You will love God more as you get older. The glory will shine even more on your face as you become an older person.

But we all, with open face beholding as in a glass the glory of the Lord, are changed into the same image FROM GLORY TO GLORY, even as by the Spirit of the Lord.

2 Corinthians 3:18

I declare that you shall be listed among the giants who ended well!

Members of your family will also be listed in the army of the Lord!

Your children and your children’s children shall be mighty in your lifetime!

Your light will not go out!

You are released as a blessing to the world!

You will emerge as a giant and a super-normal blessing to many people in the world!

Receive strength to enlarge endlessly in the way Joshua and Caleb did!

No matter how hard and harsh the world becomes, you will be gloriously exempted from distraction and failure!

Go in the power of this prophecy and experience something far above what others are wallowing in.
