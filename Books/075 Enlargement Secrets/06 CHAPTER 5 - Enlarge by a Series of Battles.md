### CHAPTER 5\

Enlarge by a Series of Battles\
====================================================

SO JOSHUA SMOTE ALL THE COUNTRY OF THE HILLS, and of the south, and of the vale, and of the springs, and all their kings: he left none remaining, but utterly destroyed all that breathed, as the Lord God of Israel commanded.

Joshua 10:40

Enlarge by a series of battles. Do not be tired of fighting!

Joshua and Caleb engaged in endless fighting from village to village, killing God’s enemies and taking new territories.

You must be ready for endless fighting. You must be ready for war! You must be ready to engage in one war after another.

Do not long for peace! Do not long for rest! Do not say to yourself, “Why do I have one issue after another? When will I have peace? When will I have rest?” Once you are in the season of enlargement you must expect one battle to follow another. You must expect endless fighting.

When Moses died, Joshua entered a season of endless battles. The list of wars that Joshua went through is so long that I can only produce a few of his battles. A man who is into enlargement will have many battles.

It is impossible to say how many battles you are going to experience as you fight your way to experience the enlargement from God. Just like Joshua, you can expect many battles as you press on to enlargement.

Battles You Can Expect

1\. Expect battles at home: Expect to have a battle with your parents, your family and your home. There may be serious conflicts at home because of your calling. You may have conflict at home because of your children.

Think not that I am come to send peace on earth: I came not to send peace, but a sword. For I am come to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter in law against her mother in law. And a man’s foes shall be they of his own household.

Matthew 10:34-36

2\. Expect battles in your bedroom: You may have battles in your marital bedroom. An anaconda or a python spirit will embrace you for life. This type of spirit slowly squeezes you for the rest of your life. The two snakes will not stop squeezing you until your life is over. Expect that battle to last your lifetime!

3\. Expect financial battles: As you progress in the spirit of enlargement, expect to have financial battles. It costs a lot of money to accomplish anything in the ministry. You will always need money and there will always be a fight about money.

4\. Expect battles over land: The devil will fight you about every piece of land. You must wage war and possess the lands that God has given you. Without land you cannot build the church and you cannot fulfil your calling.

5\. Expect battles with disloyal people: There will be traitors! There will be sad days! There will be people you love who will not love you back. There will be people you care for who will repay your love with ungratefulness and wickedness.

6\. Expect battles with wicked and unreasonable people: There are many unreasonable people who will not yield to reason. They become obstacles to your calling. There will be people who are stubborn, who never yield, never agree and who never bow to your wishes.

7\. Expect battles with liars and deceivers: There will be liars! There will be deceivers. David, the psalmist, encountered these wicked and unreasonable liars. You must overcome in all these battles.

Hold not thy peace, O God of my praise; For the mouth of the wicked and the mouth of the deceitful are opened against me: they have spoken against me with a lying tongue. They compassed me about also with words of hatred; and fought against me without a cause. For my love they are my adversaries: but I give myself unto prayer. And they have rewarded me evil for good, and hatred for my love.

Psalm 109:1-5

God’s power is with you for a series of battles and a series of victories. You must be resolute. Your mind must be made up. Do not tire easily. After fighting on the left-hand side, just turn to the right and continue the fight there.
