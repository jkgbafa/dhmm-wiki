### CHAPTER 3\

Enlargement through Meditation\
====================================================

This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt MEDITATE therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then THOU SHALT HAVE GOOD SUCCESS.

Joshua 1:8

Enlargement will come through meditation. Most people do not think about the word of God enough! By thinking about the word of God you will begin to have revelation. Revelation is a spirit. The Bible speaks of the spirit of revelation. You need the spirit of revelation if you are to achieve anything for God. A teacher needs a spirit of revelation! A pastor needs a spirit of revelation! A prophet needs a spirit of revelation! Revelation will come through meditation.

Joshua’s mission was to enlarge into The Promised Land. He was to occupy more land, more territories and more space. He was to enlarge until he displaced the enemies of God. He needed a master key to become successful. God gave him the master key of meditation. Meditate upon the word of God!

What is Meditation?

1\. Meditation is the art of becoming successful. Joshua was told to meditate on the Word and become a success. Meditation was the master key given to Joshua so that he could be successful. Has it ever occurred to you that successful people are deep thinkers? You do need to think a little deeply if you want to experience salvation. You do need to reflect a little more deeply on the word of God if you want to be successful. When I meditated on the scripture, “Give thyself wholly to these things” I received revelation and jumped out of practicing medicine into full-time ministry.

Meditate upon these things; give thyself wholly to them; that thy profiting may appear to all.

1 Timothy 4:15

2\. Meditation is the art of extracting wisdom! Meditation is the art of becoming successful by extracting the fearful instructions out of God’s word. It is the art of becoming successful by developing a fear of God. Meditation is the art of becoming successful by extracting the details of things you have been told to do.

Meditation is the art of extracting justice, equity, counsel and understanding out of the word of God. You may receive a proverb but you will not get the wisdom out of it. You may hear a dark saying but you will not get the counsel or the instructions out of it. Meditation on the Law is the master key to extracting wonderful revelations for yourself.

3\. Meditation is the art of thinking deeply. Few precious metals are found on the surface of the earth. It is those who dig deep who find these precious minerals. When you meditate on the Word, it shows you are trying to go deeper to get the real valuables out of the Word. The lack of meditation is the reason why people do not understand or believe in the wisdom of the word of God. The lack of meditation is the reason why people hear dark sayings and proverbs but do not get anything out of them. A lack of meditation is the reason why the prophecies in the word of God are disregarded. God’s word is real and it is going to come to pass.

4\. Meditation is the art of repetition. Meditation speaks of repeating something over and over. When you listen to a message over and over you are meditating on the message. You are attempting to get the treasure out of the message. When you repeat words to yourself, you are attempting to extract the knowledge and the wisdom out of the word you are repeating. The lack of meditation is the lack of repeating something to yourself.

The lack of meditation is the reason why people read the Word and are still not wise. The lack of meditation is the reason why people listen to instructions and still do not follow them. If you repeat something over and over, you will remember to do it. People listen to messages and do not understand the dark sayings and proverbs in them. They listen to messages and do not notice the prophecies in them. They listen to preaching and miss the instructions God has for them. People miss out because they listen to messages only once. Listening to things once, will make you miss the key of meditation.

Seven Reasons for Meditation

1. You must meditate to have wisdom. You will not have wisdom if you do not meditate. The proverbs you know will not lead you to know wisdom and instruction. Rather, meditation will lead to the infiltration of wisdom into your soul.

The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel; to know wisdom and instruction; to perceive the words of understanding;

Proverbs 1:1-2

2. You must meditate to know justice, judgment and equity. Proverbs are the statements that will release wisdom, justice, judgment and equity into your life. However, you will not really know justice and judgment until you learn to meditate. You may know the proverbs, but the wisdom, justice and equity in them will not come to you until you meditate.

The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel; … To receive the instruction of wisdom, justice, and judgment, and equity;

Proverbs 1:1, 3

3. You must meditate to have subtilty, knowledge and discretion. Without meditation you will never really know much. Repetition makes knowledge sink into your mind. Start repeating things to yourself and thinking about God’s word so that you become knowledgeable through meditation.

The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel; ... To give subtilty to the simple, to the young man knowledge and discretion.

Proverbs 1:1, 4

4. You must meditate to increase learning. You start learning when you start meditating. Increase your rate of learning new things by increasing your rate of meditation.

A wise man will hear, and will increase learning; and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels:

Proverbs 1:5

5. You must meditate to attain wise counsel.

A wise man will hear, and will increase learning; and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels:

Proverbs 1:5

6. You must meditate to understand proverbs and dark sayings.

To understand a proverb, and the interpretation; the words of the wise, and their dark sayings.

Proverbs 1:6

7. You must meditate to develop the fear of the Lord which is the beginning of wisdom.

The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge: but fools despise wisdom and instruction.

Proverbs 1:7
