### CHAPTER 34\

Enlarge by the Fathers’ Instructions\
==========================================================

The LORD had commanded his servant Moses to do all of those things. Moses had passed that command on to Joshua. And Joshua carried it out. He did everything the LORD had commanded Moses.

Joshua 11:15 (NIRV)

In order to experience enlargement, you must follow the instructions that God gives to you. But you must also take note of the instructions given to the fathers that existed before you.

The instructions given to your fathers may be very relevant for your life and ministry.

The Joshua generation respects all the instructions given to fathers before them. Notice that the scripture above reveals that Joshua completely fulfilled the instructions given to Moses (and not to Joshua).

Throughout my life and ministry, I have always taken note of instructions given to fathers who lived before me. They have formed an important part of the things that guide me in the ministry. You must remember what God spoke to your fathers about and consider them as guidance for your life and ministry.

I remember listening to Kenneth Hagin as he spoke about a vision he had of Jesus. He had had an accident in which he fell from the stage and broke his hand. The Lord appeared to him and explained what had happened. The Lord said He had allowed the devil to attack him.

He continued, “Instead of being angry with Me for allowing this to happen, you should be grateful that I allowed this to happen so that I could get your attention.” Then He rebuked Kenneth Hagin for paying more attention to the teaching ministry than to the prophetic ministry.

He concluded, “If I had not gotten your attention to make you change course, you would not have lived beyond the age of fifty-five.” Indeed, I shivered when I read this story and have always been concerned that I would not make the same mistake.

I was very happy when I was fifty-five years old because I felt that I had crossed an important barrier. To wrongfully give attention to one calling above another is a serious mistake. I also learnt that little accidents and events are spiritually orchestrated. Nothing happens by chance! Kenneth Hagin described how the wood on the stage was very well polished and smooth and that is how come he slipped and fell on the stage.

He described how a tape recorder had been placed at the right-hand side of the stage and how he fell on it. Kenneth Hagin described how the Lord spoke to him audibly in the car as they rushed to the hospital. I learnt how a combination of the natural and the supernatural were working in the life of this man of God.

The messages given to your fathers will always be important to you. The message given to Moses by the Lord was important to Joshua. The message was not given directly to Joshua but God’s message to Moses was equally important to Joshua.

I remember listening to Derek Prince as he spoke about God commissioning him to the ministry. He said that God had told him to be obedient in little things as well as in big things. He stressed on how God had showed him that it was important to be as obedient in little things as in big things. Many people ignore the little things that turn out to be as important as the big things. When I heard him describing his calling, I took it as an instruction for myself. It was important for me to be obedient in little things as well as in big things. Enlargement comes when you listen to and apply instructions given to your fathers.

I remember reading from Reinhard Bonnke on how the Lord gave him a vision about his ministry. In the vision he saw a very large ship that was manoeuvring its way down a river. The ship was unable to turn around within the river. The Lord told him that his ministry was not versatile and easily manoeuvrable. He needed to become more versatile and flexible so that the Spirit would lead him more easily.

Every time I meditated on that vision, I sensed God speaking to me to be flexible and versatile in my own ministry. It is easy for a ministry to become too large, too heavy and not easy to control and direct. When you are too stiff, God cannot use you to do many things.

I remember listening to Yonggi Cho preaching. He spoke about how he had had a heart problem and seen a Japanese doctor who advised him to play golf. The doctor explained how he would add ten more years to his life if he played golf. He was explaining to the congregation why it was important for him to play golf. When I listened to Yonggi Cho sharing these things, I felt that God was showing me what was important for my life.

God spoke to Yonggi Cho about the things that are important for church growth. First of all, prayer, good preaching, the cell system and a good administration. Out of these four things, he felt the Lord showing him that prayer was the most important pillar for church growth.

These are not things that God spoke to me about. They are things that God spoke to my fathers about. The Joshua generation is keen to follow instructions given to the fathers. You do not need to have an instruction given to you directly. Once an instruction is given to your father it will work for you too.

Instructions given to fathers are like precious gems. They work for your fathers and they will work for you too.

I believe God spoke to James McKeown, the father of the Church of Pentecost, a denomination of over ten thousand churches. McKeown handed over his church to his leaders and allowed the church to run without him. This gave the Church of Pentecost the ability to survive beyond his lifetime and into the future. Seeing how the Lord led James McKeown encouraged me to follow the same pattern of handing things over whilst I am still around.

Enlargement will surely come when you listen carefully to hear what is said to your fathers and predecessors. It is time to open your ears and listen to the stories and testimonies of the fathers. It will bring great enlargement to your life.
