### CHAPTER 22\

Enlarge Suddenly\
======================================

JOSHUA THEREFORE CAME UNTO THEM SUDDENLY, and went up from Gilgal all night. And the Lord discomfited them before Israel, and slew them with a great slaughter at Gibeon, and chased them along the way that goeth up to Bethhoron, and smote them to Azekah, and unto Makkedah.

Joshua 10:9-10

Expansion and enlargement take place when you act suddenly and with decisive accuracy. The Joshua generation fight suddenly and without warning. You will overcome the enemy when you learn to attack unexpectedly.

There are many things that will not be done unless they are done suddenly. Many enemies cannot be overcome unless they are overcome suddenly. When an enemy knows that you are planning to destroy him, he prepares himself and therefore becomes ten times stronger. Would you want to fight an enemy who is ten times stronger or ten times weaker?

The greatest evil that can happen to you in ministry is disloyalty. Disloyalty is the undoing and the dismantling of what you are building by the people within your camp. Satan has always wanted to work from within because it is easier for him to work from within. Enemies within are more difficult to recognize. It is more difficult to fight an enemy that you cannot recognize. The lines are blurred when the enemy is near you.

Enlargement will come when you overcome enemies who destroy your growth. Disloyal people are those who take away from what you are building. Disloyalty is overcome by sudden and unexpected moves. All treacherous people deserve to have surprise attacks which they have no idea about.

You must learn to make sudden moves so that you can overcome your disloyal enemies. You will overcome disloyal people if you learn to make sudden moves.

When you are fighting disloyal people, they often think they can outsmart you. Disloyal people are always scheming, planning and murmuring whilst they are with you. They do not love you but they constantly pretend that they love and admire you. You can overcome treacherous and disloyal people with sudden lightning strikes.

How can you make successful, bold and sudden lightning strikes against your enemy? The key to sudden lightning strikes against your enemy is patient planning. Your plans must be well thought through. You have to analyse the steps you need to take to unravel your enemy.

Analyse how you are connected to your enemy!

Analyse why your enemy has any power in your life!

Analyse the different interactions you have with disloyal elements!

After you have done your analysis, begin to weaken your enemy by dislodging him from your life in stages. Your final strike to absolutely remove the enemy must be sudden, swift and unexpected.

Sometimes people want to enlarge by striking out at the enemy suddenly without thinking through what they are doing. You must think through everything you are planning to do.

Moves that are not well thought through will only bring more confusion. You must think through and plan all your steps carefully. When you move against your enemy, he must have no answer. It is important to make your enemy accept in his heart that he has been defeated. God has given you the supernatural ability and wisdom to move suddenly against powerful, wicked, treacherous enemies that have surrounded you. The time for your sudden strikes has come! The time to shake off disloyalty that is planted around you will come through careful planning.
