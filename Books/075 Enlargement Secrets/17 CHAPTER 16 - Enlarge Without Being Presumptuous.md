### CHAPTER 16\

Enlarge Without Being Presumptuous\
========================================================

And Joshua and all Israel made as if they were beaten before them, and fled by the way of the wilderness. And all the people that were in Ai were called together to pursue after them: and they pursued after Joshua, and were drawn away from the city. And there was not a man left in Ai or Bethel, that went not out after Israel: and they left the city open, and pursued after Israel. And the Lord said unto Joshua, Stretch out the spear that is in thy hand toward Ai; for I will give it into thine hand. And Joshua stretched out the spear that he had in his hand toward the city.

And the ambush arose quickly out of their place, and they ran as soon as he had stretched out his hand: and they entered into the city, and took it, and hasted and set the city on fire. And WHEN THE MEN OF AI LOOKED BEHIND THEM, THEY SAW, AND, BEHOLD, THE SMOKE OF THE CITY ASCENDED UP TO HEAVEN, AND THEY HAD NO POWER TO FLEE this way or that way: and the people that fled to the wilderness turned back upon the pursuers.

Joshua 8:15-20

The people of Ai made a great mistake when they rushed out against the children of Israel. They assumed that they would win the second round because the first time had been so easy.

You must enlarge without being presumptuous! From this time onwards you will experience unlimited enlargement in your life and ministry.

You must enlarge without absolutely exposing yourself to danger. The Joshua generation will not leave their city open to attack, no matter what they are doing or achieving. It is important to remain humble and realise that you can still be defeated.

You may have had an early victory over your enemy. But that enemy can rise up again. There is always the possibility of a greater and more sophisticated attack from your enemy.

At the beginning of the Second World War, Adolf Hitler fought with the British and the French. Adolf Hitler and the German Armed Forces fought against and defeated the French and the British. In 1940, Britain was totally defeated and driven out of France. Indeed, their whole army was almost wiped out in Dunkirk.

However, on the 6th of June 1944, this same British army returned to Europe and fought against the same Germans. This time, they defeated them overwhelmingly and marched all the way to Berlin to take Hitler out of power. Do not under-estimate an enemy you seem to have defeated before. Notice that the devil does not go away forever.

And when the devil had ended all the temptation, he departed from him for a season.

Luke 4:13

Three Times You Must Not be Presumptuous

1\. Do not assume that you can minister without praying. Prayer is a defence. Prayer is part of the armour of God. Many ministers do not pray. When you do not pray, it often seems as though it does not have any bad effects on you. However, prayerlessness creates a dangerous gap in your armour.

And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God: Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints;

Ephesians 6:17-18

2\. Do not assume that you can live an immoral life and get away with it. You may not be discovered today but it will surely destroy you. Babylon is fallen because of her fornication. Fornication can turn you into a fallen minister. The fact that you have not been discovered does not mean that your fornication and immorality have no effect.

And there followed another angel, saying, Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication.

Revelation 14:8

3\. Do not assume that you can do ministry without preparation. Preparation and study are necessary for the ministry. When you do not study or prepare you will be ashamed. The people of Ai were ashamed because they assumed that the children of Israel would be a small matter for them. They rushed out unprepared and were destroyed.

Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

2 Timothy 2:15
