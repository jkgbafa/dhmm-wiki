### CHAPTER 21\

Enlarge Your Sacrifices\
=============================================

And Joshua burnt Ai, and made it an heap for ever, even a desolation unto this day. And the king of Ai he hanged on a tree until eventide: and as soon as the sun was down, Joshua commanded that they should take his carcase down from the tree, and cast it at the entering of the gate of the city, and raise thereon a great heap of stones, that remaineth unto this day. THEN JOSHUA BUILT AN ALTAR UNTO THE LORD GOD OF ISRAEL IN MOUNT EBAL,

Joshua 8:28-30

God honours those who sacrifice to Him. Beginning from now, strange doors of favour will be opening onto you because of the sweet sacrifices you are making to the Lord. You will enter into new dimensions because of your sacrifices. All through the next season of your life, you will experience goodness, mercies and loads of blessings because of your great sacrifices for the Lord.

Your sacrifices to God are important in determining how far you will go. There is no one who enlarges his life and ministry without enlarging his sacrifice! Modern Christians want to get away with not paying the price. You will surely pay the price if you want to serve God. You will become a surprise to yourself and to your world only if you enlarge your sacrifice (1 Samuel 10:11-12).

What great sacrifices have you made for God? Joshua is known for fighting wars and taking territories. Is it not amazing that he was also a man of sacrifice? A man who built an altar onto the Lord! Go into the lives of all those who have been used greatly by the Lord. You will see the sacrifices they made for the Lord. Sacrifices are abundant in the lives of Bible characters and they are also evident in the lives of modern Christians who have made great strides for the Lord. You too will make great strides for the Lord! It will be said of you, “We have seen strange things today.”

And they were all amazed, and they glorified God, and were filled with fear, saying, WE HAVE SEEN STRANGE THINGS TO DAY.

Luke 5:26

Sacrifice is the door-opening key. Sacrifice changes your size in the spirit. Everyone who made sacrifices to God experienced a change in size, proportion and magnitude of ministry. God is the Creator of heaven and of earth. He is everything. We are nothing. If you do not turn your attention to Him and honour Him for who He is, you must not expect to be honoured. Check the Bible out and you will see that everyone who honours God is honoured in return. God ignores the people who ignore Him!

Do you want God to ignore you? Do you want God to honour you? Will you join the long list of those who choose to honour God in their lives, or will you be among those who do not honour God with a sacrifice?

Wherefore the Lord God of Israel saith, I said indeed that thy house, and the house of thy father, should walk before me for ever: but now the Lord saith, Be it far from me; for THEM THAT HONOUR ME I WILL HONOUR, and they that despise me shall be lightly esteemed.

1 Samuel 2:30

Those Who Sacrifice

1\. Abel sacrificed to the Lord and he was accepted before God for all eternity.

And Abel, he also brought of the firstlings of his flock and of the fat thereof. And the Lord had respect unto Abel and to his offering:

Genesis 4:4

2\. Noah made a sacrifice to the Lord and he became one of the heroes of faith.

And Noah builded an altar unto the LORD; and took of every clean beast, and of every clean fowl, and offered burnt offerings on the altar. And the LORD smelled a sweet savour; and the LORD said in his heart, I will not again curse the ground any more for man’s sake; for the imagination of man’s heart is evil from his youth; neither will I again smite any more every thing living, as I have done.

Genesis 8:20-21

3\. Abraham repeatedly built altars and made large sacrifices to the Lord. Abraham became the father of faith for the whole world because he was enlarged in the area of sacrifice.

And the Lord appeared unto Abram, and said, Unto thy seed will I give this land: and there builded he an altar unto the Lord, who appeared unto him. And he removed from thence unto a mountain on the east of Bethel, and pitched his tent, having Bethel on the west, and Hai on the east: and there he builded an altar unto the Lord, and called upon the name of the Lord.

Genesis 12:7-8

Then Abram removed his tent, and came and dwelt in the plain of Mamre, which is in Hebron, and built there an altar unto the Lord.

Genesis 13:18

And he took unto him all these, and divided them in the midst, and laid each piece one against another: but the birds divided he not. And when the fowls came down upon the carcases, Abram drove them away.

And when the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell upon Abram; and, lo, an horror of great darkness fell upon him. And he said unto Abram, Know of a surety that thy seed shall be a stranger in a land that is not theirs, and shall serve them; and they shall afflict them four hundred years; And also that nation, whom they shall serve, will I judge: and afterward shall they come out with great substance.

Genesis 15:10-14

4\. Moses made many sacrifices onto the Lord. Moses enlarged the concept of sacrifice. He demanded sacrifices from Pharaoh so that he could offer them to the Lord. He made sacrifices a law.

And Moses said, Thou must give us also sacrifices and burnt offerings, that we may sacrifice unto the Lord our God.

Exodus 10:25

And Moses built an altar, and called the name of it Jehovahnissi: For he said, because the Lord hath sworn that the Lord will have war with Amalek from generation to generation.

Exodus 17:15-16

5\. Isaac made many sacrifices to the Lord. Isaac was multiplied and blessed because of his great sacrifices to the Lord.

And he went up from thence to Beersheba. And the Lord appeared unto him the same night, and said, I am the God of Abraham thy father: fear not, for I am with thee, and will bless thee, and multiply thy seed for my servant Abraham’s sake. And he builded an altar there, and called upon the name of the Lord, and pitched his tent there: and there Isaac’s servants digged a well.

Genesis 26:23-25

6\. Jacob enlarged in his sacrifices to the Lord. Through his large sacrifices, he became the father of a great nation.

And Jacob came to Shalem, a city of Shechem, which is in the land of Canaan, when he came from Padanaram; and pitched his tent before the city. And he bought a parcel of a field, where he had spread his tent, at the hand of the children of Hamor, Shechem’s father, for an hundred pieces of money. AND HE ERECTED THERE AN ALTAR, AND CALLED IT ELELOHE-ISRAEL.

Genesis 33:18-20

And God said unto Jacob, Arise, go up to Bethel, and dwell there: and MAKE THERE AN ALTAR UNTO GOD, that appeared unto thee when thou fleddest from the face of Esau thy brother.

Genesis 35:1

So Jacob came to Luz, which is in the land of Canaan, that is, Bethel, he and all the people that were with him. And he built there an altar, and called the place Elbethel: because there God appeared unto him, when he fled from the face of his brother.

Genesis 35:6-7

7\. Samuel made sacrifices to the Lord. At the first mention of Samuel, we see him organising a sacrifice in the city. God honoured Samuel as one of the greatest prophets ever.

And Samuel took a sucking lamb, and offered it for a burnt offering wholly unto the Lord: and Samuel cried unto the Lord for Israel; and the Lord heard him.

1 Samuel 7:9

And as they went up the hill to the city, they found young maidens going out to draw water, and said unto them, is the seer here? And they answered them, and said, He is; behold, he is before you: make haste now, for he came to day to the city; for there is a sacrifice of the people to day in the high place: As soon as ye be come into the city, ye shall straightway find him, before he go up to the high place to eat: for the people will not eat until he come, because he doth bless the sacrifice; and afterwards they eat that be bidden. Now therefore get you up; for about this time ye shall find him. And they went up into the city: and when they were come into the city, behold, Samuel came out against them, for to go up to the high place.

1 Samuel 9:11-14

8\. David made many sacrifices to the Lord. He loved God. He honoured God. God honoured him too.

And they brought in the ark of the LORD, and set it in his place, in the midst of the tabernacle that David had pitched for it: and David offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the LORD.

2 Samuel 6:17

9\. Solomon made great sacrifices to the Lord and God honoured him greatly.

And Solomon offered a sacrifice of peace offerings, which he offered unto the LORD, two and twenty thousand oxen, and an hundred and twenty thousand sheep. So the king and all the children of Israel dedicated the house of the LORD.

1 Kings 8:63

10\. Adam did not sacrifice to the Lord. Adam was not prepared to make the sacrifice of abstaining from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. He was thrown out of the garden.

11\. Cain did not make a good enough sacrifice to the Lord and he was rejected for all eternity.

And in process of time it came to pass, that Cain brought of the fruit of the ground an offering unto the LORD…. But unto Cain and to his offering he had not respect. And Cain was very wroth, and his countenance fell.

Genesis 4:3, 5

12\. Seth, Enos, Cainan, Mahalaleel, Jared, Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech are not known to have made any sacrifices to God. They are therefore mentioned only briefly in the Bible.

13\. The three sons of Noah, Shem, Ham and Japheth, are not known to have made any sacrifices to God. They were not enlarged in their sacrifices to God. They were only mentioned briefly in the Bible.

14\. The descendants of Ham; Cush, Mizraim, Phut and Canaan did not build any altars or make any large sacrifices to God. They are just mentioned briefly.

15\. The descendants of Japheth; Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech and Tiras, did not build any altar or make any great sacrifices to Almighty God. We therefore know very little about them.

16\. The descendants of Shem; Elem, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud and Aram are also not known to have made any special sacrifice to God. They are just mentioned briefly.

17\. Esau did not make any sacrifice to the Lord. He and his descendants are briefly mentioned in the Bible. God does not honour those who do not honour Him.
