### CHAPTER 24\

Enlarge by Leaving Nothing Undone\
=======================================================

As the Lord commanded Moses his servant, so did Moses command Joshua, and so did Joshua; HE LEFT NOTHING UNDONE of all that the Lord commanded Moses.

Joshua 11:15

Enlargement, expansion and growth are possible if you leave nothing undone. The Joshua generation will leave nothing undone of all that the Lord has commanded and said. Most people are not able to experience enlargement because they leave many things undone. Notice how Joshua always fulfilled his ministry by completing his assignments.

So Joshua smote all the country of the hills, and of the south, and of the vale, and of the springs, and all their kings: HE LEFT NONE REMAINING, but utterly destroyed all that breathed, as the Lord God of Israel commanded.

Joshua 10:40

Most people do not complete their ministry. Most ministers leave many things undone. If you want to experience enlargement from God, you must learn to leave nothing undone.

When you are given medicine, it is important that you take the whole course in the way it is prescribed. If you leave something undone, do not be surprised if you do not get well.

All through my ministry I have tried to leave nothing undone. I have tried to be spiritual and I have also tried to be wise in the natural. I have tried to do pastoral work and I have also tried to do evangelistic work. I have tried to do apostolic work and I have also tried to do works of compassion. I have tried to preach the pure gospel and speak about pure salvation and I have also tried to minister the healing power of God. I have tried to leave nothing undone. Leaving nothing undone is the master key to enlargement!

I have tried to build a congregation and I have also tried to build the physical buildings to house the congregations. I have tried to write books and I have also tried to write songs. I have tried to write songs and I have also tried to produce them. I have tried to produce and train choirs and I have also tried to raise up soloists.

I have tried to minister the healing power of God and I have also tried to cast out devils. Philip went down to Samaria and preached Christ. Many people gave heed to the things that he said, both seeing and hearing the miracles that he did (Acts 8:5-6). I am trying as hard as I can to experience all that Philip experienced. I do not want to leave anything undone.

I have tried to teach the word of God and I have also tried to preach. I have tried to preach about doing the work of the ministry but I have also tried to preach about tithing. I have tried to teach about leadership but I have also tried to teach about the anointing.

I have tried to stay at home but I have also tried to travel and preach the word of God. I have tried to be a pastor of one congregation but I have also tried to pastor other churches. I have attempted to conduct crusades and I have also attempted to have a ministry in the media. I have attempted to have a television ministry and I have also tried to have a radio ministry. I have tried to set up an orphanage but I have also tried to set up a hospital. I have attempted to establish churches but I have also tried to set up schools.

What has God called you to do? What is missing in your ministry? What have you left undone?

Make sure you leave nothing undone! By leaving nothing undone you will experience enlargement. Joshua and his team ministered the power of God leaving nothing undone.
