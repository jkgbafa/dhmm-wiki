### CHAPTER 27\

Enlarge Even though Your Friends Are Targeted\
===================================================================

COME UP UNTO ME, AND HELP ME, THAT WE MAY SMITE GIBEON: FOR IT HATH MADE PEACE WITH JOSHUA and with the children of Israel. Therefore the five kings of the Amorites, the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, the king of Eglon, gathered themselves together, and went up, they and all their hosts, and encamped before Gibeon, and made war against it.

Joshua 10:4-5

Gibeon was the target of demonic attacks. The five kings of the Amorites targeted Gibeon just because he had made peace with Joshua. Read it for yourself. It is not only you, the minister, who is the focus of satanic attack. Your assistants, your friends, your close workers, your associates, your secretaries, your helpers are the focus and target of many attacks.

You, your friends and associates are the targets of many demonic discussions and meetings. The Joshua generation are targeted by many evil powers. Joshua’s friends and associates were targeted because they were seen as the source of trouble.

In the ministry, your associates, your friends and your supporters are key targets for the enemy. This is why your friends, associates and helpers must also be spiritual and prayerful. Satan is able to spot the weak link in the chain. He looks ravenously with a watering mouth at your assistants, friends, secretaries, associates and helpers, desiring to have them for lunch.

Throughout the course of your ministry, you will find that many of your closest followers and friends are attacked by satan. Your wife will be attacked by evil spirits because she is your wife. This is why many ministers have bad marriages. Most ministers marry the sweetest, nicest and gentlest of church members. Unfortunately, these gentle, sweet and nice sisters metamorphose into proud, negative and argumentative creatures. Why is this? These women are constantly under attack from evil spirits. It is so sad to see ministers’ wives metamorphose right before your very eyes into the exact opposite of what they were before they married.

Your associates will be attacked by evil spirits because they are your associates. Your close confidants will be attacked because they are your confidants. Many associates are attacked with the spirit of Absalom. Many associates are attacked by the spirit of envy, jealousy, lust and pride. Many associates are attacked, especially, with the spirit of Lucifer, which teaches them to separate themselves and rebel against authority.

It is easy to tell who your enemies are by looking at people’s attitudes towards your close confidants, friends and associates. People who dislike your friends, associates and confidants are your enemies. The friend of your friend is your friend! The enemy of your friend is your enemy!

In the world today, people are more indirect and oblique about their hostility towards you. You can see what people really think of you by how they behave towards your associates, friends and confidants. There are many people who I consider as enemies because they do not like my close workers. I know they will never declare war on me directly so I am not waiting for that to happen. They hate the people who work for me and that is all I need to know.

You must know that your friends and lovers will be the focus of demonic attacks. People will dislike them. People will gossip about them. Satan will try to kill them. Satan will try to silence them. Satan will try to neutralize them.

Gibeon would have enjoyed a peaceful existence if he were not associated with Joshua. Through the years, I have seen many close associates around me fall to demonic attacks. Five demonic kings came against Gibeon. Perhaps there are five demonic powers that are targeting you and your close assistants. Rise up and encourage everyone around you to be spiritual and prayerful.

If they do not take heed to themselves, they will fall to demonic powers. If they are not spiritual and humble, they will be filled with the spirit of Lucifer. If they are not prayerful, they will deny you like Peter denied Christ. If they are not spiritual and humble, they will turn into Judases. If they are not careful, they will fall to lust. If they are not humble and spiritual, they will become adulterers. If they are not spiritual and humble, they will end up divorced. Satan seeks to engulf your pastors, your friends, your acquaintances and associates with satanic shame and slime.

It is time to rise up and ready yourself for satanic attacks on your associates, spouses, assistants, friends, close workers and helpers. We are in a war! This is a war where they take no prisoners. You will either fall out or be left standing. Rise up today and be strong in the Lord. Satan wants to finish you and your friends but you will overcome. I see you winning the fight to preserve every friendship and association that is important for your ministry!
