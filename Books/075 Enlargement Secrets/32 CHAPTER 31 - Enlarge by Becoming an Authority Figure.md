### CHAPTER 31\

Enlarge by Becoming an Authority Figure\
=============================================================

And Joshua adjured them at that time, saying, Cursed be the man before the Lord, that riseth up and buildeth this city Jericho: he shall lay the foundation thereof in his firstborn, and in his youngest son shall he set up the gates of it. So the Lord was with Joshua; and his fame was noised throughout all the country.

Joshua 6:26-27

And Ahab made a grove; and Ahab did more to provoke the Lord God of Israel to anger than all the kings of Israel that were before him. In his days did Hiel the Bethelite build Jericho: he laid the foundation thereof in Abiram his firstborn, and set up the gates thereof in his youngest son Segub, according to the word of the Lord, which he spake by Joshua the son of Nun.

1 Kings 16:33-34

You will be enlarged as you become an authority figure. When a man of authority speaks a word, it is more likely to be fulfilled. Authority is derived from the things you suffer and the things you have survived. A soldier who has been through a real war and survived is different from a trained soldier who has never been to the battlefield. They may have the same rank in the army but a survivor and a sufferer is at a much higher level of authority.

Authority is also acquired by your achievements. A doctor who has just qualified from the medical school is called “Doctor.” However, a doctor who has performed several complicated surgeries and has seen his patients survive and live on, is at a different rank. His medical achievements give him much greater authority than a greenhorn who has just arrived with his medical school certificate.

As you can see from these two examples, authority is derived from the things you suffered, survived and went through as well as the achievements you chalk. What have you been through? What have you survived? What have you suffered?

You must seek to be a man of real authority and not just to have symbols of authority.

Many people clamour for titles, robes and other symbols of authority. What you need is real authority. Joshua was a real authority figure and he used his authority to protect the work that God had used him for. Authority figures must be feared because it is God who placed authority in their lives. Their authority is manifested in their words, their predictions, their prophecies, their oracles, their preaching, their teaching and their declarations. Symbols of authority, on the other hand, are manifested in things like rings, chains, hats, robes, titles and ceremonial clothes. Many people have symbols of authority but have no real authority.

Authority figures are people like pastors, bishops, spiritual leaders, fathers, mothers and guardians. An authority figure is someone whom God has placed over your life for your good. These authority figures can speak words that can change your life. A word from such an authority figure can cause you to wither or flourish. Enlargement comes through curses and blessings spoken by such authority figures. Words spoken by these authority figures, result in the occurrence of strange works. Enlargement becomes possible when you receive the blessing of an authority figure. Authority figures are well described and well defined in the Bible.

Every person is to be in subjection to the GOVERNING AUTHORITIES. For there is no authority except from God, and those which exist are established by God. Therefore whoever resists authority has opposed the ordinance of God; and they who have opposed will receive condemnation upon themselves.

Romans 13:1-2 (NASB)

It is important to respect and to fear what can happen to you through these authority figures. Many people dismiss the reality of authority figures to their own detriment. Your handling of an authority figure can determine the outcome of your life.

For I am a man under authority, having soldiers under me: and I say to this man, Go, and he goeth; and to another, Come, and he cometh; and to my servant, Do this, and he doeth it.

Matthew 8:9

A man’s authority is revealed in the number of people that respond to him and obey him! The centurion described his understanding of authority by explaining how people obeyed him. A man’s authority is described and defined by the people that come and go at his word. “I say to one, ‘Come’ and he comes and to another, ‘Go’ and he goes!” An even higher form of authority is demonstrated when “non-humans” obey you. When animals obey you, when the wind obeys you, when the weather obeys you, when sickness obeys you, you are truly a man of great authority.

Six Authority Figures

1\. Jesus was an authority figure. He commanded the storms of the sea, the wind, the waves, the sick, the dead and even a tree. All these responded to His authority.

And in the morning, as they passed by, they saw the fig tree dried up from the roots. And Peter calling to remembrance saith unto him, Master, behold, the fig tree which thou cursedst is withered away.

Mark 11:20-21

2\. Samuel became an authority figure; none of his words fell to the ground. Everything he said came to pass.

And Samuel grew, and the LORD was with him, and did let none of his words fall to the ground.

1 Samuel 3:19

3\. Elisha was an authority figure. He said there would be food within twenty-four hours and indeed it came to pass according to his word.

Then Elisha said, Hear ye the word of the LORD; Thus saith the LORD, To morrow about this time shall a measure of fine flour be sold for a shekel, and two measures of barley for a shekel, in the gate of Samaria…. And the people went out, and spoiled the tents of the Syrians. So a measure of fine flour was sold for a shekel, and two measures of barley for a shekel, according to the word of the LORD.

2 Kings 7:1, 16

4\. Moses was an authority figure. He commanded a river to turn into blood and it happened.

And the Lord spake unto Moses, Say unto Aaron, Take thy rod, and stretch out thine hand upon the waters of Egypt, upon their streams, upon their rivers, and upon their ponds, and upon all their pools of water, that they may become blood; and that there may be blood throughout all the land of Egypt, both in vessels of wood, and in vessels of stone. And Moses and Aaron did so, as the Lord commanded; and he lifted up the rod, and smote the waters that were in the river, in the sight of Pharaoh, and in the sight of his servants; and all the waters that were in the river were turned to blood.

Exodus 7:19-20

5\. Elijah was an authority figure. He commanded rain to cease and called in a famine for three and a half years.

And Elijah the Tishbite, who was of the inhabitants of Gilead, said unto Ahab, As the LORD God of Israel liveth, before whom I stand, there shall not be dew nor rain these years, but according to my word.

1 Kings 17:1

Elias was a man subject to like passions as we are, and he prayed earnestly that it might not rain: and it rained not on the earth by the space of three years and six months.

James 5:17

6\. Isaac was an authority figure. He spoke a blessing over his son, Jacob, and it has been fulfilled for thousands of years.

Let people serve thee, and nations bow down to thee: be lord over thy brethren, and let thy mother’s sons bow down to thee: cursed be every one that curseth thee, and blessed be he that blesseth thee.

Genesis 27:29

How Do You Become an Authority Figure?

1\. You become an authority figure by being obedient to higher authorities. The more subject you are to a higher authority, the greater your own authority. The centurion exemplified this principle. He told Jesus that he understood authority because he himself was a man of authority. He revealed that his authority to command men was based on the fact that he himself was under authority. Indeed, I have no authority to command even twelve soldiers because I am not a soldier myself. If I issued a command to three soldiers I see standing by the roadside, they would simply ignore me. If they recognized that I myself was a soldier under authority, then they would listen to me. Authority comes from being under authority. When a man is deeply subjected to God, He derives great authority from God himself.

For I also am a man set under authority, having under me soldiers, and I say unto one, Go, and he goeth; and to another, Come, and he cometh; and to my servant, Do this, and he doeth it.

Luke 7:8

2\. You become an authority figure by overcoming, fighting and winning wars. When you become an overcomer, you receive authority. Joshua, the commander of the Israelite army, gained his authority by fighting and winning wars. Everyone who has been through difficulty and has survived becomes a man of authority. Your scars make you a man of authority. That is why Jesus showed His scars, and not His crowns, when He appeared to His disciples.

And after eight days again his disciples were within, and Thomas with them: then came Jesus, the doors being shut, and stood in the midst, and said, Peace be unto you. Then saith he to Thomas, Reach hither thy finger, and behold my hands; and reach hither thy hand, and thrust it into my side: and be not faithless, but believing. And Thomas answered and said unto him, My Lord and my God.

John 20:26-28

3\. You become a man of authority by accomplishing tasks that are given to you. Many times, authority is given to you as a reward for your faithfulness. In the Bible, Jesus gave us a parable of how someone gained authority over ten cities because he had accomplished his tasks and used his talents appropriately.

Then came the first, saying, Lord, thy pound hath gained ten pounds. And he said unto him, Well, thou good servant: because thou hast been faithful in a very little, have thou AUTHORITY OVER TEN CITIES.

Luke 19:16-17
