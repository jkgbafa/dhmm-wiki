### CHAPTER 37\

Enlarge by Accepting God’s Inheritance\
============================================================

But unto the tribe of Levi Moses gave not any inheritance: THE LORD GOD OF ISRAEL WAS THEIR INHERITANCE, as he said unto them.

Joshua 13:33

Only unto the tribe of Levi he gave none inheritance; THE SACRIFICES OF THE LORD GOD OF ISRAEL MADE BY FIRE ARE THEIR INHERITANCE, as he said unto them.

Joshua 13:14

Enlarge by accepting God’s inheritance for your life! God’s inheritance is Himself! He told the tribe of Levi that they would have no land. They would have Him instead of having land. Practically speaking, they would eat the sacrifices of the people instead of having land for themselves. God has always wanted people who would trust Him alone. God wants people who have faith in His ability to provide.

Today, people prefer to work for the world than to work for the ministry. It is time to trust in the Lord. It is time to seek the kingdom of God first. How much can you accomplish if you work for God in the evenings or at weekends? Why do the banks want you to be present every day before 8.00 am, whether you have children to drop off at school or not? The bank wants to get the best out of every worker.

Why do you not tell the bank that you will come to work in the evening or at the weekend? God equally wants nice people to work for Him. He wants you to serve Him and trust Him to take care of you. Just as you trust the bank, you must trust the Lord!

It is not easy to accept God’s inheritance. You need faith to believe that God can take care of you. Every man of God must tap into the supernatural power of God and learn how to live from the sacrifices that God’s people make. If you are anointed, God will provide for you.

Some years ago, God spoke to me early in the morning. He said, “Give yourself wholly to Me. Give yourself to My work and I will provide for you.” I had to leave my business and my profession and enter into full-time ministry, trusting the Lord to provide for me and my family.

Then I had a dream. I was walking on a lonely road and I saw what looked like a heap of sand. As I got closer, I realized that it was a heap of gold coins. I was amazed and decided to arrange for a truck and to cart my newfound wealth away. Then the Holy Spirit said to me, “There is no need to do that. Just take a handful off the coins and continue on your way.” I exclaimed, “How can I leave the rest of the gold coins behind? They are so valuable. They can sustain me for the rest of my life and ministry.” Then the Lord said to me, “On the road that I have called you, there are many of such heaps. There is no need to end your journey at this heap of coins. Keep walking! I will provide for you along the way.” Indeed, He has provided for me all these years. If God has called you, there will be heaps of gold coins on the road that you are on.

Unimaginable feats and unimaginable victories in the ministry will come forth from you as you trust in the Lord. That will be your testimony! Believe in God and enter full-time ministry! Amazing achievements, in an unimaginable period of time, will be your testimony if you trust in God! Beginning from this time, when you place your trust in the Lord, expect changes that have only supernatural explanations.
