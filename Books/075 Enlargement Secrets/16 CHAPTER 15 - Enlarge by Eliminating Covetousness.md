### CHAPTER 15\

Enlarge by Eliminating Covetousness\
=========================================================

And Achan answered Joshua, and said, Indeed I have sinned against the Lord God of Israel, and thus and thus have I done: When I SAW AMONG THE SPOILS A GOODLY BABYLONISH GARMENT, AND TWO HUNDRED SHEKELS OF SILVER, AND A WEDGE OF GOLD OF FIFTY SHEKELS WEIGHT, THEN I COVETED THEM, AND TOOK THEM; and, behold, they are hid in the earth in the midst of my tent, and the silver under it. So Joshua sent messengers, and they ran unto the tent; and, behold, it was hid in his tent, and the silver under it. And they took them out of the midst of the tent, and brought them unto Joshua, and unto all the children of Israel, and laid them out before the Lord.

And Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan the son of Zerah, and the silver, and the garment, and the wedge of gold, and his sons, and his daughters, and his oxen, and his asses, and his sheep, and his tent, and all that he had: and they brought them unto the valley of Achor. And Joshua said, why hast thou troubled us? the Lord shall trouble thee this day. And all Israel stoned him with stones, and burned them with fire, after they had stoned them with stones. And they raised over him a great heap of stones unto this day. So the Lord turned from the fierceness of his anger. Wherefore the name of that place was called, the valley of Achor, unto this day.

Joshua 7:20-26

Covetousness is the desire and the campaign for money! Many who are in the ministry today have a strong desire for more and more money. Many who are in the ministry today are on a campaign for more and more money. Some of the ministers who are on a campaign for money are open about it. It is easy to see the open and sometimes arrogant drive for more money. Many ministers are on an insidious drive to acquire more and more earthly things. The Joshua generation avoids the “valley of covetous trouble”. The valley of covetous trouble is the valley of Achor. The valley of life’s troubles comes to the covetous and the money loving people of our world. The Joshua generation will expunge the spirit of covetousness. It is not possible to be enlarged without expunging covetousness.

Recently, a great man of God told a young minister, “My ministry changed when I began to go after money. I wanted more and more money. I was making millions of dollars. But my emphasis changed from the ministry to raising more and more money.” I was amazed when I heard of this advice. I myself had noticed a change in this individual’s ministry. It was no longer as spiritual and anointed as I had known it to be.

There is no question about the effect of money on ministry. Money changes everything! The desire for money, the working for money and the decisions that are made just because of money will change every ministry.

Today, you may see great ministries on television constantly asking for money. Many of these ministries are quite different from what they set out to be. Money has an amazing effect on everything. Do not allow covetousness to change your ministry.

Five Effects of Covetousness on the Anointing

1. Covetousness destroys the anointing.

But they that will be rich fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in DESTRUCTION and perdition. For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

1 Timothy 6:9-10

Covetousness is the desire and the campaign for money. Covetousness destroys the anointing. Be careful of the destruction that comes to the anointing when you focus on money. The scripture above states that men drown in destruction because of covetousness. There is destruction to the anointing when covetousness is present. It is as though a person has half of the anointing that he originally had. Why is this the case? When the anointed person shares scriptures about money all the time, the Spirit and the presence are usually not there. Although the person is speaking the word of God, the presence of God is absent! The word of God and the presence of God would have made everything complete. But the word of God without the presence of God waters down the ministry. It is as though half of the engines are not firing. Indeed, there is destruction to the anointing when covetousness is present.

2. Covetousness hurts the anointing.

But they that will be rich fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and HURTFUL lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition. For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

1 Timothy 6:9-10

Covetousness is the desire and the campaign for money. Covetousness hurts the anointing. Foolish and hurtful desires are the portion of those who love money. Desiring money and going after it is a very dangerous activity.

People that are filled with covetousness have many desires that others do not have. They have many longings and yearnings for useless things. They desire the toys of the rich, famous and wealthy. These longings and cravings make them commit sin. Always remember that many desires are demonic in origin. When Jesus had the desire to turn stones into bread, it was because satan was present in the wilderness with him, stirring up all those desires.

3. Covetousness misleads the anointing.

But they that will be rich fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition. For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, THEY HAVE ERRED FROM THE FAITH, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

1 Timothy 6:9-10

Covetousness is the desire and the campaign for money. Covetousness misleads the anointed and his anointing. Almost everyone who deviated in ministry did so because of the love of money. People who are filled with the love of money start to go off-course. The origins of errors in many ministries can be found in the rising lust for money. People that are filled with covetousness have another vision apart from the one that God gave them. They take decisions that earn more money not decisions that win more souls.

The desire for money becomes the basis for many decisions that are taken in ministry. Since most of the world is poor, a large part of the world is out of reach for men of covetousness.

4. Covetousness traps the anointing.

But they that will be rich fall into temptation and A SNARE, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition. For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

1 Timothy 6:9-10

Covetousness is the desire and the campaign for money. Covetousness traps the anointing. Many anointed people are trapped by their covetousness. Have you not noticed that people who have a strong desire for money cannot go beyond certain borders and limits? They will not hazard their lives for Christ. Remember the life of Apostle Paul. He was someone who hazarded his life for the gospel.

It seemed good unto us, being assembled with one accord, to send chosen men unto you with our beloved Barnabas and Paul, Men that have hazarded their lives for the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Acts 15:25-26

5. Covetousness introduces evil into the anointing.

For the love of money is the ROOT OF ALL EVIL: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

1 Timothy 6:10

Covetousness is the desire and the campaign for money. Covetousness introduces evil into the life of the anointed. Many evils are the result of people looking for money. Rich people and those in search of more and more money are engaged in more evil than the average person. The love of money is the root of all evil. Covetousness is the love of money. Almost every sin in the world is connected to money. Lying, stealing, fornication, adultery, drugs, murder are all connected to money. All anointed people can be corrupted by the desire for more and more money.
