### CHAPTER 33\

Enlarge by Teaching and Demonstrating\
===========================================================

And all the people returned to the camp to Joshua at Makkedah in peace: none moved his tongue against any of the children of Israel.Then said Joshua, Open the mouth of the cave, and bring out those five kings unto me out of the cave. And they did so, and brought forth those five kings unto him out of the cave, the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Eglon.

And it came to pass, when they brought out those kings unto Joshua, that JOSHUA CALLED FOR ALL THE MEN OF ISRAEL, AND SAID UNTO THE CAPTAINS OF THE MEN OF WAR WHICH WENT WITH HIM, COME NEAR, PUT YOUR FEET UPON THE NECKS OF THESE KINGS. And they came near, and put their feet upon the necks of them. And Joshua said unto them, Fear not, nor be dismayed, be strong and of good courage: for thus shall the LORD do to all your enemies against whom ye fight. And afterward Joshua smote them, and slew them, and hanged them on five trees: and they were hanging upon the trees until the evening. And it came to pass at the time of the going down of the sun, that Joshua commanded, and they took them down off the trees, and cast them into the cave wherein they had been hid, and laid great stones in the cave’s mouth, which remain until this very day.

Joshua 10:21-27

Joshua experienced enlargement by teaching his leaders and demonstrating the power of God to them. Joshua did not just experience personal victories. Joshua was someone who enlarged his ministry by teaching others and involving them in his victories. Who are you involving in your ministry? Who are you training? Who is coming up by your side? A blessed man has his quiver full of children. A blessed minister has his quiver full of up-and-coming ministers. You will be happy in the future if you have your quiver full of anointed, young and zealous ministers.

As arrows are in the hand of a mighty man; so are children of the youth. Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them: they shall not be ashamed, but they shall speak with the enemies in the gate.

Psalm 127:4-5

Every man of God is limited. Your days are numbered and your ability to minister to the world is very limited indeed. Very soon, you will be declared too old or too weak to minister in a certain way. Very soon you will realise that you cannot travel to the whole world any more. You will find out that you cannot preach all the time. No matter how great you are and how fruitful you are, God does not intend for you to live forever.

This is why you need to train other people. Your ministry must be like a teaching hospital. In a teaching hospital, the doctors practice medicine and constantly teach other medical students. Every patient becomes a specimen for medical students to learn from. In a teaching hospital, practicing medicine and training younger ones go side by side.

Your ministry must be like a teaching hospital if you want to experience enlargement. You must preach, teach and travel with up-and-coming younger ministers. Your ministry must be a demonstration of God’s power to your followers.

Start teaching and demonstrating things early in your ministry. No one around you will amount to anything unless you teach them what to do. God wants to give you enlargement. To enlarge, you need people.

To experience enlargement in your ministry, you need to have duplicates, followers, disciples and devoted students. There is no way to have new doctors unless you go through the complicated and laborious training of a doctor.

To train a new surgeon, they say, “He must watch one, he must assist one and he must do one.” The new doctor must be allowed to watch surgeries, assist in surgeries and actually do a surgery himself.

To have great and young men of God, you must allow them to watch the ministry, assist in the ministry and then do the ministry!

The Joshua generation will demonstrate the power of God to many others so that there can be enlargement, expansion and growth.
