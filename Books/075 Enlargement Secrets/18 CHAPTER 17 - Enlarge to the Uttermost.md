### CHAPTER 17\

Enlarge to the Uttermost\
==============================================

And it came to pass, when Israel had made an end of slaying all the inhabitants of Ai in the field, in the wilderness wherein they chased them, and when they were all fallen on the edge of the sword, until they were consumed, that all the Israelites returned unto Ai, and smote it with the edge of the sword. And so it was, that all that fell that day, both of men and women, were twelve thousand, even all the men of Ai. FOR JOSHUA DREW NOT HIS HAND BACK, WHEREWITH HE STRETCHED OUT THE SPEAR, UNTIL HE HAD UTTERLY DESTROYED ALL THE INHABITANTS OF AI. Only the cattle and the spoil of that city Israel took for a prey unto themselves, according unto the word of the LORD which he commanded Joshua.

And Joshua burnt Ai, and made it an heap for ever, even a desolation unto this day. And the king of Ai he hanged on a tree until eventide: and as soon as the sun was down, Joshua commanded that they should take his carcase down from the tree, and cast it at the entering of the gate of the city, and raise thereon a great heap of stones, that remaineth unto this day.

Joshua 8:24-28

Enlargement is only possible when you have a vision to go all the way.

You must finish off the enemies of the cross forever. You must wipe out every opposition to your calling.

The Joshua generation will not draw back their hand till all is accomplished. Joshua utterly destroyed the enemies of God.

You will enlarge to the uttermost.

Your ministry must lift itself up in vision. The order of ministry you must seek must be the “order of the uttermost”. You must seek to have a ministry that is after the order of the word of God.

Jesus Christ did not just send people into a neighbourhood. He sent people into the uttermost parts of the world. Most people have a “neighbourhood” vision. But Jesus is sending us to the uttermost.

But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the UTTERMOST PART of the earth.

Acts 1:8

Beginning from now, your ministry is climbing into the “uttermost” order of ministry! You will be counted amongst the uttermost mega churches, uttermost ministries and furthest reaching ministries of the world.

From this year and beyond, every form of stagnation, frustration and failure is broken in your life forever! You are receiving the anointing to go into the uttermost parts of the earth to utterly destroy the works of the devil.

No more “community” ministry for you! No more “neighbourhood” ministry for you! No more localised, regional or district-based ministry for you! Receive the power of the spirit of Joshua to defeat the enemy to the uttermost! You shall emerge as one of the deliverers who will rescue humanity from the clutches of the devil.

Beginning from today, there will be no more restrictions on your ministry, your church and your calling! You will be listed among the men who command spiritual power in your nation. You will be listed among the men who command attention in your country.

When you do not have an uttermost vision, your ministry will remain small and undeveloped. But there is much more in you. God will accomplish much more through you.

God did not call you to have a ministry that can just buy a car or a bus. God did not call you to just have a ministry that can just pay your rent. He called you to have a ministry that would reach the uttermost parts of the earth. Many ministers are happy when their ministries can just pay their rent, buy a car and buy a ticket to go to America. But there is more to ministry than paying these bills. Read the words of Jesus carefully. You are called to the uttermost parts of the earth.

One day, I felt the Lord asking me a question. “What will you say when you come to heaven and I ask you about Judea?”

I thought to myself, “What is Judea?”

Then the Lord said, “Judea is next to your Jerusalem.”

I thought to myself, “If Ghana is my Jerusalem, then Togo, Benin, Cote d’Ivoire and Burkina Faso must be my Judea.”

That was when I realised that I had very little fruits in Judea. I had been praising myself for nothing and thinking that I had fulfilled the Great Commission. But I had not even achieved anything in Judea. Although I had churches in Ghana, I had very little fruits in Judea. I had very little going on in Togo, Benin, Cote d’Ivoire and Burkina Faso.

It is easy to think you have finished, when in reality you have done little or nothing. Most ministries have done little or nothing.

It is time to emerge as one of the world-impacting “uttermost” ministries of your generation. Receive the grace to build an unlimited ministry! All through the coming season, you will experience divine openings and divine surprises in all areas of your life and ministry!
