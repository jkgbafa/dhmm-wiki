### CHAPTER 25\

Enlarge With Faithful Friends\
===================================================

Now it came to pass, when Adonizedek king of Jerusalem had heard how Joshua had taken Ai, and had utterly destroyed it; as he had done to Jericho and her king, so he had done to Ai and her king; and how the inhabitants of Gibeon had made peace with Israel, and were among them; That they feared greatly, because Gibeon was a great city, as one of the royal cities, and because it was greater than Ai, and all the men thereof were mighty. Wherefore Adonizedek king of Jerusalem sent unto Hoham king of Hebron, and unto Piram king of Jarmuth, and unto Japhia king of Lachish, and unto Debir king of Eglon, saying, Come up unto me, and help me, that we may smite Gibeon: for it hath made peace with Joshua and with the children of Israel.

Therefore the five kings of the Amorites, the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, the king of Eglon, gathered themselves together, and went up, they and all their hosts, and encamped before Gibeon, and made war against it. And the men of Gibeon sent unto Joshua to the camp to Gilgal, saying, SLACK NOT THY HAND FROM THY SERVANTS; COME UP TO US QUICKLY, AND SAVE US, AND HELP US: FOR ALL THE KINGS OF THE AMORITES THAT DWELL IN THE MOUNTAINS ARE GATHERED TOGETHER AGAINST US. So Joshua ascended from Gilgal, he, and all the people of war with him, and all the mighty men of valour.

Joshua 10:1-7

Joshua risked his life for his servants. Joshua was faithful to those who were part of his team. Most people would not fight and risk their lives for anyone other than themselves.

Enlargement is possible when you are faithful to your friends, servants, colleagues and associates. You must remember your alliances, your associates and your aides. Enlargement is only possible because of the loyal and faithful support you get from people you have known.

When Joshua received a call for help from his servants, he risked his life and the life of his men. Loyalty and faithfulness are virtues that are seen in times of trouble. What you do for someone when he is in trouble reveals your level of faithfulness. “A faithful man who can find?” (Proverbs 20:6). A faithful man is a good man. In other words, “A good man who can find?” Be faithful to your friends and colleagues! One day, they will also fight for you and not abandon you.

A rich man has many friends. A rich man is usually in a state of success, achievement and great accomplishment. This state of success, achievement and accomplishment attracts many fake friends and supporters. Success has many friends! Whenever there is a crisis, the fake friends will be exposed.

The poor is hated even of his own neighbour: but the rich hath many friends.

Proverbs 14:20

It is in the time of trouble that you see the really loyal and faithful friends. It is in time of trouble that you need to call on people to help you. When people let you down in time of trouble, they reveal how disloyal they are.

Once, I fell into a crisis and rushed to a man I thought was a friend. I thought that our relationship was so strong and deep that he would stand up for me and defend me. Instead of speaking up for me, he told someone that he did not want to get himself involved in my crisis. The next time I saw him, he smiled at me in his usual charming way and came to hug me, asking me about how things were going. I wondered in amazement at this “friend”. I looked at this gentleman and understood that his friendship, his charming words, his hugs, his inquiries about my health and my family meant nothing at all. It was not a relationship worth having.

Look around you and choose people based on those who have stood with you in time of trouble. Choose the people who spoke up for you when there was a problem. Watch what people say when there is a crisis! Watch what people do not say when there is a crisis!

There was no ambiguity or indecisiveness about Joshua’s actions. Joshua’s actions were clear. He would rise up and fight for his friends. He would risk his life for those he called his servants.

Confidence in an unfaithful man in time of trouble is like a broken tooth, and a foot out of joint.

Proverbs 25:19

At another time, I experienced a crisis and I was expecting my friend and colleague to rise up and defend me. I had great expectations that my friend would stand up and fight for me because of our past relationship. I hoped and hoped! I waited and waited! I prayed and cried! I tried and tried to persuade him! Then I listened and listened! I watched and watched! But there was no word! There was no intervention! And there was no help from this friend! Apparently, this friend did not want to speak out on my behalf. Perhaps, he sided with the people that were persecuting me. Perhaps, he understood them more than he understood me. Perhaps, he wished that they would continue to persecute me to the logical conclusion.

I experienced what it meant to have confidence in an unfaithful man in my time of trouble. I experienced a broken tooth and a foot out of joint! When you experience a broken tooth or a foot out of joint, you are affected badly. Many people break their relationships and friendships after these experiences.

You can lose the most important friendship or relationship in your life when you are not faithful in time of trouble. If you lose the most important relationships and friendships of your life through unfaithfulness, you have really lost something big. I have relationships that I have been forced to change because a crisis revealed that my friends were not faithful to me in my time of trouble. Keep watching and waiting! You will soon have a chance to filter your friendships and leave out the fake ones!

Enlargement is possible by maintaining amazing friendships and relationships through the years. Be a faithful man! God will give you amazing relationships that will be stepping-stones to your enlargement and advancement in ministry.

From now on, you will experience enlargement in your ministry. Your path will be shining more and more until Jesus returns.
