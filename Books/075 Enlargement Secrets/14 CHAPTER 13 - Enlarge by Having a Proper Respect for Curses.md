### CHAPTER 13\

Enlarge by Having a Proper Respect for Curses\
===================================================================

And the Lord said unto Joshua, Get thee up; wherefore liest thou thus upon thy face? Israel hath sinned, and they have also transgressed my covenant which I commanded them: for they have even taken of the accursed thing, and have also stolen, and dissembled also, and they have put it even among their own stuff. THEREFORE THE CHILDREN OF ISRAEL COULD NOT STAND BEFORE THEIR ENEMIES, BUT TURNED THEIR BACKS BEFORE THEIR ENEMIES, BECAUSE THEY WERE ACCURSED: neither will I be with you any more, except ye destroy the accursed from among you.

Joshua 7:10-12

Beginning from this chapter, you will operate in the fullness of blessings! Whatever represents a curse is annulled in your life! This year, you will become a surprise both to yourself and the world around you!

You will not succeed against the enemy when you are under a curse. The scripture above tells us that the children of Israel could not stand before the enemies because they were accursed. Curses are the reasons for failure, defeat, humiliation, frustration, depression, disgrace, poverty, backwardness, alienation, insufficiency, retrogression, degradation, decline, failure to thrive and downfall. You must have a proper respect for curses.

Curses are strange things. They are relentless, inevitable, unavoidable conditions that persist in people’s lives until they are fully engulfed in failure, defeat, humiliation, disgrace and degradation. Many people do not have the proper respect that they ought to have for spiritual things. The prophet Isaiah warned the people not to be mockers. People make fun of curses but they will be shocked when the curses happen practically.

For the Lord shall rise up as in mount Perazim, he shall be wroth as in the valley of Gibeon, that he may do his work, HIS STRANGE WORK; and bring to pass his act, HIS STRANGE ACT. Now therefore BE YE NOT MOCKERS, lest your bands be made strong: for I have heard from the Lord God of hosts a consumption, even determined upon the whole earth.

Isaiah 28:21-22

Enlargement is only possible if you respect the reality of blessings and curses. I realise that many Christians do not believe that curses are real. They also do not believe that blessings are real. Enlargement is only possible if you fight curses with all your might. You must do everything to neutralize curses in your life. Enlargement will only come when you put away accursed things and accursed activities. Joshua had to put aside the accursed things so that he could expand and enlarge. Without a proper respect for curses you will cross the line and activate curses in your life.

Perhaps, the only things that can explain the world in which we live are curses and blessings. Many countries have enough oil, water, food, gold, silver and bauxite to meet the needs of the whole world. And yet, many people live in starvation, poverty and difficulty.

The Second World War was fought between 1939 and 1945. Over fifty million people were killed during this war. After the war, everyone returned to their country and life continued exactly as it had been before the war began. It is as though madness made the world come together and kill each other for some years until they were tired of it all. Death, war, destruction and sadness came on the world without any good reason.

Many things in our world do not make sense. In Africa, there is an abundance of oil, bauxite, gold, uranium, platinum and diamonds. Yet, the continent wallows in deep poverty and need. It is important to respect curses. I once heard a pastor saying to his congregation, “Let a curse be on me if I am lying.” And yet he was telling a big lie! I realised that this pastor did not have a proper respect for curses and blessings.

If you want to experience enlargement, you must recognize that enlargement originates from blessings. The blessings that Israel experienced were spoken by Isaac. A few simple words determined the destiny of the nation, Israel. It is hard to explain the history of Israel without believing that these words of blessing have caused things to be the way they are. When Isaac was one hundred and thirty-seven years old, which was five years past the age at which his mother Sarah died, Isaac did not expect to live much longer. He called his sons, intending to pass on a blessing to his first-born son. These are the words of blessings he spoke:

Therefore God give thee of the dew of heaven, and the fatness of the earth, and plenty of corn and wine: Let people serve thee, and nations bow down to thee: be lord over thy brethren, and let thy mother’s sons bow down to thee: cursed be every one that curseth thee, and blessed be he that blesseth thee.

Genesis 27:28-29

You will notice how these words have come to pass in the life of the nation Israel. Perhaps, the one who did not have a proper respect for curses in the family was Rebekah. Rebekah, the mother of Jacob seemed to take the curse of deception lightly. When Jacob complained that the curse would come on her, she simply said, “Don’t worry, let your curse come on me.” Jacob was afraid of the curse of being a deceiver. He knew that a deceiver is a cursed person. Deceivers often deceive themselves that they will not be cursed for their lies and deception.

My father peradventure will feel me, and I shall seem to him as A DECEIVER; and I SHALL BRING A CURSE upon me, and not a blessing. And his mother said unto him, UPON ME BE THY CURSE, my son: only obey my voice, and go fetch me them.

Genesis 27:12-13

Rebekah simply shrugged off the possibility of the curse that could come on her for deceiving Isaac. This is how lightly people take curses. Deceiving God’s servant brings curses. Many people in the ministry tell lies and take it lightly. They do not realise that they are bringing curses on themselves because of their deceptions. I have watched people tell lies and then shrug it off. Don’t worry, they say, “Tell a lie nothing will happen.” If you want to experience enlargement, you cannot shrug off the reality of curses like Rebekah did. After this incident, Rebekah was not heard of any more. Be careful that you do not bring the curse of the deceiver on yourself. Your deceptions will cause you to vanish from the earth and never be heard of again. You will wither and fade away into oblivion because of your lies and deception. All liars are truly cursed because liars bestow contempt and derision on the one they deceive!

Even the death of Rebekah was not reported in the Bible. The death and burial of Deborah, Rebekah’s nurse, was reported. But there is no mention of when Rebekah died. Do not trivialize curses that are spoken. Take them seriously! You may not know how it will be implemented. Failure, defeat, humiliation, frustration, depression, disgrace, poverty, backwardness, alienation, insufficiency, retrogression, degradation, decline, failure to thrive and downfall are waiting for those who trivialize and belittle the power of curses.

Beginning from now, every long-standing ordeal in your life comes to an end! You will never suffer from the curses of liars and deceivers! Every family curse, spell or enchantment is dropping off your life at this time! All attempts to shorten your life have failed in the name of Jesus! You are being re-positioned and re-aligned for a blessing instead of a curse!

From today and beyond today, expect to be free from every captivity of hell in the name of Jesus! I declare blessings on your life in place of a curse!
