### CHAPTER 30\

Enlargement by Judgment\
=============================================

Then said Joshua, Open the mouth of the cave, and bring out those five kings unto me out of the cave. And they did so, and brought forth those five kings unto him out of the cave, the king of Jerusalem, the king of Hebron, the king of Jarmuth, the king of Lachish, and the king of Eglon. And it came to pass, when they brought out those kings unto Joshua, that Joshua called for all the men of Israel, and said unto the captains of the men of war which went with him, COME NEAR, PUT YOUR FEET UPON THE NECKS OF THESE KINGS. And they came near, and put their feet upon the necks of them. And Joshua said unto them, Fear not, nor be dismayed, be strong and of good courage: for thus shall the Lord do to all your enemies against whom ye fight. AND AFTERWARD JOSHUA SMOTE THEM, AND SLEW THEM, AND HANGED THEM ON FIVE TREES: AND THEY WERE HANGING UPON THE TREES UNTIL THE EVENING. And it came to pass at the time of the going down of the sun, that Joshua commanded, and they took them down off the trees, and cast them into the cave wherein they had been hid, and laid great stones in the cave’s mouth, which remain until this very day.

Joshua 10:22-27

Enlarge by declaring and implementing sound judgment. Do not be afraid of judgment. Without judgment there would be confusion, disorder, chaos and anarchy. You cannot expand in the midst of confusion, disorder, chaos and anarchy.

In this story, Joshua executed the five kings who had teamed up against the children of Israel. In a show of power and victory, he pulled out the five kings from a cave and made them lie on the floor. He encouraged God’s people and said, “This is what is going to happen to all your enemies.” The Joshua generation understands the need for judgment. Without killing these kings, the people of God would never be established.

The fact that people are low and defeated does not mean they are repentant! Many people act repentant and sorry because they have been caught but not because they have truly repented. Without appropriate judgment, evil is allowed to grow and even diversify. People begin to add sin to sin because judgment is not applied.

Woe to the rebellious children, saith the LORD, that take counsel, but not of me; and that cover with a covering, but not of my spirit, THAT THEY MAY ADD SIN TO SIN:

Isaiah 30:1

Death is the judgment God gave to mankind for disobeying Him. God decided to terminate the lives of His creation because they had disobeyed Him. If you think about it, you will realise that death is necessary to end the evil on the earth. If certain people were allowed to live, the evil they practised would live on too. Imagine if Hitler, Stalin, Caligula and Nero were still alive. Imagine if many of the serial killers and murderers of our world were still alive and allowed to roam the streets after being in prison for many years. Imagine if the perpetrators of genocides were allowed to come right back to continue their evil deeds. Indeed, judgment (death) brings much evil to an end. God’s plan of judgment through death has truly helped to bring an end to evil. When we understand the plan of God, we may not be so sad when certain people die.

Enlargement takes place through a combination of judgment and mercy. Every leader is going to juggle with mercy and judgment throughout his ministry. Should you pass judgment or should you show mercy. Should you dismiss someone or should you keep him? Should you overlook what has happened or should you pass judgment? Every leader will face this difficulty throughout his ministry.

God is merciful and He forgives us, often putting us right back into the place of ministry. The difference between you and God is that God sees the heart but we cannot see people’s hearts. Has the person truly repented or is the person just pretending? Has the person truly repented or is the person just lying low, only to resurface as the worst possible “Judas”? These are things that are not easy to determine. It is important to be merciful, and good leaders must tend more towards mercy than towards judgment. Scripture is always encouraging us to be merciful. A man of God must have the mercy of God if he is an imitator of God.

Why a Leader Must be Merciful

1\. A leader must be merciful because mercy is better than judgment. Mercy triumphs over judgment. You will have more favour in your life if you show mercy whenever you can.

Let not mercy and truth forsake thee: bind them about thy neck; write them upon the table of thine heart: So shalt thou find favour and good understanding in the sight of God and man.

Proverbs 3:3-4

2\. A leader must be merciful because it is wise to be merciful. All leaders will learn that showing mercy is the wise thing to do.

But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.

James 3:17

However, true enlargement will only come through righteous judgment. Establishment will never come unless real judgment is allowed to happen to those who deserve it. Joshua executed judgment on the five kings who attacked the children of Israel. He placed his foot on their necks and called everyone to come and see them being executed. It may sound cruel but a certain establishment will not come unless there is real judgment.

The king by judgment establisheth the land: but he that receiveth gifts overthroweth it.

Proverbs 29:4

When you do not pass judgment but rather forgive and overlook the sins of someone who has not repented, you make a mistake that can cost you a lot.

I once had a vision in which there was a large pot of groundnut soup boiling on a stove. In the vision, I needed to urinate and I decided to do it into the boiling groundnut soup. As I urinated into the groundnut soup I wondered, “Is this not what we are going to eat?” I became alarmed when I realised that I was doing the wrong thing. I was pouring urine into the soup that we were all going to eat. Suddenly, I awoke out of my dream. The Lord said to me, “If you allow this unrepentant person to go back to his old position, you are pouring urine into the groundnut soup. You are putting an evil back into the ministry and it will poison and destroy other things. Do not put unrepentant people back into their old positions, lest they destroy the ministry.”

Do not destroy your ministry by dealing benevolently with disloyalty. Being a Judas is the worst spiritual crime possible. Jesus said it would have been better that Judas were not born (Matthew 26:24).

Many immoral people continue in immorality because they take sin lightly. The Bible says fornication should not be mentioned even once (Ephesians 5:3). Do not trivialise or minimize the risk that sin introduces to your life. Self-judgment is important.

For if we would judge ourselves, we should not be judged.

1 Corinthians 11:31

If you do not judge yourself, you will find evil growing in your life. If you do not judge others properly the evil will grow. If you do not take proper decisions about your leaders, evil will multiply and the whole congregation will be affected. Do not forget, as the priest are, so are the people! Whatever is found among the pastors will soon spread into the congregation.

‘AND WHAT THE PRIESTS DO, THE PEOPLE ALSO DO.’ So now I will punish both priests and people for their wicked deeds.

Hosea 4:9 (NLT)

Believe in the judgment of God and practice judgment like the Joshua generation so that the kingdom will be established in the land.
