### CHAPTER 8\

Enlarge through Victory over King Hoham\
=============================================================

Now it came to pass, when Adonizedek king of Jerusalem had heard how Joshua had taken Ai, and had utterly destroyed it; as he had done to Jericho and her king, so he had done to Ai and her king; and how the inhabitants of Gibeon had made peace with Israel, and were among them; That they feared greatly, because Gibeon was a great city, as one of the royal cities, and because it was greater than Ai, and all the men thereof were mighty.

Wherefore Adonizedek king of Jerusalem sent unto HOHAM king of Hebron, and unto Piram king of Jarmuth, and unto Japhia king of Lachish, and unto Debir king of Eglon, saying, Come up unto me, and help me, that we may smite Gibeon: for it hath made peace with Joshua and with the children of Israel.

Joshua 10:1-4

Five demonic kings attacked Joshua at the outset of his enlargement campaign. In this chapter, we will look at the second demonic king that you must overcome in your quest to achieve enlargement and growth in the ministry.

The second demonic prince you need to fight in order to enlarge and expand your ministry is the demonic power of King HOHAM. King HOHAM represents a demonic king who came against the people of God. Whatever fights the people of God is fighting God’s purpose. HOHAM means to impel, to drive and to urge.

There are many things that are demonic compulsions. There are longings, lusts and yearnings that build up in us. Many of these are demonic in origin. King HOHAM is a symbol of demonic desires, cravings, needs, wishes and impulses that come to you to destroy your ministry.

Being forty days tempted of the devil. And in those days he did eat nothing: and when they were ended, he afterward hungered. And the devil said unto him, If thou be the Son of God, command this stone that it be made bread.

Luke 4:2-3

There are many demonic urges that come to pastors and Christian leaders through the flesh. In order to serve God, you must kill, quench and extinguish all demonic urges in your life. Many desires and urges are prompted by the devil. When Jesus was in the wilderness, waiting on God, He began to have urges to convert the stones that were lying about into bread. The hunger He felt was so strong! These terrible demonic urges and feelings were impressed upon Him in the wilderness. We can see from scripture that the urge to turn stones into bread was demonic in its origin. The urge was actually a satanically inspired craving and yearning.

If Jesus Christ could have such urges, cravings and longings, then you must expect the same thing. You must overcome urges and longings to sleep. You must overcome the strong yearning to eat.

You must overcome urges to have sex with ladies you meet. You must overcome demonic cravings to have sex with every beautiful lady you encounter. You must overcome evil passions that build up and drive you to commit fornication and adultery. You must overcome the urge to watch pornography. Whatever is driving you to spend time studying pornography is cursed in the name of Jesus! Whatever urge you have to take over someone’s husband is cursed!

You must overcome the urge to have more and more money. You must overcome the urge to steal. You must overcome the urge to smoke cigarettes! You must dry out the desire to smoke weed. You must overcome the urge to drink alcohol. You must stamp out the craving and longing for drugs. You must extinguish all wicked impulses to fight, kill and steal.

King Hoham is a demonic power whose name means to impel, to urge, to drive. Today begins your victory over King Hoham and all the wicked cravings, desires, urges and impulses that he inspires.

“Which when the apostles, Barnabas and Paul, heard of, they rent their clothes, and ran in among the people, crying out, And saying, Sirs, why do ye these things? WE ALSO ARE MEN OF LIKE PASSIONS WITH YOU, AND PREACH UNTO YOU that ye should turn from these vanities unto the living God, which made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and all things that are therein:” (Acts 14:14-15)

People who are preachers are men of like passions and desires. Paul explained clearly that he was a man of passions, desires and cravings but these did not prevent him from preaching the gospel.
