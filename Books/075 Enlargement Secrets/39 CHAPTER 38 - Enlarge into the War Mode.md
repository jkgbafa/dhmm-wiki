### CHAPTER 38\

Enlarge into the War Mode\
===============================================

And Joshua the son of Nun SENT OUT OF SHITTIM TWO MEN TO SPY SECRETLY, saying, Go view the land, even Jericho. AND THEY WENT, and came into an harlot’s house, named Rahab, and lodged there. And it was told the king of Jericho, saying, Behold, there came men in hither to night of the children of Israel to search out the country. And the king of Jericho sent unto Rahab, saying, Bring forth the men that are come to thee, which are entered into thine house: for they be come to search out all the country. And the woman took the two men, and hid them, and said thus, There came men unto me, but I wist not whence they were: And it came to pass about the time of shutting of the gate, when it was dark, that the men went out: whither the men went I wot not: PURSUE AFTER THEM QUICKLY; FOR YE SHALL OVERTAKE THEM. But she had brought them up to the roof of the house, and hid them with the stalks of flax, which she had laid in order upon the roof.

Joshua 2:1-6

War changes everything! During war, it seems it is legal for soldiers to shoot fellow human beings and drop bombs on other fellow human beings because they have declared war on them. One soldier can kill a thousand other men; yet he will not be charged with murder. War changes everything! War involves things like spies, secrets, lies, hiding, deception, covenants, agreements, promises, secrecy, hidden things, treachery, covered things and even harlots. In times of peace, many of these things would be unacceptable.

In the war that Joshua fought, he had to deploy the friendship of a harlot, he had to sleep in a harlot’s house, he had to use spies, he had to tell lies, he had to hide, he had to deceive the authorities, he had to enter agreements with enemies and have secret covenants with some others. These would all be considered wrong in a time of peace.

The Joshua generation will be able to win any war they find themselves in. They will walk confidently and wage war on an enemy who only wants to kill them. To fail to operate according to the rules of war can make you lose a war. Often, it is some particular things that will give you the key to victory over an enemy who does not have any rules at all.

I do not know what the war mode is for you. However, you need to war a good warfare so that you achieve the victory. Do not underestimate your enemy. Fight extremes with extremes. Your enemy is fighting to kill you. He is deploying all sorts of wicked strategies to kill you, to steal from you and to destroy you. Do not hesitate to wipe out your enemy.

When Jesus was ministering on earth, He was challenged severely about certain things He did. He was questioned about the legitimacy of His ministry. Jesus was accused of violating the Law. They were so angry with Him that they wanted to destroy Him.

And he entered again into the synagogue; and there was a man there which had a withered hand. And they watched him, whether he would heal him on the sabbath day; that they might accuse him. And he saith unto the man which had the withered hand, Stand forth. And he saith unto them, IS IT LAWFUL TO DO GOOD ON THE SABBATH DAYS, OR TO DO EVIL? TO SAVE LIFE, OR TO KILL? But they held their peace. And when he had looked round about on them with anger, being grieved for the hardness of their hearts, he saith unto the man, Stretch forth thine hand. And he stretched it out: and his hand was restored whole as the other. And the Pharisees went forth, and straightway took counsel with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him.

Mark 3:1-6

Jesus asked an important question, “Is it right to do good or to do evil? You may have to ask yourself the question that Jesus asked, “Is it lawful to do good or to do evil. Is it lawful to save lives or to kill?” In every battle of your life and ministry, you must be doing good and you must be saving lives!
