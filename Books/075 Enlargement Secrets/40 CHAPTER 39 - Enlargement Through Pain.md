### CHAPTER 39\

Enlargement Through Pain\
==============================================

At that time the Lord said unto Joshua, Make thee sharp knives, and circumcise again the children of Israel the second time. And Joshua made him sharp knives, and circumcised the children of Israel at the hill of the foreskins.

Joshua 5:2-3

Enlargement is possible when you understand pain. The whole world is living in pain. There are many painful things going on all around you. People have been raped, robbed and murdered. Much injustice has taken place in the last twenty-four hours. There is a lot of suffering, sickness and death everywhere. People are living in sin and do not know how to get out of it.

Without understanding the pain that is in the world, you cannot minister to people properly. The majority of the world is not experiencing a prosperity boom of new cars and houses. The majority of the world is experiencing the opposite. The majority of the world is living in sin, sickness, difficulty and complicated problems. The pain of the world must be on your heart. You cannot live in utopia and expect to understand the world.

Joshua and his team were asked to circumcise the children of Israel for the second time. They made sharp knives to circumcise the children of Israel. The Israelites were made to experience pain as part of their calling. Sometimes, experiencing pain is part of your call. It is not that you have done anything wrong. It is simply a part of God’s purpose for you – to go through certain painful things. Circumcision is part of the call and its attendant pain is part of the call.

There is no ministry without pain. Pain is a part of your call. If you are following Jesus, you will experience all the things that Jesus experienced. When Jesus was arrested, some people tried to prevent Him from being taken away. They pulled out swords and attacked the team that had come to arrest Jesus. Jesus stopped them and asked an important question, “How else will the scriptures be fulfilled?

Then said Jesus unto him, Put up again thy sword into his place: for all they that take the sword shall perish with the sword. Thinkest thou that I cannot now pray to my Father, and he shall presently give me more than twelve legions of angels? BUT HOW THEN SHALL THE SCRIPTURES BE FULFILLED, THAT THUS IT MUST BE?

Matthew 26:52-54

Jesus asked the question, “How else will the scriptures be fulfilled?” Jesus wanted to know how the prophecies would come to pass if they would not come to pass through the very people standing there. The scriptures can only be fulfilled through the people that are around you.

Think about it: the only qualification for being a Judas is that you must be very close to the leader. Jesus said that a disciple is not above his master. You must expect to go through the same things that Jesus experienced.

The scripture cannot be broken. Everything that is written will surely happen. There are many things that have to come to pass in your life and ministry. As you follow Jesus, you must expect to see all the things that Jesus saw. The disciple is not greater than his master. Jesus suffered greatly at the hands of wicked and unreasonable men. You can expect to meet wicked and unreasonable men. Your peace will be painfully circumcised from you.

Jesus was betrayed by Judas! You can expect to encounter Judases in your life and ministry. Jesus experienced pain! You can expect to have pain! Jesus was abandoned by His disciples! You can expect your disciples to abandon you! Peter denied Jesus! You can expect some of your closest disciples to deny you and say they do not know you. There will be times where your closest disciples will be painfully circumcised away from you.

He is chastened also with pain upon his bed, and the multitude of his bones with strong pain:

Job 33:19

God can chasten you with pain and it is a sign that you are His son. When you are not chastened you are a bastard.

For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth. If ye endure chastening, God dealeth with you as with sons; for what son is he whom the father chasteneth not? But if ye be without chastisement, whereof all are partakers, then are ye bastards, and not sons.

Hebrews 12:6-8

I wish I could tell you that you are going to be happy all the time because you are serving God. But that is not the case. If you stay on the straight and narrow path, I can assure you that there will be times of great sadness and pain ahead. There will be times of circumcision. The painfulness of your experiences will have a good effect on your soul. You will be wiser because of the tears you have shed.

It is better to go to the house of mourning, than to go to the house of feasting: for that is the end of all men; and THE LIVING WILL LAY IT TO HIS HEART.

Ecclesiastes 7:2
