### CHAPTER 36\

Enlarge by Wiping out Giants\
==================================================

But the men that went up with him said, We be not able to go up against the people; for they are stronger than we. And they brought up an evil report of the land which they had searched unto the children of Israel, saying, The land, through which we have gone to search it, is a land that eateth up the inhabitants thereof; and all the people that we saw in it are men of a great stature. And THERE WE SAW THE GIANTS, THE SONS OF ANAK, which come of the giants: and we were in our own sight as grasshoppers, and so we were in their sight.

Numbers 13:31-33

The generation of Moses was frightened by the giants they encountered. Everyone is going to encounter giants in life and ministry. A giant is an extra-large problem! There are many extra-large problems waiting for you on the road of ministry. The Moses generation was afraid of giants. The existence of giants destroyed them. You must not run away from giants like the Moses generation did. You must not bring back an evil report. You must not sow seeds of doubt in everyone around you. You must not speak evil of God’s purpose just because you have seen a giant.

The Joshua generation kills the long-necked giants of their day. Whatever represents a giant in your life will bow to your ministry. You can overcome the giants in your life and ministry. Because of your faith and obedience you will cut off all large-sized enemies.

And at that time CAME JOSHUA, AND CUT OFF THE ANAKIMS from the mountains, from Hebron, from Debir, from Anab, and from all the mountains of Judah, and from all the mountains of Israel: Joshua destroyed them utterly with their cities. There was none of the Anakims left in the land of the children of Israel: only in Gaza, in Gath, and in Ashdod, there remained.

Joshua 11:21-22

What Are the Giants of Ministry?

1\. The giant of poverty

Then said I, Wisdom is better than strength: nevertheless the poor man’s wisdom is despised, and his words are not heard.

Ecclesiastes 9:16

In many places, one of the giants of ministry is poverty. It is a giant that seeks to wipe out your ministry. The giant of poverty will seek to drown the words and the ministry that God has given to you. A poor man’s words are not heard!

Books written in certain parts of the world are rarely read. The poverty and difficulty in these places seem to tell the rest of the world that no wisdom can be found there, so do not read any books that come from there.

The giant of poverty will prevent you from building church buildings and conducting crusades. It is important that you overcome this giant because it can destroy your ministry. Many ministers are destroyed by financial giants and that is why they become motivational speakers and financial preachers. In their quest to overcome the giant of poverty, they metamorphose into another version of ministry. True ministry is to preach about the cross of Jesus Christ.

2\. The giant of “pornia”

Wherefore my sentence is, that we trouble not them, which from among the Gentiles are turned to God: But that we write unto them, that they abstain from pollutions of idols, and from fornication, and from things strangled, and from blood.

Acts 15:19-20

Fornication is from the Greek word, “pornia” which encompasses fornication, adultery, incest, homosexuality, prostitution, bestiality, pornography and so on. Fornication represents the entire range of immoral activities that exist in the world.

Many people have had some kind of sexual and immoral experience in their lives. Most of the time, these sexual and immoral experiences influence their future lives and ministries. Sexual sin is a spiritual giant that consumes those who fall for it. Satan has ensured that sexual immorality is an extra-large problem today.

Today, all kinds of sexual activities and perversions are freely available for almost anyone. It is important to overcome this giant because it is not possible to minister the word of God whilst living in immorality. The early church was given guidelines of things to avoid. One of the important things for the early church was the giant of fornication.

The giant of “pornia” often remains a giant because people do not see it and deal with it as a giant. Most people who fall for immorality have dealt mildly and casually with the problem of fornication. You must rise up and be extreme in your fight to keep yourself from sexual immorality. You can and you will overcome the giant of sexual immorality!

3\. The giant of bad marriages

If anyone does not know how to manage his own family, how can he take care of God’s church?

1 Timothy 3:5 (NIV)

Contrary to the picture that is commonly given, many marriages of ministers are not happy ones. I was saddened to hear both Derek Prince and Kenneth Hagin say that most of the ministers they knew about had bad and difficult marriages. Bad marriages create a de-housing effect for ministers.

During the Second World War, the British Air Force decided to bomb the cities of Germany and destroy the homes of the German citizens. This controversial decision had the effect of de-housing the German citizenry who no more had homes to go to. This act of war was a terrible blow against the Germans who were effectively de-housed. The homeless population of Germans became more vulnerable and easier to defeat.

The giant of bad marriages de-houses ministers who do not have a peaceful home to go to. They no longer feel happy in their homes. They no longer have a place of rest. They no longer have a place to relax and feel free.

Satan seeks to de-house God’s servants by giving them bad marriages. It is time for you to fight the root of bad marriages in the ministry.
