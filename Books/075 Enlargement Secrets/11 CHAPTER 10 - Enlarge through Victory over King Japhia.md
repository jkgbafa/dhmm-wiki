### CHAPTER 10\

Enlarge through Victory over King Japhia\
==============================================================

Now it came to pass, when Adonizedek king of Jerusalem had heard how Joshua had taken Ai, and had utterly destroyed it; as he had done to Jericho and her king, so he had done to Ai and her king; and how the inhabitants of Gibeon had made peace with Israel, and were among them; That they feared greatly, because Gibeon was a great city, as one of the royal cities, and because it was greater than Ai, and all the men thereof were mighty.

Wherefore Adonizedek king of Jerusalem sent unto Hoham king of Hebron, and unto Piram king of Jarmuth, and unto JAPHIA king of Lachish, and unto Debir king of Eglon, saying, Come up unto me, and help me, that we may smite Gibeon: for it hath made peace with Joshua and with the children of Israel.

Joshua 10:1-4

Enlargement is only possible when you fight and conquer all the five demonic powers that confront you at the beginning of your ministry.

The next demonic power you need to fight in order to enlarge your ministry is the demonic power of King JAPHIA. King JAPHIA represents a demonic king who came against the people of God. Whatever fights the people of God is fighting God’s purpose. JAPHIA means “shining”.

You must overcome all shining and attractive things in this world. As long as you are consumed by the demonic and deceptive shine of King JAPHIA, you cannot do much for God.

One missionary said, “Shame on London.” He had been attracted to the city of London so much that he had not been of much use to God. Our world is filled with shiny and attractive deceptions. Many things are made to shine and look far more glamorous than they really are.

Once you are attracted to the glamour and the glitter in this world you will not be of much use to God. The world is full of deceptions. The god of this world has ensured that everything in this world is based on shiny deceptions.

Three Shining Deceptions You Must Overcome

1\. You must overcome the shining deceptions and attractions of money and riches.

If you do not overcome the deception of riches, you will follow it and it leads you to nothingness. This is why Jesus said, “Do not lay up treasures on earth. Lay up treasures in heaven” (Matthew 6:19-20). Do not be attracted to the riches and wealth of this world.

For in one hour so great riches is come to nought. And every shipmaster, and all the company in ships, and sailors, and as many as trade by sea, stood afar off,

Revelation 18:17

People who follow the demonic power of Japhia will amount to nothing in God. People cannot go on church planting missions because the demonic shining rewards of silver and gold neutralize the call of God.

2\. You must overcome the shining deceptions and attractions of glamorous cities.

Standing afar off for the fear of her torment, saying, Alas, alas, that great city Babylon, that mighty city! for in one hour is thy judgment come. And the merchants of the earth shall weep and mourn over her; for no man buyeth their merchandise any more: The merchandise of gold, and silver, and precious stones, and of pearls, and fine linen, and purple, and silk, and scarlet, and all thyine wood, and all manner vessels of ivory, and all manner vessels of most precious wood, and of brass, and iron, and marble, And cinnamon, and odours, and ointments, and frankincense, and wine, and oil, and fine flour, and wheat, and beasts, and sheep, and horses, and chariots, and slaves, and souls of men.

And the fruits that thy soul lusted after are departed from thee, and all things which were dainty and goodly are departed from thee, and thou shalt find them no more at all. The merchants of these things, which were made rich by her, shall stand afar off for the fear of her torment, weeping and wailing, And saying, Alas, alas, that great city, that was clothed in fine linen, and purple, and scarlet, and decked with gold, and precious stones, and pearls!

Revelation 18:10-16

Those fulfilling their ministries are often found in poor and humble towns and villages. Jesus sent us to preach the gospel to the poor. You and I have been sent to preach the gospel to the poor. The more you are attracted to glamorous cities, the less you can go where you are supposed to go.

3\. You must overcome the shining deceptions and attractions of beautiful women.

Many beautiful women attract a series of men to themselves. They shine, they sparkle and they call for you, as they have called for a host of others. Being a spiritual man who is called to build the kingdom of God, you must overcome the spirit of Japhia that operates through some shiny, glamorous and attractive women.

For all nations have drunk of the wine of the wrath of her fornication, and the kings of the earth have committed fornication with her, and the merchants of the earth are waxed rich through the abundance of her delicacies. And I heard another voice from heaven, saying, Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues. For her sins have reached unto heaven, and God hath remembered her iniquities.

Revelation 18:3-5

The more attractive a woman is, the more you must be careful about her. Beauty is vain! A lot of beauty often implies a lot of vanity. That is why you must be careful about shining beauties. A shining, glorious and attractive woman can take away your ministry through the spirit of King Japhia. You are overcoming every demonic King Japhia that has targeted you!
