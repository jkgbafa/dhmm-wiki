### CHAPTER 12\

Enlarge by Involving Everyone in the Work\
===============================================================

But the children of Israel committed a trespass in the accursed thing: for Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took of the accursed thing: and the anger of the LORD was kindled against the children of Israel. And Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, which is beside Bethaven, on the east side of Bethel, and spake unto them, saying, Go up and view the country. And the men went up and viewed Ai. And they returned to Joshua, and said unto him, LET NOT ALL THE PEOPLE GO UP; BUT LET ABOUT TWO OR THREE THOUSAND MEN GO UP AND SMITE AI; AND MAKE NOT ALL THE PEOPLE TO LABOUR THITHER; for they are but few. So there went up thither of the people about three thousand men: and they fled before the men of Ai. And THE MEN OF AI SMOTE OF THEM about thirty and six men: for they chased them from before the gate even unto Shebarim, and smote them in the going down: wherefore the hearts of the people melted, and became as water. And Joshua rent his clothes, and fell to the earth upon his face before the ark of the LORD until the eventide, he and the elders of Israel, and put dust upon their heads.

Joshua 7:1-6

Enlargement is achieved when all the people in the church are mobilized to do the work of God. In most churches, just a few people get involved in the work. One of Joshua’s greatest mistakes and defeats was when he went up to war with just a section of the nation. He took only three thousand men to fight against the city of Ai. He was soundly defeated and many of the Israelites were killed.

There was also a spiritual reason why they were defeated. There was sin in their camp. The spiritual sin is what made them take steps in the natural that would lead to their defeat. God was not pleased with them so their wisdom was defective! What was this defective wisdom? It was the mistake of involving only a section of the people in the campaign. After they were soundly defeated and after they repented of their sin, God gave them better wisdom for the campaign. This time, they had the idea of involving everyone in the war. Notice how radically different the strategy was this time.

And the Lord said unto Joshua, Fear not, neither be thou dismayed: TAKE ALL THE PEOPLE OF WAR WITH THEE, and arise, go up to Ai: see, I have given into thy hand the king of Ai, and his people, and his city, and his land: And thou shalt do to Ai and her king as thou didst unto Jericho and her king: only the spoil thereof, and the cattle thereof, shall ye take for a prey unto yourselves: lay thee an ambush for the city behind it. SO JOSHUA AROSE, AND ALL THE PEOPLE OF WAR, TO GO UP AGAINST AI: and Joshua chose out thirty thousand mighty men of valour, and sent them away by night.

Joshua 8:1-3

Notice that in Joshua chapter seven, they took only three thousand people and in Joshua chapter eight they took all the people. If you want to experience the enlargement that God is taking you to, then understand the wisdom of involving all the people.

Do not departmentalize evangelism. Do not make evangelism a side mission. Do not let the outreach of the church be done by a few people. Do not allow prayer to be done by a section of the church. Do not departmentalize full-time ministry. Everyone must be all out! Everyone must be ready to die for the Lord. Everyone must go on missions. Everyone must serve the Lord with all their heart and their soul. All through the Bible, you will see God stretching forth His hand to raise up many people. It is always a mistake to departmentalize what God has given to everybody. Notice how God always extends His hand to everyone.

God Calls All People

1\. All who labour and are heavy laden are pulled into the work of God.

Come unto me, ALL YE THAT LABOUR and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.

Matthew 11:28

2\. God sends us to all people in the world.

And he said unto them, Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature.

Mark 16:15

3\. God draws all men to Himself.

And I, if I be lifted up from the earth, will draw all men unto me.

John 12:32

4\. God wants all men to be saved. God wants all men to come to the knowledge of the truth.

Who will have ALL MEN to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth.

1 Timothy 2:4

5\. God commands all men to repent and to change their minds.

And the times of this ignorance God winked at; but now commandeth ALL MEN every where to repent:

Acts 17:30

6\. God saves all men by His power.

For therefore we both labour and suffer reproach, because we trust in the living God, who is the Saviour of ALL MEN, specially of those that believe.

1 Timothy 4:10

7\. God teaches all nations.

Go ye therefore, and teach ALL NATIONS, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:

Matthew 28:19
