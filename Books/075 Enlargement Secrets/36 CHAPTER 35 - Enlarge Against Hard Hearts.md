### CHAPTER 35\

Enlarge Against Hard Hearts\
=================================================

Joshua made war a long time with all those kings. There was not a city that made peace with the children of Israel, save the Hivites the inhabitants of Gibeon: all other they took in battle. FOR IT WAS OF THE LORD TO HARDEN THEIR HEARTS, that they should come against Israel in battle, that he might destroy them utterly, and that they might have no favour, but that he might destroy them, as the Lord commanded Moses.

Joshua 11:18-20

Enlargement, expansion and growth are possible in spite of the hardened hearts that you encounter.

In the ministry you will meet people with hard hearts. A person with a hard heart is the same as a stubborn person. A stubborn and difficult person is a catalyst for conflict and war.

The Joshua generation is forced to fight against hard-hearted people. Do not be discouraged when you encounter hard-hearted people. When you encounter a hardened person, sharpen all your skills for fighting wars. Study the art of war and the art of being a good general. Rise up and fight a good fight because God has hardened the hearts of these people for your sake.

Hardened people are part of God’s plan for your enlargement. Hardened people will stir up the spirit of the warrior in you.

All through my ministry, I have encountered hardened people who were unyielding in their dealings with me. Every single one of them was used by God to carve out His will. The Joshua generation encountered people who hardened themselves against the Israelites. These hard-hearted people gave the Israelites no other option than to fight and eliminate them. It was of the Lord to harden their hearts so that God would destroy them utterly.

Do not be sad when you meet people who never agree, never say yes, never give in, never yield, never soften, never bow and never flow. Stubbornness is always a negative thing. Stubbornness always leads to destruction. I have never seen a stubborn person who is not eventually destroyed. Stubbornness in your enemy is God’s way of destroying him. Notice many scriptures which say how God intentionally caused the enemies of Israel to harden their hearts.

1\. God hardened Pharaoh’s heart because He wanted to destroy Egypt. Sometimes it is God who is hardening the heart of your enemy.

And the LORD said unto Moses, Go in unto Pharaoh: for I have hardened his heart, and the heart of his servants, that I might shew these my signs before him:

Exodus 10:1

2\. God hardened the hearts of the kings in The Promised Land so that Joshua would destroy them.

There was not a city that made peace with the children of Israel, save the Hivites the inhabitants of Gibeon: all other they took in battle. FOR IT WAS OF THE LORD TO HARDEN THEIR HEARTS, THAT THEY SHOULD COME AGAINST ISRAEL IN BATTLE, THAT HE MIGHT DESTROY THEM UTTERLY, and that they might have no favour, but that he might destroy them, as the Lord commanded Moses.

Joshua 11:19-20

The victory over the hard-hearted and the unchanging ones is part of the call and destiny of Joshua and Caleb. Do not be discouraged that you have to fight with so many hard-hearted ones who never agree, never give in, or never soften at all unless there is a full-fledged fight.

3\. God hardened the heart of Sihon the King of Heshbon so that he could expand Israel. Throughout your ministry you will encounter people with stubborn hearts. The stubborn hearts you encounter will lead you to fight for total deliverance.

But Sihon king of Heshbon would not let us pass by him: for the LORD thy God hardened his spirit, and made his heart obstinate, that he might deliver him into thy hand, as appeareth this day.

Deuteronomy 2:30

Some years ago, we encountered some hardened characters in our city. We tried to make peace with them but nothing worked out. Every meeting with the community seemed to backfire. Meetings with government officials did not yield anything. No matter what we did, the community in which our church was planted was hostile towards us. Some people pledged that they would not rest until our church was completely destroyed. Through the stubbornness of those community leaders, we were forced to find another location that became our new expanded base. Through these stubborn people, the will of God came to pass and today we have experienced church expansion and enlargement.

Perhaps, you wanted to keep a friend that you really cherished but the person was unyielding and stubborn. Do not worry too much. Perhaps God has hardened the heart of that person. God wants to purge you of that relationship. God wants to take that person out of your life and ministry.

You must enlarge even when you encounter the hardened hearts of opponents and wicked people. Your encounter with hardened people will lead to your expansion and deliverance.
