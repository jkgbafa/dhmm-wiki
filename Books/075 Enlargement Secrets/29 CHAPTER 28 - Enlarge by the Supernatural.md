### CHAPTER 28\

Enlarge by the Supernatural\
=================================================

And it came to pass, as they fled from before Israel, and were in the going down to Bethhoron, that the Lord cast down great stones from heaven upon them unto Azekah, and they died: they were more which died with hailstones than they whom the children of Israel slew with the sword. Then spake Joshua to the Lord in the day when the Lord delivered up the Amorites before the children of Israel, AND HE SAID IN THE SIGHT OF ISRAEL, SUN, STAND THOU STILL UPON GIBEON; AND THOU, MOON, IN THE VALLEY OF AJALON. AND THE SUN STOOD STILL, AND THE MOON STAYED, until the people had avenged themselves upon their enemies. Is not this written in the book of Jasher? So the sun stood still in the midst of heaven, and hasted not to go down about a whole day.

Joshua 10:11-13

Expansion is a supernatural occurrence. A short man becomes a tall man and no one knows why! You must believe in the supernatural. God is a God who does supernatural wonders. God is fearful! God is glorious! God is holy! God is special! God is fearful! God must be praised! God does wonders! The God you serve is supernatural!

Who is like unto thee, O LORD, among the gods? who is like thee, glorious in holiness, fearful in praises, doing wonders?

Exodus 15:11

The Joshua generation are supernatural through and through. Every natural effort that the Joshua generation made to expand was connected to the supernatural. Fighting wars, was accompanied by supernatural support from God. At one point the sun and the moon were made to stand still so that Joshua could continue fighting and destroying the enemy. Being born again puts you in the realm of the supernatural. Every born-again Christian is supernatural because he is born of the Spirit. Everyone born of the Spirit is supernatural and mysterious.

The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it cometh, and whither it goeth: so is every one that is born of the Spirit.

John 3:8

If your ministry consists of only giving Bible lessons every Sunday then you are not in the real ministry. Ministry is more than giving Bible teachings on Sunday. Ministry is a supernatural thing. Ministry is a supernatural experience. You will be learning new things every day. There is a big difference between being a school teacher and being a supernatural minister of God. Joshua was an army commander but he was also a supernatural minister of God. There were supernatural things that accompanied his ministry.

What supernatural signs and wonders accompany your ministry? Today, people simply look at finances in order to tell whether God is with them or not. If they have more money, they think that God is still with them. Perhaps you should look for supernatural signs and wonders to be sure that God is still with you. Notice how Jesus was approved by God.

Ye men of Israel, hear these words; Jesus of Nazareth, A MAN APPROVED OF GOD AMONG YOU BY MIRACLES AND WONDERS AND SIGNS, which God did by him in the midst of you, as ye yourselves also know:

Acts 2:22

What supernatural things are happening in your ministry? You must have supernatural dreams! You must have supernatural words of knowledge. You must have divine revelations. You must experience the supernatural power of God fighting for you and striking down your enemies. God will supernaturally neutralize all those who have lifted up their horns against your life and ministry.

There are three things that every minister must allow the supernatural power of God to do for you. If you want to experience enlargement as Joshua did, you must allow the supernatural to happen in your life and ministry.

1\. You must allow God to supernaturally avenge you.

Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath: for it is written, Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord.

Romans 12:19

All through life, you will have people treating you wrongly.

Vengeance is mine; I will repay, says the Lord! God is into the repayment business. He will repay everyone who treats you badly. God pays people back for their wickedness. You must wait and see how God avenges you speedily. Jesus was sent into this world to experience injustice. As the Father sent Him into the world, you are also being sent into the world to experience injustice and wickedness. It will humble you and soften you. However, God will one day unleash His vengeance on those who mistreat you. The Jews who killed innocent Jesus Christ have never ceased to reap unending injustice, hatred, discrimination and loathing by the rest of the world.

2\. You must allow God to supernaturally deal with those who touch the anointed.

Saying, Touch not mine anointed, and do my prophets no harm.

Psalm 105:15

If you are God’s anointed, when people touch you and attack you, they will experience a boomerang and an explosion of power against them. The Lord has warned in His word, “Do not touch my anointed.” You must learn to trust that God will deal with those who mishandle you. You are the anointed of the Lord. When they strike at you, they are striking you through the anointing. Blasphemy against the Holy Ghost is a very dangerous thing to do. I have watched God strike at those who have struck at me. I have watched them fall and come down very low. I have seen with my own eyes the negative effects of touching the anointed of God.

3\. You must allow God to supernaturally envelope your enemies with curses.

But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the LORD thy God, to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day; that all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee:

Deuteronomy 28:15

Curses are real things. I believe that curses and blessings are realities. They are perhaps the only explanations of the unfortunate occurrences in our world. A supernatural minister of God must believe in the power of blessings and curses. A man of God must believe in the supernatural power of spoken words. Moses had no way of protecting the nation of Israel from backsliding leaders. He used the power of blessings and curses to guide Israel through the centuries. Blessings and curses are supernatural statements spoken into the atmosphere. Blessings and curses come to pass mysteriously.

A curse is an appeal to a supernatural power to intervene in a situation. A man of God must appeal to the supernatural. I have appealed to the supernatural many times. I have appealed and implored the supernatural power of blessings and curses on many occasions. A time will come when you will have nothing but the supernatural hand of God to look up to.
