### CHAPTER 29\

Enlarge by Overcoming Distraction\
=======================================================

And the Lord discomfited them before Israel, and slew them with a great slaughter at Gibeon, and chased them along the way that goeth up to Bethhoron, and smote them to Azekah, and unto Makkedah …AND IT WAS TOLD JOSHUA, SAYING, THE FIVE KINGS ARE FOUND HID IN A CAVE AT MAKKEDAH. AND JOSHUA SAID, ROLL GREAT STONES UPON THE MOUTH OF THE CAVE, AND SET MEN BY IT FOR TO KEEP THEM: AND STAY YE NOT, BUT PURSUE AFTER YOUR ENEMIES, and smite the hindmost of them; suffer them not to enter into their cities: for the Lord your God hath delivered them into your hand. And it came to pass, when Joshua and the children of Israel had made an end of slaying them with a very great slaughter, till they were consumed, that the rest which remained of them entered into fenced cities. And all the people returned to the camp to Joshua at Makkedah in peace: none moved his tongue against any of the children of Israel. Then said Joshua, Open the mouth of the cave, and bring out those five kings unto me out of the cave.

Joshua 10:10, 17-22

Do not be deceived by the opportunity to celebrate half-baked victories. Enlargement and expansion efforts are cancelled by unnecessary celebrations, jollity and frivolity. Pastors who are involved with many diverse activities cannot make their churches grow. Church growth is one of the most difficult achievements to attain to.

Joshua maintained his aim of destroying the armies of the enemy. A lesser leader would have been distracted from his real goal. Many people celebrate when there is nothing really to celebrate. Joshua was not ready to celebrate anything when he had not accomplished his goals. As part of the campaign, five key kings were captured. These were King Adonizedek of Jerusalem King Hoham of Hebron, King Piram of Jarmuth, King Japhia of Lachish, and King Debir of Eglon. This victory would have distracted lesser people from their original aim or vision. But Joshua simply put the five kings into a cave and rolled a stone over the mouth of the cave. He kept the five kings in a cave until he had finished the real business of destroying the armies of the enemy. It was only after Joshua had eliminated the real enemies that he turned his attention to kings and other powerful men.

What is distracting you from your real goal? There are people who spend a lot of time and money celebrating events. Indeed, there are many things to celebrate. There will always be anniversaries, birthdays, housewarming parties, engagements, weddings, christenings, funerals, wake-keepings, celebrations for winning elections, Easter, Christmas holidays, family reunions, promotions and even mortgages to celebrate.

Most celebrations are distractions from the real job you have to do. Joshua and his team had a great opportunity to celebrate the capture of the five kings: King Adonizedek of Jerusalem, King Hoham of Hebron, King Piram of Jarmuth, King Japhia of Lachish, and King Debir of Eglon. There was much to celebrate and there was much to thank God for.

The Joshua generation maintained their aim of destroying their enemies. Joshua and his powerful team were not distracted by pride-filled and boastful activities.

Do not be deceived by the opportunity to celebrate half-baked victories. All through my ministry, I have rarely celebrated anything. I usually do not celebrate anniversaries of the church. There is nothing wrong if you do so. However, when I see the work that remains undone, I wonder what there is to celebrate.

People spend lots of money and time to hire decorations, chairs, organise music, make T-shirts, make special clothes, get fireworks, buy flowers, special drinks, cakes, make souvenirs and create the most amazing and spectacular scenery for their special celebrations. Some brides are even made to walk through water as they come through the aisle. You would be amazed to find that some of these celebrants do not have a house to live in. Many people take loans just to make a big splash. Some of the people organising these celebrations end up quarrelling and even separating over their big event.

What has God called you to do? Has He called you to pursue the enemy? Has He called you to build churches? Has He called you to preach the gospel? Has He called you to sing songs? Then concentrate on your work and on the divine opportunity God has given you. Do not turn aside from the blessed opportunity that lies before you. Work hard and work now! The time will come when the opportunity will be gone forever. I am writing this book in the midst of many challenges and difficulties. I am pressing on in spite of many things that seek to take up my time and energy. I am attempting great things, even though I have a thousand serious and threatening distractions that are calling for my attention.

Be careful of confusion! Confusion occurs when many distractions happen at the same time. Confusion is a state of disorder where there is a lack of clearness and distinctness. When something happens on your left, on your right and behind you, you are left wondering which way to turn. Be careful of confusion because it is one of the basic satanic strategies to keep you from pursuing your God-given task.

In thee, O LORD, do I put my trust: let me never be put to confusion.

Psalm 71:1

You must always realise when you are under an attack of confusion. Whilst you are confused the enemy will slip out from under your hand. Whilst you are confused from your many distractions, your opportunity to serve God will silently slip away.

Remember this master key for enlargement. Joshua did not turn aside from following his enemies because of the news of the capture of the five kings. If he had turned aside to celebrate the capture of the five kings, it would have allowed the enemy to escape. These enemies would have strengthened themselves and come back against him like a boomerang. Joshua would have had the difficulty of facing a revived, revengeful and bitter enemy. A wounded lion is still a lion!
