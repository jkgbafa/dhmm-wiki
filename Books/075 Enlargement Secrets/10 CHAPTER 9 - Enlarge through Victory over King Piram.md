### CHAPTER 9\

Enlarge through Victory over King Piram\
=============================================================

Now it came to pass, when Adonizedek king of Jerusalem had heard how Joshua had taken Ai, and had utterly destroyed it; as he had done to Jericho and her king, so he had done to Ai and her king; and how the inhabitants of Gibeon had made peace with Israel, and were among them; That they feared greatly, because Gibeon was a great city, as one of the royal cities, and because it was greater than Ai, and all the men thereof were mighty.

Wherefore Adonizedek king of Jerusalem sent unto Hoham king of Hebron, and unto PIRAM king of Jarmuth, and unto Japhia king of Lachish, and unto Debir king of Eglon, saying, Come up unto me, and help me, that we may smite Gibeon: for it hath made peace with Joshua and with the children of Israel.

Joshua 10:1-4

The next demonic power you need to fight in order to enlarge your ministry is the demonic power of King PIRAM. King PIRAM represents a demonic king who came against the people of God. Whatever fights the people of God is fighting God’s purpose. PIRAM means a wild ass, the demonic power of wild spirits and attacks.

PIRAM speaks of a wild ass. What does a wild ass speak of? A spirit of a wild ass is the spirit of being uncontrollable. An uncontrollable spirit makes you uncontrollable. Satan wants to turn you into a wild and uncontrollable person.

A wild rebellious spirit is one that is out of control, no longer under submission. The more you overcome the devil in your life, the more controllable you will be.

Three Reasons for Overcoming the Wildness of King Piram

1\. Being controllable reveals that you have a balanced approach to the word of God.

Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

2 Timothy 2:15

One day, one of my disciples was angrily emphasizing how every man of God must be left to follow the Holy Spirit. He felt the people in the church should be allowed to do what they believed instead of following instructions from those higher than them.

He said, “It is not following the apostle Paul that makes you a son of God.” He said, “It is in following the Spirit that makes you a son of God.” Indeed, this is true because Romans 8:14 says, “... as many as are led by the spirit of God, they are the sons of God.”

He also said, “Sometimes, to obey God and to be about His business, you must disobey man or a man of God.” This is also true because when Jesus was about His Father’s business, He was clearly not where His parents wanted Him to be. He even seemed to be disobeying His parents who were clearly upset with Him.

These statements have some truth in them. Such statements also have some scriptural support. Indeed, Peter said we must rather obey God than men.

Then Peter and the other apostles answered and said, We ought to obey God rather than men.

Acts 5:29

But half-truths are dangerous things. Satan thrives on half-truths. The whole universe is in a fallen state because Adam and Eve believed the half-truths that satan told them. Scripture is clear about the need to obey people that have the rule over you. Scripture is also clear about the importance of submitting yourself.

OBEY THEM THAT HAVE THE RULE OVER YOU, AND SUBMIT YOURSELVES: for they watch for your souls, as they that must give account, that they may do it with joy, and not with grief: for that is unprofitable for you.

Hebrews 13:17

Therefore, being controlled by men reveals you are under submission. Submission is a beautiful trait that reveals humility. Without humility you are not going anywhere!

2\. Being controllable reveals the Holy Spirit in you.

The more demons you have in you, the more uncontrollable you will be. Demons are uncontrollable spirits who rebelled against God. They do not want to be in submission to the heavenly authority. This is why the mad man of Gadara was uncontrollable. He was demon-possessed. The more the devil is in you, the wilder you become. The more you have the Holy Spirit, the more submissive you will be. Read the scripture below. You will see the effects of being filled with the Holy Spirit. When you are filled with the Holy Spirit, you will be thankful, you will sing spiritual songs, you will make melody in your heart to the Lord and you will be submissive.

And be not drunk with wine, wherein is excess; but BE FILLED WITH THE SPIRIT; Speaking to yourselves in Psalm and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody in your heart to the Lord; Giving thanks always for all things unto God and the Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ; SUBMITTING YOURSELVES ONE TO ANOTHER in the fear of God.

Ephesians 5:18-21

Do not let anyone fool you with rebellion. Rebellious people are possessed with devils of self-destruction. Every rebellious person has a fire within himself. This fire that comes from within, consumes and eventually destroys the person.

Thou hast defiled thy sanctuaries by the multitude of thine iniquities, by the iniquity of thy traffick; therefore will I BRING FORTH A FIRE FROM THE MIDST OF THEE, IT SHALL DEVOUR THEE, and I will bring thee to ashes upon the earth in the sight of all them that behold thee.

Ezekiel 28:18

Sometimes the fire from within is the way a person speaks. Sometimes, the way a person speaks turns against him and destroys him. Rebels often hang themselves with their own words. The words that are coming out of the rebel is the fire from within. When your slander, your backbiting, your gossiping and your treachery is revealed, it completely unravels you and destroys you. When you discover that a person you trust is speaking against you secretly, that person loses his position of trust forever. There are some mistakes that you cannot recover from. Rebellious and wild people speak words that destroy themselves.

3\. Being controllable reveals your maturity.

When Jesus rose from the dead, He said something profound to Peter:

He saith unto him the third time, Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou me? Peter was grieved because he said unto him the third time, Lovest thou me? And he said unto him, Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee. Jesus saith unto him, feed my sheep. Verily, verily, I say unto thee, when thou wast young, thou girdedst thyself, and walkedst whither thou wouldest: but when thou shalt be old, THOU SHALT STRETCH FORTH THY HANDS, and another shall gird thee, and carry thee whither thou wouldest not.

John 21:17-18

In this message Jesus was saying to Peter, “When you are young, you are wild and uncontrollable. As you mature, you humbly stretch forth your hands and allow another person to bind you and carry you to places you do not want to go to.”

Jesus was saying to Peter, “When you are mature you will be controllable. You will allow yourself to be controlled, guided and carried.” Is it not amazing that maturity rather makes you controllable and submissive? You would have thought that the older and more experienced you get, the more uncontrollable you become. It is the opposite. The more mature you are, the more mellow, sober and submissive you become.

You must overcome the demonic power of Piram that speaks of being wild and uncontrollable.

Remember that being controllable is actually a sign of maturity. It is not a sign that you are weak. It is not a sign that you are a “small boy”. Being controllable is not a sign that you are a nobody. It is a sign of your maturity, your faith and your trust. Jesus told Peter, “When you are old, when you are mature, when you are developed and established, you will stretch out your hands and someone else will carry you to places that you do not even want to go to.” Indeed, that is a sign of trust and love.
