### CHAPTER 23\

Enlarge With All Night Work\
=================================================

So Joshua ascended from Gilgal, he, and all the people of war with him, and all the mighty men of valour. And the LORD said unto Joshua, Fear them not: for I have delivered them into thine hand; there shall not a man of them stand before thee. Joshua therefore came unto them suddenly, and WENT UP FROM GILGAL ALL NIGHT. And the LORD discomfited them before Israel, and slew them with a great slaughter at Gibeon, and chased them along the way that goeth up to Bethhoron, and smote them to Azekah, and unto Makkedah.

Joshua 10:7-10

All through this season of your life, you will be enlarging your coast. You will see things you have never dreamed of. You are receiving the wisdom of God for enlargement in your ministry.

Watch out for those who cannot work through the night. Joshua travelled all night in order to defeat the enemy. By the time he was fighting and achieving a great slaughter of the enemy, he had been awake all night, moving at top speed.

Many jobs need long hours of work to accomplish anything. This is where all night work comes in. Sometimes the day is not enough and you need all night work. Sometimes, you need to labour all through the night in order to enlarge, expand and break through.

The Joshua generation will fight hard to enlarge by doing all night work. If you have a good job, you will one day find yourself working all night. If you want to experience enlargement, you must work hard and long. The fact that you are a Christian does not exempt you from the curse on Adam. That curse is clear: “In the sweat of thy face will thou eat bread” (Genesis 3:19). You will need to sweat in order to eat. Sweating means you have been working very hard.

You must get used to the idea of long hard work. You must rise out of mediocrity, laziness and being unproductive. I once met someone who worked for the government in a certain country. He said to me, “We employ five hundred people in our government department.”

He continued, “In actual fact, we do not need more than fifteen people to work there.”

I was amazed as he described how the government insists on him employing five hundred people to do the work of fifteen people. When you work in such an organisation, you will get used to doing very little work. You will also get used to not being paid well. You will get used to lazing around and being unproductive. Those who work in such places often become petty thieves or even all out criminals.

Do not be happy when you do not have much work to do. Do not congratulate yourself when you are able to sleep at work. Do not be excited because you have been given a job that involves sleeping in a chair for half a day. There are many things to do. Indeed, there is so much to do that you may be required to work all night. When you are on an enlargement drive, you will begin to work longer and longer hours until you even work all night.

Seven Activities that Need All Night Work

1\. All night prayer. There are times you need to pray all night. You must learn how to pray with a group all night. You must learn how to pray all night on your own. There is an art to praying all night on your own. The fact that you cannot pray all night on your own reveals something about your lack of discipline.

2\. All night reading with prayer. There are times you must read all night whilst you pray.

3\. All night discussions and strategizing meetings. There are times you need to meet all night as you strategize to do the work of God.

4\. All night counselling. There are times you need to counsel people all through the night. I once counselled a couple all night. At the end of my counselling, there was still no change in the marital crisis.

5\. All night computer work. There are times you need to work on your computer all through the night. In order to write books, I have spent many nights at work. There are many times I started work on one day and ended the next day.

6\. All night construction. Most efficient construction takes place in the day as well as in the night. To save money and to work better, many construction companies work in the night as well. Indeed, if you were searching for flood lights, you would find that flood lights are mostly used for construction.

7\. All night rehearsals. I have been involved in music rehearsals that last all night. Indeed, many times it has taken a whole night to learn a few songs. Good singers practice for long hours and rehearse through the night.
