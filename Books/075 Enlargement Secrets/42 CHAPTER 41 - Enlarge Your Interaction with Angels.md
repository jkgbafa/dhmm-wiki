### CHAPTER 41\

Enlarge Your Interaction with Angels\
==========================================================

And it came to pass, when Joshua was by Jericho, that he lifted up his eyes and looked, and, behold, THERE STOOD A MAN OVER AGAINST HIM WITH HIS SWORD DRAWN IN HIS HAND: and Joshua went unto him, and said unto him, Art thou for us, or for our adversaries? And he said, Nay; but as captain of the host of the Lord am I now come. And Joshua fell on his face to the earth, and did worship, and said unto him, what saith my lord unto his servant? And the captain of the Lord’s host said unto Joshua, Loose thy shoe from off thy foot; for the place whereon thou standest is holy. And Joshua did so.

Joshua 5:13-15

Enlargement is not possible unless you deal with the invisible world.

Joshua was not just an army commander. He was a supernatural and spiritual person who interacted with angels. It is sad to see pastors who behave like secular school teachers without any sign of the supernatural on their lives. There are supernatural factors at work. God is Spirit, angels are spirits, demons are evil spirits! We are dealing with the invisible world. Enlargement is not possible unless you deal with the invisible world!

It is important that you receive the ministration of angels. There are angels everywhere. Angels will fly to the place of your ministry and assist you there. They will strike down your enemies and fight against those who fight against you. You must believe in the presence of these supernatural agents of God’s power. Angels are always at work and your faith in supernatural intervention must be great if you are to experience the power of God.

One day, the Lord Jesus appeared to the prophet Kenneth Hagin and told him that He had sent His angel to him on an earlier occasion. However, because Kenneth Hagin had not been open to the angel, the angel had left without giving his supernatural message. Kenneth Hagin recounted how he had sensed the presence of someone in the room but had not been able to receive the angelic visitation. Because of this, the angel left without saying anything.

If you are not open to the presence of angels, you will not receive what you could from them.

Interacting with Angels

1\. The presence of an angel

And in the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent from God unto a city of Galilee, named Nazareth, To a virgin espoused to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin’s name was Mary. And the angel came in unto her, and said, Hail, thou that art highly favoured, the Lord is with thee: blessed art thou among women.

Luke 1:26-28

You must be able to sense the presence of an angel. God sends angels to minister to you. You may not see the angel but you may sense his presence. The angel who is present in the room with you is sent from the Lord with a message. The angel Gabriel was sent to Mary with a message from the Lord. God will send an angel to bring you a message. I once sensed the presence of an angel when I was in Scotland. It was a very strong presence at the foot of my bed. I received a mysterious message from the Lord. I have kept that Word up till now.

2\. Seeing an angel

For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.

Psalm 91:11

It is important for you to see angels when the Lord sends them to you. Pray for God to open your eyes. You will see angels. One day, on a flight across the Atlantic Ocean, I saw “someone” sitting by me on the plane, wearing a light blue robe. I was surprised. I had just seen an angel and he was flying with me to where I was going. You would be surprised at the number of angels that accompany you on your various trips.

On a number of occasions people have seen angels around me. One day, someone saw a large angel standing by me on the church car park. She had seen an angel with me before but this angel was larger. She told me that she sensed that God had promoted me because the angel that she saw was much larger and looked much more senior than the previous angel.

One time, whilst ministering in Canada, I noticed a lady sitting in the congregation and weeping. I wondered what I was doing wrong. As I continued ministering, she continued weeping. After the service, I asked her what was wrong. She pointed to some chairs on the stage and said Jesus was sitting behind me and watching me as I ministered. This lady had experienced a supernatural vision with her eyes open.

On another occasion, whilst ministering at a conference in South Africa, a white South African lady saw several angels in the hall. This lady said she had a special gift to see angels. She was so amazed at the number of angels that entered the hall during the ministration. She made sure that the message got to me about the numerous angels that she had seen in the hall during the ministration.

3\. The strike of a redeeming angel

The Angel which redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads; and let my name be named on them, and the name of my fathers Abraham and Isaac; and let them grow into a multitude in the midst of the earth.

Genesis 48:16

Jacob said that an angel had redeemed him from evil throughout his life. God sends angels to help His precious servants. God sends angels to contend against those who contend against you. God will send angels to strike your enemies. Instead of you having to chase your enemies, fighting angels will be released to fight for you.

One day, during a particular season in our church, a brother noticed some movements behind me as he reviewed the video of the service. As he looked more closely and intently at the video, he saw an angel standing behind me, flexing his muscles. Initially, I passed it off as a shadow on the screen. But after looking at it closely, it was impossible to ignore the reality. It was an awesome sight to behold a real angel caught on camera. You can have a look at that video and enjoy the supernatural power of God through angels.
