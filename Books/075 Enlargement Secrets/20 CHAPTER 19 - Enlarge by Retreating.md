### CHAPTER 19\

Enlarge by Retreating\
===========================================

And it came to pass, when the king of Ai saw it, that they hasted and rose up early, and the men of the city went out against Israel to battle, he and all his people, at a time appointed, before the plain; but he wist not that there were liers in ambush against him behind the city. AND JOSHUA AND ALL ISRAEL MADE AS IF THEY WERE BEATEN BEFORE THEM, AND FLED BY THE WAY OF THE WILDERNESS. And all the people that were in Ai were called together to pursue after them: and they pursued after Joshua, and were drawn away from the city. And there was not a man left in Ai or Bethel, that went not out after Israel: and they left the city open, and pursued after Israel. And the Lord said unto Joshua, Stretch out the spear that is in thy hand toward Ai; for I will give it into thine hand. And Joshua stretched out the spear that he had in his hand toward the city.

Joshua 8:14-18

When Joshua retreated, many did not understand what he was doing! Beginning from now, people are going to be amazed at your moves, your strategies, your steps and your enlargement. They will say, “We never saw it on this fashion.”

And immediately he arose, took up the bed, and went forth before them all; insomuch that they were all amazed, and glorified God, saying, WE NEVER SAW IT ON THIS FASHION.

Mark 2:12

Do not be worried if the steps you take look strange to people. God did not call you to conform to this world. God did not call you to be like everyone else. God called you to follow the Holy Spirit and to do what He tells you. Remember what they said about Jesus. They said, “We have seen strange things today.” All through the season of enlargement of your life, people will discuss you and use these very words that were spoken about Jesus.

And they were all amazed, and they glorified God, and were filled with fear, saying, WE HAVE SEEN STRANGE THINGS TO DAY.

Luke 5:26

There is a time to retreat so that you do not become offensive to the Lord. Too much human effort is offensive to the Lord. The priests were not allowed to wear things that made them sweat. Man was cursed to eat through the sweat of his face. Sweat, therefore, represents the punishment of God. Sweat represents things done by human effort and human strength. Human strength and human effort always speak of this original curse. Notice how the priests were not allowed to gird themselves with anything that caused sweat. The sweat would remind God of the curse. This is where the concept of retreating comes in.

Retreating takes away the human effort! Retreating allows the power of God to work without you.

But the priests the Levites, the sons of Zadok, that kept the charge of my sanctuary when the children of Israel went astray from me, they shall come near to me to minister unto me, and they shall stand before me to offer unto me the fat and the blood, saith the Lord God: They shall enter into my sanctuary, and they shall come near to my table, to minister unto me, and they shall keep my charge.

And it shall come to pass, that when they enter in at the gates of the inner court, they shall be clothed with linen garments; and no wool shall come upon them, whiles they minister in the gates of the inner court, and within. They shall have linen bonnets upon their heads, and shall have linen breeches upon their loins; THEY SHALL NOT GIRD THEMSELVES WITH ANY THING THAT CAUSETH SWEAT.

Ezekiel 44:15-18

There comes a time when retreating is what you need to do. Retreating is necessary because there is a need to rest from attacking, from fighting and from toiling. Retreating gives you an opportunity to think and clear your mind so you can fight better. People who are always on the move miss God. Busy people who are fighting and toiling often fail to retreat when they should.

Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land which I give you, then shall the land keep a sabbath unto the Lord. Six years thou shalt sow thy field, and six years thou shalt prune thy vineyard, and gather in the fruit thereof; But IN THE SEVENTH YEAR SHALL BE A SABBATH OF REST UNTO THE LAND, a sabbath for the LORD: thou shalt neither sow thy field, nor prune thy vineyard.

Leviticus 25:2-4

Retreating and resting are necessary for fruitfulness. Joshua and his team retreated from the city of Ai and ran away before the armies of the enemy. When they turned around to face the enemy who was pursuing them, they had a greater punch. They crushed the enemy with greater strength.

It has been shown scientifically that when land is allowed to rest for one year, the yield for the next six years is more than if the land had been used for seven years continuously.

Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuary: I am the LORD. If ye walk in my statutes, and keep my commandments, and do them; Then I will give you rain in due season, and the land shall yield her increase, and the trees of the field shall yield their fruit.

Leviticus 26:2-4

It is necessary for you to retreat and pray. It is important to retreat and wait on God. You must retreat and learn to be on your own. You must pray in the gardens. You must pray in your home. You must pray away from home. You must go to the mountains to wait on God. Retreat now and release the power of fruitfulness!

Beginning from this year, everyone is going to notice your new level of fruitfulness!
