### CHAPTER 20\

Enlarge by Divine Turnarounds\
===================================================

AND JOSHUA BURNT AI, AND MADE IT AN HEAP FOR EVER, even a desolation unto this day. And the king of Ai he hanged on a tree until eventide: and as soon as the sun was down, Joshua commanded that they should take his carcase down from the tree, and cast it at the entering of the gate of the city, and raise thereon a great heap of stones, that remaineth unto this day. Then Joshua built an altar unto the Lord God of Israel in mount Ebal,

Joshua 8:28-30

Joshua was once defeated by Ai but God turned things around in his favour. Joshua’s defeat of the king of Ai was the beginning of divine turnarounds for Joshua. I predict that the beginning of divine turnarounds for your ministry has begun.

Every resistance to your ministry is crushed by the power of God! Beginning from now and all through the coming years, you will experience divine turnarounds that will catapult you into an enlarged ministry. Your church is turning around and is being listed as one of the mega churches in the nation. Instead of being unknown, inconspicuous and irrelevant, you will be listed among the men that command attention in your country.

Today, I declare that every resistance to your rising comes under a curse! Every agent of the devil and every personality who conspires against your calling comes under the judgment of heaven this year. Spiritual carpenters are released in this moment against the opposing forces in your life.

And the LORD shewed me FOUR CARPENTERS. Then said I, What come these to do? And he spake, saying, These are the horns which have scattered Judah, so that no man did lift up his head: BUT THESE ARE COME TO FRAY THEM, to cast out the horns of the Gentiles, which lifted up their horn over the land of Judah to scatter it.

Zechariah 1:20-21

Joshua wiped out the enemy and hanged the king of Ai on a tree until evening. After that, he cast the body of the king onto the gate of the city and threw a heap of stones on it. It is important to wipe out your enemy in such a way that he is forgotten.

You must defeat your enemy in such a way that he believes from the bottom of his heart that he has been defeated. You are surrounded by enemies, whether you believe it or not! But you are not alone! The greater One is in you! The power of God is all around you! You will experience a divine turnaround!

It is important that you do not deal with your enemy in an ambiguous or indecisive manner. The devil and his agents are bent on closing every door against you. The king of Ai was bent on closing the gates of the city of Ai to Joshua and his armies. Every closed door in your life opens today in the name of Jesus!

You must be aware that if the king of Ai had won the war, he would have tortured Joshua and thrown his dead body to the wild animals. The king of Ai represents the demonic crises in your life. The king of Ai represents the demonic challenges to your authority. When you deal with the enemy, do not make the mistake of leaving him half alive. Wipe him out in such a way that he will know that he is well and truly eliminated, separated and silenced. Notice how God dealt with the devil. The angel of the Lord bound the devil! The angel of the Lord cast the devil into a pit! The angel of the Lord removed the floor of the pit, giving the devil a bottomless foundation with nothing to stand on. The angel of the Lord shut up the devil. You must learn to silence and to shut up your enemies! The angel of the Lord set a seal and marked the devil. The devil is forever marked as the evil one. You must mark your enemies and fight them till they are defeated. Many enemies are indirect and ambiguous. Enemies do not like to be known as enemies. That is why it is important to mark the enemy in the way that the devil was marked.

And he laid hold on the dragon, that old serpent, which is the Devil, and Satan, and BOUND him a thousand years, And cast him into the BOTTOMLESS PIT, and SHUT HIM UP, and SET A SEAL upon him, that he should deceive the nations no more, till the thousand years should be fulfilled: and after that he must be loosed a little season.

Revelation 20:2-3

God is knocking out every crisis in your life, beginning from now! God is lowering every mountain of resistance that stands in your way. God is silencing every threatening speech that is lifted up against your life!

The wicked kings and princes of the dark world are destroyed by the anointing of the Holy Spirit. The wickedness of the king of Ai has laid an ambush for your life. Something wants to destroy you and cut short your destiny and ministry. But today, the Lord is giving you enlargement through a supernatural turnaround in your ministry.

When the LORD TURNED AGAIN THE CAPTIVITY of Zion, we were like them that dream. Then was our mouth filled with laughter, and our tongue with singing: then said they among the heathen, The LORD hath done great things for them. The LORD hath done great things for us; whereof we are glad. TURN AGAIN OUR CAPTIVITY, O LORD, as the streams in the south.

Psalm 126:1-4

A supernatural turnaround has taken place in your life. This supernatural turning is the doing of the Lord.

Every attempt by the king of Ai to cut short your life and ministry is crushed today in the name of Jesus!

Whatever the enemy has planted in front of you will not succeed in stopping you. I prophesy that God will make your mouth to be filled with laughter!

The happenings in your life all through the coming year will be a testimony of the power of God on your life.

Just as God enlarged Joshua by turning around the defeat of Ai, God is turning your life around. The initial defeat by Ai was not able to eliminate Joshua and his team. This will be your story too! Every initial defeat you have experienced will turn around to the glory of God! May you continue to experience divine turnarounds all through your ministry!
