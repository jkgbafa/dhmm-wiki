### CHAPTER 32\

Enlarge Without Stealing\
==============================================

And so it was, that all that fell that day, both of men and women, were twelve thousand, even all the men of Ai. For Joshua drew not his hand back, wherewith he stretched out the spear, until he had utterly destroyed all the inhabitants of Ai. Only the cattle and the spoil of that city Israel took for a prey unto themselves, according unto the word of the LORD which he commanded Joshua.

Joshua 8:25-27

Prospering, increasing and acquiring the nice things of this life must be according to the will of the Lord. In Jericho, God commanded that they take no money, cattle or food for themselves at all. However, in the city of Ai, God commanded them to take the cattle, the food, the money and anything they could lay their hands on.

Your ministry will begin to enlarge and you will experience financial prosperity. Beginning this year, receive supernatural supplies in a way that you have never had before! Enlargement is taking you far away from all forms of financial stress. All through this season of enlargement, you will command supernatural financial provision in your life.

God is not against prosperity! God desires for you to prosper! God blesses His servants but there are times He tells them to hold back and not to touch the money. Most ministries have changed because they did the opposite of this. They flew upon the spoil when they had a chance to. King Saul was rejected because he rushed to take the spoil.

But Saul and the people SPARED AGAG, AND THE BEST OF THE SHEEP, AND OF THE OXEN, AND OF THE FATLINGS, AND THE LAMBS, AND ALL THAT WAS GOOD, AND WOULD NOT UTTERLY DESTROY THEM: but every thing that was vile and refuse, that they destroyed utterly. Then came the word of the LORD unto Samuel, saying, It repenteth me that I have set up Saul to be king: for HE IS TURNED BACK FROM FOLLOWING ME, and hath not performed my commandments. And it grieved Samuel; and he cried unto the LORD all night.

1 Samuel 15:9-11

This event drew a sharp rebuke from the Lord. He said, “The people have stopped following me. They have gone after the money!” Indeed, those who are following money have stopped following the Lord. As Saul enlarged, he quickly shifted from following the Lord to following other things. You must never do this. God has a plan to miraculously open the windows of heaven and supply all your needs. The season just ahead will bring new things, including financial blessings from above.

Joshua was careful to be obedient about money and prosperity. You must enlarge but be very careful about money. If you keep this principle in your heart, you will be safe. When God keeps the spoil for Himself, He is not depriving you of anything. He is setting Himself up as your number one Helper. Do not be taken in by what many men of God are doing. Do not fly upon the spoil. Do not rush to get money or to become rich quickly. The desire for money corrupts all true ministry. Joshua wisely set aside the income from Jericho and concentrated on his core ministry of capturing cities and taking territories for the Lord.

And ye, in any wise keep yourselves from the accursed thing, lest ye make yourselves accursed, when ye take of the accursed thing, and make the camp of Israel a curse, and trouble it. BUT ALL THE SILVER, AND GOLD, AND VESSELS OF BRASS AND IRON, ARE CONSECRATED UNTO THE LORD: THEY SHALL COME INTO THE TREASURY OF THE LORD. So the people shouted when the priests blew with the trumpets: and it came to pass, when the people heard the sound of the trumpet, and the people shouted with a great shout, that the wall fell down flat, so that the people went up into the city, every man straight before him, and they took the city. And they utterly destroyed all that was in the city, both man and woman, young and old, and ox, and sheep, and ass, with the edge of the sword.

Joshua 6:18-21

On the other hand, when Joshua took his campaign to the city of Ai, he was not restricted in what he could do. He took the spoil and everything he could lay his hands on. God was making him rich through this campaign.

For Joshua drew not his hand back, wherewith he stretched out the spear, until he had utterly destroyed all the inhabitants of Ai. ONLY THE CATTLE AND THE SPOIL OF THAT CITY ISRAEL TOOK FOR A PREY UNTO THEMSELVES, according unto the word of the Lord which he commanded Joshua. And Joshua burnt Ai, and made it an heap for ever, even a desolation unto this day.

Joshua 8:26-28

God will make you rich as you serve Him. In your lifetime, you will see good things that other members of your family have not seen. From this time onwards, every step of your life is going forward! The favour of God is establishing you! Enlargement is God’s grace for your life! There is no need to steal money. God will provide for you!
