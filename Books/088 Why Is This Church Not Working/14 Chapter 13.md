### Chapter 13\

The Church is Not Working because You Suffer from Dependency Thinking and a Reliance on Assistance from Outsiders\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**AND THEY ALL WITH ONE CONSENT BEGAN TO MAKE EXCUSE. The first said unto him, I have bought a piece of ground, and I must needs go and see it: I pray thee have me excused. **

**Luke 14:18**

Anyone who relies on outsiders doing certain things will always be a failure. A person who does not blame himself and take responsibility, but rather looks outside for help is often set for failure. People who always look outside for help have one unique characteristic - excuses!

Poor leaders are loaded with excuses!

Failing ministers always blame someone else for their failures.

It is time to accept that the church is not working because you are the problem. 

Failures often say, "These people are like this ...and those people are like that. These people are bad and hopeless and good for nothing."

Indeed, it is important to stop talking about the people and start looking at yourself.

Stop relying on someone else to change!

Stop relying on others to send money!

Stop relying on people to help you!

Rise and depend on yourself for the increase and the breakthrough you require! As soon as people stop relying on outside help, they begin to rise and do well.
