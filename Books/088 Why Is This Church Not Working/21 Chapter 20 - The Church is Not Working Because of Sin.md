### Chapter 20\

## The Church is Not Working Because of Sin 

**Follow peace with all men, and holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord:**

**Hebrews 12:14** 

You will not see the Lord's hand in your ministry as you continue to live in sin. You cannot even have the anointing in your life because of your sin. The more you love and live in righteousness, the more you qualify for the anointing. Loving righteousness and hating sin will cause God to anoint you even more. 

**Thou hast loved righteousness, and hated iniquity; therefore God, even thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.**

**Hebrews 1:9**

If you remove the foundation of holiness, you cannot do much! Your church is not growing because, behind the scenes, you are living in sin and unholiness. You must understand that fornication corrupts and destroys ministry. 

**Fornication Corrupts Your Ministry**

**For true and righteous are his judgments: for he hath judged the great whore, WHICH DID CORRUPT THE EARTH WITH HER FORNICATION***,* **and hath avenged the blood of his servants at her hand.**

**Revelation 19:2**

Fornication corrupts the earth! 

Fornication corrupts the ministry!

Fornication corrupts and spoils things.

Fornication spoils and destroys your ministry and calling.

Fornication corrupts and spoils your ministry and church.

Fornication corrupts your ministry; this means that fornication mars, debases, depraves, perverts and lowers your ministry. 

Fornication introduces wickedness and evil into your life and ministry.

Fornication infects and taints the ministry.

Fornication corrupts and spoils your ministry; fornication makes you a dishonest and crooked person. Totally corrupted!

You may not accept this reality but fornication will corrupt you and spoil your ministry and also prevent the church from working as it should.

**For true and righteous are his judgments: for he hath judged the great whore, which did corrupt the earth with her fornication, and hath avenged THE BLOOD OF HIS SERVANTS AT HER HAND*.***

**Revelation 19:2** 

What is the blood of His servants? It is the life of His servants. Remember that the life of the person is in his blood. If the woman took the blood of God's servants, it means she took away the life of God's servants. Fornication will end up taking away the life of God's servants. This is why fornication is so devastating for ministry. Your very life is targeted by the enemy. This is why marriages of pastors are the focus of the first attack. When the marriage of a pastor is destroyed, it is easier to lead him into fornication. 

**Behold, the Lord's hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear: But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he will not hear.**

**Isaiah 59:1-2**

No matter the reasons given, God does not accept unholiness. He expects you to be holy!  Holiness speaks of being separate, consecrated and different.

Most of the world lives naturally and easily in licentiousness, lasciviousness, immorality, debauchery, fornication, adultery and perversions of all sorts.

To be different and special to God, you will have to separate yourself from these things which everyone walks in. Your church is not working because maybe you are living in sin, wickedness and deception.

You may be deceiving the entire congregation, and God is just giving you space to repent. So, repent and turn around whilst you have the chance. A time will come when it will be more difficult to repent. It is better that you fall on the stone, than that the stone falls on you. If we judge ourselves, we will not be judged. 

**Whosoever shall fall upon that stone shall be broken; but on whomsoever it shall fall, it will grind him to powder.**

**Luke 20:18 **
