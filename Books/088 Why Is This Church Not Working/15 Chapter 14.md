### Chapter 14 \

The Church is Not Working because You Lack Training in Ministry\
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**A pupil is not above his teacher; but everyone, after he has been fully trained, will be like his teacher.**

**Luke 6:40 (NASB95)** 

Your church is not working because you lack training in ministry! You need the experience of being trained specifically for ministry work. You probably need practical training to do hands-on ministry. You may have passed exams in theology and other academic ministry subjects, but you still need training in the practical art of doing ministry. 

You need training as a shepherd! 

You need training as a leader! 

You need training as a fighter! 

You need training as a minister of the gospel!

You lack the development and improvement in church work that comes through practice and instruction over a period of time. To be trained is to have been taught a particular skill or type of behaviour, through practice and instruction over a period of time.  To be trained is to have been developed and improved through instruction.

Perhaps you have a desire to serve God but you have not been trained. This lack of training (or bad training) in ministry is the reason why you are not successful in ministry.

Can you imagine a surgeon, who has not been trained well in heart surgery, who keeps on operating on people who die shortly after the surgery?  Would you like a surgeon who has never operated before to try his luck on you? Would you not want a surgeon who has been taught and trained in what he is doing?

Good training is necessary if you are to become an effective minister. Humble yourself so you can learn the skills of a good shepherd. Give yourself to be trained and taught. 

Who can train a proud person like yourself?

Humble yourself and accept to go through whatever you must go through so you can be trained to acquire some shepherding and leadership skills!
