### Chapter 2\

The Church is Not Working because Your Income is low\
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

**And in those days, when the number of the disciples was multiplied, there arose a murmuring of the Grecians against the Hebrews, because their widows were neglected in the daily ministration. Then the twelve called the multitude of the disciples unto them, and said, It is not reason that we should leave the word of God, and serve tables. Wherefore, brethren, look ye out among you seven men of honest report, full of the Holy Ghost and wisdom, whom we may appoint over this business.**

**Acts 6:1-3**

The scripture above shows how the early church was quickly engulfed by many overwhelming financial needs. A church that is working will always have enough finances to meet the needs!

Every church has needs which are created by its very existence. This is why a church needs to have a good enough income. When God sends you, you will not lack anything. When Jesus puts you on a mission, you will never be in need. The ministry will have more than enough income. The power of God will cause the income of the church to rise according to the needs of the ministry. Remember the words of Jesus: 

**And he said unto them, WHEN I SENT YOU WITHOUT PURSE AND SCRIP, AND SHOES, LACKED YE ANY THING? And they said, Nothing.**

**Luke 22:35**

The income of a church reveals how many real and committed members you have in the church.

There may be a large crowd in the church but few real committed members. This is revealed in the low income of the church.

If someone takes away all the real committed members of a church, he has taken away the heart of the church and the income of the church.  It is like taking out all the meat from the soup and leaving only bones. 

Having a large crowd of uncommitted people left in the church will not be able to make up a good income for the church.  A working church needs to have a good enough income to meet the needs that will arise because the church exists.

Every church has needs. Every church will need a lot of money to function properly.

Every church needs a place to meet.

Every church needs a lot of money to secure a meeting place or to build one.

Every church needs a meeting place for children.

Every church needs eating places on site.

Every church needs toilets and other such facilities.

Every church needs chairs for the people to sit on.

Every church needs musical equipment.

Every church needs other kinds of equipment like projectors and screens.

Every church needs to pay the salaries of those who work there.

Every church needs money to conduct programs, camps, conventions, retreats and so on.

Every church needs money to help the needs of the poor and needy.

This is why every church needs a good income. Therefore, if your church has a low income, it is not functioning properly. It is therefore right and proper that you assess a church by analysing its attendance and income.

There are two main parameters by which we monitor a church's well-being. The number one parameter is church attendance and the number two parameter is church income.

Sometimes the attendance is good but the income is very little. This is also not good because the income of the church reflects the commitment and training of the church in the word of God.
