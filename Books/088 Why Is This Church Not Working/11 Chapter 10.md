### Chapter 10\

The Church is Not Working because You Have a Low Interest in Having a Great Ministry \
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he. **

**Proverbs 29:18**

 

A person with a low interest in a good life will always remain poor. Intractable poverty happens when people have a low desire for a good life. 

Your vision will make you turn out to become a great person. Your vision is the most important invisible thing you need. Perhaps, your church is not working because you have a low interest in having a great ministry.

I always thought that all pastors had a desire for a greater ministry. My greatest shock has been that many pastors do not have a desire for a better life and a better ministry. When you have a low desire or vision for church growth, it is nearly impossible to drive you to do all the things you have to do for church growth to happen.
