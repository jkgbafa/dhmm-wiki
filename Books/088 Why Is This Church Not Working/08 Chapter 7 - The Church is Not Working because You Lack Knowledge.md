### Chapter 7\

 The Church is Not Working because You Lack Knowledge \
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.     **Knowledge is a key to church growth. **

**Woe unto you, lawyers! for ye have taken away the key of knowledge: ye entered not in yourselves, and them that were entering in ye hindered. **

**Luke 11:52**

Indeed, knowledge is a key!  It is a key to moving forward, out of failure, poverty, difficulty and worthlessness. Knowledge that is acquired *and* used is the master key to making things work! Indeed, knowledge is a key to almost every breakthrough in this world. All medical and scientific advances have happened because of an increase in knowledge.  Knowledge is also a key to church growth. Knowledge is the key to any organization working well.

2.     **Knowledge is a kind of light. Knowledge is a light to illuminate your way.**

**The entrance of thy words giveth light; it giveth understanding unto the simple. I opened my mouth, and panted: for I longed for thy commandments.**

**Psalm 119:130-131**

**For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light; and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:**

**Proverbs 6:23**

 

**Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.**

**Psalm 119:105**

Light tells you what to do. Light helps you to do what you have to do. You can see your way better. When there is a lack of knowledge about church building, church growth and church development, it means there is a lack of light about everything. A lack of light means there is a poverty of souls! A lack of light means there is a poverty of members. A lack of light means there is poverty of money.

**3.**     **Knowledge is a mystery solution. **

According to research on world poverty, it has been found that intractable poverty (unsolvable poverty, incurable poverty, failure and backwardness) among human populations is explained by a lack of knowledge.

THE **SOLUTION** 

1.     Go to school! Get a degree! There is a difference between those who have a university education and those who do not have a university degree.

2.     Read books about church growth.

3.     Watch videos on church growth.

4.     Listen to messages on how to go higher and deeper in ministry.
