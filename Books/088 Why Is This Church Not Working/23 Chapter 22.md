### Chapter 22\

The Church is Not Working because You Do Not Follow Instructions\
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Now when he had left speaking, he said unto Simon, Launch out into the deep, and let down your nets for a draught. And Simon answering said unto him, Master, WE HAVE TOILED ALL THE NIGHT, AND HAVE TAKEN NOTHING: NEVERTHELESS AT THY WORD I WILL LET DOWN THE NET. AND WHEN THEY HAD THIS DONE, THEY INCLOSED A GREAT MULTITUDE OF FISHES: AND THEIR NET BRAKE. And they beckoned unto their partners, which were in the other ship, that they should come and help them. And they came, and filled both the ships, so that they began to sink. When Simon Peter saw it, he fell down at Jesus' knees, saying, Depart from me; for I am a sinful man, O Lord. **

**Luke 5:4-8**

What did Peter get from his toil? *Nothing*!!!

What did Peter get from following a simple instruction from Jesus? *Everything he needed*!!! A net-breaking ship-sinking miracle of abundance!

Your struggle is in you not obeying instructions! Perhaps the instructions came to you in a book or a conversation. Whichever way, make sure you obey the instructions.

You will be lucky if you ever get a personal and direct instruction on what to do. If you do have the privilege of getting a direct and personal instruction, make sure you obey it immediately and completely.

**My son, keep my words, and lay up my commandments with thee. Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye. Bind them upon thy fingers, write them upon the table of thine heart.**

**Proverbs 7:1-3**

 

Many people simply cannot follow instructions. They simply cannot obey a clear instruction when it is given. 

I have two groups of sons in the ministry. Those who obey instructions and those who do not obey instructions!  I once met with a group of pastors and gave them some simple instructions that would help their churches grow.  I told them that they would not obey what I had told them, even though they all pledged to obey. Magically, I went back three months later and they had not obeyed what I had told them.

If there is one single thing I could point to as a source of failure in people, it is this inability to immediately and completely follow instructions.
