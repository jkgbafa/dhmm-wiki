### Chapter 18\

The Church is Not Working because of The Location of Your Church \
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Take heed to thyself that thou offer not thy burnt offerings in every place that thou seest: But IN THE PLACE WHICH THE LORD SHALL CHOOSE in one of thy tribes, there thou shalt offer thy burnt offerings, and there thou shalt do all that I command thee.**

**Deuteronomy 12:13-14**

Many churches are not growing because they are located in the wrong place. In the scripture above, you see that you are not supposed to worship just anywhere. This means you are not supposed to set up your tent to worship the Lord in just any location. It must be the right place which God has chosen. When God chooses the place, there will be success in the ministry. 

Could it be that your church is in the wrong place? Seek the Lord about it! Maybe your church is in the north of the city but it should be in the south. We once planted a church in the north of a city and it did not work. When we moved the church to the south, it began to work and it grew into a mega church. 

Perhaps your church is located in a dangerous location and people are not prepared to come there.

Perhaps your church is too far for people to come to.

Perhaps your church is in a neighbourhood that is racially hostile to people you invite to church.

Perhaps it is time you relocated your church so that many more people can come. It is time for your church to work!
