### Chapter 17\

The Church is Not Working Because You Are Using the Wrong Church Growth Strategy\
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**By wisdom a house is built, And by understanding it is established; And by knowledge the rooms are filled With all precious and pleasant riches.**

**Proverbs 24:3-4**

A house is built through wisdom! A house is built by a series of steps!

The wisdom that achieves great things with a series of steps is called strategy.

A pastor must accept the reality that no great achievement involves just one step. Church growth will, by all means, involve a series of steps, a series of manoeuvres or a series of stratagems. The series of steps, manoeuvres or stratagems is what is called strategy. 

Strategy is defined as a series of manoeuvres that are undertaken to achieve a specific goal. Many pastors are not aware that there are strategies to achieve church growth. Many pastors are not aware that there are church growth strategies that involve more than one step.

There are three main strategies that I suggest that you decide upon for church growth. Use one of these strategies and apply it to the uttermost:

1.     The Strategy of PVCI

2.     The strategy of the OCTOPUS

3.     The strategy of SHABBIE SHEPHERDING MUST GO

You can learn about these strategies for church growth in the books and messages that are available for those who want to go deeper!
