### Chapter 4\

The Church is Not Working because You Are isolated and Non-Communicative\
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**And when he was come out of the ship, immediately there met him out of the tombs a man with an unclean spirit, Who had his dwelling among the tombs; and no man could bind him, no, not with chains:**

**Mark 5:2-3**

People with serious mental illness withdraw into isolation in a similar way with which some pastors withdraw from the rest of us.

In the serious illness of schizophrenia, the patient has what we call negative "withdrawal" symptoms. These symptoms involve the person withdrawing from many things. These are the five negative symptoms of schizophrenia.  You will notice these negative things in anyone you know who develops this mental illness.

ALOGIA: the patient becomes quiet and does not speak much. He becomes completely non-communicative. He withdraws from speaking and communicating and becomes a very quiet person.

ANHEDONIA: the patient loses interest in things that were nice and pleasurable to him. He has no interest in the church and ministry. 

AVOLITION: the patient loses motivation and a drive to do anything. He loses interest in the ministry and loses the passion to do the work of ministry. 

ASOCIALITY: the person withdraws from society. He is no more sociable and friendly. He is no longer a flowing happy person who mixes with the rest of the group.

AFFECTIVE FLATTENING: the person withdraws from showing any emotion at all. There is no laughter, no smiles and no joy from this person suffering from affective flattening. He has become flat and inexpressive.

Remember these scriptures and fight isolation and non-communicative behaviour:

1.     **You will never be made perfect without the input of others.** 

**God having provided some better thing for us, that they without us should not be made perfect.**

**Hebrews 11:40**

To be non-communicative is a serious flaw in your ministry! 

To be isolated is a serious mistake in ministry! 

Your church will not work without the input of some other people, other ministers, and other churches. Your isolation and non-communicative posture will kill your ministry. If you do not communicate properly you will never amount to anything. I have two types of pastors in my ministry; *those* *who communicate properly* and *those who are isolated and non-communicative.* Those who are non-communicative rarely do well in ministry. They may say they are naturally quiet, etc., but that is just a smoke screen for a serious flaw in their lives and ministries.

2.     **You can never say that you do not need the input of other people.**

\

**But now are they many members, yet but one body. And the eye cannot say unto the hand, I have no need of thee: nor again the head to the feet, I have no need of you.**

**1 Corinthians 12:20-21**

You do need to connect with others just like you. You desperately need to communicate! Your isolation in this world is killing your potential! Many who are isolated and non-communicative are filled with delusions and unfounded hurts. We need each other! We need to speak to other people and know the will of God through them. Speaking and communicating will drive away demons from your life and ministry.

3.     **The body increases through what every joint supplies.**

**From whom the whole body fitly joined together and compacted by THAT WHICH EVERY JOINT SUPPLIETH, according to the effectual working in the measure of every part, MAKETH INCREASE OF THE BODY unto the edifying of itself in love.**

**Ephesians 4:16**

Some supplies will come to you in the realm of the Spirit when you are connected to the body. If you cut off the joints and inputs, how will you increase? I began to increase when I connected to joints like Yonggi Cho, Fred Price and Kenneth Hagin. 

God has joints for you to be connected to and He intends to supply you with what you need through these joints. Watch out for isolated people who are cut off from essential fellowship and friendships. They are doomed to not increase!
