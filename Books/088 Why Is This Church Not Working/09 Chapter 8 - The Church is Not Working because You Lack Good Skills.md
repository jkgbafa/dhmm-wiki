### Chapter 8\

The Church is Not Working because You Lack Good Skills\
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

**As for these four children, God gave them KNOWLEDGE AND SKILL in all learning and wisdom: and Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams.**

**Daniel 1:17**

What are skills? A skill is a special ability or training that a person has. A skill is the ability that enables you to do something well.

When it comes to church growth, you do need special abilities and training to be able to shepherd the people and make the church grow. People who lack the special training or the special abilities of a shepherd, a leader and a pastor cannot make a church grow or work well.

***According to various research on*** ***poverty, it has been found that intractable poverty (unsolvable poverty, incurable poverty, failure and disastrous backwardness) among human populations is caused by*** ***a lack of good skills******.*** 

*It is also proven that your church is not working because you lack the good skills of ministry.* You lack the skills to engineer church growth. 

You lack shepherding skills! 

You lack leadership skills! 

You lack the skill of waiting on God!  

All these skills are needed to make a church work.  The lack of good skills of the leader is the reason why things do not work. You need the skills of a good shepherd to make a church work. You need the skills of "following" someone in order to catch up. You need the skills of humility to do ministry. It takes a skill to walk humbly before the Lord. As you can see, Daniel and his friends needed more than knowledge to survive and do well. They needed skills! You need good skills!

Intractable poverty in ministry is always caused by a lack of knowledge and a lack of good skills. Research all over the world has shown that wherever poverty is established, people lack good skills. A lack of skills to plan and organize your life and ministry can also cause deep intractable poverty.

**THE SOLUTION** 

1.     Identify and name the specific skills you lack. For instance, the skills of shepherding, the skills of bussing people to church, the skills of leadership, the skills of preaching and the skills of teaching.

2.     Identify people who have the skills you desire.

3.     Get close to the people who have the skills you need.

4.     Humbly learn new skills by listening, observing, assisting and implementing the new skills that you need.
