### Chapter 5 \

The Church is Not Working because You Are Not Anointed Yet\
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised.**

**Luke 4:18** 

Without the anointing, you cannot do very much. Jesus had to be anointed before He could do the work of ministry. If Jesus needed to be anointed, you will need to as well! I meet many pastors who are simply not anointed.

**THE SOLUTION** 

1.     Have faith in the reality of the anointing. Believe that there is something like “the anointing" and that belief will propel you to seek the anointing and eventually receive the anointing.

**But the anointing which ye have received of him abideth in you, and ye need not that any man teach you: but as the same anointing teacheth you of all things, and is truth, and is no lie, and even as it hath taught you, ye shall abide in him.**

**1 John 2:27 **

2.     Jesus told His disciples to wait until they were anointed before going into the ministry. “Wait for the promise of the Father which is the Holy Spirit,” is what Jesus said.

**And, being assembled together with them, commanded them that they should not depart from Jerusalem, but WAIT FOR THE PROMISE OF THE FATHER, which, saith he, ye have heard of me.**

**Acts1:4**

3.     Seek to be anointed by catching an anointing through listening to messages and watching preaching messages.

**And the spirit entered into me when he spake unto me, and set me upon my feet, that I heard him that spake unto me.**

**Ezekiel 2:2 **

 

4.     Seek to be anointed by copying an anointed person. Copy his preaching! Copy his teaching! Copy his way of ministering to people!

**That ye be not slothful, but followers of them who through faith and patience inherit the promises.**

**Hebrews 6:12**
