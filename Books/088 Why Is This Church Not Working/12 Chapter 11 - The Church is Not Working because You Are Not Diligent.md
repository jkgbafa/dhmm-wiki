### Chapter 11\

 The Church is Not Working because You Are Not Diligent\
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Seest thou a man DILIGENT IN HIS BUSINESS*?* he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.**

**Proverbs 22:29**

 

Diligence speaks of the constant effort to accomplish. The reason why you have not risen in ministry, nor have you stood in high places in ministry is because you are passive, not diligent and not applying constant effort to ministry.

 

You suffer from a terrible and debilitating passivity. You are a passive, laid-back person!

Passivity means that you do not participate readily, actively, or emotionally in something when expected to! Because you do not participate readily and actively in your ministry work, you constantly need supervision.

 

Once you need constant supervision, you will not excel!  That is why your church is not growing.

Pastors who are diligent and make a constant effort to accomplish something are the only ones who have working churches.
