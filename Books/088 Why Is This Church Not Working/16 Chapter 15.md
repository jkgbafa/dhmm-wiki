### Chapter 15\

The Church is Not Working because You are Slow and Sluggish \
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**That ye be not slothful, but followers of them who through faith and patience inherit the promises.**

**Hebrews 6:12**

 Slothful people do not inherit the promises!  They do not amount to anything in ministry!  People who are slothful do not have working churches. They do not inherit the promise of church growth.

The meaning of the word "slothful" is full of revelation. I always thought that slothful simply meant "laziness". However, in the Greek, this word means something quite different. Slothful means slow, sluggish, indolent, dull and languid. Wow! These are interesting words. Slow! Sluggish! Dull! Amazing meanings! 

When you call a person slothful it means he is slow and sluggish. When you say a person is slothful, it means he is lacking in spirit or interest; He is listless and indifferent to the instruction and vision.

Perhaps slothfulness is the main problem with older pastors who are tired of new ideas and visions. I often hesitate to share my exciting instructions and revelations with the slow and the sluggish. They are simply uninterested in the new ideas and new moves that God is bringing.

*Obeying instructions is often a problem* for *the slow and sluggish*. A committee made up of older men who are slow and sluggish in implementing the vision will only lead to failure. You may not like this definition but it is a reality that the slow and sluggish are a cause of failure in every church, every committee, every council and every group!

Your church is not working because you are slow and sluggish to obey instructions and flow with the vision. The ministry will never respond and yield to slowness and sluggishness. Everyone must press zealously into the kingdom of God. 

**The law and the prophets were until John: since that time the kingdom of God is preached, and EVERY MAN PRESSETH INTO IT.**

**Luke 16:16**
