### Chapter 9\

The Church Is Not Working becauseYou Are Lazy\
-------------------------------------------------------------------

**I WALKED BY THE FIELD OF A LAZY PERSON, the vineyard of one with no common sense. I saw that it was overgrown with nettles. It was covered with weeds, and its walls were broken down. Then, as I looked and thought about it, I learned this lesson: A LITTLE EXTRA SLEEP, A LITTLE MORE SLUMBER, A LITTLE FOLDING OF THE HANDS TO REST- then poverty will pounce on you like a bandit; SCARCITY WILL ATTACK YOU LIKE AN ARMED ROBBER.**

**Proverbs 24:30-34 NLT**

 

In the scripture above, we see a field. The field is your church. This field (church) was attacked by poverty and scarcity. The poverty of finances attacks churches all the time! A scarcity of members attacks churches all the time!

The cause of this scarcity and poverty is simple; laziness! Many churches are not growing because the pastors are lazy.

The pastors simply love too much sleep, too much rest, too much vacation and too much holiday.

There is no place for lazy people in the ministry! Diligence is the opposite of laziness. Jesus rebuked the servant for not using the gift and talent that he was given. Jesus said the servant was WICKED and LAZY! 

Indeed, laziness is the reason why many do not work hard enough in the ministry to accomplish anything. Indeed, many lazy men have infiltrated the ranks of the ministry. This laziness has many manifestations. 

**But his master answered and said to him, 'You wicked, lazy slave, you knew that I reap where I did not sow and gather where I scattered no seed.**

**Matthew 25:26 (NASB95)**

**Eight Manifestations of Laziness**

According to various research on poverty, it has been found that intractable poverty (unsolvable poverty, incurable poverty, failure and disastrous backwardness) among human populations, is caused by the *EIGHT MANIFESTATIONS OF LAZINESS**.*

 

1.     A low interest in a good life.

2.     Deep passivity.

3.     A lack of motivation and initiative.

4.     A low intellect.

5.     Dependency thinking.

6.     Reliance on assistance from others.

7.     A lack of life skills to plan and organize their life.

8.     Bad training and care of children by parents.

 

Men who lack motivation have often become ministers of the gospel. Such lazy people are found in all works of life. In the ministry, they have been known to be the reason for failure in ministry.

Why is this church not working? Because the leaders are lazy, passive and lack motivation! They are dumb dogs who love to sleep all day.

**His watchmen are blind: they are all ignorant, they are all dumb dogs, they cannot bark; SLEEPING, LYING DOWN, LOVING TO SLUMBER.**

**Isaiah 56:10**

 

In this wonderful scripture, Isaiah speaks of God's servants and describes them as dumb and sleepy, who love to slumber! Dogs are supposed to bark spontaneously, but passive sleepy dogs will not bark. These particular dogs are always sleeping and love to slumber. This is a perfect description of pastors who do not do well.

Passivity! Passivity! Passivity! A Lack of motivation, and a lack of drive. Dumb dogs who love slumber and love to sleep!

The church does not work when it is in the hands of lazy men!
