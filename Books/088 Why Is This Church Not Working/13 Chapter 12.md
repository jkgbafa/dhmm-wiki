### Chapter 12\

The Church is Not Working because You Have a Low Capacity for Studying\
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.**

**2 Timothy 2:15** 

You need to study to show yourself a pastor who is not ashamed of the size of his congregation. It is shameful to have an undeveloped midget church. A midget is something small for its kind! There are midget churches and you must not have a midget church. A midget church has a low attendance for a church of its kind where located.

Indeed, it is more difficult to be a successful pastor than to be a medical doctor! If doctors have to read large complicated books to become doctors, then a pastor will also need to study hard to become a good pastor. People who do not study books on ministry and church growth will not have growth.

It is time to start thinking and understanding complex things. Church growth is a complex thing. It takes a great understanding to achieve church growth.

When you have a low ability to understand things, especially of a complex order, it reveals that you have a low intellect. A low intellect has been known to be a cause of intractable poverty and unsolvable backwardness in the world. People with a low intellect do not read much. 

**And when the sabbath day was come, he began to teach in the synagogue: and many hearing him were astonished, saying, From whence hath this man these things? and WHAT WISDOM IS THIS WHICH IS GIVEN UNTO HIM, THAT EVEN SUCH MIGHTY WORKS ARE WROUGHT BY HIS HANDS*?***

**Mark 6:2**

Wisdom is always revealed in the great works you can achieve. Your failing church which is not growing is a revelation of your lack of knowledge, wisdom and understanding!

Remember that mighty works are always a result of wisdom. Get the understanding you need to solve the complex problem of achieving church growth.

If you have a high intellect, show it by the great works you have achieved for God. Good works are always the proof of your ability to think and understand things.

**IF YOU ARE WISE AND UNDERSTAND GOD'S WAYS, PROVE IT BY LIVING AN HONORABLE LIFE, DOING GOOD WORKS with the humility that comes from wisdom.**

**James 3:13 (NLT)**
