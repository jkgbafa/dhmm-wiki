### Chapter 16\

The Church is Not Working because You Do Not Have Helpers\
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**HAVING THEREFORE OBTAINED HELP OF GOD, I CONTINUE unto this day, witnessing both to small and great, saying none other things than those which the prophets and Moses did say should come:**

**Acts 26:22**

Your ministry will continue into greatness through divine help. Without help, you cannot go on higher into real success in ministry. 

Many people do not fulfil their ministry because they do not have good helpers. Missionaries who go out into the field alone are often doomed to fail. Missionaries without helpers are like trees without leaves.

Every Apostle needs the help of God.  Pastors who do not have helpers are unable to grow their churches.  Your church is not working because you do not have helpers like good assistant pastors, good music directors, good choir directors, anointed builders, anointed singers, anointed administrative workers and honest financial helpers!

This is why satan desperately attacks assistants and helpers. This is why satan works on assistants to turn them into traitors. Traitors are painful experiences that cause the pastor to turn away from all potential helpers.  Instead of welcoming the people who could help you, you become wary of everyone and drive away every new person.

Your church is not working because you do not have helpers working in the different areas of ministry! Open your heart to receive helpers to work with you! Your church will start working as soon as the helpers are in place. Paul obtained the help of God. That is how he was able to achieve the things he achieved in the ministry.

When you obtain help, you go further, you go deeper! Remember this verse: "Having obtained the help of God, I continue unto this day, doing big things and small things and speaking the word of God according to what the prophets and Moses said."

Without the help of God, your ministry will not develop! You will not be able to say and do what you are supposed to.

**I will ask the Father, and He will give you another Helper, that He may be with you forever;**

**John 14:16 (NASB)**

 

When Jesus left the earth, He promised a helper to the disciples. The Holy Spirit is the helper that was promised to the disciples. The one thing that was promised to the followers of Jesus was help. Jesus knew that every minister needed help.

One of the manifestations of the Holy Spirit helping your life is when people are sent to help you. Those human beings give you what the Holy Spirit is sent to do for you. They give you help! They assist you! And they enable you to fulfil your ministry!
