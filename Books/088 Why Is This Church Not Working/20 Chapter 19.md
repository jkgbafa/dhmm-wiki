### Chapter 19\

The Church is Not Working because You Have Targeted the Wrong People \
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**You have made sure that children and infants praise you. You have done it because of your enemies. You have done it to put a stop to their talk.**

**Psalms 8:2 (NIrV)**

I know a country in which only four per cent of the population is over sixty-five years old.

In this same country, seventy per cent of the population is below forty years old.

In this same country, you find pastors targeting the elderly and the rich. 

Obviously, the churches will be very small because they are targeting people that do not exist. Most people in that country are young and poor. Those are the people who are more interested in coming to church.

Most pastors target the older and richer people in society. After all, such people will be able to do more to help the pastor. Affluent people are expected to give more money and solve the financial problems of the ministry. However, targeting the older and richer people does not often yield much church growth. 

You must reach out to the young people in society if you want church growth. Most people get saved as teenagers and grow up in Christ from there on.

Stop wasting your time to get rich and powerful people into your church. God will bring them when He wants to. Few of such people are really called to the ministry. It is time for you to target babes and sucklings! God will provide the money in many different ways and you will be alright!
