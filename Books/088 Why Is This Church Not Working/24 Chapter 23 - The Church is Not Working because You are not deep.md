### Chapter 23\

The Church is Not Working because You are not deep\
------------------------------------------------------------------------

**And the remnant that is escaped of the house of Judah shall again TAKE ROOT DOWNWARD, AND BEAR FRUIT UPWARD:**

**Isaiah 37:31** 

To bear fruit upward, your roots need to go deeply downwards. It is important to go deeply into what you are following.

I do not mind showing people the books and videos I am watching and learning from. Most people will not go deep enough to be able to bear fruit upwards. The deeper you go, the more your fruit will be.

There are many official followers but few really go deeply into what they say they believe.

Your church is simply not working because you are not deep! 

Go deeper and then you will do more! 

Go deeper into the YouTube channels and soak in the Word until you become anointed!

Go deeper into church growth and mega-church teachings. Then you will bear fruit upwards.

It is easy to say someone is your father. But to really benefit from a father you need to go deeper into his ideas, his teachings, his laws, his secrets, his ways, his unspoken ways and methods.  

Going deeper is the key to many fruits. I went deep into every single mentor I have ever had and the fruits are there for all to see. You need to be baptized into your mentors just as the children of Israel were baptized into Moses.

**And were all baptized unto Moses in the cloud and in the sea;)**

**1 Corinthians 10:2**

 

**Thus was he fair in his greatness, in the length of his branches: FOR HIS ROOT WAS BY GREAT WATERS.**

**Ezekiel 31:7**

The greatness of a person depends on what is happening to his roots. How deep is your root? Has your root penetrated to where the water is? Did you know that water is found deep in the earth?

I once met a man who ran a drilling company. I asked him " Do you drill for oil?

"Oh no." he said, "I drill for water."

He explained that water was also found deep in the earth. All he did was drill for water deep in the earth. 

Many roots need to go deep into the soil to find water. That is why the greatest trees have the deepest roots.
