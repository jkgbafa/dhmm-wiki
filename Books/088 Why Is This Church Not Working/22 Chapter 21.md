### Chapter 21\

The Church is Not Working because You Do Not Have Branches\
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**'All the birds of the heavens nested in its boughs, And under its branches all the beasts of the field gave birth, And all great nations lived under its shade. 'SO IT WAS BEAUTIFUL IN ITS GREATNESS, IN THE LENGTH OF ITS BRANCHES; For its roots extended to many waters. 'The cedars in God's garden could not match it; The cypresses could not compare with its boughs, And the plane trees could not match its branches. No tree in God's garden could compare with it in its beauty. 'I MADE IT BEAUTIFUL WITH THE MULTITUDE OF ITS BRANCHES, And all the trees of Eden, which were in the garden of God, were jealous of it.**

**Ezekiel 31:6-9 (NASB95)**

The first revelation God showed me about church growth was the revelation of branches. Indeed, when my single church struggled to grow, I started branches and that became the great breakthrough of the ministry. My church became beautiful through branches. Branching churches are working churches. God will make your church beautiful through the multitude of branches. 

There are times when a single church will not be able to grow much. There are times when the only growth you are going to get will be the growth that comes through branches. Forming cells or *bacentas* is also a form of branching churches.

**Thy mother is like a vine in thy blood, planted by the waters: SHE WAS FRUITFUL AND FULL OF BRANCHES BY REASON OF MANY WATERS*.* And she had strong rods for the sceptres of them that bare rule, and her stature was exalted among the thick branches, and she appeared in her height with the multitude of her branches.**

**Ezekiel 19:10-11** 

Managing branching churches requires great leadership skills because you will have to control the many pastors and leaders who take the reins of these new branches. Loyalty and faithfulness are key to branching churches. You will need to understand the different facets of loyalty and teach and practice them constantly.

People are naturally unfaithful and disloyal. It takes great grace and leadership skill to keep people together for ten years, twenty years or even thirty years.
