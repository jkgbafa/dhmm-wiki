### Chapter 25\

The Church is Not Working because You Have Not Introduced New Ideas and Innovations\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**In that he saith, A new covenant, he hath made the first old. Now that which decayeth and waxeth old is READY TO VANISH AWAY.**

**Hebrews 8:13** 

A covenant that is ready to vanish away! A church that is ready to VANISH AWAY! Can a church vanish away? Yes, indeed I know many churches that have vanished away. They simply faded away into oblivion, closed down and vanished away. The church in which I was ordained has vanished away!

The words "decay" and "old" actually refer to the word OBSOLETE!  Your church is not working because it is obsolete in many of its practices, strategies, ways and ideas.

Indeed, many churches are not working and will soon vanish. Why is that? It is because they are decaying and waxing old. Such churches have obsolete practices and obsolete strategies as their principal methods.

Churches must introduce new ideas and new things! 

Where is the new music? 

Where are the new worship songs? 

Where are the new ways of doing outreach? 

Where are the new books? 

Where are the new ideas for church growth? 

Where are the new improvements to your church building? 

Where is the new interior design of your auditorium? 

Where are the new data monitoring technologies? 

Where are your new accounting methods? 

Where is your new software? 

Where are the new security measures? 

Where are your new ushering techniques? 

Where is the new worship leader? Where are the new, young people?

Just as secular businesses vanish and go out of circulation, many churches have the potential to disappear and vanish because they are outmoded and obsolete in many of their ways. Why are you resistant to the new things and new ways that God is moving in? Why are you resistant to becoming young again?  Why are you resistant to new music?  Why are you resistant to a new name?  Why are you resistant to new books and new revelations on the same basic truths of the gospel?

For instance, the secular world has shifted to drones as their main weapon for waging war. It is difficult to imagine that tanks and manned fighter jets are a thing of the past but that is the reality. Many things become obsolete by the invention of new things. Those who do not rise up and use the new methods are left behind in a fantastic way.

Indeed, it has been shown that prosperous nations that rise into riches ALWAYS INTRODUCE NEW IDEAS into their nations. The country that becomes rich, always has innovations, new technologies and new things that take over the old. A nation which is making televisions, mobile phones, electric cars and other new electronic gadgets is always richer than a nation which has stayed with the old and obsolete attempts, to become rich through agriculture.

Farming vegetables, pepper, cocoa, tomatoes and ginger has not been able to make any nation prosper. Historically, it is the countries that have introduced new ideas, new technologies, and innovations of all sorts that have advanced into prosperity.

The same applies to church work. It is the churches that accept new ideas, new technologies and innovations in ministry that move forward. Just look around you! You will see many churches that are ready to vanish. They are in decline! They are fading! The old and obsolete have dominated the entire ministry!  Some things will never be obsolete. The word of God, the principles, the prayer, the gift of God and the Holy Spirit will never go out of fashion.

But the way we administer the word of God and the gift of God is always open to new ideas, new technologies and innovations.

Our lives, our obedience, our style and methods of following God can be blessed with new ideas and innovations!
