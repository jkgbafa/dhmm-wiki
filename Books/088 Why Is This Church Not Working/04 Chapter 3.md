### Chapter 3\

The Church is Not Working because You Are Disconnected from the Head \
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Do not let anyone who delights in false humility and the worship of angels disqualify you. Such a person also goes into great detail about what they have seen; they are puffed up with idle notions by their unspiritual mind. THEY HAVE LOST CONNECTION WITH THE HEAD, from whom the whole body, supported and held together by its ligaments and sinews, grows as God causes it to grow.**

**Colossians 2:18-19 NIV**

Your connection with the head is of utmost importance. Once you are disconnected from the head, you are doomed!

It is when your body is disconnected from the head that it becomes paralyzed. When an arm or a leg does not move it is because it is disconnected from the brain. The nerves no longer communicate with the brain, so the brain can no longer give instructions to the leg or arm telling it to move.  

All the parts of the body are connected to the brain or head.  

Your arms are connected to your brain! 

Your ability to live and act is connected to your head.

Your legs are connected to your brain! 

Your very movement is controlled by your head.

The heart is connected to the brain and the speed of the heartbeat is controlled by the brain.

Your stomach is connected to your brain and the nerves control how hungry you feel and how much you eat, etc.

The lungs are connected to the brain. Your breathing is controlled by your brain. If you disconnect the lungs from the brain, you will stop breathing.

Make sure you are not disconnected from God by your sin and your lifestyle! 

**Behold, the LORD'S hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear: But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he will not hear.**

**Isaiah 59:1-2**

It is also important that you do not become disconnected from the founders and heads of churches. Disconnection with any kind of head will lead to destruction and desolation.

Disconnection of the Methodist church from its head and founder, John Wesley, will cause the Methodist church to look and function in a manner so different from what it was originally. The church could even adopt practices which were once an abomination to John Wesley and its original leadership. That is what happens when a church is disconnected from its head.

Disconnection of the Salvation Army Church from its head and blessed founder, William Booth, will cause the Salvation Army Church to look and function in a manner so different from what it was originally. The church could turn into an organization which has no similarity to the ministry of salvation that William Booth and its original leadership espoused.

I have noticed that pastors in my ministry who become disconnected from me, as the head of my little church, rarely do well. The church does not work when you are disconnected from your head!
