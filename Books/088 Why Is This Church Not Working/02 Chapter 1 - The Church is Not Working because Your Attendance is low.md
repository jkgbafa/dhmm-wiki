### Chapter 1\

The Church is Not Working because Your Attendance is low\
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your church is not working if the attendance is too low. The very first church was assessed by its attendance, which was three thousand souls.

How many souls are there in your church?

A working church has good attendance. It is only fair that we assess your church by its attendance.

It is shameful to have an undeveloped midget church. A midget is something small for its kind! There are midget churches. A midget church has a low attendance for a church of its kind in the city. You must not have a midget church!

A pastor will always be assessed by the attendance of his church. If we do not use attendance to assess the performance of a pastor, then we do not have any parameter to assess the performance of the pastor and the church. We need something to determine if you are doing well or not. *We will always use the basic attendance and the basic income of a church to assess if a church is working.*

1.      **The first recorded attendance of the first church was three thousand.**

Then they that gladly received his word were baptized: and the same day there were added unto them about three thousand souls.

Acts 2:41

2.      **The second recorded attendance of the early church was five thousand.**

Howbeit many of them which heard the word believed; and the number of the men was about five thousand.

Acts 4:4

 

3.     **The third recorded attendance of the early church was that the multitudes attended the church.**

 And believers were the more added to the Lord, multitudes both of men and women.

Acts 5:14

\

4.     **The fourth recorded attendance of the early church was that a multiplied number of disciples attended the church.**

 And the word of God increased; and the number of the disciples multiplied in Jerusalem greatly; and a great company of the priests were obedient to the faith.

Acts 6:7

5.      **The fifth recorded attendance of the early church was that the whole city attended the church.**

And the next sabbath day came almost the whole city together to hear the word of God. But when the Jews saw the multitudes, they were filled with envy, and spake against those things which were spoken by Paul, contradicting and blaspheming.

Acts 13:44-45

Good church attendance always provokes jealousy!

There are two main parameters by which we monitor a church's well-being.

The number one parameter is church attendance.

The number two parameter is church income.

Thus, with the church attendance and income, we can determine if a church is flourishing or not.

Run away from having a midget church. Do whatever you need to do to deliver yourself from having a midget church which is small for its kind.
