### Chapter 12\

Maintain the Victory Attitude\
---------------------------------------------------

Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.

John 12:24

Are you hurt because you have been thrown into the ground to die and to bear fruit?

It is important that you maintain a good attitude throughout all your tests and trials. Without a good attitude, you will not bear fruit. A victory attitude is a good attitude.

1\. Give no place to the devil in your attitude.

Neither give place to the devil.

Ephesians 4:27

Don’t give the devil a way to defeat you.

Ephesians 4:27 (ERV)

Give no place to worry!

Give no place to regret!

Give no place to bitterness!

Give no place to hatred!

Give no place to anxiety!

Give no place to evil thoughts of revenge!

Give no place to anger!

Give no place to discontentment!

Give no place to your fears!

2\. Maintain the victory attitude of long life.

A SOUND HEART IS THE LIFE OF THE FLESH: but envy the rottenness of the bones.

Proverbs 14:30

A RELAXED ATTITUDE LENGTHENS A MAN’S LIFE; jealousy rots it away.

Proverbs 14:30 (TLB)

A HEART AT PEACE GIVES LIFE TO THE BODY, but envy rots the bones.

Proverbs 14:30 (NIV)

Get rid of all evil attitudes because they affect how long you live. A relaxed attitude lengthens a man’s life. A heart at peace gives life to the body. A sound heart is the life of the flesh! Indeed, having a good victory attitude will prolong your life!

As you go through life, you will have troubles, with people hurting you many times. You are also going to make many mistakes. Life will happen to you!

Offences will come! But you must not hold on to the offences. You must relax. You must be happy. You must make yourself merry. Keep your spirit free of the burden of offences. Gradually, God will fill your life with good things, and that will keep you young and strong.

God does not want you to become old, grouchy and grumpy. When that happens, your life will fall apart and be shortened. God wants you to stay young, strong, good-looking, full of faith, joy, and energy. Do not allow negative attitudes to take root in your life.

3\. Maintain the victory attitude of youthfulness.

Who satisfieth thy mouth with good things; so that thy youth is renewed like the eagle’s.

Psalm 103:5

He gives us plenty of good things. He makes us young again, like an eagle that grows new feathers.

Psalm 103:5 (ERV)

Who satisfies your desires with good things, so that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s.

Psalm 103:5 (NIV)

He satisfies me with the good things I desire. Then I feel young and strong again, just like an eagle

Psalm 103:5 (NIrV)

It is important that you enjoy the good things that God brings to you. He satisfies your desires with good things. Satisfying your desires helps you to remain young. “Who satisfies your desires with good things, so that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s” (Psalm 103:5 (NIV)). Every man has desires for food, rest, sex, play, happiness and friendship.

According to the Bible, when the desires for good things are satisfied, it increases a person’s youthfulness. It is important to have a youthful, cheerful and hopeful attitude because young people have cheerful and hopeful attitudes. To renew your youth is therefore to renew yourself to a higher level of cheerfulness and hopefulness. To renew your youth is to allow yourself to be satisfied with food, relaxation, rest, sex, play, happiness and socialisation.

God is going to satisfy your mouth with good things like food, rest, sex, play and happiness. Accept these blessings in your life to help you relax so that you can grow even more youthful.

4\. Maintain the victory attitude of forgiveness.

Looking diligently lest any man fail of the grace of God; lest any root of bitterness springing up trouble you, and thereby many be defiled;

Hebrews 12:15

To forgive people is to develop a strong victory attitude! Forgive others and maintain a good heart without the impurities of poisonous bitterness that will shorten your life. All forms of unforgiveness and bitterness will destroy and defile you.

5\. Maintain the victory attitude of focusing on forgetting the past.

No, dear brothers, I am still not all I should be, BUT I AM BRINGING ALL MY ENERGIES TO BEAR ON THIS ONE THING: FORGETTING THE PAST AND LOOKING FORWARD TO WHAT LIES AHEAD, I strain to reach the end of the race and receive the prize for which God is calling us up to heaven because of what Christ Jesus did for us.

I hope all of you who are mature Christians will see eye-to-eye with me on these things, and if you disagree on some point, I believe that God will make it plain to you.

Philippians 3:13-15 (TLB)

It takes a lot of energy to forget the past so that you can build the future. Forgive by focusing your efforts on the future. Do new things that help you forget the past. Do not let your past mistakes and failures dominate your present life. This was Paul’s strategy that helped him move forward.

Apply strength to remove, delete and scrape out every memory and every memento of past unfortunate occasions of your life that bring sadness.

6\. Maintain the victory attitude by dropping painful issues.

And David sent and enquired after the woman. And one said, IS NOT THIS BATHSHEBA, THE DAUGHTER OF ELIAM, THE WIFE OF URIAH THE HITTITE?

2 Samuel 11:3

These be the names of the mighty men whom David had: The Tachmonite that sat in the seat, chief among the captains; the same was Adino the Eznite: he lift up his spear against eight hundred, whom he slew at one time…Eliphelet the son of Ahasbai, the son of the Maachathite, ELIAM THE SON OF AHITHOPHEL THE GILONITE,

2 Samuel 23:8, 34

And Absalom sent for AHITHOPHEL THE GILONITE, David’s counsellor, from his city, even from Giloh, while he offered sacrifices. And the conspiracy was strong; for the people increased continually with Absalom.

2 Samuel 15:12

From the scriptures above, we see that Bathsheba was Ahithophel’s granddaughter. When David abused Bathsheba, Ahithophel could not forgive him. Instead of Ahithophel forgiving David, he bore a grudge against David.

Ahithophel, the grandfather of Bathsheba, did not drop the painful issue, and it ended up consuming him. Why did Ahithophel betray King David? There is a reason why Ahithophel turned against David when he had a chance to. He had been David’s close adviser and friend for so many years. Why else would a trusted friend of King David suddenly turn on him and follow the madness of Absalom?

Ahithophel smiled and laughed on the outside whilst he gave David advice, but inside, something wasn’t right. He ended up hanging himself.

What David did was wrong. In the natural, Ahithophel had a reason to be upset, angry, and bitter. But because he did not maintain a victory attitude of forgiveness, he ended up being destroyed by David’s sin.

Ahithophel did not drop the painful issue. Not dropping the painful issue cost Ahithophel his life.

7\. Maintain the victory attitude of not poking and prying into things that you should not.

LORD, my heart is not haughty, nor mine eyes lofty: neither do I exercise myself in great matters, or in things too high for me.

Psalm 131:1

To appoint unto them that mourn in Zion, to give unto them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness; that they might be called trees of righteousness, the planting of the Lord, that he might be glorified.

Isaiah 61:3

Develop a victory attitude by leaving things as they are and not poking deeper into issues and going over and over the same things.

Exchange the bitterness in your heart for joy and peace.

Give God your ashes and take His beauty.

Give Him your mourning and take His oil of joy.

Give Him your heaviness and take His praises.

I found out in medical school that when people have gunshot wounds, the bullet and bullet fragments are often left in the person. This is because the bullet is on fire and is sterile as it penetrates the human body. When the bullet finally stops, it is not wise to extract it from deep inside the body, unless it is destroying some vital structure in the body. Poking around to find the bullet is sometimes more dangerous than leaving the bullet in the body. The key thing in gunshot wounds is to stop the bleeding and detect any significant damage, rather than to find and remove the bullet.

I read this interesting story that illustrates the importance of not poking things too much.

What Killed President James Garfield?

In 1881, President James Garfield of the USA was shot six months into his presidency. The doctors were able to save his life, but they could not find the bullet that was lodged behind his pancreas.

He was recovering well, but back in those days, they thought if they didn’t remove the bullet, it would cause problems.

Unfortunately, they performed more and more surgeries, poked and probed all around, but still could not find the bullet. The famous inventor, Alexander Graham Bell, even created an electrical device to try to locate the bullet but that did not work either.

Sadly, almost three months after being shot, President Garfield died; but he did not die from his original bullet wound. He died from the various infections and complications that came upon him because of the continuous surgeries to probe, to poke and locate the bullet.

This story illustrates the importance of not poking, probing, searching, or digging into things, because the constant probing and searching will not heal the matter – it will only make it worse.
