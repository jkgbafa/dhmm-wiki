### Chapter 5\

The Next Step in Your Life Will Lead to Your Victory\
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord: and he delighteth in his way.

Psalm 37:23

The purpose of your life is to bear much fruit. In order to bear much fruit, you are going to have to fall into the ground and die. Without falling to the ground and dying you will never bear much fruit. God leads you through many steps of destiny until you get to the place of bearing much fruit.

To bear much fruit, you must take the next step that is ordained by God.

Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.

John 12:24

Every step of your life is ordained so that you end up at the place of “much fruit”. That is what glorifies God. “Herein is my Father glorified, that ye bear much fruit; so shall ye be my disciples” (John 15:8). That is what God desires from you. He is going to lead you on a series of steps that will eventually bring you to a place where you will be described as “bearing much fruit”.

The life of Joseph is a good example of how following a series of steps brings you to a place of much fruit.

If you believe that your life is made up of a number of steps that are guided by the Lord, you will be more relaxed as you go through each step. Perhaps, the next step will change everything. I want you to know about these steps that are ordered by the Lord.

1\. What you are experiencing now is actually one of the steps that are ordered by the Lord.

The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord: and he delighteth in his way.

Psalm 37:23

What you are experiencing today is one of the divine steps of your life. Do not be discouraged by today’s events. Do not be discouraged by the stage you find yourself in! Every stage of your life is a step into your destiny. Every stage in Joseph’s life was actually a step towards him becoming the Prime Minister of Egypt.

2\. Every step leads to the next step in God’s master plan.

Thus saith the Lord, thy Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel; I am the Lord thy God which teacheth thee to profit, which leadeth thee by the way that thou shouldest go.…. And they thirsted not when he led them through the deserts: he caused the waters to flow out of the rock for them: he clave the rock also, and the waters gushed out.

Isaiah 48:17, 21

This is the next important truth about the steps of destiny in a man’s life. Every step leads to the next step. Where you are will lead to the very next step into what God has destined for you. As the children of God went through the wilderness, God was leading them in the way they were supposed to go.

3\. If you take out one step the other steps will not happen.

A close look at the life of Joseph will show you that one thing led to another. If Joseph had not been thrown into the well, the people passing by would not have heard him nor bought him nor taken him to Egypt. Every step led to the next step of his life.

Without the step of resentment and hatred by his brothers, the next stage of his life and ministry would not have happened.

It is amazing that Joseph had to meet a woman like Potiphar’s wife in order to go to the next step of his ministry. Most people would have considered meeting Potiphar’s wife as a curse. However, without Potiphar’s wife, Joseph would not have gone to prison for twelve years. It was in prison that he found the next step to his destiny.

Do not be discouraged that you are in prison.

Do not be disheartened because you met a woman like Potiphar’s wife.

Do not be discouraged because you met a woman who behaves like Potiphar’s wife and tries to take advantage of your youthfulness.

Joseph was about sixteen years old when he encountered Potiphar’s wife. He may have considered it the greatest curse of his life to be abused, and taken advantage of by a mature, grown-up woman. However, these bad things are leading you to your next step. Notice forty divine steps that led to Joseph’s fruitfulness.

4\. Learn from the Forty Divine Steps of Joseph’s Life

1\. Joseph becomes a favourite and favoured child.

2\. Joseph receives a multi-coloured coat from his father.

3\. Joseph experiences resentment from his brothers.

4\. Joseph has a series of dreams.

5\. Joseph mistakenly shares his dreams.

6\. Jacob, his father, is unaware of the resentment and hatred of the brothers.

7\. Jacob sends Joseph to his brothers.

8\. Joseph is captured and thrown into a well.

9\. Reuben tries to save Joseph but is not forthright about it.

10\. Joseph is sold as a slave.

11\. No one listens to Joseph and no one is kind to him.

12\. Slavery becomes a reality for Joseph.

13\. Joseph experiences slavery in Potiphar’s house.

14\. Potiphar’s marriage is in trouble.

15\. Potiphar’s wife falls in love with Joseph.

16\. Joseph encounters Potiphar’s wife.

17\. Potiphar’s wife brings up an accusation of rape.

18\. The rape accusation is not set aside.

19\. Injustice prevails and Joseph is thrown into jail.

20\. Joseph meets other prisoners.

21\. The prisoners have dreams.

22\. Joseph’s dream interpretation ministry flourishes in prison.

23\. The butler forgets Joseph for another two years until Joseph is 30 years old.

24\. The butler suddenly remembers Joseph.

25\. Joseph is summoned to meet Pharaoh.

26\. Joseph suggests to Pharaoh to find a wise man to help him.

27\. Pharaoh appoints Joseph as the wise prime minister.

28\. Joseph rules Egypt and causes the nation to prosper.

29\. Joseph gets a beloved.

30\. Joseph marries and has two children.

31\. Famine strikes Egypt.

32\. Famine drives Joseph’s brothers to Egypt.

33\. Joseph saves his family.

34\. Joseph’s family migrates to Egypt.

35\. Jacob blesses Joseph twice and adopts his two children.

36\. Jacob dies and is buried.

37\. Joseph’s brothers fear reprisals

38\. Joseph forgives his brothers again!

39\. Joseph dies.

40\. Joseph’s bones are taken to Israel.

5\. Every step on the way to the Promised Land, when taken in isolation, seemed like something bizarre.

So Moses brought Israel from the Red sea, and they went out into the wilderness of Shur; and they went three days in the wilderness and found no water. And when they came to Marah, they could not drink of the waters of Marah, for they were bitter: therefore the name of it was called Marah. And the people murmured against Moses, saying, What shall we drink?

Exodus 15:22-24

When the Lord led the children of Israel to the waters of Marah, they were shocked at the kind of bitter waters they were made to drink. “What on earth is this?” they asked. “Why would anyone bring us to this place?” However, Marah was not their final destination. Marah was a step on the way to the Promised Land. Taken out of context, Marah would seem to be a bizarre destination.

Indeed, you must consider each step of destiny as a stage and a phase of your life. Marah is not your final destination. Neither is it an end. Marah is one of the several steps you must take. If you do not consider what is happening in your life today as one of several steps in God’s plan, it will seem like something bizarre is happening in your life.

Have faith in God! Believe that you are experiencing a set of divine steps towards a great future that God has planned for you.

God has great plans for you! Do not be discouraged by any bizarre event in your life. God has great things ahead! Today’s events are just one step in a series of steps towards the future God has prepared for you.

6\. Every step that happens in your life is for your sake.

For all things are for your sakes, that the abundant grace might through the thanksgiving of many redound to the glory of God.

2 Corinthians 4:15

It is important that you believe that all things are for your sake. Every single thing that happens is actually for your sake. Everything that is happening right now is for your sake. God loves you so much that He has arranged circumstances to work out for your sake. Beginning from today, you must accept that what is happening around you is for your sake.
