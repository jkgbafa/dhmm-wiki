### Chapter 11\

God Has Not Forgotten You\
-----------------------------------------------

Can a woman forget her sucking child, that she should not have compassion on the son of her womb? YEA, THEY MAY FORGET, YET WILL I NOT FORGET THEE.

Isaiah 49:15

1\. God has not forgotten about you at all. Every time He opens the palms of His hands, He sees your name.

But Zion said, The Lord hath forsaken me, and my Lord hath forgotten me. Can a woman forget her sucking child, that she should not have compassion on the son of her womb? YEA, THEY MAY FORGET, YET WILL I NOT FORGET THEE. Behold, I have graven thee upon the palms of my hands; thy walls are continually before me.

Isaiah 49:14-16

I am not surprised that you feel lonely and forgotten. Many people feel lonely and forgotten. God’s word says He will not forget you. Every time God opens His palms, He sees your name. Every time He sees your name, He is reminded of you. Under your name, there are notes about you. God reads the notes to remind Himself of everything about you! Wow!

God has not forgotten about you at all. God has not forgotten the visions and dreams He gave to you. Your life is very important to Him. God is always thinking about you. How can God forget you when your name is written in His palm?

Get ready to come out of the horrible pit. God is activating His angels to move on your case. Your case will not remain unsolved for long. Solutions are coming faster than you think. Expect to come out of that horrible pit because God has remembered you!

2\. God will replace the irreplaceable. God knows your irreplaceable loss, and He will work a miracle in your life to replace them.

The children born during your time of sorrow will speak to you. They will say, “This city is too small for us. Give us more space to live in. “

Then you will say to yourself, “Whose children are these? I lost my children. And I couldn’t have any more. My children were taken far away from me. And no one wanted them. Who brought these children up? I was left all alone. So where have these children come from? “

Isaiah 49:20-21 (NIrV)

God will replace the irreplaceable.

Someone may have changed your life forever through his wickedness.

Maybe you have lost a child.

Maybe you lost a husband.

Maybe you lost your decency.

Maybe your wife passed away.

Perhaps you lost a dear friend.

God has not forgotten you!

God will replace the irreplaceable!

Someone may have taken away your virginity and your innocence.

Perhaps, a man abused you.

Perhaps you lost your purity.

Perhaps you lost your decency.

Perhaps you have lost your sense of self-worth.

Perhaps, someone played with your body and changed you sexually.

Somebody may have taken away your very life.

Perhaps, someone has taken away your valued church members.

I tell you, God is going to pay them back.

God is also going to pay you back.

He knows exactly how much you are owed. That is the way God is!

He will never allow the injustice to prevail. God will always make the enemy pay. God will bring you out better.

God has not forgotten you!

God will replace the irreplaceable!

3\. God is going to restore all the injustices done against you in your childhood.

And David said, Is there yet any that is left of the house of Saul, that I may shew him kindness for Jonathan’s sake? And there was of the house of Saul a servant whose name was Ziba. And when they had called him unto David, the king said unto him, Art thou Ziba? And he said, Thy servant is he. And the king said, IS THERE NOT YET ANY OF THE HOUSE OF SAUL, THAT I MAY SHEW THE KINDNESS OF GOD UNTO HIM? AND ZIBA SAID UNTO THE KING, JONATHAN HATH YET A SON, WHICH IS LAME ON HIS FEET. And the king said unto him, Where is he? And Ziba said unto the king, Behold, he is in the house of Machir, the son of Ammiel, in Lodebar….

And David said unto him, Fear not: for I will surely shew thee kindness for Jonathan thy father’s sake, and will restore thee all the land of Saul thy father; and thou shalt eat bread at my table continually. And he bowed himself, and said, What is thy servant, that thou shouldest look upon such a dead dog as I am? Then the king called to Ziba, Saul’s servant, and said unto him, I have given unto thy master’s son all that pertained to Saul and to all his house. Thou therefore, and thy sons, and thy servants, shall till the land for him, and thou shalt bring in the fruits, that thy master’s son may have food to eat: but Mephibosheth thy master’s son shall eat bread alway at my table. Now Ziba had fifteen sons and twenty servants.

2 Samuel 9:1-4, 7-10

Mephibosheth was dropped as a baby and became a cripple. He suffered a calamity and injustice as a child! But God remembered him. Mephibosheth was remembered by King David. That is how you will be remembered! The king is about to summon you to the palace.

God is going to pay you back for the wrong done to you as a child. This is the goodness of God, paying you back and bringing in justice. You may not know how it can happen, but God is speaking to certain people about you. He’s softening the right hearts.

He’s going to pay you back for that abuse and all the wrongs of your childhood.

Perhaps you were mocked as a child. Perhaps they made fun of you as a child. Perhaps they insulted you and laughed at you. I tell you; God will pay you back for all the shame you suffered.

God is going to pay you back for those people who did you wrong!

God is going to pay you back for that loved one you lost!

God is going to pay you back for that baby you couldn’t have.

You may have been dropped, but you need to get ready, for there’s a seat at the King’s table waiting just for you.

It is a seat of honour!

It is a seat of restoration!

It is a seat of remembrance!

It is a seat of promotion!

You are just about to be lifted to a better place! A place of blessing, a place of victory! A place of God’s love!

4\. God is your glory and the lifter of your head.

Lord, how are they increased that trouble me! many are they that rise up against me. Many there be which say of my soul, There is no help for him in God. Selah. But thou, O Lord, art a shield for me; MY GLORY, AND THE LIFTER UP OF MINE HEAD.

Psalm 3:1-3

God will not allow you to stay in that horrible hole. God is the only one who can lift up your head.

God will not allow your head to stay down.

Beginning from today, your head will be lifted up! The beauty of your life will come from God Himself.

God is your glory!

God is your beauty!

Nothing is beautiful about your life except God Himself.

He surely will lift you up! Expect the lifting of your life to begin from today.

The prophecy you believe is the prophecy you will receive!
