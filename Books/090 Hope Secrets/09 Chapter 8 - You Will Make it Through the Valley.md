### Chapter 8\

You Will Make it Through the Valley\
---------------------------------------------------------

The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters. He restoreth my soul: he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name’s sake. Yea, though I WALK THROUGH THE VALLEY of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff they comfort me. THOU PREPAREST A TABLE before me in the presence of mine enemies: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over. Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever.

Psalm 23:1-6

What is a valley? A valley is a low place! A valley is a dip! A valley is far from the heights and high points! A valley represents the difficulties of life and ministry. But God has promised great things after the valley; A table prepared for you comes just after the valley!

According to Psalm 23 verse 4, there will be a valley in your life. But in Psalm 23 verse 5, we see a table prepared for you in the presence of the enemies. This is an amazing blessing! God will take you through the valley and bring you straight to a table of abundant provisions and blessings.

A valley is an elongated depression in your life! Expect prolonged times of lowliness and depression. That is what a valley is.

A valley is a depression, a crisis, a difficulty, a quarrel, a divorce, a family concern, a pregnancy that is not happening, a lost child, a lost loved one, a failure, a loss, a weakness, a marital crisis, a financial setback, some bad news, a terror, a moral failure, an evil wind, a failed relationship, an unfortunate happening, a low point or a disaster.

Keep pressing through every valley! You must press through every elongated and depressed place in your life! You must keep walking even when you enter the valley, because, just after the valley is a prepared table full of food.

Keep pressing on in faith! God has plans to strengthen you in the valley and bring you to a prepared table.

Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.

John 12:24

Jesus really wants you to bear fruit. He wants you to bear much fruit. You were created to be fruitful. Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and dies it will simply not bear fruit.

To bear fruit you will have to go low and fall into the ground! The ground is a low place to fall into. You must walk through the valleys of life and come to the table prepared by the Lord.

1\. After the valley is a prepared table! A prepared table speaks of overflowing abundance, goodness and mercy and establishment in the house of the Lord. These prepared tables are found just after the valleys of your life. After the lows of your life will come establishment, blessings and peace.

Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over. Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever.

Psalm 23:5-6

2\. God is very powerful and very present in your valleys! For He is a God of the valleys! God knows all about valleys and God will bring you through all the valleys of your life and ministry.

And there came a man of God, and spake unto the king of Israel, and said, Thus saith the Lord, Because the Syrians have said, THE LORD IS GOD OF THE HILLS, BUT HE IS NOT GOD OF THE VALLEYS, therefore will I deliver all this great multitude into thine hand, and ye shall know that I am the Lord.

1 Kings 20:28

God is not just the God of the mountains and high spots of your life. He is not just the God of the glorious finish; He is the God of the valleys too! God is moving right now in your life.

He has you in the palms of His hands. Right now, He’s working out His plan for your life.

Do not get discouraged by the process. You may be in the fire, but God is working on pulling you through. You are going to pass through it.

Every power has an expiry date!

Your crisis is only temporary!

You will pull through!

You will come out well!

You will do well!

It’s just a matter of time!

3\. King David came through many valleys!

And the Lord said unto Samuel, How long wilt thou mourn for Saul, seeing I have rejected him from reigning over Israel? fill thine horn with oil, and go, I will send thee to Jesse the Bethlehemite: for I have provided me a king among his sons.

1 Samuel 16:1

God called David even though He knew there would be valleys! God knew about the valleys when He anointed David to be king. He had plans to take him through the valleys.

God was with David all through the valleys of his life!

David could have said, “O God, You promised me that I would be the king, but You didn’t tell me that I would have to face a giant called Goliath. You did not give me those details.

Oh God, You didn’t tell me that even though I was faithfully serving King Saul, he would try to kill me.

Oh God, You didn’t tell me that I would be a fugitive for so long.

Oh God, You didn’t tell me that my own son would organise a rebellion and a coup d’état against me!”

4\. Joseph came through many valleys!

And he said unto them, Hear, I pray you, this dream which I have dreamed: For, behold, we were binding sheaves in the field, and, lo, my sheaf arose, and also stood upright; and, behold, your sheaves stood round about, and made obeisance to my sheaf. And his brethren said to him, Shalt thou indeed reign over us? or shalt thou indeed have dominion over us? And they hated him yet the more for his dreams, and for his words.

Genesis 37:6-8

God knew about the valleys when He prophesied that Joseph would be a great leader. God had plans to take Joseph through slavery, through imprisonment, through being abandoned and through being forgotten by men. God knew the end would be glorious but He also knew there would be valleys and low points in the journey.

There were many low points and valleys in Joseph’s journey. I am sure Joseph was shocked by these low points of his journey:

There was the hatred of his brothers

There was the well he was thrown into

There was the slavery

There was imprisonment

There was Potiphar’s wife

There was betrayal.

Please don’t be discouraged in the valley. Many people have been in valleys just like yours. The valley is not the end of your journey. You are just passing through the valley!

5\. Israel came through many low points in the wilderness!

And it came to pass, when Pharaoh had let the people go, that God led them not through the way of the land of the Philistines, although that was near; for God said, Lest peradventure the people repent when they see war, and they return to Egypt: BUT GOD LED THE PEOPLE ABOUT, THROUGH THE WAY OF THE WILDERNESS OF THE RED SEA: AND THE CHILDREN OF ISRAEL WENT UP HARNESSED OUT OF THE LAND OF EGYPT. And Moses took the bones of Joseph with him: for he had straitly sworn the children of Israel, saying, God will surely visit you; and ye shall carry up my bones away hence with you. And they took their journey from Succoth, and encamped in Etham, in the edge of the wilderness. And the Lord went before them by day in a pillar of a cloud, to lead them the way; and by night in a pillar of fire, to give them light; to go by day and night: He took not away the pillar of the cloud by day, nor the pillar of fire by night, from before the people.

Exodus 13:17-22

Israel was led directly into the valley of death as they embarked on a journey through the wilderness. God knew about the valley when He prophesied to them a promised land, flowing with milk and honey. Get ready for all the prophecies to be fulfilled in your life. Brace yourself to go through every valley that you encounter.

All through the valleys, God provided for the children of Israel. God never said, “You are on your own now that you are in the valley of your life.” When they needed meat, He gave them quails. When they needed bread, He gave them manna. When they needed water, God provided supernatural sources of water from rocks. When they needed an army to defend them, God provided deliverance from enemy nations. When they were chased, God opened the Red Sea.

God has plans to see you through the valleys! When there was bitterness in the water, God turned it around and gave them fresh water. God is a God who takes you through the valleys of life successfully.

So Moses brought Israel from the Red sea, and they went out into the wilderness of Shur; and they went three days in the wilderness, and found no water. And when they came to Marah, they could not drink of the waters of Marah, for they were bitter: therefore the name of it was called Marah. And the people murmured against Moses, saying, What shall we drink? And he cried unto the Lord; and the Lord shewed him a tree, which when he had cast into the waters, the waters were made sweet

Exodus 15:22-25

6\. Mary came through valleys!

And the angel said unto her, Fear not, Mary: for thou hast found favour with God. And, behold, thou shalt conceive in thy womb, and bring forth a son, and shalt call his name JESUS. He shall be great, and shall be called the Son of the Highest: and the Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David: And he shall reign over the house of Jacob for ever; and of his kingdom there shall be no end. Then said Mary unto the angel, How shall this be, seeing I know not a man? And the angel answered and said unto her, The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God.

Luke 1:30-35

Mary faced a valley as she heard the call of God’s promise of greatness! God gave Mary an amazing promise. But the valleys were waiting for her.

God showed Mary the end of the vision. She was going to be the mother of Christ: “You will have a baby without knowing a man, and He will be the Messiah, the Saviour of the world.” She would have honour and be admired for generations to come. I am sure Mary was excited. She couldn’t believe it.

But I can hear Mary years later, saying, “O God, you did not tell me about the valleys of this great calling.

You did not tell me how people would make fun of me for claiming that I was pregnant without having sex.

You did not tell me how ridiculous it would sound to the rest of the world when I announced my faith in you.”

I am sure she said to God, “You did not tell me what it would be like to watch my own son being crucified on the cross.”

Perhaps, she wished that she had never given birth to such a son.

But God is a God of the valleys too! You will find Jesus standing by you in every low moment of your life.

7\. Jesus came through valleys!

Then answered the Jews and said unto him, What sign shewest thou unto us, seeing that thou doest these things? Jesus answered and said unto them, DESTROY THIS TEMPLE, AND IN THREE DAYS I WILL RAISE IT UP.

John 2:18-19

Jesus was fearless as He approached the valley of death! He knew that after the valley of death, there would be a table prepared for Him!

Jesus knew God was in charge and that’s why He was fearless before Pilate. Jesus told Pilate that there was nothing Pilate could do to change His destiny. After the valley is a prepared table.

When you trust in God, you will not be afraid of what man can do unto you. The lowest point in the valley is never too low for Jesus to reach out to you.

Do not be discouraged!

Do not be disillusioned!

Do not be disheartened!

Better days are coming!

In three days, God will raise you up!

Always remember that after the valley comes a well-prepared table before your enemies.
