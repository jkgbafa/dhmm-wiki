### Chapter 7\

God Will Use Your Enemies to Promote You\
--------------------------------------------------------------

Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.

John 12:24

Some of the greatest promotions of my life came through enemies. The actions, the writings and the slander of my enemies have been used by God to help me along the way. I could tell you countless stories of how important enemies have been in my life. Enemies are people who hate you, people who fight you and people who seek to destroy you.

The arrival of enemies is often the beginning of a dark season. Enemies make you uncomfortable – so much so that you have a desire to never see them again. But there is an amazing truth about enemies that you must remember – God uses your enemies!

In a sense, God used Judas more than any of the disciples to bring about the greatest victory of Jesus’ ministry. The accomplishment of the cross would not have happened without Judas.

How did Thomas or Bartholomew help Jesus accomplish His suffering on the cross?

They were simply deserters!

How did Peter help Jesus accomplish His suffering on the cross? Peter was completely opposed to the idea of the cross.

However, Judas actually organized and mobilized the people who would crucify Jesus on the cross. This is why we can say that God used the traitor Judas even more than He used the faithful disciples.

Do not underestimate the importance of your enemies. They are a specially chosen group of people who God ordains and allows to be a part of your life.

What is the name of your number one enemy? Is he called Jedder? Or is he called Koliba? Is she called Sheba? I don’t know their name, but I assure you that they are a significant part of your life and your calling. There are things you will not accomplish without having enemies!

1\. God used Goliath to turn David into a national hero!

And there went out a champion out of the camp of the Philistines, named Goliath, of Gath, whose height was six cubits and a span... And the Philistine said, I defy the armies of Israel this day; give me a man, that we may fight together. When Saul and all Israel heard those words of the Philistine, they were dismayed, and greatly afraid.

1 Samuel 17:4,10-11

The enemy of David was used to make David into a national hero. God will use your enemies to make you into a national hero.

Goliath was strategically placed in David’s path - not to defeat David, but to promote him. Without Goliath, David would never have taken the throne. Don’t complain too much about your enemies! What may look like a setback may really be a set-up to get you to your throne.

God could have used King Saul, who had the authority, to promote David. All God had to do was move on Saul’s heart and tell him, “Promote that young man.” But God chose instead to bless David through his enemies. That is why we do not need everybody to be our friends. God does not only use your friends or associates to help you. He will also use your enemies, your critics, and the people who are trying to push you down. He will use them to push you up.

After David defeated Goliath, we do not read anything more about Goliath. He was created for David’s purpose. Part of Goliath’s destiny was to establish David as a great person! Perhaps, you will advance most in ministry through the person who seems to be opposing you in your life and ministry.

2\. God used Daniel’s haters to make him famous.

It pleased Darius to set over the kingdom AN HUNDRED AND TWENTY PRINCES, which should be over the whole kingdom; And over these three presidents; of whom Daniel was first: that the princes might give accounts unto them, and the king should have no damage. THEN THIS DANIEL WAS PREFERRED ABOVE THE PRESIDENTS AND PRINCES, because an excellent spirit was in him; and the king thought to set him over the whole realm. THEN THE PRESIDENTS AND PRINCES SOUGHT TO FIND OCCASION AGAINST DANIEL concerning the kingdom; but they could find none occasion nor fault; forasmuch as he was faithful, neither was there any error or fault found in him. Then said these men, We shall not find any occasion against this Daniel, except we find it against him concerning the law of his God. THEN THESE PRESIDENTS AND PRINCES ASSEMBLED TOGETHER to the king, and said thus unto him, King Darius, live for ever…. And the king commanded, and they brought those men which had accused Daniel, and they cast them into the den of lions, them, their children, and their wives; and the lions had the mastery of them, and brake all their bones in pieces or ever they came at the bottom of the den.

Daniel 6:1-6, 24

God will use your haters to make you famous. God used the presidents and princes who hated Daniel to make him famous! These enemies of Daniel were used to make him famous for being thrown in a lions’ den. To this day, Daniel is famous for having survived the lions’ den! Little children around the world know the story of “Daniel in the lions’ den”!

3\. God used Judas to help Jesus accomplish much in ministry!

And Simon he surnamed Peter; And James the son of Zebedee, and John the brother of James; and he surnamed them Boanerges, which is, The sons of thunder: And Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James the son of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Canaanite, And Judas Iscariot, which also betrayed him: and they went into an house.

Mark 3:16-19

God uses enemies even more than friends and loyal people. Judas was more significant in the ministry of Jesus than any of the other chosen disciples.

In one sense, God used Judas more than He used the other disciples. Judas was ordained to betray Jesus—that was his purpose. It wasn’t just his idea; it was part of the plan of God.

If Judas had not betrayed Jesus, there would not have been a crucifixion, and without the crucifixion there would not have been a resurrection, and without a resurrection we would not have had any salvation.

We celebrate Mary, the mother of Jesus for giving birth to Jesus in the manger! We celebrate John the Baptist for baptizing Jesus in the River Jordan. We celebrate Peter, James, and John for walking with Jesus and being His friends. But the man who betrayed Jesus, the one who sold Him out for thirty pieces of silver, was just as critical to His destiny, if not more so than the others.

4\. God used the Egyptians! God will use your enemies to multiply you.

Now there arose up a new king over Egypt, which knew not Joseph. And he said unto his people, Behold, the people of the children of Israel are more and mightier than we: Come on, let us deal wisely with them; lest they multiply, and it come to pass, that, when there falleth out any war, they join also unto our enemies, and fight against us, and so get them up out of the land. Therefore they did set over them taskmasters to afflict them with their burdens. And they built for Pharaoh treasure cities, Pithom and Raamses. BUT THE MORE THEY AFFLICTED THEM, THE MORE THEY MULTIPLIED AND GREW. And they were grieved because of the children of Israel.

Exodus 1:8-12

The Egyptians, enemies of Israel, were used to multiply them. The more they afflicted them, the more they multiplied. Pressure is good for you! Many people perform better under pressure. You may not like it, but you will grow better under pressure.

Expect your character to be developed under pressure.

Expect to discover talents you did not know you had because of the trouble you have been through.

When Pharaoh squeezed the Israelites with slave labour, Moses was unveiled. The pressure from the enemies of the Israelites caused a saviour and a prophet called Moses to be revealed.

5\. God uses enemies as an audience to see your greatness and victories.

Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over.

Psalm 23:5

Your enemies will be there to see how God remembered you and helped you. Many people will play a role in helping you fulfil destiny. Enemies will also play their role in helping you to your final destiny. They will be there to clap and see how you enjoy the pork, the beef and the shrimps at the table God prepares for you. Get used to having enemies around and get used to enduring their presence. God always uses enemies to do something great in your life.

6\. God uses the afflictions of enemies to work out good things in you. It is good that there were Egyptians! It is good there was Goliath! It is good there was Judas!

IT IS GOOD FOR ME THAT I HAVE BEEN AFFLICTED; that I might learn thy statutes.

Psalm 119:71

Many enemies are actually good news for you. There is some form of spiritual energy that is derived from the troubles you have. Many enemies bring out good things in your life. Sometimes when God wants to promote you, He sends you an enemy.

God may cause you to be put in a situation where you are treated wrongly. Injustice is part of the experiences in this life. Encountering people who simply cheat you, abuse you and mishandle you, will bring out the best in you.

Don’t get discouraged by all these terrible experiences. The more you seem defeated, the more you will experience the miracle hand of God. When you are weak, you are strong!

That is why Paul gloried in his weaknesses. The more opposition you have, the more grace you will receive from heaven.

7\. Consider it all joy when you fall into the hands of your enemies.

My brethren, count it all joy when ye fall into divers temptations; Knowing this, that the trying of your faith worketh patience. But let patience have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, wanting nothing.

James 1:2-4

You may have fallen into the hands of enemies. Do not lose hope! Count it all joy! The Egyptians you see today, you will see them no more!

Be happy. Consider it a good thing. The tests, trials and temptations are working out something wonderful in your life. Why would you be told to count it all joy if there was not something really joyful coming up?
