### Chapter 10\

You Are Coming Out of the Horrible Pit\
------------------------------------------------------------

I waited patiently for the Lord; and he inclined unto me, and heard my cry. HE BROUGHT ME UP ALSO OUT OF AN HORRIBLE PIT, OUT OF THE MIRY CLAY, and set my feet upon a rock, and established my goings. And he hath put a new song in my mouth, even praise unto our God: many shall see it, and fear, and shall trust in the Lord. Blessed is that man that maketh the Lord his trust, and respecteth not the proud, nor such as turn aside to lies.

Psalm 40:1-4

When God sees you in a pit, He is alarmed and agitated. God says, “That’s My son, that’s My daughter. That is the apple of my eye. That is my servant whom I uphold.

My son is in a pit! My daughter is in a pit! My servant is in a pit! The one I anointed is in a pit! He is lying on his face. My dear child is down and out! He is lying on his face. I have to rush there to help him out of that horrible pit. I want to help My son. I want to help My daughter!” This is all that God is thinking about as He sees you in a horrible pit.

1\. You may have to go into a horrible pit if you want to bear fruit.

Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.

John 12:24

Jesus has made it clear that you have to go into a horrible pit and die so that you can come forth and bear much fruit. To bear your fruit, you need to come out of the horrible pit in which you have been planted as a seed.

2\. God knows how to bring you out of that horrible pit.

He brought me up also out of an horrible pit, out of the miry clay, and set my feet upon a rock, and established my goings.

Psalm 40:2

There are horrible pits in this life.

If you are part of humanity, sooner or later, you will be dropped into a pit somewhere.

You may have been dropped into a horrible pit of sickness!

You may have been dropped by a friend into a horrible pit of loneliness.

Perhaps, you have been dropped into a horrible pit of betrayal!

Perhaps you have been dropped into a horrible pit of broken relationships.

It is just a matter of time before you will be dropped into a horrible pit of darkness and sadness.

Maybe a man has taken advantage of you and mistreated you - that’s when you were dropped into a horrible pit.

Maybe the girl you loved has been unfaithful to you and loved another man - you just fell into a horrible pit.

Maybe you are covered with shame and guilt because of your sin - you have just been dropped into a horrible pit!

Maybe someone just dropped you by the wayside.

You cannot live very long without falling into a pit somewhere.

Perhaps, you have been dropped into a horrible pit of incurable illness. Perhaps you have been dropped into a pit of loneliness by an unfaithful spouse.

Perhaps, you were dropped into a pit by a friend who turned against you.

God will bring you out of the horrible pit!

3\. Get ready for God to pull you out of the pit! Get ready to be established - God will bring you out of the pit and establish you.

I WAITED PATIENTLY FOR THE LORD; AND HE INCLINED UNTO ME, AND HEARD MY CRY. He brought me up also out of an horrible pit, out of the miry clay, and set my feet upon a rock, and established my goings. And he hath put a new song in my mouth, even praise unto our God: many shall see it, and fear, and shall trust in the Lord. Blessed is that man that maketh the Lord his trust, and respecteth not the proud, nor such as turn aside to lies.

Psalm 40:1-4

Do not just sit in the darkness!

It is time to wait upon the Lord and to cry out to Him.

God is about to lift you up in response to your prayers.

He will set you up on a high place because you asked Him to.

From the horrible pit, you will go up into a new level with new opportunities, new friendships, new relationships, new joy and a new life. You will never be the same as God brings you out of the horrible pit.

4\. Get ready for double: God actually plans to give you double of everything you had before you went into the horrible pit.

For your shame ye shall have double; and for confusion they shall rejoice in their portion: therefore in their land they shall possess the double: everlasting joy shall be unto them.

Isaiah 61:7

Instead of shame and dishonor, you will enjoy a double share of honor. You will possess a double portion of prosperity in your land, and everlasting joy will be yours.

Isaiah 61:7 (NLT)

God is going to lift you out of the horrible pit. God is going to compensate you for all the time you have spent in the horrible pit.

He will do something for you that you cannot do for yourself, even if it seems you have so much shame in your life.

Everyone passes by and sees you in that horrible pit, and they turn up their noses at you.

But I prophesy that you are coming out of that horrible pit! Beginning from today, I declare that you are out and you are not coming out empty-handed.

You will be promoted, respected and vindicated. Very soon, you will be enjoying favours and blessings you do not deserve.

God will compensate you for all the time that you spent in that horrible pit.

For your shame you will have double!

5\. God will right the wrongs. God has not forgotten you. Get ready for Him to right all the injustices done against you in the horrible pit.

And God heard their groaning, and God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob. And God looked upon the children of Israel, and God had respect unto them.

Exodus 2:24-25

Compensation is coming! Promotion is coming! Vindication is coming! Healing is coming! A Blessing is coming!

Life is not always fair, but God is fair. God will add up all the losses, the disappointments, the heartaches, the tears, and He will pay you back.

God has not forgotten about you. He has seen every lonely night, every wrong that’s been done, and every person who ever harmed you.

God is a God of vengeance! God is a God of justice.

When the Israelites were mistreated in slavery and taken advantage of by the Egyptians, He spoke to them and said, “I’m coming down to make your wrongs right.”

God is coming to the horrible pit.

Keep watching!

He’s coming to pick you out Himself!

His hands will reach out and pull you up and out of that horrible pit.

“I’m coming down to pick you back up again.”

“I’m coming down to bring justice, to deal with the people who have done you wrong.”

God will deal with everyone who has done something wrong to you!

Do not worry about those who have abused you.

God will deal with them Himself.

You can never punish anyone as much as God can.

God is a God of justice and fairness.

He will ensure that everybody gets their due!

6\. God knows your sorrows. God knows the things that have made you sad. Get ready, God is doing everything to correct the wrongs done to you when they put you into the horrible pit.

And the Lord said, I HAVE SURELY SEEN THE AFFLICTION OF MY PEOPLE WHICH ARE IN EGYPT, and have heard their cry by reason of their taskmasters; for I know their sorrows;

Exodus 3:7

And I will stretch out my hand, and smite Egypt with all my wonders which I will do in the midst thereof: and after that he will let you go. And I will give this people favour in the sight of the Egyptians: and it shall come to pass, that, when ye go, ye shall not go empty: But every woman shall borrow of her neighbour, and of her that sojourneth in her house, jewels of silver, and jewels of gold, and raiment: and ye shall put them upon your sons, and upon your daughters; and ye shall spoil the Egyptians.

Exodus 3:20-22

When God sees how you have been mistreated, He is moved with compassion.

God does not just say, “It’s too bad that you have had a negative experience.”

God moves into action. His hands can reach out to where you are.

God is saying, “I have to get down there to do something about this.” God moves into action with His angels.

God will pay back what you have lost with interest and with value.

When He sees you being mistreated, when He sees that injustice, He doesn’t just sit back and say, “Too bad.”

He says, “That’s My son, that’s My daughter, My most prized possession.”

“My daughter is in a pit, and now I have to get down there to do something about it.”

“My son is lying on his face! My dear son is down and out. I have to rush there to help him.”

When God goes to work, even the demons of hell cannot stop Him.

Satan panics when God starts moving. I see and feel God moving to rescue you!

He will make your wrongs right!

He will pay you back for the trouble!

Your shame will be forgotten by most people!

Those who remember your shame will be gone forever.

God will get you to where you are supposed to be.
