### Chapter 9\

When I Sit in Darkness the Lord Shall Be a Light Unto Me\
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Rejoice not against me, O mine enemy: when I fall, I shall arise; when I sit in darkness, the Lord shall be a light unto me.

Micah 7:8

1\. When I sit in darkness, the Lord shall be a light unto me.

Rejoice not against me, O mine enemy: when I fall, I shall arise; when I sit in darkness, the Lord shall be a light unto me.

Micah 7:8

When I sit in darkness, the Lord shall be a light unto me. God knows everyone who is sitting in darkness today. God can see you sitting alone in the darkness. God sees you sitting alone and crying all day long.

I have news for you! The darkness in your life is not the end of everything but rather a mysterious sign of a new beginning.

The death of your spouse may be the start of a season of darkness in your life.

Your divorce may be the start of a season of darkness in your life.

A sickness may be the start of a season of darkness in your life.

Loneliness, disappointment, failure and loss may be a season of darkness that has descended upon you.

Amazingly, you are going to come out of the darkness much better, much stronger and bearing much fruit. The darkness makes you feel as though you are dying but out of that dark season, the power of God is going to bring new life, new opportunities, new growth and a new day. When I sit in darkness, the Lord shall be a light unto me!

2\. Darkness has power.

And one of them smote the servant of the high priest, and cut off his right ear. And Jesus answered and said, Suffer ye thus far. And he touched his ear, and healed him. Then Jesus said unto the chief priests, and captains of the temple, and the elders, which were come to him, Be ye come out, as against a thief, with swords and staves? When I was daily with you in the temple, ye stretched forth no hands against me: BUT THIS IS YOUR HOUR, AND THE POWER OF DARKNESS. Then took they him, and led him, and brought him into the high priest’s house. And Peter followed afar off.

Luke 22:50-54

Darkness has power! Jesus spoke of the power of darkness. When darkness descends, it brings about certain things. Darkness engineers certain things in your soul, your spirit, your mind and your body. Darkness is a season that comes upon a person.

Who hath delivered us from the power of darkness, and hath translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son:

Colossians 1:13

God will deliver you from the power of darkness.

Whatever represents the power of darkness in your life is broken forever!

The Bible speaks of being transferred from the power of darkness. You are delivered from the rulers of darkness!

When I sit in darkness, the Lord shall be a light unto me! Jesus came to save us when we were sitting in darkness.

3\. The darkness comes to everyone.

For, behold, the darkness shall cover the earth, and gross darkness the people: but the LORD shall arise upon thee, and his glory shall be seen upon thee.

Isaiah 60:2

If you are part of humanity, you will experience darkness one day.

Elijah’s season of darkness came when he was depressed and when he ran away from Jezebel.

David’s season of darkness came when he had an affair with Bathsheba and murdered her husband. A dark shadow descended over David’s life because of his sin.

Joseph experienced a season of darkness when his brothers rejected him and sold him into slavery and imprisonment.

Esther began a season of darkness when she became an orphan.

But always remember; “When I sit in darkness, the Lord shall be a light unto me!”

3\. In the darkness, there will be a sign of a new beginning.

We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place, until the day dawn, and THE DAY STAR arise in your hearts:

2 Peter 1:19

Expect to see the day star.

In the darkness, you must watch out for the sign of the coming light. A light is beginning to shine towards you. That is the day star or the morning star.

The morning star is the star that appears alone in the sky, heralding the dawn. It is a clear sign of the coming of the morning.

Keep your eyes open. God is showing you a sign that things are about to change forever.

The black darkness which dominated your life will be gone soon, and another day will begin.

Expect it! That’s how God works!

He permits darkness, but He gives hope and a sign of the coming light and brightness!

Look for it because it is on the way!

Perhaps the sign is a new friend you have made.

Perhaps the sign of the day is a new level of spirituality you have developed out of your crises.

Perhaps the sign of a new day is the presence of some unexpected financial relief.

Expect a sign. God is in the business of giving daystars to herald a bright new season.

4\. Darkness will lead to fruitfulness in your life.

Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.

John 12:24

Unless a seed falls into the ground, it will abide alone and it will not bear fruit. Before you bear fruit, you will fall into the ground and go through a season of darkness.

Much fruit is going to come through your season of darkness. You will emerge fruitful and full of abundance!

No one can prevent the darkness from coming. A dark season comes to everyone.

A dark season is a time of difficulty. At night, when it is dark, no one can see properly, and no one understands what is happening.

In the darkness, evil seems to flourish! The dark season of your life is when evil seems to abound and take over.

Amazingly, it is in this dark season that the seed will die and begin to bear much fruit. When a seed falls into the ground, it experiences the darkness of the earth and the pressure and heat of the earth. It is this very season of darkness that begins the process of fruitfulness in your life. When I sit in darkness, the Lord shall be a light unto me!

5\. Enlargement takes place in the darkness.

Hear me when I call, O God of my righteousness: THOU HAST ENLARGED ME WHEN I WAS IN DISTRESS; have mercy upon me, and hear my prayer.

Psalm 4:1

Enlargement happens in the dark season of life. As the Psalmist said, you enlarged me when I was in distress. Enlargement did not come when everything was okay. The Psalmist didn’t get enlarged in the good times; he was enlarged when things weren’t going his way. Enlargement came in the time of distress. This is why God sometimes leads us straight into the darkness so that He can expand us and enlarge us to become what He wants us to be.

He hath led me, and brought me into darkness, but not into light.

Lamentations 3:2

When I sit in darkness, the Lord shall be a light unto me!

6\. Hardening and strengthening with an iron heart happens in the dark seasons.

They have afflicted with fetters his feet, IRON HATH ENTERED HIS SOUL,

Psalm 105: 18 (YLT)

What an amazing scripture! Iron entering the soul of a man!

Iron stiffened and hardened the soul of Joseph! He was turned from a softie and a “daddy’s pet” into a hardened leader! The dark seasons of Joseph’s life as family reject, as a slave and as a prisoner hardened his soul.

Iron entered into the soul of Joseph and made him a hardened leader, capable of leading a great nation like Egypt.

It was during his dark years of imprisonment, slavery and injustice that iron entered his soul.

7\. After the darkness you will see a table prepared for you.

Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff they comfort me. Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over. Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever.

Psalm 23:4-6

A prepared table always comes after the darkness. If you can trust God for good experiences in the green pastures, then you are going to have to trust Him to bring you through the dark valley of the shadow of death. After the valley of the shadow of death, you can expect to find a table made ready. You must trust God with the dark seasons. Expect Him to bring you through beautifully until you see the table He has prepared for you.

8\. The darkness will break you, and God will notice it.

The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O God, thou wilt not despise.

Psalm 51:17

God is touched when He sees the brokenness that has happened to you because of the season of darkness.

God will not refuse the sacrifice of a broken spirit. The sacrifice that God likes is a broken spirit.

You may be in a dark season after a breakup of your relationship. Perhaps you are lonely, unhappy and disillusioned. Perhaps you are broken and disappointed. But I tell you, if you keep serving the Lord and moving forward with a cheerful attitude, the darkness will be dispelled, and a new season of fruitfulness will emerge.

Keep moving forward!

Keep serving the Lord!

When I sit in darkness, the Lord shall be a light unto me!
