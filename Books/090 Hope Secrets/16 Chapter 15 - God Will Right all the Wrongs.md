### Chapter 15\

God Will Right all the Wrongs\
---------------------------------------------------

Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath: for it is written, Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord.

Romans 12:19

To bear much fruit, you must suffer many things, including injustice. Every seed must be pushed into heat and darkness without oxygen.

1\. GOD WILL TAKE REVENGE HIMSELF; HE WILL RIGHT ALL THE WRONGS.

THOU SHALT NOT AVENGE, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, BUT THOU SHALT LOVE thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

Leviticus 19:18

God wants you to develop yourself in the love of God. God wants you to learn how to forgive and walk in love. Your duty is to love. God will take care of the revenge part. Many Christians do not understand this division of labour. Your part of the work is to love. God has assigned the “revenge” part of the work to Himself. Unfortunately, many Christians leave the love part and get themselves involved in the “revenge” part.

Every time you get involved in revenge, God is forced to pull out. Leave everything to God! God will avenge you for all the injustices and wicked things that have been done to you.

Perhaps you have been abused and used by boys. Perhaps you have been made a fool of. Perhaps you were downgraded, defiled and humiliated when you did not know what was happening to you. Perhaps there are boys who meet and have a good laugh when they remember the things they did to you and the things they made you do. To them, it was just a fleeting moment of ecstasy as they defrocked you and degraded you. But God saw everything and He has a plan to right every wrong. God is a God who rights the wrongs that have been done to people. Perhaps you want to rise up and strike at the one who humiliated you most. Do not bother to react against a man. Leave him to God!

God will right all the wrongs! God will visit the people one by one. God has a sheet of paper with the details of everything that has happened. Rest assured! It is just a matter of time! God will soon remind everyone that He is a restorer, a healer, and also a judge! God will right all the wrongs!

2\. GOD SEES ALL THE INSULTS YOU HAVE ENDURED; HE WILL RIGHT ALL THE WRONGS.

For we know the one who said, “I WILL TAKE REVENGE. I WILL PAY THEM BACK.” He also said, “The Lord will judge his own people.” It is a terrible thing to fall into the hands of the living God. Think back on those early days when you first learned about Christ. Remember how you remained faithful even though it meant terrible suffering. SOMETIMES YOU WERE EXPOSED TO PUBLIC RIDICULE AND WERE BEATEN, and sometimes you helped others who were suffering the same things. You suffered along with those who were thrown into jail, and when all you owned was taken from you, you accepted it with joy. You knew there were better things waiting for you that will last forever. SO DO NOT THROW AWAY THIS CONFIDENT TRUST IN THE LORD. REMEMBER THE GREAT REWARD IT BRINGS YOU! Patient endurance is what you need now, so that you will continue to do God’s will. Then you will receive all that he has promised.

Hebrews 10:30-36 (NLT)

God plans to pay back everyone who has harmed you. All you have to do is to maintain your faith, trust and confidence in God. God is sending a message to you right now. He says that He will revenge. He will pay them back! He will fight for you, and He will lift up your head in the land. Come next week, you will be smiling in this city and speaking of the confidence you have in God.

3\. GOD WILL RESTORE EVERYTHING; HE WILL RIGHT ALL THE WRONGS.

AND I WILL RESTORE TO YOU THE YEARS THAT THE LOCUST HATH EATEN, the cankerworm, and the caterpiller, and the palmerworm, my great army which I sent among you.

Joel 2:25

God is going to right all the wrong things that have been done to you. God is going to restore all you have lost. I know you think that you can never get back what you have lost. Perhaps you feel like Humpty Dumpty, who sat on a wall and had a great fall; and all the king’s horses and all the king’s men could not put Humpty Dumpty together again\*. But God is greater than all the king’s horses and all the king’s men who could not put Humpty Dumpty together again. God has special powers of restoration and redemption. That is why He is called the Redeemer. Jesus is the real Saviour! He is the Restorer. He is the Redeemer.

I once heard of someone who called himself the saviour and the redeemer of his nation. However, there is only one Saviour and one Redeemer! Ultimately, this human saviour was not even able to save himself.

I have good news for you! God is your Saviour and your Restorer! God will use His special redemptive and restorative powers on your case. He will turn everything around for your good. God will right all the wrongs that have been done to you! God is just about to move on all your issues for good. May the Lord remember you for good!

\*A popular nursery rhyme

4\. GOD WILL COMPENSATE YOU; HE WILL RIGHT ALL THE WRONGS.

If a man shall steal an ox, or a sheep, and kill it, or sell it; he shall restore five oxen for an ox, and four sheep for a sheep.

Exodus 22:1

Because you got a double dose of trouble and more than your share of contempt, YOUR INHERITANCE IN THE LAND WILL BE DOUBLED AND YOUR JOY GO ON FOREVER.

Isaiah 61:7 (MSG)

God is actually going to compensate you! In addition to restoring your losses, God will compensate you.

God’s nature is to restore. That means that apart from restoring your losses, He will give you something extra to comfort and console you. Keep watching as God compensates you! The principle of compensation is from the law and the prophets.

5\. GOD SEES ALL THE MOCKERS IN YOUR LIFE; HE WILL RIGHT ALL THE WRONGS.

And it came to pass in the evening, that he took Leah his daughter, and brought her to him; and he went in unto her. And Laban gave unto his daughter Leah Zilpah his maid for an handmaid. And it came to pass, that in the morning, behold, it was Leah: and he said to Laban, WHAT IS THIS THOU HAST DONE UNTO ME? DID NOT I SERVE WITH THEE FOR RACHEL? wherefore then hast thou beguiled me? And Laban said, It must not be so done in our country, to give the younger before the firstborn. Fulfil her week, and we will give thee this also for the service which thou shalt serve with me yet seven other years.

Genesis 29:23-27

AND WHEN THE LORD SAW THAT LEAH WAS HATED, HE OPENED HER WOMB: but Rachel was barren. And Leah conceived, and bare a son, and she called his name Reuben: for she said, Surely the Lord hath looked upon my affliction; now therefore my husband will love me. And she conceived again, and bare a son; and said, Because the Lord hath heard that I was hated, he hath therefore given me this son also: and she called his name Simeon.

Genesis 29:31-33

God reacted to those who mocked Leah. God will react to your mockers.

Are people laughing at you today? God sees it all, and God will react to all of them. God will right all the wrongs that have been done to you.

God is a God of justice, and He will right all the wrongs. God is going to deal with all those who have mocked you. All your mockers will be ashamed.

Are people making fun of you? Are they laughing at you because of something that you cannot even control? Are you different from everyone else? Do you feel strange when you are in a group? Do not worry! Jesus knows exactly how you feel. God knows exactly why you are being mocked. He sees all the cackling and hooting by your mockers, and God does not like it when He hears the sound of that laughter.

God does not like it that you are being mocked.

He sees those who are mocking you.

God reacts to such things. I tell you, God understands why you are being mocked.

Leah was so embarrassed when Jacob found out that she was not Rachel. Jacob may have insulted her for allowing herself to be used as a false bride, knowing well that it was Rachel that he loved. I can imagine Jacob pacing up and down in the room with Leah sitting on the bed, crying, and afraid. She was a complete reject!

Leah must have felt so rejected at Jacob’s reaction to finding out she was not Rachel -she was rejected, unwanted and embarrassed. But God noticed how Leah was rejected and mocked through no fault of hers.

God sees all the mockers and despisers of your life, and He will right the wrongs! God gave Leah children, whilst Rachel was unable to have a child. God opened Leah’s womb because He did not like the way she was being treated. God does not like it when people mock you for things that are no fault of yours.

6\. GOD SEES YOU CRYING; HE WILL RIGHT ALL THE WRONGS.

And it came to pass in process of time, that the king of Egypt died: and the children of Israel sighed by reason of the bondage, AND THEY CRIED, AND THEIR CRY CAME UP UNTO GOD BY REASON OF THE BONDAGE. And God heard their groaning, and God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob. And God looked upon the children of Israel, and God had respect unto them.

Exodus 2:23-25

God sees all the injustices in your life. God sees all the unfair things that have happened. God has seen all the things that have happened to make you cry so much. God is going to deal with all those who have made you cry.

God saw how the Israelites were being treated. He knew that it was not right for them to be kept in bondage and made to work like slaves. God knew that they had not done anything bad to Egypt to deserve such treatment. God was not happy that His children were suffering so much. God is provoked when He sees you struggling and suffering under a bad and wicked leader. God is not happy that you are living under oppressive leadership. God is going to right all the wrongs!

God sees all the injustices that have been done to you in secret. No one sees it as much as God sees it. God will correct it. God will change it! God will make something good come out of everything bad that is happening. This is what happened to the Israelites. Because of their sufferings in Egypt, God decided to give them a brand-new land flowing with milk and honey. It is because of your hardships and difficulties that the new promised land will be uncovered. Get ready to experience the promised land! God is going to right all the wrongs! You will not believe how God will change your circumstances. From a land flowing with rivers of water, he is going to give you a land flowing with milk and honey! Imagine that!

God sees you suffering in Egypt. He sees the River Nile which you are working by, and has decided to change your river from a river of water to a river of milk and honey. God has really good thoughts for you! He really seeks to change your life and right all the wrongs.

Expect good things! Expect great things! Expect God to turn things around. God is great! God is “wild”! God sees all the injustices in your life. God sees all the things that have made you cry and weep and wail. God sees all the unfair and unfortunate experiences that you have had. God will correct everything for your sake.

7\. GOD SEES ALL THE INJUSTICES; HE WILL RIGHT ALL THE WRONGS.

Saying, There was in a city a judge, which feared not God, neither regarded man: And there was a widow in that city; and she came unto him, saying, Avenge me of mine adversary. And he would not for a while: but afterward he said within himself, Though I fear not God, nor regard man; Yet because this widow troubleth me, I will avenge her, lest by her continual coming she weary me. And the Lord said, Hear what the unjust judge saith. And shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him, though he bear long with them?

Luke 18:2-7

God knows what is happening to you because you are as helpless as a widow. God plans to correct them like a judge who brings justice. God knows how helpless you are. God sees that you are just like a widow. You may not be called a widow but you are just like a widow because you do not have anyone to help you. It is not right that you should be alone. God is going to right all the wrongs! Perhaps you were supposed to marry someone, but they ended up not marrying you. Perhaps you were abandoned by your lover and now you are all alone, just like a widow. God sees the wrong things that have been done to you. God knows how you feel and He has decided to right all the wrongs.

God is like a judge who avenges the weak and the vulnerable. God can see that you cannot do much for yourself. Perhaps you are sick and weak, and there is no one to help you. You must trust God to right all the wrongs. Perhaps you never smoked a cigarette, unlike your friend smoked many cigarettes, but now you have cancer of the lungs. Why did you get this cancer? What did you do to deserve it? God sees all the wrongs that have happened and He is going to right all the wrongs.

8\. GOD IS NOTING ALL THE DETAILS OF THE EVILS DONE AGAINST YOU; HE WILL RIGHT ALL THE WRONGS.

And I heard a great voice out of the temple saying to the seven angels, Go your ways, and pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth. And the first went, and poured out his vial upon the earth; and there fell a noisome and grievous sore upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image. And the second angel poured out his vial upon the sea; and it became as the blood of a dead man: and every living soul died in the sea. And the third angel poured out his vial upon the rivers and fountains of waters; and they became blood. And I heard the angel of the waters say, Thou art righteous, O Lord, which art, and wast, and shalt be, because thou hast judged thus. FOR THEY HAVE SHED THE BLOOD OF SAINTS AND PROPHETS, AND THOU HAST GIVEN THEM BLOOD TO DRINK; FOR THEY ARE WORTHY.

Revelation 16:1-6

God is noting the details of the wickedness that has been done against you and plans to pay back the evil done to you in exactly the same way it was done to you. Notice how God gives the people blood to drink because they shed the saints’ blood. God punishes people according to exactly what they have done. God will right all the wrongs done against you. Do not be worried about whether God can see everything. God knows more details than you do.

Perhaps you kept yourself a virgin whilst your friends gave themselves to many boys. Now all your friends have children, but you do not. God can see what has happened, and He intends to right all the wrongs.

Perhaps your boyfriend made you abort all your pregnancies, and today you are barren. Perhaps you are crying because your womb has been damaged through multiple abortions that your boyfriends forced you to do. God is going to right all the wrongs. God knows the details. God knows who is right and who is wrong. God is going to correct everything that is putting you in a bad light.

God loves you very much. He knows the shame that you have endured, and He will arise suddenly and correct the wrong impressions that have been made about you. God is really powerful! God is going to change the picture. You will no longer be associated with failure and embarrassment. Whatever represents shame is being taken away from you right now!

9\. GOD HAS SET A TIME TO PUNISH AND AVENGE THE WRONGS DONE; GOD WILL RIGHT ALL THE WRONGS.

Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath: for it is written, Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord.

Romans 12:19

My friends, don’t try to punish anyone who does wrong to you. Wait for God to punish them with his anger. In the Scriptures the Lord says, “I AM THE ONE WHO PUNISHES; I will pay people back.”

Deuteronomy 32:35 (ERV)

Beloved, never avenge yourselves, but leave the way open for God’s wrath \[and His judicial righteousness\]; for it is written \[in Scripture\], “VENGEANCE IS MINE, I WILL REPAY,” says the Lord.

Deuteronomy 32:35 (AMP)

Dear friends, don’t try to get even. LET GOD TAKE REVENGE. In the Scriptures the Lord says, “I am the one to take revenge and pay them back.”

Deuteronomy 32:35 (CEV)

Do not be confused about God’s timing. Do not even think that God is delaying anything. Do not interfere with God’s revenge plan. Give place, chance and opportunity for the wrath of God.

10\. GOD ALLOWS PEOPLE TO FULFIL SCRIPTURES SO THAT HE CAN JUDGE THEM; GOD WILL RIGHT ALL THE WRONGS.

“If anyone sins against someone else, God can defend the one who is wrong; but who can defend someone who sins against the LORD?” BUT THEY WOULD NOT LISTEN TO THEIR FATHER, FOR THE LORD HAD DECIDED TO KILL THEM.

1 Samuel 2:25 (GNB)

God allows people to fulfil scriptures, and then He judges them. The sons of Eli were destined for death and judgment. God had decided to destroy them, and that is actually why they would not listen to their father nor honour him.

Do not be sad when people do not honour God or honour you. Do not be sad when your sons do not do the right thing. There are sons who dishonour fathers and are destined for judgment. It is not because you are not a good father that your sons are not honouring God or honouring you. It is not because you are not a good mother that your sons are not honouring God or honouring you. Perhaps it is because God wants to judge them. God needs a legal basis to act against them. God will right all the wrongs. At the right time, God moved on Eli’s sons and dealt with them.

Now Eli was very old, and heard all that his sons did unto all Israel; and how they lay with the women that assembled at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation. And he said unto them, Why do ye such things? for I hear of your evil dealings by all this people. Nay, my sons; for it is no good report that I hear: ye make the Lord’s people to transgress. If one man sin against another, the judge shall judge him: but if a man sin against the Lord, who shall intreat for him? Notwithstanding they hearkened not unto the voice of their father, because the Lord would slay them.

1 Samuel 2:22-25

11\. GOD WILL ALLOW THE CUP OF EVIL TO BE FULL BEFORE HE JUDGES PEOPLE.

And he said unto Abram, Know of a surety that thy seed shall be a stranger in a land that is not theirs, and shall serve them; and they shall afflict them four hundred years; And also that nation, whom they shall serve, will I judge: and afterward shall they come out with great substance. And thou shalt go to thy fathers in peace; thou shalt be buried in a good old age. But in the fourth generation they shall come hither again: FOR THE INIQUITY OF THE AMORITES IS NOT YET FULL.

Genesis 15:13-16

God will definitely right all the wrongs. People have to be guilty enough before they are judged. God is going to wait for the iniquity of the Amorites to be full. Sometimes, a person may have committed a crime but it is not a big enough crime to send the person to jail. This is why God holds on with His judgment. He is waiting for the crimes and sins of your enemy to be up to the level where He can destroy them utterly. God is a God of justice, and you will learn to appreciate that. He will not do anything bad to your enemy if your enemy does not deserve it. That is why He waited so long for the Amorites to commit sins up to a certain level before their judgment.

Your children’s grandchildren will come back here. That is because THE SIN OF THE AMORITES HAS NOT YET REACHED THE POINT WHERE I MUST JUDGE THEM.

Genesis 15:16 (NIrV)

After four generations your people will come to this land again and defeat the Amorites. That will happen in the future BECAUSE THE AMORITES ARE NOT YET GUILTY ENOUGH to lose their land.

Genesis 15:16 (ERV)

12\. GOD ALLOWS PEOPLE TO BE STUBBORN, SO HE CAN JUDGE THEM; GOD WILL RIGHT ALL THE WRONGS.

Joshua made war a long time with all those kings. There was not a city that made peace with the children of Israel, save the Hivites the inhabitants of Gibeon: all other they took in battle. FOR IT WAS OF THE LORD TO HARDEN THEIR HEARTS, THAT THEY SHOULD COME AGAINST ISRAEL IN BATTLE, THAT HE MIGHT DESTROY THEM UTTERLY, AND THAT THEY MIGHT HAVE NO FAVOUR, BUT THAT HE MIGHT DESTROY THEM, as the Lord commanded Moses. And at that time came Joshua, and cut off the Anakims from the mountains, from Hebron, from Debir, from Anab, and from all the mountains of Judah, and from all the mountains of Israel: Joshua destroyed them utterly with their cities. There was none of the Anakims left in the land of the children of Israel: only in Gaza, in Gath, and in Ashdod, there remained. So Joshua took the whole land, according to all that the Lord said unto Moses; and Joshua gave it for an inheritance unto Israel according to their divisions by their tribes. And the land rested from war.

Joshua 11:18-23

THE LORD HAD MADE THEM DETERMINED TO FIGHT THE ISRAELITES, SO THAT THEY WOULD BE CONDEMNED TO TOTAL DESTRUCTION AND ALL BE KILLED WITHOUT MERCY. This was what the LORD had commanded Moses.

Joshua 11:20 (GNT)

THE LORD MADE THEIR ENEMIES STUBBORN enough to continue fighting against Israel so that he could claim them all for destruction without mercy, as he had commanded Moses.

Joshua 11:20 (GW)

Never think that God does not know what is being done against you. God listens to every conversation. God knows those who hate you so much. God does not like the people who hate you. God is not happy with the people who persecute you. The time is coming when He will arise and destroy your enemies.

God has decided to destroy your enemies, and so He has caused them to harden their hearts. God needs a reason to destroy your enemies. God cannot just rise and destroy people. There must be some good reason for Him to act. This is why your enemies have hardened their hearts. The reason why the fight is not over is because God wants them to be destroyed in the battle. I predict that your enemies will harden their hearts until the time of their judgment.

The prolonging of the war and the conflict is not because you are too weak to overcome the enemy. You are stronger than your enemy, but God does not want the conflict to end just yet. He wants it to carry on so that He can count the months and years they have caused trouble for you and use it as a basis to destroy them. Do not worry at all! God is going to right all the wrongs in your life. Keep watching!
