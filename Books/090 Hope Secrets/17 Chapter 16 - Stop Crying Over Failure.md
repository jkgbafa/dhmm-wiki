### Chapter 16\

Stop Crying Over Failure\
----------------------------------------------

Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.

John 12:24

A seed will not bear fruit as long as it is sitting on a shelf in a shop somewhere. This is why a seed must fall into the ground and be allowed to die, far away and out of view. To bear much fruit, the seed must be pushed out and buried deep into the ground.

1\. Stop crying over a failure like Saul.

And the Lord said unto Samuel, HOW LONG WILT THOU MOURN FOR SAUL, seeing I have rejected him from reigning over Israel? fill thine horn with oil, and go, I will send thee to Jesse the Bethlehemite: for I have provided me a king among his sons.

1 Samuel 16:1

Stop crying over a failure like Saul.

God will remove Saul to bring in David.

Stop crying over someone like Saul who left his calling.

Stop crying over someone like Saul who has wasted your time.

Stop complaining about people who have wasted your love and investment.

Stop crying over the disappointments.

God is using it to bring about something newer and better.

Fruit is coming!

The chosen one is coming!

God has something greater and better for the future.

You are about to step up to a higher level.

People with greater talents are about to walk into your life.

God is showing you new possibilities. You knew about Saul, but there are people like David who are equally important for your life.

Do not complain!

God is stretching you! God is enlarging you! You are about to see new people, new children, new growth, new opportunities and new levels of happiness.

2\. God is pushing you out of the nest like an eagle.

AS AN EAGLE STIRRETH UP HER NEST, FLUTTERETH OVER HER YOUNG, spreadeth abroad her wings, taketh them, beareth them on her wings: So the Lord alone did lead him, and there was no strange god with him. He made him ride on the high places of the earth, that he might eat the increase of the fields; and he made him to suck honey out of the rock, and oil out of the flinty rock;

Deuteronomy 32:11-13

The higher qualities of an eagle are going to come forth when you are stirred up in the nest and made to fly up to higher grounds. God is stirring you up and out of the nest!

By pushing you out of your resting place, God is releasing in you the ability to fly, to soar, to move faster and to have supernatural agility and mobility. You will never develop these abilities until God pushes you out into the frightening new experiences you are now having!

Not every closed door is a bad thing.

When someone walks away from your life, it is not necessarily a tragedy. Sometimes God is pruning your life so that you can bear much fruit. God is pushing you on, and He is taking you to a better place.

Indeed, you cannot succeed with some of the people you are with now. When someone walks away from you, it is not necessarily a bad thing. Perhaps it is intended to make you stronger. The problems you have experienced are to push you forward and to mature you.

3\. God will use your crisis to give you more.

And the Lord turned the captivity of Job, when he prayed for his friends: also the Lord gave Job twice as much as he had before. Then came there unto him all his brethren, and all his sisters, and all they that had been of his acquaintance before, and did eat bread with him in his house: and they bemoaned him, and comforted him over all the evil that the Lord had brought upon him: every man also gave him a piece of money, and every one an earring of gold. So THE LORD BLESSED THE LATTER END OF JOB MORE THAN HIS BEGINNING: FOR HE HAD FOURTEEN THOUSAND SHEEP, AND SIX THOUSAND CAMELS, AND A THOUSAND YOKE OF OXEN, AND A THOUSAND SHE ASSES. HE HAD ALSO SEVEN SONS AND THREE DAUGHTERS. AND HE CALLED THE NAME OF THE FIRST, JEMIMA; AND THE NAME OF THE SECOND, KEZIA; AND THE NAME OF THE THIRD, KEREN-HAPPUCH. And in all the land were no women found so fair as the daughters of Job: and their father gave them inheritance among their brethren. After this lived Job an hundred and forty years, and saw his sons, and his sons’ sons, even four generations. So Job died, being old and full of days.

Job 42:10-17

God will give you much more than you have now. If you can overcome the sorrow and shame and still have a good attitude, many blessings will come to you.

Instead of crying over spilt milk, have faith in God. Believe that whatever has happened has not happened without God’s supernatural hand guiding everything.

When you face something you do not understand, remember that Job faced a crisis that he did not understand. A crisis only happens within the limits of what God allows. You must believe that God is using your current crisis to push you to a new level.

God used Job’s crisis to push him to a new level of wealth and influence. I prophesy to you that your problems are going to lead to greater and better things. Nothing is going to defeat you. You are actually standing on the stepping stone to a new level of glory.

4\. God will push you out of Jerusalem to bring you to Samaria.

As for Saul, he made havock of the church, entering into every house, and haling men and women committed them to prison. Therefore they that were scattered abroad went every where preaching the word. Then Philip went down to the city of Samaria, and preached Christ unto them. And the people with one accord gave heed unto those things which Philip spake, hearing and seeing the miracles which he did. For unclean spirits, crying with loud voice, came out of many that were possessed with them: and many taken with palsies, and that were lame, were healed.

Acts 8:3-7

God pushed Philip out of Jerusalem. There was a crisis in the church, and Philip was forced to flee. Perhaps circumstances have arisen in your life which are forcing you to move on. But there is another side to your crisis, just as there was another side to Philip’s crisis. Jerusalem may be closing down, but Samaria is opening up!

God was actually pushing Philip out of Jerusalem so that He could introduce him to Samaria. In Samaria, Philip was going to enter fully into the ministry of an evangelist. What happened in Samaria is the only recorded account of an evangelistic crusade in the Bible.

Jerusalem was closing down to Philip but Samaria was opening up with new chapters and dimensions for the ministry. Maybe you have been forced to leave Jerusalem because of some unfortunate happenings. I prophesy that the Samaria ministry of world-class evangelism will be opening up to you as the Jerusalem ministry of pastoral work is closing to you. Indeed, Jerusalem may be closing down, but Samaria is opening up!

5\. God will use the winds and storms to blow you into Malta.

But not long after there arose against it a tempestuous wind, called Euroclydon. And when the ship was caught, and could not bear up into the wind, we let her drive. And running under a certain island which is called Clauda, we had much work to come by the boat: But after long abstinence Paul stood forth in the midst of them, and said, Sirs, ye should have hearkened unto me, and not have loosed from Crete, and to have gained this harm and loss. And now I exhort you to be of good cheer: for there shall be no loss of any man’s life among you, but of the ship. For there stood by me this night the angel of God, whose I am, and whom I serve, Saying, Fear not, Paul; thou must be brought before Caesar: and, lo, God hath given thee all them that sail with thee.

Acts 27:14-16, 21-24

God will use winds and storms to blow you to your destiny just like He blew Paul to Malta. God will use the winds that were meant to harm you to push you into your destiny.

You may not understand the kind of storms and the kind of winds that are blowing in your life today.

It may be painful and miserable, but if you keep the right attitude, the storm will not defeat you.

Instead, the storm will be used to take you to the place of your destiny.

6\. God will use captivity to change your scent.

Moab hath been at ease from his youth, and he hath settled on his lees, and hath not been emptied from vessel to vessel, NEITHER HATH HE GONE INTO CAPTIVITY: THEREFORE HIS TASTE REMAINED IN HIM, AND HIS SCENT IS NOT CHANGED. Therefore, behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will send unto him wanderers, that shall cause him to wander, and shall empty his vessels, and break their bottles.

Jeremiah 48:11-12

When God changes your scent, He makes you more acceptable and more usable in the ministry.

Captivity is a restriction that is painful to endure. Some believe that life in prison is more difficult than being sentenced to death. I know someone who has been sentenced to a prison measuring seven by eleven feet for the rest of his existence on earth. People go mad in these places. Captivity is no small experience.

Many people go crazy in captivity and all they want to do is to escape. God will use captivity to change your very nature. The restrictions you have today are not going to destroy you! The restrictions are going to make you into a better person and they are going to change your very nature.
