### Chapter 13\

God Will Use Your Night Season\
----------------------------------------------------

The burden of Dumah. He calleth to me out of Seir, WATCHMAN, WHAT OF THE NIGHT? WATCHMAN, WHAT OF THE NIGHT? The watchman said, The morning cometh, and also the night: if ye will enquire, enquire ye: return, come.

Isaiah 21:11-12

The night season is a season of darkness. The darkness represents difficulty, trials, temptations and testing.

The night season is the season when the sun does not shine.

The night season is a season when there is not much light.

The night season is a season when you cannot find your way easily.

The night season is the season when there is much danger.

The night season is the season when there is evil everywhere.

The night season is a season of uncertainty. You are never sure of yourself in the night season.

The night season is a season you must go through if you are to bear fruit.

The night season is the season when you must pray hardest.

The night season is when the watchman must be alert and awake. The night season can be so long that you never know when it is going to end.

1\. To bear much fruit, the seed must fall into the ground and die.

Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.

John 12:24

To bear much fruit, the seed must be pushed deep into the darkness and further into the night. The seed must enter the night season in order to bear fruit. Night seasons are very important for the fulfilment of the will of God. You will definitely experience night seasons on your journey to bear much fruit.

2. God uses the night season to transform a caterpillar into a butterfly.

Who are these that fly as a cloud, and as the doves to their windows?

Isaiah 60:8

The night season of a caterpillar is a great revelation for you. A worm is transformed into a flying creature. Everyone wonders and asks, “Who are these that fly? How did they learn how to fly?” They learnt how to fly in the night season.

The night season is the season when the greatest transformation takes place.

When the caterpillar spins a cocoon around itself and hangs itself upside down from a branch, it has entered a great stage of transformation. When the caterpillar enters its cocoon, it has entered the season that will make all the difference. It is dark! The caterpillar cannot even move! It cannot even eat! But a great transformation is taking place in the dark. Soon, the wings of the caterpillar develop, and it pushes itself out of the dark cocoon. The caterpillar now has the strength and the capacity to fly around as a beautiful butterfly. All the beautiful butterflies you see floating in the air have come out of a night season.

Nobody likes the night seasons.

Nobody likes being restricted and uncomfortable.

Nobody likes the night season when nothing is happening.

Nobody likes the night season when you cannot even move.

Nobody likes the misery and the gloominess of the dark season.

But it is in the night season that the transformation in your heart takes place. You are being transformed from the dark places of your life.

Your wings are developing! You are going to a higher level! You are ending a life of crawling, creeping and sneaking around!

God has made you for higher things! Beginning from now, you are about to go places that you never dreamed were possible.

As you stay in the cocoon, do not complain.

Maintain a good victory attitude!

Have a cheerful, hopeful attitude!

Have a youthful attitude!

Believe that everything is possible!

Believe that the night season is going to lead you to a new level in your life!

You will be flying instead of crawling! Soon, people will be asking, “Who is this person who is flying so high?”

3\. Ruth had a night season when her husband died young. God used it to bring her to her new husband and to bring forth King David.

Ruth had a night season when she became a young widow. However, God used that night season to bring forth the great king, David.

The story of Ruth did not end in the night. Every night season is a temporary season.

The problem you are going through is temporary. The crisis you are going through is temporary.

Your broken relationship is temporary. Your loneliness is temporary.

This uncertain season is temporary! Your period of darkness is temporary!

When things are not working out, do not be discouraged. Do not be upset because it seems you have been going in the wrong direction for so long. It is all part of a temporary night season.

That is not how your story is going to end. No story ends in the night. That is why God allows every night to be followed by the day.

God is at work! Have faith in God! There is no rush!

God wants you to rest in between victories. Perhaps this night season is a rest period for you. You will be transformed more in this night season than in any other season in your life. Amen!

When I was in university, our school was shut down for an entire year. As students, we felt that our lives were being delayed. Medical school stretched on for seven years, instead of five or six. We just wanted things to hurry up so we could get out of school. But that one year of night season, when nothing seemed to be happening, turned out to be the best year of my school life.

That night season was when I was trained in the ministry on how to start a church. That is when I received training for church work. That is when I met the pastor who ordained me into the ministry. That is when I sowed many seeds for my life and ministry. That is when I formed a choir, played instruments, joined a band and learned how to work in a church like a normal Christian.

It was in that year that I went around distributing books by Kenneth Hagin to anyone that I could find. I had certain experiences in that year which have stayed with me for the rest of my ministry.

O, what a precious year of training that was for me! My life, my ministry and my church would have been different if I had not had that one year of “doing nothing”. My friend, be not afraid of the night season when nothing seems to be happening. It is the season when you will be transformed the most!

4\. Good people have dark, rainy seasons, and bad people have rainy seasons too.

That ye may be the children of your Father which is in heaven: for he maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good, and SENDETH RAIN ON THE JUST AND ON THE UNJUST.

Matthew 5:45

There are dark seasons of rain and storms that happen in every nation. The rainy season comes to everyone.

Perhaps, you are in a difficult time and going through a dark storm right now. It does not mean you have done something wrong. It does not mean that God is not pleased with you. It does not mean that you don’t have His favour.

It means that you are getting some rain. Without the rain, you cannot grow.

5\. Job had a night season. God used Job’s night season to double everything he had.

Like other Old Testament heroes of the faith, Job went through a night season. Job was a famous, wealthy, successful businessman. Everything had been going great until life took a sudden downward turn.

Out of nowhere, Job came down with a very painful illness. He developed a bacteraemia and a septicaemia with boils all over his body. Job lost his business. He lost his sons and his daughters, and his whole world was turned upside down.

Was Job a sinner? Had he committed a terrible sin that was leading to all these horrible harvests? No! Job was a good man. He served the Lord with all his heart, and he had shown much compassion to the poor and the needy. He summarised his own righteousness in the Book of Job Chapter 29.

God is showing you today that even though you have experienced something terrible, He is still with you and He is the lifter up of your head.

What happened to Job was not fair. He did not deserve any of the things that happened to him. You may also not have been treated fairly. Do not live your life being discouraged, overwhelmed and disillusioned by things that seem not to be fair.

I remember a brother whose wife died after a short illness. After the funeral, he had the opportunity to marry a nice lady friend who also liked him. Everyone waited for him to propose to the lady, but he never did until he himself developed a serious illness. The lady also developed a serious illness. He then decided to propose to the lady, but it was too late. They were both too ill to marry and cohabitate. It had taken him several years to accept the loss of his wife and accept that he needed to move on with his life.

God wants to fill your mouth with laughter. God wants to do something unusual for you. God wants to turn your mourning into dancing. After the night season, joy will come in the morning.

Do not resist God in your night season when He is bringing you twice as much as you had before. This is what happened with Job. He not only made it through the night season, but God restored double of what he had lost. He came out with twice the number of oxen, sheep, camels, and donkeys.

God always makes the enemy pay for bringing you trouble. If you walk by faith, you will not just come out of this trouble by the skin of your teeth; you will come out better than you were before!

The Scripture says, “Job lived 140 years after that, living to see four generations of his children and grandchildren. Then he died, an old man who had lived a long, full life” (Job 42:16-17 (NLT)).

6\. God uses the night season to prepare for the arrival of the light.

UNTO THE UPRIGHT THERE ARISETH LIGHT IN THE DARKNESS: he is gracious, and full of compassion, and righteous.

Psalm 112:4

When darkness overtakes him, light will come bursting in. He is kind and merciful.

Psalm 112:4 (TLB)

God has promised that light will come to you in your darkness. Light will come bursting to you in the darkness! Light always speaks of revelation, knowledge and understanding. Expect to have waves of new knowledge, new understanding, new truths and new information in your night season. Beginning from today, light will come bursting in.

7\. God used the night season of Moses’ life to make him the humblest man on earth.

(Now Moses was a very humble man, more humble than anyone else on the face of the earth.)

Numbers 12:3 (NIV)

In the night season, the light of humility will come rushing in. Moses made the mistake of murdering someone, and God made Moses the most humble man in the world after he made that mistake. The night season of Moses’ life and ministry began when he made the mistake of murdering someone.

God has already taken into account every mistake you will ever make. God is aware of every wrong turn, every mistake, every blunder and every slip of your life.

It may look like the end, but it is not the end. God is preparing you for your comeback! It was not the end for Moses! It was the beginning of a night season of testing, proving and searching.

God was preparing Moses to be the president. His mistakes were part of the preparation. Your mistakes are part of your preparation. God uses your mistakes to keep you permanently humble. If you had not made certain mistakes, you would have been destroyed long ago through pride.

God is preparing you for something great.

8\. God used the night seasons of David’s life to prepare him for the anointing.

And he said unto his men, The Lord forbid that I should do this thing unto my master, the Lord’s anointed, to stretch forth mine hand against him, seeing he is the anointed of the Lord. So David stayed his servants with these words, and suffered them not to rise against Saul. But Saul rose up out of the cave, and went on his way.

1 Samuel 24:6-7

David was a fugitive in the wilderness. He spent many months running away from Saul.

David had many opportunities to attack and kill Saul but he knew that it was not the right thing to do.

When you are in your dark season, do not make even more mistakes. Do not compound the crisis you are in by making even more missteps and false steps.

The night season is to make you come out ready for the anointing.

When David passed these tests, he received the anointing to be the king. God sorted out David’s problem with Saul, and David went on to lead Israel without any restraint. Allow the night seasons to work in you all that God has planned.

9\. God used the night season of Jacob to visit him and bless him.

And he lighted upon a certain place, and TARRIED THERE ALL NIGHT, because the sun was set; and he took of the stones of that place, and put them for his pillows, and lay down in that place to sleep. And he dreamed, and behold a ladder set up on the earth, and the top of it reached to heaven: and behold the angels of God ascending and descending on it. And, behold, the Lord stood above it, and said, I am the Lord God of Abraham thy father, and the God of Isaac: the land whereon thou liest, to thee will I give it, and to thy seed; And thy seed shall be as the dust of the earth, and thou shalt spread abroad to the west, and to the east, and to the north, and to the south: and in thee and in thy seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed.

Genesis 28:11-14

Jacob is another person who benefited from the night season. Jacob was someone who cheated people and even tricked his brother out of his inheritance.

One would expect God to avoid having anything to do with such a person. But God does not write anybody off! He keeps showing mercy and kindness until the very end.

It was in a night season that this wicked, cheating personality was transformed. One lonely night, when Jacob lay down to rest, he considered his life with regret. He had cheated people! He had changed people’s lives with his actions! There were people out there who hated him because of what he had done to them. His pillow was a hard rock.

Indeed, Jacob was in a hard place and in a night season; and that was when God spoke to him. That was when Jacob saw angels ascending and descending. It was in the night that he heard the promise of God about the land which had been promised to Abraham, his grandfather.

I have good news for you! The night season of your life will lead to your breakthrough. The night season of your life will lead you to the promises of God and to angelic interventions.

10\. God put Paul and Silas through a night season and brought them out famously. We all remember the famous midnight deliverance of Paul and Silas.

AND AT MIDNIGHT PAUL AND SILAS PRAYED, AND SANG PRAISES UNTO GOD: AND THE PRISONERS HEARD THEM. And suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken: and immediately all the doors were opened, and every one’s bands were loosed. And the keeper of the prison awaking out of his sleep, and seeing the prison doors open, he drew out his sword, and would have killed himself, supposing that the prisoners had been fled. But Paul cried with a loud voice, saying, Do thyself no harm: for we are all here. Then he called for a light, and sprang in, and came trembling, and fell down before Paul and Silas, And brought them out, and said, Sirs, what must I do to be saved?

Acts 16:25-30

Paul and Silas had been preaching the word of God. They had been holding crusades, breakfast meetings and conferences all over Asia. What was their reward for these good works?

They were beaten with rods, imprisoned and held underground in dungeons.

They were in danger of death, and they were chained like animals in a cage.

However, it was at midnight, whilst they were singing praises to God, that there was an earthquake and the prison doors opened. God rescued them in the night season.

It is important to keep a good attitude of praise and thanksgiving in your night seasons! God really does move in the night seasons!

11\. God put Adam through a night season so that He could change his life and give him love, sex, companionship and happiness.

And Adam gave names to all cattle, and to the fowl of the air, and to every beast of the field; but for Adam there was not found an help meet for him. And the Lord God caused a deep sleep to fall upon Adam, and he slept: and he took one of his ribs, and closed up the flesh instead thereof; And the rib, which the Lord God had taken from man, made he a woman, and brought her unto the man. And Adam said, This is now bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh: she shall be called Woman, because she was taken out of Man.

Genesis 2:20-23

Expect greater and better things! Adam was created to be the king of the earth. All the animals were his subjects. He enjoyed control over wild animals, no matter what they were. Elephants, lions and leopards were his pets. He spoke to them and they simply obeyed. Wild snakes would dance at his request, just as it happens in some countries today.

The Garden of Eden was simply fantastic. Life was good for Adam. The River of Life was an experience on its own. Boats went up and down it, and the scenes were simply breathtaking. Adam did not know of any better life.

One day, God decided to put Adam to sleep, and Adam entered into a night season.

When he woke up, he saw an amazing creature, the likes of which he had never known, standing before him. A luxurious, voluptuous, delightful woman! She seemed to exude comforts such as he had never experienced before. She seemed to be softer and more delicate than himself. He was so attracted to her!

He turned to God and said, “O Lord, you have outdone yourself. This is too much.”

God smiled at Adam and said, “This is why I took you through the night season. I put you through the night season so that I could introduce you to something greater and better.” Greater and better it was, indeed!

This is exactly what is happening to you. You are being taken through a night season so that God can introduce you to something greater and better.
