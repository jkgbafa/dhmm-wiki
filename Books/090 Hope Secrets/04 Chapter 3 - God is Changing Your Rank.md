### Chapter 3\

God is Changing Your Rank\
-----------------------------------------------

For our light affliction, which is but for a moment, worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory;

2 Corinthians 4:17

1\. There are various ranks in the realm of the spirit.

Blow ye the trumpet in Zion, and sound an alarm in my holy mountain: let all the inhabitants of the land tremble: for the day of the Lord cometh, for it is nigh at hand; A day of darkness and of gloominess, a day of clouds and of thick darkness, as the morning spread upon the mountains: a great people and a strong; there hath not been ever the like, neither shall be any more after it, even to the years of many generations. A fire devoureth before them; and behind them a flame burneth: the land is as the garden of Eden before them, and behind them a desolate wilderness; yea, and nothing shall escape them. The appearance of them is as the appearance of horses; and as horsemen, so shall they run. Like the noise of chariots on the tops of mountains shall they leap, like the noise of a flame of fire that devoureth the stubble, as a strong people set in battle array. Before their face the people shall be much pained: all faces shall gather blackness. THEY SHALL RUN LIKE MIGHTY MEN; THEY SHALL CLIMB THE WALL LIKE MEN OF WAR; AND THEY SHALL MARCH EVERY ONE ON HIS WAYS, AND THEY SHALL NOT BREAK THEIR RANKS:

Joel 2:1-7

A change of rank always comes after a test. That is why we are encouraged to pass our tests well. God is promoting you so that He can change your rank.

In the prophetic book of Joel, a spiritual army is described. A key characteristic of the spiritual army is their ability to run without breaking rank. God has given you a rank in the realm of the spirit and you will have to operate in the realm and rank you are given.

2\. God teaches us to war and to fight using the principles of keeping rank.

Of Zebulun, such as went forth to battle, expert in war, with all instruments of war, fifty thousand, which could keep rank: they were not of double heart. And of Naphtali a thousand captains, and with them with shield and spear thirty and seven thousand. And of the Danites expert in war twenty and eight thousand and six hundred. And of Asher, such as went forth to battle, expert in war, forty thousand. And on the other side of Jordan, of the Reubenites, and the Gadites, and of the half tribe of Manasseh, with all manner of instruments of war for the battle, an hundred and twenty thousand. ALL THESE MEN OF WAR, THAT COULD KEEP RANK, CAME WITH A PERFECT HEART TO HEBRON, to make David king over all Israel: and all the rest also of Israel were of one heart to make David king.

1 Chronicles 12:33-38

A change of rank always comes after a test. That is why you are encouraged to pass your tests well. God is testing you so He can promote you and change your rank.

In your war, you will need to learn how to keep rank. One of the important principles of belonging to an army is to keep rank.

3\. Evil spirits understand the principle of rank.

And he asked him, What is thy name? And he answered, saying, MY NAME IS LEGION: for we are many.

Mark 5:9

God wants you to be at a higher rank when you tackle the enemy. A change of rank always comes after a test. Do not run away from your test! When the seven sons of Sceva attempted to cast out devils, they had the shock of their lives. The demons knew the rank of the seven sons of Sceva. “And the evil spirit answered and said, Jesus I know, and Paul I know; but who are ye?” (Acts 19:15). Demons know those Christians who are at a higher rank and those on a lower rank.

When the demons answered Jesus, “MY NAME IS LEGION”, they were actually saying, “WE ARE A ROMAN REGIMENT.” (A legion was a body of soldiers whose number differed at different times. In the time of Augustus, it consisted of 6826 men, 6100-foot soldiers, and 726 horsemen.) The demons were saying “We are a military grouping! WE UNDERSTAND RANK AND WE KNOW WHO WE ARE TALKING TO.” Demons think in terms of rank and are organized into military groupings. You must be careful as you tackle evil powers, knowing that rank is an important thing in the realm of the spirit.

4\. At various times, God promotes His servants and raises the rank of His servants.

For our light affliction, which is but for a moment, worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory;

2 Corinthians 4:17

We have small troubles for a while now, but these troubles are helping us gain an eternal glory. That eternal glory is much greater than our troubles.

2 Corinthians 4:17 (ERV)

God raises the rank and weight of His servants by the afflictions that they pass through. Every affliction you survive gives you more weight. In the realm of the spirit, everyone has a level of weighted glory. The level of weighted glory you carry determines your rank in the realm of the spirit.

5\. You will be promoted into higher ranks of ministry.

And when THE CHIEF SHEPHERD shall appear, ye shall receive a crown of glory that fadeth not away.

1 Peter 5:4

All pastors can be promoted to a higher rank. There are greater pastors and lesser ones too. Promotion is something that must happen in your life and ministry.

6\. All apostles are not the same.

I am become a fool in glorying; ye have compelled me: for I ought to have been commended of you: for in nothing am I behind THE VERY CHIEFEST APOSTLES, though I be nothing.

2 Corinthians 12:11

Some apostles have been promoted to a higher rank. There are greater apostles and lesser ones. There are higher ranks amongst the apostles. There are senior ranking apostles in the ministry. Satan knows this well and he does not want you to be promoted.

7\. There are higher and higher ranks amongst the priests.

Now when the HIGH PRIEST AND THE CAPTAIN OF THE TEMPLE and the chief priests heard these things, they doubted of them whereunto this would grow.

Acts 5:24

All priests are not the same. There are greater priests and lesser priests. There are higher and higher ranks in the ministry. There are senior ranking high priests, and there are captains of the temple.

8\. There are senior ranking speakers and preachers.

And they called Barnabas, Jupiter; and Paul, Mercurius, because he was THE CHIEF SPEAKER.

Acts 14:12

May you become a senior ranking preacher! A higher-ranking speaker will have a greater effect when he speaks. Paul was a chief speaker!

9\. You must pass your tests now and be promoted to the next rank.

My brethren, count it all joy when ye fall into divers temptations; Knowing this, that the trying of your faith worketh patience. But let patience have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, wanting nothing.

James 1:2-4

How do you pass tests so that you change your rank? You pass your tests when you count it all joy and grow in patience towards God. Passing tests means that you go through with patience and without any bad attitude. The word “patience” means cheerful, hopeful endurance. If you are not cheerful and hopeful in what you are experiencing, you have failed your test.

10\. You must rejoice in times of testing because the tests cause you to qualify for promotion.

And not only so, but WE GLORY IN TRIBULATIONS ALSO: knowing that tribulation worketh patience; And patience, experience; and experience, hope: And hope maketh not ashamed; because the love of God is shed abroad in our hearts by the Holy Ghost which is given unto us.

Romans 5:3-5

Every test works a promotion in you through patience and through quiet, cheerful and hopeful endurance! We glory in our tribulations because these qualities necessary for promotion are being confirmed in us by every test.
