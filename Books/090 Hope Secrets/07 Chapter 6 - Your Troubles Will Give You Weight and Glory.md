### Chapter 6\

Your Troubles Will Give You Weight and Glory\
------------------------------------------------------------------

For our light affliction, which is but for a moment, worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory;

2 Corinthians 4:17

1\. Trouble makes you weighty and gives your words weight!

For our light affliction, which is but for a moment, worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory;

2 Corinthians 4:17

The afflictions that we go through are working out a weight of glory. There is a type of glory and beauty that only comes from trouble, affliction and difficulty. Indeed, trouble makes you weighty in the ministry!

I promise you that after you go through trouble successfully, you will be “more” weighty. You will have a weight of divine and heavenly glory on your life that no one will be able to set aside.

Why is it that some people’s words carry so much weight? Why is it that other people say the same things but their words are light and airy? It takes experiencing trouble to make you weighty in the ministry.

This is why older, experienced people use proverbs that have amazing depth and meaning. A simple comment by an older, experienced person can send shock waves and fear into those listening.

An old man once said, “You cannot keep poisonous snakes in your backyard and expect them to only bite the neighbours.” This is a weighty statement.

Another old man said, “When a king’s palace is burnt, a more beautiful one is built for him.” This weighty statement only comes from someone who has seen trouble.

Yet another person said, “It is inside the blackest pot that one cooks white rice.” This person has seen how a good thing can come out of an apparently dark and difficult situation.

Yet another person said, “A hunter with one arrow does not shoot carelessly.” Obviously, he is someone who knows what it is like to be left with no options.

2\. Trouble prepares you for the next level of fruitfulness.

And thou shalt remember all the way which the LORD thy God led thee these forty years in the wilderness, to humble thee, and to prove thee, to know what was in thine heart, whether thou wouldest keep his commandments, or no. And he humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with manna, which thou knewest not, neither did thy fathers know; that he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the LORD doth man live.

Deuteronomy 8:2-3

God led the children of Israel through the wilderness. He wanted them to live and multiply. But before they could live and multiply, He had to test them, to humble them and to prove them. God took the Israelites through the wilderness to humble them and to prepare them for a great work.

No one would like to sit on a plane that has not been tested. Do the engines work? Will they fall off once the plane is airborne? I heard of a plane that fell straight out of the sky because one of the engines fell off the plane.

I once watched a plane that was being tested. It took off and continued flying straight ahead without gaining altitude. It flew straight into a mountain that was near the airport where these tests were being done.

Indeed, it is important to have planes tested if people are going to trust their lives to them. If God is going to entrust many people into your care, He is going to have to test you first.

Trouble is the only thing He can use to test you to see your resilience, your persistence, your humility and your reaction to difficulties, hardships and problems.

Do not fail your test! Make sure you pass your tests with flying colours! God really wants you to pass these tests! God is hoping that you will come through with flying colours!

3\. Trouble pushes you towards your destiny of fruitfulness.

All the commandments which I command thee this day shall ye observe to do, that ye may live, and multiply, and go in and possess the land which the Lord sware unto your fathers. And thou shalt remember all the way which the Lord thy God led thee these forty years in the wilderness, to humble thee, and to prove thee, to know what was in thine heart, whether thou wouldest keep his commandments, or no. And he humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with manna, which thou knewest not, neither did thy fathers know; that he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the Lord doth man live. Thy raiment waxed not old upon thee, neither did thy foot swell, these forty years.

Deuteronomy 8:1-4

The troubles that the Israelites encountered only served to push them towards fruitfulness. Everything they experienced was a test to prove them and to humble them so that they would be better prepared to bear fruit.

God allowed them to be hungry. God allowed them to experience thirst. It was necessary for them to experience hunger and thirst, not knowing where the next meal was going to come from.

God is taking you through a stage where you will have to trust Him. He wants to see your reaction and response to lack, to poverty and to frustration. How you behave when you lack will determine how God will promote you! Please do not fail this test!

4\. Trouble will work itself out and become a good thing.

And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose. For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren.

Romans 8:28-29

Trouble will work itself out and become a good thing that has happened in your life. Trouble will eventually give you an advantage, otherwise God would not allow it at all. If it does not work out to your advantage, how will it work out for your good? Expect every trouble in your life to work out for good.

5\. Trouble, difficulties and crises will lead to your approval in God.

BUT IN ALL THINGS APPROVING OURSELVES AS THE MINISTERS OF GOD, in much patience, in afflictions, in necessities, in distresses, In stripes, in imprisonments, in tumults, in labours, in watchings, in fastings; By pureness, by knowledge, by longsuffering, by kindness, by the Holy Ghost, by love unfeigned,

2 Corinthians 6:4-6

Trouble is God’s examinations that leads to God’s approval of us.

God’s approval of you is not seen in the good things that He has given you but is seen through the troubles that you survive. How are you ever going to get the approval you need from God if there are no troubles in your life? It is through afflictions, necessities, distresses, stripes, imprisonments, tumults and labours that we see God approving of us!

6\. Trouble and difficulty give you a message.

Blessed be God, even the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies, and the God of all comfort; Who comforteth us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble, by the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God. For as the sufferings of Christ abound in us, so our consolation also aboundeth by Christ.

2 Corinthians 1:3-5

Who comforts (consoles and encourages) us in every trouble (calamity and affliction), so that we may also be able to comfort (console and encourage) those who are in any kind of trouble or distress, with the comfort (consolation and encouragement) with which we ourselves are comforted (consoled and encouraged) by God.

2 Corinthians 1:4 (AMPC)

Troubles give you a ministry and a message. Paul said that he had a message from the Lord through the tribulations and troubles that he experienced. The message that he had was a message to comfort people who were in trouble. The only way he could speak to people who were in trouble was to share what he had learnt when he went through trouble himself.

The whole world lies in wickedness and there is trouble everywhere. You will never have a message for this world unless you experience some of the troubles that are in the world.

I am sure you have noticed that several men of God have a special, powerful message which is related to some type of trouble they have experienced themselves. Kenneth Hagin’s most powerful message, the message of faith, is about how God delivered him from a deathbed.

Indeed, your troubles and difficulties are what give you a ministry and a message!
