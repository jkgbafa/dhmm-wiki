### Chapter 17\

You Will Experience Wound Healing\
-------------------------------------------------------

Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, THAT YE MAY BE HEALED. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.

James 5:16

You will be healed from your deadly wounds! Whatever has hurt you and wounded you in this life will not be able to destroy you. God has a plan to heal your wounds. Throughout this chapter, you will see many people who were healed of their deadly wounds. Being able to be healed of your deadly wounds is a sign of spiritual victory.

Expect to be hurt! But also expect to be healed. You will come out of the deepest hole and become a better person. You will be a healed person who has recovered nicely.

Perhaps, you met people who dragged you down into a pit. Today, you cannot imagine how you will recover from being in those pits. Your soul is wounded! Your heart is bleeding! But God will heal you of your wounds.

Watching people recover from surgery is watching a miracle of wound healing. Sometimes, a surgeon’s knife cuts so deep and so wide that you wonder how anyone could recover from the cut. And yet, within three days, you will see this same person standing up and walking around as though nothing major had happened.

1\. Wounds and offences are part of this life!

But whoso shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me, it were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were drowned in the depth of the sea. Woe unto the world because of offences! For it must needs be that offences come; but woe to that man by whom the offence cometh!

Matthew 18:6-7

If you are part of this life, you will one day need healing of your wounds. Confess your faults one to another and pray for one another so that you may be healed. If you are part of this life, expect to be hurt and expect to be wounded. Many things will hurt you. If you are in the ministry, expect to be hurt and wounded by fellow pastors. If you are married, expect to be wounded by your spouse. If you are in business, expect to be hurt and wounded in the business world.

2\. All spiritual wounds must be healed!

The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; HE HATH SENT ME TO HEAL THE BROKEN HEARTED, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised, To preach the acceptable year of the Lord.

Luke 4:18-19

Your broken heart must be healed! Your spiritual wounds must be healed! You will not bear fruit until you endure offences well and come out perfectly healed. You are healed when you are no longer bitter.

The scripture James 1:4, is clear on how you are supposed to come out of the tests of your life. You are supposed to come out with patience. (Patience means cheerful, hopeful endurance.) You must be someone who is cheerful and hopeful after enduring a crisis. Are you cheerful? Are you hopeful? Are you enduring what God is expecting you to endure and coming out with hope and cheerfulness?

But let patience \[CHEERFUL HOPEFUL ENDURANCE\] have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, wanting nothing.

James 1:4

“Cheerful and hopeful” is the sign of wound healing. Depression and hopelessness are signs of unhealed wounds. Lack of joy and happiness are signs that the wound is not yet healed.

It is time to consider yourself fortunate for having had all these trials. It is time to consider yourself fortunate for having had all these tests. It is good news that you are being severely tested and tried.

My brothers and sisters, be very happy when you are tested in different ways.

James 1:2 (GW)

3\. Wound healing is a great achievement. The beast in the Book of Revelation experienced wound healing and the world was impressed at its ability to be healed.

And I stood upon the sand of the sea, and saw a beast rise up out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns, and upon his horns ten crowns, and upon his heads the name of blasphemy.

AND THE BEAST WHICH I SAW was like unto a leopard, and his feet were as the feet of a bear, and his mouth as the mouth of a lion: and the dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority.

AND I SAW ONE OF HIS HEADS AS IT WERE WOUNDED TO DEATH; AND HIS DEADLY WOUND WAS HEALED: AND ALL THE WORLD WONDERED AFTER THE BEAST.

And they worshipped the dragon which gave power unto the beast: and they worshipped the beast, saying, Who is like unto the beast? who is able to make war with him?

And there was given unto him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies; and power was given unto him to continue forty and two months.

Revelation 13:1-5

You will experience things that will hurt you and wound you severely. It is your ability to be healed that matters. The beast in the Book of Revelation was wounded but managed to get itself healed. It was that healing of the beast that impressed the world.

Even the beast achieved wound healing! Even the devil knows the importance of wound healing. Until you go through your deadly wounds and get healed, you will not be impressive in the realm of the spirit.

It is important to be healed because it is a great spiritual achievement to overcome your hurts and wounds. It is a great spiritual achievement to be healed.

4\. Joseph was wounded! Joseph experienced wound healing and impressed the world.

Joseph did not bear fruit until his deadly wound was healed. Joseph was wounded severely, but God healed him and gave him a double portion. Joseph needed to be healed from the betrayal of his brothers.

Joseph experienced wickedness from his brothers and needed to be healed from the wickedness of his brothers.

Joseph became a slave at a point in his life. He needed to be healed of the experience of being a slave.

Joseph became a prisoner. He needed to be healed of the experience of being a prisoner.

Joseph experienced the deception of a woman. Joseph needed to be healed of that experience.

Joseph was forgotten by the butler and left to languish in the prison for an extra two years. He needed healing from the pain of being forgotten by people.

Joseph spent twelve years in prison. Joseph experienced the hatred of human beings. Indeed, he was sorely grieved and hurt. If you have experienced any of these different things, you are just like Joseph.

Yet, Joseph was healed and God used him to save Israel. Read for yourself how he was sorely grieved and yet remained strong.

Joseph is a fruitful bough, even a fruitful bough by a well; whose branches run over the wall: THE ARCHERS HAVE SORELY GRIEVED HIM, AND SHOT AT HIM, AND HATED HIM: BUT HIS BOW ABODE IN STRENGTH, and the arms of his hands were made strong by the hands of the mighty God of Jacob; (from thence is the shepherd, the stone of Israel:) Even by the God of thy father, who shall help thee; and by the Almighty, who shall bless thee with blessings of heaven above, blessings of the deep that lieth under, blessings of the breasts, and of the womb: THE BLESSINGS OF THY FATHER HAVE PREVAILED above the blessings of my progenitors unto the utmost bound of the everlasting hills: they shall be on the head of Joseph, and on the crown of the head of him that was separate from his brethren.

Genesis 49:22-26

“But his bow abode in strength”! The blessings of God prevailed and helped him to overcome the multiple sores, wounds and injuries that he received in his life and ministry.

This is what is going to happen to you, too. You are coming out with the blessings of God.

You may have had a terrible experience in your relationship or in your marriage. Do not stay down! You must abide in strength.

Let your bow abide in strength. Let your arms be strong because God will help you and God will bless you.

The help of God and the blessing of God will keep you. You will remain forgiving and loving to the end.

6\. Job was wounded. Job recovered from the wounds of his family tragedies.

God will give you much more than you have now if you can overcome your difficulty with a good attitude. Job’s good attitude is revealed in that he stopped his wife and friends from speaking in the wrong way. Job was on his way to a greater life with more abundance and a greater and better family. He needed to maintain a good attitude throughout his crises. He needed to fight bad attitudes in all the people around him.

It is through the scars of Jesus that we know He was healed of His terrible wounds. The wounds you are healed from will be the basis of your ministry. Unless you can recover from certain things, you will not be qualified to talk about them with authority. A soldier who has learned about war in a classroom is different from someone who has been wounded on the front line.
