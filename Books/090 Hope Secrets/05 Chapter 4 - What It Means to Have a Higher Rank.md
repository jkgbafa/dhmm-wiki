### Chapter 4\

What It Means to Have a Higher Rank\
---------------------------------------------------------

Wherefore God also hath HIGHLY EXALTED HIM, and given him a name which is above every name: That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth;

Philippians 2:9-10

You must desire that your rank changes. A change in rank only comes about because you have passed difficult tests. It is of utmost importance that your rank changes because without a change in rank, there will be no change in many aspects of your life. It is of utmost importance that you go through your difficulties, trials and temptations successfully. This is going to result in a change in rank.

You will know your rank has changed when you notice important changes in your life, that can only come about because your rank has changed. A change of rank is seen in many ways. When Jesus was highly exalted after His victory on the cross, He experienced a change in rank. The change of rank that Jesus experienced resulted in many changes in His life. Let us see how a change in rank brings about changes in your life and ministry. I am only writing these things so that you watch out for genuine changes in your rank!

1\. A change in rank results in a change in income.

And they sung a new song, saying, THOU ART WORTHY to take the book, and to open the seals thereof: for thou wast slain, and hast redeemed us to God by thy blood out of every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation; And hast made us unto our God kings and priests: and we shall reign on the earth. And I beheld, and I heard the voice of many angels round about the throne and the beasts and the elders: and the number of them was ten thousand times ten thousand, and thousands of thousands; Saying with a loud voice, WORTHY IS THE LAMB THAT WAS SLAIN TO RECEIVE POWER, AND RICHES, and wisdom, and strength, and honour, and glory, and blessing.

Revelation 5:9-12

There is always more money at a higher rank. Jesus became worthy to receive riches because His rank changed. You will become worthy and qualified to receive a higher income and more money when your rank changes. Your level of income is not changing because your rank is not changing.

When a medical student becomes a doctor after seven years of medical school, his income changes. His rank in society changes from the rank of a student to the rank of a medical doctor. This goes immediately with a change in income. You will notice a financial change in your life and ministry when your rank changes.

2\. A change in rank results in a change in the level of respect and honour.

Then Samuel took a vial of oil, and poured it upon his head, and kissed him, and said, Is it not because the Lord hath anointed thee to be captain over his inheritance?... And THEY WILL SALUTE THEE, and give thee two loaves of bread; which thou shalt receive of their hands.

1 Samuel 10:1, 4

When Saul was anointed, his rank changed from a common citizen of Israel to the king. When his rank changed, he was respected and honoured much more by the people. People began to salute him because he had a higher rank. Beginning from today, your rank will change and your level of respect will change. You will no longer be set aside and ignored. I was set aside and ignored for many years of ministry. As I moved up in rank, I received more and more respect from people who once despised me.

3\. A change in rank results in a change in positioning in society.

Wherefore God also hath HIGHLY EXALTED HIM, and given him a name which is above every name: That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth;

Philippians 2:9-10

When your rank changes in the realm of the spirit, your position in society will also change. Perhaps, you are at the lower end of the society. As you go higher in God, you will find your position in society also changing. Instead of being ignored and set aside, you will be welcomed in the higher echelons of society.

When a student becomes a medical doctor, his position in society changes. He is no longer reckoned amongst students but amongst the privileged fraternity of doctors.

4\. A change in rank results in a change in where you sit and who you meet.

And, behold, I purpose to build an house unto the name of the Lord my God, as the Lord spake unto David my father, saying, THY SON WHOM I WILL SET UPON THY THRONE in thy room, he shall build an house unto my name.

1 Kings 5:5

When your rank changes you will be given a new seat amongst a new group. As a newly qualified doctor, you will no longer sit amongst students but amongst doctors. Today, I sit amongst the pastors because my rank has changed. I have been promoted from sitting in the general congregation to sitting alongside other pastors in the front row of the church. All these are signs of my change of rank.

5\. A change in rank results in a change in the kind of gifts you receive.

And when the queen of Sheba had seen all Solomon’s wisdom, and the house that he had built, And the meat of his table, and the sitting of his servants, and the attendance of his ministers, and their apparel, and his cupbearers, and his ascent by which he went up unto the house of the Lord; there was no more spirit in her. And she said to the king, It was a true report that I heard in mine own land of thy acts and of thy wisdom. Howbeit I believed not the words, until I came, and mine eyes had seen it: and, behold, the half was not told me: thy wisdom and prosperity exceedeth the fame which I heard. Happy are thy men, happy are these thy servants, which stand continually before thee, and that hear thy wisdom. BLESSED BE THE LORD THY GOD, WHICH DELIGHTED IN THEE, TO SET THEE ON THE THRONE OF ISRAEL: because the Lord loved Israel for ever, therefore made he thee king, to do judgment and justice. And SHE GAVE THE KING AN HUNDRED AND TWENTY TALENTS OF GOLD, AND OF SPICES VERY GREAT STORE, AND PRECIOUS STONES: THERE CAME NO MORE SUCH ABUNDANCE OF SPICES as these which the queen of Sheba gave to king Solomon.

1 Kings 10:4-10

When you are operating at a higher rank in the realm of the spirit, you will see greater and greater gifts being given to you. There is a correlation between your rank and the kinds of gifts you receive in your life. It does not occur to anyone to give you certain kinds of gifts because of your lower rank. When your rank changes in the realm of the spirit, no one will advise anyone to give you greater gifts. It will just happen naturally.

6\. A change in rank results in a change in the power and authority of your words.

For I also am a man set under authority, having under me soldiers, and I SAY UNTO ONE, GO, AND HE GOETH; AND TO ANOTHER, COME, AND HE COMETH; and to my servant, Do this, and he doeth it. When Jesus heard these things, he marvelled at him, and turned him about, and said unto the people that followed him, I say unto you, I have not found so great faith, no, not in Israel.

Luke 7:8-9

When your rank changes, your words and commands are taken more seriously.

Like newly qualified doctors, a change of rank comes with a change of the power and authority in your words. A medical student may suggest a treatment. However, no one may take him seriously. The more senior and the more experienced a doctor is, the more people take his advice seriously.

When a pastor has a high rank, there is a greater impact from his messages, teachings, instructions, commands, directives and preaching. As you climb higher in God, your words may be fewer but they will have a greater impact.

7\. A change in rank results in a change of name.

And Jesus answered and said unto him, Blessed art thou, Simon Bar-jona: for flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven. And I say also unto thee, THAT THOU ART PETER, and upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.

Matthew 16:17-18

A change of rank often comes with a change of name. Peter was promoted to be the head of the church and was given a new name. When Peter received a new name, it was because his rank had changed. Like newly qualified doctors, a change of rank comes with a change of name. When I was in medical school, I was called, “Mills.” When I became a doctor, I was called “Dr. Mills.” There are people who desperately want to be called “Father” or “Papa.” Do not worry about being called, “Papa.” When your rank changes in the realm of the spirit, you will not have to tell people to call you Papa or Daddy. When your rank has changed in the realm of the spirit, anyone who interacts with you will know that they are standing before a father and they will acknowledge you as such.

8\. A change in rank results in a change in the access you have.

And Jesus answered and said unto him, Blessed art thou, Simon Bar-jona: for flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven. And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it. And I will give unto thee the keys of the kingdom of heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.

Matthew 16:17-19

When you are at a higher rank, you have greater access to certain places. In the realm of the spirit, you will have greater access to the throne of God. You will have a greater access to God’s presence. You will switch faster from the flesh into the spirit.

Like newly qualified doctors, a change of rank comes with a change in the keys you carry. A doctor will have access to the doctors’ lounge just because he is a doctor. When he was a medical student, he was cut off from all the places where doctors were allowed to go. Indeed, a change in rank will change the access you have to important places.

9\. A change in rank results in a change in your glory.

This light, temporary nature of our suffering is producing for us an everlasting weight of glory, far beyond any comparison,

2 Corinthians 4:17 (ISV)

A change in rank and glory will come about because of the sufferings you have been through and what you have survived. A change in rank is seen by a change in the glory. A change in rank is seen by a change in the level of beauty and glory in your life. A soldier who has a higher rank has more stripes, more pips, more studs and more decorations. He is called a highly decorated soldier! Receive a greater glory on your life, beginning from now!

10\. A change in rank results in a change in your privileges.

And I wept much, because no man was found worthy to open and to read the book, neither to look thereon. And one of the elders saith unto me, Weep not: behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, hath prevailed to open the book, and to loose the seven seals thereof.

Revelation 5:4-5

Jesus Christ was considered worthy to open the Book of Life. What a privilege! A change in your rank in the ministry is going to lead to many more privileges.

Like newly qualified doctors, a change of rank comes with more privileges. A doctor will have privileged access to patients, access to doctors’ lounges, access to doctors’ parking spaces and so on.
