### Chapter 14\

Don’t Let Fear of the River Keep You Back\
---------------------------------------------------------------

For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.

2 Timothy 1:7

1\. Cross all the rivers you encounter!

Behold, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth passeth over before you into Jordan. Now therefore take you twelve men out of the tribes of Israel, out of every tribe a man. AND IT SHALL COME TO PASS, AS SOON AS THE SOLES OF THE FEET OF THE PRIESTS THAT BEAR THE ARK OF THE LORD, THE LORD OF ALL THE EARTH, SHALL REST IN THE WATERS OF JORDAN, that the waters of Jordan shall be cut off from the waters that come down from above; and they shall stand upon an heap.

And it came to pass, when the people removed from their tents, to pass over Jordan, and the priests bearing the ark of the covenant before the people; And as they that bare the ark were come unto Jordan, and the feet of the priests that bare the ark were dipped in the brim of the water, (for Jordan overfloweth all his banks all the time of harvest,) That the waters which came down from above stood and rose up upon an heap very far from the city Adam, that is beside Zaretan: and those that came down toward the sea of the plain, even the salt sea, failed, and were cut off: and the people passed over right against Jericho. And the priests that bare the ark of the covenant of the Lord stood firm on dry ground in the midst of Jordan, and all the Israelites passed over on dry ground, until all the people were passed clean over Jordan.

Joshua 3:11-17

Cross all the rivers you encounter! Do not let the fear of any river keep you back!

Like Joshua, you must trust God when you meet the unusual, the unknown and the unfamiliar. The priests had to step into unknown waters. There are rivers you will never cross until you trust God enough to step into the unknown and uncertain waters of life.

You must overcome the fear of the unknown.

You must overcome the fear of the unusual.

God is about to do wonderful things in your life so you have to overcome all the fears. Do not let the fear of the river keep you back.

You will never know all the details of the depths of the river in front of you. You are not going to know all the details about how it is going to work out. You will never know where all the money is going to come from. Trust God and take your steps into the rivers of the unknown!

2\. Take the opportunities that will never come again.

Then Mordecai commanded to answer Esther, Think not with thyself that thou shalt escape in the king’s house, more than all the Jews. For if thou altogether holdest thy peace at this time, then shall there enlargement and deliverance arise to the Jews from another place; but thou and thy father’s house shall be destroyed: and who knoweth whether thou art come to the kingdom for such a time as this? Then Esther bade them return Mordecai this answer, GO, GATHER TOGETHER ALL THE JEWS THAT ARE PRESENT IN SHUSHAN, AND FAST YE FOR ME, AND NEITHER EAT NOR DRINK THREE DAYS, NIGHT OR DAY: I ALSO AND MY MAIDENS WILL FAST LIKEWISE; AND SO WILL I GO IN UNTO THE KING, WHICH IS NOT ACCORDING TO THE LAW: AND IF I PERISH, I PERISH. So Mordecai went his way, and did according to all that Esther had commanded him.

Esther 4:13-17

Like Esther, you must trust God for the opportunities that will never come again. You may never have the opportunity to serve God in this way again. Esther had the chance to go in to the king and do something to save Israel.

Opportunities that will never come again are before you right now:

The opportunity to serve God!

The opportunity to preach the gospel!

The opportunity to sing!

Your future depends on trusting God for outcomes you do not yet know. Trust God and you will bear much fruit. You must speak like Esther – “I will obey God today! If I perish, I perish!”

3\. God is giving you enough light for what is just ahead.

Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.

Psalm 119:105

God is giving you enough light for what lies just ahead. You will have to trust Him for the rest, which remains unknown.

“A lamp” implies you have enough light to see right in front of you. God is not giving you a light that shows how your life will be for the next seventy years. Yes, you will need light for seventy years, but God is only giving you a lamp for your feet.

Perhaps you are going on a journey that is one mile long. A lamp can only give you light for about three feet ahead of you. A lamp is not intended to light up the entire mile of the road ahead.

Just as the headlights of your car cannot shine very far, the lamp of God does not show you too far ahead. The headlights of your car may only show you about thirty meters ahead; but you do not stop driving just because you cannot see your destination that is ten kilometres away.

Rejoice not against me, O mine enemy: when I fall, I shall arise; when I sit in darkness, the LORD shall be a light unto me.

Micah 7:8

It is in the midst of the darkness that God will give you His characteristic light.

When you sit in darkness, you can expect the Lord to be a light to you. Light speaks of revelation, wisdom, understanding and knowledge. God will give you fresh knowledge and revelation when you sit in darkness.

4\. Do not worry if you do not know the details of the future.

Now the Lord had said unto Abram, Get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and from thy father’s house, unto a land that I will shew thee: And I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and make thy name great; and thou shalt be a blessing:

Genesis 12:1-2

God has it all planned out for you, just like He did for Abraham. Abraham had no idea of what would happen in the future, but he trusted God!

Will you trust God even though you don’t have the details? Will you take that step of faith even though you’re nervous, uncomfortable and unsure of how it will all work out?

5\. God has planned and ordered all the steps of your future.

The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord: and he delighteth in his way.

Psalm 37:23

6\. Take a few steps into the unknown.

And Peter answered him and said, Lord, if it be thou, bid me come unto thee on the water. And he said, Come. And when Peter was come down out of the ship, he walked on the water, to go to Jesus. But when he saw the wind boisterous, he was afraid; and beginning to sink, he cried, saying, Lord, save me. And immediately Jesus stretched forth his hand, and caught him, and said unto him, O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?

Matthew 14:28-31

Peter has done something none of us has ever done – he walked a few steps on water! None of the disciples ever achieved this. They probably wanted to know more details before attempting such a thing. Peter is famous today because of his ability to trust in the unknown. Peter is famous today because he took a few steps into the unknown. Perhaps this is the reason why Jesus appointed him as the head of the church.

God will do the unbelievable and the incredible if you are prepared to take a few steps into the unknown.
