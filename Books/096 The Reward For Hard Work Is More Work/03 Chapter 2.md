### Chapter 2\

The Reward for Hard Work is More Work: The Unprofitable Servant\
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

But which of you, having a servant plowing or feeding cattle, will say unto him by and by, when he is come from the field, Go and sit down to meat? AND WILL NOT RATHER SAY UNTO HIM, MAKE READY WHEREWITH I MAY SUP, AND GIRD THYSELF, AND SERVE ME, TILL I HAVE EATEN AND DRUNKEN; AND AFTERWARD THOU SHALT EAT AND DRINK? Doth he thank that servant because he did the things that were commanded him? I trow not. So likewise ye, when ye shall have done all those things which are commanded you, say, We are unprofitable servants: we have done that which was our duty to do.

Luke 17:7-10

God is about to speak to you concerning your new mission. He wants to give you another job! As long as you are in this world, remember that the reward for hard work is to be given more work. The reward for your good work is more work!

I believe you have done well in your past assignments. However, it is not time to rest. Rest is for heaven. Rest comes after this life! Whilst on this earth, there will be more and more work for every one of God’s special servants.

Expect a new assignment from God!

Look out for the new job that God has for you! In the opening scripture, the servant, after doing a good job feeding cattle in the field, was given more work in the house. He was asked to prepare a nice meal for the master and to serve his master. What a reward for working hard in the field! More work! The reward for hard work is more work!

Expect God to speak to you again. Do not expect to retire if you are God’s servant. The reward for building a church is to be given even more things to build for God. Build till you die! God’s servants do not retire, but keep moving from assignment to assignment as long as they live on earth.

Service is the pathway to leadership in the kingdom of God. Service is hard work! Being a servant is definitely hard work! But rulership is also very hard work. On many occasions, servanthood is rewarded with leadership duties. That is one of the ways in which the reward for hard work becomes more work. The reward for doing the work of a servant is to be given the work of a leader.

This message is important because many Christians are deceived by their accomplishments in God. The accomplishments of God’s ministers sometimes lead them into distractions and delusions.

The reward for good pastoral work is more pastoral work. The reward for your good season of pastoral work is not for you to veer off into retirement and leisure. The reward for hard work is not for you to live in sin, nor for you to live in unnecessary restfulness, nor for you to take unnecessary holidays or other frivolous activities.

The reward for a hard-fought ministry is more ministry!

The reward for being a good evangelist is more evangelism!

The reward for being a good pastor is more pastoral work!

The reward for being a great prophet and teacher is to teach and prophesy even more!

The great works of compassion you did for orphans is greatly appreciated. But God wants to reward you by giving you a great ministry to prisoners. The reward for great works is more work!

Make no mistake; satan is not happy about the things you have done for God. He would love to smear you and mess you up. Satan wants to divert your attention from the second, third and fourth missions of your life. Do not let satan mislead you or deceive you about the rest of the time you have on earth. There is more work for you to do. Much more!

I remember when I heard this phrase from the lips of Reinhardt Bonnke: “The reward for hard work is more work!” What a life transforming revelation came to me from those amazing words! I pray you will be convinced in your heart that the reward for all that you have done is to be given more work until your very last day.

I heard Derek Prince saying that he would consider it a great tragedy to be asked not to be a servant of God. He said it would be the saddest day of his life if God did not want him to be His servant anymore.

What a blessing it is to be a servant of God! What a blessing to have something to do in the house of God right up until the end of our lives on this earth!

Let us see how God rewards His servants with more work!

THE HARD WORK: PLOUGHING IN THE FIELD

BUT WHICH OF YOU, HAVING A SERVANT PLOWING OR FEEDING CATTLE, WILL SAY UNTO HIM BY AND BY, WHEN HE IS COME FROM THE FIELD, GO AND SIT DOWN TO MEAT? And will not rather say unto him, Make ready wherewith I may sup, and gird thyself, and serve me, till I have eaten and drunken; and afterward thou shalt eat and drink? Doth he thank that servant because he did the things that were commanded him? I trow not. So likewise ye, when ye shall have done all those things which are commanded you, say, We are unprofitable servants: we have done that which was our duty to do.

Luke 17:7-10

The work of God is to plough the field. Ploughing speaks of following deep into ministry with Jesus. The reward for ploughing the field will be more work for you. Ploughing also speaks of preparing the ground for a major investment. After ploughing and investing in the ministry, your reward will be to do even more hard work.

THE HARD WORK: FEEDING THE ANIMALS

…Jesus saith unto him, Feed my sheep.

John 21:17

The work of God is to feed the animals on the farm. Feeding the animals speaks of the shepherd’s duty of feeding sheep.

THE HARD WORK: WORKING IN THE FIELD

Sow to yourselves in righteousness, reap in mercy; break up your fallow ground: for it is time to seek the Lord, till he come and rain righteousness upon you.

Hosea 10:12

The work of God is represented by being out on the field. The field represents the field of souls and the fields of people who need God. Working in the field is working on souls and winning souls for Jesus. What is the reward for this?

Let us read further to find out how God rewards you for all your hard work in the field of souls.

THE REWARD FOR HARD WORK: SERVING FOOD AND DRINK

On the contrary, will he not say to him, ‘Prepare something for me to eat, and properly clothe yourself and serve me while I eat and drink; and afterward you may eat and drink’?

Luke 17:8 (NASB)

1\. The reward for your hard work of ploughing in the field is to change your clothes and serve again.

The reward for all your work in the field is to be asked to dress properly for your next task. God has more things for you to do. He expects you to gird yourself and make yourself ready for even more service. To gird yourself is to prepare your mind for the work.

Wherefore gird up the loins of your mind, be sober, and hope to the end for the grace that is to be brought unto you at the revelation of Jesus Christ;

1 Peter 1:13

2\. The reward for ploughing in the field is to prepare food for your master.

To prepare food speaks of the preparation of the gospel. The preparation of the gospel is the best reward for your hard work.

Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness; And your feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace;

Ephesians 6:14-15

3\. The reward for ploughing in the field is to serve food and drinks to the master.

Serving food and drinks speaks of ministering the word of God in a delightful way to God’s people. What an honour it is to deliver God’s word to His people. This is what happened when Amos was taken from the field and made to serve in God’s house as a prophet.

Then answered Amos, and said to Amaziah, I was no prophet, neither was I a prophet’s son; but I was an herdman, and a gatherer of sycamore fruit: And the Lord took me as I followed the flock, and the Lord said unto me, Go, prophesy unto my people Israel.

Amos 7:14-15
