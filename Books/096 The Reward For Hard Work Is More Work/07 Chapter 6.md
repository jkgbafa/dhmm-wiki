### Chapter 6\

The Reward for Hard Work is More Work: The Sensible Slave\
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Who then is the faithful and sensible slave whom his master put in charge of his household to give them their food at the proper time? Blessed is that slave whom his master finds so doing when he comes. TRULY I SAY TO YOU THAT HE WILL PUT HIM IN CHARGE OF ALL HIS POSSESSIONS. But if that evil slave says in his heart, ‘My master is not coming for a long time,’ and begins to beat his fellow slaves and eat and drink with drunkards; the master of that slave will come on a day when he does not expect him and at an hour which he does not know, and will cut him in pieces and assign him a place with the hypocrites; in that place there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.

Matthew 24:45-51 (NASB95)

THE HARD WORK: TO BE FAITHFUL AND SENSIBLE

1\. The work of God is to be a faithful and sensible slave.

Who then is the faithful and sensible slave whom his master put in charge of his household to give them their food at the proper time?

Matthew 24:45 (NASB95)

The work of God is to be sensible and faithful. “Sensible” means being level headed especially when it comes to ministry.

2\. The work of God is to be put in charge of a household.

Who then is the faithful and sensible slave whom his master put in charge of his household to give them their food at the proper time?

Matthew 24:45 (NASB95)

3\. The work of God is to give food to the household at the proper time.

Who then is the faithful and sensible slave whom his master put in charge of his household to give them their food at the proper time?

Matthew 24:45 (NASB95)

One of the important jobs in the kingdom is to give God’s people food at the right time. You must write the books God tells you to write at the right time. Sometimes I look at the many books I have written, and I know that if I had not written them at the time I did, I would not be able to write them now.

There is an important element of timing when it comes to giving out the food of the Word. You can teach someone the morals in the word of God after they have been sexually polluted and defiled. But it would have been better if the person had learnt all these principles before they were polluted and defiled by sexual monsters and sexual predators.

There is an important element of timing in the teaching ministry. You must deliver the important Word and the important messages from God on time. People must be taught the word of God when they are young. It is because people are not taught to have a quiet time when they are young that they grow to become unstable Christians.

THE REWARD FOR HARD WORK: TO BE IN CHARGE OF ALL POSSESSIONS

I tell you the truth, the master will put him in charge of all his possessions.

Matthew 24:47 (NET)

The reward for your hard work as a wise and sensible slave, is to be put in charge of all the Master’s possessions. Once you are in charge of all the Master’s possessions, you will have a lot more work to do.

The reward for good work done is more work! The reward for giving God’s people their food in due season is to be given charge of all the Master’s goods.

God will let you have all His possessions! The New English Version of the Bible says “God rewarded him by putting him in charge of all his possessions.” This is why ministers seem to get rich at a certain stage of ministry. God has actually given them possessions and goods that are His. God expects ministers to use His possessions to do even more.
