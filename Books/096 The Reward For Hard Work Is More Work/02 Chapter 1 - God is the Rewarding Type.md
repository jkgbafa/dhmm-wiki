### Chapter 1\

God is the Rewarding Type\
-----------------------------------------------

But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.

Hebrews 11:6

God is the rewarding type. God believes in rewarding His servants. You must believe in God’s reward plan. God has many great rewards. One of God’s great rewards for hard work is to give you more work.

1\. You are worthy of a reward. There is a time when you are qualified to receive a reward. God is the rewarding type and He will bestow a reward upon you. In other words, you are worthy of a reward!

Let the elders that rule well be counted worthy of double honour, especially they who labour in the word and doctrine. For the scripture saith, Thou shalt not muzzle the ox that treadeth out the corn. And, THE LABOURER IS WORTHY OF HIS REWARD.

1 Timothy 5:17-18

2\. Rewards are coming. Expect rewards when you are visited by God. When there is a visitation of God in your life, there will also be rewards. The coming of the Son of man is always with rewards.

For the Son of man shall come in the glory of his Father with his angels; and then he shall reward every man according to his works.

Matthew 16:27

3\. Rest is a reward for hard work. Hard work is often rewarded by lots of rest. That they may rest from their labours! God indeed has rest for His servants. However, this book will reveal that God does not only give rest but actually gives more work after the period of rest.

Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus. And I heard a voice from heaven saying unto me, Write, Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth: Yea, saith the Spirit, that THEY MAY REST FROM THEIR LABOURS; AND THEIR WORKS DO FOLLOW THEM.

Revelation 14:12-13

4\. God Himself is a reward. Hard work is rewarded by God Himself coming to you as a reward. May you enjoy God’s reward of Himself. When you have God, you have everything and you do not need anything else.

After these things the word of the Lord came unto Abram in a vision, saying, Fear not, Abram: I AM thy shield, and THY EXCEEDING GREAT REWARD.

Genesis 15:1

5\. The reward for hard work is more work. One of the great rewards for hard work is more hard work. Even in politics, people who work hard as agitators in opposition are rewarded with jobs and more opportunities to work when they do win an election.

In the next chapters of this book, we will find the principle that “the reward for hard work is more work”.
