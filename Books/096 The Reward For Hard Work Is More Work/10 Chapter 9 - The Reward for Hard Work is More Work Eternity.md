### Chapter 9\

The Reward for Hard Work is More Work: Eternity\
---------------------------------------------------------------------

And he shewed me a pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb. In the midst of the street of it, and on either side of the river, was there the tree of life, which bare twelve manner of fruits, and yielded her fruit every month: and the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations. And there shall be no more curse: but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; AND HIS SERVANTS SHALL SERVE HIM. And they shall see his face; and his name shall be in their foreheads. And there shall be no night there; and they need no candle, neither light of the sun; for the Lord God giveth them light: and they shall reign for ever and ever. And he said unto me, These sayings are faithful and true: and the Lord God of the holy prophets sent his angel to shew unto his servants the things which must shortly be done. Behold, I come quickly: blessed is he that keepeth the sayings of the prophecy of this book.

Revelation 22:1-7

Many good things will happen when we enter eternity.

There will be a wonderful river of life!

There will be a wonderful tree of life available to us all!

There will be healing for the nations!

There will be twelve manner of fruits available to us every month!

We shall see His face!

His Name shall be in our foreheads!

There shall be no night anymore!

There will be no need of the sun because of the glory of God! We shall reign forever and ever!

We are all headed for this eternity experience. However, one more glorious revelation of eternity is that we shall be made servants. We shall have more work to do in heaven. The Bible says, “And his servants shall serve him”!

“And there shall be no more curse: but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; and his servants shall serve him:” (Revelation 22:3)

This scripture shows how we will be His servants in eternity. What an honour! His servants shall serve Him. We shall be in the new Jerusalem and we shall be His servants over there. The reward for all the obedience and all the ministry we have performed on earth will be to become His servants.

The reward for hard work is more work!

Let the revelation of “more work” guide you until you get into eternity!

The reward for hard work is indeed more work!

“And further, by these, my son, be admonished: of making many books there is no end; and much study is a weariness of the flesh.” Ecclesiastes 12:12
