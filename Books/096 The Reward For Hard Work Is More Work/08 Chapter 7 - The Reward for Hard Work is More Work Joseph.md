### Chapter 7\

The Reward for Hard Work is More Work: Joseph\
-------------------------------------------------------------------

And Joseph was brought down to Egypt; and Potiphar, an officer of Pharaoh, captain of the guard, an Egyptian, bought him of the hands of the Ishmeelites, which had brought him down thither. And the Lord was with Joseph, and he was a prosperous man; and he was in the house of his master the Egyptian. And his master saw that the Lord was with him, and that the Lord made all that he did to prosper in his hand. And Joseph found grace in his sight, and he served him: and he MADE HIM OVERSEER OVER HIS HOUSE, and all that he had he put into his hand. And it came to pass from the time that he had made him overseer in his house, and over all that he had, that the LORD blessed the Egyptian’s house for Joseph’s sake; and the blessing of the LORD was upon all that he had in the house, and in the field.

Genesis 39:1-5

1\. The reward for the hard work of Joseph as Potiphar’s slave, was to be given more work as Potiphar’s overseer.

Being made an overseer meant more responsibilities and more work for Joseph! God always rewards hard work with more work!

God has even more work for you. This is not the time to rest. The reward for hard work is more work. You will rest in the grave! Now is the time to accept new jobs, new missions and new visions.

See the hand of God at work in your life and ministry. Open your heart and accept the new work ahead.

AND JOSEPH FOUND GRACE IN HIS SIGHT, AND HE SERVED HIM: AND HE MADE HIM OVERSEER OVER HIS HOUSE, and all that he had he put into his hand. AND IT CAME TO PASS FROM THE TIME THAT HE HAD MADE HIM OVERSEER IN HIS HOUSE, and over all that he had, that the LORD blessed the Egyptian’s house for Joseph’s sake; and the blessing of the LORD was upon all that he had in the house, and in the field.

Genesis 39:4-5

2\. The reward for the hard work of Joseph as Potiphar’s overseer, was to be given more work as a prison overseer.

Being made the overseer in the prison meant more responsibilities and more work for Joseph. Joseph had worked hard as Potiphar’s overseer.

Keep looking ahead. Your life and ministry are going to be like Joseph’s. Another strange assignment will arise and you will be at the centre of it all. As you have been faithful in the little, God will give you much.

And Joseph’s master took him, and put him into the prison, a place where the king’s prisoners were bound: and he was there in the prison. But the Lord was with Joseph, and shewed him mercy, and gave him favour in the sight of the keeper of the prison. AND THE KEEPER OF THE PRISON COMMITTED TO JOSEPHS HAND ALL THE PRISONERS THAT WERE IN THE PRISON; AND WHATSOEVER THEY DID THERE, HE WAS THE DOER OF IT. The keeper of the prison looked not to any thing that was under his hand; because the Lord was with him, and that which he did, the Lord made it to prosper.

Genesis 39:20-23

3\. The reward for the hard work of Joseph as overseer of the prison, was to be made the overseer of Egypt.

Being made an overseer of the whole of Egypt meant more responsibilities and more work for Joseph. If you are faithful in prison work, God will give you much more. Being faithful in prison work means that you are faithful even where important people cannot be found. Faithful in prison means you are faithful in darkness and obscurity!

And Pharaoh said unto Joseph, FORASMUCH AS GOD HATH SHEWED THEE ALL this, there is none so discreet and wise as thou art: THOU SHALT BE OVER MY HOUSE, and according unto thy word shall all my people be ruled: only in the throne will I be greater than thou. And Pharaoh said unto Joseph, SEE, I HAVE SET THEE OVER ALL THE LAND OF EGYPT. And Pharaoh took off his ring from his hand, and put it upon Joseph’s hand, and arrayed him in vestures of fine linen, and put a gold chain about his neck; And he made him to ride in the second chariot which he had; and they cried before him, Bow the knee: and he made him ruler over all the land of Egypt.

Genesis 41:39-43
