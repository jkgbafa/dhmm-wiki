### Chapter 3\

The Reward for Hard Work is More Work: The Talents\
------------------------------------------------------------------------

For the kingdom of heaven is as a man travelling into a far country, who called his own servants, and delivered unto them his goods. And unto one he gave five talents, to another two, and to another one; to every man according to his several ability; and straightway took his journey.

Matthew 25:14-15

And so he that had received five talents came and brought other five talents, saying, Lord, thou deliveredst unto me five talents: behold, I have gained beside them five talents more. His lord said unto him, WELL DONE, THOU GOOD AND FAITHFUL SERVANT: THOU HAST BEEN FAITHFUL OVER A FEW THINGS, I WILL MAKE THEE RULER OVER MANY THINGS: enter thou into the joy of thy lord. He also that had received two talents came and said, Lord, thou deliveredst unto me two talents: behold, I have gained two other talents beside them. His lord said unto him, Well done, good and faithful servant; thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.

Matthew 25:20-23

The reward for hard work is more work. The servant, after doing a good work with the two talents was asked to rule over many things.

THE HARD WORK: USING THE TALENTS

Then he that had received the five talents went and traded with the same, and made them other five talents. And likewise he that had received two, he also gained other two.

Matthew 25:16-17

1\. The hard work is using your talents and using the gift of God.

The men who received the talents were indeed given a great job. Once you’ve been given a gift you must expect to work hard. It is not easy to use your talent. A lazy man cannot and will not find a place in the house of God. Gifted but lazy singers will not amount to much.

It takes hard work to use the gift of God. Many people live and die without using the gifts God has given them.

In 1988, God said to me “From today you can teach.” That was a commission to teach. I have had to work very hard since I heard those words. Without hard work you cannot fulfil your mission. Many gifted people are also lazy. It takes real, hard work to put your talents to use.

But his master answered and said to him, ‘You wicked, lazy slave, you knew that I reap where I did not sow and gather where I scattered no seed.

Matthew 25:26 (NASB95)

2\. The hard work is being a good person.

His lord said unto him, Well done, thou GOOD AND FAITHFUL SERVANT: thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.

Matthew 25:21

The men with the talents were called “good and faithful”. Using your talents requires you to become a good person. Without becoming a good person, you cannot use the talent God has given you.

Without holiness, you cannot see God! Without a good character you will not last long in the ministry! Developing goodness and staying good is very hard work.

3\. The hard work is being faithful with the talent and gift of God.

His lord said unto him, Well done, thou GOOD AND FAITHFUL SERVANT: thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.

Matthew 25:21

The men with the talents were called “good and faithful”. To be faithful is hard work because you need to exert great effort to remain constant. You need hard work to repeat yourself over and over again. It takes hard work to preach the same things faithfully for years.

It takes hard effort to remain reliable and unchangeable. The main work of a steward is to remain faithful and doggedly committed to his master’s instructions.

Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.

1 Corinthians 4:2

Most men will proclaim every one his own goodness: but a faithful man who can find?

Proverbs 20:6

THE REWARD FOR HARD WORK: BECOME A RULER

And so he that had received five talents came and brought other five talents, saying, Lord, thou deliveredst unto me five talents: behold, I have gained beside them five talents more. His lord said unto him, Well done, thou good and faithful servant: THOU HAST BEEN FAITHFUL OVER A FEW THINGS, I WILL MAKE THEE RULER OVER MANY THINGS: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.

Matthew 25:20-21

The reward for your work with the talents, is to be given the work of a ruler. To be a ruler is one of the most stressful jobs on earth. Most rulers of nations are stressed out, hardworking men. They develop grey hair prematurely as a result of their extreme stress. God is a rewarder of your extreme efforts at making use of the talents He has given to you.

God knows that there are many people who do not use their talents. He appreciates those who use their talents. As long as you are on this earth, remember that the reward for hard work is more work! It is time to learn about the work of a ruler and to get to the job of more work as a ruler.
