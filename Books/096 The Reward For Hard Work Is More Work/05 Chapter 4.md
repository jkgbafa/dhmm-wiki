### Chapter 4\

The Reward for Hard Work is More Work: A Certain Nobleman\
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

He said therefore, A certain nobleman went into a far country to receive for himself a kingdom, and to return. And he called his ten servants, and delivered them ten pounds, and said unto them, OCCUPY TILL I COME But his citizens hated him, and sent a message after him, saying, We will not have this man to reign over us. And it came to pass, that when he was returned, having received the kingdom, then he commanded these servants to be called unto him, to whom he had given the money, that he might know how much every man had gained by trading. Then came the first, saying, LORD, THY POUND HATH GAINED TEN POUNDS. AND HE SAID UNTO HIM, WELL, THOU GOOD SERVANT: BECAUSE THOU HAST BEEN FAITHFUL IN A VERY LITTLE, HAVE THOU AUTHORITY OVER TEN CITIES. And the second came, saying, Lord, thy pound hath gained five pounds. And he said likewise to him, Be thou also over five cities. And another came, saying, Lord, behold, here is thy pound, which I have kept laid up in a napkin:

Luke 19:12-20

THE WORK: OCCUPY TILL I COME

Occupy till I come! That is the work given to the servant of God. The reward for hard work is more work. The good work done with one pound gives you more work in ten cities.

1\. The work - Occupy till I come: The work of God is to occupy yourself and to busy yourself with anything in the kingdom. Mary busied herself about Jesus’ work.

Greet Mary, who bestowed much labour on us.

Romans 16:6

2\. The work - Occupy till I come: The work of God is to occupy many territories for Jesus.

To preach the gospel in the regions beyond you, and not to boast in another man’s line of things made ready to our hand.

2 Corinthians 10:16

THE REWARD FOR HARD WORK: AUTHORITY

1\. The reward for hard work is to be given authority.

And he said unto him, Well, thou good servant: because thou hast been faithful in a very little, HAVE THOU AUTHORITY over ten cities.

Luke 19:17

The reward for the work of occupying is to be given authority over ten cities.

“Authority” means hard work! Authority is not something you store away in the wardrobe. Authority means work! Having authority is to receive the work of exercising power over ungrateful and disloyal people. It means more hard work of exercising authority over wicked men.

Having authority is to have the job of commanding people, sending people and putting people to work. This means you have been made into an overseer. The best example of a man of authority was the centurion whose servant was healed. A centurion had authority over a hundred men. Do you think the centurion was working or resting? Indeed, he was a hardworking man because of the authority he had. When God gives you authority over ten cities, He is giving you a very great job.

For I also am a man set under authority, having under me soldiers, and I say unto one, Go, and he goeth; and to another, Come, and he cometh; and to my servant, Do this, and he doeth it.

Luke 7:8

2\. The reward for hard work is to be made an overseer.

And he said unto him, Well, thou good servant: because thou hast been faithful in a very little, HAVE THOU AUTHORITY OVER TEN CITIES.

Luke 19:17

After doing the work of occupying, you will be rewarded with overseeing ten cities and with having great authority. Welcome to the new job of being an overseer. An overseer is a supervisor. God bless you, and congratulations for being made an overseer. What an honour to be given the job of supervising people. You have been rewarded with hard work!

Here is a trustworthy saying: Whoever aspires to be an overseer desires a noble task.

1 Timothy 3:1 (NIV)
