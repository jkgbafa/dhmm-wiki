### Chapter 5\

The Reward for Hard Work is More Work: The Faithful Man\
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much: and he that is unjust in the least is unjust also in much. If therefore ye have not been faithful in the unrighteous mammon, who will commit to your trust the true riches? And if ye have not been faithful in that which is another man’s, who shall give you that which is your own?

Luke 16:10-12

THE HARD WORK: BE FAITHFUL WITH SMALL THINGS

He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much: and he that is unjust in the least is unjust also in much.

Luke 16:10

The work of God consists of many little jobs. If you are proud, you will despise the many little chores that make up the work of God. The work of God is to be diligent with the little errands you are asked to perform. Truly, the ministry is made up of many little odd jobs! Once you are too big to do little jobs you cannot be a part of the kingdom ministry.

The reward for hard work is more work. The reward for good work is more work. The reward for working well with small things is only to be given bigger things to work on.

THE REWARD FOR HARD WORK: TO BE GIVEN MANY THINGS

He that is faithful in that which is LEAST is faithful also in MUCH: and he that is unjust in the least is unjust also in much.

Luke 16:10

The reward for hard work with the least, is to be given more work with much.

God will thank you for your efforts with the little church, by giving you a big church to work with. That is why you must not think of retiring or following delusions. Keep pressing on in the ministry. God has plans to give you more hard work which He is going to expect you to be faithful with.

THE HARD WORK: TO BE FAITHFUL WITH UNRIGHTEOUS MAMMON

If therefore ye have not been FAITHFUL IN THE UNRIGHTEOUS MAMMON, who will commit to your trust the true riches?

Luke 16:11

When you are given a secular job, your ability to perform it faithfully is a great accomplishment in the realm of the spirit. Your accomplishments in the secular world give you a good report. A good report from your secular work is a necessary qualification for the ministry. This is why successful lay persons who excel in their secular professions often make excellent full-time pastors.

THE REWARD FOR HARD WORK: TO BE GIVEN TRUE RICHES

If therefore ye have not been faithful in the unrighteous mammon, who will COMMIT TO YOUR TRUST THE TRUE RICHES?

Luke 16:11

The reward for your good work with secular finances is to be given true riches to handle. The reward for being a hard and faithful worker with secular money is to be given real wealth (true riches). The reward for hard work in the secular world is to be given more work in the church.

God will thank you for your efforts with secular things. Your secular work is something that is giving you credentials for ministry work. That’s how you must think about your secular work.

God has seen all your faithfulness in secular things and is rewarding you with more work in the ministry.

THE HARD WORK: TO BE FAITHFUL WITH THAT WHICH IS SOMEONE ELSE’S

And if ye have not been faithful in that which is another man’s, who shall give you that which is your own?

Luke 16:12

A great qualification for reward is to be constant and faithful with things that are clearly not your own. When you are able to work hard and energetically with something that does not belong to you, it reveals a good spirit.

Most women can only love their own children. Most men can only work hard and fast with their own money and their own possessions. This is why working in the civil service and for the government brings out the worst in mankind. Government work is like working in no man’s land.

THE REWARD FOR HARD WORK: TO BE GIVEN YOUR OWN

The reward for hard work with a ministry that does not belong to you, is to be given some more work with that which belongs to you. God is grateful to you for your efforts with the things that did not belong to you. Your work with other people’s money and ministry will become your credentials for future ministry.

God has seen all your faithfulness as an assistant, as a driver, as a worker and is rewarding you not with rest, but with more hard work in your own ministry.
