### Chapter 7\

The Test of the Wilderness\
------------------------------------------------

All the commandments that I am commanding you today you shall be careful to do, so that you may live and increase, and go in and take possession of the land which the Lord swore to give to your forefathers. And YOU SHALL REMEMBER ALL THE WAY WHICH THE LORD YOUR GOD HAS LED YOU IN THE WILDERNESS THESE FORTY YEARS, IN ORDER TO HUMBLE YOU, PUTTING YOU TO THE TEST, to know what was in your heart, whether you would keep His commandments or not.

Deuteronomy 8:1-2 (NASB)

Before you see the promised land of milk and honey, there is a wilderness for you to go through! This is one of the greatest tests of Israel, and it is one of the tests of the righteous.

In this wilderness test, the key lesson is in the area of humility. Without humility, you will not survive even one year in the wilderness.

In the wilderness, you have to accept lowness, lowliness of mind and subservience as a lifestyle. Without accepting to be a servant, you will not make it through the wilderness. God has milk and honey for everyone. You can have your share of God’s glory, but before that, you must endure the harsh wilderness test of humility.

The wilderness is a place of shortages.

The wilderness is a place of heat.

The wilderness is a place of discomfort.

There are no soft cushions or air conditioners in the wilderness.

Lovers of themselves, lovers of comforts, and lovers of riches will not make it through a wilderness.

John the Baptist was a man of the wilderness! Jesus described him in a way that should make everyone understand what it means to be a man who survives the test of the wilderness.

And as they departed, Jesus began to say unto the multitudes concerning John, What went ye out into the wilderness to see? A reed shaken with the wind? But what went ye out for to see? A man clothed in soft raiment? behold, they that wear soft clothing are in kings’ houses. But what went ye out for to see? A prophet? yea, I say unto you, and more than a prophet.

Matthew 11:7-9

By the time you come out of the wilderness test, you will no longer be a softy! You will no longer be bendable and shakeable by winds of change and the winds of public opinion!

1\. The test of the wilderness is a test of humility.

For whether is greater, he that sitteth at meat, or he that serveth? is not he that sitteth at meat? but I am among you as he that serveth.

Luke 22:27

The wilderness test is a test of humility!

In the wilderness, you cannot have all your comforts and little pleasures. God will take you through the test of becoming a servant and becoming a child again.

Most grown-ups cannot become children again. However, in your quest to become great servants of God, you will have to become a child again. It is this important devolution into childlikeness that is necessary for your ministry.

Also, you will have to become a servant to many. Jesus was a servant to us! He was amongst us as a servant - that is truly amazing! Yet, we want to be masters, lords and the first in everything. May God help us all to pass the test of humility!

The test of the wilderness is a test of humility!

2\. The test of the wilderness is a test of obedience.

And the Lord God commanded the man, saying, Of every tree of the garden thou mayest freely eat: But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it: for in the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die.

Genesis 2:16-17

It is important to pass the tests of obedience, which are all wrapped up in the test of the wilderness! If you are not obedient in small things and in big things, you will not be able to survive the wilderness test.

In the wilderness, you will see and encounter many things which are dangerous and life-threatening.

In the wilderness, there will be small things like snakes, scorpions and food shortages.

In the wilderness, there will also be big things like armies of enemy nations attacking you and threatening your very existence.

You will notice that the Israelites had to obey God in small matters relating to food and water, and they also had to obey God in how to defeat the advancing armies of the Philistines and Amalekites.

Be obedient! Do not think too much before you obey! You will discover that your opinion is not so important in the scheme of things. Learn to love and obey and you will see great improvements and changes in your life!

3\. The test of the wilderness is a test of time.

Fear none of those things which thou shalt suffer: behold, the devil shall cast some of you into prison, that ye may be tried; and ye shall have tribulation ten days: be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee a crown of life.

Revelation 2:10

There is a time for every test. No examination goes on endlessly. The test mentioned above in the Book of Revelations was a test that lasted only ten days. The wilderness test lasted forty years! Forty years is a long time to be tested.

There are tests from God that are simply a test of endurance - whether you can go the long haul or not! God will allow you to be tested with time and to see how long you can and will hold out in obedience. This is why you are encouraged to be faithful and obedient until death.

To be faithful for forty years means that you will have to endure something for your entire life, without any breaks or reliefs at all. That is what it means! Forty years is the lifetime of an adult. God Himself is allowing the test to carry on till your whole life is over.
