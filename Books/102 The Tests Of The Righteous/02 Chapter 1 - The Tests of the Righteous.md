### Chapter 1\

The Tests of the Righteous\
------------------------------------------------

YET, LORD OF ARMIES, WHO TESTS THE RIGHTEOUS, Who sees the mind and the heart; Let me see Your vengeance on them, For to You I have disclosed my cause.

Jeremiah 20:12 (NASB)

This book is to reveal to you that righteous people are subject to special testing, temptations and trials.

One would have thought that tests are reserved for the wicked and the disobedient. But no! The tests of the righteous do not happen because you are a bad person. God has tests reserved for you, JUST BECAUSE YOU ARE A RIGHTEOUS MAN!

THE LORD TESTS THE RIGHTEOUS and the wicked, And His soul hates one who loves violence.

Psalm 11:5 (NASB)

These tests do not happen because you have sinned or are disobedient. The tests of the righteous happen because you are a good person. The tests are happening because you are a righteous man. It is because you are on the path of righteousness that God has decided to test you.

Why would God do something like that and subject good people to tests?

1\. The tests of the righteous will refine your mind and your heart.

Vindicate me, Lord, for I have walked in my integrity, And I have trusted in the Lord without wavering. EXAMINE ME, LORD, AND PUT ME TO THE TEST; REFINE MY MIND AND MY HEART. For Your goodness is before my eyes, And I have walked in Your truth.

Psalm 26:1-3 (NASB)

When a man of God is refined, he is a finer, more polished and more elegant version of his former self. To refine something is to improve it by inserting finer distinctions and superior elements into it. The scripture states clearly that tests serve to refine a person! Indeed, your righteous heart and mind will be refined as you keep passing the tests that are destined for the righteous!

2\. The tests of the righteous will refine you like silver.

For YOU HAVE PUT US TO THE TEST, GOD; YOU HAVE REFINED US AS SILVER IS REFINED.

Psalm 66:10 (NASB)

Silver refining is typically the final process in the production of high-purity silver, suitable for sale in the market. The refining of silver is associated with purity and typically needs to meet a minimum of 99.9% purity units. In most cases, 99.99% purity is required, and in other cases, 99.999% purity is required, depending on the end-use.

The words of the LORD are pure words: as silver tried in a furnace of earth, purified seven times.

Psalm 12:6

The number “seven” in Scripture represents “perfection,” “finished,” or “complete.” Typically, refining silver once, leaves it fairly pure. If you were refining silver and repeated the process seven times, it would become wholly pure. Seven times refining refers to the intensely pure standard that God is bringing you to.

3\. The tests of the righteous will refine you like gold.

But He knows the way I take; WHEN HE HAS PUT ME TO THE TEST, I WILL COME OUT AS GOLD.

Job 23:10 (NASB)

What does it mean that God refines us like gold? How is gold purified by fire? Indeed, the value of gold is realised when gold is purer. Therefore, the goldsmith has to refine the ore first.

Gold melts at around 1000 degrees Celsius. Gold is very dense, so the impurities tend to come to the surface as the gold melts. Those impurities are then scraped off, and the gold is cooled. Through the process of burning and heating with fire, the gold comes out purer and much more valuable.

That is how you come out of the fires of testing. You come out purer and more valuable!

1\. Refining you as gold means you will come out of your tests purer. When you are refined as gold, the impurities in your life come floating upwards and away from you.

2\. Refining you as gold means you will come out more valuable. The purer the gold is, the more valuable it is!

3\. Gold is a “noble” metal, meaning that it does not rust or lose its shine. When you are refined as gold, you will never be corrupted or lose your shine.

4\. Gold is the only yellow metal. When you are refined as gold, you become a unique, one-of-a-kind servant of God.

5\. Gold is one of the heaviest and densest of all metals in the Periodic Table; a cubic foot weighs more than half a ton. When you are refined gold, you are weighty! To be as weighty as gold is to become very important and to exert influence.

6\. Pure gold melts at 1064.43°C and boils at 2856.1 °C. When you are refined as gold, it will be almost impossible to destroy you. A temperature of 2856 degrees Celsius is needed to make you boil, and that temperature is very difficult to attain.

There are seven tests that you will encounter as a servant of God. Expect to experience all these seven tests of the righteous: the test of Marah, the test of war, the test of the Sabbath, the test of manna, the test of Mount Sinai, the test of the wilderness and the test of the slanderer. Understand each of the tests of the righteous and overcome them all.
