### Chapter 4\

The Test of the Sabbath\
---------------------------------------------

Then the Lord said to Moses, “BEHOLD, I WILL RAIN BREAD FROM HEAVEN FOR YOU; AND THE PEOPLE SHALL GO OUT AND GATHER A DAY’S PORTION EVERY DAY, SO THAT I MAY TEST THEM, whether or not they will walk in My instruction. On the sixth day, when they prepare what they bring in, it will be twice as much as they gather daily.” So Moses and Aaron said to all the sons of Israel, “At evening you will know that the Lord has brought you out of the land of Egypt; and in the morning you will see the glory of the Lord, for He hears your grumblings against the Lord; and what are we, that you grumble against us?”

Exodus 16:4-7 (NASB)

The test of the Sabbath is a test of your ability to trust God and to be able to rest in Him. Your ability to rest and to trust is important in your service to God.

1\. The test of the Sabbath brings out your WILLINGNESS TO TRUST MORE IN GOD THAN IN YOUR WORK.

But the seventh day is the sabbath of the LORD thy God: IN IT THOU SHALT NOT DO ANY WORK, THOU, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates:

Exodus 20:10

Indeed, it takes faith to believe that God will take care of you even when you do not work.

2\. The test of the Sabbath brings out your WILLINGNESS TO REST.

And he said unto them, This is that which the Lord hath said, TOMORROW IS THE REST OF THE HOLY SABBATH unto the Lord: bake that which ye will bake to day, and seethe that ye will seethe; and that which remaineth over lay up for you to be kept until the morning.

Exodus 16:23

Many people cannot sit idle without working. They must work, work, work. It is not restful to be with such people. You must find time to rest.

3\. The test of the Sabbath brings out your WILLINGNESS TO BE HOLY AND SPECIAL.

Six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day THERE SHALL BE TO YOU AN HOLY DAY, a sabbath of rest to the LORD: whosoever doeth work therein shall be put to death.

Exodus 35:2

“Holy” means “special for a purpose.” Perhaps the most distinguishing feature of the Jews today is their holy observance of the Sabbath. Experiencing the Sabbath on a Saturday and refusing, worldwide, to do any work on that holy day has separated the Jews from every other nation and kept them as a holy, special people.

The test of the Sabbath brings out your willingness to be different and special to God. It brings out your ability to be different, odd and unique for God’s sake. Are you ready to be different because of your calling to God’s work?

4\. The test of the Sabbath brings out your WILLINGNESS TO BE OBEDIENT.

But the children rebelled against me: They did not follow my decrees, they were not careful to keep my laws, of which I said, “THE PERSON WHO OBEYS THEM WILL LIVE BY THEM,” AND THEY DESECRATED MY SABBATHS. So I said I would pour out my wrath on them and spend my anger against them in the wilderness.

Ezekiel 20:21 (NIV)

The test of the Sabbath reveals whether you are obedient or not. An obedient person has many blessings, but disobedience brings in the curse. There are many logical reasons to disobey the Sabbath. Today, many nations have no Sabbath whatsoever. Shops and businesses are open and fully operational for seven consecutive days a week. God will bless you when you pass the test of keeping His Sabbath.
