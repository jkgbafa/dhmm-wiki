### Chapter 8\

The Test of the Slanderer\
-----------------------------------------------

THEN JESUS was led (guided) by the \[Holy\] Spirit into the wilderness (desert) to be TEMPTED (TESTED AND TRIED) BY THE DEVIL \[SLANDERER\].

Matthew 4:1 (AMPC)

The test of the slanderer is one of the principal tests of the righteous. Jesus was righteous but He was tested with the tests of a slanderer. The test of your encounter with a slanderer is one of the tests of a righteous person. Good people are slandered all the time! God’s servants are slandered all the time! You will be slandered if you are working for God. Jesus was slandered terribly till He died on the cross.

Jesus endured the slanderer, and so will everyone who truly serves the Lord. It is a severe and painful test but you must pull through.

Jesus was tested by the devil (slanderer). The word “devil” in the verse above, is translated as “slanderer, traducer, speaking maliciously and falsely, a false accuser, accusing falsely, a calumniator and one who defames.”

The test of a slanderer is to endure satan personified. A slanderer is an accuser. Satan is an accuser. A slanderer is someone who attempts to discredit and damage a person by directly accusing him of many things. A slanderer will speak out many outrageous and preposterous accusations.

One of the greatest tests of ministry is the test of enduring people who accuse you falsely or maliciously, and who spread wicked and malicious tales about you. This test is complicated. It has many aspects to it. The test of the slanderer is one of the principal tests of the righteous. At a certain level of ministry, you will be forced to go through the test of a slanderer.

What is the Test of a Slanderer?

1\. The test of a slanderer is the test of enduring a bitter personality who campaigns to lower the value and admiration that others have of you.

To endure a slanderer is to endure a demon-possessed and extremely bitter personality who seeks to destroy you by informing people about confidential things, tales, secrets, stories and rumours which he feels will damage you.

A slanderer is a jealous person who hates the success and leadership others have and who goes about intentionally sending malicious information to spread rumours, scandals, and unverifiable stories about people who otherwise have a good reputation.

2\. The test of a slanderer is to endure a disgruntled former employee who seeks to destroy his former place of employment by destroying its good image.

A disgruntled former employee-turned-slanderer is usually a low or a poor performer who seeks to justify to the public why he lost his job by spreading false stories about his former employer.

3\. The test of a slanderer is to endure a preacher who, instead of preaching and testifying about Jesus, spends his time and effort maliciously sharing shameful and unfortunate news about fellow Christians.

A slanderer’s aim is not to win souls, but to inform the church of the mistakes of others so that they can never minister again to anyone. A slanderer has the aim of destroying the ministry of someone who has a good and fruitful ministry by spreading messages on social media, through texts, stories, scandals, and questions about the life, character and ministry of that person. Slanderers attack God’s servants by intentionally spreading as much slime and scandalous information about them as possible. This is done to lower people’s estimation of God’s servants and to therefore, reduce the scope of their ministry.

4\. The test of a slanderer is to endure a campaigner who goes around from person to person to convince them of the evil and unacceptable nature of the person they honour.

A slanderer is a smear campaigner. He goes around from house to house with his vitriolic stories and information. A slanderer is a campaigner and he will call person after person diligently to give information he believes will damage the calling and ministry of the one he hates.

5\. The test of a slanderer is to endure someone who has lost all power to harm the one he hates, so he resorts to spreading lies, part truths, unverifiable stories and suspicions about God’s servant.The slanderer hopes that what he says will damage God’s servant.

6\. The test of a slanderer is to endure a man who portrays a church or ministry as less acceptable, less commendable, less important and less impressive than people generally think it is. A slanderer is someone who attempts to belittle ministers of the gospel. A slanderer is someone who wants to cast the servant of God in a bad light.

7\. The test of a slanderer is to endure someone who writes, publishes, spreads and circulates malicious and injurious stories about a person. A slanderer is someone who attacks another person’s character. The test of a slanderer is to endure someone who attempts to discredit and damage God’s honoured and anointed servants by speaking and writing against them using insinuations and innuendos.

8\. The test of a slanderer is to endure someone who spreads shame. The test of a slanderer is to endure a person who is a spreader of shame. A slanderer is someone who writes or speaks in such a way as to shame and dishonour a person. A slanderer aims to lower the estimation of God’s chosen servant and to depreciate the Lord’s anointed in the eyes of the public.

9\. The test of a slanderer is to endure someone who misrepresents you to the general public. A slanderer writes and speaks incorrectly, falsely, improperly and unsatisfactorily in order to achieve his aim of hurting you.

10\. The test of a slanderer is to endure someone who speaks unfavourably about you even though you are honoured by others. A slanderer does this often in order to make people dislike or disapprove of you.

11\. The test of the slanderer is the test of your ability to endure lies and slander without reacting.

Apostle Paul had lots of evil reports going on around him; but the suspicion created by slander did not matter much to him. He simply continued preaching and travelling.
