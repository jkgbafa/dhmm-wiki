### Chapter 2\

The Test of Marah\
---------------------------------------

Then Moses led Israel from the Red Sea, and they went out into the wilderness of Shur; and they went three days in the wilderness and found no water. WHEN THEY CAME TO MARAH, THEY COULD NOT DRINK THE WATERS OF MARAH, BECAUSE THEY WERE BITTER; for that reason it was named Marah. So the people grumbled at Moses, saying, “What are we to drink?” Then he cried out to the Lord, and the Lord showed him a tree; and he threw it into the waters, and the waters became sweet. There He made for them a statute and regulation, AND THERE HE TESTED THEM.

Exodus 15:22-25 (NASB)

What is the Test of Marah?

The test of Marah is the test of life’s bitter experiences. The test of Marah comes early in life, because the bitterness and disappointments of life start quite early.

The test of Marah will often differentiate people quite early in the race of life. This is because the bitterness of life happens so early that two groups soon emerge: grateful, happy, flowing people and the discontented, unhappy, murmurers.

The murmurers start to fail after Marah, whilst the thankful and grateful start rising after the test of Marah.

1\. Marah is an experience that affects your whole life.

Jesus answered and said unto her, Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again: But whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst; but the water that I shall give him shall be in him a well of water springing up into everlasting life.

John 4:13-14

Jesus said, “Whoever drinks this water shall have life.” Water represents life! All through the universe, water represents life! No water, no life!

God brought the people to drink water and to test them with water. Water represents life! Therefore, the test of Marah is your test at the waters of life on this earth! It is a test of the things that happen in life. This life is full of bitter experiences, pain, hurts and disappointments. These real-life experiences constitute your test at the waters of Marah.

2\. A test of Marah is an experience God Himself leads you to.

God is the one who brought the Israelites to Marah. It was not the devil who brought them to Marah. It was not demons that brought the Israelites to the bitter waters of Marah. It was the Holy Spirit who led them there. At Marah, you will not even be able to blame yourself for what has happened. God is the one who brought you straight to the bitter waters of Marah!

3\. A test of Marah is a test of your responses to bitter experiences.

There are many tests of disappointment and disillusionment! Many things happen to your heart when things are not as they should be. My father once told me, “It’s not as it seems.” I realised later that he was trying to tell me something important.

People who are disappointed are unhappy. Someone becomes bitter, angry and unhappy because he cannot forget bad things that have happened in the past. Some people are bitter because they simply cannot overcome their memories from childhood. Some cannot forget what they have been through and simply fail the tests of life.

On the other hand, some people suffer terribly over the years, but do not become bitter.

Men are going to fail you! Expect it! That is the test of Marah. That is the test of bitter experiences in life and ministry.

There will be disappointments! There will be disappointments in business, in marriage, in church, in leadership, in friendships and in life. Bitter experiences are just ahead in your walk with God. It is God who is taking you there and not the devil!

Marah is a test of your reactions and responses to life! A test of Marah is a test of this life’s experiences. There are many bitter experiences in this life. Treachery and betrayal are bitter experiences. The deaths of loved ones are bitter experiences. Marriage has many bitter life experiences. Financial realities of life have many bitter experiences embedded in them. Marah is a test of your reactions and responses to life’s bitterness!

Life is full of many bitter experiences, and people react differently to bitter experiences! Some turn out to be terrible husbands or evil wives as a result of their poor reactions to the realities of life. Such people are failing badly at the test of Marah.

Some people become traitors when they are tested with life. Some people become thieves when tested with the bitter tests of lack and poverty. At the waters of Marah, the sheep are separated from the goats. The bad attitudes are separated from the good attitudes in the waters of Marah! And it is Almighty God Himself who brings you to the test of Marah to see what is in you.
