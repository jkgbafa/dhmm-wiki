### Chapter 6\

The Test of Mount Sinai\
---------------------------------------------

And all the people were watching and hearing the thunder and the lightning flashes, and the sound of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking; and when the people saw it all, they trembled and stood at a distance. Then they said to Moses, “SPEAK TO US YOURSELF AND WE WILL LISTEN; BUT DO NOT HAVE GOD SPEAK TO US, OR WE WILL DIE!” However, Moses said to the people, “DO NOT BE AFRAID; FOR GOD HAS COME IN ORDER TO TEST YOU, AND IN ORDER THAT THE FEAR OF HIM MAY REMAIN WITH YOU, SO THAT YOU WILL NOT SIN.” So the people stood at a distance, while Moses approached the thick darkness where God was.

Exodus 20:18-21 (NASB)

Mount Sinai represents one of the saddest events to have ever occurred on earth! It represents the day that people rejected closeness to God! It represents the day that people did not want to see or hear from God Himself! It represents the day that humans rejected the presence of God and rejected a direct, personal interaction with God. These Israelites did not want to meet God themselves. They told Moses to deal with God on their behalf.

Perhaps, this event offended God so much that He rarely gives visions of His glory and holy Throne Room any more. Indeed, it is so difficult and so rare to have deep experiences of God Almighty today. Perhaps God has not forgotten how the Israelites rejected His presence. Let’s pray to be close to God! Our very lives depend on this closeness to the Lord.

Just wanting a pastor to meet God on your behalf is as offensive as the Israelites not wanting to speak to God.

I have had people who did not want to meet me but wanted to meet my assistant or someone else. They had heard various things about me and felt they didn’t want to meet me. That was offensive to me. It is distasteful when you refuse to meet an important person. “We don’t want to see him. We want to meet someone else.” “We are okay with another person.”

The test of Mount Sinai is where you are tested on whether you want to be close to God or not. Many people do not value closeness to God. That is a colossal mistake. God will test whether you desire to be close to Him. God will test you on whether you will pay the price to be close to Him or not.

Those who are close to God are those who make the effort to seek Him and find Him. The test of Mount Sinai is the test of whether you want to be close to God or not. The Israelites who came to Mount Sinai failed this test miserably! They wanted to see Moses but they didn’t want to meet with God.

What Does the Test of Mount Sinai Entail?

1\. The test of closeness is a test of your ability to draw near.

Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you. Cleanse your hands, ye sinners; and purify your hearts, ye double minded.

James 4:8

Some people simply cannot come close! By reflex, they just stay away from you. Some people, by reflex, take the furthest back seat in the auditorium. They are naturally tuned to stay away and to retreat from the light.

Are you like that? Learn from close people! Watch what they do to come close! Do not stay far away! Do not be one of the distant people who only say “hi” and “bye” all the time. There is an art to being close. Learn the art of being a close daughter or son.

2\. The test of closeness is a test of survival.

FOR, LO, THEY THAT ARE FAR FROM THEE SHALL PERISH: thou hast destroyed all them that go a whoring from thee. BUT IT IS GOOD FOR ME TO DRAW NEAR TO GOD: I have put my trust in the Lord GOD, that I may declare all thy works.

Psalm 73:27-28

They that are far from thee shall perish! What a statement! Indeed, your closeness is a test of your ability to survive in life and ministry.

Fight to be close to God and to those who are important to your life. Closeness to the important people in your life is critical.

It is important to be close to God!

It is important to be close to your father!

It is important to be close to your pastor!

It is important to be close to God’s servant!

They that are far from thee shall perish! Do you want to perish? Do you want to die? I’m sure you don’t! Rise and do what it takes to be close to your God and to those who matter.

Do not be like the Israelites who said “We don’t need or want to see God.” Many people perish in their ministries because they draw away from closeness to God. It is good to be near God!

But as for me, THE NEARNESS OF GOD IS GOOD FOR ME; I have made the Lord God my refuge, So that I may tell of all Your works.

Psalm 73:28 (NASB)

Since nearness to God is a good thing, you must fight to have it. How do you know that you are close to God? You are close to God when you talk to Him all the time. You are close to anyone you talk to all the time.

Pray without ceasing and God will talk to you without ceasing!

3\. The test of closeness is a test of sincerity.

And since we have a great priest over the house of God, LET US DRAW NEAR TO GOD WITH A SINCERE HEART AND WITH THE FULL ASSURANCE that faith brings, having our hearts sprinkled to cleanse us from a guilty conscience and having our bodies washed with pure water.

Hebrews 10:21-22 (NIV)

You cannot draw near to God without sincerity. God loves truth! God loves real things! Let us draw near to God with a sincere heart!

Sincerity speaks of freedom from deceit, hypocrisy, or duplicity. Sincerity is a type of truthfulness. Truth is essential if you are to draw close to God. Sincerity is essential! Inner truth is critical! Why is that? Because lies and deception are the hallmarks of the presence of devils.

You cannot go near God with the stains of deception and lies. As soon as you appear in the presence of God, you will smell of devils and it will not go well.

Get rid of hypocrisy because you cannot pretend to God! God is not a man that you can deceive easily.

I remember some of my pastors who tried so hard to deceive me. Indeed, they succeeded, very much, for some time. Why were they so successful? They were successful at deceiving me because I am a man.

A man is easy to deceive but God is not so easy to deceive! Do not bother trying to come to God with your insincerity.

Get free of your deceit, your hypocrisy and your duplicity! God sees everything! It is time to be close to God. The nearness of God is a good thing!

4\. The test of closeness is a test of apostleship.

Therefore this is what the Lord God says: “BEHOLD, I AM LAYING A STONE IN ZION, A TESTED STONE, A precious cornerstone for the foundation, firmly placed. The one who believes in it will not be disturbed.

Isaiah 28:16 (NASB)

The foundation of Zion is laid on a tested stone. The foundation of the church is also laid on a tested stone. A true apostle must be a well-tested stone. An apostle is a well-tested man. A man who lays a foundation must be well-tested. Paul was well-tested about his desire to be close to God! Paul revealed his true heart when he said he wanted to know God. Paul wrote half of the New Testament but still his great desire was to be close to God.

Yea doubtless, and I count all things but loss for the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord: for whom I have suffered the loss of all things, and do count them but dung, that I may win Christ, And be found in him, not having mine own righteousness, which is of the law, but that which is through the faith of Christ, the righteousness which is of God by faith: THAT I MAY KNOW HIM, and the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings, being made conformable unto his death;

Philippians 3:8-10

Paul’s great apostleship in ministry was linked to his closeness to God. The book of Philippians outlines Paul’s great commitment to knowing God and getting close to Him. We see a real apostle whose drive was not even to reach the world, but to attain a certain closeness to God!

I want to know God! I want to see God! I want to be found where He is!

What a vision! Few men of God have such clear spiritual goals of closeness to God! It is time to renew your visions for life and ministry.

Do not run away from God! Run to Him! Come closer to Him. He will bless you with His presence! Don’t fail the test of coming close to God.

5\. The test of closeness is a test of a psalmist.

O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee: my soul thirsteth for thee, my flesh longeth for thee in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is; To see thy power and thy glory, so as I have seen thee in the sanctuary.

Psalm 63:1-2

Singing songs is not the same as desiring to see God. Singing songs and worshipping is not the same as genuinely longing for His presence. When we have worshippers who have the desire of King David to be close to God, we will see the beauty of His presence even more.

All true worship leaders are close to God. I have always wanted to be a worship leader. Worship and thanksgiving shoot you faster into God’s presence than anything else.

Always remember how the Israelites offended God by not wanting to interact with Him personally! Always remember how the Israelites failed the test of Sinai!
