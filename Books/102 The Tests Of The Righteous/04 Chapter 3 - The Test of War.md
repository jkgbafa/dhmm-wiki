### Chapter 3\

The Test of War\
-------------------------------------

I IN TURN WILL NO LONGER DRIVE OUT FROM THEM ANY OF THE NATIONS WHICH JOSHUA LEFT WHEN HE DIED, IN ORDER TO TEST ISRAEL BY THEM, whether they will keep the way of the Lord to walk in it as their fathers did, or not. So the Lord allowed those nations to remain, not driving them out quickly; and He did not hand them over to Joshua.

Judges 2:21-23 (NASB)

Now these are the nations that the Lord left, to test Israel by them (that is, all the Israelites who had not experienced any of the wars of Canaan; ONLY IN ORDER THAT THE GENERATIONS OF THE SONS OF ISRAEL MIGHT BE TAUGHT WAR, those who had not experienced it previously). These nations are: the five governors of the Philistines and all the Canaanites and the Sidonians, and the Hivites who lived on Mount Lebanon, from Mount Baal-hermon as far as Lebo-hamath. THEY WERE LEFT TO TEST ISRAEL BY THEM, TO FIND OUT IF THEY WOULD OBEY THE COMMANDMENTS OF THE LORD, which He had commanded their fathers through Moses.

Judges 3:1-4 (NASB)

The scripture above is very clear as to why God did not wipe out all the enemies before Israel. The test of war is when God allows undefeated enemies to flourish around you so you can have someone to spar with.

God wants His people to grow up and learn how to fight and how to survive. God will allow some enemies to remain so you can have someone to fight with. This is the reason why your enemies are not simply struck down when they show up.

Today, every nation that has not been to war before has a “theoretical armed force”, which is probably more of a police force than a military one. An army that lacks experience is an army in theory. An armed force which has never been threatened, lacks something.

What is the Effect of the Test of War?

1\. The test of war brings out your strengths.

So these young men of the princes of the provinces came out of the city, and the army which followed them. And they slew every one his man: and the Syrians fled; and Israel pursued them: and Benhadad the king of Syria escaped on an horse with the horsemen. And the king of Israel went out, and smote the horses and chariots, and slew the Syrians with a great slaughter. And the prophet came to the king of Israel, and said unto him, GO, STRENGTHEN THYSELF, AND MARK, AND SEE WHAT THOU DOEST: FOR AT THE RETURN OF THE YEAR THE KING OF SYRIA WILL COME UP AGAINST THEE. And the servants of the king of Syria said unto him, Their gods are gods of the hills; therefore they were stronger than we; but let us fight against them in the plain, and surely we shall be stronger than they.

1 Kings 20:19-23

The Syrians felt that the Israelites would be weak in the plains because the Israelites seemed more confident and stronger in the hills.

You must allow your enemies to show you what to work on and how to develop the undeveloped aspects of your ministry. There will be great improvements, modifications and adjustments to your life and ministry as you take seriously the threats that come to you from your undefeated enemies.

Everybody has weaknesses. Everyone has undeveloped areas of ministry. War with your undefeated enemies will help you to see your weaknesses. War is good because it tests all the weaknesses you have and shows you clearly what you need to work on.

God allows certain wars in your life, not because you have sinned, but because He wants you to develop all the weak aspects of your ministry.

2\. The test of war cures passivity.

Cursed be he that doeth the work of the Lord deceitfully, and cursed be he that keepeth back his sword from blood.

Jeremiah 48:10

During the war, you will be forced to give all you can. Passivity and half-heartedness will be cured by the war itself. War is good because of what it brings out in you. Having enemies is good because it intensifies your drive and cures your phlegmatic personality and passivity. Passivity is permanently cured by the presence of a threatening enemy. Enemies wake up sleeping giants! Enemies make you active! Enemies make you have meetings! Enemies get you into the war mode!

3\. The test of war draws new followers and new support.

The greater the number and quality of formidable enemies you have, the greater the support you will generate. Indeed, the more you have enemies, the greater the influence you wield.

4\. The test of war exposes hidden enemies.

War causes traitors to be exposed! War exposes the presence of unfaithful and disloyal men. When our church was attacked by rebels, all the silently disloyal people came out of the woods and expressed their support for our enemies. I was happy to see the end of all these hidden moles and enemies around me. War is good because it uncovers the devils in your midst.

5\. The test of war exposes the unthankful and the ungrateful.

Because that, when they knew God, they glorified him not as God, NEITHER WERE THANKFUL; but became vain in their imaginations, and their foolish heart was darkened. Professing themselves to be wise, they became fools, And changed the glory of the uncorruptible God into an image made like to corruptible man, and to birds, and fourfooted beasts, and creeping things.

Romans 1:21-23

War exposes murmurers and complainers. War makes disgruntled employees pop out of the woodwork. Unthankfulness reveals monsters. All kinds of evil are created by the unthankful and the wicked. War brings out the worst in men.

6\. The test of war brings out your patience and faith in God.

And they worshipped the dragon which gave power unto the beast: and they worshipped the beast, saying, Who is like unto the beast? WHO IS ABLE TO MAKE WAR WITH HIM? And there was given unto him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies; and power was given unto him to continue forty and two months. And he opened his mouth in blasphemy against God, to blaspheme his name, and his tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven. And it was given unto him to make war with the saints, and to overcome them: and power was given him over all kindreds, and tongues, and nations. And all that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world. If any man have an ear, let him hear. He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword. HERE IS THE PATIENCE AND THE FAITH OF THE SAINTS.

Revelation 13:4-10

The beast made war against the saints, but God allowed the beast and the dragon to prevail against the saints. Why is that? God wanted to bring out two great qualities in His people: faith and patience. The test of war brings out patience in you because it takes years to win some wars. The test of war also brings out your faith in God. There are many wars which need the help of God in a real way.

Plead my cause, O Lord, with them that strive with me: fight against them that fight against me.

Psalm 35:1

For you to win certain wars, God must fight against those who fight against you. If God withholds His hand in war, your enemy will prevail against you.

7\. The test of war is a test of maturity.

Maturity comes when you survive treachery and betrayal. Betrayal is the most difficult thing to overcome. War is all about surviving the wickedness of people who pretend to be with you but are not. Treachery will make you “spiritually dizzy” and emotionally unstable.

War is good and enemies are necessary to make you mature. Jesus handled the betrayal of Judas with such calmness, knowing that Judas was just an accursed and doomed man. Jesus also handled the betrayal of Peter and the other disciples with great maturity and continued to work with them in spite of their mistakes. Jesus was not destroyed or defeated by betrayal and desertion. He handled it well and overcame all!

Those who are negatively affected by war and treachery become:

a\. Constantly uncertain

b\. Constantly hopeless

c\. Constantly indifferent

d\. Constantly emotionally flat

e\. Constantly paranoid

f\. Constantly prejudiced

g\. Constantly vindictive

War will make you experience all the feelings above, but you will have to overcome them all.

War causes people to be constantly uncertain and constantly hopeless. This is because war is genuinely depressing. After the Second World War, Winston Churchill, Britain’s Prime Minister at the time, told Billy Graham, a famous American evangelist, that he had no hope for this world! It is normal to feel hopeless after a war.

War makes you not care anymore until you become emotionally flat. War makes people constantly afraid that something terrible is going to happen. Many seasoned warriors are paranoid and even vindictive because they have been in wars. God allows you to go through wars so that you experience all these feelings.
