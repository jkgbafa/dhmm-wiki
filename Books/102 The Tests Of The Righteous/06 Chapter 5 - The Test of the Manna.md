### Chapter 5\

The Test of the Manna\
-------------------------------------------

In the wilderness it was He WHO FED YOU MANNA WHICH YOUR FATHERS DID NOT KNOW, IN ORDER TO HUMBLE YOU AND IN ORDER TO PUT YOU TO THE TEST, to do good for you in the end.

Deuteronomy 8:16 (NASB)

Exodus 16:15-29

God gave the children of Israel manna every day. Why did He do this? He wanted them to learn to come to Him EVERY DAY for sustenance.

This is why the manna stank when the children of Israel took more than they needed for one day. God wanted the people to come to Him every day. This is one of the greatest tests for all Christians and pastors. Do you go to God every day? Or do you just go to God when you are going to preach?

The test of the manna was given to see if man would depend on God’s word every single day. The test of the manna was given to see if man would love God every single day.

However, most humans do not want to see God or meet Him every day. Most Christians do not have a daily quiet time with God. But God wants to fellowship with us every day through His daily Word. What a difference there would be if we could only get a word from the Lord every day!

1\. The test of manna is a test of daily light.

Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.

John 8:12

Do you have light daily for this life? Do you know the way to go? You need a daily light! When a man gropes about in darkness, he often hurts himself by bumping awkwardly into hard and painful objects. How painful it is to hit your shin against a table! The pains and hurts we experience in our lives are because we have not taken the light of the Word as our daily guide.

Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.

Psalm 119:105

There will I make the horn of David to bud: I have ordained a lamp for mine anointed.

Psalm 132:17

What is this lamp that God has specially prepared for His anointed ones? It is the Word of God! The test of the daily manna is a test of your commitment to a daily, quality quiet time with God, where He speaks to you. That is why the Lord gave the children of Israel manna every day; to make them dependent on a daily Word from God.

2\. The test of manna is a test of investment.

For he that soweth to his flesh shall of the flesh reap corruption; but he that soweth to the Spirit shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting.

Galatians 6:8

A daily, good-quality quiet time guarantees a good daily investment in yourself. This is because a daily quiet time is the sowing of spiritual seeds into your spirit. The word of God is the great seed that you plant in your heart when you have a quiet time.

Now the parable is this: The seed is the word of God.

Luke 8:11

The seed of the word of God will yield good fruit in your life and ministry. Just as a word from God could cause a man to be born again and be changed, a daily word from God can bring about great changes to your life.

Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever.

1 Peter 1:23

3\. The test of manna is a test of fire.

Wherefore thus saith the Lord God of hosts, Because ye speak this word, behold, I WILL MAKE MY WORDS IN THY MOUTH FIRE, and this people wood, and it shall devour them.

Jeremiah 5:14

Some things can only be removed by a daily fire. The word of God is a fire. A daily manna is a dose of the daily fire! The daily manna lights a daily fire which burns away many undesirable things. When this fire is kindled daily, no evil can remain as part of your character. A daily dose of that fire will take care of every unwanted element in your life and ministry.

4\. The test of manna is a test of the hammer.

Is not my word like as a fire? saith the Lord; and like a hammer that breaketh the rock in pieces?

Jeremiah 23:29

A hammer is a tool for breaking things down. It breaks down all hard and resistant objects. In the same way, daily manna ensures that there are regular blows against resistant ideas and obstinate views. Because the daily manna leads to the breaking of evil things in our lives, a Christian who does not have quiet times often has a bad character. The strongholds in the lives of most Christians cannot be broken away by one powerful sermon. They need constant hammering. Anyone who does not have a regular quiet time has a lot of things in their character that may need to be broken down.

5\. The test of manna is a test of the sword.

For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.

Hebrews 4:12

A daily manna leads to the daily cutting away of unwanted aspects of our lives and character. The test of manna is the test of the daily sword. Will you allow the sword to swing and cut through every unwanted thing in your life?

You will notice that Christians who just listen to sermons on Sundays, but have no regular cutting away of evil things, are very different people from those who receive the daily manna.

6\. The test of manna is the test of growth.

Daily manna is daily spiritual food. Obedience to the manna will daily ensure you have good spiritual food like milk, bread, meat, fruit and honey.

a\. MILK

As newborn babes, desire the sincere milk of the word, that ye may grow thereby:

1 Peter 2:2

b\. BREAD

But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.

Matthew 4:4

c\. MEAT

For when for the time ye ought to be teachers, ye have need that one teach you again which be the first principles of the oracles of God; and are become such as have need of milk, and not of strong meat.

Hebrews 5:12

d\. HONEY

How sweet are thy words unto my taste! yea, sweeter than honey to my mouth!

Psalm 119:103

Churches today have thousands of undeveloped spiritual babies because they do not have a daily quiet time.

Every Christian must pass the test of the daily manna and enjoy real daily spiritual growth before it is too late.
