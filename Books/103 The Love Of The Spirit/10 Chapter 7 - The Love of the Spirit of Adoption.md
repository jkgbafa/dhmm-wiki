Chapter 7

# The Love of the Spirit of Adoption

For ye have not received the spirit of bondage again to fear; but ye have received THE SPIRIT OF ADOPTION, WHEREBY WE CRY, ABBA, FATHER. The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God.Romans 8:15–16

One of the lovely aspects of the Holy Spirit is that He is a spirit of adoption. His presence causes the adoption of sons and daughters and the creation of new super families. You will grow to love the Spirit because of this particular feature of His presence.

The Holy Spirit is the spirit of adoption. Adoption is where people become fathers and mothers to children they did not bring forth biologically speaking. The spirit of adoption causes men to identify as their sons. Where the spirit of adoption is working, people who do not have any biological connection begin to see and accept others as their parents or children. This is possible through the spirit of adoption.

An adopted child receives a new parent whom he otherwise would not have had. *The spirit of adoption is the Holy Spirit. He is moving around, causing people to identify people as fathers, causing people to identify sons and others to adopt daughters.*

1\. The Holy Spirit makes you identify your own father.

> And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth THE SPIRIT OF HIS SON INTO YOUR HEARTS, CRYING, ABBA, FATHER. Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ.Galatians 4:6–7

Through the anointing of the Holy Spirit, we call God Abba Father. It is only by the power of the Holy Spirit that you can come to the place where you call someone “Father”.

God sends His Holy Spirit to work on your heart so that you see Him as a father. God also gives the Holy Spirit so that we recognize certain individuals as fathers or sons. That is the spirit of adoption!

It is in every father to long for his child’s recognition of him as the father. God wants us to see Him in that unique loving role. The natural attitude of our hearts toward God is not that of sons. It takes the anointing of the Holy Spirit to really make you see God as your father.

You do not create sons by telling people to call you father. However, when the Spirit of His Son bears witness together with our spirits to our sonship, then we are filled and thrilled with the sense that we are sons. You will become a son or a daughter to anyone whom the Holy Spirit impresses you to relate to in that way.

2\. The spirit of adoption makes you live the life of a son.

It takes power to live as a son and practice proper sonship.

> For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.Romans 8:14

> But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:John 1:12

3\. The spirit of adoption leads you to the place where you enjoy sonship.

Unless the Holy Spirit leads you, you will never experience what it means to be the son of God.

> For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.Romans 8:14
