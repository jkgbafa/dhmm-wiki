Chapter 4

# The Love of the Person and Love of the Personality

And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.Genesis 1:26

1\. Love of the Person of the Holy Spirit

The beauty of the Holy Spirit is that He is a person who has a body that looks like a man. Let US make man in OUR image! God the Father, Son and Holy Spirit are US. We are made in THEIR image.

When you actually imagine the Holy Spirit as someone who is indeed in the image of a man, it helps you to talk to Him and commune with Him as a person. God’s image includes the Holy Spirit. We look like the Holy Spirit too because we are made in His image. The Holy Spirit is a real person.

> And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever; Even the Spirit of truth; whom the world cannot receive, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him: but ye know him; for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you.John 14:16–17

Jesus could not have used this language if the person coming to replace Him was simply an impersonal influence or power. One divine person was going and another divine person was coming to take His place.

2\. Love of the Personality of the Holy Spirit

A person has a personality. The Holy Spirit is a person and He has a personality. You must love the personality of the Holy Spirit! In the Bible, the characteristics of a personality are repeatedly ascribed to the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit has all the marks of a personality!

3\. The Spirit is a lovely personality we can commune with.

> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and THE COMMUNION OF THE HOLY GHOST, be with you all. Amen.2 Corinthians 13:14

We can converse and talk together with the Holy Spirit. To commune means to talk together with a deep level of intensity or intimacy. Imagine that we can actually have a conversation with the Holy Spirit!

4\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who knows many things about God.

> But God hath revealed them unto us by his Spirit: for the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God... THE THINGS OF GOD KNOWETH NO MAN, BUT THE SPIRIT OF GOD.1 Corinthians 2:10–11

5\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who has a will.

> But all these worketh that one and THE SELFSAME SPIRIT, dividing to every man severally AS HE WILL.1 Corinthians 12:11

The Holy Spirit has a will. He can decide to move and to manifest Himself if He wants. We must never forget that the lovely Holy Spirit has a will of His own. He can choose to reveal things to you or He can choose to be silent.

6\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who has a mind.

> And he that searcheth the hearts knoweth what is THE MIND OF THE SPIRIT, because he maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God.Romans 8:27

The Holy Spirit has a mind and therefore He thinks. He is thinking about you and about the things you have whispered to Him.

7\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who has love and who feels love.

> Now I beseech you, brethren, for the Lord Jesus Christ’s sake, and for THE LOVE OF THE SPIRIT, that ye strive together with me in your prayers to God for me;Romans 15:30

The Holy Spirit has love and therefore He can choose to love someone and be in love with someone. He loves the humble! He stays far from the proud!

8\. The Spirit is a lovely personality and a good person.

> THOU GAVEST ALSO THY GOOD SPIRIT TO INSTRUCT THEM, and withheldest not thy manna from their mouth, and gavest them water for their thirst.Nehemiah 9:20

9\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who can be tenderly and touchingly grieved by us.

> And GRIEVE NOT THE HOLY SPIRIT of God, whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption.Ephesians 4:30

This lovely personality can be grieved because He is full of love. It is when you love someone that the person can hurt you. The Spirit loves us very much, that is why He can be grieved.

10\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who can search for things.

> But God hath revealed them unto us by his Spirit: for THE SPIRIT SEARCHETH ALL THINGS, yea, the deep things of God.1 Corinthians 2:10

The Holy Spirit will search your heart and reveal to you the deep things which you are not even conscious of. This is why it is important to leave repenting people to the Holy Spirit. It is only the Holy Spirit who can search their hearts and convince them of who they really are.

11\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who speaks and guides the churches of God.

> He that hath an ear, let him hear WHAT THE SPIRIT SAITH UNTO THE CHURCHES.Revelation 2:7

The Holy Spirit is always saying something to the church. The more you love the Spirit, the more you will hear His voice concerning your church. The Spirit is not a silent or depressive personality. He speaks! He chats! He guides!

> For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.Romans 8:14

> As they ministered to the Lord, and fasted, the Holy Ghost said, Separate me Barnabas and Saul for the work whereunto I have called them.Acts 13:2

12\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who cries out within your heart.

> And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth THE SPIRIT OF HIS SON INTO YOUR HEARTS, CRYING, Abba, Father.Galatians 4:6

Learn to hear the cries of the Spirit! This is why you rarely doubt your salvation and the fact that God is your father. Your calm conviction and your strong faith in your Father, is the result of the Holy Spirit crying in your heart, “Abba Father.”

13\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who prays.

> Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but THE SPIRIT ITSELF MAKETH INTERCESSION FOR US with groanings which cannot be uttered.Romans 8:26

The Holy Spirit is not just an influence who makes us pray, He actually prays for us. The Holy Spirit helps you pray but also prays for you.

14\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who testifies and gives you evidence.

> But when the Comforter is come, whom I will send unto you from the Father, EVEN THE SPIRIT OF TRUTH, WHICH PROCEEDETH FROM THE FATHER, HE SHALL TESTIFY OF ME:John 15:26

The Holy Spirit loves giving evidence. Many things are the evidence that the Holy Spirit is giving to prove things He has spoken to you about.

15\. The Spirit is a lovely personality but He will be vexed by rebellion.

> BUT THEY REBELLED, AND VEXED HIS HOLY SPIRIT: therefore he was turned to be their enemy, and he fought against them.Isaiah 63:10

The Holy Spirit can become your enemy. The Holy Spirit is an enemy to rebels!

16\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who is sensitive to despisement and dishonour.

> Just think how much worse the punishment will be for those who have trampled on the Son of God... and have insulted and disdained the Holy Spirit who brings God’s mercy to us.Hebrews 10:29 (NLT)

The lovely Holy Spirit is very sensitive to despisement and dishonour. If you scoff at the Holy Spirit and His manifestations, you will find out that He will move very far from you. Be careful that you do not despise or dishonour the Holy Spirit.

17\. The Spirit is a lovely personality who is very sensitive to lies and deception.

> But Peter said, Ananias, why hath Satan filled thine heart to lie to the Holy Ghost?Acts 5:3

Lies are a direct product of satan. The Holy Spirit is very sensitive to lies. If you want to quench the Holy Spirit in your life, then tell a lie. You can choose for yourself: live with lying and deception and satan, or live with truth, honesty and the Holy Spirit.

18\. The Spirit is a lovely personality but He will not forgive blasphemy against Himself.

> Wherefore I say unto you, All manner of sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven unto men: BUT THE BLASPHEMY AGAINST THE HOLY GHOST SHALL NOT BE FORGIVEN UNTO MEN.Matthew 12:31–32

There is one sin that will not be forgiven: that is when you blaspheme or speak against the Holy Spirit! You must be very careful, lest you cross the line and insult the Holy Spirit Himself.
