Chapter 1

# The Love of the Beautiful Holy Spirit

Now I beg you, brothers, by our Lord Jesus Christ, and by the love of the Spirit, that you strive together with me in your prayers to God for me,Romans 15:30

The love of the Spirit is the love of the anointing. You may know about the Holy Spirit but it does not mean that you love Him. You can develop a great love for the Holy Spirit. Those who love the anointing are those who experience it. God does not give His treasures to people who do not value them.

It is important to love the Spirit. You must fall in love with the Holy Spirit. The way to fall in love with someone is to see the person and begin to like all the qualities that the person possesses.

The anointing is a beautiful thing and you must fall in love with the beauty of the anointing. The Holy Spirit is right before you! It is time to admire Him! It is time to notice Him and fall in love with Him. Indeed, the anointing is beautiful! You can love the Spirit!

There are beautiful features of the Holy Spirit that I want you to know about. You must think on the revelation of our beautiful Holy Spirit. Each of the virtues listed below makes the Holy Spirit altogether lovely and altogether beautiful.

1\. The lovely Holy Spirit is an eternal, everlasting presence.

> How much more shall the blood of Christ, who THROUGH THE ETERNAL SPIRIT offered himself without spot to God, purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God?Hebrews 9:14

The Holy Spirit is an eternal spirit! He is the anointing that is eternal and forever! O, the beauty of being an eternal anointing! The Holy Spirit is an eternal spirit. Once He is with you, He is with you forever! This is why the gifts and callings of God are without repentance. The lovely eternal spirit will not stay with you for a short season of your life. He is an eternal spirit.

2\. The lovely Holy Spirit is a lovely presence that is everywhere.

> WHITHER SHALL I GO FROM THY SPIRIT? Or whither shall I flee from thy presence? If I ascend up into heaven, thou art there: if I make my bed in hell, behold, thou art there. If I take the wings of the morning, and dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea; even there shall thy hand lead me, and thy right hand shall hold me.Psalm 139:7–10

The Holy Spirit is everywhere. O, the beauty of an anointing that is present everywhere! He is here right now. No matter where you are, you can experience the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit is in every country. People can only be seen and experienced in certain locations and under certain circumstances. The Holy Spirit can be experienced in every location and under every circumstance. You can experience the presence of the Holy Spirit at home, in bed, in the bathroom, in the kitchen, driving a car, working in the office, sitting in church, after church, on a plane and in a restaurant. He is everywhere! He is right by your side!

3\. The lovely Holy Spirit is beautiful because He is full of revelation.

The Holy Spirit is a spirit of revelation. Expect to have sudden bursts of light and new knowledge because of the Holy Spirit. Expect to have new knowledge when the Holy Spirit comes on you. Expect revelation of knowledge and wisdom as a sign that the Holy Spirit has come into your life.

> That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you THE SPIRIT OF WISDOM AND REVELATION in the knowledge of him:Ephesians 1:17

> BUT GOD HATH REVEALED THEM UNTO US BY HIS SPIRIT: for the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God. For what man knoweth the things of a man, save the spirit of man which is in him? even so the things of God knoweth no man, but the Spirit of God.1 Corinthians 2:10–11

> But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, HE SHALL TEACH YOU ALL THINGS, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.John 14:26

> I have yet many things to say unto you, but ye cannot bear them now. Howbeit when he, THE SPIRIT OF TRUTH, IS COME, HE WILL GUIDE YOU into all truth: for he shall not speak of himself; but whatsoever he shall hear, that shall he speak: and he will shew you things to come.John 16:12–13

4\. The lovely Holy Spirit is super powerful.

The great power of the Holy Spirit makes Him very attractive. Another name for the Holy Spirit is “the power of the highest.” When the Holy Spirit comes over you, the power of the highest overshadows you.

> And the angel answered and said unto her, THE HOLY GHOST SHALL COME UPON THEE, AND THE POWER OF THE HIGHEST SHALL OVERSHADOW THEE: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God.Luke 1:35

5\. The lovely Holy Spirit is creative.

The creative power of the Sprit makes Him even more beautiful. The creative power of the Holy Spirit makes Him even more lovely. When He is present, you can expect new ideas, new ways and new projects. The Holy Spirit creates new books, new songs, new ideas and new things. Notice how all forms of creation are ascribed to the Holy Spirit. Notice how new things are ascribed to the presence of the Holy Spirit.

> THOU SENDEST FORTH THY SPIRIT, THEY ARE CREATED: and thou renewest the face of the earth.Psalm 104:30

> THE SPIRIT OF GOD HATH MADE ME, and the breath of the Almighty hath given me life.Job 33:4

> In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth. And the earth was without form, and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep. AND THE SPIRIT OF GOD MOVED UPON THE FACE OF THE WATERS. And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.Genesis 1:1–3

6\. The lovely Holy Spirit is full of life.

You will notice how young people are attractive because they are full of life. Where the Holy Spirit is moving, it seems as though everything is full of life. The Holy Spirit is full of life! The Holy Spirit imparts life! It was the Holy Spirit who moved on the earth when God was bringing life to the planet Earth.

> And the Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and BREATHED INTO HIS NOSTRILS THE BREATH OF LIFE; AND MAN BECAME A LIVING SOUL.Genesis 2:7

Man is actually dust! If you look into a coffin after several years, you will just find some coffee-coloured grains of dust. In creation, God actually breathed into a pile of dust which turned into a man. It was the Holy Spirit that turned the dust into a living, breathing man.

> IT IS THE SPIRIT THAT QUICKENETH; THE flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.John 6:63

You must fall in love with the Holy Spirit because He brings so much life. When you go to a church where the Holy Spirit is moving, you will notice how alive it seems to be. Life is always a sign of the presence of the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit is the spirit that “quickeneth”! This means that the Holy Spirit is the spirit that gives life.

> But if the Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead dwell in you, he that raised up Christ from the dead SHALL ALSO QUICKEN YOUR MORTAL BODIES by his Spirit that dwelleth in you.Romans 8:11

7\. The lovely Holy Spirit is beautiful because of His prophecy.

> For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man: but holy men of God spake as they were MOVED BY THE HOLY GHOST.2 Peter 1:21

> THE SPIRIT OF THE LORD SPAKE BY ME, AND HIS WORD WAS IN MY TONGUE.2 Samuel 23:2–3

The Holy Spirit is beautiful because all prophecy comes through Him. All those who have spoken prophecies have done so by the Spirit of God.

> And I fell at his feet to worship him... for the testimony of Jesus is THE SPIRIT OF PROPHECY.Revelation 19:10

The Holy Spirit is called the spirit of prophecy. He is the spirit that generates prophecy. Prophecy is one of the most beautiful things in the church. It raises your natural speech to the level of a supernatural utterance.

8\. The lovely Holy Spirit is beautiful because He is always linked to the Father and linked to the Son.

The Holy Spirit is inseparably connected to the Father and to Jesus. This linkage, connection and fusion makes the Holy Spirit even more beautiful and attractive.

> Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:Matthew 28:19

In the Great Commission, we see the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit all working together.

> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Ghost, be with you all. Amen.2 Corinthians 13:14

In the sharing of the Grace, we see the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit all working together. The Holy Spirit is always linked to the Father and to the Son.

9\. The lovely Holy Spirit is beautiful because He is God Himself.

> But Peter said, Ananias, why hath Satan filled thine heart TO LIE TO THE HOLY GHOST... THOU HAST NOT LIED UNTO MEN, BUT UNTO GOD.Acts 5:3–4

The Holy Spirit is God! When Ananias lied to the Holy Spirit, he lied to God! To lie to God is to remind Almighty God of rebellious demons and their leader, Lucifer. Lying causes a horrible smell to arise in the presence of God. Christians should be careful as they lie and deceive people freely, thinking that they are getting away with it. There can be a sudden supernatural rejection and judgment of liars and deceivers.
