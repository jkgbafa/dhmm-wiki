Chapter 5

# The Anointing of Regeneration

For we ourselves also were sometimes foolish, disobedient, deceived, serving divers lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, and hating one another. But after that the kindness and love of God our Saviour toward man appeared, Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy HE SAVED US, BY THE WASHING OF REGENERATION, AND RENEWING OF THE HOLY GHOST;Titus 3:3–5

You will fall in love with the Holy Spirit because of the way He re-creates people and regenerates them. He is the anointing for regeneration!

1\. The anointing of regeneration is the power of the Holy Spirit to regenerate and to recreate a human being.

Our salvation is not an experience of education or re-education. Our salvation involves the regeneration of a human being. Our salvation is where we are re-born into this world by the Holy Spirit. That is why we claim to be born again.

Outward reform cannot replace the need for regeneration and being reborn. You must be born again is what Jesus said. You must have a new nature! You must become like Jesus Christ. Such a radical regeneration is only possible by the power of the mighty Holy Spirit.

2\. The anointing of regeneration is necessary to replace the old lust-filled nature with a new nature.

> Among whom also WE ALL HAD OUR CONVERSATION IN TIMES PAST IN THE LUSTS OF OUR FLESH, FULFILLING THE DESIRES OF THE FLESH AND OF THE MIND; and were by nature the children of wrath, even as others.Ephesians 2:3

Every human being is born into this world filled with horrible lusts of the flesh and of the mind. The fallen nature of man has to be completely changed. Through the power of the Holy Spirit, God’s own nature is given to replace the old, horrible nature.

3\. The anointing of regeneration is necessary to generate a brand-new divine nature.

> According as his divine power hath given unto us all things that pertain unto life and godliness... that by these ye MIGHT BE PARTAKERS OF THE DIVINE NATURE, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust.2 Peter 1:3–4

Through the power of the anointing of regeneration, a new nature is revealed — the divine nature which is filled with love, joy, peace, gentleness, goodness and patience.

> THE FRUIT OF THE SPIRIT IS LOVE, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.Galatians 5:22–23

4\. The anointing of regeneration is necessary to give birth to a spiritual person who can receive from God.

> But the natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God: for they are foolishness unto him: neither can he know them, because they are spiritually discerned.1 Corinthians 2:14

We come into this world with a mind that is blind to the truth of God. Only the Holy Spirit can make you to receive the things of God.

5\. The anointing of regeneration is necessary to realign our desires.

> FOR I KNOW THAT IN ME (THAT IS, IN MY FLESH,) DWELLETH NO GOOD THING: for to will is present with me; but how to perform that which is good I find not.Romans 7:15–22

We come into this world with affections and desires that are in opposition to God. Most of us have come into this world loving the things that we ought to hate and hating the things that we ought to love! Thank God for the spirit of regeneration!

6\. The anointing of regeneration is necessary to change our wills and our minds.

> BECAUSE THE CARNAL MIND IS ENMITY AGAINST GOD: for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be. So then they that are in the flesh cannot please God.Romans 8:7–8

> For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure.Philippians 2:13

7\. The anointing of regeneration causes us to be born again.

> Jesus answered, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God.John 3:3–5

The Holy Spirit is involved in the rebirth of Christians by the word of God. The word of God is the seed but the Spirit brings life into the seed.

> Now we have received, not the spirit of the world, but THE SPIRIT WHICH IS OF GOD; THAT WE MIGHT KNOW THE THINGS THAT ARE FREELY GIVEN TO US OF GOD.1 Corinthians 2:12–13

No amount of preaching or teaching, and no amount of mere study of the Word will regenerate a new character, unless the Holy Spirit works His work of regeneration. It is He and He alone who makes a man a new creature! What a lovely Holy Spirit we have!
