Chapter 8

# The Love of the Spirit of the Teacher

It is written in the prophets, AND THEY SHALL BE ALL TAUGHT OF GOD. Every man therefore that hath heard, and hath learned of the Father, cometh unto me.John 6:45

Loving the Spirit is loving the teacher. Fall in love with the Spirit of the teacher! The wonderful Holy Spirit is the spirit of truth! He teaches you many things and guides you in many ways. It is important for you to prepare yourself to learn from the Holy Spirit. The lovely Holy Spirit will teach you in ten distinct ways.

1\. The Spirit of the teacher has a voice that teaches you directly!

> But the anointing which ye have received of him abideth in you, and ye need not that any man teach you: BUT AS THE SAME ANOINTING TEACHETH YOU OF ALL THINGS, and is truth, and is no lie, and even as it hath taught you, ye shall abide in him.1 John 2:27

You may hear the voice of the Holy Spirit directly. The voice of the Spirit may sound like an impression, a perception or conviction. Many times, the voice of the Spirit is hidden, vague and unclear. But the voice of the Spirit may be even more clear and explicit at other times.

> Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils;1 Timothy 4:1

2\. The Spirit of the teacher has a voice that teaches you through teachers!

> And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and TEACHERS;Ephesians 4:11

God gives teachers! The Holy Spirit will use these teachers to speak and teach you. The Holy Spirit will speak to you through people who are anointed to teach the word of God. Do not miss the voice of the Holy Spirit that comes to you through an anointed teacher of the Word.

3\. The Spirit of the teacher guides you towards truth.

> HOWBEIT WHEN HE, THE SPIRIT OF TRUTH, IS COME, HE WILL GUIDE YOU INTO ALL TRUTH: for he shall not speak of himself; but whatsoever he shall hear, that shall he speak: and he will shew you things to come.John 16:12–13

Deceptions, untruths, hypocrisies are in the opposite direction of where God is. God is where truth is! That is why Jesus said, “I am the way, the truth and the life.” When you are being guided towards truth, you are being guided towards Jesus. Go towards truth, no matter how hard it is.

4\. The Spirit of the teacher points you towards certain books.

> AND THE VOICE WHICH I HEARD FROM HEAVEN SPAKE UNTO ME AGAIN, AND SAID, GO AND TAKE THE LITTLE BOOK... And I took the little book out of the angel’s hand, and ate it up; and it was in my mouth sweet as honey.Revelation 10:8–11

The Holy Spirit teaches you by leading you to books that you must read. The Holy Spirit is always pointing you to a book. At this very moment, the Holy Spirit is guiding you to read certain books. Expect it! Look out for it!

5\. The Spirit of the teacher reminds you of things.

> But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and BRING ALL THINGS TO YOUR REMEMBRANCE, whatsoever I have said unto you.John 14:26

Anytime you are reminded of things in the past, think of the Holy Spirit. God uses the past to guide us today. He brings things to your memory to remind you not to go the wrong way again. Remembering helps you not to go round in circles.

6\. The Spirit of the teacher predicts the future.

> Howbeit when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will guide you into all truth... AND HE WILL SHEW YOU THINGS TO COME.John 16:13

The Holy Spirit will speak of the future. The Holy Spirit is greatly concerned with what is going to come. The Holy Spirit makes us concerned about the future by predicting things accurately all the time.

7\. The Spirit of the Teacher glorifies Christ.

> HE SHALL GLORIFY ME: for he shall receive of mine, and shall shew it unto you.John 16:14

You will fall in love with the Spirit because the Holy Spirit glorifies Jesus all the time. If you love Jesus, you will love the Spirit because He is glorifying Jesus all the time.

8\. The Spirit of the Teacher teaches through revelation.

> That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you THE SPIRIT OF WISDOM AND REVELATION in the knowledge of him:Ephesians 1:17

You will enjoy the Holy Spirit because He teaches through revelation — sudden bursts of revelation that are exciting and exhilarating. Expect to receive revelation as you walk with the Holy Spirit, who is a teacher.

> THOU GAVEST ALSO THY GOOD SPIRIT TO INSTRUCT THEM, and withheldest not thy manna from their mouth, and gavest them water for their thirst.Nehemiah 9:20

9\. The Spirit of the Teacher teaches through nature.

> Doth not even nature itself teach you, that, if a man have long hair, it is a shame unto him?1 Corinthians 11:14

Anyone who exposes himself to the wonders of nature will find the Holy Spirit teaching him many things. The study of nature reveals the will and the character of God.

10\. The Spirit of the Teacher teaches through people you meet.

> It is clear that Christ himself wrote this letter and sent it by us. It is written, not with ink but with the Spirit of the living God, and not on stone tablets but on human hearts.2 Corinthians 3:2–3 (GNB)

The spirit of the teacher teaches you through people you encounter. Every time you meet someone, think of what the Holy Spirit may be trying to tell you or show you through that encounter. He may be telling you to follow a good example that has ended well. He may also be telling you to avoid certain things.

> *And further, by these, my son, be admonished: of making many books there is no end; and much study is a weariness of the flesh.*Ecclesiastes 12:12
