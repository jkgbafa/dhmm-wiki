Chapter 2

# The Lovely Interplay, Interchange and Interaction of the Holy Spirit

Now when all the people were baptized, it came to pass, that Jesus also being baptized, and praying, the heaven was opened, And the Holy Ghost descended in a bodily shape like a dove upon him, and a voice came from heaven, which said, Thou art my beloved Son; in thee I am well pleased.Luke 3:21–22

In the scripture above, we see the interplay, interchange and interaction between the Holy Spirit, the Father and the Son. We see the whole trinity in action. We can learn from what happened at the baptism of Jesus that the Father is unique and separate and distinct from the Son. We also see that the Son is completely separate and operating uniquely and distinctly from the Father and the Holy Spirit. We also see the Holy Spirit distinctly operating like a dove and descending from heaven.

It is a great blessing to see this majestic, divine, awesome trio in action! What an amazing unique interplay, interchange and interaction that goes on between the Holy Spirit, the Son and the Father! The Father speaks from heaven, whilst the Son walks upon the earth, praying, and the Holy Spirit descends separately and uniquely on the Son.

Our lovely Holy Spirit, whom we are studying, is indeed an amazing part of the trinity, and yet a distinctively separate part of the Holy Trinity.

1\. The Holy Spirit has a unique name which is different from the unique name of Father and the unique name of the Son.

> Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:Matthew 28:19

The Holy Spirit is uniquely important and uniquely distinct from the Father and also from the Son. This is why we are told to go forth and teach nations in the distinct name of the Father and in the distinct name of the Son and in the distinct name of the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit has a name that is distinct from the Father and also distinct from the name of the Son.

2\. The Holy Spirit is a distinctive gift from the Father, upon the request of the Son.

> And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever;John 14:16

In the scripture above, we see that the lovely Holy Spirit operates in a distinctively unique way. Here again, we see an amazing interchange, interplay and interaction between the Father and the Son.

- The Holy Spirit is a gift requested by the Son.
- The Holy Spirit is a gift given by the Father.
- The Holy Spirit distinctively arrives into our midst because the Son has requested that it should be done.
- The Holy Spirit distinctively arrives into our midst because the Father has answered the Son’s request.

3\. The Holy Spirit moves uniquely and independently of the Father and the Son.

> In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth. And the earth was without form, and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep. And the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters.Genesis 1:1–3

In the creation of the universe, the sun, the moon and the stars, we see a super interchange, interaction and interplay between the Holy Spirit, the Father and the Son.

- The Father’s role was to speak the world into existence.
- The Son’s role was to work with His Father for the creation of every single thing.
- The Holy Spirit had a unique role — to move on the face of the waters.

> Nevertheless I tell you the truth; It is expedient for you that I go away: for if I go not away, the Comforter will not come unto you; but if I depart, I will send him unto you.John 16:7

The movement of the Holy Spirit is separate and distinct from the movement of the Son. When the Son leaves the earth, the Holy Spirit leaves heaven and comes to the earth to replace Him!
