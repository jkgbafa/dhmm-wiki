Chapter 6

# The Lovely Spirit that Brings Satisfaction

For the kingdom of God is not meat and drink; but righteousness, and peace, and joy in the Holy Ghost.Romans 14:17

One of the almost-romantic features of the Holy Spirit is how He brings satisfaction, peace and rest to the life of striving, grasping human beings. This will make you love the Holy Spirit.

The human race is a relentless force constantly running helter-skelter in search of things that satisfy. People do anything to have a feeling of satisfaction or ecstasy. Drugs, sex, alcohol, perversions, food, family, children, holidays, money, riches are never able to bring the satisfaction that human beings are seeking for. Amazingly, the Holy Spirit gives us righteousness, peace and joy.

Righteousness, peace and joy amount to the highest dimension of satisfaction, peace and calm. You will love the Holy Spirit because He is the anointing that brings satisfaction, peace and joy.

1\. The lovely Holy Spirit brings satisfaction, fullness and a sense of accomplishment.

> Jesus answered and said unto her, Whosoever drinketh of this water shall thirst again: But WHOSOEVER DRINKETH OF THE WATER THAT I SHALL GIVE HIM SHALL NEVER THIRST; but the water that I shall give him shall be in him a well of water springing up into everlasting life.John 4:13–14

Anyone who drinks of the Holy Spirit will never thirst again. Anyone who drinks of the Holy Spirit will never need or want anything else.

2\. The lovely Holy Spirit brings a deep satisfaction that is from within.

The satisfaction does not come from any external realities that you may be seeing or experiencing. The water of the Holy Spirit springs up from within as a well of everlasting life.

3\. The lovely Holy Spirit is a river of life.

A river is a source of life, livelihood, prosperity and well-being. This is why major cities of the world are built around rivers. A river is a source of everything you need — for domestic needs, pleasure, cleansing, health and food.

> He that believeth on me, as the scripture hath said, out of his belly shall flow RIVERS OF LIVING WATER. (But this spake he of the Spirit, which they that believe on him should receive.)John 7:38–39
