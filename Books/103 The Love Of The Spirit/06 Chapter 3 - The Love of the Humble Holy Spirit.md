Chapter 3

# The Love of the Humble Holy Spirit

For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.2 Timothy 1:7

Satan is a spirit of pride. The Holy Spirit is the exact opposite. The Holy Spirit does not exalt Himself. The Holy Spirit does not vaunt Himself. God has given us the spirit of power, love and a sound mind!

The Holy Spirit is lovely because He is full of power! He is lovely because He is full of love! Remember that love does not boast and is not arrogant. Love is a form of humility! The Holy Spirit is the spirit of a sound mind. A sound mind is a humble, careful and cautious mind!

1\. The lovely humble Holy Spirit is someone who can be sent by the Father!

> But the Comforter, which is THE HOLY GHOST, *WHOM THE FATHER WILL SEND* in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.John 14:26

The humility of the Holy Spirit is seen in the humble way in which He can be sent by both the Father and the Son. The lovely Holy Spirit is both beautiful and glorious because He is a humble, sendable Holy Spirit.

2\. The lovely humble Holy Spirit is someone that can be sent by Jesus!

> But WHEN THE COMFORTER IS COME, WHOM *I WILL SEND* unto you from the Father, even the Spirit of truth, which proceedeth from the Father, he shall testify of me:John 15:26

The lovely Holy Spirit can be sent by the Son. Remember that Jesus was sent by the Father and Jesus sent the Holy Spirit.

> And HE THAT SENT ME IS WITH ME: the Father hath not left me alone; for I do always those things that please him.John 8:29

3\. The lovely humble Holy Spirit does not speak about Himself.

> Howbeit when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will guide you into all truth: FOR HE SHALL NOT SPEAK OF HIMSELF; but whatsoever he shall hear, that shall he speak: and he will shew you things to come.John 16:13

The lovely Holy Spirit is so powerful, and yet He does not speak about Himself. The humble Holy Spirit does not talk about His exploits in the creation of the world. He does not talk about how He is the supernatural presence everywhere in the world. He does not speak about Himself!

He is humble! He is all powerful! He is glorious! Yet, He does not glorify Himself. He glorifies Jesus! Let us learn from the Holy Spirit what true humility is.

> He shall glorify me: for he shall receive of mine, and shall shew it unto you.John 16:14
