### CHAPTER 10\

The Privilege of Royalty\
-----------------------------------------------

\

But watch thou in all things, endure afflictions, do the work of an evangelist, make full proof of thy ministry. For I am now ready to be offered, and the time of my departure is at hand. I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith: Henceforth THERE IS LAID UP FOR ME A CROWN OF RIGHTEOUSNESS, which the Lord, the righteous judge, shall give me at that day: and not to me only, but unto all them also that love his appearing.

2 Timothy 4:5-8

A great privilege is bestowed upon you when you take up the job of a shepherd.

Jesus Christ is the King of kings and the Lord of lords! If you accept to be a shepherd, you are actually accepting to be a part of the royal family of heaven.

You are privileged to join a royal family! In heaven, you will not be outside the royal family and looking in.

Jesus Christ is a shepherd and a king! When you become a shepherd, you will also become a shepherd and a king. What a privilege it is to join the royal family where Jesus Christ Himself will be the King of kings and the Lord of lords!

By doing the work of a shepherd, you are most likely to inherit a crown of life, a crown of righteousness and a crown of glory. Remember that a crown is given to only those who are royalty. Apart from the Queen of England, and other royalty, few other people wear crowns on earth.

Imagine that you are going to be part of the royal family when you get to heaven. Those of us who could not be part of a royal family on earth and who could never enter a palace to see what was behind the walls, will have a chance to experience royalty at its best.

Three different crowns are promised. A crown of life, a crown of righteousness and a crown of glory! What will you be doing wearing a crown if you are not part of a royal family? The crown of life is promised in two different passages of the Bible. However, the crown of glory and the crown of righteousness are promised in one passage.

Blessed is the man that endureth temptation: for when he is tried, HE SHALL RECEIVE THE CROWN OF LIFE, which the Lord hath promised to them that love him.

James 1:12

And unto the angel of the church in Smyrna write; These things saith the first and the last, which was dead, and is alive; I know thy works, and tribulation, and poverty, (but thou art rich) and I know the blasphemy of them which say they are Jews, and are not, but are of the synagogue of Satan.

Fear none of those things which thou shalt suffer: behold, the devil shall cast some of you into prison, that ye may be tried; and ye shall have tribulation ten days: be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee A CROWN OF LIFE.

Revelation 2:8-10

The elders which are among you I exhort, who am also an elder, and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and also a partaker of the glory that shall be revealed: feed the flock of God which is among you, taking the oversight thereof, not by constraint, but willingly; not for filthy lucre, but of a ready mind; Neither as being lords over God’s heritage, but being ensamples to the flock.

And when the chief Shepherd shall appear, ye shall receive a CROWN OF GLORY that fadeth not away.

1 Peter 5:1-4

Imagine that! Three whole crowns waiting for you! You will soon be even more glorious than the king or queen of England! And all this, just because you were a shepherd!

As part of the royal family in heaven, you will have many new privileges and blessings.

Blessings of becoming Royalty in Heaven

1\. You will have the honour of wearing a crown.

2\. You will have the honour of staying in a palace.

3\. You will have the honour of wearing jewels.

4\. You will have the honour having many servants.

5\. You will have the honour of wearing beautiful royal clothes.

6\. You will have the honour of relating with other members of the royal family in heaven.

7\. You will have the honour of having special food made for you.

8\. You will have the honour learning the king’s protocol.

9\. You will be closer to the throne and closer to where the action is.

10\. You will have the honour of going for royal tours and royal holidays.

11\. You will have the honour of greeting the public with a royal wave.

12\. You will be honoured and celebrated as royalty throughout eternity.

Conclusion

It is time to take up the privilege of shepherding. God has His eyes on you and that is why He is giving you this special privilege. Forget those things that are behind. Many sad and bad things have already happened. They are such that you cannot undo them. You cannot unwind! You cannot change them! There are many things like that in this world. The only way to overcome them is to forget them. Thinking about them takes you deeper and further into despair and despondency. Only God can heal certain things! It is time for you to forget about the past so that you can reach out and be the shepherd that God wants you to be.

Reach forward to the privileges that are before you. God has called you to do great things for Him. You must reach forward to the mark of the high calling. You will not attain to the high calling by sitting in one position.

You must reach forth to the privileges that seem to be out of range. It is only by reaching forth to new things that you will forget the past and bear fruit. If you are following the Holy Spirit, you are not likely to be at the same spot in a year’s time. The Spirit is always moving and as you follow Him, the main characteristic of your life will be movement. I see God changing things and shifting things around!
