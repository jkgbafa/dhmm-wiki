### CHAPTER 1\

What is a Privilege?\
-------------------------------------------

\

For I testify that they gave as much as they were able, and even beyond their ability. Entirely on their own, THEY URGENTLY PLEADED WITH US FOR THE PRIVILEGE OF SHARING IN THIS SERVICE TO THE LORD’S PEOPLE.

2 Corinthians 8:3-4 (NIV)

Serving God’s people will always be a privilege. There is no greater honour and no greater privilege than to become a servant of the Most High God. If you understand what it means to be a shepherd, you would plead for a chance to serve in that capacity.

A privilege is a special opportunity to do something. Actually, becoming a shepherd is a special opportunity given to you by God Himself.

A privilege is a right or benefit given to some people and not to others. Indeed, the benefits of being a shepherd are not enjoyed by everyone.

A privilege is a special advantage that is not enjoyed by everyone.

A privilege is a benefit enjoyed only by certain people.

A privilege is a special exemption granted to certain people.

A privilege is an advantage or source of pleasure granted to someone. God gives you an advantage and a source of blessings when He makes you a shepherd. Being a shepherd will be a blessing to you! Shepherds will care for people but the shepherd also receives many blessings because he is a shepherd.

The ministry of Jesus Christ is a privilege indeed. To be in the ministry is to be given a special opportunity to do something.

To become a shepherd is to be given a right and a benefit that is not given to others.

Not everyone enjoys the benefit of being a shepherd. Shepherds are given a special advantage that is not enjoyed by others.

You must grasp every opportunity that God gives you to be one of His shepherds.

The ministry of a shepherd is not a heavy load that changes your life negatively.

The ministry of Jesus Christ is not a burden. It is a privilege!

When God called you to be a shepherd, He was giving you a special advantage that is not experienced by many people.

It is important that you do not see God’s call on your life as a bother. Do not see the work of a shepherd as an irritating inconvenience in your life!

God cannot inconvenience you! You must never show irritation on your face when it comes to the work of God. You must never complain when you are given a chance to be a shepherd. No one in his right mind will complain about a great privilege being given to him. It is only when you do not understand the privilege and the honour being done you that you would complain, murmur or grumble about being a shepherd.

This little book is an attempt to show you how privileged you are to be trained and appointed as a shepherd. From this time onwards, you must appreciate God’s grace to you when invited to become a shepherd. Indeed, the grace of God that brings salvation has appeared to you! God is calling you to join the army of shepherds.
