### CHAPTER 14\

Lucifer: Delusional Disorder\
--------------------------------------------------

For thou hast said in thine heart, I will ascend into heaven, I will exalt my throne above the stars of God: I will sit also upon the mount of the congregation, in the sides of the north: I will ascend above the heights of the clouds; I will be like the most High. Yet thou shalt be brought down to hell, to the sides of the pit.

Isaiah 14:13-15

A Delusional Disorder is a type of madness which has as its main symptom the presence of one or more delusions. A delusion is an unshakable belief in something that is not true. When a person has a delusion, everyone else knows that what he believes is false. There are delusions in schizophrenia. However, in the condition called “Delusional Disorder”, there are no other symptoms apart from the presence of these delusions.

A delusional person believes things that couldn’t possibly be true. For instance, a person with a Delusional Disorder may be convinced that the air conditioner is controlling his thoughts. Delusional comes from a Latin word meaning “deceiving.” So delusional thinking is deceiving yourself by believing outrageous things.

Every single act that is recorded about Lucifer reeks of the logic of madness, absurdity, irrationality, insanity, lunacy, stupidity, psychosis, foolishness, craziness, senselessness and idiocy. Satan exhibited completely irrational and inexplicable behaviour when he rose up to attack God and try to replace the maker of the universe with himself. He must have been filled with delusions to think that he could attack God. Only a Delusional Disorder could have made Lucifer do what he did.

1\. DELUSIONS OF LUCIFER: ATTACKING GOD

Lucifer had to deceive himself in order to build up the courage to attack God. Lucifer must have had many delusions of grandeur. A delusion of grandeur is a delusion that you are much greater, more powerful and more influential than you really are. A delusion of grandeur is a false or unusual belief about one’s power, wealth, talents, and traits. A person may believe, for instance, that they are famous, that they belong to the royal family or that they can end a world war, or that they are immortal!!

I once saw a nation which had two fighter planes parked at the airport, attempting to fight with a nation that had thousands of planes. In one raid, the two fighter planes were put out of use. They never took to the skies. You have to assess your enemy very well and decide whether you can fight him or not.

It is only a mad man who will go out and fight with someone who is far superior to him. It must have been some kind of Delusional Disorder working in Lucifer’s mind for him to think he could ascend God’s throne and replace God.

There are pastors all over the world who operate on social media thinking that they can destroy God’s church.

There are pastors’ wives who think that they can destroy their husbands by divorcing them or speaking out against them.

2\. DELUSIONS OF LUCIFER: REPLACING GOD

Lucifer must have had severe psychosis with delusions of grandeur. It is no wonder that he transmits to other Luciferian disciples delusions of grandeur and offers them things that they will never have. In psychiatry, we see how delusions affect people. 11A patient who has fictitious beliefs about his or her power or authority may believe himself or herself to be a ruling monarch who deserves to be treated like royalty.11 Some patients believe they are God. Someone with delusions can believe she is the Queen of England! Someone with delusions can believe that he is a president’s son! Someone with delusions can believe that he is a famous rock star or movie star! Some people with delusions can believe that they are skilled athletes or great inventors!

According to psychiatric teaching, grandiose-type symptoms include exaggerated beliefs of self-worth, power, knowledge, identity and exceptional relationship to a deity or famous person. Obviously, Lucifer had grandiose delusions in which he thought his self-worth, his power, his knowledge and his relationship with God could elevate him to the point where he could replace God himself.

Grandiose delusions are a characteristic of deceived men who launch out to fight against God’s work. I have encountered many men who have had a delusion that they were greater than they actually are. Most of them fell to the ground like ripe mangoes off a tree and rotted on the ground only to be eaten by insects and animals. How the proud are fallen!

Such delusions are maintained by auditory hallucinations, in which they hear voices which advise the patient that they are something great.

A person with delusions may have what is called confabulations, when, for example, the patient can give a detailed description of their coronation or their wedding with the king. Grandiose delusions can be a part of fantastic hallucinosis in which all forms of hallucinations occur.
