### CHAPTER 2\

Those Who Are Mad: A Warning from the Dictionary\
----------------------------------------------------------------------

And they were filled with madness; and communed one with another what they might do to Jesus.

Luke 6:11

Madness has many interesting definitions in the dictionary.

1\. Madness is defined as having a mind that does not work in a normal way, resulting in very strange behaviour. Therefore, those who are mad could be described as those who have a mind that does not work in a normal way such that their behaviour is very strange.

2\. Madness: Used to describe something that relates to, is characterized by, or derives from serious mental illness. Therefore, those who are mad could be described as those who have some serious mental illness.

3\. Madness speaks of someone who is deranged and not of a sound mind. Therefore, those who are mad could be described as those who are deranged and not of a sound mind.

4\. Madness speaks of very stupid behaviour that could be dangerous. Therefore, those who are mad could be described as those who engage in very stupid behaviour.

5\. Madness is a state of mind which prevents normal perception, behaviour or social interaction. Therefore, those who are mad could be described as those whose minds work in such a way that they do not have a normal perception and understanding of things and are somehow prevented from having normal behaviour and normal social interaction.

6\. Mad: Someone who is extremely foolish or unwise; imprudent; irrational. Therefore, those who are mad could be described as those who are extremely foolish, extremely unwise, extremely irrational and extremely imprudent.

7\. Mad: Someone who is emotionally overwhelmed; consumed by mood or feelings. Therefore, those who are mad could be described as those who may be emotionally overwhelmed and consumed by their moods and feelings.
