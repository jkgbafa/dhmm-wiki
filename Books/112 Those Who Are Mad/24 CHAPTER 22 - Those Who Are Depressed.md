### CHAPTER 22\

Those Who Are Depressed\
---------------------------------------------

To appoint unto them that mourn in Zion, to give unto them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for THE SPIRIT OF HEAVINESS; that they might be called trees of righteousness, the planting of the Lord, that he might be glorified.

Isaiah 61:3

Depression is a condition, which many people in the ministry struggle with. Many people in this life are hurting, lonely and desperate. In the Bible, there are many words, which speak of this same condition of depression. Words like “broken-hearted,” “downcast”, “troubled,” “mourning,” all describe the same condition of depression.

If you go through this life without experiencing several bouts of depression, you probably deserve to enter the Guinness Book of World Records. There are few pastors who can say that they have never had moments of despair, moments of disappointment, moments of misery and hopelessness.

The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised,

Luke 4:18

Our world is depressed. Our world is broken hearted. Jesus was called to preach the gospel to the whole world. The first group of people Jesus was sent to were the poor and the broken-hearted. God is nigh to those that are of a broken heart.

There are few people who can say they have never been disillusioned, discouraged and troubled in this life. Even today, as you read this book, you may be depressed, which may be why you chose to read this chapter.

Major characters of the Bible experienced depression in a real way.

1\. JEREMIAH WAS DEPRESSED AND MOODY

CURSED BE THE DAY WHEREIN I WAS BORN: LET NOT THE DAY WHEREIN MY MOTHER BARE ME BE BLESSED. CURSED BE THE MAN WHO BROUGHT TIDINGS TO MY FATHER, SAYING, A MAN CHILD IS BORN unto thee; making him very glad. And let that man be as the cities which the LORD overthrew, and repented not: and let him hear the cry in the morning, and the shouting at noontide; Because he slew me not from the womb; or that my mother might have been my grave, and her womb to be always great with me. WHEREFORE CAME I FORTH OUT OF THE WOMB TO SEE LABOUR AND SORROW, THAT MY DAYS SHOULD BE CONSUMED WITH SHAME?

Jeremiah 20:14-18

Jeremiah felt depressed about the ministry. He felt hopeless about his ministry. He had had great visions and God had proven Himself to him. He had been prophesying since he was a child. But obvious victories do not mean much to a person who is depressed. Jeremiah was so negative about his life. This is typical of someone who is experiencing depression.

A person with depression may be moody! A person with depression may express excessive sadness, hopelessness and a lack of excitement. Hopelessness is the hallmark of depression. Some people with depression feel hopeless all or most of the time. Some people who are depressed feel that they are worthless. Others feel that everything is an effort all the time. Other people with the hopeless outlook may think, “What is the use of all that I am doing?”

2\. KING DAVID WAS DEPRESSED AND QUIET

Depressed people are quiet and do not communicate much. A person with depression may not say much and may have a difficulty in understanding information. King David went into deep depression after the Lord struck Bathsheba’s child.

And Nathan departed unto his house. And the LORD struck the child that Uriah’s wife bare unto David, and it was very sick. David therefore besought God for the child; and David fasted, and went in, and lay all night upon the earth. And the elders of his house arose, and went to him, to raise him up from the earth: but he would not, neither did he eat bread with them.

2 Samuel 12:15-17

Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted within me? hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him, who is the health of my countenance, and my God.

Psalm 42:11

Depressed people also have a lowered sex drive: Losing interest in sex is a sign of depression. A decreased sex drive and even impotence is often a sign of depression. A person with depression loses interest in things that everyone has pleasure or enjoyment in. A loss of interest in activities that were previously enjoyed is frequently a sign of depression.

And he said, While the child was yet alive, I fasted and wept: for I said, Who can tell whether GOD will be gracious to me, that the child may live? But now he is dead, wherefore should I fast? can I bring him back again? I shall go to him, but he shall not return to me. And DAVID COMFORTED BATHSHEBA HIS WIFE, AND WENT IN UNTO HER, AND LAY WITH HER: AND SHE BARE A SON, and he called his name Solomon: and the LORD loved him.

2 Samuel 12:22-24

3\. JONAH WAS DEPRESSED AND SUICIDAL

But it displeased Jonah exceedingly, and he was very angry. And he prayed unto the LORD, and said, I pray thee, O LORD, was not this my saying, when I was yet in my country? Therefore I fled before unto Tarshish: for I knew that thou art a gracious God, and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repentest thee of the evil. Therefore now, O LORD, TAKE, I BESEECH THEE, MY LIFE FROM ME; FOR IT IS BETTER FOR ME TO DIE THAN TO LIVE. Then said the LORD, Doest thou well to be angry? So Jonah went out of the city, and sat on the east side of the city, and there made him a booth, and sat under it in the shadow, till he might see what would become of the city. And the LORD God prepared a gourd, and made it to come up over Jonah, that it might be a shadow over his head, to deliver him from his grief. So Jonah was exceeding glad of the gourd. But God prepared a worm when the morning rose the next day, and it smote the gourd that it withered. And it came to pass, when the sun did arise, that God prepared a vehement east wind; and the sun beat upon the head of Jonah, that he fainted, and wished in himself to die, and said, IT IS BETTER FOR ME TO DIE THAN TO LIVE. And God said to Jonah, Doest thou well to be angry for the gourd? And he said, I DO WELL TO BE ANGRY, EVEN UNTO DEATH.

Jonah 4:1-9

Jonah was depressed and very suicidal. Jonah prayed that he would die. People with depression are suicidal. Many people who suffer from depression commit suicide. Often, depressed patients will talk about it first and then make a first or second attempt at committing suicide before succeeding in ending their lives.

4\. ELIJAH SUFFERED FROM DEPRESSION

Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time. And when he saw that, he arose, and went for his life, and came to Beersheba, which belongeth to Judah, and left his servant there. But he himself went a day’s journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down under a juniper tree: and he requested for himself that he might die; and said, It is enough; now, O LORD, TAKE AWAY MY LIFE; FOR I AM NOT BETTER THAN MY FATHERS.

1 Kings 19:2-4

Elijah was famous for killing four hundred prophets of Baal. Elijah demonstrated the power of God in a way that no one else had. Yet, when the spirit of Jezebel threatened him, he became depressed and retreated from his ministry into the wilderness, where he stayed away from human contact.

Social withdrawal is one of the most common and telling signs of depression. Depression often leads people to isolate themselves. Isolation during depression occurs because people feel miserable, worthless, unmotivated, irritable, angry, ashamed, misunderstood and hopeless. Depressed people do not like to spend time with other people.

Elijah began to pray for death to come for him. Elijah wanted to die and he actually prayed for death. If this could happen to such a great prophet, I do not see how it cannot happen to you.

5\. JOB SUFFERED FROM DEPRESSION

Why died I not from the womb? why did I not give up the ghost when I came out of the belly?

Job 3:11

Or as an hidden untimely birth I had not been; as infants which never saw light.

Job 3:16

My soul is weary of my life; I will leave my complaint upon myself; I will speak in the bitterness of my soul.

Job 10:1

Job is given as an example of a patient person who endured many things in his quest to be faithful to God. Even Job’s wife wondered at Job’s decision to continue serving the Lord.

Terrors are turned upon me: they pursue my soul as the wind: and my welfare passeth away as a cloud. And now my soul is poured out upon me; the days of affliction have taken hold upon me. My bones are pierced in me in the night season: and my sinews take no rest.

Job 30:15-17

Even though Job was faithful to God, he had uncontrollable emotions with outbursts of anger at the situation he was experiencing. When someone is depressed, in one moment there may be outbursts of anger and in another moment, there may be uncontrollable tears. These fluctuating mood swings may have nothing to do with what the person is experiencing at the time.

Reading the words of Job as he went through his situation, reveals a desperate depression in his life.

The Answer to Depression

But GOD, WHO COMFORTS THE DEPRESSED, comforted us by the coming of Titus;

2 Corinthians 7:6 (NASB)

The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.

Psalm 34:18

God is near to people that are depressed and broken hearted. This is what the Bible says. God is with you in the good days but He is also with you in the difficult times. God does not condemn the people who were depressed in the Bible for speaking out their feelings in their times of depression.

Jesus cares! God cares! God has compassion and mercy for you in your disillusionment and confusion. God is working out the victory even though you are in tears and in pain. Do not drive away people who bring you joy and hope.

Everybody needs joy-givers and hope-givers! Jesus is the Saviour of the world and He is anointed for the broken-hearted and the depressed. He understands every weakness, every hurt and every traumatic experience of your life. He is your healer! He is your redeemer!

In the Garden, throughout the night, Jesus prayed alone, calling on His Father. Jesus was overwhelmed with sorrow to the point of death. Jesus was desperate as He prayed for God to change the circumstance. Jesus wanted it changed! Jesus was so affected in Gethsemane that He sweated drops of blood.

I do not know where you are today. Maybe you are on a dark road. When you look around, you cannot see anybody like yourself. I want you to know that you are not alone. God is with you! He will never leave you and He will never forsake you!

For we have not an high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities; but was in all points tempted like as we are, yet without sin.

Hebrews 4:15

Your season of suffering will not be wasted. Somehow, it will bring the presence of God closer to you. Through God’s mysterious ways, something good and purposeful will come out of every suffering and every unfortunate experience you have gone through. It will only make you stronger. Do not feel hopeless because of your frequent depression! Jesus is anointed to heal every broken-hearted person! Jesus was tempted in all points just as we are. Jesus will heal your depression because He knows exactly how you feel.
