Mental Illness in the Mad Man of Gadara

The story of the mad man of Gadara reveals many of the classic signs of schizophrenia and other mental illnesses. Indeed, many of the symptoms of well-known mental illness are demonstrated in the mad man of Gadara. This scripture is one of the cardinal reasons why Christians believe that mental illness is caused by evil spirits.

Indeed, mental illness, just like any other illness, can be caused by something natural or it can be caused purely by evil spirits. Indeed, smoking cannabis is a well-known cause of schizophrenia. Spiritual people are however entitled to believe the Bible and what it says about the mad man of Gadara.

Let us look closely at the symptoms of the madman of Gadara. You will see that demonic influence is closely linked with mental illness, which in turn is closely linked to disloyalty and other abnormal behaviour.

1. The demonized man of Gadara was separated from society. This separation from society is one of the negative withdrawal symptoms of schizophrenia. The withdrawal and separation from a church, fellowship or family to which you rightfully belong, is characteristic of those with disloyalty and treachery.

2. The demonized man of Gadara did not want the pleasure of living at home but chose rather to live in the cemetery. This loss of interest in anything pleasurable is one of the “negative withdrawal symptoms of schizophrenia” and is also called anhedonia. The withdrawal from a church, fellowship or group to which you belong and which you have enjoyed for years is characteristic of betrayers.

3. The demonized man of Gadara was uncontrollable. No one could advise him! This stubbornness is the medical symptom called “disorganized behaviour” of schizophrenia. Stubborn refusal to change is characteristic of several mental disorders such as, “Delusional Disorder, schizophrenia and the schizotypal personality disorder.” Similarly, resistance to instructions, refusal to change and refusal to obey any commandments is characteristic of those with disloyalty and treachery.

4. The demonized man of Gadara was harming and destroying himself. No one could stop him or help him! This self-harm and self-destructive behaviour is a medical symptom called “disorganized behaviour” of schizophrenia. It is also a symptom of medical condition called depression. Similarly, those with disloyal and treacherous behaviour harm themselves in the ministry.

They move out of position and move into obscurity, never bearing the fruit that they would have borne if they had remained faithful. They are aware that their unfaithfulness leads to self-harm and self-destruction but they choose to harm themselves and destroy themselves anyway!

5. The demonized man of Gadara exhibited two extremes. On one hand, he was violent, harmful and uncontrollable and in the next moment he was kneeling at the feet of Jesus and worshipping.

This extreme behaviour of a person, sometimes very sad, slow and depressed, combined with very excited, and exuberant is the medical condition called a “bipolar disorder.” Some people exhibit extreme loyalty and faithfulness, even fighting battles and taking bullets for you. The next moment, they may be found hating you, fighting you and seeking your destruction. Surely, the demons were the cause of the bipolar condition in the madman of Gadara. The bipolar condition is comparable to someone who switches from being very faithful to being very disloyal.

6. The demonized man of Gadara was hearing from demons that were controlling his behaviour and making him abnormal. In medical science, this is a medical symptom called “hallucinations.” When you hear voices speaking to you, you are exhibiting a medical symptom called auditory hallucinations. It is interesting that many people who leave churches, families and fellowships unceremoniously, attacking and destroying as they go, claim to have heard from the Holy Spirit. They claim to have heard the voice of God, just like the mad man of Gadara was hearing from demons.

The mad man of Gadara was hearing from demons, which made him destroy his life and live in a cemetery.

7. The demonized man of Gadara tried to kill himself by cutting himself with stones. The pigs on the other hand, immediately “committed suicide”, when the demons entered them. There are many medical mental health conditions, which lead to suicide. Depression patients are prone to committing suicide. Many people on psychotic medication have suicidal tendencies as a side effect of their drugs.

Interestingly, when pastors become possessed with demons, they tend to self-destruct. They destroy themselves utterly and destroy their chance to ever be in the ministry again. They seem not to care about their lives or what they have believed in, in the past.

Like the pigs, which “committed suicide” when the demons entered them, these rebellious pastors run into the water, destroy themselves and are forever gone from the world of ministry.

Be careful that demons do not cause you to behave like a mad man in the ministry of the Lord Jesus Christ.
