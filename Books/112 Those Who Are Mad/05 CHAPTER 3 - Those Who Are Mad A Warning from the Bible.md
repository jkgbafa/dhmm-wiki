### CHAPTER 3\

Those Who Are Mad: A Warning from the Bible\
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The Lord shall smite thee with madness, and blindness, and astonishment of heart:

Deuteronomy 28:28

The Bible has many things to say about madness. In this chapter, let us see exactly what the Word of God tells us about madness.

1\. According to the Bible, madness is a condition that is found in the human race.

This is an evil among all things that are done under the sun, that there is one event unto all: yea, also THE HEART OF THE SONS OF MEN IS FULL OF EVIL, AND MADNESS IS IN THEIR HEART while they live, and after that they go to the dead.

Ecclesiastes 9:3

Just as evil is found in the whole world, madness is also evenly distributed across the planet. There is no country and there is no group of people that does not have issues with madness or mental health. All across the world, the hearts of the sons of men are not only full of evil but equally of madness.

2\. According to the Bible, madness is pronounced on men as a curse from God.

The Lord shall smite thee with madness, and blindness, and astonishment of heart:

Deuteronomy 28:28

In the book of Deuteronomy, madness is found in the list where Moses describes several curses of defeat, failure, humiliation, frustration, sickness and disease. Madness was inflicted on the people of Israel by the curse of Moses. This was no small curse. Madness is very difficult to cure, even with modern drugs. Unfortunately, many of the modern drugs have distressing side effects which lead to other mental conditions.

3\. According to the Bible, madness is something we must study and learn about so that we can relate with it and handle it properly. Madness must be studied and greatly analysed.

And I gave my heart to know wisdom, AND TO KNOW MADNESS and folly: I perceived that this also is vexation of spirit.

Ecclesiastes 1:17

Failure to understand mental health and its ramifications will cause us to misunderstand unusual, unexpected and antisocial behaviour. It will cause us to mistreat people who rather need counselling, medication or other forms of help.

I remember a brother whose wife was behaving very badly. He finally decided to divorce her. As he was preparing to divorce her, she lunged out at him and attacked him physically. He was shocked and that was when it occurred to him that his wife was probably mentally ill. Indeed, it turned out that she was mentally ill. The brother put aside the divorce and helped his wife go through several years of treatment for her condition. It happened that this brother was a medical doctor and knew something about medical health. That is why it occurred to him that his wife could have had a mental health challenge.

In this book, we want to study about different kinds of madness and how they relate to us in the ministry and in the church. This is not a book for medical students. It is a book that a lay person can appreciate and understand how mental health issues relate to the church and to the ministry. You will discover that many mental illnesses are being manifested in your leadership team. Mental health issues are rampant in the church. Often, these conditions go undetected and undiagnosed. As we begin to make the diagnoses of “those who are mad” it will allow for greater understanding and a greater victory over the wickedness of satan.

4\. According to the Bible, people in the ministry do go mad. The best example is the prophet Balaam.

But was rebuked for his iniquity: the dumb ass speaking with man’s voice forbad THE MADNESS OF THE PROPHET.

2 Peter 2:16

According to the Word of God, a prophet can also go mad. If a prophet can go mad, then an apostle, a pastor, a bishop, an evangelist or a teacher can also go mad. What is madness? One of the definitions of a mad person is someone who is extremely foolish, unwise, imprudent or irrational.

There are bishops and pastors who are extremely foolish, unwise and irrational. Madness has gripped their souls! A Mad person could also be defined as someone who is emotionally overwhelmed or consumed by mood or feelings. Sometimes, when people experience emotional trauma, they are consumed by their mood and feelings and then they go mad.

Indeed, from my experience in the ministry, many pastors and prophets are emotionally overwhelmed and act foolishly, unwisely and irrationally. It is this foolish, unwise, irrational and anti-social behaviour of pastors or bishops who have hitherto been normal, that is troubling. Perhaps, being able to make a diagnosis of madness will help Christians to have a greater understanding and capacity to love seemingly errant and deviant brothers and sisters. A diagnosis of madness often invokes compassion in a person who has understanding.

5\. According to the Bible, the opposers of Moses were considered mad men.

History has given us an example of this with the Egyptian sorcerers Jannes and Jambres, who stood against Moses in their arrogance. So it will be in the last days with those who reject the faith with their corrupt minds and arrogant hearts, standing against the truth of God. But they will not advance, FOR EVERYONE WILL SEE THEIR MADNESS, just as they did with Jannes and Jambres!

2 Timothy 3:8-9 (TPT)

The Egyptian sorcerers, Jannes and Jambres, fought against Moses in their arrogance. Pride often makes you behave irrationally and out of character. Jannes and Jambres rose up against a great anointing, fighting and challenging it. This was not a wise move! It will never be a wise move to stand up against a great prophet and apostle whom God has raised up. And yet, there are people who have made it their jobs to fight God’s holy prophets and servants. Today, just like Jannes and Jambres, there are ministers of the gospel who have gone completely mad.

It is amazing that people do not learn from history nor from the Bible. Madness is not something that has been eradicated from the earth. Madness still occurs today.

Just as Jannes and Jambres fought against Moses, there are modern day Jannes’ and Jambres’ who are equally mad and determined to fight against the great gifts and anointings on earth.

There are modern day Jannes’ and Jambres’ whose demon-glazed eyes look at God’s servants with hatred and malice, wishing to do them harm. The gleaming, jaundiced eyes of these jealous and hateful merchants of slander, stare helplessly at God’s servants as God’s servants move from victory to victory and from grace to grace.

These hateful people are mad people! They are mad because the Bible calls them mad. Everyone will see their madness as time goes by!

6\. According to the Bible, there is such a thing as wicked madness.

The words of a wise man’s mouth are gracious and win him favor, but the lips of a fool consume him; the beginning of his talking is foolishness and THE END OF HIS TALK IS WICKED MADNESS.

Ecclesiastes 10:12-13 (AMP)

Mad behaviour is often a manifestation of wickedness. This is why human beings have historically reacted against mentally ill patients and administered all kinds of brutal treatment to them.

I remember when a nurse at the mental hospital told me about how a mentally ill patient had attacked another mentally ill patient and plucked his eyes out. I could not believe the story but it happened.

Why did the mad patient pluck out the eyes of a fellow patient? Why did he not just scratch the skin of his fellow patient? This is why madness is sometimes called “wicked madness” in the Bible.

There are people in the church who act foolishly, irrationally and unreasonably against the leadership of the church. It is as though they are no longer reasoning or thinking rationally. This is why mental illness has often been defined as a loss of reason.

People who leave churches have been known to attack and destroy the church they once helped to build. Wickedness combines with madness to become wicked madness and these people wreak havoc in the church, spreading slander, hatred, contention and strife everywhere and to everyone. Sometimes, it is as though they have gone completely mad!

It is only wicked madness that will cause someone to attack his own church; a place which has been his home for the past twenty years in order to destroy his former church, to destroy his former place of employment, to destroy his former employer, to destroy his spiritual father, to destroy his mentor, to destroy his relationships, to destroy his friends, to destroy his marriage and to destroy his home. Only a mad man takes a firebrand and goes around burning down houses indiscriminately. This is wicked madness!

7\. According to the Bible, there is such a thing as mischievous madness.

The words of a wise man’s mouth are gracious; but THE LIPS OF A FOOL will swallow up himself. The beginning of the words of his mouth is foolishness: and THE END OF HIS TALK IS MISCHIEVOUS MADNESS.

Ecclesiastes 10:12-13

Many of the people who hate you are filled with foolishness and madness. The lips of a fool can produce mischievous madness. Mischief is behaviour that is intended to cause trouble for people. People who aim to cause trouble for you are filled with mischievous madness. Indeed, foolishness and madness are related. They are related because the outcome of pure mischief is similar to the outcome of pure madness. Mischief causes trouble for people and madness equally does the same.

Do you know anyone who suffers from mischievous madness? Mischievous madness is a double portion of craziness, foolishness, irrationality and nonsense! There are pastors, bishops and reverends who suffer from a double portion of mischievous madness.

Some pastors can fall into a double portion of craziness, foolishness, irrationality and nonsense! Indeed, some people are walking examples of mischievous madness! These are men of mischief with an intention to harm or destroy what they once loved. Watch out for every fool whose lips are producing mischievous madness! Men with mischievous madness are those with a double portion of craziness.

8\. According to the Bible, religious men and priests go mad sometimes.

AND THE SCRIBES AND PHARISEES WATCHED HIM, whether he would heal on the sabbath day; that they might find an accusation against him.

But he knew their thoughts, and said to the man which had the withered hand, Rise up, and stand forth in the midst. And he arose and stood forth.

Then said Jesus unto them, I will ask you one thing; Is it lawful on the sabbath days to do good, or to do evil? to save life, or to destroy it?

And looking round about upon them all, he said unto the man, Stretch forth thy hand. And he did so: and his hand was restored whole as the other.

AND THEY WERE FILLED WITH MADNESS; and communed one with another what they might do to Jesus.

Luke 6:7-11

Some people who interacted with Jesus were filled with madness. It happened to Jesus and it will happen to you too. The jealousies and the accusations of those who hate you has made them into mad men. There are many people who watch you on social media and are filled with madness.

There are pastors, reverends and bishops who have gone completely mad. This is exactly what happened in the time of Jesus. The madness of the priests and the religious people made them blind to the good things that Jesus had done. The madness of your haters will make them blind to the good things that you are doing. On this particular occasion, Jesus healed a man with a withered hand. The religious people were standing by, ready to accuse Jesus. This shows you that many people who are filled with accusations are actually filled with madness.

Madness is what motivates some people to accuse you endlessly. The madness blinds them to every good thing that is being done or that has been done. The madness made the priests and the Pharisees act irrationally, foolishly and without reason.

Remember that madness is defined as “a loss of reason”. Many people who persecute you are filled with “a loss of reason.” How could people be angry that someone had been healed of an incurable, debilitating and crippling illness? What kind of irrational, mischievous, wicked, deranged thoughts were giving rise to this abnormal behaviour in the priests and Pharisees?

It is this same madness that caused the Pharisees to be blind to all the good preaching, teaching and healing that Jesus had performed over the past three years, and then crucify Him out of their madness.

Their jealousies and their accusations made them into mad men and they ended up crucifying the innocent and only son of God. That is profound madness manifesting itself in an ultimate way! Beware of mischievous madness! Beware of wicked madness! People’s jealousies and accusations make them into mad men.

9\. According to the Bible, leaders can be smitten with madness.

In that day, saith the LORD, I WILL SMITE EVERY HORSE WITH ASTONISHMENT, AND HIS RIDER WITH MADNESS: and I will open mine eyes upon the house of Judah, and will smite every horse of the people with blindness.

Zechariah 12:4

Do not allow yourself to be smitten with madness. Do not follow anyone who is smitten with madness. Be aware that some people are smitten with madness!

The rider of a horse is the leader of the horse because he guides the horse as to where it should go. Riders and leaders can be smitten with madness. In the scripture above we see how a rider can be smitten with madness.

The Hebrew word for madness is schiggaon, which means craziness. When the rider of a horse is smitten with craziness, you cannot expect a good outcome. There will be irrational, unreasonable, unexpected and indecent behaviour.

Pastors with schiggaon

Pastors who are struck with schiggaon are without the faculty of reason. Leaders and riders of horses who are struck with schiggaon are deprived of normal mental clarity. A person with normal mental clarity or sound judgment will not do certain things or say certain things. How many bishops have done things, which are contradictory, inconsistent, unsound and unreasonable? It is a pity to see breakaway pastors and rebellious leaders take decisions that are clearly illogical and irrational!

Just ask yourself a question: if you saw a man spend a whole week arranging blocks into a pattern, and then spend the next week dismantling the blocks he had carefully arranged; and then spend the week after re-arranging the blocks into that special pattern, and then spend the following week dismantling the blocks from the well-laid out pattern, would you not conclude that there was something wrong with this man?

This is what many leaders and pastors do. They spend fifteen years of their lives building a church through preaching, teaching and sacrificing their lives and then they spend the next few years of their lives dismantling it. No one can advise or speak reason to these mad men of ill-will, hatred and malice who are only bent on dismantling the blocks they have just finished laying. Even a ten year-old would conclude that such a person was not normal. Watch out for madness! Watch out for pastors with schiggaon! Watch out for bishops who have gone mad and lost all faculties of reason!

10\. According to the Bible, you can be healed of anxiety!

Be anxious for nothing, but in everything by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God.

Philippians 4:6 (NKJV)

Be healed of anxiety! Anxiety is one of the main mental conditions in the world. Most of us have suffered from bouts of anxiety before. Anxiety involves repeated episodes of fear and terror, which do not go away easily. Anxiety disorders are more than temporary worry or fear. Indeed, many people are plagued by fears and anxiety.

In the Word of God, we see a great solution to our panic, our fear of tomorrow and our fear of the unknown. In everything, through prayer and supplication, we can make our requests known to God. We can run away from our anxieties by running to prayer. Prayer is the spiritual antidote to fear. There are many things to fear in our world today. Though the nervousness, the unease, the severe fear and worry are often justified, they are not good things for our lives.

People have become rebellious, irrational, foolish and treacherous because they were anxious about their future. If they had been able to quell their fears and anxieties, they would have weathered the storm and would have been delivered from the madness and its deadly effects.

Dear reader, may you not be a rider smitten with anxiety, craziness, foolishness and insanity!
