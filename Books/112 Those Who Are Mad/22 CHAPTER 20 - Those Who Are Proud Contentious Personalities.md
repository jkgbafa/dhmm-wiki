### CHAPTER 20\

Those Who Are Proud: Contentious Personalities\
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Only by pride cometh CONTENTION: but with the well advised is wisdom.

Proverbs 13:10

A contentious personality must be kept out of your leadership. Do not employ contentious personalities. You will have a lot of strife. Contention and conflict come out of pride. We can therefore conclude that every contentious personality is someone who is steeped in pride. The person may be a humble looking lady but her manner of thinking and living lead to continuous conflict. He may be an assistant pastor but his manner of speaking and living lead to conflict. All the proud people of the Bible were high conflict personalities.

Pride is an evil spirit but is also the character of a High Conflict Personality in display. A High Conflict Personality is always one of the personality disorders that lead to conflict.

A High Conflict Personality creates contention all the time. Marriage to a High Conflict Personality will be a marriage of continuous conflict. Any of the personality disorders can have increased conflict and contention. This is because a person with a personality disorder does not think, act or feel the same way that everyone else does. Even a quiet, shy, avoidant personality can engender conflict.

Those who are proud (contentious personalities) must be kept at a distance. When you draw high conflict personalities into your inner circle, you may live to regret it. Some people must never be employed!

High conflict personalities must not be employed into full time ministry. How would you know that someone is a High Conflict Personality? Often, contentious people have manifested their traits for many years.

Personality disorders are things that last for a long time. People with personality disorders rarely change. You will find that they have a history of contention and conflict from primary school, secondary school or the university. They have been at the centre of every controversy that has happened in their past.

Contentious personalities have been at the centre of controversies and contention in different churches and fellowships that they have ever belonged to.

High Conflict Personalities of the Bible

1\. THOSE WHO ARE PROUD - LUCIFER: The primary example of those who are proud is Lucifer. He brought about all the contention and conflict in heaven.

For thou hast said in thine heart, I will ascend into heaven, I will exalt my throne above the stars of God: I will sit also upon the mount of the congregation, in the sides of the north: I will ascend above the heights of the clouds; I will be like the most High.

Isaiah 14:13-14

2\. THOSE WHO ARE PROUD - VASHTI: Vashti is another example of those who are proud. She brought about conflict to the royal family of King Ahasuerus. No woman in the royal family had probably ever behaved the way she was behaving. You are a High Conflict Personality when you do not obey your husband’s wishes.

What shall we do unto the queen Vashti according to law, because she hath not performed the commandment of the king Ahasuerus by the chamberlains?

Esther 1:15

3\. THOSE WHO ARE PROUD - KORAH: Korah is another example of those who are proud. Korah brought conflict between Moses and hundreds of the Israelites.

Now Korah, the son of Izhar, the son of Kohath, the son of Levi, and Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, and On, the son of Peleth, sons of Reuben, took men: And they rose up before Moses, with certain of the children of Israel, two hundred and fifty princes of the assembly, famous in the congregation, men of renown: And they gathered themselves together against Moses and against Aaron, and said unto them, Ye take too much upon you, seeing all the congregation are holy, every one of them, and the LORD is among them: wherefore then lift ye up yourselves above the congregation of the LORD?”

Numbers 16:1-3

4\. THOSE WHO ARE PROUD - REHOBOAM: Rehoboam is an example of those who are proud. He was puffed up when he was a newly appointed ruler and did not respect his elders. He got into a conflict with the elders on the very first day of office.

And the king answered the people roughly, and forsook the old men’s counsel that they gave him; And spake to them after the counsel of the young men, saying, My father made your yoke heavy, and I will add to your yoke: my father also chastised you with whips, but I will chastise you with scorpions.

1 Kings 12:13-14

5\. THOSE WHO ARE PROUD - NEBUCHADNEZZAR: Nebuchadnezzar got into conflict with the prophet, Daniel, when Daniel refused to obey him. His pride led to his downfall and his destruction.

Wherefore, O king, let my counsel be acceptable unto thee, and break off thy sins by righteousness, and thine iniquities by shewing mercy to the poor; if it may be a lengthening of thy tranquillity.

Daniel 4:27

6\. THOSE WHO ARE PROUD - PHARAOH: Pharaoh was another High Conflict Personality. His arrogant responses to Moses revealed pride and a highly contentious personality.

And afterward Moses and Aaron went in, and told Pharaoh, Thus saith the LORD God of Israel, Let my people go, that they may hold a feast unto me in the wilderness. And Pharaoh said, Who is the LORD, that I should obey his voice to let Israel go? I know not the LORD, neither will I let Israel go.

Exodus 5:1-2

Contentious Personalities

(Those Who Are Proud)

1\. Contentious personalities GET INTO CONFLICT MORE than the average person. Such people have marriages that are in constant turmoil.

2\. Contentious personalities BLAME OTHERS CONSTANTLY.

3\. Contentious personalities HAVE A VICTIM MENTALITY. 12Having a victim mentality helps high conflict people give themselves valid excuses for being quarrelsome. It’s always the other person’s fault. They’re the victims. They don’t see how they might have contributed to the issue.12

4\. Contentious personalities CONSIDER QUARRELS TO BE A NORMAL THING. They say things like, “Every marriage has challenges. All relationships have challenges that we have to work through. Conflicts always happen in every relationship.”

5\. Contentious personalities CANNOT COMMUNICATE PROPERLY. They will not listen to what the other person is saying. They may not allow the other person to speak.

6\. Contentious personalities LACK SELF-AWARENESS AND SELF-REFLECTION. They lack insight into their own behaviour and do not know how much conflict and contention they cause. They may never know how much pain and trouble they cause.

7\. Contentious personalities are aggressive and HUNGRY FOR POWER. In marriage, a seemingly unassuming wife may actually be dissatisfied with the fact that she has to submit to and be led her man.

8\. Contentious personalities are often INSECURE AND FEARFUL. You will be surprised how insecure people generate contention and conflict.

9\. Contentious personalities are often people who are OFFENDED about something.

10\. Contentious personalities must be KEPT AT A DISTANCE. When you draw high conflict personalities into your inner circle, you may live to regret it. Do not employ contentious personalities!
