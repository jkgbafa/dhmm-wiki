### CHAPTER 19\

Those Who Pretend: Multiple Personality Disorder\
----------------------------------------------------------------------

A double minded man is unstable in all his ways.

James 1:8

A double minded man is someone who has two ways of thinking. This causes instability in the person. These two patterns of thinking, one pure and the other impure, lead to unstable relationships. The two minds of a person may lead to a multiple personality: someone with two distinct personalities, one righteous and one full of wickedness. Jesus had a lot to say to people who were living in this way. They seemed to have two different extreme personalities; one beautiful outward personality and one horrible unclean personality.

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness. Even so ye also outwardly appear righteous unto men, but within ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.

Matthew 23:27-28

Traitors and treacherous people seem sometimes to have a Multiple Personality Disorder. This is because there seems to be the existence of two or more distinct personalities within one individual. Only one distinct personality is evident at each point in time and to a particular group of people. People who encounter someone with multiple personality disorder are often shocked to discover that the person has another side to him.

Indeed, some pretenders may not meet the criteria for the diagnosis of multiple personality disorder. However, they certainly do show some traits of having two characters.

1\. Delilah, the pretender, seemed to have multiple personalities.

After this it came about that he loved a woman in the valley of Sorek, whose name was Delilah. The lords of the Philistines came up to her and said to her, “Entice him, and see where his great strength lies and how we may overpower him that we may bind him to afflict him. Then we will each give you eleven hundred pieces of silver.” So Delilah said to Samson, “Please tell me where your great strength is and how you may be bound to afflict you…. When Delilah saw that he had told her all that was in his heart, she sent and called the lords of the Philistines, saying, “Come up once more, for he has told me all that is in his heart.” Then the lords of the Philistines came up to her and brought the money in their hands. She made him sleep on her knees, and called for a man and had him shave off the seven locks of his hair. Then she began to afflict him, and his strength left him. She said, “The Philistines are upon you, Samson!” And he awoke from his sleep and said, “I will go out as at other times and shake myself free.” But he did not know that the LORD had departed from him.”

Judges 16: 4-6, 18-20 (NASB)

Delilah seemed to be a loving girlfriend to Samson. Samson really liked her. She comforted him, she relaxed him and she made him feel good. Yet, on the other hand, she was a businesswoman looking for eleven hundred pieces of silver. She actually hated him! She was willing to destroy Samson for a few pieces of silver. Yet, when she related with Samson, he was so impressed, aroused and enchanted by her softness, her comforts and her delights.

2\. Judas, the pretender, seemed to have multiple personalities.

And Satan entered into Judas who was called Iscariot, belonging to the number of the twelve. And he went away and discussed with the chief priests and officers how he might betray Him to them. They were glad and agreed to give him money. So he consented, and began seeking a good opportunity to betray Him to them apart from the crowd…. “When He rose from prayer, He came to the disciples and found them sleeping from sorrow, and said to them, “Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray that you may not enter into temptation.” While He was still speaking, behold, a crowd came, and the one called Judas, one of the twelve, was preceding them; and he approached Jesus to kiss Him. But Jesus said to him, “Judas, are you betraying the Son of Man with a kiss?

Luke 22:3-6, 45-48 (NASB)

In one of his personalities, Judas was a faithful disciple called to the apostolic ministry. He was a man of vision who was going to send the gospel to the ends of the world. This personality was a man of great integrity who would not touch any of God’s money.

Right on the other hand, he seemed to have another personality where he was a businessman, very far from being an apostle. In this other personality, he loved money and was actually a thief. No one could have imagined that Jesus’ top disciple was into stealing, into business and into shady deals.

In the good personality, he had meetings with other apostles and Jesus Christ the Saviour of the world. But on the other hand, he had meetings with shady characters. These two personalities seemed to be present and take charge at different times.

Even on the night of the betrayal, he showed the sweet personality when he kissed Jesus. Then he showed the treacherous wickedness when he signalled the men with swords and weapons to capture Jesus. What a contrast! What a man of multiple personalities! One personality so sweet and another personality so wicked!

3\. Hushai the Archite was a classic pretender.

And it came to pass, when Hushai the Archite, David’s friend, was come unto Absalom, that Hushai said unto Absalom, God save the king, God save the king. And Absalom said to Hushai, Is this thy kindness to thy friend? why wentest thou not with thy friend? And Hushai said unto Absalom, Nay; but whom the LORD, and this people, and all the men of Israel, choose, his will I be, and with him will I abide. And again, whom should I serve? should I not serve in the presence of his son? as I have served in thy father’s presence, so will I be in thy presence.

2 Samuel 16:16-19

Hushai pretended to give good advice to Absalom. He claimed he was fully behind Absalom. In Hushai’s speech, he explained everything he did with good reasons. But on the other hand, Hushai was actually a very loyal person working for King David. One personality of Hushai was a supporter of Absalom but the other personality of Hushai was a supporter of his enemy.

4\. Jael was an amazing pretender.

Now Sisera fled away on foot to the tent of Jael the wife of Heber the Kenite, for there was peace between Jabin the king of Hazor and the house of Heber the Kenite. Jael went out to meet Sisera, and said to him, “Turn aside, my master, turn aside to me! Do not be afraid.” And he turned aside to her into the tent, and she covered him with a rug. He said to her, “Please give me a little water to drink, for I am thirsty.” So she opened a bottle of milk and gave him a drink; then she covered him. He said to her, “Stand in the doorway of the tent, and it shall be if anyone comes and inquires of you, and says, ‘Is there anyone here?’ that you shall say, ‘No.’” But Jael, Heber’s wife, took a tent peg and seized a hammer in her hand, and went secretly to him and drove the peg into his temple, and it went through into the ground; for he was sound asleep and exhausted. So he died.

Judges 4:17-21 (NASB)

Jael seemed to have two personalities. Imagine it. She deceived Sisera, the commander of the Canaanite army. She welcomed him into her tent. She comforted him and reassured him. She told him not to be afraid. She gave him milk to drink. She covered him with a blanket. She cuddled him and made him fall asleep.

Right on the other hand, there was another personality in Jael. She was a hard woman who could kill a man. As she lay by him, cuddling him, comforting him and giving him the blanket, she noticed that he had fallen asleep. She could hear it from his breathing. Her second personality took over and she became an aggressive killer. She took a nail, placed it on his head and struck a nail into the man who thought her to be a loving, comforting, warm, caring, caressing, hugging, affectionate, tender and sympathetic woman. The blood spurted out from his head like a fountain. The whole tent was filled with Sisera’s blood. His skull was broken open and his brains spilled out into this killer’s tent. Remember, she had switched personalities from a lover to a killer.

Are there two personalities in you?
