### CHAPTER 9\

Those Who Forget: Dementia\
------------------------------------------------

For God is not unrighteous to forget your work and labour of love, which ye have shewed toward his name, in that ye have ministered to the saints, and do minister.

Hebrews 6:10

Those who forget are guilty of unrighteousness, a sin that is mentioned clearly in the Bible. To forget and to fail to acknowledge, are sins before God. People forget God when they prosper! People forget God when things are going well with them! People forget to acknowledge God after they become millionaires.

God does not forget! Man forgets but God does not forget! People who forget do not have the nature of God. The natural man does not want to remember those who have helped him. The natural man does not like to remember where he came from. The natural man does not want anyone to know how he came to be who he is.

The proud, natural man believes he is self-made and thinks that he came to where he is by his own power.

You must take the concept of “remembering” very seriously. The Psalmist knew that it would be a tragedy if he could not remember important things that God had done for him in Jerusalem.

But how can we sing the songs of the Lord while in a pagan land?

IF I FORGET YOU, O Jerusalem, let my right hand forget how to play the harp.

May my tongue stick to the roof of my mouth IF I FAIL TO REMEMBER YOU, if I don’t make Jerusalem my greatest joy.

Psalm 137:4-6 (NLT)

It is interesting that the illness, dementia, which is one of the serious mental health conditions plaguing our world, leads to a loss of important memory. Let us note the comparisons that exist between “those who forget” and those who have dementia.

Many disloyal people forget important things. They act as though they cannot remember any good thing that has been done for them. There is an interesting parallel between those who forget and those who are mad. Perhaps forgetful traitors and wicked “forgetters” of important things actually have gone mad!

1\. EVIL MEN FORGET THEIR PERSONAL HISTORY AND DEMENTIA PATIENTS ALSO FORGET THEIR PERSONAL HISTORY.

And thou shalt remember that thou wast a bondman in Egypt: and thou shalt observe and do these statutes.

Deuteronomy 16:12

Remember these signs of dementia:

A person with dementia may forget his personal history.

A person with dementia may forget important events that have taken place in his life.

Those who forget (traitors, disloyal men, those who dishonour fathers, dangerous sons, ignorant people) do not remember important events in their lives. They do not remember their own personal history. Those who forget do not remember their training in Bible School. They do not remember who they were and how far out they were in this life when God reached out to them and blessed them.

Forgetting is a terrible spiritual evil but it is also one of the main signs of dementia. The children of Israel forgot the major events of their lives, including how they crossed the Red Sea.

Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits:

Psalm 103:2

2\. EVIL MEN FORGET THE MOST BASIC THINGS AND DEMENTIA PATIENTS ALSO FORGET THE MOST BASIC THINGS.

Can a maid forget her ornaments, or a bride her attire? yet my people have forgotten me days without number.

Jeremiah 2:32

A person with dementia may forget the most common items on a dining table. A person with dementia may not know what to do with a fork, a knife, a spoon or a plate. A person with dementia may even put food on the table, rather than on the plate because she has forgotten what the plate is for.

It is amazing that those who forget in the church, forget the most basic scriptures on forgiveness, love, obedience and faithfulness. A person who becomes unfaithful has often forgotten the most basic scriptures of all. A maid cannot forget her basic ornaments or her wedding dress, but those who forget, can forget the most basic rules of the Christian life.

Those who forget often become disloyal, treacherous and wicked. Traitors, disloyal men and those who dishonour fathers are the best examples of those who forget. They forget basics like “Honour your father and mother that it may be well with you; that you may live long on the earth” (Ephesians 6:2-3).

Forgetting is indeed a terrible spiritual evil!

3\. EVIL MEN FORGET THEIR FAMILY MEMBERS AND THOSE WITH DEMENTIA ALSO FORGET THEIR FAMILY MEMBERS.

For God is not unrighteous to forget your work and labour of love, which ye have shewed toward his name, in that ye have ministered to the saints, and do minister.

Hebrews 6:10

Unfortunately, a person with dementia may forget the most important people in his life. A person with dementia can set aside his pastor, his father, his friend of eighteen years and walk away as though he has never known them. Such loss of memory and abnormal behaviour is baffling!

People with dementia will look at their husbands and call them their sons! People with dementia will see their sons and call them their husbands.

People with dementia will see their fathers and say the electrician is here. People with dementia will call the security man their husbands, because they do not remember relationships any longer.

It is amazing that “those who forget” in the church forget the most basic of relationships.

Those who forget often become disloyal, treacherous and wicked! Traitors, disloyal men and those who dishonour fathers are the best examples of those who forget. Incredulously, such people do not remember the all-important relationships that they have developed over the years.

They do not remember fathers; they do not remember mothers and they do not remember brothers. Relationships mean nothing to those who forget!

Forgetting relationships is a terrible spiritual evil but it is also one of the main signs of dementia.

How can you forget the love relationships that you have enjoyed for fifteen years and turn into an enemy?

4\. EVIL MEN ARE CONFUSED ABOUT MANY THINGS AND THOSE WITH DEMENTIA ALSO GET CONFUSED ABOUT MANY THINGS.

Lest when thou hast eaten and art full, and hast built goodly houses, and dwelt therein; And when thy herds and thy flocks multiply, and thy silver and thy gold is multiplied, and all that thou hast is multiplied;

Then thine heart be lifted up, and THOU FORGET THE LORD THY GOD, which brought thee forth out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage;

Deuteronomy 8:12-14

A person with dementia is characterized by increasing confusion about where they are or even what day it is. Dementia is characterized by confusion! Dementia patients may require help in choosing proper clothing for the season or even in controlling their bladder.

Incredulously, disloyal people change so much when they are filled with pride in their achievements. As the scripture says, when they have eaten and are full, they forget God. People do not forget at the beginning. However, with time, when they have eaten, when they have built goodly houses, they forget how they came to be where they are.

Confusion, poor analysis and bad decisions are the hallmarks of those who forget. Relationships mean nothing to those who forget. The help people have received means nothing to those who forget. God warns us severely never to become one of those who forget.

5\. EVIL MEN HAVE PERSONALITY CHANGES AND DEMENTIA PATIENTS ALSO UNDERGO PERSONALITY CHANGES.

Lest when thou hast eaten and art full, and hast built goodly houses, and dwelt therein; And when thy herds and thy flocks multiply, and thy silver and thy gold is multiplied, and all that thou hast is multiplied;

THEN THINE HEART BE LIFTED UP, and THOU FORGET the Lord thy God, which brought thee forth out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage;

Deuteronomy 8:12-14

It is amazing that those with dementia experience significant behaviour and personality changes. Dementia patients may become more and more moody and withdrawn. People with dementia also become irritable, upset or emotional.

Those who forget are often proud, rude and disloyal. Rebellious pastors are good examples of those who forget. Rebellious pastors seem to have undergone a change in their personalities! A person who is filled with pride seems to have undergone a complete metamorphosis in his personality.

People do not change their behaviour at the beginning. However, with time, as pride grows in a person, there are real personality changes and changes in their way of speaking.

Men of pride also become withdrawn, irritable, upset and emotional about little things. If they were not proud, they would not be upset, they would not be withdrawn and they would not be irritable when they were rebuked or corrected.

6\. EVIL MEN BECOME DESOLATE IN MINISTRY AND DEMENTIA PATIENTS ARE CONFINED TO A BED OR A CHAIR, DOING NOTHING.

Lest when thou hast eaten and art full, and hast built goodly houses, and dwelt therein; And when thy herds and thy flocks multiply, and thy silver and thy gold is multiplied, and all that thou hast is multiplied;

THEN THINE HEART BE LIFTED UP, and THOU FORGET the Lord thy God, which brought thee forth out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage;

Deuteronomy 8:12-14

Dementia is characterized by serious limitations in a person’s life. A dementia patient is no longer fruitful or active. Many people with dementia die within eight to ten years of diagnosis. Often, those who suffer from dementia have damaged brain and nerve cells, which can lead to more and more illnesses until the person dies prematurely.

Perhaps, these patients who are confined to a bed or a chair could have been roaming around the world doing things they loved but the illness confines them to a single room, to a single chair and to a single bed.

Those who forget are also often fruitless, barren and desolate.

In school, those who forget their notes and their points during exams do not do well in those exams. Those who forget their biology notes can never become doctors. Those who forget their history points can never become lawyers.

Forgetting has serious effects on the life of the one who forgets. Those who forget are confined to a limited sphere of ministry. Those who forget are cut off from the people who would have related with them and helped their churches to grow.

People are afraid of pastors who seem to have forgotten their roots. People are afraid of founders who do not remember where they learnt their ministry from. The first question they may ask is “Who is your father? Whose son is he?”

And when Saul saw David go forth against the Philistine, he said unto Abner, the captain of the host, Abner, WHOSE SON IS THIS YOUTH? And Abner said, As thy soul liveth, O king, I cannot tell.

1 Samuel 17:55

How much more fruitful would some ministers have been if they had just remembered who they were and how they came to be where they are!

God requires remembrance! All through the Bible, the Word of God teaches us that remembrance is an important spiritual thing. May we be delivered from all forms of spiritual dementia!
