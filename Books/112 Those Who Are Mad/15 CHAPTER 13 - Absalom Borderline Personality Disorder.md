Absalom: Borderline Personality Disorder\
--------------------------------------------------------------

The Behaviour of a Normal Son

1\. A son is someone who is connected closely to his father. A son resembles his father in many ways.

Jesus saith unto him, Have I been so long time with you, and yet hast thou not known me, Philip? HE THAT HATH SEEN ME HATH SEEN THE FATHER…

John 14:9

2\. A son is forever. A son has a permanent, intense and close relationship with his father.

And the servant abideth not in the house for ever: but the Son abideth ever.

John 8:35

3\. Sons love their fathers intensely and trust them for everything.

After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name.

Matthew 6:9

4\. Sons honour their fathers and show them a lot of respect. Sons give gifts of great value to their fathers because they honour them so deeply.

A son honoureth his father, and a servant his master: if then I be a father, where is mine honour? and if I be a master, where is my fear? saith the Lord of hosts unto you, O priests, that despise my name. And ye say, Wherein have we despised thy name?

Malachi 1:6

5\. Sons love and obey their fathers. This is both emotional and also an act of conviction.

Children, obey your parents in the Lord: for this is right. Honour thy father and mother; (which is the first commandment with promise;) That it may be well with thee, and thou mayest live long on the earth.

Ephesians 6:1-3

6\. Sons are so close to their fathers that they end up doing what they see their fathers do.

… The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father do: for what things soever he doeth, these also doeth the Son likewise.

John 5:19

7\. Sons tend to speak in the same way that their fathers speak. This is why the Father said about Jesus, “This is my beloved son.”

When I was a son to my father, Tender and the only son in the sight of my mother, He taught me and said to me, “Let your heart hold fast my words; Keep my commandments and live;

Proverbs 4:3-4(NASB)

Yet, it is a well-known fact of history that there are sons who turn around and become the opposite of all these things I have listed. Absalom was one such dangerous son! His behaviour smacked of someone with a personality disorder. You must remember that a person with a personality disorder thinks and acts differently from a normal person!

Absalom: A Man with a Personality Disorder

Absalom qualifies to be a person with a personality disorder.

1\. A personality disorder is a condition in which an individual differs significantly from an average person in terms of how they think and feel. Truly, Absalom differed significantly from any of King David’s other sons. His behaviour of dishonouring his own father differed from any of David’s sons.

2\. A person with a personality disorder has an unhealthy pattern of thinking and behaving. Indeed, Absalom had an unhealthy pattern of thinking with conspiracies and plans of rebellion and plans of overthrowing his own father.

3\. To be classified as a personality disorder, a person’s way of thinking, feeling and behaving must deviate from the expectations of the culture. Absalom was a complete deviant! He attempted to kill his own father and slept with his father’s wives. This went contrary to the expectations of the Israeli culture where they were taught to honour their fathers.

4\. To be classified as a personality disorder, one’s way of thinking, feeling and behaving must cause distress or problems that last over time. Indeed, personality disorders can last for a long time. Absalom qualifies for a personality disorder because his way of thinking and behaving caused the greatest problems and distress to King David. King David was overthrown and ousted from office, through the activities of his son Absalom.

5\. If you have a personality disorder, you may have a difficult time relating to others in the way that is expected by your culture. A person with a personality disorder may not be aware of this discrepancy between his thoughts and behaviour and those accepted by society. Absalom had a difficult time relating with the other army commanders and other children of David. Joab, the commander of David’s armies, could not accept the existence of Absalom in the nation and therefore he put him to death and buried him under a heap of stones.

Absalom: A Man with a Borderline Personality Disorder

1\. ABSALOM - BORDERS ON REAL MADNESS:

Borderline Personality Disorder is called ‘borderline’ because doctors previously thought that it was right on the border of full-blown madness.

Indeed, Absalom’s mental state borders on outright madness. Absalom’s actions, where he attempted to overthrow a legitimate king and bring down the institution of the royal family reeks of the logic of a mad man.

And it came to pass after forty years, that Absalom said unto the king, I pray thee, let me go and pay my vow, which I have vowed unto the LORD, in Hebron. For thy servant vowed a vow while I abode at Geshur in Syria, saying, If the LORD shall bring me again indeed to Jerusalem, then I will serve the LORD. And the king said unto him, Go in peace. So he arose, and went to Hebron. But Absalom sent spies throughout all the tribes of Israel, saying, AS SOON AS YE HEAR THE SOUND OF THE TRUMPET, THEN YE SHALL SAY, ABSALOM REIGNETH in Hebron.

Absalom was a bizarre prince and son! He was a son who could engage in a conspiracy against his own father. Borderline Personality Disorder is the most common personality disorder around. In other words, it is the most common type of bizarre personality that you could encounter. Therefore, it is the most common type of bizarre personality someone could marry. It is also the most common type of bizarre personality an employee could have. It is also the most common type of bizarre personality that could become a pastor or a bishop. This type of personality disorder leads to weird sons, daughters, employees, pastors and bishops.

3\. ABSALOM - THINKS DIFFERENTLY: Absalom’s views were different from the views of the people in town. Most of the people in town loved David and accepted him as their king. Not so with Absalom! Absalom saw something wrong with David’s leadership.

Borderline Personality Disorder thinks differently from other people. The pattern of thinking in a Borderline Personality Disorder is uneasy and disturbed. The person with this disorder has views that are at variance with everyone else.

And Absalom rose up early, and stood beside the way of the gate: and it was so, that when any man that had a controversy came to the king for judgment, then Absalom called unto him, and said, Of what city art thou? 4\. ABSALOM - UNDERSTAND THINGS DIFFERENTLY:

Borderline Personality Disorder feels, understands and perceives everything differently from other people, giving rise to conflicts and bizarre relationships. When everyone understands the issue, the person with Borderline Personality Disorder does not understand it. David forgave Absalom for killing his brother Amnon and accepted him back in town.

The king said to Joab, “Very well, I will do it. GO, BRING BACK THE YOUNG MAN ABSALOM.” Joab fell with his face to the ground to pay him honor, and he blessed the king. Joab said, “Today your servant knows that he has found favor in your eyes, my lord the king, because the king has granted his servant’s request.” Then Joab went to Geshur and brought Absalom back to Jerusalem.

2 Samuel 14:21-23 (NIV)

However, as a person with Borderline Personality Disorder, Absalom did not live his life in gratitude towards his father but saw his father’s mercy and love as an opportunity to conspire against him and seize the throne.

A person with borderline personality understands everything differently. Absalom understood the forgiveness and the love in a different way. He took the love and forgiveness as an opportunity to overthrow his father.

Do you have anybody who works in your organization like that? Do you have a relative or family member who always understands love, kindness and goodness in the wrong way? Do you know anyone who took the kindness of an organisation as an opportunity to fight against it and overthrow it?

Absalom was insecure from the moment he proclaimed himself king. He was afraid that he would lose his newfound position. Such is the fear of a person with Borderline Personality Disorder!

They are constantly afraid of losing their place because they know that they are not doing the right thing. Borderline Personality Disorder is afraid of being abandoned. What does this mean? Borderline Personality Disorder is afraid of being dismissed from his job. It is not normal to live in constant fear of being dismissed or set aside. Borderline personalities are in constant fear that they will be dumped or pushed away for some reason. I have encountered a number of employees who lived in constant fear of being dismissed. Obviously, they were deviants and were not working well.

Borderline personalities have intense, close and dependent relationships. A borderline personality is actually insecure. They may have uncontrollable anger, fear, anxiety, hatred, sadness and love.

Absalom had great love for his sister, Tamar. Absalom also had great hatred for his brother Amnon. Just like every borderline personality, he was over-emotional, possessive and filled with uncontrollable anger and hatred. These mood swings of a borderline personality, change frequently and suddenly and may last a few hours or a few days. Absalom had a close relationship with his father, David. He is one of the sons that we hear about and know about. King David loved him and had such a close relationship with him that he could not imagine Absalom being killed, even though Absalom was trying to kill David. 7\. ABSALOM - UNSTABLE FRIENDSHIPS:

Borderline Personality Disorder has close and intense relationships, which are also unstable. These close relationships can change suddenly. They have relationships with family and friends and employment relationships that swing from very close to extreme anger. A paranoid Borderline Personality Disorder can be suspicious of everyone around them, and believe there are conspiracies to block their careers at work. Borderline personalities can carry grudges for years, and try to punish their targets of blame.

He was a man without forgiveness. A man of intense, but unstable, relationships! He had a close relationship with his sister and with his father, but each of these relationships turned around overnight and became a source of confusion and conflict.

9\. ABSALOM - EXTREME ANGER AND EMOTIONAL SWINGS:

Borderline Personality Disorder have inappropriate, dangerous and explosive anger. The anger of a person with a Borderline Personality Disorder can be dangerous to his personal life, to his reputation and to his employment. Therapists and lawyers have noted that many frivolous and vexatious cases in labour courts, employment courts and family courts are brought there by people with Borderline Personality Disorders. Many people who fight vexatious cases in court are simply contentious personalities with a Borderline Personality Disorder. Their personality disorder causes them to hover on the border of madness.

Absalom’s extreme emotional swings helped to confirm his madness and his Borderline Personality Disorder. On one end, he was an emotional protector of his sister who had been violated. On the other end, he was so angry with his brother, Amnon, that he murdered him when the opportunity presented itself. Absalom controlled his emotions for two years but his extreme anger and hatred for his brother caused him to become a murderer of his brother and an attempted murderer of his own father!

This impulsive decision cost Absalom his life. It is clear from all these examples that Absalom fulfils the picture of an irrational and impulsive Borderline Personality Disorder patient.
