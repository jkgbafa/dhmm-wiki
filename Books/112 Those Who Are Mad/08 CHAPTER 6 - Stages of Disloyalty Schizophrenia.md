### CHAPTER 6\

Stages of Disloyalty: Schizophrenia\
---------------------------------------------------------

Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.

1 Corinthians 4:2

The stages of disloyalty\* describe how a person gradually becomes disloyal and unfaithful. No one plans to be treacherous. No one wants to be Judas! No one wants to be cast away like Lucifer! But how is it that so many good people become disloyal? How did they change to become the monsters that we know them to be?

Disloyalty is often a gradual process and the stages of disloyalty reveal how this process happens. Amazingly, every single stage of disloyalty is comparable to a significant mental symptom or sign. Most pastors have not analysed how the stages of disloyalty match up with significant mental health symptoms. Remember that mental illness is diagnosed through the abnormal thoughts, moods and behaviour that people have.

What are the stages of disloyalty?

The stages of disloyalty are the stages which a person goes through when he is becoming disloyal.

The stages of disloyalty are the stages which a person goes through when he is becoming a Judas.

The stages of disloyalty are the stages which a person goes through when he is becoming an Absalom.

The stages of disloyalty are the stages which a person goes through when he is turning into Lucifer.

The stages of disloyalty are simple:

1\. The independent spirit

2\. The stage of offence

3\. The stage of passivity

4\. The stage of being critical

5\. The stage of being political

6\. The stage of deception

7\. The stage of open rebellion

8\. The stage of destruction

1\. THE STAGE OF INDEPENDENCE:

O come, let us worship and bow down: let us kneel before the LORD our maker.

For he is our God; and WE ARE THE PEOPLE OF HIS PASTURE, AND THE SHEEP OF HIS HAND. …

Psalm 95:6-7

The Bible teaches us to work without being independent of our shepherd or of the house of God. Attempting to become independent of what God has created you to depend on will result in abnormality, destruction and defeat. The Word of God teaches inter-dependence. God does not teach us to be independent. On the contrary, the Word of God reveals that we need one another.

God having provided some better thing for us, that THEY WITHOUT US SHOULD NOT BE MADE PERFECT.

Hebrews 11:40

Independence occurs when people withdraw from the group they belong to. Schizophrenia is all about a negative and senseless withdrawal from the world you belong to.

Independence is an abnormality in nature. A sheep who seeks to gain independence of its shepherd is abnormal. Sheep are the most dependent animals on earth.

Independence is an abnormality in nature. An unborn child who pushes his way out of his mother’s womb to gain independence before time is abnormal.

The stage of independence is an abnormality, which matches closely with what is known as the “Negative withdrawal from life” symptoms of schizophrenia. People who withdraw from a group which they should have been a part of, exhibit behaviour that is uncannily similar to the negative withdrawal symptoms of schizophrenia.

People with the “negative symptoms” of schizophrenia are so called because the patients negatively withdraw from the world around them and take no interest in every day social interactions (Alogia, Anhedonia, Avolition, Affective flattening, Asociality). People in the independent stage of disloyalty also withdraw from the church world around them and take little interest in the goings on in the church.

People in the independent stage of disloyalty withdraw emotionally and physically from the church and its leadership. In the “negative symptoms’ of schizophrenia, there is a withdrawal of the emotions of the person, and he now appears emotionless and flat. Always remember the word “withdrawal” when you read about the “negative symptoms” of schizophrenia. They are the “withdrawal from life” symptoms. They are the symptoms that create independence and separation.

When people have the “negative symptoms” of schizophrenia, there is a loss of interest, a loss of motivation, a loss of enjoyment, a loss of emotions and a loss of function. Similarly when people are in the independent stage of disloyalty, there is a loss of interest, a loss of motivation, a loss of enjoyment, a loss of emotions and a loss of function in the church and the group they once belonged to.

2\. THE STAGE OF OFFENCE:

Woe unto the world because of offences! for it must needs be that offences come; but woe to that man by whom the offence cometh!

Matthew 18:7

The stage of offence often triggers the “negative withdrawal symptoms” of schizophrenia. The Bible teaches us that offences and stumbling blocks will surely happen. Offences and stumbling blocks cause people to withdraw. Being offended is inevitable. However, there are people who are offended so deeply that they never recover from their hurts and offences. They have an abnormal reaction to offence and develop withdrawal and negative symptoms. Their hurts and offences lead to their destruction and the end of their ministries. The stage of offence is a common occurrence in Christian leadership.

The stage of offence also matches up closely with a Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder. In Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder, there are individuals who have been greatly offended, hurt and affected by a traumatic experience. In the church setting, there are people who are so offended, hurt and affected by a traumatic experience that they cannot think normally any more. Such people can be said to be suffering from Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder.

I have seen people so offended because they were asked questions about money they had used for a project. I have seen people so offended because they were rebuked at a meeting. The offence was so deep that they resigned from the church and went away huffing and puffing!

I have seen people so offended because they were asked questions which others were also asked. They were so offended by the implications of those questions that they went off on a tangent and became enemies of the church. Some people cannot stand the stress of strong administrative and corrective meetings.

3\. THE STAGE OF PASSIVITY:

Cursed be he that doeth the work of the LORD deceitfully, and cursed be he that keepeth back his sword from blood.

Jeremiah 48:10

The Bible teaches us to work without holding back or being passive. Holding back, quietness and withdrawal into passivity is a classic symptom of the “passivity stage” of disloyalty.

The stage of passivity matches up closely with the “negative or withdrawing symptoms” of schizophrenia. Remember that you need five important symptoms to make a diagnosis of schizophrenia. The five symptoms of schizophrenia are delusions, hallucinations, disorganized speech, disorganized behaviour and negative (withdrawing) symptoms.

In schizophrenia, the “negative (withdrawing) symptoms” are exhibited when an individual withdraws from normal social interaction, normal friendships and normal pleasures.

A schizophrenic patient will also exhibit a withdrawal from normal thought life and a withdrawal of normal speech resulting in a quiet or silent person.

This is called Alogia. In the church or work setting, there are people who are so offended, hurt and affected by experiences in the church that they stop speaking and withdraw from fellowship. They become silent people with no comment to make!

In the stage of passivity, the people do not interact or speak out as much as they should. These quiet people are often breeding dangerous, negative thoughts within themselves. They will soon manifest as yet another Lucifer! They will soon manifest as yet another Absalom! They will soon openly rebel and show the whole world that they are the next Judas Iscariot!

4\. THE CRITICAL STAGE:

Do all things without murmurings and disputings:

Philippians 2:14

The Bible teaches us to work without murmuring and disputing. Murmuring and disputing are classic symptoms of the criticism stage of disloyalty. People in this stage of disloyalty say many things, jumping from one slanderous story to another, raising questions, doubts and suspicions about many things.

Amazingly, a person with schizophrenia has disorganized speech and also quickly jumps from one unrelated topic to another. He engages in an incoherent “word salad” repeating things which other people have said or speaking about things that do not exist.

In the stage of criticism, a person says things that you would not expect him to say. He criticizes everything, including things that he praised some time ago. This is truly disorganized thinking.

The critical stage of disloyalty matches up closely with the symptom of disorganized thinking and disorganized speech of a schizophrenia patient. Remember that schizophrenia has five important symptoms for diagnosis. The five symptoms of schizophrenia are delusions, hallucinations, disorganized speech, disorganized behaviour and negative (withdrawing) symptoms.

In schizophrenia, the person’s thoughts are disorganized and do not make sense any longer. In disorganized speech, a person may jump from one unrelated topic to another, speak very quickly and stumble over words so that other people find it difficult to hear or understand what he is saying. In disorganized speech, communication is difficult and sometimes impossible to understand.

Similarly, in the church world, it is difficult to communicate with rebellious people. Rebels are difficult to understand. No one can understand what they want or what they are thinking about. Do you love God? Do you want the anointing? Do you want money? Do you want God? Do you want to be in the ministry? Do you want to be employed? Do you want to continue to be a servant of God?

Why do you want to destroy yourself? What is wrong with you? Why have you changed so dramatically? Why are you exhibiting such abnormal speech and behaviour? Why do you speak lies and slander? Why do you speak with incomplete sentences and so many untruths?

Why do you speak a series of jumbled up half-truths and half lies? Why do you say the opposite of what you said a few years ago, as though your Bible has been edited? Why do you speak words of honour yesterday and then speak words of dishonour and ingratitude today? Do you change your beliefs so radically every few years?

Why do you say somebody is your father and at the same time attack him in public? Why do you say somebody is your father and dishonour him at the first chance? Your thinking seems to be disorganised! You seem to have gone mad! Why do you say you believe in God and yet disobey the Word of God so blatantly? Your speech and your thinking are disorganised and it is a symptom of madness! It is the incoherent and conflicting confusion of ministers gone mad! You are mad!

5\. THE POLITICAL STAGE:

I HAVE NOT SAT WITH VAIN PERSONS, neither will I go in with dissemblers. I have hated the congregation of evil doers; and will not sit with the wicked.

Psalm 26:4-5

The Bible teaches us not to sit with wretched and miserable men. Do not sit down to have lunch or fellowship with empty barrels and wretched miserable personalities who only plan evil. A good person will never plan to attack God’s servant. You lack biblical intelligence when you attack God’s work.

IF THEY SAY, “COME ALONG WITH US; let’s lie in wait for innocent blood, let’s ambush some harmless soul; let’s swallow them alive, like the grave, and whole, like those who go down to the pit; WE WILL GET ALL SORTS OF VALUABLE THINGS and fill our houses with plunder; cast lots with us; we will all share the loot”—

my son, DO NOT GO ALONG WITH THEM, do not set foot on their paths; FOR THEIR FEET RUSH INTO EVIL, they are swift to shed blood.

Proverbs 1:11-16 (NIV)

The Bible teaches us to avoid being drawn into groups that are up to no good. There are many groups in churches, which are up to no good. Rebellious pastors always group together! They form cliques, associations and friendships! They take pictures together and they go for dinner together! They show the whole world that they are associated with each other!

However, the Bible teaches us, “If they say, come along with us, let us swallow up our former church and pastor with grief and in evil, do not go along with them.” It is amazing that such people will always reach out to others to see who will join them. Indeed, they need the company of each other to strengthen themselves in their delusions and madness. Such people are willingly ignorant of the prophecy that predicts the doom of wretched rebels!

ASSOCIATE YOURSELVES, O YE PEOPLE, AND YE SHALL BE BROKEN IN PIECES; and give ear, all ye of far countries: gird yourselves, and ye shall be broken in pieces; gird yourselves, and ye shall be broken in pieces.

Isaiah 8:9

The political stage of disloyalty is an abnormality which matches closely with a key symptom of schizophrenia known as the “delusions of schizophrenia”. A delusion is a belief that someone maintains in spite of whatever evidence is shown. There is a special delusion called “Induced delusions” or “folie à deux”.

Shared Madness

Induced delusions (folie à deux) is a form of delusion where two people who are usually in a close relationship, share the same delusions. One of the partners may be the dominant personality who influences the weaker personality into adopting the delusion.

It is amazing how people form groups based on an evil belief or a delusion. After a while, you will realise that all members of the group get lost.

This shared delusion becomes the basis for the destruction of the entire group. It is amazing that a delusion in one mentally ill patient can be transmitted to another perfectly normal person.

If people are causing divisions among you, give a first and second warning. AFTER THAT, HAVE NOTHING MORE TO DO WITH THEM. For people like that have turned away from the truth, and their own sins condemn them.

Titus 3:10-11 (NLT)

It is important that you ensure that someone’s madness is not transmitted to you. Have nothing more to do with them. Associations with evil, treacherous and disloyal people are not neutral encounters. You may receive an impartation of their madness! Watch out! You can easily imbibe the delusions and wickedness of the person you interact with. We are learning here that you can actually receive a transmission of the mental health symptom that another person has.

I have watched how people, filled with madness and hatred, transmit their madness to others until they have formed a group of crazy men. All the people in that group are filled with the same hatred which has no reason nor basis. Be careful that no one co-opts you to follow the madness of a wretched and wicked rebel!

6\. THE STAGE OF DECEPTION:

While evildoers and impostors will go from bad to worse, deceiving and being deceived.

2 Timothy 3:13 (NIV)

The stage of deception in disloyalty is an advanced condition in which people become convinced that right is wrong and wrong is right! Armed with these deceptions, they are able to carry out their slander, their blasphemies and their malicious acts with conviction. Many evildoers are convinced that they are doing the right thing. Some even see themselves as reformers and correctors of God’s church. Delusions and deceptions are strong symptoms of madness.

However, the Bible teaches us to avoid and to overcome deception. Deceiving and being deceived are classic symptoms of the deception stage of disloyalty. People in this stage of disloyalty are completely deluded and taken up in their deception. Satan is the father of deception and originator of all lies. When people are fully taken over by the devil, they tell lies, they deceive people and they deceive themselves too. Deception is necessary for almost every kind of evil to thrive. Without some form of deception, many evils would not exist.

Ye are of your father the devil, and the lusts of your father ye will do. He was a murderer from the beginning, and abode not in the truth, because THERE IS NO TRUTH IN HIM. When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father of it.

John 8:44

The deception stage of disloyalty is an abnormality which matches closely with a key symptom of schizophrenia. In terms of mental illness, the deception stage matches closely with what is known as the delusions of schizophrenia.

A delusion is a belief that someone has in spite of whatever evidence is shown!

The Bible warns us about the danger of delusions. Indeed, the scripture reveals that delusions are a prelude and precursor to damnation and destruction.

And for this cause GOD SHALL SEND THEM STRONG DELUSION, that they should believe a lie: THAT THEY ALL MIGHT BE DAMNED who believed not the truth, but had pleasure in unrighteousness.

2 Thessalonians 2:11-12

A delusion is a fixed and false belief that is resistant to reason! Many rebellious people have fixed and false beliefs that are resistant to reason.

A delusion is a fixed and false belief that is resistant to confrontation with actual fact! Many rebellious people have fixed and false beliefs that are resistant to confrontation with actual fact.

Most disloyal people are resistant to truth and facts. They prefer to hold on to their deceptions until they perish completely. This is why delusions are a cardinal symptom of madness.

No amount of negotiations and discussions will change a person with delusions. You may present them with all the truths and facts that you have and they will still not yield! They will insist on maintaining the course which they have chartered. It is so sad to see bishops, pastors and reverend ministers stuck in their delusions; unable to change, unable to stop doing evil and unable to say sorry.

A person who is unable to repent often has a delusion which he is holding onto. A person who is unable to change often has a delusion which he is holding onto.

A delusion is a mistaken or an unfounded opinion that is resistant to reason!

A delusion is an erroneous belief that a person holds on to in the face of contrary evidence!

You can see from the definitions of a delusion that it is a highly dangerous thing to have.

There must be some form of madness that will cause a person to hold firmly onto a deception until he is absolutely destroyed.

7\. THE STAGE OF OPEN REBELLION:

FOR I KNOW THY REBELLION, AND THY STIFF NECK: behold, while I am yet alive with you this day, ye have been rebellious against the LORD; and how much more after my death?

Gather unto me all the elders of your tribes, and your officers, that I may speak these words in their ears, and call heaven and earth to record against them.

For I know that after my death ye will utterly corrupt yourselves, and turn aside from the way which I have commanded you; and evil will befall you in the latter days; because ye will do evil in the sight of the Lord, to provoke him to anger through the work of your hands.

And Moses spake in the ears of all the congregation of Israel the words of this song, until they were ended.

Deuteronomy 31:27-30

Open rebellion is where a person openly attacks an authority figure that he should rather honour. Open rebellion is the last stage in the series of unfortunate and improper patterns of behaviour called disloyalty.

Rebellion is found in all men. However, through teaching and through the scriptures, the natural instincts of mankind to rebel are quelled!

Moses spoke from his heart when he warned the children of Israel before he died. He told them that he knew they would rebel! He knew the Israelites would be disobedient and disloyal because that was the nature of man. Man is often irrational and irreverent.

The open rebellion stage of disloyalty is an abnormality which matches closely with a key symptom of schizophrenia. The open rebellion stage of disloyalty matches closely with what is known as the disorganised behaviour of schizophrenia.

The symptom of disorganised behaviour manifests as bizarre and inappropriate behaviour and actions.

Most people in the open rebellious stage of disloyalty exhibit bizarre and inappropriate behaviour. They behave as though they have schizophrenia.

They may openly attack someone they have previously hailed and honoured.

They may go out into the public, insult and accuse someone they have loved before.

They may dishonour dignitaries and speak words that are inappropriate and bizarre.

People with schizophrenia have disorganized thoughts, speech and behaviour.

Indeed, the thoughts, speech and behaviour of openly rebellious people are completely bizarre, completely out of order and completely inappropriate.

People with open rebellion spread preposterous lies and wicked stories with the aim of demeaning, debasing and degrading God’s servants.

Whilst good Christians seek to honour God’s servants, openly rebellious individuals do the exact opposite! Many mad men seek to disgrace, embarrass, defame and insult God’s holy prophets. Openly rebellious behaviour is completely inappropriate and bizarre and that is why it is so comparable to schizophrenia!
