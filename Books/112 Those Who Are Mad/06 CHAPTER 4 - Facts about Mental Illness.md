### CHAPTER 4\

Facts about Mental Illness\
------------------------------------------------

This is an evil among all things that are done under the sun, that there is one event unto all: yea, also THE HEART OF THE SONS OF MEN IS FULL OF EVIL, AND MADNESS IS IN THEIR HEART while they live, and after that they go to the dead.

Ecclesiastes 9:3

1\. Understanding mental illness will help a minister of the gospel relate with abnormal patterns that are often ascribed to demonic presence, satanic presence or other things like treachery and disloyalty.

2\. Mental illnesses can create abnormalities in the way you think, feel, or behave.

3. 7Mental illnesses can affect people of any age, race, religion, or income level. A mental illness is a medical condition that disrupts a person’s thinking, feeling, mood and ability to relate to others and their daily functioning.7

4\. Many factors (such as trauma or a history of abuse), biological factors, life’s experiences and family history of mental illness cause mental health conditions.

5\. Abnormal mental behaviour can affect working and personal relationships and can create unsafe living environments.

6. 8970 million people around the world struggle with some mental illness or drug abuse.

7. 1 in 4 people will be affected by a mental illness at some point in their lives.

8. 14.3% of deaths worldwide are attributable to mental health problems.8

9. 98 million deaths that occur in the world each year are attributable to mental disorders.9

10\. Depression is the leading cause of disability worldwide.

11\. There are some top mental illnesses every minister should know about because they will affect pastors, ministers, pastors’ wives and congregations alike. They are:

1\. Schizophrenia

2\. Bipolar Disorder

3\. Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder

4\. Anxiety

5\. Depression

6\. Antisocial Personality Disorder

7\. Borderline Personality Disorder

8\. Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder

9\. Dementia

10\. High Conflict Personalities
