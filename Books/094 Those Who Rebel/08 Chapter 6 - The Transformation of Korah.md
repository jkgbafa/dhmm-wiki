### Chapter 6\

The Transformation of Korah\
-------------------------------------------------

Woe to them! For they have gone the way of Cain, and for pay they have rushed headlong into the error of Balaam, AND PERISHED IN THE REBELLION OF KORAH. These are the men who are hidden reefs in your love feasts when they feast with you without fear, caring for themselves; clouds without water, carried along by winds; autumn trees without fruit, doubly dead, uprooted; wild waves of the sea, casting up their own shame like foam; wandering stars, for whom the black darkness has been reserved forever.

Jude 1:11-13 (NASB95)

All rebels are transformed personalities! Rebellion transforms a person into a completely different person. They are not what they used to be before they entered into rebellion and disobedience. Korah is an example of someone who was radically turned into a wretched, miserable failure.

Korah was a Levite who led a rebellion against Moses and Aaron during the Israelites’ journey in the wilderness. He, along with Dathan and Abiram from the tribe of Reuben, defied Moses’ leadership and Aaron’s priesthood, claiming that all Israelites were holy and that Moses was taking too much authority for himself.

Korah was a defiant and rebellious man!

Now Korah the son of Izhar, the son of Kohath, the son of Levi, with Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, and On the son of Peleth, sons of Reuben, took action, and they rose up before Moses, together with some of the sons of Israel, two hundred and fifty leaders of the congregation, chosen in the assembly, men of renown. They assembled together against Moses and Aaron, and said to them, “You have gone far enough, for all the congregation are holy, every one of them, and the LORD is in their midst; so why do you exalt yourselves above the assembly of the LORD?”

Numbers 16:1-3 (NASB95)

You will notice how rebels you used to know as noble and upright servants of God have turned into miserable monsters. A monster is someone who is grotesque, terrifying and abnormal in every respect. Indeed, Korah turned into a grotesque, terrifying and abnormal version of what he was created to be.

Korah turned into someone who was a hidden reef, doubly dead, a cloud without water, a tree without fruit and a wandering star! What a change came upon this honourable elder of Israel!

In this chapter I want us to study the transformation of Korah into the monstrosity he became.

1\. Those who rebel are changed into hidden reefs.

Woe to them! For they have gone the way of Cain, and for pay they have rushed headlong into the error of Balaam, and perished in the rebellion of Korah. THESE ARE THE MEN WHO ARE HIDDEN REEFS IN YOUR LOVE FEASTS when they feast with you without fear, caring for themselves; clouds without water, carried along by winds; autumn trees without fruit, doubly dead, uprooted; wild waves of the sea, casting up their own shame like foam; wandering stars, for whom the black darkness has been reserved forever.

Jude 1:11-13 (NASB95)

A rebel is a hidden reef! A reef is an underwater formation of rocks. Hidden reefs are very dangerous to ships. Many ships have run aground and been shipwrecked on hidden reefs.

Rebellious men are very dangerous to the church. A hidden reef is someone who is hidden but extremely harmful. People who are hidden reefs within the leadership structure can cause damage and hurt to the church and ministry. Do not allow dangerous rebels to operate in your church. Mark and avoid them! Dangerous rebels must be driven away from the church.

Cast out the scorner and strife will cease! (Proverbs 22:10). Scorners are defiant mockers. They are hidden reefs! They must be treated for what they really are - very dangerous people. Have no fellowship with dangerous rebels who are dedicated to attacking their former church and their former pastor.

2\. Those who rebel are transformed into clouds without water.

Woe to them! For they have gone the way of Cain, and for pay they have rushed headlong into the error of Balaam, AND PERISHED IN THE REBELLION OF KORAH. These are the men who are hidden reefs in your love feasts when they feast with you without fear, caring for themselves; CLOUDS WITHOUT WATER, carried along by winds; autumn trees without fruit, doubly dead, uprooted; wild waves of the sea, casting up their shame like foam; wandering stars, for whom the black darkness has been reserved forever.

Jude 1:11-13 (NASB95)

A rebel is a cloud without water! He looks as though he has a lot to offer but actually has nothing to offer! How sad it is to encounter all these clouds without water who look so impressive and righteous though they have nothing to give.

Someone who refuses to obey rules is a cloud without water.

Someone who refuses to accept the normal standards of behaviour is just a rebel and is a cloud without water.

Someone who challenges authority and leadership is often an empty barrel making a lot of noise. Do not follow a cloud without water; you will be following an apparition. The rebel you admire is simply a mirage!

3\. Those who rebel are transformed into trees without fruit.

Woe to them! For they have gone the way of Cain, and for pay they have rushed headlong into the error of Balaam, AND PERISHED IN THE REBELLION OF KORAH. These are the men who are hidden reefs in your love feasts when they feast with you without fear, caring for themselves; clouds without water, carried along by winds; AUTUMN TREES WITHOUT FRUIT, doubly dead, uprooted; wild waves of the sea, casting up their own shame like foam; wandering stars, for whom the black darkness has been reserved forever.

Jude 1:11-13 (NASB95)

A rebel is a tree without fruit!

Rebellious people are trees without fruits because they usually have nothing to show for their lives. They do not have churches! They cannot grow a church! They cannot build a church! They cannot gather people! They cannot pray. They are not spiritual. They are not anointed. They may be intelligent but are spiritually stupid. Trees without fruit! They cannot pastor a church! They cannot do the work of God!

Someone who resists authority is often a tree without fruit.

Someone who openly opposes a person in authority is often a tree without fruit.

Someone who defies authority and leadership is often a tree without fruit.

Someone who purposefully disobeys authority and rises against authority or leadership is often a tree without fruit.

Someone who openly opposes authority and undermines leadership becomes a tree without fruit.

Someone who takes part in revolting against authority or an established government becomes a tree without fruit.

4\. Those who rebel are twice dead.

Woe to them! For they have gone the way of Cain, and for pay they have rushed headlong into the error of Balaam, AND PERISHED IN THE REBELLION OF KORAH. These are the men who are hidden reefs in your love feasts when they feast with you without fear, caring for themselves; clouds without water, carried along by winds; autumn trees without fruit, DOUBLY DEAD, uprooted; wild waves of the sea, casting up their own shame like foam; wandering stars, for whom the black darkness has been reserved forever.

Jude 1:11-13 (NASB95)

A rebel is twice dead!

Rebels are doubly dead! They are twice dead!

A rebel is dead in two different ways. He is dead because he is part of the Adamic rebellion and he is dead a second time because of his rebellion against the church.

Someone who acts in a way that shows that he does not accept the control or influence of authority and leadership is dead in two different ways. That is why he is described as doubly dead.

Someone who fights against the rule of a leader or a government is doubly dead!

Someone who fights against an established leadership is doubly dead.

Someone who acts against or shows disobedience to the conventions that are normally accepted by authorities is twice dead.

5\. Those who rebel are turned into uprooted trees.

Woe to them! For they have gone the way of Cain, and for pay they have rushed headlong into the error of Balaam, AND PERISHED IN THE REBELLION OF KORAH. These are the men who are hidden reefs in your love feasts when they feast with you without fear, caring for themselves; clouds without water, carried along by winds; AUTUMN TREES without fruit, doubly dead, UPROOTED; wild waves of the sea, casting up their own shame like foam; wandering stars, for whom the black darkness has been reserved forever.

Jude 1:11-13 (NASB95)

A rebel is an uprooted tree! Many rebellious ministers are uprooted trees.

Someone who refuses to obey the rules of the authority of their church is often just an uprooted tree. Someone who does not accept the rules of their home church is just another uprooted tree.

I remember one uprooted tree who said, “I do not want to be the subject of any more meetings.” This fellow was uprooted from his position and became a withered tree. He resisted the authorities and the leadership decisions of his church. Obviously, he did not want to accept the standards of behaviour that were part of that ministry. He became an uprooted tree because he refused to obey the rules and accept the normal standards of behaviour that were accepted by anyone who was part of that church. Rebels are men who are uprooted from their rightful position.

6\. Those who rebel are changed into wandering stars.

Woe to them! For they have gone the way of Cain, and for pay they have rushed headlong into the error of Balaam, AND PERISHED IN THE REBELLION OF KORAH. These are the men who are hidden reefs in your love feasts when they feast with you without fear, caring for themselves; clouds without water, carried along by winds; autumn trees without fruit, doubly dead, uprooted; wild waves of the sea, casting up their own shame like foam; WANDERING STARS, for whom the black darkness has been reserved forever.

Jude 1:11-13 (NASB95)

A rebel is a wandering star! A wandering minister! An unstable personality. A man who belongs nowhere in particular.

A rebel is someone who has no stability because he will not obey the rules in his home church.

He is forced to move around from place to place, claiming that he is an international or itinerant speaker. Such wandering stars seek to start new churches because they are disobedient to the authorities in their home churches.

Someone who purposefully disobeys authority and rises against authority or leadership is a wandering, unstable personality.

Someone who openly opposes authority and undermines leadership becomes a wandering star.

Someone who takes part in revolting against authority becomes a wandering star because he is unstable.

Wandering stars are usually broke and poverty-stricken, without any stability in their lives.

7\. Those who rebel are transformed into shameful men.

Woe to them! For they have gone the way of Cain, and for pay they have rushed headlong into the error of Balaam, AND PERISHED IN THE REBELLION OF KORAH. These are the men who are hidden reefs in your love feasts when they feast with you without fear, caring for themselves; clouds without water, carried along by winds; autumn trees without fruit, doubly dead, uprooted; wild waves of the sea, CASTING UP THEIR OWN SHAME LIKE FOAM; wandering stars, for whom the black darkness has been reserved forever.

Jude 1:11-13 (NASB95)

A rebel is a man of shame! A rebel is a man who casts up shame like the foam of the sea. You can virtually see the white foam coming out of the mouths of wretched rebels as they speak their slander, their hatred and their venom against God and against the leaders of the church.

Someone who purposefully disobeys authority and rises against the leadership of the church becomes a man of shame. Rebels are men of shame who constantly beg for money. There are rebels who seek to extort money from their former employers. Such people are men who cast up their own shame like foam.

Rebels cannot hide their hatred from the world. I remember a friend of mine who described the hatred of a group of rebel pastors. He said, “I have never, in all my life, seen such hatred and venom in anybody I have encountered.”

Rebels cannot hide their desire for dishonest wealth. I remember a friend who encountered a group of rebellious pastors. He said, “I have never seen clergymen who are so shamelessly desirous of money like these fellows. These rebel pastors shamelessly demand huge amounts of money, seeking to become suddenly wealthy through extortion and manipulation.” It is sad to see rebellious men shamelessly seeking for wealth through extortion and manipulation.

This is how the Bible describes them: “They cast up their own shame like foam”!

8\. Those who rebel are reserved for the black darkness.

Woe to them! For they have gone the way of Cain, and for pay they have rushed headlong into the error of Balaam, AND PERISHED IN THE REBELLION OF KORAH. These are the men who are hidden reefs in your love feasts when they feast with you without fear, caring for themselves; clouds without water, carried along by winds; autumn trees without fruit, doubly dead, uprooted; wild waves of the sea, casting up their own shame like foam; wandering stars, FOR WHOM THE BLACK DARKNESS HAS BEEN RESERVED FOREVER.

Jude 1:11-13 (NASB95)

A rebel is headed to the judgment of blackness and darkness. A rebel is someone who does not obey rules and therefore will suffer in the difficulties of black darkness. Rebels are reserved for the blackness of darkness.

Someone who fights against an established government is headed for judgment. I remember watching a documentary of some rebels who tried to overthrow a government. They were caught, tried and summarily executed. No one even attempted to defend these fellows in court. There was no hope for them. The black darkness had been reserved for them because they were rebels who actually tried to overthrow a government. Black darkness is the portion of all rebels!

There are pastors who seek to harass, persecute, intimidate, overthrow, overturn and fight against authorities in established churches. Such people will have the fate of all rebels. Black darkness!

The blackness of darkness speaks of the difficulties, the financial hardships, the problems, the crises, the distress, the poverty, the spiritual darkness and the demonic hordes that will flood the lives of rebels. Prepare for the blackness of darkness if you want to be a rebel.
