### Chapter 7\

Letters of Defiance\
-----------------------------------------

Every person is to be in subjection to the governing authorities. For there is no authority except from God, and those which exist are established by God. Therefore whoever resists authority has opposed the ordinance of God; and they who have opposed will receive condemnation upon themselves.

Romans 13:1-2 (NASB95)

Defiance is the open rebellion of a bold and fearless person who has lost all regard and respect for authority. Defiance is rebellion! Defiance is a daring and bold resistance to authority! Make sure you never fall for the deceptions that will make you speak or think like a rebel. A rebellious spirit is what comes upon people when they defy authorities.

Some defiant men manifest their rebellion by refusing to come to meetings or refusing to have any discussions whatsoever.

Lucifer was a rebel. He defied and resisted Almighty God Himself. When he fell, his nature was changed drastically and he turned into the horrible creature called satan. Lucifer means “the bright morning light” but satan means “the adversary” and “opposer”. Since Lucifer fell, he has inspired all other deviants and rebels.

In the following letters, you will see the defiance, resistance and opposition to a senior pastor by his assistant. The letters show how a rebel defies someone who has been set over him. When a man falls into rebellion, his words give him away. A rebellious man’s words reveal his utter contempt for his former boss.

Rebellions are inspired by the devil and the words of a rebellious person are often demonic. They are words from hell and are easy to identify. In every country and in every situation, rebellious people speak in the same way and with the same spirit.

In this chapter, I have produced examples of words you should expect from a rebellious person who has risen up to challenge, defy and oppose you. As you develop in the ministry, you will hear words like these.

Rebels Are Men with No Fruits!

Fortunately, God has given us a sign that will help us identify these rebellious and empty words. Rebellious men give appealing and impressive speeches but have no fruits! You shall know them by their fruits! You shall know a tree by its fruits. The pride, bitterness and defiance of rebellious people come out through their words. They have well-framed words which are laced with the spirit of defiance, unrighteous judgment, self-righteousness and piety.

But these rebellious and defiant men have no fruits!

Rebellious men have big mouths but no fruits!

They have loud mouths and sharp fingers to type wickedness!

They spend hours writing defamatory words and speeches.

Yea, they shall be judged for their wickedness!

Jude describes such rebellious people as trees without fruit! Jesus said we shall know them by their fruits. The fruit that a person has in the ministry tells you all that you need to know about him.

Why don’t these defiant people have fruits?

Where are their churches?

Where are the souls that they have won?

Where are the works of God that they have built?

Why can’t they grow a church or a fellowship?

Jesus said, “I will build my church.” Why can’t they help Jesus build His church? Why do they spend all their time fighting those who are building the church?

Watch out for men without fruits who constantly attack you on social media and other platforms. Such people cannot build a church of twenty members but can speak great swelling words of arrogance. They speak the words that come from “clouds without water” and “trees without fruit”. These words are the messages of “wandering stars” who are “twice dead” and “reserved for the blackest of darkness”.

In the following letters, you will recognize the spirit of defiance and a certain bold resistance to authority. You must not be moved by the letters or the writing of rebellious men. Pray that you never become a defiant or rebellious person.

I have produced these letters so that you will not be moved if someone speaks to you in this way. They are the words of a defiant, rebellious spirit. They are words that hurt your spirit because they are words that come from satan who inspires all rebels.

Letters of a Junior Pastor to His Bishop

I want to show you twenty letters of a junior pastor to his spiritual father. The following are twenty letters written by a junior pastor to his senior pastor, bishop and father. The following letters are from a disgruntled former employee to his former employer. The following letters are written to the bishop of a large and successful church.

The following letters are written by a pastor to the bishop who appointed him as a pastor and ordained him into the ministry. The following letters are written by someone who could not grow a church beyond twenty members. The following letters are written by a pastor whose marriage was rescued by his spiritual father. The following letters are written by a man who literally failed in his ministry and in his marriage.

He writes these letters to his employer, his spiritual father and to the one who was in authority over him. You will notice the tone of self-righteous judgment and piety that this insolent and defiant pastor has.

TWENTY LETTERS OF DEFIANCE

1\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: FALSE LOVE

Dear Bishop,

I urge you to hear what I have to say to you. I do not mean to be disrespectful. I am warning you because I am concerned about you.

Be humble and consider your ways. I hope by the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ you will have some humility for some deep reflection upon your ways. I want to help you to do the right things for the glory of God.

Now, why do I care? Because I simply do. I care because if you do what is right, it will only be to God’s glory. I care because I won’t be loving enough if I watch you carry on in these dangerous ways.

Your faithful assistant

2\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: OVERCONFIDENCE

Dear Pastor,

You have veered from the right path and departed from your original beliefs: There is a gradual erosion of healthiness in the church that you lead. Knowing who you are, where you started from and your family background, I do know that your intentions for venturing into ministry were genuine.

However, it needs to be said that the church you are leading now has gradually metamorphosed into becoming a toxic culture and has largely deviated from sound biblical doctrine. I have watched you develop into a phantom of what you once were.

3\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: FAMILIARITY

Dear Pastor,

I have known you for a long time. No one knows you the way I do. That is why I am speaking to you in this way. I was there when you began; that is why I can see that you have deviated from the right course. I am bringing these issues up because I am concerned for you.

It is heart-breaking to even write this to you but I only do so out of concern and hope that there is still a glimpse of integrity in you somewhere so that you will save yourself from self-destruction. I have known your family for years and your arrogance and haughtiness are not something I can associate with your family.

Please don’t forget your roots!

4\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: ACCUSATIONS

Dear Pastor,

You are turning our church into a cult. Your teachings are no longer sound. Remember, on the internet, we see the characteristics of a cult. One is that it is difficult to leave and also a cult teaches a lot about loyalty. Based on these two characteristics, I believe you are building a cult now.

It is not by mistake that the word “culture” has in it the word “cult” and it is worth knowing that when a culture is not built on integrity, it turns into a cult. I wish to stress that you are leading the church, by your teachings, into a cult with an evil culture.

(Author’s comment: Please note that churches that emphasize being a family are often accused of being a cult. Jesus stressed on all Christians being one big family in His last prayer on earth. Jesus prayed, “That they may be one.”)

5\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: ACCUSATORY

Dear Pastor,

You are not God. Do not build the church around your personality or your desire for greatness. I can see that you are now building the church around your desire for greatness, so stop it.

6\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: ACCUSATIONS

Dear Pastor,

You now have an unquenchable desire for wealth. You have lost your integrity because your desire for money cannot be quenched. Your being the head has, to a large extent, prompted this.

Let us start with the myriad of projects which you are forcing the church to undertake. Consider the erratic means by which money can be requested for projects that are unsustainable or beyond the church’s means. There is nothing wrong with dreaming big and having faith in big plans yet if the means of executing these big plans would require putting undue stress on the people you lead, then it is wrong.

You must not force your congregation to cough up whatever finances are needed to accomplish your many dream projects.

With all due respect, it is the opinion of several that these projects are only serving to balloon your ego and are not necessary for the church.

7\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: FAULT FINDING

Dear Bishop,

God is not happy with your desire to do these church projects with speed. It is the work of the flesh. God rejects the work of the flesh. The end justifies the means, as they say; but if doing church projects hurriedly would impose an untold burden on church members then I beg to say, the Lord won’t be pleased with the results.

I know from personal experience the unpleasant financial strain being put on members due to your erratic and unplanned church projects.

A word of warning worth taking here is, God does not recognize the work of the flesh. There are many people today trying to help God out through fleshly activities for God, and such work is unrecognized by God.

Please take a moment to consider if many of the projects you are undertaking could be the work of the flesh. I implore you to consider if it is possible that the Lord is not pleased with you. Do you remember the song, “What if God is not happy with our praise, what if He is not pleased with the words we say; what if God is not happy with the way we live; What if He is not pleased with the way we give?”

Meditate on it!

8\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: COMPLAINTS

Dear Pastor,

You are manipulating people to give towards church work. You are wrong in using scriptures from Exodus to make people give to God. You are wrong to suggest any amount that people should give.

You have taken scriptures from the Old Testament and are using them out of context. Using scriptures like Moses’ call for an offering in Exodus is a misapplication of the Bible and is a clear manipulation. This is not right! Even Solomon’s famous offering which opened the heavens for him to encounter the glory of God was not given under any sort of compulsion.

Using financial suggestibility as is done in some churches lacks integrity and is detestable. It is not pleasing to the Lord.

Please remember that the scriptural example of giving, states emphatically that each person should give what they have decided in their heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver. Do not use financial suggestibility to build the church.

9\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: WARNINGS

My dear Pastor,

Return to God before it is too late. Do you have any respect for the scripture or will you argue against it just to achieve what you want? Please return to scriptural ways. It is not too late for you to repent.

10\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: ARROGANCE

Dear Senior Pastor,

You are abusing people when you ask them to honour pastors. This system of celebrating pastors is a well-intentioned idea, but again it baffles my mind as to why every form of honour has to be quantified in money. You should be aware that gradually you are creating a culture in which pastors manipulate the flock and fleece them with a sense of unwarranted entitlement in demanding monetary gifts or “seeds” on certain days. Through this system, you have burdened the people and enriched yourself and your family.

Stop abusing the church before it is too late.

11\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: JEALOUS

Dear Honourable Pastor,

Please, it is time to repent of your greed and discontentment! God is blasphemed by it!

At what point will you have enough? How much do you need to reach the level of satisfaction? Greed is an unsatiable thing and in all humility, I challenge you to do better.

Why should you receive gifts from people? Do you still have a conscience?

On top of receiving a salary as a pastor and the numerous gifts people would freely give you, why do you feel entitled to demand such materialist things? Doesn’t your conscience bite you a bit that all these would bring an untold burden to some of our poor members?

12\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: PIETISTIC

Dear Bishop,

Be mindful that as you sit in wealth accumulated by foul means, God is the most blasphemed. Mammon is a dangerous god and greed is insatiable. You cannot serve God and money. It’s never too late to repent and take steps towards contentment. I hope you take heed to this humble admonishing of mine. I know you will and I can see what is happening to you, albeit slowly.

13\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: UNCTUOUS

Dear Pastor,

You are relating to, associating with and learning from pastors who have many flaws. No persons are perfect and no church is perfect and we have to be circumspect about what we learn from others. Most of the people and churches you are learning from and associating with have big flaws and you have to be careful what you learn.

No pastor or mentor is so great to make you want to learn so much from them.

I challenge you to repent and learn from God Himself.

Return to God and to sound, accountable ways instead of trying to impress certain men of God.

14\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: DISAPPROVING

Dear Pastor,

Do not be deceived. It is not the mark of success to have a large church accompanied by financial influence.

Sadly, most people have come to regard ministry success as having large numbers and financial influence. If that is your aim, then perhaps these ministries are good enough but I hope you know better.

Being loud doesn’t make someone right.

Being popular doesn’t mean God is with you!

Having a multitude doesn’t mean God is pleased with your ways. Remember that the majority did not support Moses but it did not mean that they were right.

15\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: DISAPPROVAL

Dear friend,

You need to reflect on your leadership system and structure.

I have been part of the church council before. I know there is no sense of serious accountability whether personal, financial, or systemic.

In my opinion, your record keeping is simply to cover up for any future scrutiny but not to give account to your followers on how their money is being used.

16\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: FEARLESS

Dear Pastor,

I am not afraid of you. I have served my term as a humble follower. Those around you are unable to confront you and bring you to order because they are simply YES MEN. I do not need to agree with you on everything any more. That is why I am telling you these truths.

17\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: DESPISES

Dear Bishop,

How can you give awards to people for being the biggest givers or the best shepherds in the church? You are not qualified to reward people for what they do for the Lord because it is carnal!

How do you reconcile that with scriptures that challenge us to give privately and leave our rewards to the Lord? Are you qualified to reward people for things they do as to the Lord?

You are trying to make yourself God to these people. You just want them to like you.

18\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: REJECTS LOYALTY

Dear Bishop,

Since I left the church, I have been irked by your continual harping on loyalty teachings.

Loyalty is not biblical. Stop copying this teaching blindly! It is destroying people. Where can you find any teaching on loyalty in the Bible?

Pastor, stop misusing and taking advantage of people! You have imbibed this loyalty false doctrine too much! You have elevated yourself to the status of God who alone deserves our unwavering loyalty!

Since when did it become okay for you to discuss people who have left the church in a demeaning, insulting, and disparaging manner? How could you instruct people to cut off those who leave the church? I forgive you but stop saying bad things about those who leave you.

Are church members and helpers simply tools for your use and subsequent disposal, when there are disagreements? There must be mutual submission and respect! We are all the children of God! The Bible calls for mutual submission and respect!

Do not go through this rabbit hole of this loyalty junk, as it only sets you and the church up for toxicity!

Some of the things said against me personally after I left were not right and I thank God for granting me the strength to forgive you.

I am concerned for you because your loyalty teachings will lead to dangerous outcomes for both you personally and the church as a whole.

19\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: REBELLIOUS

Dear Bishop,

Stop appointing and ordaining ministers unnecessarily.

Stop the random appointment of pastors.

Stop sharing titles and creating a desire in people for mere titles!

Many in the church just work for the next title.

Stop denigrating the pastoral calling!

There is a need to re-examine the qualifications for a pastor and not belittle the gracious place of pastoral calling.

Beware of elevating skills over character.

It is treacherous to assume that a person who brings large numbers of people to church is worthy of being appointed as a pastor. It is inconceivable that people who bring in large amounts of finance to the church are worth promoting to the pastoral office.

Be careful, you are peddling carnality!

20\. A MAN OF DEFIANCE: PHARISAICAL

Dear Pastor,

Repent, so that you can be right with God again!

Yes, you did appoint me as a pastor and you did help me in my initial ministry. But how long shall I be saying “thank you” for that initial swing?

When will you be tired of hearing “Thank you”. Are you not tired of hearing the sycophants sing your praise at every birthday celebration?

I have many things I could point out to you. But out of respect, I will end here. I will not go any further. My last questions are simply these:

Do you still have any integrity at all as a person? Can you still discern between right and wrong? I am praying for you to heed and not to have a hardened heart.

May your heart not be hardened to hear my counsel!

I pray for you to experience a great reformation before it is too late.
