### Chapter 2\

What Is Rebellion?\
----------------------------------------

An evil man seeketh only REBELLION: therefore a cruel messenger shall be sent against him.

Proverbs 17:11

1\. Rebellion is defiance!

2\. Rebellion is an open disregard and contempt for an authority figure.

3\. Rebellion is resistance to a higher authority!

4\. Rebellion is an open opposition toward a person who is the leader.

5\. Rebellion is when a group of people stand in open defiance of the leadership!

6\. Rebellion is when a group disobeys leadership!

7\. Rebellion is when people confront leaders!

8\. Rebellion is when people gang up to challenge authorities!

9\. Rebellion is when a group is inspired to fight, oppose and resist leaders.

10\. Rebellion is the refusal to obey rules or accept normal standards of behaviour.

11\. Rebellion is when a group of people gang up together to oppose the leader.

12\. Rebellion is a fight against the leader by blackmail and extortion.

13\. Rebellion is a group attack against authority.

14\. Rebellion is a group insubordination or insurrection that often quickly fails.

15\. A rebellion is opposition that undermines a leader and his authority.

16\. A rebellion is an act of revolting against authority or an established government.

17\. Rebellion is to act in a way that shows that you do not accept the control or influence of someone.

18\. Rebellion is to launch a fight using deception, slander, social media, extortion, blackmail, the law, manipulation and any means available to hurt the church leadership.

19\. Rebellion is the action of a group which fights against an established leadership and government.

20\. Rebellion is to act or show disobedience against conventions that are normally accepted.

21\. Rebellion is to fight someone who is in authority or control of your life.

22\. Rebellion is a strong effort by a group of people against the church leadership.

23\. Rebellion is bad behaviour that goes against the expected behaviour of a subordinate.

24\. Rebellion is a sudden, radical and complete change against the norm.

25\. Rebellion is an activity or movement that is designed to hurt the church leaders.

26\. Rebellion is the act of defying authority, defying control, or defying tradition. The art of rebellion is the art of defiance!
