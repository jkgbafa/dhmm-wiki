### Chapter 8\

Why Rebellion Must be Punished\
----------------------------------------------------

Joshua said to the people, “You are not able to serve the Lord. HE IS A HOLY GOD; HE IS A JEALOUS GOD. HE WILL NOT FORGIVE YOUR REBELLION AND YOUR SINS.”

Joshua 24:19 (NIV)

1\. REBELLION IS A UNIQUE SIN.

FOR HE ADDETH REBELLION UNTO HIS SIN, he clappeth his hands among us, and multiplieth his words against God.

Job 34:37

Rebellion is a specific evil that is distinct from every other sin. To have committed the sin of rebellion is to have committed a specific sin of resistance, disobedience, confrontation, challenge and defiance to authorities and leadership.

2\. REBELS ARE EVIL MEN.

AN EVIL MAN SEEKETH ONLY REBELLION: therefore a cruel messenger shall be sent against him.

Proverbs 17:11

Rebels are evil men who constantly and continually defy, challenge and confront authorities. These evil men often start their defiance in their school days and continue as they grow up into adulthood. All through their lives, rebellious men seek out opportunities to oppose leadership and challenge authority. Such evil people are part of every insurrection and rebellion in the classroom, in the school, at the workplace and in the church.

It is important to find out about the history of a person’s rebellion. I remember one fellow who caused much confusion in a church that he belonged to. He then moved on to cause and stir up rebellion in another church. He then came to our church and started a rebellion to challenge and fight the bishops of the church. Indeed, an evil man seeketh only rebellion!

When such a person enters your congregation, his eyes will target the leadership and he will see only reasons to rise up to defy leadership. Such a person will see only faults and reject whoever is in leadership. As the years go by, such an evil person will have a track record of being part of rebellion after rebellion.

3\. GOD WILL NOT FORGIVE YOUR REBELLION.

Joshua said to the people, “You are not able to serve the LORD. He is a holy God; he is a jealous God. HE WILL NOT FORGIVE YOUR REBELLION and your sins.”

Joshua 24:19 (NIV)

There are very few things that God has said He will not forgive, and rebellion is one of them. God declared to the Israelites that He would not forgive their rebellion!

You must avoid rebellion at all costs. Blasphemy against the Holy Ghost will not be forgiven! Rebellion will also not be forgiven! Read it for yourself!

4\. REBELS ARE STUBBORN MEN.

FOR I KNOW THY REBELLION, AND THY STIFF NECK: behold, while I am yet alive with you this day, ye have been rebellious against the LORD; and how much more after my death?

Deuteronomy 31:27

Rebels are stubborn men. Rebellion is always associated with stiff necks! A rebel is someone who is wrong about his beliefs and yet is highly resistant to change. This is why to overcome rebels you have to destroy them completely.

Rebellion, which is resistance and defiance of leaders and authorities, is always associated with the terrible evil of stubbornness.

5\. REBELLION IS WITCHCRAFT.

For REBELLION IS AS THE SIN OF WITCHCRAFT and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry. Because thou hast rejected the word of the LORD, he hath also rejected thee from being king.

1 Samuel 15:23

Rebels have some power within them that inspires the challenge against authorities. The power of witchcraft works in every rebel. A rebel uses manipulation inspired by hell.

Rebellion which is resistance and defiance of authority is as terrible as the sin of witchcraft. Remember that God’s word says “Suffer not a witch to live “. Indeed, rebels must be put away for good.

6\. REBELLION IS BETRAYAL.

For our offenses are many in your sight, and our sins testify against us. Our offenses are ever with us, and we acknowledge our iniquities: REBELLION AND TREACHERY AGAINST THE LORD, turning our backs on our God, inciting revolt and oppression, uttering lies our hearts have conceived.

Isaiah 59:12-13 (NIV)

Rebellion is associated with treachery, betrayal and treason. Treachery is betrayal! Often, those who rise up in rebellion need to do so under the cover of deception and treachery. Treachery is the act of betrayal, deception, or breaking trust in a way that harms others. Treachery leads to conflict and division and fosters resentment.

A leader often looks to his followers for support. If instead of support, the leader’s authority and leadership style are resisted and challenged, the leader will feel betrayed.

Rebellion causes deep, emotional wounds and leaves lasting pain in loved ones. Rebellion destroys trust. Once betrayed, trust is hard to rebuild. Rebelling, confronting, attacking, challenging and disobeying those in charge are often done through treachery, trickery and deception.

7\. TO REBEL IS TO GRUMBLE.

But the Lord said to Moses, “Put back the rod of Aaron before the testimony TO BE KEPT AS A SIGN AGAINST THE REBELS, THAT YOU MAY PUT AN END TO THEIR GRUMBLINGS AGAINST ME, so that they will not die.”

Numbers 17:10 (NASB95)

Grumbling and murmuring are signs of rebellion. They are an insidious form of defiance against leadership.

Murmuring and having secret meetings against the leadership is a sneaky, underhand form of rebellion! Avoid becoming a murmurer. Avoid people who murmur! Reject secret meetings!

Be open in your discussions! Be open about what you think. Avoid ever being associated with rebellion!

Beware of secret rebellion! Beware of open rebellion!

8\. REBELLION LEADS TO DESOLATION.

Then I heard a holy one speaking, and another holy one said to him, “How long will it take for the vision to be fulfilled-the vision concerning the daily sacrifice, THE REBELLION THAT CAUSES DESOLATION, the surrender of the sanctuary and the trampling underfoot of the LORD’s people?”

Daniel 8:13 (NIV)

Rebellion leads to desolation. Many ministers with a bright future are destroyed by rebellion. Their lives and ministries enter into desolation, bleakness and emptiness because of their rebellion. Watch every pastor who starts a church out of rebellion against his senior pastor. After challenging and confronting the senior pastor, they move out and begin a new ministry. But the new ministry they begin is birthed into a strong desolation, emptiness, unhappiness, misery, isolation and anguish.

9\. REBELS ARE DECEIVED MEN.

Against whom do you jest? Against whom do you open wide your mouth And stick out your tongue? ARE YOU NOT CHILDREN OF REBELLION, OFFSPRING OF DECEIT,

Isaiah 57:4 (NASB95)

Rebellion is an offspring of deception. Rebels are products of self-deception. They deceive themselves into thinking that they can overthrow an established government. Rebels are often deceived into thinking that because there is an error or weakness in the government, they have a right to overthrow it. Rebels are an offspring of deceit because they are deceived into thinking that the majority will follow after them because of the accusations and slander they spread. As they spread stories, gossip and accusations, they think that the whole world will leave and follow them. Many rebels are so shocked that people continue to flock to their leader. Many rebels are disappointed that people do not follow their slander.

10\. A REBEL IS A THREAT.

Then sent Sanballat his servant unto me in like manner the fifth time with an open letter in his hand; Wherein was written, It is reported among the heathen, and Gashmu saith it, THAT THOU AND THE JEWS THINK TO REBEL, FOR WHICH CAUSE THOU BUILDEST THE WALL, THAT THOU MAYEST BE THEIR KING, according to these words.

And thou hast also appointed prophets to preach of thee at Jerusalem, saying, There is a king in Judah: and now shall it be reported to the king according to these words. Come now therefore, and let us take counsel together. THEN I SENT UNTO HIM, SAYING, THERE ARE NO SUCH THINGS DONE AS THOU SAYEST, BUT THOU FEIGNEST THEM OUT OF THINE OWN HEART. For they all made us afraid, saying, Their hands shall be weakened from the work, that it be not done. Now therefore, O God, strengthen my hands.

Nehemiah 6:5-9

A rebel is a serious threat to your leadership and stability. To be accused of rebellion is a very serious thing.

Nehemiah was building the walls of Jerusalem when he was accused of rebellion. An accusation of rebellion, if proved right, could have caused great difficulty for Nehemiah.

You must never be accused of rebellion. You must never be involved with rebellion. Nehemiah went to great lengths to prove that he was not in rebellion but just building the walls of Jerusalem.

11\. SOME ARE REBELS FROM BIRTH.

You have not heard, you have not known. Even from long ago your ear has not been open, Because I knew that you would deal very treacherously; AND YOU HAVE BEEN CALLED A REBEL FROM BIRTH.

Isaiah 48:8 (NASB)

Rebels must be punished because many of them have been rebels from birth. They have challenged authorities from their youth upwards. They are rebellious in their very nature and rebellion shows up in everything they do. It shows up when they are babies; in the way they cry, in their insistence on crying and in their persistence in crying. They are indeed born rebels!

Rebellion shows up in their childhood, it shows up in school and it shows up at work. At work, these born rebels would join or form groups to challenge authorities and would be at the forefront of every demonstration or riot. In church, these rebels would be at the heart of every dissenting group. Such rebels would be the instigators of all kinds of discontentment and murmuring. They are simply rebels from birth!

12\. SOME CHURCHES ARE KNOWN FOR REBELLION.

And I commanded, and search hath been made, and IT IS FOUND THAT THIS CITY OF OLD TIME HATH MADE INSURRECTION AGAINST KINGS, AND THAT REBELLION and sedition have been made therein.

Ezra 4:19

Rebellion is associated with certain cities and groups of people. Some churches have more rebellion than others. In some spiritual groups, the leader is constantly challenged, confronted and defied. He does not lead with ease and confidence because rebellion is always in the air.

13\. DO NOT REBEL AGAINST GOD OR HIS SERVANT.

Notwithstanding, if the land of your possession be unclean, then pass ye over unto the land of the possession of the LORD, wherein the LORD’S tabernacle dwelleth, and take possession among us: BUT REBEL NOT AGAINST THE LORD, NOR REBEL AGAINST US, in building you an altar beside the altar of the LORD our God.

Joshua 22:19

Rebellion can be against the Lord and His servants. Many times, when you are defying God’s servants, you are defying God Himself.

Rebellion against the commandments of the Lord happens when a person confronts, challenges and disobeys the commandments of the Lord.

Disobeying God is seen as rebellion! Disobeying God’s servant is also rebellion! Beware of rebelling against God or His servant!

14\. YOU MUST REJECT REBELS.

Do I not hate those who hate you, Lord, AND ABHOR THOSE WHO ARE IN REBELLION AGAINST YOU?

Psalm 139:21 (NIV)

Do not admire rebels! Do not use a rebellion as a good example. Rebellion is always evil, no matter who does it. It is important to abhor those who are in rebellion. You must hate all forms of rebellion. If you find anyone rebelling, you must not have anything to do with the person. If you admire a rebellious person, you are in great danger of becoming a rebel yourself.

15\. REBELS DESIRE ALTERNATIVE LEADERSHIP.

And refused to obey, neither were mindful of thy wonders that thou didst among them; but hardened their necks, and IN THEIR REBELLION APPOINTED A CAPTAIN TO RETURN TO THEIR BONDAGE: but thou art a God ready to pardon, gracious and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and forsookest them not.

Nehemiah 9:17

Sometimes people who are part of a ministry defy and insult their pastors. They are therefore forced to go to another ministry to submit to another man of God who does not even know them. I have seen junior pastors leave a church in rebellion and seek spiritual appointments from other apostles elsewhere. Sometimes they would go to other countries and find someone to ordain them as reverend ministers, apostles, or even bishops. This is a common phenomenon.

I once had a rebellious pastor who travelled to another country to submit to someone else as his bishop and mentor. Unfortunately, this new mentor died prematurely and he was left again without an authority figure over his head. I waited to see whether he would find another authority to appoint over his soul. Perhaps, he was tired of appointing alternative authorities over himself!

People who live in rebellion want to have new leaders appointed over them because they have rejected, confronted, challenged and insulted the leadership and authority that was set over them.
