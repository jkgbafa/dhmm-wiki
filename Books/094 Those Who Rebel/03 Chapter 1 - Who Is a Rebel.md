### Chapter 1\

Who Is a Rebel?\
-------------------------------------

According as we hearkened unto Moses in all things, so will we hearken unto thee: only the LORD thy God be with thee, as he was with Moses. WHOSOEVER HE BE THAT DOTH REBEL AGAINST THY COMMANDMENT, AND WILL NOT HEARKEN UNTO THY WORDS in all that thou commandest him, he shall be put to death: only be strong and of a good courage.

Joshua 1:17-18

1\. A rebel is someone who is defiant and resistant.

2\. A rebel is someone who openly opposes the leaders.

3\. A rebel is someone who defies the leadership.

4\. A rebel is someone who disobeys the leader’s instructions.

5\. A rebel is someone who gets into conflict and confrontation with authorities.

6\. A rebel is someone who challenges fathers.

7\. A rebel is someone who refuses to obey rules.

8\. A rebel is someone who does not behave properly as a subordinate ought to.

9\. A rebel is someone who makes efforts to resist the leader by undermining him in any way.

10\. A rebel is someone who hates the leadership.

11\. A rebel is someone who hates having an authority over him.

12\. A rebel is someone who hates to acknowledge that he is under authority.

13\. A rebel is someone who fights the leadership of his church in court.

14\. A rebel is someone who mobilizes others to fight against his pastor in court.

15\. A rebel is someone who fights against his father in court.

16\. A rebel is someone who uses blackmail to fight against the leadership.

17\. A rebel is someone who uses extortion to fight authorities.

18\. A rebel is someone who is always in conflict with authorities.

19\. A rebel is someone who is usually not successful in overthrowing the established leadership.

20\. A rebel is someone who breaks out against the known leaders.

21\. A rebel is someone who challenges authorities but is not often successful.

22\. A rebel is someone who purposefully disobeys instructions and rises against people in authority.

23\. A rebel is someone who openly opposes father figures.

24\. A rebel is someone who joins groups that oppose authorities.

25\. A rebel is someone who does not accept the control or influence of the organization.

26\. A rebel is someone who fights against the rules of the ministry.

27\. A rebel is someone who does not obey the rules of the establishment that he belongs to.

28\. A rebel is someone who fights against an established and powerful leader.

29\. A rebel is someone who acts against or shows disobedience to the conventions that are normally accepted in the church or institution that he belongs to.

30\. A rebel is someone who changes suddenly and no longer submits to authority.

31\. A rebel is someone who openly fights the church leadership.

32\. A rebel is someone who radically and completely goes against the normal standards of decent behaviour.

33\. A rebel is someone who desires the resignation, overthrow or handover of his leader.

34\. A rebel is someone who follows the pattern of Korah.

35\. A rebel is someone who is cursed and doomed.
