### Chapter 9\

Nine Punishments for Rebellion\
----------------------------------------------------

Why do you keep on rebelling? Do you want to be punished even more? …

Isaiah 1:5 (GNT)

1\. A rebellious person should expect punishment for himself and his children. In this scripture, we also see that rebellion can be taught.

Therefore thus saith the LORD; Behold, I WILL PUNISH SHEMAIAH THE NEHELAMITE, AND HIS SEED: he shall not have a man to dwell among this people; neither shall he behold the good that I will do for my people, saith the LORD; BECAUSE HE HATH TAUGHT REBELLION against the LORD.

Jeremiah 29:32

2\. Rebellious men should know that the reward for their continual defiance, resistance and rejection of authority is that a cruel messenger shall be sent against them. A rebel must therefore expect cruelty from a cruel messenger as his reward.

An evil man seeketh only rebellion: THEREFORE A CRUEL MESSENGER SHALL BE SENT AGAINST HIM.

Proverbs 17:11

3\. A rebellious person should expect to die an unnatural and premature death. A rebellious person will be cast from the face of the earth because of his rebellion.

Therefore thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will cast thee from off the face of the earth: THIS YEAR THOU SHALT DIE, BECAUSE THOU HAST TAUGHT REBELLION AGAINST THE LORD.

Jeremiah 28:16

4\. Rebellion will be punished with death. The children of Israel pledged to execute and eliminate anyone who rebelled against Joshua’s command. So is the destiny of all rebels!

WHOSOEVER HE BE THAT DOTH REBEL AGAINST THY COMMANDMENT, and will not hearken unto thy words in all that thou commandest him, HE SHALL BE PUT TO DEATH: only be strong and of a good courage.

Joshua 1:18

5\. When you rebel against the commandments of the Lord, you must expect that the hand of the Lord will be against you.

But if ye will not obey the voice of the LORD, but rebel against the commandment of the LORD, THEN SHALL THE HAND OF THE LORD BE AGAINST YOU, AS IT WAS AGAINST YOUR FATHERS.

1 Samuel 12:15

Whatever punishment will come to a rebel will not be by the hand of man but by the hand of God. Your punishment will be manifest because the hand of the Lord will be against you and no man will be able to stop it or change it. The hand of God is very different from the hand of man. When the hand of man is involved in your punishment, you will see the human effort to judge you. When the hand of God takes over, there is no human engineering or manipulation of events! All rebels must watch out for the hand of the Lord which is against them.

6\. One of the severe punishments of rebellion is to be destroyed by the sword.

BUT IF YE REFUSE AND REBEL, YE SHALL BE DEVOURED WITH THE SWORD: for the mouth of the LORD hath spoken it.

Isaiah 1:20

7\. Every rebel should expect a lion from the forest to attack him.

Therefore a lion from the forest will attack them, a wolf from the desert will ravage them, a leopard will lie in wait near their towns to tear to pieces any who venture out, FOR THEIR REBELLION IS GREAT AND THEIR BACKSLIDINGS MANY.

Jeremiah 5:6 (NIV)

Rebels should expect a wolf from the desert to ravage them. Rebels should expect a leopard to jump out at them and surprise them. Indeed, there is a great curse for all those who have developed the confidence to defy the Lord’s ordained authorities in His church.

8\. People who live in rebellion have a lot of headaches and heartaches.

Where will you be stricken again, AS YOU CONTINUE IN YOUR REBELLION? THE WHOLE HEAD IS SICK AND THE WHOLE HEART IS FAINT.

Isaiah 1:5 (NASB95)

The head is sick and the heart is sick! The hearts and heads are sick because of resistance, defiance, confrontation and the challenging of God’s servants. Rebels are not well. Rebels are not normal people. Rebels have many problems. They are not well in their hearts and in their minds.

9\. People who rebel will not enter their promised land.

Aaron will be gathered to his people; FOR HE SHALL NOT ENTER THE LAND which I have given to the sons of Israel, BECAUSE YOU REBELLED against My command at the waters of Meribah.

Numbers 20:24 (NASB95)

Aaron died and did not enter the Promised Land because of the rebellion that occurred at the waters of Meribah.

“And further, by these, my son, be admonished: of making many books there is no end; and much study is a weariness of the flesh.” Ecclesiastes 12:12
