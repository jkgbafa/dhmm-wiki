### Chapter 4\

The Transformation of Adam\
------------------------------------------------

For as by one man’s disobedience many were made sinners, so by the obedience of one shall many be made righteous.

Romans 5:19

There are seven terrible changes that come over every rebel. These changes played out clearly in the life of Adam, the very first rebel. You must be wary of these terrible changes that will take place in the character of every rebellious person.

1\. FEAR

2\. SECRECY

3\. DEMOTION

4\. DEMONIZATION

5\. VANITY

6\. CORRUPTION

7\. DEATH

Rebellion transforms a person into a completely different person from whom he used to be before he rebelled! All rebels are transformed personalities! They are not what they used to be before they entered into rebellion and disobedience. Adam is the best example of someone who was transformed into a phantom of what he used to be.

You will notice people you used to know who were noble, upright servants of God who have turned into monsters. A monster is someone who is grotesque, terrifying and abnormal in every respect. Indeed, mankind is a grotesque, terrifying and abnormal version of what we were supposed to be.

In this chapter, I want us to study the transformation of Adam into a grotesque and terrifying version of what he used to be before he rebelled.

Rebellion is when someone disobeys authority and leadership! This New Testament verse - Romans 5:19 - explicitly refers to Adam’s act as “disobedience,” which is the essence of rebellion.

For as by one man’s disobedience many were made sinners, so by the obedience of one shall many be made righteous.

Romans 5:19

Adam and Eve rebelled against God’s word in the Garden of Eden. The essence of rebellion is wilful defiance against authority, which is precisely what Adam and Eve did when they chose to eat the forbidden fruit despite God’s clear instruction. While the word “rebel” isn’t used in Genesis, the concept of rebellion is clearly present. Their actions were a direct violation of God’s command, which constitutes rebellion. The disobedience of Adam is the foundational act of human sin and rebellion against God.

And Adam was not deceived, but the woman being deceived was in the transgression.

1 Timothy 2:14

This verse describes Eve’s action as transgression, which is another term for rebellion against God’s law.

When Adam fell into rebellion, he experienced many radical changes. We know this by looking at the fall and rebellion of man in detail and the drastic changes that came upon mankind when we fell.

I am writing this book because I have been forced to accept the reality that after people rebel, there is a change in their personalities. They become unrecognizable! They become nasty and different from how I ever knew them. Pastors who rebel after being in the ministry for so many years change completely and become hardened monsters set on a road of destruction. I have been forced to come to terms with the fact that people I knew and loved are changed by their actions of rebellion.

Indeed, when I studied the change that rebellion produced in mankind, I understood that people actually undergo a degeneration and a deterioration into corruption after they rebel, disobey and defy authority.

Mankind has been affected profoundly by the rebellion of Adam. Becoming a rebel produced seven ultimate and profound changes in the human race. Let us study seven terrible transformations that took place in fallen man.

1\. FEAR: REBELS ARE TRANSFORMED INTO MEN OF FEAR.

And the Lord God called unto Adam, and said unto him, Where art thou? And he said, I heard thy voice in the garden, and I WAS AFRAID, because I was naked; and I hid myself.

Genesis 3:9-10

Adam said, “I was afraid and I hid myself!” Fear! Fear was a new condition previously unknown to man. The human race tasted its first experience of fear in the Garden of Eden. They had never been afraid before. When Adam and Eve rebelled, they opened themselves to demons of fear. There had been nothing like fear in a human being until after the rebellion and fall of man. It was after the fall of Adam and Eve that man became subject to the evil spirit of fear. Fear is the most common motivation for men today. Many things are done simply because of fear. That is why today, the spirit of fear is the commonest evil spirit that afflicts man.

Obedience to God should be a great motivation for men. We should live, work, travel, and preach because we love God and not because we are afraid of something bad. All rebels are full of fear and insecurity. Rebels are always looking around the corner to see if anyone is coming to get them.

I remember one rebel who attacked me fiercely. I perceived that he was afraid that he was going to die. He began to go to the gym and workout, doing many exercises and walking several miles a day to lose weight. He also began to take his medicine very diligently. He was frightened that he would die suddenly because of what he had done. Rebels are full of fear!

The human race is now a race of rebels; full of fear. Most people do things out of fear. Fear is the greatest and most common motivation behind the things that men do. Many people go to school out of fear. Many people go to work out of fear. Many people migrate to other countries out of fear that if they stay behind, they will not prosper. Indeed, fear is a great and common motivation for men today. Rebellious man has fallen into the grips of fear!

And the Lord said unto Gideon, The people that are with thee are too many for me to give the Midianites into their hands, lest Israel vaunt themselves against me, saying, Mine own hand hath saved me. Now therefore go to, proclaim in the ears of the people, saying, WHOSOEVER IS FEARFUL AND AFRAID, let him return and depart early from mount Gilead. AND THERE RETURNED OF THE PEOPLE TWENTY AND TWO THOUSAND; AND THERE REMAINED TEN THOUSAND.

And the Lord said unto Gideon, The people are yet too many; bring them down unto the water, and I will try them for thee there: and it shall be, that of whom I say unto thee, This shall go with thee, the same shall go with thee; and of whomsoever I say unto thee, This shall not go with thee, the same shall not go.

So he brought down the people unto the water: and the Lord said unto Gideon, Everyone that lappeth of the water with his tongue, as a dog lappeth, him shalt thou set by himself; likewise every one that boweth down upon his knees to drink. And the number of them that lapped, putting their hand to their mouth, were three hundred men: but all the rest of the people bowed down upon their knees to drink water.

And the Lord said unto Gideon, By the three hundred men that lapped will I save you, and deliver the Midianites into thine hand: and let all the other people go every man unto his place.

Judges 7:2-7

In the story of Gideon, we see that 68.7% of the army was possessed with the spirit of fear. This shows that the majority of human beings are affected strongly by fears. Indeed, the majority of human beings are subject to raging fears.

Rebellious men are subject to many fears. They are afraid of their own shadows! I remember one rebellious man who seemed to think that I was cursing him whilst I was preaching. He slandered me in a video and sent it around the world, claiming I was cursing him. It was one of the greatest surprises of my life when I found out that this fellow thought I was cursing him, because I did not think of him at all when I was speaking. This rebellious man had no idea of what I was talking about. But I understood that rebellious men are full of fear. They fear that they are cursed! They fear their impending judgment! They fear impending doom! They fear their own shadows! That is the lot of rebels!

I remember another rebellious fellow who listened to my preaching. I thought because he had left the church, he would never listen to me preaching again. Apparently, he was glued to his television, watching me all the time. He sent me an amazing message, accusing me of discussing someone’s tragedy in my sermon. I almost fell out of my chair when I read the message. I could not even find the words to respond to his preposterous accusation. He was as far from the truth as the east is from the west! This miserable rebellious pastor was afraid of his own shadow, fearing that tragedy would strike him. Rebellious people are simply full of fear and accusations! The fallen and rebellious human race is full of fear and accusations which destroy relationships.

When man fell, he became subject to many fears. Fear is full of torment! Fear destroys relationships! Fear causes people to be insecure, to look over their shoulders all the time, and to accuse people who only love them. Fear destroys people’s personalities, turning calm souls into accusatory personalities.

2\. SECRECY: REBELS ARE TRANSFORMED INTO MEN OF SECRECY AND DARKNESS.

And they heard the voice of the Lord God walking in the garden in the cool of the day: and Adam and his wife HID THEMSELVES FROM THE PRESENCE OF THE LORD GOD AMONGST THE TREES of the garden.

Genesis 3:8

Rebellion is defined as a resistance and a defiance of authority. A rebel is someone who defies authority. Adam and Eve defied God’s authority and when they did, they fell into a life of darkness, secrets and fear. When Adam and Eve rebelled against God, they descended into a life pattern of secrecy, secrets and darkness.

Those who rebel become men of darkness and secrets. When people live in sin they sit at the back of the church, as far as possible from everyone. When people live in sin and rebellion, they have many secrets, secret friends, secret relationships and secret activities. A fallen and rebellious man is full of secrets, secrecy, hiding and darkness. All his activities are works of darkness.

AND HAVE NO FELLOWSHIP WITH THE UNFRUITFUL WORKS OF DARKNESS, but rather reprove them. For it is a shame even to speak of those things which are done of them in secret. But all things that are reproved are made manifest by the light: for whatsoever doth make manifest is light.

Ephesians 5:11-13

Men of darkness and secrets are men of many evils. Almost every evil work is done under the cover of darkness. A rebellious and disobedient pastor has many secrets and hidden things. He murmurs and complains about things in secret. But when he sees you, he smiles and acts like everything is perfectly fine.

A rebellious man is full of secret meetings and conspiracies. He has secret comments, secret phone calls, secret texts, and secret rendezvous. One secret leads to another, and one discovery leads to another discovery!

This then is the message which we have heard of him, and declare unto you, that God is light, and in him is no darkness at all.

1 John 1:5

God has no darkness at all around Him. Imagine that! No darkness at all! So then how did Adam and Eve end up in darkness and hiding? Are you a man of secrets and hidden things? Indeed, the secrets and hidden things are evidence that you have fallen into sin and rebellion. In God, there is no darkness at all.

3\. DEMOTION: REBELS ARE DEMOTED TO A LOWER STATE OF EXISTENCE.

But God be thanked, that YE WERE THE SERVANTS OF SIN, but ye have obeyed from the heart that form of doctrine which was delivered you.

Romans 6:17

Rebels try to go higher but end up going lower! Man tried to be like God but ended up being a wretched and miserable servant of sin. Men have become servants of sin. Mankind has been demoted to become servants of sin instead of dominating in this life. Those who rebel are demoted in this world. Man was created to rule and to have dominion. Rebellion reversed all of that and converted man into a servant and a slave. A rebel is always lowered to become a servant and a slave instead of being a king.

A rebellious person is someone who confronts, challenges, overthrows and disobeys established authority and ends up being demoted! Shockingly, the rebellious man ends up being the opposite of what he tried to become. He tries to go higher in ministry by becoming independent but he ends up being lower.

I remember a senior pastor of my church who was respected and honoured to preach and minister in many countries when he was part of our ministry. He rebelled and ended up as a lazy, good for nothing personality who just sat at home playing board games. He ended up becoming a driver for a few pennies a day, living as a menial servant for a relative who pitied him. What a fall from grace! This is someone who was welcomed with a bouquet everywhere he went. This is someone who was hailed as a great man of God and accorded many privileges. Rebellion led to his utter demotion! How pitiful rebels are! They aim so high and challenge men in authority, thinking that they can perhaps replace them.

Adam and Eve were demoted from being the lords and kings of this world. They became fugitives and slaves and fell into a cowardly state of insignificance. Instead of being in charge of the animals, the animals became a threat to them. Even the animals realised that they were no longer dealing with someone who had dominion over them. Man’s dominion was lost through the rebellion. Through this wholistic demotion, mankind is now terrorized by creatures as small as insects. Snakes, scorpions, lions, crocodiles and other fierce creatures now terrorize men and frighten men away. Man runs away from animals today. How unfortunate this is! Before the fall, Adam did not run away from animals. He summoned them, he spoke to them and he named them.

And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them HAVE DOMINION OVER the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.

Genesis 1:26

Every rebel undergoes a total transformation of his status. When a person tries to overthrow a government and fails to do so, he is quickly captured and he becomes a prisoner of the lowest order until he is executed for his treacherous plans.

Man was created to have dominion, lordship and absolute domination of this world. Man was the king! Man was the lord! Man was subject only to God. When he fell, he was thrown out as a fugitive and he became subject even to animals.

Is Israel a servant? Is he a homeborn slave? Why is he spoiled? The young lions roared upon him, and yelled, and they made his land waste: his cities are burned without inhabitant.

Jeremiah 2:14-15

This is what happens to rebellious pastors and leaders who fight against the authority that God has set up in the church. They are demoted further and further into insignificance and oblivion. It is rare for a rebel to excel! I am yet to see a rebellious person who has excelled in this life. All forms of rebellion lead to demotion!

4\. DEMONIZATION: REBELS ARE TRANSFORMED INTO DEMONIZED MEN.

Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour:

1 Peter 5:8

Neither give place to the devil.

Ephesians 4:27

To be demonized is to be harassed and afflicted by demons. The entire human race has been plagued by demons and devils since the rebellion and fall of man. Once demons spot a weakness and an opening in your life, they will invade you and afflict you.

What happened after the fall of man? The rebels became subject to evil spirits. The human race became subject to evil spirits. Rebellion is the reason why a fallen, rebellious man becomes subject to demons. Demons are always seeking rest in the bodies and lives of men of rebellion.

Rebellious pastors are plagued with demons. When pastors become rebellious and begin to challenge authority, they also become afflicted with evil spirits. They become progressively demonized until you can hardly recognize the person any longer. They have new, unrecognizable beliefs that reveal their demonic mindsets.

Just as weak antelopes are subject to attacks from lions, rebellious human beings became weaker than demons after the fall and became subject to attacks from these evil spirits. Unfortunately, men have opened themselves up to evil spirits because of their rebellion. Notice this about the ministry of Jesus:

And in that same hour HE CURED MANY of their infirmities and plagues, AND OF EVIL SPIRITS; and unto many that were blind he gave sight.

Luke 7:21

When Jesus came to this world, He found a world of men plagued by evil spirits. Jesus constantly confronted evil spirits in human beings. It was not so at the beginning of the world when Adam and Eve were created.

Men were not created to be occupied by demons. Man rebelled and fell, and that opened up mankind to these spirits who were waiting for a chance to invade and occupy the human race. Today all over the world, everywhere I go, I encounter many who are possessed with devils. People in rebellion have given place to the devil and are exposed to many evil spirits.

Have you noticed how rebellious and breakaway pastors become increasingly demonized? You can see it from their words, their stance and their statements. The demons lead them further and further into delusion and destruction.

I remember a group of rebellious pastors who increasingly became deluded about their stance against their former church. I was amazed at their ludicrous stance and attitude. I could only conclude that these people had become completely demon-infested. A delusion and a deception are always evidence of demonic infestation and invasion.

5\. VANITY: REBELS ARE TRANSFORMED INTO MEN OF VANITY.

FOR THE CREATURE WAS MADE SUBJECT TO VANITY, not willingly, but by reason of him who hath subjected the same in hope,

Romans 8:20

Those who rebel become subject to vanity! Human beings became subject to vanity when they rebelled in the Garden of Eden. When man fell, he became subject to vanity! When pastors and leaders rebel, they also become subject to vanity. A rebel becomes a useless person. He is unfit for service in the church where he rebelled and he is also unfit for service in any other church.

Rebellion is an ugly thing and God has no place for that. Rebellion will never flourish in the sight of God.

Vanity of vanities, saith the Preacher, vanity of vanities; all is vanity.

Ecclesiastes 1:2

This verse speaks of uselessness, worthlessness and hopelessness. When men rebelled against God, they become vain, useless, worthless and hopeless!

Solomon was able to see that the life of a human being, who is essentially a rebel towards God, has been subjected to a terrible and utterly profound hopelessness through sin. Our world means nothing! Our successes mean nothing! Everything about our lives is vain and hopeless. It is this hopelessness that Solomon was talking about when he said, “Vanity of vanities, all is vanity.”

A man of God who lifts himself to confront, to challenge, to accuse men of authority whom God has established, is rebellious. Rebels become nothing! They are cursed to vanity just as Adam and Eve were cursed to the vanity we now experience. This is why the book of Jude describes them as twice dead!

Oh, how sad it is to see men who were ministering powerfully fall to vanity, to uselessness, to worthlessness and hopelessness! Men of honour who were loved and respected become despised and unwanted dropouts and losers.

Satan so desires to turn men into waste material. Demons often succeed in inspiring men to rebel and that turns them into failures and losers and waste material! Do you want to become waste material that has to be discarded? If not then stay far away from defiance, disobedience, deviation, pride and rebellion!

6\. CORRUPTION: REBELS ARE TRANSFORMED INTO MEN OF CORRUPTION.

Because THE CREATURE ITSELF ALSO SHALL BE DELIVERED FROM THE BONDAGE OF CORRUPTION into the glorious liberty of the children of God. For we know that the whole creation groaneth and travaileth in pain together until now.

Romans 8:21-22

All creation has been made subject to corruption. Indeed, our state is described as “bound to corruption” (bondage of corruption). Adam and Eve delivered themselves into a state where everything became helplessly corrupted and spoilt. To be corrupted means to be debased in character, perverted and depraved. To be corrupted means to be made inferior by errors and unfortunate alterations.

When pastors rebel against authority, they undergo corruption in the same way that Adam and Eve underwent corruption when they rebelled. They became inferior and the human race was altered forever. Indeed, Adam and Eve were lowered to a “deteriorated and decayed” state.

Rebellion had the effect of corrupting Adam, Eve and all their descendants. Today, we live as the corrupted and spoilt descendants of Adam and Eve.

Rebellious men undergo a major change in their character after they rebel. They are corrupted, dishonourable, disliked, degraded, depreciated, disfigured, reduced and spoilt forever!

You will notice an unbelievable deterioration and decay in the character and personality of men who have become rebels in the church. You cannot recognize them or understand how people can change so much. How does a very respectful person turn into a monster, insulting his spiritual father and mocking the one he has loved for many years?

Indeed, a rebellious man is subject to corruption. A rebel’s very nature changes and becomes corrupt. How do I know this? The scripture tells us so!

That ye put off concerning the former conversation THE OLD MAN, WHICH IS CORRUPT ACCORDING TO THE DECEITFUL LUSTS;

Ephesians 4:22

The nature of man changed when he fell into sin and rebellion against God. This terrible transformation is described by Apostle Paul as the “old man” or the “flesh” who is corrupt in his deceitful lusts. “Lie not one to another, seeing that YE HAVE PUT OFF THE OLD MAN WITH HIS DEEDS; And have put on the new man, which is renewed in knowledge after the image of him that created him:” (Colossians 3:9-10). Man became evil, filled with deceptions, lusts, desires and negative character patterns.

The voice said, Cry. And he said, What shall I cry? All flesh is grass, and all the goodliness thereof is as the flower of the field:

Isaiah 40:6

Corruption is an inbuilt decay, deterioration and degeneration of a thing. The human race is subject to this inbuilt decay and deterioration in character, in behaviour, in the human body and in every conceivable way.

Corruption occurs in all aspects of human life. You may see a successful person achieving a lot, moving around sprightly, full of life, full of energy with a desire to achieve and accomplish many things. Years later, this healthy and lively fellow may be bent over, speaking slowly, smiling very little, unable to remember many things, speaking softly and hardly able to move around. What has happened? Corruption! The human being has decayed and deteriorated. This natural, inevitable corruption was transmitted to the whole world by Adam. Remember that Adam did not have a child until he became a rebel. It was after he rebelled that he gave birth to children who then carried the corrupt nature.

Two things that are subject to corruption are the human flesh and the human character. Our human bodies have become subject to corruption since the fall. Our personalities are also subject to corruption.

Because THE CREATURE ITSELF ALSO SHALL BE DELIVERED FROM THE BONDAGE OF CORRUPTION into the glorious liberty of the children of God. For we know that the whole creation groaneth and travaileth in pain together until now.

Romans 8:21-22

You may see somebody living well, healthily and happily. Because the flesh is subject to deterioration, forty years later, this same person may lose weight, become bent over, wizened, hardly able to walk and uninterested in life. This deterioration is because the person is subject to “corruption”!

Corruption may not be apparent initially. If you leave a loaf of bread outside for some time, you will come back to find a horrible and uneatable loaf of bread. That is corruption!

Corruption is an inbuilt deterioration that everything on this earth is subject to. Apart from being subject to vanity, human beings are also subject to corruption. Indeed, corruption is inevitable wherever there are human beings.

That ye put off concerning the former conversation THE OLD MAN, WHICH IS CORRUPT according to the deceitful lusts;

Ephesians 4:22

When man fell into sin, he fell into corruption. This nature is described by Apostle Paul as the old man who is corrupt in deceitful lusts. Man became evil, filled with lusts, desires and negative personality traits.

The personality of the human being is described as corrupt. The human nature is indeed subject to a terrible corruption. The human nature is undergoing a continuous, negative deterioration. Apostle Paul warned of even more deterioration as we approach the last days.

Knowing this first, that there shall come in the last days scoffers, walking after their own lusts,

2 Peter 3:3

Human nature is deteriorating! You can see from movies today how human beings are deteriorating. The kind of deterioration we see today was unheard of some time ago. Like a piece of bread left outside to deteriorate, human beings have deteriorated unbelievably.

You cannot stop corruption. You can only slow it down.

When pastors rebel against authority, they undergo corruption in the same way that Adam and Eve underwent corruption. Rebellious men undergo a major change in their character after they rebel. You will notice an unbelievable deterioration and decay in the character and personality of men who have become rebels in the church.

7\. DEATH: REBELS ARE TRANSFORMED INTO DEAD MEN.

But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it: FOR IN THE DAY THAT THOU EATEST THEREOF THOU SHALT SURELY DIE.

Genesis 2:17

For as in Adam all die, even so in Christ shall all be made alive.

1 Corinthians 15:22

Those who rebel become subject to death. Disobedience and defiance of authority is rebellion. A rebel is resistant to leadership and authority. This defiance, disobedience, confrontation and rejection of authority leads to death. God assured Adam and Eve of death if they disobeyed or defied Him. The ability to die or the capacity to die is called mortality. When Adam sinned, he became a mortal man. He was no longer immortal.

FOR THIS CORRUPTIBLE MUST PUT ON INCORRUPTION, AND THIS MORTAL MUST PUT ON IMMORTALITY. So when this corruptible shall have put on incorruption, and this mortal shall have put on immortality, then shall be brought to pass the saying that is written, Death is swallowed up in victory.

1 Corinthians 15:53-54

Mortality and corruption will be exchanged for immortality and incorruptibility. When Adam rebelled against God, he became subject to death. He died and experienced a terrible separation from God. Instead of being connected directly to the power of life, he became like a battery that was losing power slowly. Today, our lives are fading gradually and heading towards the endpoint called death. It is just a matter of time and all of us will be gone from this earth.

Those who rebel in the ministry, those who disobey, those who confront authorities, those who challenge and fight authorities are condemned to die in the same way that Adam died. They fade away inevitably and gradually become nothing.

A man who was once living, moving and vibrant, can be converted into dust in a glass jar. Those who rebel are cursed and that is how the human race has been cursed to die.

Rebellion is one of the most terrible evils known to man. The rising up to challenge authorities, and to oppose them brings a curse of separation unto the rebel.

Therefore the LORD God sent him forth from the garden of Eden, to till the ground from whence he was taken. So he drove out the man; and he placed at the east of the garden of Eden Cherubims, and a flaming sword which turned every way, to keep the way of the tree of life.

Genesis 3:23-24

Adam and Eve were disobedient and resistant to God’s instructions. They defied God’s authority and attempted to become like God. Through this process, they lost their lives and became separated from God.

Man has become like a phone which is disconnected from the electricity that powers it up. As soon as Adam and Eve rebelled against God, they were disconnected from their source of life and began to die.

Man has become like a phone that is disconnected from power with a battery that is gradually failing until it comes to zero.

When pastors rebel against authorities, they become separated from the source of life of their ministries. Their ministries undergo a terrible separation from what gave them life and power. This is how people become a phantom of what they would have been if they had been faithful.

Do not forget to watch out for these seven changes that come into the lives of rebellious people and their followers:

1\. FEAR

2\. SECRECY

3\. DEMOTION

4\. DEMONIZATION

5\. VANITY

6\. CORRUPTION

7\. DEATH

Be careful that you never become a rebel!
