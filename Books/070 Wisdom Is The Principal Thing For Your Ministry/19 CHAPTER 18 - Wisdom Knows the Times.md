### CHAPTER 18\

Wisdom Knows the Times\
============================================

YEA, THE STORK IN THE HEAVEN KNOWETH HER APPOINTED TIMES; AND THE TURTLE AND THE CRANE AND THE SWALLOW OBSERVE THE TIME OF THEIR COMING; but my people know not the judgment of the Lord. HOW DO YE SAY, WE ARE WISE, and the law of the Lord is with us? Lo, certainly in vain made he it; the pen of the scribes is in vain.

Jeremiah 8:7-8

In the scripture above, we see how different animals know the times and the seasons. The stork, the turtle, the crane and the swallow know the time of day. They know that it is time to move from one place to another. The prophet Jeremiah asked an important question: “If the people of God do not know the time of their judgment, how can they claim to be wise?”

Indeed, a wise man knows the time and seasons. You must know the times and seasons in your life. You must understand what God is doing with you at any particular time. It is wise to understand what is happening so that you can flow along with God.

Jesus was someone who knew the timings of God.

Then Jesus said unto them, My time is not yet come: but your time is alway ready.

John 7:6

It is wisdom to flow with the timings of God.

There are times that God is breaking things up.

There are times that God is bringing judgment.

There are times that God is bringing revelation.

There are times that God is bringing church growth.

You must flow along with what God is doing. In the ministry of Jesus you see different seasons. Jesus understood the seasons when God was working with Him. Jesus was full of wisdom!

1\. Recognize a time of recruitment.

Jesus knew that it was time for the recruitment of special people who would be the pillars of His ministry.

During this season, Jesus recruited many people and many people joined Jesus’ ministry during this period. These were the pillars of His ministry. During this season of recruitment, almost everybody he encountered was to become a foundational member of his ministry.

Now as he walked by the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew his brother casting a net into the sea: for they were fishers. And Jesus said unto them, Come ye after me, and I will make you to become fishers of men. And straightway they forsook their nets, and followed him. And when he had gone a little further thence, he saw James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, who also were in the ship mending their nets.

Mark 1:16-19

2\. Recognize a time of power.

Jesus knew that it was time for a season of power.

During this season, Jesus experienced the greatest miracles and manifestations of power. In the fifth chapter of Mark alone, we see the season of miracle power.

In one eventful day, Jesus cast the demons out of the mad man of Gadara. Then he proceeded to Jairus’ house to raise the dead. On the way to Jairus’ house he healed the woman with the issue of blood. Three notable miracles occurred in one day of Jesus’ ministry.

By the end of the sixth chapter of Mark, the whole region was carrying sick people on their beds to the streets where Jesus would pass by.

And ran through that whole region round about, and began to carry about in beds those that were sick, where they heard he was. And whithersoever he entered, into villages, or cities, or country, they laid the sick in the streets, and besought him that they might touch if it were but the border of his garment: and as many as touched him were made whole.

Mark 6:55-56

3\. Recognize a time of offenses.

Many therefore of his disciples, when they had heard this, said, This is an hard saying; who can hear it? When Jesus knew in himself that his disciples murmured at it, he said unto them, Doth this offend you?

From that time many of his disciples went back, and walked no more with him. Then said Jesus unto the twelve, Will ye also go away? Then Simon Peter answered him, Lord, to whom shall we go? thou hast the words of eternal life. And we believe and are sure that thou art that Christ, the Son of the living God.

John 6:60-61, 66-69

Expect a season of offenses. It is a season of trouble. It is a season you will not enjoy. You must recognise that it is a season and it will pass.

There was a season in the ministry of Jesus when many people were offended.

There will be a season in your ministry where people will be offended at you.

You must accept that there are seasons and times for all these things. In your walk with Jesus, you will experience everything that Jesus experienced. If you are really following Jesus, you will see everything that Jesus saw.

4\. Recognize the time of disciples.

After these things Jesus walked in Galilee: for he would not walk in Jewry, because the Jews sought to kill him.

John 7:1

Jesus knew that it was time for training disciples privately.

From the sixth chapter of John, Jesus’ following drastically reduced. Masses of people forsook him. Many people were offended by his teaching. People could not stand his teachings any more. Jesus was left with His twelve disciples. There is a time of your ministry where you must concentrate on the leadership and not on the masses.

5\. Recognize a time of betrayal.

And Jesus saith unto them, All ye shall be offended because of me this night: for it is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered. But after that I am risen, I will go before you into Galilee. But Peter said unto him, Although all shall be offended, yet will not I. And Jesus saith unto him, Verily I say unto thee, That this day, even in this night, before the cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice. But he spake the more vehemently, If I should die with thee, I will not deny thee in any wise. Likewise also said they all.

Mark 14:27-31

Expect a season of treachery. It is a season of trouble. It is a season you will not enjoy. You must recognise the season of betrayal. This is something you must go through. Jesus went through it.

Count it all joy when you experience various trials and temptations. If you have been betrayed by one of your closest disciples, then you are getting nearer to the place where Jesus was.

There comes a time in the ministry when you must experience betrayal, desertion and treachery. During this season you will meet Judas who will sell you to the enemy.

You will also meet Peter who will say big things but will not keep to his word. You will meet disciples who will desert you in spite of all their training and experiences.

Jesus knew that it was the time for betrayal.

He accepted it and knew that many of His disciples would not make the standard.

6\. Recognize a time of sacrifice.

And Jesus answered them, saying, THE HOUR IS COME, that the Son of man should be glorified. Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.

John 12:23-24

There is a time to sacrifice. It is not the time to receive gifts. It is not the time to be honoured. It is not the time to become wealthy. It is a time to lay down your life and go low in everything until the time that God exalts you.

Jesus experienced a time of going low and disappearing from this earth in shame. It was a time that would lead to His final exaltation.

Today, Jesus is worthy to receive honour and glory and power and riches because he laid down his life when it was time to do so.

7\. Recognize a time of evil.

When I was daily with you in the temple, ye stretched forth no hands against me: but this is your hour, and the power of darkness.

Luke 22:53

There is a time where evil is allowed to triumph over good. This is mysterious but God works in a mysterious way.

As the mysteries of God unfold, you will discover seasons where evil is allowed to prevail over God’s people.

Jesus said, “This is your hour and the power of darkness.” In other words, this is the time for evil to prevail.

Maybe you are going through such a season. Do not be disheartened. I have some good news for you. Every evil that is working in your life has an expiry date. There is a set time within which evil is allowed to work in your life. After that set time it will not work any more. You must overcome the evil day!

In the book of Revelation, we see how the believers in Smyrna were to be tested for ten days. After ten days the tribulation would be over! Satan has not been give free access to torment Christians indefinitely. Your crisis is coming to an end very soon!

Fear none of those things which thou shalt suffer: behold, the devil shall cast some of you into prison, that ye may be tried; and ye shall have tribulation ten days: be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee a crown of life.

Revelation 2:10

Also, you will notice how the temple and the holy city were given to the Gentiles to trample upon. Even though it was a season of evil for the Holy City, it was limited to forty-two months.

But the court which is without the temple leave out, and measure it not; for it is given unto the Gentiles: and the holy city shall they tread under foot forty and two months.

Revelation 11:2

When the dragon and the beast arose in the book of Revelation, they spoke great things and blasphemed God’s servants. However, their blasphemy was also limited to forty-two months. The wickedness of satan against your life and ministry will be cut short! Satan’s ability to torment your life will expire shortly!

And there was given unto him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies; and power was given unto him to continue forty and two months.

Revelation 13:5
