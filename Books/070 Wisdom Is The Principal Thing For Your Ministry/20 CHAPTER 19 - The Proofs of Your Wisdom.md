### CHAPTER 19\

The Proofs of Your Wisdom\
===============================================

But wisdom is proved right by all her children.

Luke 7:35 (NIV)

What proves that you are a wise man? In this chapter, I want to show you, out of scripture, some things that you must look for when you are looking for evidence of wisdom. This list of proofs is based on clear and unambiguous scriptures. I believe that all these proofs will soon be found in your life! Every single one of these proofs must be associated with your life and ministry.

Ask yourself whether these twelve things are found in your life. Is there promotion in your life? Is there honour in your life? Is there promotion in your life? Is there happiness in your life? Is there pleasantness in your life? Is there peace in your life? Is there treasure in your life? Do you have long life? Do you have the ability to lead people? Are there riches and honour in your life? Is there righteousness in your life? Have you built anything in your life? Are you creative?

This is an important checklist. Each and every one of these twelve things are proofs of wisdom. God’s word is your guiding post! It must be your aim to be a wise man. People must point to you and say, “That is a man of wisdom.” But there must be proofs of your wisdom.

The Proofs of Wisdom

1\. Promotion is a proof of your wisdom.

Exalt her, and she shall PROMOTE THEE: she shall bring thee to honour, when thou dost embrace her.

Proverbs 4:8

2\. Honour is a proof of your wisdom.

Exalt her, and she shall promote thee: she shall bring thee to HONOUR, when thou dost embrace her.

Proverbs 4:8

3\. Happiness is a proof of your wisdom.

She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her: and HAPPY is every one that retaineth her.

Proverbs 3:18

4\. Pleasantness is a proof of your wisdom.

Her ways are ways of PLEASANTNESS, and all her paths are peace.

Proverbs 3:17

5\. Peace is a proof of your wisdom.

Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are PEACE.

Proverbs 3:17

6\. Treasures and substance are proofs of your wisdom.

That I may cause those that love me to inherit SUBSTANCE; and I will fill their treasures.

Proverbs 8:21

7\. Long life is a proof of your wisdom.

Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings; and THE YEARS OF THY LIFE SHALL BE MANY.

Proverbs 4:10

8\. The ability to lead and rule is proof of your wisdom.

By me kings reign, and princes decree justice.

Proverbs 8:15

9\. Riches with honour are proofs of your wisdom.

RICHES AND HONOUR are with me; yea, durable riches and righteousness.

Proverbs 8:18

10\. Riches with righteousness are proofs of your wisdom.

Riches and honour are with me; yea, durable riches and RIGHTEOUSNESS.

Proverbs 8:18

11\. The things you have built are proofs of your wisdom.

THROUGH WISDOM IS AN HOUSE BUILDED; and by understanding it is established: And by knowledge shall the chambers be filled with all precious and pleasant riches

Proverbs 24:3-4

12\. Your creativity is proof of your wisdom.

THE LORD CREATED THE EARTH BY HIS WISDOM; by his knowledge he set the sky in place.

Proverbs 3:19 (GNB)
