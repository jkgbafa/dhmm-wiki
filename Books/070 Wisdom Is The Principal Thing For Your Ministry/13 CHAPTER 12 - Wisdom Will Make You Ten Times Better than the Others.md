### CHAPTER 12\

Wisdom Will Make You Ten Times Better than the Others\
===========================================================================

AS FOR THESE FOUR CHILDREN, GOD GAVE THEM KNOWLEDGE AND SKILL IN ALL LEARNING AND WISDOM: and Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams. Now at the end of the days that the king had said he should bring them in, then the prince of the eunuchs brought them in before Nebuchadnezzar. And the king communed with them; and among them all was found none like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah: therefore stood they before the king. AND IN ALL MATTERS OF WISDOM AND UNDERSTANDING, THAT THE KING ENQUIRED OF THEM, HE FOUND THEM TEN TIMES BETTER THAN ALL THE MAGICIANS AND ASTROLOGERS THAT WERE IN ALL HIS REALM. And Daniel continued even unto the first year of king Cyrus.

Daniel 1:17-21

God wants you to be a good example of many things. You can become a good example to the church, the community, the village and the town. Be an example in faith, in purity, in holiness, in love, in forgiveness and in obedience.

You can be ten times better than every other minister in your category.

What is going to distinguish you from the others? Your wisdom!

What is going to make a difference in your ministry? Your wisdom!

The wisdom of God is the master key for you to be distinguished from your colleagues. Daniel and his three colleagues were found to be ten times better than all the magicians and astrologers of the time.

Do you want to have a ministry that is ten times better?

Your opportunity to have a ministry that is ten times better is here. The wisdom of God is the principal thing. Wisdom is profitable to direct you.

How do you receive wisdom that will distinguish you from the others? The answer is simple: if anyone lacks wisdom let him ask! (James 1:5) You must learn to ask God extensively for wisdom.

Look at the scripture above. It says “God gave them knowledge and skill and learning and wisdom.”

It is God who gives you wisdom that will distinguish you from your colleagues. It is God who will give you wisdom that will make you ten times better than your colleagues.

Praying for wisdom and revelation should be one of the most important prayers of your life.

God is the One who gave the four children the wisdom they had. God made them ten times better and God is going to make you ten times better too. Wisdom is the Spirit! The Spirit of wisdom is a real spirit! The more you pray for this Spirit of wisdom, the more you will receive.

I have prayed for the Spirit of wisdom for many years. I believe the Spirit of wisdom and revelation really affects the way I think. Let most of your prayers centre on praying for the Spirit of wisdom and revelation.

If any of you lack wisdom, LET HIM ASK OF GOD, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

James 1:5

How to Acquire the Wisdom that Will Make You Ten Times Better

1\. Pray for the Holy Spirit in general.

If ye then, being evil, know how to give good gifts unto your children: how much more shall your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to them that ask him?

Luke 11:13

Praying for the Holy Spirit is very important. This prayer will make God release the Holy Spirit into your life. Most Christians do not pray for the Holy Spirit. They pray for many things from God. Christians ask God for many things except the Holy Spirit.

Jesus said, “If you as parents know how to give good things to children, God as a parent will equally know how to give the Holy Spirit to His children.”

2\. Pray specifically for the Spirit of wisdom.

And the spirit of the Lord shall rest upon him, THE SPIRIT OF WISDOM and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of the Lord;

Isaiah 11:2

3\. Pray for the Spirit of revelation.

That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you THE SPIRIT OF wisdom and REVELATION in the knowledge of him:

Ephesians 1:17

4\. Pray specifically for the Spirit of understanding.

And the spirit of the Lord shall rest upon him, THE SPIRIT OF wisdom and UNDERSTANDING, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of the Lord;

Isaiah 11:2

5\. Pray specifically for the Spirit of counsel.

And the spirit of the Lord shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, THE SPIRIT OF COUNSEL and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of the Lord;

Isaiah 11:2

6\. Pray specifically for the Spirit of knowledge.

And the spirit of the Lord shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, THE SPIRIT OF KNOWLEDGE and of the fear of the Lord;

Isaiah 11:2

7\. Pray specifically for the Spirit of a sound mind.

For God hath not given us THE SPIRIT of fear; but of power, and of love, and OF A SOUND MIND.

2 Timothy 1:7

8\. Pray for the Spirit of the fear of the Lord.

And the spirit of the LORD shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of THE FEAR OF THE LORD;

Isaiah 11:2

9\. Pray specifically for the Spirit of humility.

Better it is to be of an humble spirit with the lowly, than to divide the spoil with the proud.

Proverbs 16:19

A humble spirit is the Spirit of humility! The spirit that is at work in your life forms your mind and thoughts. Praying for the Spirit of humility is praying to have the mind that was in Christ. Let this mind be in you. The Spirit that was in Christ made Christ a very humble person. Today, as you pray for the Spirit of humility, your thoughts will become thoughts of humility.

Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus: who, being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God: but made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men: and being found in fashion as a man, HE humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.

Philippians 2:5-8

10\. Pray specifically for the Spirit of holiness.

And declared to be the Son of God with power, according to the SPIRIT OF HOLINESS, by the resurrection from the dead: By whom we have received grace and apostleship, for obedience to the faith among all nations, for his name:

Romans 1:4-5

11\. Pray specifically for the spirit of love.

For God hath not given us THE SPIRIT of fear; but of power, and OF LOVE, and of a sound mind.

2 Timothy 1:7

12\. Pray specifically for the Spirit of Christ.

But ye are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit, if so be that the Spirit of God dwell in you. Now if any man have not THE SPIRIT OF CHRIST, he is none of his.

Romans 8:9
