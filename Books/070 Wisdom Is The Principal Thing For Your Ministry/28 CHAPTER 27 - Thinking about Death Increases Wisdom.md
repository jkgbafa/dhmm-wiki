### CHAPTER 27\

Thinking about Death Increases Wisdom\
===========================================================

So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.

Psalm 90:12

Numbering your days is another way of saying you should think about death. Life has more meaning in the face of death! When you count your money, you are forced to estimate when it will get finished.

Counting your money helps you to plan. If you are on a holiday and you have three days left, counting your money helps you to plan for the end of the holiday. Your mind is always forced to focus on the end.

“Numbering your days” is an ancient King James English expression teaching you to think of death so that you become wise. Life has more meaning in the face of death!

Why Should We Think about Death?

1\. Thinking about death makes you live your life abundantly.

The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly.

John 10:10

You must think about death so that you enjoy life as it is today. Many people do not make the most of life’s good experiences till death knocks on the door and ends it all. You must think about death because death is a part of this life. Death is not the opposite of life, per se. Death is not the end of life, per se. Death is a part of this life. Death is everywhere.

There are cemeteries, mortuaries and dead people everywhere. There are bodies of dead people somewhere near you, only that you cannot see them.

Death is the beginning of another state of existence. Eternity is a new way of existing, in which time, as we know it, will not exist. Thinking about death means you must live as though you would die tomorrow. Life has more meaning in the face of death.

2\. Thinking about death makes you wise for the future.

A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.

Proverbs 22:3

Thinking about death makes you wise. It makes you wisely prepare for the future. Death never takes a wise man by surprise. A wise man is always ready to go. Death is the destination that all men are heading towards. No one has ever escaped it. Since no one escapes death, it is only wisdom to be well prepared for its arrival at any time.

Those who have the wisdom of thinking about death will write their Will. It is amazing that few people actually write their Wills. Thinking about death will make you do things so that after you are gone there is no confusion or uncertainty.

3\. Thinking about death makes you count the days you have left on earth.

The days of our years are threescore years and ten; and if by reason of strength they be fourscore years, yet is their strength labour and sorrow; for it is soon cut off, and we fly away . . . So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.

Psalm 90:10, 12

Thinking about death is an instruction that is intended to make you wise. You are supposed to count the days you have left. Instead of counting how many years you have lived, count how many more years you have left to live.

For instance, you must say, “I have twenty more years to seventy.”

If you are forty-seven years old, you must say, “I have twenty-three more years to seventy.”

If you are fifty-three years old you must say, “I have seventeen more years to seventy.” If you are fifty-seven years old you must say, “I have thirteen more years to seventy.”

Saying you have thirteen more years to seventy feeds you with more urgency than saying you are fifty-seven years old. Saying you are fifty-seven years old makes you sound mature and wise. But saying you have thirteen years left makes you desperate to rise up and finish things.

4\. Thinking about death makes you value the people in your life.

But the people answered, Thou shalt not go forth: for if we flee away, they will not care for us; neither if half of us die, will they care for us: but now thou art worth ten thousand of us: therefore now it is better that thou succour us out of the city.

2 Samuel 18:3

When the people thought of the death of David, they realised how valuable he actually was. David was worth more than ten thousand men.

Thinking about death can be depressive. However, thinking about death can also greatly increase your wisdom.

It is loss that often teaches us about the worth of things. It is the loss of people that makes you realise how valuable they are.

Death, therefore, can make you appreciate people greatly. Thinking about death can make you value the important people in your life. The thought of death can stir up your commitment and your zeal to love these people.

5\. Thinking about death makes you value the things you have.

And also that every man should eat and drink, and enjoy the good of all his labour, it is the gift of God.

Ecclesiastes 3:13

Thinking about death always makes you wise. It makes you live your life better. You must be thankful for your house, your cars, your husband or your wife, your children and all the physical things you possess.

However, you must remember that all these things will not fit into your coffin when it is time to go. You must therefore seek to enjoy them as much as you can. Storing everything for the future that never comes is not wisdom.

6\. Thinking about death makes you have a good marriage.

Live joyfully with the wife whom thou lovest all the days of the life of thy vanity, which he hath given thee under the sun, all the days of thy vanity: for that is thy portion in this life, and in thy labour which thou takest under the sun.

Ecclesiastes 9:9

After death there will be no marriage. It takes wisdom to enjoy the blessings of marriage and the partner you have.

Many people are full of complaints and perpetually discontented because they do not have the wisdom that comes from thinking about death.

The thought of your dead spouse may spur you on to do everything you should be doing. When the person is dead, you will no longer have the opportunity to be a good husband or a good wife. It is usually the loss of something good that reveals its worth.

We suddenly become aware of how great a spouse we had when that spouse dies. I have seen people become frightened when they suspected that their spouse was dying. Even before the spouse died, they began to panic. Just the thought of death can make you wise.

7\. Thinking about death makes you wise to prepare for eternity.

And as it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment:

Hebrews 9:27

After death comes eternal judgment. Eternal judgment is like an examination where you are assessed, weighed and evaluated. Since your life and works are going to be evaluated, you will live your life with that in mind.

Eternal judgement will involve four main things.

a. Be wise because the QUANTITY OF FRUITS of your life and ministry will be judged.

And it came to pass, that when he was returned, having received the kingdom, then he commanded these servants to be called unto him, to whom he had given the money, that he might know HOW MUCH every man had gained by trading.

Luke 19:15

At the eternal judgment, the fruits of your ministry will be assessed in terms of quality and quantity. Eternal judgment of your fruits will involve the quantity of what you have done for the Lord.

b. Be wise because the QUALITY OF FRUITS of your life and ministry will be judged.

Every man’s work shall be made manifest: for the day shall declare it, because it shall be revealed by fire; and the fire shall try every man’s work OF WHAT SORT IT IS. If any man’s work abide which he hath built thereupon, he shall receive a reward.

1 Corinthians 3:13-14

The judgment will also involve the quality of what you have done for the Lord. These two aspects: the quality and quantity of your fruits will be assessed at the Judgment Seat of Christ.

c. Be wise because the HIDDEN ASPECTS AND SECRETS of your life will be judged.

Therefore do not go on passing judgment before the time, but wait until the Lord comes who will both bring to light THE THINGS HIDDEN IN THE DARKNESS and disclose the motives of men’s hearts; and then each man’s praise will come to him from God.

1 Corinthians 4:5 (NASB)

Apart from the fruits you have borne, there are hidden things in everyone’s life. There are weaknesses and there are sins that are hidden from man’s view. There are murderers who are praised by men who do not know the whole truth. There are wicked sinners who are hailed and praised by men. On the judgment day, all these secret aspects will be brought out.

d. Be wise because THE MOTIVES of your life will be judged.

Therefore do not go on passing judgment before the time, but wait until the Lord comes who will both bring to light the things hidden in the darkness AND DISCLOSE THE MOTIVES OF MEN’S HEARTS; and then each man’s praise will come to him from God.

1 Corinthians 4:5 (NASB)

There are also motives and reasons for which people do good works. Many good works are done with the wrong motives. When we stand before God, our motives will be brought out. Many of our apparently good works may be declared works of no value.
