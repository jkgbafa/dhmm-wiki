### CHAPTER 30\

Wisdom Increases by Observing High Places\
===============================================================

DOTH NOT WISDOM CRY? and understanding put forth her voice? SHE STANDETH IN THE TOP OF HIGH PLACES, by the way in the places of the paths. She crieth at the gates, at the entry of the city, at the coming in at the doors. Unto you, O men, I call; and my voice is to the sons of man.

Proverbs 8:1-4

The high places of this earth are the great places of this earth. There are many great places and great destinations on this earth. The high places of this earth are great sources of wisdom. Going to any of these places will definitely increase your wisdom. The high places also speak of the high points of life and the high points of a career.

Great achievements are high places or high points in people’s careers and lives.

Great achievements in any one must always stir up great wisdom. It should make you think great thoughts and get filled with wisdom.

The great places on this earth are great destinations that you must visit. Visiting these great destinations stirs up the wisdom of God. This is why a person who has travelled around the world seems to be more educated than someone who has never travelled anywhere, but has a degree from his community university.

I have often travelled with large groups of people in an effort to educate them with wisdom. Seeing things, and experiencing places will change your life.

One of the high places I visited was South Korea. South Korea is one of the great places on earth. I recognized how the Koreans had copied everything from outside their country. They replicated the Toyota, the Opel and the Mercedes Benz cars. There were no foreign-made cars in sight anywhere. Every car, every bus and every tractor I saw was Korean made.

I understood how great advancement could come to a nation through copying and following humbly. Copying had made them surge forward and catch up with Japan, Germany, England and America.

Today, Korean cars are used everywhere. Koreans also copied the technologies of televisions and phones. Once again, they surged forward and caught up with those who seemed to be fathers of technology, wealth and achievement.

Visiting a high place, Korea, taught me one of the greatest lessons of ministry – the art of copying and the art of following.

Another high place I visited was Yoido Gospel Church. There, I saw the greatest church in the world. I saw and observed a huge ministry with a strong pastor. There was a lot of wisdom for me in this high place. I went home and considered for many years, the wisdom that called out to me from Yoido Full Gospel Church.

Another high place that I visited was Israel. Israel is one of the great places on the earth. I remember driving from Tel Aviv to Jerusalem at night. I thought to myself, “These people know the importance of Jerusalem and therefore have built a beautiful road to link two important cities.”

I thought of many other nations that have not been able to link their two main cities with a road. I thought of many nations that are ruled by inept politicians who are simply incapable of constructing the necessary road between its two most important cities.

Another great and high place I visited was the Masai Mara in Kenya. Masai Mara is one of the famous and important wildlife conservation and wilderness areas in Africa. It is well renowned for its exceptional populations of lions, leopards, cheetahs and elephants. In other words, it is a wildlife paradise!

As I drove to this amazing high place of the earth, I was struck by the absence of a complete, good road linking Nairobi to Masai Mara. I thought to myself, “The whole world comes here to see these amazing sights and yet the nation will not pull itself together and make a safe and beautiful highway to link this place to the capital.”

I realized the need to make the most of God’s gift. God had given that nation an amazing gift of wildlife. I know of other countries that also have amazing wildlife reservations but have no road linking them. What amazing gift has God given you? Are you making the most of it or do you want something else?

Do you value what God has given you enough to make the necessary investment in it?

Stop looking outside for help! Stop blaming others for your poverty! Look at what God has given you and make the most of it!

Another great place I visited was the Victoria Falls. I was amazed at this natural wonder and how beautiful it was. However, that was not what struck me the first time I visited it. I was struck by the Victoria Falls steel bridge that crosses the Zambezi River. This bridge links the two countries: Zimbabwe and Zambia.

Instructions to build this bridge were given by Cecil Rhodes, Prime Minister of the Cape Colony, as part of his Cape Town to Cairo railway scheme. This bridge was pre-fabricated in England and transported through the port in Beira, Mozambique to the Victoria Falls.

Cecil Rhodes gave instructions that the bridge should be built so that when the trains passed by they would feel the spray of the waterfalls. The bridge was finally opened in 1905. It took just fourteen months to construct. The achievement of this one hundred and ninety-eight metre long steel bridge was a historic engineering landmark.

Imagine that! Pre-fabricating a bridge in England for installation in Africa in the nineteenth century!

I wondered to myself how many nations are not able to construct roads and bridges in their countries in the twenty-first century. Consider what Britain was able to do for its far away colony in 1905. Consider how they did not need a loan from China to make this bridge. Today, many governments claim that nothing can be done except a huge loan is acquired from an international financial institution. As I considered this “high place”, I realized that many leaders make empty excuses for things they could actually do. If the invading colonisers could build this great work one hundred and twenty years ago, how come the locals do not rise up and develop their own countries now that they are free and independent?

Another high place I visited was the Kennedy Space Center at Cape Canaveral. This is one of the great places on earth because it has been NASA’s primary launch centre for human space flights since December 1968. The great achievement of launching the Apollo rockets and taking human beings into space were done from this location. Indeed, my visit to this place revealed how great an achievement it is to have taken human beings to the moon and brought them back safely.

Visiting and seeing the high places of the earth will always give you wisdom. What struck me at the Kennedy Space Center was the determination to overcome all obstacles in order to achieve the goal. I saw a sign that said, “Failure is not an option” and I saw another sign that said, “Journey to Mars; Explorers Wanted”.

Apparently, they were planning to take human beings to Mars and seeking to solve all the problems that they would encounter. It takes a minimum of six months to fly to Mars at top speed and another six months to fly back at top super sonic speed. The flying time to Mars and back would be one continuous year at super sonic speed. The astronauts would suffer from the lack of gravity and the stress of confinement and isolation.

Once on Mars, where would the astronauts live and what would they breathe? What are they going to eat on Mars? The amount of food the astronauts would need to eat for two years would fill at least one huge sixteen-wheeler truck. How would you lift this amount of food unto a rocket? It would be impossible to lift that weight and fly it at that speed to Mars just for the meals of the astronauts.

Astronauts are trying to grow vegetables in space and scientists are figuring out how to get astronauts to convert their own faeces into something edible. Imagine that! Poopoo biscuits for lunch! All things are possible!

They are also exploring getting astronauts to recycle urine into drinkable water. Imagine that! Wee-wee water to refresh yourself!

Indeed, it is simply amazing to see some human beings overcome every obstacle in order to achieve their goals. It is time to receive the wisdom that says, “No problem is too big to be solved. There is no mountain that cannot be climbed. There is no river that cannot be crossed.” In space there are no resources for food. Think about it! Using urine and faeces as food and drink! Such ideas only come to those who are determined to make the impossible possible. It is important to receive wisdom from every high and great place you visit.

Without a determination to overcome problems and to believe in finding solutions, many things cannot be achieved. This is the kind of wisdom that you need when you live in the poorest part of the world and are without resources.
