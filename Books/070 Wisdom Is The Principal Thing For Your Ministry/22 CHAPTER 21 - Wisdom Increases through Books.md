### CHAPTER 21\

Wisdom Increases through Books\
====================================================

IN THE FIRST YEAR OF HIS REIGN I DANIEL UNDERSTOOD BY BOOKS the number of the years, whereof the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah the prophet, that he would accomplish seventy years in the desolations of Jerusalem.

Daniel 9:2

There are books that will greatly increase your wisdom. Instead of remaining in darkness, you will move into the light. Your life will change when you study certain books. Daniel, the prophet gained great understanding by reading books. If Daniel the prophet who saw visions of heaven, needed to have books to increase his understanding, so will you. You are going to receive understanding from books.

Seven Books that Increased My Wisdom

1\. “Plead Your Case1” by Kenneth Hagin

From a little book entitled “Plead Your Case1” by Kenneth Hagin, I increased in wisdom. Through this little book, I sensed how real heaven is. I also came to believe that my prayers were being heard directly in heaven. When you believe in prayer you truly become a wise man. This little book helped me to believe in prayer.

2\. “I believe in Visions2” by Kenneth Hagin

There is a book called, “I believe in Visions2” by Kenneth Hagin. Through this book, I received a lot of revelation and wisdom for the ministry. I cannot share all the different things that I learnt because they are so many amazing revelations and pieces of wisdom to receive from this book. It is just amazing to hear that Jesus Christ Himself would appear to a real person who is on earth today.

I learnt about how important it is to focus on the ministry. In one vision, Kenneth Hagin was told that he would not have lived beyond the age of fifty-five if he continued to place the teaching ministry above the prophetic ministry. I received great wisdom and prayed earnestly to be able to cross the age of fifty-five.

In this book, I also received wisdom on how to use both medical science and spiritual power to overcome sickness and disease. I found out that the great prophet, Kenneth Hagin, was on admission in the hospital and the Lord appeared to him right there. The Lord said to him to always pray for people who are in hospital. Prayer changes things for people who are on admission in the hospital.

In another vision, I learnt how demons affected a pastor’s wife and led to a divorce. I realised that demon activity is real amongst ministers and their wives.

3\. “The Final Quest3” by Rick Joyner

Perhaps, the book that affected my life more than any other would be “The Final Quest3” by Rick Joyner. This book, along with its sequels “The Call” and “The Torch and the Sword” have had an amazing impact on my level of wisdom.

I received wisdom for eternal judgment as I read about pastors who had died and gone on to eternity. One of the stories stood out for me. This was about a pastor who had a conflict with his spiritual sons. God had called him to be a father to many sons. He was expected to overlook the rebellion of his children. In heaven, he was judged because he did not act as a father and overlook the rebellion of his spiritual sons. I received wisdom to be careful in my relationships with the sons God had given me in the ministry.

Another amazing wisdom key I received was to be careful about the books that I wrote. In Rick Joyner’s book, a pastor had been judged in heaven for the books he wrote. His books were utterly rejected in the courts of heaven as being utterly secular and unspiritual. Again, this gave me wisdom to write my books in a way that they would stand the judgment of eternity.

Another wisdom key that changed my life was when I read how Rick Joyner had confronted Apostle Paul. The things Paul told him about the ministry and the message were so revealing. Apostle Paul told him that the current church had thrown out the message of sacrifice and therefore had become a phantom of what it was called to be.

4\. “The Torch and the Sword4” by Rick Joyner

“The torch and the Sword4” is an amazing book. In this book, I received the wisdom of valuing the presence of God, staying close to God and being in His presence. In this book, we see Rick Joyner carrying a torch easily when he was close to the Lord. However, as soon as there was a little bit of distance between him and the Lord, the torch became too heavy to carry.

The Lord taught him how many pastors had moved away from the Lord and were unable to carry their torch and replaced it with something else.

Today, it is easy to see how many men of God do not have the presence of God with them. You can hear it in the messages that they preach. They are secular, earthly-minded and filled with human wisdom instead of being filled with the treasures of the pure word of God. What wisdom it is to keep yourself in the presence of God!

5\. “God’s Generals5” by Roberts Liardon

Another amazing book is “God’s Generals5” by Roberts Liardon. This is actually a series of biographies of great men that God used in the past. What amazing parallels there are between what happened to those great men of God and what is happening today! I received great wisdom to persist through trials, temptations and difficulties. I received wisdom from the stories of A. A. Allen, Alexander Dowie, Kathryn Kuhlman, Aimee Semple McPherson, William Seymour and Jack Coe. Each name I mention reminds me of a series of lessons of great wisdom. When you read biographies, you increase learning greatly.

6\. “When God Walked the Earth6” by Rick Joyner

“When God Walked the Earth6” by Rick Joyner is another revealing book about the life of Jesus. I received great wisdom when I read about the temptation of Jesus in the wilderness. I realised how wicked satan is, and how real it is that angels are everywhere in this great battle. I was touched when I learnt how superior angels were called in to guard the new disciples because the Lord had chosen them.

7\. “He Gave Gifts Unto Men7” by Kenneth Hagin

In this book, “He Gave Gifts Unto Men7” there is a testimony of how Kenneth Hagin prophesied the death of William Marion Branham. It frightened me and alerted me to stay on the course of my calling.

Branham was a great prophet who lived from 1909 to 1965. Towards the end of his ministry, he waded into various controversial teachings that were rejected by the charismatic movement. According to Kenneth Hagin, God revealed that Branham was teaching false doctrine and God was removing him because of his disobedience. At a prayer session in January 1964, Kenneth Hagin prophesied:

“At the end of ‘65, he who now stands in the forefront of the healing ministry as a prophet will be taken out of the way. He’ll make a false step and satan shall destroy his life, but his spirit shall be saved, and his works will follow him. ‘Ere ’66 shall come, he shall be gone.”

Kenneth Hagin then approached Gordon Lindsey (who worked for Branham) in his office with the prophecy, two years before Branham’s death. Two years later, on December 18, 1965, Branham and his family were returning from Indiana from their Christmas holiday. Just after dark, a car driven by a drunken driver, driving in the opposite direction collided head-on with Branham’s car. He was rushed to the hospital where he remained comatose for several days and died of his injuries on Christmas Eve, 24th December 1965.

His death occurred exactly as the prophecy had declared and he died just before 1966. The need to obey God and the need to stay within the calling are great wisdom lessons from this testimony.
