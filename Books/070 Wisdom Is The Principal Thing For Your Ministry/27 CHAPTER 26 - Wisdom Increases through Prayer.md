### CHAPTER 26\

Wisdom Increases through Prayer\
=====================================================

If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

James 1:5

If you want your wisdom levels to increase, you must learn to pray for wisdom. Wisdom is something that comes to you when you pray for it. If you do not pray for wisdom, you will not receive the wisdom that you need.

Praying for the Spirit of wisdom is praying for wisdom. Jesus taught us to pray for the Holy Spirit therefore you must pray for the Spirit of wisdom. “If ye then, being evil, know how to give good gifts unto your children: how much more shall your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to them that ask him?” (Luke 11:13) Solomon is the best example of someone who prayed for wisdom.

Give therefore thy servant an understanding heart to judge thy people, that I may discern between good and bad: for who is able to judge this thy so great a people? And the speech pleased the Lord, that Solomon had asked this thing. And God said unto him, Because thou hast asked this thing, and hast not asked for thyself long life; neither hast asked riches for thyself, nor hast asked the life of thine enemies; but hast asked for thyself understanding to discern judgment;

1 Kings 3:9-11

Seven Effects of Praying for Wisdom

AND GOD GAVE SOLOMON WISDOM and understanding exceeding much, and largeness of heart, even as the sand that is on the sea shore. AND SOLOMON’S WISDOM EXCELLED THE WISDOM OF ALL THE CHILDREN OF THE EAST COUNTRY, AND ALL THE WISDOM OF EGYPT. FOR HE WAS WISER THAN ALL MEN; than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Chalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol: and his fame was in all nations round about. And he spake three thousand proverbs: and his songs were a thousand and five. And he spake of trees, from the cedar tree that is in Lebanon even unto the hyssop that springeth out of the wall: he spake also of beasts, and of fowl, and of creeping things, and of fishes. AND THERE CAME OF ALL PEOPLE TO HEAR THE WISDOM OF SOLOMON, FROM ALL KINGS OF THE EARTH, WHICH HAD HEARD OF HIS WISDOM.

1 Kings 4:29-34

1\. Praying for wisdom makes you have a large heart. Solomon received a large heart in response to his prayer for wisdom. A large heart speaks of having a great depth of understanding and insight.

And God gave Solomon wisdom and understanding exceeding much, and largeness of heart, even as the sand that is on the sea shore.

1 Kings 4:29

2\. Praying for wisdom makes you have a higher level of wisdom than the wisdom prevailing in your nation.

And Solomon’s wisdom EXCELLED THE WISDOM OF ALL THE CHILDREN OF THE EAST COUNTRY, and all the wisdom of EGYPT.

1 Kings 4:30

Having wisdom which is superior to the prevailing wisdom in your country is a sign of having prayed for wisdom. There is a prevalent kind of wisdom in every nation of the world. America has its wisdom. Africa has its wisdom. Asia has its wisdom. The wisdom of God is above the wisdom of all these great nations. When you pray for wisdom, God will give you wisdom that supersedes all wisdom in the world.

3\. Praying for wisdom makes you have a higher level of wisdom than your colleagues. Becoming wiser than your contemporaries is the direct effect of Solomon’s prayer for wisdom.

FOR HE WAS WISER THAN ALL MEN; than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Chalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol: and his fame was in all nations round about.

1 Kings 4:31

Solomon received wisdom from God. Obviously, the wisdom he had made him wiser than his classmates. If God gives you wisdom, you will have a greater kind of wisdom than anyone in your life.

4\. Praying for wisdom will make you a man of proverbs and of songs. The wisdom you receive will cause you to speak with proverbs and to write special songs.

And he spake three thousand proverbs: and his songs were a thousand and five.

1 Kings 4:32

Those who write proverbs are able to couch wisdom cleverly, in a way that is difficult to forget. Those who write songs are able to couch wisdom in a way that fits into a melody. Those who sing these songs do not even realise that they are being influenced and taught the wisdom of the songs they are singing. Praying for wisdom will lead to supernatural abilities and insight.

5\. Praying for wisdom will make you appreciate nature. The wisdom you receive will make you appreciate the vegetation, the animals, the flying creatures and the creatures in the sea.

And he spake of trees, from the cedar tree that is in Lebanon even unto the hyssop that springeth out of the wall: he spake also of beasts, and of fowl, and of creeping things, and of fishes.

1 Kings 4:33

Solomon began to appreciate nature in response to the prayers he had prayed. God’s wisdom descended on him and he began to love nature. The wonders of creation will start to intrigue you as you grow in the wisdom that God is giving you. People who are not enchanted by wildlife often do not appreciate God’s amazing wisdom in creating the world.

A bird can fly out of its nest in North America and fly continuously, crossing the equator, and go into South America. After the winter, this same bird will fly thousands of miles back to North America to the very same state, the very same city, the very same tree and the very same nest that it left a year before. These wonders will intrigue any deep thinker.

Appreciating nature is truly a sign of wisdom. A shark will swim under the water in the black and dark sea and navigate itself from the cold waters of North America, down to the shores of Cape Town in South Africa. This same shark will swim back to America without a compass, a GPS or any other guidance.

Anyone with a little “something” in his head will know that there is an amazing creator of all these things. It is important to grow in wisdom by praying the same prayers that Solomon prayed.

6\. Praying for wisdom will cause people to travel from afar to listen to the wisdom God has given you.

And there came of all people to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all kings of the earth, which had heard of his wisdom.

1 Kings 4:34

The fact that someone will leave his home to come to church to listen to you speak is a sign that you possess God’s great wisdom in your life. People travelling from their homes, their cities and their nations just to listen to your preaching is indeed a sign that you possess great wisdom.

7\. Praying for wisdom will cause the kings of the earth and great men of this earth to pay attention to your wisdom.

And there came of all people to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all kings of the earth, which had heard of his wisdom.

1 Kings 4:34

Kings reign through wisdom. Many kings are not interested in the wisdom we have to offer. When you get their attention, you have crossed an important barrier. One day, kings will listen to the wisdom that you speak because God has made you wiser. By me \[wisdom\] kings reign, and princes decree justice (Proverbs 8:15).
