### CHAPTER 33\

Wisdom Increases As You Meditate\
======================================================

My mouth shall speak of wisdom; and the meditation of my heart shall be of understanding.

Psalm 49:3

Seven Powerful Effects of Meditation

1\. Meditation, contemplation, reflection, deep thinking and going over the same thing several times are KEYS TO WISDOM AND UNDERSTANDING.

I have more understanding than all my teachers: for thy testimonies are my meditation.

Psalm 119:99

The Psalmist has more wisdom and understanding because he meditates on the word of God. Meditation is the art of thinking deeply, contemplating, reflecting and going over the same thing several times! Most people do not think deeply about anything. It is important to think deeply about events that happen all around you. It is also important to think deeply about what you read in the Bible.

It is important to think deeply about the word of God. As you reflect on what you hear or read, you receive a deeper understanding.

Meditation greatly helps you to remember the things you are reading. People who do not meditate are shallow. Wisdom increases through active meditation.

2\. Meditation, contemplation, reflection, deep thinking and going over the same thing several times are KEYS TO GOOD SUCCESS.

This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.

Joshua 1:8

Joshua was instructed to meditate on the word of God. His meditation was the master key to his successful career in the military. The key to your successful career in the ministry is meditation. I have encountered several ministers of the gospel who asked me questions about the ministry. When I pointed them to my books, they would say, “I have read it but I did not see that.” They were amazed when I showed them things in the book. They were simply people who never engaged in thinking deeply, contemplating, reflecting and going over the same thing several times!

Truly, without going over the same thing several times, you will not get the revelation, the wisdom and the guidance that you need.

3\. Meditation, contemplation, reflection, deep thinking and going over the same thing several times are KEYS TO SUCCESS IN MINISTRY.

Neglect not the gift that is in thee, which was given thee by prophecy, with the laying on of the hands of the presbytery. MEDITATE UPON THESE THINGS; GIVE THYSELF WHOLLY TO THEM; that thy profiting may appear to all.

1 Timothy 4:14-15

Success in ministry depends on you focussing on the gift God has given you. To be successful in ministry, you need to think deeply, contemplate and reflect on the gifts God has given to you. If you do not think about the gift of God, how can you give yourself wholly to it? How would you know how to use the gift properly if you did not meditate on the gift?

4\. Meditation, contemplation, reflection, deep thinking and going over the same thing several times are KEYS TO A GREAT CHANGE.

He went to work for someone who lived in that country, who sent him to the fields to feed the pigs The son wanted to fill his stomach with the food the pigs were eating. But no one gave him anything. “THEN HE BEGAN TO THINK CLEARLY AGAIN. HE SAID, ‘HOW MANY OF MY FATHER’S HIRED WORKERS HAVE MORE THAN ENOUGH FOOD! BUT HERE I AM DYING FROM HUNGER! I will get up and go back to my father. I will say to him, “Father, I have sinned against heaven. And I have sinned against you.

Luke 15:15-18 (NIRV)

The prodigal son experienced a great change when he thought deeply about his condition. It was only when he thought deeply about how bad his condition was that the change began. Because he did not think deeply about how terrible his state was, he continued living like a pig. When he thought deeply and reflected on the reality that he had become a pig or a piglet, he began the journey towards the great change.

Many of the things in our world are deceptive in nature. People are portrayed as rich when in actual fact they are deep in debt. Owing millions is presented as being successful. People who do not need to borrow money are forced to borrow money so that they can say they have a good credit rating. Absurdity is glorified and emptiness, shallowness and hollowness are honoured.

It is only when you think deeply about these things that you realise how fake they actually are. Change comes when you reflect on realities! Major changes are going to take place in your life as you become someone who sits quietly to meditate!

5\. Meditation, contemplation, reflection, deep thinking and going over the same thing several times are KEYS TO RECEIVING INSTRUCTIONS FOR YOUR LIFE.

I went by the field of the slothful, and by the vineyard of the man void of understanding;

And, lo, it was all grown over with thorns, and nettles had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall thereof was broken down.

Then I SAW, AND CONSIDERED IT WELL: I looked upon it, and RECEIVED INSTRUCTION.

Proverbs 24:30-32

Instructions coming from a trusted source are some of the best gifts you can ever have. What shall I do next? Where shall I go? What should I become?

The wise man in Proverbs saw the field, saw the thorns, saw the broken down wall and began thinking. As his mind went over and over the issues, he received instruction.

He knew what God was telling him to do. He knew what God was warning him about. He knew what he had to avoid. There were other passersby who did not think deeply about the broken walls and the neglected fields.

Because they did not think deeply about it, they never received instructions. From today you will begin to receive instructions for your life!

6\. Meditation, contemplation, reflection, deep thinking and going over the same thing several times are KEYS TO LOVING GOD.

O how love I thy law! It is my meditation all the day. Thou through thy commandments hast made me wiser than mine enemies: for they are ever with me.

Psalm 119:97-98

You fall in love with what you think about. O how you will love the law that you meditate on! You will fall in love with books if you meditate on them. Meditating on a book is to go over the book several times.

Reading a book is to go through it once. Meditating on a book is to go over the same book several times. It is only when you go over the book several times that you will receive the wisdom and instructions therein.

If you think about somebody all day long, you are probably in love with the person. You fall in love with God as you think about His word all day long.

The book you have in your hand has much more in it for you.

Without reading it over and over again, you will never glean the wisdom that is contained in it.

7\. Meditation, contemplation, reflection, deep thinking and going over the same thing several times are KEYS TO COMMUNICATION WITH GOD.

Give ear to my words, O LORD, CONSIDER MY MEDITATION.

Psalm 5:1

Meditation is a type of prayer! God listens to your meditations!

God considers the things you meditate about.

Meditation occurs when you think deeply.

God listens to the thoughts of your heart as well as the words of your mouth.

That is why the words of your mouth and the meditation of your heart must be acceptable to God.

There is a realm of meditation which becomes prayer.

Your deep thoughts, your deep contemplation, your deep reflection and your deep thinking become prayers to God.

It is not only the words of your mouth that God listens to. God also listens to the meditation of your heart.

Let the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be acceptable in thy sight, O LORD, my strength, and my redeemer.

Psalm 19:14
