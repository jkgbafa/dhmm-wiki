### CHAPTER 17\

Wisdom is the Secret to Catching the Anointing\
====================================================================

Now when Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judaea in the days of Herod the king, behold, THERE CAME WISE MEN FROM THE EAST TO JERUSALEM, SAYING, WHERE IS HE THAT IS BORN KING OF THE JEWS? for we have seen his star in the east, and are come to worship him. When Herod the king had heard these things, he was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him.

Matthew 2:1-3

The wise men from the east came to Jerusalem, looking for the saviour. Their wisdom made them seek the saviour. The whole world remembers these wise men because they looked for Jesus. Wisdom will make you find the anointed person and his anointing.

It is a supreme act of wisdom to seek for the anointing. God recognizes the journey you make when you seek for the anointing.

One day, I decided to go and wait upon the Lord. To do that, I had to travel to the wilderness. As I was preparing to go, the Holy Spirit began to speak to me, giving me direction on what to do in the future. On another occasion I was also travelling to wait on the Lord somewhere. I noticed how I began to receive supernatural direction before I arrived at my destination. Then I realised that my journeys to go and wait on the Lord were recognized by the Holy Spirit. Even the decision to go and pray was a prayer! Each journey was noted by the Lord and respected by the Holy Spirit.

You will notice how Jesus recommended the Queen of the South for coming all the way to hear the wisdom of Solomon. This queen travelled from the uttermost parts of the earth just to hear the wisdom of Solomon. Indeed, your travels are recognized by God as an act of wisdom.

The queen of the south shall rise up in the judgment with this generation, and shall condemn it: for she came from the uttermost parts of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon; and, behold, a greater than Solomon is here.

Matthew 12:42

Wisdom will cause you to seek for the Saviour. The wisdom of God will make you travel to find God. When you see a man travelling far to find the treasures of the anointing, you are seeing a wise man.

There are people who will not even go to the church in their neighbourhood. Such people may travel far to watch a football game but will not lift a finger for spiritual things.

One day, I asked after some missing church members. I found out that they had travelled to Germany for the weekend to watch a World Cup soccer match.

Wisdom is the art of downloading the invisible things of God into this human world. Through wisdom, the things of God will not remain unreal. God is real! God is in heaven!

Wisdom is the application of God’s word to locate the man of God who will practically help you to know God.

People who are not practical never see God nor experience him.

Now therefore go, and I will be with thy mouth, and teach thee what thou shalt say. And he said, O my Lord, send, I pray thee, by the hand of him whom thou wilt send. And the anger of the Lord was kindled against Moses, and he said, Is not Aaron the Levite thy brother? I know that he can speak well. And also, behold, he cometh forth to meet thee: and when he seeth thee, he will be glad in his heart. And thou shalt speak unto him, and put words in his mouth: and I will be with thy mouth, and with his mouth, and will teach you what ye shall do. AND HE SHALL BE THY SPOKESMAN UNTO THE PEOPLE: AND HE SHALL BE, EVEN HE SHALL BE TO THEE INSTEAD OF A MOUTH, AND THOU SHALT BE TO HIM INSTEAD OF GOD.

Exodus 4:12-16

God has ordained people for you to see, touch and handle. These are the people you will see instead of seeing God Himself.

It is not easy to see God himself. We are so far from God! There is nothing about us that deserves to know God or see God. We are desperate sinners. God is so great and he is in the third heavens. You are on earth now! When are you going to get to the third heavens, where God dwells?

If you do not have the wisdom of seeking the people who can link you up with God, you will never experience God practically.

The wisdom of God turns you into a seeker of the anointing!
