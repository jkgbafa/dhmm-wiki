### CHAPTER 38\

The Wisdom from Above\
===========================================

But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.

James 3:17

The wisdom from above has several pillars. These pillars are well-defined and described by the apostle James. This famous scripture above can be considered as a map of wisdom for your life. If you remember this scripture, you will be remembering what it means to be wise at every point of your life. Any time you are faced with a decision, just quote to yourself all the eight pillars of James 3:17. Say to yourself: it is wise to be pure; it is wise to be peaceable; it is wise to be gentle; it is wise to be easily entreated; it is wise to be full of mercy; it is wise to bear good fruits; it is wise to be impartial and it is wise to be without hypocrisy. These points will act as a map of wisdom to you!

1\. It is wise to be pure.

It is wise to be pure. Purity speaks of continuity. Purity speaks of the absence of evil. The presence of a foreign body contaminates and changes everything. Even a foreign voice contaminates and defiles a person. It is wise to have within what you advertise on the outside.

When you are not pure, there is something wrong with you. When the impurity eventually comes out, everything about you will be set aside, cancelled and over-ridden. You will not be able to function anymore because your impurities have been discovered. Impurities are often small things but they are game changers.

If you add just two drops of ink to a glass of water, the water becomes undrinkable. It is true that the ink is very small but it still makes the water undrinkable.

What small things are there in your life that completely change who you are? They may be little things but they change everything. What is unholy in your life? What is wicked in your life? Get rid of the impurities before they kill you.

2\. It is wise to be peaceful.

And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of them that make peace.

James 3:18

Peace is an important pillar of wisdom. Without peace, you cannot develop. He who seeks for revenge must build two graves\!1 Those who cannot live at peace must prepare for devastation on both sides. There is always a reason to have conflict. God’s wisdom will lead you to walk in the path of peace. The scripture says, the fruit of righteousness is borne in peace by those who love peace.

It is wise to be peaceable. You must determine to have peace in your life. You must seek peace with all men.

3\. It is wise to be gentle.

By little and little I will drive them out from before thee, until thou be increased, and inherit the land.

Exodus 23:30

It is wise to be gentle. Instead of being jumpy, rash, erratic or pushy, receive the wisdom to be gentle and gradual in your approach. Many problems cannot be solved.

Even more importantly, many problems cannot be solved with one strong push or one big bang.

Little by little, you will overcome many huge problems.

Do not push too hard! It is wise to be gentle!

What mountain is there that you are trying to move? The Lord promised to clear your greatest mountains and your greatest troubles gradually.

4\. It is wise to be easily entreated.

It is wise to be easily entreated! What is the use of your mind if it cannot change?

Those who are resistant to change are not wise. They are stubborn!

Stubbornness is connected to demon activity because stubbornness is as iniquity.

Iniquity is the crookedness and the perversity that is found as a result of sin and demonic activity.

Decide to have the wisdom of being easily entreated, easily advised or easily counselled.

5\. It is wise to be full of mercy.

Let not mercy and truth forsake thee: bind them about thy neck; write them upon the table of thine heart:

Proverbs 3:3

It is wise to be full of mercy. Being forgiving is the same as being wise. Unforgiveness is the only sin that God will not forgive you for. It is not wise to hold onto unforgiveness! Most of the time you are guilty of the very things that you accuse others of.

Scripture teaches that it is wise to bind mercy and truth around your neck. One day you will be glad that you were merciful.

Blessed are the merciful for they shall obtain mercy!

6\. It is wise to be of good fruits.

It is wise to be full of good fruits. If you have only one fruit, it is likely to be taken away from you. The more fruits you bear, the more stable you are. Many people are satisfied with the little they have produced. It is a great thing that I have trained a lot of pastors. It is indeed a wise move to train two hundred pastors.

It is a great thing to have raised up a singer. It is wise to have even more singers to perform. More fruit is always a sign of wisdom. The Bible says, children are like arrows in the quiver. The more arrows you have, the more options, the more opportunities and the more reason s you have to be successful.

It is wise to be fruitful because Jesus told us clearly that the Father is glorified when we bear much fruit. It is wise to follow Jesus. It is wise to obey the Lord.

7\. It is wise to be without partiality.

It is wise to be impartial. Some people are openly partial. Being partial is to lay a foundation for disloyalty. People are very concerned about fair treatment. One day I invited a brother to be in full time ministry. He said to me, “I would be interested in working full time if I will be treated fairly.”

Human beings are often full of partiality, favouritism and nepotism. Where there is partiality, you do not advance or improve based on merit.

You are neglected because you do not fall into the right group.

This is very offensive to those who fall into the wrong group.

A lot of disloyal reactions are based on partiality.

A good leader must be the master of playing a fair game. It is wise to be impartial. It is wise to demonstrate to people that you are indeed treating everybody fairly.

My mother went to great lengths to demonstrate to me that she had treated all her children fairly. She kept a record of everything that she did for us and showed it to all of us.

She wanted everyone to see that no one had been treated better than the other.

Although I found it unnecessary, I realised later that experience was making her show us all that we had all been treated the same.

It is wise to be without partiality.

8\. It is wise to be without hypocrisy

BUT WOE UNTO YOU, SCRIBES AND PHARISEES, HYPOCRITES! for ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men: for ye neither go in yourselves, neither suffer ye them that are entering to go in.

Matthew 23:13

It is wise to be without hypocrisy. Hypocrisy is when you act the part that you are really not. It is very difficult to keep a secret forever. Jesus said everything that is hidden will be spoken on the rooftops. It is therefore wise to live as far from hypocrisy, pretence, duplicity and duality as possible.

I have found that those who put their trust in a life of hypocrisy are eventually totally devastated when the truth about their lives is exposed.

Indeed, Christians have been known to go for years, living shockingly dual lives. These people have their trust in something that will not survive the test of time. It is not a wise thing to be a hypocrite, a pretender or an actor.

Acting is hard work. That is why actors are paid so much.

Christian hypocrites have to pretend all through their lives. Christian hypocrites have to lead everyone to think that they are something that they are not. How difficult and laborious it is to live two lives!
