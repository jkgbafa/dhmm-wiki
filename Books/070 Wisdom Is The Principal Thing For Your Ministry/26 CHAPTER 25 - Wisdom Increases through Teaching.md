### CHAPTER 25\

Wisdom Increases through Teaching\
=======================================================

GIVE INSTRUCTION TO A WISE MAN, AND HE WILL BE YET WISER: teach a just man, and he will increase in learning.

Proverbs 9:9

Every time you listen to teaching from the word of God, your wisdom increases. The teaching of the word of God is one of the greatest sources of wisdom. Every teacher I have exposed myself to has caused me to increase in wisdom! It is a great mistake to not expose yourself to teaching of the Word. Giving instructions to a wise person will make him even wiser.

How do you expose yourself to teaching? How do you expose yourself to instruction? You expose yourself to teaching by listening to preaching and teaching videos.

You expose yourself to teaching by listening to preaching and teaching audio messages.

You expose yourself to teaching by reading certain kinds of books.

You expose yourself to teaching by attending church services where the word of God is taught properly. Every time you hear the teaching of the word of God you are increasing in wisdom.

You expose yourself to teaching by attending conferences where the word of God is taught. There is a great teacher called Kenneth Hagin. Exposing myself to his teaching over the years made me wiser in ministry. It is wise to hear the call of God and to follow it diligently.

The prophetic teaching of Kenneth Hagin has made me conscious of how real Jesus is. My wisdom levels were raised greatly through my addiction to the teachings of Kenneth Hagin. I can still hear the voice of Kenneth Hagin saying, “A minister must bend over backwards to be honest.”

In other words, you must do whatever it takes to be an honest, truthful and trustworthy person. I remember that phrase because he was talking about how he had been invited to preach and his host had cheated him out of his offering. His host had made certain promises and completely cheated him when he realised that people appreciated Kenneth Hagin’s ministry and gave very generously.

Exposing myself to the teaching of Rick Joyner has given me the wisdom to prepare for eternity and eternal judgment. His teachings have livened up my spirit and made me wiser in my preparation for eternity. Although I was well hooked onto the wisdom that I received through Kenneth Hagin, reading from Rick Joyner made me even wiser.

Every time you expose yourself to teaching, you become wiser. I remember many words I read from Rick Joyner’s book, “The Final Quest.1” I remember how Rick Joyner met different ministers who had died and experienced judgment. How can I forget the things they shared about judgment in heaven?

The teachings and writings of Rick Joyner made me wiser concerning eternal judgment. In the visions and teachings, he described his encounter with various dead ministers. Speaking to dead people and listening to their experiences at the judgment throne can only make you wiser.

A dead Christian he met in the outer darkness of heaven said, “We knew the Lord and we trusted the cross for His salvation, but we lived for ourselves more than we really lived for him.” He continued, “I am in the lowest rank of heaven. There is no greater folly than to know the great salvation of God but to then go on living for yourself. To come here and learn the reality of that is a grief beyond what an earthly soul can experience. We are those in the outer darkness because of this great folly.”1

2A dead pastor he met in heaven said to him, “I had been given the grace to be a spiritual father but I was a very poor one. All children are rebellious. They are all self-centred and think the world revolves around them. Almost every child will at times bring reproach on his family, but he is still part of the family. I turned away many of God’s own children, precious people he had entrusted to me so they could be brought to maturity. I failed with many of those who stayed with me. Most of them suffered terrible and unnecessary wounds of failure that I could have helped them avoid.”

He said, “I built a large organisation but the greatest gifts were the people who were sent to me for discipling. But I rejected them. Had I not been so self-centred and concerned with my own reputation, I would be a king here.”

Another pastor he met in heaven told him about his marriage. The pastor described his marriage and said, “Everyone thought we had a good marriage but I did not love my wife at all. Neither did she love me. In fact, after just a few years of marriage, we did not even like each other. But we both needed each other so we found a way to work together. Our marriage was not a yoke of love but of bondage. The more successful we became, the more unhappy we became, and the more deception we used to fool those who followed us. We were empty wretches by the end of our lives.”

Yet another pastor Rick Joyner met in heaven spoke about how he began to touch the Lord’s anointed and to do the prophets harm.

The minister confessed to him, “I incited people to investigate other ministers and do my dirty work. I had them scour the earth to find any error or sin in the lives of others so they could be exposed. I became the worst thing that a man could become on the earth – a stumbling block that produced other stumbling blocks. We sowed fear and division throughout the church, all in the name of protecting the truth. In my self-righteousness, I was headed for perdition.”

The man continued and told him, “In a great mercy of the Lord, the Lord allowed me to be struck by a disease that would bring about a slow and humiliating death. Just before I died, I came to my senses and repented. I am thankful to be here at all.” 2

This testimony which was a teaching by Rick Joyner, would make anyone wise enough to avoid attacking the Lord’s anointed and seeking to destroy other ministers. It also shows how the evil of becoming a stumbling block in the church will be judged.

Another dead pastor that Rick Joyner met in heaven told him, “I am sorry that I did not serve you better. I was a shallow person and my writings were shallow, filled with more earthly wisdom than divine truth. When I beheld the Lord here, I wanted to grind my writings into powder, just as Moses did with the golden calf.”

He said to him, “Study to show yourself approved unto God, not unto men. Do not follow men.”

Reading the prophetic teachings of Rick Joyner in his books, “The Final Quest”, “The Call”, “The Torch and the Sword”, have been amazing sources of wisdom for my preparation for eternity.

Another teacher who has helped me to increase in wisdom is Derek Prince. When I exposed myself to the teachings of Derek Prince, I became even wiser. Derek Prince taught on the wisdom of avoiding curses and inducing blessings. I always hear his voice ringing in my ears and saying, “If you do not honour your father, it will never be well with you and you will not live long.” As a younger man, I always remembered these words and it made me much wiser in relation to my biological father and my spiritual fathers.

Indeed, teachings of the word of God will make you wiser. Who are the teachers of your life? If you want your wisdom levels to increase, expose yourself to more and more teachings.
