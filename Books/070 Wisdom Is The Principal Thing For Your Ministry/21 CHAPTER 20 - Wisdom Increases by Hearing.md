### CHAPTER 20\

Wisdom Increases by Hearing\
=================================================

A wise man will hear, and will increase learning; and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels:

Proverbs 1:5

Wisdom comes to you by hearing. The more you listen to messages the wiser you will become. The more you listen to fathers the wiser you will become. The more you listen to pastors the wiser you will become. As you listen to preaching, you will become wiser.

When you listen to a wise person speaking you receive an impartation of wisdom.

Five People Who Will Increase Your Wisdom

1\. Your wisdom will increase when you hear from God.

And it shall come to pass, IF THOU SHALT HEARKEN DILIGENTLY UNTO THE VOICE OF THE LORD THY GOD, to observe and to do all his commandments which I command thee this day, that the Lord thy God will set thee on high above all nations of the earth:

Deuteronomy 28:1

A wise man will hear and increase learning! It is important to wait on the Lord and hear from God directly. A person who never hears from God will never have a certain level of wisdom. As you hear from God directly, your wisdom increases and you get promoted because of your wisdom.

2\. Your wisdom will increase when you hear from your prophet.

NOW THEREFORE HEARKEN, O ISRAEL, UNTO THE STATUTES AND UNTO THE JUDGMENTS, WHICH I TEACH YOU, for to do them, that ye may live, and go in and possess the land which the Lord God of your fathers giveth you. Ye shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall ye diminish ought from it, that ye may keep the commandments of the LORD your God which I command you.

Your eyes have seen what the Lord did because of Baalpeor: for all the men that followed Baalpeor, the Lord thy God hath destroyed them from among you. But ye that did cleave unto the Lord your God are alive every one of you this day. BEHOLD, I HAVE TAUGHT YOU STATUTES AND JUDGMENTS, EVEN AS THE LORD MY GOD COMMANDED ME, THAT YE SHOULD DO SO IN THE LAND WHITHER YE GO TO POSSESS IT. KEEP THEREFORE AND DO THEM; FOR THIS IS YOUR WISDOM AND YOUR UNDERSTANDING IN THE SIGHT OF THE NATIONS, which shall hear all these statutes, and say, surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people.

Deuteronomy 4:1-6

A wise man will hear and increase learning! It is important to hear from your prophet!

Great wisdom is going to come into your life as you allow God to bless you through your apostle and prophet.

Moses gave the Israelites their laws and the commandments. He told them “These statutes and laws are your wisdom.” In other words, the more they listened to his words, the more they would have wisdom from God.

3\. Your wisdom will increase when you hear from your father.

My son, if thou wilt receive my words, and hide my commandments with thee; So that thou incline thine ear unto wisdom, and apply thine heart to understanding.

Proverbs 2:1-2

My son, forget not my law; but let thine heart keep my commandments:

Proverbs 3:1

Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding.

Proverbs 4:1

My son, attend unto my wisdom, and bow thine ear to my understanding:

Proverbs 5:1

A wise man will hear and increase learning! Many children do not hear the wisdom of their fathers. A rebellious child will not have the opportunity to sit down with his father and hear him speak. Because disobedient, rebellious and dishonourable children do not ever have conversations with their fathers, they rarely hear from them. They rarely hear the wisdom that their fathers have to offer.

I once met an old man who had a large family. Indeed, this man had several rebellious children who would make fun of their father, as though he were a buffoon. As I got to know this man, I found out first hand how great and wise he was.

Every time I attended a function where the old man was to speak, I noticed the disdain with which his children referred to him and his speeches. As I listened quietly, I noticed that the old man was full of wisdom but his children did not hear him nor did they receive his wisdom.

Over the years, I realised that none of his children have attained to the level of financial stability that their father had. It is important to increase in wisdom by hearing your father.

4\. Your wisdom will increase when you hear from your mother.

My son, keep thy father’s commandment, and forsake not THE LAW OF THY MOTHER: Bind them continually upon thine heart, and tie them about thy neck. When thou goest, it shall lead thee; when thou sleepest, it shall keep thee; and when thou awakest, it shall talk with thee.

Proverbs 6:20-22

A wise man will hear and increase learning! It is also important to listen to your mother. There is a difference between mothers and fathers. The wisdom that comes from a father may be very different from the wisdom that comes from a mother. Somehow, both mothers and fathers contribute greatly to your wisdom.

5\. Your wisdom will increase when you hear from your pastor.

And I will give you pastors according to mine heart, which shall feed you with knowledge and understanding.

Jeremiah 3:15

A wise man will hear and increase learning! There is a great promise for those who hear their pastors. God promises to give us pastors who will feed us with knowledge and understanding. God will feed you and make you fat through your pastor.
