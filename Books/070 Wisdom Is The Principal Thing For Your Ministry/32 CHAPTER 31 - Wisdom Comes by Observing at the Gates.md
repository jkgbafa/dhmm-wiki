### CHAPTER 31\

Wisdom Comes by Observing at the Gates\
============================================================

DOTH NOT WISDOM CRY? and understanding put forth her voice? She standeth in the top of high places, by the way in the places of the paths. SHE CRIETH AT THE GATES, AT THE ENTRY OF THE CITY, AT THE COMING IN AT THE DOORS. Unto you, O men, I call; and my voice is to the sons of man.

Proverbs 8:1-4

The gate of a city is the entrance of a city. Every time you arrive in a new country you are likely to receive wisdom at the very entrance. On many occasions, I have arrived at international airports and received great wisdom. Open your heart and be ready to receive wisdom from God, every time you enter a new place.

At the gates of Holland in the city of Amsterdam, I received wisdom many times. Looking out of the window of the KLM flight arriving early in the morning, I saw well-arranged farms in rectangles and squares.

I noticed that the farmlands extended right up to the perimeter of the airport. Not an inch of ground was spared or wasted. I compared this with the fields in many African countries where you just see extensive areas of bush lands and savannah.

I received wisdom! Poverty-stricken African nations waste their land and leave them uncultivated. The rich nations of the world have not wasted even one square metre of their land; even land around their high-tension electric cables was cultivated.

The neatly arranged squares and rectangles are so clearly marked out that the entire land is used up to produce something useful. Waste not! Want not!

It is easy to complain that you were colonised and cheated. But years and years after independence from colonizing slave traders, these patterns of waste, lack of productivity, lack of leadership, lack of ideas and the lack of frugality continue to stare at you in almost every African nation.

At the Amsterdam airport, I would walk through and find them constantly building something. I hardly went there without finding them re-building something they were already using.

I took note of how a certain kind of person is always developing, building and re-modelling existing structures to make them more relevant, more usable and always fresh.

Why are some people never building anything?

Why are some people never re-developing?

What is the problem?

Is it just the lack of resources?

Is it just the lack of money?

Certainly not! It is the lack of practical men. It is the lack of people who will roll up their sleeves and get to the job. It is the lack of people who are hands-on workers.

In 1996 I arrived at Johannesburg, the gate of South Africa, and marvelled at the similarity that existed between that airport and many other European airports. I wondered, “Is this Africa?”

I compared this airport with other African nations that I had visited. In many African countries, the airport can be compared to a three-bedroom estate house.

At the gates of these nations, you get an impression of the kinds of people that rule and lead the nation. One type of person will cause the entire nation to be poor and undeveloped. Another type will cause riches, development, beauty and modernity to prevail.

At the gates of these African nations and at the gate of Johannesburg, I learnt that it is not being out of Africa that makes you develop and prosper. It is not being located in a particular place that determines your future.

There is a kind of impractical leader who destroys the nation he leads by his many speeches and theoretical propositions. Such impractical leaders are unable to actually create, build and move the nation forward.

Anyone who always complains of a lack of money is impractical. There is always a shortage of money. As I write this book, I have many things that I need. Just like everybody else, I wish I had more money.

It is time to stop complaining about what you do not have and find a way of using what you do have, to create what you need.

One day, I arrived at the gate of a great nation. I understood immediately why this great nation, which should have been one of the richest nations in the world, was actually one of the poorest. It was as though there was no government, no order and no law.

This nation’s border was characterized by confusion and disorder. There were several barriers at the border. A barrier consisted of two barrels linked with a stick to form a barricade. There was a man or a group of men standing by each barrel, demanding different things. Some would demand passports. Some would demand to see a drivers’ licences. Some would accuse us of having foreign currency and demand that we open our wallets for inspection.

At other barrels, some would demand that we take out our luggage so that they rummage through it, at will. None of the men at this border post wore any uniform. They wore rubber slippers and shorts. Some were dressed as though they had just come out of the bathroom.

To our amazement, we spent several hours at this border post and had to pay all sorts of unauthorized monies in order to get by each barrel. Each barrel was a world on its own and each barrel seemed as determined to make as much money from us as possible before allowing us to move to the next barrel.

If the border of that nation was a reflection of the inner workings of that nation, then you could see why a nation that could have been rich was actually very poor. The absence of a good leader leads to the absence of safety, security, order and stability. I did not need to drive any further into that country to be able to tell that this nation would be in great difficulty. There is a lot of wisdom to discover at the gates of a city.

You will receive a lot of wisdom at the gates of every nation and at the coming in at the doors. It is important to learn from these gates! God gives us these gates and entries of cities, so that He can increase our wisdom and cause us to learn from what we see and experience.
