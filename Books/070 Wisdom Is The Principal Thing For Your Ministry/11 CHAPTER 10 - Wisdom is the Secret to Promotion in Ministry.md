### CHAPTER 10\

Wisdom is the Secret to Promotion in Ministry\
===================================================================

Exalt her, and she shall promote thee: she shall bring thee to honour, when thou dost embrace her. She shall give to thine head an ornament of grace: a crown of glory shall she deliver to thee.

Proverbs 4:8-9

Your wisdom is the way you think. The way you think makes you act in a certain way. Your wisdom makes you think in a certain way. It is the wisdom that God gives you that causes you to be promoted over and over again. You will be promoted in the ministry through wisdom! Pray every day for the Spirit of revelation and wisdom! Through the Spirit of wisdom, you will be highly promoted in the ministry. Instead of being a nobody, you will become a “somebody”. Instead of being rejected, your ministry will be accepted and received by many people. Promotion will come to you from the Lord when you walk in this wisdom!

NOW THEREFORE LET PHARAOH LOOK OUT A MAN DISCREET AND WISE, AND SET HIM OVER THE LAND OF EGYPT. Let Pharaoh do this, and let him appoint officers over the land, and take up the fifth part of the land of Egypt in the seven plenteous years. And let them gather all the food of those good years that come, and lay up corn under the hand of Pharaoh, and let them keep food in the cities. And that food shall be for store to the land against the seven years of famine, which shall be in the land of Egypt; that the land perish not through the famine. And the thing was good in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of all his servants. And Pharaoh said unto his servants, Can we find such a one as this is, a man in whom the Spirit of God is? AND PHARAOH SAID UNTO JOSEPH, FORASMUCH AS GOD HATH SHEWED THEE ALL THIS, THERE IS NONE SO DISCREET AND WISE AS THOU ART: THOU SHALT BE OVER MY HOUSE, AND ACCORDING UNTO THY WORD SHALL ALL MY PEOPLE BE RULED: ONLY IN THE THRONE WILL I BE GREATER THAN THOU.

And Pharaoh said unto Joseph, See, I have set thee over all the land of Egypt. And Pharaoh took off his ring from his hand, and put it upon Joseph’s hand, and arrayed him in vestures of fine linen, and put a gold chain about his neck; And he made him to ride in the second chariot which he had; and they cried before him, Bow the knee: and he made him ruler over all the land of Egypt. And Pharaoh said unto Joseph, I am Pharaoh, and without thee shall no man lift up his hand or foot in all the land of Egypt.

Genesis 41:33-44

Wisdom is the primary gift given by God to make you a highly promoted ministry.

Let the scripture tell you what the best thing to do is. Do what this scripture teaches and you will be promoted. Believe in the wisdom of God! Do not trust your own ideas! Do not believe in devilish wisdom! Do not believe in old wives’ fables! Do not live by traditional proverbs and wise sayings. Follow what the Word says is “wisdom”.

But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.

James 3:17

1\. Do what is pure, chaste, uncorrupted and unpolluted and you will be highly promoted.

Doing something impure and corrupted will only take you deeper into trouble.

2\. Do what is peaceful, non-aggressive, calm, non-violent and you will be highly promoted.

Being aggressive and violent will only take you deeper into crisis.

3\. Be gentle, mild, kind, tender and moderate and you will be highly promoted.

Rushing into things without duly thinking about the implications will get you into more crises.

4\. Be easy to be entreated, easy to plead with, easy to beg and easy to implore, easy to change your mind and you will be highly promoted.

Being stubborn will make your crises worse. So will having long meetings to explain simple things because you are difficult to reason with.

5\. Be merciful, forgiving, gracious, lenient and generous and you will be highly promoted.

6\. Take a decision to bear much fruit, produce a good harvest, have great results and you will be highly promoted.

When you bear much fruit, you end up being promoted. Planting many churches, winning many souls, writing many books, following up many converts and counselling many people, are ways to bear much fruit. Being filled with good fruits is wisdom and it will always cause you to be promoted. Instead of conniving and scheming to be known and favoured by the boss, you must fight to bear much fruit. Bearing fruit will always lead to your promotion.

7\. Be fair, impartial, unbiased, objective, open-minded, reasonable and you will be highly promoted.

Being fair and impartial will cause many people to appreciate your leadership. They will respect your judgment and will acknowledge that you are just and true. People resent being treated unfairly. Many people will desert you because you do not treat them objectively and reasonably.

Your promotion will not come on because you have not been fair and unbiased. People who are tribalistic, race-conscious and colour-conscious do not get promoted. They end up being leaders of little groups that have no connection with the larger community. Pastors of churches of one ethnic group or one colour are usually pastors of small churches. They never dominate cities or nations. Learn to be fair and impartial in your dealings with people.

8\. Be without hypocrisy and you will be highly promoted.

Be without pretence!

Be without insincerity and two-facedness!

Two-faced people are discovered after a while.

Two-faced people are hated for their deception. They are hated for their pretence.

A hypocrite is a traitor! A hypocrite can become a Judas! Traitors are not loved! Traitors are eventually rejected by both sides. Get out of pretence, deception and hypocrisy! It is not a wise state to be in. Through your openness and sincerity, you will be liked by many people and it will lead to your promotion.

Open rebuke is better than secret love.

Proverbs 27:5

Open rebuke is better than secret love. An open rebuke demonstrates more love to a friend than a secret love which withholds rebuke when it should be given.
