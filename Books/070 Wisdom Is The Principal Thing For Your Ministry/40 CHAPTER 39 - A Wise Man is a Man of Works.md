### CHAPTER 39\

A Wise Man is a Man of Works\
==================================================

WHO IS A WISE MAN and endued with knowledge among you? LET HIM SHEW OUT OF A GOOD CONVERSATION HIS WORKS with meekness of wisdom.

James 3:13

Who is a wise man? Do you know any wise man? Do you know anyone who is actually wise in God’s sight? It is important to recognize wisdom when you see it. Wisdom is recognized by its works. The scripture above asks the question, “Who is a wise man?” it answers the question immediately and says a wise man should prove that he is a wise man, by his good works.

“If you are wise and understand God’s ways, prove it by living an honourable life, doing good works with the humility that comes from wisdom” (James 3:13 NLT).

Do you know how to identify a wise man? When Jesus was on the earth, He was marked out by His works. His works revealed that He was a wise man.

“And when the sabbath day was come, he began to teach in the synagogue: and many hearing him were astonished, saying, From whence hath this man these things? and WHAT WISDOM is this which is given unto him, that even such MIGHTY WORKS are wrought by his hands?” (Mark 6:2)

If you study the outcome of many elections, you will realise that people do not necessarily choose wise men to rule a nation.

People often choose handsome men, tribesmen, friendly men, sociable men, affable men, approachable men, pleasant men, outgoing men, rich men, welcoming men, hospitable men, generous men instead of choosing a wise man. Often, a man of great works is not a man of many words.

A wise man must be identified by his works. What have you built? What have you achieved? What have you accomplished? Show us the practical works you have done and we will choose you!

I once saw a man teaching wisdom, success and prosperity on a television. He had many followers and admirers. Many were impressed with his choice of English words and his quotations from Encyclopaedia Britannica. Many followed him to learn the wisdom for this life.

However, in real life, this man was a failure in business. His failed businesses were up for sale and he was unable to fulfil his obligations. I wondered how someone who could not run a business could teach life’s skills and share success keys.

Yet, the world is filled with many such people: people who have words without works. Our world is filled with people who have “word salads” to offer but nothing substantial to show as fruits of their wisdom. A man of wisdom is a man of works!

1\. SOLOMON: A WISE MAN OF WORKS:

Behold, I have done according to thy words: lo, I have given thee a wise and an understanding heart; so that there was none like thee before thee, neither after thee shall any arise like unto thee.

1 Kings 3:12

The wisdom of Solomon made him the richest man in the world.

The wisdom of Solomon made him expand the kingdom of Israel in great stability and wealth.

The wisdom of God caused Solomon to write three of the books of the Old Testament: Proverbs, Ecclesiastes and the Songs of Solomon.

The wisdom of God caused Solomon to build the temple, build his house and build the kingdom of Israel. Through the wisdom of God, he reigned extensively in stability and prosperity. Solomon did not reign so extensively because he was tall and handsome. Certainly not! Solomon was not a successful ruler because he was from a particular tribe. Solomon was successful because of the wisdom God gave him.

Solomon did not stand for elections. He did not need to be popular to rule Israel. He was a wise man and his wisdom created wealth and riches. It was the wisdom of Solomon that created Solomon’s temple. It was not Solomon’s friendliness that enabled him to build the temple of God. It was not the fact that Solomon was open, approachable or handsome that enabled him to do his great works.

2\. DAVID: A WISE MAN OF WORKS:

To fetch about this form of speech hath thy servant Joab done this thing: and MY LORD IS WISE, ACCORDING TO THE WISDOM OF AN ANGEL OF GOD, to know all things that are in the earth.

2 Samuel 14:20

The wisdom of David made him the greatest king of Israel. David died full of age, riches and honour. Today, the star of David is displayed in the Israeli flag.

David’s achievements as a king who conquered vast regions for Israel is well known.

Do you think that David conquered vast territories because he was handsome or approachable? Do you think David was the most successful king of Israel because he was friendly and polite?

Do you think David was so successful because he was a good orator? Certainly not!

3\. JOSEPH: A WISE MAN OF WORKS:

NOW THEREFORE LET PHARAOH LOOK OUT A MAN DISCREET AND WISE, AND SET HIM OVER THE LAND OF EGYPT.

Let Pharaoh do this, and let him appoint officers over the land, and take up the fifth part of the land of Egypt in the seven plenteous years.

And let them gather all the food of those good years that come, and lay up corn under the hand of Pharaoh, and let them keep food in the cities.

And that food shall be for store to the land against the seven years of famine, which shall be in the land of Egypt; that the land perish not through the famine.

And the thing was good in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of all his servants.

And Pharaoh said unto his servants, Can we find such a one as this is, a man in whom the Spirit of God is?

And Pharaoh said unto Joseph, Forasmuch as God hath shewed thee all this, THERE IS NONE SO DISCREET AND WISE AS THOU ART:

Genesis 41:33-39

The wisdom of Joseph made him the Prime Minister of the greatest nation of his world.

Joseph bought the whole land of Egypt for Pharaoh and made every citizen of the country pay taxes to the king.

Joseph rescued the nation of Egypt from famine and from an economic holocaust.

Joseph fed the nations all around and supplied all the needs of his hungry population. Joseph did not feed the nations because he was handsome, friendly, sociable, affable, approachable, pleasant, outgoing, rich, welcoming, hospitable and generous.

Joseph was a wise man. He was a really wise man and his works proved it. The biblical way to identify a wise man is by his works!

4\. DANIEL: A WISE MAN OF WORKS:

THERE IS A MAN IN THY KINGDOM, IN WHOM IS THE SPIRIT OF THE HOLY GODS; AND IN THE DAYS OF THY FATHER LIGHT AND UNDERSTANDING AND WISDOM, LIKE THE WISDOM OF THE GODS, WAS FOUND IN HIM; whom the king Nebuchadnezzar thy father, the king, I say, thy father, made master of the magicians, astrologers, Chaldeans, and soothsayers;

Forasmuch as an excellent spirit, and knowledge, and understanding, interpreting of dreams, and shewing of hard sentences, and dissolving of doubts, were found in the same Daniel, whom the king named Belteshazzar: now let Daniel be called, and he will shew the interpretation.

Then was Daniel brought in before the king. And the king spake and said unto Daniel, Art thou that Daniel, which art of the children of the captivity of Judah, whom the king my father brought out of Jewry?

I HAVE EVEN HEARD OF THEE, THAT THE SPIRIT OF THE GODS IS IN THEE, AND THAT LIGHT AND UNDERSTANDING AND EXCELLENT WISDOM IS FOUND IN THEE.

And now the wise men, the astrologers, have been brought in before me, that they should read this writing, and make known unto me the interpretation thereof: but they could not shew the interpretation of the thing:

Daniel 5:11-15

The wisdom of Daniel made him a national hero. Daniel was filled with excellent wisdom, understanding and counsel. Daniel was promoted and served as Prime Minister in three different regimes. He served under Nebuchadnezzar. He served under Belshazzar. And he served under Darius!

The book of Daniel, testifies of the great works which Daniel accomplished. From the account in the book of Daniel, we see the work of Daniel and the great things he accomplished. This qualifies him to join the list of wise men.

Daniel was not just handsome, friendly, sociable, affable, approachable, pleasant, outgoing, rich, welcoming, hospitable and generous. Daniel was a wise man. He was a really wise man and his works prove it.

Stop choosing people because they are friendly, tall or short. Choose them because they are wise men who have proved their wisdom with their works.

Conclusion

To the making of many books there is no end. It is my prayer that the wisdom of God about which I have written in these few pages will be a blessing to you, your family and your ministry!
