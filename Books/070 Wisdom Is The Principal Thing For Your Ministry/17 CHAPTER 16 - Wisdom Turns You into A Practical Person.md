### CHAPTER 16\

Wisdom Turns You into A Practical Person\
==============================================================

Then shall the kingdom of heaven be likened unto ten virgins, which took their lamps, and went forth to meet the bridegroom. And FIVE OF THEM WERE WISE, and five were foolish. They that were foolish took their lamps, and took no oil with them:

Matthew 25:1-3

The story of the five wise virgins and five foolish virgins is the story of how some people applied practical wisdom in their lives and ended up achieving their aims. The word “Wisdom” is the word “Phronimos” and it speaks of being practical and hands-on.

Wisdom is the capacity to be practical and down to earth. The five virgins who did not make it to the wedding were those who were not practical enough to buy extra oil.

Some people consider themselves above everyday, down-to-earth activities. If you want to walk in the wisdom of God, you must become an everyday down-to-earth person. If you read the bible, you will find a lot of down to earth instructions. There is no practical aspect of life that God has left out of the bible. You have God’s counsel for every aspect of your life; from the day you are born till the day you die.

Wisdom is the capacity to be practical. Today, many leaders sit in their castles, not knowing what is actually happening on the ground. You can be a practical person even though you are the head of a large organisation.

I remember a government minister in a certain country who had oversight of the nation’s harbour. One day, his country was visited by a delegation from the World Bank. The delegation from the World Bank wanted to see the harbour. When the World Bank delegates arrived with him at the harbour, the government minister blurted out, “This is the first time I am seeing the harbour”! Even though he had been in charge of this very important national asset, he had not bothered to go and see what was happening there. He had been the minister in charge of the harbour for eight years! Such leaders are not practical hands-on men. When they are in charge, you can expect things to fall apart. They only receive invoices and bills of expenses that they do not understand. They approve everything because they do not know what is false.

They sign out millions of dollars because they do not know that what they are asking for can be done with a few thousands.

Wisdom is the application of practical steps to your calling in such a way that you fulfil your ministry. Wisdom is the ability to be a practical person.

Many people have failed in their ministry because they did not develop in the wisdom of being practical.

I remember four different men of God whom I interacted with. I gave each of them a piece of advice. I gave them advice on how to live their lives and on how to avoid evil. I sensed that satan had planned to sift them like wheat, and each piece of advice was uniquely designed to practically deliver them from falling headlong into satan’s hands. I wished all of the four of them had listened to me; but that was not the case. Two of them heeded the practical advice that I gave to them; but the other two despised me.

Two of them gave me feedback that the steps I had given them had practically saved their lives. On the other hand, the two other pastors did not take heed to my advice. In fact, both of them went on to ridicule me. One of them was so incensed at my advice that he called a meeting of his friends and spoke angrily about me. The other pastor also called some friends and ridiculed me because of the advice I had given him.

Some years later, I heard of their destruction and I marvelled to myself at how people do not believe in practical wisdom. Indeed, many things in the ministry require practical steps that must be followed. The foolish virgins were simply not practical enough to buy the extra oil they needed to make it to the end.

The reason why people do not fulfil the Great Commission is because they are not practical. God may have told you to go out into the world to preach the gospel; but you will need to do practical things if you are to fulfil this holy calling. No one has achieved great things in the ministry without practical wisdom.

Many years ago I learnt some practical things from Billy Graham. I read a book in which he described how their crusades were conducted. He described how he would send teams ahead into the cities where he would be holding crusades. Sometimes, he would let preparatory teams live for two years in the city where they were planning to have a crusade. Sometimes the crusade preparatory crusade teams would spend several months in preparation. I realised that Billy Graham had a very practical approach to preparing for a crusade. Billy Graham did not just arrive at a crusade ground and expect the whole world to appear at the stadium. Today, many men of God think that they can just arrive in a city and everyone would be interested in them.

I also learnt from Oral Roberts about the need to have partners and supporters for crusades. He spoke about how it was necessary to constantly raise money to preach the gospel. He said he wished that it were not necessary to constantly raise money to hold crusades. I learnt how he kept a close relationship with his partners and board members who helped him to have crusades. These great men had a very practical side to their ministries.
