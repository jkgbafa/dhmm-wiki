### CHAPTER 15\

Wisdom Is the Secret to Creativity in the Ministry\
========================================================================

The Lord by wisdom hath founded the earth; by understanding hath he established the heavens.

Proverbs 3:19

O Lord, how manifold are thy works! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.

Psalm 104:24

I wisdom dwell with prudence, and find out knowledge of witty inventions.

Proverbs 8:12

The wisdom of God makes you a creative person. It is the secret that will stir up your creativity. Allow the wisdom of God to seep into you. Wisdom makes you create new things. Wisdom is what stimulates creativity, inventions, innovations and the new things you need for your ministry to move forward in leaps and bounds. Wisdom is the master key to becoming a creator of new things and new ideas for ministry and work.

Seven Ways to Walk in Creative Wisdom

1\. WALK CLOSER TO GOD and you will become more creative and wise.

In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.

Genesis 1:1

God is the creator! Every time you become more creative you are closer to God. Walking closer with God will cause you to be more creative. The wisdom of creativity causes you to walk closer to God. God is the creator of heaven and earth. Whenever you walk in the spirit of creativity, you walk closer to God.

2\. WELCOME NEW IDEAS and you will become more creative and wise.

Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert.

Isaiah 43:19

The wisdom of creativity will cause you to welcome new ideas into your life and ministry. There are new ideas that will make a difference in your life and ministry.

3\. TRY NEW THINGS and you will become more creative and wise.

The wisdom of creativity will cause you to try new things in your life and ministry.

Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert.

Isaiah 43:19

4\. WELCOME NEW PEOPLE and you will become more creative and wise.

The wisdom of creativity will cause you to welcome new people into your world.

In that he saith, A new covenant, he hath made the first old. Now that which decayeth and waxeth old is ready to vanish away.

Hebrews 8:13

5\. GO OUT INTO A NEW WORLD and you will become more creative and wise.

The Holy Spirit will lead you to go out into the world. You know only a small part of the world. The wisdom of creativity will cause you to go out into the world that is new to you in fulfilment of the Great Commission. The Holy Spirit is a Spirit of creativity and He always leads us to do new things.

Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.

Matthew 28:19-20

6\. GIVE NEW NAMES TO PEOPLE and you will be more creative and wise. The wisdom of creativity will cause you to give new names to people.

And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.

Matthew 16:18

Giving names to people gives them a new life. A new name creates a new personality. Old things are passed away. The person with the new name begins to take on the character of the name that is given to them. Peter took on a new character. His old name, Simon, signified someone who was unstable and weak. His new name, Peter, spoke of stability of a rock.

And out of the ground the LORD God formed every beast of the field, and every fowl of the air; and brought them unto Adam to see what he would call them: and whatsoever Adam called every living creature, that was the name thereof. And Adam gave names to all cattle, and to the fowl of the air, and to every beast of the field; but for Adam there was not found an help meet for him.

Genesis 2:19-20

7\. START NEW CHURCHES, CHOIRS, GROUPS, MINISTRIES and you will be operating in creativity and wisdom. God is the author of many new things. God is always doing new things.

The wisdom of creativity will cause you to start new ministries, new choirs and new groups. Beginning new things is a sign of the spirit of creativity.
