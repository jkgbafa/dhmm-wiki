### CHAPTER 11\

Wisdom Will Turn You into a National Ministry\
===================================================================

And it came to pass, when he had made an end of speaking unto Saul, that the soul of Jonathan was knit with the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul. And Saul took him that day, and would let him go no more home to his father’s house. Then Jonathan and David made a covenant, because he loved him as his own soul. And Jonathan stripped himself of the robe that was upon him, and gave it to David, and his garments, even to his sword, and to his bow, and to his girdle.

And DAVID WENT OUT WHITHERSOEVER SAUL SENT HIM, AND BEHAVED HIMSELF WISELY: and Saul set him over the men of war, and he was accepted in the sight of all the people, and also in the sight of Saul’s servants. And it came to pass as they came, when David was returned from the slaughter of the Philistine, that the women came out of all cities of Israel, singing and dancing, to meet king Saul, with tabrets, with joy, and with instruments of musick.

And the women answered one another as they played, and said, Saul hath slain his thousands, and David his ten thousands. And Saul was very wroth, and the saying displeased him; and he said, They have ascribed unto David ten thousands, and to me they have ascribed but thousands: and what can he have more but the kingdom? And Saul eyed David from that day and forward.

And it came to pass on the morrow, that the evil spirit from God came upon Saul, and he prophesied in the midst of the house: and David played with his hand, as at other times: and there was a javelin in Saul’s hand. And Saul cast the javelin; for he said, I will smite David even to the wall with it. And David avoided out of his presence twice. And Saul was afraid of David, because the LORD was with him, and was departed from Saul. Therefore Saul removed him from him, and made him his captain over a thousand; and he went out and came in before the people.

AND DAVID BEHAVED HIMSELF WISELY IN ALL HIS WAYS; AND THE LORD WAS WITH HIM. WHEREFORE WHEN SAUL SAW THAT HE BEHAVED HIMSELF VERY WISELY, HE WAS AFRAID OF HIM. BUT ALL ISRAEL AND JUDAH LOVED DAVID, BECAUSE HE WENT OUT AND CAME IN BEFORE THEM.

1 Samuel 18:1-16

It is time to learn from King David.

God’s wisdom is the application of God’s word until you become a national ministry. You will become a nationally recognized minister through the wisdom of God.

David was full of the wisdom of God. Because he behaved himself wisely, he gradually gained national status. Through the wisdom of God, you will become a national hero. Your ministry will soon be recognized in your nation. Through the wisdom of God, you are going to rise from being a community leader to a city leader to a regional leader and then a national leader. Only the wisdom of God can turn a nobody into a national hero.

How to Become a National Hero through the Wisdom of God

1\. Respect the special love that is shown you and you will become a national figure.

And it came to pass, when he had made an end of speaking unto Saul, that the soul of Jonathan was knit with the soul of David, and Jonathan loved him as his own soul.

1 Samuel 18:1

Jonathan loved David! David was a commoner! Prince Jonathan, a member of the royal family, had taken a special interest in David.

This special love and interest must be appreciated and valued. You would think that everyone would appreciate love when it is given. That is not the case! Have people appreciated the love of Jesus? The answer is “No”!

Has this world returned the love of God with love?

The answer is “No”! Many people do not know how to respond to love. It is a reality you will grow to discover.

When you respect the love that is shown to you, you receive more love.

The more love you receive, the higher you will go. Many people do not respect the love that is shown to them.

Some of the people I have loved have returned my love with flatness.

Some of the people that I have loved have returned my love with hatred.

Some of the people I have loved returned my love with treachery.

Because they did not appreciate the love that was shown them, they ended up being cast away as profane branches.

2\. Respect the special access that is given to you and you will become a national figure.

And Saul took him that day, and would let him go no more home to his father’s house.

1 Samuel 18:2

Respect the access that is given to you and you will learn a lot! It is a great privilege to have access to a great person. The access given to you can be a stepping-stone for you to become a great person and a national hero.

When you despise the special access that is given to you, you can become a monster. You can also become proud because of the special access that is given to you.

One day, a sister was honoured to receive a special job. Because of her work she was given special access to her pastor. She had the opportunity to work closer than other people. She saw many of the inner workings of the ministry. One day somebody asked her a question about her pastor whom she worked with. He wanted to know what he was like in real life.

She sarcastically remarked, “Oh, I know that man very well. He is five hundred per cent inquisitive about everything.”

This was not a nice remark nor was it a compliment. This lady had taken her special access for granted. That is why she would venture to make such a remark about her father.

Many people would have liked to work in her position. They just did not have the access she had. Like Lucifer who was the anointed cherub that covered the throne, she had rubbished the special entrance that was given to her. When people rubbish the closeness they are given, it is often taken away from them forever.

Blessed is the man whom thou choosest, and causest to approach unto thee, that he may dwell in thy courts: we shall be satisfied with the goodness of thy house, even of thy holy temple.

Psalm 65:4

3\. Respect the covenants and agreements that you have made and you will become a national hero.

Then Jonathan and David made a covenant, because he loved him as his own soul.

1 Samuel 18:3

Respect your marital covenant. Respect covenants of privacy and secrecy that you have made so that you are a man whose word can be trusted.

As soon as it is discovered that you cannot keep a covenant, you will lose everything you have.

You will not become a national hero, nor will you receive national recognition because no one can trust you. Your word is good for nothing. Your promises are meaningless and therefore you cannot become a national hero.

4\. Respect the gifts that you are given and you will become a national minister.

And Jonathan stripped himself of the robe that was upon him, and gave it to David, and his garments, even to his sword, and to his bow, and to his girdle.

1 Samuel 18:4

A wicked person does not respect the gifts that he is given. David could have trivialised the gifts he was given and said, “Jonathan gave me a second-hand robe.” He could even have said, “Jonathan gave me his old sword because he has a new one that is far better.”

Your attitude towards a gift reveals a lot about your character. Some people do not even say, “Thank you.”

One day, I gave a young couple a gift; but they never said “Thank you” to me. Ingratitude reveals presumption and wickedness lurking beneath the smiles and politeness.

5\. Respect the small errands and missions you are sent to do and you will become a national minister.

And David went out whithersoever Saul sent him, and behaved himself wisely: and Saul set him over the men of war, and he was accepted in the sight of all the people, and also in the sight of Saul’s servants.

1 Samuel 18:5

David went wherever Saul sent him. Whatever you are asked to do, just do it. You will discover that God will use the various errands and missions of your life to give you experience and the fortitude that you will need.

6\. Overcome the problems of a successful person and you will become a national figure.

And Saul was very wroth, and the saying displeased him; and he said, They have ascribed unto David ten thousands, and to me they have ascribed but thousands: and what can he have more but the kingdom? And Saul eyed David from that day and forward.

1 Samuel 18:8-9

You must overcome the unsolicited jealousy, hatred and rivalry that come to those who are successful in God’s kingdom. Overcoming jealousy and animosity are part of the journey to becoming a national hero.

7\. Continue using your gift when you experience promotion and you will go even higher.

And it came to pass on the morrow, that the evil spirit from God came upon Saul, and he prophesied in the midst of the house: and David played with his hand, as at other times: and there was a javelin in Saul’s hand.

1 Samuel 18:10

Continuing to use your gifts will lead to even greater promotion. David played the harp just as he played at other times. It was that small and insignificant musical gift that made David great and is still making him great today. Today, David’s psalms are read across the whole world. Most people in the world have heard of the psalms of David.

I began preaching and teaching the word of God when I was in secondary school. I have been preaching and teaching for many years. Today, I am experiencing certain blessings because I am a preacher and a teacher of the word of God. It is this same gift of preaching and teaching that has brought me this far.

Many people do not realise how their apparently little talents have brought them very far.

Many people put aside the gift that God has given to them because they trivialise and minimise the grace of God in their lives.

8\. Overcome the evil that is thrown at you and you will become a national minister.

And Saul cast the javelin; for he said, I will smite David even to the wall with it. And David avoided out of his presence twice. And Saul was afraid of David, because the LORD was with him, and was departed from Saul. Therefore Saul removed him from him, and made him his captain over a thousand; and he went out and came in before the people.

1 Samuel 18:11-13

Evil attacks of envy, hatred and death were hurled at David. He overcame the evil by avoiding it. He evaded the relentless attacks of King Saul. It is important that you learn how to avoid certain things without confronting them directly. There are times you are expected to overcome evil by directly confronting it. There are certain evils that you simply need to avoid.

There is no need to confront a father. There is no need to fight a father figure. Simply avoid his jealous attacks. Trust God to outlive these dangerous, sometimes demonized, personalities and bear fruit in spite of their attacks.

9\. Respect the need to be a good example and you will become a national figure.

And David behaved himself wisely in all his ways; and the LORD was with him. Wherefore when Saul saw that he behaved himself very wisely, he was afraid of him. But all Israel and Judah loved David, because he went out and came in before them.

1 Samuel 18:14-16
