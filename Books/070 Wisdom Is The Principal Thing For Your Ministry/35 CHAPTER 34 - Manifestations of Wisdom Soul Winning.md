### CHAPTER 34\

Manifestations of Wisdom: Soul Winning\
============================================================

The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life; and HE THAT WINNETH SOULS IS WISE.

Proverbs 11:30

1\. He that wins souls and builds churches has a great wisdom about him.

The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life; and HE THAT WINNETH SOULS IS WISE.

Proverbs 11:30

The world will not consider a soul winner as a wise man!

Billy Graham or Reinhard Bonnke would not be considered as wise men by world.

I think the world would rather consider people like Einstein, Socrates and other famous scientists as wise men.

However, God’s word says that soul winning is one of the greatest manifestations of wisdom. Become a wise man by becoming a soul winner!

2\. It is wise to win souls because winning souls and building churches makes your life glorious. Wise people shall inherit the glory.

THE WISE SHALL INHERIT GLORY: but shame shall be the promotion of fools.

Proverbs 3:35

The glory of God will be given to those who win souls. Winning souls is the practice of wisdom. There comes a time we must set aside all reason and believe the word of God.

He that winneth souls is wise! Win souls! You will inherit the glory of God. Be a soul winner! You will be covered with such glory for winning souls.

In eternity, you will be surprised that winning souls will be declared the wisest thing you ever did.

3\. It is wise to win souls because winning souls, turning many to righteousness, and building churches will make you into a star.

AND THEY THAT BE WISE SHALL SHINE as the brightness of the firmament; and they that turn many to righteousness as the stars for ever and ever.

Daniel 12:3

Apart from inheriting glory, you will become a star shining forever. Stars have been in existence for billions of years. They have been shining in the distance for billions of years. God has promised that you will also shine like a star. This great promise is for soul winners.

4\. It is wise to win souls because winning souls gives you special wages. As a soul winner, expect a stable income.

Say not ye, There are yet four months, and then cometh harvest? behold, I say unto you, Lift up your eyes, and look on the fields; for they are white already to harvest. AND HE THAT REAPETH RECEIVETH WAGES, and gathereth fruit unto life eternal: that both he that soweth and he that reapeth may rejoice together.

John 4:35-36

As a soul winner, expect a wise and stable job. Soul winners are blessed with financial increase as well. He that reapeth the harvest will receive wages from above.

5\. It is wise to win souls because soul winning causes you to reap a harvest of joy.

I say unto you, that likewise JOY SHALL BE IN HEAVEN over one sinner that repenteth, more than over ninety and nine just persons, which need no repentance.

Luke 15:7

Whenever you win souls you reap a harvest of joy. Every time you win a soul, you cause so much joy in heaven. You are destined to reap joy on earth. If you bring joy to people, you can expect to reap joy in your own life. If you bring sorrow and anguish to someone, you can expect to reap sorrow, anguish, depression, disappointment and disillusionment.

Whatever a man sows, he shall reap. Every time you bring joy to heaven, God will multiply joy back to you. Decide to bring a lot of joy to God in heaven by being a soul winner. God will ensure that you have a hundredfold return of joy in your own life.

6\. It is wise to win souls because soul winning is not in vain.

Therefore, my beloved brethren, be ye stedfast, unmoveable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, forasmuch AS YE KNOW THAT YOUR LABOUR IS NOT IN VAIN IN THE LORD.

1 Corinthians 15:58

Almost every earthly venture is an activity in vain. Soul winning is the Lord’s work. It is not in vain! Winning souls is the wisest thing you can ever do with your life. When you win souls, you will be doing something with a lasting and permanent result.

7\. It is wise to win souls because soul winning allows the power of God to work freely in your life.

FOR I AM NOT ASHAMED OF THE GOSPEL OF CHRIST: FOR IT IS THE POWER OF GOD unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.

Romans 1:16

The gospel of Jesus Chris is the power of God onto salvation. Everybody who preaches the gospel dabbles with the power of God. The gospel of Jesus Christ is a simple message. It is a humble message.

It is a message that the so-called wise in this world are unable to fathom. Many churches have replaced this famous message with motivational speeches and with excuses for not mentioning heaven or hell any more.

The death of Billy Graham and the acclaim he received for his simple gospel message through the years, rebuked the modern church for drifting away from the simple truth of the gospel.

Because the church has left the gospel of Jesus behind, it has inadvertently left the power of God behind. The powerless church is now begging for money every day.

The gospel of Jesus Christ is the power of God.

If you preach the gospel, you will see the power of God in every area of your life. It is wise to preach the gospel and win souls because it releases the power of God into your life.
