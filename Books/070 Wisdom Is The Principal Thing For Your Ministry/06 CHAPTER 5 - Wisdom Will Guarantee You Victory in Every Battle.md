### CHAPTER 5\

Wisdom Will Guarantee You Victory in Every Battle\
=======================================================================

A WISE MAN IS STRONG; yea, a man of knowledge increaseth strength.

Proverbs 24:5

WISDOM STRENGTHENETH THE WISE more than ten mighty men which are in the city.

Ecclesiastes 7:19

Wisdom makes you strong! Wisdom strengthens you! Strength to obey God is found through wisdom. Remember that your wisdom is in the word of God.

Wisdom is the carrier of the divine strength that you need for your ministry. Strength of character is found through wisdom.

Strength of leadership is found through wisdom. Your strength will not diminish until your ministry is done. The wiser you are, the stronger you are!

If you are not strong, you cannot lead people, you cannot govern people, you cannot take the decisions that you need to take. Democracy weakens leaders and they are unable to guide and lead the way they want to. Many people in positions of authority are not strong enough. They do not have the strength to lead and guide people.

Most people are resistant to change. Even those who know that change is good are resistant to important changes. If your ministry does not have a major change every few months, you are probably stagnating.

I notice there are major changes that take place in my ministry every one to two years. You need strength to implement changes for people who are resistant to change and improvement.
