### CHAPTER 4\

Wisdom Will Make You a Great Leader\
=========================================================

By me kings reign, and princes decree justice. By me princes rule, and nobles, even all the judges of the earth.

Proverbs 8:15-16

I lead in the way of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of judgment:

Proverbs 8:20

By wisdom, kings reign! This means that by wisdom kings lead their subjects forward into prosperity. In other words, wisdom is the principal thing used by kings to lead and rule their subjects.

One of the greatest challenges of ministry is leading people. Human beings are difficult to lead and to control. The more democratic people become, the more they behave like spoilt children.

Proud, democratic and opinionated groups are virtually ungovernable and unleadable. That is why anointed and spiritual churches are not led by democratic systems.

Wisdom is the master key to the leadership skills you need for ministry. Wisdom is the key to dealing with the challenges of leadership. Wisdom is the key you need to control human behaviour.

The most evil group of beings on the earth are devils and demons. The next most evil group are human beings. It is no small task to lead a group of human beings anywhere. Human beings are deceptive. Human beings are treacherous. Human beings are ungrateful. Human beings are envious. Every modern movie depicts the evil, the immorality, the treachery and the wickedness of mankind today.

Jesus said, “Beware of men” (Matthew 10:17).

Jesus never said, “Beware of devils” but He did warn us to beware of men.

Being a leader is like managing a zoo. Every animal member of the zoo wants to be free. Every animal will attack you if it gets the chance.

Every animal will eat you for lunch, if it can.

Even though it is better for the animals to be safely in the zoo, they all resent their captivity. Most animals will actually live longer in a zoo than in the wild. Yet all the animals hate you and would bite off your arms, crush your skull and eat up your intestines if they got the chance.

Lions live to be about twenty years old when they are kept in a zoo. Lions that live in the wild live only up to about eight years. Indeed, it is better for a lion to have food brought to it every day. Yet, this same lion would kill you without blinking an eye.

To lead people is like managing this zoo. The animals will eat up each other if they get the chance. Some of the animals are quiet-looking but still cannot be trusted.

The crocodile, for instance, lies very still without uttering a sound. But you cannot trust them at all. The snakes sleep all day but you cannot trust them either. Some of the animals ignore you when you pass by, but they are still very dangerous and deadly. This is how human beings are.

It is as though the animals are ungrateful, unreasonable and hateful. That is exactly how human beings are. You need great skill to live amongst them and even more to lead them to become a united community dwelling together in peace and harmony. The last time I walked through a zoo, I realised how much thought had gone into keeping all these animals together in one place. Great wisdom had been used to protect the animals from each other. Great wisdom had been used to protect the animals from those of us who would come to see them and vice versa.

By wisdom kings reign! By wisdom kings decree justice! Without wisdom you cannot reign, you cannot rule, you cannot govern! Leadership is the art of reigning over people who hate you, who lie about you, who are always trying to cheat and deceive you. If you think this is a simple job then you do not understand what it means to be a leader.

Moses learned first-hand how evil human beings were. Moses struggled with the ungrateful Israelites for years until God Himself rejected them. Moses needed all the wisdom that he could gather to lead these people out of Egypt and into Israel. You would have thought that because Moses was delivering the people from captivity, he would be hailed and honoured; but not so!

He was insulted, criticized and accused deeply throughout the years he led the Israelites out of Egypt and into the Promised Land.

Desire wisdom so that you can become a great leader! Desire the wisdom of leadership. Read all you can about leadership and implement the great wisdom of leadership.
