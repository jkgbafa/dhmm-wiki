### CHAPTER 23\

Wisdom Increases through Instructions\
===========================================================

GIVE INSTRUCTION to a wise man, and HE WILL BE YET WISER: teach a just man, and he will increase in learning.

Proverbs 9:9

An instruction is a practical order, command or directive that you can actually follow.

When you are training to be a pilot, an instructor will teach you what to do.

The instructor gives instructions. Pilots are expected to follow instructions. Pilots are not expected to come out with new ideas on what to do. Pilots may have up to three hundred passengers whose lives depend on the pilots’ following instructions.

Would you like to have a pilot who does not follow instructions?

Thank God for how you have received wisdom, knowledge, understanding and counsel. Thank God for all the good advice you have ever received. This chapter is about something quite different. This chapter is about receiving INSTRUCTIONS.

You must learn that instructions are different from advice.

Instructions have to do with orders, commands and directives.

There are times that God gives us orders, commands and directives. It is when you are childlike and humble that you can receive instructions.

It is a great blessing to receive instructions. Many people would do well if they were ever to have instructions. Pray that you will be a person who is qualified to receive orders, commands and directives.

You must value relationships where you receive instructions, orders, commands and directives.

You must be obedient to instructions more than to any other type of wisdom. Obeying instructions will save your life!

You will discover that many of the passages in the Bible are giving counsel, advice and doctrine but very few instructions.

Wherever there are instructions you must be zealous to obey them. Instructions are orders! In the army, instructions are called orders.

Instructions to Ministers

1\. Go ye into all the world and preach the gospel! The Great Commission is an example of an instruction. It is an order from God. We are instructed to go into the whole world and preach the gospel. Those orders have not changed! Until Jesus comes, there are no new orders. It is in our interest to obey this great instruction.

Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.

Matthew 28:19-20

2\. Owe no man anything! This instructions means you should not go into debt. This instruction means that you should practically not get into debts by taking mortgages, taking loans, overdrafts, buying on credit and using credit cards.

Owe no man any thing, but to love one another: for he that loveth another hath fulfilled the law.

Romans 13:8

3\. Seek the kingdom of God first! This instruction helps you to know what to prioritize. The kingdom of God comes first.

But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.

Matthew 6:33

4\. Forgive! God’s instruction to us is to forgive people who have hurt us. Christians are a race of forgiving, loving people who do not take into account a wrong suffered. Jesus taught us to forgive seventy times seven times.

And when ye stand praying, forgive, if ye have ought against any: that your Father also which is in heaven may forgive you your trespasses.

Mark 11:25

5\. Feed my sheep! This was a practical instruction to Peter to focus on preaching, teaching and ministering to the sheep.

So when they had dined, Jesus saith to Simon Peter, Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou me more than these? He saith unto him, Yea, Lord; thou knowest that I love thee. He saith unto him, FEED MY LAMBS.

He saith to him again the second time, Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou me? He saith unto him, Yea, Lord; thou knowest that I love thee. He saith unto him, FEED MY SHEEP.

He saith unto him the third time, Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou me? Peter was grieved because he said unto him the third time, Lovest thou me? And he said unto him, Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee. Jesus saith unto him, FEED MY SHEEP.

John 21:15-17

6\. Walk in love! This is a practical instruction to be kind, patient and to not take into account a wrong suffered.

And walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us, and hath given himself for us an offering and a sacrifice to God for a sweetsmelling savour.

Ephesians 5:2

7\. Ask and it shall be given to you! This is a practical instruction to pray. Just get up and start praying and many doors will open to you.

Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you:

Matthew 7:7
