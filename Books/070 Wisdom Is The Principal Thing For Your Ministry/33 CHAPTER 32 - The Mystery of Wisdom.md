### CHAPTER 32\

The Mystery of Wisdom\
===========================================

But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, even the hidden wisdom, which God ordained before the world unto our glory:

1 Corinthians 2:7

God’s wisdom has certain characteristics. It is these characteristics that make the wisdom of God very different from the wisdom of man. You must get used to the wisdom of God otherwise you will reject it as foolishness. You will miss the sweet revelation and wisdom that come from heaven.

I want to share with you seven characteristics of God’s heavenly wisdom so that you recognize them each time they come your way.

1\. The wisdom of God is hidden and mysterious.

But we speak THE WISDOM OF GOD IN A MYSTERY, even the hidden wisdom, which God ordained before the world unto our glory:

1 Corinthians 2:7

The wisdom of God is different from the wisdom of man. The wisdom of man can be explained to reasoning men. The wisdom of God is not always understood by reasoning and understanding. The wisdom of God defies logic. Many things about God’s wisdom are not logical. Many things about God’s wisdom do not add up. That is why people miss the wisdom of God so easily. The wisdom of God is hidden from the natural man. “The wisdom of God is full of secrets. For the froward is abomination to the Lord: but his secret is with the righteous” (Proverbs 3:32).

There are many secrets that God has kept from man. As the years go by, they are revealed. The salvation that came to us through Christ was hidden for many years and the prophets diligently sought to find out what the salvation of Christ was all about. God shows His secrets to those who fear Him.

THE SECRET OF THE LORD IS WITH THEM THAT FEAR HIM; and he will shew them his covenant.

Psalm 25:14

2\. The wisdom of God is not understood by evil spirits.

Which NONE OF THE PRINCES OF THIS WORLD KNEW: for had they known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.

1 Corinthians 2:8

The word “Prince” is the same as “Principality”. You will remember that Paul said we should not war against flesh and blood but we should war against principalities, powers and the rulers of the darkness of this world (Ephesians 6:12).

To overcome devils and to confuse them, you must operate in the wisdom of God. Devils do not know or understand the wisdom of God; that is why Jesus defeated them. The wisdom of the cross is the wisdom of God.

The princes and principalities of this world did not understand the power of sacrifice, the power of the cross, the power of losing, the power of suffering and the power of dying.

3\. The wisdom of God comes through the Holy Spirit.

The wisdom of God is taught by the Holy Spirit. The more of the Holy Spirit you have, the more of God’s wisdom you will enjoy. Pray for the Holy Spirit! He will impart to you the wisdom of God. It may not make sense to the human mind. But the Holy Spirit will be leading you. Unbelievers do not have the Holy Spirit. They cannot receive nor understand the wisdom of God.

But as it is written, Eye hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither have entered into the heart of man, the things which God hath prepared for them that love him. But GOD HATH REVEALED THEM UNTO US BY HIS SPIRIT: for the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God.

1 Corinthians 2:9-10

4\. The wisdom from above is different from man’s wisdom.

Which things also we speak, NOT IN THE WORDS WHICH MAN’S WISDOM TEACHETH, but which the Holy Ghost teacheth; comparing spiritual things with spiritual.

1 Corinthians 2:13

The wisdom of God is not the wisdom that man teaches. Human wisdom is taught in universities. Many words are used. Bigger and bigger words are invented. Many times, man’s wisdom is full of deception. A lot of the words used in the financial sector serve to draw people into arrangements, debts and confusion. Newer and newer words are invented to the point where people listening to a speech on economics cannot understand what is being said. With time, it is perceived that economists and financial people have a higher IQ and an even greater understanding than scientists.

If you remember, from your secondary school days the brightest students often did Science and many people who studied the Arts were actually unable to qualify for the Sciences.

It is amazing that medical science is even more understandable today than finance and economics. Indeed, it seems that if you do not use more and more confusing terminology, you are not seen as wise.

The wisdom of this world is often shallow, lacking depth and also deceptive. As someone said, “It takes a colossal mind to state the obvious.” Simple clear facts like, “Zero is greater than minus one million” are completely overlooked by these inventors of words. Listening to these many newly-invented words gives you an impression of a “word salad”.

What is a “word salad”? A salad is an assorted mixture of miscellaneous leaves, fruits and vegetables. A word salad is a collection of words, some more difficult to pronounce, some short words, some long ones, some easy to understand words and some confusing words.

Financial people seem to enjoy speaking word salads. They have amazing words like, profit participative bonds, hybrid instruments, private placements, mergers and acquisitions, money markets, brokerage, debt ratios, acid test, premium, futures, amortization, securitizations, stocks, mortgages, re-financing and leverages.

Most people do not understand what these things are and what they mean. Is there a reason why people invent all these words? Why give a speech which very few people understand? This is man’s wisdom and it is different from God’s wisdom.

5\. The wisdom of God has been ordained by God to make us glorious.

But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, even THE HIDDEN WISDOM, which God ordained before the world UNTO OUR GLORY:

1 Corinthians 2:7

The wisdom of God is ordained for your glory. If you follow the wisdom of God, you will become glorious and your life will be beautiful. The wisdom of God is ordained to change your life and your ministry.

Many people who are in the ministry do not follow the wisdom of God. They believe in the secular methods of doing things.

They believe in advertising, they believe in the media, they believe in psychology, they believe in connections, they believe in the power of finances, they believe in loans, they believe in mortgages, they believe in doing business, they believe in earthly acclaim and they believe in the honour of men.

That is not the wisdom of God. The wisdom of God has to do with prayer, fasting, the word of God, the blood of Jesus, the anointing, humility, the Spirit of God, the grace of God, the love of God, the help of God and the holiness of Almighty God.

You must choose to believe in the wisdom of God because it is ordained for your glory. It is following the cross and the sacrifice it demands that will make you glorious. People who do not follow the cross of Jesus Christ do not have the glory of God in their lives.

If you lose your life, you will find it! It is the wisdom of God!

These are not words that can be explained in the book of logic.

These are mysterious words of God’s wisdom.

The glory I see in my life and ministry did not come from following logic. Common sense and logic are not what we are following after. We are seeking the word of God and the wisdom of God and it will lead us to glory, beauty and joy.

6\. A natural man cannot receive the wisdom from above. It is always foolishness to a natural man so do not try to impress the world.

But the natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God: for they are foolishness unto him: neither can he know them, because they are spiritually discerned.

1 Corinthians 2:14

The natural man does not receive the wisdom of God. As long as you remain in your natural state, you will not be able to receive God’s wisdom. Sometimes you must assess people’s spirituality before you speak to them. The natural man simply cannot receive the wisdom of God.

The wisdom of God teaches about things like tithing, giving, sacrificing, the blood of Jesus and the cross of Jesus Christ.

The wisdom of God teaches you not to borrow but rather to follow the Holy Spirit and be led by the voice of God.

The wisdom of God speaks the Ten Commandments.

There is nothing greater than the wisdom in the Ten Commandments.

The Ten Commandments encapsulate all the things that human beings should live by.

Today, some pastors have stopped preaching about heaven and hell. We do not want to frighten our congregation, they say. There are others who do not mention the devil because they do not want the church to focus on the spirit world. Others leave out the amazing teaching on how the world was created.

The wisdom of God tells us how the world was created. Worldly wisdom tells us that the world was created out of chaos when billions of cosmic bingo chips came together to form this amazing universe. What are cosmic bingo chips? Indeed, it takes faith to believe in such nonsense.

Keep preaching the wisdom of God, the call of God, the timings of God, the birth of Christ, the Virgin Mary, Jonah and the whale, the death of Jesus Christ and the resurrection from the dead. These things are the wisdom of God. It is time to proclaim and publish God’s will and God’s ways in His dealings with men. God is kind! God is merciful! God is patient! However, He is a God of severe judgment.

7\. You must determine to be a minister of God’s wisdom rather than a minister of man’s wisdom.

And I, brethren, when I came to you, came not with excellency of speech or of wisdom, declaring unto you the testimony of God. FOR I DETERMINED NOT to know any thing among you, save Jesus Christ, and him crucified.

1 Corinthians 2:1-2

A motivational speaker may be acclaimed by the world. A man who speaks with word salads may be considered a very wise man. Do not worry about being acclaimed by the world.

Be like Apostle Paul who determined to know and speak about Jesus Christ and Him crucified. When you determine to know only Jesus Christ and Him crucified, it does not mean that you do not know any other thing. It means that you have decided to stick with the wisdom of God.

I used to hear Reinhard Bonnke say, “I preach the A-B-C of the gospel.” Sometimes he would add, “I know the X-Y-Z as well but I am an evangelist and my message is the A-B-C of the gospel.” Apostle Paul also said that he was determined to stick with Jesus Christ and the message of the cross. Nothing would make him veer off into vain jangling!

It is sad to say that many famous preachers have veered off into vain jangling. It is no wonder that the church is as weak as it is today. If you were forced to eat only sweets, lollipops, ice cream from morning to evening, and from Monday to Friday, you would certainly not be healthy.

You must determine to teach only the wisdom of God. Determine to stay in the glorious wisdom of God and reject earthly ideas.
