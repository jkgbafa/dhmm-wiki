### CHAPTER 8\

Wisdom Is the Secret To Surviving Crises\
==============================================================

Therefore whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, I will liken him unto a wise man, which built his house upon a rock: AND THE RAIN DESCENDED, AND THE FLOODS CAME, AND THE WINDS BLEW, AND BEAT UPON THAT HOUSE; AND IT FELL NOT: for it was founded upon a rock. And every one that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them not, shall be likened unto a foolish man, which built his house upon the sand: And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell: and great was the fall of it.

Matthew 7:24-27

A

storm represents a crisis. A storm represents a difficult season. A wise man is able to pass through a difficult season and come out shining. Wisdom is the application of God’s word to make you survive every crisis.

Wisdom will help you to survive every disaster.

Wisdom is the application of God’s word for you to become someone who survives and prevails through the changing scenes of life. It is the master key to being an evergreen tree.

The wise man builds his house on a rock. The wise man’s house, which was built on a rock, survived the storm beautifully. God wants you to survive every crisis. If you think that you are going to float through this life on flowery beds of ease, then you do not know what is in this life.

There are going to be lots of storms, trials, difficulties and problems. Every man who comes into this world experiences many evil days. Look at how Jacob described his life: he said his life had been made up of a few evil days.

And Jacob said unto Pharaoh, The days of the years of my pilgrimage are an hundred and thirty years: FEW AND EVIL HAVE THE DAYS OF THE YEARS OF MY LIFE BEEN, and have not attained unto the days of the years of the life of my fathers in the days of their pilgrimage.

Genesis 47:9

Whenever you get into a crisis, you will need to find a solution that takes you out safely. It is important to believe in the wisdom of God. Do what the wisdom of God tells you to do and you will come out shining.

There is nothing like a crisis-free life!

There will always be emergencies, disasters, catastrophes, crises and calamities.

How to come out using the wisdom of God is what you need to learn. Always remember that the word of God contains the wisdom of God for your crisis.

How to Survive a Crisis Using the Wisdom of God

But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.

James 3:17

Always remember this verse when you fall into a crisis situation and you will come out shining. Use this scripture to guide every decision in the time of crisis and trouble. This verse will give you the wisdom to know how to navigate out of the troubles that seek to drown you.

The wisdom of God is very well described in this scripture. The wisdom of God is characterized by purity, peacefulness, gentleness, malleability, mercy, good fruits, fairness and the absence of pretence. Every decision taken should be characterized by these eight traits.

What is the wise thing to do? What is the right thing to do? Use this scripture to help you take proper decisions that will eject you from the crisis. Do not use our own mind! Use the wisdom of God! Stop using little quips and sayings as your guidepost.

People say things like, “What goes around comes around.”

They say, “If you do good, you do it for yourself.”

They say, “Every little crocodile becomes a big crocodile.”

These apparently wise sayings are nice and wise-sounding but Christians have the word of God as our great source of wisdom.

You have the word of God and you know what the wisdom of God is. Use the wisdom of God as your guidepost. Let the scripture tell you what the best thing to do is.

But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.

James 3:17

1\. Do what is pure, chaste, uncorrupted and unpolluted and you will come out of your crisis by the wisdom of God. Doing something impure and corrupted will only take you deeper into trouble.

2\. Do what is peaceful, non-aggressive, calm, non-violent and you will come out on top of your crisis. Being aggressive and violent will only take you deeper into crisis.

3\. Be gentle, mild, kind, tender and moderate and you will come out of your crisis and calamity unscathed. Rushing into things without duly thinking about the implications will get you into more crises.

4\. Be easy to be entreated, easy to plead with, easy to beg and easy to implore, easy to change your mind and you will be doing the right thing that will take you out of your crisis. Being stubborn, difficult to reason with, having long meetings is a sign of a lack of wisdom.

5\. Be merciful, forgiving, gracious, lenient and generous and you will escape from the crisis without being harmed.

6\. Take a decision to bear much fruit, produce a good harvest, have great results and you will come out of your crisis.

7\. Be fair, impartial, unbiased, objective, open-minded, reasonable and you will overcome every crisis you find yourself in.

8\. Be without hypocrisy and you will overcome every crisis you find yourself in.
