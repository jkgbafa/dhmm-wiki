### CHAPTER 3\

Wisdom is the Secret to Mighty Works in Ministry\
======================================================================

And he went out from thence, and came into his own country; and his disciples follow him. And when the sabbath day was come, he began to teach in the synagogue: and many hearing him were astonished, saying, From whence hath this man these things? And WHAT WISDOM IS THIS WHICH IS GIVEN UNTO HIM, THAT EVEN SUCH MIGHTY WORKS ARE WROUGHT BY HIS HANDS?

Mark 6:1-2

Wisdom is the principal thing! Wisdom is the reason for mighty works, great works and great achievements in ministry. When Jesus walked the earth, He did great things. He accomplished great things.

People followed Jesus and were greatly impressed with the mighty accomplishments of His ministry. His ministry is effective up till today. Notice how the people attributed Jesus’ great success in ministry to His wisdom. They said, “What wisdom is this that such mighty works are done?”

They did not say, “What prayer is this?”

They did not ask, “What power is this?”

They did not say, “What fasting has been done!”

They just exclaimed, “What wisdom is this that such mighty works are accomplished through Him!”

Indeed, it takes great wisdom to accomplish mighty works. Let that settle in your mind for all time.

Those who accomplish great things for God know something you don’t know.

They have some wisdom you do not have.

If you knew what they knew, if you had the understanding that they had, you would have the wisdom to accomplish great things as well.

Why have you not accomplished certain great things in life and in ministry?

Why have you not achieved certain great and mighty works?

It is because there is some wisdom you do not have! Wisdom is the principal thing! Wisdom is the reason for mighty works, great works and great achievements in ministry.

You may look up to some of the great people in ministry. You may see people doing great works for the Lord.

Always remember that they must know something you do not know.

Great accomplishers have some wisdom, some understanding, some knowledge and some counsel that you do not have. Read it for yourself:

And when he was come into his own country, he taught them in their synagogue, insomuch that they were astonished, AND SAID, WHENCE HATH THIS MAN THIS WISDOM, AND THESE MIGHTY WORKS?

Matthew 13:54
