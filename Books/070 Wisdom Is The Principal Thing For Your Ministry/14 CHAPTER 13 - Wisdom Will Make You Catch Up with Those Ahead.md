### CHAPTER 13\

Wisdom Will Make You Catch Up with Those Ahead\
====================================================================

Wherefore, beloved, seeing that ye look for such things, be diligent that ye may be found of him in peace, without spot, and blameless. And account that the longsuffering of our Lord is salvation; EVEN AS OUR BELOVED BROTHER PAUL ALSO ACCORDING TO THE WISDOM GIVEN UNTO HIM HATH WRITTEN UNTO YOU; As also in all his epistles, speaking in them of these things; in which are some things hard to be understood, which they that are unlearned and unstable wrest, as they do also the other scriptures, unto their own destruction.

2 Peter 3:14-16

Wisdom is the master key to catching up and overtaking all those who were there before you in ministry. The apostle Paul was given special wisdom from God and it is this wisdom that we see in his letters to the churches.

Paul is the best example of someone who caught up with those ahead of him. Wisdom is the master key for catching up with your seniors. Paul did not have the opportunity to meet Jesus personally. He did not have the privilege that the other twelve disciples had. Paul did not see five thousand people being fed. Paul did not see the sick being healed. Neither did he see the dead being raised. Yet, God used him even more than He used the other disciples.

How did Paul catch up with the ministers that were ahead of him? What did he do to bridge the gap? What did he do to overcome this great handicap of not seeing Jesus in person?

Paul’s master key was the key of wisdom. Peter acknowledged that Paul had been given special wisdom. The special wisdom Paul had been given made him write books and catch up with those who had been apostles before him. The special wisdom to catch up with those ahead of you is called “the catching-up wisdom”. The catching-up wisdom has two components. The first component is the wisdom of walking in the grace of God and the second component is the wisdom of working harder than those ahead of you.

What is The Catching-Up Wisdom?

1\. The catching-up wisdom is to walk in the grace that you have been given.

For I am the least of the apostles, that am not meet to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of God. BUT BY THE GRACE OF GOD I AM WHAT I AM: AND HIS GRACE WHICH WAS BESTOWED UPON ME WAS NOT IN VAIN; but I laboured more abundantly than they all: yet not I, but the grace of God which was with me.

1 Corinthians 15:9-10

Apostle Paul revealed the wisdom that God gave him to catch up with the other apostles.

Walk in the grace of God! Many people do not walk in the grace that they have been given. The grace you have been given is the gift that you have. Many people leave the grace that they have and try to do what others are doing. Instead of trying to be like someone else, find out what grace you have received from God. When you walk in the grace of God, you walk in your gift and you stay in your calling.

Years ago, I discovered that I had the grace to hold camp meetings and to teach people to become leaders and pastors in the house of God. Through this grace, I was able to raise up many young pastors and workers. I noticed that other ministers were holding powerful conventions with great manifestations of the Holy Spirit.

I was secretly jealous of these people and wanted to be like them because they were so powerful. But I sensed the Holy Spirit encouraging me to simply walk in the grace that He has given me. I decided to walk in my grace and carry on training people in small groups. Today, I am enjoying the fruit of having planted thousands of churches. If I had left the grace of God and gone on to other things, I would not have been able to catch up with those ahead of me. Walking in the grace of God is the catching-up wisdom that you need.

2\. The catching-up wisdom is to work harder than all those around you.

For I am the least of the apostles, that am not meet to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of God. But by the grace of God I am what I am: and his grace which was bestowed upon me was not in vain; BUT I LABOURED MORE ABUNDANTLY THAN THEY ALL: yet not I, but the grace of God which was with me.

1 Corinthians 15:9-10

The catching-up wisdom of Paul was to work harder than everybody else. Paul laboured more than all the other apostles. He put in more time and more effort. Most people do not give persevering attention to the work of God. Many people are not constant in their efforts to accomplish great things for God.

Apostle Paul was persistent and constant in his efforts until he died. Even in prison he was writing letters that are read today. His hard work is felt through reading his letters. You can sense his constant and tireless labour for the church. You can feel his diligent work through these letters.

Even before Paul came on the scene, Solomon had told us the effect of hard, constant effort. Hard work and constant efforts always yield much fruit.

Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.

Proverbs 22:29

It is time to labour more abundantly than everyone else! Apostle Paul did not labour less than everyone else. He worked more than everyone else!

That is the key to catching up!

Work hard! Do not expect to amount to anything if you do not work very hard.

It is time to study more than anyone else!

It is time to pray more than anyone else!

It is time to fast more than the average person!

It is time to travel more than anyone else!

It is time to preach more than anyone else!

It is time to teach!

It is time to labour for the Lord!

It is time to work day and night!

You will soon be catching up with those far ahead of you.
