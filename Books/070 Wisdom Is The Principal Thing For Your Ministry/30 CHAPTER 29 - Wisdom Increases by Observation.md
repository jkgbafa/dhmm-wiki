### CHAPTER 29\

Wisdom Increases by Observation\
=====================================================

I went by the field of the slothful, and by the vineyard of the man void of understanding; and, lo, it was all grown over with thorns, and nettles had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall thereof was broken down.

THEN I SAW, AND CONSIDERED IT WELL: I LOOKED UPON IT, AND RECEIVED INSTRUCTION.

Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep: So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth; and thy want as an armed man.

Proverbs 24:30-34

The wise man saw and considered it well! Wisdom increases as you make observations in life. Wisdom comes by deeply considering people and events in your life. Whenever you notice something unusual, God expects you to think about it deeply. As you think about what you observe, you will receive revelations and wisdom. Funerals are usually notable events that stir up your mind and your heart. Your mind and heart can be stirred up unto wisdom.

Whenever someone dies, there is an opportunity to increase in wisdom. Funerals of certain people are momentous events that God allows to increase your wisdom. Funerals will stir up wisdom in you.

The funeral of Kenneth Hagin stirred up great wisdom in me. I spent several hours watching the video of Kenneth Hagin’s funeral. My wisdom increased greatly by considering his death and legacy. Listening to the testimonies of different pastors who had been affected by Kenneth Hagin’s ministry gave me wisdom keys for my own ministry. All kinds of pastors from near and far gave testimonies of how, when and where they were impacted by Kenneth Hagin.

Kenneth Hagin died preaching what he had been preaching as a young man. He never changed or shifted from his calling of preaching on faith. I saw the wisdom in staying focussed on your specific calling.

I considered that I had first encountered Kenneth Hagin when he was sixty-three years old. I realised that a person can bear fruit even into his much later years. The Holy Spirit impressed upon me that if Kenneth Hagin had changed his message I would not have been touched when I encountered him in his sixties.

Today, as I look around and see how many men of God have become motivational speakers and financial preachers, shifting far from what they originally started out with, I am grateful that Kenneth Hagin did not make that mistake. I am grateful that what he preached in his forties was what he was still preaching in his sixties.

I also noticed Kenneth Hagin’s funeral was attended by many pastors and ministers from all walks of life. Oral Roberts said that he did not know anyone who had made a greater impact in the world than Kenneth Hagin. Such testimonies increased my faith to minister to ministers of the gospel rather than to have more popular programmes for the larger crowds.

The funeral of Billy Graham was another momentous event that stirred up great wisdom in me. I noticed how all the United States presidents attended his funeral. I noticed how Billy Graham was given the highest honour of lying in state in the US Capitol Rotunda. The US Capitol Rotunda has been used as a place to pay tribute to America’s most distinguished citizens. Twelve American presidents had been laid in state this way. Billy Graham, a preacher of salvation, was honoured with this great honour.

I noticed that preachers of prosperity, success, motivation, happiness were not honoured as much as this man who had stuck with the unsophisticated and apparently simplistic message of salvation through the blood of Jesus Christ. I knew the wisdom God was telling me: stay with the cross! Stay with salvation! You are doing the right thing when you do that! Do not listen to those who say you are preaching basics because you do not have a revelation!

At a big funeral that was organized for someone I knew, I noticed a pattern in the way this man had died. His death was similar to some other people that I knew. The Holy Spirit whispered to my heart certain things concerning this man. The Holy Spirit whispered to me, “This type of death is caused by a certain pattern of activities.”

Years later, I learnt how Moses predicted that Korah and his family would not die a natural death. They would die in a particular way that would signify something. I had already learnt this lesson at a funeral. Funerals are events where you can increase your wisdom.

If these men die a natural death and suffer the fate of all mankind, then the LORD has not sent me.

Numbers 16:29 (NIV)

My own father’s funeral stirred up wisdom in me. I noticed how he lay in repose without speaking a word. My father’s words and opinions had governed my life and my home for many years. I was used to his wishes being carried out. I was used to him knowing and saying what was to be done next. At his funeral, I noticed his complete silence. He never said a word and never contributed to all that was done.

We did not need his opinion and he never made any comment about the various traditional rituals that were performed at the funeral. I was stunned by his silence. I realised that when I die, I will be silent.

Everyone who dies will be completely silent. If you have any wishes or desires, you must say them now and ensure that they are done.

It is time to preach now!

It is time to teach!

Teach the word of God!

Preach all you can!

Travel all you can and give all you can!
