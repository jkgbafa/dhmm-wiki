### CHAPTER 1\

Wisdom is the Principal Thing for Your Ministry\
=====================================================================

Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.

Proverbs 4:7

It is important that you believe that wisdom is the principal thing for your ministry.

Wisdom is the principal thing for every minister! Indeed, it is true that wisdom is the most fundamental thing you will need for your ministry. Wisdom is the greatest asset for your ministry. It is the primary gift of God for your ministry. Through wisdom, you will receive all the other things that are necessary for ministry. Most of the failure we experience in ministry is because of a lack of God’s wisdom.

Wisdom is the principal thing because the word of God is the principal thing. In the beginning was the Word! The Word was with God! The Word was God! God and His Word are always at the centre and the beginning of anything worthwhile. If your life is to amount to anything, God and His Word must be the principal thing. God is His Word and the Word is God! All the things that you can desire can never compare with the value of having God and His word in your life.

Is it true that power is not the principal thing for ministry?

Is it true that miracles are not the principal thing for ministry?

Is it true that prayer is not the principal thing for ministry?

Is it true that fasting is not the principal thing for ministry?

Is it true that waiting on God is not the principal thing for ministry? Wow!

That is amazing! Wisdom is the principal thing I need for ministry!

Receive my instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than choice gold. For wisdom is better than rubies; and all the things that may be desired are not to be compared to it.

Proverbs 8:10-11

Before we go any deeper, it is important that you understand that the wisdom we are speaking about comes from the Word of God. Through Thy commandments Thou has made me wiser than my enemies. Wisdom comes to you through the Word of God. Through the commandments of God your life changes. You are filled with wisdom when you meditate on the commands of God.

THOU THROUGH THY COMMANDMENTS HAST MADE ME WISER than mine enemies: for they are ever with me.

Psalm 119:98

All the wisdom we are speaking of in this book has its source in the word of God. There are different kinds of wisdom. I am not talking about human cleverness or deceptive speeches. I am not talking of high-sounding words. I am not talking about declaring that you are a millionaire when you actually owe a trillion.

What is the point in using big words to confuse people?

What is the use of over complicating simple subjects until no one understands what you are saying?

What is the use of giving a speech on the budget after which no one remembers or understands what you said?

These cleverly presented impressions are not the wisdom of God I am sharing about. You will become far wiser than your enemies who seek to destroy you. This wisdom will come to you through the Word of God.

It is time to make wisdom the principal thing in your life! Exalt God’s wisdom in your life and ministry. Believe in the Word!

Believe that the Word is the main thing!

Believe that the wisdom of God is the principal thing.

Exalt wisdom and it will promote you!

Exalt her, and she shall promote thee: she shall bring thee to honour, when thou dost embrace her.

Proverbs 4:8

Become a wise man by believing that wisdom is more important than money. Take your mind off the importance of money, gold and silver; till you believe more in the importance of wisdom than in the importance of money.
