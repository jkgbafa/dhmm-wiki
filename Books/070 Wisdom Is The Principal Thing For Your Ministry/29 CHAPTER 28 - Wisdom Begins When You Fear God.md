### CHAPTER 28\

Wisdom Begins When You Fear God\
=====================================================

The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments: his praise endureth for ever.

Psalm 111:10

The fear of God is the beginning of wisdom. The scientists who espouse great theories and inventions but do not respect God are actually not wise. They may have some intelligence but they do not prove themselves to be wise when they do not fear God. Those who have a lot of money, a lot of education but do not honour God are also not wise people.

You begin to be wise when you begin to fear God! Your foolishness multiplies when you set God aside. Foolishness is manifested by those who do not fear God.

Why Fearing God Makes You Wise

1\. Fearing God, honouring God and respecting God are necessary because there is no theory that can explain God away: Nothing can explain the wonder of the human body.

A theory in which blind chaos develops miraculously into human beings with perfectly working neurological systems, gastro intestinal systems, cardio vascular systems, respiratory systems, urinary systems, reproductive systems and biochemical cycles leaves me wondering.

How billions and billions of lucky cosmic bingo atoms moving together can create such perfectly working systems with special tubes, fluids, solids and molecules and chemical reactions all working in tandem in one human being makes me wonder.

It takes more faith to believe that the complex human body was created by the coming together of random chaos devoid of all intelligence and love. Blind confusion could not have produced what we experience today. Blind chaos could never create the wonder of the human body, as we know it today. Where did such super creative intelligence come from? Was the human body created by random movements of lucky bingo chips? What are bingo chips anyway?

2\. Fearing God, honouring God and respecting God are necessary because there is no theory that can explain God away: Nothing can explain the creation of the intangible emotions and feelings of men.

Even if the physical things we see were created by a physical explosion, how do you explain the existence of emotions like love joy, peace, hatred and depression? A theory in which blind chaos develops miraculously into emotions like love, joy, peace, patience, hatred, depression and elation is simply untenable. How billions of cosmic bingo chips moving together can create the existence of such feelings is difficult to understand.

It takes more faith to believe that the emotions and feelings which human beings experience, were created by the coming together of random chaos devoid of all intelligence and love. Blind confusion could not have produced what we experience today. Where do intelligence, love, art and spirituality come from? Were they created by random movements of bingo cosmic chips?

3\. Fearing God, honouring God and respecting God are necessary because there is no theory that can explain God away: Nothing can explain the creation of the planets and the stars.

The theory in which the world comes together by a series of explosions which cause random, chaotically moving molecules to converge miraculously to form planets like the planet earth is simply not believable nor acceptable.

Where do our planets come from?

How come only the earth has oceans filled with fish?

How come only the earth has skies filled with birds?

How come only the earth has intelligent human beings?

Where did they all come from?

Are they all a result of accidentally moving, lucky bingo molecules?

How much deception can we take?

How come only the earth has human life?

How come life is sustained so miraculously by the conditions on the earth? All the other planets are either too hot or too cold.

How come there are no trees or rivers with fresh water on Mercury, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and all the other planets?

How come there are no animals anywhere else but on earth? Is this the product of blind chaos moving mystically together without intelligence to create an accident called Earth, with all its living creatures? What sort of accident is that?

So where do the stars come from? How come they are so far away that no human technology or ability can go there? How did they get themselves into the sky? How come stars are so big and so far? How can one star be as big as the distance between the sun and Jupiter?

4\. Fearing God, honouring God and respecting God are necessary because there is no theory that can explain God away: Nothing can explain the creation and order of the universe.

The universe has a beauty and an order that suggests that there is intelligence and a creator behind it all.

The existence of a Mercedes Benz in all its beautiful complexities points to the existence of a creator and an inventor. The beautiful Mercedes Benz car does not point to a gigantic nuclear explosion that happened several thousand million years ago. Such reasoning is not wisdom! Reasoning that does not include God and does not fear God is actually foolishness.

Blind chaos did not develop unintelligently into a Mercedes Benz automobile. Lucky bingo chips did not fall together to form a Mercedes Benz car with power steering, sensors and many other amazing features. The intelligent design of the universe also points to a creator and an inventor.
