### Preface

I first noticed the phrase, “strange woman” in the Bible some years ago. I was then a young Christian growing up in the Lord. I often wondered to myself, “What is a strange woman?”We understand a strange woman to be an unusual person. But what is unusual about this person? What unusual things does this person do?This book is all about that. I believe that God will use this book to deliver the unsuspecting Christian from her deadly traps. I realize that the devil can recognize the precious life from afar. He knows when God’s hand is on you, so he tar-gets you. One of the devil’s most successful agents is this strange woman.My prayer is that this book will deliver us all from one of the oldest strategies of the devil.
