### CHAPTER 2\

A Faithful Man Who Can Find?\
==================================================

Most men will proclaim every one his own goodness: but a faithful man who can find?

Proverbs 20:6

Why Are People Faithful for Short Periods?

Most people are faithful to something for short periods and seasons of their life. Human beings find it difficult to stay faithful for long periods. Rarely do you find someone who is faithful to one thing for his entire life. The scripture asks a very pertinent question: Who can find a faithful man? Before marriage people make all sorts of promises. Experienced people look on and smile to themselves, knowing that very few of those promises will be kept. Experienced people look on and know that the people who are making the promises will find it very difficult to keep them. Experienced people know that there are very few people who are really faithful.

Most men will proclaim every one his own goodness: but A FAITHFUL MAN WHO CAN FIND?

Proverbs 20:6

God knows that there are few people who are faithful men. God knows that there are few people who will be faithful unto death! Being faithful unto death is only possible if you are a faithful man. You will be one of the faithful men! You must become one of the faithful men!

To be faithful unto death is to maintain faithfulness and loyalty all the way to the end of the race.

To be faithful unto death is to run well till you die.

Be faithful unto death means be the same until you die.

Be faithful unto death means be constant and unwavering until you die.

Be faithful unto death means be loyal until you die.

Be faithful unto death means be unchanging until you die.

Indeed, if you find a faithful person, how long will his faithfulness last?

To be faithful is to be loyal, constant, obedient and never changing. Many people smile today but do not continue smiling tomorrow.

1\. Be Faithful in school and after school

Being faithful unto death means being loyal and dependable when you are young and also faithful when you are older. A younger person is always happier, freer, more innocent, more joyful and more excited about everything. A young person is also easily impressionable. All the disciples of Jesus were young men. Jesus Himself was a young man. Jesus was faithful unto death. Judas was not faithful unto death. Some people cannot be faithful for even three years.

Being faithful unto death means being faithful and loyal whilst in the university and until your last day on earth. There are many people who forsake the God of their youth.

One day, I was shocked to find out that a die-hard politician who had no smell of Christianity on him had been the prayer secretary of the Christian Fellowship when he was in the University.

Actually, I have encountered a number of prayer secretaries who were active during their university days but abandoned active Christian service when they began to work. All prayer secretaries should take note. Satan targets you for the zealous prayers that you offered up when you were in school.

Being faithful unto death means being constant and unwavering all through your youthful years before you are married and after, until the day you fall down and die. Plan to be faithful unto death! Thank God for your faithfulness before you get married. God wants far more from you than faithfulness before marriage. God wants faithfulness, loyalty, unflinching and unwavering commitment before you are married and unto death.

2\. Be Faithful before and after marriage

Being faithful unto death means being loyal and unchanging before and after marriage, all the way into your grave. One day, I interacted with a man who was lamenting about the poor performance of a manager of a legal firm. He said that he belonged to a board that had mistakenly employed a lady who was unstable. He described how this lady was unstable because she was unmarried. Being unmarried had affected her performance and stability.

Sometimes marriage stabilizes a person and at other times it causes instability. Will you be faithful after you are married? Will you be the same all the way through marriage and unto death?

3\. Be Faithful before and after having children

Be faithful unto death means that you must be faithful even after you have children. There are jobs that require you to be married with children. Children cause a lot of instability and bring changes to our lives. Will you be constant after you have children?

4\. Be Faithful before and after your mid-life

Being faithful unto death means you can be faithful until you are in your forties. Many people experience a mid-life crisis when they are in their forties. Midlife crisis usually occurs between the ages of thirty-seven to fifty-five and peaks in the mid-forties. When people are in their mid-life crisis, they do strange things. During a mid-life crisis, a person may quit a job impulsively, spend a lot of money or even divorce their spouse. People in their mid-life crisis are often depressed.

Many pastors re-assess their lives during this period. They analyse whether they have been successful and whether they have achieved anything with their lives. Pastors in mid-life crises tend to jump out of the things they have been faithful to for many years and end their season of faithfulness.

If a person does not have moderation and wisdom, he may end his career abruptly and harm himself. Many unstable pastors jump out of their ministries during this period. I have watched as dear sons of mine turned into monsters during their mid-life crises.

This is the reason why the Bible teaches us to be faithful unto death. If you have this scripture firmly embedded into your heart, you will be forewarned when you enter that season of life. You will remember that it is important to be faithful unto death. The words, “Be faithful unto death” are not my words. They are the words of Jesus that He spoke unto the Church. What He says to one church, He says to all!

5\. Be Faithful before and after you retire

Be faithful unto death means you that you must be faithful after you are sixty years old. What is the point of being a faithful and loyal person when you are young only to change dramatically when you enter your fifties and sixties? Do you know how much you can achieve for God when you are fifty years old?

My mother told me that people in their fifties can experience the best years of their lives. She said, “When you hit fifty, it’s like a light comes on!” Indeed, when this light comes on, you must grow up and bear much fruit.

Being faithful unto death means that you are faithful before you retire and after you retire. By the power of God, you will cross the age of seventy with ease! You must be faithful until you are seventy and beyond!

6\. Be Faithful through all the changing scenes of life

Be faithful unto death means that you must be faithful through all the different changing scenes of life until you finally die. Indeed, there are many changing scenes of life. There is faithfulness in the season of marriage. There is also faithfulness in the season after your spouse passes away.

7\. Be faithful to God and to the church

Be faithful unto death means that you must be loyal to God and to the church even if you have not achieved some of your life’s goals.

Do not allow a fear of humiliation among your more successful colleagues to drive you to become a disloyal monster. Some people have metamorphosed into spiritual Absaloms and Lucifers just because they felt they had not achieved as much as some others. If God has called you to the ministry, it is your faithfulness to Him that matters. Achieving great things in the eyes of men is not success per se. Having a good reputation and giving a good impression of yourself is not success per se. Jesus did not have a good reputation in this world. He was executed on a Roman cross. Most people in Jerusalem were under the impression that Jesus Christ was a common criminal.

8\. Be Faithful before you are appointed and after you are appointed

Jesus said, “One of you is a devil” (John 6:70). He was referring to Judas Iscariot. Why did He call Judas Iscariot a devil? Because Judas was not faithful unto death! Judas must have initially been a good person because he was appointed to be in charge of the money.

As time passed, Judas was not able to maintain his initial goodness. Judas could not even be faithful for more than three years. Judas’ faithfulness lasted only three years, after which he tried a surprise move against his master. Judas thought he was giving Jesus a surprise by arriving at the Garden of Gethsemane with a troop of enemies.

What a disgusting person! You cannot surprise God! As someone said, “There is nothing worse than a traitor.” Indeed, it is sad when people are not faithful unto death but are faithful for a very short while.
