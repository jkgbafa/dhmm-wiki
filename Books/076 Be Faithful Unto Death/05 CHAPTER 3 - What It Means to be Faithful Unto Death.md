### CHAPTER 3\

What It Means to be Faithful Unto Death\
=============================================================

Who then is a faithful and wise servant...?

Matthew 24:45

1\. Be faithful unto death: Be a good ambassador until you die.

A wicked messenger falleth into mischief: but A FAITHFUL AMBASSADOR is health.

Proverbs 13:17

You must be a faithful ambassador until death. Faithfulness is important if you want to be an ambassador. When you are an ambassador, you are sent away from the headquarters and expected to represent the organization properly. You are expected to give a good account of yourself and to exemplify all the principles and standards of the one who sent you on a mission. Most ambassadors begin to take on the character of the place they have been sent to, rather than imparting the character of the one who sent them. For instance, pastors are influenced more by the world they have been sent into. Pastors often become worldly rather than influencing the world with the godliness and spirituality of heaven.

As the cold of snow in the time of harvest, so is A FAITHFUL MESSENGER to them that send him: for he refresheth the soul of his masters.

Proverbs 25:13

Indeed, you must be a faithful messenger for the Lord. Do not forget the word of God by which you were commissioned. Go into the world and preach the gospel. Stay with the gospel. Do not preach from the books of first and second “Imaginations”!

Preach from John 3:16! Preach the Word! Preach about Jesus Christ! Be faithful to the message of the cross of Jesus Christ and Him crucified.

2\. Be faithful unto death: Do not bother to proclaim your own goodness.

Most men will proclaim every one his own goodness: but a faithful man who can find?

Proverbs 20:6

Most people talk about their own goodness. They never speak truthfully about their own shortcomings and failings.

I once heard someone accusing me of spoiling his life. He claimed that he had suffered when he worked in the ministry. He claimed that he had had marital problems when he worked in the ministry. He claimed that I was a false prophet.

He claimed that I had so much power over his life that he was overwhelmed with the power that I had. He claimed that he had great difficulty in finding a job after he left our ministry. He claimed that his church would not grow because of my teachings. In other words, I had virtually destroyed his life. I was saddened to read about his difficulties but intrigued to notice that he never mentioned that he was a thief and had stolen many things and been found out. I was also surprised that he did not mention how thousands of dollars evaporated under his watch. But this is the nature of man. Most people will proclaim their own goodness.

People do not mention anything negative about themselves. The scripture is quite clear that the faithful man does not bother to talk about his goodness and to proclaim that he is anything. Do not bother to proclaim your own goodness! Trust in God! The truth will always prevail! Be faithful unto death.

3\. Be faithful unto death: You will abound with blessings until you die.

A FAITHFUL MAN SHALL ABOUND WITH BLESSINGS: but he that maketh haste to be rich shall not be innocent.

Proverbs 28:20

A faithful man will abound with blessings until he dies. Faithfulness is the reason for great promotion and great rewards. Through faithfulness you will eventually prosper. Rash, impatient men miss out on the blessings of faithfulness.

Many people become unfaithful and disloyal just before their big reward arrives. When I look back on my life and ministry, I realize that I did not reap or receive much reward for anything for a long time.

As the years passed, however, I seemed to benefit more from faithfulness. Why do I say I was benefitting from faithfulness? Because I was not doing anything new and I was not doing anything differently. It was the same preaching on the same topics that were yielding better results.

The difference was that I had been faithfully saying the same things for many more years. The faithful man will abound with blessings. The Bible does not say a knowledgeable man will abound with blessings. The Bible did not say an intelligent man would abound with blessings. It is a faithful man who will abound with blessings.

4\. Be faithful unto death: Maintain your qualifications for ministry until death.

Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.

1 Corinthians 4:2

A faithful man will qualify for ministry. You must remain qualified for ministry until your death. When you are fifty, sixty or seventy you must still be qualified for real ministry. It is required in stewards that a man be found faithful. Be faithful unto death is God’s command to you today. Maintain your qualifications.

Solomon started out in holiness and obedience to God. But he did not maintain his qualifications for the throne. When he was older, he loved many strange women and followed idols, unlike his father.

For it came to pass, WHEN SOLOMON WAS OLD, that his wives turned away his heart after other gods: and his heart was not perfect with the LORD his God, as was the heart of David his father. FOR SOLOMON WENT AFTER ASHTORETH the goddess of the Zidonians, and after Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites. And Solomon did evil in the sight of the LORD, and went not fully after the LORD, as did David his father. THEN DID SOLOMON BUILD AN HIGH PLACE FOR CHEMOSH, THE ABOMINATION OF MOAB, in the hill that is before Jerusalem, and for Molech, the abomination of the children of Ammon. And likewise did he for all his strange wives, which burnt incense and sacrificed unto their gods. And the LORD was angry with Solomon, because his heart was turned from the LORD God of Israel, which had appeared unto him twice

1 Kings 11:4-9

Solomon fulfilled the requirements for ministry when he was young. But he was not faithful unto death. Will you be faithful unto death? Many people despise young men and consider them immature, lustful and incapable of holding leadership positions in the church.

There are those who think that you must be above forty or fifty to hold important ecclesiastical positions. However, as you can see from this scripture, it was when Solomon was young that he was faithful to God. It was when he was old that he became unfaithful to God. This is why the concept of being faithful unto death is so important. When a young man serves the Lord, he uses his strength to overcome his lusts and deficiencies in wisdom. It is important that he maintains faithfulness unto God and unto death.

5\. Be faithful unto death: Conceal the matter until you die.

A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter.

Proverbs 11:13

If you are entrusted with secrets, facts and knowledge that you are expected to keep, you must do so until death. That is what it means to be faithful unto death. Recently, I met a man who worked personally with Kenneth Hagin for a number of years. Kenneth Hagin was a great prophet who had amazing visions of Jesus Christ and served the Lord mightily in his generation.

He told me something about Kenneth Hagin that shocked me. He said that Kenneth Hagin said he was not allowed to speak about ninety-five percent of the things he was told in his visions.

That meant that Kenneth Hagin did not reveal ninety percent of the things that Jesus told him in his visions. I was amazed. I wondered how Kenneth Hagin was able to keep those secrets till he died. Perhaps, that is why we have not had any such visions in our lifetime. If you do not have a faithful spirit, you will reveal important secrets that you are supposed to conceal for your lifetime. It takes faithfulness to conceal secrets. It takes faithfulness to conceal a matter!

I remember a pastor who was entrusted with an important secret. He went around telling people what he knew. In my estimation, the act of leaking confidential information which was entrusted to him, greatly lowered him. This fellow was demoted shortly after it was discovered that he was such an unfaithful person.

6\. Be faithful unto death: You will receive a reward for being faithful unto death.

Fear none of those things which thou shalt suffer: behold, the devil shall cast some of you into prison, that ye may be tried; and ye shall have tribulation ten days: BE THOU FAITHFUL UNTO DEATH, and I will give thee a crown of life.

Revelation 2:10

Your reward for being faithful unto death will be a crown. A crown is the most precious thing you can ever receive in heaven. Indeed on this earth, few people will ever wear a crown. In England, the Queen is the only person who wears the crown.

If you wear a crown, you are highly exalted, privileged and honoured. God intends to grant you a very special and privileged place in heaven for being faithful. You will be distinguished from other people just because of your faithfulness. You will actually wear a crown in heaven. If wearing a crown is such a big deal on earth, can you imagine what it would be like to wear a crown in heaven?

Your faithfulness will by all means be mentioned on the judgment day. Your faithfulness is being watched and marked every day. In a number of stories that Jesus told, faithfulness was a key condition for receiving rewards.

It means your ability to be faithful and loyal all the way to the end of the race is critical for your reward. Notice that good and faithful servants are acknowledged. Also notice that faithful and wise stewards are noticed. In both cases, faithfulness is spotted and rewarded. These qualities must be maintained until death.

His lord said unto him, Well done, GOOD AND FAITHFUL SERVANT; thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.

Matthew 25:23

And the Lord said, WHO THEN IS THAT FAITHFUL AND WISE STEWARD, whom his lord shall make ruler over his household, to give them their portion of meat in due season?

Luke 12:42

7\. Be faithful unto death: Give your unwavering support to help another man until you die.

And if ye have not been FAITHFUL IN THAT WHICH IS ANOTHER MAN’S, who shall give you that which is your own?

Luke 16:12

There are those who think they must never work for someone. They say, “Why should I work under someone? Why should I be under someone?” Sometimes it is better to be the leg of an elephant than the head of an ant. There are many times that working for someone is far better than working for yourself.

It takes a lot of skill to lead yourself! If you do not have the skill to lead yourself, guide yourself, invest in yourself and fight for yourself, you will not be good at working on your own. God humbles us by causing us to need other people. In the end you will discover that you cannot be perfect without the brethren.

God having provided some better thing for us, that THEY WITHOUT US SHOULD NOT BE MADE PERFECT.

Hebrews 11:40

8\. Be faithful unto death: Be a constant and personal worker unto death.

For this cause have I sent unto you TIMOTHEUS, who is my beloved son, and FAITHFUL in the Lord, who shall bring you into remembrance of my ways which be in Christ, as I teach every where in every church.

1 Corinthians 4:17

All my state shall TYCHICUS declare unto you, who is a beloved brother, and a FAITHFUL minister and fellowservant in the Lord:

Colossians 4:7

With ONESIMUS, a FAITHFUL and beloved brother, who is one of you. They shall make known unto you all things which are done here.

Colossians 4:9

Paul had several people who worked for him. In his letters, you get to know his personal workers. People like Timotheus, Tychicus and Onesimus were mentioned as his personal workers and helpers. In each mention of them, he never forgot to say that they were faithful people. It is the one characteristic that you need in a personal worker. Be faithful unto death is to run well as a personal worker until you die.

Be faithful unto death and be a good personal assistant until you die.

Be faithful unto death and be constant and unwavering in your love, devotion and service until you die.

Be faithful unto death and be very loyal until you lie down on the bed to die and disappear from this world. Make sure you never change until you go out of this world.

9\. Be faithful unto death: Stay constant and qualify for a role in the next level of ministry.

Faithfulness is always a factor in choosing people. When you have to choose somebody, you must always look at the person’s faithfulness. God looked at Paul and considered that he would be a faithful person. That is why he chose Paul. He did not choose Paul because he was clean and sinless. Obviously, Paul was not clean. He was a blasphemer. He was a murderer. He was an opposer of the ministry. But he was faithful!

And I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who hath enabled me, for that HE COUNTED ME FAITHFUL, PUTTING ME INTO THE MINISTRY;

1 Timothy 1:12

Just as God chose people to work in the ministry because of their faithfulness, Apostle Paul directed that those to be chosen for the next level of ministry should be faithful people. You must respect this quality of faithfulness just as God respects this quality of faithfulness. Paul was quick to look for faithfulness in others.

And the things that thou hast heard of me among many witnesses, THE SAME COMMIT THOU TO FAITHFUL MEN, who shall be able to teach others also.

2 Timothy 2:2

10\. Be faithful unto death: Do not tell lies until you die.

A faithful witness will not lie: but a false witness will utter lies.

Proverbs 14:5

A faithful person does not tell lies. The presence of lies reveals the beginning of unfaithfulness. Many Christians do not realize how serious it is to tell a lie. All forms of falsehood, deception, pretence, hiding, misleading comments, incomplete stories are deceptions and indications of unfaithfulness. Are you a faithful man? You are a faithful man when you do not tell lies.

The moment you tell a lie, you reveal that you are not a faithful person!
