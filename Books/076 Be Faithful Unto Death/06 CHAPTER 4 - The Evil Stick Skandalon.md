### CHAPTER 4\

The Evil Stick: Skandalon\
===============================================

Ye did run well; who did hinder you that ye should not obey the truth?

Galatians 5:7

Many people run a race and get to a place where they suddenly fall and crash to the ground. This is what you have to prevent! Make sure you do not fall to the ground and crash out of the race. You will make it to the very end! You can be faithful unto death!

I have some difficult news for you. There are some evil sticks called Skandalon that have been put out to trip you up. These little sticks, Skandalon, are wicked tricks of the enemy that destroy people who have a great future. Jesus predicted this and was worried for the people in this world who would experience Skandalon.

Woe unto the world because of OFFENCES (SKANDALON)! for it must needs be that offences come; but woe to that man by whom the offence cometh!

Matthew 18:7

“Skandalon” is a Greek word that refers to a snare or a trap stick. A Skandalon will disturb you and make you trip, fall and crash in the middle of your race. Skandalon is the stumbling block that topples you in the middle of your race to victory.

As you continue in your service to the Lord, you will surely come across the occasion to stumble, to fall and to be unfaithful. A Skandalon will disturb you, upset you, unsettle you and confuse you.

Jesus said that Skandalon must come. In other words, there is no way to escape from Skandalon. There will by all means be the occasion to fall, the occasion to stumble, the occasion to trip and the occasion to crash to the floor in the midst of your race to the finish line.

Skandalon is the reason why people who are faithful today are not faithful tomorrow.

It is important to be aware of what is going to happen to you. There are going to be great opportunities to completely deviate from your set course.

God’s plan for you is for you to be faithful unto death in such a way that you will get a crown at the end of this life.

Skandalon is also translated as “offence”. Offence is the reason why many people’s faithfulness is not unto death. Offence is something that is predicted by Jesus. Every single person is going to experience Skandalon.

Satan will attempt to put an impediment in your way which will cause you to trip and fall and to abandon your course.

Satan has sent out scouts whose aim is to cause you to distrust and desert someone you need to trust.

Satan sends out scouts to see if there are any openings and opportunities to trip some-one.

There will be opportunities for you to become one of those who accuse endlessly with false accusations and slander.

There will be opportunities for you to become one of those who leave the church with bitterness and cursing.

There will be opportunities for you to become one of those who are proud and arrogant.

I recently saw one of my sons in ministry threatening people with strong words. He had grown in pride, that is how come he could speak such “great” words.

There will be opportunities to become a dangerous son who can turn around and blaspheme.

You must watch out for different types of Skandalon that will try to topple you, trip you up and make you crash to the ground in the middle of your race. Just as Paul said, “Ye did run well; who did hinder you that ye should not obey the truth?” (Galatians 5:7)

Who Is Prone to “Skandalon”?

The Skandalon is the magical power that brings you crashing down and out. Skandalon works on those who have the tendency to fall down. You must be prone to falling before a trap stick or a stumbling block will bring you down. Skandalon is the trap stick and the stumbling block that will remove you from the race.

Satan is looking for those who are prone to falling and tripping. Not everyone is prone to fall in the same way. Some people are overweight and unstable and therefore easily stumble and fall. Some people are so skinny and light that they easily topple over. Also, people run in such a way that they easily fall. Some people wear shoes that make them trip more easily. Some people wear dresses that get caught in the ground and it makes them unstable. Some people wear gowns that get caught under their feet and can cause them to trip. Some people are old and unstable. Indeed, older people fall more easily. Many older people die from falling down.

It is these tendencies to trip and to fall that demonic scouts are seeking to find in you. When demonic powers find that you have certain characteristics they are more sure that their Skandalon trap stick will be successful in bringing you down.

In Rick Joyner’s Final Quest, he states that, 1“Scouts and smaller companies of demons with such names as rejection, bitterness, impatience, unforgiveness and lust were sent in advance of this army to prepare for the main attack. These smaller companies and scouts were much fewer in number but they were no less powerful than some of the larger divisions of evil spirits.”1

1-1 The Final Quest by Rick Joyner, Nashville, Tennessee: Thomas Nelson Incorporated, 2001; Extract from Chapter 1: “The Hordes of Hell Are Marching”

Remember what Paul said, I have finished the race! I have finished my course!” Paul did not crash out of his race and make nonsense of all that he had done in the past. None of the Skandalon, stumbling blocks or trap sticks that were placed before Paul as he ran his race were able to topple him and make him go out of the race.

Who are those in ministry who are prone to falling away easily? Who are the ones who are being identified as best targets for the deployment of Skandalon?

Satan’s main aim is to push you out of your race of ministry.

1\. People who have a close relationship with an offender can fall out of the race easily.

An offender is someone who is into various forms of sin and evil. His life and practices offend the Holy Spirit. He practices malice and wickedness.

You cannot be faithful unto death if you are closely connected to people who practice malice and wickedness.

I once met someone who was determined and committed to serve the Lord. However, she became friends with an “offender.” Through this offender, her ministry was completely derailed and destroyed. Your friends and associations will definitely have an effect on your ability to be faithful and loyal unto death.

Once you are happy in the company of an evil person, his evil ideas and wicked thoughts will pass like a leaven into your life. In the Bible, malice and wickedness are described as leaven. Malice and wickedness are things that spread.

Therefore let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, neither with THE LEAVEN OF MALICE AND WICKEDNESS; but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

1 Corinthians 5:8

I once encountered someone who loved me but made friends with someone who hated me. With time, the hatred of the person who hated me seeped into the life of the person who loved me. As the years went by, the person who loved me became as hateful, wicked and ungrateful as the one who hated me.

Look at your linkages. Look at your connections. Look at your deep friendships. Look at your boyfriends and look at your girlfriends. They are people who take you away from your calling and prevent you from being loyal and faithful to the end.

Anyone who has malicious and evil friends is a candidate for Skandalon. It is going to be difficult for someone who has a close relationship with an offender to be faithful unto death. You will soon trip and fall very low because of your bad friendships.

But whoso shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me, it were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were drowned in the depth of the sea. Woe unto the world because of offences (Skandalon)! For it must needs be that offences come; but woe to that man by whom the offence cometh!

Matthew 18:6-7

As you can see from this scripture, offence, Skandalon, comes through somebody. That is why he says WOE TO HIM THROUGH WHOM OFFENCES COME. If you have a friend who is a dangerous person, you will be infected with his evil. There are people who will connect you to immorality. They will sin with you or they will cause you to sin.

There are men who are disloyal and they will infect you with their evil way of speaking. By associating with and befriending disloyal people, you will be affected and infected with the evil of disloyalty, unfaithfulness and treachery. You will not be able to be faithful unto death because of your evil associations and friendships.

That is why it is important to find the right marriage partner. I remember a sister who desperately wanted to be close to me in the ministry. She had been in the church since she was a baby. All that she wanted to do was to serve the Lord with me.

When she finished university, she met and married an erratic personality. As she was entering into this relationship, I thought to myself, “I hope my daughter will be able to continue serving the Lord as she desires.” But it was not to be so. Within two years of her marriage, she was sitting in front of me with her husband informing me that they were leaving the church. I marvelled as I watched this young lady who was so desirous to serve in the ministry being taken away by this erratic man.

2\. People who are prone to unforgiveness can fall out of the race.

To whom ye forgive any thing, I forgive also: for if I forgave any thing, to whom I forgave it, for your sakes forgave I it in the person of Christ; LEST SATAN SHOULD GET AN ADVANTAGE OF US: for we are not ignorant of his devices.

2 Corinthians 2:10-11

Do you really want to be faithful unto death? Do you want to be loyal to the very end or do you want to join the list of those who could not make it to the very end?

Anyone who has a pattern of unforgiveness will definitely be a target for the devil. A person who is prone to unforgiveness is someone who has, over the years, shown a pattern of not forgiving people.

I cannot see an unforgiving person surviving the race for faithfulness to the end. There is no way you will even reach the end of your marriage with your pattern of unforgiveness.

Most people who struggle with unforgiveness claim that they have forgiven everyone. Usually, it is not so! Unforgiving people disconnect from relationships because they bear many grudges against many people.

A person who maintains relationships over many years is someone who practices forgiveness. Anyone who is prone to unforgiveness opens the door to satan and makes himself easy prey for the Skandalon stick. The evil Skandalon stick of unforgiveness is what will make you trip, fall and crash out of the race.

Watch out for those who continually break friendships and relationships. You must fight to keep all important friendships and relationships. That is the sign that you are a forgiving and loving person. As you see from the scripture in 2 Corinthians 2:10-11, satan gains the upper hand in anyone who does not forgive.

3\. People who are prone to bitterness can fall out of the race.

Looking diligently lest any man fail of the grace of God; lest any root of bitterness springing up trouble you, and thereby many be defiled;

Hebrews 12:15

Bitterness is perhaps the greatest condition that prevents a person from being faithful unto death. An analysis of rebellious and fallen pastors reveals a consistent strain of bitterness and unforgiveness in them.

Bitterness is a convoluted, complicated and deep-seated version of unforgiveness. Bitterness is the spiteful, malevolent, malicious and vengeful version of unforgiveness. A person who is deeply rooted in unforgiveness becomes bitter. Bitterness is something that troubles and defiles many people.

I have watched many pastors become hurt and bitter after they were corrected. I remember different occasions where pastors were rebuked and never recovered from their hurt and offence. Because they were types of people that did not let go of issues, a simple correction became the door through which satan destroyed them.

I remember a lady who was dismissed from her job and grew in bitterness until she became a traitor walking in the footsteps of Judas himself. Imagine a female Judas!

I remember an ignorant young man who had never worked in the secular world before. He had no idea about the rules of employment. When he was asked to follow the rules of employment, he became so upset that he launched himself into a bitter fight against me. His response of bitterness and hatred was so unusual that satan took advantage of him and destroyed him.

I remember a pastor, who was transferred from his city to another place, who became so bitter that he sought to destroy me with all his strength and might.

Be careful that you never walk in convoluted, vengeful, implacable, rancorous unforgiveness and hatred. Bitterness will destroy you! Bitterness will destroy many people!

4\. People who are prone to pride and arrogance can fall out of the race.

Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall.

Proverbs 16:18

Pride was the first sin of the universe. Lucifer fell because of his arrogance and pride towards God. He lifted himself up against Almighty God Himself. He was marked out for destruction and punishment because of his pride. Those who are prone to falling out, follow this exact same pattern of pride and stubbornness.

People who are proud are marked out for destruction. Some people are more prone to pride than others. When people get a little older, they are stiffer and more rigid in their responses and behaviour. Stiffness and rigidity are found in people who are proudly unbending in any matter.

Young people are more flowing and easily impressionable. You cannot impress or convince an older person as easily as you would a young person.

I have watched a number of pastors grow up from university into their mid-life when they began to attack me and insult me. When they were younger, I laid hands on them and appointed them as pastors. Then I ordained and consecrated some of them to the ministry. When they were younger, they were humble and appreciative of the honour bestowed on them. As they grew older, they were filled with arrogance and pride. They became easy targets for the Skandalon stick and crashed out of the ministry.

5\. People who are prone to envy and jealousy will not finish their race.

Now at that feast he released unto them one prisoner, whomsoever they desired. And there was one named Barabbas, which lay bound with them that had made insurrection with him, who had committed murder in the insurrection. And the multitude crying aloud began to desire him to do as he had ever done unto them. But Pilate answered them, saying, Will ye that I release unto you the King of the Jews? FOR HE KNEW THAT THE CHIEF PRIESTS HAD DELIVERED HIM FOR ENVY. But the chief priests moved the people, that he should rather release Barabbas unto them.

Mark 15:6-11

People who are filled with envy cannot be faithful to the end. The jealousy and envy that they have will cause them to stumble and crash out of the race. Satan is looking for those who are harbouring these terrible traits.

Envious people are the next candidates for the evil trap stick that brings them crashing down to the ground.

Spite and resentment at seeing the success of another is what is called envy. When a person harbours resentment towards another person’s success, he is harbouring a dangerous evil called jealousy.

Although it is not apparent, an envious person can harm and kill you if he gets the chance. Fortunately, most envious and jealous people do not get the opportunities they wish for. They earnestly hope for the downfall of the person they envy.

Unfortunately, envious people are often near or close to the person they are envious of. They often have to express admiration and praise for someone that they actually resent. Envious people are often forced to express grudging admiration. They wished things would not work as well as they did.

I once discovered the secret envious ill intents of a fellow minister. He had hoped for my downfall for many years and had expressed his secret desires for my destruction on several occasions. Unfortunately for him, the person to whom he expressed his wicked expectations and hopes told me all about it. It gives you goose bumps when you think of how much people want your downfall.

It is a dangerous thing to be near someone who wishes you evil but is forced to admire you grudgingly.

People want what you have! It is painful to them to see you enjoying advantages, privileges with success after success. Most of the time envious people do not have the opportunity to directly strike at you but if they had the opportunity, they would.

Envy and jealousy are terrible evil traits and characteristics. Do not be found harbouring these evil snakes. The scripture says, “Wrath is cruel, and anger is outrageous; but who is able to stand before envy?” (Proverbs 27:4)

When Jesus was on earth, He was murdered by pastors. The Pharisees did what you would expect murderers and armed robbers to do. They killed the Holy One of God. They murdered a man who had only done good in His lifetime. Jesus had preached, taught, healed, delivered and blessed the multitudes endlessly. The response of the people to the good works of Jesus was to kill Him like an animal.

Indeed, a great evil had transpired because these men had allowed jealousy and envy to grow in them.

6\. People who are filled with the love of money are prone to falling and crashing out of the race of ministry.

For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

1 Timothy 6:10

Those who love money and are very conscious of money will eventually fall to the evil that destroys many. You will discover that some pastors are serving God only because of financial benefits. It is important to root out all forms of covetousness from your heart. Out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaks about money all the time.

O generation of vipers, how can ye, being evil, speak good things? for out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh.

Matthew 12:34

There was a pastor who told me that he had received the gift of writing books. I was excited to hear of this gift. When he finally released his first book, it was all about money and about becoming rich. One day, I had the privilege of watching a video of him preaching in a church and I noticed again that his preaching centred around finances and money.

Eventually, this brother was transferred to another region. The bishop who was supposed to receive him under his council objected to having this pastor within his domain because he felt the pastor was too conscious of money and made too many financial demands on the church system.

Eventually, when this brother had the opportunity to be a travelling preacher, he ministered mostly about money and riches. Eventually, this fellow got into various conflicts with various churches, making outrageous claims for money which he had not earned nor deserved. Indeed, it was as though he was wearing a t-shirt on which was written, “I love money, the root of all evil.”

Money consciousness is an evil thing. You must not be money conscious in your relationships. You must not be money conscious at work! Making financial demands and having very high expectations of financial rewards can be offensive to your employer.

One day, a brother went up to his employer and said, “I think I have worked very hard this year.” He presented figures and facts and explained to his boss why he should receive a certain amount of money as a Christmas bonus. The boss was silent and immediately began to make arrangements to lay off this fellow. The brother was surprised that his apparently innocent request for a well-deserved Christmas bonus caused him to be laid off; but the boss was offended by his request for a bonus.

I think it is offensive to God when you make certain claims. The truth is we do not deserve anything. Make sure you take away the scent of the love for money from your ministry. It is repugnant to God and to man that you are filled with the love of money.

Money is not the root of all evil. The love, the liking and the desire for money is the root of all evil. Check your heart’s desires, dreams and visions. Check your casual conversations and make sure they are not filled with expressions of desire for more and more money.

I once had the privilege of reading a letter that a pastor wrote to his friend. In the letter, he shared his dream of becoming a millionaire. There were no dreams of catching an anointing. There were no visions of dreams of building the church. There were no visions and dreams of winning souls and having crusades. It was all about making millions of dollars and becoming rich. These pastors were filled with covetousness and the root of all evil.

Are you Elisha or Gehazi?

What are you? Are you an Elisha or a Gehazi? Elisha is the example of a person who desired the anointing. Gehazi is an example of someone who desired money.

Elisha wanted two portions of the anointing!

Gehazi, on the other hand, wanted two portions of clothing and provisions. Gehazi was a man of covetousness. He was a wicked, covetous servant of the Lord and his ministry ended up on a rubbish heap. The love of money is the root of your not being able to make it to the end. If you want to be faithful to the end, you must set aside the love of money.

And Naaman said, Be content, take two talents. And he urged him, and bound two talents of silver in two bags, with two changes of garments, and laid them upon two of his servants; and they bare them before him.

2 Kings 5:23

7\. People who are impatient will fall out of the race.

Be patient therefore, brethren, unto the coming of the Lord. Behold, the husband-man waiteth for the precious fruit of the earth, and hath long patience for it, until he receive the early and latter rain.

James 5:7

Today, there is a lot of talk about the Faith Movement. Faith is so important in serving God. Without faith you cannot achieve anything and you cannot accomplish much for God. Without faith there would be no miracles, there would be no preaching and there would be no power.

That ye be not slothful, but followers of them who through faith and patience inherit the promises.

Hebrews 6:12

Remember that faith works with patience and through patience. Faith without patience will not achieve much.

Impatient people will never live to see the great things that God has for them. As you mature in God, He will give you the grace to develop patience.

Every minister must learn to suppress his restlessness in life and ministry. Every minister must learn to suppress annoyance when confronted with delay.

God is doing great things and He will surely bring to pass all His promises in your life.

Conclusion

Be faithful unto death! Close the door to satan! Protect yourself from anything that will remove you from this great ministry. Faithfulness is a good thing! Loyalty is a good thing! Be faithful unto death and you will receive the crown of life.

To the making of many books there is no end!
