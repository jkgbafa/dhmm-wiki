CHAPTER 12\
--------------------------------

### Pray Every Day: The Qualities You Need\

1. **You need DILIGENCE to pray every day.**  It takes diligence to sleep early and wake up early.  If you do not sleep early, you will never wake up early.  Most people hang around and end up sleeping very late.  When you sleep late you cannot wake up to pray. When you do not sleep enough, you will be sleepy throughout the prayer time.    

> **That ye BE NOT SLOTHFUL, but followers of them who through faith and patience inherit the promises.**

**Hebrews 6:12\**

2. **You need FAITH to pray every day.** Only men of faith pray.  Faithless people do not pray to God.  If you are a man of faith you will be a man of prayer.  Anyone found praying for hours, alone, in a room is a man of faith.  

> **For EVERY ONE THAT ASKETH RECEIVETH; and he that seeketh findeth; and to him that knocketh it shall be opened.**

**Luke 11:10**

3. **You need PERSISTENCE to pray every day.**  Sometimes you need to pray for long hours.  Some problems require “asking”, some require “seeking” and some require “knocking”!  This is because the different types of problems require different kinds of solutions. Some problems need five minutes of prayer and others need five hours.  You need to be RELENTLESS in your prayer. The devil is relentless in his efforts to destroy you. You need to be relentless in your efforts to pray.  

> **And I say unto you, ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.**

**Luke 11:9\**

> **Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.**

**Matthew 17:21 **

4. **You need IMPUDENCE to pray every day.** Impudence reveals confidence and faith in relation to God.\
Being impudent means that you are bold and being daring to ask for certain things.\
From today, be daring in your prayer.  Be bold in your requests! God is listening to you! God will answer your prayers!  When you are impudent you are shameless.  You have no shame and you ask anything that comes to your mind. Shamelessness reveals your openness and your closeness to God.  It takes shamelessness to pray properly.  Most of our heartfelt requests are shameful.  It is sometimes embarrassing to reveal what you really want or need.  Be shameless in your requests!  God is listening to you!  

> **And he said unto them, Which of you shall have a friend, and shall go unto him at midnight, and say unto him, Friend, lend me three loaves; For a friend of mine in his journey is come to me, and I have nothing to set before him?**
>
> **And he from within shall answer and say, Trouble me not: the door is now shut, and my children are with me in bed; I cannot rise and give thee.**
>
> **I say unto you, Though he will not rise and give him, because he is his friend, yet because of his IMPORTUNITY (IMPUDENCE, SHAMELESSNESS) he will rise and give him as many as he needeth.**

> **And I say unto you, Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.**

**Luke 11:5-9**
