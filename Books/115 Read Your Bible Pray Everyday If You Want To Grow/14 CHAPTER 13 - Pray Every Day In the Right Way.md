### Pray Every Day: In the Right Way\

1. **Pray every day for long hours.**  Learn how to pray for a long time!  It takes time to learn how to pray for long hours. The disciples saw Jesus praying for long hours and they wanted to learn how to do the same.  

> **And it came to pass, that, as he was praying in a certain place, when he ceased, one of his disciples said unto him, Lord, TEACH US TO PRAY, as John also taught his disciples.**

**Luke 11:1**

2. **Pray every day without pretending that you are spiritual.**  Avoid hypocrisy and pretense in prayer.  There is no need to pretend that you are prayerful. It is better to admit that you are not prayerful so that you can change.  

> **And when thou prayest, THOU SHALT NOT BE AS THE HYPOCRITES are: for they love to pray standing in the synagogues and in the corners of the streets, that they may be seen of men. Verily I say unto you, they have their reward. **

**Matthew 6:5**

3. **Pray every day in a private place.** Private and personal prayer times are more important than group prayers.  Develop yourself in private solitary prayer.  Most of the prayers Jesus prayed were in private.  A man of prayer is a man who can spend hours alone with God.  

> **But thou, WHEN THOU PRAYEST, ENTER INTO THY CLOSET, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret; and thy Father which seeth in secret shall reward thee openly. **

**Matthew 6:6**

4. **Pray every day without useless repetitions. **

> **But when ye pray, use not VAIN REPETITIONS, as the heathen do: for they think that they shall be heard for their much speaking.**

**Matthew 6:7**

5. **Pray every day with the attitude of thanksgiving.** Always thank God in your prayers.  Enter His gates with thanksgiving. It is important to do this every day. Start with praise and end with thanksgiving.

> **...Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name.**

**Matthew 6:9**

6. Pray for the work of God every day. Pray for the kingdom of God every day. Do not be selfish!  Do not only pray for yourself. Pray for the kingdom of God! Pray for the church! Pray for the work of God. 

> **THY KINGDOM COME. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven.**

> **Matthew 6:10**

7. **Pray for the will of God to be done in your life every day. ** If you do not pray for the will of God, circumstances will direct you away from the will of God.  Jesus prayed for three hours for the will of God to be done.  Why would Jesus have to pray for three hours for the will of God to be done if it did not matter?  It means the will of God would not have happened if Jesus had not prayed.  We experience many things that are not the will of God because we did not pray for the will of God.  From today, start praying that God’s will be done in your life.  Pray for it every day so that only the will of God will happen.   

> **Thy kingdom come. THY WILL BE DONE in earth, as it is in heaven.**

**Matthew 6:10**

8. **Pray every day for money, for food, for clothes, for cars, for jobs, for good health and for anything that you need.** God will supply all your needs.  God will  take care of you.  God will help you.  He is powerful.  He will make you rich. He will make you wealthy.  You will lack nothing because you pray every day.

> **Give us this day our daily bread.**

**Matthew 6:11**

9. Confess your sins every day!  Confess your known sins and your unknown sins.  Not a single day goes by without you sinning. You need to confess your sins every day.  Even if you are the school prefect and everybody sees you as a good person, you still have sins that you need to confess. If we say we have no sin, we only deceive ourselves.  Do not think of yourself as a good person.  Think of yourself as a sinner and confess your sins every day.  Confess your sins of commission as well as your sins of omission.  

> **And FORGIVE US our debts, as we forgive our debtors. **

**Matthew 6:12**

10. **Pray every day against evil.** Pray every day for deliverance from wickedness and temptation.  Bind the devil every single day.  Every time you speak a word binding devils and rebuking evil spirits, something takes place in the spirit realm.  The very words you speak are powerful and they resonate through the dark realms.  Demons panic when you start binding them and cursing them in the name of Jesus.  Lift up firewalls between you and all demonic forces.  Block satanic surprises and attempts to kill you.  Bind satan! Bind devils!  Bind evil spirits!  Bind fallen angels!  Bind animal spirits! Bind entities and creatures that are hiding in the trees and shadows around your house.  Curse the works of darkness.  God is listening to your word and binding them in heaven.  

> **And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: ….**

**Matthew 6:13**
