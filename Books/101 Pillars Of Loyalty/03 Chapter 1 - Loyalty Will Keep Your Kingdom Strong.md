### Chapter 1\

Loyalty Will Keep Your Kingdom Strong\
-----------------------------------------------------------

A king who is LOYAL and true will keep his power. LOYALTY will keep his kingdom strong.

Proverbs 20:28 (ERV)

Most of the Book of Proverbs is attributed to King Solomon. Proverbs 20:28 was therefore written by the wisest man in the world. The wisest man in the world promoted the teaching on loyalty. The wisest man in the world, who became the richest man in the world, declared that loyalty will keep your kingdom strong.

King Solomon gives us many good reasons to teach about the wisdom of loyalty and faithfulness. Loyalty and faithfulness are revealed as a kind of wisdom that creates and builds stability into whatever you are doing.

Wisdom is the principal thing for your ministry. Therefore, the wisdom of loyalty is a principal thing for your ministry. Through the wisdom of teaching on the subjects of faithfulness and loyalty, you will create and build a large, stable, worldwide ministry. Do not be misled by miserable, treacherous men who do not want you to teach about “Those who leave you”, “Those who pretend”, “Those who accuse you”, “Those who are ignorant”, “Those who forget” and “Those who slander”. Emphasize your teachings on “Those who make shipwreck”, “Those who are dangerous sons”, “Those who rebel”, “Those who are proud”, “Those who dishonour you”, “Those who are offended” and “Those who are devils”. These teachings are the wisdom of loyalty and disloyalty.

It is Solomon, the wisest man on earth, who showed us that loyalty will keep a kingdom strong. If you want to build a big and stable church, then feed your congregation on these amazing subjects.

You cannot keep your church strong, stable, and united without emphasizing faithfulness and loyalty. Solomon had this wisdom and wrote the Book of Proverbs. Solomon used his great wisdom to build a world-famous kingdom and ministry.

In Gibeon the Lord appeared to Solomon in a dream by night: and God said, Ask what I shall give thee…. Behold, I have done according to thy words: lo, I have given thee a wise and an understanding heart; so that THERE WAS NONE LIKE THEE BEFORE THEE, NEITHER AFTER THEE SHALL ANY ARISE LIKE UNTO THEE.

1 Kings 3:5, 12

A king who is loyal will keep his power. A pastor who is loyal will maintain his relevance and his position in the ministry. A pastor who is not loyal to his church members will lose his power. A pastor who is not loyal will lose his members and his control over the flock.

Loyalty helps a king or leader to remain in power and maintain a strong position of leadership. Solomon remained in a position of strength and built a worldwide ministry through the wisdom of loyalty which he wrote about.

And Solomon reigned over all kingdoms from the river unto the land of the Philistines, and unto the border of Egypt: they brought presents, and served Solomon all the days of his life...FOR HE HAD DOMINION OVER ALL THE REGION ON THIS SIDE THE RIVER, FROM TIPHSAH EVEN TO AZZAH, OVER ALL THE KINGS ON THIS SIDE THE RIVER: AND HE HAD PEACE ON ALL SIDES ROUND ABOUT HIM. And Judah and Israel dwelt safely, every man under his vine and under his fig tree, from Dan even to Beersheba, all the days of Solomon.

1 Kings 4:21, 24-25

Loyalty makes the kingdom and the church strong. That is what Solomon sought to teach and share when he wrote Proverbs 20:28! Loyalty makes a kingdom strong! Loyalty makes a church strong! When a church is filled with disloyal people and disloyal leaders, there is confusion, criticism, murmuring and division.

LOYALTY and truth watch over the king, And he upholds his throne by LOYALTY.

Proverbs 20:28 (NASB)

Loyalty and mercy, truth and faithfulness, protect the king, And he upholds his throne by lovingkindness.

Proverbs 20:28 (AMP)

I remember a church that was once the leading light in a city. As the years went by, many people did not stay faithful and loyal to the ministry. This church became filled with critical, murmuring, and complaining people. As people complained and slandered the church (the kingdom), it grew weaker until it lost its relevance in the city.

Loyalty will keep a kingdom strong! Loyalty will keep a church strong! Do not be dissuaded by miserable men who do not want you to teach about loyalty and disloyalty. Do not be confused by losers who do not want you to describe them in your clear biblical teachings.

Do not be misled by wretched breakaway individuals who do not want you to teach about “Those who leave you”, “Those who pretend”, “Those who accuse you”, “Those who are ignorant”, “Those who forget”, “Those who slander”, “Those who make shipwreck”, “Those who are dangerous sons”, “Those who rebel”, “Those who are proud”, “Those who dishonour you”, “Those who are offended”, or “Those who are devils”.\
