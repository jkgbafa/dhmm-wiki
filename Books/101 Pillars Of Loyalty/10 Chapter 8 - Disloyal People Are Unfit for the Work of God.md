### Chapter 8\

Disloyal People Are Unfit for the Work of God\
-------------------------------------------------------------------

To the pure \[in heart and conscience\] all things are pure, but to the defiled and corrupt and unbelieving nothing is pure; their very minds and consciences are defiled and polluted. They profess to know God \[to recognize, perceive, and be acquainted with Him\], but deny and disown and renounce Him by what they do; they are detestable and loathsome, unbelieving and DISOBEDIENT AND DISLOYAL AND REBELLIOUS, AND \[THEY ARE\] UNFIT AND WORTHLESS FOR GOOD WORK (DEED OR ENTERPRISE) OF ANY KIND.

Titus 1:15-16 (AMPC)

Disloyalty and rebellion make a person worthless! What can you do with a rebellious person?

This scripture reveals how disloyalty and unfaithfulness reduce a person’s value in the most profound way. You make yourself worthless by dabbling in disloyalty, unfaithfulness, treachery, deception duplicity and wickedness!

When I think of the rebellious people I have encountered, I realize how their value has been reduced to almost zero. They are unfit for ministry work of any kind. Other sins do not have this effect of making you unfit for ministry in this ultimate and profound way. People fall into all other kinds of sins but are still able to function in the ministry. I have seen people laden with all kinds of sins, continuing to serve in the ministry.

But once people get into treachery, deception, betrayal, disloyalty, duplicity, duality, hypocrisy, pretence and wickedness they quickly become worthless and unfit for ministry work of any kind!

Most rebellious people are unable to build churches. They criticize and slander so much and yet they can do nothing much in ministry. I have watched and waited for many of my critics and haters to build something in the ministry, but alas, nothing much seems to be coming forth from them. Indeed, unfaithful people make themselves unfit for ministry.

One day, I had lunch with a great man of God from Nigeria called Archbishop Benson Idahosa. He told me something I have never forgotten.

He said, “No one should criticize me until the person has achieved 10 percent of what I have achieved.”

Then he told me something I didn’t know. He said, “70 percent of all pastors in Africa are my products or products of my products!”

And truly many of the pastors in Africa at that time were sons or products of Archbishop Benson Idahosa, directly or indirectly! Indeed, he had achieved a lot and yet many empty barrels and slanderers would criticize him bitterly, though they could not accomplish even 10 percent of his great achievements!

How to Reduce Your Value to Zero

Make yourself worthless by dabbling in disloyalty, unfaithfulness, treachery, deception duplicity and wickedness!

How good is an unfaithful person for God’s work? How good is an unfaithful wife? Her value is almost zero! The unfaithful person reduces his value to almost zero! The disloyal are unfit for work of any kind.

This is what the opening scripture above is telling us! This is an amazing scripture that gives guidance to all employers. Do not accept or keep disloyal people. They are good for nothing. They are indeed a danger that you will constantly have to protect yourself from. Read the scripture for yourself!

To the pure \[in heart and conscience\] all things are pure, but to the defiled and corrupt and unbelieving nothing is pure; their very minds and consciences are defiled and polluted. They profess to know God \[to recognize, perceive, and be acquainted with Him\], but deny and disown and renounce Him by what they do; they are detestable and loathsome, unbelieving and DISOBEDIENT AND DISLOYAL AND REBELLIOUS, AND \[THEY ARE\] UNFIT AND WORTHLESS FOR GOOD WORK (DEED OR ENTERPRISE) OF ANY KIND.

Titus 1:15-16 (AMPC)

The disloyal are unfit for employment in the government!

The disloyal are unfit for employment in private businesses!

The disloyal are unfit for employment in politics!

The disloyal are unfit for employment in the ministry!

The disloyal are unfit to be employed in any job!

Once a person becomes disloyal, he becomes worthless in the ministry. Disloyal and rebellious people are unfit for the work of God. Disobedient, disloyal and rebellious people are worthless in the house of God. You cannot do much with a disloyal person. You rather have to protect yourself from disloyal people.

Anyone who acts treacherously and moves in disloyalty is a dangerous person and is therefore worthless to you. How can a dangerous person be helpful to you? You rather need to spend time and energy protecting yourself from the traitor and mole in your midst.

1\. A disloyal person is ready to betray, likely to betray and willing to betray you. This makes him unfit for any kind of work.

2\. A disloyal person is deceptive, which makes him worthless.

3\. A disloyal person is unstable and that makes him useless to the ministry.

4\. A disloyal person is dangerous and that makes him a miserable person we don’t need around us.

5\. A disloyal person is hazardous and harmful.

6\. A disloyal person is unreliable, untrustworthy and unfit for service.

7\. A disloyal person is not what you think he is. He is worthless to you!

8\. A disloyal person has a life of secrets and mysteries; and that makes him a dangerous person.

9\. A disloyal person is a wicked man and may harm you if he gets the chance.

10\. A disloyal person is a bad man acting as a good man, and that makes him unfit for the work of ministry.

11\. A disloyal person is an impostor.

12\. A disloyal person is an evil member of your team and that makes him useless to your cause.

The scripture is very clear: disobedient, disloyal people are unfit for the work of God. Disobedient, disloyal people are worthless for good work of any kind.

Disobedient, disloyal people are worthless, even when it comes to business.

Disobedient, disloyal people are worthless in the church.

Disobedient and disloyal people are useless for your ministry.

Cast Out the Scorner!

Cast out the scorner, and contention shall go out; yea, strife and reproach shall cease.

Proverbs 22:10

This is why God wants you to cast out the scorner. He is worthless to the ministry. A scorner is useless to you. A scorner is against you, and he must go away.

God wants you to throw out the disloyal serpent in your midst. Of what use is a snake that is lying behind the curtain in the living room? It is only a danger to you. Of what use is this traitor in your camp? Cast out the scorner and strife, confusion and commotion will go away. Scorners and mockers are of no use to us in ministry. They must be discarded and gotten rid of.

Every disobedient person is also rebellious! Disobedient and rebellious people must leave.

Achan disobeyed Joshua’s instructions and he paid the price for it. He was cast out of the glorious army. He was condemned to disappear into the ground forever with his family and all those associated with him.

All the rebels of your ministry are Achans who must be counted as a loss in the ministry. They will not march to the end of the glorious call and ministry God has given you. A great heap of stones will be raised up as a memorial for all to fear. May you never be an Achan!

And Joshua said unto Achan, My son, give, I pray thee, glory to the LORD God of Israel, and make confession unto him; and tell me now what thou hast done; hide it not from me. And Achan answered Joshua, and said, Indeed I have sinned against the LORD God of Israel, and thus and thus have I done:... And Joshua said, Why hast thou troubled us? The LORD shall trouble thee this day. And all Israel stoned him with stones, and burned them with fire, after they had stoned them with stones. And they raised over him a great heap of stones unto this day. So the LORD turned from the fierceness of his anger. Wherefore the name of that place was called, The valley of Achor, unto this day.

Joshua 7:19-20, 25-26
