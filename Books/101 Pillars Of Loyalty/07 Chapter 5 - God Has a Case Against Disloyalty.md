### Chapter 5\

God Has a Case Against Disloyalty\
-------------------------------------------------------

Listen to the word of the Lord, you sons of Israel, Because THE LORD HAS A CASE AGAINST THE INHABITANTS OF THE LAND, FOR THERE IS NO FAITHFULNESS, NOR LOYALTY, Nor knowledge of God in the land.

Hosea 4:1 (NASB)

God has a case against all unfaithful people! God has a case against all disloyal people!

That is what the scripture says. I would not want God to have a case against me.

God is building a serious legal case against you for your unfaithfulness and wickedness. You are going to have to answer to God Himself for not being constant and reliable.

Why did you stop being a faithful person?

Heaven has taken notice! Heaven is building a legal civil and criminal case against you now.

God has a case against all pastors who have stopped preaching the gospel.

God made the sun, the moon and the stars! Our earth is only one of the estimated 100 billion to 3.2 trillion planets in the Milky Way.

You and I have only one duty on this earth and it is to save souls! How can we be unreliable and unfaithful to this Great Commission given to us by this great God?

What else are we doing on earth? Why and how could we abandon God’s work to do other things? No wonder God has a case against the unfaithful and disloyal.

Truly, God has a case against all pastors who have stopped emphasizing the basic truths of His Word.

God has a case against all pastors who have stopped preaching about missions to the ends of the world.

God has a case against all pastors who have stopped evangelizing.

God has a case against all pastors who have stopped winning souls.

God has a case against all pastors who have stopped visiting the sheep.

God has a case against all pastors who have stopped loving the sheep.

God has a case against those who preach another gospel.

God is watching all the wicked, wretched and miserable Judases and Absaloms in the church who have been unfaithful to their fathers. God has a case against them too. God has a case against rebellious sons!

God has a case against all orangus\* and rebels.

God has a case against all breakaway pastors.

God has a case against all Judas Iscariots.

God has a case against those who pretend.

God has a case against those who deceive.

God has a case against those who are dangerous sons.

God has a case against those who accuse.

God has a case against those who rebel.

God has a case against those who are ignorant.

God has a case against those who think they can destroy their senior pastors.

God has a case against those who are devils to their senior pastors.

God has a case against those who make shipwreck of their ministry.

This is why we teach and emphasize the subject of loyalty and disloyalty! We teach about loyalty because God has a case against anyone who dabbles in disloyalty, unfaithfulness, treachery, wickedness, slander, rebellion and duplicity. Imagine that!

This is why we teach about loyalty! We must teach, explain, preach, emphasize, and promote it - so we don’t find ourselves in a position where God Himself has a case against us.

Indeed, the Lord has a case against the inhabitants of the land, FOR THERE IS NO FAITHFULNESS, NOR LOYALTY, nor knowledge of God in the land!

\*“Orangu” is a coined term to describe rebellious persons
