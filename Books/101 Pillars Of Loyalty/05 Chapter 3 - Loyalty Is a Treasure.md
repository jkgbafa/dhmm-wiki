### Chapter 3\

Loyalty Is a Treasure\
-------------------------------------------

Many a person proclaims his own LOYALTY, But who can find a trustworthy person?

Proverbs 20:6 (NASB)

Who can find a diamond in the field? Who can find a faithful man in the church? A loyal person is a treasure in the field. Many people in church claim to be loyal but it is difficult to find a truly faithful, loyal and trustworthy person. Because of this, loyalty and faithfulness are treasures you must seek for in people. If you have a faithful person, you have a treasure! A truly loyal person is a special treasure given to you by God.

Treachery, disloyalty and unfaithfulness are common manifestations of human nature today. That is why we say, “Who can find a faithful man?” It is like saying, “Who can find a diamond for us in this field?”

Who can find a faithful man? Who can find a trustworthy person? Who can find a diamond? Who can find a treasure in this field? What do these statements mean? They mean that a faithful and trustworthy person is not a common thing.

These statements also mean that disloyalty is pervasive. You must therefore expect to experience disloyalty rather than faithfulness from human beings. This is why Jesus did not commit Himself to anyone.

But Jesus did not commit himself unto them, because he knew all men,

John 2:24

In the end, Jesus was right not to commit Himself to anyone. Judas betrayed Him! Peter betrayed Him! All the other disciples forsook Him! “And they all forsook him, and fled” (Mark 14:50). Jesus was left completely alone! And that is why He had decided that He would simply not trust any of them.

Why is it so difficult to find a faithful person? It is because the human race is polluted and destroyed by our contact with the serpent. By one man’s disobedience, sin, corruption and evil have entered into the human race.

For as by one man’s disobedience many were made sinners, so by the obedience of one shall many be made righteous.

Romans 5:19

The nature of the devil is deception, wickedness, unfaithfulness, murder, treachery, duality, duplicity, hypocrisy and betrayal. The sin and the seed of the serpent have truly entered the human race and completely destroyed everything. Human beings are now an evil race, totally dominated by deception, war, murder, treachery and all manner of evil. These sins have spread to the whole world and no part of our planet is free of the curse of satanic contamination. That is why you cannot easily find a faithful person.

Wherefore, as by one man sin entered into the world, and death by sin; and so death passed upon all men, for that all have sinned:

Romans 5:12

It is difficult to find a loyal person whom you can trust, so let us get down to teaching about it so that there will be more loyalty and faithfulness.

The advantage of pursuing, teaching, explaining, introducing and emphasizing loyalty is that it creates more and more trustworthy people. Unless you teach faithfulness and loyalty, your church will be filled with traitors, liars, pretenders, betrayers, deceivers, hypocrites, and duplicitous characters.

Loyalty is truly difficult to find! You may feel you have found loyalty in certain people but at the end of your life, you will discover that the word of God is true - “A faithful man who can find?” (Proverbs 20:6).

I will encourage you to read and teach about faithfulness because a faithful person is difficult to find. Teaching about faithfulness and loyalty will create loyal and faithful people out of the corrupted human race.

Those who forget, those who are disloyal, those who accuse you, those who pretend, those who are mad and those who are dangerous sons will multiply around you if you do not teach these truths.

Unless you teach and emphasize the word of God on this great and important subject, you will suffer at the hands of wolves, Absaloms, Ahithophels, Lucifers, and prodigal sons.

For I know this, that after my departing shall grievous wolves enter in among you, not sparing the flock.

Acts 20:29
