### Chapter 2\

Loyalty Leads to Life, Righteousness and Honour\
---------------------------------------------------------------------

One who pursues righteousness and LOYALTY, finds life, righteousness, and honour.

Proverbs 21:21 (NASB)

King Solomon received wisdom from God. Solomon’s revelation of wisdom is written in the Book of Proverbs. Solomon used his great wisdom to build a world-famous kingdom and ministry.

King Solomon revealed that it is important to pursue and seek loyalty all the time.

King Solomon revealed that pursuing loyalty will lead to a ministry with life, a ministry with righteousness, and a ministry with honour.

Many churches are dying because they did not pursue, teach, explain, or introduce the subject of loyalty to the congregation. There are three clear benefits of teaching loyalty that are listed in this amazing scripture.

1\. Loyalty leads to a ministry with life.

Pursue loyalty to God! Faithfulness to your call will extend your life so that you can fulfil your ministry. Pursue loyalty and righteousness and find life for your ministry and church.

The first benefit of pursuing, teaching, explaining, introducing and emphasizing loyalty is life! Your faithfulness to the Great Commission will make you a saviour of men! Death comes to all ministries and churches when they are not faithful to the Great Commission.

To be faithful is to be constant and persistent to the call of God! When a church moves away from the Great Commission, it changes a lot and ends up dying. All dead churches are dead because they were not faithful and loyal to the main duty of Christians, which is to win souls and build churches. Churches die because they are not loyal to Jesus and to His words. Loyalty leads to life! Loyalty will give life to your life! Loyalty will give life to your ministry!

2\. Loyalty leads to a ministry with righteousness.

Pursue loyalty and prevent the sons of your ministry from becoming devils. Jesus said, “One of you is a devil.” You must prevent your team members from becoming devils. Pursue loyalty, and righteousness will follow!

The majority of your team members will be protected from becoming devils because you teach about loyalty and disloyalty. Pursuing, teaching, explaining, introducing, and emphasizing loyalty will lead to righteousness in the leadership.

By teaching about loyalty and faithfulness, people develop a good character. They are guided away from wickedness and treachery. When you teach people loyalty, they are encouraged to remain the same, to remain faithful, and to remain good, loyal servants of the Lord.

Pursuing loyalty definitely leads to righteousness. Through the teachings of loyalty, people avoid the wretchedness of rebellion, and many people are helped to never become miserable prodigal sons.

3\. Loyalty leads to a ministry with honour.

Pursue loyalty and prevent the sons of your ministry from becoming disloyal and dangerous sons.

The chief benefit of pursuing, teaching, explaining, introducing and emphasizing loyalty is honour. Pursue loyalty and let the people in your church become those who honour fathers, those who honour pastors, and those who honour their prophets.

Demonized people do not honour their fathers. When people are not taught the Word, they quickly become influenced by demons and are ready to dishonour, to betray, to destroy and to attack their leaders.

Jesus answered, I HAVE NOT A DEVIL; BUT I HONOR MY FATHER, and ye do dishonour me.

John 8:49

Leaders who do not teach about loyalty are constantly dishonoured, disgraced and vilified by their followers. When people are taught to be faithful, they honour their parents, their pastors and their leaders. When people are taught about loyalty, they learn how to speak properly and honour those who deserve honour, instead of becoming naturally critical and independent.
