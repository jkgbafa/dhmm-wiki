### Chapter 6\

Disloyal People Are Like a Faulty Bow\
-----------------------------------------------------------

But they put God to the test and rebelled against the Most High; they did not keep his statutes. LIKE THEIR ANCESTORS THEY WERE DISLOYAL AND FAITHLESS, AS UNRELIABLE AS A FAULTY BOW.

Psalm 78:56-57 (NIV)

A faulty bow is undependable - it might look fine, but it fails when needed. Similarly, a disloyal person is an unreliable person. Years ago, bows and arrows were the weapons of choice of the people. Everyone needed his bow to be strong and accurate so that it would shoot the arrows without missing the target. All warriors depended on their bows in time of war. To have a faulty bow was to have a terrible setback, a terrible disappointment and a terrible let-down in time of need. You must not become a disappointment, a setback or a let-down to God and to His work.

There are three important characteristics of a faulty bow.

3 Characteristics of a Faulty Bow

1\. A faulty bow lacks consistency - such people do not stay true to their commitments.

2\. A faulty bow fails under pressure - like a bow that misfires, such people cannot be trusted in crucial moments.

3\. A faulty bow misses the mark - instead of aiming toward righteousness, they turn to sin and rebellion.

Becoming a faulty unreliable bow was the sin of the ancestors. Absalom and Judas are examples of our ancestors who became traitors.

God does not like it when you are like an undependable, unreliable, faulty bow! God is not happy when Christians walk in the sins of their fathers and ancestors. We must rather learn from the mistakes of the past.

The Ancestors: Absalom, Judas, Ham

Absalom: Absalom made a mistake and attacked his father. He was an unreliable son! He was a faulty bow! He was disloyal to his own father. Why should you grow up and commit exactly the same sin as your ancestors? If you become another Absalom, you have become as unreliable, disloyal and unfaithful as your ancestors.

Judas: Judas was another faulty bow! Judas was a betrayer! You must learn from Judas and never become a betrayer. The treachery of Judas has never been forgotten. Judas was untrustworthy! Judas was unreliable! Judas revealed things that Jesus would have preferred to keep private. Judas turned against Jesus Christ. Judas left the team! Judas had to be replaced! Judas was an evil man! Judas was full of the devil! Why on earth would you want to follow the way of Judas? Psalm 78 teaches us that we should not be like our disloyal and unfaithful ancestors!

Judas made his mistake and turned against the Lord to his own demise. Judas committed suicide because he crossed a line and could not return. Why would you want to do the same thing that Judas did?

Years ago, a man called Uncle James said to me, “The devil has no new tricks. He keeps using the same tricks, and people keep falling for exactly the same things.” The devil keeps turning people into Absalom and Judas and people keep falling into the same sins that their ancestors fell into.

They do not turn to the Most High; THEY ARE LIKE A FAULTY BOW. Their leaders will fall by the sword because of their insolent words. For this they will be ridiculed in the land of Egypt.

Hosea 7:16 (NIV)

HAM: Ham was yet another faulty bow. An inconsistent and unreliable son! A son should be the most supportive and dependable person. But Ham was not consistent, supportive or reliable. Ham tried to destroy his own father who had just saved him from the flood.

Ham, the son of Noah, attempted to humiliate, shame, embarrass and disgrace his father. What he got in return was a debilitating curse. God had made Noah into a great father of the whole earth. It was dangerous to attack or humiliate Noah. Yet, that is exactly what Ham did.

Today, the descendants of Ham are struggling with a million times more disgrace, poverty, lowness, inferiority, defeat, humiliation, hunger and shame. Ham sowed a seed of disgrace and shame and he and his descendants have reaped shame and disgrace.

Almost all of Ham’s descendants are constant and continuous beggars on the world scene. Why on earth would anyone want to make the same mistake? This is why the psalmist laments about people following the sins of ancestors like Ham.

May God help us not to walk in the sins of disloyalty and unfaithfulness of our ancestors, Ham, Absalom and Judas!

May you not become an unreliable and inconsistent bow!
