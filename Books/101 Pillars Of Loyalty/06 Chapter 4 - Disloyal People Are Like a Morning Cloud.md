### Chapter 4\

Disloyal People Are Like a Morning Cloud\
--------------------------------------------------------------

What shall I do with you, O Ephraim? What shall I do with you, O Judah? FOR YOUR LOYALTY IS LIKE A MORNING CLOUD and like the dew which goes away early.  Therefore I have hewn them in pieces by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of My mouth; And the judgments on you are like the light that goes forth.  For I DELIGHT IN LOYALTY RATHER THAN SACRIFICE, And in the knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings.

Hosea 6:4-5 (NASB95)

Hosea, the prophet, lamented about Israel’s loyalty and faithfulness to God. He said their loyalty was like a morning cloud! Morning clouds create the mist and the fog which disappear in a few hours. Ephraim was not able to stay loyal for long. They would make commitments and within a short while, change everything they had said.

They would say: “I love you, I will be with you, I belong to you, I like you, you are my special friend”; but within a short time, everything would change. That is what it means to have morning cloud loyalty.

Today, the whole world is filled with people whose loyalty is like a morning cloud. This is why we must teach about loyalty.

The advantage of pursuing, teaching, explaining, introducing and emphasizing loyalty is that it causes more people to have longer lasting loyalty and faithfulness.

What can you do with a disloyal person? Very little! Ephraim had very little loyalty; a loyalty that departed after a slight rain!

What can you do with a man who will be faithful for a few months? Can you marry him? How many women would be happy to have a man whose loyalty is like a morning cloud? If you, a woman, are not satisfied with morning cloud loyalty, do you think God is? It is time to rise and emphasize this truth of faithfulness and loyalty. We are children of a King. He has entrusted things to us. Our loyalty must not be like a morning cloud.

We must be committed for years! We must be committed to one another for many years! We must be committed to one another for life! Indeed, we must be committed all through our lives!

Beware of disloyalty! You cannot build a church with people who are committed to morning cloud loyalty. Because of the lack of commitment, faithfulness, devotion and loyalty, God says “Therefore I have hewn them in pieces by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of My mouth”. God has only strong rebukes for you because your loyalty is like a morning cloud.

For I desire LOYALTY rather than sacrifice, And the knowledge of God rather than burnt offerings.

Hosea 6:6 (NASB)

It is amazing that God prefers loyalty to sacrifices. We all know how God is impressed with sacrifices and yet He says that He prefers faithfulness and loyalty to sacrifice. What an amazing revelation!
