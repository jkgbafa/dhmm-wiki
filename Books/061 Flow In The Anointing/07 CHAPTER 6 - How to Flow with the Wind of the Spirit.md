### CHAPTER 6 \

How to Flow with the Wind of the Spirit\
-------------------------------------------------------------

**And suddenly there came a sound from heaven as of a rushing mighty wind, and it filled all the house where they were sitting. And there appeared unto them cloven tongues like as of fire, and it sat upon each of them. And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance.**

**Acts 2:2-4**

**The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it cometh, and whither it goeth: so is every one that is born of the Spirit.**

**John 3:8 **

**1. Flow with the mighty rushing wind.  The Holy Spirit is the mighty rushing wind.  In the scripture above, we see the characteristics of the mighty rushing wind of the Spirit. **

1. The wind of the Spirit is mysterious. Thou “canst not tell whence it cometh!” 

2. The wind of the Spirit is powerful in movement. It “bloweth”!  

3. The wind of the Spirit is independent.  It “bloweth where it listeth”!

4. The wind of the Spirit is invisible.  No one can see the wind!

5. The wind of the Spirit is varied in direction.  No one can tell “whither it goeth”! 

6. The wind of the Spirit makes a sound that tells you where He is going. “Thou hearest the sound thereof”!

7. The wind of the Spirit brings on the fire of God.  Wind stirs up fire and when the Holy Ghost fell there were cloven tongues of fire!  

**What the Wind of the Spirit Can Do**

**Whatever winds can do is what the Holy Spirit can do.  If you study the effect of winds, you will see the great things that are accomplished by winds. The Bible makes mention of the east wind and the west wind. By understanding the effect of winds, you can understand what it is like if the wind of the Holy Spirit blows on you. Expect each and every one of these powerful effects of the wind of the Spirit.  **

**1. Notice the wind of the Spirit causing things to be wither.  The wind of the Spirit can cause a withering of anything that displeases God. **

And I saw in my dream, and, behold, seven ears came up in one stalk, full and good: And, behold, SEVEN EARS, WITHERED, THIN, AND BLASTED WITH THE EAST WIND, sprung up after them: 

Genesis 41:22-23

**The seven ears were withered and thin because of the east wind that blew on them. God’s enemies will become thin and will wither because of the work of the Spirit against them. **

**2. Notice the wind of the Spirit driving away demonic powers.  The wind of the Spirit can blow away demonic powers. **

Then Pharaoh called for Moses and Aaron in haste; and he said, I have sinned against the Lord your God, and against you. Now therefore forgive, I pray thee, my sin only this once, and intreat the Lord your God, that he may take away from me this death only. And he went out from Pharaoh, and intreated the Lord. AND THE LORD TURNED A MIGHTY STRONG WEST WIND, WHICH TOOK AWAY THE LOCUSTS, and cast them into the Red sea; there remained not one locust in all the coasts of Egypt.

Exodus 10:16-19

**Locusts speak of demonic powers.  The wind of God blew away the locusts which had devoured the land of Egypt.  Whatever has eaten the harvest of souls in your ministry will be blown away by the mighty rushing wind of the Spirit.  The wind of the Spirit in your life is going to blow away the demonic powers that have invaded your church.  **

**3. Notice the wind of the Spirit blowing away death.  The wind of the Spirit can blow away the plague of death.   **

And God remembered Noah, and every living thing, and all the cattle that was with him in the ark: and GOD MADE A WIND TO PASS OVER THE EARTH, AND THE WATERS ASSWAGED; The fountains also of the deep and the windows of heaven were stopped, and the rain from heaven was restrained; And the waters returned from off the earth continually: and after the end of the hundred and fifty days the waters were abated.

Genesis 8:1-3

**Death covered the whole earth in the days of Noah. Every living thing died because of the waters.  It took the wind of God to blow away the waters of death.  Whatever represents death in your life is blown away by the Holy Spirit!  The Holy Spirit is driving away death from your life and ministry. **

**4. Notice the wind of the Spirit making a way for you.  The wind of the Spirit can make a way for you. **

**In the days of Moses the children of Israel were trapped between the Red Sea and the Egyptians. There was no way for them. When the wind began to blow, God made a way through the waters of the Red Sea.  God will make a way for you because of the wind of the Spirit.  The wind of God will make a way for you when you seem to be trapped. **

And Moses stretched out his hand over the sea; and the Lord caused the sea to go back by A STRONG EAST WIND ALL THAT NIGHT, AND MADE THE SEA DRY LAND, and the waters were divided. And the children of Israel went into the midst of the sea upon the dry ground: and the waters were a wall unto them on their right hand, and on their left.

Exodus 14:21-22

And the Lord shall utterly destroy the tongue of the Egyptian sea; and with HIS MIGHTY WIND SHALL he shake his hand over the river, and shall smite it in the seven streams, and MAKE MEN GO OVER DRYSHOD. And there shall be an highway for the remnant of his people, which shall be left, from Assyria; like as it was to Israel in the day that he came up out of the land of Egypt.

Isaiah 11:15-16

**5. Notice the wind of the Spirit drying up evil things in your life.  The wind of the Spirit can dry up the evil springs of the enemy. **

**The wind of the Holy Spirit will dry up the spring of the enemy.  Some things need to be dealt with by drying up. Most of the evil sexual habits need to be dealt with by drying up. They are not things that can just stop. They are habits and tastes that have been developed over the years. Most of these habits and tastes are demonically empowered and enforced.  The wind of the Spirit will dry them out and ease you out of all sexual perversions and deviations from normality.  **

Though he be fruitful among his brethren, an east wind shall come, THE WIND OF THE LORD SHALL COME UP FROM THE WILDERNESS, AND HIS SPRING SHALL BECOME DRY, and his fountain shall be dried up: he shall spoil the treasure of all pleasant vessels.

Hosea 13:15

**6. Notice the wind of the Spirit destroying the enemy’s forces.  The wind of the Spirit can utterly the destroy enemy’s forces. **

**Whatever is fighting you can be destroyed by the mighty Holy Spirit! The Holy Spirit is a wind and is a mighty rushing wind that can destroy enemies and empty their land.  When God is displeased with the church, he can empty it by the mighty rushing wind of His Spirit.  Many churches are empty because the Spirit of God has blown the members away. Mercy! **

Thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will raise up against Babylon, and against them that dwell in the midst of them that rise up against me, A DESTROYING WIND; And will send unto Babylon fanners, that shall fan her, and SHALL EMPTY HER LAND: for in the day of trouble they shall be against her round about.

Jeremiah 51:1-2

Let them be as chaff before the wind: and let the angel of the Lord chase them.

Psalms 35:5

**7. Notice the wind of the Spirit bringing in the supplies that you need.  The wind of the Spirit can bring in our needs and supplies.  **

**Through the wind of the Spirit, Moses was able to feed two million people.  The power of God moved mightily and brought in all the supplies he needed to feed two million people everyday.  This was a mighty miracle. Expect to receive mighty financial miracles of provision through the powerful rushing wind of the Spirit!  Pray for the Holy Spirit!  Pray for the manifestations of the Holy Spirit. Pray that the wind of the Holy Spirit will move!**

And Moses gat him into the camp, he and the elders of Israel. And THERE WENT FORTH A WIND FROM THE LORD, AND BROUGHT QUAILS FROM THE SEA, and let them fall by the camp, as it were a day’s journey on this side, and as it were a day’s journey on the other side, round about the camp, and as it were two cubits high upon the face of the earth. And the people stood up all that day, and all that night, and all the next day, and they gathered the quails: he that gathered least gathered ten homers: and they spread them all abroad for themselves round about the camp.

Numbers 11:30-32

**8. Notice the wind of the Spirit that comes with angels.  The wind of the Spirit blows in the presence of angels. Wherever the Holy Spirit is, you find angels.** In this amazing scripture, we see the cherubs hiding on the wings of the wind.  As the Holy Spirit flows as a wind, the cherubs and other angels come along and minister to the saints.  Many storms are not just natural occurrences.  Many storms contain the movement of angels and other spirits.

In my distress I called upon the Lord, and cried to my God: and he did hear my voice out of his temple, and my cry did enter into his ears. Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations of heaven moved and shook, because he was wroth. There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, and fire out of his mouth devoured: coals were kindled by it. He bowed the heavens also, and came down; and darkness was under his feet. And HE RODE UPON A CHERUB, AND DID FLY: AND HE WAS SEEN UPON THE WINGS OF THE WIND.

2 Samuel 22:7-11
