## The Spirit of Foolishness

> Reprove not a scorner lest he hate thee…

Proverbs. 9:8

> He that reproveth a scorner getteth to himself shame.

Proverbs. 9:7

Many of life’s opportunities are discovered with good advice.

> Without counsel purposes (opportunities) are disappointed: but in the multitude of counsellors they are established.

Proverbs 15:22

> Every purpose is established by counsel: and with good advice make war.

Proverbs 20:18

However, not everybody wants advice or can handle it. The cardinal sign of the spirit of foolishness is found in the response of the foolish person to wisdom: He gets angry when he is advised.

A fool’s reaction to advice will give him away.In fact, the fool will hate you for giving him sound advice. This is the spirit of foolishness.

> … fools despise wisdom and instruction.

Proverbs 1:7

Being a pastor, I have learnt not to advice certain people in my church. Any attempt to counsel them will be misinterpreted.There are also many parents who can no longer counsel their children, because the spirit of foolishness is embedded in the children.I would not be surprised if many people spoke to the prodigal boy. Perhaps, his father spoke to him. His mother and brother may also have counselled him. But he was foolish and refused to listen.Uncles and aunties were called in. Pastors spoke. Bishops added their voice. But to no avail!The spirit of foolishness makes you deaf to advice and counsel.A married woman became extremely familiar with another man. Her husband and another relative advised her not to get too close to this other man.She got so angry with this relative, and has hated this person to this day. Fools get angry when they are advised.She insisted that she was not having any extra-marital affairs. But her husband suspected foul play.When she was confronted again concerning the paternity of one of her children, she got even more outraged.You see, what was happening was that, someone was making the mistake of advising a fool.After several years however, she admitted that she had unfortunately had an affair with this man. She went on to confess that one of her children was actually the child of this other man.Everyone was shocked! Remember, this woman became very angry when she was advised.

> But ye have set at nought all my counsel, and would none of my reproof.

Proverbs 1:25

The spirit of foolishness makes you hate advice. It makes you hate your counsellor. When you shut out advice and godly counsel from your life, you have shut the door to great opportunities and blessings!Do you have feelings of resentment towards your pastors? Do you get angry with your parents when they are advising you?Then perhaps you have the spirit of foolishness!
