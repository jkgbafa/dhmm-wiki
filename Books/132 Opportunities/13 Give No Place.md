## Give No Place

Cast out the spirit of evil independence.

Overcome the spirit of selfishness.Do not yield to the spirit of a wild goose chase.Defeat the spirit of ungratefulness.You have one life and you cannot afford to waste it.Dear Friend, determine not to waste GOLDEN OPPORTUNITIES given to you by God!
