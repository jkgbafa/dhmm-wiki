### Preface

After teaching on the subject frugality, the Lord stirred me up to teach on the tendency of some people to waste or misuse the good opportunities, which God gives them.This inclination, which leads Christians to destroy and waste their lives, is even misguiding some ministers today.At the Lighthouse Cathedral, we have a pet name for a person who moves out of his good position or opportunity to become a rebel. We call him an “ORANGU”.Anyone who misuses money, opportunities, or resources is wasteful and needs to break out of this cycle of failure.This book is dedicated to the extinguishing of this destructive trait in our lives. May you value your God-given opportunities.
