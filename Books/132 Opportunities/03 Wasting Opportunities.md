## Wasting Opportunities

The word “opportunity” means “favourable time, occasion or set of circumstances”. Many people in life lose a lot of good opportunities that come to them.

They do not only waste their money, but also their lives, resources and opportunities. Such people are living wasteful lives.One way that people lose good opportunities that come to them is by being extravagant. When you live beyond your means or spend money in excess, you have an extravagant lifestyle. This is a waste of good opportunity.Jesus told the story which has come to be known as the ‘Parable of The Prodigal Son’.

> ...A certain man had two sons: And the younger of them... took his journey into a far country, and there wasted his substance with riotous living. And when he had spent all, there arose a mighty famine in that land; and he began to be in want.

Luke 15:11-14.

### Five Good Opportunities Wasted by the Prodigal Son

1\. He wasted the opportunity to benefit from the advice his father gave him.

Just like Judas, most Christians have wasted the advice and preaching they received from their pastors. After many years have gone by, they have to join the song writer in singing, “Wasted years, wasted years, oh, how foolish!”A man who was dying at the age of ninety-six was asked by his children what he wanted written on his tombstone after his death.He replied, “Write that I was four years old.”He explained that he was four years old because he only got saved when he was ninety-two years. As far as he was concerned, the only time that his life had been useful was the four years after he found Christ. He had just wasted ninety-two years of his life!The prodigal son wasted a number of good opportunities. Some people are privileged to get the best advice, yet it seems they are unable to make good use of it. Judas had the best counselling, the best teaching, the best anointing, and the best example of glory and power; but he wasted it and went to Hell.Though we are living two thousand years after the death and resurrection of Jesus, we still believe in Him. Judas did not only hear Jesus. He actually saw Him and lived with Him.Do not waste the opportunity of good advice that comes your way!

2\. He wasted an opportunity to be great.

The loving father had a party for the prodigal boy. But note that he told the older son, “...all that I have is thine.” Meaning that all that was left of his father’s property belonged to the older son.Yes, there was a party for the prodigal son when he returned home, but he had already squandered what his father had bequeathed him.So, if he would get anything, it would depend on the benevolence of his elder brother.The opportunity to have a good start in life had slipped away from him forever.In the same way, you could be in a good church, have a good pastor who teaches you the Word of God, and take it all for granted. Be careful not to waste the opportunities God is giving you!

3\. He wasted his position.

He wasted his position as a son, and ended up a servant! He wasted his money and all the wealth that had been given to him.As a nation, my country Ghana is well-positioned to receive a lot of sunshine and rain. We have all that we need to reap an abundant agricultural harvest.Yet we import a lot of food, and are very far from being affluent. On the other hand, some European countries have very little land and a harsh, cold climate.In winter there are long nights and short days with little sunshine. Seeds cannot germinate when the ground is frozen for long periods. Yet most European countries produce so much grain that they have to throw some away.In Geneva I was surprised to find a car park under a lake, going down several floors. Under a lake! These people are using every inch of space.Some people have little, yet they do very much. But others have so much yet they do so little!You need to see where God has positioned you and make the most of it. You must stay in your God-given position. I have been in the ministry for several years now and have watched some young pastors move out of position. They probably don’t realize that success is very much related to correct positioning.Let me ask you some questions now. What is wrong with being a younger son? What is wrong with being a younger brother? What is wrong with being an associate pastor? What is wrong with being a branch pastor?After all, you are still a fruitful member of the family. That is what matters!But this prodigal boy was unhappy that he was not the senior. He wanted to be on his own. He wanted to be in charge. So he moved out. What a terrible mistake!Some pastors had the opportunity to be great and fruitful in ministry, when they were associates or well-positioned ministers.However, just because they were assistants, they moved out of their good positions and became oddities in ministry. Often this comes out of a spirit which says, “I’m as good as he is!”When does a minister become an oddity in ministry? This is when a fruitful and successful minister becomes a struggling and hustling rebel.This is exactly what the prodigal boy did. He moved out of his good God-given position as a younger aristocrat brother and ended up as a servant.In our local Ghanaian dialect we call this phenomenon “M’ate me ho.” (I have cut myself off.)Many churches have not been able to grow because assisting pastors have been plagued with this wasteful spirit of M’ate me ho.A Nigerian pastor friend of mine said something which vividly describes this phenomenon.He observed — IT IS BETTER TO BE THE LEG OF AN ELEPHANT THAN THE HEAD OF AN ANT.Indeed, the leg of an elephant is bigger and greater than the head of an ant, even though it is just a leg.God wants us to be as fruitful as possible in this short life of opportunities.Are you positioned in a church or in a family or in a job? Sit well in your ‘chair’. There are many people standing by ready to replace you!Do not waste your position.

4\. He wasted his inheritance.

There are many people who inherited properties and lands but made nothing out of them.I remember the story of a rich man who left an inheritance for his widow and wasteful sons. There was a dispute in court over some of the property. Whilst the case was still in court, one of his sons took an air-conditioner from the house and deposited it at a drinking bar.Then he told the owner of the bar to deduct the cost of his drinks from the cost of the air-conditioner each time he came to the bar.He greedily drank away that whole air-conditioner!And believe it or not — He did the same thing with all the other air-conditioners in the house!This was a boy who was ready to misuse and waste valuable things. Just like the prodigal boy, he made nothing out of the inheritance from his father.Let us come to the concept of spiritual inheritance.If you are like a son or daughter to your pastor, an inheritance of anointing and spiritual gifts will naturally pass to you — just as property naturally passes from a father to a son.Receive all the anointing and gifts you can. Don’t waste your great spiritual inheritance! Elisha received Elijah as his father and called him “father”.

And Elisha saw it, and cried, My father, my father… 2 Kings 2:12.

Elisha had a biological father called Shaphat. But he still called Elijah, “father”. The anointing naturally passed from Elijah to his spiritual son, Elisha.My brother, make sure you receive all of your spiritual inheritance.

5\. He wasted his sonship.

> …Father, I… am no more worthy to be called thy son: Make me as one of thy hired servants.

Luke 15:18,19.

Although the prodigal boy was a son, he wasted his position as a son and became a servant. Because he had wasted all his inheritance, he now had to apply for the position of a servant in his own father’s house.This was a man who was born, bred and belonged to a house. All the servants knew him to be a son. Because he had wasted what originally belonged to him, he knew that he was not worthy to have the position of a son anymore.Instead he pleaded to be regarded as a servant.A son and a servant are two different people.

> The servant abideth not in the house for ever: but the Son abideth ever.

John 8:35.

I have two sons. There are other people in my house who are not sons to me. These are two different classes of people altogether. Everything that I have belongs to my children.When I die, the things I own will not go to servants who work in the house — they are for my children. Just as when my father died, he left everything for us his children.At the High Court my father’s will was read, “I leave to my dear wife who has suffered with me this… and that…, for my children Dag, Beatrix, Karen, Marilyn, Julia Heward-Mills…”None of his many servants were mentioned in the will.Do not walk away from valuable sonship relationships. When you leave the position God has given you, it is very difficult to return to that point again. The prodigal boy wasted his sonship.
