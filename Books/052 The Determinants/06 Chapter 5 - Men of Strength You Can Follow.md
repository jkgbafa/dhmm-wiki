###  Chapter 5

Men of Strength You Can Follow\
----------------------------------------------------

**I returned, and saw under the sun, that the race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the STRONG, neither yet bread to the wise, nor yet riches to men of understanding, nor yet favour to men of skill; but time and chance happeneth to them all.**

**Ecclesiastes 9:11**

**1. Joseph was a man of strength.** He survived many accusations.  May you survive all the suspicions and accusations levelled against you!  You are a winner and you have the upper hand. Whatever campaign is going on to smear your name and bring you to shame will not succeed.  You are declared a winner!  

JOSEPH is a fruitful bough, even a fruitful bough by a well; whose branches run over the wall: The archers have sorely grieved him, and shot at him, and hated him: But HIS BOW ABODE IN STRENGTH, and the arms of his hands were made strong by the hands of the mighty God of Jacob; (from thence is the shepherd, the stone of Israel:)

Genesis 49:22-24

**2. Samson was a man of strength.** He crushed the enemy.  May you crush the enemy in your life!  May you become a man of strength! Let your enemies be afraid of you! Let the sound of your name bring fear into the heart of your enemy!   

And the lords of the Philistines came up unto her, and said unto her, Entice him, and see wherein his great strength lieth, and by what means we may prevail against him, that we may bind him to afflict him: and we will give thee every one of us eleven hundred pieces of silver.

Judges 16:5

**3. Saul and Jonathan were strong. ** They won wars for Israel. May you win many wars for Jesus! 

SAUL AND JONATHAN were lovely and pleasant in their lives, and in their death they were not divided: they were swifter than eagles, they WERE STRONGER THAN LIONS.

2 Samuel 1:23

**4. David was strong.** David was a fighter and a killer of the enemy.  May your enemies perish because of your strength!

For THOU HAST GIRDED ME WITH STRENGTH to battle: them that rose up against me hast thou subdued under me.

2 Samuel 22:40

**5. Uzziah was strong.**

And UZZIAH prepared for them throughout all the host shields, and spears, and helmets, and habergeons, and bows, and slings to cast stones. And he made in Jerusalem engines, invented by cunning men, to be on the towers and upon the bulwarks, to shoot arrows and great stones withal. And his name spread far abroad; for he was marvellously helped, till he WAS STRONG. 

But when he was strong, his heart was lifted up to his destruction: for he transgressed against the Lord his God, and went into the temple of the Lord to burn incense upon the altar of incense.

2 Chronicles 26:14-16
