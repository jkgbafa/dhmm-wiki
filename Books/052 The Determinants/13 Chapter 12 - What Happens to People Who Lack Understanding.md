### Chapter 12\

What Happens to People Who Lack Understanding\
-------------------------------------------------------------------

**And Jesus said, are ye also yet WITHOUT UNDERSTANDING?**

**Matthew 15:16**

**1. A man without understanding must be given hard commands and instructions. **

Be ye not as the horse, or as the mule, which have no understanding: whose mouth must be held in with bit and bridle, lest they come near unto thee.

Psalms 32:9

You must be hard on someone who lacks understanding.  A person who lacks understanding is stubborn.  A person who lacks understanding is slow to turn and slow to yield.  A person who lacks understanding cannot be spoken to gently. He understands nothing.  Do not invite him for an executive meeting.  He will not fit in.  He is of a different cut. He must be forced to comply and brought into subjection. 

However, if you are dealing with a man of understanding, you must not use force.  A man of understanding will be driven away by the use of force. 

In my work as a leader, I have encountered many people who lack understanding. They just have to be told what to do. 

You cannot leave anything to chance when you are dealing with someone who lacks understanding. A man that lacks understanding is like a horse or a mule that must be controlled. He must be controlled with force and strength because he will not understand what he must do.  

You cannot explain a concept or a vision to a man without understanding and expect him to know what to do.  He needs clear instructions to be able to do well. A man who lacks understanding is not necessarily an evil person. He works best with commands, instructions and strong directives. You cannot expect a man who lacks understanding to know what to do.  You must always ask yourself whom you are dealing with.  Is this a person who needs commands or is this a person whom you can leave to work things out?  

**2. A man without understanding does not naturally know what he must do.  **

And of the children of Issachar, which were men that HAD UNDERSTANDING of the times, TO KNOW WHAT ISRAEL OUGHT TO DO; the heads of them were two hundred; and all their brethren were at their commandment.

1 Chronicles 12:32

**3. A lack of understanding makes you unable to occupy important positions. **

Give therefore thy servant AN UNDERSTANDING HEART TO JUDGE THY PEOPLE, that I may discern between good and bad: for who is able to judge this thy so great a people?

1 Kings 3:9

A lack of understanding makes you unable to occupy important positions.  When a person without understanding is put in charge of things, he will destroy everything.  This is why Solomon prayed for understanding when he was made the head of Israel.  It is only with understanding that your feet can fit into the big shoes of honour.  

I once put some pastors in charge of an existing church.  Because they lacked understanding they began to quarrel amongst themselves. They began to bicker among themselves. They were in constant contention about who was more important and who should preach.  It was sad to watch as these immature pastors displayed a gross lack of understanding.  They did not understand that they were all the same and were all being given the privilege of being in charge of something great that existed before they came on the scene.  They did not understand that the greatness they were enjoying was not of their own doing.  It is sad when you put a person without understanding into an honoured position. 

Vashti was someone who did not understand her great position.  She had gotten used to the glory of the throne. She did not understand that her greatness was only because she was married to the king.  She did not understand that the king had a choice. It was her privilege to be called his wife.  People without understanding must not be put into high up positions. They will simply mess things up.  

**4. A lack of understanding makes you not to be chosen.   **

GOOD UNDERSTANDING GIVETH FAVOUR: but the way of transgressors is hard.

Proverbs 13:15

This scripture clearly teaches us that having a good understanding will cause you to receive favour.  A man was over fifty years old and had to choose a new wife because his first wife had gone to be with the Lord. He was not interested in any of the pretty young girls who were all around him.  The people in his life tried to get him to marry but he simply did not want to marry any of the pretty fresh virgins who were dying for him to choose then.  

One day, he chose an older, weather-beaten lady who no one expected him to choose. Everyone was shocked.  “Why did you choose this woman with a big stomach when there are all these fresh-looking ladies in the church?”  

He simply answered, “It is about understanding. These young ones will not understand my life. They do not understand a man and they will not understand an older man. I want someone who understands me and understands what I want.”  Indeed, the lady he married was like a weather-beaten eagle. But with time, it was clear that he had chosen well because the new weather-beaten lady with the big stomach understood the needs of the man she had married.  

**5. A lack of understanding will not allow you to be near or close to important people. **

Be ye not as the horse, or as the mule, which have no understanding: whose mouth must be held in with bit and bridle, LEST THEY COME NEAR UNTO THEE. 

Psalms 32:9

A lack of understanding will not let you be close to important people. If you want to be close to an important person, you must be a man of understanding.  A person without understanding will not know why you are eating chicken.  A person without understanding will not know why you have a certain type of car. A person who lacks understanding will not know why you have a certain amount of money.  A person without understanding will not appreciate why you have certain people working with you. 

A person without understanding will not understand why you have a bodyguard. A person without understanding will not understand why you come and go at the time you do.  A person without understanding will criticize things he does not understand.  You do not want to be near a person without understanding. You will feel uneasy. He will never understand why you have the privileges you have. You will have a thousand meetings to explain things but he will still not get it.  The Bible is clear, “lest they come near unto thee.”  

And Azariah the son of Nathan was over the officers: and ZABUD the son of Nathan was principal officer, and THE KING’S FRIEND:

1 Kings 4:5

A lack of understanding will not allow you to be a friend of the great. 

Your presence brings uneasiness. When Ham came close to Noah, he only criticized his father.  Why would you want someone like that near you?  King Solomon had many workers but we only hear of Zabud as the king’s friend.  Zabud had certain qualities that made Solomon choose him.  Solomon chose him and brought him closer than a normal employee.  

**6. A lack of understanding makes you less valuable than others.  **

And in all matters of wisdom and understanding, that the king enquired of them, he found them ten times better than all the magicians and astrologers that were in all his realm.

Daniel 1:20

When you have a greater understanding you are more valuable. A lack of understanding makes you unable to hold senior and elevated positions.  Although nurses and doctors study similar things in school, doctors have a far higher understanding of the same subjects. Both doctors and nurses study anatomy, physiology, medicine, surgery, pharmacy, etc. But doctors have a far greater understanding of these same subjects.  Doctors are therefore paid much more than nurses.  When you have a greater understanding of something you are more valuable.  

The young men in Babylon were found to be ten times better in understanding and wisdom.  They were ten times more valuable.  They were elevated by the king and brought close and made to work in positions that were far higher than those of ordinary slaves.  

**7. A lack of understanding makes you proud and argumentative. **

Now the name of the man was Nabal; and the name of his wife Abigail: and she WAS A WOMAN OF GOOD UNDERSTANDING, and of a beautiful countenance: but the man was churlish and evil in his doings; and he was of the house of Caleb. . . .

And Nabal answered David’s servants, and said, WHO IS DAVID? AND WHO IS THE SON OF JESSE? there be many servants now a days that break away every man from his master.

1 Samuel 25:3, 10

Abigail was a woman of good understanding.  Her husband, Nabal, did not have that characteristic.  She is the one who understood that David and his men were the reason why they were so prosperous and blessed. Nabal could not see the connection between David’s activities and his prosperity. She was the one who understood that they owed him something.  

People who lack understanding are ungrateful and argumentative. They will have long discussions and arguments because they simply cannot see through the maze. They do not understand how things work.  They do not understand how things are achieved.    They are like brute beasts. Discussions do not lead anywhere.  Meetings take forever.  Arguments are heated and longwinded.
