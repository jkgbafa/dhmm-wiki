### Chapter 1\

What Are Determinants?\
--------------------------------------------

**I returned, and saw under the sun, that the race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the strong, neither yet bread to the wise, nor yet riches to men of understanding, nor yet favour to men of skill; but time and chance happeneth to them all.**

**Ecclesiastes 9:11**

The determinants are the characteristics and the virtues that determine the outcome of everything you do.  Every area of life is therefore affected by determinants. Both spiritual and non-spiritual things are governed by determinants.  

1. The outcome of your life is governed by the determinants. 

2. The outcome of your ministry is governed by the determinants.

3. The outcome of your business is controlled by the determinants.

4. The outcome of your education is determined by the determinants. 

5. The outcome of your marriage is determined by the determinants.

6. The outcome of your project is determined by the determinants.

7. The outcome of your relationships is determined by the determinants.

8. The outcome of your journey is determined by the determinants.

9. The outcome of this season is determined by the determinants. 

10. The outcome of the crisis is determined by the determinants. 

11. The outcome of your leadership is determined by the determinants.

12. The outcome of your pastoral work is determined by the determinants.

13. The outcome of your calling is determined by the determinants. 

14. The outcome of your career is determined by the determinants. 

15. The outcome of your friendships is determined by the determinants. 

16. The outcome of your job is determined by the determinants. 

17. The outcome of your apostleship is determined by the determinants. 

18. The outcome of your singing career is determined by the determinants.

19. The outcome of your crusade is determined by the determinants.

20. The outcome of your children is determined by the determinants. 

**And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.**

**Romans 8:28**

Usually a number of factors are responsible for the success of any endeavour. It takes more than one thing to make anything work well.  That is why the Bible says that “All things work together for good to them that love God.”  Good is accomplished by a number of things working together and not by one isolated factor.  

**A Common Mistake**

Most people are impressed by one feature of a successful person.  For instance, if a singer is successful, people may be impressed with her voice. If a woman is successful, people may be impressed with her strength of character.  People may be impressed with a boxer’s strength.  A successful pastor may be noted for his preaching style. A common mistake is to think that “one thing” has given rise to these successes.  However, that is never the case.  

The boxer you are impressed with may also be diligent, hardworking, consistent and even prayerful.  He may also have been trained by a very good coach.   

The pastor you are impressed with may have many hidden strengths, talents and practices that you do not know of.  Since most of the pastor’s life is hidden from your view, you are not likely to know all the other things that are working together for good to happen.     

**I returned, and saw under the sun, that the race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the strong, neither yet bread to the wise, nor yet riches to men of understanding, nor yet favour to men of skill; but time and chance happeneth to them all.**

**Ecclesiastes 9:11**

Ecclesiastes 9:11 is a simple scripture which teaches and declares that speed, strength, wisdom, understanding and skill usually determine the outcome of anything. This scripture also teaches that the common determinants of life can be set aside because time and chance could neutralize the effects of these determinants. God can intervene and cause you to have far more than you deserve. A powerful curse could also change the outcome you expect.

Indeed, how true that is!  There are people who work hard and fast but are still very poor. There are many excellent singers who have amounted to nothing in this world.   There are many powerful preachers who do not have much of a crowd to preach to.  There are many excellent footballers who do not have a team to play in.  It takes more than just being a good player to have a good outcome in your career. 

Ecclesiastes 9:11 is a beautiful scripture and it reveals that speed often determines the winner of a race. (However, time and chance once set aside all the known determinants of a race, when the slowest swimmer in an Olympic Game won the gold medal. All the other swimmers were disqualified by a false start and the slowest and poorest swimmer was the only one left to swim the several lengths and win the gold medal without a challenger).   

Ecclesiastes 9:11 is a beautiful scripture because it reveals that strength usually determines the outcome of a battle (However, time and chance have occurred on several occasions and unexpected people, without much strength, have won amazing battles.  Goliath was far stronger than David but the young boy killed the giant).   

Ecclesiastes 9:11 is a beautiful scripture that teaches that wisdom and understanding are common determinants of prosperity.  It reveals how people who have bothered to give themselves to learning and growing in understanding will prosper. (However, time and chance have also been known to wipe out the effect of these determinants.  There are many learned professors who are very poor whilst some uneducated footballers are millionaires).  

Ecclesiastes 9:11 is an amazing scripture because it shows how skill is a determinant of favour.  Your skill determines how much people will choose or select you for anything.  (Indeed, there are many skilled footballers, singers and dancers who are not chosen in spite of their talents).   

We must therefore accept the revelation of Ecclesiastes that these things are the common determinants of life on earth. Speed, strength, wisdom, understanding, skill, time and chance are the common determinants.  You must also be aware of the fact that there are things that can undo the effect of these powerful determinants.  

This book is a study on the determinants identified by Solomon.  It is important to know and understand the determinants identified by King Solomon. It is important to use these determinants to move your life forward. To ignore the power of the determinants is to ignore what usually governs and controls life on this earth.  

The Bible is full of God’s wisdom and direction for our lives.  God’s word is a prophecy that you will do well to take heed to.  When you take heed to the beautiful scriptures, you will become a wise man.  

**But continue thou in the things which thou hast learned and hast been assured of, knowing of whom thou hast learned them; and that from a child thou hast known THE HOLY SCRIPTURES, which ARE ABLE TO MAKE THEE WISE unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus.**

**2 Timothy 3:14-15**
