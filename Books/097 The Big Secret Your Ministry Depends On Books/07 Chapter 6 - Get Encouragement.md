### Chapter 6\

Get Encouragement\
---------------------------------------

And there was great joy throughout the church that day as THEY READ THIS ENCOURAGING MESSAGE.

Acts 15:31 (NLT)

There are books that will encourage you and guide you in the ministry. Make sure you find them. God will bless you greatly as you read encouraging messages found in books. Few people can encourage pastors and leaders. This is because there are not many successful pastors and leaders who have survived the storms and crises of ministry. There are also not many people who understand what is happening to them and who can explain the ministry to you.

Kings are lonely people. Leaders are lonely people. David encouraged himself because there were not many people who could encourage the king. How does a leader encourage himself? One way is through books!

A minister is supposed to encourage himself because there is often no one who can encourage him. Few people understand the real pressures and tensions a leader experiences.

No one loves you as you expect or hope to be loved.

And David was greatly distressed; for the people spake of stoning him, because the soul of all the people was grieved, every man for his sons and for his daughters: BUT DAVID ENCOURAGED HIMSELF IN THE LORD HIS GOD.

1 Samuel 30:6

Loneliness is a normal experience for people in the ministry! Books can be the answer to your loneliness and problems in the ministry. End depression in your life through books! Assure yourself that you are on the right path through books! Books have a great effect on your well-being and stability.

I remember praying many times in great discouragement and despair. For many hours, I have paced up and down in the house, seeking God and not understanding my struggles and difficulties. I have been in anguish many times, wondering and not knowing whether God was angry with me or whether He liked me or not. I’ve always wanted God to like me and choose me. I’ve only worked for God so that He would like me and be pleased with me. If He is not pleased with me, then what is the point?

Books have been a source of amazing comfort and reassurance. I have often been amazed at what I find in a book. When I discover a revelation or read a testimony of another fellow minister, I am amazed and encouraged to press on.

And there was great joy throughout the church that day as THEY READ THIS ENCOURAGING MESSAGE.

Acts 15:31 (NLT)
