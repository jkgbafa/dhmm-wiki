### Chapter 10\

Joshua and Books\
--------------------------------------

THIS BOOK OF THE LAW shall not depart out of your mouth, but you shall meditate on it day and night, that you may observe and do according to all that is written in it. For then you shall make your way prosperous, and then you shall deal wisely and have good success.

Joshua 1:8 (AMPC)

Joshua the famous general was successful through a book.

This book of the law! This book! This book!

Take your church into new territories through books.

Achieve what Joshua achieved in ministry. Take people to a new land!

Joshua’s ministry depended on a book. He was told directly about the book he needed to read and study. The secret of success for Joshua’s ministry was a book. From the very beginning, God pointed out a book to Joshua.

The need for knowledge that comes from books cannot be overemphasized. The poverty of your ministry, both in numbers and in finance, is a result of a lack of knowledge and a lack of skills.

Why are ministries not successful? Why are generals not successful at war?

Often, they are not successful because of a lack of knowledge, a lack of skills and laziness! These three will always feature among the causes of the problems you have.

A book will give you the knowledge needed for success in ministry. This is why Joshua was told to meditate on the books. Success is created by reading books, understanding books and implementing things you find in books.

You are not likely to find a greater key to moving forward than the key of reading, studying and meditating on the books that God brings to you. Go right now and find the book that will change everything about your ministry.

1Martin Luther, the Catholic priest, was changed completely by reading a book. He was forced to read and study the Bible because he had to lecture from it. When he did, he was shocked to find out scriptures that changed him and the world forever. He found out that we are saved by faith and not by works. He found out that if we believe in our hearts and confess with our mouths, we shall be saved.1 These amazing truths were discovered just because a priest was forced to read his Bible. Can you imagine the change that will come into the world, if more people read the books in front of them and available to them?
