### Chapter 5\

Get a Blessing\
------------------------------------

BLESSED IS HE THAT READETH, and they that hear the words of this prophecy, and keep those things which are written therein: for the time is at hand.

Revelation 1:3

Get a blessing for your life and ministry through a book. You need a blessing to push you forward. Let me show you an easy way to get a blessing. Get a book! There is a blessing in a book. There is a famous blessing for a person who reads!

Blessed is he that readeth! How true that is! God will bless the readers! Anointed books have a blessing upon them. Blessed is he that readeth!

Anointed books bring blessings and advancement into your life as you read them. Transform your ministry, your life and your church, by receiving a blessing from books! Blessed is he that readeth!

Perhaps the greatest blessing that ever came to me after my salvation, came to me through the reading of certain books. Blessed is he that readeth!

There are many curses everywhere. Curses are like landmines scattered all over the place. There are curses for almost every sin in this world. There are curses for lying, curses for stealing, curses for dishonouring parents, curses for mocking fathers and also curses for disobedience. It is almost impossible to live without stepping on one of these curses. We therefore need things that bring blessings into our lives. You need something that will advance you out of the accursed place and push you out of range of the curse. You need something to neutralize the curse in your life! I am happy to announce to you that a blessing will come into your life through reading books. Blessed is he that readeth!

May you obtain great blessings in your life as you read God-ordained books.

You will defeat and neutralize the curses of continual frustration, demotion, defeat, sickness, failure and loss that are working in your life.

A blessing will come into your life and ministry as you read this book!
