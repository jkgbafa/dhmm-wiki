### Chapter 11\

John and Books\
------------------------------------

And I saw another mighty angel come down from heaven, clothed with a cloud: and a rainbow was upon his head, and his face was as it were the sun, and his feet as pillars of fire: AND HE HAD IN HIS HAND A LITTLE BOOK OPEN: and he set his right foot upon the sea, and his left foot on the earth, And cried with a loud voice, as when a lion roareth: and when he had cried, seven thunders uttered their voices. And when the seven thunders had uttered their voices, I was about to write: and I heard a voice from heaven saying unto me, Seal up those things which the seven thunders uttered, and write them not... And I went unto the angel, and said unto him, Give me the little book. And he said unto me, Take it, and eat it up; and it shall make thy belly bitter, but it shall be in thy mouth sweet as honey. AND I TOOK THE LITTLE BOOK OUT OF THE ANGEL’S HAND, AND ATE IT UP, AND IT WAS IN MY MOUTH SWEET AS HONEY: AND AS SOON AS I HAD EATEN IT, MY BELLY WAS BITTER. AND HE SAID UNTO ME, THOU MUST PROPHESY AGAIN BEFORE MANY PEOPLES, AND NATIONS, AND TONGUES, AND KINGS.

Revelation 10:1-4, 9-11

You must find the book that God is supernaturally bringing to you. You must eat up this book and it will have a supernatural effect on your life and ministry.

Your ministry is about to start working. Your church is about to start working and growing.

The greatest revelation of what a book can and will do for you is found in the book of Revelation! Many people have browsed through books that had little impact on their lives.

The passage of scripture above teaches you to eat up books! Eating is different from reading! When you eat a book, it becomes a part of you. To eat a book is to study a book. Books are meant to be eaten! Books are meant to be absorbed until the revelations in them become a part and parcel of you.

And he said unto me, Thou must prophesy again before many PEOPLES, and NATIONS, and TONGUES, and KINGS.

Revelation 10:11

This verse from the book of Revelation, reveals the four dimensions your ministry can attain to if you eat books.

The progression of John’s ministry is clearly stated. He was to go through four dimensions of ministry. You are also destined to go through these four dimensions of ministry.

You will go through these phases of ministry through the power you receive from a little book.

Four Dimensions of Ministry through Books

1\. First dimension: You will minister to many people!

2\. Second dimension: You will minister in other nations!

3\. Third dimension: You will minister in other languages!

4\. Fourth dimension: You will minister to kings!

Most ministers of the gospel never minister to many people (large crowds of people). Most pastors never rise up out of small groups. Many people reading this book have less than a hundred people to minister to every week. All that can change by a book!

It is true and real that reading and meditating on the revelations that are in a book can radically transform your ministry. If only you would implement the wonderful revelations God gives you through books, you would see the results in your ministry.

Most ministers of the gospel never minister in other nations. Most pastors never have to travel outside their country for anything. There is a reason why some people introduce themselves and say, “I have preached in seventy nations” or “I have preached in one hundred nations.” The number of nations a person preaches in are important, because of what the angel said in Revelation 10:11.

As you read and eat this book brought to you supernaturally, you will travel to many nations which have no link or connection to you in the natural. You will truly be an international minister!

Most ministers of the gospel never minister to people who speak a different language. Most pastors only need to preach in their own language and they will be fine. It is when you start advancing in ministry that you begin to need an interpreter. Most people who write books never need to translate them into another language. This large angel specifically said that John would prophesy to people of other tongues after eating up the book. And truly we are here today reading John’s books in many languages around the world.

Most ministers of the gospel never minister to kings. Most pastors never ever meet heads of state or the kings and queens of this earth. It is clear from this passage that in the progression of ministry, you will see and encounter kings and men of authority. Meeting the great men of our world is a natural consequence of reading and absorbing a book until it becomes a part of you. It is your turn to become God’s chosen vessel who will meet the kings of this earth.

Advance in all these four dimensions of ministry through books! The breaking through of your ministry into all these levels, depends on your determination to find your “little book” and to eat it up when you do.
