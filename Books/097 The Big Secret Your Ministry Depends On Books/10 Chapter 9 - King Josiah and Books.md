### Chapter 9\

King Josiah and Books\
-------------------------------------------

Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned thirty and one years in Jerusalem. And his mother’s name was Jedidah, the daughter of Adaiah of Boscath... And Hilkiah the high priest said unto Shaphan the scribe, I HAVE FOUND THE BOOK OF THE LAW IN THE HOUSE OF THE LORD. And Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan, and he read it... And Shaphan the scribe shewed the king, saying, Hilkiah the priest hath delivered me a book. And Shaphan read it before the king. And it came to pass, WHEN THE KING HAD HEARD THE WORDS OF THE BOOK OF THE LAW, THAT HE RENT HIS CLOTHES... Go ye, enquire of the Lord for me, and for the people, and for all Judah, concerning the words of this book that is found: for great is the wrath of the Lord that is kindled against us, because our fathers have not hearkened unto the words of this book, to do according unto all that which is written concerning us.

2 Kings 22:1, 8, 10-11, 13

An eight-year-old king is a severely handicapped person. A handicap is a condition that makes success more difficult. An eight-year-old boy will certainly not know what to do to govern a nation, but he can overcome his handicaps through books.

You can overcome your handicaps in ministry through books. You can attain success in your life and ministry through books. Achieve what Josiah achieved in life and ministry by reading!

King Josiah’s ministry became powerful through the books. A great revival was birthed in the life of King Josiah when he discovered a book, the Book of the Law.

There are many aspects of life and ministry that you are blind to. Blindness is part of most ministers’ lives. We simply do not see what God is doing everywhere.

Blindness is part of ministry because of our human pride. Most ministers cannot see what God is using others to do. The world is so big that a combination of your pride plus the sheer size of the world blinds you. Prophet Isaiah accurately described God’s servants as some of the blindest groups of people.

Who is blind but My servant, Or so deaf as My messenger whom I send? Who is so blind as he that is at peace with Me, Or so blind as the servant of the LORD?

Isaiah 42:19 (NASB95)

Jesus also described the Pharisees as blind and ignorant men. The pride and hypocrisy of the Pharisees blinded them to the Messiah, whom they had been waiting for.

Then the disciples came and said to Him, “Do You know that the Pharisees were offended when they heard this statement?” But He answered and said, “Every plant which My heavenly Father did not plant shall be uprooted. Let them alone; THEY ARE BLIND GUIDES OF THE BLIND. And if a blind man guides a blind man, both will fall into a pit.”

Matthew 15:12-14 (NASB95)

Woe unto you, ye blind guides, which say, Whosoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gold of the temple, he is a debtor!

Matthew 23:16

YE BLIND GUIDES, which strain at a gnat, and swallow a camel.

Matthew 23:24

A great awakening and a great vision can come to a church through the discovery of a book. Most of the changes and newness of ministry I have had in my church have come through the discovery of books. The transformation of my good self into a minister was through books.

It may seem that you have accidentally stumbled upon a book, but actually, God is the one who has sent His angel to show you the book. I seemed to have casually come across Kenneth Hagin’s books. But that was no accident. In the realm of the spirit, God brought me to Kenneth Hagin through his books.

I also seemed to have stumbled across Rick Joyner’s books in a bookshop in South Africa. No one I knew had ever read Rick Joyner’s books. But perhaps they are the most profound and prophetic books I have ever read. Rick Joyner’s books are life changers for me!

One day, I again stumbled across a small biography of John Wesley. Oh, how profound and utterly life-changing it was! I became hooked onto the life story of this man and found so many similarities between his ministry and mine that brought comfort and strength to my life. I ended up reading his story many times over by different authors.

From today, do not consider the discovery of a book an accident. God is showing you important truths and revelations that will change your life forever. You are the new King Josiah who will bring great revival to the world through a book!
