### Chapter 4\

Get An Approved Ministry\
----------------------------------------------

STUDY TO SHEW THYSELF APPROVED unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

2 Timothy 2:15

To be approved is to be spoken of favourably.

To be approved is to be adjudged good.

To be approved is for people to have a positive opinion about your ministry.

Do you want your ministry to be adjudged a good ministry?

Do you want people to have a positive opinion about your ministry?

Do you want to be the subject of severe rebukes and continual reprimanding because of your failing ministry?

Are you not tired of failing and losing?

Do you not want some victories in the ministry?

Do you not want to be approved in ministry?

Transform your church and ministry into an approved ministry by obeying one of the greatest apostolic commandments given to pastors: study!

Study what? Study books!

Study to show yourself approved!

Study until people’s opinions about your ministry become positive.

To study means to devote time and attention to learning and understanding something. Devote time and attention to learn and understand ministry.

To study also means to analyse and think deeply about something. You must analyse and think deeply about the ministry.

To study means to prepare for a test. You must prepare for all the tests of ministry. To study means to gain knowledge. You must gain all the knowledge you can so that you can do well in ministry.

All studies are related to books. Get the books and study them! The big secret is the books!

Timothy’s ministry unfolded through studying books. It is no surprise that Apostle Paul told Timothy, his son in the ministry, to study books. I once told a congregation that I had only one thing to tell them. I said to them; “If I never get the chance to speak to you again, I would urge you to simply study the books.”

Indeed, that is the greatest command you can give to any serious minister - find the books that God is using to bless you and read them a hundred times over in the next ten years. You will be changed! You will be transformed! You will advance! You will be approved in the ministry!

Today, many ministers are not approved. Perhaps you are a failure in the ministry; and if God were to give marks for your ministry, you would get less than 50%.

Perhaps your pastoral ministry is not even approved, nor is it acceptable. You are far below par! You are not doing well!

Perhaps you are a failed evangelist, and no one attends your crusades.

Perhaps if your teaching ministry came under scrutiny, you would get less than 50% because your teachings are not anointed, nor are they interesting. You are a dried-out river that produces little freshness. People fall asleep whilst you teach and preach because there is no freshness or life in you.

You have not read the books by which a man of God is approved. Your apostolic ministry and prophetic ministry are failing and fading.

There is good news for you. You can become an approved minister! How can you be approved? By studying! Apostle Paul told Timothy how his ministry could change into an approved ministry.

A great change can come into your ministry through the books that you study. It is time for you to study the necessary books and receive revelation.

Books are an essential part of a minister’s life. That is why the Apostle Paul read so many books and quoted from them. Without reading and studying certain books, your ministry is not likely to gain approval! You will not be recognised or accepted until you study certain books. You need to do exactly what Paul said: study books so that you become approved by God!
