### Chapter 1\

Respect the Books\
---------------------------------------

And Ezra the scribe stood upon a pulpit of wood, which they had made for the purpose; and beside him stood Mattithiah, and Shema, and Anaiah, and Urijah, and Hilkiah, and Maaseiah, on his right hand; and on his left hand, Pedaiah, and Mishael, and Malchiah, and Hashum, and Hashbadana, Zechariah, and Meshullam. AND EZRA OPENED THE BOOK IN THE SIGHT OF ALL THE PEOPLE; (FOR HE WAS ABOVE ALL THE PEOPLE;) AND WHEN HE OPENED IT, ALL THE PEOPLE STOOD UP: And Ezra blessed the Lord, the great God. And all the people answered, Amen, Amen, with lifting up their hands: and they bowed their heads, and worshipped the Lord with their faces to the ground.

Nehemiah 8:4-6

Transform your ministry, your life and your church by respecting the greatest asset of a minister - a book!

Why did the whole congregation stand up when the book was opened? Respect! You must develop a proper respect for books. The people stood up when the book was opened and this was a sign of great honour. There was a great respect for the Holy Book that was about to be read. That respect for books is what you need for your life and ministry to be transformed. Great men of God have always shown great respect for books. That is why the people stood up when the book was opened.

The action of standing up is the respect that would have been shown to a president of a country who walked into a church!

Develop a proper respect for the books! The greatest book in existence is the Bible. The second greatest set of books in existence is any book that explains the Bible to us. Every book that brings the word of God is a great book and worthy of your respect. You must regard a book as something that can and will change you. Books have changed my life!

You must admit that everything you know about God is what you have learnt from the Book of books - the Bible! When Jesus read the Holy Book in the synagogue, the people stood up for the reading. Jesus read the book of Isaiah for the people to receive a truly wonderful revelation.

And Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit into Galilee: and there went out a fame of him through all the region round about. And he taught in their synagogues, being glorified of all.

And he came to Nazareth, where he had been brought up: and, as his custom was, HE WENT INTO THE SYNAGOGUE ON THE SABBATH DAY, AND STOOD UP FOR TO READ.

And there was delivered unto him the book of the prophet Esaias. And when he had opened the book, he found the place where it was written, The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised, To preach the acceptable year of the Lord.

AND HE CLOSED THE BOOK, AND HE GAVE IT AGAIN TO THE MINISTER, AND SAT DOWN. And the eyes of all them that were in the synagogue were fastened on him.

And he began to say unto them, This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears.

And all bare him witness, and wondered at the gracious words which proceeded out of his mouth. And they said, Is not this Joseph’s son?

Luke 4:14-22

Jesus stood up for the reading of the book, but sat down for the preaching. Jesus showed great respect for the reading of the Holy Scriptures. Do you have the same reverence for the books and the scriptures? Perhaps that is the missing key in your ministry - a great respect for the books! A great respect for the word of God!

One day a young man wrote a fictional book and put it out for sale. Later on, his brother wrote a book that contained teachings from the Bible. Many people bought the books that held teachings from the Bible, but the fictional book was soon gone from view. No one wanted to buy those fictional stories. The writer of the fictional stories was upset with people for patronizing his brother’s book much more than they did his. But you cannot compare the two books!

A book about sacred and honoured scriptures cannot be compared with a human storybook based on emotional, runaway thoughts. You cannot compare the word of God with anything. No one can ever show the same respect to God’s word as to a man’s bedtime stories. Even Jesus Christ stood up for the reading of Isaiah’s book! Why did Jesus stand up for the reading of Isaiah’s book? Because it was worthy of His respect. It was a book that contained the word of God!

When you pay proper respect to books from God, you will invest in them and read them diligently and carefully. You will pray for understanding and revelation as you approach the word of God. God will speak to you and guide you through these holy books.

I believe all the books I have written are holy books because they contain the word of God.

Treat them specially and grow in your respect for the holy and special word of God.

Your life and ministry will change forever through a book.
