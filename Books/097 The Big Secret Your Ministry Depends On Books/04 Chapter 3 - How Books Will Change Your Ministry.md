### Chapter 3\

How Books Will Change Your Ministry\
---------------------------------------------------------

THIS SECOND EPISTLE, beloved, I now write unto you; in both which I STIR UP YOUR PURE MINDS by way of remembrance:

2 Peter 3:1

According to this beautiful scripture, books will stir you up into the works of God!

Your ministry will unfold through books because they will stir up your mind and heart.

The power of a book is to stir up the mind and heart!

1\. Books will affect you greatly!

2\. Books will rouse you from indifference!

3\. Books will rouse you from complacency!

4\. Books will rouse you from inactivity!

5\. Books will provoke you!

6\. Books will cause you to move rapidly!

7\. Books will stir you up to do greater works!

How the Books Will Change Your Ministry

1\. Allow single words, sentences or phrases to change your life and ministry! Watch out for words that jump at you when you are reading books! Watch out for single words! Watch out for single phrases! Watch out for striking sentences that stir you up! That is why God places a curse on anyone who takes away any of the words, phrases or sentences from His book. There is a phrase, “Give thyself wholly” which is found in the Bible (1 Timothy 4:15). This phrase changed my life and put me into the ministry. How important it is to take certain phrases literally so that God has the chance to speak to you!

For I testify unto every man that heareth the words of the prophecy of this book, If any man shall add unto these things, God shall add unto him the plagues that are written in this book: AND IF ANY MAN SHALL TAKE AWAY FROM THE WORDS OF THE BOOK of this prophecy, God shall take away his part out of the book of life, and out of the holy city, and from the things which are written in this book.

Revelation 22:18-19

2\. Join the successful group of pastors who keep the sayings and teachings in books. Many pastors do not read and do not keep the sayings in the books.

Then saith he unto me, See thou do it not: for I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren the prophets, AND OF THEM WHICH KEEP THE SAYINGS OF THIS BOOK: worship God.

Revelation 22:9

3\. End the curse in your ministry by obeying everything in the books. Many pastors are labouring under a curse because they are not continuing in the things written in the books. What will it take for you to become an obedient person? Don’t you want the curse that brings frustration and difficulty in ministry to end? It is time to start believing the sayings that are in the books.

For as many as are of the works of the law are under the curse: for it is written, CURSED IS EVERY ONE THAT CONTINUETH NOT IN ALL THINGS WHICH ARE WRITTEN IN THE BOOK of the law to do them.

Galatians 3:10

4\. Command blessings on your ministry by keeping the sayings in a book. There is a blessing for those who keep the sayings and obey the books. A new and special blessing came upon me when I began to obey the sayings in Kenneth Hagin’s books.

Behold, I come quickly: BLESSED IS HE THAT KEEPETH THE SAYINGS of the prophecy of this book.

Revelation 22:7

Become great like Jesus through a book. Jesus became great by choosing to read Isaiah and to believe what He was reading.

And he came to Nazareth, where he had been brought up: and, as his custom was, he went into the synagogue on the sabbath day, and stood up for to read. AND THERE WAS DELIVERED UNTO HIM THE BOOK OF THE PROPHET ESAIAS. AND WHEN HE HAD OPENED THE BOOK, HE FOUND THE PLACE WHERE IT WAS WRITTEN, … AND HE CLOSED THE BOOK, and he gave it again to the minister, and sat down. And the eyes of all them that were in the synagogue were fastened on him. And he began to say unto them, This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears.

Luke 4:16-17, 20-21

5\. Become successful in ministry by believing that books are written for you and about you. When you adjust your mind and believe that a book is written of you and because of you, you will start to receive fantastic revelations from God. You will see the book as God’s detailed instructions for you. You will see yourself in the volume of the book. The details of what you should do practically will be revealed to you in the volume of the book. A book will become like the voice of Jesus to you today. There are books that I read and consider that they were written for me and about me. These books change my life every time I read them. I receive personal direction and guidance all the time from these books. You are missing a great secret because you are leaving out the books.

Then said I, Lo, I come (in the volume of the book it is written of me,) to do thy will, O God.

Hebrews 10:7

So I said, “I’m here to do it your way, o God, the way it’s described in your book.”

Hebrews 10:7 (MSG)

6\. Enter new realms by breaking the seals of a book. Every book has a seal, and it is when the seals are broken that you understand a book. Sealed books are realities. Many people are staring at sealed books. It is only when the seals of a book are broken that the book begins to bless you. I entered new realms of the ministry by breaking the seals of Rick Joyner’s prophetic books.

AND THE VISION OF ALL IS BECOME UNTO YOU AS THE WORDS OF A BOOK THAT IS SEALED, WHICH MEN DELIVER TO ONE THAT IS LEARNED, SAYING, READ THIS, I PRAY THEE: AND HE SAITH, I CANNOT; FOR IT IS SEALED: And the book is delivered to him that is not learned, saying, Read this, I pray thee: and he saith, I am not learned. Wherefore the Lord said, Forasmuch as this people draw near me with their mouth, and with their lips do honour me, but have removed their heart far from me, and their fear toward me is taught by the precept of men.

Isaiah 29:11-13

As you can see from this passage, it is impossible to be blessed by a book that is sealed. You may need someone to break the seals of books for you. John the Revelator was very sad because no one could break the seals of the book.

And I saw in the right hand of him that sat on the throne A BOOK WRITTEN WITHIN AND ON THE BACKSIDE, SEALED WITH SEVEN SEALS. And I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice, Who is worthy to open the book, and to loose the seals thereof? And no man in heaven, nor in earth, neither under the earth, was able to open the book, neither to look thereon. AND I WEPT MUCH, BECAUSE NO MAN WAS FOUND WORTHY TO OPEN AND TO READ THE BOOK, NEITHER TO LOOK THEREON.

Revelation 5:1-4

7\. Transform your ministry by connecting with a teacher. Your teacher will break the seals on the books. You will benefit from books when you have a teacher to explain the books to you. Books need teachers to break their seals.

It is important to listen to messages that teach the things that are written in books. The teachings you hear will explain what you are reading. This is why we teach from the Bible. If there were no teachings from the Bible, we would not understand large sections of the Bible. Thank God for all the Bible teachers who have made us understand the Bible in a real and practical way. Today, the Bible is more real to me because I have heard so many teachings from the Bible. My books will become much clearer to you when you hear teachings about what is written in the books.

An angel of the Lord spoke to Philip. “Go south to the desert road,” he said. “It’s the road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza.” So Philip started out. On his way he met an Ethiopian official. The man had an important position in charge of all the wealth of the Kandake. Kandake means queen of Ethiopia. This official had gone to Jerusalem to worship.

On his way home he was sitting in his chariot. He was reading the book of Isaiah the prophet. The Holy Spirit told Philip, “Go to that chariot. Stay near it.” So Philip ran up to the chariot. He heard the man reading Isaiah the prophet. “DO YOU UNDERSTAND WHAT YOU’RE READING?” PHILIP ASKED. “HOW CAN I?” HE SAID. “I NEED SOMEONE TO EXPLAIN IT TO ME.” SO HE INVITED PHILIP TO COME UP AND SIT WITH HIM. Here is the part of Scripture the official was reading. It says, “He was led like a sheep to be killed. Just as lambs are silent while their wool is being cut off, he did not open his mouth. When he was treated badly, he was refused a fair trial. Who can say anything about his children? His life was cut off from the earth.” (Isaiah 53:7,8) The official said to Philip, “Tell me, please. Who is the prophet talking about? Himself, or someone else?” Then Philip began with that same part of Scripture. He told him the good news about Jesus.

Acts 8:26-35 (NIRV)

8\. Take control of your church through the power of books. Churches can be controlled and managed through books and the teachings in books. Ezekiel, the prophet, was given the roll of a book to control people.\* When a congregation is made to read books, they are educated and trained through the books. You can achieve much more with people who are educated and trained by books. Many churches have learned this secret and insist on entire groups of people in the church reading certain books. All churches that I lead are trained by the books. No one can become a leader without reading certain books.

I have met pastors who make people read some of my books as compulsory training. Imagine a nation full of people who cannot read or write. There would be no lawyers, judges, accountants, pharmacists, doctors, engineers or teachers. No one in the nation would understand anything. This is why nations spend so much money to educate the population. A nation becomes wild and uncontrollable when it is full of people who do not read or write. A church becomes wild and uncontrollable when it is full of people who do not read books.

But you, son of man, give ear to what I say to you, AND DO NOT BE UNCONTROLLED LIKE THAT UNCONTROLLED PEOPLE: let your mouth be open and take what I give you. And looking, I saw a hand stretched out to me, and I SAW THE ROLL OF A BOOK IN IT;

Ezekiel 2:8-9 (BBE)

But thou, son of man, hear what I say unto thee; Be not thou rebellious like that rebellious house: open thy mouth, and eat that I give thee. And when I looked, behold, an hand was sent unto me; and, lo, a roll of a book was therein; And he spread it before me; and it was written within and without: and there was written therein lamentations, and mourning, and woe.

Ezekiel 2:8-10

9\. Seek the Lord through books! Change your ministry today by developing a desire for books. Seek the books! Love books! Love your ministry! Love your church! Look for books! Search for them! The time has come for us to stop being told to read books and to start reading books by ourselves.

God responds to seekers, to askers and finders! They are the ones who find things. When you seek God on your own by seeking Him through books, you will find Him and you will enjoy Him.

SEEK YE OUT OF THE BOOK OF THE LORD, AND READ: no one of these shall fail, none shall want her mate: for my mouth it hath commanded, and his spirit it hath gathered them.

Isaiah 34:16

10\. Transform your ministry through a library of books! Transform your ministry and church through wisdom that comes through a collection of books. Become a great nation through a collection of books. The key wisdom for your ministry is a body of structured knowledge. The Bible is an example of a body of structured knowledge. The Bible is a library that stands on its own. It is a library of 66 books. It is this library of books that feeds us and trains us to be what we are supposed to be in the ministry.

Behold, I have taught you statutes and judgments, even as the Lord my God commanded me, that ye should do so in the land whither ye go to possess it. KEEP THEREFORE AND DO THEM; FOR THIS IS YOUR WISDOM AND YOUR UNDERSTANDING IN THE SIGHT OF THE NATIONS, WHICH SHALL HEAR ALL THESE STATUTES, AND SAY, SURELY THIS GREAT NATION IS A WISE AND UNDERSTANDING PEOPLE. For what nation is there so great, who hath God so nigh unto them, as the Lord our God is in all things that we call upon him for? And what nation is there so great, that hath statutes and judgments so righteous as all this law, which I set before you this day?
