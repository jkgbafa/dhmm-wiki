REFERENCES

Chapter 2

1-1 Jeremiah 36:22–25 (Bible in Basic English). Describes King Jehoiakim cutting Jeremiah’s scroll with a penknife and burning it in the fire.

John Wycliffe

2\. Workman, Herbert B. John Wyclif: A Study of the English Medieval Church. Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1926. (Covers the 1382 Blackfriars Council condemning Wycliffe’s writings).

3\. Deanesly, Margaret. The Lollard Bible and Other Medieval Biblical Versions. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1920. (Details Thomas Arundel’s 1408 decree against Bible translations).

4\. Fudge, Thomas A. The Trial of John Wyclif. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 2019. (Discusses the 1413 papal decree to burn Wycliffe’s works).

5\. Loserth, Johann. Wyclif and His English Precursors. London: Swan Sonnenschein, 1884. (Council of Constance, 1415 condemnation of Wycliffe).

6\. Foxe, John. Acts and Monuments (Book of Martyrs). 1563; reprint, London: Seeley & Burnside, 1837. (Describes Pope Martin V’s 1428 order to exhume and burn Wycliffe’s bones).

William Tyndale

7\. Mozley, J. F. William Tyndale. London: Society for Promoting Christian Knowledge, 1937. (Background on illegality of vernacular translations).

8\. Daniell, David. William Tyndale: A Biography. New Haven: Yale University Press, 1994. (On the burning of Tyndale’s English New Testament in 1526).

9\. Demaus, Robert. William Tyndale, a Biography. London: Religious Tract Society, 1886. (Tyndale’s execution for heresy in 1536).

Martin Luther

10\. Bainton, Roland. Here I Stand: A Life of Martin Luther. New York: Abingdon Press, 1950. (Covers burning of Luther’s writings by the Catholic Church after his excommunication).

John Calvin

11\. Gordon, Bruce. Calvin. New Haven: Yale University Press, 2009. (On opposition to Calvin’s works and their burning, especially in France).

Jan Hus

12\. Kaminsky, Howard. A History of the Hussite Revolution. Berkeley: University of California Press, 1967. (Covers Hus’ De Ecclesia and burning of his writings).

13\. Spinka, Matthew. John Hus at the Council of Constance. New York: Columbia University Press, 1965. (Details Hus’ execution and destruction of his books).

Watchman Nee

14\. Lee, Witness. Watchman Nee: A Seer of the Divine Revelation in the Present Age. Anaheim: Living Stream Ministry, 1991. (On the banning and burning of Watchman Nee’s books in China).

15\. Wu, Joseph Tse-Hei. “Watchman Nee and the Little Flock Movement in Maoist China.” Church History 74, no. 1 (2005): 68–96. (On the persecution of Nee, imprisonment, and survival of his works).
