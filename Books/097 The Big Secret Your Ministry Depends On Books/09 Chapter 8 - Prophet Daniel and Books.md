### Chapter 8\

Prophet Daniel and Books\
----------------------------------------------

In the first year of Darius the son of Ahasuerus, of the seed of the Medes, which was made king over the realm of the Chaldeans; In the first year of his reign I DANIEL UNDERSTOOD BY BOOKS the number of the years, WHEREOF THE WORD OF THE LORD CAME TO JEREMIAH the prophet, that he would accomplish seventy years in the desolations of Jerusalem.

Daniel 9:1-2

Achieve what the great prophet Daniel achieved. Daniel’s ministry unfolded through books.

Daniel had been taken away as a captive to Babylon when he was a young teenager, probably made a eunuch, and trained to serve in Nebuchadnezzar’s court.

The prophets—Daniel, Zephaniah, Habakkuk, Ezekiel, and Jeremiah—were all contemporaries.¹ Jeremiah began his ministry as a young man during the reign of King Josiah.² It is likely that Jeremiah was forced to join a remnant that ran off to Egypt, when others were taken into captivity. Thus, Jeremiah’s ministry was largely restricted to that group in Egypt.³

Ezekiel was taken to the rivers of Babylon where, as a slave labourer, he and his fellow captives dug and maintained the canals branching off the Euphrates River.4 This was several miles from Babylon itself and it is here that Ezekiel performed his ministry.5

Daniel was one of the young men who was taken to the king’s court in Babylon and eventually rose to very high ranks in the government of the day.6

Each of these men was a prophet, but they ministered to different groups of people and probably never came into contact with each other. It was only by reading each other’s books that they could receive the anointing.

Daniel was so great that he could interpret dreams.

Daniel was so great that he could interpret dreams even when he was not told what the dream was.

Daniel was so great that he was greatly beloved in heaven.

Daniel was so great that he had Angel Gabriel appearing to him.

Yea, whiles I was speaking in prayer, even THE MAN GABRIEL, WHOM I HAD SEEN IN THE VISION AT THE BEGINNING, being caused to fly swiftly, touched me about the time of the evening oblation. And he informed me, and talked with me, and said, O Daniel, I am now come forth to give thee skill and understanding. At the beginning of thy supplications the commandment came forth, and I am come to shew thee; for thou art greatly beloved: therefore understand the matter, and consider the vision.

Daniel 9:21-23

Yet, we do know that Daniel advanced in ministry through books. Daniel gained an understanding of the ministry by reading the books written by Jeremiah who was in Egypt. Read the book of Daniel and see how great a prophet he was! Yet, he had to read and gain more understanding through books written by other ministers.

In the first year of his reign I Daniel understood by books the number of the years, whereof the word of the LORD came to Jeremiah the prophet, that he would accomplish seventy years in the desolations of Jerusalem.

Daniel 9:2

Notice a great combination of the very supernatural and the very fantastic in the visions of Daniel which came through reading the books of Jeremiah. This great man only received understanding when he read the books written by Jeremiah.
