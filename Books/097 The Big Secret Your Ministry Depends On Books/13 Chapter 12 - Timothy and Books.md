### Chapter 12\

Timothy and Books\
---------------------------------------

Until I come, give your time and effort to the public READING OF THE SCRIPTURES and to preaching and teaching.

1 Timothy 4:13 (GNT)

Timothy was a local pastor who was guided by Apostle Paul on what to do. Timothy received many instructions through the books that Paul wrote.

There are several ways to grow, stabilize and manage churches through a solid books’ ministry. Apostle Paul is a good example of an apostle who controlled and managed his churches through books.

How to Manage Churches through Books

1\. Make every leader become a reader of the scripture and the books. Make your leaders read the books out in church. Paul told Timothy to pay attention to reading.

Till I come, devote yourself to \[public and private\] reading, to exhortation (preaching and personal appeals), and to teaching and instilling doctrine.

1 Timothy 4:13 (AMPC)

2\. Make your entire church read every single book that is available. The life and growth of the Thessalonian church depended on the books. Paul commanded that his epistles and books be read to the churches. He wanted EVERYONE TO READ EVERYTHING.

I charge you by the Lord that this epistle be read unto all the holy brethren.

1 Thessalonians 5:27

3\. Notice and deal with people who do not read, study or obey the books. Distinguish between those who obey and use the books, and those who do not obey the books.

The growth of the Thessalonian church depended on the members obeying the books. We must expect people to read and obey what is in the books. Failure to read and obey what is in the books is a very serious thing and should be taken as such!

And IF ANY MAN OBEY NOT OUR WORD BY THIS EPISTLE, NOTE THAT MAN, AND HAVE NO COMPANY WITH HIM, that he may be ashamed. Yet count him not as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.

2 Thessalonians 3:14-15

4\. Develop a special culture in your church through the books. The life, growth and culture of the church depend on the books. Repeated teaching from books will create a special, holy tradition and a holy culture in the ministry. A culture of unity, faithfulness and loyalty can be created by repeated teachings from books. If you do not read and minister the knowledge and wisdom in the books, a culture of complaining, murmuring and rebellion will establish itself in your church. Paul noted that traditions had been taught and established through his letters and his words.

Therefore, brethren, stand fast, and HOLD THE TRADITIONS WHICH YE HAVE BEEN TAUGHT, WHETHER BY WORD, OR OUR EPISTLE.

2 Thessalonians 2:15

5\. Govern, manage and control the branches of churches through the books. Apostle Paul ensured that his books were read in every different branch he established. Whatever the Laodicean church had, he ensured that the Colossian church also had.

And when this epistle is read among you, cause that it be read also in the church of the Laodiceans; and that ye likewise read the epistle from Laodicea.

Colossians 4:16

6\. Remove unstable people from the churches through books. It is a well-known fact that wretched and miserable people always have a problem with epistles and books. They say, “Do we have to read these books? Do we need to show people that we are learning from these books? Don’t we also have the Holy Spirit? Can’t God tell us what to preach without reading these books?”

Impart knowledge and stability to your churches and members through books! Stability is imparted through books, and thereby church growth. My experience is that wicked, ignorant and unstable people struggle with books, as they do with other scriptures.

As also in all his epistles, speaking in them of these things; in which are some things hard to be understood, which they that are unlearned and unstable wrest, as they do also the other scriptures, unto their own destruction.

2 Peter 3:16

“And further, by these, my son, be admonished: of making many books there is no end; and much study is a weariness of the flesh.” Ecclesiastes 12:12
