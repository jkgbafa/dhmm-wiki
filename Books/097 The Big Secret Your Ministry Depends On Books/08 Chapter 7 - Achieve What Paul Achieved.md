### Chapter 7\

Achieve What Paul Achieved\
------------------------------------------------

AND LEST I SHOULD BE EXALTED ABOVE MEASURE THROUGH THE ABUNDANCE OF THE REVELATIONS, there was given to me a thorn in the flesh, the messenger of Satan to buffet me, lest I should be exalted above measure.

2 Corinthians 12:7

Achieve what Paul achieved in the ministry through an abundance of books and revelations. Exaltation, advancement and promotion in ministry come through the abundance of revelations you receive.

It is of note that you go higher in rank in the ministry through the abundance of revelations that you receive. Indeed, the Apostle Paul was promoted highly because of the abundance of revelations he received.

So how does one become a man of many revelations? The answer is simple - books!

So how does one become promoted highly? The answer is simple - books!

I am speaking about the great Apostle Paul. The man of great revelations! The man who had an abundance of fantastic revelations! The man who was caught up to the third heavens! How did Paul do it? Did he only depend on the abundance of fantastic heavenly revelations he received? No, he did not.

Apostle Paul was also a man of books. He read books and quoted abundantly from these books. Apostle Paul was a man of abundant revelations because of the many books he read and quoted from.

Do not be fooled into thinking that Apostle Paul just had visions and dreams. Certainly not.

No one has so many visions that he does not need or read books. Apostle Paul must have been a man of abundant revelations that came to him through the books he read and quoted from. Achieve what the Apostle Paul achieved by using books just like he did!

Paul’s ministry unfolded through the books that he read. Transform your ministry, your life and your church, by the books you read.

Perhaps the greatest part of Paul’s ministry was the books he wrote for the church. It is worth noting that Apostle Paul, in his ministry of writing books, quoted over 260 times from Old Testament books.

That is why books were so important to Paul. You will notice that Paul’s ministry was built deeply on other books that he read. Why would he quote from or paraphrase all these passages if he was not a deep reader of other books?

Indeed, Paul did receive revelation directly from God. Yes, Paul was caught up to the third heavens. But he still needed books! He still read many books. His whole life and ministry depended on books, even though he had fantastic prophetic revelations and experiences.

The cloke that I left at Troas with Carpus, when thou comest, bring with thee, AND THE BOOKS, but especially the parchments.

2 Timothy 4:13

1In Paul’s book of Romans, he quoted or paraphrased 84 times from Old Testament books.

In the book of Hebrews, (which many scholars believe was written by Paul) he quotes 83 times from different books of the Old Testament.

To write 1 Corinthians, Paul quoted from 26 different passages of the Old Testament books.

To write 2 Corinthians, Apostle Paul quoted 18 times from passages in Old Testament books.

To write the book of Galatians, the Apostle Paul quoted 14 different passages from books in the Old Testament.

To write the book of Ephesians, Apostle Paul quoted 12 different passages from books in the Old Testament.

To write the book of Philippians, Apostle Paul quoted from 6 different passages of books in the Old Testament.

To write the book of Colossians, Apostle Paul quoted from 3 different passages of books in the Old Testament.

To write the two books of Thessalonians, Apostle Paul quoted from 8 different passages of books in the Old Testament.

To write the two books of Timothy, the Apostle Paul quoted from 13 different passages of books in the Old Testament.

Only Paul’s two shortest epistles, Titus and Philemon, contain no quotes from any known book.

As your ministry grows in maturity, you will find yourself quoting from many other books. Your revelation is the fruit of the books you read. Take your ministry higher with books\!1
