### Preface

I am sure you are wondering what more there is to say about the subject of fornication. I also wondered, when the Spirit of God directed me to feed the Body of Christ along these lines.In Jeremiah 3:15, the Lord said, “Behold, I will give you pastors after my own heart who will feed you with knowledge and understanding.” Being a pastor of God’s flock has meant for me, the teaching of a wide range of subjects.I must admit that, when the Lord first led me to write a book on this subject, I was a bit hesitant. But God has shown me that deliverance does not only come by the laying on of hands but by simple childlike faith in the Word of God.Jesus said, “You shall know the truth and the truth shall set you free.”For this reason I’m dedicated to preaching the truth which is the Word of God. In my ministry, God has delivered mass- es of people by the constant and regular teaching of the Word. That is why our churches grow: people are being delivered through the Word! I pray that this book will help many peo- ple out of what I call, “the international and cross-cultural sin of fornication.”
