### Chapter 9\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Become Anointed\
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Now when all the people were baptized, it came to pass, that Jesus also being baptized, and PRAYING, the heaven was opened, AND THE HOLY GHOST DESCENDED IN A BODILY SHAPE LIKE A DOVE UPON HIM, AND A VOICE CAME FROM HEAVEN, which said, Thou art my beloved Son; in thee I am well pleased. And Jesus himself began to be about thirty years of age, being (as was supposed) the son of Joseph, which was the son of Heli,

Luke 3:21-23

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am very sure you do! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to become anointed again and again.

Every time you pray, you become anointed. When Jesus prayed, the heavens opened, and He became anointed. This is what happens every time you pray. Being anointed did not only happen when Jesus prayed in the River Jordan. Being anointed happens every time you pray!

You will notice that anointed people are prayerful people. Dry and empty ministers are often not prayerful. The dryness is from the lack of prayer. The lack of prayer results in the lack of the anointing! There is no way around this reality. If you want to be anointed, you have to be prayerful!

And WHEN THEY HAD PRAYED, the place was shaken where they were assembled together; and THEY WERE ALL FILLED WITH THE HOLY GHOST, and they spake the word of God with boldness.

Acts 4:31

When the disciples prayed, the place was shaken, and they were all filled with the Holy Ghost and spoke the word of God with boldness. Being filled with the Holy Ghost is the same as becoming anointed. Prayer always leads to your becoming anointed!

And when the day of Pentecost was fully come, they were all with one accord in one place. And suddenly there came a sound from heaven as of a rushing mighty wind, and it filled all the house where they were sitting.

Acts 2:1-2

On the Day of Pentecost, the disciples were all together in the Upper Room. They had been there for a long time. What do you think they were doing in the Upper Room? They were waiting on God, ministering to God, praying and waiting for the promise. Suddenly, they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began speaking with other tongues.

Those in the Upper Room received the Holy Spirit and became the anointed Church of God which took the gospel to the ends of the world. The leadership was anointed and they became evangelistic and fruitful in a short time. The anointed church won thousands of souls in a few days.

The anointing flows down when prayer goes up to heaven.

Anointed people are prayerful people!
