### Chapter 1\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Show an Interest in God\
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

And to Seth, to him also there was born a son; and he called his name Enos: THEN BEGAN MEN TO CALL UPON THE NAME OF THE LORD.

Genesis 4:26

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am sure you do!

Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual.

Prayer is calling upon God!

In the beginning, men did not call on God.

Many men lived and died without acknowledging God. Several men are mentioned in the genealogies in the Bible, and their achievements are noted. None of them called on God, and none of them prayed. However, in the days when Seth had a son named Enos, men began to pray.

And Cain knew his wife; and she conceived, and bare Enoch: and he builded a city, and called the name of the city, after the name of his son, Enoch.

And unto Enoch was born Irad: and Irad begat Mehujael: and Mehujael begat Methusael: and Methusael begat Lamech.

And Lamech took unto him two wives: the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the other Zillah. And Adah bare Jabal: he was the father of such as dwell in tents, and of such as have cattle. And his brother’s name was Jubal: he was the father of all such as handle the harp and organ.

And Zillah, she also bare Tubalcain, an instructer of every artificer in brass and iron: and the sister of Tubalcain was Naamah.

And Lamech said unto his wives, Adah and Zillah, Hear my voice; ye wives of Lamech, hearken unto my speech: for I have slain a man to my wounding, and a young man to my hurt. If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamech seventy and sevenfold.

And Adam knew his wife again; and she bare a son, and called his name Seth: For God, said she, hath appointed me another seed instead of Abel, whom Cain slew.

AND TO SETH, TO HIM ALSO THERE WAS BORN A SON; AND HE CALLED HIS NAME ENOS: THEN BEGAN MEN TO CALL UPON THE NAME OF THE LORD.

Genesis 4:17-26

In the days of Enoch, Cain built a city and named it after his son, Enoch. Cain did not pray and his son, Enoch did not call on God either.

In the days of Lamech, polygamy was introduced to the world. Lamech had two wives. Lamech had a child called Jabal with his first wife Adah. In the days of Jabal, living in tents became fashionable and cattle rearing was established.

Lamech had another child with his first wife, Adah, and gave birth to Jubal. Jubal and Jabal were children of Adah. In the days of Jubal, musical instruments were introduced into the world. Harps and organs were invented.

Lamech had a second wife called, Zillah. Zillah gave birth to a boy and a girl. The boy was called Tubalcain and the girl was called Naamah. In the days of Tubalcain, metalworks and tools made out of bronze and iron were introduced into the world.

Lamech also introduced more murder into the world. He said he had done ten times more evil than his ancestor Cain.

And Lamech said unto his wives, Adah and Zillah, Hear my voice; ye wives of Lamech, hearken unto my speech: for I have slain a man to my wounding, and a young man to my hurt. If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamech seventy and sevenfold.

Genesis 4:23-24

Lamech said he had killed a man and also killed a young man. According to some ancient books, Lamech killed Cain by accident and also killed his own son.

And Lamech was old and advanced in years, and his eyes were dim that he could not see, and Tubal Cain, his son, was leading him and it was one day that Lamech went into the field and Tubal Cain his son was with him, and while they were walking in the field, Cain the son of Adam advanced towards them; for Lamech was very old and could not see much, and Tubal Cain his son was very young. And Tubal Cain told his father to draw his bow, and with the arrows he struck Cain, who was yet far off, and he killed him, because he appeared be an animal to them. And the arrows entered Cain’s body although he was at a distance from them, and he fell to the ground and died. And the Lord rewarded Cain’s evil according to his wickedness, which he had done to his brother Abel, according to the word of the Lord which he had spoken.

And it came to pass when Cain had died, that Lamech and Tubal went to see the animal which they had slain, and they saw, and behold Cain their grandfather was fallen dead on the earth. And Lamech was very much grieved at having done this, and in clapping his hands together (in grief) he struck his son and caused his death.

And the wives of Lamech heard what Lamech had done, and they sought to kill him. And the wives of Lamech hated him from that day, because he killed Cain and Tubal Cain, and the wives of Lamech separated from him, and would not listen to him in those days. And Lamech came to his wives, and he begged them to listen to him about this matter.

And he said to his wives Adah and Zillah, Hear my voice O wives of Lamech, attend to my words, for now you have imagined and said that I killed a man with my wounds, and a child with my stripes for they did no violence, but certainly know that I am old and grey-headed, and that my eyes are heavy through age, and I did this thing unknowingly. And the wives of Lamech listened to him in this matter, and they returned to him with the advice of their father Adam, but they bore no children to him from that time, knowing that God’s anger was increasing in those days against the sons of men, to destroy them with the waters of the flood for their evil acts. (Book of Jasher Chapter 2:26-36)

The introduction of prayer into the world was notable. Many people do not pray and do not call on God. Yet, prayer is so important that we are taught never to stop praying and never to stop calling on God. Of all the things that were introduced by Adam’s lineage, the most significant was the miracle-working prayer, which was introduced in the days of Enos.

When prayers were introduced into the world, it was a sign that men were developing an interest in the things of God.
