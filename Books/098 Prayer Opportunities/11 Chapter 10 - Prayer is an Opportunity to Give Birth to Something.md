### Chapter 10\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Give Birth to Something\
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

But it is good to be zealously affected always in a good thing, and not only when I am present with you. My little children, of whom I travail in birth again until Christ be formed in you,

Galatians 4:18-19

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am very sure you do! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to give birth to something! Prayer is a type of travail. It is a type of labour. When women go to give birth, they enter into what is called a labour ward. It is a place where they labour and travail.

Prayer is an opportunity to labour and travail. Prayer is an opportunity to give birth to something.

Perhaps, you have not given birth to anything in the ministry. You can give birth to a church through prayer. Many people who claim to be pastors only know how to slander and speak evil of dignitaries. They seek to draw men after themselves instead of travailing and giving birth to something in ministry.

Whenever a carnal man is seeking to start a church, he simply slanders other pastors and spreads blasphemies, rumours and stories about them to draw a small crowd to himself. This is the standard protocol of a rebellious, Lucifer-inspired pastor or bishop who is seeking to start a church. “For I know this, that after my departing shall grievous wolves enter in among you, not sparing the flock. Also of your own selves shall men arise, SPEAKING PERVERSE THINGS, TO DRAW AWAY DISCIPLES AFTER THEM” (Acts 20:29-30).

However, that is not the only way to gather a crowd. Prayer is an opportunity to gather a crowd by giving birth to them in the realm of the spirit.

Prayer is a birth-giving opportunity! It is possible to give birth to things in the realm of the spirit. It is possible to give birth to churches in the realm of the spirit! It is possible to give birth to ministries in the realm of the spirit! It is possible to give birth to converts in the realm of the spirit! It is possible to give birth to souls in the realm of the spirit!

Who hath heard such a thing? who hath seen such things? Shall the earth be made to bring forth in one day? or shall a nation be born at once? FOR AS SOON AS ZION TRAVAILED, SHE BROUGHT FORTH HER CHILDREN. Shall I bring to the birth, and not cause to bring forth? saith the LORD: shall I cause to bring forth, and shut the womb? saith thy God.

Isaiah 66:8-9

The prophet Isaiah reveals in this passage that a nation or church cannot be formed unless there is some travailing and labouring in the realm of the spirit.
