### Chapter 7\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Fight back\
------------------------------------------------------------

And the Lord said, Simon, Simon, behold, Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat: But I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not: and when thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren.

Luke 22:31-32

I am sure you want an opportunity to do something spiritual! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to fight back the spiritual offensive!

There are attacks by the enemy that you cannot prevent. Jesus could not prevent satan from attacking Simon Peter. It was something that had to happen. Jesus informed Simon that he was the target of a big satanic strike. Peter had no way of escaping the demonic onslaught that was to come to him. He had no way of preventing it from happening. In the realm of the spirit, it was determined.

What could Peter do to overcome it or to fight back? There was only one thing that would save Peter’s life and ministry, and that was prayer!

Jesus explained, “I have prayed for you.” Because of the prayer, satan’s strike at the heart of Peter’s ministry would not succeed.

1\. Prayer is your opportunity to do something about the evil that has come your way.

2\. Prayer is your opportunity to do something about temptations that have come your way.

3\. Prayer is your opportunity to do something about the persecutions that have come your way.

4\. Prayer is your opportunity to do something about the distress that has come your way.

5\. Prayer is your opportunity to do something about the wickedness that has come your way.

6\. Prayer is an opportunity to fight back after a satanic strike.

7\. Prayer is a counter-offensive move.

8\. Prayer is an opportunity to defend yourself.

9\. Prayer is an opportunity to strike back.

10\. Prayer is an opportunity to prevent total collapse after a bad attack from the enemy has come your way.

Jesus said, “But I have prayed for you.” When you are attacked by the enemy, you must respond in the same way Jesus did. You must rise up in prayer and respond. The prayers that you send up are the only appropriate responses that you have to fight back against a bad attack on your life.
