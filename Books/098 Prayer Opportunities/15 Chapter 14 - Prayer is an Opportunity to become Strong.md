### Chapter 14\

Prayer is an Opportunity to become Strong\
---------------------------------------------------------------

In the day WHEN I CRIED THOU answered me, and STRENGTHENEDST ME with STRENGTH IN MY SOUL.

Psalm 138:3

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am very sure you do! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to become strong!

1\. Strength is needed for ministry all the time. Through prayer, you will develop the strength you need for ministry. Strength is needed to make it in ministry. That is why the Lord Jesus Christ strengthened Paul when He decided to put him into the ministry.

I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, WHO HAS STRENGTHENED ME, because He considered me faithful, putting me into service,

1 Timothy 1:12 (NASB)

2\. Strength is needed to withstand the attacks of the enemy. There are many attacks in the spirit that can be likened to the attack of a lion. Few people can survive the attack of a lion. Through prayer, you will receive strength so that you can survive all attacks from all lions.

Notwithstanding the Lord stood with me, and STRENGTHENED ME; that by me the preaching might be fully known, and that all the Gentiles might hear: and I WAS DELIVERED OUT OF THE MOUTH OF THE LION.

2 Timothy 4:17

3\. You will become stronger in your soul through prayer. Prayer gives you emotional strength. You need emotional strength when you are in the ministry. Your soul is your emotions, your will and your intellect. Many people are emotionally unstable. They know the right thing to do but their emotions take them in the wrong direction. Through prayer, your soul is strengthened.

In the day WHEN I CRIED THOU answeredst me, and STRENGTHENEDST ME with STRENGTH IN MY SOUL.

Psalm 138:3

4\. Pray for strength because Paul prayed for strength for the Colossian Christians. All through the epistles, you find Paul praying and asking the Lord to strengthen people who have become new Christians. He prayed for strength for the Colossians. He also prayed for this strength for others.

For this cause WE also, since the day we heard it, DO NOT CEASE TO PRAY FOR YOU, and to desire that ye might be filled with the knowledge of his will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding; THAT YE might walk worthy of the Lord unto all pleasing, being fruitful in every good work, and increasing in the knowledge of God; STRENGTHENED WITH ALL MIGHT, according to his glorious power, unto all patience and longsuffering with joyfulness; Giving thanks unto the Father, which hath made us meet to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light:

Colossians 1:9-12

5\. Pray for strength because Paul prayed for strength for the Ephesian Christians. All through the epistles, you find Paul praying and asking the Lord to strengthen people who have become new Christians. He prayed for strength for the Ephesians. He prayed for strength for others also.

For this cause I bow my knees unto the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, of whom the whole family in heaven and earth is named, THAT HE WOULD GRANT YOU, ACCORDING TO THE RICHES OF HIS GLORY, TO BE STRENGTHENED WITH MIGHT BY HIS SPIRIT IN THE INNER MAN; That Christ may dwell in your hearts by faith; that ye, being rooted and grounded in love, may be able to comprehend with all saints what is the breadth, and length, and depth, and height; and to know the love of Christ, which passeth knowledge, that ye might be filled with all the fulness of God.

Ephesians 3:14-19

6\. Strength from prayer can come to you through the presence of angels. Many times, angels appear and manifest themselves when there is prayer.

And HE was withdrawn from them about a stone’s cast, and KNEELED DOWN, AND PRAYED, Saying, Father, if thou be willing, remove this cup from me: nevertheless not my will, but thine, be done. AND THERE APPEARED AN ANGEL UNTO HIM FROM HEAVEN, STRENGTHENING HIM.

Luke 22:41-43

7\. Praying in tongues builds you up and therefore makes you stronger. Every time you pray in tongues, you become strong.

HE THAT SPEAKETH IN AN UNKNOWN TONGUE EDIFIETH HIMSELF; but he that prophesieth edifieth the church.

1 Corinthians 14:4

8\. Fear makes you weak. The spirit of fear is the spirit of weakness. Pray for the Spirit of power, love and a sound mind to replace every spirit of fear in you. Power, love and a sound mind combine to make you far stronger than you were when you were weakened by the spirit of fear.

Wherefore I put thee in remembrance that thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee by the putting on of my hands. For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.

2 Timothy 1:6-7
