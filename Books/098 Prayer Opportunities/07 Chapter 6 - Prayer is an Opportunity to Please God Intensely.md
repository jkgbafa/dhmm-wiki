### Chapter 6\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Please God Intensely\
----------------------------------------------------------------------

But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.

Hebrews 11:6

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am sure you do! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to please God intensely! Prayer is a “pleasing God opportunity” because prayer is a major act of faith. Because prayer is an act of faith, it is an act of pleasing Him intensely.

Not everything pleases God; but one thing that pleases God greatly is your prayer. God must be so impressed when He sees you walking about in a room speaking into the air when you can see no one and hear no one.

What a great act of faith it is when you have spoken in the air for six hours and have not heard a single audible word in response to the multitude of words you have spoken to God. As each minute goes by, God must be intensely impressed and excited. As each day goes by, and you continue to wait on Him, surely God will be highly impressed that you are still there.

After three days of prayer, God must be impressed that you have not given up and that you are still hopeful that the words you are speaking are being heard in heaven. Indeed, prayer is your great opportunity to please God intensely.

When you walk around in a garden praying from 10:00 am to 5:00 pm, spending seven hours in prayer and looking like a raving lunatic or appearing to people like an ape walking through a forest, you have moved in great faith and it must please God intensely.
