### Chapter 2\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Call on God\
-------------------------------------------------------------

Then he proceeded from there to the mountain on the east of Bethel, and pitched his tent, with Bethel on the west and Ai on the east; and there he built an altar to the Lord and CALLED UPON THE NAME OF THE LORD.

Genesis 12:8 (NASB95)

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am sure you do! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual.

Seth was the third son of Adam. Seth gave birth to Enos. In the days when Seth gave birth to Enos, people began to call on God. That was when prayer began in this world. None of the descendants of Cain introduced prayer into the world.

As the years went by, other people decided to call on God and involve God in their lives. “Calling on God” was the term used for prayer in the ancient world. Many things were learnt and experienced by those who took the opportunity and learnt to call on God and to include God in their lives.

1\. Abraham took the opportunity to call on God.

Then he proceeded from there to the mountain on the east of Bethel, and pitched his tent, with Bethel on the west and Ai on the east; and there he built an altar to the Lord and CALLED UPON THE NAME OF THE LORD.

Genesis 12:8 (NASB95)

2\. Isaac took the opportunity to call on God.

So he built an altar there and CALLED UPON THE NAME OF THE LORD, and pitched his tent there; and there Isaac’s servants dug a well.

Genesis 26:25 (NASB95)

3\. David took the opportunity to call on God.

Then on that day David delivered first this psalm to thank the Lord into the hand of Asaph and his brethren. Give thanks unto the Lord, CALL UPON HIS NAME, MAKE KNOWN HIS DEEDS AMONG THE PEOPLE. Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him, talk ye of all his wondrous works.

1 Chronicles 16:7-9

4\. Samuel took the opportunity to call on the Lord.

Moses and Aaron were among His priests, AND SAMUEL WAS AMONG THOSE WHO CALLED ON HIS NAME; They called upon the Lord and He answered them.

Psalm 99:6 (NASB95)

“Is it not the wheat harvest today? I WILL CALL TO THE LORD, THAT HE MAY SEND THUNDER AND RAIN. Then you will know and see that your wickedness is great which you have done in the sight of the Lord by asking for yourselves a king.” So Samuel called to the Lord, and the Lord sent thunder and rain that day; and all the people greatly feared the Lord and Samuel.

1 Samuel 12:17-18 (NASB95)

5\. Naaman expected Elisha to take the opportunity to call on God.

But Naaman was furious and went away and said, “Behold, I thought, ‘He will surely come out to me and stand and CALL ON THE NAME OF THE LORD HIS GOD, and wave his hand over the place and cure the leper.’”

2 Kings 5:11 (NASB95)

6\. Jonah took the opportunity to call on God in the belly of the whale.

And he said, “I CALLED OUT OF MY DISTRESS TO THE LORD, AND HE ANSWERED ME. I cried for help from the depth of Sheol; You heard my voice.

Jonah 2:2 (NASB95)

7\. The psalmist called on God.

OH GIVE THANKS TO THE LORD, CALL UPON HIS NAME; Make known His deeds among the peoples.

Psalm 105:1 (NASB95)

8\. Forgiveness happens to those who take the opportunity to call on God.

For You, Lord, are good, and ready to forgive, AND ABUNDANT IN LOVINGKINDNESS TO ALL WHO CALL UPON YOU.

Psalm 86:5 (NASB95)

9\. God has inclined His ears to those who call on Him.

Because He has inclined His ear to me, Therefore I SHALL CALL UPON HIM as long as I live.

Psalm 116:2 (NASB95)

10\. Those who flee from lusts and pursue righteousness join up with those who call on God.

Now flee from youthful lusts and pursue righteousness, faith, love and peace, with THOSE WHO CALL ON THE LORD from a pure heart.

2 Timothy 2:22 (NASB95)

11\. Wicked people do not call on the Lord. Prayerlessness is characteristic of wicked people.

Do all the workers of wickedness not know, Who eat up my people as they eat bread, And DO NOT CALL UPON THE LORD?

Psalm 14:4 (NASB95)

12\. Those who do not call on God can expect the wrath of God.

Pour out Your wrath upon the nations which do not know You, And upon the kingdoms which DO NOT CALL UPON YOUR NAME.

Psalm 79:6 (NASB95)

13\. Calling on God will save you in the day of trouble.

CALL UPON ME in the day of trouble; I shall rescue you, and you will honor Me.

Psalm 50:15 (NASB95)

I CALLED ON YOUR NAME, O LORD, Out of the lowest pit.

Lamentations 3:55 (NASB95)

I CALL UPON THE LORD, who is worthy to be praised, And I am saved from my enemies.

2 Samuel 22:4 (NASB95)

14\. Calling on God will relieve you from distresses.

Answer me when I call, O God of my righteousness! YOU HAVE RELIEVED ME IN MY DISTRESS; Be gracious to me and hear my prayer.

Psalm 4:1(NASB95)

15\. Call on God because He will answer.

I HAVE CALLED UPON YOU, FOR YOU WILL ANSWER ME, O God; Incline Your ear to me, hear my speech.

Psalm 17:6 (NASB95)

AS FOR ME, I SHALL CALL UPON GOD, And the Lord will save me.

Psalm 55:16 (NASB95)

HE WILL CALL UPON ME, AND I WILL ANSWER HIM; I will be with him in trouble; I will rescue him and honour him.

Psalm 91:15 (NASB95)

16\. God will hear you calling on Him when you stop accusing others.

Then YOU WILL CALL, AND THE LORD WILL ANSWER; You will cry, and He will say, ‘Here I am.’ IF YOU REMOVE THE YOKE FROM YOUR MIDST, THE POINTING OF THE FINGER AND SPEAKING WICKEDNESS,

Isaiah 58:9 (NASB95)

17\. God can answer you whilst you are still praying.

It will also come to pass that BEFORE THEY CALL, I WILL ANSWER; and while they are still speaking, I will hear.

Isaiah 65:24 (NASB95)

18\. You will see great and mighty things when you call to God.

CALL TO ME AND I WILL ANSWER YOU, AND I WILL TELL YOU GREAT AND MIGHTY THINGS, which you do not know.

Jeremiah 33:3 (NASB95)

19\. Salvation and deliverance come to those who call on God.

And it shall be that EVERYONE WHO CALLS ON THE NAME OF THE LORD WILL BE SAVED.

Acts 2:21 (NASB95)

And it will come about that WHOEVER CALLS ON THE NAME OF THE LORD WILL BE DELIVERED; For on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem there will be those who escape, as the Lord has said, even among the survivors whom the Lord calls.

Joel 2:32 (NASB95)

20\. Calling on God is for everyone.

For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek; for the same Lord is Lord of all, abounding in riches for all who call on Him; for “WHOEVER WILL CALL ON THE NAME OF THE LORD WILL BE SAVED.” How then will they call on Him in whom they have not believed? How will they believe in Him whom they have not heard? And how will they hear without a preacher?

Romans 10:12-14
