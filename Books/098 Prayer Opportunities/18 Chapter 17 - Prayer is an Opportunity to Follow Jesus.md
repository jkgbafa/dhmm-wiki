### Chapter 17\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Follow Jesus\
--------------------------------------------------------------

After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.

Matthew 6:9-13

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am sure you do! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to follow Jesus!

1\. Prayer is indeed an opportunity to follow Jesus. Jesus lived a life of private prayer. Do you want to follow Jesus? Let’s follow Jesus in prayer. Let us follow Jesus’ rules of prayer.

Jesus taught us that private prayers will give us the greatest outward results.

Do you want great public recognition and acknowledgement for your life and ministry? Then invest in private, hidden and unseen prayers. Don’t be worried if no one knows what you are doing.

Do not bother to announce that you are doing a certain number of days of prayer and fasting.

Let people even think that you went on a holiday. What matters most is what God sees! The public, outward reward that God is going to give you will follow.

And when thou prayest, thou shalt not be as the hypocrites are: for they love to pray standing in the synagogues and in the corners of the streets, that they may be seen of men. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward. But thou, WHEN THOU PRAYEST, ENTER INTO THY CLOSET, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret; and THY FATHER WHICH SEETH IN SECRET SHALL REWARD THEE OPENLY... Be not ye therefore like unto them: for your Father knoweth what things ye have need of, before ye ask him.

Matthew 6:5-6, 8

2\. Prayer is indeed an opportunity to follow Jesus. Follow Jesus and avoid useless repetitions in prayer. Jesus taught that praying and repeating yourself to make a great show is useless. God is simply not impressed with outward shows of piety and religiosity.

But when ye pray, USE NOT VAIN REPETITIONS, as the heathen do: for they think that they shall be heard for their much speaking. Be not ye therefore like unto them: for your Father knoweth what things ye have need of, before ye ask him.

Matthew 6:7-8

3\. Prayer is an opportunity to follow Jesus. Follow Jesus and repeat yourself when it is genuine and heartfelt because Jesus repeated Himself when He prayed in the Garden of Gethsemane. Interestingly, Jesus spoke of useless and vain repetition in prayer. However, Jesus used repetitions that were useful and helpful when He prayed in the Garden of Gethsemane.

Then saith he unto them, My soul is exceeding sorrowful, even unto death: tarry ye here, and watch with me. And he went a little further, and fell on his face, and PRAYED, SAYING, O MY FATHER, IF IT BE POSSIBLE, LET THIS CUP PASS FROM ME: NEVERTHELESS NOT AS I WILL, BUT AS THOU WILT. And he cometh unto the disciples, and findeth them asleep, and saith unto Peter, What, could ye not watch with me one hour? Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak. HE WENT AWAY AGAIN THE SECOND TIME, AND PRAYED, SAYING, O MY FATHER, IF THIS CUP MAY NOT PASS AWAY FROM ME, EXCEPT I DRINK IT, THY WILL BE DONE. And he came and found them asleep again: for their eyes were heavy. And HE LEFT THEM, AND WENT AWAY AGAIN, AND PRAYED THE THIRD TIME, SAYING THE SAME WORDS. Then cometh he to his disciples, and saith unto them, Sleep on now, and take your rest: behold, the hour is at hand, and the Son of man is betrayed into the hands of sinners.

Matthew 26:38-45

4\. Prayer is an opportunity to follow Jesus. Follow the Lord’s Prayer as a pattern for long hours of prayer. Through the Lord’s prayer, we have many topics that can keep us in prayer for hours. We also have important topics that we can pray about. We also know the things that are not out of order or inappropriate to ask for.

After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.

Matthew 6:9-13

5\. Jesus showed us that prayer is an opportunity to forgive others and to also ask for forgiveness.

For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you: But if ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.

Matthew 6:14-15

Jesus taught us the need to forgive whilst we pray. Prayer for forgiveness is essential because we are so sinful that we even smell of sin. Sin has a smell! The smell of sin is called the savour of death.

For we are unto God a sweet savour of Christ, in them that are saved, and in them that perish: TO THE ONE WE ARE THE SAVOUR OF DEATH UNTO DEATH; and to the other the savour of life unto life. And who is sufficient for these things?

2 Corinthians 2:15-16

6\. Follow Jesus by fasting and praying. Jesus expects us to fast. He said, “When you fast” not “If you fast”. No matter what anybody says, fasting will always be a part of our religious and spiritual lives.

Fasting is necessary for prayer. Fighting carnality and removing distractions is necessary for spirituality. Fasting helps you to fight carnality and remove distractions.

Moreover WHEN YE FAST, be not, as the hypocrites, of a sad countenance: for they disfigure their faces, that they may appear unto men to fast. Verily I say unto you, They have their reward. But thou, when thou fastest, anoint thine head, and wash thy face; That thou appear not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret: and thy Father, which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly.

Matthew 6:16-18

7\. Prayer is another opportunity to follow Jesus Christ. Enter the narrow gate of prayer. Enter the narrow gate of prayer where few enter. Few ministers enter the narrow gate of prayer.

Most ministers do not pray. Jonah did not pray until he got into the belly of the whale. There are many prophets like that – they will not pray until they get into trouble. Decide to enter the narrow gate and be one of the ministers of God who prays a lot.

Enter ye in at the strait gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and many there be which go in thereat: Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it.

Matthew 7:13-14

8\. Prayer is an opportunity to follow Jesus and build your house on the rock. Jesus taught every minister to build on a rock. Prayer will give you a great foundation for your ministry. Prayer will make you as solid as a rock and capable of surviving storms. Others have fallen away because they did not build on the real foundation of prayer and spirituality. Build a solid foundation of prayer. Whenever the storm comes, you will find yourself still standing after the storm.

Therefore whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, I will liken him unto a wise man, which built his house upon a rock: And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell not: for it was founded upon a rock. And every one that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them not, shall be likened unto a foolish man, which built his house upon the sand: And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell: and great was the fall of it. And it came to pass, when Jesus had ended these sayings, the people were astonished at his doctrine:

Matthew 7:24-28

9\. We know people by their fruits. Prayer is one of the fruits that will come out of your life to make you known. Your prayers will make you noticeable in the house of the Lord. Prayerless pastors are in the barren category and prayerful pastors stand out by their fruits.

YE SHALL KNOW THEM BY THEIR FRUITS. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles? Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit; but a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit. A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, neither can a corrupt tree bring forth good fruit. Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire. Wherefore by their fruits ye shall know them.

Matthew 7:16-20

10\. Follow Jesus’ style of prayer and great multitudes will follow you when you come down from the mountain of prayer.

Great multitudes followed Jesus when He came down from the mountain. What do you think Jesus was doing on the mountain? Jesus went into the mountains to pray and be alone with God. On this occasion, some people followed Him to hear His teaching. But when He came down from the mountain, great multitudes followed Him. Indeed, great multitudes will follow a man of prayer.

When he was come down from the mountain, GREAT MULTITUDES FOLLOWED HIM.

Matthew 8:1

11. Prayer is an opportunity to follow Jesus into the mountain of prayer. When Jesus came down from the mountain, He exhibited a powerful deliverance ministry. Demons which did not respond to His disciples responded to Him and were expelled from a child. Follow the laws of prayer in Jesus’ ministry, and you will experience powerful deliverance in your ministry.

And it came to pass about an eight days after these sayings, he took Peter and John and James, and went up into a mountain to pray.…

And it came to pass, that on the next day, when they were come down from the hill, much people met him. And, behold, a man of the company cried out, saying, Master, I beseech thee, look upon my son: for he is mine only child. And, lo, a spirit taketh him, and he suddenly crieth out; and it teareth him that he foameth again, and bruising him hardly departeth from him. And I besought thy disciples to cast him out; and they could not.

Luke 9:28, 37-40
