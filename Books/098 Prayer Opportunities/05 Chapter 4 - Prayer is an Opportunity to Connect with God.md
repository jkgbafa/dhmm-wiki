### Chapter 4\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Connect with God\
------------------------------------------------------------------

THEY HAVE LOST CONNECTION WITH THE HEAD, from whom the whole body, supported and held together by its ligaments and sinews, grows as God causes it to grow.

Colossians 2:19 (NIV)

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am sure you do! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to connect with God!

A prayerless person is a disconnected person because he has very little connection with the Father.

Prayer is an opportunity to connect with the Father and to fellowship with God’s presence. Through prayer, you connect and become close to God. Without this connection, you cannot be a normal Christian and you cannot bear fruit.

Prayer is talking to someone. When you talk to someone, you are close to that person. Anytime you talk to someone, you are in the presence of the person you are talking to.

In the book of John, Jesus explains how we need to abide in Him and be close to Him. We are not only expected to abide with Him but also to abide in Him. It is through abiding in Him that we will be fruitful.

Many people are not fruitful because they have lost connection with the head. They have lost the ability to stay close. They are disconnected. They have detached from the branch. When I look at some of my pastors, I can simply see disconnected and detached ministers who have become fruitless.

I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing. If a man abide not in me, he is cast forth as a branch, and is withered; and men gather them, and cast them into the fire, and they are burned.

John 15:5-6

Prayer is your great opportunity to reconnect and establish a connection with God. Prayer is a great opportunity to establish the great connection you need to bear fruit. A prayerless person is a disconnected person because he has very little connection with the Father. A prayerless person is someone who has very little interaction with God.

Prayer connects you to God. Jesus said some people will come to Him saying, “I did great works for You”, but He will say He does not know them. How is it possible to do great works for Him without His knowing you?

It is possible because you can do great works without praying. There are many prayerless people who do great works. Even unbelievers do great works without praying. But through prayer, you become connected to God. Then you will have a personal connection with God. Prayer will make you know the God you talk to.

Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven. Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name? and in thy name have cast out devils? and in thy name done many wonderful works? And THEN WILL I PROFESS UNTO THEM, I NEVER KNEW YOU: depart from me, ye that work iniquity.

Matthew 7:21-23
