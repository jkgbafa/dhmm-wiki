### Chapter 11\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Dedicate Yourself to God\
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Then cometh Jesus with them unto a place called Gethsemane, and saith unto the disciples, Sit ye here, while I go and pray yonder. And he took with him Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, and began to be sorrowful and very heavy. Then saith he unto them, My soul is exceeding sorrowful, even unto death: tarry ye here, and watch with me. AND HE WENT A LITTLE FURTHER, AND FELL ON HIS FACE, AND PRAYED, SAYING, O my Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass from me: nevertheless not as I will, but as thou wilt. And he cometh unto the disciples, and findeth them asleep, and saith unto Peter, What, could ye not watch with me one hour?

Matthew 26:36-40

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am sure you do! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to dedicate yourself to God.

When Jesus prayed in the Garden, He dedicated Himself to God. He submitted Himself to the will of God. He humbled Himself before God.

Jesus agreed with God in the prayer time. He listened to God during the prayer time. He surrendered Himself to God in the prayer time.

Prayer is an opportunity to accept the will of God. Prayer is a guidance-confirmation opportunity for your life. What a chance to submit yourself to the will of God!

You must dedicate yourself to God and surrender yourself to His will. As you get deep into prayer, you will find yourself surrendering to the will of God. The will of God is mysterious! There are many aspects of the will of God that you may not like or want, but through prayer, you will find yourself saying, “Yes”, to God.

Every time you come to pray, you must seize the opportunity to consecrate yourself to the will of God and to the plan of God. You must tell God that you love Him and that you want to do His will.

You can also tell God what you really want and what you really desire. When you do so, you can also tell Him that you are ready to do His will. God will be so impressed with you for the delicate words of consecration and dedication that you say to Him.
