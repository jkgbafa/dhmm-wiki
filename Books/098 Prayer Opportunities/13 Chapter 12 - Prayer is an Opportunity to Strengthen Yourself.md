### Chapter 12\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Strengthen Yourself\
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.

Matthew 26:41

Are you looking for an opportunity to do something spiritual? Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual! Prayer is an opportunity to strengthen yourself. Prayer is a spiritual strengthening opportunity for you. Every time you pray, your spirit is strengthened!

You are made up of a spirit, a soul and a body. Your spirit needs to be strong! Your soul needs to be strong! And your body also needs to be strong!

Today, we see men who have developed their muscles by exercising a lot and eating up to twelve eggs a day. Indeed, it is impressive to see their well-developed biceps, triceps, and deltoid muscles. It is impressive to see their well-developed pectoralis major and pectoralis minor muscles. We are impressed to see the hardened and well-developed latissimus dorsi muscles at their backs. It is a wonder to see their flat stomachs, their ripples and six-packs formed by their rectus abdominis muscles.

These muscular men are strong physically but are they strong in the spirit? Their muscles may be built up but their flesh is still weak if it cannot be controlled and made to walk on the narrow road of righteousness. Flesh that cannot be controlled and managed is weak.

Often, people who emphasise strength in the flesh are weak in the spirit. Many muscular men are willing and interested in the Lord but have no inner strength that matches their extreme muscle build-up. Their flesh may have well-developed pectoralis major and latissimus dorsi muscles, but it is still weak flesh because it is uncontrollable flesh.

There is a difference between being willing and being strong. Many people are willing and interested in doing the work of the Lord. They like God! They want to go on missions! They want to serve the Lord! They are willing to sacrifice for the Lord. But willingness is a very different thing from having the strength to do the will of God.

I look over my congregation and I see many willing people who love the Lord dearly. Their eyes are full of desire and interest in God and His wonderful service. How they want to be servants of the Lord!

How do you translate this desire and willingness into real ministry? The answer is prayer! Through prayer, you can and will become a great servant of the Lord. Your weak flesh will be controlled by your strong spirit through prayer.
