### Chapter 8\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Minister to God\
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Now there were in the church that was at Antioch certain prophets and teachers; as Barnabas, and Simeon that was called Niger, and Lucius of Cyrene, and Manaen, which had been brought up with Herod the tetrarch, and Saul. AS THEY MINISTERED TO THE LORD, and fasted, the Holy Ghost said, Separate me Barnabas and Saul for the work whereunto I have called them. And when they had fasted and prayed, and laid their hands on them, they sent them away

Acts 13:1-3

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am sure you do! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to minister to God.

The scripture above shows that the prophets and teachers just “ministered to the Lord”.

For many people, prayer is simply the presentation of a shopping list: “O God, these are the things I need from you, O God, please give me the following items.” The kind of prayer that was being made by these prophets and teachers was not the presentation of a shopping list; they were ministering to the Lord.

1\. To minister to someone is to do things for the person.

2\. To minister to someone is to love the person.

3\. To minister to someone is to give attention to the person.

4\. To minister to someone is to have time to attend to the person. When you minister to a person, you often end up fasting because you don’t have time to eat while you are attending to the person you are ministering to. That’s how the prophets and teachers were ministering to the Lord – with fasting!

5\. To minister to someone is to talk to the person gently and patiently.

6\. To minister to someone is to nurse and gently care for the person.

There is a way to minister to God Himself, and that is through prayer. You can pray in such a way that God will know that you did not come with a list of items that you need. O, what a great opportunity you have to minister to the Lord!

Many people fall in love with their nurses and their servants. Many “queenly” wives are surprised when their husbands fall in love with their maidservants. Many years ago, a man called Uncle James told me that “Service is beautiful.” He said to me that anyone who serves you will be beautiful to you because service is beautiful and attractive. It is not so attractive to be a “queen”. You must learn this principle - being a servant and ministering to your master makes you beautiful.

Prayer is your great opportunity to minister to God.

Prayer is an opportunity to relax with God and to love Him.

Prayer is an opportunity to sit quietly by God’s side and minister to Him.

Prayer is your opportunity to be like a nurse and a lover to God Himself.

God will love you as you come to Him and minister to Him.

Impress God by hiding your shopping list.

Talk to God without coming up with so many things that you need Him to do for you.

No one likes feeling that he is being used.

God is not happy when He is only remembered when people are needy and hungry. God also wants to be loved and ministered to!
