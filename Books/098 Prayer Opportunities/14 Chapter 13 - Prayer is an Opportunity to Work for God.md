### Chapter 13\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Work for God\
--------------------------------------------------------------

But we will continue to devote ourselves steadfastly to prayer and the ministry of the Word.

Acts 6:4 (AMPC)

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am sure you do! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to work for God!

1\. Prayer is half the work of a pastor.

But we will continue to devote ourselves steadfastly to PRAYER and the ministry of the WORD.

Acts 6:4 (AMPC)

Pastors should understand that ministry is basically prayer and the ministry of the Word. The very first pastors who existed defined this for us in the Book of Acts. Peter said he would not submit himself to secular work, but he would devote himself steadfastly to prayer and the ministry of the Word.

Peter had learned from Jesus that ministry is prayer and the ministry of the Word. He had followed Jesus for three years and watched how Jesus prayed and ministered the Word. Jesus’ pattern of prayer and the ministry of the Word had released the greatest power and established the church of God. Peter was determined to follow the same pattern to establish the church of God.

2\. Not praying is the sin of negligence.

Moreover as for me, GOD FORBID THAT I SHOULD SIN AGAINST THE LORD IN CEASING TO PRAY FOR YOU: but I will teach you the good and the right way:

1 Samuel 12:23

The prophet Samuel promised to pray and teach the people in the right way. He swore that he would not stop praying for them. He knew that not praying for the people was a sin. The sins of a pastor are sometimes different from the sins of a normal Christian.

Pastors, make sure you pray for the people! As soon as you are appointed a pastor you must pray for the people or you will be declared a sinful pastor.

3\. Prayer is the labour of a pastor.

Epaphras, who is one of you, a servant of Christ, saluteth you, ALWAYS LABOURING FERVENTLY FOR YOU IN PRAYERS, that ye may stand perfect and complete in all the will of God.

Colossians 4:12

Prayer is the hard and laborious work of a pastor. Do you want to walk in the will of God? Do you want to be in the ministry and do the work of God? Then you must learn to labour in prayer! You must learn to sweat, suffer and persist in the monotonous drudgery of the hard labour of prayer.

Epaphras is an example of a prayerful person who laboured for the Colossians in prayer.

4\. The labourers are few! What does this mean? It means the prayerful people are few.

Then saith he unto his disciples, The harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few; PRAY YE THEREFORE THE LORD OF THE HARVEST, THAT HE WILL SEND FORTH LABOURERS into his harvest.

Matthew 9:37-38

Prayer is the labour that we must undertake to reap the harvest of lost souls. The human race has fallen low into degradation, misery and wickedness. The appalling state of the human race is apparent to all. When we pray for labourers, we are praying for people who will join in the prayer work that is so necessary for ministry.

5\. Prayer is the work of a priest.

There was in the days of Herod, the king of Judaea, a certain priest named Zacharias, of the course of Abia: and his wife was of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elisabeth. And they were both righteous before God, walking in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord blameless. And they had no child, because that Elisabeth was barren, and they both were now well stricken in years. And it came to pass, that while he executed the priest’s office before God in the order of his course, ACCORDING TO THE CUSTOM OF THE PRIEST’S OFFICE, HIS LOT WAS TO BURN INCENSE when he went into the temple of the Lord. And the whole multitude of the people were praying without at the time of incense.

Luke 1:5-10

Priests were servants of God whose duty it was to offer sacrifices and prayers to the Lord. As you can see, Zacharias, the father of John the Baptist, was a priest. He had the duty of burning incense before the Lord’s presence. His lot was to burn incense in the presence of the Lord. Your lot is to pray before the Lord and burn spiritual incense that will ascend to heaven.

When He had taken the book, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb, each one holding a harp and GOLDEN BOWLS FULL OF INCENSE, WHICH ARE THE PRAYERS OF THE SAINTS.

Revelation 5:8 (NASB95)

The golden bowls are full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints. Your prayers are called incense. You must offer much incense before the Lord.

Yet again, in the Book of Revelation, we see that prayers are the golden bowls full of incense. “And another angel came and stood at the altar, having a golden censer; and there was given unto him MUCH INCENSE, THAT HE SHOULD OFFER IT WITH THE PRAYERS OF ALL SAINTS upon the golden altar which was before the throne. AND THE SMOKE OF THE INCENSE, WHICH CAME WITH THE PRAYERS OF THE SAINTS, ascended up before God out of the angel’s hand. And the angel took the censer, and filled it with fire of the altar, and cast it into the earth: and there were VOICES, AND THUNDERINGS, AND LIGHTNINGS, AND AN EARTHQUAKE” (Revelation 8:3-5).

In the realm of the spirit, there are reactions to these bowls of incense that you release into the heavens. Through your prayers and supplications, there are voices, thunderings, lightnings and earthquakes that are unleashed into the world.
