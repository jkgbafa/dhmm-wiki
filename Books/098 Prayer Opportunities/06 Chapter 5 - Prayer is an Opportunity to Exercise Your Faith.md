### Chapter 5\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Exercise Your Faith\
---------------------------------------------------------------------

And he spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint; Saying, There was in a city a judge, which feared not God, neither regarded man: And there was a widow in that city; and she came unto him, saying, Avenge me of mine adversary. And he would not for a while: but afterward he said within himself, Though I fear not God, nor regard man; Yet because this widow troubleth me, I will avenge her, lest by her continual coming she weary me. And the Lord said, Hear what the unjust judge saith. And shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him, though he bear long with them? I tell you that he will avenge them speedily. NEVERTHELESS WHEN THE SON OF MAN COMETH, SHALL HE FIND FAITH ON THE EARTH?

Luke 18:1-8

I am sure you want an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to exercise your faith!

Jesus established the truth that prayer is an act of faith. This is why He asked directly after teaching on prayer if the Son of man would find men of faith on the earth.

It is important to understand that prayer is your opportunity to exercise faith. Prayer is an opportunity to walk by faith. Prayer is a faith opportunity. Prayer is walking by faith and living by faith.

Every time you pray, you are exercising faith. Every time you pray, you are speaking to someone you cannot see, and that is an act of faith. Faith is the evidence of things you cannot see. Therefore, anytime you are dealing with things you cannot see, you are exercising faith. Faith is the assurance of things hoped for. Any time you speak to God, you are speaking about things you hope for. Any time you speak to God, you are hoping that all that you pray for will come to pass.

Now faith is the assurance of things hoped for, the conviction of things not seen.

Hebrews 11:1 (NASB)

When you sit up all night, speaking into the air to someone you cannot feel, someone you cannot touch, someone who does not say a word back to you, you need great faith to continue speaking.

I once counselled a young lady who gave short, curt answers to every question I asked. She was not interested in having a conversation with me. Her brief, single-word sentences discouraged me greatly, and I lost hope in relating to her. I did not believe that anything I said would save her or change her. I quickly disconnected from her and stopped calling or attempting to counsel her.

Why did I lose hope? Why did I lose faith? I lost faith because there was very little response from this lady. When you pray, there is indeed very little response from the Lord. It seems as though He does not hear. It seems as though He is not there. It seems as though the Bible is just a fairy tale.

Indeed, it takes great faith to carry on in prayer. Where are the answers to all the prayers we have ever prayed? Where are the answers to all the cries that have come from our hearts? It seems as though God does not hear, and yet, we must continue praying and believing that God will avenge us and contend against those who contend against us. Anyone who persists in prayer is a man of faith!

I see many pastors who do not pray much. If a man of God does not pray, it means he is not a man of faith. If a man of God does not pray, he is not a man who walks and lives by faith in Almighty God. If your pastor does not pray much, it means your pastor is not a man of God and he is not a man of faith.
