### Chapter 3\

Prayer is an Opportunity to Turn Things Around\
--------------------------------------------------------------------

FOR I KNOW THAT THIS SHALL TURN to my salvation THROUGH YOUR PRAYER, and the supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ

Philippians 1:19

Do you want an opportunity to do something spiritual? I am sure you do! Prayer is an opportunity to do something spiritual. Prayer is an opportunity to turn things around. Many things will turn around because you prayed.

Perhaps you are looking for situations to turn around. The way things are made to turn around is through prayer. Your captivity can be turned around as you learn to pray. Those who end up singing the song in this scripture are those who have learnt to turn things around through prayer.

When the LORD turned again the captivity of Zion, we were like them that dream. Then was our mouth filled with laughter, and our tongue with singing: then said they among the heathen, The LORD hath done great things for them. The LORD hath done great things for us; whereof we are glad.

Psalm 126:1-3

After singing about how the Lord had turned their captivity, the psalmist prayed that the Lord should do it again. Then he declared that those who sow in tears would reap in joy. He was explaining how the joy of the turning of the captivity came about. That joy comes to those who sow in tears. Prayer is sowing seeds in tears. The turning of captivities is the reaping of joy from what has been sown in tears.

Turn again our captivity, O LORD, as the streams in the south. They that sow in tears shall reap in joy. He that goeth forth and weepeth, bearing precious seed, shall doubtless come again with rejoicing, bringing his sheaves with him.

Psalm 126:4-6
