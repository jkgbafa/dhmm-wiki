### What is Bible Memorisation?\

Bible memorisation is a great foundation of a great ministry. Today, many people do not stand on a strong foundation and cannot therefore generate a mighty work of God on the earth.

By hard work and with a diligent and humble attitude, you will develop the necessary character that can carry the great gifts of God.  Through Bible memorisation your ministry will go to the highest possible levels.

Bible memorisation is simply committing key scriptures to memory so that you can quote them when necessary. 

**Become a Follower of Christ**

> **And he saith unto them, Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men.**

**Matthew 4:19 **

You must understand that the aim is to make you a true follower of Jesus Christ who memorised scriptures. 

Every day in life is a day for memorising a verse in the Bible. 

Every day in your life is a great chance to develop a good and improved character. 

Every interaction with another person is a chance to memorise a scripture.

Every interaction with a fellow believer is a chance to be tested on your memorisation skills.  

In the following pages, you will find a set of scriptures every Christian is expected to memorise.  I have personally chosen each one of these scriptures. These scriptures are not intended for you to just be able to refer to. They are for memorisation! There are many scriptures that you will know about but there are certain scriptures you must be able to quote! 

Carry this book with you at all times. Be ready to quote any of these scriptures at any time when asked to. When you have been a Christian for some few months, you must be able to quote any of these scriptures. Indeed you must become a Bible-quoting man of God.  

I declare you to be a follower of Jesus Christ and a good example of ministry through this unique Bible Memorisation book!
