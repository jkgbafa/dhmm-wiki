Chapter 3\
-------------------------------

### Genesis \

**1. Genesis 8:22**

> While the earth remaineth, seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease.   

**2. Genesis 32:28**

> And he said, Thy name shall be called no more Jacob, but Israel: for as a prince hast thou power with God and with men, and hast prevailed.

**3. Genesis 28:16-17**

> And Jacob awaked out of his sleep, and he said, Surely the Lord is in this place; and I knew it not. And he was afraid, and said, how dreadful is this place! This is none other but the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven.

**4. Genesis 45:7-8**

> And God sent me before you to preserve you a posterity in the earth, and to save your lives by a great deliverance. So now it was not you that sent me hither, but God: and he hath made me a father to Pharaoh, and lord of all his house, and a ruler throughout all the land of Egypt.

**5. Genesis 49:25**

> Even by the God of thy father, who shall help thee; and by the Almighty, who shall bless thee with blessings of heaven above, blessings of the deep that lieth under, blessings of the breasts, and of the womb:
