### Chapter 7\

Loyalty is Necessary for You to Have a Full Eternal Reward\
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

His lord said unto him, WELL DONE, THOU GOOD AND FAITHFUL SERVANT: thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.

Matthew 25:21

Well done, good and faithful servant! Are these not the words you want to hear? Remember that it is not only your good works that matter. Your faithfulness (loyalty) is very important. It is not, “Well done, good servant”. It is, “Well done, GOOD AND FAITHFUL servant.”

Faithfulness means loyalty, constancy, reliability and fidelity; it also means to be unchanging.

A person can have only a few years of practical and effective ministry. Jesus only ministered for three and a half years, but He extended His ministry and His influence through an effective and loyal team. Notice that Jesus’ ministry has branched out into the whole world and has spanned two thousand years.

The church will continue! None of us is indispensable! God can do without us! We need loyal people who will stay faithful to the vision so that the ministry will continue into the future. One day, we all hope to hear those famous words, “Well done, good and faithful servant.”

Those who stick with you through difficult times are different from those who come in when everything is working well. Those who benefit from the blessings of success are the faithful and loyal ones.

Well Done!

Jesus Himself told His twelve disciples that they would be treated differently from any other minister. They would have a special kingdom, and their names would even be written on the foundations of the New Jerusalem.

And the wall of the city had twelve foundations, and in them the names of the twelve apostles...

Revelation 21:14

Even the great men of God we know today will not qualify for this special reward. Jesus gave a reason for this special reward – they had been loyal and faithful to Him through the most difficult part of His ministry.

Ye are they which have CONTINUED with me in my temptations... I appoint unto you a kingdom...

Luke 22:28-29

In the good times, everybody seems loyal. But you see, loyalty is most appreciated in hard times.

A Few Have Stood

I appreciate very much the pastors who have stood with me throughout my ministry. They have watched me rise and supported me, even in my mistakes. To me, they are different from others who joined me later. As Jesus said, they have a “special reward!”

Be faithful! Be loyal! Be constant! Learn all you can about loyalty and disloyalty. Build your church and ministry on these foundations and truths. The pillars of loyalty will give you long-lasting fruit in the earth.

“And further, by these, my son, be admonished: of making many books there is no end; and much study is a weariness of the flesh.” Ecclesiastes 12:12
