### Chapter 1\

Loyalty Is a Prophetic Teaching\
-----------------------------------------------------

WE HAVE ALSO A MORE SURE WORD OF PROPHECY; WHEREUNTO YE DO WELL THAT YE TAKE HEED, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place, until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts.

2 Peter 1:19

1\. Loyalty is a prophetic teaching

The teachings on loyalty and disloyalty are prophetic and describe human behaviour. The teachings on loyalty describe how traitors will arise in the future. The teachings on loyalty describe how wolves will arise in the churches as the years go by. The teachings on loyalty describe how men will make shipwreck of their faith. The teachings on loyalty describe those who will leave you, those who will be ungrateful, those who will pretend and those who will become dangerous sons.

The teachings on loyalty and disloyalty prophetically describe the behaviour of rebels and ungrateful men and exposes them, making it difficult for them to operate and wreak their havoc and destruction. The teachings on loyalty and disloyalty are prophetic because people will fulfil the words of this book without even knowing about them.

2\. Loyalty is the highest kind of wisdom

Loyalty is a subject that was taught by King Solomon, the wisest man who ever lived. That is a good enough reason to teach about loyalty and disloyalty. Do not let any miserable ignoramus dissuade you from studying or teaching the subject of loyalty and disloyalty. Wretched losers, who are traitors, do not like it when you preach and teach on the subject of loyalty and disloyalty. Breakaway pastors, rebellious men and disgruntled former employees absolutely dislike these teachings because the teachings prophetically describe them in great detail.

3\. The loyalty teaching is essential for raising leaders

People who do not build churches do not understand why we teach on the subjects of loyalty and disloyalty. Once you are dealing with leaders, you will need to teach them about loyalty and disloyalty. You will need faithfulness and loyalty as a prime characteristic of every leader and steward.

Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.

1 Corinthians 4:2

4\. The loyalty teaching is required to re-set and re-direct the human nature

People are more primed to be disloyal than to be loyal! The nature of satan has been imparted to the human race through the fall of man. Men, including pastors, often lie, steal, deceive and betray without effort. If you do not teach about loyalty, you will have a bunch of traitors, betrayers and disloyal men as your key leaders.

5\. The loyalty teaching is important to save innocent ones from disloyal men

But Absalom sent spies throughout the tribes of Israel, saying, “As soon as you hear the sound of the trumpet, then you shall say, ‘Absalom is king in Hebron!’” Then two hundred men went with Absalom from Jerusalem, who were invited and WENT INNOCENTLY, for they did not know anything.

2 Samuel 15:10-11 (NASB)

Many people follow treacherous men innocently, not fully understanding what they are doing. Two hundred people followed Absalom in the innocence of their minds. They did not understand what Absalom was doing. They did not realise that Absalom was actually trying to overthrow and kill his own father. Slanderers depend on their bad and deceptive stories to gain a following. Slanderers are dirty talkers who spread slander and ill-will against their pastor. No one should follow a slanderer.

A slanderer is a devil! The word “slanderer” is translated from the word “diabolos”. A man with a lot of negative stories about God’s servant is a dangerous person. Do not weld yourself to his future disasters by joining him. It is important for us to teach about loyalty and disloyalty so that people do not follow modern day Absaloms.

I have encountered a few such Absalom personalities in my life and ministry and I can tell you, it will not end well for anyone who walks in the ways of Absalom.
