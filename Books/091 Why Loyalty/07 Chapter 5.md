### Chapter 5\

Loyalty is Necessary for the Love of God to Fill the Church\
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For in Jesus Christ neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision; BUT FAITH WHICH WORKETH BY LOVE.

Galatians 5:6

Your faith to have a mega church will only work through love. Your faith to build God’s house and to be obedient to His will, so that the church marches forward, is a good thing. But your faith in God will only work through love. You must love the Lord, and you must love His people. Having bitterness and hatred in your heart will not help your faith to work. Faith works by love! Faith works by loving all the people whom God brings to you. All things work together for good to those who love God. Loving God is essential if you want all things to work together for your good.

Eye has not seen, and neither has ear heard the things that God has prepared for those who love Him. Do you want to see the “eye has not seen” and hear the “ear hath not heard” things that God has prepared? Then you need to walk in love!

You cannot say that you love God when you do not love the brethren. How can you say you love God, whom you have not seen, when you do not love your brother, whom you have seen? The ministry is supposed to operate with the power of love, unity and teamwork.

By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples, if ye have love one to another.

John 13:35

To minister as an effective leader, you need to exhibit the love that Jesus spoke about. People are attracted by love. When they see leaders who flow together in genuine love, they are attracted. You must never forget that your church members are not blind. Neither are they deaf. They can see and feel disunity and discord when it is there. Confusion and disunity drive people away from the church.

Sheep Only Drink from Still Waters

One thing that every pastor must know about sheep is that they drink from still waters. If the water is murky and rough, the sheep will stay away. You see, they are not sure if there is a crocodile or a snake in the water!

…he leadeth me beside the still waters.

Psalm 23:2

Whenever there is treachery and distrust, your church members become scared and wary and stay away.
