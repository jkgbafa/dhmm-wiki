### Chapter 3\

Loyalty is the Main Qualification for Ministry\
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.

1 Corinthians 4:2

Faithfulness and loyalty are the requirements for being in the ministry. A requirement is something you cannot do without. For instance, you can do without a music player, a coffee cup holder, an air conditioner, or a fridge in your car. None of these are requirements for a car. However, you cannot do without windscreen wipers. You cannot do without the lights! You cannot do without brakes! Windscreen wipers, headlights and brakes are requirements for a car.

In the same way, loyalty, faithfulness, dependability, constancy, fidelity and being unchangeable are requirements for ministry. Cheerfulness, good singing, having a beard, having a deep voice, and being handsome are not qualifications for being in the ministry.

Loyalty is the principal qualification for every minister. The best kind of person you need to employ in ministry is a faithful, dependable, devoted, loyal, unchanging and constant person. An inexperienced person is likely to think that the more gifted you are, the more qualified you are for the ministry. My little experience has shown me that it is the faithful and loyal people in the church who are most qualified to be leaders.

Someone who is inexperienced would think that a friendly brother would make a good pastor. He may also think that someone with good oratory skills would make the best preacher. Do not make that mistake. The Bible teaches us that the cardinal requirement for leadership is faithfulness and not anything else!

…it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.

1 Corinthians 4:2

I have many wonderful pastors who work with me. Many of them are not overly friendly, flashy, or particularly gifted. But time has proven that faithful people are the best gifts that God has given to me.

The best kind of person you need for ministry is a faithful, dependable, devoted, loyal, unchanging and constant person!
