### Preface

One of the most disturbing things in modern-day Christianity is the inconsistent lifestyles of some believers.The double standards practised by some Christians have given all believers a bad report among them that are without.As you read this book, you will see the many areas in which our day-to-day lives fall short of true Christian standards.Then, after you have honestly located yourself, you will be in a better position to climb out of pretense and hypocrisy.I have learnt to be myself, and so should you. There’s no need to try and impress anyone. There’s no need to try to be like anyone else.It is my prayer that this teaching will go a long way to make our secret lives conform to our public image.
