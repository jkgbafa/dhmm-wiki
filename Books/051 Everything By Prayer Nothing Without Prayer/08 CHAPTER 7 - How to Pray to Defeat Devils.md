### CHAPTER 7\

How to Pray to Defeat Devils\
--------------------------------------------------

**And there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit; and he cried out, Saying, LET US ALONE; what have we to do with thee, thou Jesus of Nazareth? Art thou come to destroy us? I know thee who thou art, the Holy One of God.**\

**Mark 1:23-24**

You will notice how the demons shouted out, “Let us alone!”   It is important that you do not leave demons alone!  Do not let the devil be comfortable around you.  Bind him! Pour fire on him! Fasten him with chains! Curse his presence! Rebuke him! Reject him! Block him! Make declarations against him. Prophesy against him! \

What you may not know is that devils were here before we arrived. The same demons that challenged Jesus Christ two thousand years ago are still alive and inhabiting more and more people. These demons are the ones resisting all that you do. Every kind of stress and resistance you feel is demonic in its origins.  \

**The Example of Jesus**

Jesus was in the wilderness for forty days and nights. What did He pray about for forty days and nights?  It is amazing that we never hear of any prayer topic that Jesus prayed in the wilderness.  We just read of Him dealing with the devil.  Jesus bound the devil in the wilderness for forty days and nights.  The significant things that Jesus did in the wilderness were rebuking the devil and putting him in his place. \

An important part of your prayer involves talking to the devil. Jesus spoke to the devil directly in the wilderness.  Notice in the scriptures below how Jesus spoke directly to satan and demons.\

BUT HE ANSWERED and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God. \

Matthew 4:4 

JESUS SAID UNTO HIM, it is written again, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God. \

Matthew 4:7 

THEN SAITH JESUS UNTO HIM, Get thee hence, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve. \

Matthew 4:10

But HE TURNED, AND SAID unto Peter, GET THEE BEHIND ME, SATAN: thou art an offence unto me: for thou savourest not the things that be of God, but those that be of men. \

Matthew 16:23

Jesus never left the devil alone. From the day that He began His ministry to the very end, He was dealing with the devil. \

At the very beginning He dealt with him in the wilderness.  On the cross, Jesus dealt the final blow to satan’s power over mankind.  

All territories are marked by demons. Wherever you live or work now have residential demons. Demons behave like intelligent animals. All through the Bible demons are described as animals: serpents, frogs, lions, dragons, flies and so on.  \

There are scriptures that prove that demons are actually spiritual animals with a high level of intelligence and communication capabilities. You will remember that the serpent in the Garden of Eden was able to communicate with Eve. This serpent was actually the devil. There are certain movies like Jurassic Park that depict intelligent animals capable of communicating with each other. These movies are frightening indeed and show how dangerous, intelligent communicating animals are.  \

There are demons where you live. These intelligent communicating spiritual animals are very dangerous. They were living there before you came on the scene. In the scripture below, you see a dragon that lies in the midst of the rivers. At one point Ezekiel mentions Pharaoh, the king of Egypt then he switches to the dragon that lies in the midst of the rivers. The dragon in the midst of the rivers is the intelligent, communicating spiritual animal behind Pharaoh.  That is what a demon is!  \

Son of man, set thy face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him, and against all Egypt:  Speak, and say, Thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I am against thee, Pharaoh king of Egypt, THE GREAT DRAGON THAT LIETH IN THE MIDST OF HIS RIVERS, which hath said, My river is mine own, and I have made it for myself.  But I will put hooks in thy jaws, and I will cause the fish of thy rivers to stick unto thy scales, and I will bring thee up out of the midst of thy rivers, and all the fish of thy rivers shall stick unto thy scales.  And I will leave thee thrown into the wilderness, thee and all the fish of thy rivers: thou shalt fall upon the open fields; thou shalt not be brought together, nor gathered: I have given thee for meat to the beasts of the field and to the fowls of the heaven.\

Ezekiel 29:2-5

Also, in Ezekiel, he speaks against the prince of Tyrus who is obviously a man. But then he switches to speak about the King of Tyrus who is obviously a demon spirit.  The physical King of Tyrus was not in the Garden of Eden and was not a cherub.  So the King of Tyrus is a spiritual and demonic power behind the Prince of Tyrus.  \

Son of man, take up a lamentation upon the KING OF TYRUS, and say unto him, Thus saith the Lord God; Thou sealest up the sum, full of wisdom, and perfect in beauty. Thou hast been in Eden the garden of God; every precious stone was thy covering, the sardius, topaz, and the diamond, the beryl, the onyx, and the jasper, the sapphire, the emerald, and the carbuncle, and gold: the workmanship of thy tabrets and of thy pipes was prepared in thee in the day that thou wast created. THOU ART THE ANOINTED CHERUB that covereth; and I have set thee so: thou wast upon the holy mountain of God; thou hast walked up and down in the midst of the stones of fire.\

Ezekiel 28:12-14

The things that are fighting you have a demonic power behind them. That is the power you must deal with. You must be prepared to deal with entities like the king of Tyrus as well as entities like the dragon in the midst of the rivers.  \

Perhaps, you are dealing with the prince of your country.  \

Perhaps there is a tree in your yard or in your garden that houses an evil and demonic bird.  Perhaps you are dealing with a dragon or a serpent in the river near your house. Perhaps there is a lake, a lagoon or a river near your house in which there is an evil spirit. Perhaps the sea behind your house is hosting an evil demonic being. Notice how the beast rose up out of the sea.  The beast was living in the ocean and it came out of the water to manifest itself.  \

And I stood upon the sand of the sea, and saw a beast rise up out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns, and upon his horns ten crowns, and upon his heads the name of blasphemy.\

Revelation 13:1

All your prayers must include the binding of the devil. Do not forget this rule: Do not leave the devil alone! The demons shouted, “Let us alone”. That is exactly what you must not do. You must not leave them alone! You must bind them continuously! The word “bind” in the scripture below means, “to fasten with chains.” \

Every time you bind the devil you fasten him with chains and you throw him into more chains. To bind the devil means “to forbid” and “to prohibit” him.  Every time you bind the devil, you forbid him from manoeuvring around you. Continue to forbid the activities of intelligent animal demons around you.  \

Verily I say unto you, WHATSOEVER YE SHALL BIND on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.\

Matthew 18:18

Every time you watch a wild life movie you can learn something about demon spirits. For instance, wild dogs have a strategy of running after their prey until the prey is tired.  They can pursue an antelope for several kilometres until it drops to the ground out of pure exhaustion. Then the pack of wild dogs descends on it and consumes it. One of the strategies of demon powers fighting you is to wear you out just as wild dogs wear out antelopes.  Note the scripture below.  \

And he shall speak great words against the most High, and SHALL WEAR OUT THE SAINTS of the most High, and think to change times and laws: and they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time.\

Daniel 7:25

Another animal to watch is the viper.  Vipers simply inject a drop of venomous corruption, poison, confusion, and disloyalty into the church. After one bite, the church is never the same.  Just as a human being will struggle to live after being bitten by a viper, the church struggles to live after it has experienced an attack from a spiritual viper.  All forms of disloyalty, treachery, divisions and accusations in the church are demonic in origin.  Confusion and disloyalty are the works of vipers and cobras. These vipers are spiritual, intelligent communicating creatures that are ready to destroy everything you have built. You must cut off their heads in your prayers. These vipers may operate through an assistant pastor or a proud church member. 

You must rise up every night and fasten demonic powers with chains. You must forbid the manoeuvring, roaming and hunting demon packs around you.  You must prevent demons from being comfortable around your dwelling place. You must block the activities of deadly flies bringing poison and death into your life.  All works of darkness tend to oppose and resist you. This is because the devil is the opposer. Bind the opposer!  Curse the accuser! Fasten the tempter! Chain the deceiver! Burn the liar! Bind the devil in the name of Jesus!   

Watch out for the lion! Demon powers eat up the flesh. They bite through the flesh and lick up the flesh.  It is unbelievable to see a lion literally eat an entire zebra. They devour every part and leave nothing behind. Satan is most interested in your flesh. All works and activities of your flesh are of interest to the lion.  He is ready to sink his teeth into your flesh and make you his meal. 

Do not allow demons to occupy your anus, your vagina, your penis, your feelings, your mouth, your tongue and your eyes. Forbid it!  Block it! Fasten demonic powers with chains. Bind them in the day. Bind them at night. Prophesy against them. Pour the fire of God on them. Make declarations against the forces of wickedness arrayed against you.  You are free from devils in the name of Jesus!  Read this chapter again.  \
