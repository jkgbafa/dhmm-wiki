### CHAPTER 18\

How to Pray Long Prayers\
----------------------------------------------

\

Many lazy Christians do not want to spend much time in prayer.  However, Jesus Christ had long times of prayer with His Father. His long times of prayer struck His disciples and they could only ask, “What do you say when you pray?” 

One day, one of my pastors saw me interacting with a group of students. I stood with them for hours after church, chatting and laughing. He was intrigued and asked me privately as we were going home, “What were you talking to the young students about?”  He said, “I am amazed that you are able to talk to these students for so long. What do you tell them?”  

Perhaps, that was the same kind of question that the disciples asked Jesus. “What do you talk to the Father about?” They wanted to know why He spent so much time in prayer. 

If you want to follow Jesus Christ, you are going to have to learn how to pray for a long time.  

1. **Pray for a long time when you are starting your ministry.** This is what Jesus did.  He spent six weeks in the wilderness, waiting on God and praying for His future ministry. 

Then was Jesus led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil. And when he had fasted forty days and forty nights, he was afterward an hungred.

Matthew 4:1-2

2. **Pray for a long time when you are taking vital decisions. ** This is what Jesus did. He prayed all night when He was choosing His disciples. He had come from heaven. He knew the Father’s opinion and He knew many other things. Yet, He spent all night praying just to take a quality decision.  Cover all important decisions with long hours of prayer.  

And it came to pass in those days, that he went out into a mountain to pray, and continued all night in prayer to God.  And when it was day, he called unto him his disciples: and of them he chose twelve, whom also he named apostles;\

Luke 6:12-13

3. **Pray for a long time when you are ministering.** 

Jesus separated Himself a great while before day and prayed. He did this in the middle of an evangelistic trip.  Many people stop praying when they are in the midst of a hectic schedule.  You must maintain your prayer life at all times. 

And in the morning, rising up a great while before day, he went out, and departed into a solitary place, and there prayed.

Mark 1:35

4. **Pray for a long time when you are facing a crisis.** \

Jesus prayed for hours in the Garden of Gethsemane. His disciples fell asleep and could not keep up with Him.   Praying for long hours is the mark of a person who has developed a relationship with God. 

Then cometh Jesus with them unto a place called Gethsemane, and saith unto the disciples, Sit ye here, while I go and pray yonder. . . .

And he went a little further, and fell on his face, and prayed, saying, O my Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass from me: nevertheless not as I will, but as thou wilt. . . 

And he left them, and went away again, and prayed the third time, saying the same words.

Matthew 26:36, 39, 44

Then said Jesus unto them, Be not afraid: GO TELL MY BRETHREN THAT THEY GO INTO GALILEE, and there shall they see me. . . THEN THE ELEVEN DISCIPLES WENT AWAY INTO GALILEE, INTO A MOUNTAIN WHERE JESUS HAD APPOINTED THEM. And when they saw him, they worshipped him: but some doubted. And Jesus came and spake unto them, saying, all power is given unto me in heaven and in earth. Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost.

Matthew 28:10, 16-19
