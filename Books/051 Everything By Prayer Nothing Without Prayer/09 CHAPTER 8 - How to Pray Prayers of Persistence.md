### CHAPTER 8\

How to Pray Prayers of Persistence\
--------------------------------------------------------

**And he spake a parable unto them to this end, THAT MEN OUGHT ALWAYS TO PRAY, AND NOT TO FAINT; Saying, There was in a city a judge, which feared not God, neither regarded man: And there was a widow in that city; and she came unto him, saying, Avenge me of mine adversary. And HE WOULD NOT FOR A WHILE: but afterward he said within himself, Though I fear not God, nor regard man; Yet because THIS WIDOW TROUBLETH ME, I will avenge her, lest by her continual coming she weary me. And the Lord said, Hear what the unjust judge saith. And shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him, though he bear long with them? I tell you that he will avenge them speedily. Nevertheless when the Son of man cometh, shall he find faith on the earth?**\

**Luke 18:1-8**

Jesus Christ encourages us to pray with persistence.  He has information about how the Father operates.  \

**Jesus Christ’s Principles of Persistence**

1. **God does not fear anyone.**  God has no favourites and no reason to be partial towards you or anyone.  

2. **Many prayers are not answered for a while.**  In the parable, Jesus said the judge would not answer for a while.  “But afterward he said within himself, though I fear not God, nor regard man….” Do not worry if your prayers are not answered for a while. That is the pattern that Jesus said would exist.  

3. **Your continual prayers trouble God and wear Him out.**  He said, “Yet because this widow troubleth me, I will avenge her, lest by her continual coming she weary me.”  You may not know how much your prayers trouble God. But they do!  Keep up the prayer. Open your mouth. Stand at the same place and say the same things to God. He will hear you and He will avenge you.  \

4. **God will execute vengeance on your enemies.** Your enemies are going to experience fire because of your prayers.  Your enemies can expect trouble because of the prayers that you are sending into the heavens. God is a God of vengeance.  God will come with a vengeance and save you. Persistent prayers result in you being saved and vengeance being executed on your enemies.  \

Say to them that are of a fearful heart, Be strong, fear not: behold, your God will come with vengeance, even God with a recompence; he will come and save you.

Isaiah 35:4

5. **You must learn how to bear long in prayers.**   I do not know why you have to pray for so long. But according to Jesus, there is a need to bear long in prayers.    “And shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him, though he bear long with them? I tell you that he will avenge them speedily…” (Luke 18:7,8)

Jesus Christ is giving us information about how prayers are transmitted and how they are answered. Bearing long is essential for getting your prayers answered.  

6. **You must learn how to pray and never give up.**  Jesus told us this parable so that we would pray and NOT FAINT!  Don’t stop praying!  Keep on with the work of God!  God is hearing your prayers.

7. **Persistent prayer requires a lot of faith.**  You require faith to pray to a God you cannot see. You require faith to pray to a God repeatedly about the same thing. We know that God heard us the first time we prayed. So why do we have to keep on praying?  

I don’t know why! But I do know that you require faith to pray in the first place and even more faith to continue praying.  This is why Jesus asked, “…Nevertheless when the Son of man cometh, shall he find faith on the earth?  (Luke 18:8).
