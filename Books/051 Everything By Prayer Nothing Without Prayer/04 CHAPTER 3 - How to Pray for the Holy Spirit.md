### CHAPTER 3\

## How to Pray for the Holy Spirit

**There is a man in thy kingdom, in whom is the spirit of the holy gods; and in the days of thy father light and understanding and wisdom, like the wisdom of the gods, was found in him; whom the king Nebuchadnezzar thy father, the king, I say, thy father, made master of the magicians, astrologers, Chaldeans, and soothsayers; **

**Daniel 5:11**

Make up your mind to pray for the Holy Spirit! Decide to pray for the seven Spirits of God.  The Holy Spirit will make you stand out.  You will be called up for special occasions. \

When you have the Holy Spirit, you will stand out because of the light and understanding that is in you. Daniel was outstanding because of the Holy Spirit in him.  \

Belshazzar’s wife who did not know the Holy Spirit recognized the presence of an excellent spirit in him.  She called the Holy Spirit “the spirit of the holy gods.” She realized that there was a level of light, understanding and wisdom in Daniel that could only be supernatural.  It is time to believe in praying for the Holy Spirit.  

**Things You Should Know about Praying for the Holy Spirit**

**1. You must pray for the Holy Spirit because Jesus specifically asked us to do so.  **

**If a son shall ask bread of any of you that is a father, will he give him a stone? or if he ask a fish, will he for a fish give him a serpent?  Or if he shall ask an egg, will he offer him a scorpion?  If ye then, being evil, know how to give good gifts unto your children: HOW MUCH MORE SHALL YOUR HEAVENLY FATHER GIVE THE HOLY SPIRIT TO THEM THAT ASK HIM?**

**Luke 11:11-13**

This is the only stand-alone topic that Jesus taught His disciples to pray about.  Jesus promised to send the Holy Spirit, the Comforter, to help us. He promised to pour out His Spirit on us. And yet He still asked us to pray for the Holy Spirit. Here, we see this principle again. Even though it is the will of God and the purpose of God, it will not happen until we pray for it.  

**2. You must pray for the Holy Spirit because you are a human being clothed with weakness and sin. **

**For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.**

**2 Timothy 1:7**

The Spirit of power, love and a sound mind are what you need. This Spirit of power will strengthen you.  The Holy Spirit will make your mind work well because it is the Spirit of a sound mind.  

It is difficult to find a group of human beings who are not corrupt, weak, sinful and evil. The scripture teaches that when we die, we are “…Sown in corruption; raised in corruption: sown in dishonour; raised in glory: sown in weakness; raised in power:” (1 Corinthians 15:42-43).  \

What a vile fleshly garment we wear as human beings!  Our corruption, our failings, our temptations, our desires, our negative tendencies, our distorted temperaments, our personality disorders, our deceptions, our carnality, our pride have left us almost incapable of doing any good.  The Holy Spirit is the sweet power and presence of God that transforms our nothingness into something. I cannot imagine what we would do without the power of the Holy Spirit.  

**3. You must pray for the Holy Spirit so that you have Godly convictions. **

**But I tell you the truth, it is to your advantage that I go away; for if I do not go away, the Helper will not come to you; but if I go, I will send Him to you. AND HE, WHEN HE COMES, WILL CONVICT THE WORLD CONCERNING SIN AND RIGHTEOUSNESS AND JUDGMENT; concerning sin, because they do not believe in Me; and concerning righteousness, because I go to the Father and you no longer see Me; and concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world has been judged.**

**John 16:7-11 (NASB)**

Praying for the Holy Spirit is very important because we are convicted by His presence.  One of the works of the Holy Spirit is to give you strong convictions. Convictions are beliefs and strong persuasions. Most of your Christian life is governed by the strong convictions you have. Without strong convictions you cannot do much for God.  \

Weak Christians are people who do not have strong convictions about God. They do not have strong convictions about serving God. They do not have strong beliefs about the importance of fellowship. Their lack of conviction is what makes them weak. One of the things the Holy Spirit does is to give you convictions. \

I have strong convictions about full-time ministry. I have strong convictions about giving myself all out to Jesus. I have strong convictions about working in the ministry. I have strong convictions about debt. These strong convictions have guided me in the ministry.  

Today, lots of Christians walk about claiming to know Jesus Christ. If they really had strong convictions about the things they claim to believe, this world would be different.  The lack of the Holy Spirit is what leads to a lack of conviction. More prayer for the Holy Spirit will lead to more conviction.  

**4. You must pray for the Holy Spirit so that you bear the fruits of the Spirit.  **

**But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.**

**Galatians 5:22-23**

As you receive the Holy Spirit through your prayers, love, joy, peace, gentleness will become your second nature.  

The Holy Spirit comes along with fruit. Everyone will enjoy you more. Your weaknesses and deficiencies will be covered by the Spirit of God and His fruit. \

The fruit of the Spirit is the result of the long-term presence of the Holy Spirit.  Think about the weakness, the dishonour and the corruption that is in human beings.  Through the power of the Holy Spirit, you will begin to exhibit beautiful characteristics that do not come from your earthly family, but from God Himself.  \

**5. You must pray for the Holy Spirit so that you have power to be a witness of Jesus Christ. **

**But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth.**

**Acts 1:8**

You can be a witness of Jesus Christ. A witness of Jesus Christ is someone who stands up to give a testimony that Jesus is real. Your personal testimony of how Jesus has changed you and how the power of God has transformed you is the best witness for Jesus Christ.

Many people cannot stand up and declare that they are Christians. To be a witness, you must stand up in public to declare what you have seen and what you know. You have to tell people, “Look at me. See how I have changed! See what has happened to me!  Look at the difference He has made in my life! Everyone will look at you and believe in Jesus Christ. That is what happens when you are a witness. The power for radical change comes because the Holy Spirit works in your life.  

**6. You must pray for the Holy Spirit so that you manifest the gifts of the Spirit.**

**But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal.  For to one is given by the Spirit the word of wisdom; to another the word of knowledge by the same Spirit; To another faith by the same Spirit; to another the gifts of healing by the same Spirit; To another the working of miracles; to another prophecy; to another discerning of spirits; to another divers kinds of tongues; to another the interpretation of tongues:**

**1 Corinthians 12:7-10**

Pray for the Holy Spirit and you will become a powerhouse of miracles, signs and wonders.  Praying for the Holy Spirit is also important because gifts start to flow in your life. The gifts of supernatural knowledge and supernatural wisdom are given through the power of the Holy Spirit. 

Gifts of healing and miracles become common in your life when you have the Holy Spirit. Speaking in tongues and prophecy will flow effortlessly from your lips.  Pray for the Holy Ghost and you will become a vessel for miracles, signs and wonders.  It is no wonder that Jesus asked us to pray for the Holy Spirit.  

**7. You must pray for the Holy Spirit so that you have increasing wisdom, understanding, knowledge and counsel.  **

**And the spirit of the Lord shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of the Lord;**\

**Isaiah 11:2**

You must pray for all the seven spirits of God.  When you pray for all the seven spirits of God, you will become like Daniel who had an excellent spirit.  

Most of the wrong moves of your life have come because you lacked the wisdom to take the right decision.  Most governments fail because they have become blinded and ignorant to the realities on the ground. We need knowledge, we need truth and we need reality. We need the Holy Spirit to give us counsel, understanding and truth.  The Holy Spirit is also called the Spirit of truth.  You will know the truth as you pray for the Holy Spirit.
