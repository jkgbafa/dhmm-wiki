### CHAPTER 17\

## How to Pray Short Prayers 

**And I say unto you, ASK, and it shall be given you; SEEK, and ye shall find; KNOCK, and it shall be opened unto you. For every one that asketh receiveth; and he that seeketh findeth; and to him that knocketh it shall be opened.**

**Luke 11:9-10**

Jesus taught us to pray prayers of different lengths. He prayed short prayers and He also prayed long prayers. When you love someone, you do not only have long discussions with the person. There are times you have brief interactions and there are times you have long drawn-out interactions with the person.  \

Unfortunately, many people think that short prayers are ineffective. That cannot be true. Some of Jesus’ most powerful prayers were short prayers.  Let us learn from Jesus and pray short prayers when we have to. Believe in the short sentence you have spoken.  Believe that it has been heard by God!  

**The Short Prayers of Jesus Christ**

1. **Pray short prayers when you are in an awkward situation.** Jesus was on a cross and He was in a very awkward situation but He still prayed a short prayer.  

And when Jesus had cried with a loud voice, he said, Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit: and having said thus, he gave up the ghost.\

Luke 23:46

2. **Pray short prayers to perform financial miracles.**  

Jesus’ greatest financial miracle was the feeding of five thousand people. He prayed a very brief prayer of thanksgiving and the finances started flowing!   \

And Jesus took the loaves; and when he had given thanks, he distributed to the disciples, and the disciples to them that were set down; and likewise of the fishes as much as they would.\

John 6:11

3. **Pray short prayers for the forgiveness of the sins of your enemies.**

Then said Jesus, Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do. And they parted his raiment, and cast lots.

Luke 23:34

4. **Pray short prayers for the healing of the sick.**  

Jesus prayed very short prayers for the sick.  Short prayers are very powerful prayers. Do not be deceived into thinking that you always need to pray long and elaborate prayers.   

And looking up to heaven, he sighed, and saith unto him, Ephphatha, that is, be opened. And straightway his ears were opened, and the string of his tongue was loosed, and he spake plain.

Mark 7:34-35

5. **Pray short prayers for the raising of the dead.**  

Jesus did not spend a long time in the prayer for the raising of the dead. Indeed, brief prayers were the hallmark of His public ministry.  

Then they took away the stone from the place where the dead was laid. And Jesus lifted up his eyes, and said, Father, I thank thee that thou hast heard me. And I knew that thou hearest me always: but because of the people which stand by I said it, that they may believe that thou hast sent me.

John 11:41-42
