### CHAPTER 2\

## How to Pray for the Will of God

**And he took with him Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, and began to be sorrowful and very heavy. Then saith he unto them, My soul is exceeding sorrowful, even unto death: tarry ye here, and watch with me.  And he went a little further, and fell on his face, and prayed, saying, O my Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass from me: nevertheless not as I will, BUT AS THOU WILT.  And he cometh unto the disciples, and findeth them asleep, and saith unto Peter, What, could ye not watch with me one hour?  Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak. He went away again THE SECOND TIME, and prayed, saying, O my Father, if this cup may not pass away from me, except I drink it, THY WILL BE DONE.  And he came and found them asleep again: for their eyes were heavy.  And he left them, and went away again, and PRAYED THE THIRD TIME, saying THE SAME WORDS.  **

**Matthew 26:37-44**

Jesus prayed for the will of God. Apart from His all night prayers, these are the longest prayers of Jesus we know of.  Why did Jesus pray for such a long time for the will of God to happen?  

**Things You Should Know about Praying for the Will of God**

**1. You must pray for the will of God otherwise the will of God will not happen!  **

Where there are human beings, you must always pray for the will of God. I believe in terms of evil, human beings are next only to devils. Much evil is done by human beings and many evil decisions are taken by human beings! All through the trial of Jesus, it was evident that things could have gone either way. Pilate could have decided to release Jesus. Pilate was a wicked man who did not care about true justice. 

Like many politicians, Pontius Pilate simply wanted to maintain his political power and position. It was the mention of Caesar’s name and the threat to Pontius Pilate’s political future that made Pontius Pilate hand over an innocent man to the executioner.    

All through your life, human beings will be taking decisions that will affect you. If you do not pray about it, you will not see the will of God happening in your life.  The will of capricious and unreasonable men will be implemented in your life when you do not pray. Think about it! Your boss may be taking a decision about you right now!   Your teacher may be taking a decision about you right now! Your husband may be taking a decision to leave you!  Your wife may be taking a decision treat you in a particular way! A young man may be taking a decision to become your beloved. A young lady may be taking a decision to hunt for your life. All these decisions by these people around you can affect your life. This is why you must rise up in prayer like Jesus did. 

If Jesus had not prayed the way He did, the will of God would not have happened.  Joseph of Arimathea may have risen up earlier to fight for Jesus, thereby blocking the crucifixion. Crowds of people who were healed by Jesus could have been informed to come to the rescue of Jesus. The common people who were ardent followers of Jesus Christ may have risen up to fight for Jesus, just as Peter had tried to do earlier. All these things did not happen because the people simply did not take the decision to do something. People’s decisions are always affecting you one way or the other. You must rise up and pray and not leave things to chance.  

It is the will of God for you to have a big church.  It is the will of God for people to support you in the ministry. However, if you do not pray for the will of God to be done, people will not take decisions to join your church.  People will not take decisions to support you and help you financially. Praying for the will of God is one of the most critical prayers you must pray.  

**2. You must pray for the purposes of God to be done even though it is God’s purpose.  **

Even though you know the will of God and even though God has spoken to you, it is still important to pray about it so that it comes to pass. This is indeed a great mystery.  

Jesus knew the will of God. That is why He came to Jerusalem.  He said, no one takes my life from me but I lay down my life. He voluntarily and intentionally came to Jerusalem to die on a cross for the sins of the world. 

He had known the will of God for a long time. At one point He said, “The hour is come for the seed to die.” Jesus knew the will of God and knew that the hour had come for Him to do it.  Yet, He still had to pray for the purpose of God to be done. This makes praying for the will of God one of the most mysterious prayers ever. It seems as though the will of God will not happen unless you pray for it.

**3. You must pray repeatedly about the will of God. **

Jesus prayed three times. Each time He prayed, He prayed about the same thing and used the same words. Praying for the will of God is not something that you do just once.

Jesus is the one who taught us not to pray with useless repetition.  

But when ye pray, USE NOT VAIN REPETITIONS, as the heathen do: for they think that they shall be heard for their much speaking.

Matthew 6:7

However, in this prayer, we see Jesus repeating the topic, “Thy will be done, Thy will be done, Thy will be done”.  Why did He repeat these prayers? Jesus repeated these prayers because they were not useless repetitions but valuable, effective and meaningful repetition of prayers. These were repetitions that had an effect on God. Jesus was teaching us that repeating prayers about the will of God being done are not useless repetitions.   

**4. You must pray for hours about the will of God.  **

Jesus prayed for several hours for the will of God to be done. He did not pray a five-minute prayer.  There are five-minute prayers! There are one-minute prayers!  There are prayers we must pray for hours! Praying for the will of God is one of the longest prayers I have ever prayed.  I do not think the will of God will be done in your life until you spend hours praying for it. Perhaps your life will improve greatly and change its very course when you start praying for the will of God.
