### CHAPTER 20\

How to Pray Prayers of Intercession\
---------------------------------------------------------

\

**And he saw that there was no man, AND WONDERED THAT THERE WAS NO INTERCESSOR: therefore his arm brought salvation unto him; and his righteousness, it sustained him.**

**Isaiah 59:16 **

\

**1. Intercession is a priority ministry of the church.  **

\

**I exhort therefore, that, FIRST OF ALL, supplications, prayers, INTERCESSIONS, and giving of thanks, be made for all men; For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.**

**1 Timothy 2:1-2**

Intercession is a priority!  There must be intercession in the church for the souls of men. First of all, there must be supplications, prayers and intercessions in every church. Intercession must go on all the time. Intercession is a spiritual intervention that blocks the work of the devil. 

\

**2. Intercession saves lives.  **

\

**And he said, Oh let not the Lord be angry, and I will speak yet but this once: Peradventure ten shall be found there. And he said, I will not destroy it for ten’s sake.**

**Genesis 18: 32**

\

Abraham negotiated for the lives of the Sodomites.  He spoke the language God could understand.  He persuaded God. Can you speak God’s language?  Can you quote scriptures that God will listen to?  Can you use the laws of prayer that Jesus taught in order to move the hand of God?  

\

Can you save lives through your prayer?  Your life may be spared because someone is praying for you. You must have personal intercessors. You must have intercessory groups.  People must be praying for you all the time.  Their prayers may be saving you from fire and from judgment.  

\

**4. Intercession gives you victory without casualties. **

\

**So Joshua did as Moses had said to him, and fought with Amalek: and Moses, Aaron, and Hur went up to the top of the hill. And it came to pass, WHEN MOSES HELD UP HIS HAND, THAT ISRAEL PREVAILED: and when he let down his hand, Amalek prevailed. But Moses’ hands were heavy; and they took a stone, and put it under him, and he sat thereon; and Aaron and Hur stayed up his hands, the one on the one side, and the other on the other side; and his hands were steady until the going down of the sun.**

**Exodus 17:10-12**

\

Do you want to have victory without casualties and setbacks?  Sometimes, you have victory but the losses that you encounter are too costly.  Through intercession, you can have victory without casualties.  Through intercession you will become like Moses and have victory over the Amalekites.  

\

**5. Intercessors are great people. **

\

**Therefore will I DIVIDE HIM A PORTION WITH THE GREAT, and he shall divide the spoil with the strong; because he hath poured out his soul unto death: and he was numbered with the transgressors; and he bare the sin of many, and MADE INTERCESSION FOR THE TRANSGRESSORS. **

**Isaiah 53:12 **

\

Making intercession is what qualified Jesus to have a portion with the great!  If you become an intercessor, you will be a very great person.  When you are an intercessor, you pour out your soul for people who are in sin.  Through your intercessions many are saved.  Where few people are saved, it is because there is little intercession. 

\

God sees intercession as a very great thing.  “I will divide him a portion with the great because he poured out his soul and made intercession for the transgressors.”  Because you pour out your soul in prayers for people you do not even know, God will make you great.
