### CHAPTER 10\

## How to Pray in the Closet

**And when thou prayest, thou shalt not be as the hypocrites are: for they love to pray standing in the synagogues and in the corners of the streets, that they may be seen of men. Verily I say unto you, they have their reward. But thou, WHEN THOU PRAYEST, ENTER INTO THY CLOSET, and when thou hast shut thy door, pray to thy Father which is in secret; and thy Father which seeth in secret SHALL REWARD THEE OPENLY.**

**Matthew 6:5-6**

It is important to learn how to pray privately. There are people who only know how to pray in a group.  They can only attend a prayer meeting where a lot of people are praying and shouting together. Jesus taught us to pray by going into the closet.  What is a closet? The closet represents your private room and your private place. God wants to meet you all alone.  \

Why must you learn how to pray in private? The private prayer is the prayer that results in open rewards.  \

Have you ever wondered why you do not have certain public demonstrations of God’s love on your life? Public acknowledgement by God will only come out of a private and secret meeting you have had with Him. \

We watched the royal wedding on television. There was great joy, great happiness and much celebration all over the world to see a prince getting married in style. However, the public celebration we all witnessed was the product of secret times and private moments that the couple had together.   Without those secret times and private moments there would be no public celebration.  \

Do you want God to celebrate you?  Do you want Him to have a big splash on you and show everyone that He loves you? Then you will need to have private moments and secret times when you speak to Him all alone. \

Get into the closet and spend hours with Him alone. It is those hours that will lead to Him acknowledging you openly.  Learn how to wait on God when there is no one with you.  Learn how to stay in your room and pray alone for hours.  Learn how to pray alone without going on your phone to talk to anyone.  Learn how to pray without announcing to the world that you are doing twenty-one days of fasting and prayer. Learn how to pray quietly where no one can hear you praying.  Learn how to pray when God alone sees you.  \

Do not be deceived when you see public announcements of people who claim to have forty days of prayer meetings.  Do not be deceived when you see people who claim they are doing thirty days of all-night prayers.  Public announcements of prayers are different from prayers in the closet. Prayers in the closet have no such announcements. No one sees you in the closet.  Only God sees what you are doing in the closet. Only God hears you in the closet.  \

Prayers in the closet are the prayers that have open and public rewards. Let others do public announcements of prayer, if they want. Decide to discover for yourself the superior blessing of secret and private prayers.  Do you want a public announcement of God’s approval of you or do you want a public announcement of your prayer meetings?  Which one do you want?  \

Get into the closet!  Spend time alone with God!  Do not tell anyone about it!  God will announce His public approval of you!\
