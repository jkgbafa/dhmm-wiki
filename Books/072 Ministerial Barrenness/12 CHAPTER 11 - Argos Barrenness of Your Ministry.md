### CHAPTER 11

Argos Barrenness of Your Ministry\
=======================================================

For if these things be in you, and abound, they make you that ye shall neither be barren nor unfruitful (ARGOS) in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.

2 Peter 1:8

Greek words are important in understanding the revelation God is trying to bring to us. One of these words is the word “Argos.”

ARGOS

This word means, “Inactive, unemployed, lazy and useless”. It also speaks of “Being idle, slow and barren”. Laziness is one of the principal causes of unfruitfulness in ministry. Lazy people are inactive in church. Their laziness makes them useless to God.

For if these things be in you, and abound, they make you that ye shall neither be barren nor unfruitful (ARGOS) in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.

2 Peter 1:8

Unfortunately, the church is made up of masses of spiritually inactive people. To break this type of barrenness, it is important to preach about diligence. It is important to teach the congregation that they must win souls for the Lord.
