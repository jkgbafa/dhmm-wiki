## Each Age Has Its Beauty

There is the beauty of a sixteen-year-old girl that a thirty-year-old woman does not have. There is the beauty of a thirty-year-old woman which is different from that of a sixteen-year-old. There is a beauty of the forty-two-year-old lady, and another beauty of a fifty-six-year-old.

At each age, a person possesses a particular beauty. Therefore, there is no need to wish that your wife or husband would be sixteen years old again.There are men who are married to beautiful thirty-year-old women, and yet when left alone their eyes hover all over for teenagers. At a point, they begin to think that their wives are not beautiful. But they do not realize that each age has its own type of beauty, which cannot be repeated. You cannot become young again!The beauty of each age was created by God. You can never rewind to the beauty of a past age. You cannot go backward and you cannot go forward. You are what you are.Some people think that you can only be beautiful when you are very young. But that is not true. I know some beautiful fifty-year-old ladies. And I know some beautiful sixty-year-old ladies. I have even seen beautiful seventy-year- old women.Exploit that beauty, corresponding to whatever age you are.
