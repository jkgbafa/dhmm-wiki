# Preface

“I want to marry a beautiful girl.” This is the unspoken cry of many young men.However, many of these aspiring husbands do not know the ABC’s of beauty.It is my intention that this short but instructive lesson will show much of what there is to know about this subject.Husbands and wives will also benefit from this lesson by learning what they can do to enhance beauty, maintain beauty, and sustain attractiveness through all the changing scenes of life.
