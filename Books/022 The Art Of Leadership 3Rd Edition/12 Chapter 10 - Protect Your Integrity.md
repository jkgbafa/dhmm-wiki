### Chapter 10\

Protect Your Integrity\
--------------------------------------------

Integrity is wholeness. Having integrity means being totally or completely sound. To have personal integrity means you are upright, sincere, loyal and pure. You need personal integrity to be a good leader. Are you sound? Are you whole?

Your wholeness is shattered by lies and deception, treachery, disloyalty and immorality. The reason why people do not really believe in the words of politicians is because their integrity is greatly compromised by their frequent deceptions. The reason why pastors struggle to make people believe in us is because we often lack integrity. You can protect your integrity by protecting yourself from lies, disloyalty and immorality.

Three Things That Destroy Your Integrity

1\. Lies and deception. After somebody has lied to you, it is difficult to trust him any more. Everyone struggles to believe someone who has consistently lied to them in the past.

2\. Disloyalty and treachery. After someone has betrayed you, it is difficult to trust him any more. Everyone struggles to trust people who have been disloyal to them.

3\. Immorality. After someone has had an immoral affair, it is difficult to trust him. When a person has been immoral, a clear gap has been broken in the hedge.
