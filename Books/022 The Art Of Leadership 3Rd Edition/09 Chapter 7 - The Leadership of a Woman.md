### Chapter 7\

The Leadership of a Woman\
-----------------------------------------------

O My people! Their oppressors are children, and women rule over them. O My people! Those who guide you lead you astray, and confuse the direction of your paths.

Isaiah 3:12 (NASB)

You must study leadership, understand leadership, and practise appropriate principles of leadership otherwise you may lead people with the mind of a woman. Of course, a woman who is tempered by the Holy Spirit will exhibit some good characteristics. However, there are differences between women, men and children. Both men and women suffer from the same evils. But women are more prone to certain things just as men are more prone to others. Women tend to have certain characteristics that are contrary to leadership.

Both Men and Women Are Beset with Evil

In its rawest form, the leadership of a woman is a dangerous thing. The unrefined, crudest women are beset with numerous characteristics, which are injurious, damaging and harmful to leadership. God made women differently and because of the way they are made, they are more prone to certain evils than men. Men are also beset with evils but the types of evils they struggle with do not affect leadership in the same negative way.

Men may be beset by even more serious spiritual and character problems than women but the kinds of problems that women have affect leadership more than the “evils” of the men. For instance, a person may have a very serious illness such as cancer but it may not affect his ability to drive. But a boil under sole of the foot (which is not a serious condition) will affect your ability to press the brakes or the accelerator. It is therefore difficult and dangerous to drive when you have a boil under your foot.

What I am trying to say is that there are some defects, illnesses or problems that tend to affect driving more than others. The evils that women have may be less serious than the evils that men are plagued with, but the fact is that the problems of women greatly affect their ability to lead.

For example, a man naturally suffers from the evil of lust. This problem makes him draw people to himself, which is good for leadership and church growth. A woman suffers from insecurity, which makes her eliminate people she perceives as threats. Obviously, such a problem is contrary to leadership which involves gathering people around you and not eliminating them.

Do not be angry with me for what I am saying. I did not write the bible. Be angry with God if you want to be angry with someone.
