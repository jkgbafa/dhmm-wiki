### Chapter 90\

Translate Your Visions into Reality\
---------------------------------------------------------

Where there is no vision, the people perish: …

Proverbs 29:18

Visions give life to a leader. I always pray for visions and dreams so that I can know what to do. What dreams do you have? If you are a leader, you will be able to make those dreams a reality. Everyone has dreams. But when a leader dreams it must become a reality. I have a dream of many souls being saved. I have a dream of many churches being established. I have a dream of fulfilling my ministry. I believe I can see these dreams come to pass.

What are you doing about your dreams? If there is a leader in you, rise up and pay the price to have your dreams come true.

Why Your Visions And Dreams Are Important

1. Your visions and dreams are from the Holy Spirit. You cannot ignore something that is from God.

But this is that which was spoken by the prophet Joel;

And it shall come to pass in the last days, saith God, I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh: and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, and your young men shall see visions, and your old men shall dream dreams:

Acts 2:16-17

2. Your visions and dreams are a picture of God’s destiny for you. They are given by God to lead you to His will.

And Joseph dreamed a dream, and he told it his brethren: and they hated him yet the more.

Genesis 37:5

3. Your visions and dreams will make you do the right things and cause you to take the right decisions for your life. If you follow these visions and dreams, you will be forced to relate to certain people and accept certain things in your life.

And he dreamed yet another dream, and told it his brethren, and said, Behold, I have dreamed a dream more; and, behold, the sun and the moon and the eleven stars made obeisance to me.

Genesis 37:9
