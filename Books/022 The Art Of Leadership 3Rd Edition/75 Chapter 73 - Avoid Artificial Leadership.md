### Chapter 73\

Avoid Artificial Leadership\
-------------------------------------------------

What Jesus Said About Artificial Leadership

1\. Artificial leaders do not go to the right place and also prevent people from going to the right place.

But woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men: for ye neither go in yourselves, neither suffer ye them that are entering to go in.

Matthew 23:13

2\. Artificial leaders are like vampires who prey on the weak and vulnerable.

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye devour widows’ houses, and for a pretence make long prayer: therefore ye shall receive the greater damnation.

Matthew 23:14

3\. Artificial leaders give rise to even more artificial leaders.

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte, and when he is made, ye make him twofold more the child of hell than yourselves.

Matthew 23:15

4\. Artificial leaders are hypocrites.

Woe unto you, ye blind guides, which say, whosoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gold of the temple, he is a debtor!”

Matthew 23:16

5\. Artificial leaders are a generation of serpentine, sly and evil men.

Ye serpents, ye generation of vipers, how can ye escape the damnation of hell?

Matthew 23:33

6\. Artificial leaders emphasize minor details but forget their real purpose and vision.

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For ye pay tithe of mint and anise and cummin, and have omitted the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith: these ought ye to have done, and not to leave the other undone.

Matthew 23:23

7\. Artificial leaders are concerned about making a good impression on the outside, but are full of the opposite on the inside.

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye make clean the outside of the cup and of the platter, but within they are full of extortion and excess.

Matthew 23:25

8\. Artificial leaders are blind to the real problems.

Thou blind Pharisee, cleanse first that which is within the cup and platter, that the outside of them may be clean also.

Matthew 23:26

9\. Artificial leaders are full of hidden evil and uncleanness.

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness.

Matthew 23:27

10\. Artificial leaders honour people of the past but are unable to recognize and do the right thing today.

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! because ye build the tombs of the prophets, and garnish the sepulchres of the righteous, “And say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.

Matthew 23:29-30

Artificial Leadership and Real Leadership

1\. A real leader inspires and speaks off the cuff. He acts out his belief, is practical and leads by example. He makes great sacrifices for his beliefs and uses himself as an example.

2\. A real leader is God-made and God-appointed and says what he believes. He loves people and is not motivated by money.

3\. A real leader has a genuine concern for people and is concerned about the real things. He is keen on analysis. He wants to get to the job.

4\. A real leader is not interested in what title he is given but is more interested in the job.

5\. An artificial leader is boring and often reads his speeches. He usually shows no emotion, prepares ideal speeches, says the right things but does nothing. He often says what people want him to say and fits the accepted mode.

6\. An artificial leader is theoretical, holds seminars and conferences but takes no action. He delights in titles, is concerned in an official capacity but not in a real way, and often he does not love the people.

7\. An artificial leader never uses himself as an example (as he is not a good example). He is man-made and man-appointed and avoids criticism. He is concerned about ceremonies but not the job. An artificial leader is most concerned about salaries and benefits.

How to Become a Real Leader

1\. Form an opinion and develop a conviction about something.

It is your conviction that makes you stand out.

2\. Observe natural leaders. Admire natural leaders. Notice what they do and do not do. Watch artificial appointed leaders and don’t do what they do.

3\. Fellowship with natural leaders. Listen to great natural leaders.

Do not miss an opportunity to spend a few moments with great leaders.

4\. Study the lives of real leaders.

5\. Fellowship with great leaders through their books and tapes.
