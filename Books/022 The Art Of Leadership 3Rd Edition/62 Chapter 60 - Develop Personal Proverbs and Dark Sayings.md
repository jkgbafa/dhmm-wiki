### Chapter 60\

Develop Personal Proverbs and Dark Sayings\
----------------------------------------------------------------

And moreover, because the preacher was wise, he still taught the people knowledge; yea, he gave good heed, and sought out, and set in order many proverbs.

Ecclesiastes 12:9

This preacher was someone who developed many proverbs. What is a proverb? It is a wise saying with a deep meaning. I once heard an old man say, “It is not money you need to build a house; it is wisdom.” This was a wise saying from a man who had led the way to build many houses.

As you become a successful leader, you will develop wisdom through experience. This wisdom will be transmitted through your proverbs.

A wise man will hear, and will increase learning; and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels: To understand a proverb, and the interpretation; the words of the wise, and their dark sayings.

Proverbs 1:5, 6

Ten Dark Sayings

Every dark saying takes time to understand. Dark sayings are given by fathers, experienced people and wise men. Hidden within them are depths of wisdom and rivers of counsel. A dark saying is a saying that is not immediately understood. Some of the statements are even shocking. That is why they are called dark sayings. Perhaps you will meditate on some of them yourself and discover what they mean. Here are some dark sayings I have once heard.

1\. The old man at the bottom of the irokko tree can see further than the young man who has climbed to the top of the irokko tree!

2\. A visitor has eyes but cannot see!

3\. When you see a goat in the sun, it is sweating but you cannot see that it is sweating.

4\. When an experienced person is speaking, he often sounds like a mad man!

5\. You do not need money to build a house. You need wisdom to build a house!

6\. A frog does not jump around on the lawn in the afternoon without a good reason!

7\. It is only when you are by the riverside that you can hear the crabs coughing!

8\. Any business that a Lebanese man does is a good business!

9\. When a crocodile comes from under the water and tells you that there is a snake below, you must believe him.

10\. An old man advised his son as he planned for marriage and said, “The only advice I can give you is: do not marry a virgin!” Ooh!
