### Chapter 64\

Build Something if You Are a Leader!\
----------------------------------------------------------

Many people think that it takes wealth and riches to build a house. From experience, I know that it does not require money per se, but wisdom. People who are able to rally their resources together and use them wisely often become builders. Often such a person is a leader. Have you built a house? Have you built a church? What you build will testify of your leadership. Decide to build something for God in your lifetime.

What Building Reveals About You

1\. Building reveals that you are a man of frugality.

2\. Building reveals that you are a man of great wisdom.

This is what the Bible says. It takes all the wisdom a person can muster to build something. Building is always an indication of a wise person somewhere. Developed countries that have built up their cities and their nations demonstrate their wisdom by what they have built. Nations which cannot pull themselves together to build their roads, their hospitals and their nations demonstrate a gross lack of wisdom. A nation which needs others to step in to build its toilets reveal an unfortunate state of affairs. Nations which need others to come in to build their roads, bridges and dams and hand them over as gifts reveal a lack of wisdom.

THROUGH WISDOM IS AN HOUSE BUILDED; and by understanding it is established: And by knowledge shall the chambers be filled with all precious and pleasant riches.

Proverbs 24:3-4

This verse demonstrates that when you do not build you do not have wisdom.

I made me great works; I builded me houses; I planted me vineyards: I made me gardens and orchards, and I planted trees in them of all kind of fruits: I made me pools of water, to water therewith the wood that bringeth forth trees:

Ecclesiastes 2:4-6

3\. Building reveals you have overcome the curse of Ham.

And he said, Cursed be Canaan; A SERVANT OF SERVANTS SHALL HE BE unto his brethren.

And he said, Blessed be the LORD God of Shem; and Canaan shall be his servant.

Genesis 9:25-26

The curse of Ham was to make Ham and Canaan into a servant of servants. How many servants build houses? A servant is usually unable to build a house. Because the curse of Ham is operative on many people they are unable to build their schools and they are unable to build their houses. Rise up and build something great for the people who are looking up to you. Demonstrate to everyone that you have overcome this curse of being unable to build anything substantial in your lifetime.

4\. Building reveals that you are prudent and pragmatic. It shows you are practical.

Then shall the kingdom of heaven be likened unto ten virgins, which took their lamps, and went forth to meet the bridegroom.

And FIVE OF THEM WERE WISE, and five were foolish.

Matthew 25:1-2

The ten virgins had been called for a wedding but five of then had what the Bible calls phronimos. Phronimos is the practical application of wisdom. Five of the virgins displayed practical wisdom by making arrangements for extra oil. They were planning for the future and taking practical steps even though they were virgins specially invited for this dinner. If you fail to take practical steps, your calling and election will be cut short. The call of God can be aborted because you failed to be practical!

Unrealistic theoreticians only propound theories about what must be done. But it takes a practical person who is wise and judicious in practical affairs to lead the way in building and developing. This is why people from the highest and best universities of the world are not necessarily great builders. It takes a person who understands practical realities to actually lay a foundation and build something useful.

5\. Building reveals that you have an eye for the future.

A PRUDENT MAN FORESEETH THE EVIL, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.

Proverbs 22:3

Prudence speaks of a person who is careful in providing for the future. If you are led by someone who is not careful in providing for the future, you will be without a roof over your head.

The wife who depends on such a bad leader will be left homeless and at the mercy of charity. The church which depends on such a bad leader will be left without a church building and will be at the mercy of landlords. A nation which is led by men who have no eye for the future will be left without roads, hospitals and electricity. The lights will be turned on and off at random because the nation is led by people without eyes for the future.

Leadership qualities make a pastor acquire a church building for his church. Buying buses or instruments for your church is different from building a church facility. A wise manager would acquire properties for his company. I can assure you that it takes the strength of leadership to build a nation. Weak leaders cannot build anything! Decide today to be a builder. A husband who builds a house for his wife and children is a true leader.

The first president of Ghana, Kwame Nkrumah, is often hailed as a great leader. One of the things he did was to build many things for the nation. For instance, he built the Akosombo Dam, which has the largest man-made lake in the world. The things he built testify to the fact that Ghana once had a great leader. Leadership is manifested by the presence of buildings. A good leader leaves something behind.

Western countries have well-developed infrastructure that testify of the kind of leadership they have enjoyed. Underdeveloped countries that have been ruled by illiterate dictators and vampire-style despots, have very little infrastructure. Truly, buildings and development are a sign of good leadership. Solomon was a wise leader.

I made me great works; I builded me houses; I planted me vineyards: I made me gardens and orchards, and I planted trees in them of all kind of fruits: I made me pools of water, to water therewith the wood that bringeth forth trees:

Ecclesiastes 2:4-6

Because I have been involved in building, I can understand why most people shy away from it. But I believe that God has called leaders to build. Nehemiah, one of the great leaders of the Bible, motivated the people of God to rise up and build. His famous sermon was simple – arise and build!

…The God of heaven, he will prosper us; therefore we his servants will arise and build…

Nehemiah 2:20

6\. A man who builds a church is a good person, a good leader and a good pastor.

A bad leader will never build anything for you. The Jews said that the centurion was worthy. They said he was a good man. He deserved to have a miracle. Why? Because he had built a synagogue for the people. When you build a church you are a good person. A good leader builds churches, houses, schools, hospitals, roads and everything else that is needed for his followers. Read the bible for yourself!

And when they came to Jesus, they besought him instantly, saying, that HE WAS WORTHY for whom he should do this: For he loveth our nation, and he hath built us a synagogue.

Luke 7:4-5

7\. Building reveals that you are humble.

A man who builds a church has the grace of God on his life.

EXCEPT THE LORD BUILD the house, they labour in vain that build it: except the Lord keep the city, the watchman waketh but in vain.

Psalm 127:1

The Scripture tells us that it is God’s power that helps you to labour and build. Through the power of God, you will be able to build the things He has called you to build. Trust in God and walk by the Spirit. if you are led by the Spirit you will build many things for the Lord.

8\. Building reveals that you are courageous and bold.

Consider now, for the Lord has chosen you to build a house for the sanctuary; be courageous and act.”

1 Chronicles 28:10

Then David said to his son Solomon, “Be strong and courageous, and act; do not fear nor be dismayed, for the Lord God, my God, is with you. He will not fail you nor forsake you until all the work for the service of the house of the Lord is finished.

1 Chronicles 28:20, NASB

9\. Building reveals strength.

And David said to Solomon his son, Be strong and of good courage, and do it: fear not, nor be dismayed:for the Lord God, even my God, will be with thee; he will not fail thee, nor forsake thee, until thou hast finished all the work for the service of the house of the Lord.

1 Chronicles 28:20

10\. Building reveals youthfulness.

It means you are young enough and that you are going to live long enough. If you were old, God would not say:

Furthermore David the king said unto all the congregation, Solomon my son, whom alone God hath chosen, is yet young and tender, and the work is great: for the palace is not for man, but for the Lord God.

1 Chronicles 29:1
