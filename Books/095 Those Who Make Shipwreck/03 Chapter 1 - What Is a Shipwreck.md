### Chapter 1\

What Is a Shipwreck?\
------------------------------------------

Keeping faith and a good conscience, which some have rejected and suffered shipwreck in regard to their faith.:

1 Timothy 1:19 (NASB)

A shipwreck is a destroyed and sunken ship. A shipwreck is therefore symbolic of ruin, downfall, destruction and decay.

If you make a shipwreck of your ministry, it means your ministry has come to a calamitous and catastrophic end.

If you make a shipwreck of your ministry, it means your ministry is tragically destroyed, ruined and overthrown.

There are clear examples of shipwrecks of ministry in the Bible. Each one of these examples reveals why and how a shipwreck occurs. I have been a Christian for so many years, serving the Lord and working in the church. I have seen several people making a shipwreck of their ministry.

To make a shipwreck of your ministry is to self-destruct and initiate your own downfall and destruction.

To make a shipwreck of your ministry is to make a false step that leads to devastation and desolation in ministry.

To make a shipwreck of your ministry is to be misled by your flesh to step into ruin.

To make a shipwreck of your ministry is to be led by the devil into temptation and downfall.

In this book, you will have clear patterns of shipwrecks in ministry that you will recognise plainly from now on.

When Peter declared that he would stand by Jesus even to death, Jesus noticed some emptiness in his words and declarations. Jesus knew that Peter was about to make a shipwreck of his ministry. Because of this, Jesus did not pray for Peter that he would not be tempted! Jesus did not pray for Peter that he would not fall! I believe that Jesus wanted Peter to be tempted and also to fall so that he would learn an invaluable lesson of humility and carefulness.

It is not only important to make stout declarations of your loyalty and commitment, but it is also important to know how you might, just perhaps, break your promises and fall into what you hate. Many people have made declarations that they violated. Many people have made promises that they have broken easily. A faithful person, who swears to his own hurt and does not change, is not so easy to find. This is why it is important to learn about shipwreck disasters in ministry so you can prevent them.

… but he honoureth them that fear the LORD. HE THAT SWEARETH TO HIS OWN HURT, AND CHANGETH NOT.

Psalm 15:4

Through the teaching in this book, you can see how a real shipwreck occurs and what happens in a real-life shipwreck of ministry.
