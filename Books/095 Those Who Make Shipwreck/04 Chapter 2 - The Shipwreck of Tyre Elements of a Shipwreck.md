### Chapter 2\

The Shipwreck of Tyre: Elements of a Shipwreck\
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The ships of Tarshish serve as carriers for your wares. You are filled with heavy cargo as you sail the sea. Your oarsmen take you out to the high seas. But the east wind will break you to pieces far out at sea. Your wealth, merchandise and wares, your mariners, sailors and shipwrights, your merchants and all your soldiers, and everyone else on board will sink into the heart of the sea on THE DAY OF YOUR SHIPWRECK.

The shorelands will quake when your sailors cry out. All who handle the oars will abandon their ships; the mariners and all the sailors will stand on the shore. They will raise their voice and cry bitterly over you; they will sprinkle dust on their heads and roll in ashes. They will shave their heads because of you and will put on sackcloth. They will weep over you with anguish of soul and with bitter mourning. As they wail and mourn over you, they will take up a lament concerning you: “Who was ever silenced like Tyre, surrounded by the sea?” When your merchandise went out on the seas, you satisfied many nations; with your great wealth and your wares, you enriched the kings of the earth. Now you are shattered by the sea in the depths of the waters; your wares and all your company have gone down with you. All who live in the coastlands are appalled at you; their kings shudder with horror and their faces are distorted with fear. The merchants among the nations scoff at you; you have come to a horrible end and will be no more.

Ezekiel 27:25-36 (NIV)

The scripture above describes the shipwreck of Tyre. I have decided to list the elements of a shipwreck:

The Elements of a Shipwreck

1\. A shipwreck occurs when a person and his ministry are irreparably broken into pieces just like Humpty Dumpty.

Thy rowers have brought thee into great waters: THE EAST WIND HATH BROKEN THEE IN THE MIDST OF THE SEAS.

Ezekiel 27:26

Humpty Dumpty sat on a wall!

Humpty Dumpty had a great fall!

All the king’s horses and all the king’s men,

Couldn’t put Humpty together again!

As the rhyme goes, Humpty Dumpty was an egg that fell to the ground and was irreparably destroyed. This little rhyme reveals an experience which seems irreversible and irreparable.

Indeed, there are situations in the ministry which look irrecoverable. The places where there is no possibility of recovery are called places of shipwreck.

However, Humpty Dumpty could not be put together by the king’s men and the king’s horses because Humpty Dumpty called on a natural king. You need to call on the right king if you want to recover from such situations. Call on the King of kings! The King of kings believes in restoration, redemption, forgiveness, reconciliation, recovery and salvation from the worst kind of situation.

2\. A shipwreck is when a person and his ministry are financially ruined.

Thy riches, and THY FAIRS, THY MERCHANDISE, thy mariners, and thy pilots, thy calkers, and the occupiers of thy merchandise, and all thy men of war, that are in thee, and in all thy company which is in the midst of thee, SHALL FALL INTO THE MIDST OF THE SEAS IN THE DAY OF THY RUIN.

Ezekiel 27:27

3\. A shipwreck has happened when people weep over you, even at the thought of you.

AND ARE HEARD WAILING LOUDLY OVER YOU, AND THEY CRY BITTERLY. They cast up dust on their heads; they wallow in ashes, And they make themselves \[utterly\] bald for you and gird themselves with sackcloth, and they weep over you in bitterness of heart and with bitter mourning and wailing.

Ezekiel 27:30-31 (AMPC)

4\. A shipwreck is when your team of collaborators are destroyed with you.

Now you are shattered by the seas in the depths of the waters; your merchandise and ALL YOUR CREW HAVE GONE DOWN WITH YOU.

Ezekiel 27:34 (AMPC)

5\. A shipwreck is when all people are frightened and horrified by the lessons they learn from your disaster.

All the inhabitants of the isles and coastlands are astonished and appalled at you, and their kings are horribly frightened and shudder greatly; their faces quiver.

Ezekiel 27:35 (AMPC)

6\. A shipwreck is when you come to a horrible end.

The merchants among the nations scoff at you; YOU HAVE COME TO A HORRIBLE END and will be no more.

Ezekiel 27:36 (NIV)

7\. A shipwreck is when you cross the line and can never recover or go back.

YOU ARE GONE, GONE FOREVER, and merchants all over the world are terrified, afraid that they will share your fate.

Ezekiel 27:36 (GNT)
