### Chapter 5\

The Titanic Shipwreck: A Warning from History\
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall.

Proverbs 16:18

One of the most captivating stories of a shipwreck is the story of the Titanic. The Titanic was destroyed on its very first voyage, contrary to all human expectations. No one expected that such a tragedy could occur. The story of the Titanic shipwreck highlights the effect of pride on the human race.

Pride indeed is our greatest enemy and destroyer! Pride is a very clever enemy because it has many different forms, including invisible forms. Pride is so well camouflaged that it is welcomed into our lives as a friend when it is actually an enemy. Pride can and must be recognised in the many ways that it manifests itself.

There is an unspoken law in the universe that declares that whoever and whatever exalts himself, whenever and wherever, will be brought low. There is another unspoken law which says that whoever and whatever humbles himself, whenever and wherever, will be exalted.

For whosoever exalteth himself shall be abased, and he that humbleth himself shall be exalted.

Luke 14:11

The Story of the Titanic Shipwreck

People all over the world are still fascinated, captivated and curious about this tragic shipwreck. The Titanic was a British luxury passenger ship that sank on 15th April 1912, en route to New York from Southampton, England, on its very first voyage.

The largest and most luxurious ship afloat, the Titanic, had a double-bottomed hull divided into 16 watertight compartments. The hull of a ship is the hollow, lowermost, partially submerged portion of a ship. The hull helps the ship to float. The Titanic was built in such a way that four compartments of the hull could be flooded without endangering the ship’s ability to float. Because of this, the Titanic was considered unsinkable.

However, after only four days’ journey and shortly before midnight on 14th April 1912, the Titanic collided with a huge iceberg at a location southeast of Cape Race, Newfoundland, near Canada. Five compartments of the hull of the ship ruptured, and the ship sank in just over 150 minutes. The ship broke into two pieces at around 2.20 a.m. on April 15 and sank, sending all remaining passengers on board into the ocean. There were 2224 passengers and crew on board the Titanic, and over 1,500 of them died when it sank.

Unfortunately, the Titanic had only 1,178 lifeboat places, even though there were 2,224 people on board. Today, after this disaster, there are rules that require that the number of passengers on a ship should be equal to the number of places in the lifeboats.

The Titanic sent distress signals to multiple ships shortly after midnight on April 15, 1912. Only “The Carpathia” arrived on the scene and saved the 712 survivors of the Titanic. The remaining 1512 lives perished in the icy waters of the ocean. It is said that if the other two ships nearby had responded, all the people on the Titanic could have been saved.

Part of the tragedy of the Titanic shipwreck was that there was a ship nearby, only 22 kilometres away, which did not hear the Titanic’s distress calls because no one was on duty. Today, after learning this vital lesson, all ships maintain a 24-hour radio watch for distress signals.

Another important development that followed the sinking of the Titanic was that an International Ice Patrol was established to monitor icebergs in shipping lanes.

So, what exactly caused this amazing Titanic shipwreck? Many people have given many reasons for the sinking of the Titanic. I believe that many technical reasons can be attributed to this shipwreck, but perhaps, pride was the big underlying evil that contributed to this tragedy.

The Pride of the Titanic

1\. Titanic Pride is in Overconfidence!

Through overconfidence comes nothing but strife, But wisdom is with those who receive counsel.

Proverbs 13:10 (NASB)

Pride makes you trust in what you should not trust. Pride causes you not to prepare for danger and possible shipwrecks. “It is impossible”, is what pride says. Because the shipbuilders were so confident about the unsinkable nature of the Titanic, they did not even have enough lifeboats on board. Although there were 2,224 passengers on board, there were only enough lifeboats to accommodate 1,178 passengers. This means that almost half of the passengers would be forced to drown in the event of a shipwreck.

Watch out for overconfidence! Being too confident can lead to serious mistakes. It seems that the mistakes of the builders of the Titanic were largely from pride and overconfidence in themselves and in their achievements.

It is good to have faith and to be confident, but you must place this great confidence in God and not in your own understanding. Pride is overconfidence: overconfidence is pride!

TRUST IN THE LORD WITH ALL THINE HEART; AND LEAN NOT UNTO THINE OWN UNDERSTANDING. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths. Be not wise in thine own eyes: fear the Lord, and depart from evil.

Proverbs 3:5-7

2\. Titanic Pride is in a Lack of Preparation!

If my people, which are called by my name, shall HUMBLE THEMSELVES, and PRAY, and SEEK MY FACE, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin, and will heal their land.

2 Chronicles 7:14

Humility always leads to preparation! When the people of God humbled themselves, they prayed and sought God’s face. That is indeed a sign of humility!

One of the signs of overconfidence is a lack of preparation! Pride makes you trust what you should not trust in. Pride causes you not to prepare for danger and possible shipwrecks. “It is impossible! No bad thing can happen” – these are the statements of pride! This is why many people do not write wills or give any instructions concerning their funerals.

Even though there was space for 1,178 Titanic passengers to be saved on lifeboats, around 700 people eventually survived the Titanic. Why is that? The Titanic’s crew was not prepared to deal with such a devastating incident. A disorganised and inefficient evacuation process was the result of poor communication, poor coordination and a lack of clear instructions, which made the death toll higher than it should have been. A few more hundred people could have been saved, even though there were not enough lifeboats. Let God lead you to humbly prepare yourself for whatever is coming.

3\. Titanic Pride Does Not Honour God!

For even though they knew God, THEY DID NOT HONOR HIM AS GOD OR GIVE THANKS, but they became futile in their reasonings, and their senseless hearts were darkened.

Romans 1:21 (NASB)

Did the makers of the Titanic honour God? Did they thank God? Rarely do you hear the mention of God in relation to the building of the Titanic. A proud person is fixated on what man can achieve and what man has achieved. A proud person is not so interested in the God factor. God is not even mentioned in the mouths of the proud and arrogant! A shipwreck is always caused when people forget God and do not honour Him.

4\. Titanic Pride Moves Forward Recklessly!

The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation is high; that saith in his heart, Who shall bring me down to the ground?

Obadiah 1:3

The Titanic moved along at a high speed in dangerous waters. The Titanic was travelling at a high speed through a region known for its dangerous icebergs. The choice to keep up this speed regardless of alerts from different boats was a huge calculated risk.

When people are proud and reckless, they move without caution, assuming that things will work out for them somehow. Reckless behaviour is utterly unconcerned about the consequences of certain actions. People become proud and reckless with sin when they have been doing it for a long time and nothing bad has happened to them.

A proud person moves very fast and without caution. Beware of excessive and reckless speed. Pride makes you go too fast. Beware of voices of haste!

Pride makes you blind to dangers. A humble person is more careful. The Titanic moved through very dangerous waters as though there were no danger. Pride makes you ignore danger signs and warnings with arrogance. When you are filled with humility, you do not see yourself as Superman. You see yourself as an average person who is in real danger of the things that ordinary people are in danger of.

Go slowly! How do you see yourself? As a superman who cannot fall into sin, or as an ordinary person who is afflicted with the failings of a normal man? Jesus saw Himself as a man! As a mere man! That is how you must see yourself too. You must tread with caution and go slowly.

Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus: Who, being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God: But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made IN THE LIKENESS OF MEN: And being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.

Philippians 2:5-8

5\. Titanic Pride Speaks with Arrogance!

I SAID TO THE BOASTFUL, ‘DO NOT BOAST,’ AND TO THE WICKED, ‘DO NOT LIFT UP THE HORN; Do not lift your horn on high, Do not speak with insolent pride.’ For not from the east, nor from the west, Nor from the desert comes exaltation; But God is the Judge; He puts down one and exalts another.

Psalm 75:4-7 (NASB)

The Titanic was widely acclaimed to be unsinkable. Titanic makers spoke great words of confidence. The Titanic was praised as the pinnacle of engineering achievement. The builders declared it virtually unsinkable. Complacency and a lack of preparedness for such a catastrophe resulted from this false sense of invincibility.

The first unqualified assertion of Titanic’s unsinkability appeared in The New York Times on 16 April 1912, a day after the tragedy. Philip A. S. Franklin, Vice President of the International Mercantile Marine Company (owned by White Star Line’s holding company), stated after being told of the sinking, “I thought her unsinkable, and I based my opinion on the best expert advice available. I do not understand it.”

6\. Titanic Pride is in its Blindness!

Woe unto you, ye BLIND guides, which say, Whosoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gold of the temple, he is a debtor! Ye fools and BLIND: for whether is greater, the gold, or the temple that sanctifieth the gold? …Ye fools and BLIND: for whether is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifieth the gift?

Matthew 23:16-17, 19

Pride makes you blind. Due to a lack of binoculars, the Titanic’s lookout crew was unable to spot the iceberg in time. The Titanic did not even have a pair of binoculars to look out for any dangers! They moved in the dark at top speed when there were icebergs all around! They might have been able to take evasive action sooner if they had this essential piece of equipment.

How could such a huge ship move so confidently in an area known to have huge icebergs?

If they had even thought of the icebergs as a problem, there would have been a lookout, carefully scanning the waters to see if the dangerous icebergs were anywhere ahead.

Pride is overconfidence! Overconfidence is pride!

7\. Titanic Pride Needs No One!

Son of man, say to the ruler of Tyre, ‘This is what the Sovereign Lord says: “‘In the pride of your heart you say, “I am a god; I sit on the throne of a god in the heart of the seas.” But you are a mere mortal and not a god, though you think you are as wise as a god.

Ezekiel 28:2 (NIV)

Pride makes you think that you do not need anyone. Pride makes you feel that there is no need to relate to anyone else. “I sit alone! I am a god!” Pride makes you independent and unconnected. Pride causes you not to relate to others like your fellows and colleagues. The Titanic was not connected to the nearby ships that could have come to rescue them. The mighty Titanic needed help badly from smaller and seemingly “inferior” ships.

When the Titanic was hit hard and started sinking, it began sending out distress signals. There were three ships which were nearby before it sank: “The Samson”, “The Californian” and “The Carpathia”. These three ships alone could have saved all the passengers on board the Titanic. One ship was just 7 miles away. Another ship was 14 miles away, and the last one was 58 miles away. Having good relationships and lots of communication with them could have saved them from the disaster that occurred.

The ship called “The Samson” was merely 7 miles away from the Titanic. They saw the white flares signalling danger, but because the crew had been hunting seals illegally and did not want to be caught, they turned and went in the opposite direction, away from the Titanic.

The second ship was called the “The Californian” and it was just 14 miles away from the Titanic. Unfortunately, this ship was surrounded by fields of ice. The captain looked out and saw the white flares but felt that the conditions were not favourable. Because it was dark, the captain decided to go to bed and wait till morning. The crew spent time convincing themselves that nothing bad was happening.

The third ship was called “The Carpathia”. This ship was 58 miles away. It was headed away from the Titanic when it heard the distress cries over the radio. The captain of this ship sensed the distress and went full steam ahead through the ice fields.

On that fateful night, The Carpathia was the first ship to respond to the distress of the Titanic. It would take approximately three and a half hours to arrive at the Titanic’s position. Captain Arthur Rostron of The Carpathia made great efforts to speed up his old ship through the dangerous ice fields. Captain Rostron’s crew kept themselves busy making preparations to receive the surviving passengers. This was the ship that saved the 712 survivors of the Titanic.

God having provided some better thing for us, that THEY WITHOUT US SHOULD NOT BE MADE PERFECT.

Hebrews 11:40

Pride makes you feel that you do not need anyone. But we do need one another! We will always need others! No matter who you are, you will need someone to help you. You may even need someone to help you at the time of your death.

Let us take great lessons from this incredible, historic tragedy and receive direction from the Lord to fight against all invisible forms of pride!

May the Lord grant you the wisdom to be delivered from all types of shipwreck and ministerial tragedies! May you never make shipwreck of your soul and ministry!
