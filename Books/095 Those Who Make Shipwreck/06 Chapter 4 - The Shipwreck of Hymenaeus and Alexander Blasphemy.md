### Chapter 4\

The Shipwreck of Hymenaeus and Alexander: Blasphemy\
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Among them are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have delivered to Satan in order that they may be disciplined \[by punishment and learn\] not to blaspheme.

1 Timothy 1:20 (AMPC)

Who were Hymenaeus and Alexander? They were blasphemers! Hymenaeus and Alexander were shipwrecked blasphemers! Hymenaeus and Alexander were Christians who had worked with Timothy and Paul, but who had made shipwreck of their lives and ministries.

How did Alexander and Hymenaeus end up as shipwrecked ministers? Through blasphemy!

Paul wanted them to learn not to blaspheme, so he committed them to satan himself. He removed their heavenly covering, so that they might be exposed directly to satan. That is an ultimate judgment for an ultimate sin.

The shipwreck of Hymenaeus and Alexander was caused by their tendency to insult, defame and speak evil of authorities. Hymenaeus was a person who spoke irreverently about God’s servants. He must have insulted the church and its leadership in the worst way possible.

Today, there are many blasphemers on social media and other forms of regular media. They insult, defame, blaspheme, vilify, denigrate, criticise and belittle God’s servants in the media. This is the ultimate sin of blasphemy in the modern world.

One day, someone showed me a whole set of blasphemies on social media. I was shocked to see pastors and former pastors who had dedicated themselves to the insulting, vilifying and defaming of God’s honourable servants. Be not deceived! God is not mocked! No matter how long it takes, God will not allow blasphemy to go scot-free. Blasphemy is an ultimate sin, and it will receive an ultimate and profound judgment!

What is Blasphemy?

Blasphemy is the ultimate sin of speaking reproachfully about God and His work! Blasphemy is when a person speaks evil in such a way as to incur the wrath of God. Blasphemy comes from the Greek word “blasphemeo”.

Blasphemeo means many things:

1\. Blasphemy is a bitter denunciation and complaint uttered by a godless man.

2\. Blasphemy is the sin of speaking impiously about God, His church and His servants.

3\. Blasphemy is the sin of attacking the good reputation of God, His church and His servants by uttering malicious and injurious words.

4\. Blasphemy is the sin of defaming and speaking damaging words against God’s ministers.

5\. Blasphemy is the sin of railing at and insulting God’s servants.

6\. Blasphemy is the sin of speaking or writing extremely evil words against God, His church and His servants!

7\. Blasphemy is the sin of speaking reproachfully against the church of God.

Blasphemers are doomed to shipwreck because blasphemy is a very serious sin, for which there will be a strong reaction. You can tell how serious blasphemy is by a little analysis of the Holy Scriptures on blasphemy.

1\. Blasphemy is an ultimate sin because it is injurious to the body of Christ. No other sin is described as injurious to the church.

And I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who hath enabled me, for that he counted me faithful, putting me into the ministry; Who was before a blasphemer, and a persecutor, and injurious: but I obtained mercy, because I did it ignorantly in unbelief.

1 Timothy 1:12-13

2\. Blasphemy is an ultimate sin because Hymenaeus and Alexander were delivered to satan for it. Blasphemy provoked Apostle Paul to hand over Hymenaeus and Alexander to satan. This blasphemy must have been a very serious sin for them to have been condemned and handed over to satan just so that they would learn not to blaspheme!

Of whom is Hymenaeus and Alexander; whom I have delivered unto Satan, that they may learn not to blaspheme.

1 Timothy 1:20

3\. Blasphemy is an ultimate evil because it is one of the most profound last-day evils. The Bible declares that several moral blemishes will come upon us in the last days, including blasphemy.

For men shall be lovers of their own selves, covetous, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,

2 Timothy 3:2

4\. Blasphemy against the Holy Spirit is an ultimate evil, and will result in never being forgiven. Blasphemy against the Holy Spirit is considered unforgivable! This reveals that blasphemy is a profound, unforgivable evil.

And whosoever shall speak a word against the Son of man, it shall be forgiven him: but unto him that blasphemeth against the Holy Ghost it shall not be forgiven.

Luke 12:10

5\. Blasphemy is the ultimate self-destructive evil! Blasphemy is considered as a form of self-hatred and self-destruction! Paul described those who blaspheme as those who oppose themselves! Those who blaspheme destroy themselves.

AND WHEN THEY OPPOSED THEMSELVES, and blasphemed, he shook his raiment, and said unto them, Your blood be upon your own heads; I am clean: from henceforth I will go unto the Gentiles.

Acts 18:6

6\. Blasphemy causes a person to become an unreasoning animal. Blasphemy converts people into the lowest kind of beasts. Blasphemy makes a person turn into an unreasoning animal.

BUT THESE PEOPLE BLASPHEME in matters they do not understand. THEY ARE LIKE UNREASONING ANIMALS, CREATURES OF INSTINCT, BORN ONLY TO BE CAUGHT AND DESTROYED, and like animals they too will perish.

2 Peter 2:12 (NIV)

7. Blasphemy is the sin of the dragon and the sin of the beast. Do you want to be identified with the sins of the dragon and the beast? Blasphemy is an ultimate sin committed by ultimate evil personalities.

And there was given unto him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies; and power was given unto him to continue forty and two months. AND HE OPENED HIS MOUTH IN BLASPHEMY AGAINST GOD, TO BLASPHEME HIS NAME, AND HIS TABERNACLE, AND THEM THAT DWELL IN HEAVEN.

Revelation 13:5-6

Why Did Hymenaeus and Alexander become Blasphemers?

Holding FAITH, and a GOOD CONSCIENCE; which some having put away concerning faith have made shipwreck: Of whom is Hymenaeus and Alexander; whom I have delivered unto Satan, that they may learn not to blaspheme.

1 Timothy 1:19-20

Hymenaeus and Alexander became blasphemers because they set aside their faith and their conscience! Hymenaeus and Alexander shipwrecked their ministries because of blasphemy. You will never be able to say certain things unless you set aside your faith and beliefs.

The scripture says in 1 Chronicles 16:22, “Touch not mine anointed, and do my prophets no harm.” It is only when you do not believe this scripture that you vilify, insult, denigrate, castigate and criticise God’s anointed servant.

Do Not Set Aside Your Faith!

I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I HAVE KEPT THE FAITH.

2 Timothy 4:7

Do not set aside the things you believe. Stay with your beliefs until the end of the race!

Many people put aside what they originally believed. Some people believed that they were called to the ministry. Yet at a point, they declared, “I am not a pastor anymore”. Meanwhile, people have known them as ministers of the gospel for years.

Someone once called me his spiritual father. Yet after many years, the same person declared that I was not his spiritual father.

Assess yourself this way all the time; do you still ardently believe the things you once believed?

WHO IS IT YOU HAVE RIDICULED AND BLASPHEMED? Against whom have you raised your voice and lifted your eyes in pride? Against the Holy One of Israel!

Isaiah 37:23 (NIV)

It is as though those who spend their time blaspheming on social media do not believe in God. They criticise God’s servants and the church with reckless ease and abandon. It is as though they do not believe in God at all! But the scripture clearly asks “Whom do you think you are blaspheming against? Do you not know there is a God up there?”

Almighty God is watching all that is being said and done. He is the one you are fighting against.

Apostle Paul blasphemed and attacked the church before he was saved.

And I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who hath enabled me, for that he counted me faithful, putting me into the ministry; WHO WAS BEFORE A BLASPHEMER, AND A PERSECUTOR, AND INJURIOUS: but I obtained mercy, because I did it ignorantly in unbelief.

1 Timothy 1:12-13

Paul’s blasphemous behaviour did not end well for him. He was arrested by the Lord and struck blind. God asked him, “Why are you persecuting me? Why are you attacking me?” God told him, “It is hard to kick against thorns.” In other words, it is painful and difficult to strike something that is well-protected and defended.

And he fell to the earth, and heard a voice saying unto him, Saul, Saul, WHY PERSECUTEST THOU ME? And he said, Who art thou, Lord? And the Lord said, I am Jesus whom thou persecutest: IT IS HARD FOR THEE TO KICK AGAINST THE PRICKS.

Acts 9:4-5

Do Not Set Aside Your Conscience!

Do not set aside the gentle inner voice that speaks to you. Always listen to the still, small voice within you. Your conscience is an inner sense of what is right or wrong. That is your conscience! Your conscience is what will guide you in your conduct and motives. Your conscience will impel you toward the right action.

You will never be able to say certain evil things unless your conscience is dead. Blasphemers know that many of the things they are saying are not true. It is their bad conscience that allows them to continue saying and doing evil. They are determined to lie and defame until the bitter end. It seems that they intend to crash out of this world screaming, lying and cursing everyone.

This is why faith and a good conscience are so important. Losing your faith and your conscience will make you open to blasphemy. Blasphemers are bitter, wicked men who cannot easily repent or turn around! Satan is the stubborn, unrepentant and sinful angel who has never changed his mind and never stopped blaspheming. It is those who are filled with the spirit of satan who continue to blaspheme relentlessly to their own destruction.

May God bless you with the strength to resist all blasphemers! May an evil speaker not be established in your life and ministry! Let all slanderers be removed from the earth!

MAY A SLANDERER NOT BE ESTABLISHED IN THE EARTH; May evil hunt the violent man speedily.

Psalm 140:11 (NASB95)
