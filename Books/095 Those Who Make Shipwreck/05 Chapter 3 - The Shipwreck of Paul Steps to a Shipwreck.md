### Chapter 3\

The Shipwreck of Paul: Steps to a Shipwreck\
-----------------------------------------------------------------

And, hardly passing it, came unto a place which is called The fair havens; nigh whereunto was the city of Lasea. Now when much time was spent, and when sailing was now dangerous, because the fast was now already past, Paul admonished them, And said unto them, Sirs, I perceive that this voyage will be with hurt and much damage, not only of the lading and ship, but also of our lives. Nevertheless the centurion believed the master and the owner of the ship, more than those things which were spoken by Paul. And because the haven was not commodious to winter in, the more part advised to depart thence also, if by any means they might attain to Phenice, and there to winter; which is an haven of Crete, and lieth toward the south west and north west. And when the south wind blew softly, supposing that they had obtained their purpose, loosing thence, they sailed close by Crete.

Acts 27:8-13

This scripture describes the shipwreck of Paul. Paul was involved in a historic shipwreck which led to his being marooned on the island of Malta. The shipwreck was a significant event in the life of Paul and almost cost him his life. How did this shipwreck come about and what were the factors that led to this disaster? What can you learn from Paul’s shipwreck? Whatever caused the shipwreck for Paul can also cause a shipwreck for you.

Eight Steps to a Shipwreck

1\. Delay Can Cause a Shipwreck.

Now when much time was spent, and when sailing was now dangerous, because the fast was now already past, Paul admonished them,

Acts 27:9

Paul’s ship captain spent so much time on land that setting off to sail was now dangerous. Do not allow unnecessary delays to lead you to a shipwreck of ministry. Do not delay in obeying God! Do not delay in taking the steps that you need to take. Do not delay in joining a group you must belong to! Do not delay in leaving a group you must leave. Do not delay in starting the church! Do not delay in going into full-time ministry. Do not delay in sending missionaries.

2\. Circumstances Can Lead You to a Shipwreck.

Because the harbor was not suitable for wintering, the majority reached a decision to put out to sea from there, if somehow they could reach Phoenix, a harbor of Crete, facing southwest and northwest, and spend the winter there.

Acts 27:12 (NASB95)

The circumstances of the harbour led the team to embark on a journey which was doomed to shipwreck. The circumstances of the harbour were simply not suitable for wintering. However, circumstances must not lead you. Circumstance means “things standing around”; Circum and Stance! Circumstances must not guide you! You must be led by God and not the circumstances of your life.

A spiritual man is led by the Spirit and not by things standing around. Determine that you will never be led by circumstances, even if the circumstances are comfortable or seem suitable for you.

3\. Ignoring Danger Signs Can Cause a Shipwreck.

But as \[the season was well advanced, for\] much time had been lost and navigation was already dangerous, for the time for the Fast \[the Day of Atonement, about the beginning of October\] had already gone by, Paul warned and advised them,

Acts 27:9 (AMPC)

It is a very bad thing for a leader to avoid warning signs. Every time a disaster occurs, people always try to find out why and how it happened. Were there any warning signs of an impending disaster? Are there any signs that we also need to know to avoid such a disaster?

That is why whenever there is a plane crash, extreme measures are taken to find out why and how the crash occurred. There is always a great effort to pick up warning signs so that a future disaster can be avoided.

One scary thing is to have a leader who ignores danger signs. How unfortunate it would be to sit on a plane with a pilot who ignores the warning signs that his fuel tank is leaking!

4\. Being in a Hurry Can Result in a Shipwreck.

NOW WHEN MUCH TIME WAS SPENT, and when sailing was now dangerous, because the fast was now already past, Paul admonished them, And said unto them, Sirs, I perceive that this voyage will be with hurt and much damage, not only of the lading and ship, but also of our lives.

Acts 27:9-10

It is a dangerous thing to be in a hurry. It is dangerous for you to become so conscious of the time that you hurry out into a shipwreck. God is not in a hurry. God is not rushing you anywhere. He that believeth shall not make haste.

Therefore thus saith the Lord God, Behold, I lay in Zion for a foundation a stone, a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation: HE THAT BELIEVETH SHALL NOT MAKE HASTE.

Isaiah 28:16

I remember a man of God who made the worst decision of his life when he was pressured and rushed into signing a document. By signing that document, he handed over his ministry to his enemies and lost it forever. He said he remembered, as he was signing the document, a message that he had heard at a pastors’ conference. That message was entitled “Beware voices of haste.” “Beware voices of haste” means beware of being hurried up to do things. Beware of impatience in yourself! There is a verse which has always guided me away from being in a hurry. It says:

He that hasteth to be rich hath an evil eye, and considereth not that poverty shall come upon him.

Proverbs 28:22

Be careful of rushing around and being in a hurry all the time. He that hasteth to be rich has an evil eye!

I remember a brother who was hasty to be rich. He looked for every opportunity to make money. Once, he was a guest of some people in America. When something went wrong in the apartment block, he suggested to his friend to sue the landlord. “You can make so much money from a lawsuit.” He continued, “This is America! You can make a lot of money from a lawsuit.”

Whenever he wrote a book or had the opportunity to preach, he spoke only about money. This made it clear to everyone who knew him that he was in a hurry to become rich. And indeed, he eventually turned out to be a rebellious, slanderous, and evil person.

Beware voices of haste! Beware of anything that hurries you up! Beware of being hurried up! Paul and his team were hurried up and made to leave quickly. They were rushed into a famous shipwreck!

5\. Failure to Heed the Word of the Lord from His Servant Caused the Shipwreck.

But as \[the season was well advanced, for\] much time had been lost and navigation was already dangerous, for the time for the Fast \[the Day of Atonement, about the beginning of October\] had already gone by, Paul warned and advised them, Saying, Sirs, I perceive \[after careful observation\] that this voyage will be attended with disaster and much heavy loss, not only of the cargo and the ship but of our lives also. However, the centurion paid greater attention to the pilot and to the owner of the ship than to what Paul said.

Acts 27:9-11 (AMPC)

It is important to heed the word of the Lord through His prophets. Not yielding to a prophetic word is to open yourself up to shipwreck. The word of the Lord is something that requires believing! You must believe in supernatural words from the Lord, through His servants.

NOW THEREFORE BE YE NOT MOCKERS, LEST YOUR BANDS BE MADE STRONG: for I have heard from the Lord God of hosts a consumption, even determined upon the whole earth. Give ye ear, and hear my voice; hearken, and hear my speech.

Isaiah 28:22-23

Do not be a mocker, lest your bands and bonds be made stronger and your destruction is even more severe. Mockers will be destroyed!

Then Elisha said, Hear ye the word of the Lord; Thus saith the Lord, To morrow about this time shall a measure of fine flour be sold for a shekel, and two measures of barley for a shekel, in the gate of Samaria. Then a lord on whose hand the king leaned answered the man of God, and said, Behold, if the Lord would make windows in heaven, might this thing be? And he said, Behold, thou shalt see it with thine eyes, but shalt not eat thereof...

And so it fell out unto him: for the people trode upon him in the gate, and he died.

2 Kings 7:1-2, 20

When the prophet declared that there would be a 24-hour miracle resulting in abundance and fullness, the king’s servant mocked him. Just as the prophecy declared, he was run over and destroyed because he did not believe the word of the Lord!

We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, AS UNTO A LIGHT THAT SHINETH IN A DARK PLACE, until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts: Knowing this first, that no prophecy of the scripture is of any private interpretation. For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man: but holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost.

2 Peter 1:19-21

Do not reject the prophetic words that God sends to you. God has decided to bless you. You must believe it and flow in it. There is darkness everywhere! It is this darkness we are living in. A prophecy is a light! It is a candle in the dark! There is thick fog everywhere! God is trying to show you the way! Listen to the prophecies!

6\. Trusting in the Majority Can Cause a Shipwreck.

And as the harbor was not well situated and so unsuitable to winter in, THE MAJORITY FAVORED THE PLAN OF PUTTING TO SEA AGAIN FROM THERE, hoping somehow to reach Phoenice, a harbor of Crete facing southwest and northwest, and winter there.

Acts 27:12 (AMPC)

Always trusting the majority is a mistake. The children of Israel murmured against Moses, and they were in the majority. Yet, the majority was wrong in the wilderness, and Moses, the minority, was right. The twelve spies were sent out to spy on the land. Only Joshua and Caleb (the minority) had a good report. The ten (the majority), gave an evil report. The majority had a bad spirit, and the minority had a good spirit.

Surely they shall not see the land which I sware unto their fathers, neither shall any of them that provoked me see it: But my servant Caleb, BECAUSE HE HAD ANOTHER SPIRIT WITH HIM, and hath followed me fully, him will I bring into the land whereinto he went; and his seed shall possess it.

Numbers 14:23-24

It is the wisdom of the world that says the majority is always right. On what basis is the majority right? Many people do not have any good reason for voting for a particular candidate. Some vote because their candidate is tall and handsome. Some vote because the candidate is from their tribe or ethnic group. Others vote because the candidate is their relative. Many times however, the majority does not necessarily make the right choice.

7\. Being More Open to Secular Advice Than to Spiritual Advice Can Cause a Shipwreck.

Nevertheless, the centurion BELIEVED THE MASTER AND THE OWNER OF THE SHIP, MORE THAN THOSE THINGS WHICH WERE SPOKEN BY PAUL.

Acts 27:11

The centurion should have listened to Paul. Be careful of rejecting spiritual advice. Be careful of being carnal in your decisions. To be carnally minded leads to death. To reject spiritual words is a very negative and worldly mistake!
