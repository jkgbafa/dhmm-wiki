### Chapter 17\

LAUNCH UNEXPECTED STRIKES\
-----------------------------------------------------

\

##### Now when Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judaea in the days of Herod the king, behold, there came wise men from the east to Jerusalem,

##### Matthew 2:1

The birth of Jesus was the most astonishing strike from Jehovah against the wickedness and pride of satan! Surprise satan by being unusually and unexpectedly humble!

Satan never expected the Son of God on this planet. Satan thought he was safe here on earth and was establishing himself as the god of this world!

Demons thought they were untouchable as they plagued the human race with unbearable diseases and torments.

Almighty God released an amazing strike against satan by sending His only Son, Jesus, to be born of a woman. This was an unusual and unexpected act of humility!

If the Almighty were to make a move against satan, surely it would not be in one of the most humble and lowly places on earth. Jesus would have been expected to come to earth as a king because He was the King of kings and the Lord of lords. Instead, He was born as a vulnerable little baby in Bethlehem.Acts of humility are always great surprise attacks that unsettle the enemy.

It is time to surprise the enemy with your unexpected and extraordinary step of humility! Launch a noteworthy, humbling surprise attack on satan. Surprise satan by being unusually and unexpectedly humble!

But He gives a greater grace. Therefore it says, “God is opposed to the proud, but gives grace to the humble.”

James 4:6 (NASB)

Satan is the father of all pride. He does not expect nor want humble things. Satan is the originator of all forms of pride in all types of people.

Put on a humble disguise and slip through all the defences of satan. You will arrive inside the stronghold of satan with all your weapons.

Surprise satan in many ways. Learn from Apostle Paul. Apostle Paul had a great revelation of humility. He shocked satan by rejoicing over the distressing aspects of his life.

And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me. Therefore I take pleasure in infirmities, in reproaches, in necessities, in persecutions, in distresses for Christ’s sake: for when I am weak, then am I strong.

2 Corinthians 12:9-10

Unexpected Strikes of Humility

1\. Hide your glory: Hide your beauty intentionally. Hide the glory and shine on your life and ministry with every opportunity. Resign from glorious, high and mighty groups and join children’s groups. That’s how to keep on the drab clothes of humility and cover the glory.

2\. Talk about your weaknesses and personal problems rather than your achievements and victories: That’s how to keep on the drab clothes of humility. That’s how to cover your glory.

3\. Be a child in allowing yourself to be cared for and looked after as a child: Be a child in allowing yourself to be ministered to in innocence, purity and holiness.

4\. Confess your sins more and more: Be humble about your sinful self. David confessed his sins more than anyone and became a man after God’s heart.

5\. Forgive others, and release others from their mistakes by considering yourself: Your consideration of yourself as a low sinner is revealed in your releasing others from their sins. A person who sees himself as low and unworthy is also not willing to prosecute and reject sinners.

6\. Go to humble places: That is a direct surprise move. It will lead you to a defenceless enemy. Satan was blind to the horse stables and other lowly places in Bethlehem. Satan was shocked to find Jesus suddenly on the earth and among animals in a stable. Satan was expecting Jesus in kings’ palaces.

7\. Be humble and listen to others: There are those who never listen to another person. Open yourself to listen to others. Hear other opinions and change direction to prove to yourself that you are malleable.

8\. Be humble and have youthful and exciting sex: Youthful sex (as against the Begrudging and Reluctant Sex of the Irritable; BRSI) makes you a young and excitable person. Proud women do not have happy or exciting sex at all. They give grudging, very poor and minimal performances because they see themselves as doing someone a favour. They also see themselves as above base and youthful sexual activities. Queenly pride is revealed in a sexual life full of excuses and reasons. Proud people are simply unable to flow as young and humble children.

9\. Be humble by embracing embarrassing situations: Embrace what people find shameful and embarrassing and press on in the service of the King.

10\. Be humble. Do not criticize or verbally attack your attackers: Criticism reveals an awful arrogance.

11\. Humble yourself; take young people as your assistants: Appoint new and young people into important positions as an act of humility. Prideful older people look down on young people. Older people see themselves as being above the young ones. They will not even attend the appointment services of these young ones because they despise them. The proud are often unable to stoop down and work properly with the young.

12. Humble yourself; appoint people from humble backgrounds: The appointment and acceptance of people with humble backgrounds is an unexpected move of humility.

13\. Humble yourself; relate with foreigners: Being able to relate well with any foreigner will be one of the greatest signs of your unexpected humility.

14\. Be devoted to teaching basics and doing basic things: Those lowly “basic teachings” and activities are the signs of humility in your ministry. Teach young people. Teach children. This is your great sign of humility.
