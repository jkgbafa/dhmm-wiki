### Chapter 16\

BIND STRONG MEN!\
--------------------------------------------

\

##### Or else how can one enter into a strong man’s house, and spoil his goods, except he first BIND THE STRONG MAN? and then he will spoil his house.

##### Matthew 12:29

Binding the strong man is about restricting the strong man. Binding the strong man is to tie him up, just like how it is done in the movies! How do you tie up satan? Which ropes are used to tie up the devil?

A strong man is the cause of your crises. The strong man is the person who holds the key to ending your conflict. The strong man is the reason why you still have a problem. When you take him down the beast will die and the crises will melt away.

First of all, you must identify the strong man. You must identify the main spirit behind the conflict. Then and only then can you deal with the strong man. Take up a special lamentation against the spirit that is behind him.

Do not allow evil spirits to operate freely in your midst. Deal with him and fight the strong man specifically.

If there is a strong man who is the cause of your conflict and crises, he needs to be dealt with! When the strong man is bound, the conflict will die naturally.

How do we bind strong men?

1\. A strong man is dealt with by prayers of agreement.

Verily I say unto you, Whatsoever ye shall bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven. Again I say unto you, That if two of you shall agree on earth as touching any thing that they shall ask, it shall be done for them of my Father which is in heaven.

Matthew 18:18-19

You can deal with the strong man through prayers of agreement. You can bind the devil when you meet with other believers and agree in prayer.

Bind up the devil in your prayer times. Do not have prayer times where there is no binding of devils. In your prayer times specifically bind demons and their activities. Prayers of binding and loosing are very effective in dealing with the strong man.

2\. A strong man is dealt with by prayers and fasting.

Then came the disciples to Jesus apart, and said, Why could not we cast him out? And Jesus said unto them, Because of your unbelief: for verily I say unto you, If ye have faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye shall say unto this mountain, Remove hence to yonder place; and it shall remove; and nothing shall be impossible unto you. Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.

Matthew 17:19-21

You can deal with the strong man through prayer and fasting. You can bind the devil in prayer and fasting!

Bind up the devil in prayer and fasting. In your prayer time specifically bind demons and their activities. Binding of the strong man is a very powerful and effective method of prayer.

3\. Evangelists must bind the strong man.

Soul-winning ministries must deal with the strong man if they are to ever win souls. How do you know when you have bound the strong man? The answer is very simple. When you have the goods (souls) of the strong man in your possession, it is evidence that your binding of the strong man was successful!

4\. Pastors must bind strong men .

And that they may recover themselves out of the snare of the devil, who are taken captive by him at his will.

2 Timothy 2:26

Church growth is only possible by binding the strong man. Many church members are taken captive by satan and that is why they behave badly. Winning souls is winning captives from satan’s prison. Pastors who do not spend quality time binding the strong man will not experience genuine church growth.

Declare prayer sessions to bind the strong man! As you keep praying, you will see a mysterious church growth taking place.

When the church grows larger, it is evident that captive souls are being set free. As strong men are bound, captives go free and the church grows. As your church grows, it shows that you have actually bound satan in his own house and walked away with his former captives!

5\. A strong man is dealt with by preaching.

The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, TO PREACH DELIVERANCE TO THE CAPTIVES and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised,

Luke 4:18

Deliverance from captivity takes place through preaching. The word of God is a sword that severs the chains that keep us bound as captives.

Preaching is a mighty tool of deliverance from captivity! As strong men are bound, captives go free and the church grows.

Captives are released by the preaching of deliverance!
