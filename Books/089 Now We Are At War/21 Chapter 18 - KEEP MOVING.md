### Chapter 18\

KEEP MOVING\
---------------------------------------

\

##### Those who want to kill me set their traps, those who would harm me talk of my ruin; all day long they scheme and lie.

##### Psalm 38:12 (NIV)

\

A trap exists to stop you from moving about freely. Your very movement is what prevents the enemy from having access to you.

A moving target is far more difficult to destroy than a stationary one. Satan’s wish is for you to be stationary, trapped in one spot. Just the fact that there is movement in your life and ministry gives you some form of safety.

Indeed, if you are stuck in one place, you are in danger of being killed or destroyed. Those who want to kill you would love to trap you and pin you down. They want to fasten you to one spot.

Your continuous movement is a great mystery to the enemy. It is not so easy to hit a moving target. Satan cannot fix his eyes on you because one moment you are here, in the next moment, you are not there.

God has embedded your victory over satan in the mystery of movement! You must escape every form of entrapment and keep moving. What is holding you down? What has trapped you? Who has trapped you? Are you in a snare?

I find more bitter than death the woman who is a snare, whose heart is a trap and whose hands are chains. The man who pleases God will escape her, but the sinner she will ensnare.

Ecclesiastes 7:26 (NIV)

Movement is important for victory against your great enemy. Satan’s great effort is to fasten you to one spot and finish you off.

Movement is mysterious because God does not change His callings and decisions. God is unchanging and seems to be stationary. Yet all that God has ever done is to create things which never stop moving. Today God is going to set you free so that you can be mobile! You will escape from any entrapment.

Our souls have escaped like a bird from the trapper’s snare; The snare is broken and we have escaped.

Psalm 124:7 (NASB)

God created you to move and never stop moving! Do not allow satan to pin you down!

Understand the mystery of movement! Understand the power of movement! Keep moving financially. Keep moving in the ministry. Keep moving in your pastoral ministry. Keep moving as a prophet. There are many new things ahead and you will only see them if you keep moving.

The Mystery of Movement

1\. Your ministry must be full of the mystery of movement.

For in him we live, and move, and have our being; as certain also of your own poets have said, For we are also his offspring.

Acts 17:28

Keep moving for your own survival! Without movement, you will not survive! Keep movements alive!

2\. The ministry of Jesus was full of the mystery of movement.

And JESUS WENT ABOUT all the cities and villages, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every sickness and every disease among the people.

Matthew 9:35

Ministry is movement. You need to keep moving to survive. Wild animals know that they must move without having read this book.

A minister of the gospel must know that movement is important for his safety, his survival and the continuation of his ministry. There must be some kind of movement in your life. Everything God has created is moving. The earth is moving right now at a speed of 107,000 km/h. The earth is rotating at a speed of 1,670km/h. Isn’t that amazing? Our sun and its solar system are moving around in the Milky Way Galaxy at a speed of 828,000 km/h.

When therefore the Lord knew how the Pharisees had heard that Jesus made and baptized MORE DISCIPLES THAN JOHN (Though Jesus himself baptized not, but his disciples,) HE LEFT JUDEA, and departed again into Galilee. And he must needs go through Samaria.

John 4:1-4

When Jesus’ ministry started working, He made many converts and had crowds thronging Him. Notice that Jesus’ response to His successful ministry was to just keep moving. When the ministry grew, Jesus simply moved out of town to the next place.

Spiritual people are always on the move! Jesus refused to be encircled! He kept moving to prevent encirclement. That is what you must do. Keep moving! Break out of entrapment and overcome every overwhelming encirclement by your enemy.

Jesus refused to be fastened to one spot. Just moving forward will cause you to escape encirclement. Everything God has created is moving!

3\. The Holy Spirit is full of the mystery of movement .

And the earth was without form, and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep. And THE SPIRIT OF GOD MOVED upon the face of the waters.

Genesis 1:2

The first time we hear of the Holy Spirit, He is moving. He moved to create the world and to do the mighty works of God. All through the Bible we see the Spirit moving in people and moving through people. The Holy Spirit leads people to keep moving.

For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man: but holy men of God spake as THEY WERE MOVED by the Holy Ghost.

2 Peter 1:21

The Holy Spirit gets men to move because He is always moving. When the Spirit comes upon you there will be movement. There will be movement in the Spirit! Movement in your heart! Movement in your mind! Movement in your body! Movement in your life! Movement in your ministry!

Everything God has created is moving! That is a great mystery and reveals God’s plans and purposes. The planets are moving at incredible speeds. Since we were born, we have never stopped moving. As I write this book, I am moving at an incredulous speed of 107,000 km/h. That’s faster than any jet in this world. That is the speed with which the earth is moving around the sun.

The Animals are Moving

Everything God has created is moving. That is a great mystery and reveals God’s plans and purposes.

It is a great mystery that animals constantly move, covering great distances. Migrating animals decide where to go and in which direction to fly. Some animals travel with the help of the sea currents.

The reindeer find their direction with the help of the sun’s rays, whilst birds follow the coastline. Animals move to escape harsh weather, to search for food and to reach their breeding grounds.

Here is a list of some animals and their amazing migrations:

13THE WILDEBEEST: These make a yearly round trip that spans hundreds of miles and two countries, Tanzania and Kenya. The herds move in a great column between the two countries.

THE EEL: Eels spend their lives in rivers. They travel from the river to the sea to lay eggs. When the eggs hatch, the parent eels die and the young ones travel back to the river from where the parents had started their journey.

THE MONARCH BUTTERFLY: The Monarch Butterfly flies 4,800 km from Canada to Mexico. Each year, millions of monarch butterflies leave the northern ranges and fly south to the Oyamel Fir Forests near the Sierra Madre mountains, where they gather in huge roosts to survive the winter.

THE ARCTIC TERN: The Arctic Tern travels a distance of nearly 35,000 km between the Arctic and the Antarctic twice a year. These small birds fly from pole to pole every year. It is the longest bird migration.

THE EUROPEAN STORK: The European Stork travels from Europe to Africa every winter. When spring comes, it returns to Europe to the same nest.

THE SIBERIAN CRANE: The Siberian Cranes nest in Western Siberia and migrate to India. Their migration route stretches to about 6,450km.

THE HUMPBACK WHALE: These giants spend their summers at feeding grounds in cold, nutrient-rich waters that support an abundance of krill and small fish. In the winter, they migrate to warmer waters to raise their calves and avoid predation by killer whales. It is a journey that can take over 8,000 kilometres each way, making it the longest migration of any mammal on earth. Humpback whales are slow swimmers, but they make up for it by travelling non-stop for days at a time.

SALMON: Salmon spend most of their lives in the Atlantic and Pacific oceans, where they feed and grow before migrating back to the rivers where they were born. Salmon swim across the ocean to the mouth of the river. To reach their final destination, the salmon must swim up the river, in an event known as “the salmon run”. In an incredible feat of endurance, they swim up to 400 kilometres (250 miles) against the current, battling rapids and leaping up waterfalls, all the while avoiding predators that congregate along the banks in hopes of catching a nutritious meal. When they finally reach their birthplace, the salmon produce and deposit eggs in large numbers and then die.13

Learn from this great mystery of movement! God will keep you alive and well just by this mystery of movement. Our unchanging God has created a world of continuous movement within a stable, orderly and unchanging structure. What an amazing design! Movement was created for survival and continuity! Keep moving!
