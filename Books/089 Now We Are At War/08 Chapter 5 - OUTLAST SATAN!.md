### Chapter 5\

OUTLAST SATAN!\
------------------------------------------

\

##### Joshua made war A LONG TIME with all those kings.

##### Joshua 11:18

\

Outlast satan’s war by fighting endlessly and relentlessly. It is important for the enemy to know what kind of person he is about to engage with. He will think twice before attacking again, knowing that you are an inflexible, adamant, unrelenting and implacable kind of person. Your enemy will discover, in his dealings with you, that it is not everyone that you should touch or attack.

Waging a long merciless, inflexible, adamant, unrelenting and obstinate war is an important spiritual response to the enemy. Many enemies do not expect a long war when they attack you. However, you must respond by attacking and retaliating with a long, debilitating and unbearable war. As your enemy continues to engage with your unrelenting posture, his energy and strength will be sapped and drained away by the long and merciless war.

As Winston Churchill said, “Now we are at war, and we are going to make war, and persevere in making war until the other side has had enough of it.”

A long war is an important response to a particularly wicked enemy. Some enemies need a long, uncompromising war. Nothing short-lived will help to end the conflict and quench the evil fires of satan.

Wickedness must be met with an equally resistant and opposite response. When a very evil enemy like Adolf Hitler came against England, the only response was to be determined to fight him as long as it would take. And it took 6 years to wipe out Adolf Hitler! 6 whole years!

There was a war between Israel and the Arabs in 1967 that lasted only 6 days. But the war against Hitler lasted 6 long years! Some enemies need a long war for their annihilation!

Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

James 4:7

You must offer satan a very stiff and unwavering resistance because it is a direct command in the word of God to resist the devil. Stiff, persistent resistance to demonic onslaught is a sure divine way of responding to the devil’s onslaughts. Resist until divine settlement comes! That is the way to go.

Respond to your attacks properly with a long war. A response to a devastating attack is to enter into a long war with your enemy. Long wars are signs of good resistance! God expects you to give a long drawn out, strong and determined resistance to the devil and all his temptations.

You enter a long war by deciding to fight for a long time. Deciding to fight for a long time changes everything. Your plans and strategies will be different as you ready yourself to be at war for years on end.

You must determine that your enemy will be tired before you are tired! He must see your determined responses which have signs of energy and long-term zeal.

Satan will soon drop you as a coal of fire and be glad to see you go. Resist steadfast in the faith!

Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour: WHOM RESIST STEDFAST IN THE FAITH, knowing that the same afflictions are accomplished in your brethren that are in the world. But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after that ye have suffered a while, make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you.

1 Peter 5:8-10
