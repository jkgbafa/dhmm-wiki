### Chapter 19\

MAINTAIN SUPERIOR INTELLIGENCE\
----------------------------------------------------------

\

##### For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.

##### Romans 8:14

Becoming a son of God is becoming mightier than a mere man.

You can maintain superiority over satan by having a superior intelligence network. The Holy Spirit is the One who will give you advanced knowledge and superiority over satan. God knows everything. If you can follow the leading of the Holy Spirit, you will be like someone with the highest level of military intelligence.

The key to superior intelligence and knowledge is the Holy Spirit’s leading and guidance. Through the Holy Spirit, you receive critical words of wisdom, revelation and knowledge. Following the Spirit is the key to having the upper hand and defeating the enemy.

Thus saith the Lord, thy Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel; I am the Lord thy God which teacheth thee to profit, which leadeth thee by the way that thou shouldest go. O that thou hadst hearkened to my commandments! then had thy peace been as a river, and thy righteousness as the waves of the sea: Thy seed also had been as the sand, and the offspring of thy bowels like the gravel thereof; his name should not have been cut off nor destroyed from before me. And they thirsted not when he led them through the deserts: he caused the waters to flow out of the rock for them: he clave the rock also, and the waters gushed out.

Isaiah 48:17-19, 21

A LESSON FROM HISTORY

Military intelligence is the collection of information of military or political value for your war. You can develop a calm overwhelming superiority over the enemy through a highly developed intelligence network which gives and provides critical information.

Defeat the enemy using superior intelligence that keeps you one step ahead. Military intelligence makes your army superhuman. Having accurate, timely, useful intelligence and information is the key to defeating an enemy.

Israel’s Mossad is known as a formidable intelligence agency because of its intelligence gathering abilities. This makes Israel a frightening enemy and gives them the upper hand in their wars.

Military Intelligence

1\. A good military intelligence network means you will know your enemy’s capabilities.

2\. A good military intelligence network lets you know about your enemy’s intentions.

3\. A good military intelligence network makes you properly estimate your own strength.

4\. A good military intelligence network lets you understand how effective your current efforts and systems are.

5\. Good military intelligence will help you to prepare for attacks.

6\. Good military intelligence will help you estimate how good your responses will be in the event of war.

7\. Good military intelligence helps prevent losses of life and territory.

The Holy Spirit’s Intelligence

It is interesting that all the above functions of a good, secular military intelligence and spy network are perfectly done by the Holy Spirit.

1\. The Holy Spirit’s intelligence and guidance means you will know your enemy’s capabilities. You will learn what satan is capable of doing to you if you allow him to.

2\. The Holy Spirit’s intelligence lets you know about your enemy’s intentions. Through the Holy Spirit, you will know what demons are planning to do to you.

3\. The Holy Spirit’s intelligence and guidance make you properly estimate your own strength. The Holy Spirit will not allow you to have a wrong impression of your own strengths and weaknesses.

4\. The Holy Spirit’s intelligence lets you understand how effective your current efforts and systems are.

5\. The Holy Spirit’s intelligence will help you prepare for attacks.

6\. The Holy Spirit’s intelligence will help you estimate how good your responses will be in the event of war.

7\. The Holy Spirit’s intelligence helps prevent loss of life and territory.
