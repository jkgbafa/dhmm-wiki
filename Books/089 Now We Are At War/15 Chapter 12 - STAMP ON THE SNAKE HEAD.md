### Chapter 12\

STAMP ON THE SNAKE HEAD\
---------------------------------------------------

\

##### And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel.

##### Genesis 3:15

Stamping on the head of the snake is needed to get an ultimate victory over our evil enemy. Prophetically, you will stamp on the head of the snake by using women to fight. There is a woman who will help you by stamping on the head of a snake in your life. Stamp on the head of the snake by training women who will be used to destroy the works of the devil.

Look for the woman who will do it! There is a woman whom God will use in your ministry. Look for her!

It is a known fact that Adam was destroyed because he listened to the voice of his wife. Sin and destruction entered into the world through the woman’s influence.

And unto Adam he said, BECAUSE THOU HAST HEARKENED UNTO THE VOICE OF THY WIFE, and hast eaten of the tree, of which I commanded thee, saying, Thou shalt not eat of it: cursed is the ground for thy sake; in sorrow shalt thou eat of it all the days of thy life;

Genesis 3:17

Every evil including sickness, disease, accidents, tragedies, coffins, cemeteries, mortuaries, weeping, sorrow and pain in this world can be attributed to this one mistake that Adam made in listening to his wife. It is therefore understandable that some people would have a natural aversion to women and think of them only as a source of trouble.

But don’t be put off by these facts. A woman will really become a blessing in your ministry. Women have the spirit of the helper which is the grace of the mighty Holy Ghost.

And the LORD God said, It is not good that the man should be alone; I will make him AN HELP MEET for him.

Genesis 2:18

A Woman Will Kill the Snake

God has given us a prophetic word that the snake will ultimately be crushed through the woman. Because of this prophecy, every spiritual man must be on the lookout for the woman or women who will be used by the Lord to destroy the devils in his life.

How to Receive the Woman to Destroy the Snake

1\. Do not see women only as a source of sin. A woman can be a blessing and a source of help too.

2\. Do not eliminate all women from your life. Some people remove every trace of a woman from their lives, seeing it as a safety measure.

3\. Do not assume that a woman cannot do certain jobs. Women can do almost every job. History shows that women have done almost everything that men have ever done.

4\. Study Jesus’ relationship with women and see if you can be like Jesus Christ in relation to women.

And it came to pass afterward, that he went throughout every city and village, preaching and shewing the glad tidings of the kingdom of God: and the twelve were with him, And certain women, which had been healed of evil spirits and infirmities, Mary called Magdalene, out of whom went seven devils, And Joanna the wife of Chuza Herod’s steward, and Susanna, and many others, which ministered unto him of their substance.

Luke 8:1-3

5\. Be on the lookout for the woman or women who will be used by the Lord to give you an ultimate and profound victory over your enemy. She is around somewhere! She will come and make a big difference in your life and ministry.

6\. Work with the mysterious women whom God sends to your life by finding a way to deploy them to the location of the serpent in your life and ministry. Release them to fight with the snake directly. The prophetic power of the women will be used to fight, bruise, overwhelm, break, snap and strike at the head of the serpent. Then sit back, relax and enjoy the defeat of that old serpent in your life.

And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; IT SHALL BRUISE THY HEAD and thou shalt bruise his heel.

Genesis 3:15

7\. Deploy women in every possible area of ministry until you have them positioned everywhere. Mysteriously women will be used in the books’ ministry, the building ministry, the music ministry, the anti-lust ministry, the anti-fornication ministry, the comfort ministry, and the general helps ministry.

A Lesson From History

Women were released to help bring victory in wartime. Women have been used to help clinch many victories in war. Women have played many varied roles in the military and in war. Allow the women to help bring in the victory. Let us see how varied the roles of women were in the war effort!

Women in War

1\. Women worked as nurses during the war.

2\. Women tended to the injured and worked as volunteer nurses with little or no medical equipment.

3\. Women assisted with amputations and surgeries.

4\. Women assisted by sitting with the dying in their final moments.

5\. Women nurses entered the arena of war to rescue injured soldiers and get them to a field hospital.

6\. Women drove ambulances.

7\. Women disguised themselves as men and enlisted to go to the frontline.

8\. Women worked at the railways, as police officers, as factory workers and as munitions operatives.

9\. Women supported the war effort by knitting soldiers’ uniforms.

10.Women raised money for charities such as the newly formed Red Cross.

11\. Women worked as spies, special agents, uniformed officers, and as administrative, technical and professional personnel of the Secret Service.

12\. Women were code breakers, decrypting secret messages.

13\. Women worked in clerical jobs during the World Wars.

14\. Women drove vehicles, repaired aeroplanes, worked in laboratories and cryptology, served as radio and telephone operators, rigged parachutes, test-flew planes and even trained their male counterparts in air combat tactics.

15\. Women bore arms and charged into battle, too. Like the men, there were women who lived in camps, suffered in prisons, and died for their respective causes.

Allow the women to bring victory according to the prophetic word of the Lord. God is working mysteriously through women to stamp on the head of the snake and finish him off permanently!
