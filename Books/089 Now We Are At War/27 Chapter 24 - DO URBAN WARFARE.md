### Chapter 24\

## DO URBAN WARFARE

\

##### And how I kept back nothing that was profitable unto you, but have shewed you, and have taught you publicly, AND FROM HOUSE TO HOUSE , Testifying both to the Jews, and also to the Greeks, repentance toward God, and faith toward our Lord Jesus Christ.

##### Acts 20:20-21

\

Urban warfare is the art of fighting from house to house, from door to door and from person to person. Winning the war in the realm of the spirit also requires a door to door and a house to house approach. The military call this kind of fighting, urban warfare. In this type of battle, you cannot just send bombs or missiles against the enemy. You must go on the ground and move from house to house and from person to person on the streets.

Day by day continuing with one mind in the temple, and breaking bread FROM HOUSE TO HOUSE they were taking their meals together with gladness and sincerity of heart

Acts 2:46 (NASB)

Many ministers of the gospel have forgotten the basics of ministry. The basics of ministry include achieving victory from house to house.

Indeed, many churches were actually churches in people’s houses. Today many see ministry as a pulpit ministry where there is a large crowd out there listening to you. But real ministry is often from house to house - urban warfare.

And every day, in the temple and FROM HOUSE TO HOUSE, they did not stop teaching and preaching the good news of Jesus as the Christ.

Acts 5:42 (NASB)

The first time I led someone to Christ was in an urban warfare situation. The first time I prayed for someone to be filled with the Spirit was an urban warfare situation. The first time I saw someone fall under the power of the Spirit was in someone’s house.

It is time to see that war is not just fighting by sending missiles from a remote location. Prayer is good! But there comes a time that you must go from house to house. Fasting is good but there comes a time when you must go ministering from door-to-door. Television and YouTube ministry are good but there comes a time when you must go out on the streets and move from house to house. Praying, sending money and sending gifts are good. But there comes a time when you must send missionaries on the ground to win the nations!

Urban warfare is the most vicious form of fighting. That is the kind of warfare that was done in the Gaza war and also in Stalingrad. Street by street! House by house! Room by room!

Even Saul knew that he had to go from house to house to root out the Christians. Saul was into urban warfare before he got saved. He continued in urban warfare after he got saved.

As for Saul, he made havock of the church, ENTERING INTO EVERY HOUSE, and haling men and women committed them to prison. Therefore they that were scattered abroad went every where preaching the word.

Acts 8:3-4

Proud pastors who are doomed to fail in ministry will not go from door to door, from house to house, and from person to person. There comes a time when urban warfare is the only way to advance with the war of ministry. Urban warfare is house to house fighting for Jesus Christ!

A LESSON FROM HISTORY

1\. Urban warfare is street fighting!

The American army call urban warfare FIBUA; “ Fighting in a Built-Up Area”.

The British army calls urban warfare FISH; “ Fighting in Someone’s House”.

2\. Urban warfare is so important that entire cities are built for training in urban warfare. Israel has built a facility called the urban warfare training centre with over 600 buildings including eight storey buildings!

Training for urban warfare is done in the USA and in the United Kingdom too. Entire cities are built specially for this kind of training.

3\. Urban warfare is warfare in urban areas such as towns and cities. Urban combat differs from combat in the open at both the operational and the tactical levels. Complicating factors in urban warfare include the presence of civilians and the complexity of the urban terrain.

4\. Urban warfare, therefore, is high-intensity warfare which requires the command of small, flexible units. The difficult terrain, narrow streets, lack of open spaces, and high-rise buildings expose soldiers to ambushes and make evacuation of the wounded more difficult.

Famous urban warfare battles

1\. The Battle of Hue, Vietnam:

16The 1968 Battle of Hue stands as a stark representation of how tactical manoeuvers can have profound strategic implications in urban warfare. As Vietnam’s third-largest city, Hue witnessed 26 days of intense fighting among its 140,000 civilians against a combined force of roughly 7,500 from the North Vietnamese Army and Viet Cong. While the US Army eventually regained control, the extensive devastation left about 80% of the city in ruins and uprooted an estimated 116,000 inhabitants.

2\. The first Chechen war, Grozny:

During the First Chechen War that began in 1994, the Russian Federation sought to reclaim the breakaway Republic of Chechnya. The urban combat that unfolded in this conflict was intense, with both sides employing distinct strategies. Chechen fighters, usually grouped in squads of about 25 men, were predominantly equipped with hand-held anti-tank weapons and precision firearms. They set up three defence lines in the city around fortified posts. Underground tunnels and basements played a pivotal role in their manoeuvers, providing shelter from Russian artillery and air raids.

3\. Fallujah, Iraq:

After the U.S. invasion of Iraq, one city would become known to Americans as the epicentre of the war - Fallujah. Vast areas of the city were levelled in two huge battles between insurgents and U.S. troops. The city’s intricate network of streets, structures, defences, and its civilian populace made for a challenging urban battlefield. Although the Coalition excelled in open-field combat, they faced increased difficulties in Fallujah’s urban confines.16

4\. The Battle of Stalingrad:

In World War II, from August 1942 into February 1943, German and Soviet forces fought in, and around Stalingrad for the city. It was a sad, hard, house to house battle for this city. Thousands died in this terrible battle.

5\. The Battle for Berlin:

Urban warfare was fought from February to early May 1945. In this period there was fierce fighting in and around Berlin to capture the city.

6\. The Israel-Hamas war in Gaza:

Bombs and missiles could not win the war in Gaza. It was a good example of urban warfare. Without going from house to house, the Israelis could have no hope of retrieving their hostages.
