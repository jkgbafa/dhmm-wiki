### Chapter 21\

PREPARE ESCAPE ROUTES\
-------------------------------------------------

\

##### There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but GOD IS FAITHFUL, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able; but will with the temptation also MAKE A WAY TO ESCAPE that ye may be able to bear it.

##### 1 Corinthians 10:13

\

There is a clear escape plan for you in the realm of the Spirit. God has an alternative route that you should know about. God has an alternative way that you can use to escape to safety.

You may never have intended to use a spare tyre, but it is a blessing that a spare tyre exists and that you can use it when the need arises.

You may not have intended to use a parachute, but you must use it if the need arises. I believe fighter pilots are happier to come back to the ground on a parachute than to die in a plane crash.

You will be happy that lifeboats exist in the hour that you need one. If the owners of the Titanic had had more lifeboats for their passengers, they would have at least saved more lives. I know the passengers of the Titanic never intended to be out on the icy sea in a small lifeboat, but that alternative was a way of escape from a far greater evil.

Believe in the idea of lifeboats! God has an alternative that will help you escape from satan’s traps and schemes.

Predetermined escapes are the strategy of choice in battling surprise attacks. What are the expected strikes of the enemy? All ministers can expect an attack of pride, lust and deception!

Previously prepared escape routes are important. Take a step in the opposite direction of all these evils and be determined to stay as far from these things as possible.

Do not be afraid of alternative routes, ways and methods God gives you. God creates ways of escape from sticky situations. You must discover the ways God has created for you to overcome and escape.

Expect your enemy to attack you with every kind of wicked strategy. Encirclement! Sieges! Flank attacks! Counter attacks! Expect them all! But prepare for yourself an escape plan!

An escape plan is a good plan to have because you will defeat the enemy by escaping with all your troops and weapons and be ready to fight again another day!

Many divine escapes are given by the Lord, and a good general needs to prepare and create escape routes ready to be used in the event of encirclement or entrapment.

Overcome and break out of a siege by using your well-prepared escape plan. Have a well-thought-through escape plan which can be used to the amazement of your enemy who has besieged and encircled you. God is in the business of creating divine escapes and alternative routes!

Satan is always seeking to trap you and to prevent your escape. Satan sought to trap Jesus on earth. Satan sought to entice Jesus with security, peace, stability and a good earthly life.

Jesus saw through all that!

Jesus was never trapped by satan!

A LESSON FROM HISTORY

The Escape of the British Army from France

After Germany invaded Poland in September of 1939, Britain sent part of its army to France to help fight against Germany. However, they did not accomplish much and were driven out of Europe.

The British armies ended up being trapped in the coastal town of Dunkirk and needed to cross the British Channel to get to England. There were not enough boats and there was not enough time to get this large army out of Dunkirk and across the sea. The Germans were closing in and threatening to wipe them out in Dunkirk.

As the mighty, well-prepared armies of Germany closed in on the British forces, the British Royal Navy organized a seaborne escape for them. An improvised assortment of ships and small vessels were mobilized to evacuate the surrounded British armies. Various ferries, private little boats, tugboats, yachts, and an assortment of “little ships” were dispatched to Dunkirk’s beaches to collect British troops and transfer men to bigger ships for the voyage to England.

Whilst the evacuation was being carried out, the Germans bombarded the ships from the air and sea, trying to prevent their escape. The vessels had to contend with attacks by German S-boats, dive-bombers, and artillery.

More than 330,000 stranded troops escaped from France; only a skeleton force was left behind to man the perimeter of Dunkirk. What an escape that was!

On 6 June 1944 (D-Day), these same British armies who escaped from Dunkirk, came back to re-invade France on their way to defeat the German armies. By escaping, the British forces were able to prepare themselves and come back to liberate Europe from Hitler’s grip.

Escaping from the enemy will always be important so that you can fight again another day.
