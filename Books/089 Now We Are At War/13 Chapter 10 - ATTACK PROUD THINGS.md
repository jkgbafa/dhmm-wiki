### Chapter 10\

ATTACK PROUD THINGS\
-----------------------------------------------

\

##### CASTING DOWN imaginations, and EVERY HIGH THING THAT EXALTETH ITSELF against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ;

##### 2 Corinthians 10:5

Those who are proud are going to be a source of trouble for you. Because of this, every good commander must work to attack pride wherever it can be found!

The main cause of most wars is pride. Wars are caused by men seeking revenge because of humiliation from an earlier defeat. World War II is an example of this kind of war. World War II happened because Germany felt it had been badly treated and humiliated at the Treaty of Versailles where a peace accord was signed about the First World War.

Proud and offended people in your midst are a spiritual emergency. These proud people are not aware that they will soon be used by the devil to wreak havoc in the church.

God Attacks Proud Things

Cut proud people down to size by not allowing them to flourish! Nebuchadnezzar, the mighty king of Babylon, ruled with unmatched power and authority. Arrogance and pride came to characterise the life of this king. Nebuchadnezzar’s pride grew until he constantly boasted about his own glory and his great achievements. Nebuchadnezzar had come to believe that he was a self-made man.

At the end of twelve months he walked in the palace of the kingdom of Babylon. The king spake, and said, Is not this great Babylon, that I have built for the house of the kingdom by the might of my power, and for the honour of my majesty?

Daniel 4:29-30

God responded to Nebuchadnezzar’s arrogance by humbling him. God will always attack pride. Nebuchadnezzar was cast out of the palace and deprived of his sanity and authority. Nebuchadnezzar was forced to live like a beast in the field, eating grass until he was humble enough to acknowledge God’s power.

One of the things to do in war is to attack proud things. There is a proverb that states; “When the bird stays too long on the tree, it invites a stone”. Some birds have stayed too long in certain positions. They need to be thrown down from those lofty positions.

War consists in attacking high and lofty things and cutting them down to size. Cast down high things! Directly attack pride in your midst! Unseat and humiliate the enemy. Pull down high and lofty things in your midst. Attack pride in your team.

Every evil has one source and it is pride. Pride is the reason for many wars. Pride makes people disloyal and treacherous and that is why you should attack pride wherever you find it.

Many people become proud because of positions of authority given to them. Removing these positions of authority will humiliate and lower the enemy.

Demote satan’s agents in your midst! Dismiss them! Lower them! Take away their positions! Take away their honour and take away their place.

The dismissal of a person is an act of war that silences satan. In many countries, the law states that a person can, without reason, give one month’s notice and terminate his employment within an organisation. Similarly, an organisation has the right to terminate the employment of its employee without giving any reason and by giving one month’s notice. It is important to use these basic human resource principles to terminate the jobs of undesirable and proud personalities in your organisation.

You can also demote people and take away the honour and power you have earlier given them. Why should you elevate your enemy so that he uses that position to attack and harass you?

Enemy-humiliating attacks are important. Anyone who attacks you does so because of pride. Proud people have the feeling that they can bring down the church! Proud people feel they can destroy a man of God.

Cast abroad the rage of thy wrath: AND BEHOLD EVERYONE THAT IS PROUD, AND ABASE HIM. LOOK ON EVERYONE THAT IS PROUD, AND BRING HIM LOW; AND TREAD DOWN THE WICKED IN THEIR PLACE. Hide them in the dust together; and bind their faces in secret. Then will I also confess unto thee that thine own right hand can save thee.

Job 40:11-14

Bring down the proud-speaking personalities by lowering and demoting them.

Humiliate satanic personalities in your midst by casting them down.

Lower proud people!

Demote and reduce proud people!

Demobilise the proud group!

Dislocate proud and high people!

Transfer the proud away from their place of pride!

Remove the privileges of the proud!

Relocate the proud!

Change the jobs of the proud!

Pull down high things, attack, demote, destroy and reduce proud things in your midst.

Cut proud people down to size by not allowing them to flourish.

Declare war on high things and proud things!

How to Attack Pride

1\. Demote proud people at their workplace, if you are their employer.

2\. Take away privileges that a proud person enjoys. Distribute the proud person’s privileges to others to remove any form of delusions and deceptions. Do not allow a proud person to have any form of exclusivity.

3\. Take away the titles that a proud person enjoys. Titles just reinforce the delusions of a proud person.

4\. Give proud people menial jobs to do. This will fight the innate pride in them. Menial jobs expose people who are unsuitable for ministry.

5\. Ignore proud people. Fight pride by simply not acknowledging proud people any longer in the way you have done in the past. Do not even mention the proud person’s name at meetings.

6\. Do not be associated with the proud person. Do not sit on stage with a proud person. Sitting by them or with them is an honour which you must deny them.

7\. Dismiss proud people from employment. Lay off proud people who criticise your leadership style. Let them get employment somewhere else. Let them go somewhere else and prosper away from your presence.

8\. Do not celebrate anything about the proud person in your midst.

9\. Do not give a proud person any important office job or duty any longer.

10\. Remove proud people’s pictures from any place of prominence.
