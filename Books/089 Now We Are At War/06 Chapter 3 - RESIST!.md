### Chapter 3\

\

RESIST!\
-----------------------------------

\

Submit yourselves therefore to God. RESIST THE DEVIL, and he will flee from you.

James 4:7

RESIST! Resist the devil! Fight back! Don’t just lie down and let the enemy walk all over you!

Be an enemy never to be forgotten!

A dreaded enemy is one who resists attacks and fights back directly and relentlessly.

Become someone whom the enemy knows will resist attacks and invasions.

And when the tempter came to him, he said, If thou be the Son of God, command that these stones be made bread. But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God….

Jesus said unto him, It is written again, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God. Again, the devil taketh him up into an exceeding high mountain, and sheweth him all the kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them; And saith unto him, All these things will I give thee, if thou wilt fall down and worship me. Then saith Jesus unto him, Get thee hence, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve. Then the devil leaveth him, and, behold, angels came and ministered unto him.

Matthew 4:3-4, 7-11

You can resist satan by developing a stubborn opposition to nonsense. You can resist a determined attacker by repelling him each time he attacks.

You can resist a determined and repeated attacker by giving equally determined and repeated sharp rebukes without any negotiations whatsoever.

1\. Resist: Do not give up any ground

Jesus did not chat with satan or discuss anything with satan. A meeting, a discussion, a negotiation, or an explanation is a sign that you understand and accept the nonsense expounded by a foolish enemy.

Adam discussed matters with Eve and Eve discussed issues with the serpent and where did that end us? The discussions of Adam and Eve put the whole world in darkness, death and curses!

The second Adam, who is Jesus Christ, quoted one scripture and dismissed satan outright. Do not allow an irrational enemy to speak or express his views or opinions. Do not have discussions with satan.

Administer a profound and ultimate rejection of satan by repeatedly saying “No” to any and every suggestion he has. Resist every suggestion just because the suggestion comes from the devil!

2\. Resist: Become a dreaded enemy

A dreaded enemy is one who is not restrained by the love of money or the love of anything else.

Have no regard for silver and gold and you will be a formidable enemy. You cannot truly prosecute a war and also love money. Prosecuting a war requires love and a passion for the purpose of the war. Having any other love will lead to the weakening of the hand of a good general.

A frightening enemy does not mind or consider things that an ordinary person would consider. An enemy who considers embarrassment is weak. It means his pride and self-respect are so important to him that he is far less aggressive in a fight than he ought to be.

BEHOLD, I WILL STIR UP THE MEDES AGAINST THEM, WHICH SHALL NOT REGARD SILVER; AND AS FOR GOLD, THEY SHALL NOT DELIGHT IN IT. Their bows also shall dash the young men to pieces, AND THEY SHALL HAVE NO PITY ON THE FRUIT OF THE WOMB; THEIR EYE SHALL NOT SPARE CHILDREN. And Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, the beauty of the Chaldees’ excellency, shall be as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah. It shall never be inhabited, neither shall it be dwelt in from generation to generation: neither shall the Arabian pitch tent there; neither shall the shepherds make their fold there. But wild beasts of the desert shall lie there, and their houses shall be full of doleful creatures; and owls shall dwell there, and satyrs shall dance there. And the wild beasts of the islands shall cry in their desolate houses, and dragons in their pleasant palaces: and her time is near to come, and her days shall not be prolonged.

Isaiah 13:17-22

God promised to punish the nation of Israel by sending them an unusual enemy - the Medes. These people were unlike any other enemy the children of God had encountered. They were a terrifying group of people to have as enemies. They had no desire for reward or money. That was unusual. They had no love or pity even for children.

3\. Resist: Become a frightening enemy

The Medes were indeed a frightening enemy. It was God’s punishment to the Jews to send such people to fight them. The Medes had no desire for money and they had no compassion or pity in their eyes. Their eyes were focused on their goal of wiping out their enemies.

Fight your enemy by being focussed on their destruction. Have no distractions! Have no visions other than to destroy the enemy!

Your enemy will never forget how you concentrated on his destruction. Let them never forget you! Let them have an experience that will make them think twice about attacking you again. Wage a long and inflexible war with the enemy.

Be a formidable enemy to satan! Have no love or desire for worldly things. Have no desire for more money. Have no pity or benevolence for wickedness.

I receive not honour from men.

John 5:41

Jesus was a formidable enemy to the Jewish Sanhedrin. He feared no one! Nothing restrained Him when He preached and taught the word of God. Jesus was unstoppable because He did not seek respect nor did He seek to be respectable.

4\. Resist: Don’t fear anything or anyone

Your fear of people and your fear of the media or any public outcry against yourself is something the enemy will use against you.

Today, Israel is a very resistant and formidable enemy. A lot of media pressure is put out against Israel to restrain them. However, the murder of 6 million Jews in the Second World War has made them a resistant nation that is largely concerned with securing themselves and nothing else. They have become a resistant and formidable enemy, not needing to impress any outsider.

Every home in Israel today has someone whose relative was murdered by the Germans during the Holocaust.

The nation of Israel has learned that securing its land and being a formidable and frightening enemy is more important than securing the respect of certain people.

Make the enemy regret ever coming against you. Be a resistant, formidable, fearless and relentless enemy. Others who are planning things against you will think twice before attacking you when they see how you fight. Destroy the enemy’s confidence in creating trouble for you.

5\. Resist: Promise the enemy an endless fight

And Moab was sore afraid of the people, because they were many: and Moab was distressed because of the children of Israel. And Moab said unto the elders of Midian, Now shall this company lick up all that are round about us, as the ox licketh up the grass of the field. And Balak the son of Zippor was king of the Moabites at that time.

Numbers 22:3-4

Instill fear in your enemies’ hearts to prevent future attacks. Become a resistant and formidable enemy. Become a kind of enemy that never surrenders.

6\. Resist: Fighting you will be a kind of self-destruction

Become the kind of enemy that resists profoundly until the end and one that causes great pain to the offending or invading force such that they never think of invading you again. That is the kind of enemy you must be.

“And all the kings of the Amorites came and took their stand in the field to consult with their counsellors what was to be done with the sons of Jacob, for they were still afraid of them, saying, Behold, two of them slew the whole of the city of Shechem.

And the Lord heard the prayers of Isaac and Jacob, and he filled the hearts of all these kings’ advisers with great fear and terror that they unanimously exclaimed,

ARE YOU SILLY THIS DAY, OR IS THERE NO UNDERSTANDING IN YOU, THAT YOU WILL FIGHT WITH THE HEBREWS, AND WHY WILL YOU TAKE A DELIGHT IN YOUR OWN DESTRUCTION THIS DAY?

Behold two of them came to the city of Shechem without fear or terror, and they killed all the inhabitants of the city, that no man stood up against them, and how will you be able to fight with them all?

Surely you know that their God is exceedingly fond of them, and has done mighty things for them, such as have not been done from days of old, and amongst all the gods of nations, there is none can do like unto his mighty deeds.

Surely he delivered their father Abraham, the Hebrew, from the hand of Nimrod, and from the hand of all his people who had many times sought to slay him.

He delivered him also from the fire in which King Nimrod had cast him, and his God delivered him from it.

And who else can do the like? surely it was Abraham who slew the five kings of Elam, when they had touched his brother’s son who in those days dwelt in Sodom.

Moab was distressed at the idea of fighting with Israel. The Israelites were not nice enemies to have.

The Amorites also feared to attack Jacob. Notice how they fearfully discussed the possibility of war with Israel. They felt that fighting with Israel was a form of self-destruction.

7\. Resist: A warning from history

Japan was a formidable and resistant enemy. The Second World War lasted from 1939 to 1945. Japan entered the Second World War on the day they attacked Pearl Harbour on December 7, 1941.

During the Second World War, Germany fought against America, Russia and Britain in Europe. However, that part of the war ended on May 7, 1945. Even though Germany was defeated in Europe, Japan continued fighting against America in the Pacific islands.

The Japanese were an unusually resistant and brutal enemy. By the end of the Second World War, they showed the world that they were a unique and terrifying enemy. No one wanted to fight with the Japanese. Even though the Americans would eventually win the war, it cost them a lot to fight with the Japanese. It was very painful to fight against the resistant Japanese soldiers.

America knew that the only way to defeat Japan was to invade the Japanese mainland itself and defeat the Japanese on their own soil in Tokyo. To invade the Japanese mainland however, America needed to defeat the Japanese on two strategic islands. These two islands were the island of Iwo Jima and the island of Okinawa. These islands were like stepping stones to the mainland of Japan. If the Americans could capture these islands, they would be able to use them as launching pads against the Japanese mainland itself.

The Resistance of Iwo Jima

Iwo Jima was a small island measuring only about eight miles wide in between Saipan, Guam and Tokyo. There were no civilians on Iwo Jima. Iwo Jima was important because it had airfields that the Americans could use to launch fighter aircrafts against mainland Japan. These fighter escorts were important to protect the long-range bombers which were flying from Saipan and Guam to Tokyo to drop bombs on them.

In February of 1945, America invaded the small island of Iwo Jima thinking that it would be a quick victory. However, it did not turn out to be so. They met a formidable, resistant enemy that had no intention of ever surrendering the island. The Japanese had the intention of fighting till every single person was dead. Within two days 3500 marines were killed by the Japanese enemy whose only objective was to make the Americans pay in time and blood for their invasion of Japanese lands.

America put 80,000 troops onto this tiny island. However, they met a resistant and brutal enemy who wanted to die fighting and would never surrender no matter what.

After five days of fighting America raised a flag on Mount Suribachi on Iwo Jima to signify victory. But it was a big mistake because the Japanese were not defeated at all.

The Japanese army had prepared to resist the invasion through caves, interconnected tunnels, pill boxes and various positions across the island causing the Americans to have to practically walk through a horror-filled minefield of fire and death.

The Japanese were under clear instructions which made them formidable and frightening enemies to fight with. These were some of their instructions to resist the invaders:

Under no circumstances will you surrender!

Do not take the shame of being captured!

You are not allowed to die until you have killed ten Americans!

These instructions meant that the Americans were fighting with resistant, ferocious and suicidal men. The Japanese would make the Americans pay very dearly for fighting for Iwo Jima. The Japanese would fight on until the bitter end, never seeing reason or the need to settle anything by discussion.

Anytime the Americans attacked a position of the Japanese, the Japanese would simply disappear into several miles of their interconnected tunnels and move to another part of the island. The Japanese became an invisible and frightening enemy to the Americans because they had created this honeycomb tunnel system throughout the entire island that connected Mount Suribachi in the south to the commander in the north. Iwo Jima turned out to be one of the most fortified islands in the world, well prepared for the invaders.

The Japanese were basically an underground enemy, and the Americans rarely saw any dead Japanese. When, finally, the Japanese ran out of water and ammunition, the commander of the Japanese force, General Kuribayashi radioed his situation and informed the imperial high command that he was about to make his final counterattack. A few days later 300 Japanese soldiers carried out a suicidal attack on the Americans and killed 100 Americans. All 300 Japanese died in this attack bringing the war for Iwo Jima to an end.

It took 36 days of brutal fighting to conquer this tiny island of Iwo Jima. Only 3,000 Japanese survived out of a total of 21,000. There were 28,000 American casualties in all. This was the first time that the American casualties outnumbered the Japanese casualties in a battle.
