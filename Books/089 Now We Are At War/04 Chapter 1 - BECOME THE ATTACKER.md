### Chapter 1\

BECOME THE ATTACKER\
-----------------------------------------------

\

##### Then the Lord spoke to Moses, saying, “BE HOSTILE TO THE MIDIANITES AND ATTACK THEM; FOR THEY HAVE BEEN HOSTILE TO YOU WITH THEIR TRICKS, with which they have deceived you in the matter of Peor and in the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of the leader of Midian, their sister who was killed on the day of the plague because of Peor.”

##### Numbers 25:16-18 (NASB)

I am writing this book to help you fight the devil and whatever opposes you. Instead of sitting down and defending yourself all the time, there is something more that you can do. You can launch attacks on the enemy. You can be the one to start an offensive against your enemy.

This book is about what to do that will constitute an attack on satan and all your adversaries. From today you are not going to sit down and let the enemy trick you.

Notice the scripture above; the enemies have been hostile to you with their tricks and have deceived you! Many enemies are indirect and diplomatic about their hostility. It is time to launch pre-emptive strikes and attacks on the enemy.

Why wait until you are attacked before doing something about satan and other evil spirits? Why wait until you are attacked before doing something about your haters? Why wait until you are attacked before doing something about your enemies? Attack your enemies now before they even plan what to do next.

After we had passed through Moab, the LORD told us, Now, start and cross the Arnon River. I am placing in your power Sihon, the Amorite king of Heshbon, along with his land. Attack him, and begin occupying his land.

Deuteronomy 2:24 (GNT)

You must launch formidable attacks on satan so that he will regret ever knowing you! You must attack first and let satan fall into the defending position. By attacking first, your enemy will be found on the wrong foot and be forced to defend himself incessantly against you. Your life is about to change as you accept this amazing role of an attacker of satanic strongholds.

As for Gad, a band of raiders shall attack him, But he will attack at their heels.

Genesis 49:19 (NASB)

It is time to turn the tables on the enemy! Your very defence against the enemy must be a fierce attack on him. He must regret ever drawing you into battle. The prophetic direction is for you to launch an attack on your haters and mockers! Your defence must always be a form of attack.

The dominant mood of war is one of danger, hesitancy, physical effort, spiritual effort, fear, the unpredictable and the unusual! You can attack the enemy and achieve success as well as safety amid this uncertainty.

You will need spirituality, physical energy, firmness, determination and strength of mind.

It is time to deliver a continuance of blows to the enemy! It is your turn to begin hostilities against the enemy!

Beginning from today, your enemy will discover that you are a painfully aggressive, hostile, forceful and spiritually violent enemy!
