### Chapter 6\

DE-HOUSE THE ENEMY\
----------------------------------------------

##### And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church; and THE GATES OF HELL shall not prevail against it.

##### Matthew 16:18

Hell is satan’s home! Everyone lives behind the gates of his house! Satan also lives behind the gates of hell. According to the scripture, as you build the church, the gates of hell will break down. Building the church of God breaks down the gates of hell!

Building the church is the same as breaking down satan’s gates and exposing his dwelling place. Satan is not happy to have his gates broken down. Satan is completely exposed by the breaking down of his gates and walls. Satan becomes unconcealed by the presence of a church which breaks down his gates!

Satan hates being uncovered, unsheltered and unprotected when you break down the gates of hell every time you build a church!

Therefore, building the church is your way of de-housing and stripping the devil of his covering; causing him to be discovered and laid bare! You can now break down satan’s dwelling places by building the church.

Satan also lives in specific places. The Bible tells us that satan lived in Pergamos.

I know thy works, and where thou dwellest, even where Satan’s seat is: and thou holdest fast my name, and hast not denied my faith, even in those days wherein Antipas was my faithful martyr, who was slain among you, WHERE SATAN DWELLETH.

Revelation 2:13

Every time a church is built, satan is unable to continue dwelling in that city in the same way.

The way to smash your way into satan’s house is to build churches. Jesus said, “I will build my church and the gates of hell will not prevail against it.”

Simply put, every time you build a church, the gates of hell suffer a strong bashing, a strong bombardment and a good battering. You must learn to attack satan by demolishing his very gates. You must understand the spiritual effect of the presence of a church.

This is why there are several countries where churches are banned completely, and church buildings are not allowed to be built.

Satan always attacks you. It is time to counter-strike. Push the enemy back with an attack of your own. Take new territories for the Lord by pushing hard and deep into the enemy’s territory and building churches there. Take lands and nations for God!

Counterstrike fast, deep and hard. Counterstrike by going deep into enemy territory and building the church in unexpected territories for the Lord. Strike deep, strike hard and strike fast with a strike that counters every strike against you. Build a church! Give a return strike against the rebel stronghold. Any attack of satan should be met with an equally devastating counterstrike.

Build a church! Building the church in many places is a very personal and direct attack on satan’s dwelling place. When you decide to build the church, you become like a “spiritual demolition man”.

Every single church you build is a bombardment on the personal gate of satan’s residence. Building the church is a de-housing campaign against satan.

A Lesson from History: Bomber Harris De-Houses the Germans

There was a marshal in the Royal Air Force, Sir Arthur Harris (Bomber Harris), who was the key architect of an area bombing policy against Germany that resulted in the destruction and flattening of German cities. This bombing campaign had one aim; to de-house the citizens of Germany. This meant that when the German workers finished work in their armament factories, they would have no homes to go to. They were effectively de-housed by the bombs! This de-housing campaign caused increasing distress and frustration for the German populace. All the cities were flattened and there were hardly any houses left standing. There was nowhere to stay after Bomber Harris had finished bombing the cities.

These bombing raids contributed greatly to the defeat of Germany. After the war, Bomber Harris received many honours for his work of de-housing the Germans. A statue of Bomber Harris was also unveiled in London.

The de-housing campaign of Bomber Harris best exemplifies this effective kind of “home destruction warfare”. Simply put, destroy and smash your enemy’s houses! Give him nowhere to rest or to dwell!

Flatten the cities, break down the homes and dwelling places of satan, the enemy. De-house your wicked enemy! Let him be displaced till he can fight no more.

It is interesting to read the words of this radical Air Marshal who initiated and directed a de-housing campaign against the German invaders who were causing so much havoc in the world during the Second World War.

Words of the De-Housing Campaigner Bomber Harris

Bomber Harris said:

1\. 5“The Nazis entered this war under the rather childish delusion that they were going to bomb everyone else, and nobody was going to bomb them. At Rotterdam, London, Warsaw, and half a hundred other places, they put their rather naïve theory into operation. They sowed the wind and now they are going to reap the whirlwind.”5

2\. 6“The purpose of bombing is to compel the enemy to give up, hit him where it hurts destroy homes and aspirations.”

3\. “I regard the destruction of cities as a wholly beneficial and deserving end.”

4\. “The destruction of cities, the killing of civilians, the reduction of every industrial enterprise to a struggling ruin, and the creation of a huge army of numbers mindlessly doing physical labour – all these prospects make me very happy.”6

5\. 7“The aim of the Combined Bomber Offensive…should be unambiguously stated as the destruction of German cities, the killing of German workers, and the disruption of civilised life in Germany.”7

6. 8“I want no nonsense about bombing civilians. I want all-out bombing. Bomb Berlin, bomb Hamburg, bomb Cologne, bomb all the other places in Germany whose names we do not know. And bomb them for all you’re worth.”

7\. “The code of the bomber is simple: destroy what is necessary, obliterate anything available, wreak havoc wherever possible, and leave the enemy struggling to survive in the rubble of their own making.”8

This de-housing campaign of Bomber Harris was instrumental in ending the Second World War. It was a very painful and horrendous assault on Germany in response to the devastating Second World War they had brought upon the world.

Christians must equally engage in a devastating campaign against satan for the evil he has brought upon this world. We must build the church of God everywhere.
