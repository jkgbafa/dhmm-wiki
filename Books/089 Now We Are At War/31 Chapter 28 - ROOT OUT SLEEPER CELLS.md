You must go from house to house rooting out sleeper cells of wickedness. You must become an expert raider of sleeper cells.

Raid sleeper cells by teaching against disloyalty. Do not ever be dissuaded from teaching against disloyalty because that is exactly what uncovers silent, ambiguous, indirect, ambivalent and mysterious enemies within the church.

Teach, so that sleeper cells like those who forget, those who are ungrateful, those who are ignorant, those who pretend, those who want money and those who are wolves are exposed.

Disloyalty is a veiled evil. It is important to teach and expose this indirect wickedness that devastates the church. The enemy within is the fifth column. The fifth column wishes to sleep quietly by your side until it can strike.

A Warning from History

On August 7, 1998, at 10:30 am, truck bombs exploded at U.S. embassies in Kenya and Tanzania, killing more than 200 people and wounding more than 4,000. The attacks were later linked to terrorists. The twin attacks of terrorists on the Tanzanian and Kenyan American embassies, carried out by sleeper cells of terrorists who laid in wait for years, are examples of the power of sleeper cells.

The commander of this sleeper cell in the field was Fadhil Muhammad, codenamed Fazul Harun, whose ethnic origin was the Comoros Islands in the Indian Ocean.

18Like many other al-Qaeda members and commanders, Fazul was recruited into the organization after volunteering to fight in Afghanistan during the Soviet invasion and after proving his ability as a warrior. While some members of the cell had trained in Afghanistan in 1994 and were of different nationalities, most were Tanzanian and Kenyan. The suicide attacker in Tanzania was the cousin of Abdallah al-Nishiri, the commander of the suicide attack in Yemen two years later.

The operation in Nairobi was conducted in the morning when a car bomb carrying hundreds of kilograms of explosives and two suicide attackers exploded next to the American embassy. Two hundred and thirteen people were killed in the explosion, and more than 4,000 were injured.

One of the two suicide attackers survived the attack after getting out of the car to pursue an embassy guard who had refused to open the embassy gate and fled from the area when the attackers threw a hand grenade at him and threatened him with their weapons. A few minutes later, a suicide terrorist driving a tanker filled with explosives attacked the American Embassy in Dar es Salaam, killing eleven people.

Preparations for the attacks in East Africa began five years before the attack. Osama Bin Laden himself, the leader of al-Qaeda, was involved in the decision to send men to Kenya to gather preliminary intelligence, check operational feasibility and assess suitable targets in order to draft a basic plan of attack. Operatives were sent to Kenya to integrate into the area, marry local women, open businesses, prepare a local infrastructure under a commercial guise, and continue gathering intelligence on selected targets.

Planning by this sleeper cell continued for a number of years with a few operational setbacks. Some feared that the cell had been compromised, and the attack was therefore delayed. When it was finally decided that the time had come to attack Western targets in Africa, the operational field commander of the attack was dispatched from Afghanistan to the region. Preparations then moved into high gear under the supervision of al-Qaeda headquarters operational commanders.18
