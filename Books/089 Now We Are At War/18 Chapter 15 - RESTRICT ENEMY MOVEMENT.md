### Chapter 15\

RESTRICT ENEMY MOVEMENT\
---------------------------------------------------

\

##### Leave no \[such\] room or foothold for the devil \[give no opportunity to him\].

##### Ephesians 4:27 (AMPC)

\

In war, you must restrict your enemy’s movement. The enemy must not have free access to you. Devils must not be able to freely walk around your space. This is what Paul meant when he said “Give no place to the devil”! Give no place to the devil means limit satan’s movement and restrict satan in your life. Give the devil very little space in your life!

Don’t give him room to manoeuvre! When the devil is free to roam around in your garden, he will notice all the options he can use to enter your house.

Give satan very few options of things he can do against you. How do you do this? Leave no space, no room or place for demons to move into and settle! To restrict your enemy is to limit him greatly and give him no room to manoeuvre around you.

Wherefore PUTTING AWAY LYING, speak every man truth with his neighbour: for we are members one of another. Be ye ANGRY AND SIN NOT: let not the sun go down upon your wrath: NEITHER GIVE PLACE TO THE DEVIL. Let him that stole STEAL NO MORE: but rather let him labour, working with his hands the thing which is good, that he may have to give to him that needeth. LET NO CORRUPT COMMUNICATION proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers.

Ephesians 4:25-29

The devil will have very little to work with if you are not a liar, not easily angered, not a thief, and not an evil speaker. Avoiding these four sins will truly limit demons and restrict them in relation to you!

In the scriptures above we learn how to restrict the enemy’s movement in our lives and to give no place to the devil. We learn the physical actions which are associated with giving a place and opportunity to satan. Satan wants opportunities to invade your life.

Limit the enemy’s movements in your life by restricting his access to you! There are certain actions of yours that will open the way and give access to demons to operate.

From the scriptures above, we see that four things - lying, anger, stealing and evil speaking - allow evil spirits to operate freely in our lives and give place to devils!

Apostle Paul described these four things (lying, anger, stealing and evil speaking) in association with giving a place for the devil to manoeuvre against you. These four things give satan many options for the types of attacks he could launch against you. Let’s look at each one of these activities.

1. Lying is a principal door opener for satan. It gives demons access to you. It gives the devil several options of temptations, tests and trials he can launch against you. Liars are often greatly demoted and mistrusted when they are found out. If you are a liar, you have allowed the enemy a free and easy room to enter and destroy you one day. Demons are moving all around you in the dark because they can see the open access to you through your many lies and deceptions!

2\. Uncontrolled anger is another activity that gives satan liberty around you. Your anger causes your brain not to function well and opens doors for demons. As long as your reasoning and understanding are darkened, you will be exposed to demonic attacks! If you have uncontrolled anger, you have given the devil a place to enter and destroy you one day. It is important to restrict the enemy’s movement around you. Demons are prowling around you in the dark because they can see open doors through your uncontrolled anger!

3\. Stealing also opens doors to the devil. Once you steal and are a thief, you have given room for devils to freely invade and feel free in your life. Most thieves are eventually discovered and dealt with harshly. If you are a thief, you have allowed the enemy to take you up and destroy you one day. Demons are everywhere around you because they can see the access to you because you steal!

4\. Evil speaking like slander, lying, criticizing, murmuring, and flattery is also a demon-liberating action. Satan is free to move in your life when you speak in an evil way. When you speak evil, slander, speak maliciously, lie, insult etc, you give the devil a place to enter in and destroy you one day. Demons are moving all around you in the dark because they can see the open access to you through your many slanderous and blasphemous statements.

Therefore, to give no place to the devil, restrict satan’s movements in your life! Completely distance yourself in every way from these four things; anger, deception, evil speaking and stealing. This will greatly limit satan’s options and opportunities when he is seeking a place to launch out against you!

The devil will have very little to work with if you are not a liar, not a thief, not an evil speaker and not easily angered. Avoiding these four sins will truly limit demons and restrict them in relation to you!
