Introduction

This is a book about spiritual warfare. It is a book about spiritual revenge and spiritual retaliation. No part of this book refers to physically attacking, seeking revenge or retaliating against a human being. This book is about attacking the devil and evil spirits. It is about fighting spiritually.

Anyone who uses this material improperly or makes any unfounded claims to the fact that I am teaching people to fight and kill one another is completely dishonest, deceptive, seeking to be mischievous and does so in bad faith.

This book will give you something to do when war breaks out. You no longer have to sit and defend yourself. You can attack! You can launch out and strike the enemy! It is important that you understand what it means to be at war. Every Christian is at war, whether you know it or not.

Use this book to give you something to do against the enemy who seeks to end your life and your ministry. Do not misunderstand the calm atmosphere around you. It does not mean that there is peace. There will be no peace until we get to heaven. The enemy will hate you until you are out of this world. Not every enemy is a murderer but our enemy is a murderer. Our enemy is a wicked liar and a deceiver.

Our enemy is the enemy of God. Satan is a stubborn, fallen angel who could have repented and apologised a long time ago.

Satan could even have apologised this year for all that he has done in spoiling the world. Yet, there is no apology! There is no retraction! There is no yielding! There is no agreeableness with the devil. It is this obstinate, adamant and wicked creature who has become our enemy too.

From the day satan deceived Eve and destroyed the peace of this world, turning it into an evil place, we recognised that satan was a malevolent, envious and wicked being with no kindness at all. Satan will take everything from you if you allow him!

Satan is full of ill-will and malice.

It is time for you to rise up and make a real war with the devil.

Now we are at war! Now we want to make a real war happen in the realm of the spirit.

We are going to make war with the devil and his representatives. We are going to fight fiercely until the other side has had enough.

We are going to fight until the other side wish that they had never known us. We are going to fight until the other side wish that they had never engaged us.

I wish you good fighting skills and revelation in your fight for God’s kingdom. Jehovah is the Lord of Hosts. This means that Jehovah is the Commander of the Host of Armies.

Jesus is our commander and He is pleased with you for joining in the war effort.
