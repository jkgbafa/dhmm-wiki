### Chapter 8\

WIPE OUT SATAN!\
-------------------------------------------

\

##### And in your faithful love destroy my enemies. WIPE OUT ALL THOSE WHO ATTACK ME, for I am your servant.

##### Psalm 143:12 (HCSB)

##### \

##### But of the cities of these people, which the LORD thy God doth give thee for an inheritance, THOU SHALT SAVE ALIVE NOTHING THAT BREATHETH: BUT THOU SHALT UTTERLY DESTROY THEM; namely, the Hittites, and the Amorites, the Canaanites, and the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites; as the LORD thy God hath commanded thee: That they teach you not to do after all their abominations, which they have done unto their gods; so should ye sin against the LORD your God.

##### Deuteronomy 20:16-18

\

##### When my enemies turn back, They stumble and perish before You. For You have maintained my just cause; You have sat on the throne judging righteously. You have rebuked the nations, You have destroyed the wicked; YOU HAVE BLOTTED OUT THEIR NAME FOREVER AND EVER. THE ENEMY HAS COME TO AN END IN PERPETUAL RUINS, AND YOU HAVE UPROOTED THE CITIES; THE VERY MEMORY OF THEM HAS PERISHED.

##### Psalms 9:3-6 (NASB)

\

In the scriptures above, we see how God wipes out enemies and anyone associated with the enemy! God is a God who utterly removes every enemy and anyone associated with the enemy!

In the Holman Christian Standard Bible, Psalm 9:5 says, “You have rebuked the nations, You have destroyed the wicked; You have erased their name forever and ever.”

God has a plan to wipe out your enemies. Their name will be forgotten! Their memory will be lost! The effect of all that the enemy has done against you will be erased and blotted out when you wipe them out with their associates, friends and relatives.

God is teaching your hands to war!

It is time to believe that God will blot out your enemies. God gave the commandment to the Israelites to wipe out their enemies. It was an instruction from God to absolutely wipe out the enemies! God’s reason for wiping out these enemies was so that the enemies would not teach the Israelites their evil ways.

In war, there are some enemies that must be blotted out. No other treatment can be given to them apart from their being blotted out. Most suspects of treason are simply executed and wiped out completely. On many occasions in the history of Israel, the armies of the Lord wiped out their enemies absolutely and totally.

1\. WIPE OUT SIHON THE AMORITE

And the Lord said unto me, Behold, I have begun to give Sihon and his land before thee: begin to possess, that thou mayest inherit his land. Then Sihon came out against us, he and all his people, to fight at Jahaz. And the Lord our God delivered him before us; and we smote him, and his sons, and all his people.

AND WE TOOK ALL HIS CITIES AT THAT TIME, AND UTTERLY DESTROYED THE MEN, AND THE WOMEN, AND THE LITTLE ONES, OF EVERY CITY, WE LEFT NONE TO REMAIN:

Deuteronomy 2:31-34

Sihon the Amorite, represents those who come out to fight against you. There are many unprovoked fights that you will have to fight. You must learn to fight back and wipe out those enemies who insist on fighting against you. You may be a peace-loving person but satan and the devils you are dealing with are not peace-loving. They hate peace and will put you into a war mode when you want to live in peace.

2\. WIPE OUT KING ARAD THE CANAANITE

And when king Arad the Canaanite, which dwelt in the south, heard tell that Israel came by the way of the spies; then he fought against Israel, and took some of them prisoners. And Israel vowed a vow unto the LORD, and said, If thou wilt indeed deliver this people into my hand, then I will utterly destroy their cities.

And the Lord hearkened to the voice of Israel, and delivered up the Canaanites; and they utterly destroyed them and their cities: and he called the name of the place Hormah.

Numbers 21:1-3

King Arad represents someone who takes prisoners and makes people into captives. Remember the thing that kept you captive and in bondage. People who held you captive in sin and evil must no longer be your friends. You must wipe them out completely. Do not keep such people as friends or associates. Who is the King Arad in your life? Why is he still alive in your life?

3\. WIPE OUT JERICHO

So the people shouted when the priests blew with the trumpets: and it came to pass, when the people heard the sound of the trumpet, and the people shouted with a great shout, that the wall fell down flat, so that the people went up into the city, every man straight before him, and they took the city. And they utterly destroyed all that was in the city, both man and woman, young and old, and ox, and sheep, and ass, with the edge of the sword.

Joshua 6:20-21

Jericho represents the enemy’s world. Jericho had to be conquered first before they could enter the Promised Land. God wants every memory of the world to be wiped out. God doesn’t want anything in the world to still smell on you. Wipe out all memories and symptoms of your former worldly life.

4\. WIPE OUT THE AMALEKITES

Samuel also said unto Saul, The Lord sent me to anoint thee to be king over his people, over Israel: now therefore hearken thou unto the voice of the words of the Lord. Thus saith the Lord of hosts, I remember that which Amalek did to Israel, how he laid wait for him in the way, when he came up from Egypt. Now go and smite Amalek, and utterly destroy all that they have, and spare them not; but slay both man and woman, infant and suckling, ox and sheep, camel and ass.

1 Samuel 15:1-3

Amalekites represent the enemy who attacks God’s people from behind as they are trying to escape. God remembers how the enemy took advantage of weakness and innocence. Wretched men abuse innocent little girls who do not know what is happening to them. God sees it all and does not approve of it.

Disloyal and treacherous men also take advantage of weak and hurting people and try to recruit them into a gang of miserable losers. God hated the very word Amalekites! He wanted them wiped out.

God was angry with Saul for keeping some Amalekites alive. God wanted every single Amalekite to be wiped out, But Saul was not ready to dissociate completely from the Amalekites. Saul was supposed to utterly remove and destroy men, women, children, animals and everything that moved. Everything that was associated with the Amalekites was supposed to go. Absolute removal of all enemies, collaborators, spies and associates is critical for the survival of your ministry.

5\. WIPE OUT DISLOYAL PEOPLE

Now I beseech you, brethren, mark them which cause divisions and offences contrary to the doctrine which ye have learned; and avoid them.

Romans 16:17

You must utterly dismiss and remove every person even remotely associated with disloyal and treacherous persons. Don’t be wiser than God! Don’t be too clever! Do as God did to Lucifer! Lucifer was utterly wiped out of heaven because of his betrayal and treachery.

6\. WIPE OUT FRIENDS OF THE DISLOYAL PERSON

Ye adulterers and adulteresses, know ye not that the friendship of the world is enmity with God? whosoever therefore will be a friend of the world is the enemy of God.

James 4:4

Do not be afraid to deal with friends of your enemy. Friendship with disloyal people means something. No relationship or friendship with a disloyal person is neutral. I have learnt by experience, that someone who even “understands” disloyal people should not be part of your team. Wipe out (dismiss, send away, release) people who are friends of your traitors and friends of your enemy. Let them go and join the enemy camp openly. Allow them to be part of the other group that they love and like so much.

7\. WIPE OUT THE ASSOCIATES OF DISLOYAL PEOPLE

And the great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan, which deceiveth the whole world: he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him.

Revelation 12:9

God wiped out Lucifer! God wiped out the angels that were associated with Lucifer. You must wipe out disloyal people and all those associated with them.
