Our works as passionate leaders and fighters are being recorded in heaven. Let us give ourselves to this great battle that we face. All the criticisms will mean nothing when eternity begins. All the fights, the insults, the blasphemies, and the setbacks will have little meaning except to give us a greater weight of glory!

I know thy works and thy labour, and thy patience, and how thou canst not bear them which are evil: and thou hast tried them which say they are apostles and are not, and hast found them liars: And hast borne, and hast patience, and for my name’s sake hast laboured, and hast not fainted.

Revelation 2:2-3

God is monitoring all our works! He is watching every single move we make. Let us therefore give ourselves to this glorious mission unreservedly! Let us never think that our time, our strength, and our families are our own. Let us dedicate them all to God and to His work. Let us give ourselves wholly that our profiting may appear to all!

1Are we justified in staying here while so many are perishing without means of grace in other lands?1 We must take every chance of doing good to the lost. We must be found labouring and we must be found travelling, day and night!

We must instruct, we must teach, exhort, rebuke and preach the Word with all long-suffering. We must have an anxious desire for the lost. We must be instant in prayer for the pouring out of the sweet Holy Spirit upon all the people. 2Surely, it is worthwhile to push ourselves with all our might in promoting the gospel and the kingdom of our Christ.2 Dear friends, God is gracious! Do not run away from Him!

A few more days and our work down here will be done! When it’s done, it’s done for eternity!

3Your life spent is irrevocable and your life will be contemplated through all eternity\!3 You will have a lot of time to contemplate what you did with your short time on earth.

These words above are the words of William Carey and Adoniram Judson. These are men who gave themselves to the war effort with all their hearts! They gave everything to the cause of the gospel!

They were men who risked their lives with the understanding that once it’s done, it’s done for eternity!

Their leadership and their efforts are noticed by discerning Christian leaders today. Most people do not know what these heroes did but eternity will reveal their greatness and how they gave themselves to fight a good fight for the Lord!

A WARNING FROM HISTORY

Even in the secular world, people who give themselves unreservedly to their task of leadership are remembered for their efforts in times of war! Winston Churchill was one such person who gave himself wholeheartedly to the war effort. In his first remarks as prime minister, which took less than six minutes to deliver, Winston Churchill famously declared that his policy was:

4To wage war by sea, land and air…

To wage war with the single aim of victory …

Victory at all costs…

Victory in spite of all terror…

Victory however long and hard the road may be!4

Winston Churchill’s approach to war was not to have a great, long-term vision and strategy, it was simply to win at all costs!

Winston Churchill was Britain’s leader through five years of war in Europe, from 10 May 1940 to 8 May 1945. As Prime Minister, he made at least twenty-five foreign trips, spending no fewer than 369 days (more than one year of the five) out of the country.

Winston Churchill travelled many times to dangerous places during the war. He crossed the Atlantic Ocean by ship to visit America, risking being sunk and drowned in the ocean at a time when the Germans had sunk over 4000 ships in that same ocean.

Winston Churchill was not young when he took over. He was 65 years old when he became Prime Minister, working long hours and not exercising at all. One would have expected him to stay at home and remain in a bunker for his safety. But that was not so! Churchill travelled farther and more extensively than any other wartime leader. He believed strongly in face-to-face negotiations with his overseas counterparts.

We see Winston Churchill as a great man of iconic status today; but he fought his way through many fires and many hard labours to achieve the victories that he did.

Winston Churchill faced much more internal opposition than people remember. He suffered three votes of no confidence, encountered many disputes in Cabinet and faced plenty of criticism from the press during his wartime administration.

Prime Minister Churchill gave himself totally to the war effort. He gave himself to defending his country, fighting for Britain and attacking the enemy relentlessly.

Winston Churchill was no sleepy, casual, apathetic leader. He took up the offensive and helped win the war. He gave himself wholeheartedly to the art and ministry of hostility, aggression and war!

Do the same! Do not be detached and laid back! There is a war going on and you must relentlessly lead the effort to fight and to win!
