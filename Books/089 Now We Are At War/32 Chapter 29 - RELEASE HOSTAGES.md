### Chapter 29\

RELEASE HOSTAGES\
--------------------------------------------

\

##### If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed.

##### John 8:36

\

Sin makes us captives! When a man sins, he becomes a captive to the sin and the consequences of the sins he has committed. Jesus came to set us free! Jesus is in the business of making people free.

Jesus answered them, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Whosoever committeth sin is the servant of sin.

John 8:34

Satan takes people hostage because of their sins. Most of us cannot overcome even the memory of our sins. After making mistakes, people are never the same. Sin changes people’s lives. People are taken hostage by their own sins! Sin has a way of taking people captive.

Today, we have many people who are gagged and unable to work for God because they feel bad about their sins and failings. Release all such people to do their best for Jesus whilst there is still time to bear fruit.

One of the acts of war is to release hostages. The release of hostages can cause a war to end. Jesus released many who were prisoners to sin and allowed them to serve and do great things for God.

1\. Release hostages: God will scourge His sons Himself.

For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth.

Hebrews 12:6

Many people with a religious spirit do not want to allow people to go free and serve God again.

Imagine this - supposing you went out and left your children with a neighbour for the afternoon. How would you feel if you came and found your neighbour thrashing your children with whips, belts and canes? Supposing your neighbour told you he was disciplining your children for misbehaving; would you be happy for your neighbour to continue beating your children? I don’t think so!

In the same way, God is not happy with people who think they can discipline His servants who have sinned and fallen short of the glory. Of course, we have to take steps to protect the church, but it is only God who can discipline and scourge His children appropriately.

2\. Release hostages: God loves those who restore sinners.

Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such a one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted.

Galatians 6:1

Only spiritual people can restore fallen sinners. Restoration and redemption are the heart of our Saviour. God is a Redeemer and a Restorer. You can never change the loving heart of our God who is love.

Supposing you came to pick up your child from school and found that your child had fallen into the septic tank of the school. Supposing the whole school was standing around, laughing and mocking your child as she tried to swim out of the cesspool. If someone rose up and went to pull your daughter out of the cesspool and rescued her from the mockery of the entire school, would you not have a special feeling towards this person? You would feel a debt of gratitude and love towards the person who rescued your daughter from the septic tank.

That is how God feels towards those who help to rescue and restore His children who have fallen into sin and difficulty.

3\. Release hostages: Jesus released the woman caught in adultery.

When Jesus had lifted up himself, and saw none but the woman, he said unto her, Woman, where are those thine accusers? Hath no man condemned thee? She said, No man, Lord. And Jesus said unto her, Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more. Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.

John 8:10-12

Jesus said He was the light of the world when He absolutely forgave the sinful woman and allowed her to go home and told her to sin no more. The sin of adultery was about to destroy this woman’s life.

The sin of adultery had happened and satan noticed a gap in the hedge. Satan planned to release unrighteous judgment, self-righteousness, revenge, murder and wickedness into the situation. However, through the forgiveness that came through Jesus, satan’s darkness was neutralized by huge shining lights of love and forgiveness. All these things- revenge, murder, unrighteous judgment, self-righteousness and wickedness, were blocked and wiped out by Jesus’ love and forgiveness for this lady.

Forgiveness and mercy are the greatest lights ever shown to a dark and sinful world of wicked men who deserve only judgment. Satan does not want the light of forgiveness at all!

Forgiveness neutralizes the hostage-taking effect of human sin. All have sinned and therefore all need forgiveness! If every sin was to be judged instantaneously, few people would be left alive. Adultery, deception, fornication and wickedness are everywhere, and many are taken hostage by these sins. Love, forgiveness, mercy, and restoration must be abundant as a weapon to release the hostages of sin.

And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.

John 1:5

You can wipe out satan’s attack and release hostages through the power of forgiveness.

Release hostages! Wipe out satan’s presence and influence by issuing a major counter-strike of forgiveness, clemency, pardon, compassion, pity, mercy and amnesty. Release hostages by releasing people from their mistakes.

Release hostages and let them work for God. Wipe out the attacks of satan by shining the light of God’s forgiveness. Pardon and mercy will cause people to live on and still have a chance to serve God, just as if it had never happened.

4\. Release hostages: Jesus released Peter to serve.

But Peter, standing up with the eleven, lifted up his voice, and said unto them, Ye men of Judaea, and all ye that dwell at Jerusalem, be this known unto you, and hearken to my words: For these are not drunken, as ye suppose, seeing it is but the third hour of the day. But this is that which was spoken by the prophet Joel;

Acts 2:14-16

Peter vehemently denied even knowing Jesus Christ. How could such a person be a leader of God’s worldwide church of Jesus Christ? Peter would have remained hostage of his failures and sins. But Jesus forgave him! JESUS DID NOT TAKE BACK THE KEYS THAT HE HAD GIVEN PETER!

And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it. And I will give unto thee the keys of the kingdom of heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever thou shalt loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.

Matthew 16:18-19

5\. Release hostages: Jesus released the other disciples.

In that same hour said Jesus to the multitudes, Are ye come out as against a thief with swords and staves for to take me? I sat daily with you teaching in the temple, and ye laid no hold on me. But all this was done, that the scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled. Then all the disciples forsook him, and fled.

Matthew 26:55-56

From this scripture, we see that it was not only Peter who denied Christ. Indeed, all the disciples deserted, forsook and fled from Jesus. Their sin of betrayal happened at the eleventh hour when Jesus needed help the most. They were not good friends to Jesus; yet Jesus set them free and commissioned them to go into the whole world on His behalf.

6\. Release hostages: Jesus released the woman of Samaria.

And many of the Samaritans of that city believed on him for the saying of the woman, which testified, He told me all that ever I did. So when the Samaritans were come unto him, they besought him that he would tarry with them: and he abode there two days. And many more believed because of his own word;

John 4:39-41

Jesus ministered in Samaria for two whole days because of the woman of Samaria. This woman was a well-known immoral person. She had several men on her list. Yet Jesus released this woman from her sins to become the first ever evangelist. It’s time to release the hostages who are isolated, rejected and held captive.

7\. Release hostages: Jesus released Paul.

And I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who hath enabled me, for that he counted me faithful, putting me into the ministry; Who was before a blasphemer, and a persecutor, and injurious: but I obtained mercy because I did it ignorantly in unbelief.

1 Timothy 1:12-13

Paul was released from the captivity of his sin. He had committed grievous sins that would haunt him for the rest of his life. There was no way he could ever have been associated with Christianity. Yet Jesus set him free to be a servant and an apostle of God. What a mighty release from the captivity of sin! What a mighty release of a spiritual hostage from his sins!

Release hostages! Do the same to those who have fallen into sin and you will have great victories in the ministry. Let the captives go and do something for Jesus whilst there is time to serve on earth!
