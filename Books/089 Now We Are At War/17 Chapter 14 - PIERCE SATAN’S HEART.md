### Chapter 14\

PIERCE SATAN’S HEART\
------------------------------------------------

\

##### For the word of God is quick, and powerful, AND SHARPER THAN ANY TWOEDGED SWORD piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.

##### Hebrews 4:12

You need an attack weapon in your fight against the devil. You need something that hurts the devil. You need something that pierces your enemy’s heart. I have good news for you. There is something that does that.

The word of God is a sword. In the realm of the spirit, the word of God is a knife! In the realm of the spirit, the word of God is a large hunting knife. Pierce satan’s heart with this knife by teaching fiercely and continually. Attacks and retaliation against satan are possible by piercing the heart of the enemy with the sword of the Spirit.

Take a sword and a knife and pass it through the heart of satan! Which sword? Which knife? Take the sword of the Holy Spirit and use it endlessly. Speak the Word endlessly! Quote it endlessly! Be a man of the Bible!

John Wesley was a man who declared himself to be Unius Libri Homos - A man of one book! What a declaration! Shamelessly declare and brandish the word of God as the basis for everything you say or do!

Use the sword for everything! Use the sword even as your cutlery.

Attack the devil and pierce his heart by preaching, teaching, writing books, writing songs, publishing books and spreading the word of God. The sword of the Spirit is the attack weapon of choice to pierce the enemy’s heart. The strongest, deepest piercings of the sword happen when the word of God is preached repeatedly and with concentration!

Have camps relentlessly and pierce the sword relentlessly into the heart of satan. Teach relentlessly, preach continuously and sink the knife deep into the heart of satan. Surely there will be a reaction in the realm of the spirit.

Remember how the demon reacted to Jesus when he was preaching in the synagogue? The demons burst out and screamed, “Leave us alone”. They could feel the spiritual knife piercing through their demonic hearts and couldn’t bear it any more.

And they were astonished at his doctrine: FOR HE TAUGHT THEM as one that had authority, and not as the scribes. And there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit; AND HE CRIED OUT, SAYING, LET US ALONE; what have we to do with thee, thou Jesus of Nazareth? art thou come to destroy us? I know thee who thou art, the Holy One of God.

Mark 1:22-24

The word of God is a sword. Swords are deadly weapons. Anyone would be frightened to encounter a frenzied enemy brandishing a sword, an axe, a cutlass or a knife. Every enemy would cringe at the thought of a sword fight! If the swords of medieval battles were deadly and frightening it means the sword of the Spirit is equally deadly and frightening to the enemy.

Rise up and let satan meet you as a crazed, frenzied man with a sharp and deadly knife!

You must rise up and use the sword of the Spirit to slash, to stab, to hit and decapitate your enemy the devil.

Take a knife and slash at your enemy by preaching the word of God. Decapitate your deadly enemy with a strong and frightening blow from the word of God.

A LESSON FROM HISTORY: SWORDS ARE DEADLY WEAPONS

1\. Swords, axes, cutlasses, hatchets and knives were the main deadly weapons in use before guns were invented.

2\. Even after the invention of the gun, knives have remained as secondary weapons used in hand-to-hand combat.

3\. The edges of most swords are strong enough to cut through flesh and bone, and cut off legs, arms and heads with a single swipe. A sword can plunge deep into the abdomen. A man can easily be incapacitated by a single well-placed stab or cut in hand-to-hand combat.

4\. If the sword hits a major artery or vein, or a nerve plexus, death can occur very quickly. A direct thrust through the heart can kill in ten seconds.

Can you imagine how Satan feels each time you take up the pure word of God and start teaching, writing, preaching and singing it? He sees you swinging a sharp sword all over the place and he is terrified.

What do the demons feel each time they see you swinging this spiritual cutlass, spiritual sword, spiritual hatchet, spiritual tomahawk, spiritual blade and spiritual knife? They are terrified!

Wow! Satan is terrified by the word of God! He is genuinely frightened by it!

Let’s do this thing! Let’s preach the word and terrify the demons with our flashing, sharpened swords!
