Your strength is a deterrent to the enemy! The enemy will consider your strength before planning an attack against you. Make yourself so strong that your strength alone will be a deterrent to anyone planning an attack against you.

Destroy the enemy by making it difficult or impossible for him to repeat an attack. Remember the reasons why satan had the upper hand in the past. Make a great investment in the prevention of a repeat attack! This is the wisdom in identifying the reasons for every defeat you have ever had.

Invest heavily in the prevention of such an attack in the same way. Identify all the weak points in your defences and strategies. Build yourself up to make it a hundred times more difficult for anyone to do the exact same thing to you again.

Teach the Word so that people do not once again become those who forget, those who are ungrateful, those who are ignorant, those who are proud and those who pretend. Defences must be raised against all bad experiences so that they cannot easily be repeated.

A LESSON FROM HISTORY: THE “MAD” POLICY

In the military world there is a policy called “MAD”. It stands for “Mutually Assured Destruction”. This is the principle of deterrence, founded on the notion that a nuclear attack by one superpower would be met with an overwhelming nuclear counter-attack such that both the attacker and the defender would be annihilated.

The investment of European countries in nuclear weapons and other weapons reveals this principle of having a strong deterrent. Europe has been turned into an armed camp filled with deadly weapons. These nuclear weapons are a deterrent to anyone who has ideas of dropping a nuclear bomb.

There was a First World War filled with many disasters and painful deaths. Yet, within thirty years, the same groups were on the battlefield again engaging in the same things.

14Only thirty years had passed between the First World War and the Second World War. Both wars were triggered by conflicts between European states and plunged the nations of the world into war.14

It has now been over eighty years since the Second World War ended. Perhaps the deterrent of Mutually Assured Destruction is helping to keep world wars away.

15At the heart of the World Wars was the conflict between Germany and its European neighbours. These were some of the most developed countries in the world and yet they sought to destroy each other.

Germany invaded France twice in 26 years, each time also invading Belgium, the Netherlands and Luxembourg, and drawing Great Britain and Russia into the conflict. Eventually, both of these wars grew to affect the whole world.

The Second World War was more brutal and bloodier than the First World War. The Second World War caused the deaths of about 70 million people. World War II was the first war that claimed the lives of more civilians than soldiers and witnessed the horror of the first systematic genocide in modern history. In addition, many cities, towns and villages across Europe were destroyed by aerial bombing and heavy artillery. The wanton destruction of homes created thousands of refugees and displaced persons. Almost everyone in Europe was affected by the war.15

It is important that a Third World War does not take place, although everyone expects it to happen one day. Perhaps the only thing that will prevent one party from starting the Third World War is the Mutually Assured Destruction (MAD) policy.
