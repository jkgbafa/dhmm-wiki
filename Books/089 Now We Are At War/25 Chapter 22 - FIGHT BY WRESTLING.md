### Chapter 22\

FIGHT BY WRESTLING\
----------------------------------------------

\

##### For WE WRESTLE not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.

##### Ephesians 6:12

\

There is a wrestling match you are going to have in the ministry! You must win the wrestling battles! Wrestling is a very personal battle. Wrestling has to do with an intimate fight. Something personal and intimate! Wrestling is a fight in the secret places!

Many ministers wrestle personally and continually with particular intimate and personal demons! Whether you believe it or not, you may be wrestling with an enemy who knows you and fights you intimately.

Wrestling speaks of close and personal combat. A wrestling demon may fight you about something private, something in your marriage, or something to do with your health or sexuality.

You must understand that the demons which target a minister of the gospel, engage in personal battles and intend to destroy him through intimate issues.

When men strive together one with another, and the wife of the one draweth near for to deliver her husband out of the hand of him that smiteth him, and putteth forth her hand, and taketh him by the secrets: Then thou shalt cut off her hand, thine eye shall not pity her.

Deuteronomy 25:11-12

There are personal battles where the enemy takes you up in the secret places of your life. In the scripture above the woman attacked the man in his secret places. What is an attack in the secrets? Usually, the sign that you have been attacked in the secrets are simple:

a\. Nobody knows the problems you have in the secrets.

b\. You cannot easily share the problem that you have.

c\. People would not understand the problem that you have.

For personal battles, you must develop man-to-man wrestling skills. When wrestling, you are alone in the fight and few people will understand what you are going through.

To help you overcome every single wrestling match you must know the common spirits that love to target you. Demons target ministers personally and torment them intimately and daily. Wrestling demons fight you based on their knowledge of you. Everyone in the ministry should expect to encounter these wrestling spirits. Many of these battles are in your mind and your inner thoughts. Prepare for satan to do battle with you by wrestling with you in the secret and intimate aspects of your life.

Wrestling Spirits

1\. EXPECT TO WRESTLE WITH THE SPIRIT OF DISOBEDIENCE

Now the serpent was more subtil than any beast of the field which the Lord God had made. And he said unto the woman, Yea, hath God said, Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden?

Genesis 3:1

Wrestle with and bind the spirit of disobedience that is fighting against you. Obedience is sometimes a very personal thing. Whatever God has shown you that is right and good, is what you must do. You must personally obey the Lord because you will stand alone before Him at the judgment.

There is always pressure from your relatives and friends to do something close and parallel to the will of God.

Sometimes your spouse will operate by the prince of the power of the air. This is the spirit of disobedience that personally attacked Adam, through Eve. Demons know your circumstances and target you and are always suggesting things that appeal to you personally. These demons constantly push you to disobey God.

You must win this close and personal wrestling match. You must obey the Lord. You must win!

2\. EXPECT TO WRESTLE WITH THE ANTI-ANOINTING SPIRIT

And every spirit that confesseth not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is not of God: and this is that spirit of antichrist, whereof ye have heard that it should come; and even now already is it in the world.

This is the spirit of anti-anointing and anti-giftings. This is the spirit of opposition to high anointings, great giftings and senior offices of ministry. Great opposition and great accusations are often very personal things. Satan knows that he should fight anointed people privately.

Out in the open, the anointing works wonders, and it is not easy to fight the anointed out in public. The anti-anointing spirit will take up the Lord’s anointed by private, secret and intimate oppositions and accusations! Expect to win every wrestling match against the spirit that has decided to oppose the anointing on your life.

God will set you free! I declare you the winner of every surprise wrestling match!

John Wesley and the Criminal

John Wesley was delivered from damnation when he rejected hypocrisy in his life. He was a minister of the gospel but believed that a man was only saved by his good works. One day he and his brother Charles Wesley prayed for a criminal as part of their prison ministry. This man was executed the very next day. This event made John Wesley analyse the hypocrisy of his own beliefs. How could this criminal do any good works to be saved since he was executed just after receiving Christ?

He realized then that there was something wrong with his beliefs of salvation through good works. He was forced to consider that salvation could not be by works of righteousness, but by the grace of God and the blood of Jesus. This event helped John Wesley to eventually receive Christ as his Saviour through faith and not through works.

John Wesley’s salvation and the entire Methodist church came into existence because John Wesley rejected the falsity and the hypocrisy of his own long-standing beliefs! Amazing!
