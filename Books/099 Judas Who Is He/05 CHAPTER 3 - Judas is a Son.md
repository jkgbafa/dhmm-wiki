### CHAPTER 3\

Judas is a Son\
------------------------------------

While I was with them in the world, I kept them in thy name: those that thou gavest me I have kept, and none of them is lost, but the SON OF PERDITION; that the scripture might be fulfilled.\

John 17:12

1\. Judas was a son.

Judas was more than a follower of Jesus Christ. He was a son! There are people who think that Judas was just an employee. Many people think that Judas was a person who was just hanging around. Judas was more than a servant. He was more than an employee. Judas was more than a follower. Judas was more than a disciple. Judas was a son! The person who will become Judas in your ministry is someone who is a son to you.

2\. Judas was a son of perdition.

Judas was a special kind of son. He was a son of perdition! What on earth is a son of perdition?

A son of perdition is a son of utter destruction and ruin.

A son of perdition is a son of Abaddon.

A son of perdition is a son of Gehenna.

A son of perdition is a son of Hades.

A son of perdition is a son of the abyss.

A son of perdition is a son of the bottomless pit.

A son of perdition is a son of condemnation.

A son of perdition is a son of damnation.

A son of perdition is the son of the lower world.

A son of perdition is the son of everlasting fire.

A son of perdition is a son of punishment.

A son of perdition is a son who brings devastation.

Judas Iscariot was a son to the Lord Jesus, just as the other disciples were. However, he was a son of perdition! Judas Iscariot was the son who was sentenced to perish in the infernal regions and the lower world. Judas Iscariot is the son who went to hell and to the underworld. Judas is the person who descended into fire and brimstone.

Judas’ activities led to the embarrassment and humiliation of Jesus Christ.

Judas forsook Jesus Christ, abandoned Him and turned against Him.

Judas Iscariot is the son who organised people to capture his father, the Lord Jesus Christ.

Judas is the person who caused the death of Jesus Christ.

Judas is the person who organised the brutal killing of the Son of God.

Judas Iscariot is the person who caused the death of Jesus Christ at the age of thirty-three.

Judas Iscariot is the son who did the unthinkable and the unspeakable!

Judas Iscariot is the son who was trusted and ended up doing the unimaginable.

Judas exposed Jesus Christ to the enemy. Judas turned against Jesus Christ.

Judas was a disappointment.

Judas was unfaithful in guarding the trust that was given to him.

Judas was a deceiver.

Judas was someone who misled and misguided people.

Judas proved that he was undependable and wicked.

Which kind of son will you be? Will you become a son of perdition? Make sure you are not a son of perdition!

3\. Judas is a son with a mocking eye.

The eye that mocketh at his father, and despiseth to obey his mother, the ravens of the valley shall pick it out, and the young eagles shall eat it.

Proverbs 30:17

A son of perdition is a son of the abyss. A son of perdition is a son of damnation, a cursed child.

A son of perdition has a mocking eye. He sees his father as someone who is expiring. A son of perdition sees his father as someone who is becoming irrelevant. A son of perdition is someone who looks at his father and hopes that he will die.

Jesus was not becoming irrelevant. Neither was He becoming powerless. Many fathers are seen as something that has an expiry date on it.

Many fathers are seen as someone who is outmoded or irrelevant. But a father never becomes irrelevant. Actually, fathers become more relevant as time goes by. If your mind is open and you have a humble spirit you will recognize that a father is neither outmoded nor irrelevant.

Many of the blessings spoken in the Bible were given by fathers in the very last hours of their lives. Those words which were spoken by old departing fathers have affected generations of people.

Isaac prophesied over his children in his last days. Obviously, he had not become irrelevant to the lives of his children. If he had been irrelevant, what he said would not have come to pass.

4\. A Judas is a son who cannot see far.

The eye that mocketh at his father, and despiseth to obey his mother, the ravens of the valley shall pick it out, and the young eagles shall eat it.

Proverbs 30:17

A Judas is a mocking son whose eyes have been plucked out by the ravens of the valley. Therefore, a Judas is someone who cannot see far. A Judas is a son of perdition whose eyes have been eaten by young eagles. A Judas is someone who is blind.

There are many people who feel that they have received all the teachings that their father has to give. I have met many people who say within themselves, “I know what he teaches. I know what he has to say. I know what he does.”

You may ask, “How do I know?” It is the easiest thing in the world to perceive when people think they know you and know all about you.

The devil said to Jesus, “I know thee who thou art...”

And there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit; and he cried out, Saying, Let us alone; what have we to do with thee, thou Jesus of Nazareth? art thou come to destroy us? I KNOW THEE WHO THOU ART, the Holy One of God.

Mark 1:23-24

Demons always want to claim knowledge. I know you. I know everything about you. I know all about you. I can talk about you. I can slander you. I can reveal your secrets. I can tell everyone things that you do not want them to know. I know thee, who thou art!

I know thee! I know all about you! I know everything! I know what you will do! I know how you will react. It is this demonic way of thinking that makes ignorant, short-sighted sons of perdition think they can take on a father and destroy him.

It is this line of demonic thought that inspires sons to leave their fathers unceremoniously.

It is this line of demonic thought that inspires sons to attack their fathers.

I often notice pastors who think they know all about me. I smile to myself as they walk away in their ignorance. Judas thought he knew all about Jesus. Judas thought he knew Jesus’ secrets. Judas thought he could say things to the Pharisees that would hurt Jesus Christ.

5\. A Judas is a son who will miss the latter blessings.

Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.

Matthew 28:19-20

Judas missed the latter blessings. Judas missed the blessings of “I am with you always, even unto the end of the world.” Judas missed the blessings of the greatest commission ever given to mankind.

Sometimes a father gives a portion away but keeps back some things which he intends to give at the very end. How immature and narrow-minded you show yourself to be when you think you have walked away with every bit of knowledge and wisdom your father has.

Indeed, a Judas, has a limited understanding of the things he will get from his father. Judas Iscariot walked away from Jesus Christ and abandoned Him, thinking that he had gotten all that he could ever get from Jesus.

Some children see the relationship with their fathers in terms of money they can receive from him. Because of this, they do not benefit from the true blessings of their father.

Some children look at their parents as people to pay their school fees. Beyond school fees they do not expect or want anything else from their fathers.

There are other children who see their parents as people who will give them an inheritance. They are interested in the inheritance, seeing it as the only reason to stay connected to their father. Some children even want their parents to die so they can acquire what their parents have. Because of this attitude, they lose out on many of the blessings of a father.

Isaac gave blessings to his sons at the very end of his life which he did not bother to give earlier.

And it came to pass, that when Isaac was old, and his eyes were dim, so that he could not see, he called Esau his eldest son, and said unto him, My son: and he said unto him, Behold, here am I. And he said, Behold now, I am old, I know not the day of my death: Now therefore take, I pray thee, thy weapons, thy quiver and thy bow, and go out to the field, and take me some venison; And make me savoury meat, such as I love, and bring it to me, that I may eat; that my soul may bless thee before I die.

Genesis 27:1-4

Jacob gave blessings to his sons at the very end of his life.

And Jacob called unto his sons, and said, Gather yourselves together, that I may tell you that which shall befall you in the last days. Gather yourselves together, and hear, ye sons of Jacob; and hearken unto Israel your father… And when Jacob had made an end of commanding his sons, he gathered up his feet into the bed, and yielded up the ghost, and was gathered unto his people.

Genesis 49:1-2, 33

Judas is someone who misses the final blessings which are destined to come at the end. Judas is a son of the inferno, a son of damnation, a son who misses the blessing.

How truly myopic Judas was when he thought his father’s usefulness was limited to three years of ministry. What did Judas do with his training? Did he prosper with the thirty pieces of silver? Did he become a successful man with those thirty pieces of silver? Did Judas’ life change when he got those thirty pieces of silver?

Certainly not! He became poorer and lost everything even though he actually received the thirty pieces of silver. Obviously, there were many things that Judas did not know about. Those who are ignorant! Those who are dangerous sons! Those who are sons of perdition!

A son of perdition is a son of utter destruction and ruin. A son of perdition is a son of Abaddon. A son of perdition is a son of Gehenna.

A son of perdition is a son of Hades. A son of perdition is a son of the abyss. A son of perdition is a son of the bottomless pit.

A son of perdition is a son of condemnation. A son of perdition is a son of damnation. A son of perdition is the son of the lower world. A son of perdition is a son of everlasting fire. A son of perdition is a son of devastation.
