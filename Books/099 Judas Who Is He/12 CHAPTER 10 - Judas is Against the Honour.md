### CHAPTER 10\

Judas is Against the Honour\
-------------------------------------------------

Then took Mary a pound of ointment of spikenard, very costly, and anointed the feet of Jesus, and wiped his feet with her hair: and the house was filled with the odour of the ointment. THEN SAITH ONE OF HIS DISCIPLES, JUDAS ISCARIOT, SIMON’S SON, WHICH SHOULD BETRAY HIM, WHY WAS NOT THIS OINTMENT SOLD FOR THREE HUNDRED PENCE, AND GIVEN TO THE POOR? This he said, not that he cared for the poor; but because he was a thief, and had the bag, and bare what was put therein. Then said Jesus, Let her alone: against the day of my burying hath she kept this. For the poor always ye have with you; but me ye have not always.

John 12:3-8

Judas is against the honour of God’s servant.

Judas is against the prosperity of God’s servant.

Judas is someone who is against the honour and the prosperity of God’s servant. On that fateful day, Mary honoured Jesus Christ with a lovely alabaster box of perfume. The whole house was filled with the smell of this perfume. No one could control the lady as she poured out her expensive honour on Jesus. Everyone stood back in amazement! They were taken by surprise by this move of Mary. She had honoured Jesus because of a personal conviction that she had. Her honour was not as a result of a committee meeting.

All the disciples stood by in amazement as this lady unilaterally honoured Jesus Christ. However, Judas did not approve of this expense.

Judas murmured against the woman’s act of honour. People who are against the bestowal of gifts and good things on God’s servants must be noted.

They are often Judases in the making!

The Concerned Judas

Some years ago, I received a message from a brother who claimed to be a concerned Christian. His concern was that people were gathering gifts to bestow upon me on a particular occasion.

Once and again, he sent messages to me that he did not think it was appropriate for people to gather such gifts for me. He claimed that he was concerned for my reputation.

However, I sensed the spirit of Judas in the realm of the spirit.

A few months later, I found out that he was interacting deeply with some of the people who hated me the most. I found out that he was in cahoots with wretched orangus who were slandering me and the church constantly. I realised that I had not been wrong in thinking that this person who was concerned about gifts being given to me was actually a Judas.

Judas Iscariot will always be remembered for being against the honour that was bestowed on Jesus Christ by Mary. I will advise you to watch everyone’s reaction when God’s servant is being honoured.

Let him that is taught in the word communicate unto him that teacheth in all good things.

Galatians 6:6
