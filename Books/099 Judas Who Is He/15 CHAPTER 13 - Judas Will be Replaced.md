### CHAPTER 13\

Judas Will be Replaced\
--------------------------------------------

\

FOR IT IS WRITTEN IN THE BOOK OF PSALMS, LET HIS HABITATION BE DESOLATE, AND LET NO MAN DWELL THEREIN: AND HIS BISHOPRICK LET ANOTHER TAKE. Wherefore of these men which have companied with us all the time that the Lord Jesus went in and out among us, ….And they prayed, and said, Thou, Lord, which knowest the hearts of all men, shew whether of these two thou hast chosen, …And they gave forth their lots; and the lot fell upon Matthias; and he was numbered with the eleven apostles.

Acts 1:20-21, 24, 26

God will send a replacement for every rebellious person who chooses the way of Judas.

God is ahead of Judas! Jesus knew who was going to betray Him long before it happened.

Jesus knew who was going to betray Him in chapter 6 of the book of John. In other words, Jesus knew long before Judas manifested, that He was going to need a replacement for Judas. Jesus knew that he was a thief and a wicked person. But there is always a use for a thief and a wicked person, if you know how to handle them.

Jesus answered them, Have not I chosen you twelve, and one of you is a devil? He spake of Judas Iscariot the son of Simon: for he it was that should betray him, being one of the twelve.

John 6:70-71

Jesus was preparing to replace Judas long before he manifested his wickedness.

God has a replacement for Judas! A replacement so perfect that it will surprise you!

The first replacement of Judas was Matthias. Matthias was chosen by the twelve disciples. Even though Mathias was chosen, Joseph Barsabbas Justus was standing by in the wings, hoping for an opportunity to take the position. All Judases must be aware that there is a string of replacements who will do even better than they did.

Right on the other hand, there seems to have been another replacement chosen by God and not chosen by casting lots. This other replacement was Apostle Paul. Apostle Paul saw himself as one of the apostles. Paul described himself as a replacement, who had come in late. Indeed, Apostle Paul was seen as a replacement for the missing Judas Iscariot.

And last of all he was seen of me also, AS OF ONE BORN OUT OF DUE TIME. For I AM THE LEAST OF THE APOSTLES, that am not meet to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of God.

1 Corinthians 15:8-9

Paul saw himself as part of the family of apostles. Paul saw himself as someone who was a significant member of the apostolic family. Apostle Paul compared himself with none other than Apostle Peter.

For I suppose I was not a whit behind the very chiefest apostles.

2 Corinthians 11:5

Perhaps, it was the prayers of Stephen asking for forgiveness for Saul that allowed Paul to be saved.

And cast him out of the city, and stoned him: and the witnesses laid down their clothes at a young man’s feet, whose name was Saul. And they stoned Stephen, calling upon God, and saying, Lord Jesus, receive my spirit. And he kneeled down, and cried with a loud voice, LORD, LAY NOT THIS SIN TO THEIR CHARGE. And when he had said this, he fell asleep.

Acts 7:58-60
