### CHAPTER 8\

Judas is Someone Who Pretends\
---------------------------------------------------

And Satan entered into Judas who was called Iscariot, belonging to the number of the twelve. And he went away and discussed with the chief priests and officers how he might betray Him to them. They were glad and agreed to give him money. So he consented, and began seeking a good opportunity to betray Him to them apart from the crowd. When He rose from prayer, He came to the disciples and found them sleeping from sorrow, and said to them, “Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray that you may not enter into temptation.”While He was still speaking, behold, a crowd came, and the one called Judas, one of the twelve, was preceding them; and he approached Jesus to kiss Him. BUT JESUS SAID TO  HIM, “JUDAS, ARE YOU BETRAYING THE SON OF MAN WITH A KISS?

Luke 22:3-6, 45-48; (NASB)

Judas pretended to be a faithful disciple of Jesus Christ. In the end it was revealed that he was a traitor, deceiver and a murderer.

1\. A Judas is a pretender!

2\. A Judas is a disloyal person who pretends to be loyal.

3\. A Judas is a false and fake personality who pretends to be something that he is not.

4\. A Judas is a hypocrite who pretends to be a good person but is actually a bad person.

5\. A Judas is a deceiver who intends to fool you.

6\. A Judas is a liar who pretends that he is telling the truth.

7\. A Judas is a destroyer who pretends that he is building something.

8\. A Judas is a murderer who pretends to be a peacemaker.

9\. A Judas is a covenant breaker who pretends that he respects agreements.

10\. A Judas cannot be trusted but pretends to be the most trustworthy person around.

How to Deal with the Threat of a Judas

Every leader will face the threat of Judas Iscariot. Many people are not loyal but they pretend to be loyal; pretending to be loyal when they are actually your enemies. Do not be deceived by the meek-faced people with sheepish grins who appear to admire your every decision. Many of these people are pretending. You must go further and uncover the truth about everyone around you. Watch out for those who do not talk much. They are the ones who have much to hide.

People who do not say anything when they are in your presence, but talk a lot when they are away from you, are good examples of pretenders. Why have one picture when you are near and another picture when you are far?

Samson was in great danger when he relaxed in the presence of a pretending woman. So also, will you be in great danger when you relax in the presence of fake followers who do not really like you! Pretenders are very dangerous people and can lead to the worst kind of crisis in your ministry. Do not be trusting and accepting of quiet and unknown people. The Bible teaches us to prove all things and hold fast to that which is good. (1 Thessalonians 5:20)

Watch out for the signs and symptoms of another Judas!

How are you supposed to uncover and discover pretenders who do not speak their minds? The answer is simple - in the same way that doctors use symptoms to diagnose illnesses in your body!

How do doctors find things out? By learning about symptoms and signs! Each symptom or sign in the body can lead to an important diagnosis. Similarly, each symptom you notice in a person can lead to the all-important diagnosis that you are dealing with Judas Iscariot.

Watch out for the signs and symptoms of a pretender! Like a doctor, believe the signs and symptoms. Do not simply trust what you see. Jesus Christ did not judge with his eyes.

And the spirit of the LORD shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of the LORD; And shall make him of quick understanding in the fear of the LORD: AND HE SHALL NOT JUDGE AFTER THE SIGHT OF HIS EYES, NEITHER REPROVE AFTER THE HEARING OF HIS EARS: But with righteousness shall he judge the poor, and reprove with equity for the meek of the earth: and he shall smite the earth with the rod of his mouth, and with the breath of his lips shall he slay the wicked.

Isaiah 11:2-4

Look beyond the perfect picture that is presented to you and see the real thing. You must judge by the actions and not by the outward presentation. If you allow a pretender to flourish in your midst, you are grooming a potential traitor and murderer. Let’s look at a few symptoms and signs that are important in picking up pretenders.

How to Notice Pretenders

1\. Watch out for someone with a quiet brooding personality.

Notice people who are too quiet! They could be pretenders. Quiet people are dangerous. All their positive and negative thoughts are stored up within them. They stand still and watch quietly with a lean and hungry look in their eyes. You must develop a proper fear and respect for quiet people! Quiet withdrawn people remind us of schizophrenia. Schizophrenia is a serious illness that affects the mind.

2\. Watch people with dual personalities.

When they are with others, they are sanguineous, jovial, chatty and friendly. When they are in your presence, they are quiet, serious, pensive and melancholy. Is it that such a person has two personalities? Or could it be that they pretend to be something else when they are in your presence?

3\. Watch those who are too nice.

Many pretenders present themselves publicly as perfect family men. Publicly, they will say, “In this family, we marry only once.” But in private, they have multiple partnerships without rules!

A very nice personality indeed. Notice people who are too nice. How can it be that you are always smiling and grinning in every circumstance? Are you never sad? Are you never unhappy? Are you always elated and excited? That cannot be the case. Such people have a picture of goodness, righteousness, perfection, orderliness and holiness. Such people have a good picture of their marriage. Often, this is a façade for hidden things and dark secrets. A Judas is often too nice and too good!

4\. Watch those who are non-committal.

Notice people who do not say either good or bad. Notice people who never take a stance about anything. It cannot be that you never have anything good to say. And it cannot be that you never have anything bad to say. You must have something in your mind.

Watch out for people who are non-committal. I have noticed that people who say ‘I don’t belong here or there’ actually belong somewhere.

Absalom did not say enough. Absalom said neither good nor bad about his sister being raped. How could that be? Did he not even have a bad comment?

5\. Watch unfaithful adulterers.

A Judas is an unfaithful man. He is disloyal. Judas is wicked! Judas is two-faced! Judas is a dual character! Judas is somebody who does the unthinkable, the unspeakable and the unimaginable. You need all the characteristics of a Judas to have extramarital affairs. That is why most Judases have extramarital affairs. Having an extramarital affair is the easiest thing for a Judas to do.
