Treachery is the general term for betraying one’s country, other people, one’s family or one’s friends.

The words “treachery” and “betrayal” are often interchangeable.

Treachery and betrayal are general terms for deceiving other people and turning against people who trust you.

Treachery is to give away people’s secrets.

Betrayal is to give away people’s secrets.

Treachery is losing sight of one’s principles or beliefs.

Treachery is an unforgettable experience. Anyone who has been betrayed or experienced betrayal is changed forever.

You Will Remember Judas

Someone who betrays your trust is not easily forgotten. A Judas is someone whose treachery and betrayals will always be remembered by those he betrayed. You can see from the verses below that Judas was always described as a betrayer.

From the appointment of the disciples to the last Supper, from Gethsemane, to the handing over of Jesus to the Pharisees, Judas’ presence as the betrayer was always noted. A betrayer is an unforgettable person.

The wounds of a betrayer are very deep and many never overcome the effects of a betrayer till they die.

Some people even die because of the effects of a betrayer on their lives.

Judas deeply impacted both Jesus and the other disciples by his actions. It has been over two thousand years since Jesus was betrayed. What has happened has happened but it keeps coming up because betrayal is never forgotten.

The behaviour of a betrayer touches people in a strange way.

This is because the betrayer does the unthinkable, the unspeakable and the unimaginable. The surprise alone can kill you!

This is why when bad news is being given to people, they ask them to sit down first.

The shock of the unimaginable, the unthinkable and the unspeakable is difficult to forget.

Notice how Judas is consistently mentioned in the scriptures as a betrayer. No one forgets betrayal! Do not be surprised if experiences of betrayal keep coming up in discussions years after it has happened,
