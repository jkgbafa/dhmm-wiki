### CHAPTER 1\

Judas is Someone Who Should Not Have Been Born\
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The Son of man goeth as it is written of him: but woe unto that man by whom the Son of man is betrayed! IT HAD BEEN GOOD FOR THAT MAN IF HE HAD NOT BEEN BORN.

Matthew 26:24

Jesus met many evil people in His lifetime. He met the adulterous woman who was caught in the very act. He met the woman of Samaria who had had five husbands. He encountered thieves like Zacchaeus. He encountered two robbers on the cross. He encountered wicked soldiers who whipped Him and punished Him for nothing. Yet He never said to any of them, “It would have been better that you were not born.” According to this statement, Judas was more evil than all the murderers, liars, adulterers, and thieves Jesus had encountered in His ministry. Jesus never said that these people should not have been born.

In other words, Judas was the worst person that He had met in His entire life and ministry. Jesus wished for Judas that he had never come into this world because he was the worst kind of person to have ever lived. In other words, any other kind of evil was more acceptable than the evil of Judas Iscariot. Judas Iscariot was the most evil person that Jesus had encountered on earth! In other words, being a treacherous betrayer is the worst kind of evil you can ever engage in. According to Jesus, being a disloyal and ungrateful assistant is the worst kind of wickedness, and again, according to Jesus, being a betrayer is the worst thing a person can be.

Why did Jesus say, “It would have been better if Judas were not born.”? Why did Jesus imply that it would have been better if Judas’ mother had had an abortion and bled him away?

Jesus called Judas a son of perdition. A son of perdition is a son of ruin and a son of destruction. Judas was a son that went to hell! Judas is the son that was destroyed for eternity! It would have been better if he was not born! It would have been better if Jesus had never met Judas.

While I was with them in the world, I kept them in thy name: those that thou gavest me I have kept, and none of them is lost, but THE SON OF PERDITION; that the scripture might be fulfilled.

John 17:12

Judas will give you nightmares. He is the worst kind of person you could ever appoint to be part of your ministry team. He is the son of perdition! The son of destruction! Judas will end up being the worst character you ever meet in your life. Judas will be someone you would wish you had never met.

Jesus said, it would have been better for Judas to never have been born. Why is this?

There are many things that a Judas does that make him the worst experience ever.

Fifteen Treacherous Things You Will Experience When You Meet a Judas

1\. A Judas will deliver or expose you to an enemy.

2\. A Judas will give you away to the enemy.

3\. A Judas will turn against you suddenly.

4\. A Judas will leave you.

5\. A Judas is unfaithful in guarding or maintaining a trust given to him.

6\. A Judas will disappoint your hopes and expectations and shatter your heart.

7\. A Judas will give away information in violation of your trust for him.

8\. A Judas will reveal unconsciously or consciously something you would prefer to conceal.

9\. A Judas will deceive, misguide and mislead many people.

10\. A Judas will pretend for a long time and live a dual life.

11\. A Judas will deceive everyone.

12\. A Judas will do the unthinkable, the unspeakable and the unimaginable.

13\. A Judas will prove himself to be undependable.

14\. A Judas will abandon and forsake you.

15\. A Judas is someone whose activities will lead to your embarrassment and humiliation.

Jesus Christ experienced all these things because He appointed Judas as His trusted accountant. Judas exposed Jesus to the enemy. Judas gave Him away to the enemy! Judas was unfaithful in maintaining the trust that was given to him by Jesus.Judas disappointed the hopes and expectations that Jesus had for him.

Judas revealed where Jesus was, which is something Jesus would have preferred to conceal. Judas misguided the rest of the disciples into thinking he was a faithful, hardworking accountant. Judas pretended for a long time, deceiving the whole world and pretending to be something that he was not. Judas did the unthinkable, the unspeakable and the unimaginable. Judas abandoned Jesus. Judas turned against Jesus. Judas proved to everyone that he was not dependable.

In order for you to follow Jesus ultimately, God is going to allow you to encounter somebody who will be a “Judas” to you. This person will expose you to the enemy. This pastor will give you away to the enemy. This person will be unfaithful in maintaining the trust that you gave him.

The Judas who is coming into your life will be a disappointment in ministry. He will be a source of disappointment, discouragement and disillusionment.

When Judas comes into your life, he will seek to embarrass and humiliate you. Judas will expose you and reveal things that you would have preferred to conceal. They may not necessarily be evil things but they may be things that are exposed to embarrass you or humiliate you.

Judas will mislead you and make you think that he is a faithful, hardworking person.

The Judas of your life and ministry will make everyone think that he is something that he is not.

Expect it! Judas will do the unthinkable, the unspeakable and the unimaginable!

You will never forget Judas when you encounter him. The world has not forgotten what Judas did to Jesus Christ. That is why no one names his child “Judas”. Judas proved to everyone that he was a horror.

Expect Judas! He is coming soon! Judas abandoned Jesus! Judas turned against Jesus! Expect some people to turn against you!

While I was with them in the world, I kept them in thy name: those that thou gavest me I have kept, and none of them is lost, but THE SON OF PERDITION; that the scripture might be fulfilled.

John 17:12
