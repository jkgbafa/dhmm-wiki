### CHAPTER 11\

Judas is Someone Who is Ignorant\
------------------------------------------------------

And as he journeyed, he came near Damascus: and suddenly there shined round about him a light from heaven: And he fell to the earth, and heard a voice saying unto him, Saul, Saul, why persecutest thou me? And he said, Who art thou, Lord? And the Lord said, I am Jesus whom thou persecutest: IT IS HARD FOR THEE TO KICK AGAINST THE PRICKS. And he TREMBLING AND ASTONISHED said, Lord, what will thou have me to do…

Acts 9:3-6

O how ignorant is Judas! He thinks he can destroy God’s servant. Judas can never destroy what God is doing. No one can destroy the purpose of God. Read this scripture for yourself. Can you turn back the hand of God?

This is the purpose that is purposed upon the whole earth: and this is the hand that is stretched out upon all the nations. For THE LORD OF HOSTS HATH PURPOSED, AND WHO SHALL DISANNUL IT? AND HIS HAND IS STRETCHED OUT, AND WHO SHALL TURN IT BACK?

Isaiah 14:26-27

A Judas is someone who thinks he can destroy you. O, how ignorant he is! I have experienced many people who hated me so much they wanted to destroy me and the church I founded.

I have had people who felt that they would spread so much slime and slander against me that it would destroy me and make it impossible for me to continue in the ministry.

I have had people threaten to drive me out of the country. I have heard people cursing me from the bottom of their hearts.

I have heard of people having meetings and planning how to destroy me and the ministry.

Saul (who later became Paul) was ignorant when he fought against the church. He did not know that fighting the church is like kicking against nails with all your strength.

This is what Saul discovered when he had fought against the church and supervised the elimination of one of its leaders, Stephen. Saul was a man with a good conscience. He earnestly thought that he was eliminating troublemakers from the peaceful city of Jerusalem. In his fight for righteousness, he sought to wipe out elements that he thought were harmful to society.

There are Judas Iscariots who declare that they are on a holy war to expose false preachers and ministries. Like Saul, they think they have a divine mandate to let everyone know the truth about the hypocrites in the pulpits. The Apostle Paul was so surprised to find out that he was actually fighting against Christ.

Paul was astonished when he discovered what he was really doing! When people do not know what they are doing, they often do the wrong thing. Paul the wretched sinner and blasphemer could not destroy Jesus Christ and the church. He claimed later on that he had received mercy from God because he had attacked God’s church in his ignorance.

Who was before a blasphemer, and a persecutor, and injurious: but I obtained mercy, because I DID IT IGNORANTLY IN UNBELIEF.

1 Timothy 1:13

Judas Iscariot was a fool to think that he could destroy Jesus Christ. In the beginning was the Word. The Word was with God and the Word was God (John 1:1). Everything in the universe was created by Jesus Christ.

One day, a pastor who had worked with me in the past, went on a quest to destroy me. He met someone who told him, “You lack biblical intelligence to attack God’s servant the way you are doing.” Unfortunately, he did not heed the advice he was given. As a result of his futile quest, the sword of the Lord entered his dwelling place and scattered everything!

I have watched as people wither as they try to destroy God’s church.

It is sad that Christians can be tricked into following rebels and modern-day Judases. Today, there are many ignorant people who are following empty-headed rebels. No one can destroy God’s servant, no matter who you are.

I remember a man of God who thought he could destroy Reinhard Bonnke. His name was Richard Ngidi. Richard Ngidi was gifted in miracles, signs and wonders. During those days, Bonnke would preach first and step down from the platform for Ngidi to do the miracles, signs and wonders part of the ministry. Richard Ngidi was a solid part of the team and he gave great support to Reinhard Bonnke, causing the ministry to grow rapidly.

Perhaps Richard Ngidi began to think that he was the one who made Reinhard Bonnke great. After all, he was the man doing the miracles and yet Reinhard Bonnke was taking the credit for the great ministry that bore Reinhard Bonnke’s name.

One day, Richard Ngidi said to Reinhard Bonnke’s assistant, “The day I leave Bonnke, he is finished! The day I leave will mark the end of Bonnke and his ministry.”

One day, Richard Ngidi didn’t show up at a crusade. Bonnke waited and waited for Richard but he did not show up. Frustrated and disappointed, Reinhard mounted the platform, fully depending on the Holy Spirit this time. And guess what? God used Bonnke mightily in miracles, signs and wonders. This was the beginning of Bonnke’s ministry of miracles, signs and wonders.

Instead of that day being the end of Bonnke, it marked a new beginning for him and unfortunately heralded the end of Richard Ngidi.

Time passed, and one day Richard Ngidi came to attend one of Reinhard Bonnke’s crusades. He saw multitudes and multitudes gathered there. It seems Richard Ngidi had a chance to meet Bonnke.

All that Ngidi could say that day was “I missed it, I missed it”

That was the last time Richard Ngidi met Bonnke.

Richard Ngidi died soon after that encounter.

This is a lesson to all of us. Do not think you can destroy God’s servant! Judas thought he would finish the ministry of Jesus.

Jesus was just getting started in His ministry to the whole wide world. The cross was the beginning of the ministry of Jesus Christ. Today, the name of Jesus is mentioned in the whole wide world and the name Judas invokes a heartfelt horror.
