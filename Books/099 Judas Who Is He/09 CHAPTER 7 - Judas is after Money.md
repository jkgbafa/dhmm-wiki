### CHAPTER 7\

Judas is after Money\
------------------------------------------

\

But those who \[are not financially ethical and\] CRAVE TO GET RICH \[WITH A COMPULSIVE, GREEDY LONGING FOR WEALTH\] FALL INTO TEMPTATION AND A TRAP AND INTO MANY FOOLISH AND HARMFUL DESIRES THAT PLUNGE PEOPLE INTO RUIN AND DESTRUCTION \[leading to personal misery\]. For the love of money \[that is, the greedy desire for it and the willingness to gain it unethically\] is a root of all sorts of evil, and some by longing for it have wandered away from the faith and pierced themselves \[through and through\] with many sorrows.\

1 Timothy 6:9-10 AMP

Judas is someone who is filled with a desire for more and more money. Such a person is never satisfied with what he has. What a shame that one of the disciples of Jesus Christ absolutely fulfilled the prophecies of destruction that are promised to those who love money!

He that loveth silver shall not be satisfied with silver; nor he that loveth abundance with increase: this is also vanity.

Ecclesiastes 5:10

Judas was not content with the provision that he had through Jesus. This constant desire for more and more money led to the undoing of Judas.

1\. Judas’ desire for money will cause him to be tempted with many temptations.

BUT THEY THAT WILL BE RICH FALL INTO TEMPTATION and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition.

1 Timothy 6:9

A Judas will have many temptations, just as predicted. Your strong desire for money sets you up for temptations. Judas was tempted to betray Christ because of his desire for money. The other disciples did not have any such temptations. Many temptations are simply because of your love and desire for money.

2\. Judas’ desire for money will cause him to be trapped.

BUT THEY THAT WILL BE RICH fall into temptation and A SNARE, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition.

1 Timothy 6:9

Today, I know a number of Judas characters who have fallen into traps that they cannot come out of. I once saw a group of pastors trapped by a lawyer. They could not escape from this lawyer because they were trapped in a web of greed. I know pastors who are ensnared by legal advisers who promised them that they would have millions if they sued a church. These miserable Judases are now captives of wretched extortionists. Ministers who long to be rich are likely to fall into many traps and unexpected entanglements.

3\. Judas is a person who is filled with foolish and unreasonable desires.

But they that will be rich fall into temptation and a snare, and into many FOOLISH AND HURTFUL LUSTS, which drown men in destruction and perdition.

1 Timothy 6:9

A Judas will have many desires, just as predicted. Judas was tempted to betray Christ because of his desire for money. When people have a desire for money, they experience foolish lusts. For instance, they may desire to drink from diamond-studded golden cups. I know a pastor who was offered two and a half million dollars to disobey the word of God. His foolish desire for millions of dollars made him fall in to foolishness and hurt. There was nothing like two and a half million dollars! The foolish idea of two and a half million dollars was just dangled in front of this Judas’ beaming eyes. This Judas foolishly accepted the bait.

4\. Judas is a person who will be drowned in destruction.

BUT THEY THAT WILL BE RICH fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which DROWN MEN IN DESTRUCTION and perdition.

1 Timothy 6:9

All the Judas characters that I know have drowned in destruction. Their desire for money has been their undoing. Today, most of these people can only be described as destroyed ministers of the gospel. They are a pale shadow of what they used to be. Some are beggars! Some are stubbornly defiant but they are destroyed. They are drowned in destruction. Their ministries have been destroyed. Their lives have been destroyed. Their children and their future have been destroyed. Destruction awaits every single betrayer.

5\. Judas is a person who will go into perdition.

BUT THEY THAT WILL BE RICH fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and PERDITION.

1 Timothy 6:9-10

Because Judas desires money so badly, he ends up in perdition. A Judas must be careful because he can easily go to hell. Being strong willed and defiant will not protect you when you approach the gates of hell. It is widely believed that Judas is in hell. This is because Jesus said that he was the son of perdition, the son that perished, the son that went to hell.

While I was with them in the world, I kept them in thy name: those that thou gavest me I have kept, and none of them is lost, but the SON OF PERDITION; that the scripture might be fulfilled.

John 17:12

6\. Judas is a person who grasps at the root of all evil.

BUT THEY THAT WILL BE RICH fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition. For the love of MONEY IS THE ROOT OF ALL EVIL: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

1 Timothy 6:9-10

Because the love of money is the root of all evil, the root of betrayal is always money. The love of money is at the root of the behaviour of betrayers.

One day, during a negotiation with some betrayers who had turned against me, a respected statesman asked these rebellious pastors what exactly they wanted from the church. (These Judas characters had set about suing the church). They answered simply, “We want money. Give us money. We want the church to pay us huge sums of money.”

When I received this report, I felt so embarrassed that pastors whom I had trained, loved and raised for so many years would fall this low. These people were fulfilling all the traits of Judas Iscariot. For the love of money, they had turned against their own church and sued the church with outrageous claims, all in the quest to get money. The desire for money makes people to turn into Judas.

7\. Judas is a person who is in error because of money.

BUT THEY THAT WILL BE RICH fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition. For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have ERRED FROM THE FAITH, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

1 Timothy 6:9-10

Error in ministry is to seriously deviate from the true path. So many of us have deviated from the right pathway. Many Judas characters who were called to the ministry are today doing something else, living in error. The error of a Judas causes him to spew out nonsensical words, insults and abuse against authority. It is the love of money that makes people deviate into such error.

8\. Judas is a person who is pierced through with sorrow.

BUT THEY THAT WILL BE RICH fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition. For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, AND PIERCED THEMSELVES THROUGH WITH MANY SORROWS.

1 Timothy 6:9-10

Every Judas regrets what he has done. The sorrow of most Judas Iscariots is that they have betrayed innocent blood. They say to themselves in their private moments, “I have fought against someone who did me no harm. I have hated my father who loved me. I have done evil to a person who was good to me. I know in my heart that he only had good plans for me.”

Then Judas, which had betrayed him, when he saw that he was condemned, repented himself, and brought again the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders, Saying, I have sinned in that I HAVE BETRAYED THE INNOCENT BLOOD. And they said, What is that to us? see thou to that. And he cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, and departed, and went and hanged himself.

Matthew 27:3-5

Judas Iscariot regretted what he had done so much that he killed himself. Judas committed suicide because he was pierced through with sorrows that he could not overcome. He had done something he could not undo.

Many Judas Iscariots regret what they have done. It makes them sad privately, even though they may say something else publicly. Apostle Paul’s prediction for lovers of money came to pass in the life of Judas. He was pierced through and through with many sorrows. Such is the portion of betrayers of trust. I have watched as those who have betrayed me have been pierced through with many sorrows as they wished that they had never harmed the person who only loved them.
