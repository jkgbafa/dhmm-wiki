### CHAPTER 6\

Judas is Someone Who Will Leave\
-----------------------------------------------------

Now this man purchased a field with the reward of iniquity; and falling headlong, he burst asunder in the midst, and all his bowels gushed out. And it was known unto all the dwellers at Jerusalem; insomuch as that field is called in their proper tongue, Aceldama, that is to say, The field of blood. For it is written in the book of Psalms, Let his habitation be desolate, and let no man dwell therein: and his bishoprick let another take.

Acts 1:18-20

Oh the tragedy of people becoming modern-day Judases! Much pain and suffering are released when one person moves out of his position and becomes a “Judas”.

Perhaps, the greatest tragedy is the waste of life and ministry that is caused by people leaving their position. Judas will waste his money, his life, his giftings and his calling. Judas will live in isolation, fruitlessness and emptiness. A Judas never bears the fruit he was supposed to bear. A Judas rarely repents! He just vanishes and disappears into oblivion. A Judas never bears the fruit he could have borne by being faithful and staying where he belonged.

Unfortunately, many of these Judas characters stay in rebellion and never actually repent. Often, Judas does not relate his problems to the fact that he should never have left his place.

1\. JUDAS IS SOMEONE WHO WILL LEAVE HIS POSITION

Men and brethren, this scripture must needs have been fulfilled, which the Holy Ghost by the mouth of David spake before concerning Judas, which was guide to them that took Jesus. For he was numbered with us, and had obtained part of this ministry. Now this man purchased a field with the reward of iniquity; and falling headlong, he burst asunder in the midst, and all his bowels gushed out. And it was known unto all the dwellers at Jerusalem; insomuch as that field is called in their proper tongue, Aceldama, that is to say, The field of blood. For it is written in the book of Psalms, LET HIS HABITATION BE DESOLATE, AND LET NO MAN DWELL THEREIN: AND HIS BISHOPRICK LET ANOTHER TAKE.

Acts 1:16-20

If God has not called you to be on your own, do not make the mistake of moving out on your own. If you make this mistake, you will be forced to re-apply for a lower position. Most high positions belong to people who have stayed faithful for many years. Satan’s dream is to bring you down. The urge to leave your God-given position is a demonic desire that comes from demon spirits seeking your downfall and disgrace.

As I write this book, I can think of so many people who exemplify this point. Their lives today are a phantom of what they would have been if they had only stayed in their positions. Judas became a news story. Judas made the headlines as a victim of suicide. What a tragedy! What a completely different destiny Judas had just because he left his place as one of the trusted disciples of Jesus Christ.

The Famous Worship Leader

One day, I put on the television and saw a famous Christian singer whom I had not seen or heard of for many years. He had produced several praise and worship albums which contained classic songs that had been sung and are still sang in our churches. Because I recognised this singer, I decided to listen to the interview. To my amazement, I discovered that this singer had fallen prey to the error of leaving his God-given position.

The singer described how he had become one of the most prominent worship leaders of all time. At the height of his fame in the ministry, he decided to leave his position. He had received invitations from a rich country offering him glorious jobs in other churches. This famous singer narrated how he believed those offers and left his position as the most senior worship leader of this very large church and followed the offers of greater glory. He narrated a pathetic story of how he had lost everything he owned.

He explained that when he got to this rich country the offers for employment did not work out as he thought. Most of the promises he had received did not materialise. He was forced to leave the new ministry and launch out on his own. He continued his narration, explaining how his ministry had descended into the darkest hole of his life. He had tried one thing after another with little success.

He confessed on television that he had made one of the greatest mistakes of his life in leaving that church where he was the number one worship leader. He described how he had experienced the loss of ministry, the loss of visibility and a lack of all things, including the lack of a basic job. This man had tried to be the “head of an ant” when he would have been better off as the “leg of an elephant.”

The tragedy of Judas is to leave your God-given position and to follow illusions!

One day, I preached in the church where this man had been a worship leader. As I sat on the front row getting ready to preach, I stared at the lady leading the worship and couldn’t help remembering the once-famous worship leader. His position had now been filled with people who did not have half of his gift. O Judas! What a waste of talent! What a waste of a God-given position.

What can cause a fruitful and successful minister to become a hustling, struggling rebel? The spirit of Judas! Moving out of your God-given position and wasting the opportunities that would have come to you because of your position is a clear manifestation of the spirit of Judas. Do you want to become the next Judas? Certainly not!

2\. JUDAS IS SOMEONE WHO WILL WALK INTO DESOLATION

Now when the even was come, he sat down with the twelve. And as they did eat, he said, Verily I say unto you, that one of you shall betray me. And they were exceeding sorrowful, and began every one of them to say unto him, Lord, is it I? And he answered and said, He that dippeth his hand with me in the dish, the same shall betray me. The Son of man goeth as it is written of him: BUT WOE UNTO THAT MAN BY WHOM THE SON OF MAN IS BETRAYED! it had been good for that man if he had not been born. Then Judas, which betrayed him, answered and said, Master, is it I? He said unto him, Thou hast said.

Matthew 26:20-25

The father of the prodigal son explained to the older brother, “All that I have is for you. I am only having this party to celebrate the return of your brother. In reality he has nothing and you have everything.”

Did you say nothing? Is it true that the returning son had nothing? Yes, nothing means nothing. Having nothing, being nothing and becoming nothing is one of the tragedies of becoming a Judas. Judas is someone who walks into desolation.

Judas Wives

I have encountered a number of ministers of the gospel whose wives turned around and betrayed their husbands. I personally know a number of pastors’ wives who divorced their husbands despite their husbands’ pleas for them not to leave and walked straight into desolation. They were not faithful to their calling and covenant to submit themselves to their husbands all the days of their lives. One wife said to her husband, “You know that I can destroy you.” Today, each and every single one of these wives is begging for money and living off the handouts and compassion of others.

I know a number of divorced pastors’ wives who have tried starting churches on their own but the churches ended up closing down after a few months. One pastor said to me, ‘My former wife is struggling to live so I support her and send her money every month through a friend of mine. He added, “She does not even know that I am the one sending the money to her.”

Why do these prominent women do this to themselves? There was no need to leave! There was no need to fight their husbands in the public domain! There was no need to attempt to start churches on their own.

3\. JUDAS IS SOMEONE WHO WILL LEAVE IMPORTANT RELATIONSHIPS

Now when the even was come, he sat down with the twelve. And as they did eat, he said, Verily I say unto you, that one of you shall betray me. And they were exceeding sorrowful, and began every one of them to say unto him, Lord, is it I? And he answered and said, He that dippeth his hand with me in the dish, the same shall betray me. The Son of man goeth as it is written of him: BUT WOE UNTO THAT MAN BY WHOM THE SON OF MAN IS BETRAYED! it had been good for that man if he had not been born. Then Judas, which betrayed him, answered and said, Master, is it I? He said unto him, Thou hast said.

Matthew 26:20-25

When Jesus said it would have been better that Judas was not born, it meant that he would have preferred never to have met Judas. After the cross, Jesus was no longer going to interact with Judas.

When you leave your God-given position, you lift up barriers between you and your spiritual father. This alters your relationship and cuts you off from the advice and inputs that you could have once received.

By leaving, Judas wasted years of relationship and advice that he could have benefited from. Perhaps this is the greatest tragedy of all; to lose the great wisdom, great fellowship and great anointing that comes through a relationship.

4\. JUDAS IS SOMEONE WHO WILL LOSE HIS FOUNDATION

And the wall of the city had twelve foundations, and in them the names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.

Revelation 21:14

Judas lost his place in the foundation of the new Jerusalem. Judas did not have his name written in the twelve foundations in the wall of the city.

There are many people who never receive a drop of spiritual inheritance because of the way they left home.

When your father is writing his will, how will he know whether you are alive or not? Why should he include you when he has not seen you for so many years? Should he leave his property to a ghost? Certainly not!

In the same way, a Judas will never receive the spiritual inheritance of the anointing.

Have you noticed that pastors who leave their spiritual fathers in rebellion never carry a certain kind of anointing?
