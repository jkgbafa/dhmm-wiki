### CHAPTER 14\

Judas Will Die the Death of the Wicked\
------------------------------------------------------------

Who can count the dust of Jacob, and the number of the fourth part of Israel? LET ME DIE THE DEATH OF THE RIGHTEOUS, AND LET MY LAST END BE LIKE HIS!

Numbers 23:10

What is the death of the wicked? The death of the wicked is to die and be turned into hell. The wicked shall be turned into hell! The wicked shall be turned into hell!

THE WICKED SHALL BE TURNED INTO HELL, and all the nations that forget God.

Psalm 9:17

The Scriptures are clear. God will throw out the people who have forgotten God. You do not want to go to Hell. Hell is reserved for those who forget God. Forgetting God is the ultimate unrighteousness of man. Judas forgot whom Jesus Christ was, as he sold Him for thirty pieces of silver. How can you sell God for thirty pieces of silver?

Judas was turned into hell. Judas Iscariot was destined for hell. Judas is called the son of perdition. The son who perishes! The son who goes to hell!

While I was with them in the world, I kept them in thy name: those that thou gavest me I have kept, and none of them is lost, but THE SON OF PERDITION; that the scripture might be fulfilled.

John 17:12

All modern day Judases should watch out! A Judas may actually end up in hell, instead of going to heaven. People who forget all the scriptures they have ever known and disobey them may be turned into hell. It is possible that pastors who have walked in total rebellion and wickedness may end up in hell instead of heaven. It remains to be seen what will be the eternal judgment of wretched betrayers.

Absalom had the spirit of Judas. Absalom died the death of the wicked.

Absalom had the spirit of Judas. Absalom was hanged on a tree and struck in his heart. Absalom died the death of the wicked.

Absalom was chased like a thief, killed by loyal troops and buried under a heap of stones.

Joab said, “I’m not going to waste any more time on you.” SO HE GOT THREE JAVELINS. THEN HE WENT OVER AND PLUNGED THEM INTO ABSALOM’S HEART. HE DID IT WHILE ABSALOM WAS STILL HANGING THERE ALIVE IN THE OAK TREE. TEN OF THE MEN CARRYING JOAB’S ARMOR SURROUNDED ABSALOM. THEY STRUCK HIM AND KILLED HIM. Then Joab blew his trumpet. He ordered his troops to stop chasing Israel’s army. Joab’s men threw Absalom into a big pit in the forest. They covered him with a large pile of rocks. While all of that was going on, all the Israelites ran back to their homes.

2 Samuel 18:14-17 NIRV

There is something called “The death of the wicked” and “the death of the righteous”. Judas did not die the death of the righteous because he was a wicked betrayer.

Judas died prematurely. Judas died before the person he betrayed. Judas did not live long enough to enjoy the thirty pieces of silver.

All Judas-like people will also die the death of the wicked. Expect to die the death of a wicked man if you are a Judas.

Say unto them, As I live, saith the Lord GOD, I HAVE NO PLEASURE IN THE DEATH OF THE WICKED; but that the wicked turn from his way and live: turn ye, turn ye from your evil ways; for why will ye die, O house of Israel?’

Ezekiel 33:11

Then Judas, which had betrayed him, when he saw that he was condemned, repented himself, and brought again the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders, Saying, I have sinned in that I have betrayed the innocent blood. And they said, What is that to us? See thou to that. And he cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, and departed, AND WENT AND HANGED HIMSELF.

Matthew 27:3-5
