### CHAPTER 2\

You Are Good for Nothing If You Do Not Prevent Corruption\
===============================================================================

Ye are the salt of the earth: but IF THE SALT HAVE LOST HIS SAVOUR, wherewith shall it be salted? IT IS THENCEFORTH GOOD FOR NOTHING, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men. Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid. Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

Matthew 5:13-1

Salt preserves food! Salt is a preservative! Salt is good for nothing when it does not act as a preservative and prevent corruption any more.

When salt does not prevent the spread of evil corruption it is useless. The Christian’s presence is to prevent the spread of corruption in the world. Your presence must prevent the spread of the corruption, sexual perversions and wickedness in the world.

According as his divine power hath given unto us all things that pertain unto life and godliness, through the knowledge of him that hath called us to glory and virtue: Whereby are given unto us exceeding great and precious promises: that by these ye might be partakers of the divine nature, HAVING ESCAPED THE CORRUPTION THAT IS IN THE WORLD THROUGH LUST.

2 Peter 1:3-4

Through the lusts and desires of men, much corruption is spread about in the world. The whole world is full of dishonesty, treachery, depravity, perversions, decadence and immorality. These things are found everywhere. Through the gospel of Jesus Christ and through your life, you are preventing the spread of this evil corruption.

How to Prevent the Spread of Corruption

According as his divine power hath given unto us all things that pertain unto life and godliness, through the knowledge of him that hath called us to glory and virtue: Whereby are given unto us exceeding great and precious promises: that by these ye might be partakers of the divine nature, HAVING ESCAPED THE CORRUPTION THAT IS IN THE WORLD THROUGH LUST.

2 Peter 1:3-4

The scripture above shows us that through the knowledge of God, we have escaped the corruption that is in the world. It is therefore important that we prevent the spread of corruption in the world by spreading the knowledge of God. Teaching the word of God and teaching the promises of God prevent all manner of financial, spiritual and moral corruption in the world. Spreading the knowledge of God is important in preventing and curing the corruption that is in the world. When people are filled with the word of God, depravity and crookedness go away. It is only through the promises of God that you can escape the corruption that is in the world.

1\. Spread the knowledge of God and prevent corruption by preaching the gospel. You must preach the gospel to prevent the spread of corruption.

Without the preaching of the gospel, people will not be saved. It is only people who are saved by the power of God who escape corruption. As salvation spreads throughout the nations, corruption is also prevented from spreading.

2\. Spread the knowledge of God and prevent corruption by teaching the word of God. You must teach the word of God to prevent the spread of corruption.

As teachings of the word of God go forth, people are transformed by the renewing of their minds. Transformation takes place when people hear the word of God. People are transformed by the rebukes, the correction, the instruction and the reproof that come from the word of God.

And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

Romans 12:2

3\. Spread the knowledge of God by living pure and preventing corruption. You must live pure to prevent the spread of corruption.

As you live pure, you set an example in the world. Your example is a shining light in a very dark world. You prevent the spread of darkness by your lifestyle.

Do all things without murmurings and disputings: That ye may be blameless and harmless, the sons of God, without rebuke, IN THE MIDST OF A CROOKED AND PERVERSE NATION, AMONG WHOM YE SHINE AS LIGHTS IN THE WORLD;

Philippians 2:14-15

4\. You must spread the knowledge of God about sex in order to prevent the spread of corrupted sexual perversions. God has a lot to say about sex and sexual sin. The word of God is not silent about sexual corruption. God has given rules for human beings to live by. As we teach God’s word about sex and sexuality, it will prevent the spread of abnormal variants and sexual perversions.

Wherefore God also gave them up to uncleanness through the lusts of their own hearts, to dishonour their own bodies between themselves: … For this cause God gave them up unto vile affections: for even their women did change the natural use into that which is against nature: And likewise also the men, leaving the natural use of the woman, burned in their lust one toward another; men with men working that which is unseemly, and receiving in themselves that recompence of their error which was meet.

Romans 1:24, 26-27

As varied sexual perversions and alternative lifestyles spread as legitimate practices throughout the world, nations have become more perverted and corrupted. The church must stand strong on the word of God and reject the corruption that is spreading. You must refuse to be pushed around by sick men who want to super-impose their depraved lifestyles on the rest of the world. The word of God prevents corruption. The word of God grants divine escapes from corruption.
