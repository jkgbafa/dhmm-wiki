### CHAPTER 9\

You Are Good for Nothing If You Do Not Bring Healing\
==========================================================================

YE ARE THE SALT OF THE EARTH: BUT IF THE SALT HAVE LOST HIS SAVOUR, WHEREWITH SHALL IT BE SALTED? IT IS THENCEFORTH GOOD FOR NOTHING, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men. Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid. Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

Matthew 5:13-16

Salt is used as medicine! Salt must be used to bring healing. Natural salt is one of the most healing substances on earth. Just as salt is used to bring health and healing to mankind, Christians must become a source of health and healing to this world. If we are not a source of health and healing to the world, we have lost our savour and have become good for nothing.

How Salt Brings Healing

1. Salt neutralizes acid: Salt neutralizes and reduces acids in our body that are associated with life-threatening diseases. If the body’s pH level is either too acidic or too alkaline, the body is at risk for disease to take hold.

2. Salt helps with weight loss: The salt water flush, also known as the colon cleanse, is used to assist the digestive system. This forcefully flushes out the content in your colon to prevent any toxins or unwanted material from sitting in your system for too long.

3. Salt relieves skin conditions: Many skin conditions such as eczema, psoriasis, acne can be helped by a salt bath. The minerals in the salt deeply cleanse the pores, kill bacteria, and detoxify the skin.

4. Salt helps maintain our cells: Salt is the main reason our cells are able to function! Salt has 92 trace minerals, and 24 of them are proven to play a role in our health. The lack of some of these elements can lead to cell damage and cause muscle spasms, brain damage, and nervous disorders.

5. Salt relieves joint pains: Epsom salt contains two important elements: magnesium and sulphate. These elements help the muscles and nerves in your body to function properly. Salt baths are a great remedy to reduce joint pain and arthritis. While magnesium has anti-inflammatory properties, sulphate promotes the absorption of nutrients into the skin.

Be the Salt of the World by Bringing Healing to this World

1\. Become a source of healing by praying for the sick.

The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised,

Luke 4:18

You can become a source of healing by praying for the sick. God is interested in the sick and the suffering people of this world. Through your prayers, healing and deliverance will come to many people. On many occasions I have felt the Lord telling me to pray for the sick. Healing is on the heart of God! Jesus said that He was anointed to heal the broken-hearted. We must believe in the ministry of healing. It is a real ministry! Many people are comforted when hands are laid on them.

2\. Become a source of healing by building hospitals and practicing medicine.

The harvest is past, the summer is ended, and we are not saved. For the hurt of the daughter of my people am I hurt; I am black; astonishment hath taken hold on me. Is there no balm in Gilead; IS THERE NO PHYSICIAN THERE? Why then is not the health of the daughter of my people recovered?

Jeremiah 8:20-22

Building hospitals and practicing medicine is also God’s mercy to mankind. When Adam and Eve fell into sin, God mercifully showed them how to make clothes out of leaves. God helped fallen man to climb out of the hole into which he had fallen. God wants to show mercy to mankind by ministering through hospitals, doctors and nurses. The science which makes the practice of medicine possible is also the gift of God.
