### CHAPTER 3\

You Are Good for Nothing If You Do Not Preach Properly\
============================================================================

YE ARE THE SALT OF THE EARTH: BUT IF THE SALT HAVE LOST HIS SAVOUR, WHEREWITH SHALL IT BE SALTED? IT IS THENCEFORTH GOOD FOR NOTHING, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men. Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid. Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

Matthew 5:13-16

Salt makes food acceptable, tasty and palatable! Salt is good for nothing if it does not make food more palatable and enjoyable to us. The food we have to give is the word of God.

When there is no salt in this food, it is uneatable. A believer is to make the word of God more palatable. The world cannot receive the word of God because it is often difficult to understand and difficult to believe.

How to be the Salt of the Earth and Make the Word of God Acceptable

1\. You are the salt of the earth, so make the word of God acceptable by preaching properly. When you preach well, people understand what you are saying and light and understanding are imparted to them. It is important the word of God enters people.

The entrance of thy words giveth light; it giveth understanding unto the simple.

Psalm 119:130

I can remember different occasions in my life when I tried to eat saltless food. I stopped eating almost immediately. There is saltless rice, saltless chicken and saltless meat. Try eating meat that has not been well salted or seasoned. You will find out how uneatable it is! The world is looking for good preachers who can preach the word of God in such a way that they will be saved.

Develop the art of preaching and teaching until you are an expert in delivering the word of God. People must be saved and changed when you preach the word of God. When changes come to people’s lives through your preaching and teaching, you are truly being the salt of the earth.

2\. You are the salt of the earth, so make the word of God acceptable by removing every offensive element in your preaching and teaching ministry.

Some years ago, we conducted crusades in various towns and villages. We would arrive, set up the crusade and minister powerfully for three or four nights. One day, my mother-in-law told me that we had offended the chiefs in the town because we had not paid a courtesy call to them. I discovered that my preaching was offensive in these towns because I had not paid a courtesy call to the traditional rulers. From that time on, we adjusted our schedule and would pay a courtesy call to all the city and traditional authorities. By doing this, I was making the word of God more acceptable to the people of that town.

One day, I preached at a place where it was offensive for a pastor to whistle. On another occasion, I preached in a town where it was unacceptable to point at anyone with the left hand. I also preached at a place where it was offensive for a man to wear a long shirt because it made him look like a woman in their eyes. Once I noted all these rules, I made extra efforts to avoid them. I learnt to make my message as acceptable as possible. What can you do to make your preaching more palatable?

3\. You are the salt of the earth, so make the word of God acceptable by teaching in an orderly fashion. Some people do not like preaching that has no structure. If you have to introduce some points into the preaching to make it easier for someone to receive, you must do so.

4\. You are the salt of the earth, so make the word of God acceptable by showing how the prophecies from the word of God have come to pass.

We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place, until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts:

2 Peter 1:19

Jesus Christ fulfilled many scriptures when He walked among us. Everything about Jesus was written hundreds of years before He lived. It was predicted that He would die amongst thieves and be buried with the rich. No one controls his own death and burial. Yet Jesus fulfilled the little details concerning His own death and burial. Many Jews have turned to Christ because of the irrefutable evidence from their own Holy Book. Fulfilled prophecies help to make the Bible more palatable.

I became more interested in the Bible as I saw the prophecies and predictions coming to pass. God’s Word is amazing! One day I saw a young lady fulfilling a prophecy in the book of James. I was amazed that she had fulfilled the scripture to the uttermost. I have watched rebellious people fulfil scriptures time and time again. I have watched ungrateful and disloyal people magically fulfil the scriptures. Each time, I have been amazed at how true the word of God is. When you point out to people how true scriptures are, it helps them to receive the Bible as an awesome book.

5\. You are the salt of the earth, so make the word of God acceptable by showing that the word of God does not conflict with science. The word of God does not conflict with science. I am a medical doctor. I studied science from Form One up to sixth form and for another seven years in medical school. That makes a total of fourteen years studying science. It is possible to teach and demonstrate that scientific values complement the word of God and do not contradict it.

6\. You are the salt of the earth so make the word of God acceptable by preaching with signs, wonders, miracles and healings.

And a great multitude followed him, because they saw his miracles which he did on them that were diseased.

John 6:2

There are many people who are not interested in your orderly sermon with many points. Such people are convinced by miracles, signs and wonders. You can make the word of God more palatable by ministering with manifestations of the Holy Spirit.
