### CHAPTER 8\

You Are Good for Nothing If You Are Not Used for De-Icing\
===============================================================================

YE ARE THE SALT OF THE EARTH: BUT IF THE SALT HAVE LOST HIS SAVOUR, WHEREWITH SHALL IT BE SALTED? IT IS THENCEFORTH GOOD FOR NOTHING, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men. Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid. Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

Matthew 5:13-16

One of the important uses of salt is to remove ice.Removal of ice from roads in cold temperate zones is an important use of salt. De-icing salt is a particularly effective mixture of very pure, fine and coarse salt crystals. While the fine crystals initiate an immediate de-icing effect, the coarser crystals guarantee the necessary long-term effect of removing thicker layers of snow and ice. Salt is good for nothing if it is not used for de-icing the roads.

God wants to use every Christian to remove the icy, cold and backslidden spirit from the church. The Christian who is the salt of the world must be used to remove the stubbornness and coldness that characterize backslidden churches.

The church must be full of zealous Christians who are on fire for the Lord. Your presence must bring the fire of God. Through your words and your zeal, fire and heat will be introduced to the church and all the icy blocks will melt away.

Today the church is gradually being filled with people who are ice cold and without zeal. All Christians who are the salt of the world will be used to remove icy, uninterested attitudes in the church.

How to Remove Ice

1\. Remove ice by the baptism of the Holy Ghost.

John answered, saying unto them all, I indeed baptize you with water; but one mightier than I cometh, the latchet of whose shoes I am not worthy to unloose: he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost and with fire:

Luke 3:16

The only way to remove ice is to be zealous for the Lord. Jesus Christ was full of zeal. He came to baptize the church with the Holy Ghost and with fire. The baptism of the Holy Spirit changes a Christian’s life. Instead of living a cool, uninspiring life, the baptism of the Holy Spirit will lift you up to speak in tongues and become a powerful preacher like Peter on the day of Pentecost.

And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance.

Acts 2:4

2\. Remove ice by the baptism of fire.

And the Jews’ passover was at hand, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem, And found in the temple those that sold oxen and sheep and doves, and the changers of money sitting: And when he had made a scourge of small cords, he drove them all out of the temple, and the sheep, and the oxen; and poured out the changers’ money, and overthrew the tables; And said unto them that sold doves, Take these things hence; make not my Father’s house an house of merchandise. And his disciples remembered that it was written, The zeal of thine house hath eaten me up.

John 2:13-17

The baptism of fire speaks of the baptism of zeal. Jesus Christ came to baptise us with fire and zeal. The zeal of the Lord speaks of the enthusiasm and diligence which was found in Jesus. It is so sad to see unenthusiastic priests going about their priestly duties without any passion whatsoever.

In the scripture above, we see the fire of God and the zeal of Jesus. He was not content with giving a simple Bible lesson to the thieves who had occupied the church. He whipped them! Jesus whipped traders who had set up shop in the church and drove them out. Perhaps it was unorthodox and unacceptable for Jesus to whip these grown-ups who had occupied the church; but God’s Word shows us that the zeal of the Lord was what really mattered. God saw the whipping of the traders as an act of zeal and not as a manifestation of human rage.
