### CHAPTER 4\

You Are Good for Nothing If You Do Not Destroy Invisible Demonic Powers\
=============================================================================================

YE ARE THE SALT of the earth: but if the salt have lost his savour, wherewith shall it be salted? IT IS THENCEFORTH GOOD FOR NOTHING, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men. Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid. Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

Matthew 5:13-16

Germs are invisible enemies! The corona virus for example, is an invisible enemy. Salt kills germs! Salt deals with the invisible enemies of our lives. If you are the salt of the earth, you will constantly deal with the invisible enemies of our world. Salt is used as an antiseptic and is able to kill and neutralize many germs. Salt is therefore good for nothing if it does not act as antiseptic that kills germs. When Christians who are the salt of the earth do not deal with the invisible enemies of the world, then they have lost their saltiness and are useless and unprofitable.

Demons are invisible, spiritual enemies that fight against human beings. Just as germs cause diseases, plagues and problems for human beings, demons also cause diseases, plagues and pandemics. When Christians fail in the fight against demons they are failing to be salt! We must fight the invisible enemies in the world.

How to be the Salt of the Earth and Destroy Invisible Enemies

1. You are the salt of the earth so you must destroy invisible demonic strongholds through prayer. If you do not rise up in prayer, you are not being the salt of the earth. Your presence on earth is felt through the prayers that you offer up. Your powerful prayers make you the salt of the earth. As you pray for hours you are destroying the works of darkness. Every hour of prayer that goes up from your house is pumping salt into the system. The salt is inhibiting the growth of germs. The salt is inhibiting the activities of demons.

Or else how can one enter into a strong man’s house, and spoil his goods, except he first bind the strong man? And then he will spoil his house.

Matthew 12:29

2\. You are the salt of the earth so you must destroy invisible demonic strongholds by casting down evil thoughts. If you do not cast down imaginations and ideologies that are against the word of God, you are not being the salt of the earth. As you cast down imaginations you are destroying the works of darkness. Every effort you make to cast down evil thoughts is pumping salt into the system. The salt is inhibiting the growth of germs. The salt is inhibiting the activities of demons.

(For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds;) Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ;

2 Corinthians 10:4-5

3\. You are the salt of the earth so you must destroy invisible demonic strongholds by using the word of God.

The word of God is an invisible sword that strikes to the heart of the enemy. If you do not rise up and preach the word of God, you are not being the salt of the earth. As you preach the word of God, you are destroying the works of darkness. Every sermon that goes out from you is pumping salt into the system. Every book that you write is pumping salt into the system. The salty sermons are inhibiting the activity of demons. The salty books and writings are blocking the activities of devils.

For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.

Hebrews 4:12
