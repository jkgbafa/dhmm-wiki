### CHAPTER 11\

What Happens When You Are Good for Nothing?\
=================================================================

YE ARE THE SALT OF THE EARTH: BUT IF THE SALT HAVE LOST HIS SAVOUR, WHEREWITH SHALL IT BE SALTED? IT IS THENCEFORTH GOOD FOR NOTHING, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men. Ye are the light of the world.

Matthew 5:13-14

1\. Those who are good for nothing will be cut down.

He spake also this parable; A certain man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard; and he came and sought fruit thereon, and found none. Then said he unto the dresser of his vineyard, Behold, these three years I come seeking fruit on this fig tree, and find none: cut it down; WHY CUMBERETH IT THE GROUND? And he answering said unto him, Lord, let it alone this year also, till I shall dig about it, and dung it: And if it bear fruit, well: and if not, then after that thou shalt cut it down.

Luke 13:6-9

God is not going to sit around and allow you, a good for nothing brother, to occupy space. In the scripture above, Jesus pointed out that it is not appropriate to leave a good for nothing tree standing in the middle of the garden forever. It must be cut down and replaced. It is my prayer that this short exhortation will help you to jump out of every good for nothing status that you have acquired for yourself. Being nothing in the church and doing nothing in the church makes you good for nothing.

2\. Those who are good for nothing will be replaced.

Esther was warned not to be good for nothing! Esther had been elevated to the palace by the grace of God. She was expected to play her part in the palace. God is expecting you to play your part. You cannot sit in the palace and be good for nothing. What is the use of your being in the palace when we cannot call on you? Do you think you have been elevated to the palace so you can buy fancy clothes and attend endless ceremonies and parties? What happens to the rest of us? Did God make a mistake in promoting you to that high position in the palace?

Then Mordecai commanded to answer Esther, Think not with thyself that thou shalt escape in the king’s house, more than all the Jews. For if thou altogether holdest thy peace at this time, then shall there enlargement and deliverance arise to the Jews from another place; but thou and thy father’s house shall be destroyed: and who knoweth whether thou art come to the kingdom for such a time as this?

Esther 4:13-14

3\. Those who are good for nothing will be thrown into outer darkness.

I know a country whose citizens always complain when the electricity goes off. People hate darkness! Darkness induces fear! Endless darkness induces endless fear. Be careful you are not thrown into outer darkness.

And cast ye the unprofitable servant into outer darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.

Matthew 25:30

4\. Those who are good for nothing will be cursed.

Curses await those who are good for nothing. The fig tree Jesus cursed was good for nothing. No one eats fig leaves and fig branches. Once there was no fruit on the fig tree, it was cursed. If Jesus comes to you seeking fruit and finds none, you can only expect a curse. The lack of fruit in your life makes you good for nothing.

And on the morrow, when they were come from Bethany, he was hungry: And seeing a fig tree afar off having leaves, he came, if haply he might find any thing thereon: and when he came to it, he found nothing but leaves; for the time of figs was not yet. And Jesus answered and said unto it, No man eat fruit of thee hereafter for ever. And his disciples heard it.

Mark 11:12-14

5\. Those who are good for nothing will be poor.

The prodigal son turned himself into a good for nothing and worthless son. He was condemned to poverty and living with pigs. If you make yourself into a good for nothing son, you will be condemned to lack and endless searching for things which you would have enjoyed automatically.

And he said, a certain man had two sons: And the younger of them said to his father, Father, give me the portion of goods that falleth to me. And he divided unto them his living. And not many days after the younger son gathered all together, and took his journey into a far country, and there wasted his substance with riotous living. And when he had spent all, there arose a mighty famine in that land; and he began to be in want.

Luke 15:11-14

6\. Those who are good for nothing will be eaten by “the birds”.

Unfortunately, many seeds that are sown, turn out to be good for nothing. If you make yourself into a good for nothing seed, you will be eaten by the birds. The birds speak of the devil coming to feed on wasted seeds that have fallen by the wayside. The devil makes good for nothing seeds his food. Do you want the devil to make you his food? Do not allow the devil to feed on you.

Good for nothing seeds are also choked by thorns. Thorns descend on good for nothing seeds and choke out the very life from them. Some of the good for nothing seeds wither away under the heat of the sun. People who are good for nothing are sentenced to wither and to fade away into oblivion.

A sower went out to sow his seed: and as he sowed, some fell by the way side; and it was trodden down, and the fowls of the air devoured it. And some fell upon a rock; and as soon as it was sprung up, it withered away, because it lacked moisture. And some fell among thorns; and the thorns sprang up with it, and choked it. And other fell on good ground, and sprang up, and bare fruit an hundredfold. And when he had said these things, he cried, He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.

Luke 8:5-8

Conclusion

To the making of many books there is no end!

It is my prayer that, with these few words, you will be encouraged to give yourself wholly to God’s purpose of making you fruitful and useful in His house. From the beginning of creation, as recorded in the book of Genesis, God blessed man to be fruitful. Fruitfulness has always been the purpose of God for mankind. Being “good for nothing” is the exact opposite of God’s will for your life.

Through this book, you are being restored to your original purpose. You are turning out to be the salt of the earth that has not lost its savour! You will always be useful to God! You will always be needed in this world!

Salt is always needed and used by men. Therefore you will always be needed and useful in the house of God.

Beginning from now, your usefulness and fruitfulness will increase every day! No one will ever be able to call you “good for nothing”!
