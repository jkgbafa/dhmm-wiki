### CHAPTER 5\

You Are Good for Nothing If You Do Not Do Enough\
======================================================================

YE ARE THE SALT of the earth: but if the salt have lost his savour, wherewith shall it be salted? IT IS THENCEFORTH GOOD FOR NOTHING, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men. Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid. Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

Matthew 5:13-16

There may be salt in the food but if the salt is not enough the food is still not tasty. Salt is good for nothing when it is not enough! There may be salt but it must be enough if it is to have any effect. It is important that salt makes its “presence” felt.

If you are the salt of the earth there are several things that you must do enough of to make your presence felt. You must put enough salt in the rice if you want to change the flavour of the rice. You must put enough salt into stew if you want the stew to taste nice. You must put enough salt into meat if you want the meat to be eatable. God is speaking to you about doing enough. You must work hard until you have done more than enough. You must put in more effort and more strength until you have done so much for God.

Six Things You Need to Do Enough of

1\. You must pray enough if it is to have an effect on the world. You are the salt of the earth so you must pray enough.

When salt is not enough it simply does not have the effect that it should have. Jesus prayed for three hours. Jesus prayed all night. Jesus prayed for forty days and nights in the wilderness. Jesus wondered why His disciples could not pray for just one hour. Clearly, if you do not pray enough it will not have the necessary impact on the world.

2\. You must preach enough if it is to have an effect on the world. You are the salt of the earth so you must preach enough.

When salt is not enough it simply does not have the effect that it could have. Preaching the word of God has a great effect on the world. However, there needs to be enough of preaching before it truly impacts a nation. You will notice that many nations are filled with wickedness, even though there are powerful preachers in those nations. Indeed, there is preaching but often there is not enough preaching. Crusades need to be held repeatedly for them to have an effect on a city. Having one crusade every twenty-five years is not going to change anything. If you analyse the nations that have been visited repeatedly by evangelists, you will notice that they are quite different from countries that have never had a visit.

3\. You must write and publish enough books if they are to have an effect on the world.

When salt is not enough it simply does not have the effect that it could have. The Bible has a great impact and effect on the world because it has been printed and published enough. The Bible has been printed and published more than any other book and that is why it has had the impact it has. If the Bible were not published enough, it would never have the effect that it has.

Authors who have written many books are the ones who make an impact. Authors like Kenneth Hagin, Derek Prince and Tim LaHaye have written so many books that they have had a great impact on the world. There are other Christian authors who have written one or two books. Often, these books do not have a certain salty effect on the world. When salt is not enough it simply does not have the effect that it could have.

4\. You must plant and build enough churches if they are to have an effect on the world.

When salt is not enough it simply does not have the effect that it could have. One church in your city is not going to have the effect that it needs to have. To have an effect on that city, you need to have several churches in all the different corners of the city. If there is salt but the salt is not enough, it is as though there is no salt. When the church is present in a city but does not have enough of a presence, it still does not influence the city.

5\. You must give enough if it is to have an effect on the world.

When salt is not enough it simply does not have the effect that it could have. It is important to give at a certain level if you are to make a certain impact with your giving. There are many givers whose giving does not make a great enough impact on the church. King David made a huge offering towards the building of the temple. This offering was so great that it is recorded in the Bible.

Moreover, because I have set my affection to the house of my God, I have of mine own proper good, of gold and silver, which I have given to the house of my God, over and above all that I have prepared for the holy house.

1 Chronicles 29:3

King Solomon also made a substantial offering when the temple was dedicated. I am sure many other kings gave offerings towards the temple but their offerings were not big enough to make an impact on the ministry.

Also king Solomon, and all the congregation of Israel that were assembled unto him before the ark, sacrificed sheep and oxen, which could not be told nor numbered for multitude.

2 Chronicles 5:6

6\. You must travel enough if it is to have any effect on the world.

When salt is not enough it simply does not have the effect that it could have. John Wesley travelled a lot and that is why he made the impact that he did. Billy Graham travelled all over the world and that is why his ministry had a great effect on the world. Reinhard Bonnke travelled all over Africa and that is why he had such a great effect on Africa.
