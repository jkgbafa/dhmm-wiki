### CHAPTER 7\

You Are Good for Nothing If You Do Not Produce Oil\
========================================================================

YE ARE THE SALT OF THE EARTH: BUT IF THE SALT HAVE LOST HIS SAVOUR, WHEREWITH SHALL IT BE SALTED? IT IS THENCEFORTH GOOD FOR NOTHING, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men. Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid. Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

Matthew 5:13-16

Salt is used to produce oil! Salt is used in the oil industry and is a very important ingredient in the production of oil.

Industrial salt is used as a component of drilling fluids, primarily in the exploration and extraction of crude oil and natural gas.

Salt makes an essential contribution to the stability of the drill hole and to the lubrication and cooling of the drill head. Salt is also an important raw material for downstream steps in the processing of crude oil or for industrial water treatment.

Salt is good for nothing when it cannot be used to produce oil.

In the same way, Christians who are the salt of the earth are expected to produce and release the oil and the anointing to this world. The anointing oil will break the yokes that keep people in bondage.

When Christians do not “produce the anointing” they are good for nothing. The anointing speaks of the gift of God and the grace of God for ministry. You must become a source of oil today. You must be a good example of the salt of the earth. You must not allow yourself to be good for nothing.

How to become a Source of Oil

And I will give power unto my two witnesses, and they shall prophesy a thousand two hundred and threescore days, clothed in sackcloth. THESE ARE THE TWO OLIVE TREES, and the two candlesticks standing before the God of the earth.

Revelation 11:3-4

1\. You will become a source of oil when you become a witness of the Lord Jesus Christ.

In the scripture above, witnesses of Jesus Christ were called olive trees. The most anointed people in the world are those who speak about Jesus Christ. Today, many people are preaching on secular topics in the church. The words of Jesus are not preached about very much today. Secularised motivational speakers have filled the pulpits. This is the reason why there is very little anointing flowing out of God’s ministers. It is witnesses of Jesus Christ who are the real olive trees.

You can become a source of oil by simply having the life of an olive tree. God sees you as an olive tree. The two witnesses in the book of Revelation ministered by shutting up heaven and manifesting many signs and wonders in the earth. They were also called olive trees. An olive tree is a source of oil. The witnesses of Jesus Christ will produce much anointing in the last days.

2\. You will become a source of oil when you become a prophet of the Lord Jesus Christ.

You can become a source of oil by simply being an olive tree. God sees you as an olive tree. A prophet is an olive tree! An olive tree is a source of oil. You can be a prophet of God and be a source of oil and anointing. A prophet is someone who speaks the mind of God. The two prophets in the book of Revelation ministered by shutting up heaven and manifesting many signs and wonders in the earth.

3\. You will become a source of oil when you survive “the shaking”.

When thus it shall be in the midst of the land among the people, there shall be as the shaking of an olive tree, and as the gleaning grapes when the vintage is done.

Isaiah 24:13

You can become a source of oil by simply having the life of an olive tree. God sees you as an olive tree. A good olive tree is shaken every year. Olives are harvested from an olive tree by shaking the tree. These olives are then the source of the precious oil. There are many shakings that are going to come your way. If you survive the shaking, you will become a source of oil to the world. Just knowing a few scriptures will not make you a source of oil. Being appointed as a pastor will not make you a source of oil. Surviving the shaking that will come will cause you to be a source of oil.

4\. You will become a source of oil when you survive “the beating”.

When thou beatest thine olive tree, thou shalt not go over the boughs again: it shall be for the stranger, for the fatherless, and for the widow.

Deuteronomy 24:20

You can become a source of oil by simply having the life of an olive tree. God sees you as an olive tree. A good olive tree is beaten every year! Olives are harvested from a tree by beating the tree. These beaten olives are then the source of the precious oil. The beating of the olive tree speaks of persecution that will come to God’s servant. You can expect to have severe beatings from time to time in order to extract the anointing from you. Expect many beatings, persecutions, painful trials and difficulties. That is the life of an olive tree. Every olive tree receives many beatings in its lifetime. If you survive the beating, you will become a source of oil to the world.

5\. You will become a source of oil when you survive many years.

It takes about fifteen years for an olive tree to bear its first fruits. Most olive trees reach the age of between three hundred to six hundred years. Older olive trees have survived forest fires, natural disasters and the emergence of human civilizations.

Olive trees are some of the oldest trees in the world. There are many olive trees that are said to be thousands of years old. The oldest olive tree in the world is over three thousand years old!

To become an olive tree, you must be someone who can survive many years in the ministry. It is going to take a long standing survivor to produce olive oil. Are you ready to survive many years in the ministry? Are you ready to go through forest fires, natural disasters and human invasions? Are you ready to survive shaking and beating? Will you be around after many years so you can produce oil for many more years to come?
