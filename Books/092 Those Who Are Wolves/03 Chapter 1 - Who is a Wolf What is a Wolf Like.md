### Chapter 1\

Who is a Wolf? What is a Wolf Like?\
---------------------------------------------------------

Wherefore a lion out of the forest shall slay them, AND A WOLF OF THE EVENINGS SHALL SPOIL THEM, a leopard shall watch over their cities: every one that goeth out thence shall be torn in pieces: because their transgressions are many, and their backslidings are increased.

1\. A wolf is someone who will spoil the church. “A wolf of the evenings shall spoil them.”

Wolves spoil many farms. Wild wolves cause a lot of trouble for farmers and villagers by attacking the flocks, killing the sheep and even sometimes, killing human beings. That is why they are hated by farmers and villagers.

Apostle Paul warns the church about the trouble they will have when the wolves come. A wolf does not care about the trouble he causes within the flock. He will not spare anyone. He will attack no matter what. He is after blood! A wolf is someone who attacks the sheep in the church, never sparing the flock.

Wolves attack sheep, farms and livestock. Wolves will not spare the flock, and they will eventually cause financial loss to the farmer.

The behaviour of evil men matches the behaviour of wild wolves. If you study the characteristics of wolves born in the wild, you will see that wolves will not lose an opportunity to kill and to destroy defenseless sheep. As scripture says, “They will not spare the flock”.

Spiritual wolves are men who do not spare the flock. They kill, eat and destroy the sheep without caring for their souls. Real wild wolves do not care about what they do to sheep. They just eat them up and devour their very lives. After you have encountered a real spiritual ministerial wolf, you can never be the same.

Wolves spoil everything! That is why they must be eliminated. Wolves can and must be eliminated. In the past, France, Spain, Italy, the United Kingdom, and most European states took action against wolves. The hostility against wolves was mainly because of their attacks on domestic livestock. Up until the 19th century, wolves destroyed livestock and were harmful to many sectors of the economy. Anyone who took the menace of wolves lightly did so to his own destruction. Wolves damaged farms so much that they affected the economy of Europe.

For I know this, that after my departing shall grievous wolves enter in among you, not sparing the flock.

Acts 20:29

2\. A wolf is someone who is independent. Wild wolves are independent of human beings.

Dogs submit to human beings. Wolves will not submit to a man!

A wolf has an independent spirit. Spiritual wolves cannot depend on or submit to anyone. Even if a spiritual wolf seems to submit, it can only do so for a short while. Soon, they reveal their true selves. Dogs, on the other hand are often dependent on men.

Unlike dogs, wolves live independent lives and do not depend on man for anything. Wolves are independent creatures! Wolves are independent of man! Wolves do not want or need human company. Wolves live independently and fend for themselves. They do not depend on the men who live in the forests. Wolves do not need humans to survive: Wolves cannot submit to anything or anyone!

Watch out for men who are independent and do not ever seem to blend in or require the fellowship of others. God’s word teaches us to be submitted and dependent on one another, rather than living independently.

Submitting yourselves one to another in the fear of God.

Ephesians 5:21

3\. A wolf is someone who cannot be trained or made into a loyal and faithful person. Wild wolves cannot be trained or domesticated.

Train up a child in the way he should go … (Proverbs 22:6)! A human being can be trained! Apostle Paul also taught us in 2 Timothy 2:2 to train other disciples. “And the things that thou hast heard of me among many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also” (2 Timothy 2:2).

However, wolves cannot be trained or re-trained! Wolves cannot be trained or domesticated. You are wasting your time trying to make a wolf into a disciple. Jesus came to train us and change us. Unfortunately, wolves cannot be trained or retrained!

Wolves cannot be made into pets! Unlike dogs, wolves have not been domesticated for thousands of years. No matter how you love, feed and care for wolves, they cannot be made into loving pets like dogs. In other words, something within a wolf prevents it from being grateful, loving and kind to someone who has only shown it love and care for many years.

Wolves can cause grave injury to humans, especially as they mature. Dogs, on the other hand, can become great companions!

Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.

Proverbs 22:6

Can a wolf be tamed? This is a frequently asked question. Scientists have confirmed through genetic testing that wolves and domestic dogs are quite similar. In spite of this, wolves are very difficult to tame. With such a similar genetic background, scientists have long wondered why wolves tend to stay wild, while dogs gladly assume the role of being man’s best friend. Why is this so?

a\. Wolves are non-responsive to training: Dogs are generally more responsive to training because they are motivated by social rewards, such as, praise and attention from their human handlers. Wolves are more independent and less motivated by social rewards, making them hard to train.

b\. Wolves challenge authority and are territorial: Wolves tend to be more aggressive and territorial than dogs. They are also more likely to exhibit behaviours that are difficult to manage in a domestic setting, such as marking territory and challenging authority.

c\. Wolves live in packs without humans: Wolves operate in packs which do not include human beings. Dogs, on the other hand, have been bred for years to live and work closely with humans. Dogs are highly social and eager to please humans. Wolves, on the other hand, are less inclined to see humans as leaders or companions.

d\. Wolves have the instincts of predators: Wolves have strong predatory instincts and a natural wariness of humans. Instinctively, wolves do not cooperate with humans, whereas dogs cooperate with humans.

e\. Wolves are highly suspicious: Wild wolves are highly suspicious of human beings, making them difficult to be trained.

4\. A wolf is someone who is fearful and suspicious. Wolves are filled with fear, and do not have the trusting eyes of a domestic dog.

Unlike dogs, wolves interpret the love and kindness of humans differently. They are truly suspicious and vicious animals. Their response to great love and kindness is to attack. Wolves do not trust and accept humans as their leaders. Wolves do not believe that any good thing will come out of being submitted to men. They move away from men and carve out an isolated existence, keeping their distance, out of suspicion and fear.

5\. A wolf is someone who is apprehensive and mistrustful.

Even if wolves are raised by a man, they relate only to the person who trained them. They do not trust anyone. Likewise, spiritual wolves are inclined to suspect evil!

A person who points the finger is an accuser! Spiritual wolves are accusative and suspicious, pointing out faults and seeing faults everywhere!

6\. A wolf is someone who is suspicious and timid in the presence of strangers. Wolves may be playful, but only with the man who raised and fed them.

Wolves do not have the capacity to love, forgive and be kind. They are forever suspicious! Notice that Apostle Paul urged Christians to be kind and to forgive. This is directly opposite to being suspicious.

And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ’s sake hath forgiven you.

Ephesians 4:32

7\. A wolf is someone who cannot be made into a loving friend. Wild wolves cannot be turned into loving pets. You cannot befriend them no matter how hard you try.

Jesus was able to turn fishermen into His disciples and friends. Unfortunately, He was not able to make Judas into a genuine friend. Wolves cannot be made into friends. Wolves cannot receive love from men. They do not interpret love properly and so they cannot be made into friends.

Someone may ask; “Can wolves be friendly like dogs?” The answer is no! Wolves are not friendly to humans!

Generally speaking, where an experiment to tame wolves has been carried out, the greatest success is to create what is called a “one-man dog”. In other words, the “tame wolf” would only relate with the man who reared it and fed it but remain unfriendly and suspicious to strangers.

Some wives behave like that. They only relate with their husbands but will not relate with anyone else in the church (“one-man dog”). They are indeed suspicious of other Christians. That is a wolf characteristic!

Wolves will not engage deeply with other people. Wolves have no real friends. They do not develop deep friendships with anyone! Spiritual wolves give themselves away because they are loners and do not have deep friendships with others. You wonder how they survive in the fellowship for so long.

Ye are my friends, if ye do whatsoever I command you. Henceforth I call you not servants; for the servant knoweth not what his lord doeth: but I have called you friends; for all things that I have heard of my Father I have made known unto you.

Good shepherds cause wolves to go into hiding!
