### Chapter 2\

What is a Grievous Wolf?\
----------------------------------------------

For I know this, that after my departing shall GRIEVOUS WOLVES enter in among you, not sparing the flock. Also of your own selves shall men arise, speaking perverse things, to draw away disciples after them. Therefore watch, and remember, that by the space of three years I ceased not to warn every one night and day with tears.

Acts 20:29-31

A grievous wolf is someone who is a danger to the church. Apostle Paul warned the church of the coming of grievous wolves. Grievous wolves must be dealt with and resisted at all costs.

A grievous wolf will scatter the flock and divide the church.

A grievous wolf is someone who is a threat to the church.

A grievous wolf is someone who will destroy the church, if he gets the chance, and will not care about the havoc he causes.

A wolf is the sheep’s worst nightmare! The wolf stands as the greatest enemy to the peaceful life of a sheep. The wolf is the reason for the shepherd’s existence. The shepherd exists to fight grievous wolves. The shepherd exists to defend the sheep from attacks of grievous wolves. A shepherd who is not ready to fight wolves is not worth anything.

Sheep are not equipped to fight wolves. Sheep can only eat grass. They do not have the teeth to bite a wolf or to defend themselves against being devoured. To be a good shepherd and to care for the sheep, you must know all about the dangers of grievous wolves.

1\. A grievous wolf is someone who waits for the main shepherd to leave and then attacks the flock.

FOR I KNOW THIS, THAT AFTER MY DEPARTING SHALL GRIEVOUS WOLVES ENTER IN AMONG YOU, NOT SPARING THE FLOCK. Also of your own selves shall men arise, speaking perverse things, to draw away disciples after them. Therefore watch, and remember, that by the space of three years I ceased not to warn every one night and day with tears.

Acts 20:29-31

Certain evils cannot take place as long as there is a strong shepherd around somewhere. When a shepherd departs from the flock, the wolves hear about it. Wolves are so excited when there is no real pastor around. A shepherd departs from the flock when he takes his attention off the flock. Wolves are always happy to eat as many defenseless sheep as possible.

2\. A grievous wolf is someone who does not care about what happens to the flock. He will not spare the flock.

For I know this, that after my departing shall GRIEVOUS WOLVES ENTER IN AMONG YOU, NOT SPARING THE FLOCK. Also, of your own selves shall men arise, speaking perverse things, to draw away disciples after them. Therefore watch, and remember, that by the space of three years I ceased not to warn every one night and day with tears.

Acts 20:29-31

Not sparing the flock is the cardinal sign of the wolf. A wolf does not care what happens to the church.

When rebels attack the church with their slander and division, they do not care whether souls are being lost or not. They do not mind that people who were once stable in churches are now being destabilized and thrown off the narrow way of Christ. Wolves do not care whether young Christians are becoming confused or not with all the accusations and stories flying about in the church. They simply do not spare the flock!

Some people attempt to spare the flock and protect young Christians from falling away. But wolves do not care what happens to the lambs; young converts and young members of the church.

I remember a church which had about twenty-five thousand members. The resident pastor rose against his bishop and caused a massive split and confusion in the church. Some of the members stayed with the loyal pastor and some went along with the rebel pastor. In the end, about twenty-one thousand people disappeared from church life through this church split. The loyal pastor had about three thousand members whilst the rebellious pastor took a few hundred people to his church. Indeed, most of the twenty-one thousand members did not go to church anymore. The flock was scattered, and the rebellious, treacherous pastor did not care what happened to them. That is exactly how a wolf operates! A wolf does not care what will happen to the flock through his slanderous and treacherous acts.

3\. A grievous wolf is someone who speaks perverse, slanderous and defamatory things to draw people away from the shepherd.

For I know this, that after my departing shall grievous wolves enter in among you, not sparing the flock. Also of your own selves shall men arise, SPEAKING PERVERSE THINGS, TO DRAW AWAY DISCIPLES AFTER THEM. Therefore watch, and remember, that by the space of three years I ceased not to warn every one night and day with tears.

Acts 20:29-31

A characteristic of a grievous wolf is that he speaks perverse things. He says and writes horrible things about his fellow Christian brothers. Perverse things are nasty stories, whether true or not true. A perverse statement is a defamatory statement calculated to lower the image and reputation of someone in the eyes of the right-thinking public.

A wolf needs to speak perverse things because he has no anointing to start a church or to build a congregation. Perverse and corrupt things come out of the mouths of corrupted men to build their own following. The reason for the corrupt and wicked speech of these wolves is to draw people after themselves.

I remember a church which was attacked by wolves. The wolves campaigned against the church by posting and texting negative slanderous materials on social media to confuse the members. The wolves had many stories and names they bandied about, till the most bizarre, corrupted and twisted stories were circulating in the church. Many members were indeed confused, and many left the church in anger.

This is the work of wolves! Wolves are men who do not care about the flock. Wolves are men who will go to great lengths to misinform, mislead and throw questions and suspicions into the air to draw men after themselves.

4\. A grievous wolf is someone who grows up as one of the leaders but is actually a wolf.

For I know this, that after my departing shall grievous wolves enter in among you, not sparing the flock. ALSO OF YOUR OWN SELVES SHALL MEN ARISE, speaking perverse things, to draw away disciples after them. Therefore watch, and remember, that by the space of three years I ceased not to warn every one night and day with tears.

Acts 20:29-31

“Of your own selves, men shall arise.” A wolf is one of our very own members. A grievous wolf is someone who grows up as a leader in the church but turns into a “bird of another feather”. He becomes a wretched and miserable rebel who only causes trouble and pain in the church. Do not expect a wolf to be an outsider. Expect grievous wolves to arise from within your ranks!

5\. A grievous wolf is known by his fruits.

BEWARE OF FALSE PROPHETS, WHICH COME TO YOU IN SHEEP’S CLOTHING, BUT INWARDLY THEY ARE RAVENING WOLVES. Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles?

Matthew 7:15-16

Many wolves are out in sheep’s clothing and look like harmless sheep, but inwardly, they are not sheep at all. They are wolves within. How can we tell when someone is a ravening wolf? Ye shall know them by their fruits. You know you are dealing with a wolf by the fruits they bear.

I remember a brother who caused a lot of problems in our church. As I researched this fellow, I found out that he had also caused a lot of problems in two other churches he had been a part of. Indeed, I knew he was a wolf by the fruits of strife, slander and confusion that followed him in every ministry he joined, and in every country in which he lived.

A ravening wolf is someone who will cause trouble in your church. A ravening wolf is a grievous wolf! A ravening wolf is a hungry robber and an extortioner! Expect trouble from a ravening wolf!

6\. A grievous wolf is someone who destroys the lives of others.

Her priests have done violence to My Law and have profaned My holy things; they have made no distinction between the holy and the common, and they have not taught the difference between the unclean and the clean; and they have closed their eyes from My Sabbaths, and I am defiled among them. HER LEADERS WITHIN HER ARE LIKE WOLVES TEARING THE PREY, BY SHEDDING BLOOD AND DESTROYING LIVES in order to make dishonest profit.

Ezekiel 22:26-27 (NASB)

7\. Some wolves specialize in taking out female sheep. They destroy lives by capturing weak women.

These are the KIND OF PEOPLE WHO SMOOTH-TALK THEMSELVES INTO THE HOMES OF UNSTABLE AND NEEDY WOMEN AND TAKE ADVANTAGE OF THEM; women who, depressed by their sinfulness, take up with every new religious fad that calls itself “truth.” They get exploited every time and never really learn.

2 Timothy 3:6 (MSG)

8\. Some wolves destroy the flock through extremes of fornication.

It is reported commonly that THERE IS FORNICATION AMONG YOU, AND SUCH FORNICATION AS IS NOT SO MUCH AS NAMED AMONG THE GENTILES, that one should have his father’s wife. And ye are puffed up and have not rather mourned, that he that hath done this deed might be taken away from among you.

For I verily, as absent in body, but present in spirit, have judged already, as though I were present, concerning him that hath so done this deed, In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when ye are gathered together, and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ, To deliver such an one unto Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus. Your glorying is not good. Know ye not that a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump?

1 Corinthians 5:1-6
