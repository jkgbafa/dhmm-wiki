### Chapter 4\

How to Defeat Wolves\
------------------------------------------

Fight the good fight of faith...

1 Timothy 6:12

1\. FIGHT WOLVES BY BEING AGGRESSIVE TOWARDS THEM.

FIGHT THE GOOD FIGHT of faith, lay hold on eternal life, whereunto thou art also called, and hast professed a good profession before many witnesses.

1 Timothy 6:12

Campers and explorers are taught how to defend themselves if they encounter a wolf in the forest. They are taught that, 1“If the wolf does not retreat and is acting aggressive by holding its tail high, raising its hackles, barking or howling, you should yell and throw things at it, while continuing to back away. If it attacks, fight back aggressively to show you are too dangerous to attack.”1

It is important to show wolves that you are too dangerous to attack. Let every wolf in your life and ministry get the message that it will be costly, hurtful and painful to attack you. Inflict pain, hurt and damage on every attacking wolf!

Miserable, disloyal personalities must learn a lesson when they attack you and your church. They must find out that it is a hard thing to kick against the pricks. They must regret even starting the fight.

Be aggressive in teaching against every type of disloyalty! Teach, explain and repeat the teachings till every kind of wolf lurking around your life and ministry is exposed and defeated!

Let unfaithful pastors have a hard time dealing with you! Give them a good fight for every inch they want to take from you! Prepare for them as you would prepare for the coming of wolves in the natural!

2\. FIGHT WOLVES WITH FIRE.

John answered, saying unto them all, I indeed baptize you with water; but one mightier than I cometh, the latchet of whose shoes I am not worthy to unloose: HE SHALL BAPTIZE YOU WITH THE HOLY GHOST AND WITH FIRE:

Luke 3:16

Wolves hate fire! This is why those out in the forest at night would light a fire to keep the wolves away. You can fight the threat of wolves with fire!

Jesus is also someone who fights with fire. He baptizes with the Holy Ghost and fire. Fire, passion and zeal are killers of wolves. Wolves operate mostly with the power of deception and wickedness. The power of deception and wickedness cannot stand against the power of the Holy Ghost and fire! Defeat all wolves using the fire of the Lord.

Be a man of God who is on fire, and it will drive away disloyalty and unfaithfulness!

Be a pastor on fire! Your passion and zeal for the Lord alone will drive away wolves.

Churches on fire do not struggle much with things like disloyalty and unfaithfulness. It is often churches which have lost their zeal and are on the defensive that are ravaged by ravenous wolves.

Fire speaks of the anointing of the Holy Spirit! Fire speaks of the power, zeal and fearlessness that comes from the Holy Spirit. Jesus promised to baptize us with fire. Fire is a relentless and overwhelming force. Work in the ministry with relentless, overwhelming zeal! The zeal you have will be a fire that drives away wolves.

And there appeared unto them CLOVEN TONGUES LIKE AS OF FIRE, and it sat upon each of them. And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance.

Acts 2:3-4

3\. FIGHT WOLVES BY CLIMBING OUT OF REACH.

After this I looked, and, behold, a door was opened in heaven: and the first voice which I heard was as it were of a trumpet talking with me; which said, COME UP HITHER, and I will shew thee things which must be hereafter.

Revelation 4:1

Wolves cannot climb trees! A well-known way to escape from wolves is to climb a tree. If you are alone, climb a tree to escape the wolves! A whole pack of wolves may gather below the tree you climbed, but they still will not be able to get to you. This is a mighty revelation from God!

Go higher in God! The higher you go, the less the access wolves can gain to you. Wolves can only devour those who are low on the ground, those who have not gone higher and deeper with God. Notice how John, the Revelator, is encouraged to come up higher in the Spirit. “After this I looked, and, behold, a door was opened in heaven: and the first voice which I heard was as it were of a trumpet talking with me; which said, COME UP HITHER, and I will shew thee things which must be hereafter” (Revelation 4:1).

Going higher puts you out of the reach of the devil!

Becoming a spiritual giant will put you out of the reach of many enemies!

May you go up higher in the Spirit!

May you be delivered from every accursed wolf who comes against your life and ministry!
