### CHAPTER 4\

The Great White Throne Judgment\
=====================================================

Then I saw a great white throne and Him who sat upon it, from whose presence earth and heaven fled away, and no place was found for them. And I saw the dead, the great and the small, standing before the throne, and books were opened; and another book was opened, which is the book of life; and the dead were judged from the things which were written in the books, according to their deeds. And the sea gave up the dead which were in it, and death and Hades gave up the dead which were in them; and they were judged, every one of them according to their deeds. Then death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire. This is the second death, the lake of fire. And if anyone’s name was not found written in the book of life, he was thrown into the lake of fire.

Revelation 20:11-15 (NASB)

1\. The great white throne judgment will take place before Jehovah Himself. No one will be able to escape from the one who sits on the great white throne.

And I saw a great white throne, and him that sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away; and there was found no place for them.

Revelation 20:11

2\. The great white throne is a judgment that is reserved for unbelievers. As you can see from this passage, the people who were tried at the great white throne were transferred from death and hell straight into the lake of fire.

Born again Christians will appear before the judgment seat of Christ and not the great white throne. Born again Christians are not appearing at the great white throne in order to be transferred into the lake of fire. Born again Christians are not going to go into the lake of fire. Born again Christians are going into heaven because of the blood of Jesus.

3\. The judgment of the great white throne is the judgment for all dead unbelievers who were great, mighty, rich, noble and once lived on the earth. On earth, many great people are able to escape from judgment. However, at the great white throne, the great people of this earth will not escape justice, as they have done so many times on earth.

AND I SAW THE DEAD, small and GREAT, stand before God; and the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works.

Revelation 20:12

4\. The judgment of the great white throne is the judgment for all dead people who were small, insignificant, poor, unimportant and once lived on the earth. No matter how insignificant you are, you will not escape your judgment. Some people make a great effort to look more insignificant and innocent than they really are. I know of people who were assassins and who have converted to Christ. They looked innocent so the police never caught them. The great white throne will catch up with all such innocent looking but wicked people.

AND I SAW THE DEAD, SMALL and great, stand before God; and the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works.

Revelation 20:12

5\. The judgment of the great white throne is the judgment for all dead people out of the books that contain and describe the works, the lives, the sins, and the achievements of men. It is out of the facts and information written in these books that judgment will be determined for all men who are judged at the great white throne. Many people do not believe that books that record all our life’s activities exist. No circumstance will escape the great books of the Lord. Today, we have computers that store huge amounts of data and information. It is also possible for seven billion people to appear before the judgment seat of Christ and have the details of their lives revealed. Everyone must get ready for the opening of the great white throne books.

And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and THE DEAD WERE JUDGED OUT OF THOSE THINGS WHICH WERE WRITTEN IN THE BOOKS, according to their works.

Revelation 20:12

6\. The judgment of the great white throne is a judgment of all those who died and were buried in the sea. Indeed, the sea contains many, many dead people. During the world wars and other historical wars, hundreds and thousands of people died and were buried at sea. The sea is an endless mass of water. No matter how many dead bodies you throw into it, it seems to absorb them all easily. It is only on the day of the judgment at the great white throne that the sea will vomit out all its dead. Adoniram Judson is one of the famous people who was buried at sea.

And THE SEA GAVE UP THE DEAD which were in it; and death and hell delivered up the dead which were in them: and they were judged every man according to their works.

Revelation 20:13

7\. The judgment of the great white throne is the judgment of the millions who are already dead. Many people have died in this world.

And the sea gave up the dead which were in it; AND DEATH AND HELL DELIVERED UP THE DEAD which were in them: and they were judged every man according to their works.

Revelation 20:13

8\. The judgment of the great white throne is the final judgment of some who were already in hell. Many of those appearing at the great white throne will have already been in hell for some time. When they originally died, they had to be kept somewhere before being sent to their final destination.

And the sea gave up the dead which were in it; AND DEATH AND HELL DELIVERED UP THE DEAD which were in them: and they were judged every man according to their works.

Revelation 20:13

9\. The judgment of the great white throne involves the final transfer to the Lake of Fire of all those who have died and of those who are in hell. Hell is actually more than one place. There are different areas within the network of damned and doomed prisons.

Sometimes a section is called hell. Other sections are given names like Tartarus. Yet other sections are called Hades. Some sections are called Gehenna. Yet other sections are called Paradise. Indeed, today, prisoners on earth are transferred from one prison to another. Sometimes they are transferred from medium security prisons to maximum security prisons. The transfer from prison to prison is something that is very common on earth. It should come as no surprise that people who are in hell should be transferred from one prison to another. The transfer from death and hell into the lake of fire is a momentous event that will take place at the great white throne.

And death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death.

Revelation 20:14

10\. The judgment of the great white throne is based on the Book of Life.

If your name was not found in the Book of Life, you were sent into the lake of fire. It is important, therefore, for all human beings to have their names written in the Book of Life. Make sure that your name is in the Book of Life.

And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire.

Revelation 20:15
