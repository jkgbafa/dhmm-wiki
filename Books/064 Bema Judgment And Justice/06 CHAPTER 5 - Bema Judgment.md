### CHAPTER 5\

Bema Judgment\
===================================

Therefore we also have as our ambition, whether at home or absent, to be pleasing to Him. For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may be recompensed for his deeds in the body, according to what he has done, whether good or bad. Therefore, knowing the fear of the Lord, we persuade men, but we are made manifest to God; and I hope that we are made manifest also in your consciences.

2 Corinthians 5:9-11 (NASB)

The judgment seat of Christ has to do with believers whereas the white throne judgment has to do with unbelievers. Unbelievers will be judged at the white throne and a decision will be taken as to whether to admit them into heaven or not. Believers will be judged at the BEMA, which is the judgment seat of Christ.

Even though you are forgiven for your sins and admitted into Christ, there is a judgment that awaits all believers.

In this chapter, we see ten things that will happen at the Bema judgment seat of Christ. These ten things are clearly outlined in the word of God. There are only two places in the New Testament that this judgment seat of Christ is mentioned. Apostle Paul’s brief mention of the judgment seat of Christ is enough to reveal what will happen there. You must be ready for all that will happen at the Bema judgment seat of Christ.

1\. LABOUR will be rewarded.

WHEREFORE WE LABOUR, that, whether present or absent, we may be accepted of him.

2 Corinthians 5:9

When referring to the Bema, Apostle Paul said that he spent a lot of time labouring in order to be accepted before the Lord. Without hard labour you will never have a good experience at the judgment seat. If you have been living a life of leisure, rest and comfort, you will be shocked at the judgment seat of Christ. Jesus said we should pray for labourers because the harvest is plenty but the labourers are few. Make sure you are one of the labourers who will be ready at the judgment seat of Christ.

2\. THOSE WHO PLEASE THE LORD will be rewarded.

Therefore we also have as our ambition, whether at home or absent, TO BE PLEASING TO HIM.

2 Corinthians 5:9 (NASB)

Most Christians have lived for themselves, rather than trying to please the One who called them. Apostle Paul prepared for the judgment seat of Christ by seeking to be well pleasing to the Lord. Anyone who seeks to please the Lord and lives his life by that rule will do well at the judgment seat of Christ. You will not be afraid of the judgment seat of Christ if you live to please God.

3\. PUNISHMENT FOR CHRISTIANS will take place at the Bema.

For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may be RECOMPENSED FOR HIS DEEDS in the body, according to what he has done, WHETHER GOOD OR BAD.

2 Corinthians 5:10 (NASB)

Many Christians have lived their lives in Christ doing bad things. At the judgment seat of Christ, you will be held accountable for the bad things you have done. We are washed and cleansed by the blood of Jesus and entitled to enter into heaven. But you will still account for the bad things you have done.

We will all account for the bad things we have done in Christ. Whilst on earth, many Christians live carelessly in sin. They do not obey the word of God and they indulge in everything that worldly people do. There is very little difference between these Christians and worldly people.

Many Christians are steeped in stealing, lying, fornication, idolatry and adultery. Do you think there will be a difference between those who practiced all these things and those who did not at the judgment seat? Of course there will be! We will be held accountable for our lives at the judgment seat of Christ. Those who do not bother to take God seriously will regret it when they stand at the judgment seat of Christ.

4\. GREAT REWARDS will be given out at the Bema judgment seat of Christ.

You must be ready to receive your rewards in Christ. The Bema judgment seat of Christ will be the greatest event for believers who have served the Lord. That is where you will receive your crowns and other rewards. That is where you will hear about your eternal rank in heaven. That is where you will be allocated your mansion in heaven. That is where you will be made to start shining as one of the stars in the heavens. That is where you will be rewarded for the good things you have done.

For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may be recompensed for his deeds in the body, according to what he has done, whether good or bad.

2 Corinthians 5:10 (NASB)

5\. The TERROR OF THE LORD will be experienced at the judgment seat of Christ. Prepare for terror of the Lord. There will be terror at the Bema seat.

Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord, we persuade men; but we are made manifest unto God; and I trust also are made manifest in your consciences.

2 Corinthians 5:11

There will be great terror at the judgment seat of Christ. Fear like no man has ever known will grip the hearts and minds of those who did not fear God and His word. Everyone who took God for granted will be shocked. Apostle Paul describes it as “the terror of the Lord”. I am sure that those who will experience the terror of the Lord are not reading this book.

Make sure you preach about the terror of the Lord so that Christians fear the Lord and prepare properly for the judgments. God is not a fool. In eternity, those who have obeyed God and those who have ignored the word of God will not be the same. God is just and fair!

There are many terrible judgments that befall Christians on earth. When you experience a judgment on earth, it is so that you do not experience the terror of the Lord at the eternal judgment. If you pass on and seem to escape any form of judgment on earth, in spite of all the bad things you do, be it known to you that you will meet the terror of the Lord at the Bema judgment seat of Christ. After the Bema judgment seat of Christ experience, no one will ever think that anyone has gotten away with doing bad things.

6\. JUDGING OF THE BRETHREN will be done at the Bema seat.

BUT WHY DOST THOU JUDGE THY BROTHER? or why dost thou set at nought thy brother? for we shall all stand before the judgment seat of Christ.

Romans 14:10

There is no need to judge your fellow brother today. Judgment will happen at the Bema. Today, Christians despise one another and speak words of judgment against each other constantly. There is no need for that.

Wait for the judgment seat of Christ where all issues will be made clear. Liars will be proven to be liars. Hypocrites will be shown to be hypocrites. Pretenders will be exposed. No one will escape the fierce judgment of Christ. Stop passing judgment on fellow brothers. Pray for yourself so that you have an easier experience at the judgment seat of Christ.

7\. SETTING AT NOUGHT will happen at the judgment seat of Christ.

But why dost thou judge thy brother? OR WHY DOST THOU SET AT NOUGHT THY BROTHER? For we shall all stand before the judgment seat of Christ.

Romans 14:10

At the judgment seat of Christ, puffed up people will be brought low. They will be exposed and made to stop deceiving everyone.

In heaven, no one will be able to deceive, as we have been able to deceive on earth. The church is filled with deceivers. There are many liars, hypocrites and pretenders in the church. These kinds of people are able to successfully stay in the church because so much about our lives are hidden from view. In heaven nothing will be hidden from view. At the judgment seat of Christ, even your thoughts will be broadcast.

8\. EVERY KNEE SHALL BOW submit, yield, accept, agree, flow and kowtow at the judgment seat.

For it is written, as I live, saith the Lord, every knee shall bow to me, and every tongue shall confess to God.

Romans 14:11

Today, Christians gleefully quote the scripture that every knee shall bow and that every tongue shall confess that Jesus Christ is Lord. Christians think that this scripture only applies to unbelievers who will be forced to kneel down before Jesus.

Amazingly, this scripture refers to Christians bowing down to God and to His word at the judgment seat of Christ. Everyone will yield himself at the judgment seat of Christ. The resistance of man to the counsel of the Lord will break down at the judgment seat of Christ. Our great pride, defences and arguments will vanish at the judgment seat of Christ.

Today, many people argue about things they should not argue about. Many of us defend the indefensible. Many never admit they are wrong. Many stand proudly defending and proclaiming their own goodness. All that will end at the Bema judgment seat of Christ. Every confession and admission you have refused to make on earth will be made at the Bema seat.

Indeed, once we are before the judgment seat of Christ, there will be no more denials but instead there will be confessions, declarations, admissions, revelations and acknowledgments of our wretchedness and guiltiness before the Lord.

Every tongue will confess to God! Every tongue will make declarations and admissions to God. Every tongue will acknowledge the right and the wrong. Every tongue will speak and reveal the truth about all matters and issues we experienced on earth. What a day that will be! What a day of confession that will be!

9\. At the Bema judgment seat of Christ, everyone will give an account.

So then every one of us shall give account of himself to God.

Romans 14:12

Even those who did not study accounting in school will be forced to give an account of their lives. Everyone will have a chance to give an account of the things they did and the details of their lives. The things you did! The things you didn’t do. Why you did those things? How you did them? Who was involved? Who helped you? Who didn’t help you? Explanations, reasons and defences will be given when you are giving your account. Incomes, inflows, expenditures, the costs and the challenges you faced will all be declared. You will account for why you did not achieve certain things.

10\. True and fair judgment based on all factors will happen at the Bema judgment seat of Christ.

But to me it is a very small thing that I may be examined by you, or by any human court; in fact, I do not even examine myself. For I am conscious of nothing against myself, yet I am not by this acquitted; but the one who examines me is the Lord. Therefore do not go on passing judgment before the time, but wait until the Lord comes who will both bring to light the things hidden in the darkness and disclose the motives of men’s hearts; and then each man’s praise will come to him from God.

1 Corinthians 4:3-5 (NASB)

The Justice of Bema

It is a mistake to judge others based only on what you see and know. Anyone who attempts to enact the judgment seat of Christ before time will make a mistake. Judgment is based on three things: what you have done, what is hidden and what was your motive. Therefore you must not bother to judge others. Neither must you bother to judge yourself because there are factors that are hidden from our eyes.

Our motives for doing things are also not clear. Remember that motive is very important in deciding whether something is a crime or not. A woman who is raped is not considered to be a fornicator, even though she might have undergone the act of sexual intercourse. Her motive for being in that situation is clearly not because she desires to have a sexual or romantic experience with a rapist. Her lack of motive and desire for sex exonerates her completely from the sin of fornication.

This is why you must not pass judgment just based on what you see and know. What you see and know is very limited. You have only two eyes and you have only two ears. You are definitely limited in your ability to pass judgment. Apart from this fact, many people are clever at hiding their ways. You would be surprised at what you would find in the church if you dug a little deeper beneath the surface. You would be surprised at how many people are good at acting good when they are actually evil.

If you try passing judgment before time, you will make the mistake of judging another man’s servant. It is indeed a great mistake to judge another man’s servant. A servant is a servant only to his master. There is a sense in which ONLY the master can assess his servant because he serves only the master.

Who art thou that judgest another man’s servant? to his own master he standeth or falleth. Yea, he shall be holden up: for God is able to make him stand.

Romans 14:4

It is also a great mistake to judge people based on your personal standards. Many things are actually grey areas and people have different opinions about different things. For instance, all through the Old Testament, polygamy was practiced even as an act of goodness and kindness to care for widows and women who were without husbands.

When Jesus was asked about a woman who kept marrying because her successive husbands were dying, He did not make any comment about it being wrong for the woman to be an additional wife to anyone. Today, polygamy is frowned upon but the question many people ask is: “When did polygamy become a sin and why are widows not taken care of by surviving brothers as was done in the Old Testament?”

There are many varied opinions that Christians have about drinking alcohol, smoking cigarettes, pornography, masturbation and so on. Generally speaking, all these are frowned upon. However, different opinions do exist. Apostle Paul warned that Christians would stand or fall only before their own Master.

Do not make the mistake of judging people based on your personal standards that are not the Lord’s standards.

Let us not therefore judge one another any more: but judge this rather, that no man put a stumblingblock or an occasion to fall in his brother’s way. I know, and am persuaded by the Lord Jesus, that there is nothing unclean of itself: but to him that esteemeth any thing to be unclean, to him it is unclean.

Romans 14:13-14

Therefore let us not judge one another anymore, but rather determine this—not to put an obstacle or a stumbling block in a brother’s way.

Romans 14:13 (NASB)

As the apostle has taught us, no one should practice anything he considers to be right in such a way as to cause other believers to stumble, to fall and to depart from the faith.

For we know in part and we prophesy in part; but when the perfect comes, the partial will be done away. When I was a child, I used to speak like a child, think like a child, reason like a child; when I became a man, I did away with childish things. For now we see in a mirror dimly, but then face to face; now I know in part, but then I will know fully just as I also have been fully known.

1 Corinthians 13:9-12 (NASB)

On the judgment day, many controversies will be settled. We will be shocked to find out what was right and what was wrong. Anyone who claims that the laws and the rules for Christianity are clear and easy to discern is mistaken. There are many so-called grey areas. We must humbly accept that we do not know everything. We know only part of the truth. We do see dimly, through a glass. On that day we will see clearly! The mysteries will be made known. The revelations will be abundant. Light will come into our darkness and we will see clearly and understand clearly. Let us wait for Judgment Day!
