### CHAPTER 8\

Apostolic Judgment\
========================================

Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end, upon the throne of David, and upon his kingdom, to order it, and TO ESTABLISH IT WITH JUDGMENT AND WITH JUSTICE from henceforth even for ever. The zeal of the LORD of hosts will perform this.

Isaiah 9:7

Apostolic judgments are the judgments of church leaders against sin and evil in the church. Apostolic judgments are the judgments that must be passed in the church to order it and to establish it with judgment and justice. Churches are established on judgment and justice.

Every church will have issues. Every church will have its fair share of sinful characters, immoral behaviour and evil activities. It is the duty of the apostles, prophets and pastors to root out evil when it seeks to be planted in our midst. The church is a human institution that has been anointed by the Holy Spirit. Every human institution is filled with human weaknesses. You can only expect to see weakness, dishonour and shame in the church as it spreads out and grows bigger.

Recently we spoke with an older apostle of a very established church, which has grown into a movement with several thousand churches all over the world. We asked this elderly apostle what he thought was the master key for the persistence of his church into the future.

The elderly apostle said the master key to the future was to take discipline and judgment very seriously. He said that discipline and judgment amongst pastors is very important especially at the very highest level. The key of discipline and judgment was the key this pastor gave as the master key for the continued growth and expansion of the church. The king by judgment establishes the land! If you want the church to be established there must be judgment and discipline against all unrighteousness.

Every church can be overwhelmed with immorality, fornication and adultery as it grows larger and advances into the future. Indeed, many churches are overwhelmed with immorality, fornication, sexual perversions and adultery. The Corinthian church was overrun by these very sins. Apostle Paul spent large sections of his letter addressing these issues that were very important for the life of the Corinthian church. In his letter, he spoke judgment against the immorality and perversions that were rampant in the church.

Human beings are weak and God simply does not accept fornication and adultery to be part of the church. Many large denominations are overwhelmed with adulterous, fornicating or sexually pervert priests and pastors. The manifestation of evil at this level spells doom for the church. Evil and immorality in the church open the doors to demons and devils. Many groups of pastors have become the habitation of devils because of their immoral lifestyle. Immorality has caused them to fall to the level where demon activity is heightened.

The word of God is full of warnings about judgment. We hear clear warnings that we should not judge.

Judge not, that ye be not judged.

Matthew 7:1

However, there are also scriptures that teach us to judge with righteous judgment.

Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment.

John 7:24

So, what must be done when a pastor falls into sin? What must be done when an apostle falls into sin? What must be done when a prophet falls into sin? Apostolic judgments will have to come into play. Apostle Paul gave the best examples of apostolic judgments when he dealt with the Corinthians.

Apostle Paul passed judgments on several people who were involved in immorality in the Corinthian church. His judgments serve as great examples of what to do when such problems befall the church.

Judgment is necessary to establish righteousness and order in the church. “I speak to your shame. Is it so, that there is not a wise man among you? no, not one that shall be able to judge between his brethren?” (1 Corinthians 6:5). We need to judge between the brethren with the wisdom of God.

1. Immorality, fornication, sexual perversions and adultery are leavens that spread within a congregation. Judgment and discipline is important in the church because evil spreads mysteriously within a congregation. Evil is like leaven which mysteriously influences the rest of the dough. Evil is perpetuated by evil spirits who are behind the wickedness that is being manifested in the church.

Your glorying is not good. Know ye not that a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump?

1 Corinthians 5:6

Immorality, fornication, sexual perversions and adultery are leavens. They spread mysteriously in the congregation. Without discussing with each other, fornicators and adulterers mysteriously multiply in a congregation. When a scandal hits a church, several people will be found to be living similar immoral and unacceptable lives. This is why it is important to root out the leaven of immorality from the church.

2. Immorality, fornication, sexual perversions and adultery spread naturally from the head downwards. Apart from the leaven that spreads from member to member within the church, evils at the level of the leadership spread downwards to the congregation.

It is like the precious ointment upon the head, that ran down upon the beard, even Aaron’s beard: that went down to the skirts of his garments;

Psalm 133:2

The scripture makes it clear that the anointing flows from the top of Aaron’s head down through his beard and to the skirts below. The anointing of the Holy Spirit is the good influence of the Holy Spirit. It flows from the head downwards. All influences flow from the head downwards.

It is like the precious ointment upon the head, that ran down upon the beard, even Aaron’s beard: that went down to the skirts of his garments;

Psalm 133:2

When a pastor is filled with rebellion, he spreads his rebellion to the congregation because he is the head. When Hananiah the prophet misled the people of God, it was because he was a man of authority and everything that he taught was believed. Evil saturated the Israelites because Hananiah the prophet misled them and taught evil.

Then said the prophet Jeremiah unto Hananiah the prophet, HEAR NOW, HANANIAH; THE LORD HATH NOT SENT THEE; BUT THOU MAKEST THIS PEOPLE TO TRUST IN A LIE. Therefore thus saith the LORD; Behold, I will cast thee from off the face of the earth: this year thou shalt die, because thou hast taught rebellion against the LORD. So Hananiah the prophet died the same year in the seventh month.

Jeremiah 28:15-17

All through the Bible, the principle is clear. As the priests are, so are the people!

And there shall be, like people, like priest: and I will punish them for their ways, and reward them their doings.

Hosea 4:9

‘And what the priests do, the people also do.’ So now I will punish both priests and people for their wicked deeds.

Hosea 4:9 (NLT)

Apostolic judgments are the judgments of church leaders against sin and evil in the church. Such judgments are very difficult to come to because they are the judgments of a human being against another human being.

Every right thinking person knows that he is capable of doing whatever his brother who has fallen into sin has done. Judging your fellow brother is the worst job you can ever have to do in the church. Indeed, the scripture teaches that you should consider yourself carefully in every case of judgment.

Every time you are called upon to pass apostolic judgments on a brother who has fallen into difficulty, you must carefully consider your own weaknesses. You must not think of yourself as above that sin or difficulty. Always remember that you are an average brother. You are truly just as weak as your fellow brother who has fallen into difficulty.

Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted.

Galatians 6:1

Below are some rules for apostolic judgments. When it is time to pass apostolic judgments, your failure to do so will spell the end of your ministry. Today, many churches are riddled with immorality. Today many churches are powerless against immorality because the highest level of ministers are deeply involved in scandalous secret sexual activity.

Decide to practice apostolic judgments based on the principles below.

1\. Apostolic judgments are based on wisdom from above. Without wisdom you cannot pass good judgments.

Dare any of you, having a matter against another, go to law before the unjust, and not before the saints? Do ye not know that the saints shall judge the world? And if the world shall be judged by you, are ye unworthy to judge the smallest matters? Know ye not that we shall judge angels? How much more things that pertain to this life? If then ye have judgments of things pertaining to this life, set them to judge who are least esteemed in the church. I speak to your shame. Is it so, THAT THERE IS NOT A WISE MAN AMONG YOU? NO, NOT ONE THAT SHALL BE ABLE TO JUDGE BETWEEN HIS BRETHREN?

1 Corinthians 6:1-5

2. Apostolic judgments must be delivered in matters of immorality. When immoral cases arise in the church, apostolic judgments similar to the ones which Apostle Paul passed on the church must be enacted.

It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you, and such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father’s wife. And ye are puffed up, and have not rather mourned, that he that hath done this deed might be taken away from among you. FOR I VERILY, AS ABSENT IN BODY, BUT PRESENT IN SPIRIT, HAVE JUDGED ALREADY, as though I were present, concerning him that hath so done this deed, In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when ye are gathered together, and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ,

1 Corinthians 5:1-4

3. Apostolic judgments can be decided remotely by an apostle who does not live there. The apostolic anointing will enable the apostle to take decisions about things even when he is not present.

It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you, and such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father’s wife. And ye are puffed up, and have not rather mourned, that he that hath done this deed might be taken away from among you. FOR I VERILY, AS ABSENT IN BODY, BUT PRESENT IN SPIRIT, HAVE JUDGED ALREADY, AS THOUGH I WERE PRESENT, CONCERNING HIM THAT HATH SO DONE THIS DEED, In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when ye are gathered together, and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ,

1 Corinthians 5:1-4

4. Apostolic judgments can deliver church members to destruction. In the scripture, we see how Apostle Paul commended certain people into the hands of the devil. What happens when you are recommended into the hands of the devil? When the person is handed over to satan it means he has been handed over to the enemy. The enemy will do what he feels like doing. The enemy will steal, kill and destroy because that is all that he can do. People who are handed over to the devil can expect to be destroyed, stolen from and killed by evil spirits.

To deliver such an one unto Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus. Your glorying is not good. Know ye not that a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump?

1 Corinthians 5:5-6

5\. Apostolic judgments include apostolic counselling.

It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you, and such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father’s wife. And ye are puffed up, and have not rather mourned, that he that hath done this deed might be taken away from among you. FOR I VERILY, AS ABSENT IN BODY, BUT PRESENT IN SPIRIT, HAVE JUDGED ALREADY, AS THOUGH I WERE PRESENT, CONCERNING HIM THAT HATH SO DONE THIS DEED, In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when ye are gathered together, and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ,

1 Corinthians 5:1-4

Apostolic judgments always include direction and counselling. Psychiatrists deal with mental problems. Many times psychiatrists work by giving counsel to their patients. Many mentally deranged people need counselling. Perhaps you are in a situation in your church where sin has taken a foothold. Never forget that counselling is a very important element to bring about the solution that you need.

Most people do not realize that apostle Paul fought against the sin of fornication and immorality all through the books of first and second Corinthians.

Apostle Paul dealt with a serious case of fornication that was more extreme than even the types of fornication we find in the world today. He called it “such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles.”

Apostle Paul did a lot of counselling to the Corinthians. After dealing with the matter of high-level fornication in the church and passing judgment on it, he advised everyone to FLEE FORNICATION (1 Corinthians 6:18). Then he went further and advised everyone to AVOID FORNICATION by having their own husbands and wives and being kind to each other.

Nevertheless, to avoid fornication, let every man have his own wife, and let every woman have her own husband. Let the husband render unto the wife due benevolence: and likewise also the wife unto the husband. The wife hath not power of her own body, but the husband: and likewise also the husband hath not power of his own body, but the wife. Defraud ye not one the other, except it be with consent for a time, that ye may give yourselves to fasting and prayer; and come together again, that Satan tempt you not for your incontinency.

1 Corinthians 7:2-5
