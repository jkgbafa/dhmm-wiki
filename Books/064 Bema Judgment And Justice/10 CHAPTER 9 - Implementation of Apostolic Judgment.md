### CHAPTER 9\

Implementation of Apostolic Judgment\
==========================================================

Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, therefore the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil.

Ecclesiastes 8:11

There are some basic levels of apostolic judgment that must be implemented. Apostle Paul gives us four pillars for dealing out apostolic judgments - counselling, interaction, separation from fellowship, restriction of ministry and dismissals. We learn about apostolic judgment from the example of Paul and the Corinthian church.

First of all, counselling, interaction and instruction must always be implemented. Secondly, there must be a separation from sinners.

Sin is a leaven. Sin spreads naturally amongst the congregation. Even without having a meeting, it is as though sinners agree to spread the virus of sin throughout the congregation. You will find that each of these steps of judgment that are taken against sinful people is biblical.

1. Counselling and instruction: Counselling is very important. You must speak to people that are involved in sin. Your words will have an effect. Everyone who is involved in sin must undergo counselling. Perhaps, through your counselling God will grant deliverance.

And the servant of the Lord must not strive; but be gentle unto all men, apt to teach, patient, IN MEEKNESS INSTRUCTING THOSE THAT OPPOSE THEMSELVES; if God peradventure will give them repentance to the acknowledging of the truth; And that they may recover themselves out of the snare of the devil, who are taken captive by him at his will.

2 Timothy 2:24-26

2. Separation from fellowship: It is important to learn the principles of leaven spreading in the church. Leaven is a spiritual entity that spreads, permeates and diffuses in the church. Malice, wickedness and fornication are evils that spread in the church. Entire sections of the church can be overrun by these evils. Apostle Paul said, “You must not company with fornicators.” Who are your friends? What are their lives like? What sort of things are they involved in? I can assure you that whom you fellowship with will directly influence you and your ministry.

I WROTE UNTO YOU IN AN EPISTLE NOT TO COMPANY WITH FORNICATORS: Yet not altogether with the fornicators of this world, or with the covetous, or extortioners, or with idolaters; for then must ye needs go out of the world. But now I have written unto you not to keep company, if any man that is called a brother be a fornicator, or covetous, or an idolater, or a railer, or a drunkard, or an extortioner; with such an one no not to eat.

1 Corinthians 5:9-11

3. Restriction of ministry: Ministers must be restricted when they fall into sin. Restriction of ministry is a sign of respect and honour to God. When Eli failed to restrict his sons, it was a great sign of disrespect to God. God reacted to the disrespect of Eli. Eli did not respect God enough to take action against his sons. His sons were his future. His sons were the up and coming leaders of Israel. Their moral failure meant that they could not lead after Eli was gone.

Eli’s failure lay in the fact that he did nothing to them and against them. Yes, he spoke to them and counselled them. But he needed to go a step further and withdraw them from active service.

Now Eli was very old, and heard all that his sons did unto all Israel; and how they lay with the women that assembled at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation. AND HE SAID UNTO THEM, WHY DO YE SUCH THINGS? FOR I HEAR OF YOUR EVIL DEALINGS BY ALL THIS PEOPLE. NAY, MY SONS; FOR IT IS NO GOOD REPORT THAT I HEAR: YE MAKE THE LORD’S PEOPLE TO TRANSGRESS. If one man sin against another, the judge shall judge him: but if a man sin against the LORD, who shall intreat for him? Notwithstanding they hearkened not unto the voice of their father, because the LORD would slay them. And the child Samuel grew on, and was in favour both with the LORD, and also with men. And there came a man of God unto Eli, and said unto him, Thus saith the LORD, Did I plainly appear unto the house of thy father, when they were in Egypt in Pharaoh’s house? And did I choose him out of all the tribes of Israel to be my priest, to offer upon mine altar, to burn incense, to wear an ephod before me? and did I give unto the house of thy father all the offerings made by fire of the children of Israel? WHEREFORE KICK YE AT MY SACRIFICE AND AT MINE OFFERING, WHICH I HAVE COMMANDED IN MY HABITATION; AND HONOUREST THY SONS ABOVE ME, TO MAKE YOURSELVES FAT WITH THE CHIEFEST OF ALL THE OFFERINGS OF ISRAEL MY PEOPLE? Wherefore the LORD God of Israel saith, I said indeed that thy house, and the house of thy father, should walk before me for ever: but now the LORD saith, Be it far from me; for them that honour me I will honour, and they that despise me shall be lightly esteemed.

1 Samuel 2:22-30

4\. Dismissals:

And ye are puffed up, and have not rather mourned, THAT HE THAT HATH DONE THIS DEED MIGHT BE TAKEN AWAY FROM AMONG YOU.

1 Corinthians 5:2

Certain sinful people must be taken away from your midst. Their presence is not acceptable. The time comes when he that has done certain deeds must be taken away from amongst you. Dismissal is certainly necessary and important. Dismissal is a type of rejection. God rejected Saul. God rejects people who reject Him.

For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry. Because thou hast rejected the word of the LORD, he hath also rejected thee from being king.

1 Samuel 15:23

When Must There be Dismissal?

A number of factors cause a need for dismissal to arise. Dismissal is not an easy decision to come to. However, dismissal must take place when serious dimensions complicate the original sin. There is a chart with a point system that can help you to rate sins that are presented to the council of elders of any church. (See my book The Gift of Government). Let us now consider five factors that bring in the need for dismissal.

1. A demonic element: When sin develops a demonic element, it must be noted. Demonic elements are obvious when things like stubbornness and other perversions are present. A demonic element is also present when the sin does not make any sense. Many people commit senseless sins from which they do not even benefit. David’s decision to number the Israelites made no sense. He did that senseless act because he was provoked by the devil.

And Satan stood up against Israel, and provoked David to number Israel.

1 Chronicles 21:1

2. Multiple sins: Dismissal must take place when there are multiple sins. The scripture is clear that some people add sin to sin.

Woe to the rebellious children, saith the Lord, that take counsel, but not of me; and that cover with a covering, but not of my spirit, THAT THEY MAY ADD SIN TO SIN:

Isaiah 30:1

3. Duplicity and duality: Dismissal must take place when there is strong element of duplicity, duality and pretence. When people are able to live dual lives for a long time, they are exhibiting a strong sense of the demonic. Satan is the best liar and pretender of all. A person who is able to lie and pretend for a long time has imbibed evil spirits. Jesus strongly rejected hypocrisy. Jesus reproved the hypocrites with the strongest of all rebukes.

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness.

Matthew 23:27

4. The length of time the person practices the sin is also important: Practicing a sin for a very long time must lead to dismissal.

Anything that is done for a long time is different from what is done for a short time. Those who tarry long at the wine are those who have red eyes. Those who tarry long in front of their sin are greatly affected by it. They are contaminated and corrupted by it more than those who fell into it and came out.

Who hath woe? who hath sorrow? who hath contentions? who hath babbling? who hath wounds without cause? who hath redness of eyes? They that tarry long at the wine; they that go to seek mixed wine.

Proverbs 23:29-30

5. Betrayal and disloyalty must lead to dismissal: When you betray your country it is called treason. The person who betrays is called a traitor. Betrayal is the relaying of information that is harmful or injurious to someone who trusted you. Betrayal is not accepted anywhere. The betrayal of trust is usually the end of relationships.

The betrayal of trust is usually the signal for dismissal. When Judas found out that his betrayal had been discovered he knew it was all over. He hanged himself because he knew that he would never be accepted or trusted again amongst the disciples.

Saying, I have sinned in that I have betrayed the innocent blood. And they said, What is that to us? see thou to that. And he cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, and departed, and went and hanged himself.

Matthew 27:4-5

Conclusion

To the making of many books there is no end!

It is my prayer that through these few pages you will come to understand the importance of judgment and justice in your church and ministry. May the Lord fill you with wisdom so that you follow in the steps of our Saviour, Jesus Christ. May your ministry be ordered and established with judgment and justice from henceforth and for ever!

Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end, upon the throne of David, and upon his kingdom, TO ORDER IT, AND TO ESTABLISH IT WITH JUDGMENT AND WITH JUSTICE from henceforth even for ever. The zeal of the LORD of hosts will perform this.

Isaiah 9:7
