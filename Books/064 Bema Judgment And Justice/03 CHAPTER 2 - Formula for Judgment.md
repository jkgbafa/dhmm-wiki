### CHAPTER 2\

Formula for Judgment\
==========================================

If men strive, and hurt a woman with child, so that her fruit depart from her, and yet no mischief follow: he shall be surely punished, according as the woman’s husband will lay upon him; and he shall pay as the judges determine. And if any mischief follow, then thou shalt give LIFE FOR LIFE, EYE FOR EYE, TOOTH FOR TOOTH, HAND FOR HAND, FOOT FOR FOOT, BURNING FOR BURNING, WOUND FOR WOUND, STRIPE FOR STRIPE. And if a man smite the eye of his servant, or the eye of his maid, that it perish; he shall let him go free for his eye’s sake. And if he smite out his manservant’s tooth, or his maidservant’s tooth; he shall let him go free for his tooth’s sake.

Exodus 21:22-27

And if a man cause a blemish in his neighbour; as he hath done, so shall it be done to him; BREACH FOR BREACH, EYE FOR EYE, TOOTH FOR TOOTH: AS HE HATH CAUSED A BLEMISH IN A MAN, SO SHALL IT BE DONE TO HIM AGAIN.

Leviticus 24:19-20

Within the Bible we find the basis of all judgments. This foundation is at the heart of all types of judgments and underlines all forms of responses to evil. According to scripture, if someone removes your eye it must be paid back by removing his eye as well.

These statements reveal the fairness, the justice and the even-handedness that is applied to someone who has done wrong. Justice must be served to the person who has been wronged. A decision has to be taken on what must be done for the wronged party. Failure to take a decision is to deny that judgment.

The Bible gives us the basis of what we should do. The Bible teaches us what must be done and what can be done in response to evil. A breach for a breach! Burning for burning! Wound for wound and stripe for stripe!

There are many evils perpetrated which are not in this exact format. In our modern world, no one may remove your eye, your tooth or even your hand. In our modern world, no one may burn you or give you stripes. However, there are many painful things that can be done to hurt you. Justice can be served to people when these laws are applied. Tears for tears! Wound for wound! A breach for breach! You separate from me and I separate from you! Today, we are left to interpret this scripture in the light of our current situations and experiences.

There is no need to work out judgment against people who are doing wrong against you. The scripture is clear.

Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath: for it is written, vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord.

Romans 12:19

God will reward and God will repay. God has a formula for judging people. All of the scripture bears witness to this reality and shows how God fairly and squarely metes out judgment to people. You cannot fail to see the clear pattern of God’s formula for judgment being an eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth.

Jesus introduced the element of mercy into God’s formula for judgment. Mercy can be introduced so that an eye does not have to be given for another eye and a hand does not have to be given for another hand.

Ye have heard that it hath been said, AN EYE FOR AN EYE, AND A TOOTH FOR A TOOTH: But I say unto you, that ye resist not evil: but whosoever shall smite thee on thy right cheek, turn to him the other also.

Matthew 5:38-39

Jesus threw a new light on this scripture when He recognized it as truth and law. He however brought in the aspect of love and mercy.

1\. SEED FOR SEED:

Be not deceived; God is not mocked: for whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap.

Galatians 6:7

In a sense, the principles for judgment are the principles for sowing and reaping. God has set in motion amazing principles of seedtime and harvest time. The seedtime will always come on, and the harvest time will always follow. The seed will be sown and there will be a harvest of seeds. You sow one little corn seed and you receive a number of corncobs which carry many seeds of corn.

2\. WIND FOR WIND:

For they have sown the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind: it hath no stalk: the bud shall yield no meal: if so be it yield, the strangers shall swallow it up.

Hosea 8:7

You have sown the wind and you shall reap the whirlwind! Why would you have a whirlwind coming at you? Because you sent the wind out! That is God’s formula for judgment. What you send out is going to come back at you with a stronger force. Watch out when you speak strong and terrible words that destroy someone’s life and ministry. Very strong and terrible words will also be spoken against you. Words of blasphemy will boomerang in your direction. You may not be able to recover from the effects of the boomerang of your backbiting and slanderous words!

3\. BLOOD FOR BLOOD:

And I heard the angel of the waters say, Thou art righteous, O Lord, which art, and wast, and shalt be, because thou hast judged thus. For they have shed the blood of saints and prophets, and thou hast given them blood to drink; for they are worthy.

Revelation 16:5-6

In the book of Revelation, we see an amazing judgment where God gave some people blood to drink. Why would God serve someone with blood? That does not sound very nice. God gave them blood to drink because they had shed the blood of other people. Blood for blood! God’s formula for judgment is very simple: an eye for an eye! A tooth for tooth! A stripe for a stripe! Burning for burning! A hand for a hand! A foot for a foot! And blood for blood!

4\. DESTRUCTION FOR DESTRUCTION:

And he answered and said, He that dippeth his hand with me in the dish, the same shall betray me. The Son of man goeth as it is written of him: but woe unto that man by whom the Son of man is betrayed! it had been good for that man if he had not been born. Then Judas, which betrayed him, answered and said, Master, is it I? He said unto him, Thou hast said.

Matthew 26:23-25

It is important that you believe in God’s pattern of judgment. What you do to someone will be done to you in a way that you do not believe is possible. Judas ended the ministry of Jesus Christ. Judas was not conscious of this principle of judgment. You end my ministry and your ministry will be ended too! You end my life and your life will be ended too! You destroy my happiness and your happiness will be destroyed too.

5\. A FREAK FOR A FREAK:

And Ahithophel said unto Absalom, Go in unto thy father’s concubines, which he hath left to keep the house; and all Israel shall hear that thou art abhorred of thy father: then shall the hands of all that are with thee be strong. So they spread Absalom a tent upon the top of the house; and Absalom went in unto his father’s concubines in the sight of all Israel. And the counsel of Ahithophel, which he counselled in those days, was as if a man had enquired at the oracle of God: so was all the counsel of Ahithophel both with David and with Absalom.

2 Samuel 16:21-23

And Absalom met the servants of David. And Absalom rode upon a mule, and the mule went under the thick boughs of a great oak, and his head caught hold of the oak, and he was taken up between the heaven and the earth; and the mule that was under him went away.

2 Samuel 18:9

It is a rare and bizarre aberration for a son to sleep with his father’s wife. It is an abnormality par excellence! It is even more bizarre that a son should sleep with a number of his father’s wives and precious women. It is a complete and unfortunate deviation from normality that a man should do such things in the view of servants and the general public. By doing these things Absalom opened himself up to receive a judgment. An eye for an eye! A tooth for a tooth! A stripe for a stripe!

Absalom received a rare and bizarre accident in which he was caught by his hair in a tree. He hanged with his hair. It was a very unusual and bizarre accident. Bizarre for bizarre! Aberration for aberration! Be careful when you do these unusual things.

Absalom embarrassed his father and openly defiled his father’s wives. These women were the precious chosen women whom David had selected from among the population of Israel, throughout the years that he had been king. Absalom reduced them to nothing by having sex with them openly in front of the whole of Israel.

Everyone pointed to the tent on the roof and said, “Absalom is in the tent having sex with his father’s wives.” One after the other, Absalom had sex with David’s precious brides. Absalom humbled, humiliated, embarrassed and shamed his father, David, by stomping from woman to woman in the sight of all David’s servants.

The women had to humbly give themselves to Absalom in the same way that they had been giving themselves to his father, David. Indeed, openly sleeping with his father’s wives and concubines was the greatest embarrassment for King David.

Watch out when you take your father’s precious things and embarrass him in the eyes of all. You are cursed beyond measure because you have humiliated your father and laid his precious treasure bare. You will receive a knock on your head that matches your impudence and wickedness. You will receive a freak accident that matches your freak behaviour. You will receive an aberration and an abnormality that matches your aberration and inappropriate behaviour.

6\. A TRAITOR FOR A TRAITOR:

Absalom was a traitor to his father. He betrayed him. His father trusted him and never looked back to see what he was doing. Yet he organised a complete rebellion against his father and attempted to kill his own father. He reaped the same thing according to the principle of judgment. He sowed a traitor and reaped a traitor!

When it was time to choose a trusted adviser whom he could depend on, he chose someone who turned out to be an equally good traitor. Hushai the Archite turned out to be equally good at deceiving and betraying Absalom.

No one knows why Absalom chose to reject Ahithophel who had been his faithful conspirator from the beginning and rather chose to believe someone whose loyalties were definitely questionable. Be careful as you betray people! Be careful as you live the life of a traitor! You will be shocked when your own wife turns out to be a traitor to you.

And Absalom and all the men of Israel said, the counsel of Hushai the Archite is better than the counsel of Ahithophel. For the Lord had appointed to defeat the good counsel of Ahithophel, to the intent that the Lord might bring evil upon Absalom. Then said Hushai unto Zadok and to Abiathar the priests, Thus and thus did Ahithophel counsel Absalom and the elders of Israel; and thus and thus have I counselled. Now therefore send quickly, and tell David, saying, Lodge not this night in the plains of the wilderness, but speedily pass over; lest the king be swallowed up, and all the people that are with him.

2 Samuel 17:14-16

7\. I WILL FOR I WILL:

How art thou fallen from heaven, O Lucifer, son of the morning! How art thou cut down to the ground, which didst weaken the nations! For thou hast said in thine heart, I WILL ASCEND INTO HEAVEN, I WILL EXALT MY THRONE above the stars of God: I WILL SIT ALSO UPON THE MOUNT of the congregation, in the sides of the north: I WILL ASCEND ABOVE THE HEIGHTS of the clouds; I WILL BE LIKE THE MOST HIGH. Yet thou shalt be brought down to hell, to the sides of the pit.

Isaiah 14:12-15

By the multitude of thy merchandise they have filled the midst of thee with violence, and thou hast sinned: therefore I WILL CAST THEE AS PROFANE out of the mountain of God: and I WILL DESTROY THEE, O covering cherub, from the midst of the stones of fire. Thine heart was lifted up because of thy beauty, thou hast corrupted thy wisdom by reason of thy brightness: I WILL CAST THEE TO THE GROUND, I WILL LAY THEE BEFORE KINGS, that they may behold thee. Thou hast defiled thy sanctuaries by the multitude of thine iniquities, by the iniquity of thy traffick; therefore will I bring forth a fire from the midst of thee, it shall devour thee, and I WILL BRING THEE TO ASHES upon the earth in the sight of all them that behold thee.

Ezekiel 28:16-18

Five “I Wills” of Lucifer

1\. I will ascend into heaven

2\. I will exalt my throne.

3\. I will sit on the mount of the congregation

4\. I will ascend above the heights of the clouds

5\. I will be like the Most High God

Five “I Wills” of God

1\. I will cast thee as profane out of the mountain of God

2\. I will destroy thee

3\. I will cast thee to the ground

4\. I will lay thee before kings

5\. I will bring thee to ashes

“I will” for “I will”

The five “I wills” of Lucifer were met with five “I wills” of God. Lucifer rose up with his strong will and pride. He rose up against God and declared his intentions to rise up and overthrow Almighty God. He had completely forgotten that he was appointed and created. God Almighty met him and threw him down with a greater show of strong will and determination. Lucifer deserved to be thrown down with strength because he had raised up himself against God.

8\. AN END FOR THE END:

Saying, I have sinned in that I have betrayed the innocent blood. And they said, what is that to us? See thou to that. And he cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, and departed, and went and hanged himself.

Matthew 27:4-5

Notice how the scripture on God’s formula for judgment is fulfilled in the life of Judas Iscariot.

Judas ended the life of Jesus; his life was also ended abruptly! Judas ended the ministry of Jesus and his own ministry as an apostle was ended abruptly. Be careful how you destroy other people’s lives and ministries. Your life will equally be destroyed. Your ministry will equally be destroyed.

One day, I watched a pastor rise up strongly against me and mislead many people. His activities ended up driving away good tithe-paying members from the churches. Through his rebellious activity, our church lost a lot of its income. I am sure that the income of the church was reduced by millions over the period of his rebellion. Divine judgment, which is based on “an eye for an eye” principle was implemented on this brother and he also lost all his finances. He became a pauper and had no income and no money. I considered it deeply and said to myself, “This brother has cost us a lot of money and now he has equally lost a lot of money and is penniless today.” Fortunately, our church was able to survive, even though we lost a lot of money.

9\. DISRESPECT FOR DISRESPECT:

Wherefore the Lord God of Israel saith, I said indeed that thy house, and the house of thy father, should walk before me for ever: but now the Lord saith, Be it far from me; for them that honour me I will honour, and they that despise me shall be lightly esteemed.

1 Samuel 2:30

Eli did not respect the ministry highly enough to restrain and restrict his sons openly. God’s response to Eli was to remove honour from him. God’s response to Eli was to despise him and his family. Notice how the scripture on God’s formula for judgment is fulfilled in the life of Eli and his sons. Eli did not honour the ministry and God did not honour Eli!

10\. HUMILIATION FOR HUMILIATION:

And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and told his two brethren without. And Shem and Japheth took a garment, and laid it upon both their shoulders, and went backward, and covered the nakedness of their father; and their faces were backward, and they saw not their father’s nakedness. And Noah awoke from his wine, and knew what his younger son had done unto him. And he said, Cursed be Canaan; a servant of servants shall he be unto his brethren.

Genesis 9:22-25

Notice how the scripture on God’s formula for judgment is fulfilled in the life of Noah and Ham, his son.

Ham sought to shame his father by exposing his drunkenness. Ham sought to embarrass his father in such a way that his father would lose respect and dignity in society. In response to this, Ham and his family received a withering curse to become servants of servants. Indeed, they were truly lowered in society and shamed forever. Today, Ham and his descendants have lost dignity for hundreds of years and have reaped an unprecedented lowering in the eyes of other races.

The descendants of Ham have often lived their lives as slaves, as poor people, as destitute, as unintelligent, as incapable and as low-class people. Indeed, it has been a lowering for a lowering! It has been humiliation for humiliation. It has been shame for shame. It has been demotion for demotion. It has been difficulty for difficulty.

In the ministry, I have encountered people who have tried desperately to shame me and say negative things about me to bring me low in the eyes of the public. There are times I have looked at these evangelists of shame and mused to myself, “Do these people know what is going to happen to them?” As they have tried with all their hearts to spread stories about me, so will their shame be multiplied on themselves and their descendants! As they have sought to poison people about me, so will an innumerable company be poisoned against them!

11\. REJECTION FOR REJECTION:

For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry. Because thou hast rejected the word of the LORD, he hath also rejected thee from being king. And Saul said unto Samuel, I have sinned: for I have transgressed the commandment of the LORD, and thy words: because I feared the people, and obeyed their voice. Now therefore, I pray thee, pardon my sin, and turn again with me, that I may worship the LORD. And Samuel said unto Saul, I will not return with thee: FOR THOU HAST REJECTED THE WORD OF THE LORD, AND THE LORD HATH REJECTED THEE FROM BEING KING OVER ISRAEL.

1 Samuel 15:23-26

Saul rejected the word of God. God responded with a fair and just judgment. God’s formula for judgment was implemented against Saul. An eye for an eye! A tooth for a tooth! A stripe for a stripe! Burning for burning! A hand for a hand! And rejection for rejection! Saul rejected God’s word and he received an equal and opposite rejection from being God’s appointed king.

When you reject God He will reject you too. This is the eternal law of judgment! Rejection for rejection! Disregard for disregard!

Disrespect for disrespect! Since you have no regard for the word of God, God will have no regard for you and your life and ministry. If you reject His word, God also will reject you.

12\. REFUSAL FOR REFUSAL:

My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: BECAUSE THOU HAST REJECTED KNOWLEDGE, I WILL ALSO REJECT THEE, that thou shalt be no priest to me: seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I will also forget thy children.

Hosea 4:6

Rejection for rejection! When you reject knowledge, you will also end up being rejected. There are those who reject books. There are those who reject knowledge. There are those who reject information. There are those who reject preaching messages. There are those who reject videos. Because they rejected all this information they will also be rejected. They will be refused entry into many places. They will be refused entry into the room of an evangelist. They will be refused entry into the room of a prophet. They will be refused entry into the room of the mega church pastors. Refusal for refusal! Disregard for disregard! Rejection for rejection!

13\. EXCLUSION FOR EXCLUSION:

And the LORD God said, Behold, the man is become as one of us, to know good and evil: and now, lest he put forth his hand, and take also of the tree of life, and eat, and live for ever: Therefore the LORD God sent him forth from the garden of Eden, to till the ground from whence he was taken. So he drove out the man; and he placed at the east of the garden of Eden Cherubims, and a flaming sword which turned every way, to keep the way of the tree of life.

Genesis 3:22-24

Exclusion for exclusion! Separation for separation! Rejection for rejection! And an eye for an eye!

Adam and Eve chose the devil’s company and words, rejecting God and His words. Adam and Eve were also rejected from the close company of God. When Adam and Eve had a chance, they excluded God and preferred the devil’s ideas and words. They had not met the devil before. They did not know who he was. But he convinced them very quickly to follow him. Adam and Eve had a cosy relationship with the devil that excluded God.

God felt so sad when He was excluded from the three-man fellowship. Adam, Eve and the devil had formed a fellowship. God was not wanted and He was not invited. The devil’s ideas were reigning. Satan had succeeded in winning the hearts, minds and company of Adam and Eve.

When the Lord God came to visit in the cool of the day, He was shocked that He had been excluded from the special fellowship. Adam and Eve were distant and did not seem so interested in His arrival and His fellowship.

God was suddenly excluded from His friends and His creation. Adam and Eve did not need electricity. They did not need running water. They did not need the internet. They did not need accommodation. They did not need houses or cars. They only needed the presence of God!

The presence of God made life in the Garden of Eden far greater than any life human beings are leading today with light, internet, mobile phones, cars, microwave ovens, dishwashers, electric stoves, washing machines and aeroplanes.

What was going to be the judgment for Adam and Eve? What had they done to God? They had excluded Him and separated themselves from Him. God’s formula for judgment was simple. A stripe for stripe! Burning for burning!

The Lord God separated Himself from them just as they had separated themselves from Him. The Lord excluded them from the Garden just as they had excluded God from their fellowship. Exclusion for exclusion! Separation for separation!

14\. MERCY FOR MERCY, JUDGMENT FOR JUDGMENT:

Blessed are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy.

Matthew 5:7

For he shall have judgment without mercy, that hath shewed no mercy; and mercy rejoiceth against judgment.

James 2:13

Blessed are the merciful! Why are the merciful blessed? The merciful are blessed because of the principle of an eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth. That principle applies to mercy as well. Mercy for mercy! Love for love! Kindness for kindness! Forgiveness for forgiveness! We love Him because He first loved us!

Because you show mercy you are due for mercy. Mercy will boomerang back to you and be multiplied in your face. Whenever you have a chance to show mercy, do not miss the opportunity. You are filling up your bank account with multiples of mercy. This mercy will be deployed one day when you are in need of mercy. The scripture is clear: he shall receive judgment without mercy that has shown no mercy.

This is why the scripture tells you not to judge people because when you judge people you are going to end up being judged. God would prefer that you do not judge anyone. When you do not judge anyone, you will not be judged. These are the words of Jesus Christ. Whenever possible, avoid passing judgment or commenting on things that do not need your opinion.

Judge not, that ye be not judged.

Matthew 7:1

I always remember when God kept me away from passing judgment on a brother. One day, a brother, a leader of a Christian group had fallen into difficulty. There was to be a meeting of an angry caucus of this fellowship one afternoon. The agenda of this meeting was to deal with the brother who had fallen into sin.

Everyone was heated up, ready to condemn the mistake and immoral behaviour of this brother whom we had all held in high esteem. The meeting had been set for two o’clock in the afternoon. At one o’clock, I was struck with a bizarre diarrhoea which held me pinned down in my house from one o’clock till five pm.

I could not believe that I was not going to be part of this meeting that was going to sort out our sinful leader. As soon as the meeting was over, the diarrhoea strangely stopped, just as suddenly as it had started. I would have been one of those who would have judged this brother. I would have ended my ministry by passing judgment on something I did not even understand.

By passing an unwarranted judgment, I would have set myself up for my own failures that would definitely happen in the future. I was going to need mercy and I did not need anything that would keep me from getting that mercy.

15\. SWORD FOR SWORD:

Then said Jesus unto him, Put up again thy sword into his place: for all they that take the sword shall perish with the sword.

Matthew 26:52

Jesus commanded the young man to put away his sword. The sword represents violence. Violence begets violence! Jesus knew that anyone who was violent would receive a violent response. It is an eternal law. An eye for an eye! A tooth for a tooth. Judgment for judgment. Mercy for mercy and a sword for a sword.

16\. PERSECUTION FOR PERSECUTION:

Paul persecuted the church and he equally received a harvest of persecution like no one else in the ministry.

Apostle Paul is the perfect example of someone who experienced the fair judgment of God on his ministry. He started out by attacking the church. He persecuted the church and persecuted pastors and servants of God who were seeking to spread the gospel. Notice how Paul describes himself. He described himself as a blasphemer, as injurious and as a persecutor of the church. Paul sowed the seeds of injury and persecution.

And I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who hath enabled me, for that he counted me faithful, putting me into the ministry; Who was before a blasphemer, and a persecutor, and injurious: but I obtained mercy, because I did it ignorantly in unbelief.

1 Timothy 1:12-13

Indeed, God saved him and washed him with the blood of Jesus. He was called and anointed and sent out to preach the gospel. But the wickedness that he had manifested against the church was not to be so easily forgotten. He would reap a harvest of persecution for the rest of his life. I want you to notice all these amazing scriptures that show how Paul was persecuted throughout his ministry. No one experienced as much persecution as Paul did.

And he was talking and arguing with the Hellenistic Jews; but they were attempting to put him to death.

Acts 9:29 (NASB)

But Jews came from Antioch and Iconium, and having won over the crowds, they stoned Paul and dragged him out of the city, supposing him to be dead.

Acts 16:22-23 (NASB)

For the multitude of the people kept following them, shouting, “Away with him!”

And as a great dissension was developing, the commander was afraid Paul would be torn to pieces by them and ordered the troops to go down and take him away from them by force, and bring him into the barracks.

Acts 23:10 (NASB)

To this present hour we are both hungry and thirsty, and are poorly clothed, and are roughly treated, and are homeless; and we toil, working with our own hands; when we are reviled, we bless; when we are persecuted, we endure;

1 Corinthians 4:11-12 (NASB)

For which I suffer hardship even to imprisonment as a criminal; but the word of God is not imprisoned.

2 Timothy 2:9 (NASB)

As you can see, Apostle Paul reaped a harvest of suffering, persecution, despair, assault and imprisonment like no one else. Why is this? The laws of God’s judgment are real and they do happen! Assault for assault! Insult for insult! Imprisonment for imprisonment! Stoning for stoning! Persecution for persecution! Beatings for beatings! Lashes for lashes! Mistreatment for mistreatment! Attempted murder for attempted murder! It is important that we believe in God’s laws of judgment. “Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves”, is all that He says to us (Romans 12:19). His eternal laws of judgment are in place.

And if ye will not be reformed by me by these things, but will WALK CONTRARY unto me; Then will I also WALK CONTRARY unto you, and will punish you yet seven times for your sins….

And if ye will not for all this hearken unto me, but WALK CONTRARY unto me; Then I will WALK CONTRARY unto you also in fury; and I, even I, will chastise you seven times for your sins.

Leviticus 26:23-24, 27-28

To walk contrary to something is to oppose it and to be hostile to it. Paul opposed the church and he was hostile to the church. If you walk contrary to God, He will walk contrary to you. Notice the eternal laws of judgment: Hostility for hostility! Opposition for opposition! God says if you walk contrary to Him, He will walk contrary to you.

17\. DENIAL FOR DENIAL:

And Peter remembered the word of Jesus, which said unto him, Before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice. And he went out, and wept bitterly.

Matthew 26:75

To deny means to abstain or to disown. Peter denied Jesus Christ when He was on trial. Since denial means to disown or abstain, we can safely say that Peter abstained from Christ and disowned Christ in the last hours of Jesus’ ministry on earth. I am sad to say that Peter disowned Christ just before He died on the cross. This is what happened.

If we are to go by the words of Peter, Jesus would have had nothing to do with Peter any longer. He would have thrown him out and completely dissociated himself from Peter. Jesus could not believe that Peter was disowning him at such a critical moment. I am sure Jesus was saying to Himself, “Peter says he does not know Me? Really? We just had dinner together.”

It was amazing that Peter seemed not to know Jesus when he was in the company of these other people. When he was in Jesus’ company and with the other disciples, he seemed to really know Jesus. Just a change of company seemed to change everything for Peter. He denied Jesus, which is to disown and abstain from Jesus. He abstained from mentioning Jesus’ name.

He said, “I do not know Him. I have never heard of Him.” Peter declared, “I am not religious.” He became completely secular. There was no sign that he had any pastoral training at all. No one believed that he could preach. This was all because of a little girl. Peter did this on three occasions to emphasise that the disowning and abstaining was real and deep. Jesus in His great love accepted Peter into His fold. Indeed, He accepted all His other disciples back into His fold. However, the judgment of God stands sure in its fairness and equity.

Keeping mercy for thousands, forgiving iniquity and transgression and sin, and that will by no means clear the guilty; visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children, and upon the children’s children, unto the third and to the fourth generation.

Exodus 34:7

Notice that God will by no means clear the guilty. He visits iniquity upon generations. Even though Peter was forgiven, he was also disowned in a sense.

In a sense Jesus’ glory and power also left Peter. Another person called Paul rose up and began to have the pre-eminence in the church. Even though Paul was not among the original twelve disciples, he became like the father of the church, instead of Peter.

Today, we are far more connected to Paul than to Peter. Indeed, Paul had no personal interactions with Jesus. But he became such a mighty apostle that no one could deny it. Paul himself said that he was not behind the chiefest of apostles. Who was the chiefest apostle? Was it not Peter? Paul said, I am not a whit behind the chiefest apostle.

In other words, “I am not a whit behind Peter.”

For I suppose I was not a whit behind the very chiefest apostles.

2 Corinthians 11:5

I am become a fool in glorying; ye have compelled me: for I ought to have been commended of you: FOR IN NOTHING AM I BEHIND THE VERY CHIEFEST APOSTLES, though I be nothing. Truly the signs of an apostle were wrought among you in all patience, in signs, and wonders, and mighty deeds.

2 Corinthians 12:11-12

Paul knew that Jesus had elevated him to the level of Peter who was the chiefest apostle. How did this happen? It seemed that the grace of revelation visited Paul, rather than Peter. Paul worked harder than Peter. Paul said that he laboured more abundantly than all the apostles. Obviously, he knew what Peter was doing. That was why he was bold enough to say that he laboured more than Peter did.

But by the grace of God I am what I am: and his grace which was bestowed upon me was not in vain; but I laboured more abundantly than they all: yet not I, but the grace of God which was with me.

1 Corinthians 15:10

Peter disowned Jesus at the crucifixion. Indeed, Jesus forgave Peter but there was a sense in which the power of Jesus lifted from Peter’s ministry after the crucifixion. This led to the pre-eminence of Paul in the church up until this day. God has raised up somebody who has laboured more than Apostle Peter. God has raised up someone who is not behind Peter in any way, even though Peter was appointed as the rock.

18\. TOUCHED BELLY FOR TOUCHED BELLY: THIGH FOR THIGH

But if thou hast gone aside to another instead of thy husband, and if thou be defiled, and some man have lain with thee beside thine husband: Then the priest shall charge the woman with an oath of cursing, and the priest shall say unto the woman, The LORD make thee a curse and an oath among thy people, when the LORD doth make thy thigh to rot, and thy belly to swell; And this water that causeth the curse shall go into thy bowels, TO MAKE THY BELLY TO SWELL, AND THY THIGH TO ROT: And the woman shall say, Amen, amen.

Numbers 5:20-22

The woman who is unfaithful to her husband shall reap a swollen belly and a rotten thigh. Belly for belly! And thigh for thigh! Instead of a belly full of a baby, she is getting a belly full of water. Instead of thighs that are full of delight and deliciousness, her thighs become rotten and ugly. “Belly-full” for “Belly-full”! Lovely thighs for ugly thighs!
