### CHAPTER 3\

Sin and Judgment\
======================================

Then was the part of the hand sent from him; and this writing was written. And this is the writing that was written, MENE, MENE, TEKEL, UPHARSIN. This is the interpretation of the thing: MENE; God hath numbered thy kingdom, and finished it. TEKEL; Thou art weighed in the balances, and art found wanting. PERES; Thy kingdom is divided, and given to the Medes and Persians.

Daniel 5:24-28

Belshazzar was tried and weighed in the balance and was found wanting. Every sin has a weight and every sin you commit tips the scales of judgment in one way or another.

Different sins have different weights. How can we know the weight of each sin? It is not easy to know the weight that a sin carries. However, we can notice the different things that Jesus said about different sins and give them a weight accordingly. We can also find out the comments that are made about different sins in the Bible.

All sins are not the same. Some sins are greater than others. If you think that all sins are the same, it is because you do not understand the scripture. Jesus pointed out to Pilate that the people who had brought Him to Pilate had committed a greater sin than he Pilate was about to commit. Jesus was pointing out to Pilate that the sin of the Pharisees and the religious leaders was greater than the sin of the politicians.

Jesus answered, Thou couldest have no power at all against me, except it were given thee from above: THEREFORE HE THAT DELIVERED ME UNTO THEE HATH THE GREATER SIN.

John 19:11

You can know the weight of a sin by considering the comments made about that sin. Some sins do not just invoke the wrath of God but they set off curses. Indeed, there are varied responses to the sins in the Bible. From today, you must have a proper estimation of a sin. Your estimation of a sin must be according to what is written in the Bible about it.

A greater sin, therefore, must invoke a greater response from you. What response is that? You must have a proper respect and a greater fear for great sins. Of course you must have a proper respect and the fear of God in you for every sin. However, since the Bible clearly makes certain sins greater than others, your fear, your respect and your defences against certain sins must be even greater.

In this chapter, you will see five different groupings of sins. Please take note of the different kinds of sins and the heavenly comments made about them. There are accursed sins. Accursed sins are sins that open the door to a curse. There are sins that Jesus made specific mention of and warned against. There are satanic sins. These are the sins that satan himself committed. Walking in these satanic sins unites you with the devil himself. There are sins that are called abominations. And finally there are sins that God hates. All these groups of sins are clearly outlined in the Bible.

Accursed Sins

Accursed sins are heavily weighted sins because they bring on curses, they invoke curses and they stir up a curse. Moses pronounced a curse on everyone that ventured into any of these evils. Please take note of how deadly these sins are because they truly bring curses. A curse is something you cannot escape from. A curse is condemnation to perpetual failure, poverty, sickness and disease from which you cannot be healed. Surely, you will not want a curse on your life.

Abominations

These are heavily weighted sins because they are called abominations. An abomination is a bad, shameful and detestable action. It is something that is greatly disliked and abhorred. Please take note that God greatly dislikes and abhors pride, wickedness, lying lips, a froward heart and injustice.

1\. Pride

Every one that is PROUD IN HEART IS AN ABOMINATION to the LORD: though hand join in hand, he shall not be unpunished.

Proverbs 16:5

2\. Wickedness

It is an ABOMINATION TO KINGS TO COMMIT WICKEDNESS: for the throne is established by righteousness.

Proverbs 16:12

3\. Lying

LYING LIPS ARE ABOMINATION to the LORD: but they that deal truly are his delight.

Proverbs 12:22

4\. A froward heart

They that are of A FROWARD HEART ARE ABOMINATION to the LORD: but such as are upright in their way are his delight.

Proverbs 11:20

5\. Injustice

He that JUSTIFIETH THE WICKED, and he that condemneth the just, even they both are ABOMINATION to the LORD.

Proverbs 17:15

Four Sins Jesus Specially Warned About

Jesus warned us about four dangerous sins. According to Jesus, there are certain things which we must avoid at all cost.

Jesus’ comments about these things are enough to warn us to stay off any possibility of getting involved in these sins.

You must notice Jesus’ comments about abuse of authority.

Causing little ones to fall is the abuse of authority and is considered a terrible sin by Jesus. Those who abuse their authority change the lives of those whom they abuse. They rob people of their ability to live normal lives. They take away people’s ability to have a healthy life.

Causing others to fall and to go low is another terrible sin which Jesus warned about. People are going to fall anyway. But the one through whom they fall is earmarked for a severe judgment. Jesus warned that those who cause people to fall and to go low are in danger of a terrible judgment.

Jesus then warned about the Pharisees’ hypocrisies. Jesus simply hated the pretence of the Pharisees. He could not stop Himself from calling them vipers. They hated Him all the more because He confronted their evil, pretentious and wicked ways.

Jesus also warned about betrayal. His only comment was that it would have been better for Judas that he was not born. That is a terrible saying. Jesus met many sinners. He encountered the woman who had committed adultery. He met the woman of Samaria. He met other tax collectors. He never made any such comments about them. But for the one who betrayed Him, He said it would have been better if he were not born. Indeed, Jesus’ comments about these four particular sins serve us a strong warning to stay as far from them as possible.

1\. Abuse of authority: A sin worthy of an unusual death.

Then said he unto the disciples, it is impossible but that offences will come: but woe unto him, through whom they come! It were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and he cast into the sea, than that he should offend one of these little ones.

Luke 17:1-2

2\. Causing others to fall: A sin worthy of an unusual death.

Then said he unto the disciples, it is impossible but that offences will come: but woe unto him, through whom they come! It were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and he cast into the sea, than that he should offend one of these little ones.

Luke 17:1-2

3\. Betrayal and treachery: Judged to be the worst of sins by Jesus

The Son of man indeed goeth, as it is written of him: but woe to that man by whom the Son of man is betrayed! GOOD WERE IT FOR THAT MAN IF HE HAD NEVER BEEN BORN.

Mark 14:21

4\. Hypocrisy: Judged to be a terrible thing

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness. Even so ye also outwardly appear righteous unto men, but within ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.

Matthew 23:27-28

Satanic Sins

Satan is the one who committed the first sin in the universe. The first sin in the universe was pride. Satan is not called a fornicator. There is no scripture which says that satan is a father of fornication. Fornication is a sin of human fleshly weakness but lying and deception are demonic in origin. You may fall into a sin of human weakness but enter into satanic sins when you start telling lies and involving yourself in deception.

Satan is the father of lies. Satan is a murderer. As soon as you get into sins such as murder and deception, you are getting nearer the devil. There are those who fall into sin but do not tell lies about it. Such people are better off than those who tell lies.

1. The sin of lying like the devil: When you become a liar like the devil, you are able to deceive a lot of people, the way satan deceived a lot of people. Hypocrisy takes you deep into that realm where you deceive a lot of people about who you really are.

Ye are of your father the devil, and the lusts of your father ye will do. He was a murderer from the beginning, and abode not in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father of it.

John 8:44

2. The sin of murder like the devil: Ruthlessly killing and murdering people is another satanic evil. Murderers must expect God to deal with them like the devil.

Ye are of your father the devil, and the lusts of your father ye will do. He was a murderer from the beginning, and abode not in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father of it.

John 8:44

3. The sin of accusing people like the devil: Blasphemy and speaking injurious words to accuse God’s servants and his prophets, day and night, is a terrible sin and will incur the same judgment that satan has received.

And I heard a loud voice saying in heaven, Now is come salvation, and strength, and the kingdom of our God, and the power of his Christ: for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night.

Revelation 12:10

4. The sin of tempting people like the devil: Satan is a tempter. He pushes people to the place where they fall deep into a hole where they cannot come out from. When you abuse your authority, you tempt people in a way that they cannot escape. God will judge you in the same way that He judged the devil. You assume demon-like characteristics when you are a tempter of human beings.

Then was Jesus led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil.

Matthew 4:1

5. The sin of opposing like the devil: Satan’s name was Lucifer. His name was changed from Lucifer to satan. Satan means opposer. Do not allow yourself to become an opposer. Do not always be part of the opposition. God wants you to be someone who is positive and a can-do person.

Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour: Whom resist stedfast in the faith, knowing that the same afflictions are accomplished in your brethren that are in the world.

1 Peter 5:8-9
