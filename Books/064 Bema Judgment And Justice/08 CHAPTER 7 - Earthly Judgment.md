### CHAPTER 7\

Earthly Judgment\
======================================

SOME MEN’S SINS ARE OPEN BEFOREHAND, GOING BEFORE TO JUDGMENT; and some men they follow after. Likewise also the good works of some are manifest beforehand; and they that are otherwise cannot be hid.

1 Timothy 5:24-25

Judgment for sin will surely come. It is about the timing. It may seem that some people totally escape judgment but that is not the case! Some people seem to die just before they are tried. Some people die just before they go to jail. It often seems that some people are lucky when it comes to judgment and justice.

That is not the case! God would not be fair and just if He allowed large numbers of sinners and wicked people to escape from judgment.

And he shall judge the world in righteousness, he shall minister judgment to the people in uprightness.

Psalm 9:8

When you experience judgment on earth, it obviously affects the judgments you will also receive in eternity.

Although most of the judgment is going to take place at the judgment seat of Christ and before the white throne, some judgments are going to take place on earth. There are two reasons why you may have to receive some judgments on earth.

First of all, people receive judgments on earth to prevent them from continuing in their evil ways. In one instance, Apostle Paul commended some of his followers to satan so that they would learn not to blaspheme.

Holding faith, and a good conscience; which some having put away concerning faith have made shipwreck: Of whom is Hymenaeus and Alexander; whom I have delivered unto Satan, that they may learn not to blaspheme.

1 Timothy 1:19-20

The second reason for receiving judgments on earth is so that your judgment in eternity would be modified. When you receive judgment on earth, it modifies your eternal judgment. Is it better to receive judgment on earth or in eternity? I cannot tell. What I do know is that God is fair and He will ensure that you do not suffer from double taxation.

It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you, and such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father’s wife. And ye are puffed up, and have not rather mourned, that he that hath done this deed might be taken away from among you. For I verily, as absent in body, but present in spirit, have judged already, as though I were present, concerning him that hath so done this deed, In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when ye are gathered together, and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ, To deliver such an one unto Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.

1 Corinthians 5:1-5

In the scripture above, we see how Apostle Paul allowed judgment to come onto some fornicators in Corinth so that they would have a different judgment before the judgment seat of Christ. Indeed, some people have judgments on earth so that their eternal judgments would be modified and they would be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.

Some people have judgments right away whilst some people have them later. If you are one of those chosen to have judgments on earth, you will experience certain responses to your sins whilst those who have not been chosen for that will have to wait until eternity to realise the full impact of their sins. This is why some people steal and seem to get away with it while others are punished in full.

Many people live long after committing many atrocities in this world. Many people die as wise old harmless gentlemen whereas they have lived an outrageous life of barbaric brutality, murder and depravity. You would never ascribe certain evils to some of these elderly gentlemen. Adolf Hitler himself looked like a harmless old man who loved going for mountain walks with his dog. Meanwhile, Adolf Hitler, by his life, leadership and decisions, caused the deaths of over fifty million people.

All in all, the judgment of God stands sure. Whatsoever a man sows, he shall reap (Galatians 6:7).

Then Peter opened his mouth, and said, of a truth I perceive that God is no respecter of persons:

Acts 10:34

Whatsoever sin or crime you commit, you can rest assured that you will receive a just response from the divine courts of heaven. No one is going to escape the divine judgments that have been laid out concerning the affairs of men. God still rules in the affairs of men!

This matter is by the decree of the watchers, and the demand by the word of the holy ones: to the intent that the living may know that THE MOST HIGH RULETH IN THE KINGDOM OF MEN, and giveth it to whomsoever he will, and setteth up over it the basest of men.

Daniel 4:17

I want to share with you several examples of how God caused judgments to come upon people whilst on earth. Many people on earth suffer terrible consequences for their sins. Others seem to simply pass by, committing crimes and escaping any form of judgment and justice. Make no mistake, the principles of God’s word; an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth, a hand for a hand, a stripe for a stripe, burning for burning, rejection for rejection, will always apply to human beings in this world.

1\. The Earthly Judgment of David for the sin of numbering Israel.

God was angry with David for counting the people of Israel. The judgment for this was to have seventy thousand people die from an epidemic. The angel of death was released against the population. The release of this angel of death was the cause of the epidemic. Perhaps, it was a viral epidemic similar to the COVID 19 pandemic that hit the whole world.

And SATAN STOOD UP AGAINST ISRAEL, AND PROVOKED DAVID TO NUMBER ISRAEL. And David said to Joab and to the rulers of the people, Go, number Israel from Beer-sheba even to Dan; and bring the number of them to me, that I may know it…

And God was displeased with this thing; therefore he smote Israel. And David said unto God, I have sinned greatly, because I have done this thing: but now, I beseech thee, do away the iniquity of thy servant; for I have done very foolishly. And the Lord spake unto Gad, David’s seer, saying, Go and tell David, saying, Thus saith the Lord, I offer thee three things: choose thee one of them, that I may do it unto thee. So Gad came to David, and said unto him, Thus saith the Lord, Choose thee Either three years’ famine; or three months to be destroyed before thy foes, while that the sword of thine enemies overtaketh thee; or else three days the sword of the Lord, even the pestilence, in the land, and the angel of the Lord destroying throughout all the coasts of Israel. Now therefore advise thyself what word I shall bring again to him that sent me. And David said unto Gad, I am in a great strait: let me fall now into the hand of the Lord; for very great are his mercies: but let me not fall into the hand of man. SO THE LORD SENT PESTILENCE UPON ISRAEL: AND THERE FELL OF ISRAEL SEVENTY THOUSAND MEN. AND GOD SENT AN ANGEL UNTO JERUSALEM TO DESTROY IT: AND AS HE WAS DESTROYING, THE LORD BEHELD, AND HE REPENTED HIM OF THE EVIL, AND SAID TO THE ANGEL THAT DESTROYED, IT IS ENOUGH, stay now thine hand. And the angel of the Lord stood by the threshingfloor of Ornan the Jebusite. And David lifted up his eyes, and saw the angel of the Lord stand between the earth and the heaven, having a drawn sword in his hand stretched out over Jerusalem. Then David and the elders of Israel, who were clothed in sackcloth, fell upon their faces. And David said unto God, Is it not I that commanded the people to be numbered? Even I it is that have sinned and done evil indeed; but as for these sheep, what have they done? Let thine hand, I pray thee, O Lord my God, be on me, and on my father’s house; but not on thy people, that they should be plagued.

1 Chronicles 21:1-2,7-17

2\. The Earthly Judgment of David for the sin of adultery: family troubles.

King David suffered dearly for committing adultery with Bathsheba. His judgment involved the death of his child, experiencing continuous conflict in his family and having his son sleep with his wives.

Indeed, it is frightening to think of the judgment that King David experienced for his adultery. Considering that so many people commit adultery and fornication, judgment according to the “eye for an eye” principle is scary to even contemplate if it is to be carried through for everybody. Although David committed his sin secretly, his punishment was done openly. Indeed, David paid for his adultery.

I am sure that many people on earth do not pay in the same way for the adulteries and fornications they have committed. Whether it is better to go to heaven and receive the recompense for such sins or to receive a judgment on this earth for these sins, I cannot tell! I know a pastor who was a longstanding adulterer who lived happily beyond the age of eighty while he successfully pastored his church.

I do not know of any judgment comparable to what happened to King David that happened to this pastor. His church declined towards the end of his life but he survived and seemed to live longer than most other people. It is possible that he had his judgment on earth. But it is also possible that a fiery judgment awaits him in heaven.

And I gave thee thy master’s house, and thy master’s wives into thy bosom, and gave thee the house of Israel and of Judah; and if that had been too little, I would moreover have given unto thee such and such things. Wherefore hast thou despised the commandment of the Lord, to do evil in his sight? THOU HAST KILLED URIAH THE HITTITE WITH THE SWORD, AND HAST TAKEN HIS WIFE TO BE THY WIFE, and hast slain him with the sword of the children of Ammon. NOW THEREFORE THE SWORD SHALL NEVER DEPART FROM THINE HOUSE; BECAUSE THOU HAST DESPISED ME, AND HAST TAKEN THE WIFE OF URIAH THE HITTITE TO BE THY WIFE. THUS SAITH THE LORD, BEHOLD, I WILL RAISE UP EVIL AGAINST THEE OUT OF THINE OWN HOUSE, AND I WILL TAKE THY WIVES BEFORE THINE EYES, AND GIVE THEM UNTO THY NEIGHBOUR, AND HE SHALL LIE WITH THY WIVES IN THE SIGHT OF THIS SUN. For thou didst it secretly: but I will do this thing before all Israel, and before the sun. And David said unto Nathan, I have sinned against the Lord. And Nathan said unto David, The Lord also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die. Howbeit, because by this deed thou hast given great occasion to the enemies of the Lord to blaspheme, the child also that is born unto thee shall surely die.

2 Samuel 12:8-14

3\. The Earthly Judgment of Korah and his family for rebellion against Moses; an unusual death;

And Moses said, Hereby ye shall know that the Lord hath sent me to do all these works; for I have not done them of mine own mind. IF THESE MEN DIE THE COMMON DEATH OF ALL MEN, OR IF THEY BE VISITED AFTER THE VISITATION OF ALL MEN; THEN THE LORD HATH NOT SENT ME. BUT IF THE LORD MAKE A NEW THING, AND THE EARTH OPEN HER MOUTH, AND SWALLOW THEM UP, WITH ALL THAT APPERTAIN UNTO THEM, AND THEY GO DOWN QUICK INTO THE PIT; THEN YE SHALL UNDERSTAND THAT THESE MEN HAVE PROVOKED THE LORD. And it came to pass, as he had made an end of speaking all these words, that the ground clave asunder that was under them: And the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed them up, and their houses, and all the men that appertained unto Korah, and all their goods. They, and all that appertained to them, went down alive into the pit, and the earth closed upon them: and they perished from among the congregation. And all Israel that were round about them fled at the cry of them: for they said, lest the earth swallow us up also. And there came out a fire from the Lord, and consumed the two hundred and fifty men that offered incense.

Numbers 16:28-35

Korah paid the price for his rebellion against Moses. His judgment consisted of a premature and unnatural death for himself and for his entire family. Death also came upon all his associates and those who partook in the rebellion. Indeed, if this is the judgment for disloyalty, all those who partake in such fights against God’s legitimate authorities must be careful.

Many of those who forget, those who dishonour, those who leave you, those who are disloyal, those who pretend, those who are traitors, those who betray, those who oppose and those who vaunt themselves against authority do not experience such judgments. If Korah experienced this judgment on earth for his disloyalty and rebellion, you must watch out for yourself. Jesus said to Judas, “It would have been better for you if you had not been born.” (Matthew 26:24).

Disloyalty and treachery will have terrible consequences. All disloyal people must take note that the judgment for betrayal and treachery is very severe. God would not be fair to do this to Korah and leave other rebels untouched.

4\. The Earthly Judgment of Pharaoh for not letting God’s people go to serve Him.

Pharaoh was judged severely by God. God’s judgment is on those who do not allow people to serve Him. Anyone who prevents people from serving the Lord must watch out for a possible reaction from God. Stubbornness will always be judged with destruction. The helpers of Pharaoh warned Pharaoh that Egypt would be destroyed.

Perhaps, your life will be destroyed for not allowing people to work for God. What do you want to see or hear or experience before you soften up towards your God?

Stubbornness is a very dangerous trait to have. Pharaoh’s stubbornness landed him in deep trouble. Many stubborn people seem to get away with their sins and wickedness. It seems that not all judgments are going to be experienced on earth. Some people’s sins are judged early whilst some will receive their judgment in eternity.

And Pharaoh’s servants said unto him, how long shall this man be a snare unto us? Let the men go, that they may serve the Lord their God: knowest thou not yet that EGYPT IS DESTROYED? And Moses and Aaron were brought again unto Pharaoh: and he said unto them, Go, serve the Lord your God: but who are they that shall go?

Exodus 10:7-8

5\. The Earthly Judgments of Nadab and Abihu for offering strange fire and doing things differently from what had been commanded.

And Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, took either of them his censer, and put fire therein, and put incense thereon, and offered strange fire before the Lord, which he commanded them not. And there went out fire from the Lord, and devoured them, and they died before the Lord. Then Moses said unto Aaron, This is it that the Lord spake, saying, I will be sanctified in them that come nigh me, and before all the people I will be glorified. And Aaron held his peace. And Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Uzziel the uncle of Aaron, and said unto them, Come near, carry your brethren from before the sanctuary out of the camp. So they went near, and carried them in their coats out of the camp; as Moses had said. And Moses said unto Aaron, and unto Eleazar and unto Ithamar, his sons, Uncover not your heads, neither rend your clothes; lest ye die, and lest wrath come upon all the people: but let your brethren, the whole house of Israel, bewail the burning which the Lord hath kindled.

Leviticus 10:1-6

Nadab and Abihu were Levites who died suddenly because they did strange, unexpected and inappropriate things in the temple. All those who behave inappropriately in the house of God must watch out for judgment. Nadab and Abihu did not escape judgment on this earth.

They died suddenly even though they were pastors. Once again, if all those who behave inappropriately in the church were to die suddenly, most of us would be dead by now. Indeed, some people’s sins are judged immediately whilst others carry on and are judged much later. This is the nature of judgment. Some happen now and some happen later!

6\. The Earthly Judgment of sickness for Miriam for criticizing Moses marriage.

And Miriam and Aaron spake against Moses because of the Ethiopian woman whom he had married: for he had married an Ethiopian woman. And they said, Hath the Lord indeed spoken only by Moses? Hath he not spoken also by us? And the Lord heard it. (Now the man Moses was very meek, above all the men which were upon the face of the earth.) And the Lord spake suddenly unto Moses, and unto Aaron, and unto Miriam, Come out ye three unto the tabernacle of the congregation. And they three came out. And the Lord came down in the pillar of the cloud, and stood in the door of the tabernacle, and called Aaron and Miriam: and they both came forth. And he said, Hear now my words: If there be a prophet among you, I the Lord will make myself known unto him in a vision, and will speak unto him in a dream.

My servant Moses is not so, who is faithful in all mine house. With him will I speak mouth to mouth, even apparently, and not in dark speeches; and the similitude of the Lord shall he behold: wherefore then were ye not afraid to speak against my servant Moses? And the anger of the Lord was kindled against them; and he departed.

And the cloud departed from off the tabernacle; and, behold, Miriam became leprous, white as snow: and Aaron looked upon Miriam, and, behold, she was leprous. And Aaron said unto Moses, Alas, my lord, I beseech thee, lay not the sin upon us, wherein we have done foolishly, and wherein we have sinned. Let her not be as one dead, of whom the flesh is half consumed when he cometh out of his mother’s womb. And Moses cried unto the Lord, saying, heal her now, O God, I beseech thee. And the Lord said unto Moses, If her father had but spit in her face, should she not be ashamed seven days? Let her be shut out from the camp seven days, and after that let her be received in again. And Miriam was shut out from the camp seven days: and the people journeyed not till Miriam was brought in again.

Numbers 12:1-15

Criticism is one of the sins you must be careful of. Miriam received an instant judgment for her sin of criticism. Many people criticize things today and seem to get away scot-free. Nothing happens to them for fifteen years! They may even seem to prosper, flourish and do well as their mouths are filled with wicked criticisms of God’s anointed servants.

Miriam did not have it that way. She was struck down with leprosy almost immediately. God’s earth-bound judgment was swift, direct and severe.

Do not think you have escaped if you do not get leprosy. God will judge you in eternity for all your criticisms.

He may allow some judgment to come your way on earth but He may reserve it wholly for eternity. Do not forget our opening scripture: “Some men’s sins are open beforehand, going before to judgment; and some men they follow after. Likewise also the good works of some are manifest beforehand; and they that are otherwise cannot be hid” (1 Timothy 5:24-25).

This is why you must confess your sins everyday. You must seek to cleanse yourself of sins that attract judgment from God. You must pray that you do not carry certain things into eternity.

7\. The Earthly Judgment of Saul for rejecting the commandments of God.

Saul suffered dearly for his rejection of God’s instructions. He disobeyed God’s clear instructions to destroy the Amalekites. Almighty God interpreted this disobedience as rejection. His judgment was based on the principle of an eye for an eye, a tooth for a tooth, rejection for rejection.

Saul was rejected from being the king and David was appointed even while Saul still sat on the throne. A young man called David was anointed to be king in place of Saul. This rejection was God’s judgment on Saul for his disobedience.

There is so much disobedience in the church today. Perhaps, many of us are already rejected and do not even know it. The appointment of others to replace you is one of the judgments you must watch out for. A silent rejection with the appointment of your successor whilst you are still alive, is one of the most deadly punishments in the world.

Then came the word of the Lord unto Samuel, saying, it repenteth me that I have set up Saul to be king: for he is turned back from following me, and hath not performed my commandments. And it grieved Samuel; and he cried unto the Lord all night.

For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry. Because thou hast rejected the word of the Lord, he hath also rejected thee from being king. And Saul said unto Samuel, I have sinned: for I have transgressed the commandment of the Lord, and thy words: because I feared the people, and obeyed their voice.

Now therefore, I pray thee, pardon my sin, and turn again with me, that I may worship the Lord. And Samuel said unto Saul, I WILL NOT RETURN WITH THEE: FOR THOU HAST REJECTED THE WORD OF THE LORD, AND THE LORD HATH REJECTED THEE FROM BEING KING OVER ISRAEL.

And as Samuel turned about to go away, he laid hold upon the skirt of his mantle, and it rent. And Samuel said unto him, The Lord hath rent the kingdom of Israel from thee this day, and hath given it to a neighbour of thine, that is better than thou.

1 Samuel 15:10-11, 23-28

8\. The Earthly Judgment of Er for his wickedness; premature death:

And Judah took a wife for Er his firstborn, whose name was Tamar. And Er, Judah’s firstborn, WAS WICKED IN THE SIGHT OF THE LORD; AND THE LORD SLEW HIM.

Genesis 38:6-7

Er was a man whom God found to be wicked. The Lord did not like his wickedness and slew him. He experienced death prematurely because he was a wicked person. Be careful of wickedness. Be kind, loving and patient.

Wickedness attracts the death sentence! I am sure you know that many people seem to be living happily ever after on earth. Nothing bad seems to be happening to many of these wicked people on earth. I can assure you that if they do not receive their punishment on earth, they will receive it as soon as they enter into eternity. God is fair and just! If He slew Er for wickedness, why should He allow you to live on in your wickedness?

9\. The Earthly Judgment of Onan for not giving his brother’s wife a child :

And Judah said unto Onan, Go in unto thy brother’s wife, and marry her, and raise up seed to thy brother. And Onan knew that the seed should not be his; and it came to pass, when he went in unto his brother’s wife, that he spilled it on the ground, lest that he should give seed to his brother. And the thing which he did displeased the Lord: wherefore he slew him also.

Genesis 38:8-10

Onan suffered greatly because he was unwilling to marry his brother’s wife and refused to impregnate her. In those days, it was an important duty to marry your brother’s widow. His failure to show this kindness to a widow cost him his life. God was unhappy with Onan and his mistake was recorded to serve as a reminder for us all. If all those who do not show kindness to widows were judged in this way, there would be many more sudden and unexplained deaths in the world today.

Indeed, God is reserving His judgment for our lack of compassion. It is likely that heaven will reveal the judgment of those who do not show compassion.

Remember how Jesus warned that the nations would be divided into two groups. The “sheep” nations would be set to the right and the “goat” nations would be set to the left. What was the basis of this great judgment of the nations? The basis of this judgment was the lack of compassion.

Onan had his punishment for his lack of compassion on his brother’s widow. His judgment was instant and it happened whilst he was on earth. In eternity, you will hear those words, “I was sick and you did not visit me. I was hungry and you did not feed me. I was in prison and you did not visit me. I was thirsty and you did not give me anything to drink” (Matthew 25:42-43)

Your lack of compassion will be judged severely in eternity. Today, you may be getting away with your lack of compassion. But in eternity, all who failed to show compassion will not escape the judgment.

10\. The Earthly Judgment on the prophet who ate bread in Bethel.

And it came to pass, as they sat at the table, that the word of the Lord came unto the prophet that brought him back: And he cried unto the man of God that came from Judah, saying, Thus saith the Lord, Forasmuch as thou hast disobeyed the mouth of the Lord, and hast not kept the commandment which the Lord thy God commanded thee, But camest back, and hast eaten bread and drunk water in the place, of the which the Lord did say to thee, Eat no bread, and drink no water; thy carcase shall not come unto the sepulchre of thy fathers. And it came to pass, after he had eaten bread, and after he had drunk, that he saddled for him the ass, to wit, for the prophet whom he had brought back. And when he was gone, a lion met him by the way, and slew him: and his carcase was cast in the way, and the ass stood by it, the lion also stood by the carcase. And, behold, men passed by, and saw the carcase cast in the way, and the lion standing by the carcase: and they came and told it in the city where the old prophet dwelt. And when the prophet that brought him back from the way heard thereof, he said, IT IS THE MAN OF GOD, WHO WAS DISOBEDIENT UNTO THE WORD OF THE LORD: THEREFORE THE LORD HATH DELIVERED HIM UNTO THE LION, WHICH HATH TORN HIM, AND SLAIN HIM, ACCORDING TO THE WORD OF THE LORD, WHICH HE SPAKE UNTO HIM.

1 Kings 13:20-26

The prophet who ate bread in Bethel is famous for dying an unusual death for disobeying a little instruction. Many years ago, I heard a man of God describing how God called him. He said, “God told me, you must obey me in big things and also in the little things.”

In this great call, this man of God realised that big instructions are as important as small instructions. The prophet of Bethel obeyed God in the big things. He prophesied the word of God and declared judgement on the king. There were great manifestations of God’s power.

However, he disobeyed one little instruction: “Do not eat bread in Bethel!” “Do not have your dinner in Bethel.” Unfortunately, this prophet died because he disobeyed the little instructions.

This is an amazing story and it reveals to all of us that judgment does come to people because they disobey little instructions. I am sure that most of us do not imagine that we can suffer great judgment because we disobey little instructions.

11\. The Earthly Judgment of Hananiah for making people trust in a lie and teaching people to be rebellious.

Then said the prophet Jeremiah unto Hananiah the prophet, Hear now, Hananiah; The Lord hath not sent thee; but thou makest this people to trust in a lie. Therefore thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will cast thee from off the face of the earth: this year thou shalt die, because thou hast taught rebellion against the Lord. So Hananiah the prophet died the same year in the seventh month.

Jeremiah 28:15-17

There was a prophet called Hananiah who misled the people of God. His teachings were wrong. His life was misleading. He caused a great error to fall on the people of God. Large numbers of people were misled because of this man. He taught rebellion and inspired disloyalty. His judgment was swift. A death sentence was pronounced on him. He was to die within a year. To everyone’s amazement, Hananiah the prophet died seven months later.

Today, we have many prophets who are misleading people. Today, we have mighty men of God who control large congregations and mislead God’s people. It is clear that many of them are living good lives and prospering every day. Judgment may not come in this lifetime. But you can bet your last dollar that God is fair, righteous and just. He will not judge one prophet for committing a crime and ignore the others who are doing exactly the same thing.

12\. The Earthly Judgment of Jezebel for manipulating her husband into becoming a murderer.

And of Jezebel also spake the Lord, saying, the dogs shall eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel. Him that dieth of Ahab in the city the dogs shall eat; and him that dieth in the field shall the fowls of the air eat.

1 Kings 21:23-24

And when Jehu was come to Jezreel, Jezebel heard of it; and she painted her face, and tired her head, and looked out at a window. And as Jehu entered in at the gate, she said, Had Zimri peace, who slew his master? And he lifted up his face to the window, and said, who is on my side? Who? And there looked out to him two or three eunuchs. And he said, throw her down. So they threw her down: and some of her blood was sprinkled on the wall, and on the horses: and he trode her under foot. And when he was come in, he did eat and drink, and said, Go, see now this cursed woman, and bury her: for she is a king’s daughter. And they went to bury her: but they found no more of her than the skull, and the feet, and the palms of her hands. Wherefore they came again, and told him. And he said, This is the word of the Lord, which he spake by his servant Elijah the Tishbite, saying, In the portion of Jezreel shall dogs eat the flesh of Jezebel: And the carcase of Jezebel shall be as dung upon the face of the field in the portion of Jezreel; so that they shall not say, This is Jezebel.

2 Kings 9:30-37

Jezebel received a terrible judgment because she influenced her husband in the wrong way. There are many strong women who are manipulating their husbands into doing things the husbands would never have done on their own.

One day, I heard a pastor make a comment. He said, “I have never met a pastor from ‘such and such a country’ who is not controlled by his wife.” I thought to myself, “I know a number of people who are pastors from that country and I think I agree with him.”

Jezebel was a dangerous woman. Her husband did much evil and she was credited with stirring him up. You must be careful what you stir up your husband to do.

But there was none like unto Ahab, which did sell himself to work wickedness in the sight of the LORD, WHOM JEZEBEL HIS WIFE STIRRED UP.

1 Kings 21:25

Ahab had no intentions of becoming a murderer. Ahab actually entered into negotiations with Naboth for his vineyard. Ahab had no plans of killing people for their lands and properties. It was Jezebel who stirred up Ahab until he became a terribly wicked person.

If all women who stir up their husbands to do evil and to disobey God were to be judged on earth, we may have much fewer wives alive today. As you can see, not all judgment happens on the earth. Some people’s sins are dealt with immediately. Others simply pass on and are judged in eternity.

Eve also stirred up Adam to disobey God. Through Eve’s influence, Adam became a sinner and rejected God’s company and God’s direction. Even though Adam was the head of the house, he received the influence to do evil from his wife.

You will notice that Eve did not escape judgment, even though she was not the head of the family. In God’s judgment, there is equity. Judgment is and was shared fairly to everyone who had a part to play in the fall of man. Eve could not hide behind her excuse that she was deceived. Adam could not hide behind the fact that his wife had stirred him up.

In heaven, we will see true judgments based on the right things. No one will be judged unfairly. No one will receive condemnation for something he did not do. Whilst we are on earth we can expect to continually see some people getting away with every bad thing they do whilst others get judged immediately. Never forget this principle: “Some men’s sins are open beforehand, going before to judgment; and some men they follow after. Likewise also the good works of some are manifest beforehand; and they that are otherwise cannot be hid” (1 Timothy 5:24-25).

13\. The Earthly Judgment of Ahaziah to not recover from his sickness because he trusted in an idol, rather than trusting in God.

And Ahaziah fell down through a lattice in his upper chamber that was in Samaria, and was sick: and he sent messengers, and said unto them, “Go, inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, whether I will recover from this sickness.”

But the angel of the Lord said to Elijah the Tishbite, Arise, go up to meet the messengers of the king of Samaria, and say unto them, Is it not because there is not a God in Israel, that ye go to enquire of Baal-zebub the god of Ekron? Now therefore thus saith the Lord, Thou shalt not come down from that bed on which thou art gone up, but shalt surely die. And Elijah departed.

2 Kings 1:2-4

And he said unto him, THUS SAITH THE LORD, FORASMUCH AS THOU HAST SENT MESSENGERS TO ENQUIRE OF BAAL-ZEBUB THE GOD OF EKRON, IS IT NOT BECAUSE THERE IS NO GOD IN ISRAEL TO ENQUIRE OF HIS WORD? therefore thou shalt not come down off that bed on which thou art gone up, but shalt surely die. So he died according to the word of the Lord which Elijah had spoken. And Jehoram reigned in his stead in the second year of Jehoram the son of Jehoshaphat king of Judah; because he had no son. Now the rest of the acts of Ahaziah which he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Israel?

2 Kings 1:16-18

Ahaziah, the king received an instant judgment of being judged with a sickness from which he could not recover. God is offended when we do not turn to Him first. God wants us to look to Him for everything. If judgment were to come to all those who do not seek God first, large sections of the church would pass away next week.

How many people in the church seek God as a first option? Most of us are like Ahaziah. To many of us, God is a side plate or a spare tyre. We seek other things first and God has to fit in at the end, if at all.

In eternity, there will be eternal points of regret where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth because we did not take seeking God first seriously. The church has become like King Ahaziah. Most of us are seeking God as a last option. I can assure you that eternity will reveal the seriousness of this grave mistake of putting God last.

I once read a book in which a pastor visited heaven. He met several people he had known on earth who happened to have died. They were all in heaven but many of them were in the outer and darkest parts of eternity.

He met a man who said to him, “The greatest mistake is to find the Lord and yet go on living for yourself after that.” He said, “If I had known on earth what I know here, I would not have lived the way I did.” He told the prophet, “You are greatly blessed to come here before you die. We knew the Lord but we lived for ourselves rather than living for Him. The grief that we experienced when we understood how we had greatly wasted our lives was beyond any grief possible on earth. To come to heaven and discover this reality is the most terrible experience of all. I felt that I was in the deepest dungeon of hell as I stood before the Lord. The Lord Jesus was resolute until my life had been reviewed. I had to say ‘Sorry’ and ask for mercy before He took away the darkness from my life. Now I am in the darkest part of heaven.”1
