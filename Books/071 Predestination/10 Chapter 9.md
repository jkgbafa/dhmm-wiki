### Chapter 9\

The Predestination of Jesus\
-------------------------------------------------

\

For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon his shoulder: and his name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace.

Of the increase of his government and peace there shall be no end, upon the throne of David, and upon his kingdom, to order it, and to establish it with judgment and with justice from henceforth even for ever. The zeal of the LORD of hosts will perform this.

Isaiah 9:6-7

The life of Jesus proves that predestination is real! All the scriptures in the Old Testament predict in detail how the Messiah would come and what will happen in His life and ministry. There are many things that Jesus could not control. The fact that He would be crucified between thieves was not under His control. The fact that He would be buried with the rich was not determined by Him. How could Jesus have decided how He would be buried after He was dead?

A study of the predetermined life of Jesus Christ should make everyone have faith in the truth that we are basically acting out a script that has been written already. I am producing here, fifty-five scripts that were written hundreds of years before Jesus came on the scene. Truly, Jesus came in the volume of the book to fulfil the destiny that God had purposed years ago.

Perhaps, this is how your life is too! You are fulfilling a destiny that is determined by God already! Spend your life seeking the will of God and praying that you will fulfil all the purposes and plans that God has already laid out for you.

43\. Christ was predestined to be a prophet like Moses.

THE LORD YOUR GOD WILL RAISE UP FOR YOU A PROPHET like me from among you, from your fellow Israelites. You must listen to him. For this is what you asked of the Lord your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly when you said, “Let us not hear the voice of the Lord our God nor see this great fire anymore, or we will die.”

Deuteronomy 18:15–16 (NIV)

Predestination that Jesus Christ would be a prophet like Moses fulfilled.

“But do not think I will accuse you before the Father. Your accuser is Moses, on whom your hopes are set. IF YOU BELIEVED MOSES, YOU WOULD BELIEVE ME, FOR HE WROTE ABOUT ME. But since you do not believe what he wrote, how are you going to believe what I say?”

John 5:45–47 (NIV)

I WILL RAISE UP FOR MYSELF A FAITHFUL PRIEST, who will do according to what is in my heart and mind. I will firmly establish his priestly house, and they will minister before my anointed one always.

1 Samuel 2:35 (NIV)

Predestination that Jesus Christ would be a faithful priest fulfilled.

For this reason he had to be made like them, fully human in every way, in order THAT HE MIGHT BECOME A MERCIFUL AND FAITHFUL HIGH PRIEST in service to God, and that he might make atonement for the sins of the people.

Hebrews 2:17 (NIV)
