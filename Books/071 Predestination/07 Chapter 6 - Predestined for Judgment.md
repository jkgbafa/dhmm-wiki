### Chapter 6\

Predestined for Judgment\
----------------------------------------------

\

And as it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment:

Hebrews 9:27

It is appointed to men to be judged. You and I are predestined to appear before a judgment seat. You are not destined to go to hell per se. You are destined to appear in court and be judged for your life on this earth. Every little thing you did will receive judgment. Even giving a glass of water will receive its reward. It is important to know that you are hurtling towards your judgment day.

There is nothing you can do about it. Judgment is predetermined. God has destined for every one of us to appear before Him and be judged.

Predestination means predestined for judgment. Many people go through this life without ever entering a courtroom. Most people go through this life without knowing what it is like to appear before a judge. However, every single person on this earth is going to appear before the Judge of judges, the King of kings and the Lord of lords. We all have to stand in the courtroom of eternity one day.

1\. Knowing your predestination is to appear before a judgment seat makes you desire to be acceptable and to do good things whilst in the body.

Wherefore we labour, that, whether present or absent, WE MAY BE ACCEPTED OF HIM.

FOR WE MUST ALL APPEAR BEFORE THE JUDGMENT SEAT OF CHRIST; that every one may receive the things done in his body, according to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad.

Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord, we persuade men; but we are made manifest unto God; and I trust also are made manifest in your consciences.

2 Corinthians 5:9-11

Since you must appear before God, you must desire to be acceptable to Him. To be pleasing to Him must be your heart’s desire. To love Him and make Him happy must be your quest every day. Will you be acceptable on the judgment day or will you be rejected? You must assess yourself using God’s standards. You will not be judged according to your own ideas but according to the word of God.

2\. Knowing your predestination to appear before a judgment seat makes you desire to persuade men.

FOR WE MUST ALL APPEAR BEFORE THE JUDGMENT SEAT OF CHRIST; that every one may receive the things done in his body, according to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad.

KNOWING THEREFORE THE TERROR OF THE LORD, WE PERSUADE MEN; but we are made manifest unto God; and I trust also are made manifest in your consciences.

2 Corinthians 5:10-11

We will be called to account for our lives. We must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may be repaid for what has been done in the body. Whether what we have done is good or bad, we will be held responsible for it. It is a sobering thought that we will be held responsible for all our motives, actions, deeds, negligence and accomplishments. Even the use and misuse of your time, opportunities and abilities will be brought before the judgment seat of Christ.

Because you are destined to appear before the judgment seat you must persuade men. You must convince men to serve God. You must convince men to love God. You must have meetings where you persuade people to get ready for the great white throne before which they will be judged. Paul said he was constantly aware of the terror of the Lord and because of that, he was constantly persuading people to love God and to serve God.

Unfortunately, many pastors are persuading people to acquire all the riches and gold in this world. We are prophesying things that will never happen. False illusions of prosperity are presented to the congregation every week. But God knows that what is required of us, is that we persuade men to give their lives to Jesus, to serve God and to worship Him.

3\. Knowing your predestination to appear before a judgment seat makes you work urgently.

That keeps us vigilant, you can be sure. IT’S NO LIGHT THING TO KNOW THAT WE’LL ALL ONE DAY STAND IN THAT PLACE OF JUDGMENT. THAT’S WHY WE WORK URGENTLY WITH EVERYONE WE MEET TO GET THEM READY TO FACE GOD. God alone knows how well we do this, but I hope you realize how much and deeply we care.

We’re not saying this to make ourselves look good to you. We just thought it would make you feel good, proud even, that we’re on your side and not just nice to your face as so many people are. If I acted crazy, I did it for God; if I acted overly serious, I did it for you.

2 Corinthians 5:11-13 (MSG)

Indeed, it is no small thing to know that we will one day stand in that place of judgment. That’s why we work urgently and with speed to get people ready to meet God. Paul was ready to speak whether or not people thought he was mad. When you are conscious of eternal judgment you will have a certain intense urgency within you. Many people will not understand you. They will ask if you do not rest, if you do not sleep, and why you are always upset. How can you rest when the world is racing towards judgment and eternity? How can you rest when the devil is not resting? It is time to gird up your loins and speak and act and work as though the end will come tomorrow.

One day, tomorrow will be the end! One day there will be no more days after tomorrow! It is time to work urgently and do everything urgently.

4\. Knowing your predestination to appear before a judgment seat makes you desire to not live for yourself.

FOR WE MUST ALL APPEAR BEFORE THE JUDGMENT SEAT OF CHRIST; that every one may receive the things done in his body, according to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad.

Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord, we persuade men; but we are made manifest unto God; and I trust also are made manifest in your consciences.

For the love of Christ constraineth us; because we thus judge, that if one died for all, then were all dead: And that he died for all, THAT THEY WHICH LIVE SHOULD NOT HENCEFORTH LIVE UNTO THEMSELVES, But unto him which died for them, and rose again.

2 Corinthians 5:10-11, 14-15

The scripture is clear: they that live on this earth “should not henceforth live unto themselves”! Because you are going to appear before the judgment seat, you must not live for yourself or do things just for yourself.

I once met a man who was doing many things for himself. He was building houses for himself. He was buying houses for his family members. He was acquiring lands and fields for his extended family. He was building walls to secure his many properties. He was doing exercises to keep himself healthy. He was living in two houses so that he could go to work early. He was on a light diet so that he could stay healthy and live longer. He was exercising every day so that he could be stronger and more muscular. He was travelling abroad so that he could secure his business deals. But he was doing very little for the church.

Indeed, this gentleman was living for himself. All his efforts were to build his life and his businesses. What is your time spent doing? What are your efforts for?

We are told clearly that we can no longer live for ourselves “but unto Him who died for us and rose again”. It is time to live for Jesus! There is a song by Evie Tornquist that says “Live for Jesus, that’s what matters.” Indeed, one day all that will matter is what you did for Jesus.

5\. Knowing your predestination to appear before a judgment seat makes you desire to “know no man after the flesh”.

FOR WE MUST ALL APPEAR BEFORE THE JUDGMENT SEAT OF CHRIST; that every one may receive the things done in his body, according to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad. Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord, we persuade men; but we are made manifest unto God; and I trust also are made manifest in your consciences…

For the love of Christ constraineth us; because we thus judge, that if one died for all, then were all dead: And that he died for all, that they which live should not henceforth live unto themselves, but unto him which died for them, and rose again. WHEREFORE HENCEFORTH KNOW WE NO MAN AFTER THE FLESH: yea, though we have known Christ after the flesh, yet now henceforth know we him no more.

2 Corinthians 5:10-11, 14-16

From henceforth, you must not consider people according to who they are in the flesh. Since you are going to stand before the judgment seat of Christ, you must not be concerned about who people are in the flesh. Why is that? When you know someone after the flesh, you are conscious of his fleshly and human characteristics. The human and fleshly characteristics that a person has will stir you up to jealousy and racism. You will want to match and equal what the person is in the flesh. Racism is basically judging people after the flesh and after what you see.

Knowing someone after the flesh is to be conscious of the person’s colour, tribe, nationality, car, house and wealth. Indeed, if you start trying to be like certain nations and tribes, rather than trying to be like Jesus, you may go astray.

If you start trying to have the same car, house and wealth that other people have, you will be making a mistake. It is important that you do not know people after the flesh. I do not need to know men of God after the flesh. I do not need to know where they live or what cars they drive. I need to know them after the Spirit. I want to receive the word of God from them. I want to receive the anointing from them. That is what matters! Knowing things after the flesh will divert you into fleshly matters and take your mind away from heavenly things.

6\. Knowing your predestination to appear before a judgment seat makes you not judge anybody.

But why dost thou judge thy brother? or why dost thou set at nought thy brother? for WE SHALL ALL STAND BEFORE THE JUDGMENT SEAT OF CHRIST.

Romans 14:10

Why judge your friend since we will all appear before the judgment seat of God? Do not condemn or “set anyone at nought”.

Because we will all finally appear before the judgment seat of Christ, it is important to avoid judging. Judgment is going to take place any way. Nothing is going to escape God’s attention. Those who deserve a curse will receive a curse. Those who deserve a reward will receive a reward. Just leave everything to God.

Many times, we desire to step into the shoes of God and pass judgment on people. We want to punish them for what they have done and to make them suffer. But that is not your duty. We will all appear before the judgment seat.

It will be sorted out. Those who deserve to receive suffering, shame and embarrassment will receive full doses of them all. God is Judge!
