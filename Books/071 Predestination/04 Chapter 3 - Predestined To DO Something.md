### Chapter 3\

Predestined To DO Something\
-------------------------------------------------

\

For whom he did foreknow, HE ALSO DID PREDESTINATE TO BE CONFORMED TO THE IMAGE OF HIS SON, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren. Moreover whom he did predestinate, them he also called: and whom he called, them he also justified: and whom he justified, them he also glorified.

Romans 8:29-30

You are predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son. In other words, you will look like Jesus. When we look at you, we are supposed to remember Jesus. When you think of Jesus what do you think of? You think of His good works. You think of His mighty works.

Jesus did great things for God. Notice how the scriptures reveal the mighty works of Jesus. Jesus went about doing mighty works! Jesus went about doing good and healing all that were oppressed by the devil! Jesus went about cities and villages preaching and teaching and healing! This is the image you are destined to be conformed to.

You will soon be doing the mighty works of Jesus if you follow your predestined plan. You will soon be preaching, teaching and healing if you follow the predestined plan. You are predestined to be conformed to the image of His son.

And when the sabbath day was come, he began to teach in the synagogue: and many hearing him were astonished, saying, From whence hath this man these things? and what wisdom is this which is given unto him, that even such MIGHTY WORKS are wrought by his hands?

Mark 6:2

How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who went about DOING GOOD, AND HEALING all that were oppressed of the devil; for God was with him.

Acts 10:38

But the Pharisees said, He casteth out devils through the prince of the devils. And Jesus went about all the cities and villages, TEACHING in their synagogues, and PREACHING the gospel of the kingdom, and HEALING every sickness and every disease among the people.

Matthew 9:34-35

1\. You are predestined to do good works.

For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand so that we would walk in them.

Ephesians 2:10 (NASB)

This is a fundamental truth of Christianity that has been hidden from the church. You were predestined to work for God before you were born. God has works that He is expecting you to accomplish. You must get to the job. Why do you think you are still alive? Why does God not take you to heaven today? He has decided to leave you on this earth so that you can do certain good works.

I once read the testimony of a man who died of a heart attack and was resuscitated eight minutes after he was declared dead. When he came back to life, he told the story of how he went to heaven and was asked to return to earth. There was a clear command, “Go back, you have not finished your work! Your work on earth is not yet done.”

Indeed, this is the basic reason why you cannot die now. It is not because you are not married or because you do not have children. It is because you have not finished the good works that were prepared beforehand that you should walk in them.

2\. You are predestined to suffer.

FOR TO YOU IT HAS BEEN GRANTED for Christ’s sake, not only to believe in Him, but also TO SUFFER FOR HIS SAKE

Philippians 1:29 (NASB)

You are also destined to suffer for God’s sake. You have been granted the opportunity to believe in Him as well as the opportunity to suffer. Christianity involves suffering. There are persecutions, blasphemies, defamation, painfulness, hurts, bitter experiences, bitter waters, betrayals, treachery, disloyalty, discontentment, disappointment, disillusionment, dejection, depression, abandonment, rejection, accusations, offences, difficulties, financial hardships, poverty, sickness, death and many other hardships that must happen in your life and ministry. It has been granted that you will suffer for Christ Jesus.

Do not be surprised at all by these experiences. They are all part of the predestined sufferings that a man of God will encounter. How else will the scripture be fulfilled? If you do not experience these things how will the scripture that promises you suffering be fulfilled?

Then said Jesus unto him, Put up again thy sword into his place: for all they that take the sword shall perish with the sword. -- Thinkest thou that I cannot now pray to my Father, and he shall presently give me more than twelve legions of angels? BUT HOW THEN SHALL THE SCRIPTURES BE FULFILLED, THAT THUS IT MUST BE?

Matthew 26:52-54

Dear friend, it is not likely that you will be crucified on a cross. It is not likely that you will be executed in an electric chair. But you will suffer because that is the destiny of every Christian. Please note all the different ways in which suffering happens in our modern lives. The things you believe are accompanied by a set of sufferings that you will soon be ticking off and saying, “I know this and I know that. I have been through this and I have been through that.”

I want to list them again. Do not forget it, when you walk with God, you are likely to encounter many of these. You must decide to overcome these sufferings: Bitter waters, persecutions, blasphemies, defamation, insults, painfulness, hurts, bitter experiences, betrayals, treachery, disloyalty, discontentment, disappointment, disillusionment, dejection, depression, abandonment, rejection, accusations, offences, difficulties, financial hardships, poverty, sickness, emotional upheavals, sorrow, tears, mourning, sadness, and even death.

3\. You are predestined to demonstrate the wisdom of God to principalities and powers.

Unto me, who am less than the least of all saints, is this grace given, that I should preach among the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ;

And to make all men see what is the fellowship of the mystery, which from the beginning of the world hath been hid in God, who created all things by Jesus Christ:

TO THE INTENT THAT NOW UNTO THE PRINCIPALITIES AND POWERS IN HEAVENLY PLACES MIGHT BE KNOWN BY THE CHURCH THE MANIFOLD WISDOM OF GOD, according to the eternal purpose which he purposed in Christ Jesus our Lord:

Ephesians 3:8-11

You are destined to demonstrate the wisdom of God to demonic powers. You are destined to show that obedience to the word of God causes great destruction and defeat to the enemies of God. Your life of obedience to God’s Word will demonstrate such overcoming wisdom to all forces of darkness.

You will overcome such things as poverty, disease, treachery and wickedness through and by obeying the word of God.

Your destiny is to show the demonic powers that the wisdom of God is superior to any other kind of thinking. As you follow the wisdom of God you will overcome evil spirits. Living a life of holiness and humility will reveal to the forces of darkness that following God is a better life than following the flesh and evil spirits.

There is a lot of evil in this world.

When you are born into the world, you encounter evil that you do not even understand. It is only by obeying God’s Word that you circumvent evil traps without even knowing that you are doing so. The wisdom of God is the word of God. It is the wisdom of God that makes you wise unto salvation.

And that from a child thou hast known the HOLY SCRIPTURES, which are able to MAKE THEE WISE unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus.

2 Timothy 3:15

As you see from the scripture above, following the scriptures make you wise. As you obey God, you will escape the demonic powers of poverty, sickness and disease that are in the world. The principalities of failure and disappointment will be defeated as you follow the Word.

4\. You are destined to preach the unsearchable riches of Christ.

UNTO ME, WHO AM LESS THAN THE LEAST OF ALL SAINTS, is this grace GIVEN, THAT I SHOULD PREACH among the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ;

And to make all men see what is the fellowship of the mystery, which from the beginning of the world hath been hid in God, who created all things by Jesus Christ:

To the intent that now unto the principalities and powers in heavenly places might be known by the church the manifold wisdom of God,

ACCORDING TO THE ETERNAL PURPOSE WHICH HE PURPOSED IN CHRIST JESUS OUR LORD:

Ephesians 3:8-11

You are predestined to become a great preacher. Paul was destined to be a preacher among the Gentiles. Who are you destined to preach to? Where are you destined to preach? What is God’s destiny for you? Paul knew that it was an eternal purpose and destiny in Christ Jesus that he should preach among Gentiles. That is why he went to the Gentiles and ministered there all his life. Perhaps God has a city, a town, a nation or an island where He intends you to live for the rest of your life, preaching the gospel of Jesus Christ.

5\. You are predestined and created to bring pleasure to God.

Thou art worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honour and power: for thou hast created all things, and for thy pleasure they are and were created.

Revelation 4:11

We were created to give pleasure to God. Unfortunately, in the last days people will shift away from their pre-ordained destiny. Eighteen moral blemishes will characterize the human race of the last days. Men will love money, love themselves and love pleasures. Instead of trying to bring pleasure to God, they will concentrate on bringing pleasure to themselves.

For men shall be LOVERS OF THEIR OWN SELVES, covetous (LOVERS OF MONEY,) boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy, Without natural affection, trucebreakers, false accusers, incontinent, fierce, despisers of those that are good, Traitors, heady, highminded, LOVERS OF PLEASURES more than lovers of God;

2 Timothy 3:2-4

Your calling and destiny is to bring pleasure to God and not to yourself. You must seek God’s great pleasure. What does God want from you? What makes Him happy? God is happy when you give Him your love, your heart, your worship, your honour, your money, your children and your soul.

God will receive pleasure when you give Him your love! Give Him all your love.

Jesus said unto him, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind. This is the first and great commandment.

Matthew 22:37-38

God will receive pleasure when you give Him your heart! Give Him all your heart.

My son, give me thine heart, and let thine eyes observe my ways.

Proverbs 23:26

God will receive pleasure when you give Him your worship! Give Him all your love.

For thou shalt worship no other god: for the LORD, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God:

Exodus 34:14

God will receive pleasure when you give Him your honour! Give Him all your respect and honour.

A son honoureth his father, and a servant his master: if then I be a father, where is mine honour? and if I be a master, where is my fear? saith the LORD of hosts unto you, O priests, that despise my name. And ye say, wherein have we despised thy name?

Malachi 1:6

God will receive pleasure when you give Him your money! Give Him all your money.

Bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse, that there may be meat in mine house, and prove me now herewith, saith the LORD of hosts, if I will not open you the windows of heaven, and pour you out a blessing, that there shall not be room enough to receive it.

Malachi 3:10

God will receive pleasure when you give Him your children! Give Him all your children.

But Jesus called them unto him, and said, Suffer little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God.

Luke 18:16

God will receive pleasure when you give Him your soul! Give Him your soul.

Jesus said unto him, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.

Matthew 22:37
