### Chapter 4\

Predestined for the Seven Blessings\
---------------------------------------------------------

\

For whom he did foreknow, HE ALSO DID PREDESTINATE TO BE CONFORMED TO THE IMAGE OF HIS SON, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren. Moreover whom he did predestinate, them he also called: and whom he called, them he also justified: and whom he justified, them he also glorified.

Romans 8:29-30

According to the scripture above, you are predestined to be conformed to the image of the Son of God. There are many pictures of the Son of God that you must believe in and follow. One of the most amazing pictures of Jesus is found in the book of Revelation. There, you see Jesus as a Lamb that was slain. Not only was the Lamb slain but also, He was declared worthy to receive seven blessings.

Jesus Christ is endowed with seven blessings in the book of Revelation. You must have faith that these seven blessings are also your destiny. God knew beforehand that you would take up your cross and follow the Lamb. You are predestined to walk in the image of Christ. Christ suffered and died for our sins. He obeyed His father and became obedient, even to the death on the cross. After this great act of obedience, He became worthy to receive seven blessings.

AND I BEHELD, AND, LO, IN THE MIDST OF THE THRONE AND OF THE FOUR BEASTS, AND IN THE MIDST OF THE ELDERS, STOOD A LAMB AS IT HAD BEEN SLAIN, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of God sent forth into all the earth.

And he came and took the book out of the right hand of him that sat upon the throne. And when he had taken the book, the four beasts and four and twenty elders fell down before the Lamb, having every one of them harps, and golden vials full of odours, which are the prayers of saints.

And they sung a new song, saying, Thou art worthy to take the book, and to open the seals thereof: for thou wast slain, and hast redeemed us to God by thy blood out of every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation; And hast made us unto our God kings and priests: and we shall reign on the earth.

And I beheld, and I heard the voice of many angels round about the throne and the beasts and the elders: and the number of them was ten thousand times ten thousand, and thousands of thousands; SAYING WITH A LOUD VOICE, WORTHY IS THE LAMB THAT WAS SLAIN TO RECEIVE POWER, AND RICHES, AND WISDOM, AND STRENGTH, AND HONOUR, AND GLORY, AND BLESSING.

Revelation 5:6-12

I know that you are going to take up your cross and follow Jesus Christ. You are going to be a good example and go through all the pains, sufferings, trials and tribulations that God has ordained for you. As you conform to the image of Christ and go through His predetermined will, you will come out qualified to receive the seven blessings.

You are qualified for the seven blessings because you are conformed to the image of Christ. The Christ you see in the book of Revelation is full of the seven blessings of power, riches, wisdom, strength, honour, glory and blessing. Begin to declare that your life is coloured with these seven predestined blessings.

1\. You are predestined for power! Begin to walk in the power of God. Do not say you are powerless and weak. Accept the grace of God that brings you great power for your life and ministry.

2\. You are destined for riches! Just like the Lamb, in whose image you are walking, you will receive riches and wealth from God. Life on this earth yields very little. Your working life yields little. The curse ensures that you get very little for all your hard work. This is why few people are ever able to work and own a house debt-free. Owning a house debt-free is indeed an indication of your work on this earth having yielded something more than sweat and tears.

3\. You are destined for wisdom! Just like the Lamb in whose image you are walking, you will receive supernatural wisdom which is different from the wisdom of men. Through this supernatural wisdom you will circumvent all the traps that are laid for you by devils and demon spirits. You will escape and overcome in this life because you are conformed to the image of the Son of God.

4\. You are destined for strength! Strength speaks of power and might. As you walk in the will of God and follow the destiny that God has for you, you will become stronger and stronger. When you are strong, a little effort yields great results! Your declarations will come to pass. Your effort will yield much fruit. Every punch you give the enemy will result in a knockout! Your enemy will not have access to destroy you any longer because your strength will ward off evil spirits.

5\. You are destined for honour! Jesus Christ is the most honoured person in the world. We celebrate His birthday every year across the entire globe. The whole world comes to a halt when we celebrate His birth and His death. Even those who do not believe in Him somehow acknowledge His existence. As you follow in Christ’s stead, you will be conformed to His image. It is your destiny. You will soon be respected in the way that Christ is respected. Those who defame you, insult you and blaspheme against you will soon be put to shame. You are destined for honour. You are destined for respect! You are destined for nobility! You are destined for admiration! Receive the honour that comes from God.

6\. You are destined for glory! You will receive the glory and the beautification that comes only from God. God alone can make you beautiful. You may have ugly parts but beauty comes from God. There are people who are not obviously or naturally beautiful. However, they attract people and there seems to be some other source of beauty with them. That is how the glory of God is. There is something nice and attractive that glows from within. It draws people and wins their hearts. It makes people admire and desire you without any good reason. This is what you are destined for. You are destined for the glory of God, the shine that comes from God! That is the destiny of God for you.

7\. You are destined for blessings! You will receive a blessing from God. Jesus Christ received blessings from the Father. The seventh blessing is to receive a blessing. When you receive this blessing, you will be called “blessed.” No trace of a curse will be found in your life. Everyone will look at you and call you “blessed” because a blessing has been granted to you. You are predestined for a blessing!
