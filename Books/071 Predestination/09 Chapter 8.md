### Chapter 8\

How Predestination Affects Your Life\
----------------------------------------------------------

\

If in this life only we have hope in Christ, we are of all men most miserable.

1 Corinthians 15:19

If there was no eternity that is destined and determined for us, we would be the most miserable of all. You are destined for eternity. You are destined for an eternal existence.

It cannot be that our existence on this earth is all that there is to life. If that is the case then even the life of a chicken may be better because when a chicken dies, it is still useful, in that, it can be eaten.

Predestination helps you to overcome the futility, pointlessness and senselessness that characterizes life in this world.

If you do not find your destiny, expect two words to describe your life: futility and vanity.

1\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. Work on earth is useless. Painful sufferings at work are pointless. Thank God for eternity! Heaven is our destiny. You are predestined to walk on the streets of gold.

THEN I LOOKED ON ALL THE WORKS THAT MY HANDS HAD WROUGHT, AND ON THE LABOUR THAT I HAD LABOURED to do: and, behold, all was VANITY and vexation of spirit, and there was no profit under the sun.

Ecclesiastes 2:11

2\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. Pleasure on this earth is useless and pointless! Thank God for eternity! Heaven is your destiny. You are predestined to walk on the streets of gold.

I said in mine heart, Go to now, I WILL PROVE THEE WITH MIRTH, THEREFORE ENJOY PLEASURE: and, behold, this also is VANITY.

Ecclesiastes 2:1

3\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. Following the desires of your eyes is pointless and vain! Thank God for eternity! Heaven is your destiny. You are predestined to walk on the streets of gold (Revelation 21:21).

ALL THAT MY EYES DESIRED I DID NOT REFUSE THEM. I did not withhold my heart from any pleasure, for my heart was pleased because of all my labor and this was my reward for all my labor. Thus I considered all my activities which my hands had done and the labor which I had exerted, and behold all was vanity and striving after wind and there was no profit under the sun.

Ecclesiastes 2:10-11 (NASB)

4\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. Trying to be wise on this earth will not help much. Similar things happen to wise men and to fools. Thank God for eternity! Heaven is your destiny. You are predestined to walk on the streets of gold.

Then said I in my heart, As it happeneth to the fool, so it happeneth even to me; and why was I then more wise? Then I said in my heart, that this also is VANITY.

Ecclesiastes 2:15

5\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. Painful sufferings and travail at work is pointless. Thank God for eternity! Heaven is our destiny. You are predestined to live in heaven.

Therefore I hated life; because the work that is wrought under the sun is grievous unto me: for all is VANITY and vexation of spirit.

Ecclesiastes 2:17

6\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. All your works must be handed over to someone. You must leave everything behind. Will you leave it for a fool or will you leave it for a wise man? Thank God for eternity! Heaven is our destiny. You are predestined to be in heaven otherwise your life on earth would be completely useless.

Yea, I hated all my labour which I had taken under the sun: because I should leave it unto the man that shall be after me.

And who knoweth WHETHER HE SHALL BE A WISE MAN OR A FOOL? yet shall he have rule over all my labour wherein I have laboured, and wherein I have shewed myself wise under the sun. This is also VANITY.

Ecclesiastes 2:18-19

7\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. All your works must be handed over to someone. You must leave everything behind. You will leave it for someone who has not worked as hard as you did. Thank God for eternity! Heaven is our destiny. You are predestined to be in heaven otherwise your life on earth would be completely pointless and vain.

For there is a man whose labour is in wisdom, and in knowledge, and in equity; YET TO A MAN THAT HATH NOT LABOURED THEREIN SHALL HE LEAVE IT for his portion. This also is VANITY and a great evil… For all his days are sorrows, and his travail grief; yea, his heart taketh not rest in the night. This is also VANITY.

Ecclesiastes 2:21,23

8\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life and takes away vanity and futility of gathering up, heaping up only to give it away.

For God giveth to a man that is good in his sight wisdom, and knowledge, and joy: but to the sinner he giveth travail, to gather and to heap up, THAT HE MAY GIVE TO HIM THAT IS GOOD BEFORE GOD. THIS ALSO IS VANITY and vexation of spirit.

Ecclesiastes 2:26

9\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. You must die just as all the animals do. What is the difference between the human being and the animal? Thank God for eternity! Heaven is our destiny. You are predestined to be in heaven otherwise your life on earth would be completely useless.

For that which befalleth the sons of men befalleth beasts; even one thing befalleth them: as the one dieth, so dieth the other; yea, they have all one breath; so that a man hath no preeminence above a beast: for all is VANITY.

Ecclesiastes 3:19

10\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. Because you work hard many people will be jealous and envious of you. Jealousy and envy is the reward for hard work on this earth. Thank God for eternity! Heaven is your destiny. You are predestined to be in heaven otherwise your life on earth would be completely useless. The hard work of Abel was hated by his brother Cain.

Again, I considered all travail, and every right work, THAT FOR THIS A MAN IS ENVIED OF HIS NEIGHBOUR. THIS IS ALSO VANITY and vexation of spirit.

Ecclesiastes 4:4

11\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. There is endless purposeless work on this earth. The work on this earth for which there is 1) no end 2) no satisfaction 3) no fulfilment 4) no purpose. It is a blessing to be predestined to go to heaven. If God had not predestined us to be eternal beings to live in eternity with him, our lives here on this earth would not make any sense at all.

There is one alone, and there is not a second; yea, he hath neither child nor brother: yet is there no end of all his labour; neither is his eye satisfied with riches; neither saith he, For whom do I labour, and bereave my soul of good? This is also VANITY, yea, it is a sore travail.

Ecclesiastes 4:8

12\. Knowing your predestination gives meaning to your life and takes away vanity and futility of popularity. You may gain popularity but your replacement may be even more popular. Also, your popularity does not guarantee that people will always be happy with you. Rebellious people are always amongst the masses when you rise into power. Thank God that you are predestined to stand in heaven where Jesus’ presence will give you fulfilment and all the acknowledgement that you need.

A poor yet wise lad is better than an old and foolish king who no longer knows how to receive instruction.

For he has come out of prison to become king, even though he was born poor in his kingdom.

I HAVE SEEN ALL THE LIVING UNDER THE SUN THRONG TO THE SIDE OF THE SECOND LAD WHO REPLACES HIM.

There is no end to all the people, to all who were before them, and even the ones who will come later will not be happy with him, for this too is vanity and striving after wind.

Ecclesiastes 4:13-16 (NASB)

13\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. Acquiring more things is therefore useless and pointless. There is vanity and futility of never having enough money because the more you acquire the more expenses and bills you have. Thank God for eternity! Heaven is your destiny. You are predestined to walk on the streets of gold and be content in the presence of God.

When good things increase, those who consume them increase. So what is the advantage to their owners except to look on?

Ecclesiastes 5:11 (NASB)

14\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life and takes away the vanity and futility of working but being able to enjoy the fruit of your labour. Many people do not enjoy the fruits of their labour. Many people have strangers enjoying what they worked for.

Many millionaires own mansions on islands and have security men enjoying the mansions all year round. They may visit this island every two years but the security man is the one who enjoys the villa. Thank God that you are predestined to live in glory and in a mansion prepared by Jesus himself.

A man to whom God hath given riches, wealth, and honour, so that he wanteth nothing for his soul of all that he desireth, yet God giveth him not power to eat thereof, but a stranger eateth it: this is VANITY, and it is an evil disease.

Ecclesiastes 6:2

15\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life and takes away the vanity and futility of working hard and not enjoying a few good things.

If a man fathers a hundred children and lives many years, however many they be, but his soul is not satisfied with good things and he does not even have a proper burial, then I say, “Better the miscarriage than he, for it comes in futility and goes into obscurity; and its name is covered in obscurity”.

Ecclesiastes 6:3-4 (NASB)

16\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life and takes away the vanity and futility of having to share this life with people who laugh but do not understand how leadership works, how governance works and how prosperity is generated. In eternity, which is your destiny, you will not have to bear such crackling of thorns under a pot.

For as the crackling of thorns under a pot, so is the laughter of the fool: this also is VANITY.

Ecclesiastes 7:6

17\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life and takes away the vanity and futility of seeing wicked men flourishing whilst just men perish.

All things have I seen in the days of my VANITY: there is a just man that perisheth in his righteousness, and there is a wicked man that prolongeth his life in his wickedness.

Ecclesiastes 7:15

18\. Thank God for predestination! Thank God for eternity! Knowing you are predestined gives meaning to your life and takes away the vanity and futility of both holy men and wicked men being forgotten in the city where they lived. What then is the use of having been a holy man?

So then I have seen the wicked buried, those who used to go in and out from the holy place, and they are soon forgotten in the city where they did thus. This too is futility.

Ecclesiastes 8:10 (NASB)

19\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. In this life good men seem to reap what a wicked man should reap and wicked men seem to reap what a righteous man should reap. This is another unfortunate and useless characteristic of this life. If that is all we are to ever experience then our life is really useless. Thank God for eternity. In eternity such injustices will not exist. Eternity will wipe away sorrow and pain. “And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away. And he that sat upon the throne said, Behold, I make all things new. And he said unto me, Write: for these words are true and faithful.” (Revelation 21:4-5)

There is a VANITY which is done upon the earth; that there be just men, unto whom it happeneth according to the work of the wicked; again, there be wicked men, to whom it happeneth according to the work of the righteous: I said that this also is VANITY.

Ecclesiastes 8:14

20\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. There will be many days of darkness on earth, days of futility, many sad days and many painful experiences. That is what the word of God promises you. What a painful existence we have! Thank God for eternity! Knowing you are predestined for a life in glory gives you a reason to live.

But if a man live many years, and rejoice in them all; yet let him remember the days of darkness; for they shall be many. All that cometh is VANITY.

Ecclesiastes 11:8

21\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. Many of our childhood and youthful experiences are useless and pointless. When we are young, we think we are on top of the world. We think we know everything. However, many experiences are pointless, senseless and useless. In eternity, you will not have pointless, useless and senseless experiences. You are predestined for a glorious existence in eternity.

Therefore remove sorrow from thy heart, and put away evil from thy flesh: for childhood and youth are VANITY.

Ecclesiastes 11:10

22\. Knowing you are predestined to an eternity with Jesus gives meaning to your life. Writing many books and reading many books makes you acquire knowledge and wisdom which you cannot use for long. The writing of books and the reading of the same are wearisome. I once saw a man who had written a thousand books. I was wondering when I would be able to read them. By the time you know all that you need to know, it is time to leave the earth. Thank God that the vanity and futility of learning and never having enough knowledge will soon be done away with. You are predestined for glory in the presence of God.

And further, by these, my son, be admonished: OF MAKING MANY BOOKS THERE IS NO END; AND MUCH STUDY IS A WEARINESS OF THE FLESH. Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter: Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man.

For God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing, whether it be good, or whether it be evil.

Ecclesiastes 12:12-14
