### Chapter 5\

Predestined To Live Forever\
-------------------------------------------------

\

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.

John 3:16

1\. You are predestined to live forever in either heaven or in hell.

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life. For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; but that the world through him might be saved.

John 3:16-17

You are predestined to live forever. Obviously, seventy years on this earth is not your whole life. Your existence on this earth is just a small part of your real life. All dead people are still alive! They are living either in Heaven or in Hell. You are predestined to live forever. Will it be in Heaven or in Hell? That will be your choice.

Do not start thinking to yourself, “I am predestined to go to hell so nothing can change me. I am destined to self-destruct and to commit sin every day because I am destined to go to hell.” Wrong! You are destined to live forever. You are not destined to be in hell. You are destined to live forever. Living in heaven or in hell for the rest of eternity is a choice you will have to make for yourself.

2\. You are predestined to live forever in a mansion in heaven.

In my Father’s house are many mansions: if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you. And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and receive you unto myself; that where I am, there ye may be also.

John 14:2-3

You are destined to live forever in a mansion in Heaven. Jesus has gone to prepare a place for you. It is entirely up to you if you want to spend your time following mansions here on earth. God has in store for you something far greater than any mansion on this earth. Anything you have on this earth will be lived in for only seventy years or thereabouts.

Ten thousand years from now the mansions you have built on earth will be buried in the sands of time. Just like the cities which are dug out of the ground by archaeologists, where you are currently living will be buried under the ground and equally dug out by archaeologists one day.

Those who have invested in the heavenly mansions will still be smiling to themselves ten thousand years from now. Never forget that you are predestined to live in a mansion in heaven. Jesus has predicted that you will be there.

And Jesus went out, and departed from the temple: and his disciples came to him for to shew him the buildings of the temple. And Jesus said unto them, See ye not all these things? verily I say unto you, There shall not be left here one stone upon another, that shall not be thrown down. And as he sat upon the mount of Olives, the disciples came unto him privately, saying, Tell us, when shall these things be? and what shall be the sign of thy coming, and of the end of the world?

Matthew 24:1-3

The disciples were astonished when Jesus predicted that the temple would actually be destroyed. The temple of Jerusalem was one of the great monumental structures of the time. People came from all over the world to see this great temple. Yet, Jesus predicted that not one stone would remain on top of the other. None of them could have believed what Jesus was saying. But Jesus knew the future. He knows the future of every building on this earth and He did not bother to even build a church building. Honestly, our future is in eternity. We must live for the eternal and plan for the eternal.

Have you ever wondered why Solomon was so depressed? He said,

I made me great works; I builded me houses; I planted me vineyards: I made me gardens and orchards, and I planted trees in them of all kind of fruits: I made me pools of water, to water therewith the wood that bringeth forth trees: I got me servants and maidens, and had servants born in my house; also I had great possessions of great and small cattle above all that were in Jerusalem before me: …

THEN I LOOKED ON ALL THE WORKS THAT MY HANDS HAD WROUGHT, AND ON THE LABOUR THAT I HAD LABOURED TO DO: AND, BEHOLD, ALL WAS VANITY AND VEXATION OF SPIRIT, AND THERE WAS NO PROFIT UNDER THE SUN.

Ecclesiastes 2:4-7, 11

When Solomon looked on all that he had achieved, he realised that it was truly useless. We are destined to arrive in eternity. Let’s invest in eternal things! Everything we acquire on this earth is going to amount to nothing.

3\. When you know that man is predestined to live forever you do everything to prevent people from going to hell and re-direct them to heaven.

And Jesus came and spake unto them, saying, all power is given unto me in heaven and in earth. Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.

Matthew 28:18-20

Jesus wants us to win the whole world to Him. He has sent us out into the world because He knows that the whole world is predestined to live in heaven or in hell for all eternity. When you consider predestination includes either heaven or hell, you will rise up and become the greatest evangelist you can be.

4\. When you know that man is predestined to live forever you set your affections on things above.

If ye then be risen with Christ, seek those things which are above, where Christ sitteth on the right hand of God. Set your affection on things above, not on things on the earth.

Colossians 3:1-2

Being conscious of your predestination to live forever in eternity will make you set your affections on things above. Apostle Paul was very conscious of his predestination. He encouraged everyone to set their affections on things above. Why set your affection on things below? Why set your affections on things on this earth when you know that this earth is going to be set on fire and destroyed to the uttermost? Why do you set your affections on something so temporary when there are permanent things you can invest in? God is great! He has revealed to you His great plan for eternity. This is the arm that is stretched out to perform it. No one can turn back God’s arm. God’s arm is very strong. He will destroy this world with fire and replace it with a new world.

But the day of the Lord will come as a thief in the night; in the which the heavens shall pass away with a great noise, and the elements shall melt with fervent heat, THE EARTH ALSO AND THE WORKS THAT ARE THEREIN SHALL BE BURNED UP. Seeing then that all these things shall be dissolved, what manner of persons ought ye to be in all holy conversation and godliness,

2 Peter 3:10-11

One day I was standing with a friend who was looking longingly at some yachts in the ocean. We all knew that we did not have the money and the resources to ever own such yachts. So I quickly turned my affections on things above because I did not want to be sad. I declared, “I will have my yacht in heaven.”

But my friend was adamant. He said that he wanted his yacht right here on earth. I always remember that night, looking over the Pacific Ocean at the many yachts belonging to the many millionaires who had anchored in the bay.

We had different opinions. My friend had set his affections on things here on earth. But I had set my affections on things above (I knew I wouldn’t be able to get it on earth anyway). I do not see why I should bother to desire things that I will not be able to acquire any way.

5\. Being conscious of your destiny to live forever makes you focus on things which are not seen.

For our light affliction, which is but for a moment, worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory; WHILE WE LOOK NOT AT THE THINGS WHICH ARE SEEN, BUT AT THE THINGS WHICH ARE NOT SEEN: for the things which are seen are temporal; but the things which are not seen are eternal.

2 Corinthians 4:17-18

Once the basic needs of life are met permanently, the confusion of life sets in. This is why the lives of celebrities and the super-rich are often a puzzle to poorer people. We, the masses, simply do not understand why their lives become so complex, twisted and bizarre. Perhaps, the reason for the bizarre habits of taking drugs and doing other things such as marrying different people, divorcing several times, being unable to stay married, is because rich people simply do not know what to do with their lives.

The purpose of life has been stated by God. We were and are created for His pleasure. Once human beings do not live simply for the pleasure of God and their Creator, other futile purposes are thrown to them as possible pursuits that they may undertake. You no longer have several useless pursuits to choose from. The understanding of predestination simplifies your life.

6\. Being conscious of your destiny to live forever in eternity frees you from the need to heap up riches.

Heaping up of money is one of the useless pursuits that God wants to save you from. It is very difficult to know what to do when you have all the money you actually need. What to do next is the big question. As you keep heaping up riches, silver and gold and diamond, the madness of it gets to you. When will you use this money? What will you use it for? Where can you take it to? Who will you leave it for?

Are We Mad?

Many excessively rich people look like mad men because they keep piling up things that they can never use. Remember that mental patients tend to gather meaningless items together and sit in the midst of them. One wonders what they are thinking and what they will do with the rubbish they are heaping around themselves.

The Hoarding Disorder

The Diogenes Syndrome is a behavioural health condition characterized by poor personal hygiene, self-neglect, hoarding and unkempt living conditions and a lack of shame regarding one’s living condition. It is also called the Squalor Syndrome.

Hoarding is a mental health disorder characterized by a persistent and powerful difficulty in getting rid of collected items, regardless of their value. Left untreated, this behaviour can lead to a variety of damaging social and emotional defects for both the hoarder and their family members.

The Hoarding Disorder is a persistent difficulty discarding or parting with possessions, the build up of food and trash to unusually excessive unsanitary levels. It includes having problems like keeping or collecting items that have little or no monetary value, such as carrier bags. Hoarding disorders are challenging to treat because people who hoard frequently do not see it as a problem or have little or no knowledge of how it affects others. It is really important to encourage a person who is hoarding to seek help as their difficulty in discarding objects can cause loneliness and mental health problems.

People who do not understand our predestination to eternal life try to heap up silver and gold. They are never satisfied with the piles and piles of wealth which they can never use. “He that loveth silver shall not be satisfied with silver; nor he that loveth abundance with increase: this is also vanity” (Ecclesiastes 5:10). The scripture clearly describes wealth as “that which is not”. “Wilt thou set thine eyes upon THAT WHICH IS NOT? for riches certainly make themselves wings; they fly away as an eagle toward heaven.” (Proverbs 23:5)

The heaping up of silver and the heaping up of gold and the heaping up of money is one of the things that you will not give yourself to when you understand your destiny. Many scriptures warn about the futility of heaping up wealth on this earth.

Scriptures against Heaping Up of Wealth

James 5:3

Though he HEAP UP SILVER as the dust, and prepare raiment as the clay; he may prepare it, but the just shall put it on, and the innocent shall divide the silver.

Job 27:16-17

Surely every man walketh in a vain shew: surely they are disquieted in vain: he HEAPETH UP RICHES, and knoweth not who shall gather them.

Psalm 39:6

LAY NOT UP FOR YOURSELVES TREASURES UPON EARTH, where moth and rust doth corrupt, and where thieves break through and steal: But lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust doth corrupt, and where thieves do not break through nor steal:

Matthew 6:19-20

For God giveth to a man that is good in his sight wisdom, and knowledge, and joy: but to the sinner he giveth travail, to gather and TO HEAP UP, THAT HE MAY GIVE TO HIM THAT IS GOOD BEFORE GOD. This also is vanity and vexation of spirit

For he seeth that wise men die, likewise the fool and the brutish person perish, and LEAVE THEIR WEALTH TO OTHERS.

Psalm 49:10

As you can see in these scriptures, men heap up wealth and leave the heaps for others. Unfortunately, most of the people who receive these heaps do not know how to use them.
