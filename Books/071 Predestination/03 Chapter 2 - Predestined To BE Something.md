### Chapter 2\

Predestined To BE Something\
-------------------------------------------------

\

For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren.

Romans 8:29

Predestined To BE Something

Ten Things You Are Predestined to BECOME

1\. You are predestined to be in the image of God.

For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren.

Romans 8:29

You are predestined to be in the image of God. What is the image of God?

Jesus is the image of God and God is love. You are predestined to be filled with love. You are predestined to be loving. Wickedness does not suit you anymore. Unforgiveness does not sit well with you any longer.

Jesus is the image of God and God is light. In Him there is no darkness at all (1 John 1:5). You are destined to be conformed to this image of light and brightness. There must be no shadows and secrets and deceptions about you. Your life of shadows, deceptions and darkness does not conform to the image of His Son. Accept that you are a new person and your destiny is to be conformed to the image of His Son.

Jesus is The Way, The Truth and The Life (John 14:6). You are destined to be the way for people, the truth for people and a source of new life for people. Allow yourself to become a channel through which many people will find the way to God, find the truth in God and find a new life in God.

Jesus is a good shepherd (John 10:11). You are predestined to be conformed to the image of His Son. This means that you are predestined to be a shepherd. Being conformed to the image of His Son means that you must rise up and flow with your destiny.

2\. You are predestined to be called.

Moreover WHOM HE DID PREDESTINATE, THEM HE ALSO CALLED: and whom he called, them he also justified: and whom he justified, them he also glorified.

Romans 8:30

You are destined to be called. It is your destiny to be called of God. The call of God is no accident. You must accept that it is the fulfilment of your predestination that caused you to walk into a church and hear about the call of God.

Accept that your call is a supernatural thing. Do you want to know the big difference between those who seem to be called and those who seem to have no special calling? It is simple! Those who believe that they are called take things more seriously. They take a message from God’s Word more seriously than the average person. They take their dreams more seriously. They take their visions more seriously. They take their “quiet times” more seriously.

I have taken major steps in the ministry because I took my quiet time more seriously. I am sure other Christians also had the same quiet times but did not take them seriously. When I read the scripture, “Give thyself wholly to these things”, (1 Timothy 4:15) I believed with all my heart that God was speaking to me to go all out in ministry and give myself wholly.

That was the end of every secular and business activity in my life. Since that day I have not been involved in any business at all. I have given myself wholly and utterly to the work of ministry. I believe that I am predestined to serve in the ministry.

3\. You are predestined to be justified.

Moreover WHOM HE DID PREDESTINATE, them he also called: and whom he called, THEM HE ALSO JUSTIFIED: and whom he justified, them he also glorified.

Romans 8:30

You are also predestined to be justified by God. In as much as you have been a wretched sinner, God has a plan to justify you and make you appropriately righteous. God wants to use you and it is determined that He will make you righteous and holy in His sight. Before this world began, God had predestined that Jesus Christ should come and die for our sins. Your justification to serve God is simply part of a long-standing plan of God.

4\. You are predestined to be glorified.

Moreover WHOM HE DID PREDESTINATE, them he also called: and whom he called, them he also justified: and whom he justified, THEM HE ALSO GLORIFIED.

Romans 8:30

Your destiny is to be glorified. Right on this earth, God has determined that you be glorified and made beautiful. All the beauty and attractiveness of your life is because of God’s glory on you. There are many people who smile at me and greet me with kind, warm and welcoming faces. I am always thankful to God for this. I could have had the opposite with people barking angrily at me. I am one hundred per cent sure that some people are happy to see me because of Christ and not because I am a medical doctor.

I do not see people smiling at other medical doctors in the same way. Christ has made all the difference. People are only interested in me because of Jesus. Any glory or beauty in my life is because of Christ.

From reports that I am receiving, people in Heaven are also completely glorified and magnified because of the glory that is on them. In Heaven you will be so glorified that no human on earth will compare with your glorified state.

This glory that God is giving you is something that is predetermined.

5\. You are predestined to be holy and blameless.

According as he hath CHOSEN US in him BEFORE THE FOUNDATION OF THE WORLD, THAT WE SHOULD BE HOLY and without blame before him in love:

Ephesians 1:4

Before the foundation of the world, God had planned that you will be holy. To be holy means to be special. God’s plan for you to be unusual and special is nothing new.

Before the earth had been formed, God had planned for you to be His special servant. Do not run away from your calling to be holy. Holiness is a special grace from God. Purity is not an evil thing. When you are holy you are saved from being animal-like. You are saved from being filthy and dirty before the Lord. Accept your calling to be holy.

God is looking for people that are blameless. It is your destiny to be blameless. To become a bishop, you must be blameless. John Wesley, the founder of the Methodist Church, preached the doctrine of perfection. He urged Christians to attain to the highest standards. Of course, no one can attain those heights but it is your duty to aspire for the blamelessness to which you are called.

6\. You are predestined to be in His presence.

Just as HE CHOSE US in Him BEFORE THE FOUNDATION OF THE WORLD, THAT WE WOULD BE holy and blameless BEFORE HIM…

Ephesians 1:4 (NASB)

Your purity and holiness must be practiced before God. Righteousness is relative to the person who prescribes the rules. Human beings have different standards and rules of what they call righteousness.

Some people consider wearing trousers a sin whilst others wear bikinis without even considering that they have sinned. These are all human standards, which vary greatly. It is important that our holiness and blamelessness is practiced before Him (i.e. in the presence of God) in love. It is God who must approve your righteousness and blamelessness!

It is God who sets the standards. Once you are holy and perfect before Him, you are ok. Jesus gave us His standards. Listen to the standards of Jesus and you will never go wrong.

7\. You are predestined to be adopted into a family.

HE PREDESTINED US TO ADOPTION as sons through Jesus Christ to Himself, according to the kind intention of His will,

Ephesians 1:5 (NASB)

God has predestined you to have another family apart from your biological family. What a blessing. There is no point in resisting this amazing blessing. Today, you have many more brothers and sisters than you can ever relate with.

God is providing for you by creating a family into which He has adopted you.

God has predestined you to be adopted into the family of God. No matter how evil you have been, when you get saved you sense acceptance in the family of God. Many Christians become proud and wicked, not willing to admit others into the family. It is amazing that outsiders who were once sinners begin to act as house owners preventing others from coming nearer.

Today, God is your father and you have a new family in Christ. Enjoy! I wish you all the best with your new family.

8\. You are predestined to inherit the kingdom.

Then shall the King say unto them on his right hand, Come, ye blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world:

Matthew 25:34

Jesus told His disciples that the kingdom of God was prepared from the foundation of the world. The kingdom of God is for you and I to possess. Rise up and take the things of the kingdom seriously. What does it mean to inherit the kingdom? The kingdom of God brings healing, cleansing and freedom from demonic powers and devils. When you are deeply embedded in the kingdom of God, you will not be conscious of money. You will be able to freely give and freely receive. Freely have you received, freely give.

And as ye go, preach, saying, the kingdom of heaven is at hand. Heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils: freely ye have received, freely give.

Matthew 10: 7-8

In him we were also chosen, having been predestined according to the plan of him who works out everything in conformity with the purpose of his will, in order that we, who were the first to put our hope in Christ, might be for the praise of his glory.

Ephesians 1:11-12 (NIV)

9\. You are predestined to cause God to be praised.

Also we have obtained an inheritance, having been predestined according to His purpose who works all things after the counsel of His will, to the end that we who were the first to hope in Christ would be to the praise of His glory.

Ephesians 1:11-12 (NASB)

You are predestined to cause praise to come to God. Through your life and ministry many people will praise God. Churches will be filled with people who want to praise God. Your life will not cause people to insult God. Instead, through your life, through your faith and through your faithfulness many people will praise God. Ask yourself, “Are people praising God because of me?” Are groups of people giving thanks to God because of you?
