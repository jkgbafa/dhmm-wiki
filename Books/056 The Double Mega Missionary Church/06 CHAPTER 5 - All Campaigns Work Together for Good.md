### CHAPTER 5** **

All Campaigns Work Together for Good\
----------------------------------------------------------

**And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.**

**Romans 8:28**

All the campaigns must work together in order to achieve church growth.  

Since the word of God is sure, you can expect church growth to come at all costs.  It is time to apply the wisdom of God to bring about church growth.  All things work together for good.  One of the good things is church growth.  This means that many things must be working at the same time for the good experience of church growth.  The prophecies are going to work together for your good.  The wisdom of God is also going to work together for good.  

**The Human Body**

For a human body to grow, many things need to work together at the same time.  The bladder fills with urine at the same time as the heart is pumping blood.  The brain is taking decisions whilst the muscles of the legs are moving.  The liver is removing toxins from the blood and the kidney is clearing out urea from the blood. While all this is going on, the ears are hearing and the eyes are seeing.  

Unbelievably, your mouth fills up with saliva caused by secretions of your sub-mandibular and parotid glands, whilst your nose is being filled with secretions that make you feel like blowing your nose.  At the same time, the stomach dissolves meat that has been swallowed whilst the duodenum digests bread.  Whilst all this is happening, a little blood may be flowing out of the womb of a woman during menstruation.  At the same time that all this is happening, hormones are being secreted into your blood.  At the same time, your colon absorbs water and forms faeces in your rectum that makes you feel like going to the toilet.  

While all these things are going on, chemical reactions take place in your blood. Several biochemical reactions and cycles take place regularly.  

As you can see from all these, the human body is very busy indeed.  Nothing stops at any time.  Many unrelated activities go on all the time.   It is these varied, continuous and unrelated activities that cause the body to live and grow normally.  

**The Body of Christ**

**So we, being many, are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another.**

**Romans 12:5**

The church is the body of Christ.  If it is to grow normally and become what it is intended to become, it is going to need several unrelated activities to work together intelligently.  When all the different spiritual activities are in place, the body has no choice but to grow.  

In this book, I am presenting a series of scriptural activities that must continue throughout the life of the church, if it is to grow.  Imagine what would happen in the human body if your heart did not work for five minutes?  There would be confusion and chaos if different systems in the body shut down at different times.   

It is the work of the shepherd to make his church grow.  A good shepherd knows exactly what to do to cause his flock to multiply.  He knows the different things he has to do if the flock is to grow.  A good shepherd ensures that all important activities go on simultaneously.  A good shepherd ensures that none of the essential activities of the church ever cease or slacken.
