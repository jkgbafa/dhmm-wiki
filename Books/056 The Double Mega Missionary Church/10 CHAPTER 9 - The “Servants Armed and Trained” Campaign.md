### CHAPTER 9\

The “Servants Armed and Trained”  Campaign\
----------------------------------------------------------------

**And when Abram heard that his brother was taken captive, HE ARMED HIS TRAINED SERVANTS, born in his own house, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued them unto Dan.**

**Genesis 14:14**

The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign  is about having many servants of God in the church.  The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign is about arming the servants with the tools that they need.   The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign is about training many people to fight for God’s kingdom. 

Abraham had many servants!  Abraham trained his servants!  Abraham armed his trained servants!  It is important that all the servants in the church are armed and trained!     

It is time to follow the example of Abraham who had many armed and trained servants.  Let us see what the scripture teaches us to do.

**1. The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign ensures that every member is a servant of God. **

**Then shall ye return, and discern between the righteous and the wicked, between him that serveth God and him that serveth him not.**

**Malachi 3:18**

There is a big difference between a church member and a servant of God. A church member does nothing for God.  A church member just attends church.  A church member sits around and receives a lot, giving nothing. Make sure that your church is full of servants and not members.

**2. The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign ensures that every servant of God is trained with the Word. **

**Moreover, brethren, I would not that ye should be ignorant, how that all our fathers were under the cloud, and all passed through the sea; AND WERE ALL BAPTIZED UNTO MOSES IN THE CLOUD AND IN THE SEA;**

**1 Corinthians 10:1-2 **

The training of a servant of God is deeper than the teachings that ordinary Christians receive.  A servant of God is immersed into the teachings of the house.  These teachings become a part of their lives. 

The Israelites were baptized into Moses.  Israel was baptized deeply into all the doctrines, commands and teachings of Moses.  They were baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea.  This baptism was both in public and in private.  The children of Israel were deep into Moses’ teachings both in the sea and in the cloud.  All the servants of the house need to be baptized deeply into the teachings. 

**3. The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign ensures that every servant of God is grafted into the anointing.  **

**As in water face answereth to face, so THE HEART OF MAN TO MAN.**

**Proverbs 27:19**

Servants armed and trained are much higher than ordinary members.  The servants armed and trained are connected to the anointing.  Heart must be connected to heart.  Servants armed and trained have hearts that are connected to the heart of their mentor and father.  It is the only way to be grafted into the family.

**4. The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign ensures that every servant of God is trained through multiple experiences.   **

**Moab hath been at ease from his youth, and he hath settled on his lees, and hath not been emptied from vessel to vessel, neither hath he gone into captivity: therefore his taste remained in him, and his scent is not changed. **

**Jeremiah 48:11**

Moab was at ease because he had not been emptied from vessel to vessel. Being emptied from vessel to vessel speaks of being moved around from place to place.  Being transferred around and being made to work in different circumstances is very important in the development of God’s servants.  Moab’s scent was not changed.  His scent remained in him.  It is important that your natural scent (tendencies, thoughts, ideals, ideas and attitudes) is changed into a militarized servant of God. 

**5. The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign ensures that every servant of God is equipped with books. **

**And the voice which I heard from heaven spake unto me again, and said, Go and take the little book which is open in the hand of the angel which standeth upon the sea and upon the earth.**

**And I went unto the angel, and said unto him, Give me the little book. And he said unto me, Take it, and eat it up; and it shall make thy belly bitter, but it shall be in thy mouth sweet as honey.**

**And I took the little book out of the angel’s hand, and ate it up; and it was in my mouth sweet as honey: and as soon as I had eaten it, my belly was bitter. And he said unto me, Thou must prophesy again before many peoples, and nations, and tongues, and kings.**

**Revelations 10:8-11**

Every single one of God’s servants must be armed with Christian books that teach about the ministry.  Through a book a person can be trained to prophesy and to preach to many people, many nations, many tongues and kings. Books are the master key to receiving the supernatural power of God.  When God wants to bless you, He will send an angel to bring you a book. 

Many years ago God sent an angel to bring me a book.  I can still see him dropping the little book on the table of the verandah of my father’s house! “That’s a good book, read it”, he said.  With those words I received admission into the school of ministry.  Little did I know what that little book was going to lead me into. 

**6. The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign ensures that every servant of God is armed with large collections of audio and video preaching messages.**

**And the spirit entered into me when he spake unto me, and set me upon my feet, that I heard him that spake unto me.**

**Ezekiel 2:2**

Listening to messages and watching videos will ensure that the servants are armed with the anointing. The Spirit will enter you when you listen to the word of God.

Through technology you can have large collections of audio and video teaching messages. These are weapons that every servant needs to be armed with.

There are messages of God that are meant for shepherds. 

**7. The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign ensures that every servant of God is armed with messages that are designed for preachers and teachers. **

And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying, Son of man, PROPHESY AGAINST THE SHEPHERDS OF ISRAEL, PROPHESY, AND SAY UNTO THEM, THUS SAITH THE LORD GOD UNTO THE SHEPHERDS; Woe be to the shepherds of Israel that do feed themselves! should not the shepherds feed the flocks?

Ezekiel 34:1-2

You must distinguish between messages intended for the general flock and messages intended for shepherds. 

There are preaching messages that the servants of God must be armed with. In the thirty-fourth chapter of Ezekiel, we see messages that are specifically meant for the shepherds and not the ordinary congregants. “The Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign must ensure that the servants are armed with special messages for preachers and teachers. The “Machaneh\*” is one such collection of preaching and teaching messages. 

**8. The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign allows for an increase in the amount of teaching of the word of God. ** This preaching and teaching campaign increases the growth and blessing in the church.  

And the word of God increased; and the number of the disciples multiplied in Jerusalem greatly; and a great company of the priests were obedient to the faith.

Acts 6:7

**9. The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign does not aim to have church services that are short. **

And upon the first day of the week, when the disciples came together to break bread, PAUL PREACHED UNTO THEM, ready to depart on the morrow; AND CONTINUED HIS SPEECH UNTIL MIDNIGHT.  And there were many lights in the upper chamber, where they were gathered together. And there sat in a window a certain young man named Eutychus, being fallen into a deep sleep: and as Paul was long preaching, he sunk down with sleep, and fell down from the third loft, and was taken up dead.

Acts 20:7-9

Longer church services are important so that we can linger in the presence of the Lord.  Backslidden Christians want to shorten every church service! They want to dispose of God as fast as they can.

**10. The “Servants Armed and Trained” Campaign aims to have many church camps where there is no concern about how long we stay in the presence of God. **

And WHEN THE PEOPLE SAW THAT MOSES DELAYED TO COME DOWN OUT OF THE MOUNT, THE PEOPLE GATHERED THEMSELVES TOGETHER UNTO AARON, AND SAID UNTO HIM, UP, MAKE US GODS, which shall go before us; for as for this Moses, the man that brought us up out of the land of Egypt, we wot not what is become of him.

Exodus 32:1

 Long sessions of teaching must come on without the concern that the sessions are being delayed or prolonged.  The preaching and teaching campaign will ensure that the word of God increases in the church.  When the church is concerned about prolonged or delayed services they are in danger of falling into sin like the Israelites did.  We must have congregations that are not concerned with the length of time that we spend in the presence of God.

\* The Machaneh is a collection of preaching and teaching camp messages by the author, Bishop Dag Heward-Mills. It is available on podcast and at daghewardmillsaudio.org
