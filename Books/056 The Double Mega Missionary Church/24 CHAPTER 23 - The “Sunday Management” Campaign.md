### CHAPTER 23** **

The “Sunday Management” Campaign\
------------------------------------------------------

**And they, continuing daily with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread from house to house, did eat their meat with gladness and singleness of heart, Praising God, and having favour with all the people. And the Lord added to the church daily such as should be saved.**

**Acts 2:46-47**

The scripture above gives the impression that these people really loved being in church.  Church was so popular that they went every day and not once a week.  I was shocked when I visited a church that had services every other week.  Indeed, Christianity has been watered down by the modern church. 

It is important to manage the Sunday event in such a way as to accomplish the goal of the service.  The goal of a church service is to worship God and to hear from Him.  We must wait on the Lord. It is those who wait on the Lord who renew their strength.  It is not those who rush in and out of church who renew their strength.  

**But they that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength;**

**they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.**

**Isaiah 40:31**

Today, many modern churches have shortened their services so that they can give their members a quick nice experience in the church.  In the name of convenience, many churches have backslidden!  Today, backslidden Christian congregations are dictating to the pastors how long a church service should be.  The Sunday service must be managed beautifully so that everyone who comes there has a great spiritual experience.  Church must be run so well that people do not want to go home.   

You must not shorten your church service to suit the unspiritual people in the church.  You must not create unacceptably short and quick church services to suit wicked people who do not love God.  Demons must not dictate how long we should stay in church.  Many demons are screaming  “Too long, too long. Let the people go home and rest.”  

What is the aim of a church service?  Why should a backslidden Christian tell me how long I should preach? Why should those who do not love God dictate how quickly a church service must be brought to a close?  It is those who hang around who get affected by the presence of God.  Those who tarry long at the wine are those who are affected by the wine. Those who have a quick sip of alcoholic wine during the Holy Communion are not affected by the wine.  Those who tarry long at the wine are those who get drunk and overwhelmed by alcohol.  

**Who hath woe? who hath sorrow? who hath contentions? who hath babbling? who hath wounds without cause? who hath redness of eyes?  They that tarry long at the wine; they that go to seek mixed wine.**

**Proverbs 23:29-30**

Go around the world today and you will find one fast song (praises) and two slow songs (worship) followed by a quick twenty-five minute sermon and a hurried offering time followed by pressurized speedy announcements.  Pastors pride themselves in being in and out of a service in record time.  Many church services do not last more than ninety minutes. (You wonder if the pastors are aircrafts doing a quick turnaround in a transit airport.)  Such services are held to satisfy the consciences of people who no longer have time for God.  Do not be a pastor who is led by backslidden big shots! These people who put pressure on you to change your church services will not even support the ministry with their finances. 

Today, questionable Christian musical artistes are found on stage promoting their albums and musical careers at the expense of having quality worship and praise.  Many characters who call themselves worship ministers are actually hustling musicians seeking for a little fame and few more sales of their music.  

Listen carefully!  

Sunday management of the church service is not the same as the destruction of true worship on Sundays.  Sunday management of a church service is not the same as the taking away of our quality time with God.  The only purpose for us being created is to work for God.  The reason for our creation is to serve our Maker.  

**Thou art worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honour and power: for thou hast created all things, and for thy pleasure they are and were created.**

**Revelation 4:11**

People have varied experiences when they attend a church.  Some have bad experiences in the toilet, some do not understand what is being said, some members feel too hot and some people feel too cold.  Some people like the music, some people do not like it.  Some enjoy everything but do not have anyone to talk to.  All aspects of the Sunday service must be managed so that no one has a negative experience in the church.
