### CHAPTER 19\

The “Uncles and Aunties” Campaign\
-------------------------------------------------------

**But Jesus said, Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.**

**Matthew 19:14**

The “Aunties and Uncles” Campaign is where church members turn themselves into aunties and uncles and visit young children in school. Very young children can be won to the Lord.  Most people will not change when they grow up.  Most people turn into old kings who can no longer be admonished, talked to, witnessed to, advised, counselled or helped. 

**Better is a poor and a wise child than an old and foolish king, who will no more be admonished.**

**Ecclesiastes 4:13**

Real evangelism is for the young.  It is young people who will turn to the Lord.  When Jesus walked in Galilee, He witnessed to young people.  All the disciples were young people.  All the older Pharisees and Sadducees cursed Him, hated Him and rejected Him. Older people are not easy to win! *It is only unwise pastors who focus on winning the older and the elder.*  The older and the elder that you will have will be those who grow up with you. 

When I was in secondary school, a man called Uncle James visited me regularly.  He was not my biological uncle but he became an “uncle” to me because of his continuous interest in me.  He would visit me, counsel me and teach me the word of God.  

Uncle James explained salvation to me.  He told me why Jesus died on the cross for me.  He told me  “Without the shedding of blood there would be no forgiveness of sins, and that is why Jesus had to die on the cross for you”.  He told me that whenever I did not understand a scripture or a hard truth, I should just “Put it in the wardrobe” and go back for it after a long time.  He told me that there were questions I would not be able to answer for a long time, but one day I would understand them all.  He made me trust in the word of God and he became an “uncle” to me.   

Through the ministry of “Uncles and Aunties” young people will be brought to church.  Unfortunately, many pastors despise the young.  They say, “We want real souls. We don’t want children. Bring real people to church!  These are just kids! These children don’t have anything and don’t know anything.”  But that is not how Jesus was! Jesus was interested in the young.  Anyone who is interested in the very young will experience mighty church growth.  

If you are sent out as a missionary, you must forget about the old people of that city.  Reach out to the young.  Emphasize on the young.  Those are the convertible souls.  Not everyone can be transformed and changed by the word of God.  

You need to have to have a ministry of “uncles and aunties” where you reach out to children who are much younger than you are.  Through those children you will experience growth.  You will see the double mega missionary church!
