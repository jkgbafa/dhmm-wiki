### CHAPTER 27 \

The “Gathering Bus” Campaign\
--------------------------------------------------

**THEY GO FROM STRENGTH TO STRENGTH, EVERY ONE OF THEM APPEARS before God in Zion.**

**Psalms 84:7 (NASB) **

The “Gathering”  Campaign is to assemble all members of all groups together in one place. All through the Bible you will see God’s efforts to gather His people together. You will see that God desires us to make the effort to assemble our members together in one place to receive supernatural blessings. 

**1. Gathering is the recovering of the remnant of the people. ** It is important to recover a remnant from the branches that have been started. You can see how God assembles the remnants of His people from the branches in Assyria, Egypt, Pathros, Cush, Elam, Shinar, Hamath and the islands of the sea.   

And it shall come to pass in that day, that the Lord shall set his hand again the second time TO RECOVER THE REMNANT OF HIS PEOPLE, WHICH SHALL BE LEFT, FROM ASSYRIA, AND FROM EGYPT, AND FROM PATHROS, AND FROM CUSH, AND FROM ELAM, AND FROM SHINAR, AND FROM HAMATH, AND FROM THE ISLANDS OF THE SEA.

And he shall set up an ensign for the nations, and shall assemble the outcasts of Israel, and gather together the dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth.

Isaiah 11:11-12

**2. Every time the people gather, they are strengthened.**

THEY GO FROM STRENGTH TO STRENGTH, EVERY ONE OF THEM APPEARS before God in Zion.

Psalms 84:7 (NASB)

**3. Gathering is to make the people hear the Word. Gather the sheep into one large gathering so that they can receive the Word with power. **

Specially the day that thou stoodest before the Lord thy God in Horeb, when the Lord said unto me, GATHER ME THE PEOPLE TOGETHER, and I WILL MAKE THEM HEAR MY WORDS, that they may learn to fear me all the days that they shall live upon the earth, and that they may teach their children. 

Deuteronomy 4:10

**4. Gathering must be done from far and wide.**   You must overcome all the difficulties that prevent people from being gathered.  Do not give or accept excuses! Rise up and gather them! Smite the waves! Let the deeps of the river dry up! Solve the transportation problems and gather the people. Gather them until there is no space or place for them.

I WILL HISS FOR THEM, AND GATHER THEM; for I have redeemed them: and they shall increase as they have increased. And I will sow them among the people: and they shall remember me in far countries; and they shall live with their children, and turn again.

I will bring them again also out of the land of Egypt, and gather them out of Assyria; and I will bring them into the land of Gilead and Lebanon;

AND PLACE SHALL NOT BE FOUND FOR THEM.

AND HE SHALL PASS THROUGH THE SEA WITH AFFLICTION,

AND SHALL SMITE THE WAVES IN THE SEA, AND ALL THE DEEPS OF THE RIVER SHALL DRY UP: and the pride of Assyria shall be brought down, and the sceptre of Egypt shall depart away. And I will strengthen them in the LORD; and they shall walk up and down in his name, saith the LORD.

Zechariah 10:8-12

**5. God will gather the people that remember Him and will strengthen them in the Lord. **

I WILL HISS FOR THEM, AND GATHER THEM; for I have redeemed them: and they shall increase as they have increased. And I will sow them among the people: and THEY SHALL REMEMBER ME IN FAR COUNTRIES; and they shall live with their children, and turn again. 

I will bring them again also out of the land of Egypt, and gather them out of Assyria; and I will bring them into the land of Gilead and Lebanon; and place shall not be found for them.

And he shall pass through the sea with affliction, and shall smite the waves in the sea, and all the deeps of the river shall dry up: and the pride of Assyria shall be brought down, and the sceptre of Egypt shall depart away.

AND I WILL STRENGTHEN THEM IN THE LORD; and they shall walk up and down in his name, saith the LORD.

Zechariah 10:8-12

**6. Gathering the people is to counteract the scattering caused by devils.**   When the enemies of God see that there is no shepherd they come to scatter the people. The gathering of the people is the exact opposite of what the devil does. Gathering is an anti-demonic activity.  Counteract the works of demons in the church by gathering the crowds.

Then he said, I DID SEE ALL ISRAEL SCATTERED UPON THE MOUNTAINS, AS SHEEP THAT HAVE NO SHEPHERD: and the Lord said, These have no master; let them return therefore every man to his house in peace. 

2 Chronicles 18: 16

**7. Gathering of the people causes the land to be subdued and brought under control. ** Large gatherings of God’s people bring the nation under control.  The presence of God’s people subdues the land.  

And THE WHOLE CONGREGATION OF THE CHILDREN OF ISRAEL ASSEMBLED TOGETHER AT SHILOH, and set up the tabernacle of the congregation there. And THE LAND WAS SUBDUED BEFORE THEM.

Joshua 18:1
