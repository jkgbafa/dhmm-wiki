### CHAPTER 25** **

The “Telepastoring” Campaign\
--------------------------------------------------

**But the Comforter, which is the HOLY GHOST, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your REMEMBRANCE, whatsoever I have said unto you.**

**John 14:26**

Telepastoring is the art of calling up members before church and reminding them of the need for them to come to the house of the Lord.  If you have an army of people whose work it is to telephone Christians on Saturday night and encourage them to come to church, your church will definitely grow.  You will be surprised that many Christians have forgotten that they need to even go to church. 

The work of the Holy Spirit is to remind Christians of what they already know. The scripture above is clear.  The work of the Holy Spirit is to bring things into your mind.  A good pastor must not assume that the church members will remember to come to church or to serve the Lord. As you are prompted by the Holy Spirit, you must remind  Christians of the need for them to come to church.  You must remind Christians of the need to serve the Lord. Whenever you do this, you are working hand in hand with the Holy Spirit. 

**Goals of Telepastoring**

**1.** **Telepastoring makes you a good minister of the gospel.  ** Telepastoring is the art of reminding the Christians of their duties and obligations.  Call up your members! Stir them up! Remind them to come to church! Tell them why fellowshipping is important. Do not assume that they know what is right.  

IF THOU PUT THE BRETHREN IN REMEMBRANCE of these things, THOU SHALT BE A GOOD MINISTER OF JESUS CHRIST, nourished up in the words of faith and of good doctrine, whereunto thou hast attained.

1 Timothy 4:6

**2. Telepastoring is an apostolic command. ** The apostle Paul gave an apostolic command to Timothy to remind his members of the right thing to do.  An obedient son is likely to see more church growth than a proud pastor who can no longer be admonished. Paul had a son he could command and instruct.  That person was Timothy.  It is no wonder that Timothy’s church was one of the largest in his time.  

OF THESE THINGS PUT THEM IN REMEMBRANCE, charging them before the Lord that they strive not about words to no profit, but to the subverting of the hearers.

2 Timothy 2:14

**3. Telepastoring will stir up the minds of those who are called.**  People can be stirred up when you talk to them. When you speak with excitement about the approaching church service, they will be stirred up to meet you in church.  

This second epistle, beloved, I now write unto you; in both WHICH I STIR UP YOUR PURE MINDS by way of remembrance:

2 Peter 3:1

**4. Telepastoring puts the people in remembrance.**  In other words, telepastoring reminds people of what is important.  When you call your church members on Saturday night, you remind them of how important it is to be in church on Sunday.  

Wherefore I will not be negligent to PUT YOU ALWAYS IN REMEMBRANCE of these things, though ye know them, and be established in the present truth. 

2 Peter 1:12

**5. Telepastoring must be done as long as you are a pastor.**  Telepastoring is appropriate and necessary in every church and ministry.  Telepastoring will always be a relevant ministry no matter how big or small your church is.  Calling up people is what is appropriate. Paul said he would remind people as long as he was on earth.  

Yea, I THINK IT MEET, AS LONG AS I AM IN THIS TABERNACLE, to stir you up by putting you in remembrance;

2 Peter 1:13
