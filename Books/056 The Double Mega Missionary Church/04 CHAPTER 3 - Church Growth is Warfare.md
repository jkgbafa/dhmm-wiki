### CHAPTER 3** **

Church Growth is Warfare\
----------------------------------------------

**And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church; AND THE GATES OF HELL SHALL NOT PREVAIL against it.**

**Matthew 16:18**

Building a church is the greatest war you will ever get involved with.  Jesus said, “I will build my church and the gates of hell will not prevail against it.” The gates of hell are diametrically opposed to the building of the church of God.  Notice that the gates of hell are set directly against the church.  

*When you believe that building a church is warfare, you will sit up and do better.  You must have a warfare attitude if you want to build the church. You must have a warfare mind if you want to build the church.  You cannot have a peaceful and “peace time” disposition if you want to build the church.  *

The gates of hell are attempting to prevail against the church.  The gates of hell are oriented against the church, trying to overpower it.  There are wicked forces that are sent to prevent church growth, church planting and church development.  Many claim to be called to build churches but are unable to break through in ministry.  

Preaching nicely is different from building a church.  There are many nice preachers but not many can actually build a church.  Indeed, not many can build a church that has a hundred members.  Not many people can plant churches.  

The gates of hell are against the planting of churches.  Satan is opposed to the building of churches.  He is vehemently opposed to you building a double mega missionary church in your lifetime.  The gates of hell are against the building of a series of churches.  The gates of hell are trying to prevail against the construction of church buildings.  (Meanwhile, buildings of other religions are being rolled out in thousands).  Satan knows the effect of a building and how it establishes a church; that is why he opposes you.  

You must fight the gates of hell if you want to build the church. The gates of hell are the openings to hell.  All types of devils and all types of opposition come against you through the gates of hell.  These wicked powers are sent to fight those who build the church.  Every type of devil comes through these gates of hell to resist the building of the church. 

Devils of opposition, devils of resistance, devils of temptation, devils of accusation, devils of manipulation, Jezebel spirits, dogs, dragons, serpents and devils of deception are released against the building of a church.   

The pastor who gives himself to building the church meets the greatest resistance from the depths of hell.  There are many other Christian activities that can be undertaken.  Teaching Christian classes, running Christian schools, running Christian hospitals and Christian orphanages are good Christian activities. But the gates of hell are not opposed to these in the same way.  

The gates of hell are absolutely opposed to the building of the church.  That is why a man of God must be absolutely determined to build the church.  Why would Jesus say that He would build His church and immediately mention the gates of hell?  

Why would Jesus declare that He would build His church and then immediately describe the opposition to this work as being the gates of hell?      

The gates of hell are totally against the church!  Demons of deception are sent out to fight the building of the church. These resisting demons come through the gates of hell.  These demons of deception manipulate men of God and direct them to build everything else other than the church.  These demons from the gates of hell make pastors build offices, businesses, banks, NGOs (Non-Governmental Organisations), schools, universities, orphanages, hospitals and every other good work apart from the church.  

You will notice that many pastors are more ardently connected to building these things than they are to the building of the church.  I have noticed that missionaries are able to build schools more easily than they build the church.  The gates of hell are not against schools. The gates of hell are against churches.  

The gates of hell are unquestionably against the church. Demons of procrastination are sent to fight the building of the church.  Instead of starting immediately to build the church, many people delay until it is too late. They do not realise that the gates of hell are influencing them.  

Years ago, one of my missionaries wanted to build a particular NGO instead of building a church.  I quickly told him to forget about the NGO and build the church of God instead. Today, the church is standing tall but this NGO does not exist. Many NGOs which were started in those days have evaporated into thin air but the church is still standing strong.   

The gates of hell are utterly against the church.  Demons of resistance and opposition are sent to fight the building of the church.  I have not found as much resistance in any area as I have found in the building of the church.  

I have encountered resistance from my bishops and top leaders whenever I have wanted to build the church. The most heated meetings I have ever been in always had to do with building the church.  I have had meetings that virtually turned into quarrels when I wanted to build the church.  Demons possess pastors and make them divert money into everything other than building a double mega missionary church. 

The gates of hell are against the church! Demons of distraction are sent to fight the building of the church.  Pastors would rather have conventions and stay under tents than build their churches.  There have to be meetings to shake people up and remind them of their one and only task: to build the church!  

I met a pastor of a large church who gave himself to the empowerment of people through computer skills, investments, business deals and market place engineering.  Indeed, these estimable activities seemed so powerful and effective at the time but they all ended up in smoke, achieving little or nothing.  When this pastor was in his sixties, he suddenly realised the need to build churches.  In his old age, he attempted to build churches but obviously there was little he could do at his age.  

The gates of hell are against the church! Demons of worthlessness are sent to fight the building of the church.  When your church does not have a building, it is easy to be closed down.  Recently, a president in an African nation announced the closure of many churches that did not have buildings.  Churches that do not have buildings are easily attacked and wiped out. The gates of hell are against the church.  

The gates of hell are completely and totally against the church! Demons of theft are sent to fight the building of the church. Instead of building the church, money is diverted into private pockets.  Cars are bought, air tickets are bought, buses are bought, personal homes are built and pastors spend a lot of money.  However, none of this money is spent on the church.  Many pastors steal the church’s money.  Many divert the money that is meant for the building of the church into private pockets.  

The gates of hell are against the church!  The demons of frustration fight pastors who are trying to build the church.  The gates of hell are against the building of the church.  When contractors inflate the prices of projects, the church is unable to build much.  This inflation of prices, as well as stealing from the project, are all manifestations of demonic opposition to the church. 

Many of the workers and artisans are used by evil spirits to frustrate the building of a church.  Architects are inspired by the devil to design unbuildable projects.  Many projects never start because of the design presented by the architect.  Many projects cannot be completed because of the complicated designs of some architects. These are all demonic works.  The gates of hell are totally opposed to the building of any church.  

*When you recognize that building a church is warfare, you will sit up and do better.  You must have a warfare attitude if you want to build the church. You must have a warfare mind if you want to build the church.  You cannot have a peaceful and “peace time” disposition if you want to build the church.  *

Church growth is warfare!  Something is fighting you, that is why your church is not growing.  If you think satan is going to quickly and easily give up his prized souls to your church, then you do not understand what is happening.  Satan is the god of this world and has captured the hearts and minds of billions of souls.  Satan is a wicked fallen angel!  He wants to have multitudes in hell with him.  Satan is standing his ground, resisting the release of captured souls.  Satan will not give up any one without a fight.  He will only give them up through a real war.  Pastors who are not prepared to go to war cannot have church growth.  

The gates of hell are against church growth. That is why pastors are directed to read every other book apart from the books on church growth.  Pastors are excited when they see books on how to become a millionaire.   Men of God are more interested in reading books on business rather than reading books on church growth, church planting and church work. 

It is time for you to rise up with the mind of a fighter. You need the mind of someone who is at war. People at war are different from people at peace.  It is time to deploy every effort to fight the war of church growth and church building.  This book is all about fighting and campaigning relentlessly for church growth, church planting and church building.  “I will build my church and the gates of hell will not prevail against it”!
