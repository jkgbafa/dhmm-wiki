### CHAPTER 4 \

The Campaign for Church Growth\
----------------------------------------------------

**For a great door and effectual is opened unto me, and there are many adversaries.**

**1 Corinthians 16:9**

An open door means that there are great prospects for ministry and church growth.  

A great door to church growth is opened to all pastors who care to experience the fulfilment of this prophecy.  It has been prophesied, so it will surely come to pass.  An effectual door has been opened in the ministry.  

What is a door? A door is an opportunity.  A door is a chance to achieve something.  Do you believe that there are great prospects for church growth?  I do!  I believe that God is building His church.  I believe that is the most important thing happening in the world today.   

Like everything else, your call will be tested and contested.  Satan will not allow you to have it easy.  He will fight with you every step of the way. You must begin an aggressive campaign to achieve church growth and to see the fulfilment of prophecy in your life. 

A campaign is a systematic course of aggressive activity for some specific purpose.  It is important that pastors take up *a systematic progression of aggressive activities that will lead to church growth.*  Unfortunately, many ministers do not respect their ministries enough to apply themselves to these campaigns. They do not plan and develop a series of aggressive activities that will lead to church growth. Church growth is possible!  Church growth is difficult to achieve but it is possible!  It is nice to have church growth but it is not easy to have church growth!

Every church growth campaign must have these three elements. 

1. A church growth campaign must be systematic.

2. A church growth campaign must consist of a series of aggressive activities.

3. A church growth campaign must have a specific purpose.  

This book contains a systematic course of aggressive activities that are designed to help your church to grow practically. 

Many pastors think that their churches will grow by osmosis.   Many think that crowds of people will mysteriously appear in the church. That is not the case. Your church will grow through a series of campaigns.  

Wars are often called military campaigns.  This is because a war does not involve just shooting guns.  War involves propaganda.  War involves creating industries that manufacture weapons.  A military campaign involves activities like espionage, military intelligence and creating military prisons to keep prisoners of war. 

War involves travel, transportation, the provision of food for the military, the storage of food for the military, the care of the military, the care for injured soldiers, the building of air fields, the building of harbours, the building and management of military hospitals, the recruitment of doctors, the resettlement of soldiers after war and the caring for wives and children of soldiers who have lost their lives in war. 

You are in a war to achieve church growth.  God wants you to set up a campaign of systematic aggressive activities that lead towards your singular goal of church growth. Church growth is possible through a series of campaigns!  A double mega church is possible!
