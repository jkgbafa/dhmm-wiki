### CHAPTER 26 \

The “Tangerine” Campaign\
----------------------------------------------

**I therefore so run, not as uncertainly; so fight I, not as one that beateth the air:**

**1 Corinthians 9:26 **

Where do I start implementing the revelation on making my church grow? There are so many areas that I could develop.  There are so many things that I could do.   Should I start with prayer?  Should I start with a choir?  I realise that all things work together for good but I just do not know where to start from.  

Indeed, there are times that the many aspects of church growth are so varied that you are confused as to where to start and where to end.  The “Tangerine Segment” will help you to start somewhere.  The “Tangerine Segment” will help you to define a few areas that you can emphasize on.  What are “Tangerine Segments”?  

The Tangerine Segments are the easy to define groups or areas in your church. A tangerine has segments that can easily be defined.   These are the tangerine segments. Perhaps each tangerine has fourteen or fifteen major segments. These segments are easy to see.  The tangerine segments are areas that you can emphasize on and more easily make the church grow.  In God’s wisdom, He has divided a tangerine into fourteen or fifteen easily defined segments. As each segment grows, the entire tangerine becomes bigger.  

The apostle Paul was clear, “I do not fight uncertainly. I do not fight a vague, difficult to define enemy. I do not beat the air. I am not giving blows in the air.  I am fighting with a clear target in view.”  

Many people are trying to make their churches grow and do not know where to start. There is no need to punch blows into the air.  There is no need to go around shadowboxing.  Take your tangerine segment (naturally formed groups and associations in the church) and make them grow bigger. 

The tangerine segments show you the big areas that are easy to define. In every church, some aspects of the church are easy to see. For instance, the people who belong to the choir may be one tangerine segment.  The people who live close to the church may be one easy to define segment.  The ushers may be another easy to define group in the church.  These groups may have activities that could make them grow on their own.  If your choir is very large, your church will be very large.   If your ushering team is very large, your church will be very large. If you have a large neighbourhood force, your church is likely to be large.  

Look into your church and ask yourself, “What are the groups that are easy to define and what are the groups that people naturally congregate into?” Those are your tangerine segments!  You are not likely to have so many of such groups. Instead of fighting the air and not knowing what exactly to target, take out the tangerine segments and emphasize on growing these naturally forming groups.  

The tangerine revelation is to use the easily defined pillars and segments of the church as engines of growth.  Instead of trying to make everything work, try to make your tangerine segments grow. That is a good starting point.
