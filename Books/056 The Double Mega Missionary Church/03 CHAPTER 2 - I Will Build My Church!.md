### CHAPTER 2 \

I Will Build My Church!\
---------------------------------------------

**And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I WILL BUILD MY CHURCH; AND THE GATES OF HELL shall not prevail against it.**

**Matthew 16:18**

**I will build my church:  Jesus Christ declared it!**  

The greatest prophecy about church growth came from Jesus Himself. He prophesied, “I will build my church!”  

This prophecy has come to pass and is still being fulfilled.  It is because of this prophecy that I give myself to building churches; both spiritually and physically. Why swim against the tide? Why do something that God is not doing?  If Jesus is building the church, why would you build something else? Jesus is not building hotels!  Jesus is not building political parties!  Jesus is not building businesses! Jesus is not building banks! Jesus is building His church!  

I have managed a hotel before.  It is no joke to run such a business.  I have done business before. It is no small thing to buy and sell items. It is not a mean task to make profit from any activity you are involved in. You must have a proper respect for all business ventures.  Business may look like something you can do on the side but I tell you there is no work that will not require your mind, your heart, your time, your energy and your love, if it is to succeed.  You have to labour with all your heart if there will be profit.  

**We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place, until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts:**

**2 Peter 1:19**

There are many prophecies that the church will be built.  These prophecies declare that God’s kingdom will be established in the last days.  These prophecies encourage us to keep working in the church, knowing that God will increase us and bless our efforts. 

Because there are strong prophecies about the church, you must decide to walk in line with them.  We have a more sure word of prophecy that will come to pass practically in our lives. We must take heed to this prophecy and give ourselves to the work of building the church.  The church must be built at all cost because that is the will of God.  

It is amazing that pastors have given themselves to building schools, universities, hotels, businesses, banks and political parties.  Can you imagine the time and effort that a man of God will need to build a business, a hotel, a bank or a political party? All that time and effort is taken away from the building of the church of God.   

Indeed, many ministers have redirected precious time, resources and energy from the building of the church to the building of human institutions. 

Jesus said, “I will build my church!”  I am privileged to be educated and I know that education is different from faith.  Education is not the same as studying about Jesus and God. Building a school is a good thing but building a school is not the same as building the church. 

It is time to focus on the words of Jesus Christ.  “I will build my church!”  Many universities around the world started out as Bible schools.  A secular university is so different from a Bible school.  Educational authorities know this and completely exclude spiritual things from their secular universities.  They know that propagating Christianity is very separate and different from running a secular university.  

It is amazing that ministers of God would rather give themselves to the building of universities than to the building of the house of God.  The stark difference between a secular university and a Bible school is clear to secular authorities but that difference is not clear to most men of God.  Men of God tend to think that in building a university they have somehow propagated Christianity.  There can be nothing further from the truth than that.  A secular university truly helps a nation and blesses mankind.  But it is not the same as building the church of Jesus Christ.  Often, building a secular university is a diversion of funds from the church, the mission and the true ministry of Jesus Christ.  

Indeed, when a church builds a secular university, the nation benefits from having another secular institution but the church loses the chance to grow and expand into new territories.  We are not called to do “good things”. We are called to do what God says!  Make sure that God is the One who is asking you to divert money from preaching the gospel and building churches, into building secular universities!    

I once spoke to a man of God whose father, an evangelist, had built a famous secular university.  He admitted to me, “God told my father to build a Bible school but he went beyond that and built a secular university.”  Let us remember the words of Jesus Christ and let us not substitute human wisdom for the wisdom of Jesus Christ.  

And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I WILL BUILD MY CHURCH; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.

Matthew 16:18

**I will build my church:  Ezekiel prophesied it.**

**Thus saith the Lord God; I will yet for this be enquired of by the house of Israel, to do it for them; I WILL INCREASE THEM WITH MEN LIKE A FLOCK.**

**Ezekiel 36:37**

In this marvellous prophecy, Ezekiel declared the church of God will be built and become like a flock.  Whatever you have will be increased.   It will grow.  It will get bigger! You will have more! You must believe it in your heart and you must expect it!  You do not have to steal somebody’s flock.  You do not have to destroy somebody’s church.  What you have is going to increase. Even though it is small today, it is going to get bigger.  

**I will build my church: Isaiah prophesied it.**

**Lift up thine eyes round about, and see: ALL THEY GATHER THEMSELVES TOGETHER, THEY COME TO THEE: thy sons shall come from far, and thy daughters shall be nursed at thy side.**

**Isaiah 60:4**

In this amazing prophecy, there is a prediction that your spiritual sons and daughters will come from far.  There will be a great gathering of the people that God is using you to minister to. 

**I will build my church:  Isaiah prophesied it many times! **

**Fear not: for I am with thee: I will bring thy seed from the EAST and gather thee from the WEST; I will say to the NORTH, Give up; and to the SOUTH, Keep not back: bring my sons from far, and my daughters from the ends of the earth;**

**Isaiah 43:5-6**

All through the book of Isaiah, you see predictions of the church being built.  In this wonderful church building prophecy, your church members will come from all four corners of the world. You will be amazed by how people will come from everywhere to listen to you preaching the word of God.  People are going to come from every corner to fill the seats in your church.  Let us believe that these prophetic words are real.  These church building prophecies are the words of famous Jewish prophets.  The greatest Jewish prophet of all was Jesus Christ.
