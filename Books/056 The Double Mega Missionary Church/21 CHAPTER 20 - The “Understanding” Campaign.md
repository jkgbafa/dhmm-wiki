### CHAPTER 20 \

The “Understanding” Campaign\
--------------------------------------------------

The “Understanding” Campaign is all about deepening the understanding of the ordinary Christian.  When people do not understand things, they are easily offended.  When understanding is deep, they are rooted and grounded and not easy to uproot. Do you want your members to be easily uprooted?  Do you want your members to be scattered? Certainly not! Then start an Understanding Campaign! Organise classes to deepen the understanding of the people.  Start a Bible school.  Teach the Word at every opportunity.  

Do not take it for granted that the members know anything.  Many Christians do not know the basics of salvation. Many Christians do not know the basic doctrines of Christianity.  You must fight for a deeper understanding otherwise your church members will be blown away with the very next wind.  Jesus was very clear on this truth. When there is no root, there is no understanding! The lack of understanding causes Christians to be uprooted easily. 

Look at the amazing responsibility that God gives the shepherds to bring understanding to the sheep.   

**1. The understanding campaign prevents the sheep from being snatched away by the wicked one. **

When any one heareth the word of the kingdom, and UNDERSTANDETH IT NOT, then cometh THE WICKED ONE, AND CATCHETH AWAY that which was sown in his heart. This is he which received seed by the way side.

Matthew 13:19

**2. The understanding campaign must be taken up by the shepherds.  Feeding the sheep must be done with the aim of deepening their knowledge and understanding. **

And I will give you pastors according to mine heart, which SHALL FEED YOU WITH KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING.

Jeremiah 3:15

**3. The understanding campaign will cause the flock to increase.  Feeding the sheep properly to deepen their knowledge and understanding will cause the flock to multiply and increase. **

And I will give you pastors according to mine heart, which SHALL FEED YOU WITH KNOWLEDGE AND UNDERSTANDING.

AND IT SHALL COME TO PASS, WHEN YE BE MULTIPLIED AND INCREASED in the land, in those days, saith the Lord, they shall say no more, The ark of the covenant of the Lord: neither shall it come to mind: neither shall they remember it; neither shall they visit it; neither shall that be done any more.

Jeremiah 3:15-16

**4. The understanding campaign is what determines the fruitfulness and usefulness of a Christian. As the Christian understands the Word, he bears fruit.  As he lacks understanding, he becomes barren.  More understanding, more fruits!**

But he that received seed into the good ground is he that heareth the word, and UNDERSTANDETH IT; WHICH ALSO BEARETH FRUIT, AND BRINGETH FORTH, SOME AN HUNDREDFOLD, SOME SIXTY, SOME THIRTY.

Matthew 13:23
