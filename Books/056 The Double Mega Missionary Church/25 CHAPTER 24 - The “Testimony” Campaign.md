### CHAPTER 24\

 The “Testimony” Campaign\
-----------------------------------------------

**I will speak of thy testimonies also before kings, and will not be ashamed.**

**Psalms 119:46**

The “Testimonies” Campaign is to encourage testimonies to be regularly declared before the congregation.  The children must not be ashamed to declare what God has done for them.  

The “Testimonies” Campaign is to ensure that Christians hear testimonies of God’s goodness all the time.  It is important for Christians to see that God is real.  Through real testimonies from real people, you have the opportunity to charge the congregation with faith.  Through real testimonies from real people, you have the opportunity to overcome the devil in the congregation.  Through real testimonies from real people, you have the opportunity to counsel the congregation.  Hearing testimonies over and over makes the congregation excited about the living God that we serve.  Let us look at the amazing benefits of testimonies  

When it comes to church growth, testimonies are more valuable than money. Testimonies change everything about a church.  

I have rejoiced in the way of thy testimonies, as much as in all riches.

Psalms 119:14

**1. Testimonies will cause Christians to overcome the devil. ** We overcome the devil through testimonies.  Christians in the church will overcome the devil through the testimonies they give. When a Christian gives a testimony about how his life has changed, he cannot easily go back to his “vomit.” By giving the testimony, they overcome the devil in a very real way.    

And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.

Revelation 12:11

**2. Testimonies will counsel the sheep.  When they hear someone’s testimony about how they overcame, the sheep will know what to do.      **

THY TESTIMONIES ALSO ARE my delight and MY COUNSELLORS.

Psalms 119:24

**3. Testimonies will bring great joy into the church. Christians enjoy testimonies. **

Thy TESTIMONIES have I taken as an heritage for ever: for THEY ARE THE REJOICING OF MY HEART.

Psalms 119:111

**4. Testimonies will make the sheep have a deeper understanding of the Word. People have a greater understanding when they hear ordinary people giving testimonies.   **

I HAVE MORE UNDERSTANDING than all my teachers: FOR THY TESTIMONIES ARE MY MEDITATION.

Psalms 119:99

**5. Testimonies will make people put wickedness away from their lives.   **

THOU PUTTEST AWAY ALL THE WICKED of the earth like dross: THEREFORE I LOVE THY TESTIMONIES.

Psalms 119:119

**6. Testimonies will guide the sheep. The sheep will learn to stick to the way prescribed by the testimonies. **

I have stuck unto thy testimonies: O Lord, put me not to shame.

Psalms 119:31

**7. Wonderful testimonies will be remembered by the sheep.  Everyone remembers amazing testimonies of God’s goodness.  People remember the testimonies because they are wonderful.  Sometimes, people remember testimonies even more than the Word that is preached.  **

Thy testimonies are wonderful: therefore doth my soul keep them.

Psalms 119:129

**8. Testimonies will be a blessing to the whole church.  Testimonies will be a blessing to the person who gives it.  **

Blessed are they that keep his testimonies, and that seek him with the whole heart.

Psalms 119:2
