### CHAPTER 15\

How Jesus Related with the World\
======================================================

But Jesus did not commit himself unto them, because he knew all men, And needed not that any should testify of man: for he knew what was in man.

John 2:24-25

You must remember that the world is made up of millions of wicked men, millions of evil spirits and thousands of fallen angels, all living together in one space called earth. This mixture of creatures encountered Jesus Christ, the Son of God and the Saviour of the world. Jesus Christ had an amazing experience on earth. He was loved by some but He was rejected by most! The book of John outlines Jesus’ experience in this world that lies in such wickedness.

1\. Jesus Christ came into the world intentionally to meet human beings and to interact with them.

He came unto his own, and his own received him not.

John 1:11

Jesus’ journey to this earth was a necessary but sad event. I am sure He knew that He was coming to wicked people. It was for the sake of a few people who would receive salvation that He came.

Noah built the ark for just eight people. The great effort of building the ark, which took many years, was undertaken just to save eight people.

Jesus came onto His own, but His own received Him not. Jesus came to His own people! He was hopeful, he had faith; but he knew that very few people would respond to His ministry.

Jesus Himself said, “As it was in the days of Noah so shall it be for the coming of the Son of Man”. In the days of Noah very few people responded to the warnings. In the days of Jesus, very few people responded to the gospel.

2\. Jesus Christ made the world but the world did not recognize Him.

All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made. …HE WAS IN THE WORLD, and THE WORLD WAS MADE BY HIM, and the world knew him not.

John 1:3, 10

When Jesus came into the world, they simply asked Him, “Who are you?” Who do you think you are? You cannot come from heaven and tell us what to do!”

The world did not recognize Jesus Christ when He came to walk amongst them. They simply did not recognize their Creator. This astonishing failure to recognize the Son of God happened because human beings, under the influence of evil spirits are truly wicked.

3\. The world did not receive Him but as many as received Him, He gave power to become sons of God.

He came unto his own, and his own received him not. But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:

John 1:11-12

As was expected, the world rejected Jesus Christ. He came to His own but His own did not receive Him. His own country rejected Him. His own family rejected Him. You cannot expect any better behaviour from such a wicked world. However, there were a few people who received Jesus Christ and believed in Him. Those few people who received Jesus Christ were given power to become sons of God.

4\. God showed the whole wide world His love by sending Jesus.

For GOD SO LOVED THE WORLD, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.

John 3:16

This world, full of wicked men and evil spirits, received the greatest gift from our great God. The men of this wicked world quickly rose up and murdered Jesus Christ, the Saviour of the world. Perhaps they did this to confirm that they were truly evil.

In spite of the atrocious sins of the world, Jesus maintained open arms and great love for the people of this world. Human beings were given a chance to repent but Jesus did not preach the gospel to the evil spirits.

5\. God did not send Jesus into the world to condemn the world.

For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; BUT THAT THE WORLD THROUGH HIM MIGHT BE SAVED.

John 3:17

God intended to save the whole world through Jesus Christ. The world was full of wicked men who were expecting condemnation from the Son of God. They waited for Jesus to condemn them, but He never did. Even on the cross, He prayed that they would be forgiven.

Jesus simply did not come to this world to condemn people. It is unfortunate if people thought that Jesus came to condemn them.

6\. The world condemned itself.

And this is the condemnation, that light is come into the world, and men loved darkness rather than light, because their deeds were evil.

John 3:19

Men loved the darkness that they were involved with. They did not want to give up their lives of sin and wickedness. God did not send Jesus to condemn anyone in this world. The condemnation of this world is simply because it did not open its arms to receive the grace of God that had appeared to men.

7\. The world hated Jesus Christ.

THE WORLD CANNOT HATE YOU; BUT ME IT HATETH, because I testify of it, that the works thereof are evil.

John 7:7

The world hated Jesus Christ because He spoke the truth about them. Jesus Christ was hated by the world because Jesus Christ spoke the truth about the world. Today, there are many people who hate you because you tell them the truth. I have encountered people filled with so much hatred just because they were corrected and told the truth about their lives.

8\. Jesus is the light of the world.

Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I AM THE LIGHT OF THE WORLD: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.

John 8:12

When you follow Jesus, you will not walk in the darkness of this life. There is great darkness in the world today. Following Jesus Christ takes you away from the darkness and evil of this world.

9\. Jesus was very happy to go out of this dangerous world.

And NOW I AM NO MORE IN THE WORLD, BUT THESE ARE IN THE WORLD, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are.

John 17:11

By the time Jesus was about to be crucified, He knew that He was no longer a part of this world. Even though He was in the world, He was virtually out of the system. There is a time just before death that is the same as death itself.

10\. Jesus Christ prayed for His disciples because He had to leave them in a dangerous world.

And now I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are.

John 17:11

Jesus was happy to leave this world but He was concerned about His disciples. He wanted them to stay united because that way, they would be stronger together against the enemy. The disciples were going to have to take the gospel to millions of wicked and unrepentant men. The disciples were going to have to preach the word of God in spite of the hostility from millions of demons. Demons are very evil creatures who exist to oppose, accuse, tempt and deceive God’s servants. Jesus knew that He was leaving His disciples in a very toxic environment. He said to them, “ If the world hate you, ye know that it hated me before it hated you” (John 15:18).
