### CHAPTER 12\

Live Righteously in this World\
====================================================

For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men, Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should LIVE SOBERLY, RIGHTEOUSLY, AND GODLY, IN THIS PRESENT WORLD;

Titus 2:11-12

Living righteously in this world is the greatest challenge for believers. There is nothing righteous in this world. Evil spirits are not righteous. There are unclean and evil spirits. They make people unclean and evil. Next to devils, human beings are the most evil creatures on the earth! There are few righteous men on the earth today. “As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one:” (Romans 3:10).

Anyone who tries to live righteously in this world is going against the tide. Yet, without a great effort to live a righteous life, you cannot claim to be a Christian. How can you live righteously in this world? There are six rules to living righteously in this world.

1\. You must live soberly in this world.

For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men, Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should LIVE SOBERLY, righteously, and godly, in this present world;

Titus 2:11-12

Soberness is important for righteousness. Soberness speaks of being free from the influence of anything. Many armed robbers and evildoers take drugs to charge themselves up so they can perform their evil deeds. In their sober state they would not be able to commit the atrocious crimes that they do.

Many people have to come under the influence of something to be able to misbehave as they do.

If you are under the influence of alcohol, tobacco, drugs and weed, you must break out of those influences because these substances will never allow you to live righteously in this life.

Another strong influence is the opposite sex. When you are under the strong influence of a boy or a girl you are not likely to live righteously. There is a reason why they say “Love is blind.” When people are in love, they seem not to think properly or act properly. Their inability to think or act properly is because they are not sober. The influence they are under makes them unable to live righteously.

2\. You must live righteously in this world.

For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men, Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should LIVE soberly, RIGHTEOUSLY, and godly, in this present world;

Titus 2:11-12

Living righteously must be your goal. Righteousness must be your goal because righteousness is the breastplate in your armour. A large part of your defences is your righteousness. If you are able to walk in righteousness, very few of the long-term plans of satan against you will work.

Satan is constantly looking at your breastplate of righteousness to see if there are any gaps or cracks. Even if you do not know what satan’s plans are against you, it is important to keep your breastplate of righteousness intact. When you crack open a section of your armour by living in sin, you can rest assured that the demonic powers will fly into that gap and attempt to destroy you.

3\. You must live godly in this world.

For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men, Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should LIVE soberly, righteously, and GODLY, IN THIS PRESENT WORLD;

Titus 2:11-12

Godliness is to do everything that God would do. There are many grey areas in our lives. What shall I do next? What is allowed? What is not allowed? To truly overcome and live righteously, God will have to be your great example. What did God do and what will God do? By looking at God’s example, you will find the path of righteousness. God is love! God forgives! God is kind! God is gracious!

Jesus Christ is the perfect image of God. What did Jesus do? Jesus said, “He that hath seen me hath seen the Father” (John 14:9). How did Jesus handle Judas Iscariot? How did Jesus handle Peter? Exactly how God Himself would have handled them!

4\. You must condemn the world by your righteousness.

By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house; BY THE WHICH HE CONDEMNED THE WORLD, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith.

Hebrews 11:7

Your righteousness must have an effect on the world around you. Noah, by his righteousness, condemned the world around him. Noah’s righteousness created the perfect contrast and his lifestyle condemned the world. It is said that thousands of people gathered around Noah’s ark when the rain started falling.

There were also thousands of animals around the ark. All these people around the ark who could not get in were condemned. They had rejected Noah’s preaching and his righteous life and now they were condemned. Only Noah’s family was saved from the flood.

5\. You must not follow the lust of the flesh.

For all that is in the world, the LUST OF THE FLESH, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world.

1 John 2:16

To walk in righteousness, you must keep yourself from the lusts of the flesh. The flesh is filled with unholy desires. The flesh is the first problem of your life because it makes sin easy and natural for you. Holiness and righteousness are unnatural for you. Holiness and righteousness take great effort because the flesh is naturally desirous of wickedness and sin.

Decide to live the life without following the lust of the flesh. Be conscious of your flesh and what it can do. Your flesh can disgrace you and turn you into a wretched shipwreck.

Your flesh is so tuned to evil that the best word to describe you, when you follow your flesh, is “a wretch”. One day, a mother was singing a song in her house. She sang, “Amazing grace, how sweet the sound that saved a wretch like me.” Her son asked her, “Mama what is a wretch? Are you a wretch?” Indeed, she was a wretch. Everyone who lives in this flesh is a wretch. A wretch is an unfortunate and miserable person.

Be very conscious of your flesh and respect its power to destroy your righteous life on this earth.

6\. You must keep yourself unspotted from the world.

Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and TO KEEP HIMSELF UNSPOTTED FROM THE WORLD.

James 1:27

A spot is the smallest touch, the smallest influence and the smallest indication of worldly contact that has been made with you. God does not want even the smallest touch or influence of the world to be on you. To live righteously, you must avoid even the smallest touch of worldliness.

Indeed, the world has “spots” it can put onto you. Can the “spots” of the world be seen on your dressing, your private life, your music, your language, your friends, your sexual life and your conversation? It is time to put away every spot of the world in your life. Living righteously involves staying away from the spots of the world.
