### CHAPTER 4\

Overcome the Pressure to Conform to this World\
====================================================================

And BE NOT CONFORMED TO THIS WORLD: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

Romans 12:2

To be conformed to the world is to yield yourself to the worldly, social and spiritual influences around you. To conform to this world means to comply and to be obedient to this world.

You are not supposed to be compliant and obedient to worldly influences that are trying to change your behaviour. The reason why people conform to this world is so that they can fit in with a group and with the expectations of those around them.

Conformity is simply defined as yielding to group pressures. People conform because they want to be accepted. God does not want you to even desire to be accepted by the world.

1\. Do not be conformed to this world in order to please men.

For do I now persuade men, or God? or DO I SEEK TO PLEASE MEN? for if I yet pleased men, I should not be the servant of Christ.

Galatians 1:10

Paul recognized that he was under pressure to please men, to be accepted by men, to be admired by men and to be recognized by men. Today, there is great pressure to conform to the group called “the world”! You and I are under great pressure to be conformed to the world and to please the world.

Because of this pressure from the world, Christians must actually develop antagonistic attitudes towards the world rather than desiring to be accepted by the world.

What we are actually at war with is the pressure to conform to the world!

Our Christian life is a fight to overcome this pressure to be conformed to this world!

2\. Do not be conformed to this world in order to be accepted.

Nevertheless the people refused to obey the voice of Samuel; and they said, Nay; but we will have a king over us; THAT WE ALSO MAY BE LIKE ALL THE NATIONS; and that our king may judge us, and go out before us, and fight our battles.

1 Samuel 8:19-20

You may not be aware of how much pressure you are under to conform to the world system and be accepted. All through the Bible, God is against His people conforming to the world. The Israelites came under great pressure to be just like the nations all around them. Whenever the Israelites conformed to the world, they came under judgment.

You are even under pressure to admire the same things that the world admires. For instance, if you went with friends to a function and a sexually perverted person made a presentation which you found sinful and unacceptable but people around felt was perfectly alright, you might be tempted to conform by pretending to agree with them rather than being the odd one out.

So much happens in the world that we find attractive and we want to be just like the people of this world. Pastors want to be like secular chief executives or presidents. Christians want to be worldly millionaires. Christian musicians want to be worldly stars. Christian citizens want to be rich and famous like every other successful person in the world.

3\. Do not be conformed to the way the world speaks.

They are of the world: THEREFORE SPEAK THEY OF THE WORLD, and the world heareth them.

1 John 4:5

The people of this world think in a certain way and speak in a certain way. There is pressure to conform to this world and speak the way the people of this world speak.

The people of this world write words, compose songs and speak in particular characteristic ways. A Christian singer should not be happy if the world likes to listen to his music. If the world likes to listen to the words of your music, it means you are worldly and are of the world. Unfortunately, a lot of Christian music is very conformed to the world. Today, Christian music can be used in nightclubs and the world does not even know that they are dancing to Christian music.

A pastor should not be happy if the world listens or loves to listen to his messages. Worldliness in a pastor causes the world to be attracted to his preaching. Sinners do not enjoy real gospel preaching unless they are getting saved. Sinners are attracted to secularized preaching which sounds like worldly wisdom. Sinners must be attracted to gospel preaching that leads them to salvation through the power of God.

4\. Do not conform to the worldly way of thinking:

Howbeit we speak wisdom among them that are perfect: YET NOT THE WISDOM OF THIS WORLD, nor of the princes of this world, that come to nought:…

Which none of the princes of this world knew: for had they known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.

1 Corinthians 2:6, 8

The wisdom of this world will never be our wisdom. Our wisdom is the word of God. Jesus Himself taught us that a wise man is someone who hears the word of God and obeys it.

Therefore whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them, I WILL LIKEN HIM UNTO A WISE MAN, which built his house upon a rock:

Matthew 7:24

You and I have a clear source of wisdom. The word of God is the source of our wisdom. Our wisdom comes from the Holy Scriptures. You must believe that the Bible is the source of wisdom.

And that from a child thou hast known THE HOLY SCRIPTURES, WHICH ARE ABLE TO MAKE THEE WISE unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus.

2 Timothy 3:15

The people of this world have a kind of wisdom that does not lead to God. The world says things that sound wise but does not lead to God.

When pastors have yielded themselves and become conformed to this world, their preaching is filled with wise sounding sayings and mental delights that do not yield spiritual fruits. People hail such preachers as wise men because when they speak it sounds like the audio version of Encyclopaedia Britannica.

There is no need to yield yourself to the worldly way of thinking. Do not pride yourself in having read the biographies of President Bill Clinton, President Barrack Obama, President François Mitterand, Winston Churchill, Lee Kuan Yew and Margaret Thatcher instead of having read the biographies of William Carey, Adoniram Judson, Oral Roberts or Kenneth Hagin. The people you admire and the books you read reveal how much your thinking has been conformed to this world.

One day, I met a brother who was more influenced by secular things than the Bible. I encouraged him to play golf because Yonggi Cho and other pastors I knew had developed that habit for health and other good reasons. However, I could not convince him to learn how to play golf so I decided to give him the biography of Lee Kuan Yew. When he read how Lee Kuan Yew was an avid golfer, he decided to play golf. Clearly, this fellow was more influenced by secular thinking than by biblical and spiritual things. Do not become someone whose mind is conformed to the thinking of this world. We are not of those who speak the wisdom of this world.

The world has a way of thinking when it comes to financial things. The world believes in bonds, debts, mortgages, loans, financial engineering, hire purchase agreements, negotiated funding, credits, hedging and so on. Today, the richest countries in the world are laden with debt and many people who claim to be prospering are deeply in debt. There is nothing real about their prosperity because of the worldly wisdom and systems that they have adopted.

The word of God gives a different kind of wisdom when it comes to financial things. The wisdom of the Word may not have such high-sounding words but it is still the wisdom of God. God, in His word, teaches against loans and borrowing.

For the LORD thy God blesseth thee, as he promised thee: and thou shalt lend unto many nations, BUT THOU SHALT NOT BORROW; AND THOU SHALT REIGN over many nations, but they shall not reign over thee.

Deuteronomy 15:6

Through the Bible you see how God directs you away from borrowing, no matter the name given to it. It shows that you shall reign over nations and no one will reign over you if you walk in the responsibility and blessing of not borrowing.
