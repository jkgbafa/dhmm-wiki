### CHAPTER 6\

Overcome the Pressure to Befriend this World\
==================================================================

Ye adulterers and adulteresses, know ye not that THE FRIENDSHIP OF THE WORLD is enmity with God? Whosoever therefore will be a friend of the world is the enemy of God.

James 4:4 (NASB)

There are no meaningless relationships in the world; every single relationship you have has significance, importance and consequence!

Today God is telling you “do not befriend the world”. Friendship with the world means something. Friendship with the world has some significance, importance and consequence. Relationships have meanings! There are no meaningless relationships in the world. If you have a relationship with someone, it automatically means that certain other relationships are not going to work.

Friendship with your past boyfriend means your relationship with your husband will be bad. Friendship with your past girlfriend means that your relationship with your wife will be sour. This is the principle that the apostle James is speaking of today. Friendship with the world simply means enmity with God! You must never say that a relationship is meaningless. There are no meaningless relationships in the world! Every relationship has significance, importance and consequence!

Signs that You Have a Friendship with the World

The signs that you have a friendship with the world are easy to deduce. By looking at the signs of your friendship with someone, we can easily see whether you have a friendship with the world or not. Let us look at the signs that you have a friendship with the world.

1\. A good friend is someone whose company you enjoy. Therefore, the fact that you enjoy the company of the world and worldly people is a sign that you are friends with the world.

2\. A good friend is someone you talk to a lot. I once knew someone who claimed she did not speak much to a certain man. Yet, the man said of her, “She is my good friend.” How can you be a good friend who does not talk much with someone? The much talking is the sign of a good friendship. Your constant talking with worldly people, to secular people and to unbelievers is a sign that the world is your friend.

3\. A good friend is loyal and dependable. Someone who is a good friend will be faithful to you and never leave you or forsake you. The fact that you do not want to leave the world, leave your worldly friends and leave certain worldly cities is a sign that you are a friend with the world. You are loyal, dependable and faithful to the world; never forsaking them and never leaving them behind.

4\. A good friend laughs with you. I remember someone who laughed at me and mocked at me behind my back. Yet, this same fellow claimed friendship and love. How can that be? Your friendship with the world is revealed by how relaxed you are with worldly people. You laugh and chat with the wildest of sinners and you are comfortable in their presence. All that is a sign of your friendship with the world.

5\. A good friend doesn’t accuse you but comforts you. The fact that you do not present the gospel to your worldly friends shows that you do trust and believe in their ways. Instead of confronting them and “accusing” them of their need for salvation, you simply flow along with them and comfort them rather than telling them the truth.
