### CHAPTER 16\

Overcome the World through the Holy Spirit\
================================================================

Ye are of God, little children, and have overcome them: because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.

1 John 4:4

1. The world cannot receive the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit has a unique relationship with the world. He cannot be received by the world. The world cannot receive the gifts and manifestations of the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit cannot be a blessing to the world in the way that He is to the church.

If you want to receive the full blessing of the Holy Spirit, you must be a believer. Without being a believer, you cannot experience the gifts, the manifestations, the operations and the administrations of the Holy Spirit in His fullness. Once you are an unbeliever, the Holy Spirit has one ministry towards you. He wants to convict you of sin, righteousness and judgment.

Even the Spirit of truth; whom THE WORLD CANNOT RECEIVE, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him: but ye know him; for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you.

John 14:17

2\. The Holy Spirit is the only one who can convict the world of sin, of righteousness and of judgment.

And when he is come, he will REPROVE THE WORLD OF SIN, and of RIGHTEOUSNESS, AND OF JUDGMENT: of sin, because they believe not on me;

John 16:8-9

The first role of the Holy Spirit is to convict the world of sin. The Holy Spirit’s principal ministry to the world is to convict the world of sin, of righteousness and of judgment. The world, full of wicked men, has a warped understanding of sin. Men filled with evil spirits do not know when they have committed sins. Right is wrong and wrong is right! Men have become abnormal in their knowledge.

Today, we have people who are calling sin a human right. Sexual perversions are championed by the most dignified and respected heads of state. All these reveal a warped understanding of sin. Without the Holy Spirit, sin is called righteousness.

There are nations today that have passed laws making it illegal to believe in or preach about certain parts of the Bible. Such laws have been made by people who claim to champion human rights, such as the right to believe, the right to have a faith, the right to have a religion, and the right to free speech.

The second role of the Holy Spirit is to convict the world about righteousness. Only the Holy Spirit can reveal what true righteousness is. You will notice that everyone has a different opinion about what is right.

Even information from the Bible can give seemingly conflicting views about what is right, what can be done and what is acceptable. It is the Holy Spirit who convicts the world of righteousness.

The third role of the Holy Spirit is to convict the world of judgment. One of the most amazing things to see is a person who is completely unconcerned about judgments that are piling up against him. He seems not to care about anything.

Such a fellow seems not to notice and seems not to be concerned about judgments coming to him. You may warn him that he is going to hell, but he may smile at you and carry on. This fellow has not been convicted about the reality of judgment. People simply are not bothered by judgments because the Holy Spirit has not convicted them.

The One who convicts people about their judgment is the Holy Spirit. When the Holy Spirit convicts someone about judgment, he will repent and turn away from his sins.

It is important for us to pray for the Holy Spirit so that He will come into our ministries and convict people of sin, righteousness and judgment. Without the Holy Spirit helping us, our preaching is going to fall on deaf ears. The world will never be converted or changed until the Holy Spirit moves and convicts the people of sin, of righteousness and of judgment.
