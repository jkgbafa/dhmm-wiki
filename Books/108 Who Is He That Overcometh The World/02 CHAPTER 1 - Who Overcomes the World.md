### CHAPTER 1\

Who Overcomes the World?\
==============================================

WHO IS HE THAT OVERCOMETH THE WORLD, but he that believeth that Jesus is the Son of God?

1 John 5:5

The scripture above is very clear that the world is something to be overcome. Why must we overcome the world? What is the world? I thought the world was a place. I thought the world was this earth that God has created. Why would I need to overcome the earth?

You are asking these questions because you do not understand what exactly the world is. Indeed, the world is a place where we live but the world is much more than that. If the world were simply this earth, there would not be much more to overcome than to ensure that we do not drown in the river or the ocean, or to ensure that we do not fall off a tall tree.

In this book, I want to show you that the world is made up of the physical place we live in plus all the other creatures that inhabit this space. The world is actually a combination of the place, the people and the spirits on the earth and that makes up a very evil world indeed.

The scripture declares clearly that the whole world lies in wickedness (1 John 5:19). The world we live in is soaked in evil and this evil environment is what you and I have been born into.

You are never likely to see the devil himself. You are also not likely to see a demon in your lifetime. However, the presence of these evil spirits creates a strong negative and wicked environment on earth. An evil spirit causes the environment and the disposition of a place to change drastically.

Indeed, you are also not likely to meet most of the wicked people like the murderers, mass killers, rapists, paedophiles, serial killers and other wicked monsters that live on the earth. However, their presence on this earth creates an evil aura and also creates a negative pressure against every good thing on the earth.

The combination of the wilderness environment, the evil spirits and the evil men creates the very wicked world we live in today. This wicked world creates a strong pressure against a believer. It is this strong pressure that we must overcome. That is why scripture says, “Who is he that has overcome the world?” Only someone who believes in Jesus can overcome the evil pressures and influences that are present in our world today.

Indeed, this world is not our home! It is a place God has brought us to for a reason.

Let us look at the components of the world we live in. Let us understand the different components of the world that generate so much pressure against believers. As you read this book, you will understand why this world can never be your home.

1\. THE WORLD IS A PHYSICALLY DANGEROUS PLACE.

They that see thee shall narrowly look upon thee, and consider thee, saying, Is this the man that made the earth to tremble, that did shake kingdoms; THAT MADE THE WORLD AS A WILDERNESS, and destroyed the cities thereof; that opened not the house of his prisoners?

Isaiah 14:16-17

The world is a physically dangerous place that is described in the Bible. Isaiah tells us that the beautiful world that God created has been converted into a wilderness. A wilderness is a wild and uninhabited area, left in a state of disfavour and bewildering confusion. The world that God created was a beautiful place.

He hath made every thing beautiful in his time: also he hath set the world in their heart, so that no man can find out the work that God maketh from the beginning to the end.

Ecclesiastes 3:11

And God saw every thing that he had made, and, behold, it was very good. And the evening and the morning were the sixth day.

Genesis 1:31

The beautiful world that God created is no longer as it was when it was first created. Isaiah stated clearly that it had been turned into a wilderness. Our world today is full of many dangers. Many things in this world can end the life of a human being. Wild animals, snakes, wild fires, drought, excessive heat, excessive cold, floods, earthquakes, volcanoes and so on regularly destroy human life. The world we live in is filled with dangerous rivers and oceans that drown men. The world we live in is full of viruses, bacteria and parasites that kill human beings. The world we live in is full of wild animals that kill men all the time. This world has become a dangerous place.

God did not create a world that would attack and eat up its inhabitants. Human beings used to live for as long as a thousand years. Obviously, something has happened to God’s beautiful creation. Isaiah tells us that satan turned it into a wilderness. The world now attacks its inhabitants and largely wipes them out by the age of seventy or eighty.

The world we know replaced another older world that was destroyed. Since this world has been destroyed by the presence of evil spirits and evil men, it will have to be replaced by another world.

For if God spared not the angels that sinned, but cast them down to hell, and delivered them into chains of darkness, to be reserved unto judgment; AND SPARED NOT THE OLD WORLD, BUT SAVED NOAH THE EIGHTH PERSON, a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly;

2 Peter 2:4-5

For this they willingly are ignorant of, that by the word of God the heavens were of old, and the earth standing out of the water and in the water: WHEREBY THE WORLD THAT THEN WAS, BEING OVERFLOWED WITH WATER, PERISHED:

2 Peter 3:5-6

It is important to note that the world that we know as our home is only one of the worlds that exist today. I know you think that we are the centre of the universe. But you can see from the planets and the stars that there is much more out there.

Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, BY WHOM ALSO HE MADE THE WORLDS;

Hebrews 1:2

THROUGH FAITH WE UNDERSTAND THAT THE WORLDS WERE FRAMED BY THE WORD OF GOD, so that things which are seen were not made of things which do appear.

Hebrews 11:3

2\. THE WORLD IS A PLACE FULL OF WICKED PEOPLE.

And we know that we are of God, and the whole world lieth in wickedness.

1 John 5:19

The world is also made up of wicked people. These wicked people have changed this world into a terrible hellhole. I once spoke to a prisoner about his experience in prison. He described a terrible time when he was in a room with eight other men, some of whom were murderers.

He said violent fights would break out at any time and anyone could get hurt. He lived for three years in a room with these convicted murderers. He lived in fear and fright every day of his life whilst in prison. Being in the company of violent men, murderers, rapists, paedophiles and serial killers is not an easy thing. You would be under great pressure in the prison because violence and murder could occur at any time.

Indeed, it is the company you have in a place that either makes it enjoyable or horrible. Dear friend, the company you have with you at any time makes all the difference. In this world, you are forced to live in the company of wicked people. Let us have a look at the list of people who create the evil pressure in the world.

a\. A world of evil people.

Who gave himself for our sins, that he might deliver us from THIS PRESENT EVIL WORLD, according to the will of God and our Father:

Galatians 1:4

b\. A world of sinful people.

And he is the propitiation for our sins: and not for ours only, but also for THE SINS OF THE WHOLE WORLD.

1 John 2:2

c\. A world of lustful people.

For ALL THAT IS IN THE WORLD, THE LUST of the flesh, and THE LUST of the eyes, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world.

1 John 2:16

d\. A world of anti-Christ people.

Hereby know ye the Spirit of God: Every spirit that confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God:

And every spirit that confesseth not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is not of God: and THIS IS THAT SPIRIT OF ANTICHRIST, WHEREOF YE HAVE HEARD THAT IT SHOULD COME; AND EVEN NOW ALREADY IS IT IN THE WORLD.

1 John 4:2-3

The people of this world are anti-church, anti-God, anti-pastors, anti-man of God and anti-Christ. Never think that the world loves you. Never think that the world will ever like you. The world puts up with you because it cannot eliminate you totally. Through a series of hidden underground moves, you will sooner or later feel the animosity, hatred and antagonism that exist against God and the people of God.

e\. A world of liars.

For MANY DECEIVERS ARE ENTERED INTO THE WORLD, who confess not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh. This is a deceiver and an antichrist.

2 John 1:7

The people in this world have many deceivers amongst them. One of the biggest characteristics of people in this world is lying. Many people in the world are simply liars. Lying is probably the commonest thing in the world. When God wanted to differentiate Himself from men, He simply said that He was not a liar (Numbers 23:19). The human race is identified by its lying and deception.

f\. A world of corrupt men.

Whereby are given unto us exceeding great and precious promises: that by these ye might be partakers of the divine nature, having escaped THE CORRUPTION THAT IS IN THE WORLD through lust.

2 Peter 1:4

The people in this world are spoilt, destroyed and corrupt. It is not possible to reverse corruption. You can only slow it down. That is why the world is getting worse with every passing day. The corruption in this world spreads and affects believers. It is this corruption that puts pressure on anyone living on earth to also be corrupt.

3\. THE WORLD IS A PLACE FULL OF EVIL SPIRITS.

And he cried mightily with a strong voice, saying, Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen, and is become the habitation of devils, and the hold of every foul spirit, and a cage of every unclean and hateful bird.

Revelation 18:2

The world is a place full of evil spirits. This world, just like Babylon, is totally destroyed because of the presence of all these evil spirits.

These evil spirits have changed this world into a terrible place of sin and evil. The beautiful world that God created as a home and a paradise for men has been turned into the habitation of devils.

Babylon is one of the cities in this world. Notice how it is described as a fallen, destroyed and desolate place because it was inhabited by evil spirits. Notice how these evil spirits have completely changed the atmosphere in this world.

a\. The prince of the power of the air: This evil spirit controls the airwaves and atmosphere.

Wherein in time past ye walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that now worketh in the children of disobedience:

Ephesians 2:2

b\. The god of this world: This evil spirit controls the trends and circumstances in our world.

In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not, lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them.

2 Corinthians 4:4

c\. The great dragon: This evil spirit establishes the deceptions in this world.

And the great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan, which deceiveth the whole world: he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him.

Revelation 12:9

d\. The spirit of the world: This evil spirit controls the people in the world. The people of the world are influenced by the spirit of the world and the people of God are influenced by the Spirit of God!

Now we have received, not THE SPIRIT OF THE WORLD, but the spirit which is of God; that we might know the things that are freely given to us of God.

1 Corinthians 2:12

4\. THE WORLD IS THE CURRENT EVIL SYSTEM.

In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not, lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them.

2 Corinthians 4:4

The world is an evil system created by the presence of evil men and evil spirits. It is governed by evil values and beliefs. All Christians are under pressure, once they are in this evil world.

A combination of a fallen and desolate creation, plus the presence of millions of evil spirits, plus the presence of billions of wicked men creates a terrible evil system that is called the “world”. Welcome to the world that will present itself as your principal test and challenge as long as you are alive on earth.

All through the Bible, you will find exhortations and instructions to be careful of this world. You will find warnings not to love the world, not to befriend the world and not to be conformed to this evil world.
