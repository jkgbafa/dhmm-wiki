### CHAPTER 11\

Overcome the Pressure of Materialism in this World\
========================================================================

And he said unto them, Take heed, and beware of covetousness: for a man’s life consisteth not in the abundance of the things which he possesseth.

Luke 12:15

Jesus Christ spoke directly against materialism. Materialism is an evil spirit that comes from the world. Materialism is a pervading influence that has affected the church of Jesus Christ so much that it has quenched the eternity consciousness that is needed to drive us into the Great Commission.

Materialism is an attitude that attaches too much importance to acquiring things. It is an attitude that is in the world, in which your social status is determined by your possessions.

According to the materialistic spirit of the world, your wealth determines your importance and your rank. Materialism is in direct conflict with the values espoused by Jesus Christ. Materialism tends to impress upon us that possessions can provide happiness. But Jesus Christ told us that a man’s life does not consist in the abundance of things that he possesses.

There was a time when it was thought that the Great Commission was a thing of the past. That was the reason why the church did not embark on the Great Commission to all nations. Today, no one is standing up and saying, “Do not go into all the world and preach the gospel to every nation.”

Today, the reason why the church does not embark on the Great Commission to all nations is the spirit of materialism. Instead of taking up our crosses and setting aside earthly ambitions, men are filled with the unspoken vision of acquiring as much wealth and earthly possessions as possible. This vision has effectively quenched the Great Commission and the all-important work of church planting and evangelism. It is important to wipe out this evil spirit of materialism that wants to come from the world into your life.

The Four Keys to Quench Worldly Materialism

1\. You must plan to carry nothing out of this world.

For we brought nothing into this world, and it is certain we can carry nothing out.

1 Timothy 6:7

It must be your vision to carry nothing out of this world. You must work towards this vision, rather than working in the opposite direction of trying to acquire so many things to take out of this world.

You cannot carry anything out of this world any way. You must not live your life as though you will be able to exit this world with several suitcases of possessions.

When people travel to foreign countries, they often buy many things and pack them into suitcases to carry back to their homes. They do this because they know they can carry these suitcases with them on the plane. They can even take extra suitcases with them if they want to.

Unfortunately, our journey out of this world will not be like that. There will be no suitcases. There will not even be hand luggage! You cannot take your land documents and properties with you to heaven. You cannot take any of your clothes. You cannot take any of your jewellery. You cannot take diamonds to heaven.

In fact, you cannot even take your phone with you. The clothes you wear in your coffin may be specially bought for the occasion whilst the many clothes you really own will be back home.

If you plan to take nothing out of this world, you will quench the spirit of materialism. You will not equate having many possessions with success or happiness.

When the spirit of materialism is killed, you will be free to obey the word of God and follow God’s instruction of preaching the gospel to the ends of the world.

2\. You must recognise the poor people of this world.

Hearken, my beloved brethren, HATH NOT GOD CHOSEN THE POOR OF THIS WORLD RICH IN FAITH, and heirs of the kingdom which he hath promised to them that love him?

James 2:5

The poor of the world must be the group you love most because God has chosen the poor of this world to be rich in faith. When you are attracted to the poor, you are attracted to those whom God is also attracted to. God wants to do many things for the poor. God wants to save the poor and heal the poor of this world. “The Spirit of the Lord is upon me because he has anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor” (Luke 4:18).

3\. You must not follow the lust of the eyes.

For all that is in the world, the lust of the flesh, and the LUST OF THE EYES, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world.

1 John 2:16

The lust of the eyes is one of the big problems of our world. Human beings follow the things they see. People are impressed with what they see even when they know there is something evil about it.

Decide not to walk by sight and you will overcome the spirit of materialism. Do not trust things because they look nice. If you can overcome the lust of the eyes, you will do well as a Christian. If you follow things that are nice to the eyes, you are worldly. A worldly person only has his eyes to go by. A spiritual person does not go by what is nice to the eye.

Moses was successful and faithful to God because he did not follow the lust of the eyes. Moses was moved by the invisible. He was not an ordinary person whose eyes and desires were fixed on things he could see in the natural.

By faith he forsook Egypt, not fearing the wrath of the king: for he endured, as seeing him who is invisible.

Hebrews 11:27

When you overcome the tendency to be moved by the lust of your eyes, you will become a mighty pillar in the house of God.

4\. Do not follow the pride of life.

For all that is in the world, the lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and THE PRIDE OF LIFE, is not of the Father, but is of the world.

1 John 2:16

The Bible teaches us, “Mind not high things” (Romans 12:16). When Christians are conscious of high things, high cars, high houses, high wealth, they neglect the poor of the world. Their mind is on high things.

If you mind high things your mind cannot be on the harvest field because the harvest field is made up of poor people. The harvest fields that are white are made up of dangerous places filled with poor, neglected and suffering people.

Say not ye, There are yet four months, and then cometh harvest? behold, I say unto you, Lift up your eyes, and look on the fields; for they are white already to harvest.

John 4:35

Such people are not the high and mighty of our world. Materialism and wealth-consciousness kills the Great Commission. The great harvest fields of our world are not tourist cities. The great harvest fields of our world are not places that affluent visitors flock to. The great harvest fields of our world are poor, simple towns and villages, like the ones Jesus visited when He was on the earth.

The Great Commission is to the poor of this world. God has chosen the poor of this world to be rich in faith. If your eye is on high things, you will never see the poor people in the harvest fields.
