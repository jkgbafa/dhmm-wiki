### CHAPTER 2\

If This World is Not My Home, What Is It?\
===============================================================

As thou hast sent me into the world, even SO HAVE I ALSO SENT THEM INTO THE WORLD. AND FOR THEIR SAKES I SANCTIFY MYSELF, THAT THEY ALSO MIGHT BE SANCTIFIED through the truth. Neither pray I for these alone, but for them also which shall believe on me through their word; That they all may be one; as thou, Father, art in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us: that the world may believe that thou hast sent me.

John 17:18-21

We have been sent into this dangerous world to preach the gospel and to win the lost for Jesus Christ. Jesus was sent on this dangerous mission into the world and He lost His life by the age of thirty-three. We have also been sent on a “danger mission” and into this dangerous world. Welcome to your “danger mission”!

1\. This world is not your home; it is your harvest field.

THE FIELD IS THE WORLD; the good seed are the children of the kingdom; but the tares are the children of the wicked one;

Matthew 13:38

This fact, that the world is not your home, must sink deep into your heart. If this world is not your home, then what is it? This world is your harvest field. Christians must see the world as what it is. It is the harvest field that God has placed before you. No one tries to settle down in a harvest field and build a house in the midst of the corn and the wheat.

A harvest field is a work place. It is not a place of rest. Nor is it a place of comfort. It is a place of great labour and toil. Get used to the idea that this world is not your home. Get used to the idea that you are living in the midst of your harvest field. Your work is staring at you in the face.

2\. This world is not your home; it is your school.

For the invisible things of him FROM THE CREATION OF THE WORLD ARE CLEARLY SEEN, being understood by the things that are made, even his eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse:

Romans 1:20

Doth not even NATURE ITSELF TEACH YOU, that, if a man have long hair, it is a shame unto him?

1 Corinthians 11:14

If this world is not your home, then what is it? This world is a school for you. This world is a place you have come to learn about God. The invisible things of God are understood from the creation of the world. The animals in this world, the plants in this world, the rivers, the seas, the oceans, the mountains and the lakes are all revelations showing the nature of God and His awesomeness. Nature is one of the greatest teachers.

There is no other planet that exists and reveals the greatness of God as much as the earth does.

3\. This world is not your home; it is your training and testing ground.

And thou shalt remember all the way which the LORD thy God led thee these forty years in the wilderness, TO HUMBLE THEE, AND TO PROVE THEE, to know what was in thine heart, whether thou wouldest keep his commandments, or no.

Deuteronomy 8:2

The experiences you have in this world are to train you in many things. Notice how the Lord took the children of Israel through the wilderness for forty years. Forty years in the wilderness was intended to humble and test the children of Israel to see what they were made of and whether they would obey God or not. Every experience you go through is educative. If you have a teachable spirit, you will learn many things from the experiences you have.

For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth. If ye endure chastening, God dealeth with you as with sons; for what son is he whom the father chasteneth not?

Hebrews 12:6-7

The word, “chasten” means to train, to educate and to discipline. In this world, God will chasten you and scourge you like a son. To chasten also means to punish and instruct you. Many experiences you will have in this world are the chastening of the Lord. He is training you, equipping you, educating you and correcting you. God is chastening you whilst you are in this world so that you become a better person.

4\. This world is not your home; it is a place to prove that you are an overcomer.

There is a special place for overcomers. There are special rewards for overcomers. There are crowns for overcomers. Those who overcome great challenges, great temptations and great hardships and prevail to bear much fruit for the Lord must know that their works are being taken note of.

In heaven, there will be a special section for overcomers. An overcomer is someone who does not make excuses. An overcomer is someone who goes through every barrier, jumps over every hurdle, crosses every mountain, swims across every river and prevails to overturn the plans of the enemy.

All through the book of Revelation, you will notice the phrase, “he that overcometh”.

He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches; TO HIM THAT OVERCOMETH will I give to eat of the tree of life, which is in the midst of the paradise of God.

Revelation 2:7

He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches; HE THAT OVERCOMETH shall not be hurt of the second death.

Revelation 2:11

He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches; TO HIM THAT OVERCOMETH will I give to eat of the hidden manna, and will give him a white stone, and in the stone a new name written, which no man knoweth saving he that receiveth it.

Revelation 2:17

And HE THAT OVERCOMETH, and keepeth my works unto the end, to him will I give power over the nations:

Revelation 2:26

5\. This world is not your home; it is a dangerous wilderness packed with evil spirits and wicked men that you have to overcome.

You must expect to see a lot of wickedness and evil in this world. This world is not your home; it is a wicked place that you must overcome. There is too much evil in the world for it to be a home. There is a lot of wickedness that you will encounter that you must overcome.

And we know that we are of God, and the whole world lieth in wickedness.

1 John 5:19

A young person must overcome in this world. Young people are molested, abused and baptised into abominations before they know what is happening to them. The world is full of cruelty, evil, sin, perversions and wickedness. Do not take for granted the kind of place you are in! It can never be your home! It was never intended to be your home! It is a place to prove your ability to overcome!

That made the world as a wilderness, and destroyed the cities thereof; that opened not the house of his prisoners?

Isaiah 14:17

6\. This world is not your home; it is your stage.

YE ARE THE LIGHT OF THE WORLD. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid.

Matthew 5:14

That ye may be blameless and harmless, the sons of God, without rebuke, in the midst of a crooked and perverse nation, among whom ye shine as LIGHTS IN THE WORLD;

Philippians 2:15

What happens on stage? That is where you give your best performance. That is where you shine like a star. The stage is where you make yourself known to the audience.

This world is the stage where you will make yourself known to the spirit world and to the eternal world. All of heaven and all of hell will know who you are by the time you finish your performance on the world stage.

You are supposed to shine brightly when you get the chance. This is your chance! You are supposed to shine as lights in the world. Do not attempt to make this world your home. Make this world the stage from where you will shine and throw forth a great light to the world.

Do not fall asleep on the stage. Do not try to make a tent on the stage. Do not try to build a little house on the stage. Just perform your duties and shine for the Lord. You are the light of the world. Your country, your nation, your city, your town, your village is the stage from where you will shine for all men to see your light.

When your time is up you will regret not giving it your best shot. You will regret spending your performing time doing other things. You will regret not acting your best and giving your best performance. Arise and give your best performance of ministry work! Arise and give your best performance of church planting! Arise and give your best performance of missionary work! Arise and give your best performance of evangelism!

This world is the place to shine as a worthy servant, faithful to your mission and to your marching orders. The marching orders are clear: “Go into the world and preach the gospel!” For all eternity, you will be judged, assessed, marked, rewarded or rejected based on these words of Jesus. This world is not a home for you to rest and receive your comforts. This is a place to prove yourself worthy of eternal crowns.

7\. This world is not your home because it cannot compare with what God has planned for you.

(OF WHOM THE WORLD WAS NOT WORTHY:) they wandered in deserts, and in mountains, and in dens and caves of the earth.

Hebrews 11:38

This world is greatly inferior to what God has planned for men of faith. In Hebrews, in the great chapter of faith in the Bible, we learn that the faith heroes wandered around in this world, unable to settle down because the world was not worthy of them. Faith people wandered in deserts, in mountains, in dens and caves.

This world is not worthy of your presence. If you are a man of faith, this world is below you! Focus on eternity and the great mansions God has in store for you in eternity.

I once saw a little house that had been built to house the grave of a politician. They were not content to simply mark the grave with a stone. They wanted to mark the grave with a house.

It was a cute little house, about seven metres by ten metres with a special Chinese security door. I marvelled at this little house which human beings had built to house the body of an important man in his village. Indeed, I am sure that God has a better house and mansion for you up in heaven.
