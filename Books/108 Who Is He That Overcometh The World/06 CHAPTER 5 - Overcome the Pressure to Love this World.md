### CHAPTER 5\

Overcome the Pressure to Love this World\
==============================================================

LOVE NOT THE WORLD, NEITHER THE THINGS THAT ARE IN THE WORLD. If any man love the world, the love of the Father is not in him. For all that is in the world, the lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world.

1 John 2:15-16

Do not love the world, my brother! Loving this world is a dangerous spiritual mistake. Demas made this terrible mistake and abandoned the ministry.

For Demas hath forsaken me, HAVING LOVED THIS PRESENT WORLD, and is departed unto Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia, Titus unto Dalmatia.

2 Timothy 4:10

Why is it so wrong for a Christian fall in love with the world? It is wrong because the world you are in is your mission field. You have been sent into the world as an ambassador of Christ. Imagine if the American Ambassador who is sent to China falls in love with China and after four years adopts all the ideas and philosophies of China that are against America! What about if this American Ambassador becomes more Chinese than he is American? What about if he falls in love with Chinese food, Chinese culture, Chinese language, Chinese ways and Chinese politics?

What about if he begins to dislike the American language, the American culture, the American food and America’s democratic political system? Would he be a good ambassador for the United States? Certainly not!

Such an ambassador would be a failed missionary because he has fallen in love with the Chinese world that he was sent to help. Although it sounds absurd, that is exactly what Christians do today. Jesus has sent them into the world and they have fallen in love with the world! Remember Jesus said:

“ Then said Jesus to them again, Peace be unto you: as my Father hath sent me, EVEN SO SEND I YOU.

John 20:21

How unfortunate it is for Jesus to send us into the world and for us to fall in love with the world that He sent us to save and to preach to! Instead of winning the lost and having compassion for the souls that do not know about God, we have decided to buy shops, invest in farms, buy taxis and settle down in this world forever.

What does it look like when you have forgotten completely about the country that sent you? What is it like when you have been converted by the country that you were sent to convert! That is a tragedy of tragedies!

This is what has happened to the church. Do not love the world! The world is not a place to love! The world is a place to save and have compassion for! The world is not a place to admire! The world is a group of people you must pity and help to be saved!

Signs that You Have Fallen in Love with the World

You can tell whether you are in love with the world or not by looking at the signs that you have when you fall in love with someone. Let us look at the signs that you are in love with someone.

1. When you are in love with the world, you cannot stop staring at the things that are in the world and admiring them! When you are in love with a boy, you cannot stop staring at him and admiring him. You admire the riches, the silver the gold, the cars, aeroplanes and houses of this world and stare at them longingly.

2. When you are in love with the world, you abandon your spiritual activities and follow the world to engage in worldly activities! When you are in love with a girl, you abandon your usual activities and follow her around.

Instead of going to church, you attend debating club meetings. Instead of going to church, you attend International Students meetings. Instead of attending prayer meetings, you attend Old Boys’ Association meetings. Instead of going to church, you go for lunches, breakfasts and dinners.

3. When you are in love with the world, you do not mind when the world does things that are obviously wrong! When you are in love with someone, you do not mind when he does something that is obviously wrong.

If you love the world, you do not mind when the world does things that are obviously wrong. When the world is accepting sexual perversions, you will not find anything wrong with it. You love the clothes that are too short and simply unacceptable. You consider the worldly clothes as decent clothing. You love the ways of the world!

4. When you are in love with the world, you accept everything that the world has to offer! When you are in love with a boy, you are accepting of everything about him. When you are in love with somebody, the person can do no wrong.

When you are in love with the world, you swallow all the nonsense and deception of the world and you find it acceptable. When you are in love with the world, the ways of the world are always right to you!

5. When you are in love with the world, you always think about the world and what you can achieve with the world! Instead of thinking of what you can achieve for God, you are thinking about what worldly achievements you can attain to. You are always thinking of getting more degrees rather than becoming a shepherd or a pastor. You are thinking of getting a better job in the world rather than how you can be a better shepherd.

6. When you are in love with the world, you want to be accepted in the world, to please the world and to make the world happy! When you are in love with a girl, you always think about her and you want to make her happy!

Your desire to be accepted by the world is a sign that you are in love with the world. Your aversion to being the odd-one-out in the world is a sign that you are in love with the world.
