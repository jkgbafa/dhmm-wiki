### CHAPTER 10\

Three Secrets for Living in this World\
============================================================

I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me: and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and gave himself for me.

Galatians 2:20

1\. ASSOCIATION WITHOUT CONTAMINATION:

I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil. THEY ARE NOT OF THE WORLD, EVEN AS I AM NOT OF THE WORLD.

John 17:15-16

Associate without getting contaminated! We have no choice! We have to live in this world and yet not be of the world. There will always be a problem of how to associate with the world without being contaminated by the world. You must learn to stay in this world and yet not be contaminated or stained by the world. Ask the Lord to show you the secret of associating with the world without being contaminated. It takes a delicate, careful balance to associate with the world without contamination.

2\. LIVING HERE, SEEKING THERE:

For all these things do the nations of THE WORLD SEEK AFTER: and your Father knoweth that ye have need of these things. BUT RATHER SEEK YE THE KINGDOM of God; and all these things shall be added unto you.

Luke 12:30-31

Live here and seek there! You will always have a challenge of living in this world and yet seeking your welfare in another world. Even though you live in this world, you are admonished to seek for your welfare in another kingdom. It is like living in Ghana and seeking your welfare in Seoul, Korea.

How to live your life down here on earth without trying to establish a home and to settle down in great prosperity will always be a challenge. “Living here and seeking there” is a challenge for all believers who are in this world. God is giving you the grace to live here and yet to seek there. It is a bit like people living in London who do not build houses in the UK and yet seek and build houses in India because that is where they are originally from.

3\. CRUCIFY THE WORLD:

But God forbid that I should glory, save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom THE WORLD IS CRUCIFIED UNTO ME, and I unto the world.

Galatians 6:14

To crucify the world speaks of bringing a painful, permanent conclusion to your relationship with the world. It is important that you crucify the world to yourself. When Jesus was crucified, they put a painful and permanent end to His presence in Jerusalem. Jesus Christ was cut off from Israel and from this world when He was crucified outside the gates of Jerusalem.

What a permanent and utter separation came between Jesus Christ and this world! His beautiful voice was heard no more. His wonderful sermons were heard no more. His healing touch was felt no more. He was permanently and utterly separated violently from the world.

Today, that is what is expected of you. You are expected to permanently and utterly separate yourself violently from the world. The world will never have access to you, nor will it influence you any longer.

Today, there are very few things that are happening in the world that are attractive to me. The world is crucified to me. I do not like most of the things in this world. God has given me the grace to permanently cut myself off from the world.

All that you need to do is to bring a cataclysmic conclusion to the relationship between you and worldly men, worldly desires, worldly aspirations and worldly achievements. You will not achieve great things for the kingdom, until the world is crucified to you.
