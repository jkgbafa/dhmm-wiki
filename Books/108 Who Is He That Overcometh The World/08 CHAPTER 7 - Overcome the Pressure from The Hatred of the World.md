### CHAPTER 7\

Overcome the Pressure from The Hatred of the World\
========================================================================

I HAVE GIVEN THEM THY WORD; AND THE WORLD HATH HATED THEM, because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.

John 17:14

It is not a nice thing to be hated by someone. Human beings often do whatever they can to make people like them, rather than hate them. It is better to be far away from someone who hates you. Hatred leads to bitter mistreatment, elimination, intense dislike, harm and even murder.

You will always have a problem of being hated by the world. If you have not yet felt the hatred of this world, you are new to this battle. When you see the world attacking your fellow pastor, you may think he is being attacked because he has committed crimes or sins. You will discover with time that attacks from the world have nothing to do with whether you are good or bad. No matter how clean or innocent you are, the hatred of the world will be manifested against those who believe. The world hates Christians and the world hates the church.

If you were of the world, the world would love its own; but because you are not of the world, but I chose you out of the world, BECAUSE OF THIS THE WORLD HATES YOU.

John 15:19 (NASB)

Signs that the World Hates You

1\. Sign of hatred: non-recognition by the world.

Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God: therefore THE WORLD KNOWETH US NOT, because it knew him not.

1 John 3:1

You will always have a problem with non-recognition by the world. The world does not recognize Christians. That is why many governments do not recognize the church and its input unless it is politically helpful to do so. The people of this world do not see the importance of the church nor of Christians.

When someone hates you, his body language often reveals it. The body language of the world reveals their hatred for us. When someone hates you, he avoids eye contact with you. The world avoids eye contact with us!

The world does not want to know us! The world dislikes us! The world does not know or understand what we believe in. The world has a negative posture towards the church. Jesus said, “The world knoweth us not.” They do not know who we are and they do not recognize us.

2\. Sign of hatred: envy.

For we ourselves also were sometimes foolish, disobedient, deceived, serving divers lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, and hating one another.

Titus 3:3

Envy is a feeling of discontentment at someone else’s success, advantage, advancement or happiness. Envy is a manifestation of hatred and ill-will. The world hates believers and so you can expect a feeling of discontentment and ill-will when the church seems to be advancing or successful. The world has a feeling of ill-will towards us when they see that we are happy and serving the Lord. That will always be the case and we must not expect the world to love us.

3\. Sign of hatred: humiliation and mistreatment by another person.

Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer: and ye know that no murderer hath eternal life abiding in him.

1 John 3:15

You can expect the world to humiliate and mistreat you if they can. When the world had a chance to touch our Saviour, the entire city of Jerusalem rose up to humiliate and mistreat the One who had preached amongst them, healed their sick and ministered love to them. The open and spontaneous hatred of the world against Jesus Christ when they had the opportunity is a revelation for all ministers of the gospel.
