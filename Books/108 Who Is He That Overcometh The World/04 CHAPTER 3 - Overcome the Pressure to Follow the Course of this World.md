### CHAPTER 3\

Overcome the Pressure to Follow the Course of this World\
==============================================================================

And you hath he quickened, who were dead in trespasses and sins;

WHEREIN IN TIME PAST YE WALKED ACCORDING TO THE COURSE OF THIS WORLD, according to the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that now worketh in the children of disobedience:

Among whom also we all had our conversation in times past in the lusts of our flesh, fulfilling the desires of the flesh and of the mind; and were by nature the children of wrath, even as others.

Ephesians 2:1-3

It is important to recognize that the world is on a course that is dictated by the devil. The whole world, full of evil men and evil spirits, are rushing down a path laid out for them by satan.

“The course of this world” is the way in which the world goes. The world has a way of going about everything. The worldly course is the way the world goes about achieving things. The worldly way is different from the Christian way. The worldly way is dictated by the prince of the power of the air. These worldly methods of doing things come about because the world’s ways are dictated by the god of this world.

1\. There is a worldly way to get love and happiness.

There is a worldly progression of relationships. The course of relationships in the world goes down a particular path of deception, immorality and licentiousness. The world only understands this progression of events. Whilst you are living in the world, you will come under pressure to follow the course of the world and live your life with multiple wrong relationships, constant deception, immorality and licentiousness.

2\. There is a worldly progression of marriage.

The course of this world towards marriage begins with premarital sex and a full “knowledge” of one another. In the world, people get married to please the society. In the world, people have hundreds of relationships and eventually settle down with one. Most marriages that follow the course of the world follow the course of deception, unfaithfulness, and adultery with prolonged side relationships. It is important that you do everything to escape the course of this world. The course of this world is determined by the prince of the power of the air. The prince of the power of the air dictates this course of events by filling the minds of worldly men and women with secular goals that inspire this lifestyle.

3\. There is a worldly way to achieve success.

There is a worldly course to promotion. All worldly achievements are brought about by following the course of this world. The course of this world involves deception, self-aggrandizement, self-enhancement and self-promotion. Vaunting yourself in pride is the course of this world and leads to the destruction that satan so desires to happen to you.

Jesus’ method for you to be successful takes a completely different course. Jesus says, humble yourself, go lower, bow down and He will exalt you. But that is not the way of the world. The world wants you to exalt yourself, promote yourself and fight for yourself. Will you follow the dictates of the prince of the power of the air or will you follow the dictates of the Holy Spirit?

4\. There is a worldly way to overcome problems.

Jesus answered, My kingdom is not of this world: IF MY KINGDOM WERE OF THIS WORLD, THEN WOULD MY SERVANTS FIGHT, that I should not be delivered to the Jews: but now is my kingdom not from hence.

John 18:36

There is a worldly way of fighting your battles. There is a worldly way of overcoming and overpowering your enemy. The worldly way is inspired by the flesh and demon activity. In the scripture below, we see the godly way of fighting back. The worldly way to fight is to avenge yourself. The godly way to fight is to leave things to God to exact vengeance on your behalf.

Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath: for it is written, Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord. Therefore if thine enemy hunger, feed him; if he thirst, give him drink: for in so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire on his head. Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good.

Romans 12:19-21

Notice that the worldly way of thinking does not match God’s way of thinking. The foolishness of God is wiser than the wisdom of this world.

For THE WISDOM OF THIS WORLD is foolishness with God. For it is written, He taketh the wise in their own craftiness.

1 Corinthians 3:19

But THE WISDOM THAT IS FROM ABOVE is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.

James 3:17

The people of this world are led by the devil on a particular course. This is called the “course of the world”. Many people go along without even knowing that they are on a well-mapped out course that generations have followed mindlessly. They are walking according to the course of this world that is laid out by the prince of the power of the air.
