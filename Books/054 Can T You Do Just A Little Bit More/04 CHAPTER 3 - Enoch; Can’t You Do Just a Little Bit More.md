### CHAPTER 3\

Enoch; Can’t You Do Just a Little Bit More?\
-----------------------------------------------------------------

**By faith Enoch was translated that he should not see death; and was not found, because God had translated him: for before his translation he had this testimony, that he pleased God.**

**Hebrews 11:5**

I believe that Enoch is an example of someone who did a little bit more. Enoch had this testimony that he pleased God.  To please someone you always have to do a little bit more.  Many relationships break down because people are not prepared to do a little bit more. 

When a relationship between a boy and a girl is at its max, both of them are doing a little bit more.  You will find prompt responses to text messages when the relationship is really hot. When the relationship is dying or dead, you will find several hours or sometimes days between the time a text message is received and the time the answer comes. When a relationship is really hot, people are always checking their phones to see if a message has come from the important lover.  When things are dead, they will say things like, “My phone was charging! I left my phone in the office! My phone was on silent!”  When the relationship is dying, a little bit more effort is no more exerted to keep the relationship alive and pleasing.   

Enoch pleased God!  The key to pleasing God is to please Him in big things and in little things.  

Indeed, the key to pleasing people is in doing little things as well as big things.  Good relationships come by doing both little things and big things.  Once I was waiting on God and I remember listening to a message by Derek Prince.  He spoke about when God called him. He said God told him, “You must please me in little things and also in big things.”  Those words went through my heart.  I realised how important it was to please God in little things and also in big things.  I began to search my life to see the little things and the big things that God had asked me to do.  

Indeed, pleasing anyone will always involve little things and big things. You may do big things but if you leave out the little things, you will not please the person you are trying to please. If you want to please God, you will have to obey Him in big things as well as little things. 

For Enoch to have pleased God, he must have done the big things as well as the little things.  Marriages break down because people do not believe the little things that their partners warn them about.  Some men may think the big things are to provide school fees and pay the bills. 

However, the small things like saying, “I love you” or “Happy birthday” may be equally important to someone.  Being nice and caring may be a small but very important thing. 

Some ladies may think the big things are to provide breakfast and dinner.  However, the little things of smiling and being cheerful may be just as important as cooking dinner.  Being clean or having a bath may seem like a small thing to you.   Having sex and being excited about it may seem a small thing to you.  Yet, it is both the big things and the little things that make a person pleasing. God’s call to please Him is to serve Him in little things as well as in big things.  

To be pleasing to someone will always require just a little bit more than being in your natural state.  

I do not believe that Enoch lived a natural life. His whole life was oriented towards pleasing God.  Never forget this truth: to serve God properly is to serve Him in little things as well as in big things. He that is faithful in little is also faithful in much. When you please God in little things you also please Him in much.
