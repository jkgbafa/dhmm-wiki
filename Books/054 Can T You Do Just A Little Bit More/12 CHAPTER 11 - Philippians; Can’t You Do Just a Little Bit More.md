### CHAPTER 11\

Philippians; Can’t You Do Just a Little Bit More?\
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

**For even in Thessalonica YE SENT ONCE AND AGAIN unto my necessity. Not because I desire a gift: but I desire fruit that may abound to your account. But I have all, and abound: I am full, having received of Epaphroditus the things which were sent from you, an odour of a sweet smell, a sacrifice acceptable, wellpleasing to God. But my God shall supply all your need according to his riches in glory by Christ Jesus.**

**Philippians 4:16-19**

The Philippians are well known for giving a little bit more than others. They sent money to apostle Paul more than once.   The Philippians did a little bit more for Apostle Paul. They gave “once and again”. 

“Again “ was the little bit more.  This little bit more earned them the famous blessing of “My God shall supply all your needs.”  

There are many people who can give a little bit more than they do.  They just throw crumbs into the offering bowl.  But there is much more that they could do if they really wanted to help the work of God.  

There are people who can give a thousand dollars.  However, many of these people are not prepared to lift a finger and do a little bit more.  They just reach out and pick the nearest coin in their wallets.  

Many people are prepared to do things for themselves but when it comes to God’s work they do not want to do a little bit more. They cannot remember to pay a pledge. They do not even bother to try to do anything for God. They cannot lift a finger or exert any effort towards God’s work.  

It is important that you decide to give a little bit more than you are giving.  One day, whilst preparing to give an offering to a man of God, I felt the Holy Spirit whisper to my heart; “Can’t you do just a little bit more? Give this much.” He said to me.  “Never give less than this amount when you are giving to my servants!”  

From that time, I began to give a little bit more to God’s servants.  Shortly after that, I noticed a change in the way people honoured me.  Everyone who honoured me seemed to do a little bit more.  

One day, I visited a church and preached powerfully. It was a very large church with a blessed pastor.  When I finished preaching, they blessed me with “a little bit less” than they had blessed me with before.  The honour was a little bit less.  

Indeed, whilst I was preaching, I had felt that I was not so welcome any more. “A little bit less” offering was all that was needed to confirm that I was not really wanted.  Previously, the pastor was happy to do a little bit more but now his attitude had changed.  Ensuing events confirmed all that I had thought.  I was right when I interpreted that a little bit less meant I was a little bit less welcome.  

You cannot say that your offering means nothing.  A little bit more always means something and a little bit less also means something.   

It is time to honour your pastors with a little bit more. It is time to honour your church with a little bit more. It is time to honour God’s work with a little bit more of your money. A little bit more means a little bit more love.  

When you give a little bit more, you become like the Philippians and you will receive “a little bit more” blessings from God.  Blessings such as “My God shall supply all your needs according to His riches in glory in Christ Jesus.”
