### CHAPTER 15\

Thyatira; Can’t You Do Just a Little Bit More?\
--------------------------------------------------------------------

**And unto the angel of the church in Thyatira write; These things saith the Son of God, who hath his eyes like unto a flame of fire, and his feet are like fine brass; I KNOW THY WORKS, and charity, and service, and faith, and thy patience, and thy works; and the last to be more than the first. Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because THOU SUFFEREST THAT WOMAN JEZEBEL, WHICH CALLETH HERSELF A PROPHETESS, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols.**

**Revelation 2:18-20**

The Thyatira church was a great church.  It was a church of great works! It was a church of great charity! It was a church of great faith!  It was a church of great service! And it was a church of great patience!  

But God wanted a little bit more.  What was the little bit more that He wanted? He wanted them to remove a woman called Jezebel who was seducing God’s servants.  It was nice of the Thyatira church to do all these great works.  Everyone was blessed by the Thyatira church. 

But a little bit more was required of them. Get rid of Jezebel!  Fight that woman no matter the rank or title that she holds.  You see, nothing can replace the act of removing certain people from God’s house.  Many people do not realize that part of our calling is to fight.  Part of fighting is to have enemies.  In this case, the enemy was no other than a Jezebel. This woman was causing people to fall from the high and lofty ranks of ministry into the low level of sin.  

One day, a young lady entered our church to seduce one of the young pastors. This girl had previously been involved with several other ministers, causing their downfall in the ministry. Before we realised, one of the inexperienced pastors had fallen into the hands of this lady. The pastoral team responded quickly. I watched as the pastoral team met with the young girl and removed her permanently from the church.  I watched as they kept a sharp lookout in case she ever returned.  You see, Jezebels who destroy and seduce people must not be allowed to continue practicing their deeds.  There is something you can do about it.  

Jezebel was also a prophetess. In other words, she was in the ministry.  The Jezebel of the Old Testament was the wife of Ahab. The spirit of Jezebel often occupies both female ministers (Jezebel the prophetess) and wives of powerful ministers (like the wife of King Ahab).  

Many times, ministers do not want to confront a fellow minister’s wife.  They want to remain pleasing to all parties. They do not want to offend anyone. They do not want to take sides. They want to remain in the good books of the Jezebel wife.   

John Wesley’s wife may qualify as a well-known difficult wife.  One of her problems was that she felt the Methodist ministers did not like her.  The Methodist ministers did not like the way she treated their founder and leader and I am sure it was pointed out to her.  It is good that the Methodist ministers showed their disapproval of her.  Most of the time, difficult women are left to misbehave and torment their husbands. People simply do not like to deal with the Jezebels who operate against God’s servants.  Jezebels like to stay in the background and fight God’s servants from the secret place.    

People want to keep their self-respect and not touch the controversial Jezebel. The Thyatira church did not want to deal with their Jezebel just as many modern churches do not want to deal with their Jezebels.  

I met a man of God who confessed to me that he had made the greatest mistake of his ministry and career.  

He said to me, “It has cost me everything. It has cost me my life, my job, my church and my career.” He said, “I have just lost twenty-five years of my life’s work.”

I asked him, “Why? What did you do wrong?” He said to me, “My senior pastor was married to a Jezebel wife. She attacked all of us and divided the church.  Today, the whole church is scattered and it is because of that woman.  Today, we have lost our positions because of that Jezebel wife. We have lost our jobs and it is because of that Jezebel woman.  We are nothing because we acquiesced to the spirit of Jezebel.”

He said to me, “The greatest mistake of our lives was that we did not confront that woman. We should have confronted her long before. We should have stepped in to our senior pastor’s marriage and stopped the Jezebel in her tracks. We should have been her enemy! We should have fought against Jezebel openly, no matter what it cost. We failed to do that and now it has cost us everything.”

Today, that church is closed down and does not exist any more.  They were good pastors and had charity, works, service, faith and patience.  If they had done a little bit more and fought against the Jezebel of their lives and ministry, they would still have had a church today.  

Hey Mr Righteous, can’t you do just a little bit more? Can’t you take up a good fight for the Lord? Can’t you lose a bit of your good reputation for Jesus?  Do you always have to be the nice perfect guy who does no wrong?  Come on and fight for Jesus and do not worry about whose friendship you lose.  Do a little bit more by dealing with the Jezebels of your church!
