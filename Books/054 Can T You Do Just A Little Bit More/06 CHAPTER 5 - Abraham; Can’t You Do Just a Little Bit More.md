### CHAPTER 5\

Abraham; Can’t You Do Just a Little Bit More? \
--------------------------------------------------------------------

**Now the Lord had said unto Abram, GET THEE OUT OF THY COUNTRY, AND FROM THY KINDRED, AND FROM THY FATHER’S HOUSE, unto a land that I will shew thee: And I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and make thy name great; and thou shalt be a blessing: And I will bless them that bless thee, and curse him that curseth thee: and in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.**

**Genesis 12:1-3**

God is always looking for a little bit more and Abraham is a good example of someone who did a little bit more.  Abraham got to know the Lord, and I believe that changed his life forever.  However, one day, God required a little bit more from Abraham.  

**A Little Bit More: Leave Your Family**

**Now the Lord had said unto Abram, GET THEE OUT OF THY COUNTRY, AND FROM THY KINDRED, AND FROM THY FATHER’S HOUSE, unto a land that I will shew thee: And I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and make thy name great; and thou shalt be a blessing: And I will bless them that bless thee, and curse him that curseth thee: and in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.**

**Genesis 12:1-3**

Abraham’s service to God now required a little bit more. Leave your family, leave your country and leave all that is familiar behind.  You may never please God until you do the little extra that God requires of you. You may call yourself a servant of God, but what God may require of you will be to leave your family and your country.  

**A Little Bit More: Circumcise Yourself**

**And Abraham took Ishmael his son, and all that were born in his house, and all that were bought with his money, every male among the men of Abraham’s house; and circumcised the flesh of their foreskin in the selfsame day, as God had said unto him. AND ABRAHAM WAS NINETY YEARS OLD AND NINE, WHEN HE WAS CIRCUMCISED IN THE FLESH OF HIS FORESKIN. And Ishmael his son was thirteen years old, when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin. IN THE SELFSAME DAY WAS ABRAHAM CIRCUMCISED, AND ISHMAEL HIS SON. AND ALL THE MEN OF HIS HOUSE, BORN IN THE HOUSE, AND BOUGHT WITH MONEY OF THE STRANGER, WERE CIRCUMCISED WITH HIM.**

**Genesis 17:23-27**

God had a “little request” to make. He wanted Abraham to be circumcised.  He wanted the whole family to be circumcised.  This painful cutting of the skin on the penis is what God required from Abraham in his old age.  It seemed that serving God, leaving his family, leaving his country was not enough. Abraham, can’t you do just a little bit more?  

God wanted just a little bit more commitment. He wanted a commitment in the private parts of Abraham and his family.  He wanted Abraham to go through the pain and the humiliation of circumcision. What an amazing request from God!  Was leaving his home not enough? Was leaving his country not enough? God seems to always require just a little bit more.  Do not ever be tired of serving God! Do not ever be tired of doing just a little bit more!  A little bit more may be all you need to do to get yourself into the favourable reports of heaven.  

**A Little Bit More: Have Sex with an Old Lady **

**And they said unto him, Where is Sarah thy wife? And he said, Behold, in the tent. And he said, I will certainly return unto thee according to the time of life; and, lo, Sarah thy wife shall have a son. And Sarah heard it in the tent door, which was behind him. Now Abraham and Sarah were old and well stricken in age; and it ceased to be with Sarah after the manner of women. Therefore Sarah laughed within herself, saying, AFTER I AM WAXED OLD SHALL I HAVE PLEASURE, MY LORD BEING OLD ALSO? And the Lord said unto Abraham, Wherefore did Sarah laugh, saying, Shall I of a surety bear a child, which am old? Is any thing too hard for the Lord? At the time appointed I will return unto thee, according to the time of life, and Sarah shall have a son. Then Sarah denied, saying, I laughed not; for she was afraid. And he said, Nay; but thou didst laugh. And the men rose up from thence, and looked toward Sodom: and Abraham went with them to bring them on the way.**

**Genesis 18:9-16**

Abraham still had to do a little bit more after leaving his family and getting himself circumcised.  Abraham had obeyed God by leaving his family and going on the mission field. Now God was requiring of him to have sex with his wife, an old lady.  Abraham’s old wife reacted to this instruction.  She said, “After I am waxed old, shall I have pleasure?”  She said to herself, “Will my husband Abraham be able to do this?”  Do not think that Abraham gave birth to Isaac without having sex. Sara’s reaction to God’s command was because two withered and elderly people were required to have the pleasure of young people.  Abraham was required to do a little bit more: Have sex, make love and have pleasure!   The ninety-year-old man obeyed God in having active sex and pleasure with his postmenopausal wife. 

This may sound strange to some.  There are many times that God requires people to have sex. It may be a little thing that lasts a few minutes but it makes a lot of difference.  The refusal and the failure to have sex is the cause of many evils in our world.  Many marriages undergo subtle erosion because couples will not obey the word of God.   

**A Little Bit More: Kill Your Son **

**And it came to pass after these things, that God did tempt Abraham, and said unto him, Abraham: and he said, Behold, here I am. And he said, TAKE NOW THY SON, THINE ONLY SON ISAAC, WHOM THOU LOVEST, and get thee into the land of Moriah; AND OFFER HIM THERE FOR A BURNT OFFERING UPON ONE OF THE MOUNTAINS which I will tell thee of.**

**Genesis 22:1-2**

The next request was even more bizarre. Abraham had served God all his life. He had left his family, his country and his home.  He had forced his entire family to be circumcised, as grown-ups.  He had behaved himself like a very young man towards his old wife, having pleasure and doing things he had long forgotten how to do.  I am sure he thought to himself, “This is it! There is nothing else to do for the Lord. I have finished my work and I have fulfilled my ministry.”  But it was not to be so.  

God wanted just a little bit more.  “Please sacrifice your son to me.” I am sure Abraham could not believe his ears.  This was an outrageous request.  “I must be having a bizarre auditory hallucination. I must be suffering from schizophrenia or some other serious mental illness”, he must have thought.  But God maintained His request. Sacrifice your son to me!  Indeed, Abraham did just that.  At the end of his life, that little step to sacrifice his son led to the greatest blessing of Abraham’s life and ministry.  

Abraham is one of the greatest examples of the blessings of doing just a little bit more. Don’t hold back! Do a little bit more!  You can do a little bit more for God!
