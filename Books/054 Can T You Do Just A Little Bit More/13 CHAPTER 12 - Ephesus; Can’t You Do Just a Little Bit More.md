### CHAPTER 12\

Ephesus; Can’t You Do Just a Little Bit More?\
-------------------------------------------------------------------

**Unto THE ANGEL OF THE CHURCH OF EPHESUS write; These things saith he that holdeth the seven stars in his right hand, who walketh in the midst of the seven golden candlesticks; I KNOW THY WORKS, and thy labour, and thy patience, and how thou canst not bear them which are evil: and thou hast tried them which say they are apostles, and are not, and hast found them liars: And hast borne, and hast patience, and for my name’s sake hast laboured, and hast not fainted. NEVERTHELESS I HAVE SOMEWHAT AGAINST THEE, BECAUSE THOU HAST LEFT THY FIRST LOVE.**

**Revelation 2:1-4**

God sent a message to the Ephesian church.  They had great works!  They had labour! They had patience! They could not bear evil people! They were not deceived by people who pretended to be apostles but who were rather liars.  They laboured but did not faint!  

But God wanted a little bit more from the Ephesians. He wanted a bit of first love.  What is first love?  First love is different from ordinary love. It is the love of young lovers who are madly in love with each other.  

God was speaking to His bride and saying, “I remember the love of thine espousals. You used to chase Me.  You used to look for Me. You used to call Me.  You went after Me. You hunted for Me. You did not just sit there and wait.  You did not just say “Yes” if I called. You came looking for Me. You did not just open up to Me if I asked you to.  You came hunting, searching and seeking for Me.” 

First love is described in Jeremiah.

Go and cry in the ears of Jerusalem, saying, Thus saith the Lord; I remember thee, the kindness of thy youth, the LOVE OF THINE ESPOUSALS, when THOU WENTEST AFTER ME in the wilderness, in a land that was not sown. Israel was holiness unto the Lord, and the firstfruits of his increase: all that devour him shall offend; evil shall come upon them, saith the Lord.

Jeremiah 2:2-3

It is amazing that God was not satisfied with the Ephesian Christians. These were Christians with great works, great labour and great patience. These were righteous people who could not stand evil at all. *Yet, God required from them a little bit more; first love!  *

It is quite difficult to minister to people who do not love God with first love. People without first love are lifeless, dull and uninteresting.  God wants people who are excited about Him. Wouldn’t you want to marry someone who is excited about you?  Would you want to come home to someone who is angry and suspicious?  Would you want to have a dead piece of timber in the bed with you? Everyone wants life, happiness, excitement and joy.  People without first love have stopped giggling, laughing, chatting and communicating. Above all, people without first love have stopped flowing spontaneously.  

Can’t you do just a little bit more?  Can’t you flow a little bit more?  Can’t you be a little happier? Can’t you show a little more excitement?  Can’t you be a little more cheerful?  Can’t you be a little more grateful?  Can’t you show a little more love? Can’t you be a little kinder? Can’t you show some first love?  

It is nice for you to sit in church but if you were a little more spontaneous and excited, it would make a lot of difference.  

It is time to do a little bit more!
