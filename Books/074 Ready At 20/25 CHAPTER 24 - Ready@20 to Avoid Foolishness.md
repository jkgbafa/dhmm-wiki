### CHAPTER 24\

Ready@20 to Avoid Foolishness\
===================================================

And not many days after the younger son gathered all together, and took his journey into A FAR COUNTRY, and there WASTED HIS SUBSTANCE with RIOTOUS LIVING. And when he had spent all, there arose a mighty famine in that land; and he began to be in want.

But the father said to his servants, Bring forth the best robe, and put it on him; and put a ring on his hand, and shoes on his feet: And bring hither the fatted calf, and kill it; and let us eat, and be merry: For this my son was dead, and is alive again; he was lost, and is found. And they began to be merry.

Luke 15:11-24

Seven Types of Foolishness You Can Avoid When You Are Ready@20

You must be ready@20 to avoid foolishness! In the story of the prodigal son, we see a younger foolish son behaving very badly. He manifested seven types of foolishness that you should avoid in your life and ministry!

Every young man has the same tendencies of the young man in the story of the prodigal son. It is important for every young person who is ready@20 to focus on avoiding these particular types of foolishness.

1\. The foolishness of asking for things prematurely.

Your crops will be abundant, for I will guard them from insects and disease. YOUR GRAPES WILL NOT FALL FROM THE VINE BEFORE THEY ARE RIPE,” says the Lord of Heaven’s Armies.

Malachi 3:11 (NLT)

The prodigal son asked for his inheritance prematurely. This request for premature promotion triggered a chain reaction that led to his downfall.

Many young people seek to have things before they are due. The young man in this story wanted freedom and liberties long before they were due him. God is giving you wisdom to wait until you are qualified to enjoy certain things.

One day, a twenty-two year old young man graduated from our Bible school. He wanted to work in the ministry but he gave various conditions for his ministry. Among the conditions was that he wanted a house and a Land Cruiser. I was amazed at his demands and expectations.

Grapes are not supposed to fall from the vine just because they are ripe. When people demand things prematurely, they get destroyed. Demanding things prematurely reveals that you think of yourself more highly than you ought to. Demanding things prematurely reveals your presumption and your arrogance.

Years ago, when I was getting married, I spoke to my father and told him my requirements for marriage. I described an ideal house and other conditions I felt were essential for my marriage. He then proceeded to tell me how he had struggled in the early stages of his marriage. He described to me how he had only two chairs in the living room. He described how he and my mother would play musical chairs any time they had a visitor. Any time my mother came around, he would get up and go out to do something. When he came around, she would get up and go out to do something else. In this way, the two chairs were just enough to handle each visitor in a way that the visitor would not notice that there were only two chairs in the house.

I was surprised at how poor my father was when he got married. Indeed, I realised that I was presumptuous and arrogant in my expectations of how I wanted to start out in life.

When you start out in ministry, do not expect to have certain privileges. Do not make demands! Do not reveal your presumptuousness and your pride!

God is delivering you from opening your mouth and asking for things that you should never even mention.

2\. The foolishness of separation from your father.

These be they who separate themselves, sensual, having not the Spirit.

Jude 1:19

Many people seek to separate from their parents long before they should. In the ministry, you must be careful not to be separated from what you are supposed to be connected to. God did not create us to be islands on our own. We need one another.

Ignoramuses do not understand the value of being connected. Rebels are cursed to have dense and dark thoughts in which they often display stupidity of the highest order. Their sudden and rebellious separation is often the beginning of their undoing. Rebellious separatists are unraveled and condemned by their actions. All you need to do is to hold your peace when you see rebellious and ungrateful people manifesting their wickedness. They will be hanged by their own words! You cannot touch just everyone! You cannot touch fathers and get away with it!

Develop understanding and wisdom as a young man. To be ready@20 means you are full of wisdom and understanding. Wisdom and understanding will keep you connected! Thick, impenetrable and dense brains cannot understand why you should be connected and relate with others. The spirit of madness, folly and rebellion will urge you to separate yourself from your lifeline and from your source of oxygen. Receive the wisdom of the elderly to understand that everything you have will come through someone you are connected to.

3\. The foolishness of being far away.

And not many days after the younger son gathered all together, and took his journey into a far country, and there wasted his substance with riotous living.

Luke 15:13

The prodigal son decided to go into a far country. All through the Bible you will find God encouraging you to draw near to Him. Whilst God is encouraging you to draw near, satan is encouraging you to go far away. “Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you. Cleanse your hands, ye sinners; and purify your hearts, ye double minded” (James 4:8).

Do not sit at the back! Do not hide yourself amongst the crowd. Do not allow yourself to be an unknown entity. Make sure the pastor knows your name. Make sure your lecturer knows your name. Sit in the front when you are in church. Sit in the front in class. Sit in the front during lectures. Be seen! Be visible! People who stand afar off and sit far away, are often practicing some kind of evil. They do not want to be seen. They do not want to be detected. They do not want to be noticed. They do not want anyone to detect when they come and when they go. You must be ready@20 to serve the Lord. No one should be able to describe you as someone who is far away.

Thus saith the LORD, What iniquity have your fathers found in me, that they are gone far from me, and have walked after vanity, and are become vain?

Jeremiah 2:5

4\. The foolishness of riotous living.

And not many days after the younger son gathered all together, and took his journey into a far country, and there wasted his substance with riotous living…

But as soon as this thy son was come, which hath devoured thy living with harlots, thou hast killed for him the fatted calf.

Luke 15:13, 30

From today you are delivered from riotous living! Riotous living in this context has to do with having many girlfriends or boyfriends. Fooling around with the opposite sex is riotous living and that is exactly what destroyed the young man. Young people are more sexual than older people. The sexuality of a young person can lead him or her into destruction. Today, you are delivered from the foolishness of riotous living that happens when you are a young man. Even Apostle Paul admitted that he had lived an evil life following the desires of his flesh and his mind.

Among whom also we all had our conversation in times past in the lusts of our flesh, fulfilling the desires of the flesh and of the mind; and were by nature the children of wrath, even as others.

Ephesians 2:3

When Paul was converted to Christ, he put aside the foolish things that had possessed his mind and flesh in his youth. From today, you will be one of the unusual young men who will live their lives without indulging in evil and destructive sex!

One day, I spoke to a young man who enjoyed having sex with many different girls. I told him that he was creating a situation for himself in which he would be unable as a married man to live with one girl. When you develop a taste for having a different girl every other day, it becomes difficult to live happily ever after with one woman. Today marks the end of riotous living in your life! You will no longer take pride in the number of girls you have slept with! You will no longer boast about the number of ladies you have deflowered! To deflower a woman is to deprive her of her virginity. God forbid that you will be involved with such wickedness!

5\. The foolishness of joining yourself to the world.

And he went and joined himself to a citizen of that country; and he sent him into his fields to feed swine.

Luke 15:15

Unfortunately, many people who are in the ministry join themselves to the world system. The young man in the story of the prodigal son joined himself to a citizen of the country but that did not help him. The citizens of the country made things worse for him and made him grovel with pigs.

Worldly systems which encourage loans, mortgages, bonds, arrangements of all sorts will only make your finances worse than they already are. Worldly ways and worldly systems will only make you worse.

Learning to live within your means is a humble way of raising capital to fund the great things God is leading you to do. Do not believe the world systems! The world is charged with deception and falsehood. Almost everything in the world will go up in smoke as you trust and give yourself to it. Many banks give a false impression of stability and wealth! Many businesses are fake and empty! Many governments have no money! Do not trust the worldly systems. Trust in God. Young people must believe the word of God above every falsehood that exists.

6\. The foolishness of eating the wrong things.

And he would fain have filled his belly with the husks that the swine did eat: and no man gave unto him.

Luke 15:16

The prodigal son ended up eating pig food. There is no way that he would do well and be healthy by eating pig food. Many ministers are eating pig food. Many ministers do not eat the right books. The books that people read and the messages that they listen to, determine the fruit that they bear. If you read certain books, you will become fruitful. Notice what Paul said:

Now he that ministereth seed to the sower both minister bread for your food, and multiply your seed sown, and increase the fruits of your righteousness;

2 Corinthians 9:10

One day I was praying to increase the fruits that I was bearing in the Lord. The Lord directed me to this amazing scripture. Increase the seeds you invest in yourself and they will increase the fruits of your righteousness!

The scripture above gives an important revelation of the two uses of seeds. Half of the seeds are used to make bread and half of the seeds are sown as seed. The sower receives seed that he can sow as a seed! The sower also receives seed that he turns into his food! The seeds and food that the sower sows and eats are what enables him to bear the fruits of his righteousness. Today, you will increase in the fruits of your righteousness because you are increasing the seeds that you sow in yourself. Spend money to acquire the food that you need.

Ho, every one that thirsteth, come ye to the waters, and he that hath no money; come ye, buy, and eat; yea, come, buy wine and milk without money and without price. Wherefore do ye spend money for that which is not bread? and your labour for that which satisfieth not? hearken diligently unto me, and eat ye that which is good, and let your soul delight itself in fatness.

Isaiah 55:1-2

7\. The foolishness of wasting opportunities.

And he said, A certain man had two sons: And the younger of them said to his father, Father, give me the portion of goods that falleth to me. And he divided unto them his living. And not many days after the younger son gathered all together, and took his journey into a far country, and there wasted his substance with riotous living.

Luke 15:11-13

The young man in the story of the prodigal son lost the opportunity to be the second in command in his father’s house. Many great things lay ahead of him yet he threw them all away. You will not lose the opportunities that God is giving you. Instead of losing and misusing your opportunities, you will make the best use of them!

What is an opportunity? An opportunity is a chance to serve the Lord. It is a chance to preach the Word! It is a chance to travel! It is an opportunity to give yourself to the purposes of God.

When I was twenty-five years old, I became the pastor of a small church in a classroom. I just had a few members in my little church. Everybody in my church was a very young person. I was probably the oldest person in the church. I did not despise my opportunity and I did not throw it away. That was my opportunity! That was my chance to serve the Lord! That was my chance to preach!

I did not say that twenty people were too small a number for me to preach to. It was everything to me and I embraced my opportunity wholeheartedly. My opportunity grew in my hands until that little classroom church was in over a hundred countries. What are you doing with your opportunity?

Once upon a time, a young man who had been with me for many years jumped out of the church and began to insult me. He was a bright young man and very talented. I felt sad, not because he was insulting me, but because he was destroying the great opportunities that lay ahead of him. He destroyed his life by jumping out of the boat.

One day, a pastor came to see me. I looked at his withered face in pity. I had trained him in the ministry and given him many great opportunities to serve the Lord. He used to live in a certain country but I helped him relocate to another country so that he could become a pastor.

In the other country that he used to live, he worked as an orderly and a chauffeur. Through the opportunity that I gave him, he became a well-known pastor. One day, it occurred to him that he had served me enough and that he needed to move on. Just like the prodigal son, he prematurely requested his inheritance and left the ministry.

After several years of this adventure, he was left with nothing. He had no ministry, no money, no congregation, no support, no church! Satan had led him to fulfil the prophetic story of the prodigal son. He had destroyed many lives and had been destroyed himself!

Dear friend, do not be deceived! The testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy. Everything that Jesus taught was prophetic. Countless people fulfil the story of the prodigal son without realising what they are doing. It will never happen to you! You are changing levels and getting closer instead of getting further away from the truth. You are reading this book because you are going to be a good example of someone who is ready@20 to serve the Lord.
