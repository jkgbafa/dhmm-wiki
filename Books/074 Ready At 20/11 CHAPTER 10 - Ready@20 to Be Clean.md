### CHAPTER 10\

Ready@20 to Be Clean\
==========================================

Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word.

Psalm 119:9

You must be ready@20 to have a clean and holy life! Young men can have clean ways. A young man can be pure.

Jesus Christ was pure and holy.

Jesus Christ knew no sin!

Jesus said, “Which of you convinceth me of sin?” (John 8:46)

Indeed, Jesus is the best example of a young person who lived a clean life. Apart from Jesus, many other young people have lived relatively sinless and pure lives. Think about the young man who came to see Jesus to ask how he could have eternal life. This young man was confident in declaring that he had kept all the commandments from his youth upward. “And, behold, one came and said unto him, Good Master, what good thing shall I do, that I may have eternal life? And he said unto him, Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, God: but if thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments.

He saith unto him, Which? Jesus said, Thou shalt do no murder, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness,

Honour thy father and thy mother: and, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

The YOUNG MAN saith unto him, ALL THESE THINGS HAVE I KEPT FROM MY YOUTH UP: what lack I yet?” (Matthew 19:16-20)

There are many young people who have lived relatively sinless and pure lives. You will be one of the pure people who keeps the commandments from their youth upwards.

Young People Can Have the Spirit of Holiness

1\. Young people can be empowered by the Spirit of holiness.

And DECLARED to be the Son of God with power, according to THE Spirit of holiness, by the resurrection from the dead:

Romans 1:4

Holiness is engineered by the Spirit of holiness and not by being an older person. Age has nothing to do with holiness. Older people have fewer desires but that does not mean they are holier. Holiness is not the same as becoming an elderly person who has no desires.

There is a hidden Spirit of holiness who empowers a person to be holy. Jesus Christ became a holy person and became the Son of God by the Spirit of holiness. He said to the people around Him, “Which of you convinceth me of sin? And if I say the truth, why do ye not believe me?” (John 8:46)

No one could “convince” Jesus of sin. He was truly pure, sinless and sanctified. Every young person can be empowered by the Spirit of holiness to become whatever God wants him to become. The Spirit of holiness can empower you to become a great apostle or a great prophet.

2\. Young people can live free from sin.

A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh. And I WILL PUT MY SPIRIT WITHIN YOU, AND CAUSE YOU TO WALK IN MY STATUTES, and ye shall keep my judgments, and do them.

Ezekiel 36:26-27

The Spirit of holiness empowers you to walk in the word of God. The Spirit of holiness empowers a person to live his life according to the word of God. The Spirit of holiness is behind the rise of every great ministry. The Spirit of holiness is the power behind anybody’s righteousness and holiness.

3\. Young people are sanctified by the Holy Spirit.

But we are bound to give thanks alway to God for you, brethren beloved of the Lord, because God hath from the beginning CHOSEN YOU TO SALVATION THROUGH SANCTIFICATION OF THE SPIRIT and belief of the truth: Whereunto he called you by our gospel, to the obtaining of the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

2 Thessalonians 2:13-14

The Spirit is the sanctifier. The sanctification of your life is by the Spirit of holiness. The sanctification of you and your ministry is by the Spirit of holiness. The Spirit of holiness is necessary to make you sanctified. We have been selected for holiness by the power and sanctification that comes from the Holy Spirit.

4\. The Spirit of holiness was the power behind Jesus Christ’s sinlessness on earth.

For he hath made him to be sin for us, WHO KNEW NO SIN; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.

2 Corinthians 5:21

Jesus Christ was a young man, just like you. Yet, Jesus was sinless because of the Spirit of holiness. It is indeed a mystery for a man to live on the earth without sin. Yet, we are told that Jesus Christ KNEW NO SIN! Indeed, it takes a power beyond human power to turn you into a man who knows no sin.

5\. Young Daniel rose to power by the Spirit of holiness.

Then came in the magicians, the astrologers, the Chaldeans, and the soothsayers: and I told the dream before them; but they did not make known unto me the interpretation thereof. But at the last Daniel came in before me, whose name was Belteshazzar, according to the name of my god, and in whom is THE SPIRIT OF THE HOLY GODS: and before him I told the dream, saying, O Belteshazzar, master of the magicians, because I know that the spirit of the holy gods is in thee, and no secret troubleth thee, tell me the visions of my dream that I have seen, and the interpretation thereof.

Daniel 4:7-9

Even the unbelievers of Daniel’s era recognized the holiness and purity that he had. They recognized that there was a spirit that empowered him to be holy and pure. They called it “the spirit of the holy gods.” It was not called the spirit of the gods but “the spirit of the holy gods.” Holiness was behind the rise of young Daniel.

6\. Young Joseph rose to power through the Spirit of holiness.

And it came to pass after these things, that his master’s wife cast her eyes upon Joseph; and she said, Lie with me. But he refused, and said unto his master’s wife, Behold, my master wotteth not what is with me in the house, and he hath committed all that he hath to my hand; There is none greater in this house than I; neither hath he kept back any thing from me but thee, because thou art his wife: HOW THEN CAN I DO THIS GREAT WICKEDNESS, AND SIN AGAINST GOD?

Genesis 39:7-9

Young Joseph refused to commit the sin of sleeping with his master’s wife. He considered it an impossibility because of the trust reposed in him. Joseph passed all his tests of morality and holiness. All these tests of morality and holiness helped to catapult Joseph into the prison from where he would be launched as a prime minister. If Joseph had failed the test of adultery and fornication, he would have spent the rest of his life as a treacherous deceiver in Potiphar’s house. We would never have heard of Joseph if it had not been for the Spirit of holiness. I know many young men who crossed the line and lived in fornication and immorality instead of walking in the Spirit of holiness. By these acts, they entered into the wastepaper basket of ministry!

7\. The Spirit of holiness is behind very anointed people.

Thou lovest righteousness, and hatest wickedness: therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.

Psalm 45:7

Very anointed people have the Spirit of holiness in them. Because you love righteousness and hate wickedness, God anoints you with more of the oil of gladness than other people. Loving righteousness and hating wickedness is a secret of very anointed people. Very anointed people may not talk about holiness but it is the basis of their heavy anointing.
