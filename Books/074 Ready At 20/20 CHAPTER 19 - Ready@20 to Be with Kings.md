### CHAPTER 19\

Ready@20 to Be with Kings\
===============================================

And the king spake unto Ashpenaz the master of his eunuchs, that he should bring certain of the children of Israel, and of the king’s seed, and of the princes; CHILDREN IN WHOM WAS NO BLEMISH, BUT WELL FAVOURED, AND SKILFUL IN ALL WISDOM, AND CUNNING IN KNOWLEDGE, AND UNDERSTANDING SCIENCE, and such as had ability in them to stand in the king’s palace, and whom they might teach the learning and THE TONGUE OF THE CHALDEANS.

Daniel 1:3-4

Be ready@20 to be dwell with kings and nobility! The four children, Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego and Daniel were chosen to work closely and personally with the king. They needed to have special abilities in order to do that. It is not only old and wise people who are needed to work in the king’s palace.

Some kings have great respect for the young and believe that young people can add something substantial to their lives. The kings of Persia seemed to want the young children of Israel to come close to them.

Castrating the Jews was a way to take away their sexual drive as well as their drive for other things. But castrating them and turning them to eunuchs did not change their level of wisdom. The children of Israel were still intelligent and full of wisdom. There were seven things that these young children needed to have in order to work in the king’s palace. You are receiving these seven things right now. Become a young man who has the ability to work in the king’s palace. Be ready @20 to be a young man who has the ability to work in the king’s palace.

Seven Things Young People Need to Work in the King’s Palace

1\. A young man without blemish. A blemish is something that destroys or diminishes the perfection of a thing. A blemish is a mark, a defect or a flaw that takes away from your appearance and your existence. In order to achieve the greatness that God wants you to achieve as a young person, you must remove from your life all blemishes. Think carefully about your life! What defect or flaw in your life would detract from your appearance? What is there about you that will destroy or diminish the perfection of your life? What moral blemish are you hiding in your life that will remove your shine and change who you are? It is important that as a young man you clear out of your life all moral defects and flaws.

2\. A young man who is well favoured. A well-favoured person is someone who has done all the things to obtain favour. In order to be a man who is well-favoured, you need to be merciful and truthful. Without mercy you will lose favour. Without being truthful you will lose favour. “Let not mercy and truth forsake thee: bind them about thy neck; write them upon the table of thine heart: So shalt thou find favour and good understanding in the sight of God and man.” (Proverbs 3:3-4)

3\. A young man skilful in wisdom. You must become skilful in wisdom. Wisdom has to do with how you apply knowledge. How you apply all the things you have been taught reveals your wisdom.

4\. A young man with knowledge. You must become a young man who is full of knowledge. Fight hard to become a man of knowledge filled with understanding. Think of cabbage heads in a garden. They know nothing. They are filled with nothingness. The cabbage heads are only good for eating in your cabbage stew. As a young man, you must ensure that your head is worth more than a cabbage head in a vegetable garden.

5\. A young man who understands science. You must be a man of science. Do not fall asleep when scientific discussions are taking place. A failure to learn new things reveals that you are not a man of science. I notice how some people spring to life when a little gossip is injected into the conversation. They sort of come alive because there is a mention of some sleazy story. It is very noticeable to me. Yet, such people fall asleep when there is something scientific being discussed.

6\. A young man who can be close to the king. The ability to be close is an important characteristic for your ministry. Many people cannot be close to the king. They would immediately become familiar. Some would be offended by what they see. Some would speak against the king because they see his personal life. Some would criticize! Some would take pictures and send to their friends. Some would develop a severe problem of familiarity and be unable to receive wisdom and guidance from the king any longer.

Judas’ closeness to Jesus did not help him. Seeing Jesus at close range made Judas think that he could betray Him and get away with it. If Judas had been afar off, he would have just been awed by Jesus’ greatness. Perhaps Judas overheard Jesus discussing His death on the cross. Judas probably misunderstood these discussions. Judas thought that he would simply cash in on Jesus’ crucifixion. When you do not have the ability to be close to the king, you easily misunderstand what you see and hear.

7\. A young man who can learn languages. A young man must be someone who can learn languages. Older people struggle to learn languages. Young people are quickly birthed into new languages. You must become a young man who is ready to flow in two, three and even four different languages. I am always impressed when I meet people who can speak several different languages fluently.
