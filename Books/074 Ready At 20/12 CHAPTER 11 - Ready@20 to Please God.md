### CHAPTER 11\

Ready@20 to Please God\
============================================

Therefore THE LORD SHALL HAVE NO JOY IN THEIR YOUNG MEN, neither shall have mercy on their fatherless and widows: for every one is an hypocrite and an evildoer, and every mouth speaketh folly. For all this his anger is not turned away, but his hand is stretched out still.

Isaiah 9:17

You must be ready@20 to please God! You can please God as a young person. You do not need to be an older person who is devoid of energy and life before you can please God. Pleasing God does not mean you have become quiet, lifeless, morose, gloomy and anti-social. To please God, you need to obey His will and do His will.

Decide to be one of the young people who please God. Do not wait until you are older to please Him.

I remember an elderly Christian lady pastor who was preaching powerfully one day. She was preaching about holiness and righteousness. She got on to preaching about immorality, fornication and adultery. I would not have thought twice about this message but then she became even strong-er on the topic of fornication and adultery.

As I continued to listen and look at this elderly grey-haired woman, I remembered the life she had lived as a groovy, sexy and sensual young lady with several boyfriends. Now that she was old, grey-haired and shapeless, she was a strong advocate for holiness and an even stronger advocate for fighting fornication, adultery and all other forms of lewdness. The fact is that most people will be strong advocates for holiness and righteousness when they are older. From a certain age onwards, you will not even have opportunities to walk in those sins any more. There will be little desire left in you.

The Bible speaks about fleeing youthful lusts. (2 Timothy 2:22) There are things that are peculiar to your youthfulness; but even with your youthful lusts, youthful desires and sexual cravings, you can become a God-pleaser.

God can have joy in young people. Young people can please the Lord! God can have joy in the young! Decide that you will be one of the young people who please God.

Remember that older people also have their own set of spiritual problems. Older people suffer from prejudice, hatred, revenge, bitterness, unforgiveness and pride. You will hear older people say things like, “Watch out for people from this country.” “Be careful of people from this tribe!” “Be careful of so-and-so!” All these are the prejudices that have been built up through their experiences.

It is equally difficult to serve God when you are proud. Take advantage of your youthful humility and become one of the young people that give God joy.

Elijah was a man of like passions. Elijah had lots of feelings and passions. Yet God used him. He pleased God!

Elias was a man subject to like passions as we are, and he prayed earnestly that it might not rain: and it rained not on the earth by the space of three years and six months.

James 5:17

Perhaps, Elijah was much younger than we think. Maybe Elijah was a very young prophet. Elijah had lots of feelings and passions. And yet God used him mightily. You can be ready@20 to be one of those who please God.
