### CHAPTER 15\

Ready@20 to Be Sent as Ambassadors\
========================================================

Now then we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God did beseech you by us: we pray you in Christ’s stead, be ye reconciled to God.

2 Corinthians 5:20

You must be ready@20 to be an ambassador for Christ! Are you ready to be sent as a young ambassador? It is not easy to be an ambassador because ambassadors have to maintain the aim for which they were sent. It is not easy to maintain the aim! Ambassadors have to maintain the aim by frequent communication with the home base. An ambassador must maintain the aim and accomplish the purpose for which he was sent.

Most churches have not maintained the aim of preaching the gospel. Most of us preachers have lost the original purpose for which Christ sent us forth.

But Joshua had said unto the two men that had spied out the country, Go into the harlot’s house, and bring out thence the woman, and all that she hath, as ye sware unto her. AND THE YOUNG MEN THAT WERE SPIES WENT IN, AND BROUGHT OUT RAHAB, and her father, and her mother, and her brethren, and all that she had; and they brought out all her kindred, and left them without the camp of Israel.

Joshua 6:22-23

In this story, Joshua decided to use young men as ambassadors. Moses believed that young people could be ready@ 20 to be ambassadors. Moses could have chosen older, mature and experienced men to be his ambassadors. But he chose young men! Are you ready to be one of the young men to be chosen for important ambassadorial jobs?

Earlier in the ministry of Moses, twelve spies were sent out. Moses sent out twelve ambassadors but ten brought back an evil report. Ten evil ambassadors brought back an evil report about The Promised Land. Moses’ first group of spies were impossibility thinkers! These older ambassadors spoke negatively about the mission field. These older ambassadors spoke negatively about the harvest field. The older ambassadors discouraged others who were ready to go out on the field. They dampened the spirits of everyone else.

But the men that went up with him said, WE BE NOT ABLE TO GO UP against the people; for they are stronger than we. And THEY BROUGHT UP AN EVIL REPORT OF THE LAND which they had searched unto the children of Israel, saying, The land, through which we have gone to search it, is a land that eateth up the inhabitants thereof; and all the people that we saw in it are men of a great stature. And there we saw the giants, the sons of Anak, which come of the giants: and we were in our own sight as grasshoppers, and so we were in their sight.

Numbers 13:31-33

On the second occasion, Joshua sent out young men in particular. These young men were to look at the land and bring back a report. These two spies almost died because they were hotly pursued by people from the city of Jericho. These spies, who almost lost their lives on their spying mission, rather gave a good report about the land. Those who see things negatively always see things negatively!

And they went, and came unto the mountain, and abode there three days, until the pursuers were returned: and the pursuers sought them throughout all the way, but found them not.

Joshua 2:22

Young people see things differently. Perhaps, because they were younger men, they believed more in the power of God. Even though they saw how impenetrable Jericho was, they were happy to get involved in the mission. They were not moved by the fact that the battle for mighty Jericho was going to be a big test for their lives.

So the two men returned, and descended from the mountain, and passed over, and came to Joshua the son of Nun, and told him all things that befell them: And they said unto Joshua, TRULY THE LORD HATH DELIVERED INTO OUR HANDS ALL THE LAND; for even all the inhabitants of the country do faint because of us.

Joshua 2:23-24

God is, indeed, raising up young people for the mission. Not men of impossibilities but “can-do” men of many possibilities! Men who are ready@20!

I prefer to send young people on missions. Young people see problems differently. Young people see possibilities! Young people have more faith! Young people can overcome mountains! Young people do not see high walls as a message to stay away or to stay out. Rather, they see the walls as an opportunity to accomplish something for God.
