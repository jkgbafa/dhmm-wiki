### CHAPTER 26\

Ready@20 to Escape Captivity\
==================================================

The Lord is righteous; for I have rebelled against his commandment: hear, I pray you, all people, and behold my sorrow: MY VIRGINS AND MY YOUNG MEN ARE GONE IN-TO CAPTIVITY.

Lamentations 1:18

You must be ready@20 to escape from all forms of captivity! A young man who is a captive cannot obey the Lord because he is bound to some other activity or cause. Young men who serve the Lord must not go into captivity. Captivity means that you cannot go free, even if you want to. When a young person is in captivity, he is imprisoned, enslaved and confined.

The anointing of the Lord is designed to set you free from captivity. It is very sad to see young people who are unable to serve the Lord because of one captivity or another.

If you are going to work for God, you must ensure that you escape all forms of captivity. Satan takes captives so that he can keep them on a leash. Satan wants to ensure that you never serve the Lord. Any form of captivity is demonic in origin. You must do whatever you can to be free from demonic captivity.

And that they may recover themselves out of THE SNARE OF THE DEVIL, who are taken captive by him at his will.

2 Timothy 2:26

A snare is something you touch with your foot and suddenly you cannot walk away any more. A snare is something you touch with your hand and suddenly you cannot pull it out any longer.

Be careful of things which do not allow you to move away any longer. God has called you so you will escape from the snare of the devil and you will not remain in captivity. It is not everything that has the power to capture you and keep you permanently tied to it; but there are certain things that take young people captive and hold them in bondage until death. Because God chooses young people to work for Him, satan marks them out for captivity. If he can keep them captive through their youth, he can ensure that they will never be a threat to his kingdom. Many dark practices can become sources of captivity.

Be careful you do not touch an area of sin from which you cannot return! Many people have tasted evil from which they cannot withdraw themselves.

Here are some of the common captivities that take hold of young people, rendering them useless to God.

Types of Captivity

1\. The captivity of narcotic drugs. Narcotic drugs will not allow you to escape from them. Addiction to these drugs is well known. Drugs like cocaine and heroin are the best examples of drugs that can take you captive.

2\. The captivity of prescription drugs. Some prescription drugs will never allow you to escape from them. Drugs like Pethidine, which are given to relieve pain, have become sources of captivity.

3\. The captivity of alcohol. Alcohol may seize you, take you captive and not allow you to escape from it again. I have watched people die from their captivity to alcohol. Perhaps, alcohol did not grab your friend tightly, so you felt you could play with it as well. Alcohol may seize you in such a way that you will never be free from it. Alcohol has become a source of captivity for many people in many parts of the world.

4\. The captivity of nicotine. Nicotine will not allow you to escape from it. It may hold your hand and that will be it! Cigarette smoking is something that people cannot stop, in spite of the warnings about how smoking kills. The captivity forces them to stay on.

5\. The captivity of marijuana. Do not toy with marijuana! Marijuana will not allow you to escape from it. It will seize you! Many people are warned about the dangers of marijuana. Marijuana can cause schizophrenia and yet people continue to smoke it, grow it, inhale it, eat it and even drink it. When a man is in captivity, he does not want to do what he is doing but he does it anyway.

6\. The captivity of gambling. Gambling will not allow you to escape from its addiction. People who are into gambling and betting are often held captive by it. It holds them as prisoners until it has destroyed them. One day, a distressed wife described how her husband had lost one hundred thousand dollars in one night. Surely, such captivities like gambling should not be the portion of those who are ready@20.

7\. The captivity of pornography. Pornography may never let you go free if it gets hold of you. While a large number of people are free from pornography, there are many who are in captivity. They simply cannot stop watching it and using it. Anything, which keeps you bound to it against your will, is a form of captivity.

8\. The captivity of masturbation. Be careful you do not touch masturbation from which you cannot return! Many people have tasted this evil and cannot withdraw themselves from it. Masturbation is a habit, which some people acquire, and this leads them to become consistent permanent practitioners of masturbation. Masturbation usually goes along with pornography. Together, these two vices hold millions of young men and women in captivity. Anything you cannot stop holds you as a captive.

9\. The captivity of fornication. Be careful you do not touch fornication from which you cannot return! Many people have tasted this evil and cannot withdraw themselves from it. There are some people who keep you permanently tied to themselves in a life of secret fornication. Fornication may be an event that comes and goes. But for some people, it holds a strong bondage and causes a captivity to begin. The unusual and unrelenting urge to keep on doing the sexual sin leads a person on until he is destroyed.

10\. The captivity of sexual perversions. Be careful you do not touch sexual perversions from which you cannot return! Many people have tasted the evils of sexual perversions and cannot withdraw themselves from it. Sexual perversions also holds a strong captivity for many people. Many are unable to move away once they engage in these practices; and that is what makes it captivity!

11\. The captivity of social media. Be careful you do not touch the evils of social media from which you cannot return! Many people have tasted this evil and cannot withdraw themselves from it. Social media holds many people’s attention all day long. It forms a type of captivity that a person cannot break away from.

12\. The captivity of the phone. Be careful you do not get involved with your phone in such a way that you cannot be free. Many people have tasted the evils of phone captivity and cannot withdraw themselves from it. Many have been captured by their phones. Their heads are bowed down continually. Their eyes are glued to their screens. They speak to you with their heads bowed down with their eyes permanently on their screens. The screens have captured their faces and their lives.

Steps to Getting Free from Captivity

1\. Take strong decisions and set yourself free: Decide that all these types of captivity are enemies. God does not deliver you from your friends. God delivers you from your enemies! Decide to hate all these agents of captivity.

2\. Pray and escape from captivity: Pray about becoming free from captivity. Pray specifically about whatever captivity you are in. Hours of prayer will set you free. Those who pray are always counted worthy to escape.

For as a snare shall it come on all them that dwell on the face of the whole earth. Watch ye therefore, and PRAY ALWAYS, THAT YE MAY BE ACCOUNTED WORTHY TO ESCAPE ALL THESE THINGS that shall come to pass, and to stand before the Son of man.

Luke 21:35-36

3\. Fast and break loose from captivity: Fast and pray about every form of captivity in your life. There are different kinds of problems but the kind of problem that brings captivity does not go away easily.

And when he was come into the house, his disciples asked him privately, Why could not we cast him out? And he said unto them, THIS KIND CAN COME FORTH BY NOTHING, BUT BY PRAYER AND FASTING.

Mark 9:28-29

4\. Bind the devil and end the captivity: Satan is most uncomfortable when you mention his name and bind him. Satan, like a serpent, sneaks into homes, families and churches unawares. It is important to mention his name, describe the demon you are dealing with and bind him.

5\. Take practical steps to walk away from your captivity: Take practical steps to escape from the bondage. For instance, you can decide to dry out some of the sins over a period of time instead of abruptly cutting them off. Abruptly ending certain things may not work that well.

6\. Make faith declarations and overcome the mountain of captivity: Use faith confessions to come out of bondage. Call those things that are not as though they are (Romans 4:17). Declare the end from the beginning. Continually declare over your life that you are free from that particular vice.

7\. Be open and set yourself free from the snare: All forms of evil thrive in darkness. Secrecy and darkness are the breeding grounds for captivity. When you are open about your problems, they become smaller and vanish.

8\. Confess your sins to God and end all spiritual captivities: Confession brings the end to many spiritual problems. Confession is a condition that God has given to bring about the end of sin in your life. If we confess our sins He is faithful and just to forgive us.

If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

1 John 1:9

9\. Confess your faults one to another and be healed of the effects of captivity: Confessing faults one to another is the key to healing and restoration from your sins.

Confess your faults one to another and pray for one another that you may be healed. When you talk to the right person about your problem, you will find that the size of the problem reduces.

Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.

James 5:16
