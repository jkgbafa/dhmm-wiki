### CHAPTER 14\

Ready@20 to Overcome the Wicked One\
=========================================================

I write unto you, fathers, because ye have known him that is from the beginning. I write unto you, YOUNG MEN, BECAUSE YE HAVE OVERCOME THE WICKED ONE. I write unto you, little children, because ye have known the Father.

I have written unto you, fathers, because ye have known him that is from the beginning. I have written unto you, young men, because ye are strong, and the word of God abideth in you, and ye have overcome the wicked one.

1 John 2:13-14

You must be ready@20 to overcome the wicked one! Young people who serve the Lord must be strong and overcome the wicked one. It is a mistake to ignore the special role of a young man’s strength for the ministry.

Satan is wicked! The wickedness of the devil is the brutal, callous and evil nature which satan manifests without emotion or feeling. Satan is cold blooded, cold hearted, merciless, pitiless, ruthless, savage, stony-hearted, unfeeling and compassionless.

This malevolent creature is out to turn human beings into phantoms of what God created them to be.

When you see the deformities, sicknesses, diseases and infirmities that plague human beings, you will have an idea of the wickedness of the creature that we call the wicked one. These physical deformities are a picture of the spiritual deformities that satan wishes to inflict on God’s people.

If you allow the wicked one, he will turn you into a twisted, perverted, contorted version of what you are supposed to be.

If you allow the wicked one, he will turn you into a shallow, unspiritual Christian, a backslider, a liar, a thief, a sexual pervert, an immoral person, an addict and a slave.

Satan looks at you with great desire! Jesus told Peter; “...Simon, Simon, behold, Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat:” (Luke 22:31) Imagine that! Satan looks at you with desire to have you, to eat you, to enjoy you, to lick you, to consume you and to destroy you. You are the desire of satan. You are his goal! You are his target!

A young person is amazingly more capable of fending off the wicked one. You would have thought that it would be the older people who are more capable of fighting the evil one. What does the scripture say? “I write unto you, young men, because ye have overcome the wicked one.” (1 John 2:13) There are many evils that you can ward off better as a young man. It also takes physical strength to fight the enemy.

Indeed, King Solomon is a good example of someone who was able to overcome the wicked one because he was young. It was when King Solomon was young that he was able to overcome problems with strange women and idol worship.

It was when King Solomon was young that he was able to overcome the distractions of other gods. It is when Solomon was a younger man that he was able to overcome the problem of not following God fully. It is much easier to be a follower when you are a younger person. Solomon never had a problem with disobedience when he was young.

It was only when Solomon became an older man that he had problems with disobedience. You must remember that disobedience is the biggest open door to curses.

As you can see, Solomon overcame the wicked one as a young man. He was destroyed by the wicked one when he was an older man.

Young Solomon

1\. Young Solomon was ready@20 to love God.

When King Solomon was a young man, he was full of faith and love for God. He was very principled and very obedient to his father. Solomon loved God so much that his heart was bent on only loving God.

And Solomon loved the LORD, walking in the statutes of David his father: only he sacrificed and burnt incense in high places.

1 Kings 3:3

2\. Young Solomon was ready@20 to make great sacrifices.

Solomon offered a record-breaking thousand offerings. Solomon’s offerings were the highest ever heard of in human history. No one had ever been known to offer a thousand offerings.

And the king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there; for that was the great high place: a thousand burnt offerings did Solomon offer upon that altar.

1 Kings 3:4

3\. Young Solomon was ready@20 to do the will of God from his heart.

Give therefore thy servant an understanding heart to judge thy people, that I may discern between good and bad: for who is able to judge this thy so great a people? And the speech pleased the Lord, that Solomon had asked this thing.

1 Kings 3:9-10

Young Solomon was ready@20 to do the will of God. Solomon’s famous prayer was made when he was a young man. All that God wanted Solomon to do was to be a leader of God’s people. God wanted someone who would lead the children of Israel into peace and stability. You are that person whom God will use to judge and to lead His people.

4\. Young Solomon was ready@20 to serve God’s people as a shepherd.

Give therefore thy servant an understanding heart to judge thy people, that I may discern between good and bad: for who is able to judge this thy so great a people? And the speech pleased the Lord, that Solomon had asked this thing.

1 Kings 3:9-10

Solomon was ready to be a shepherd. A young person can be a shepherd. A young person can indeed be a king if he has the right kind of heart. A young person can be made the leader of many people once he is filled with the wisdom of God. Your youthfulness does not prevent you from being a leader of God’s people.

5\. Young Solomon was ready@20 to manifest great wisdom.

At a very tender age and at the very beginning of his reign, young Solomon manifested great wisdom. In a landmark case, he gave a judgment in favour of the true mother of a child. Solomon was very young when he manifested this great wisdom. Indeed, you can be ready@20 to manifest the greatest kind of wisdom.

And the king said, Divide the living child in two, and give half to the one, and half to the other. Then spake the woman whose the living child was unto the king, for her bowels yearned upon her son, and she said, O my lord, give her the living child, and in no wise slay it. But the other said, Let it be neither mine nor thine, but divide it. Then the king answered and said, Give her the living child, and in no wise slay it: she is the mother thereof. And all Israel heard of the judgment which the king had judged; and they feared the king: for they saw that the wisdom of God was in him, to do judgment.

1 Kings 3:25-28

The Older Solomon

Unfortunately, this same Solomon changed drastically when he was older. Satan, the wicked one, came in to destroy and pervert Solomon. Solomon, who was so filled with virtue, now became perverted and filled with evil. The “older version” of Solomon was unable to overcome the wicked one in the way the young Solomon did.

For it came to pass, WHEN SOLOMON WAS OLD, that his wives turned away his heart after other gods: and his heart was not perfect with the LORD his God, as was the heart of David his father.

1 Kings 11:4

Satan came in like a flood and turned the older Solomon into a phantom of what he used to be.

1\. The older Solomon’s heart was not as devoted to God as it used to be.

For when Solomon was old, his wives turned his heart away after other gods; and his heart was not wholly devoted to the LORD his God, as the heart of David his father had been.

1 Kings 11:4 (NASB)

2\. The older Solomon did not follow the Lord fully.

And Solomon did evil in the sight of the LORD, and went not fully after the LORD, as did David his father.

1 Kings 11:6

3\. The older Solomon did not copy his father as he did when he was younger.

Solomon did what was evil in the sight of the Lord, and did not follow the Lord fully, as David his father had done.

1 Kings 11:6 (NASB)

Amazingly, when Solomon was older, he felt he did not need his father’s wisdom. He did not see the use of following the things that his father had done. David never worshipped idols! David never turned away from God! David never desired to build a temple for an idol! In spite of David’s sin of immorality, he never turned away from the worship of the true God!

4\. The older Solomon was disobedient to God.

But king Solomon loved many strange women, together with the daughter of Pharaoh, women of the Moabites, Ammonites, Edomites, Zidonians, and Hittites: Of the nations concerning which the LORD said unto the children of Israel, Ye shall not go in to them, neither shall they come in unto you: for surely they will turn away your heart after their gods: Solomon clave unto these in love.

1 Kings 11:1-2

God had said that he should not associate with the women and the nations of the world; but Solomon had gone ahead and disobeyed God in relation to these. He held fast to these women, even though he was the king of Israel and could have had any number of women in Israel that he really wanted to. He held fast to these foreign women even though it was against the word of God.

5\. The older Solomon fell to strange women.

But king Solomon loved many strange women, together with the daughter of Pharaoh, women of the Moabites, Ammonites, Edomites, Zidonians, and Hittites;

1 Kings 11:1

It is not easy to serve God if you love strange women. Strange women are dangerous women who lead a person to hell. Solomon, in his older state, was even more addicted to these strange and dangerous women. You would have thought that a younger, more sexual, virile man would be going after strange women. Instead of the younger Solomon going after strange women, it was the older Solomon who fell to strange women. As the scripture says, “Hearken unto me now therefore, O ye children, and attend to the words of my mouth. Let not thine heart decline to her ways, go not astray in her paths. For she hath cast down many wounded: yea, many strong men have been slain by her. Her house is the way to hell, going down to the chambers of death.” (Proverbs 7:24-27)

6\. The older Solomon became an idol worshipper.

For Solomon went after Ashtoreth the goddess of the Zidonians, and after Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites.

1 Kings 11:5

When Solomon was a young man, he worshipped only God. When he was older, he served the gods of wood, the gods of stone, the gods of iron, the gods of gold and the gods of silver. How sad that this great wise man had fallen to the level of an idol worshipper. Solomon seemed to be wiser and stronger when he was a young man.

7\. The older Solomon built temples for idols.

Then did Solomon build an high place for Chemosh, the abomination of Moab, in the hill that is before Jerusalem, and for Molech, the abomination of the children of Ammon.

1 Kings 11:7

How sad I feel to report that the Solomon who built temples for God also built temples for Chemosh, and for Molech which were the abominations of the children of Moab and Ammon. Solomon would never have done this as a young man.

As you can see from all these examples, being a young man is not a bad thing. You can be ready@20 to do many good things for God. Solomon proved for all time that he did better as a young person than as older man.
