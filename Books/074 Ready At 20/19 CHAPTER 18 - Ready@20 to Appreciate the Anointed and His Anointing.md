### CHAPTER 18\

Ready@20 to Appreciate the Anointed and His Anointing\
===========================================================================

And David said unto the young man that told him, how knowest thou that Saul and Jonathan his son be dead? And the young man that told him said, as I happened by chance upon mount Gilboa, behold, Saul leaned upon his spear; and, lo, the chariots and horsemen followed hard after him. And when he looked behind him, he saw me, and called unto me. And I answered, Here am I. And he said unto me, who art thou? And I answered him, I am an Amalekite.

He said unto me again, Stand, I pray thee, upon me, and slay me: for anguish is come upon me, because my life is yet whole in me. So I stood upon him, and slew him, because I was sure that he could not live after that he was fallen: and I took the crown that was upon his head, and the bracelet that was on his arm, and have brought them hither unto my lord.

Then David took hold on his clothes, and rent them; and likewise all the men that were with him: And they mourned, and wept, and fasted until even, for Saul, and for Jonathan his son, and for the people of the Lord, and for the house of Israel; because they were fallen by the sword.

And David said unto the young man that told him, Whence art thou? And he answered, I am the son of a stranger, an Amalekite. AND DAVID SAID UNTO HIM, HOW WAST THOU NOT AFRAID TO STRETCH FORTH THINE HAND TO DESTROY THE LORD ‘S ANOINTED?

AND DAVID CALLED ONE OF THE YOUNG MEN, AND SAID, GO NEAR, AND FALL UPON HIM. AND HE SMOTE HIM THAT HE DIED.

2 Samuel 1:5-15

You must be ready@20 to appreciate the anointed and his anointing! The young man in the scripture above was called in to destroy someone who had touched the Lord’s anointed. Many people do not understand what the anointing is and why it must be appreciated and protected.

Saul was anointed by God and that is what changed him and made him a king. David valued the anointed and his anointing. David taught the young man a great lesson by asking him to kill someone who had touched the Lord’s anointed. David wanted the young man to appreciate the anointing upon the Lord’s anointed. That is why he said to the young man, “Were you not afraid to stretch forth your hand to destroy the Lord’s anointed?” You should be afraid to do anything negative to someone who is anointed.

If you do not value the anointing, it will not come near you. You will attract what you respect, what you cherish and what you value! Many people cherish education, nationality, societal status, wealth and a good family name. But they do not cherish the anointing. It is time for you to cherish what can make a difference in your life.

The anointing is the power of God that enables you to become something in the ministry. If you value the anointing, you will appreciate it, cherish it and attract it to yourself. Jesus became a powerhouse because He was anointed. You must remember that Jesus put on the form of a man. Jesus shed off His supernatural and superhuman abilities and took upon Himself the form of a mere man. A mere man is a weak, dishonourable and wretched person. When Jesus took up the form of a man, it was a great step of humility and sacrifice because few men have real dignity and purity.

It was necessary for Jesus to be anointed because He had taken up the form of a man. The anointing makes you super- human. The anointing makes you able to preach the gospel to the poor. Most of the world lives in poverty. Through the power of the anointing, you will be able to travel to where poor people live and minister Jesus to them.

Through the power of the anointing, you will be able to set the captives free.

Through the power of the anointing, you will be able to open the eyes of the blind.

Through the power of the anointing, you are able to proclaim amazing and great things that will come to pass.

The anointing is the God element in your life. The other elements of your life are your wretchedness, your humanness, your corruption, your weakness, your sins, your shortcomings, your false steps, your jealousies, your envies, your lusts and your unfounded pride.

The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised,

Luke 4:18

Jesus was extremely happy to announce that the Spirit of the Lord was upon Him. Jesus was happy to announce that He was anointed. Jesus understood the value of the anointing. The anointing changed Jesus from a mere man into a super human being with supernatural powers that could not be explained by anything or anyone.

To be ready@20, you must value the anointing and understand its worth. King David knew what the anointing was worth because it had changed him. David knew what the anointing had done to Saul. The anointing had transformed Saul into a great king.

When you respect the anointing, you are ready for ministry. You are not ready for ministry until you cherish and value the anointing! You are not ready for greatness in God until you respect the anointing. King David was shocked that someone would dare to kill a king upon whom the anointing oil had been poured.

The anointing is the presence of God Himself. The anointing is the God element in you.

When thou art departed from me to day, then thou shalt find two men by Rachel’s sepulchre in the border of Benjamin at Zelzah; and they will say unto thee, The asses which thou wentest to seek are found: and, lo, thy father hath left the care of the asses, and sorroweth for you, saying, What shall I do for my son? After that thou shalt come to the hill of God, where is the garrison of the Philistines: and it shall come to pass, when thou art come thither to the city, that thou shalt meet a company of prophets coming down from the high place with a psaltery, and a tabret, and a pipe, and a harp, before them; and they shall prophesy: And the Spirit of the LORD will come upon thee, and thou shalt prophesy with them, AND SHALT BE TURNED INTO ANOTHER MAN.

1 Samuel 10:1-6

Seven Reasons Why You Should Not Touch the Anointed

1\. You must not destroy the anointed because the anointing is what turns you into a captain and a leader of God’s people. Do not drive away what will turn you into a leader!

The Lord is anointing you to become a captain over God’s inheritance. The anointing makes you into a leader. If you touch the anointing, you are attacking the very thing that turns you into a leader.

Then Samuel took a vial of oil, and poured it upon his head, and kissed him, and said, Is it not because THE LORD HATH ANOINTED THEE TO BE CAPTAIN OVER HIS INHERITANCE?

1 Samuel 10:1

2\. You must not attack the anointed because the anointing helps you to recover all that is lost. Do not drive away what will cause you to recover all that is lost.

No one goes through life without losing things. Today, through the power of God and the anointing of God, you will recover everything that was lost by your past foolishness!

When thou art departed from me to day, then thou shalt find two men by Rachel’s sepulchre in the border of Benjamin at Zelzah; and they will say unto thee, The asses which thou wentest to seek are found: and, lo, thy father hath left the care of the asses, and sorroweth for you, saying, What shall I do for my son?

1 Samuel 10:2

3\. You must not touch the anointed and the anointing because the anointing is the power which will make you advance and go forward in life and ministry.

Then shalt thou go on forward from thence, and thou shalt come to the plain of Tabor, and there shall meet thee three men going up to God to Bethel, one carrying three kids, and another carrying three loaves of bread, and another carrying a bottle of wine:

1 Samuel 10:3

Do not drive away what will cause you to advance and go forward in ministry!

It is God who advances you in life. Without God, you will not make any progress and you will not step forward. “And Samuel said unto the people, IT IS THE LORD THAT ADVANCED MOSES and Aaron, and that brought your fathers up out of the land of Egypt” (1 Samuel 12:6).

4\. You must not touch the anointed and his anointing because the anointing is what will cause people to salute, you, to honour you and to receive you. Do not drive away what gives you honour. Do not attack the very thing that makes people recognize and receive you. Do not bite the hand that is feeding you.

And they will salute thee, and give thee two loaves of bread; which thou shalt receive of their hands.

1 Samuel 10:4

If you look into your life, you will see that no one ever honoured or recognised you until you became a servant of God. It is your connection to the Lord that brings honour and recognition. If you disconnect from the anointing, you will never be recognised. Saul was saluted and honoured in a way he had never been before. This honour came to him because he had been freshly anointed.

5\. You must not touch the anointing because it is the reason why you will have provision from God. Do not cut away the hand that is providing your needs. Do not pierce the fingers that are giving you your meals.

And they will salute thee, and give thee two loaves of bread; which thou shalt receive of their hands.

1 Samuel 10:4

When God anoints you with power to do His work, you also receive the financial power to accomplish His will. Financial power always goes along with the anointing to do exploits. If you are really anointed, you will notice that God sends the provision for you to do the exploits.

When you strike at the anointing you are striking at your wealth, your riches and your provisions. Do not rise up against the source of your well-being! Do not go mad just because you are angry! Do not let your anger turn you into a crazed and demonic version of what God has called you to be. Do not slice away the tender arms that are stretched out to care for you. Do not strike the very thing that blesses you with all that you need.

6\. You must not touch the anointed because that is what puts you into the right company.

After that thou shalt come to the hill of God, where is the garrison of the Philistines: and it shall come to pass, when thou art come thither to the city, that thou shalt meet a company of prophets coming down from the high place with a psaltery, and a tabret, and a pipe, and a harp, before them; and they shall prophesy:

1 Samuel 10:5

Saul joined the company of prophets because of the anointing on his life. He would never have had anything to do with prophets. Saul was very far from being a prophet. Through the power of God and the anointing, he suddenly found himself mixing with these amazing prophets of God. You will soon be walking in the right company because of the anointing of God on your life. Why would you attack something that takes you out of the company of licentious, wicked, immoral and perverted people and gives you a place amongst God’s prophets?

7\. You must not attack the anointing and the anointed because that is what changes you and turns you into another man. Do not frighten the anointing away!

And the Spirit of the LORD will come upon thee, and thou shalt prophesy with them, and shalt be turned into another man.

1 Samuel 10:6

God is turning you into another man! Don’t you want to be transformed and made into a completely different person? Those who are ready@20 and value the anointing, will be transformed.

Even at your very young age you can see that you need transformation. Without the power of God, you are doomed! David was horrified that someone would be bold enough to touch an anointed person. David knew all too well that it was the anointing that made the difference in his life.
