### CHAPTER 16\

Ready@20 to Be Anointed\
=============================================

AND SAMUEL SAID UNTO JESSE, ARE HERE ALL THY CHILDREN? AND HE SAID, THERE REMAINETH YET THE YOUNGEST, and, behold, he keepeth the sheep. And Samuel said unto Jesse, Send and fetch him: for we will not sit down till he come hither. And he sent, and brought him in. Now he was ruddy, and withal of a beautiful countenance, and goodly to look to. AND THE LORD SAID, ARISE, ANOINT HIM: FOR THIS IS HE. THEN SAMUEL TOOK THE HORN OF OIL, AND ANOINTED HIM IN THE MIDST OF HIS BRETHREN: and the Spirit of the LORD came upon David from that day forward. So Samuel rose up, and went to Ramah.

1 Samuel 16:11-13

You must be ready@20 to be anointed! David, the youngest son, was called out from his family and anointed. His life changed from that moment forward. When you are ready@20, you are ready to be chosen out of your family. Do not worry if you are the youngest in your family. When it comes to spiritual things, you may be the youngest but you may also be the one whom God has chosen.

One day I travelled to a certain country and was met by a delegation of young men. They ignored me and greeted everyone else who was with me. Then they asked where the bishop was. They were so embarrassed when they found out that I was the bishop. They apologised, explaining that they were expecting a much older person.

They had listened to my messages and thought I was a much older person. The truth is that I was, at the time, actually a very young person. When you speak the words of God, you always sound older and wiser than you actually are.

The anointing of God can turn the youngest into the oldest. David became the darling boy of Israel, even though he was the youngest in the family. The anointing of God on the youngest member of the family turned him into a national hero. When you are ready@20 you can do what the mature, the wise and the strong do. Being young does not detract from you!

Once the anointing is upon you, your age will not matter anymore. In Psalm 89, David describes how he was chosen when he was too young to be even considered. The anointing is an amazing thing.

I have found David my servant; with my holy oil have I anointed him:

With whom my hand shall be established: mine arm also shall strengthen him.

The enemy shall not exact upon him; nor the son of wickedness afflict him.

And I will beat down his foes before his face, and plague them that hate him.

But my faithfulness and my mercy shall be with him: and in my name shall his horn be exalted.

I will set his hand also in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers.

He shall cry unto me, Thou art my father, my God, and the rock of my salvation.

Also I will make him my firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth.

My mercy will I keep for him for evermore, and my covenant shall stand fast with him. His seed also will I make to endure for ever, and his throne as the days of heaven.

Psalm 89:20-29

1\. Ready@20 to be found by God.

I HAVE FOUND DAVID my servant; with my holy oil have I anointed him:

Psalm 89:20

God is choosing you right now! God is finding out where you are because He wants to anoint you. Even though you are the youngest, God has decided to pour His oil on you. I remember a vision someone had. In the vision, a cup full of oil was raised over his head. Just as the oil was to be poured on his head, a voice came from heaven and shouted, “Stop.”

In the vision, God changed His mind and decided not to pour the oil on the pastor’s head. God has to decide that He is going to pour His oil on you before you become anointed. The anointing will transform you into another man. God is always looking for young men upon whom He can pour His anointing.

2\. Ready@20 to be established and strengthened.

With whom my hand shall be ESTABLISHED: mine arm also shall STRENGTHEN him.

Psalm 89:21

Through the anointing every young man will be established. I was twenty-eight years old when I began the first project to build a cathedral for God. Establishment comes from God and through God. It is a curse to continue in a state of instability for a long time. Instability and being unsettled are things that are allowed to continue only for a season. You must not be unsettled forever!

God will settle you through the power of the anointing! God will settle you even though you are a young person. As the scripture says, “But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after that ye have suffered a while, make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you.” (1 Peter 5:10)

Even in your youth, you will be established because of the anointing.

3\. Ready@20 to overcome deception.

The enemy will not DECEIVE him, Nor will the son of wickedness afflict him.

Psalm 89:22

You can escape the deceptions in the world through the power of God. Satan is a deceiver! The strongest and most pervasive work of darkness in our world is deception. Almost every aspect of our society is riddled with deception. Our education, our politics and our financial systems are riddled with deceptions and based on deceptions.

Usually, the younger you are, the more deceived you are; but because of the anointing, you will be delivered from the deceptions around you. Pray every day for the Spirit of wisdom and understanding. Pray for the Spirit of truth so that you will no longer be deceived. Pray for the Spirit of revelation so that you will not be deceived. Pray for light so that you will not be deceived. The amount of light in you as a young person will keep you from deception.

Arise and shine @ 20 for your light has come. When light comes deception is banished and you can begin to shine brightly. Light drives away darkness and deception.

Gentiles will come to your light and kings to the brightness of your rising. (Isaiah 60:3)

4\. Ready@20 to overcome enemies.

And I will beat down his foes before his face, and plague them that hate him.

Psalm 89:23

The anointing gives power to every young person to overcome their enemies. I have encountered many enemies of my soul over the years. I have encountered people who hated me without a cause. When I was in my twenties someone told me he was sickened by the mention of my name. They hated me with a passion. They hated me without a cause! I have had people who have predicted my downfall since I was a youth.

I knew one pastor who repeatedly told his friend that I was going to fall and be destroyed. I could not believe that this man hated me so much and wished my downfall so earnestly. I have had people speak so viciously about me that it made me stay up all night in amazement and wonder.

Behold, all they that were incensed against thee shall be ashamed and confounded: they shall be as nothing; and they that strive with thee shall perish. Thou shalt seek them, and shalt not find them, even them that contended with thee: they that war against thee shall be as nothing, and as a thing of nought.

Isaiah 41:11-12

I have heard comments certain pastors made about me. I could literally feel the hatred and malice. Through the anointing of the Lord, your enemies will be beaten down. They will be crushed, according to the prophecy of Isaiah.

David also had many enemies. Take note that the anointed shall beat down his enemies. (Psalm 89:23) Because of the anointing you will enjoy victory after victory. At the end of David’s life, he wrote a psalm and outlined the victories God had granted him over his enemies. At the end of your life, you will realise that God has destroyed your enemies and humbled them before you. Encourage yourself with these ten prophecies that David gave concerning the enemies of the anointing.

Ten Predictions for the Enemies of the Anointed

1\. “He delivered me from my strong enemy, and from them that hated me: for they were too strong for me.” (2 Samuel 22:18)

2\. “The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness: according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.” (2 Samuel 22:21)

3\. “For by thee I have run through a troop: by my God have I leaped over a wall.” (2 Samuel 22:30)

4\. “Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy gentleness hath made me great. Thou hast enlarged my steps under me; so that my feet did not slip.” (2 Samuel 22:36-37)

5\. “I have pursued mine enemies, and destroyed them; and turned not again until I had consumed them. And I have consumed them, and wounded them, that they could not arise: yea, they are fallen under my feet.” (2 Samuel 22:38-39)

6\. “For thou hast girded me with strength to battle: them that rose up against me hast thou subdued under me.” (2 Samuel 22:40)

7\. “Thou hast also given me the necks of mine enemies, that I might destroy them that hate me.” (2 Samuel 22: 41)

8\. “Then did I beat them as small as the dust of the earth, I did stamp them as the mire of the street, and did spread them abroad.” (2 Samuel 22:43)

9\. “Thou also hast delivered me from the strivings of my people, thou hast kept me to be head of the heathen: a people which I knew not shall serve me.” (2 Samuel 22:44)

10\. “It is God that avengeth me, and that bringeth down the people under me, And that bringeth me forth from mine enemies: thou also hast lifted me up on high above them that rose up against me: thou hast delivered me from the violent man.” (2 Samuel 22:48-49)

5\. Ready@20 to enjoy God’s faithfulness and mercy.

But my faithfulness and my mercy shall be with him: and in my name shall his horn be exalted.

Psalm 89:24

Because of the anointing of the Lord, you will receive His mercy as a young person. A young person will need a lot of mercy. The younger you are, the more mistakes you can make! As you walk along with God expect His faithfulness to carry you through. God is faithful towards His young and anointed servant. He will lift you up and remember you.

6\. Ready@20 to cross rivers and the sea.

I will set his hand also in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers.

Psalm 89:25

Through the anointing you will become a young traveller. The anointing of the Lord will cause a young person to cross the rivers and the seas to bring the power of God to many. As I look back on the many places I have been to, I can only say that it is God’s power that took me to these places. I knew no one there and there was nothing in my background that linked me to these places.

You will soon be going to places that you do not know. There is no connection between you and those nations and yet you will be found there! God is going to work a work that will make the ears of those who hear to tingle.

I used to yearn for people to introduce me to some of the powerful people they knew in other countries but no one ever did. I remember wondering to myself, “How would I ever know any important person? Who would ever invite me to preach in another country?” And yet, without any connections, linkages and favours, God has taken me to many nations of the world to preach His Word. Do not despise your youthfulness!

7\. Ready@20 to be a great seed.

His seed also will I make to endure for ever, and his throne as the days of heaven.

Psalm 89:29

The power of God on your life causes even your children to be affected.

The anointing is an amazing blessing for all generations that will come out from you. Because you are anointed your seed will be remembered by God.

Because you are anointed, the generations after you will receive mercy and help.

God is not unrighteous to forget your labour of love. (Hebrews 6:10) He will reward you by calling your children.
