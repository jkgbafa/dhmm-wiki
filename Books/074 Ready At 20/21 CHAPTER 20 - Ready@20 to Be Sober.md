### CHAPTER 20\

Ready@20 to Be Sober\
==========================================

YOUNG MEN likewise exhort to be sober minded.

Titus 2:6

Be ready@20 to be sober! A sober person is controlled by the Holy Spirit. From today, nothing will control you except the Holy Spirit!

It is important to be sober! God wants to control you Himself. God does not want anything else to be in control of you. A sober person is not under the influence of anything or anyone. Unfortunately, many young people are under the influence of something other than God.

In this chapter, you will learn that God is fighting to take control of your life. He wants to own you! He wants to possess you! He does not want any of the usual influences to have any sway over your feelings, over your emotions and over your mind.

A sober person is marked by the seriousness and solemnity that characterize the ministry of a priest. A sober minded person has self-control. A sober minded person is free from the excesses that come from being under the control of something evil.

God has called us all to be kings and priests (Revelation 1:6). When you are ready@20, you are ready to live in such a way that you are always sober and not under the influence of alcohol, drugs, women, men or any other evil.

What It Means to be Sober

1\. To be sober means that you are not under the influence of alcohol.

Who hath woe? who hath sorrow? who hath contentions? who hath babbling? who hath wounds without cause? who hath redness of eyes? They that tarry long at the wine; they that go to seek mixed wine. Look not thou upon the wine when it is red, when it giveth his colour in the cup, when it moveth itself aright. At the last it biteth like a serpent, and stingeth like an adder.

Proverbs 23:29-32

Alcohol is a very common evil influence. I want you to believe that alcohol can lead you on a path of destruction.

Who has woes? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Who has sorrow? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Who has quarrels on end? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Who has senseless talk? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Who has been bitten by a snake? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Who has been stung by a viper? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Thine eyes shall behold strange women, and thine heart shall utter perverse things. Yea, thou shalt be as he that lieth down in the midst of the sea, or as he that lieth upon the top of a mast. They have stricken me, shalt thou say, and I was not sick; they have beaten me, and I felt it not: when shall I awake? I will seek it yet again.

Proverbs 23:33-35

Who is going to be attracted to strange women? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Whose heart will be corrupted and destroyed? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Who will see hallucinations and think he is lying down in the midst of the sea or upon the top of a mast? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Who will wake up and find out that he is feeling sick, even though he has no illness? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Who will wake up and feel that he has been beaten, even though he cannot remember whether it really happened? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Who will wake up from drunkenness and immediately look for another drink? The one who is under the influence of alcohol!

Indeed, the Bible is warning all and sundry to be careful of alcohol. You never know whether you will become an alcoholic or not. It is like playing with a snake. You may watch a snake handler play with a snake. But when you try playing with that snake, you may be bitten and die within twenty minutes. Alcohol is demons in solution! Don’t drink a solution of demons! Beware of danger!

You can live without drinking alcohol and not miss it. It is possible to live your life without drinking any alcohol at all. Drinking alcohol is an evil that you should not entertain. It is an evil that may grow in you and destroy you one day. When I was in secondary school, we had a teacher who was a drunkard. Everyone knew that this teacher had a great problem with drinking. His brother was a man of God who lived his life by preaching and teaching the word of God. When our teacher died of alcoholism, it shook me. I realised that he had been killed by this terrible habit. Perhaps, he started out thinking that he could control himself; but he ended up being consumed by this evil influence.

Today is the last day that you will give yourself to this evil! You are set free from alcoholism and other wicked bondages!

2\. To be sober means you are not under the influence of drugs.

All things are lawful unto me, but all things are not expedient: all things are lawful for me, but I will not be brought under the power of any.

1 Corinthians 6:12

Be sober! Be ready @20! You must not be under the influence of anything. You must not be brought under the power of anything evil. You are set free from the power of drugs. Be careful of anything that is addictive. It seeks to bring you under its power! It seeks to control you! Do not let anyone deceive you. Marijuana is a commonly used addictive drug that has been legalised in some countries. Marijuana is a well-known cause of schizophrenia. Cocaine is also well known for its evil effects. There are prescription drugs like pethidine which are dangerous because you can be addicted to them for life.

You must have a proper respect for drugs. An experiment was carried out in which some rats were turned into cocaine addicts by feeding them constantly with food mixed with cocaine. The rats became cocaine addicts and were kept in a room and starved of both cocaine and food. When the rats were released after some weeks of starvation, the rats that had not eaten for a long time, were offered food and cocaine. These rats, which had not eaten for three weeks, did not go near the food but rather went to the cocaine. They were more interested in having the cocaine than in eating. This experiment showed how addictive cocaine is. Today marks the end of your cocaine habit! The power of God sets you free from the demons of addiction!

Addictive drugs are things that young people experiment with. There are many people who have experimented with drugs and ended up on the rubbish dump of life.

Many years ago, there was a pile of heavy plywood in my father’s backyard. One day I decided to pull them out and see how many of them I could support with my hand. Each one I added increased the weight that I was bearing up with my hands. When I added the last one, I realised that I could no longer bear the weight of the heavy plywood. It was coming down on top of me. So I jumped away from the wood as fast as I could. The wood came crashing to the ground but my foot did not escape. I almost lost my toe in this accident. Why did I have this accident? Because I was trying to see how many pieces of plywood I could support with my hands. I always remember this experiment and accident and tell myself I do not want to find out how strong I am anymore!

Why do you want to find out how strong you are? Why do you want to tempt providence? Do you not realise that you may cross over and become an alcoholic or a drug addict whose life is destroyed?

I once met a young man who had a great future before him. God had called him and he was busy preaching and teaching the word of God. Unfortunately, he had gotten himself involved with some drugs. Instead of his life going towards the destiny God had called him to, he spent all his time, effort and money trying to free himself from these terrible drugs. Drugs are chemical demons. Every time you inject yourself or administer any of these addictive things, you are injecting yourself with evil spirits.

3\. To be sober means you are not under the influence of lust.

With her much fair speech she caused him to yield, with the flattering of her lips she forced him. He goeth after her straightway, as an ox goeth to the slaughter, or as a fool to the correction of the stocks; Till a dart strike through his liver; as a bird hasteth to the snare, and knoweth not that it is for his life. Hearken unto me now therefore, O ye children, and attend to the words of my mouth. Let not thine heart decline to her ways, go not astray in her paths. For she hath cast down many wounded: yea, many strong men have been slain by her. Her house is the way to hell, going down to the chambers of death.

Proverbs 7:21-27

To be sober is to be free from outside influences that direct, manipulate and guide you. Any girl who is under the influence of a man is not sober. Any boy who is under the influence of a girl is not sober.

In the story above, you will see how the strange woman is able to persuade a young man to come with her to her house. With charm, with her voice charged with sexual feelings, sexual moves, sexual sounds, sexual expressions of desire and a sexual touch, she would lead you to destruction. You must not be under the influence of lust. When you sense that you are experiencing strong desires for a woman or a man you must run away from it. Nowhere in the Bible are you taught to fight lust. We are taught to flee from lust.

Flee also youthful lusts: but follow righteousness, faith, charity, peace, with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.

2 Timothy 2:22

Lust is an animal desire for sexual indulgence. You must have a proper respect for your animal instincts and desires. The whole of creation has these instincts and desires. It is these instincts and desires that drive reproduction and ensure the populations of animals are kept at certain levels. If you do not respect these animal instincts they will overwhelm you, override you and lead you away from God’s plan for your life.

All human beings are born with these sexual desires. When you are young, you are most fertile and also most filled with lusts and desires for sex.

When a woman is filled with lust for a man, it will influence your thinking and she will not take the right decisions. When a man is filled with lust for a girl, he will not take the right decisions about life. This is why young people are supposed to keep themselves from influences that steal them away from the influence of the Holy Spirit.

These are they which were not defiled with women; for they are virgins. These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth. These were redeemed from among men, being the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb.

Revelation 14:4

You will notice in this scripture that people who are not defiled by women are able to follow the Lamb wherever He goes. The presence of a woman will surely influence a man. All men are influenced by women! All women are influenced by men! Women defile men by taking away their ability to follow the Holy Spirit. In the scripture above, you see that those who are able to follow the Lamb are those who are not defiled by women. Make sure that you are not defiled by women!

Today marks the end of the defilement of your ministry with the spirit of lust and animal desires!

4\. To be sober means you are not under the influence of money.

But they that will be rich fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition. For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

1 Timothy 6:9-10

Anyone with the love of money is not sober. God is raising up people who are ready@20 and who will be free from the influence of money. Perhaps you do not understand why the love of money is called the root of all evil. The love of money is called the root of all evil because it is the root of all evil!

There is hardly any problem that does not have its root in money or the love of money. Would you like to have a building with an evil root under the building? Would you like to have a garden with a large cobra living in the middle of the lawn? Certainly not! That is an evil root! You must uproot all evil roots from your life! You must not allow the desire for money to grow in you!

It is important to understand the eight different evils that can befall you because you have a strong desire for money.

But they that will be rich fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition. For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

1 Timothy 6:9-10

1\. Who are those who are going to fall into a snare? Those who love money and those who are under its influence!

2\. Who are those who are going to fall into temptation? Those who love money and those who are under its influence!

3\. Who are those who are going to fall into foolish lusts? Those who love money and those who are under its influence!

4\. Who are those who are going to fall into hurtful lusts? Those who love money and those who are under its influence!

5\. Who are those whose ministries are going to drown in destruction? Those who love money and those who are under its influence!

6\. Who are those whose ministries are going to drown in perdition? Those who love money and those who are under its influence!

7\. Who are those who are going to err from the faith? Those who love money and those who are under its influence!

8\. Who are those who are going to pierce themselves with many sorrows? Those who love money and those who are under its influence!
