### CHAPTER 6\

Ready@20 because You Were Born in the House\
=================================================================

His foundation is in the holy mountains.

The Lord loveth the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob.

Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God. Selah.

I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know me: behold Philistia, and Tyre, with Ethiopia; this man was born there.

And of Zion it shall be said, This and THAT MAN WAS BORN IN HER: and the highest himself shall establish her.

The Lord shall count, when he writeth up the people, that THIS MAN WAS BORN THERE. Selah.

As well the singers as the players on instruments shall be there: all my springs are in thee.

Psalm 87

You must be ready@20 if you were born in the house! The eighty-seventh psalm speaks of the spiritual blessings that follow young people who were born in the house.

Being born in the house is an important qualification for the service of God. Wherever you were born affects you. This is why many countries give you a passport if you are born in their country. When you are born in a country, it is assumed that you will grow up there. There is a prophetic blessing for those who were born in the house. If you were born in the church and grew up in the church, expect great things from God.

Being born in a particular church and growing up in that church to become a leader in the same church is a prophetic blessing God is giving you.

Blessings for Those Born in the House

1\. People born in the house are going to be greatly established.

And of Zion it shall be said, This and that man was born in her:

and THE HIGHEST HIMSELF SHALL ESTABLISH HER.

Psalm 87:5

It is far easier to be established in the things of God when you are born in the house. Prophetically you are destined to be established as a successful minister in the house of God.

Years ago, I would go to the horse races on Saturdays. In those days, the horses stood behind the net and when it was time for the race, the net would be raised suddenly and the horses would take off at top speed. Unfortunately, some of the horses would not be lined up at the right spot when the net went up. Sometimes, they were even facing the other direction when the net went up and they would have to turn around before starting their race.

Obviously, those horses that were not lined up properly and not facing the front would not have a good start. The way a horse started a race always determined which won the race. It was only very special horses that could overcome a bad start, catch up and win the race. The race for your ministry is just like that! When you are born in the house you have a good start for your life and ministry.

People born in the house are going to rise up and do well. Those born in the house will have special names. You will be remembered because you were born in the house.

I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know me: behold Philistia, and Tyre, with Ethiopia; this man was born there.

Psalm 87:4

2\. People born in the house will have a foundation in the holy mountains.

His foundation is in the holy mountains.

Psalm 87:1

Without holiness you cannot see God. Holiness is a great secret of ministry for young people. Jesus Christ was declared to be the Son of God with power by the Spirit of holiness. “And declared to be the Son of God with power, according to the spirit of holiness, by the resurrection from the dead:” (Romans 1:4) You will be declared to be a great man of God only by the Spirit of holiness.

Without sanctification, without purity, without holiness you are going nowhere in God. “Follow peace with all men, and holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord:” (Hebrews 12:14) Do not believe any modern doctrine that comes to belittle the importance of your purity and holiness before God.

When you are born in the house, you can grow up without having gone on any unholy excursions in the world. In the world, you will learn things that become habits. They become like social and sexual albatrosses that hang over your neck. In the world, you can receive a foundation of sexual habits and patterns that are difficult to break from. Many people develop sexual habits and addictions that make them unable to live a normal married life.

You may receive a foundation of addictions and negative attitudes from the world. A foul mouth, a seductive attitude and a treacherous character can be imparted by the company that you grew up with. People are corrupted by their interaction with one evil person. If you encounter one evil person from the world, your personality and temperament may be altered permanently.

What a blessing, therefore, it is to be born in the house of God! A young man who is born in the house will have his foundation in the holy mountain of God.

3\. People born in the house will have glorious things spoken of them.

Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God. Selah.

Psalm 87:3

Glorious things will be spoken of you in the ministry because you were born in the house. You will accomplish what grown-ups could not accomplish. Beauty will be found in your ministry. You will have a lasting foundation. Satan will threaten to destroy you with sex but you will stand sure because you have a solid foundation in holiness.

4\. People born in the house will be counted among the great.

The Lord shall count, when he writeth up the people, that this man was born there. Selah.

Psalm 87:6

You will be counted among those who are great in the house of God. You may not be included in the list of doctors or on the list of famous men. You may not make it to the Honours List. God will ensure that when the lists of His great men are made, those of you who were born in the house and had a good foundation of serving the Lord when you were young, will be included. It is amazing what a good foundation will do.

5\. People born in the house will produce good music. Singers and musicians will all be born in the house.

As well the SINGERS as the PLAYERS ON INSTRUMENTS shall be there: all my springs are in thee.

Psalm 87:7

Even the music in the church is different when you have people that were born in the church. All the children in the church will play instruments. All the children are singers. All the children are choristers. They grow up loving Christian music. They never know the depths of satanic music. They are never polluted by the music of the world.

What a blessing to have our own church music! We never need to go out there to find good music. God provides all the talents and the gifts through the children of the church.
