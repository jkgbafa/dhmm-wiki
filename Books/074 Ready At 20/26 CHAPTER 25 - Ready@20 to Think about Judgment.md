### CHAPTER 25\

Ready@20 to Think about Judgment\
======================================================

Rejoice, O young man, in thy youth; and LET THY HEART CHEER THEE IN THE DAYS OF THY YOUTH, and walk in the ways of thine heart, and in the sight of thine eyes: but know thou, that FOR ALL THESE THINGS GOD WILL BRING THEE INTO JUDGMENT. Therefore remove sorrow from thy heart, and put away evil from thy flesh: for childhood and youth are vanity.

Ecclesiastes 11:9-10

You must be ready@20 to consider eternal judgment! In this amazing scripture, young people are encouraged to have fun and do whatever they want. However, they are warned about the judgments that will come on young people for the things they do.

Most young people do not think about judgment. “Let’s have a good time! Let’s party all night long! Let’s drink! Let’s eat! Let’s take our ease! Let’s hang out! Let’s have fun!”

Most young people do not think about the consequences of their actions. Indeed, most young people do not think about eternity. But you are going to be different! You are ready@20 to go to war for God! You are ready@20 to think about judgment. You are a different kind of young person whose heart and mind are fixed on God.

Once God has called you, your attention must turn towards judgment. It is your thoughts and concerns about judgment that change the way you behave.

When I was in secondary school, there were two types of students. There were those whose minds were fixed on the looming exams and there were many carefree students whose minds and hearts were fixed on the other activities that existed in the school.

In our school, you could play hockey, volleyball, football and cricket. Indeed, there were many extra-curricular activities. You could join the cadet corps, you could join the Scripture Union or you could go to the gym. There were also loads of athletics: hundred metres, four hundred metres, eight hundred metres, one thousand five hundred metres and five thousand metre races. We had the shot putt, javelin, long jump, triple jump and high jump. There was also a marathon race through the forest that was called the cross-country race. Indeed, many of us were engaged in all these amazing activities.

Unfortunately, all these activities did not contribute to our academic scores. Many of these activities only served to distract students from the judgment day, which is exam day. Unfortunately, there were no exams on one hundred and four hundred metre races. There were only exams in biology, chemistry, physics, maths, English, history, geography, etc. If your thoughts and attention did not turn towards biology, chemistry, maths, history, etc., you would be shocked on the day of examinations.

If your thoughts and attention do not turn towards the judgment day, you will live a carefree life and be shocked on the judgment day. Most young people are attracted to the extra-curricular activities of this life without taking thought to the Judgment Day that is looming.

Your readiness for the ministry is helped by you thinking about judgment. Thinking about death will always make you think about eternity and judgment. There are specific judgments that a young person must be concerned about.

1\. Young people must be concerned about the judgments for a lustful and immoral life.

Flee fornication. Every sin that a man doeth is without the body; but he that committeth fornication sinneth against his own body.

1 Corinthians 6:18

Immorality is a habit that starts from youth and grows with you into adulthood. Young people are filled with lusts. If you allow those lusts to consume you, you will become a depraved person. When you commit fornication, you sin against your own body. This means that you harm your body.

Fornication is a strike against your own body and your own life. You must think carefully about the judgments that come upon all those who toy with the idea of fornication. If you develop the habit of committing fornication as a young person, you will become an established adulterer in your old age. You will not have a stable marriage or a loving relationship. Through a lifestyle of fornication at an early age, you may be doomed to instability, uncertainty and immorality in the future.

2\. Young people must be concerned about judgments for a lying and deceptive life.

What shall be given unto thee? or what shall be done unto thee, thou false tongue? Sharp arrows of the mighty, with coals of juniper.

Psalm 120:3-4

Lying and deception are habits that develop from youth. You must be ready@20 to be free from deception. The habits of lying and deception can stay with you until they have consumed you and destroyed everything about you. The judgment for lying and deception is a curse. All liars are subject to the curse of having “sharp arrows of the mighty” and “coals of juniper” poured on them. Whatever that means, it does not sound good. Avoid becoming a chronic and habitual liar. Fight the curse of becoming a deceiver. Do everything to prevent yourself from becoming a pretender, an actor and a hypocrite!

3\. Young people must be concerned about judgments for disobedience.

Though he were a Son, yet learned he obedience by the things which he suffered;

Hebrews 5:8

It is important for young people to learn about obedience. It takes a long time to discover that obedience is better than any other sacrificial effort you make. The judgment for disobedience can be found in the twenty-eighth chapter of Deuteronomy. There are almost sixty-eight verses of warnings about the curses that will befall those who disobey the Law. Instead of living a carefree life, those who are ready@20 begin to concern themselves with the judgments that will befall those who disobey the word of God.

4\. Young people must be concerned about judgments in eternity.

For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ; that every one may receive the things done in his body, according to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad.

2 Corinthians 5:10

What will you do when you see Jesus face to face? Will He be pleased with you?

If you want Jesus to be pleased with you on Judgment Day, you must serve Him whilst you are young. When you are older, your mind will come to eternal judgment because eternity will be looming. It is unusual for a young person to think of death. It is unusual for a young person to think of eternal judgment. Most young people think that they will live forever.

If you are ready@20, you must keep your mind on eternal judgment. Keep thinking about the possibility of appearing suddenly in eternity.

One day I had an accident when I was driving to the northern part of Ghana. Our car somersaulted and we narrowly escaped death. I realised that I could have been in eternity without even knowing what had happened. The accident happened within a few seconds. One minute earlier everything was normal. The next minute we were upside down and almost dead.

From that time, I considered deeply the possibility of dying suddenly without being given any notice. I decided to live my life by always thinking of a sudden and unexpected departure from this earth. This thought has made me take many wise decisions for my life and ministry. I always thought about what would happen if I were not there. Through thoughts of judgment, my life and ministry were upgraded tenfold. Thoughts of judgment will not kill you. Preparing for eternity is not a sign of impending death. Always preparing for a sudden departure to eternity creates an unusual kind of wisdom that brings stability and wisdom to your life and ministry.

The Bible has given us the rules for eternal judgment. Those who will be cast outside into the lake of fire are clearly listed in the Bible. The fearful, the unbelieving, the idolaters and liars will all have their part in the lake of fire. Make sure that you do not allow any of these negative traits to grow in you until they take you away from heaven.

But the fearful, and unbelieving, and the abominable, and murderers, and whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, shall have their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: which is the second death.

Revelation 21:8
