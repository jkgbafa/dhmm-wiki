### CHAPTER 12\

Ready@20 to Be a Good Example\
===================================================

LET NO MAN DESPISE THY YOUTH; BUT BE THOU AN EXAMPLE of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

1 Timothy 4:12

You must be ready@20 to be a good example! A young man can be a good example. Most of the time, we expect the older people to be the good example. A young person can also be a good example. There are five areas that a young person can set a good example.

1\. Ready@20 to be an example in the Word.

Let no man despise thy youth; but BE THOU AN EXAMPLE of the believers, IN WORD, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

1 Timothy 4:12

A young person can be an example in the Word in a way that older people cannot. A young person can memorise scriptures in an amazing way. The younger you are, the more scriptures you can commit to memory. I have watched young children quote entire books of the Bible. I have seen religious schools where the young people are memorising their entire holy book.

I was once in Israel where I saw young children memorising the Torah. Older people cannot memorise scripture in the same way. Open your heart to become the best you can at memorising scripture. By being a good example in the Word, many other young people will follow you and also begin to know the Holy Scriptures.

2\. Ready@20 to be a good example in behaviour.

Let no man despise thy youth; but BE THOU AN EXAMPLE of the believers, in word, IN CONVERSATION, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

1 Timothy 4:12

Paul asked Timothy to be an example in his behaviour (conversation). The behaviour of a young man can be a good example for others to follow. As you pattern your life after the word of God, you become a good example for someone to follow. Always ask yourself, “Is what I am doing a good example for others to follow?”

3\. Ready@20 to be a good example in charity.

Let no man despise thy youth; BUT BE THOU AN EXAMPLE of the believers, in word, in conversation, IN CHARITY, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

1 Timothy 4:12

As a young person, you will develop the ability to forgive and forget. Charity speaks of love. Love always has to do with forgiveness and letting things go. Older people remember many thing. They are more bitter and more unforgiving. A younger person has a greater chance of being a good example in the area of love.

4\. Ready@20 to be a good example in the Spirit.

Let no man despise thy youth; but BE THOU AN EXAMPLE of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, IN SPIRIT, in faith, in purity.

1 Timothy 4:12

A young man can be a good example in the Spirit. The Spirit has to do with the anointing. You can be anointed with the Holy Ghost as a young person and move in the things of the Spirit.

A young person can have a very sensitive heart and flow seamlessly between the Spirit world and the natural world. As a young person you are more likely to believe in and flow with the anointing.

Your young, soft and tender heart will believe easily in the supernatural. Is it any wonder that when the Lord used Kathryn Kuhlman powerfully, her mantle did not fall upon older seventy-year olds, sixty-year olds or fifty-year olds? The mantle of God and the anointing passed on to Benny Hinn who was only twenty-four years old when Kathryn Kuhlman died. Thereafter, Benny Hinn became an example in the Spirit and anointing of healing, miracles and manifestations! You must be ready@20 to be an example in the Spirit.

5\. Ready@20 to be a good example in faith.

Let no man despise thy youth; BUT BE THOU AN EXAMPLE of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, IN FAITH, in purity.

1 Timothy 4:12

A young person tends to believe things much more easily. Remember how little children believe in “Father Christmas” flying through the air to bring them presents. Little children believe all that they are told. Little children believe all the bedtime stories they are told. I used to tell my children bedtime stories all the time. Sometimes, I would start a story and not know how it would end. I would keep inventing the next episode, creating marvellous escapes for the characters in the stories. The children would be enchanted and they would want to hear the story again.

One of their favourite stories was the story of a snake that hitched a ride on the back of a horse to cross a river. Why did I keep telling these stories? Because my children believed them! Indeed, young people believe the Bible much more than the older. If you are twenty years old, you are ready to be an example in faith. Believe the word of God. Believe in the Great Commission. Believe in witnessing! Believe in evangelism! Believe in the promises of God!

6\. Ready@20 to be an example in purity.

Let no man despise thy youth; but BE THOU AN EXAMPLE of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, IN PURITY.

1 Timothy 4:12

A young man can also be an example in purity. Older people usually have less lustful feelings and desires. It is when you are a young man that you have a chance to be an example in purity. As a young man, you will constantly be experiencing urges and cravings for the opposite sex. It is your best chance to show yourself as an example in purity. When you are old, tired, withered, stooped over and without feelings, you will not need so much to be an example in purity. This is your chance to demonstrate that you can live free of fornication, free of immorality, free of lasciviousness and free of wickedness.

Joseph was seventeen years old when he was captured and taken as a slave in Potiphar’s house. Joseph was eighteen years old when Potiphar’s wife tried to have sex with him. He had been living in Potiphar’s house for one year when the incident happened. He spent twelve years in prison because of the false accusation of immorality. A few years later, after going to prison for some time, Joseph became prime minister at the age of thirty. “And Joseph was thirty years old when he stood before Pharaoh king of Egypt. And Joseph went out from the presence of Pharaoh, and went throughout all the land of Egypt.” (Genesis 41:46)
