### CHAPTER 27\

Ready@20 to Be a Nazarite\
===============================================

Also I brought you up from the land of Egypt, and led you forty years through the wilderness, to possess the land of the Amorite. AND I RAISED UP OF YOUR SONS FOR PROPHETS, AND OF YOUR YOUNG MEN FOR NAZARITES. Is it not even thus, O ye children of Israel? saith the Lord.

Amos 2:10-11

Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, WHEN EITHER MAN OR WOMAN SHALL SEPARATE THEMSELVES TO VOW A VOW OF A NAZARITE, TO SEPARATE THEMSELVES UNTO THE LORD:

HE SHALL SEPARATE HIMSELF FROM WINE AND STRONG DRINK, and shall drink no vinegar of wine, or vinegar of strong drink, neither shall he drink any liquor of grapes, nor eat moist grapes, or dried.

All the days of his separation shall he eat nothing that is made of the vine tree, from the kernels even to the husk.

All the days of the vow of his separation there shall no razor come upon his head: until the days be fulfilled, in the which he separateth himself unto the Lord, he shall be holy, and shall let the locks of the hair of his head grow.

All the days that he separateth himself unto the Lord he shall come at no dead body.

He shall not make himself unclean for his father, or for his mother, for his brother, or for his sister, when they die: because the consecration of his God is upon his head.

All the days of his separation he is holy unto the Lord.

Numbers 6:2-8

You must be ready@20 to become a Nazarite! A Nazarite is a man with a special calling. A Nazarite will not make himself unclean or unholy for any reason. A Nazarite was a special person who abstained from many things, in order that he would be holy and special to the Lord. He was bound by a vow to leave his hair uncut and to abstain from wine and strong drink. A Nazarite is someone who would practice an extraordinary level of purity and devotion.

The ministry of a Nazarite reveals that there are people whom God calls for special things. Sometimes, a calling requires a very high level of devotion and separation. To whom much is given, much is expected.

But he that knew not, and did commit things worthy of stripes, shall be beaten with few stripes. For unto whomsoever much is given, of him shall be much required: and to whom men have committed much, of him they will ask the more.

Luke 12:48

Are you ready for this extraordinary call? Are you ready for the separation? Are you ready for holiness? Are you ready to pay the price? Samson is an example of a Nazarite given in the Bible. His purpose was to dedicate himself and make himself holy to God.

Apostle Paul said that fornication should not be mentioned even once. Today, lots of people have been involved in fornication a few times. Most people have been involved in different sins that pollute them and change their spiritual orientation and rank.

But fornication, and all uncleanness, or covetousness, let it not be once named among you, as becometh saints;

Ephesians 5:3

A Nazarite vows a part of his life or his entire life to God. Apostle Paul may have been a kind of Nazarite. His dedication to God was phenomenal and few have matched what he did. There are two occasions in which he apparently took a special vow. This may have been a Nazarite vow. The first time Paul took this vow he was at Cenchrea, on his way to Syria.

And Paul after this tarried there yet a good while, and then took his leave of the brethren, and sailed thence into Syria, and with him Priscilla and Aquila; having shorn his head in Cenchrea: for he had a vow.

Acts 18:18

On another occasion, at the Feast of Pentecost, Paul took upon himself again the Nazarite vow.

Do therefore this that we say to thee: We have four men which have a vow on them; Them take, and purify thyself with them, and be at charges with them, that they may shave their heads: and all may know that those things, whereof they were informed concerning thee, are nothing; but that thou thyself also walkest orderly, and keepest the law. As touching the Gentiles which believe, we have written and concluded that they observe no such thing, save only that they keep themselves from things offered to idols, and from blood, and from strangled, and from fornication. Then Paul took the men, and the next day purifying himself with them entered into the temple, to signify the accomplishment of the days of purification, until that an offering should be offered for every one of them.

Acts 21:23-26

Apostle Paul stayed away from marriage and espoused the highest kinds of moral principles. Paul continued to warn Christians that they should not indulge in fornication even once. Today, that is a very high standard that many do not keep to. Apostle Paul warned the church constantly against all forms of sexual immorality. Apostle Paul may have been a special kind of minister with the highest standards whom God used as a Nazarite.

Apostle Paul described his ministry as a special one with a special calling. He was given an abundance of revelation. He was given a commission to build churches. He was used by God specially. Paul’s ministry has reached down through the centuries and affected many people.

Do you want the Lord to use you the same way that He used Paul? You may have to pay the price and become specially separated from many things so that God can use you.

God is going to raise up Nazarites who are ready@20. Would you be one of those in whom certain pollutions will never be mentioned, not even once?

The grace of God which brings salvation is coming into your life and raising you up as the next, specially separated apostle, to the world. Keep your vows! Keep your promises to God! You will be the next person with much fruit to present to the Lord.
