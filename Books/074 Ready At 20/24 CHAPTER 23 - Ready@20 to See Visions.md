### CHAPTER 23\

Ready@20 to See Visions\
=============================================

And it shall come to pass in the last days, saith God, I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh: and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, AND YOUR YOUNG MEN SHALL SEE VISIONS, and your old men shall dream dreams:

Acts 2:17

You must be ready@20 to see visions! Young people are supposed to see visions. When you are ready@20, you will be filled with visions and dreams from the Holy Spirit.

Having visions and dreams can be compared to speaking in tongues. Speaking in tongues and prophesying are the evidence of receiving the Holy Spirit. However, you will notice that the prophecy of the Holy Spirit does not mention speaking in tongues but rather, having visions and dreams.

A young person who is ready @20 to serve the Lord must be filled with visions from the Lord. You must believe in visions and you must set your heart on them.

And the man said unto me, Son of man, behold with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears, and SET THINE HEART UPON ALL THAT I SHALL SHEW THEE; for to the intent that I might shew them unto thee art thou brought hither: declare all that thou seest to the house of Israel.

Ezekiel 40:4

The first reason for visions for young people is for them to set your heart on certain things that God is showing them. In this amazing scripture, Ezekiel is told to set his heart on the vision that God had given him. You must ponder on the things that God shows you. You will see dimly!

Do not expect to see things clearly! When you get to heaven you will see clearly! For now, you are expected to make do with the hazy pictures that you see. CCTV cameras often show a hazy picture of things. Those who are used to looking at these pictures get used to the poor picture quality and are able to identify important things that help in security. Similarly, you must get used to the poor picture quality of the visions we have on earth.

God has decided that the visions and dreams that you see will have a poor quality; so that only those with a real zeal for God can find out what they mean.

The second reason for visions for young people is so that they will declare all the will of God and accomplish His purpose on earth. Notice what the scripture says:

And the man said unto me, Son of man, behold with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears, and set thine heart upon all that I shall shew thee; for to the intent that I might shew them unto thee art thou brought hither: DECLARE ALL THAT THOU SEEST TO THE HOUSE OF ISRAEL.

Ezekiel 40:4

You must declare all that you see! You must do all that God shows you!

Two Types of Visions for Young People

1\. “Visions of the Night” for those who are ready@20

These are experiences that you have whilst you are sleeping. A vision of the night is also called a dream. Visions of the night are critical for your ministry. You will not go far without dreams and visions of the night. Many people despise dreams! Dreams are considered to be lower experiences that many carnal people also have. Perhaps the greatest warning against this kind of thinking is found in the life of Joseph, the husband of Mary. Joseph had three significant dreams in which he was directed to accept Mary as his wife. He was also directed by a vision to escape to Egypt and also to come back to live in Nazareth. It is important that you accept dreams as an important part of the spiritual guidance for your life.

NOW A THING WAS SECRETLY BROUGHT TO ME, AND MINE EAR RECEIVED A LITTLE THEREOF. IN THOUGHTS FROM THE VISIONS OF THE NIGHT, WHEN DEEP SLEEP FALLETH ON MEN, Fear came upon me, and trembling, which made all my bones to shake. Then a spirit passed before my face; the hair of my flesh stood up: It stood still, but I could not discern the form thereof: an image was before mine eyes, there was silence, and I heard a voice, saying...

Job 4:12-16

According to Job, God speaks in visions of the night when deep sleep falls on you. During these visions of the night, you see spirits and interact with the spirit world. There are times God will ask you to go to bed so that He can speak to you. There are times God will cause you to sleep so that He can speak to you.

When you wait on God, you will discover that times of deep sleep are not necessarily times of rest but also times of deep communication with God. Paul had a vision of the night like this, when he saw a man calling him to come to Macedonia.

And a vision appeared to Paul in the night; There stood a man of Macedonia, and prayed him, saying, Come over into Macedonia, and help us.

Acts 16:9

2\. “Visions of the Day” for those who are ready@20

“On the morrow, as they went on their journey, and drew nigh unto the city, Peter went up upon the housetop to pray about the sixth hour: And he became very hungry, and would have eaten: but while they made ready, he fell into a trance, And saw heaven opened, and a certain vessel descending unto him, as it had been a great sheet knit at the four corners, and let down to the earth: Wherein were all manner of fourfooted beasts of the earth, and wild beasts, and creeping things, and fowls of the air. And there came a voice to him, Rise, Peter; kill, and eat. But Peter said, Not so, Lord; for I have never eaten any thing that is common or unclean. And the voice spake unto him again the second time, What God hath cleansed, that call not thou common. This was done thrice: and the vessel was received up again into heaven. Now while Peter doubted in himself what this vision which he had seen should mean, behold, the men which were sent from Cornelius had made enquiry for Simon’s house, and stood before the gate” (Acts 10:9-17).

In visions of the day, God will show you a picture and speak to you as He would speak with you in a vision of the night. When this happens during the day, your senses may be suspended for a while. Visions of the day are more memorable and you will remember them better.

Usually, visions of the day are important for your direction in the ministry. As you can see, Apostle Peter was directed on what to do in the ministry. Very soon, God will be giving you a vision that will guide your whole life and ministry.

I once had a vision in which I saw a man asking me to hold crusades and pray for the sick. I was wide awake sitting in my study when I received this amazing direction for my life and ministry.

I also received a vision one day whilst sitting in my chair. I asked about how I could be fruitful. Suddenly, I saw a hand holding a book. I was stunned! The Holy Spirit was telling me the books would be the way to bear much fruit. I suddenly knew that I should spend a lot of time and energy to bear fruit through the books.

You are going to have many visions and dreams!

Today marks the beginning of a flood of visions that you are about to receive!
