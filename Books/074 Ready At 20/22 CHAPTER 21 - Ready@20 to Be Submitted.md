### CHAPTER 21\

Ready@20 to Be Submitted\
==============================================

Likewise, ye YOUNGER, SUBMIT YOURSELVES UNTO THE ELDER. Yea, all of you be subject one to another, and be clothed with humility: for God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble.

1 Peter 5:5

You must be ready@20 to be submitted! Many young people are filled with the spirit of rebellion. They are not happy to submit to authority. In these last days, God is raising you up as someone who is ready to submit to authority. Instead of being one of the rebellious youth in the world, you will be one of the young people who are mature enough to be submitted to authority.

Submission is not an evil thing. Submission is not an evil word. Those who do not understand submission make it out to be evil and therefore rise up against it. Satan is the author of rebellion! Satan is the one who accuses and attacks God! Satan is the accuser of the brethren! Satan is the one who rises up against established authority! Satan stirs up people to hate authority! It is satan who has given submission a bad name!

There is nothing wrong with the concept of submission. In these last days, many young people are going to flow with the idea of being submitted. Satan has stirred up wives to become rebellious against their husbands. Many Christian wives are in rebellion to their husbands’ ideas and ways of doing things. Many assistant and associate pastors are stirred up by satan to rise up against the authority of their senior pastor. The inability to submit is the reason for the disloyalty and confusion in many churches.

Instead of young people being the best example of proud and unsubmissive people, those who are ready@20 will become well known for their good flow and their submission.

What exactly does it mean to submit?

What It Means to be Submissive

1\. To submit is to bow:

For it is written, As I live, saith the Lord, every knee shall bow to me, and every tongue shall confess to God.

Romans 14:11

Proud people will not bow! Big people cannot bow! Those who are ready@20 are ready to bow, ready to bend their knees in submission. To bow to someone is to salute, to give reverence and to give recognition to the person.

Those who are ready@20 will be ready to recognise and acknowledge those who are ahead of them. There is a generation that does not acknowledge the greatness of all the people whom God has used. They do not acknowledge the power of God. They do not want to submit to the great things that God has done. Soon, a powerful generation of submissive young men will arise and acknowledge the great things God is doing through others. By acknowledging what God has used others to do, they will catch the anointing.

When Napoleon invaded Russia, he desperately needed King Alexander of Russia to bow down and submit to him. Unfortunately, when Napoleon invaded Moscow, no one came to receive him or make any arrangements for the takeover of the city. When a victorious general conquered a city, the civil authorities would customarily present themselves at the gate of the city with keys to the city and make arrangements for the accommodation and feeding of the invading soldiers.

The king of the conquered city was expected to bow to the victorious general but King Alexander was nowhere to be found. This denied Napoleon of a traditional victory in which the king would bow to him. Instead of submitting to Napoleon, most of the population, including Alexander the king were nowhere to be found. The Russians opened the doors of the prisons, setting all the prisoners free. Then, they set fire to the city and burned down eighty per cent of the city of Moscow.

As Napoleon sat there in the ruins and ashes of Moscow, with no prospect of King Alexander coming to bow down to him, he had no other option but to withdraw his troops and return to France. The Russians had refused to bow down to Napoleon and therefore had refused to submit themselves to him. He could reign over ashes if he wanted to.

2\. To submit is to flow:

Lift up thine eyes round about, and see: all they gather themselves together, they come to thee: thy sons shall come from far, and thy daughters shall be nursed at thy side. Then thou shalt see, and flow together, and thine heart shall fear, and be enlarged; because the abundance of the sea shall be converted unto thee, the forces of the Gentiles shall come unto thee.

Isaiah 60:4-5

In these last days, many young people will be ready to flow along. Those who are ready@ 20 will flow along the stream of the Holy Spirit. To flow means to move steadily in a stream. Flowing brothers and sisters will fill the church. Someone who is flowing will circulate and easily move from one nation to another to do the work of God.

Open your heart and become a flowing person because young people are far more ready to keep moving. In marriage, those who are ready@ 20 will be constantly moving. They will not lie in bed like dead bodies freshly brought from the mortuary for a wakekeeping service. Keep moving because you are not dead. Being submissive and flowing means you are circulating and moving.

3\. To submit is to yield:

Neither yield ye your members as instruments of unrighteousness unto sin: but yield yourselves unto God, as those that are alive from the dead, and your members as instruments of righteousness unto God.

Romans 6:13

Those who are ready@ 20 will yield. To yield is to give up as to a superior power or authority. Young people will recognize that there are higher authorities that are worth submitting to and yielding to. Many older people are not prepared to give in to a higher authority. Unsubmissive people have arguments that last for hours. They have meetings that go on and on. Indeed, younger people must have the wisdom that comes from above and is easy to be entreated. “But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy” (James 3:17). Begin to walk in the wisdom of submission. Begin to be easy to be entreated. Begin to yield yourself!

4\. To submit is to agree:

Can two walk together, except they be agreed?

Amos 3:3

Be ready@20 to agree! Indeed, no two people can flow together unless they are agreed on many things. Also, no two people can agree together unless one of them is submitted to the other.

In a home where the parents do not agree on issues, it is because there is no submission. The presence of opposing arguments and disagreeing partners is evidence that submission does not exist. Most Christian wives will say that they are submissive but this is how to catch them out. The fact that there is disagreement all the time is the greatest evidence that there is a wife who is not submissive. As the years go by, the topics that are discussed in a home where there is no submission become fewer and fewer because there are fewer and fewer topics where there is agreement.

God is raising up young people who are going to be filled with the grace of agreement.

5\. To submit is to say, “Yes”:

Then the king of Israel called all the elders of the land, and said, Mark, I pray you, and see how this man seeketh mischief: for he sent unto me for my wives, and for my children, and for my silver, and for my gold; AND I DENIED HIM NOT.”

1 Kings 20:7

Saying, “Yes” is a great sign of submission. When King Benhadad of Syria came to Israel, he requested to have the king of Israel’s wives, children, silver and gold. The king of Israel said “Yes” to all the requests of Benhadad, king of Syria. By not denying the requests of Benhadad, the king of Israel was submitting to the king of Syria. There should have been no war because the king of Israel had submitted by not saying “No.” Saying “Yes” is always a sign of submission. A proud person will say “No” and it will open the door for a war to determine who is stronger and who is greater.

Many proud people never say, “Yes.” Their reflex response is “No!” They seem to say “No” to everything!

“No” to staying at home!

“No” to the new idea!

“No” to the new suggestion!

“No” to joining the church!

“No” to being happy!

“No” to going out for lunch!

“No” to cooking at home!

“No” to having sex once!

“No” to having sex twice!

“No” to having fun!

“No” to watching a film!

“No” to eating now!

“No” to eating hot food!

“No” to eating cold food!

“No” to sleeping early!

“No” to sleeping late!

“No” to changing the dress!

“No” to wearing the same dress!

“No” to wearing different shoes!

“No” to wearing the same shoes!

“No” to wearing a wig!

“No” to changing the hairstyle!

“No” to hurrying up!

“No” to tidying up!

“No” to working faster!

“No” to getting someone to help!

“No” to sacking someone!

“No” to the disciplinary measures!

“No” to pardoning the sin!

“No” to instituting punishment!

“No” to the time of starting work!

“No” to the closing time!

“No” TO EVERYTHING!

Watch out for those who never flow and never say “Yes.” It may seem like a difference in opinion but it is actually a revelation of a non-submissive spirit. A lack of submission is a revelation of rebellion against authority. Rebellion against authority is a revelation of satan’s presence.

Remember that most of satan’s activities are subtle and well thought out. Satan beguiled Eve through his subtlety. Subtlety is a big characteristic of satan. Slow, gentle, not easy to detect steps are always taken when satan is raising up a deceptive scheme against you.

But I fear, lest by any means, as the serpent beguiled Eve through his subtilty, so your minds should be corrupted from the simplicity that is in Christ.

2 Corinthians 11:3

Satan rarely has a one-step plan. His deceptions are huge and take many years to implement. The western world did not turn away from God in one day. The western world has turned away from God over hundreds of years. In the name of science and with cleverly crafted statements, millions of people have been turned away from God. This process of turning away from God was so subtle that it was not noticed when it was happening.

6\. To submit is to accept:

And he said, Verily I say unto you, No prophet is accepted in his own country.

Luke 4:24

A prophet is not accepted in his own country because people are not going to submit to someone they know so well. When a prophet arrives in his own country, people begin to size him up and soon they declare him unfit and unqualified to lead them, to inspire them and to speak to them. “Who are you? We do not accept you. We do not accept carpenters who turn themselves into self-appointed prophets!” (Luke 4:22).

Your ability to accept someone depends on your ability to submit to him.

When you do not accept certain things, it is because of your pride. Acceptance is a revelation of your humility. In life, you will be forced to accept many things. You will be forced to accept things that you do not like. You will be forced to accept many things that God brings your way. Your submission to the will of God will be tested by your ability to accept certain things.

Marriage brings you closest to the concept of acceptance. Through marriage, you will be brought to your knees and made to accept the temperaments, ways, behaviour and lifestyle of your partner.

Your partner may be unspiritual and you will be forced to accept it . Your partner may be rebellious and you may be forced to accept it. Your partner may be a poor performer and you will be forced to accept it. Accepting all these will reveal the extent to which you are submissive.

7\. To submit is to bend:

The sons also of them that afflicted thee shall come bending unto thee; and all they that despised thee shall bow themselves down at the soles of thy feet; and they shall call thee, The city of the LORD, The Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

Isaiah 60:14

Bending is a sign of submission. Being flexible is a sign of submission and humility. When you bend over, you agree with someone that he is victorious and you are not. Bowing almost always goes along with bending.

In the last days, many will be ready to bend over so that they can do the will of God. Proud people cannot bend. Young people are going to be happy in their marriages because they have flexible wives who bend and bow with ease.

In the last days a new generation of bending, bowing, flowing, yielding children are going to rise up to do the work of God.
