### CHAPTER 9\

Ready@20 to Be an Armour Bearer\
=====================================================

AND JONATHAN SAID TO THE YOUNG MAN THAT BARE HIS ARMOUR, Come, and let us go over unto the garrison of these uncircumcised: it may be that the Lord will work for us: for there is no restraint to the Lord to save by many or by few.

1 Samuel 14:6

You must be ready@20 to be an armour bearer! An armour bearer is basically an assistant to a warrior. An armour bearer existed to carry the heavy armour that was needed by the fighting soldier. The armour bearer would not really fight himself but observe what happens in real life battles.

When you are a young man, God can train you by giving you a role as an armour bearer. You may not be involved in the fight yourself but you will see many battles at close quarters.

Whilst your master is fighting, you will learn from the kind of wickedness that is manifested against him and you will learn how to defend yourself. An armour bearer will learn how to dodge many evils. When you see the sword aimed at your father, you will realise how wicked the enemy is and how he would like to slice off your father’s legs, hands and head.

It is important to have a revelation of the enemy. It is important to have a revelation of the extent of evil that is determined against you. It is because people do not realise how extreme the enemy is that they have postured themselves mildly against the enemy. Even as a young man, you must learn to fight extremes with extremes!

In the days when armour was used, dead soldiers lay on the battlefield, having been chopped up and butchered into pieces of meat and bloody flesh. Flies would come from miles around and the buzzing of the flies would be heard over the fields of dead, butchered, bloody-red flesh. An armour bearer is supposed to see all this and sense the evil that is purposed against the armies of the Lord. There are two types of ministers; those who sense that a great evil is determined against them and those who are carefree and happy, thinking that all is well.

I once visited a man who had had armed robbers entering his house in the middle of the night. They put a gun to his head and threatened to kill him. It was the most traumatic experience of his life. When I visited his home, the entire front door and front wall was covered with two layers of steel. The man had developed a special barricade of intricately designed iron rods to protect his house. He was so conscious of the dangers of armed robbery that he had changed his house into a virtual prison that no one could enter or come out of easily. This is what happens when you have seen evil at close range. Most Christians have not seen a certain level of evil at close range and because of that they live a carefree life, unprotected and unguarded from certain dangers.

As a young man bearing the armour and seeing the fights just ahead of you, God is preparing you for your own fights. He wants you to see how satan would like to destroy you. He wants you to see how your head can be chopped off, leaving behind a headless body, gushing with blood.

As a young man, I saw the effects of prayerlessness and unspirituality. I saw what happens to those who do not give themselves to waiting on God and praying in the Spirit. A man of God confessed how his ministry was destroyed because he did not wait on God regularly.

Perhaps God has allowed you to see what happens to those who do not take the laws of marriage seriously. As an armour bearer, you will see marriages go flat, sour, negative and foul before your very eyes. God is allowing you to see what is happening in pastors’ marriages so that you will defend yourself against that. Experiences as an armour bearer are to prepare you to avoid such evil in your life and ministry.

Perhaps, God has allowed you to see the effects of adultery in the church. I saw a church collapse through the power of adultery. Whilst in the ministry of an armour bearer, I observed the total wipe-out of churches through adultery by the pastor. These experiences are to warn you and to protect you.

Take note of all the experiences that God allows you to have. God is allowing you to observe spiritual fighting at close range. One time I was in a foreign country and I heard some church members of another church discussing a pastor who used the church offerings directly to solve his personal financial needs. They lambasted him and criticised everything about him and his church. I learnt there that church offerings should never be used personally but must go directly to the church. As I listened to church members speak bitterly against pastors misusing offerings, I learnt a very important lesson about how offerings must be used.

And Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of Moses, one of his young men, answered and said, My lord Moses, forbid them.

Numbers 11:28

Joshua was one of the young men around Moses. Joshua saw the rebellion of Korah, Dathan and Abiram. He saw how people repaid good with evil. He saw how you can lead people out of bondage, care for them, provide for them, perform miracles in their lives and yet they will still turn against you. As a young man, Joshua saw much rebellion and disloyalty from the elders and leaders of Israel. Joshua also saw how Moses’ relatives, Miriam and Aaron spoke against their brother. He saw the effect of familiarity.

It is interesting to note that under the ministry of Joshua, there was no rebellion and disloyalty at all. Rather, there was a different type of evil – deception! Under Joshua, there was the deception of Achan who stole goods and secretly hid them against the commandment of the Lord.

But the children of Israel committed a trespass in the accursed thing: for Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took of the accursed thing: and the anger of the LORD was kindled against the children of Israel.

Joshua 7:1

Achan did not openly rebel against Joshua as Korah, Dathan and Abiram did against Moses. Achan was rebellious but he did it undercover. Under Joshua, there were no more people like Korah, Dathan and Abiram who openly fought against the leader. Joshua had wiped out disloyalty and open rebellion because he knew what it was from being around Moses.

Being a young man around God’s servant will expose you to many evils. You must make sure that those evils dry up around you when it is your turn to lead. If an armour bearer does not have the right attitude, he will see the many evils that beset God’s servants and despise God’s servant. Instead of despising God’s servant who is going through many troubles, you must learn how to prevent them from happening in your life. Honouring your father makes you learn from your father. Despising your father makes you blind to the wisdom that a father has.

By staying with Moses through the various rebellions, Joshua inherited a team of leaders who understood the horrors and evils of rebellion. Almost every evil that Joshua encountered as a leader was laced with deception, hiding and pretence. No one was bold enough to come and challenge him the way they challenged Moses. Notice how the Gibeonites used deception to fight against Joshua.

And when the inhabitants of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done unto Jericho and to Ai, They did work wilily, and went and made as if they had been ambassadors, and took old sacks upon their asses, and wine bottles, old, and rent, and bound up; And old shoes and clouted upon their feet, and old garments upon them; and all the bread of their provision was dry and mouldy. And they went to Joshua unto the camp at Gilgal, and said unto him, and to the men of Israel, We be come from a far country: now therefore make ye a league with us.

Joshua 9:3-6

The Gibeonites dared not take on Joshua in a frontal attack. They deceived him in order to overcome him. Today, God is giving you an opportunity to see His servants fighting through many battles. If you see many battles, wars and fights in the ministry, it means you are an armour bearer. It is only armour bearers who see the fighting at close range.
