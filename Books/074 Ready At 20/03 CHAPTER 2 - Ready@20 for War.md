Twenty Things You Are Ready to Do at Twenty

You must be ready@20 for war! Moses declared that anyone who was twenty years and above was ready to join the army. He declared that anyone who was twenty years and above could and should fight for Israel. What does it mean when you are ready@20 to join the armies of God?

1\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready for war.

2\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to go to war and to die for his nation.

3\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to go to war and to fight for any cause that is deemed appropriate.

4\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to go to war and to fight to the death.

5\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to kill or be killed in a war.

6\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to be disciplined and prepared for war.

7\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to forsake the pleasures of civilian life.

8\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to live the life of a hardened warrior.

9\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to be in a war, which is a mission to extinguish the enemy.

10\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to be part of a war where he will march for hours in the heat and in the cold.

11\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to be at war where he will take orders from authorities.

12\. By the age of twenty a man is ready to be in a war where he will live apart from his parents.

13\. By the age of twenty a man is ready to be in a war and take up the responsibility of bearing weapons.

14\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to be loyal to a cause.

15\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to be a good soldier.

16\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to invade another person’s country.

17\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to travel far from his home.

18\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to go to war and never come back home.

19\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to be at war where he will eat little and sleep very little.

20\. By the age of twenty, a man is ready to go through untold pain and sacrifice.

In the above list, you see all the things that people were ready to do at twenty. Moses recruited anybody who was twenty years and above, to join the group of soldiers who would fight for the children of Israel.

Today, God is calling you to join the group of people who are ready to do all the twenty things that have been listed. Twenty is the age when you are ready for war! Twenty is the age when you are ready for ministry.

Ministry is war! Ministry is the good fight of faith. “Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, whereunto thou art also called, and hast professed a good profession before many witnesses.” (1 Timothy 6:12) Paul referred to the ministry as warfare. If you have any experience with ministry, you will know that it is a war. It is a fight for your life.

This charge I commit unto thee, son Timothy, according to the prophecies which went before on thee, that thou by them mightest WAR A GOOD WARFARE;

1 Timothy 1:18

Paul calls it a good fight because ministry is a fight for eternal rewards. Ministry is a good fight because there is a good reason for the war. We are fighting for people’s lives! We are fighting for souls and that is a very good cause! Most wars have a foolish cause.

The First World War was fought because an archduke of Austria - Hungary was assassinated by separatist and terrorist forces in Austria. The Second World War was fought because a madman wanted to invade and dominate the whole world. The French Revolution was fought because there was a shortage of bread. Over forty thousand people perished in the French Revolution. At the end of the revolution, the leader of the revolution, Robespierre, along with twenty-one of his followers were guillotined. Those who had organized this revolution were the last victims of the murderous holocaust.

Yet, after the French Revolution, all the things that the revolution sought to remove, such as the church and the monarchy, were re-established. Indeed, Napoleon was made the emperor of France after the revolution. The revolution had removed and executed the king of France but as the revolution ended, an emperor was installed.

Most of these fights and wars are senseless because at the end of the war, everything becomes almost exactly the same as when it started out. The First World War caused the deaths of twenty million people. The Second World War caused the deaths of fifty million people. Another twenty-five million people died from war-related diseases and famine. Therefore, seventy to eighty-five million people perished because of the Second World War.

In 1945, after the Second World War, the borders of European nations were exactly the same as when they started fighting in 1939. Everything was essentially the same. No one lost any land and no one gained anything. Fifty million people died for no good reason. So then why were these wars fought? These wars were fought when satan inspired and provoked their leaders to fight and kill each other.

With all these realities, men keep going to war. Human beings keep sending their young men to fight for useless causes. When Moses needed an army who would fight for the children of Israel, he called up those who were twenty years old and above. By the age of twenty you must be ready to go to war.

We have a good cause to fight for. Our war makes sense! Our war will have a good outcome! Our war is against the forces of wickedness who have captured this world. Our war is against the devil who is marching millions of people to hell every day.

Everything that is required of you when you are a soldier will be required of you when you are at war for the kingdom of God. The kingdom of God is advancing. Jesus is coming soon! We need to take territories for Jesus.

Older people are full of wisdom and experience. Unfortunately, they are not usually selected for combat. Many people in their fifties and sixties will not be able to march on in the heat and in the cold. Perhaps, they would have been happy to march without food and water when they were in their twenties. Armies need people who are young, strong and capable of enduring and fighting. When you are fifty years old, you are even more sure of what you believe but you are a little less capable of achieving it. The strength, manoeuvring, organization, skills, speed and the endurance needed for ministry are simply not there at an older age.

Young people must be made to realize how much depends on them. Many people assume that their youth must be spent fooling around and playing games. They assume that church is for the older, more sober and wise-hearted people. They assume that ministry is for the mature, experienced, sufferers who have been in this world and experienced many sad days. The more sombre and sober a person is, the more qualified to be a priest he seems to be. Young, happy, energetic men are not chosen for ministry because it is assumed that they are not disciplined or mature enough for important things.

However, the secular world does not think so. The secular world produces fighter jets costing ninety-four million dollars and gives them to young pilots who are aged between eighteen and thirty. They entrust this very expensive piece of equipment to a twenty-year old boy and expect him to use it wisely. Indeed, after the age of thirty you usually cannot be trained or accepted to be a fighter pilot. Do you see how much confidence is placed in a young man?

In the church, people reject God’s servants because they are twenty-one years old. How come the American government is able to train twenty-year olds to fight with F35 jets and yet we cannot entrust a twenty-year old to hold the Bible and preach to a congregation? How come we cannot entrust a twenty-two year old man with a mission? Can you imagine what it is like for twenty-one year olds and twenty-two year olds who are pilots, flying the world’s best fighter jets in the deserts of Iraq and Kuwait, having “fun” and communicating with each other as they fly along in formation? Why are young men trusted so much in war? Why are they entrusted with such responsibility? Why are they trusted with the command of an airplane loaded with weapons, missiles and bombs?

It is time to entrust our young people with great responsibility just as the armies of the world do. God can and will use the young people to do great and mighty works. God can and will entrust His young people with millions of dollars, equipment and men. He is going to use young men to do mighty works. Just as the US Army, the Chinese Army and the Russian Army entrust things to young men, God is entrusting things to the young men of our day.

Do you think a young man can be a general and command large numbers of soldiers to do what is right? I think so!

Napoleon became a general when he was twenty-four years old. He went on to achieve many great things until he became world famous.

Moses, in the book of Numbers, told us how old a person had to be to join the armies of God. Twenty! Twenty years old! All you need is to be twenty years old!

Are you twenty years old and above? Then you are ready for the army of the Lord. Do not wait until it is late. There are things you cannot do when you are older. All branches of the military require young people. God requires young people too!
