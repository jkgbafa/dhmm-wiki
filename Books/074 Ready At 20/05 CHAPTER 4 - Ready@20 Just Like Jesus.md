### CHAPTER 4\

Ready@20 Just Like Jesus\
==============================================

Now his parents went to Jerusalem every year at the feast of the Passover. AND WHEN HE WAS TWELVE YEARS OLD, they went up to Jerusalem after the custom of the feast. And when they had fulfilled the days, as they returned, the child Jesus tarried behind in Jerusalem; and Joseph and his mother knew not of it. But they, supposing him to have been in the company, went a day’s journey; and they sought him among their kinsfolk and acquaintance. And when they found him not, they turned back again to Jerusalem, seeking him. And it came to pass, that after three days they found him in the temple, SITTING IN THE MIDST OF THE DOCTORS, BOTH HEARING THEM, AND ASKING THEM QUESTIONS. AND ALL THAT HEARD HIM WERE ASTONISHED AT HIS UNDERSTANDING AND ANSWERS.

And when they saw him, they were amazed: and his mother said unto him, Son, why hast thou thus dealt with us? Behold, thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing. And he said unto them, how is it that ye sought me? WIST YE NOT THAT I MUST BE ABOUT MY FATHER’S BUSINESS?

Luke 2:41-49

Now when all the people were baptized, it came to pass, that Jesus also being baptized, and praying, the heaven was opened, And the Holy Ghost descended in a bodily shape like a dove upon him, and a voice came from heaven, which said, Thou art my beloved Son; in thee I am well pleased. And JESUS HIMSELF BEGAN TO BE ABOUT THIRTY YEARS OF AGE, being (as was supposed) the son of Joseph, which was the son of Heli,

Luke 3:21-23

You must be ready@20 to be like Jesus! When Jesus was twelve years old, He demonstrated that He was getting ready for ministry by the age of twenty. His parents found Him in the temple, sitting amongst doctors. Jesus demonstrated seven keys for readiness for ministry by the age of twenty. In this chapter, I want you to receive these seven keys so that you will become someone who is ready for ministry at twenty.

Seven Keys to Readiness for Ministry @ 20

1\. Like Jesus, do not follow your parents’ lack of spirituality.

Certainly, Joseph and Mary had no understanding of Jesus’ mission. They knew that He was a mysterious child. But they could not fathom who He really was. Many parents of called and anointed people are not as spiritual as their children. It is important to honour your parents but do not allow their lack of spirituality to dampen or quench your calling. Perhaps your parents are not even born-again. Perhaps they do not know God the way you do. Perhaps they do not have the call of God on their lives as you do. Perhaps they do not understand what it means to be called. These realities should not quench your calling. Do not let parents quench the call of God on your life.

2\. Like Jesus, develop the ability to stay in church for a long time.

Staying in church for a long time is key to developing your ministry. You cannot do well in the ministry if you do not enjoy it. To enjoy ministry, you must enjoy staying long at it. It is those who tarry long at the wine who get inebriated by it. I take communion using real wine. I have never been intoxicated or affected by it. This is because I do not stay for long at the wine. If you want to be affected by something, you need to do it persistently and continuously. When you stay in church continuously and persistently, you will be affected positively by the anointing.

3\. Like Jesus, develop the ability to have three-day retreats.

It is not just important to stay in church but to be able to go for three-day meetings. Jesus spent three days in the temple, seeking God. His parents only found Him after three days! Develop the ability to do what Jesus did and spend time alone with God. As a young person, you can become engrossed in the things of God and spend days alone with God. Do not take any notice of those who are surprised at your dedication and zeal for the Lord. Jesus Christ did not go missing with His girlfriend for three days. Jesus Christ did not go to a wild youth camp or on a spree of fornication for three days. Jesus was full of the zeal of the Lord. Jesus was seeking God for three days. People were surprised at the zeal that Jesus had and at how He could go missing for three days.

4\. Like Jesus, ask questions about the ministry.

Jesus asked important questions about the ministry. He was found in the temple hearing the doctors and asking them questions. It is important to have questions about the ministry. When I was in school, I would ask many questions in class. I often did not understand the things that were being taught. Whenever I asked a question, other students would shout me down. They would say, “You are taking us back. Don’t you understand this simple thing?”

With time, I got the impression that asking questions was a sign of ignorance and stupidity. However, Jesus asked many questions. I do not think Jesus was ignorant or stupid. Indeed, with time, I have found out that asking many questions is a sign of intelligence.

Asking many questions is a sign of interest. Your interest in ministry is expressed by the questions you ask and by the questions that are generated in your heart. Ask yourself, “Why is this preaching so nice? What is it that makes this man’s teaching ministry so exciting and exhilarating?” You must ask yourself, “How did this great man of God become anointed? How did this person achieve all this? What must I do to bear fruit like this? How can I heal the sick like this man? How can I preach the gospel with such passion?”

The more questions you ask, the more you will find the answers that you need.

5\. Like Jesus, give yourself to listening to messages. Jesus is a great example setter for our lives and ministries.

Jesus Christ gave Himself to both hearing and asking questions. If you give yourself to hearing the word of God, you have given yourself the best chance in succeeding at ministry. There is a saying, “You are what you eat!”

There is another saying, “In the ministry, you are what you hear!” Everyone who amounts to something in the ministry has given himself to hearing and hearing. Faith comes by hearing. Hearing helps you to know the word of God. Hearing changes you in a way that reading does not. The scripture teaches that the anointing comes through hearing the spoken Word.

And the spirit entered into me when he spake unto me, and set me upon my feet, that I heard him that spake unto me.

Ezekiel 2:2

The Spirit enters you when He speaks to you. Every single time you are listening to the word of God, you must expect the Holy Spirit to be moving. When Peter was sent to Cornelius’ house, he preached the Word powerfully to the whole household. As he spake the Word, and as they heard him, the Holy Spirit fell on everyone.

While Peter yet spake these words, the Holy Ghost fell on all them which heard the word.

Acts 10:44

Indeed, the Holy Spirit always falls on those who are listening. Give yourself to listening to messages, (audio or video) and you will grow to become as mighty as Jesus.

6\. Like Jesus, relate with those who are ahead of you.

Jesus was not found chatting with old friends from school. Do you think He did not have schoolmates?

Jesus was not found making friends with girls in Jerusalem. His parents could have found Him chatting with a girl in a corner of Jerusalem. It is important to make friends with older ministers of God. The fact that you do not know older servants of God is a bad sign. If you relate only with your classmates, you will not become what you are supposed to become. Jesus Christ was sitting in the midst of doctors, even though He was not a doctor. You can always tell what you are going to become by the associations you have when you are younger in ministry. Go on and make friends with older ministers. They will definitely have something to contribute to your future.

One day I was analysing my life and ministry and I realised that most of the people I am happy relating with are older than me. They are mostly people who are far ahead of me. Most of these people are fifteen to thirty years older than myself. You end up becoming like the older people that you relate with! If they survived, you are likely to survive! If they made it, you are likely to make it too! If their marriages succeeded, your marriage is likely to succeed too. If they know secrets, you are likely to find out what those secrets are and walk in them too.

Job, the richest man in the east, had secrets that made him successful. Notice what he said, “Oh that I were as in months past, as in the days when God preserved me; When his candle shined upon my head, and when by his light I walked through darkness; As I was in the days of my youth, when the secret of God was upon my tabernacle;” (Job 29:2-4). I am sure that if you were to spend some time with Job, you would discover the secrets which made him the richest man in the east.

7\. Like Jesus, be about the heavenly Father’s business.

You must spend all your life being about the Father’s business. I know there are many options. You can be about a political party’s business. You can be about an earthly moneymaking business. You can be about making money. It is important that you choose to be about your heavenly Father’s business. There are so many things we can do to make our heavenly Father’s business prosper.

Our heavenly Father’s business has to do with receiving the Word and giving it out. It takes a lot of time to receive the Word and develop yourself in the Word. A real minister spends time lying in God’s presence and waiting on Him. A real minister knows that he must spend many hours in God’s presence, seeking His face, praying, fasting and loving Him.

Often, outsiders do not understand the job of “being about your Father’s business.” They will think you are lazy and they will offer you jobs to keep you busy, to give you an opportunity to earn a decent wage. When you are a real minister, your wife may give you errands and chores to do because she may feel you are idle and have a lot of time on your hands. People may ask your wife, “Your husband does not work, does he?” They may say, “Your husband is free and resting at home. Is he going to find a job soon?”

The truth is that people do not understand what the Father’s business is. The Father’s business is unlike any other business.

The truth is that, it is difficult to understand other people’s jobs when you are not close to them.Those who fish in the deep seas have a job like no other job. They are out there in the night, sitting on a canoe till the morning. There is no other job like fishing in the deep seas in a canoe. Those who catch shrimps have a different experience in life. Those who drive septic trucks have a completely different job, going from house to house extracting the sewage.

I once saw some sanitation workers working all night, opposite the church. I had never known that they worked so hard. I simply did not understand their work.

It is easy not to understand somebody’s business. Do not take any notice of people who mock you because you are waiting on God. Just give yourself to the Father’s business. Be like Jesus! You will reap the benefits of giving yourself to the Father’s business.
