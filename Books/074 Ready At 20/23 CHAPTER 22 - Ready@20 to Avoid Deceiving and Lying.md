### CHAPTER 22\

Ready@20 to Avoid Deceiving and Lying\
===========================================================

But a certain man named Ananias, with Sapphira his wife, sold a possession, And kept back part of the price, his wife also being privy to it, and brought a certain part, and laid it at the apostles’ feet.

BUT PETER SAID, ANANIAS, WHY HATH SATAN FILLED THINE HEART TO LIE TO THE HOLY GHOST, and to keep back part of the price of the land? Whiles it remained, was it not thine own? And after it was sold, was it not in thine own power? WHY HAST THOU CONCEIVED THIS THING IN THINE HEART? THOU HAST NOT LIED UNTO MEN, BUT UNTO GOD. And Ananias hearing these words fell down, and gave up the ghost: and great fear came on all them that heard these things.

AND THE YOUNG MEN AROSE, WOUND HIM UP, AND CARRIED HIM OUT, AND BURIED HIM.

And it was about the space of three hours after, when his wife, not knowing what was done, came in. And Peter answered unto her, Tell me whether ye sold the land for so much? And she said, Yea, for so much.

Then Peter said unto her, How is it that ye have agreed together to tempt the Spirit of the Lord? Behold, the feet of them which have buried thy husband are at the door, and shall carry thee out.

Then fell she down straightway at his feet, and yielded up the ghost: AND THE YOUNG MEN CAME IN, AND FOUND HER DEAD, AND, CARRYING HER FORTH, BURIED HER BY HER HUSBAND.

Acts 5:1-10

The young men in the early church grew to fear the telling of lies and the creation of deceptions, falseness, pretence and hypocrisy. They saw the judgment that befell men and women of deceit.

Most people do not fear lying and deception! Telling lies almost seems second nature to many people. In the story above, the young men had the unfortunate privilege of watching two church members die suddenly and prematurely for telling lies to the pastor. God allowed this to happen in front of the young men so that they would put away deception forever. Every time you tell a lie, you accept a demon into your life. The more deception there is in your life, the more demons are present.

What is worse than Judas? The devil, of course! It is the devil that turned Judas into what he was. What is the difference between Judas and the devil? With Judas you see someone who was brought close and was trusted at the highest level, who turned against his master in a shocking twist.

I can tell you that there is something worse than Judas. And that is the devil! The devil is a liar and a murderer! All liars have the devil or demons working through them. The devil is the father of lies. Every lie and every deception is the product of demonic activity.

Someone who has devils in him is full of lies and deception. There is nothing real about the person! Everything about the person is deception, lies and falseness. No words of demonized people can ever be trusted. If the person tells you, “I love you” you must think of “I hate you.” If the person is a woman, do not be surprised if she comes one day to inform you that she is actually a man. With such people, you can only believe when the person says “Good morning” or “Good evening” because you can look outside and see what time of day it is.

It is good that as a young man you learn to fear the telling of lies, the making of dishonest things and the creation of deceptions. You must practically hate all forms of lying and deception, no matter how mild or harmless they seem. Can you imagine the effect this event had on the young men who carried out the dead bodies of Ananias and Sapphira? Certain events happen before your eyes to teach you the greatest lessons of your life and ministry.

In the judgment of Ananias and Sapphira, God has forever shown His attitude and judgment towards deceptions, falseness, pretence, lying, dishonesty and cheating. All these things are of satan. (Similarly, in the judgment of Sodom and Gomorrah, God has eternally showed His attitude towards sexual perversion - Genesis 18:20; 19:12-17, 24-25).

In your course as a leader, you will see a lot of lies, pretence and deception. This is because sin and wickedness need something to cover them up. Every wicked activity needs darkness to thrive. No matter the sin, there is usually some form of deception somewhere. You must fight the liars and deceivers around you! You must fight to shine light where people want to live in pockets of darkness. Always remember that liars hate you. Anyone who lies to you does not love you. Every liar is at war with you! Everyone who lies to you has declared war against you and therefore you must also go to war against them.

A lying tongue hateth those that are afflicted by it; and a flattering mouth worketh ruin.

Proverbs 26:28

You must curse the presence of liars, deceivers and traitors in your life so that they cannot flourish in your midst. The curse on liars and deceivers in your midst is biblical.

Lying and deception is a spiritual and demonic work and all demonic works in your life are cursed. You must fight extremes with extremes. Notice the curse upon all liars and deceivers in your life. The curse on liars flies into every house, consuming even the timber and the stones.

He told me, “This book is the verdict going out worldwide against thieves and liars. The first half of the book disposes of everyone who steals; the second half takes care of everyone who lies. I launched it ‘—Decree of GOD of the Angel Armies—’ and so it will fly into the house of every thief and every liar. It will land in each house and tear it down, timbers and stones.”

Zechariah 5:3-4 (MSG)

You must also fight against lies and deception in yourself. You must fight the stages of lying and deception all the time. Do not allow the enemy to deceive you into the smallest and lowest aspects of lying. Let us quickly go through the stages of lying.

Stages of Lying

1\. STAGE ONE OF LYING AND DECEPTION: NOT LOVING THE TRUTH

Not loving the truth is the first dangerous stage in the journey of deception. You begin lying by not loving the truth. Loving the truth at all costs is your great protection from deception. All those who do not really love the truth can and will fall into deception. The scripture below tells us that strong delusions descend on people because they do not have “the love of the truth”. It is not just important to know the truth or to speak the truth but to actually love the truth. A righteous man hates lying from the bottom of his heart. “A righteous man hateth lying: but a wicked man is loathsome, and cometh to shame.” (Proverbs 13:5)

Even him, whose coming is after the working of Satan with all power and signs and lying wonders, And with all deceivableness of unrighteousness in them that perish; because THEY RECEIVED NOT THE LOVE OF THE TRUTH, that they might be saved. And for this cause God shall send them STRONG DELUSION, that they should believe a lie:

2 Thessalonians 2:9-11

2\. STAGE TWO OF LYING AND DECEPTION: WITHHOLDING INFORMATION

The next stage of lying is withholding information. Withholding information is a kind of deception. You are guilty by association of the evil that you are hiding. Whenever there is an uprising against a government, all those who knew about the conspiracy are considered guilty for aiding and abetting the treachery. Withholding information is a way of conniving, aiding, encouraging, backing, assisting and supporting evil. Notice how Mordecai was rewarded for not withholding the information about the king’s assassins. People who withhold information are dangerous enemies.

On that night could not the king sleep, and he commanded to bring the book of records of the chronicles; and they were read before the king. And it was found written, that Mordecai had told of Bigthana and Teresh, two of the king’s chamberlains, the keepers of the door, who sought to lay hand on the king Ahasuerus. And the king said, What honour and dignity hath been done to Mordecai for this? Then said the king’s servants that ministered unto him, There is nothing done for him.

Esther 6:1-3

3\. STAGE THREE OF LYING AND DECEPTION: GIVING MISLEADING INFORMATION

Another stage in lying and deception is giving misleading information. In this case, a person will say things that take your mind away from the issue. I remember sitting with a liar who began to describe the sins of another person. This completely took my mind in the wrong direction and successfully led me astray. Watch out for people who give you information that mislead you from the truth. Experienced liars are clever, deceitful and wicked.

But the Spirit explicitly says that in later times some will fall away from the faith, paying attention to deceitful spirits and doctrines of demons,

1 Timothy 4:1 (NASB)

4\. STAGE FOUR OF LYING AND DECEPTION: SELF DECEPTION

Many people live in self-deception. When there is longstanding deception, people tell themselves lies and also believe their own lies. Whilst they are doing bad things, they actually believe they are doing good things.

Wherefore lay apart all filthiness and superfluity of naughtiness, and receive with meekness the engrafted word, which is able to save your souls. But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, DECEIVING YOUR OWN SELVES.

James 1:21-22

5\. STAGE FIVE OF LYING AND DECEPTION: TELLING OUTRIGHT LIES

Lying lips are abomination to the Lord: but they that deal truly are his delight.

Proverbs 12:22

These six things doth the Lord hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him: A proud look, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood,

Proverbs 6:16-17

6\. STAGE SIX OF LYING AND DECEPTION: LYING WITHOUT A CONSCIENCE

When the conscience is seared, the person speaks and lies without batting an eyelid. They speak lies are as though they are breathing. Their lies are part of their breath. Their lies are as effortless as their breathing. Watch out for those whose lies are like breathing exercises!

Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils; Speaking lies in hypocrisy; having their conscience seared with a hot iron;

1 Timothy 4:1-2

7\. STAGE SEVEN OF LYING AND DECEPTION: LIVING AND ACTING A LIE WHICH IS HYPOCRISY

Just as preaching can have signs and wonders following it, lies can have “signs and lying wonders”. A “lying wonder” is an act or an event that helps the deception to be established.

Even him, whose coming is after the working of Satan with all power and signs and LYING WONDERS, And with all deceivableness of unrighteousness in them that perish; because they received not the love of the truth, that they might be saved. And for this cause God shall send them strong delusion, that they should believe a lie:

2 Thessalonians 2:9-11

I remember a brother who stamped his feet, cursed himself and hit his chest as he lied to me. He asked that he should be struck dead if he was lying. He actually asked that God should afflict him a “stroke” if he was lying. I immediately believed all the lies he had told me. For someone to stamp his feet, curse himself and hit his chest so hard that it sounded like the bass drum in the church, I thought to myself, “He must be telling the truth. He can only be telling the truth.”

When liars develop in their deceptions, they actually start acting out their lies. For instance, a person who has a bad marriage will constantly put on a show to demonstrate a good marriage. He will hold hands in public and say how much he loves his wife. All the while, as he is holding hands and making amazing love speeches, he is secretly loving someone else.

Hypocrisy simply means acting. Indeed, in the olden days, actors were called hypocrites. When film stars were not around, people would say, “The hypocrites are not around.” When they did not have enough actors, they would say, “We do not have enough hypocrites for today’s show.”

Hypocrites are advanced liars who simply act out certain things in order to confirm and authenticate their lies.

8\. STAGE EIGHT OF LYING AND DECEPTION: HYPOCRISY WITHOUT A CONSCIENCE

The highest level of hypocrisy is where the entire life of a person is fake. This is where duplicity comes in. Duplicity is the art of living in two worlds. Duplicity is the art of having two different personalities in one person. This double-faced individual successfully moves in and out from one group to the other. Duplicity is something you will see in the church. It is the art of being dual. Two in one! This is the betrayer! This is the serpent!

Every time you touch the person, you are touching the triangular head of a brown Gaboon viper. The Gaboon viper was originally discovered in Gabon. White settlers initially thought it was harmless because it lay still for so long and slept endlessly. Little did they know that it was one of the most venomous snakes in the world. So is the liar in your midst! Initially he looks sleepy and harmless. With time, you discover the great danger you are in because you allow the serpent to flourish around you.

Every time you “caress the head” of this double-dealing dual man or woman of duplicity, you are stroking the head of a giant king cobra. Some people are serpents! Some people have serpents in them. Some people are serpents inside! All in all, you are dealing with the serpent! The liar! The deceiver! The false one! The subtle, crafty minister of righteousness who is actually the devil himself! You are dealing with the devil in a suit. You are dealing with Lucifer in a pretty dress. You are dealing with the ancient serpent hiding behind that appealing presentation. May you be saved from becoming such a monster! May you be delivered from the purposes, plans and goals of the lying, silent serpent in your life and ministry!

Spies have to live out their entire lives as though they were someone else. This is lying of the highest order. Most films last for just two hours. After two hours, the actors can come back to their real lives. In church, however, there are people who completely live out their lives as someone else, like spies do.

I once had a pastor who had two families. Each family did not know the other family existed. Each wife did not know that the other wife existed. This pastor successfully deceived everyone and would travel in between the two cities without anyone knowing why he was travelling. He would give a lame duck story and leave one family to visit the other family. Then he would give a cock and bull story to one wife and leave to visit the other family. His whole life was a lie. He lived comfortably in the midst of deception. He had a pleasing face and an appealing look. As he cooked up stories to get more money to buy goods for the church at twice the price, we had no idea that we were looking at the devil in the flesh.

Anytime you meet the devil in the flesh, you will see lies, deception, falseness, fake things, trickery, dishonesty, duality, doubleness and duplicity.

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye make clean the outside of the cup and of the platter, but within they are full of extortion and excess. Thou blind Pharisee, cleanse first that which is within the cup and platter, that the outside of them may be clean also. Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness. Even so ye also outwardly appear righteous unto men, but within ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.

Matthew 23:25-28

9\. STAGE NINE OF LYING AND DECEPTION: DECEIVING A LOT OF PEOPLE; WHOLE FAMILIES, THE WHOLE WORLD

And the great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan, which DECEIVETH THE WHOLE WORLD: he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him.

Revelation 12:9

Liars are ranked by how many people they are able to deceive. Most people deceive a few people. There are always some people who know the truth. The “dragon” is ranked as the greatest liar of all time because he is able to deceive the whole world. Today, you must ask yourself what rank of a liar you are.

I once met a brother who almost matched the dragon’s rank. Everyone in his world was deceived by him. He had successfully deceived his wife, his children, his church, his relatives, his brothers, his sisters, his friends and every other person who ever knew him. I thought to myself, “This brother is challenging the dragon himself.” Watch out that you do not cross into stage nine of lying and deception. The end of the dragon will be your end when you reach this stage of deception.

10\. STAGE TEN OF LYING AND DECEPTION: LYING TO THE HOLY SPIRIT

But Peter said, Ananias, why hath Satan filled thine heart to lie to the Holy Ghost, and to keep back part of the price of the land? Whiles it remained, was it not thine own? and after it was sold, was it not in thine own power? why hast thou conceived this thing in thine heart? thou hast not lied unto men, but unto God. And Ananias hearing these words fell down, and gave up the ghost: and great fear came on all them that heard these things.

Acts 5:3-5

Lying to the Holy Spirit is lying to God. God knows you are lying before you start speaking. Ananias and Sapphira died immediately when they lied to the Holy Spirit. It is important that you do not tell lies to the Lord’s anointed. You can never tell when the judgment of Ananais and Sapphira will fall on the next liar.

As a pastor, I have had a number of people lying to me, just like they lied to Peter. All of these liars are alive and well today. I am sure that I am not as anointed as Peter was. I am sure that the anointing that was in the early church was much stronger then than it is today in my ministry. However, it is still dangerous to lie to the Lord’s anointed. You never know when the judgment of Ananias and Sapphira will manifest itself again!
