### CHAPTER 17\

Ready@20 to Be a Prophet\
==============================================

So the young man, even THE YOUNG MAN THE PROPHET, went to Ramothgilead.

2 Kings 9:4

You must be ready@20 to become a prophet! A young person can be called to be a prophet. Many young men were called to be prophets. It is important to believe that the gift of God does not work because of your age, your riches and your standing in society. There are places where your age matters, there are places where the money you have matters and there are places where your education matters. However when it comes to being a prophet of God, you can be ready@20.

God has His own criteria for choosing who must see visions and who must be a prophet. Most of the prophets in the Bible were called as children or very young men. These examples must inspire you to serve God very well at a tender age.

Seven Prophets Who Were Ready@20

1\. The prophet of Ramothgilead was ready@20.

AND ELISHA THE PROPHET CALLED ONE OF THE CHILDREN OF THE PROPHETS, and said unto him, Gird up thy loins, and take this box of oil in thine hand, and go to Ramothgilead: And when thou comest thither, look out there Jehu the son of Jehoshaphat the son of Nimshi, and go in, and make him arise up from among his brethren, and carry him to an inner chamber; Then take the box of oil, and pour it on his head, and say, Thus saith the Lord, I have anointed thee king over Israel. Then open the door, and flee, and tarry not. So the young man, even the young man the prophet, went to Ramothgilead.

2 Kings 9:1-4

The prophet of Ramothgilead was a young man who was sent to deliver a prophetic word to Jehu, the captain of the host. This was a dangerous mission in which he was informing a captain that he was to be made king instead of Ahab. This young prophet was operating in the anointing of Elisha the prophet. He was indeed one of the sons of the prophets. His prophecy was clear;

“And when he came, behold, the captains of the host were sitting; and he said, I have an errand to thee, O captain. And Jehu said, Unto which of all us? And he said, To thee, O captain. And he arose, and went into the house; and he poured the oil on his head, and said unto him, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, I have anointed thee king over the people of the Lord, even over Israel. And thou shalt smite the house of Ahab thy master, that I may avenge the blood of my servants the prophets, and the blood of all the servants of the Lord, at the hand of Jezebel” (2 Kings 9:5-7).

This was indeed a bold step by the young man. This young prophet made no reference to Elisha. Even though Elisha had sent him, he confidently stood under the anointing and prophesied to Jehu. This shows that he was walking in the strength and the anointing of a real prophet.

2\. Samuel the prophet was ready@20.

AND THE CHILD SAMUEL MINISTERED UNTO THE LORD BEFORE ELI. And the word of the LORD was precious in those days; there was no open vision. And it came to pass at that time, when Eli was laid down in his place, and his eyes began to wax dim, that he could not see; And ere the lamp of God went out in the temple of the LORD, where the ark of God was, and Samuel was laid down to sleep; That the LORD called Samuel: and he answered, Here am I.

1 Samuel 3:1-4

Samuel was found in the house of God as a child. He received amazing prophecies from the Lord as a child. The famous prophecy of Samuel in which the Lord called out his name when he was asleep, came to him as a child.

Being a young child in no way prevents you from becoming a prophet. Indeed, many evils will come to you as you get older. Perhaps your various experiences will prevent you from seeing and hearing clearly from God. Jesus spoke about the Pharisees, saying that their hearts had become hardened and callous.

3\. Jeremiah the prophet was ready@20.

BUT THE LORD SAID UNTO ME, SAY NOT, I AM A CHILD: FOR THOU SHALT GO TO ALL THAT I SHALL SEND THEE, and whatsoever I command thee thou shalt speak. Be not afraid of their faces: for I am with thee to deliver thee, saith the Lord. Then the Lord put forth his hand, and touched my mouth. And the Lord said unto me, Behold, I have put my words in thy mouth. See, I have this day set thee over the nations and over the kingdoms, to root out, and to pull down, and to destroy, and to throw down, to build, and to plant.

Jeremiah 1:7-10

Even though Jeremiah was a child, God called him to root out and to pull down works of darkness. Jeremiah was anointed to pull down evil things and destroy the works of darkness. Jeremiah was a church planter and a builder even though he was a child. Great prophets are called to serve the Lord even though they are children.

4\. Daniel the prophet was ready@20.

AS FOR THESE FOUR CHILDREN, God gave them knowledge and skill in all learning and wisdom: and Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams. Now at the end of the days that the king had said he should bring them in, then the prince of the eunuchs brought them in before Nebuchadnezzar. And the king communed with them; and among them all was found none like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah: therefore stood they before the king. And in all matters of wisdom and understanding, that the king enquired of them, he found them ten times better than all the magicians and astrologers that were in all his realm.

Daniel 1:17-20

Daniel was a great prophet. He ministered at the highest level to kings and prime ministers. Every new government that came to power recognized him. Yet Daniel came in to his prophetic ministry as a child. He is one of the four children mentioned in the first chapter of Daniel. Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, plus Daniel, made up the four children. Daniel, who turned out to be one of the greatest prophets ever in Israel, started his ministry as a castrated young boy captive. Yet the power of God was on him! He was a great prophet and he was ready to be used by God as a very young person.

5\. David the sweet psalmist was ready@20.

Now these be the last words of David. David the son of Jesse said, and the man who was raised up on high, the anointed of the God of Jacob, and the sweet psalmist of Israel, said, The Spirit of the Lord spake by me, and his word was in my tongue.

2 Samuel 23:1-2

David was clearly a prophet. He was an anointed psalmist and the Spirit of God spoke through him many times. Goliath mocked at David because he looked so young. Disregarding young people whom God has called can cost you your life. Great advances, great victories and great progress will come when you allow the young people to join in the work of God.

And when the Philistine looked about, and saw David, he disdained him: FOR HE WAS BUT A YOUTH, and ruddy, and of a fair countenance.

1 Samuel 17:42

6\. Solomon the writer was ready@ 20.

And now, O Lord my God, thou hast made thy servant king instead of David my father: AND I AM BUT A LITTLE CHILD: I know not how to go out or come in.

1 Kings 3:7

Lemuel was a symbolic name for King Solomon. The words of Lemuel were considered as prophecies. The prophecies of Solomon were the prophecies that his mother taught him.

The words of king Lemuel, the prophecy that his mother taught him.

Proverbs 31:1

Almost every single word in the book of Proverbs is a prophecy. They are predictions of the outcomes of various things. For instance, the statement “A diligent man will stand before kings” (Proverbs 22:29) is a prophecy for diligent people. All these great prophecies are ascribed to Solomon. The books of Songs of Solomon, Ecclesiastes and Proverbs are all prophetic books written by Solomon the prophet.

Open up your heart and allow God to use you as a prophet even though you are a little child.

7\. Jesus the prophet was ready@20.

And when he was twelve years old, they went up to Jerusalem after the custom of the feast… And it came to pass, that after three days they found him in the temple, sitting in the midst of the doctors, both hearing them, and asking them questions. And all that heard him were astonished at his understanding and answers.

Luke 2:42, 46-47

We call Jesus the Saviour of the world but He was also a great prophet. Notice the scripture: “And there came a fear on all: and they glorified God, saying, That A GREAT PROPHET IS RISEN UP AMONG US; and, That God hath visited his people.” (Luke 7:16) Jesus began His ministry at a very young age. By the age of thirty He was at His peak and almost ready to end.
