### CHAPTER 3\

Jezebel Is One of Satan’s Ways of Harming You through a Woman\
===================================================================================

And in her was found the blood of prophets, and of saints, and of all that were slain upon the earth.

Revelation 18:24

All through the Bible, we see evil being released against God’s servants through women. God’s servant is always a target of evil. Satan is determined to use women to destroy God’s servants. Jezebel is simply a continuation of the plan to release evil against God’s servants through women. Jezebel is simply one of the ways that satan attacks, harms and fights God’s servants through a woman. Any woman who allows it will be turned into a source of evil for God’s servants.

“The spirit of Jezebel is the maturing of the evil that began when the first woman ate the fruit of the forbidden tree. This is deception in its ultimate form.”1

Remember that a woman is a gentle, appealing and fascinating creature, so she is a good disguise for the release of wickedness against you.

Women are nice, delightful, soft spoken, lovely and attractive human beings. A normal man will be drawn to a lovely woman and will not naturally think that evil can come to him through this lovely creature!

A woman who steps out of her place and opens the door to her heart, will be captured by demons and will be used by the devil as a “Jezebel” or a “woman out of order”.

Perhaps, the commonest way through which a woman is occupied by evil spirits and becomes a woman out of order, is by giving place to the devil through pride. Pride in a woman is a door in the realm of the spirit.

A woman is created to be subject to her husband, so when she is not submitted, she is immediately out of order and becomes a “Jezebel”.

1. Fire On The Mountain: Book II, The Valley Rick Joyner, MorningStar Publications Inc., ©2018

A woman can be used to do much evil when she is out of order. It is often in good Christian marriages that women become proud and open the door to satan. The Bible does not shy away from this reality. In my analysis of troubled marriages of great men of God, the problem has always been caused by a proud woman who cannot be controlled or advised by anyone, including and especially her own husband.

There are several examples of women being used, particularly, to bring about evil in God’s servants and God’s men of authority.

Let us look at several examples of this demonic strategy to torment and harm God’s servants through women who are out of order.

1\. Eve stepped out of place and was used by the devil to release great curses and frustration into Adam’s life, and also into the whole human race. Through Eve, the whole human race is subject to sickness, death, cemeteries, funeral homes, hospitals, accidents, tragedies, sorrow and wickedness. In a very subtle way, we can see that evil entered God’s servant through his wife Eve.

And the man said, The woman whom thou gavest to be with me, she gave me of the tree, and I did eat. And the Lord God said unto the woman, What is this that thou hast done? And the woman said, The serpent beguiled me, and I did eat.

Genesis 3:12-13

2\. Sarah stepped out of place and was used by the devil to release a great evil against Abraham’s seed. Even until today, we see Isaac, the seed of Abraham being tormented and fought against by the release of Ishmael. Ishmael was released upon the counsel and inputs of Sarah which was against God’s plan for Abraham.

And Sarai said unto Abram, Behold now, the Lord hath restrained me from bearing: I pray thee, go in unto my maid; it may be that I may obtain children by her. And Abram hearkened to the voice of Sarai.

Genesis 16:2

3\. Michal was out of her proper place when she criticized David. Michal was a woman who would release evil against God’s anointed psalmist. She was sent intentionally as a snare to put King David into great difficulty. Indeed, she is the person who despised David’s worship and dancing which God loved so much. David felt bad and wasn’t sure of himself when Michal criticized him. Michal was used by the devil to criticize the anointing and to despise God’s servant, his leadership and his style of ministry. David was forced to rebuke and ignore Michal until the day of her death because of her strong opposition and criticism of his life, his leadership and his ministry.

And Saul said, I will give him her, that she may be a snare to him, and that the hand of the Philistines may be against him. Wherefore Saul said to David, Thou shalt this day be my son in law in the one of the twain.

1 Samuel 18:21

Then David returned to bless his household. And Michal the daughter of Saul came out to meet David, and said, How glorious was the king of Israel to day, who uncovered himself to day in the eyes of the handmaids of his servants, as one of the vain fellows shamelessly uncovereth himself! And David said unto Michal, It was before the Lord, which chose me before thy father, and before all his house, to appoint me ruler over the people of the Lord, over Israel: therefore will I play before the Lord. And I will yet be more vile than thus, and will be base in mine own sight: and of the maidservants which thou hast spoken of, of them shall I be had in honour. Therefore Michal the daughter of Saul had no child unto the day of her death.

2 Samuel 6:20-23

4. Solomon’s wives were sent to be a source of subtle backsliding for one of God’s great lovers and wise men. Indeed, it was only through the evil released against Solomon through a host of women, that Solomon fell from his stable and worldwide ministry.

For it came to pass, when Solomon was old, that his wives turned away his heart after other gods: and his heart was not perfect with the Lord his God, as was the heart of David his father. For Solomon went after Ashtoreth the goddess of the Zidonians, and after Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites. And Solomon did evil in the sight of the Lord, and went not fully after the Lord, as did David his father. Then did Solomon build an high place for Chemosh, the abomination of Moab, in the hill that is before Jerusalem, and for Molech, the abomination of the children of Ammon. And likewise did he for all his strange wives, which burnt incense and sacrificed unto their gods. And the Lord was angry with Solomon, because his heart was turned from the Lord God of Israel, which had appeared unto him twice,

1 Kings 11:4-9

5\. Jezebel, a woman out of order, was used to introduce much evil into the life of one of God’s choice servants Elijah. Elijah’s ministry was cut short and truncated because of Jezebel. Elijah suffered depression, disorientation, confusion, discouragement, disillusionment and a premature ending of his ministry because of Jezebel.

Now Ahab told Jezebel everything Elijah had done and how he had killed all the prophets with the sword. So Jezebel sent a messenger to Elijah to say, “May the gods deal with me, be it ever so severely, if by this time tomorrow I do not make your life like that of one of them.” Elijah was afraid and ran for his life. When he came to Beersheba in Judah, he left his servant there, while he himself went a day’s journey into the wilderness. He came to a broom bush, sat down under it and prayed that he might die. “I have had enough, Lord,” he said. “Take my life; I am no better than my ancestors.”

1 Kings 19:1-4 (NIV)

6\. The woman of Babylon released much evil against God’s choice servant, the man child, in the book of Revelation. This woman was used to drink the blood and lives of prophets and servants of God. To drink the blood of someone speaks of totally destroying their lives.

And in her was found the blood of prophets, and of saints, and of all that were slain upon the earth.

Revelation 18:24
