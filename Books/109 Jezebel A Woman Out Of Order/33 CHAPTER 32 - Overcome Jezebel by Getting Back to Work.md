### CHAPTER 32\

Overcome Jezebel by Getting Back to Work\
==============================================================

And he said, I have been very jealous for the Lord God of hosts: because the children of Israel have forsaken thy covenant, thrown down thine altars, and slain thy prophets with the sword; and I, even I only, am left; and they seek my life, to take it away. And the Lord said unto him, Go, return on thy way to the wilderness of Damascus: and when thou comest, anoint Hazael to be king over Syria: And Jehu the son of Nimshi shalt thou anoint to be king over Israel: and Elisha the son of Shaphat of Abelmeholah shalt thou anoint to be prophet in thy room.

1 Kings 19:14-16

God had an answer for Elijah when he was depressed. More work! Get to the job and keep prophesying as you have been doing from the beginning. Your ultimate victory over Jezebel is gained by simply getting back into your ministry. Continue to serve the Lord and follow His leading for the ministry.

God knows all your problems before they started. It is a great thing to be wounded in the battle of God’s kingdom. Your wounds mean you have been genuinely hurt by a really painful attack.

Experienced warriors have a lot of scars.

Experienced warriors have a lot of wounds.

Experienced warriors also have a limp.

You will see experienced warriors advancing slowly with sometimes jerky movements. This apparent faltering walk is a result of experiences and happenings.

One word from an experienced man is worth an hour of light-hearted banter!

Conclusion

Jezebel is real! Jezebel is not a phantom or a figment of the imagination. Jezebel is alive and operational in the church today. Many powerful figures have been silenced and caused to retreat through the power of Jezebel.

It is my prayer that with these few words you will be admonished to fight hard against whatever represents a Jezebel in your life and ministry. There is nothing new under the sun! That which has been done is that which shall be done! Evil spirits have been around for thousands of years, waiting for you to come on the scene.

You have the upper hand against the spirit of Jezebel. To every spiritual problem there is a spiritual solution! This book you have just read contains a revelation which will help you to defeat the power of Jezebel today.

And further, by these, my son, be admonished: of making many books there is no end; and much study is a weariness of the flesh.
