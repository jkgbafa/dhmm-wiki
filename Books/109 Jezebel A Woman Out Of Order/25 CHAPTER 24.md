### CHAPTER 24\

Overcome Jezebel by Not Engaging Her in Discussions, Arguments or Meetings\
================================================================================================

And as Jehu entered in at the gate, she said, Had Zimri peace, who slew his master? And he lifted up his face to the window, and said, Who is on my side? who? And there looked out to him two or three eunuchs. And he said, Throw her down. So they threw her down: and some of her blood was sprinkled on the wall, and on the horses: and he trode her under foot.

2 Kings 9:31-33

Jezebel is notorious for arguing, accusing, interrupting, talking in circles, bringing up irrelevant topics, and trying to distract you from the topic. It is a waste of time to play at her level.

You will not win any argument against Jezebel and your point of view will never be accepted. Indeed, the only person who overcame Jezebel never talked directly to her. There is no record of any conversation, confrontation or argument between Elijah and Jezebel. The accounts in the Bible show that everyone stayed away from Jezebel until Elisha sent someone to kill her.

Neither Elijah nor Elisha ever spoke directly to Jezebel because there was no point in doing so.

When Jehu went to execute Jezebel he did not bother to speak to her at all. Indeed, he avoided speaking with her and simply asked, “Who is on the lords side?”

Jezebel cleverly tried to engage Jehu in a discussion about what happened to those who killed king Zimri. But Jehu was anointed to kill Jezebel so he did not speak to her at all. He waited downstairs for the eunuchs to throw Jezebel out of the window. May all Jezebels in your ministry be thrown out and thrown down!
