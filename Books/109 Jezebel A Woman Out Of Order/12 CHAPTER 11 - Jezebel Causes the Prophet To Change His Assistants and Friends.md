### CHAPTER 11\

Jezebel Causes the Prophet To Change His Assistants and Friends\
=====================================================================================

Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, so let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time. And when he saw that, he arose, and went for his life, and came to Beersheba, which belongeth to Judah, AND LEFT HIS SERVANT THERE.

1 Kings 19:2-3

Jezebel can even cause you to change your assistants and dismiss the most important people in your life.

Jezebel caused Elijah to change his precious assistant. Elijah abandoned his longtime servant who had worked with him for years and seen him cause a famine to come upon the earth. Elijah changed the servant who had been with him and seen him cause rain to fall after the famine.

Jezebel causes a man of God to move away from his assistants and associates. A Jezebel will cause you to set aside important friendships. Through the ministry of Jezebel, a man of God becomes more and more isolated.

After Elijah interacted with Jezebel, he set aside the servant that had been with him for many years. Elijah set aside the servant who saw him call a famine into this world for seven years. Elijah set aside the servant who saw him call fire down from heaven. Elijah set aside his friend who helped him to slay four hundred prophets of Baal. Elijah set aside the servant who saw him outrun the chariot of Ahab. Elijah broke up his relationship with the servant whom he sent seven times to go and look for the sign of rain.

Elijah abandoned his main friend, his great helper and his assistant! You may wonder why Elijah would separate himself from someone who had helped him practically for so many years.

Sometimes, men of God are forced to change members of their staff because their wife dislikes them. Dismissing various members of staff because a powerful woman does not like them is a classic Jezebel move. Often, Jezebel secretly forces the man of God to do away with people she does not like.

There are various circumstances that can lead to a change in the ministry team. If you look beyond the circumstances, you will discover that Jezebel is behind it all. The departure of important people from your life can lead to major changes in the ministry. After the servant was separated from Elijah, Elijah’s ministry moved into transition mode. The glory of the earlier powerful miracles was gone. Elijah was mostly preparing for the handover to Elisha.

It is important for the man of God not to “allow that woman Jezebel” to operate freely. Do not allow the spirit of Jezebel to control you and make you remove important people from your life. You must only take decisions, as you are led by the Spirit. You must not be led by Jezebel, but by the Holy Spirit!
