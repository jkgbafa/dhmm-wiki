### CHAPTER 18\

Jezebel Eliminates All Perceived Threats and Competition\
==============================================================================

And Jezebel his wife said unto him, Dost thou now govern the kingdom of Israel? arise, and eat bread, and let thine heart be merry: I will give thee the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite. So she wrote letters in Ahab’s name, and sealed them with his seal, and sent the letters unto the elders and to the nobles that were in his city, dwelling with Naboth. And she wrote in the letters, saying, Proclaim a fast, and set Naboth on high among the people: And set two men, sons of Belial, before him, to bear witness against him, saying, Thou didst blaspheme God and the king. And then carry him out, and stone him, that he may die.

1 Kings 21:7-10

Jezebel is famous for eliminating Naboth. Naboth represents a competition to Jezebel’s property and position. Jezebels indeed see every other woman as competition. A spirit of Jezebel will act as if you want to take their position. A spirit of Jezebel will therefore seek to eliminate any perceived threats. They want to be the only option. As far as Jezebel is concerned, there must be no one else for the leader to favour. Jezebel believes that others want to eliminate her. Jezebel seeks to get rid of anyone she sees as a threat. Be careful that you are not found to eliminate all but your friends and those who suck up to you.

I remember a church that had a number of ladies as pastors. I visited a lost member of that church and asked why she was no longer going to church. She answered very simply: “The woman pastor you have put in charge of the church has eliminated the rest of us who are not her direct friends. The church is now made up of her personal friends and no more.” She went on to show me a host of other ladies who were no longer a part of the church. Indeed, as I analysed the church, the data showed that this was indeed the case. Jezebel is an eliminator. Any survivors who are left around walk about on tenterhooks. Indeed, all the remaining ladies exist in a state of uneasy anxiety and painful suspense, not knowing when they may do the wrong thing and receive a rebuke.

One day I read a book in which a man described the problem of someone he knew. This person had been forced to sack several ladies who had worked for him. The man complained that, upon the request and insistence of his wife, he had dismissed seven different secretaries that had worked for him. In the end, he had been forced to employ a village girl who had no education or sophistication whatsoever. This man was voicing his frustration about his wife because she was still ranting and raving about the unsophisticated villager who was now his secretary. He did not know what to do. Jezebel is well known for eliminating every perceived threat including the most incredulous imagined threats.

One day, a man of God told me he had just received a phone call from his wife. He pointed to his phone and said, “She just called me and said, ‘I have called you to warn you about Aunty so-and-so. You know she is after you. She is in love with you and she wants you.’”

I asked, “Who is this lady that your wife is concerned about?” He pointed to a white-haired great grandmother who was attending the conference.

He asked me, “Do you know who this white-haired great grandmother is? Her contemporaries are in their nineties.”

Amazingly, this wife who was hundreds of miles away was concerned that her husband would fall in love with the white-haired, bent over great grandmother. Do not forget that a great grandmother has children, grandchildren and great grandchildren.
