### CHAPTER 17\

Jezebel Will Bring Your Ministry to a Premature End\
=========================================================================

And Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done, and withal how he had slain all the prophets with the sword. Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time. And when he saw that, he arose, and went for his life, and came to Beersheba, which belongeth to Judah, and left his servant there. But he himself went a day’s journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down under a juniper tree: and he requested for himself that he might die; and said, It is enough; now, O Lord, take away my life; for I am not better than my fathers.

1 Kings 19:1-4

Elijah handed over his ministry when it was at its very peak! Watch out for these signs when you are looking to spot a Jezebel. There is probably a Jezebel somewhere whenever there is a premature ending to a ministry.

Through deception your ministry can be brought to an end. Do not allow your ministry to be smaller than it could be because of a woman in your life. You must be determined to serve God to the max. What God has put in your heart is necessary and relevant and must be fulfilled. Don’t settle for anything less. I remember a minister from the Caribbean who wept at an ordination service he was conducting in Ghana. He was an invited minister and ordained a number of people into the ministry that Sunday morning.

He said his wife had given him an ultimatum. She told him “I want a normal husband. I want to come home to a man who is there. I can’t take it any longer as I sit at home and wait for my man to come back from ministry trips that have taken him outside the country.”

“I need a normal husband,” she insisted. “Give up the ministry or I will leave you.”

He described how he had to take the most difficult decision of his life. He chose to carry on in the ministry and lose his wife.

Indeed, he had come face to face with a Jezebel who was twisting his hand into submission. She was bending his arm until he gave up his ministry. Indeed, there are many missionaries who are stuck in an apartment because their beautiful wives secretly intimidate them with “common sense” until they can do nothing.

There is a saying that, “Behind every great man is a good woman.” Indeed, that may be the case! But if truth be told, behind many great men are difficult women.

I have had the unfortunate experience of meeting many men of God who struggle and labour with a Jezebel power seeking to truncate their ministries.

Perhaps, they are not even aware of the fact that they are truncating someone’s ministry. Most Jezebels deny wrongdoing and claim innocence. After all, they are behind the scenes and never speak up openly.

In 1904 there was a great movement of God in Wales. This great movement was brought on through the ministry of a man of God called Evan Roberts. It is believed that at least twenty per cent of the population was converted during his ministry in Wales. Many missionaries were sent out to the world during his ministry. Many hardened sinners were saved during the revival. Drinking spots, bars and alcohol centres of carousing were closed down and people rather attended church meetings and programs. Many sporting activities were affected because people would not attend these programs, especially on Sundays. Even the police had to lay off officers because crime reduced drastically. Churches were full and packed to overflowing when this revival was in full bloom. Because of the missionary focus of this revival, many parts of the world were affected by this revival. Even the Azusa Street revival was said to have been influenced by this anointed season in Wales.

At the peak of his ministry, Evan Roberts was only twenty-six years old. Evan Roberts was very dedicated to God and was known for his powerful word of knowledge and prophetic power as he preached. The preaching of Evan Roberts stirred up the power of God in such a way as has never been seen or reported since those times.

This amazing ministry came to an abrupt ending when a lady called Jessie Penn-Lewis entered Evan Roberts’ world. This woman was a supporter and follower of Roberts’ ministry. She herself was a teacher of the word of God.

In a sense she was like a prophetess who had never gained much acclaim. Churches had not really accepted her ministry at this time. Somehow, this woman who was rejected and ignored by the church, gained the attention and confidence of Evan Roberts. She began speaking to him and also isolating him from his friends. One of the main aims of a Jezebel is to isolate the man of God from other important people in his life. She ministered constantly to Evan Roberts until she became a source of correction and direction for him.

As she gained control over him, Roberts became convinced that he was taking the glory of God for himself and was therefore not doing the right thing. He soon felt the condemnation of God for his sin of taking God’s glory for the revival. That was the point at which Jessie Penn-Lewis convinced him to withdraw from ministry and isolate himself in her house. He even went as far as to recant from many of the things he had taught. He retracted many of his teachings which had brought about the revival. You must note that abnormal behaviour in a man of God’s life is caused by a Jezebel spirit.

There was a point at which Jessie Penn-Lewis was writing letters and publications in Evan Roberts’ name. People thought he was writing to them but it was actually the woman who was doing so. After some time, he eventually broke away from her control but he never recovered his ministry that was now destroyed. Clearly, a Jezebel spirit, religious and prophetic in nature, worked through this woman to change his ministry.

Many ministries are modified and truncated by Jezebel spirits. Indeed, there are many people who sit at home doing nothing for God because they listened to a Jezebel power that said, “Stay home!” The testimony of our brother Evan Roberts should be a lesson to all of us not to allow a controlling or insidiously manipulating person, whether man or woman to lead us. Always insist on being led by the Holy Spirit and not by a man or woman. It’s important not to allow Jezebel to operate unchecked in the church.

And the Lord said unto him, Go, return on thy way to the wilderness of Damascus: and when thou comest, anoint Hazael to be king over Syria: And Jehu the son of Nimshi shalt thou anoint to be king over Israel: and ELISHA THE SON OF SHAPHAT OF ABELMEHOLAH SHALT THOU ANOINT TO BE PROPHET IN THY ROOM.

1 Kings 19:15-16

A Jezebel will cause you to end your ministry prematurely. Elijah abruptly ended his ministry. He handed over the reins of his ministry to Elisha long before he had to. The effect of Jezebel is indeed mysterious!

I watched a twenty-five-year-old ministry that came to an abrupt end. A large and successful ministry was closed down suddenly by the senior pastor’s wife’s behaviour. A major church and national ministry was terminated by a wife’s behaviour. All the pastors of that great church were scattered. The founder of that church became the laughing stock of all his enemies.

Jezebel is the greatest threat to the ministry of powerful men of God. When a man of God operates at the level of Elijah, he has probably overcome many obstacles and it is only a woman like Jezebel who can influence or detract him from his mission.

Notice how Elisha was prematurely brought on the scene to take over the office of Elijah. Elijah’s handing over to Elisha is an example of a premature and abrupt end of a ministry.
