### CHAPTER 19\

Jezebel Will Not Apologize, Never Admit and Never Agree\
=============================================================================

And I gave her space to repent of her fornication; and she repented not.

Revelation 2:21

In the book of Revelation, you see what Jesus Himself said about Jezebel. He said, “I, Jesus, gave her space to repent but she did not change her mind nor change her attitude.” You can see from this that Jezebel never acknowledges her evil or her wrongdoings.

Jezebel has hidden behind the scenes for years and no one has been able to point out her wrongdoings to her. Jezebel is evasive and always denies wrongdoing. Jezebel will hold onto her sins unto death and will probably go into eternity with them. Perhaps it is at her eternal judgment that Jesus will prove her sin to her.

Jezebel is like a big thick dark forest covered with cloud and fog at night. Jezebel is like a snake hiding in this massive thick forest at night and daring you to come and find it. “Prove that I’m here,” says the snake. “Come into the forest and find me among a thousand trees and the thickest undergrowth.”

There are endless arguments that come out of every Jezebel. She will never agree to anything. She will never say “yes” when you want her to say “yes”.

She will never say “no” when you want her to say “no”. She will never see the point you are making. She will always have a counter argument. Every simple discussion will turn into a confused maze of arguments.

It is important to understand that Jezebel is given space to repent but she repenteth not. She simply does not change her mind. A Jezebel is someone who does not repent, does not turn around, does not yield and does not bow.

You will never hear the word “sorry” coming out of Jezebel’s lips. A Jezebel is someone with whom you will have long meetings, long analyses and long discussions. Why are there long meetings? Because nothing is simple or straightforward with a Jezebel. She will be given space to repent but she will simply not repent.
