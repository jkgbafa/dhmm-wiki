### CHAPTER 25\

Overcome Jezebel by Deploying Eunuchs\
===========================================================

And as Jehu entered in at the gate, she said, Had Zimri peace, who slew his master? And he lifted up his face to the window, and said, who is on my side? Who? And there looked out to him two or three eunuchs. And he said, throw her down. So they threw her down: and some of her blood was sprinkled on the wall, and on the horses: and he trode her under foot.

2 Kings 9:31-33

It is ironical that eunuchs who had no feelings were deployed in ending the life of Jezebel. Indeed, if these men had had normal feelings they would have been emotionally and sexually influenced to support and pity Jezebel in the day of her judgment.

Jezebel is pitiful like most people are in the day of their judgment. Eunuchs have no such feelings.

Strength is needed in the execution of God’s judgment.

Judgment is an important element in the kingdom.

The king by judgment establishes the land (Proverbs 29:4).

No church will be established without a good element of judgment. The church in Thyatira was unwilling to take action against the Jezebels in their midst. Such churches will reap a harvest of devastation.

I once made a call from Frankfurt airport to an old friend in the ministry. He was surprised at my call but proceeded to tell me that the church no longer existed.

He described how the senior pastor’s wife had effectively scattered friends who had been together for many years.

He said, “We failed to confront a Jezebel spirit and it has destroyed everything we had.” As he was speaking, I realized that he had suddenly become jobless and homeless after labouring in the ministry for so many years.

The extent of devastation of the church showed the power of Jezebel at work in a local congregation. If you fail to restrain the spirit of Jezebel, it will wreak havoc in your life and ministry. You must have a proper respect for the spirit of Jezebel!
