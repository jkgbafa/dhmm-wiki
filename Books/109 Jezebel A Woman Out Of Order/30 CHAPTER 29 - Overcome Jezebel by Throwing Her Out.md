### CHAPTER 29\

Overcome Jezebel by Throwing Her Out\
==========================================================

And when Jehu was come to Jezreel, Jezebel heard of it ; and she painted her face, and tired her head, and looked out at a window. And as Jehu entered in at the gate, she said, Had Zimri peace, who slew his master? And he lifted up his face to the window, and said, Who is on my side? Who? And there looked out to him two or three eunuchs. And he said, Throw her down. So they threw her down: and some of her blood was sprinkled on the wall, and on the horses: and he trode her under foot.

2 Kings 9:30-33

Throw your Jezebel out by the anointing of Jehu! Throw out means throw out! Throw out means to violently dispossess yourself of Jezebel once and for all! Throwing out a Jezebel could also mean that you divorce your Jezebel.

It may be time to divorce Jezebel! Sometimes the only way to fight Jezebel, is to divorce her. Read your Bible clearly. Jezebel was thrown out of the marriage chamber! She was thrown out of her private room where she lived with eunuchs attending to her.

Jezebel must be thrown out of the marriage chamber that she has brought a curse into.

Few people may have the boldness to take such a difficult step of throwing Jezebel out of the marriage chamber she has brought so much trouble to.

Jezebel was in her private bedroom when she was caught by eunuchs and thrown out. Jezebel was thrown out of the marriage chamber by eunuchs.

Overcome Jezebel by throwing her out of her marriage chamber. Overcome Jezebel by throwing her out of the bedroom she occupied.

She can no longer remain in that room. She must not remain in that room because she is out of order!

If you look closely, you will discover many ministers have experienced terrible “out of order wives” in their lives and ministries. Such wives must receive the only treatment that is deserving of a Jezebel. Jezebels must be thrown out! That’s what the Bible shows us. That is what Elijah was supposed to do. Elijah should have and could have confronted Jezebel and thrown her out.

I once heard Derek Prince say that many ministers of the gospel have poor marriages. In fact, he said that he had experienced two good marriages and that few men of God could give the same testimony.

I also heard Kenneth Hagin describe how he had lived in many different ministers’ homes during his travels on the field and that few ministers he had interacted with had good marriages.

It is sad that pastors specialize in taking pictures as happy couples whereas in fact they are not happy couples at all.

Some ministers may have to throw out Jezebel by divorcing her. The accursed woman must be thrown out of the marriage chamber. That is sometimes the only way to deal with a woman who is out of order and out of step!

Don’t destroy your life by being over righteous. God is against divorce but He has given circumstances under which you can and should divorce. The Bible gives clear reasons and examples why divorce can and should happen. If you are supposed to divorce and you do not, you will end up being destroyed.

Be not righteous over much; neither make thyself over wise: why shouldest thou destroy thyself?

Ecclesiastes 7:16

Jesus was clear that there were situations in the ministry where a person would have to leave his wife. This leaving of a wife is not for the reason of adultery but for the reason of the ministry.

And he said unto them, Verily I say unto you, There is no man that hath left house, or parents, or brethren, or wife, or children, for the kingdom of God’s sake, Who shall not receive manifold more in this present time, and in the world to come life everlasting.

Luke 18:29-30

Jesus makes it clear that there is a place for leaving the most important people of your life so that you can pursue the kingdom of God. Leaving a wife may mean divorcing a wife!

Clearly, Jezebel, a woman out of order, out of place, who is destructive to the work of God may need to be left behind. Clearly, a Jezebel who is constantly threatening God’s’ servant must be left behind and that may clearly mean a divorce.

A woman who shouts and angrily walks out on a discussion is out of order! A wife who is rude is out of order! A wife who is disrespectful is out of order! A woman who is persistently disobedient and rebellious is out of order!

A woman who pokes her husband and hits him is out of order! A woman who is unfaithful to her husband and has affairs with other men is out of order! An overbearing woman who seeks to have her own way is out of order! A wife who will not listen, nor allow you to speak is out of order! A wife who seeks to control her husband’s ministry is out of order! A wife who divides the church into her supporters and his supporters is out of order! A wife who fights her husband on several issues and disagrees with most of his decisions is out of order! She is proud, knowing nothing!

There may never be an end to the carnage caused by a Jezebel wife until you throw her out as Jehu did. Are you strong enough to throw out your Jezebel? If not, you will have to live under her domination and control for the rest of your life. Such people are quiet subjects of Jezebel and have to sit quietly at home under her watchful eye.

Two Bad Options

And if thy right eye offend thee, pluck it out, and cast it from thee: for it is profitable for thee that one of thy members should perish, and not that thy whole body should be cast into hell.

Matthew 5:29

Divorce is a bad thing! Jesus said, it is better to pluck out your eye and to go to heaven with one eye than to go to hell. Indeed, there are times you are faced with two bad decisions. Either go to heaven with one eye or go to hell with two eyes. These are two difficult options to choose from. However, you have to choose the better of the two options.

To divorce is a bad thing! To live with Jezebel and to allow her to destroy you in the way she destroyed Elijah is also a bad option. Indeed, the time has come to choose and the decision is only yours to take. No one can take that decision for you. You can never say that anyone asked you to divorce because no one is telling you to do so. You are only being shown that divorce may sometimes be the right thing to do for your life and ministry.
