### CHAPTER 30\

Overcome Jezebel by Ending Her Life with You\
==================================================================

And they went to bury her: but they found no more of her than the skull, and the feet, and the palms of her hands. Wherefore they came again, and told him. And he said, This is the word of the Lord, which he spake by his servant Elijah the Tishbite, saying, In the portion of Jezreel shall dogs eat the flesh of Jezebel: And the carcase of Jezebel shall be as dung upon the face of the field in the portion of Jezreel; so that they shall not say, This is Jezebel.

2 Kings 9:35-37

You need to fight extremes with extremes! Jezebel seeks to destroy the man of God and bring him to his grave. Remember that Jezebel threatened to end Elijah’s very life!

And Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done, and withal how he had slain all the prophets with the sword. Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by tomorrow about this time.

1 Kings 19:1-2

Watch out! Jezebel will bring you to depression and suicide if she continues to harass and affect you. If you do not have a major ministry like Elijah, you will probably not understand what all this fuss about Jezebel is. However, if you are at a certain place of ministry, you will see the need to end the life and experience you have with Jezebel.

How do you end Jezebel’s life with you? Obviously, you cannot and should not physically kill her! Indeed, one of the reasons for a separation from Jezebel is to prevent any physical confrontation between yourself and Jezebel.

You may overcome your Jezebel by ending her life with you forever. Divorce and death represent a permanent separation. Divorce is a permanent separation quite akin to death. Perhaps there is nothing as stressful and devastating a blow to life than to experience a divorce.

You will never be the same again after a divorce and you may never recover from a divorce. That is why divorce is something we do not recommend at all. However, in the extreme situations where you have to deal with a Jezebel, you may have to divorce. And there are enough scriptures to back the separation from someone like Jezebel.

How do you end Jezebel’s life with you? By divorcing her permanently! But is this biblical? Certainly, if you have a Jezebel to contend with, you must seek to end the domination and deceptions of Jezebel by “throwing her out of the window” and ending her life with you just like Jehu did.

I want to emphasize this is not to physically or literally throw out Jezebel to end her life. This is to symbolically and spiritually end everything you have to do with Jezebel. Death means no contact, no communication, no interaction forever more on this earth.

Is such a decision biblical? Jehu was anointed to end Jezebel’s existence in the life and ministry of Israel. The nation was rid of a very dangerous, deceptive and destructive woman called Jezebel. You will soon be free from every Jezebel in your life.

Biblical Reasons Why You Can Divorce a Jezebel

1\. Ezra commanded the Israelites to divorce their wives because they had married the wrong people. God’s servant actually commanded them to leave these women because it was wrong and would go against Israel.

And Ezra the priest stood up, and said unto them, Ye have transgressed, and have taken strange wives, to increase the trespass of Israel. Now therefore make confession unto the Lord God of your fathers, and do his pleasure: and separate yourselves from the people of the land, and from the strange wives.

Ezra 10:10-11

Indeed, several priests who had married strange and unknown women unsuitable for ministry had to separate from them. The whole of the last chapter of Ezra is dedicated to this issue of separating from wives who were simply inappropriate for the priests.

And among the sons of the priests there were found that had taken strange wives: namely, of the sons of Jeshua the son of Jozadak, and his brethren; Maaseiah, and Eliezer, and Jarib, and Gedaliah. And they gave their hands that they would put away their wives; and being guilty, they offered a ram of the flock for their trespass.

Ezra 10:18-19

2\. Jesus taught that there would be times you would have to leave your wife and children for the sake of the kingdom.

And he said unto them, Verily I say unto you, There is no man that hath left house, or parents, or brethren, or wife, or children, for the kingdom of God’s sake, Who shall not receive manifold more in this present time, and in the world to come life everlasting.

Luke 18:29-30

3\. The fruits of righteousness need an environment of peace. If you want to bear fruits in the kingdom, you need to have peace and live in peace.

And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of them that make peace.

James 3:18

4\. Your marriage may be a snare sent from hell that you need to exit from. Some marriages are just spiritual traps that God’s servants enter into. Why stay in a trap of deception, fornication, seduction and depression you can escape from? Notice that David was given a woman to marry as a snare and not as a help meet from God. May God give you boldness to escape from a snare.

And Michal Saul’s daughter loved David: and they told Saul, and the thing pleased him. And Saul said, I will give him her, that she may be a snare to him, and that the hand of the Philistines may be against him. Wherefore Saul said to David, Thou shalt this day be my son in law in the one of the twain.

1 Samuel 18:20-21

5\. Jesus said that adultery is a solid and good reason for divorce. Jesus also said that whoever looks upon a woman to lust after her has already committed adultery with that woman. Indeed, if we believe this is true, (which we do) then most men and women have looked lustfully at one another before and are thereby eligible for divorce on the grounds of adultery alone.

Ye have heard that it was said by them of old time, Thou shalt not commit adultery: But I say unto you, That whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery with her already in his heart.

Matthew 5:27-28
