### CHAPTER 1\

Jezebel Is a Woman Out of Order\
=====================================================

Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time.

1 Kings 19:2

Jezebel is a woman who is out of order. She is out of her appropriate place. Jezebel is a woman who has overstepped her boundaries. Jezebel will overstep her boundaries and dominate her husband. Jezebel will dominate her husband until he is a quiet mouse in his own house.

Jezebel is a woman who has misused her God-given position in marriage in such a way that it leads to evil in this world. Jezebel is a woman who uses power that she shouldn’t be using. Jezebel is a woman who misuses the authority, position, influence and gifts of God.

And when he was come in, he did eat and drink, and said, Go, see now this cursed woman, and bury her: for she is a king’s daughter.

2 Kings 9:34

Jezebel is a cursed woman who brings a curse to all who come under her power and influence. A curse is a great evil. A curse is a punishment inflicted on a people. Jezebel is a cursed woman who is out of place. She is a woman who brings a curse to the home and also to the ministry. What is a curse? A curse is a frustration, a never-ending difficulty, a weakness, a poverty of sorts, a punishment and a great evil. Some pastors are being punished by God through their marriage to a Jezebel.

Jezebel represents all the wives who through their words and behaviour cause the downfall of their husbands in ministry. Jezebel is the wife who is behind the “undoing” of God’s servant.

Jezebel represents a woman who ruins her husband’s divine destiny. Both Ahab and Elijah were affected drastically by Jezebel’s activities. Ahab became a murderer because of Jezebel! Elijah’s ministry was cut short because of Jezebel! The last part of Elijah’s ministry was given to someone else. Do you want the last part of your ministry to be given to someone else?

Jezebel, in the Bible, represented a woman who operated as a wife out of her proper submitted order. Jezebel is a wife but is not submitted to her husband. Jezebel is a proud woman whose pride is revealed by her proud accusative and threatening words to Elijah!

Jezebel, in the Bible, destroys her husband and also destroys the prophet of God. Her words and actions are subtle but not fitting or appropriate.

Once a wife or a woman behaves inappropriately in such a way that it is destructive to marriage and ministry, you can diagnose that a Jezebel is present!

If you joke with Jezebel, she will destroy your ministry. Jezebel will make you become an adulterer and a fornicator. Jezebel will make you into a divorcee. Jezebel will destroy the foundations of your life and stability of your ministry.

A woman who argues endlessly with her husband is out of order. A woman who says she is submitted to her husband but does not agree with him on most issues is out of order. She is not actually submitted to her husband.

A woman who does not obey her husband and does not follow her husband is out of order.

A woman who will not live with her husband but will live elsewhere apart from him is out of order.

A woman who manipulates her husband without his even knowing what is happening is out of order. That is a clear Jezebel!

A woman who pokes her husband and hits him is out of order. A woman who is unfaithful to her husband and has affairs with other men is out of order. An overbearing woman who seeks to have her own way is out of order.

The Bible makes it clear about what a Jezebel is. A Jezebel is out of place in every way!

A wife who will not listen nor allow you to speak is out of order. A wife who seeks to control her husband’s ministry is out of order. A wife who divides the church into her supporters and his (her husband’s) supporters is out of order. A wife who fights her husband on several issues and disagrees with most of his decisions is out of order. She is proud, knowing nothing!
