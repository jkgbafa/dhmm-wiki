### CHAPTER 26\

Overcome Jezebel by Separating from Her\
=============================================================

And Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done, and withal how he had slain all the prophets with the sword. Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time. And when he saw that, he arose, and went for his life, and came to Beersheba, which belongeth to Judah, and left his servant there. But he himself went a day’s journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down under a juniper tree: and he requested for himself that he might die; and said, It is enough; now, O LORD, take away my life; for I am not better than my fathers.

1 Kings 19:1-4

Overcoming Jezebel is indeed something that is possible by the power of God. The Jezebel spirit has attacked many men of God, many apostles and many prophets. To survive the campaign of this spirit, many prophets vacate their church posts and move to other locations. This was the strategy that Elijah used to overcome Jezebel.

Mature prophets know when to move away and practice their ministry away from the presence of Jezebel. This book is to help you overcome the spirit of Jezebel. It is not just to make you understand the spirit you are dealing with but also to overcome its power.

There are many ways to kill a cat. You can drown it, you can cut off its head, you can shoot it, you can poison it or you can let the dogs out. When people want to kill a pig they usually shoot it. Trying to kill a pig by catching the pig and slicing its neck is a bad idea.

Separation from Jezebel is the only answer to the conflict with Jezebel. Separation from Jezebel may be the only way to overcome that evil spirit.
