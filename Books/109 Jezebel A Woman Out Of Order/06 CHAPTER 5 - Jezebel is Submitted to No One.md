### CHAPTER 5\

Jezebel is Submitted to No One\
====================================================

Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as it is fit in the Lord.

Colossians 3:18

This truth that Jezebel is submitted to no one is derived from the meaning of the name Jezebel. The name, Jezebel, means, “Baal is husband to me.” Although Jezebel was married to Ahab, she was actually not married to him. She was not committed to him in reality. Jezebel is a showwoman who is not really committed or submitted to her husband. Instead of submitting to Ahab she made him submit to her.

Mind you, there are two ways you can make a man submit to you. You can confront him and shout at him rudely and defiantly to make him submit to you. This method is not very successful. You can also make a man submit to you by an insidious method of persuasion, manipulation and seduction.

Jezebel seemed to be a helper of Ahab, but indeed she was helping him only to take hold of him and turn him into a murderer. Remember that Ahab never intended to murder Naboth. He intended to negotiate with Naboth to buy his land from him. When the negotiations failed Ahab went away sulking. When Jezebel got involved, she manipulated the events until Ahab became a murderer.

True marriage means nothing to a Jezebel! A Jezebel is just interested in the perks, the name and the power that goes with the role of wife. While marriage is a picture of mutual respect and submission to a husband and to men in general, Jezebel submitted to no one. She was actually married to Baal!

Instead of Jezebel submitting to her husband, she rather made everyone submit to her! Jezebel required that everyone submit to her. Her good marriage was merely a show that enabled her to become the powerhouse behind the king. Jezebel was, in other words, the acting king! She always had her way! If Jezebel did not agree with something, she would just not support it.
