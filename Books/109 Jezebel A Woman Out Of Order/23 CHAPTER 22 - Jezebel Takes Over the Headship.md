### CHAPTER 22\

Jezebel Takes Over the Headship\
=====================================================

And Jezebel his wife said unto him, Dost thou now govern the kingdom of Israel? arise, and eat bread, and let thine heart be merry: I will give thee the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite. So she wrote letters in Ahab’s name, and sealed them with his seal, and sent the letters unto the elders and to the nobles that were in his city, dwelling with Naboth.

1 Kings 21:7-8

Jezebel takes over from Ahab as the head and leads the way to fight against Naboth. Naboth lost his life because Jezebel took the reins from Ahab and organized a murderous campaign against Naboth.

A Jezebel does not allow the head to be the head! A Jezebel does not allow the father to be the father! A Jezebel does not allow the authority to be the authority! A Jezebel does not allow the one in charge to be the one in charge!

A Jezebel can take over headship by stubbornly opposing the head and resisting his ideas and opinion. Her opinion on any issue is always the opposite of the head’s. Even the most frivolous activities reveal opposing opinions and opposite ideas of a Jezebel. Jezebel will always come up with an alternative to the head’s decision.

A Jezebel can take over the fathering of a child by stubbornly opposing the father and resisting his ideas and opinions. A Jezebel can take over the fathering of a child by stubbornly rejecting the father’s values and decisions and also by resisting his ideas and opinions about their children.

Watch out that your leadership and headship is not being fought against or usurped subtly. There are two types of Jezebel manifestations when they take over the headship and the leadership of a household. A Jezebel could manifest as a choleric confrontational woman, or she could manifest as a subtle, gentle accuser and opposer. A subtle Jezebel is not easy to detect or discern.

Taking over the headship by a choleric Jezebel woman is a manifestation that involves direct and open confrontations with the person who is the head. If the Jezebel spirit is in the wife of a head pastor, she will challenge him openly about his leadership of the church.

One such Jezebel wife took her children to other churches on Sunday mornings and would come home and deride her husband saying that younger men of God in the city were doing far better than he was. Jezebel then told him that his leadership of the church was very poor. This Jezebel was constantly challenging, despising and mocking at her husband’s leadership.

Most people do not recognize that a Jezebel is at work when she is subtle in her resistance, rejection and rebellion of the headship. Taking over the headship by a non-choleric Jezebel is a subtle, not easy to detect manifestation. Subtle, gentle, Jezebels are even more destructive because they go undetected and unchallenged for many years.

When a woman or wife has this Jezebel spirit, she will constantly reject the leadership decisions of her husband. Jezebel will be subtle and manifest through arguments, stubbornness and an unyielding attitude towards the head.

There may be incessant arguments and disagreements in opinion over various staff members. There may also be incessant arguments and disagreements in opinion over the children. Jezebel will reject friends or employees whether male or female and also grow to reject any opinions of the head.

The head is simply not allowed to lead freely nor is he respected as the head of the house. A Jezebel uses various subtle means to reject the leadership of the head.

A Jezebel may exert her influence on the head by deciding to be absent from important programs. A Jezebel may exert her influence on the head by deciding to be depressive and moody at important programs. All these manoeuvres of Jezebel go to neutralize the leadership of the head so that Jezebel can have her way, and her ideas and her opinions carried through.
