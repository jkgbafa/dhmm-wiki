### CHAPTER 4\

Characteristics of a Jezebel\
==================================================

All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness: That the man of God may be perfect, throughly furnished unto all good works.

2 Timothy 3:16-17

A man of God must be thoroughly furnished with wisdom and knowledge so that he will war against all forms of a Jezebel attack. What is a Jezebel? A Jezebel is a spiritual stronghold that will manifest to you through a set of symptoms and signs. Jezebel is a spiritual syndrome! A syndrome is a characteristic and predictable pattern and set of symptoms that may appear together in a person.

Some, or all, of these predictable set of symptoms are found wherever the Jezebel spirit is tormenting and harassing God’s servant. Without a revelation you will not understand how God’s servant is under attack from a Jezebel spirit. Indeed, a pastor who is under attack by a Jezebel spirit may think that his greatest lover is Jezebel. Such is the deception of a Jezebel.

Is a Jezebel a spirit or a human being? Jezebel is many things all in one! Jezebel is a spirit! Jezebel is a woman! Jezebel is a pastor’s wife! Jezebel is a “woman of God”. Jezebel is a prophetess! Jezebel is a woman in a close relationship with God’s servant, the king.

Remember that Jezebel has good sides too. She is a minister, a preacher, a prophetess and also a very good and caring wife.

In this chapter, I want to give a list that can guide you in diagnosing the presence of a Jezebel.

Here are twenty-eight characteristics of a “Jezebel”. Twenty-eight characteristics of a Jezebel pastor’s wife! Here are twenty-eight characteristics of a “Jezebel woman”! They are the characteristics of the syndrome and power of the spirit of Jezebel. Yes, you will never see an evil spirit with your physical eyes, so you need to know the symptoms and signs that the evil spirit generates.

Patterns of Jezebel’s behaviour and effects of a Jezebel include the following:

1\. Jezebel is a woman who challenges a really great man of God.

And Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done, and withal how he had slain all the prophets with the sword. Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time.

1 Kings 19:1-2

The greatness of a man of God means nothing to a “Jezebel”. A Jezebel will boldly live in defiance of the great man of God! Notice how Jezebel speaks directly and fearlessly to Elijah.

2\. Jezebel is a woman who strongly influences and manipulates God’s servant.

But there was none like unto Ahab, which did sell himself to work wickedness in the sight of the Lord, whom Jezebel his wife stirred up.

1 Kings 21:25

Jezebel manipulates great men of God and makes them do things they would not normally do. Most men of God are unaware that they are being manipulated amazingly by a Jezebel.

3\. Jezebel is someone who manifests her evils to a few important people.

And she wrote in the letters, saying, Proclaim a fast, and set Naboth on high among the people: And set two men, sons of Belial, before him, to bear witness against him, saying, Thou didst blaspheme God and the king. And then carry him out, and stone him, that he may die. And the men of his city, even the elders and the nobles who were the inhabitants in his city, did as Jezebel had sent unto them, and as it was written in the letters which she had sent unto them…. Him that dieth of Ahab in the city the dogs shall eat; and him that dieth in the field shall the fowls of the air eat.

1 Kings 21:9-11, 24

It was just Ahab and a few nobles and elders who knew what Jezebel was up to. Outside her home, people thought of Jezebel as a very good person. No one could imagine her as the source of much evil. It is always a surprise to people that a nice and gentle lady could be a Jezebel.

4\. Jezebel is a woman who turns God’s servant into something he does not want to become.

Arise, go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, which is in Samaria: behold, he is in the vineyard of Naboth, whither he is gone down to possess it. And thou shalt speak unto him, saying, Thus saith the Lord, Hast thou killed, and also taken possession? And thou shalt speak unto him, saying, Thus saith the Lord, In the place where dogs licked the blood of Naboth shall dogs lick thy blood, even thine.

1 Kings 21:18-19

Ahab was turned from a negotiator into a murderer by the works of a Jezebel wife. Through a Jezebel, some ministers become adulterers when they never intended to be. Through a Jezebel, some ministers become divorcees when they never intended to divorce. Why would a determined family man who never intended to divorce become a divorcee and get married three different times? Some ministers go to prison (when they intended to do all the right things) just because of the operation of a Jezebel in their lives. Some ministers resigned from their ministry (when they never intended to), because of a Jezebel wife. They resigned from their divine calling and became laymen because they had been manipulated to do so without even knowing what happened to them. Imagine a medical doctor resigning from being a doctor and taking up a job as a waiter. What kind of power would move him to do something like that? Jezebel is the ultimate hidden manipulator of such people who take mysterious decisions. Unfortunately, I have seen all these happen practically in my few years in ministry. Anytime you notice uncharacteristic behaviour and unusual decisions in God’s servant, you must look closely for a Jezebel. You must speak against the works of a Jezebel! You must alert all potential victims of a Jezebel that they do not fall for this profound spirit of manipulation.

5\. God is not happy when a church allows a Jezebel to operate freely.

Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols.

Revelation 2:20

It takes the collective rebuke of church leadership to deal with a Jezebel. One person identifying Jezebel and crying foul is not going to correct or control a Jezebel. I once spoke to an assistant pastor who said to me “Our greatest mistake was to allow our senior pastor’s wife to continue manifesting as a Jezebel. We should have rebuked her and stopped her!” God wants more people to identify the Jezebels in our midst and stop them. A Jezebel woman can be the one and only problem of a church, just as it was for the church of Thyatira. God wants to freely speak to His man and lead him. But Jezebel takes over the headship and does not allow God’s direction to prevail. Jezebel stands in the way of God and leads in the place of Ahab and every other Ahab-like husband.

6\. Jezebel is a mistress of seduction and persuasion.

Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols.

Revelation 2:20

Few can resist the persuasive charm of a Jezebel. She is gentle, sweet and seductive and it does not occur to anyone that they are being persuaded, charmed and convinced to do certain things by a woman. Jezebel is deceptive without even intending to be. A husband who thinks he is independent can eventually be seduced and do the wishes of the Jezebel of his life.

7\. Jezebel causes God’s servant to commit fornication.

Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols.

Revelation 2:20

It is important to understand why fornication occurs in someone’s life. Fornication is always brought about by a set of circumstances. The circumstances that lead to fornication are often stirred up by a Jezebel. Jezebel is one of the major originators of a life of fornication in ministers of the gospel.

8\. Jezebel causes divorce to happen.

Behold, I will cast her into a bed, and them that commit adultery with her into great tribulation, except they repent of their deeds.

Revelation 2:22

By causing God’s servant to commit adultery she also causes him to divorce. Look into the causes of divorce in God’s servants and you will often find a Jezebel somewhere! Unfortunately, men of God who divorce are simply branded as being unfaithful to their wives and full of lust for other women. However, from data available to me, there is always a Jezebel in the life of God’s servants who divorce.

9\. Jezebel is an unrepentant, stubborn, unchanging and unyielding woman.

And I gave her space to repent of her fornication; and she repented not.

Revelation 2:21

Jezebel will never change her mind or agree with you. Is that how someone could describe you? Don’t bother to try to win an argument with her. Nothing will change by meeting and talking to a Jezebel. She will never say sorry, never apologize, never yield, never accept, never believe, never soften, never repent, never give in, never bow and never understand your point! Read it for yourself. God Himself gave her space to repent and she still did not repent!

10\. Jezebel causes God’s servant to move around erratically.

And Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done, and withal how he had slain all the prophets with the sword. Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time. And when he saw that, he arose, and went for his life, and came to Beer-sheba, which belongeth to Judah, and left his servant there. But he himself went a day’s journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down under a juniper tree: and he requested for himself that he might die; and said, It is enough; now, O Lord, take away my life; for I am not better than my fathers.

1 Kings 19:1-4

This is what happened to Elijah when Jezebel threatened him. Elijah took the strangest journeys which ended him up in the wilderness. God’s servant may be seen taking unusual trips, abruptly changing plans, abruptly leaving programs, abruptly leaving nations, moving from house to house and going from restaurant to restaurant. God’s servant may be seen living in other countries, other houses and other cities just to escape from Jezebel. No one would know exactly why he is changing cities or moving homes.

11\. Jezebel causes God’s servant to separate from lifelong friends and helpers.

And when he saw that, he arose, and went for his life, and came to Beer-sheba, which belongeth to Judah, and left his servant there.

1 Kings 19:3

Elijah separated from his lifelong servant because he was fleeing Jezebel. Important relationships are mysteriously broken, changed and altered because of a Jezebel. I have seen men who once lived and worked happily together suddenly separated from each other because of a Jezebel. I have seen pastors resign from their jobs and ministries all because of Jezebel in the background. As mysterious as it may sound, all these things are brought about by a Jezebel.

12\. Jezebel causes depression in God’s servant.

But he himself went a day’s journey into the wilderness, and came and sat down under a juniper tree: and he requested for himself that he might die; and said, It is enough; now, O Lord, take away my life; for I am not better than my fathers.

1 Kings 19:4

Many ministers feel hopeless and even suicidal because of a Jezebel. The secret torment, the secret accusations and the frequent misunderstandings lead to disillusionment, discouragement, hopelessness and dejection in God’s servant. Many of God’s servants struggle with depression even though they are doing all the right things. Elijah constantly wanted to die even though he was doing the right thing as a minister of God.

13\. Jezebel causes an abrupt ending to the ministry of God’s servant.

And the Lord said unto him, Go, return on thy way to the wilderness of Damascus: and when thou comest, anoint Hazael to be king over Syria: And Jehu the son of Nimshi shalt thou anoint to be king over Israel: and Elisha the son of Shaphat of Abel-meholah shalt thou anoint to be prophet in thy room.

1 Kings 19:15-16

Study closely those whose ministries have ended abruptly and unceremoniously. You will be surprised to find a Jezebel somewhere. Elijah’s ministry was brought to an abrupt end by Jezebel. John the Baptist’s ministry was brought to an abrupt end by Herod’s wife. Herod’s wife was operating by a manipulative Jezebel spirit which was completely opposed to John the Baptist. Jezebel can cause God’s servant to leave his church and travel away from his place of ministry. Why would someone leave the church he belongs to and has worked in happily for many years? The answer is Jezebel.

14\. Jezebel is boldly defiant toward her man of God.

Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time.

1 Kings 19:2

She is the only one who can behave so proudly towards God’s servant. Jezebel privately threatens the man of God and causes him to be afraid. Jezebel accuses the man of God. Jezebel speaks painful words to the man of God. Jezebel challen ges and confronts the man of God. Notice how Jezebel spoke confidently and arrogantly to Elijah!

15\. Jezebel is a woman who misuses her powerful position.

And Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done, and withal how he had slain all the prophets with the sword. Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time.

1 Kings 19:1-2

Jezebel will misuse the influence she has gained through marriage or through the church. It is because of people like Jezebel in the Bible that people are wary of women coming into positions of power. People have a legitimate fear of what can happen if a Jezebel gains access to the powerful position of pastor’s wife. And rightly so!

16\. Jezebel is a spiritual woman! Jezebel is a daughter of the anointing!

Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols.

Revelation 2:20

The spirituality of a Jezebel confuses many people. How can evil come out of such an anointed woman of God? I don’t know! But I know that Jezebel functions in the fivefold ministry as a prophetess! Jezebel is a woman preacher! Isn’t it amazing that a spiritual woman of God can do so much evil? I once knew a man of God who had had two different wives at two different times of his life. I asked him directly, “Which of the two wives did you prefer?” He said to me, “I prefer the less spiritual one because the one who was a lady pastor (prophetess) was always challenging me, accusing me, controlling me, arguing with me and fighting me.” Perhaps Jezebel gets her confidence from the fact that she is also a prophetess.

17\. Jezebel is a woman of class, influence, royalty and dignity because she is a king’s daughter.

And when he was come in, he did eat and drink, and said, Go, see now this cursed woman, and bury her: for she is a king’s daughter.

2 Kings 9:34

She is elegant, dainty, beautiful and dignified. This royal dignity is part of the camouflage of a Jezebel.

18\. Jezebel is also beautiful because of her beauty enhancement efforts.

And when Jehu was come to Jezreel, Jezebel heard of it; and she painted her face, and tired her head, and looked out at a window.

2 Kings 9:30

She is very concerned about the impression she gives to outsiders. Jezebel loves to dress up and apply make-up to herself. Jezebel is a woman of good impressions. In the Bible, Jezebel is the only woman who is described as painting her face (putting on make-up) and tiring her hair! Jezebel will give a very good impression of herself through her make-up, her nails, her hair and her dressing.

19\. Jezebel can present as a good, caring and concerned wife.

But Jezebel his wife came to him, and said unto him, Why is thy spirit so sad, that thou eatest no bread? And he said unto her, Because I spake unto Naboth the Jezreelite, and said unto him, Give me thy vineyard for money; or else, if it please thee, I will give thee another vineyard for it: and he answered, I will not give thee my vineyard. And Jezebel his wife said unto him, Dost thou now govern the kingdom of Israel? arise, and eat bread, and let thine heart be merry: I will give thee the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite.

1 Kings 21:5-7

How many wives really care about how their husbands are feeling? But Jezebel was truly concerned about how her husband Ahab was feeling. Here we have an astonishingly good presentation of Jezebel as a good wife!

20\. Ordinary pastors cannot stop and neutralize a Jezebel.

And he lifted up his face to the window, and said, Who is on my side? who? And there looked out to him two or three eunuchs.

2 Kings 9:32

It takes a eunuch to kill and destroy a Jezebel. Why is that? Eunuchs are men who are not influenced by the charm, the sweetness and the innocence of a Jezebel and therefore they can rebuke her and neutralize her power. Most ministers do not have the discernment, the boldness and the harshness needed to rebuke a Jezebel and stop her. Most men are impressed with the charm, the kindness and apparent gentleness of a Jezebel.

21\. Jezebel brings a curse into the ministry.

And when he was come in, he did eat and drink, and said, Go, see now this cursed woman, and bury her: for she is a king’s daughter.

2 Kings 9:34

A supposedly perfect ministry is corrupted with a curse by the presence of a Jezebel.

22\. Jezebel may end up in sickness and in hospital. The judgments of a Jezebel begin with such things.

Behold, I will cast her into a bed, and them that commit adultery with her into great tribulation, except they repent of their deeds.

Revelation 2:22

23\. Jezebel may end her life in tribulation.

Behold, I will cast her into a bed, and them that commit adultery with her into great tribulation, except they repent of their deeds.

Revelation 2:22

Judgments abound for Jezebel. Tribulation may take time to arrive but it will eventually happen.

24\. Jezebel may lose her children to death.

And I will kill her children with death; and all the churches shall know that I am he which searcheth the reins and hearts: and I will give unto every one of you according to your works.

Revelation 2:23

Jezebel is destined to be judged because she has caused much evil. Many varying kinds of severe judgments await a Jezebel. All those connected to Jezebel should expect judgments.

25\. Jezebel destroys the ministry of many of God’s servants.

And thou shalt smite the house of Ahab thy master, that I may avenge the blood of my servants the prophets, and the blood of all the servants of the Lord, at the hand of Jezebel.

2 Kings 9:7

The lives and ministries of God’s servants are destroyed at the feet of a Jezebel. She will be called to account for the wickedness she has wrought. Anytime you see God’s servant destroyed in an astonishing or startling way, you must look deeper to see if there is a Jezebel somewhere. Divorce, scandals, adulteries, fornication, imprisonments, sickness, resignations, dismissals, sudden departures and even death in men of God, can all be traced to a Jezebel somewhere. The blood of the prophets refers to the life of the prophets who have been destroyed.

26\. Jezebel will spoil the good report of a church.

I know thy works, and charity, and service, and faith, and thy patience, and thy works; and the last to be more than the first. Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols.

Revelation 2:19-20

The church of Thyatira was perfect except for the presence of Jezebel. Jezebel will appear where there are good works, charity, service, faith, patience, only to spoil everything. Jezebel may be the only thing that is wrong with a church that is otherwise perfect.

27\. A Jezebel can cause a man of God to live in a prison, in confinement and in restriction.

For Herod had laid hold on John, and bound him, and put him in prison for Herodias’ sake, his brother Philip’s wife.

Matthew 14:3

The world was startled and astonished when John the Baptist was imprisoned. John the Baptist was imprisoned because he directly rebuked Herod’s wife. This horrible confinement of John the Baptist was caused by a Jezebel woman. When you see God’s servants reduced to being prisoners like John the Baptist was, expect a Jezebel somewhere.

There are many kinds of imprisonments. Some marriages are a form of imprisonment and confinement with a Jezebel, where the husband is not free to do what he wants. John Wesley felt confined and imprisoned and asked his wife to allow him to be free and to be controlled by God and by his own conscience. John Wesley said he felt like he was a prisoner in his own home. Many men of God feel like prisoners in their own homes and it is because they are married to Jezebels! John the Baptist will not be the last person to be imprisoned because of a Jezebel spirit.

28\. Do not let the spirit of Jezebel to take advantage of you through ignorance.

Lest Satan should get an advantage of us: for we are not ignorant of his devices.

2 Corinthians 2:11

Jezebel is a spirit and will keep manifesting herself until Jesus returns! It is important to study these characteristics and convince yourself whether they are biblically proven characteristics of a Jezebel or not. It is important to expect and to diagnose any of these manifestations when they do arise in your life and ministry! We are not ignorant of the devices of the devil! Satan will not have the upper hand in your life and ministry through the spirit of Jezebel because you are well equipped to diagnose her and deal with her when she arrives!
