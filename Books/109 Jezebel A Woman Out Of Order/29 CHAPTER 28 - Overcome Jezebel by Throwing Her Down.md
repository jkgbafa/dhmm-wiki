### CHAPTER 28\

Overcome Jezebel by Throwing Her Down\
===========================================================

And as Jehu entered in at the gate, she said, Had Zimri peace, who slew his master? And he lifted up his face to the window, and said, Who is on my side? Who? And there looked out to him two or three eunuchs. And he said, throw her down. So they threw her down: and some of her blood was sprinkled on the wall, and on the horses: and he trode her under foot.

2 Kings 9:31-33

You need the merciless and determined anointing of Jehu. Without the anointing of Jehu, you will not get rid of Jezebel.

It’s time for those who have held Jezebel in high esteem to throw her down. Throwing Jezebel down speaks of refusing to elevate or honour such a person anymore.

No more glorification, no more elevation, no more exaltation in her life. Often Jezebels are well-respected women in society. Jezebels are also well-respected pastors’ wives. But the anointing of Jehu will throw Jezebel down from her high position.

Do not elevate and glorify your wife in the church if she is out of order and has become a Jezebel. It is a great mistake to elevate a Jezebel. If you bless a devil, the devil will prosper and enlarge. Your duty is to strip Jezebel of authority and all forms of glorification in your life and ministry. Do not make your Jezebel more powerful in the public eye.

I once met a pastor who had held a Jezebel in high esteem for a number of years. He said to me that the greatest mistake of his life was to hold this lady in high esteem because she was his senior pastor’s wife.

“She has destroyed our lives,” he lamented. “We have lost everything. We had too much respect for her until everything was lost.”

Men who have been castrated and humiliated and rendered powerless must rise up and take a stand fearlessly.

And when he was come in, he did eat and drink, and said, Go, see now this cursed woman, and bury her: for she is a king’s daughter. And they went to bury her: but they found no more of her than the skull, and the feet, and the palms of her hands. Wherefore they came again, and told him. And he said, this is the word of the Lord, which he spake by his servant Elijah the Tishbite, saying, In the portion of Jezreel shall dogs eat the flesh of Jezebel:

2 Kings 9:34-36

Jehu even had lunch after killing Jezebel. It was after his lunch that it occurred to him to bury Jezebel.

God will use you to defeat Jezebel in spite of her clever disguises. Don’t be too easy on your Jezebel! Do not treat this enemy with the spirit of benevolence. The eunuchs cast her down and so must you!
