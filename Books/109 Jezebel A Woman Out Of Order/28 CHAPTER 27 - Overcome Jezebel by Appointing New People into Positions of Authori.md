### CHAPTER 27\

Overcome Jezebel by Appointing New People into Positions of Authority\
===========================================================================================

And the LORD said unto him, Go, return on thy way to the wilderness of Damascus: and when thou comest, anoint Hazael to be king over Syria:

And Jehu the son of Nimshi shalt thou anoint to be king over Israel: and Elisha the son of Shaphat of Abelmeholah shalt thou anoint to be prophet in thy room.

1 Kings 19:15-16

When the Lord finally spoke to Elijah to help him overcome Jezebel, his instructions were simple. Elijah was to anoint Elisha to be prophet in his room. He was to publicly designate his successor and anoint him for all to see.

This step was directed by God and it instantly took the pressure off Elijah as a person. Having a clear and capable successor is a victory over Jezebel.

Jezebel tried to eliminate Elijah. She tried to kill the great prophet. By anointing his successor, there were suddenly two Elijahs in town. In addition, there was no benefit in killing Elijah because Elisha existed. Instead of lamenting about the long and healthy life of Jezebel who is tormenting you, rise up and appoint your successors wisely. Set things up and fight the spirit of Jezebel by these new anointing and appointments.

Overcome Jezebel by Appointing People into Positions of Authority

Making appointments and setting up structures is a gratifying way to overcome Jezebel. Elijah would not have thought of doing these things to overcome Jezebel. Indeed, by setting up the authorities and making appointments, he set up a series of loyal appointees who would fight the spirit of Jezebel. Jezebel’s aim was to destroy Elijah and also to destroy the purposes of God Almighty. Making these appointments ensured that the purposes of God would come to pass excellently. What appointments do you have to make now? Instead of hoping to hear that Jezebel is dead, rise up and create positions and make appointments.
