### CHAPTER 12\

Jezebel Will Cause You to Commit Adultery\
===============================================================

Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols.

Revelation 2:20

To seduce someone is to influence the person in a subtle way that he is often not aware of, himself. Almost every person affected by a Jezebel becomes an adulterer or fornicator.

It is sad to see pastors admit how they have fallen into the hands of another woman and committed adultery. A Jezebel wife ends up driving away her husband into the arms of waiting beauties and adulterers.

To seduce is to lead someone astray from the right way. Through the sexual charm of this lady, Ahab was led astray. Ahab became a murderer because he was incited, enticed and drawn away from the right way by this woman. One of the key activities of the spirit of Jezebel is to seduce people.

I once met a woman who had no education and no professional training. As I looked at her I understood her life and the life of many women like her. She had nothing and no way to earn money or to look after herself. The only thing she had was her body! She used her body to earn a living! She used her body to get money to pay her rent. She used her body to get men to impregnate her! She used her body to get money to pay her children’s school fees! All she had was her body! She learnt to use her body to seduce men and to prosper.

When you get into difficulty in the ministry, just look back and see who has influenced you.

Who has caused you to enter into your present crisis? That person is often the Jezebel of your life. After Jezebel’s input, Ahab became a murderer and was hunted by the prophet of God who predicted his judgment and destruction. Ahab had a crisis and it was all caused by Jezebel! Ahab was now under a lot of stress because he was a wanted man.

One day I heard a pastor declaring about his wife, “She has caused me the most stress and problems in my entire life and ministry.” Indeed, he was clearly married to a Jezebel who was drawing him into crisis after crisis by her manipulations and enticements.

Beware, because your marriage to a Jezebel is seducing and influencing you until you turn into a man with a wife and many side girlfriends. Before you realize, all the honour of your life and ministry would be gone because of the tumultuous marriage you have to this woman who is out of order! A lack of love and sex will make you into a fornicator sooner or later!
