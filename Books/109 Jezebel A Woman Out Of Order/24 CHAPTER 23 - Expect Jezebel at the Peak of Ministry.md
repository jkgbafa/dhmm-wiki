### CHAPTER 23\

Expect Jezebel at the Peak of Ministry\
============================================================

And Ahab told Jezebel all that Elijah had done, and withal how he had slain all the prophets with the sword. Then Jezebel sent a messenger unto Elijah, saying, So let the gods do to me, and more also, if I make not thy life as the life of one of them by to morrow about this time.

1 Kings 19:1-2

Jezebel will manifest herself at the peak of your ministry. The peak of ministry is when you are shining and going higher in the realms of God.

When can you expect to see Jezebel in your life and ministry? Well, it was only after the great events of Elijah’s life and ministry that Jezebel arose to confront Elijah. After a dramatic confrontation with Baal’s prophets and an equally dramatic calling forth for rain after three and a half years of drought, Jezebel rose up fearlessly to challenge, threaten and rebuke Elijah. It takes a lot to confront a great and big anointing like Elijah. But a Jezebel is fearless before great men.

A Jezebel will rebuke and reprimand God’s servant as though she is his co-equal. She fearlessly corrects God’s man of faith and power who is respected by everyone else. A Jezebel is proud, rude and over-confident before the Lord’s anointed. She can say anything to the man of God and get away with it. No one speaks like Jezebel and no one can say the things that Jezebel says to God’s servant. You can see in the passage of the Bible above that Jezebel frightened Elijah and made him flee his God-given position of ministry. Jezebel is an evil spirit that patiently waits for you to rise in the ministry! When greatness begins to be associated with you and your ministry, you will see the manifestation of this evil Jezebel spirit. Jezebel is specially anointed from hell to confront, challenge and oppose great men.

Jezebel smote Elijah with panic and manipulation at the height of his ministry. When Elijah killed four hundred prophets of Baal at the height of his ministry and called down fire from heaven, then and only then did Jezebel attack him. Expect an attack of Jezebel as you climb higher in ministry. Jezebel will be waiting for you at the top. The problems a person has at the height of ministry are different from the problems a person has when he is starting out.

At the beginning of ministry, you may have financial problems as well as problems that come with not being known. Perhaps, at the beginning of your ministry, you will struggle to make your church grow and expand. You may not have support and stability at the beginning of your ministry. However, at the dizzying heights of ministry where you have broken all the barriers of numerical growth, financial growth and ministry instability, different problems begin to arise. One of the problems of a high-level ministry, like Elijah’s, is to encounter a Jezebel! Many wives of famous men of God actually carry the spirit of Jezebel. They may never admit it but all the characteristics are there. The manifestation of Jezebel will cause the Lord’s anointed to use every skill at his disposal to overcome that demonic and powerful influence. Often the church has to collectively reject Jezebel before she is overcome. If the senior man of God alone fights Jezebel, she will not be overcome and neutralized.

Always remember that Jezebel is a prophetess and a spiritual person. She is well accepted in the church, and it takes discernment and a strong resolve to stop her in her tracks. Jesus was not happy with the church of Thyatira because they did not collectively identify Jezebel and reject her! Notice that the main shortcoming of the church of Thyatira was their failure to jointly discern and reject the spirit and presence of Jezebel.
