### CHAPTER 6\

The Spirit of Jezebel is Mostly in a Woman\
================================================================

And it came to pass, when Joram saw Jehu, that he said, Is it peace, Jehu? And he answered, What peace, so long as the whoredoms of thy mother Jezebel and her witchcrafts are so many?

2 Kings 9:22

But there was none like unto Ahab, which did sell himself to work wickedness in the sight of the Lord, whom Jezebel his wife stirred up.

1 Kings 21:25

In both the Old Testament and New Testament cases of the Jezebel spirit, it occupied women. The person in the Thyatira church was a woman and a prophetess (Revelations 2:20). Jezebel in the Old Testament was a queen and wife of King Ahab. Because of this you can expect that almost every case of a Jezebel spirit will be a woman and especially a woman of authority.

Although the spirit of Jezebel is a demon and can occupy both men and women, it often occupies women rather than men. There are certain demons that pick the sex of their target cleverly because it works better for them. For instance, a spirit of gossip and jealousy may be more common in a woman than in men. Also a spirit of fornication and adultery may be more commonly found in men.

Women should read this book, more than men. Instead of allowing men to read this book and diagnose this spirit in women, women should read this book and exorcise all such characteristics from themselves. Humbly accepting the truth is the only way to escape from the invasion of devils. You shall know the truth and the truth shall make you free!
