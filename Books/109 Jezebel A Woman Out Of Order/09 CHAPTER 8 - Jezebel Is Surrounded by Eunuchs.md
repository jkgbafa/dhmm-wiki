### CHAPTER 8\

Jezebel Is Surrounded by Eunuchs\
======================================================

And when Jehu was come to Jezreel, Jezebel heard of it; and she painted her face, and tired her head, and looked out at a window. And as Jehu entered in at the gate, she said, Had Zimri peace, who slew his master? And he lifted up his face to the window, and said, Who is on my side? who? And there looked out to him two or three eunuchs. And he said, Throw her down. So they threw her down: and some of her blood was sprinkled on the wall, and on the horses: and he trode her under foot.

2 Kings 9:30-33

Jezebel loves to surround herself with eunuchs. A eunuch is a man who has been castrated. A castrated man loses the drive, the aggressiveness and the strength of a man. A eunuch is a man from whom the sexual drive and forcefulness has been taken. These men become docile, servile and sendable. Jezebel is always looking for a man she can control easily. Whether in a marriage or in the church, Jezebel is looking for eunuchs. Jezebel wants to be surrounded by eunuchs all the time.

Eunuchs are brothers who are emasculated and easily controlled. A spirit of Jezebel will befriend and become close to controllable men. Jezebel will also encourage others to support these eunuch leaders.

Eunuchs are used by Jezebel! Jezebel is always sending a eunuch somewhere to do something or the other. Eunuchs are loved by Jezebels! If there are several brothers around, you will find Jezebel recommending and praising the eunuchs.

You must not happily receive honour and commendation from a Jezebel. If a Jezebel praises you and works with you, it is likely that you are a eunuch. If there is an election, the Jezebel would vote for a man she can control. Eunuchs are men who have been forced to relinquish their authority and have become passive and non-assertive.

I am sure the eunuchs of Jezebel’s bedroom were happy to throw down this woman who had manipulated and controlled them for years.

Jehu was a breath of fresh air because he was willing to confront this charming beauty that won everyone’s heart with her beauty and deceptive submissiveness.
