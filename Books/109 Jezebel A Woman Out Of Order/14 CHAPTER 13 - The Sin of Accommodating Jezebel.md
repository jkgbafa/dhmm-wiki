### CHAPTER 13\

The Sin of Accommodating Jezebel\
======================================================

Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols. And I gave her space to repent of her fornication; and she repented not. Behold, I will cast her into a bed, and them that commit adultery with her into great tribulation, except they repent of their deeds. And I will kill her children with death; and all the churches shall know that I am he which searcheth the reins and hearts: and I will give unto every one of you according to your works.

But unto you I say, and unto the rest in Thyatira, as many as have not this doctrine, and which have not known the depths of Satan, as they speak; I will put upon you none other burden.

Revelation 2:20-24

Thyatira had only one sin in the eyes of God: Jezebel! Jezebel was accepted and glorified in Thyatira and Jesus was not happy about that.

There is a simple yet overlooked phrase in Revelation 2:24. “I will put upon you none other burden”! This phrase indicates the singular importance that God gave to the removing of Jezebel. The Church at Thyatira had one burden: remove Jezebel from your midst! There is a kind of church that sins by allowing a Jezebel spirit to manifest and operate freely. That kind of church is called a “Thyatira church”. Is your church a Thyatira church? The sin of the Thyatira church is that women are glorified more than they should be. How does this happen? It happens when the senior pastor’s wife is over glorified and becomes more powerful than she should be.

In some churches the senior pastor’s wife is a humble woman in the background. In some other places the senior pastor’s wife is a superstar, an idol and a proud challenger of the senior pastor. A superstar pastor’s wife is a proud, Vashti-like personality who is wide open to attacks from the spirit of Jezebel. Glorification of and honouring a Jezebel spirit is loathsome to God. The problem of the Thyatira church is that women are glorified more than they should be, and therefore need to create an unnecessarily separate women’s ministry. There is nothing wrong with a women’s ministry. I believe in women’s ministries myself and I have appointed many ladies as pastors, and, even as bishops. Remember, that the Jezebel spirit commonly attacks women, so a Jezebel spirit can easily possess anything that a large number of women are involved with.

Churches and ministries led by women are also susceptible to this demon. As Paul warned, women need to cover their heads and be protected by the authorities because of demonic attacks.

It is for this reason that a woman ought to have authority over her own head, because of the angels.

1 Corinthians 11:10 (NIV)

Women move away from their covering and expose themselves to demonic attacks when they are prouder and bigger than the authority over their heads. How does this happen?

In marriages where the spirit of Jezebel is accommodated, the married women see themselves as equal to their husbands. Jezebel is out of order so she challenges and usurps the leadership of her husband. Jezebel is a woman out of order so she defies her husband’s leadership, his wisdom and his love.

Arguments in a marriage often reveal a woman who is out of order. There are no arguments at work between the boss and those who are submitted to his leadership. If a secretary were arguing with her boss, she would be out of order. Out of order means you have stepped out of your appropriate position. Many wives are out of order and it is revealed by their arguments and defiance.

Whenever there are arguments between a couple, it is a sign of the presence of pride. Contention is the evidence of pride in the junior partner.

Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well advised is wisdom.

Proverbs 13:10
