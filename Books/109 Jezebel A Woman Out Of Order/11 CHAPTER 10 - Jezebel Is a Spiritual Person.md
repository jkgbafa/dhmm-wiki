### CHAPTER 10\

Jezebel Is a Spiritual Person\
===================================================

Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols.

Revelation 2:20

One of Jezebel’s confusing disguises is her disguise as a spiritual person. The scripture clearly says she is a prophetess. A prophetess is the most spiritual type of woman you can find. Jezebel is also a teacher of the Word. Of course, she is a teacher of the word of God. Did you think she was a teacher of chemistry or biology? Certainly not! Many people with the spirit of Jezebel can teach the word of God nicely.

Spirituality is found in the most evil of persons. When a person uses the name of God, it disarms you and makes you unable to fight against it. Beware of the manipulations of Jezebel! Jezebel will warn you not to fight her, lest you find yourself fighting against God.

In the church, you will find that spiritual women are the most stubborn and the most proud. Stubbornness and pride is revealed when they answer back on every issue. This is the reason that Paul warned that women should not occupy places of authority. The place of authority breeds pride and women are more easily deceived by authority.

Wives of senior pastors are given so much honour that they become like senior pastors themselves. Wives of senior pastors often assume some spiritual authority that make them proud unawares. Many challenge, influence and direct their husbands from the back and others actually do so openly. I know a church where the pastor and his wife divided the church into two halves and each took their part of their church and went away.

Because Jezebel is spiritual, she gets herself involved in the government of the church. She will dictate and influence decisions that are taken in the leadership.
