### CHAPTER 7\

Jezebel Works in Tandem with The Spirit of Ahab\
=====================================================================

And Ahab said to Elijah, Hast thou found me, O mine enemy? And he answered, I have found thee: because thou hast sold thyself to work evil in the sight of the LORD.

1 Kings 21:20

Ahab was sold to Jezebel! The scripture says Ahab SOLD himself to her. It was easy for Jezebel to get Ahab to do anything for her. He was for sale! He was easy! He was available! Ahab is a people pleaser. He is a man without a backbone! Ahab is ready to toe the line of the people in order to avoid any confrontation. A spirit of Ahab is the spirit that does not exercise the authority that it has been given. The spirit of Ahab is often found in a man who is willing to abdicate his authority and become passive and non-assertive.

Ahab and Jezebel Make a Great Team

Ahab and Jezebel actually work very well together. Ahab is a passive man who accepts things easily. Jezebel is aggressive and does not accept defeat easily. Because of this, Ahab and Jezebel work together and complement each other nicely. A spirit of Ahab symbolizes the abandonment of authority, or at the very least, passive authority. It speaks of a mind-set that avoids confrontation and claims innocence.

But there was none like unto Ahab, which did sell himself to work wickedness in the sight of the Lord, whom Jezebel his wife stirred up.

1 Kings 21:25

The spirit of Ahab loves the position of headship but does not want confrontation; but it is not possible to be a leader without confronting things. Someone with an Ahab spirit would rather make peace at any cost.

An individual under the influence of an Ahab spirit often makes peace instead of war.

An Ahab spirit will always sacrifice a relationship with you to make peace with aggressive people.

Working in tandem, the spirits of Ahab and Jezebel form an important alliance for evil. Both will need and feed off the other in order to accomplish each one’s goals.

A pastor who is influenced by an Ahab spirit will work well with a Jezebel and maintain his position with her help.

A man with the spirit of Ahab is not willing to confront issues. He will twist and turn just to avoid the conflict it will generate. The spirit of Ahab loves to hide behind another person who takes up the issue. A person with the spirit of Ahab does not want to look bad, strict or evil. He never says what he really thinks. An Ahab has a private opinion which he never reveals.

Watch out for an assistant who is not prepared to take on the “wrong doer”. He is an Ahab! Remember that Ahab was judged severely by the Lord. God was not happy with Ahab. Even though Ahab hid behind Jezebel, God saw through it all. I have watched men of authority remain indecisive and non-confrontational simply for fear of upsetting someone. Often they want to maintain the favour of Jezebel. As Ahab maintains the favour of Jezebel, he loses favour with God.
