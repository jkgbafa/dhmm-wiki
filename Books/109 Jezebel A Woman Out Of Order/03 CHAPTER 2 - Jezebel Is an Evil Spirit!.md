### CHAPTER 2\

Jezebel Is an Evil Spirit!\
================================================

And unto the angel of the church in Thyatira write; these things saith the Son of God, who hath his eyes like unto a flame of fire, and his feet are like fine brass;

I know thy works, and charity, and service, and faith, and thy patience, and thy works; and the last to be more than the first.

Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols.

And I gave her space to repent of her fornication; and she repented not.

Behold, I will cast her into a bed, and them that commit adultery with her into great tribulation, except they repent of their deeds.

And I will kill her children with death; and all the churches shall know that I am he which searcheth the reins and hearts: and I will give unto every one of you according to your works.

But unto you I say, and unto the rest in Thyatira, as many as have not this doctrine, and which have not known the depths of Satan, as they speak; I will put upon you none other burden. But that which ye have already hold fast till I come.

And he that overcometh, and keepeth my works unto the end, to him will I give power over the nations:

And he shall rule them with a rod of iron; as the vessels of a potter shall they be broken to shivers: even as I received of my Father. And I will give him the morning star.

He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches.

Revelation 2:18-29

Jezebel first appeared during the ministry of Elijah the prophet. Hundreds of years later Jezebel appeared again in Thyatira. The manifestations of Jezebel during the ministry of Elijah were similar to the manifestations of Jezebel in Thyatira. The re-appearance of Jezebel confirms our suspicions that Jezebel is an evil spirit. Jezebel will always rear its ugly head against God’s frontline servants.

The only problem with the church of Thyatira was the presence of Jezebel. Jesus had no other complaint about the church in Thyatira except that it had not dealt with the spirit of Jezebel as it should have.

Jezebel is a specific spirit that can destroy an entire church. Jezebel is an evil spirit that can destroy a man of God. Jezebel brought down the ministry of Elijah. We must therefore be able to identify the spirit of Jezebel and lift up great bulwarks against it. Jezebel is a powerful deception that makes war against the ministry. It is time to declare war on this evil force!

The prophet Elijah was the ultimate target of Jezebel! The church of Thyatira was also the ultimate target of Jezebel! Jezebel is identifiable because she is well described in the Bible. Jezebel is a well-defined and well-known evil spirit with specific functions. Since the patterns of a Jezebel are well described in the Bible, we have a good chance of overpowering it. Human beings catch wild animals easily because they are predictable and always do the same things. This powerful demon is, most probably, one of the fierce demonic animals that is predictable in its behaviour. It is important to study this spirit’s activities so we can detect when it is present. The spirit of Jezebel is revealed in its entirety by looking at its activities in the Old Testament and also in the New Testament.

In the Old Testament the spirit of Jezebel attacked God’s representative, who was Elijah the prophet. The spirit of Jezebel relentlessly attacked Elijah until his ministry ended.

In the New Testament the spirit of Jezebel attacked God’s representative, which is the church of Jesus Christ.

The church is the body of Christ. When Satan sees the body of Christ, he is filled with rage and hatred. Satan attacks God’s servants fiercely and wickedly. The spirit of Jezebel is often just seen as a spirit of manipulation in marriage. It is not difficult to have this skewed impression of the spirit of Jezebel. The greatest evils of Jezebel were not manifested on her husband, Ahab, but actually on God’s prophet (Elijah) and on an entire church (Thyatira). Jezebel’s deeds with Ahab her husband, with Elijah the prophet and also with the church at Thyatira provide us with all the revelation we need about this demonic power. Sometimes, people manifest just a few traits of the spirit of Jezebel. At other times, you meet people who manifest the full range of Jezebel’s traits.

Remember that satan is a master of disguise and camouflage, so his evils come in the form of good things that are perverted. Jezebel came to Ahab in the form of a supportive wife who understood her husband’s needs and wanted to “help” him to achieve all he wanted to do.

How many wives are supportive and concerned about how their husbands feel? Do many wives today even know if their husbands are happy or sad? Jezebel was a good wife to her husband because she was concerned about how he felt!

In the New Testament, Jezebel presents as a spiritual person who became a prophetess. How many women are so spiritual that they actually become prophetesses? As you can see, Jezebel was a good wife to Ahab. Jezebel was also a woman of God. She was highly respected in the church. People honoured her as a prophetess. Perhaps your church is in the state that Thyatira was in. It is possible that your church has only one problem – Jezebel!

This book will help you to see where you stand and will also help you to break out from the stronghold and stranglehold of Jezebel. I am going to give you the features of a Jezebel spirit. Jezebel had characteristics of manipulation, control, sexual perversion, and idolatry. Some important conclusions must be made about such a woman. I believe an evil spirit motivated Jezebel’s actions and contributed to her power and influence. The influence of this spirit still exists today. Like the demons that have lived on earth for centuries, this evil spirit is present with us today. The spirit of Jezebel manifested in the day of Prophet Elijah and also attacked the early church of Jesus Christ, especially the church in Thyatira. Today, the spirit of Jezebel is running free and destroying prophets and paralysing churches. Churches are incapable of rising up to the call of God because this evil spirit has bewitched and manipulated the leadership.

It is time to identify and destroy the power of Jezebel. Every single chapter of this book will help you to identify and destroy Jezebel in your life. It is possible that there is a powerful woman in your life who carries the spirit of Jezebel. It is possible that your wife carries the spirit of Jezebel. It is possible that you, who are reading this book, are a live example of a Jezebel.

Do not be offended. Be delivered, rather than being offended. It is possible that this spirit can occupy a man. I have encountered Jezebel-like men who are great manipulators. The spirit of Jezebel is much more common in a woman. The spirit of Ahab is more commonly found in a man.

Open yourself to the word of God and you will discover many amazing truths that will set you free. You shall know the truth and the truth shall make you free! Do not forget that truth will set you free!
