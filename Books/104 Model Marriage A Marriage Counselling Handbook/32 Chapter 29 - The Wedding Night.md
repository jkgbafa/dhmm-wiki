Why the Wedding Night Is Important

1\. You do not have another chance to relive your first experiences. You would be glad you consummated your marriage in a special way. We mark the beginning of many things in a grand way: outdoorings for babies, inaugurations for new governments, new buildings, new projects, etc.

The husbandman that laboureth must be first partaker of the fruits.
