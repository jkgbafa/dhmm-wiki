# Dag Heward-Mills on his relationship with David Yonggi Cho

Compiled from 372 wiki sources (messages + books) mentioning Yonggi Cho / Yoido. Transcript spellings vary: *yongi cho, yonggi, younggi, yoido*.

## The arc of the relationship

1. **Discovery from afar.** He read of the 700,000-member Yoido Full Gospel Church and Cho's *Church Growth* magazine, and deliberately "zeroed in" on Cho as the man to learn from and get close to — [[Books/009 Church Growth It Is Possible/22 Chapter 21 - Why Relationships and Friendships lead to Church Growth|Church Growth It Is Possible, ch. 21]], [[Books/080 Stir It Up/23 CHAPTER 22 - Stir It Up with Persistence|Stir It Up, ch. 22]].
2. **The failed first meeting (Switzerland).** He traveled to Switzerland to meet Cho and never got near him; in the fullest telling, his request to meet was visibly rebuffed (Cho grimaced and declined). He teaches **forgiveness** from this: had he taken offence, "a large part of my ministry" gained through the association would have been cut off — [FORGIVENESS PART 1, ISI 2012](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UFeEWbv0l_o), [HOW I CAME TO BE ANOINTED BY HUMBLY FOLLOWING, GTWC 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k3_-BWQEMpc). On that same trip he instead met **Dr. Cha** (lay millionaire coordinator of Cho's world vision) — [THE NUMBER 1 FRIEND OF MUCH FRUIT](https://rumble.com/v4tatkt/).
3. **Twenty years of persistence.** He kept pursuing the connection for ~20 years, until he found himself having lunch with Cho, with the man from Switzerland opposite him saying "You have done well" — [[Books/080 Stir It Up/23 CHAPTER 22 - Stir It Up with Persistence|Stir It Up, ch. 22]].
4. **Korea 1994 and Church Growth International.** He went to Korea in 1994 and became a **board member of Church Growth International** (Cho as chairman) — a position he still holds after Cho's death — [WHAT IS A VISION, GTWC Lusaka 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aQG6NUqzAhY), [THE PROBLEMS OF A WOMAN](https://rumble.com/v4uc21f/).
5. **Preaching within Cho's world.** Cho personally invited him to preach at **Prayer Mountain** before an international audience ("Dr. Cho wants you to preach tomorrow") — [BEAUTIFUL FEET, Beatenberg 2002](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IdIrSyX8hBU); he also preached at Cho's **Songbuk Gospel Church** in Seoul — [THE PROBLEMS OF A WOMAN](https://rumble.com/v4uc21f/).
6. **Personal access.** Dinners at Cho's table (observing that Cho left at 8pm for his bedtime/prayer discipline) — [WHAT IT MEANS TO BECOME A SHEPHERD, ISI Soweto 2015](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5VPQQnm86I); playing **golf with Cho** — he took up golf after hearing Cho explain that his doctor prescribed it for his heart — [HOW I CAME TO BE ANOINTED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k3_-BWQEMpc), [[Books/075 Enlargement Secrets/35 CHAPTER 34 - Enlarge by the Fathers’ Instructions|Enlargement Secrets, ch. 34]].
7. **What he copied from Cho.** The **vision** principle ("number one: have a vision") — [WHAT IS A VISION](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aQG6NUqzAhY); **3 hours of daily prayer**, which led him to wake at 3am — [SHEPHERD, ISI Soweto](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5VPQQnm86I); the **cell system**, adapted to Ghana by holding cells in the church on Thursdays since members had no houses — [THE POWER OF THE SOWER, Zambia 2014](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nAwguDRpKpc); **lay people as pillars** of the mega church (Yoido as case study) — [[Books/012 Laikos Lay People And The Ministry/02 Chapter 1 - LAIKOS The Layman|Laikos ch. 1]], [[Books/009 Church Growth It Is Possible/03 Chapter 2 - How You Can Achieve Great Things Through Lay People|Church Growth It Is Possible, ch. 2]]. In *Faith Secrets* he says the "little things" he learned from Cho probably outweigh the church growth lessons themselves — [[Books/060 Faith Secrets/04 Chapter 3 - Faith is Obedience in Little Things|Faith Secrets, ch. 3]].
8. **The blessing of association (not money).** Cho never gave him money — "not even a thousand dollars; I have rather given to him" — yet the association alone opened doors: a foreign pastor flew him in to preach purely because he had seen him on stage with Cho — [LUKE 6:38, First Love 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XWBiS1N6k0I), [[Books/009 Church Growth It Is Possible/22 Chapter 21 - Why Relationships and Friendships lead to Church Growth|Church Growth It Is Possible, ch. 21]].
9. **Seeing is possessing.** "If I had not seen Yonggi Cho, I would not have known what God wanted to accomplish in me" — attending Cho's conferences is his model of possessing ministry by seeing and hearing — [[Books/081 Seeing And Hearing/28 CHAPTER 27 - Seeing and Hearing Is the Key to Your Future Possessions|Seeing And Hearing, ch. 27]].
10. **Observed details.** Cho's name change from Paul to David Yonggi Cho (to escape the "troubles" of Paul) — [LOVING GOD PART 1, GTWC Cape Town 2017](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y8-jO1FkN2k); Cho saying if he started again he would "sit in my room and call the people in the spirit" (prayer over methods) — [SHEPHERD, ISI Soweto](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5VPQQnm86I).
11. **Cho's death (Sept 2021).** He watched the funeral livestream; people worldwide sent **him** condolences as though his own father had died; he says he can list some 25 things he does today that he saw in Cho — [THE BURDEN OF LOVE PART 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hFGT-BKs-_8). He remains on the CGI board after Cho's death — [WHAT IS A VISION, 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aQG6NUqzAhY).

## How Cho functions in his teaching

Cho is Dag's standing illustration for: following fathers humbly (*The Art of Following*), persistence until access is granted (*Stir It Up*), relationships that produce church growth (*Church Growth It Is Possible*), obedience in little observed things (*Faith Secrets*), observing high places (*Wisdom Is The Principal Thing*), enlargement by fathers' instructions (*Enlargement Secrets*), forgiveness of unintended offences (the Switzerland rebuff), and the blessing of association without money changing hands.
