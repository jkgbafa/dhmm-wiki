# Bema Judgment And Justice — on Marriage


## [[Books/064 Bema Judgment And Justice/02 CHAPTER 1 - Why Judgment|CHAPTER 1 - Why Judgment]]

> When a church is filled with immorality at the highest level, it is impossible for the immoral leaders to execute judgment and justice on the lower ranks. This causes immorality and perversions to thrive and spread. As immorality spreads into the church, the church is destroyed by the inner corruption.
> There must be strong responses to moral failures like fornication, adultery and other sexual perversions. Today, churches are filled with fornicators, adulterers and sexual perverts because these practices exist among the leaders and authority figures. Because the leaders are equally deep into these things they are unable to stamp them out and pass judgment on them.

> I remember a church that became a blessing to the community, the city and the region where it was established. It was noted as one of the first great independent churches in that country. As the years went by, the senior pastor and several of his aides fell into immorality. Because the evil was being practiced at the highest level, there was no judgment and justice administered. Although many people accused the pastors of immorality, justice and judgment were never applied.

> It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you, and such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father’s wife. And ye are puffed up, and have not rather mourned, that he that hath done this deed might be taken away from among you. For I verily, as absent in body, but present in spirit, have judged already, as though I were present, concerning him that hath so done this deed, In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when ye are gathered together, and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ, To deliver such an one unto Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus. Your glorying is not good. Know ye not that a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump? PURGE OUT THEREFORE THE OLD LEAVEN, that ye may be a new lump, as ye are unleavened. For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us:

> One of the mysterious things about sin is that it spreads like leaven within a dough. If you are close to an immoral, lying, stealing and wicked person, you will become like the person. Why will you become like the person? Because iniquity is a mysterious thing! Liars make you into liars. Fornicators make others fornicators. Sexual perversion spreads mysteriously through the congregation. Adultery spreads amongst pastors without each knowing about the other. This is one of the amazing mysteries of iniquity. This is the reason why Apostle Paul said that you should not fellowship with people who live sinful lives.
> But now I have written unto you not to keep company, if any man that is called a brother be a fornicator, or covetous, or an idolater, or a railer, or a drunkard, or an extortioner; with such an one no not to eat.
> 1 Corinthians 5:11
> Your interaction with pastors who live in adultery will mysteriously cause that adulterous lifestyle to pass on to you. If you fellowship deeply with money-loving and covetous pastors, you will soon be like them. Most money-loving, riches-seeking, grasping pastors are friends with each other. They love each other’s company and they impart to each other the spirits that they have.
> A church is a human institution. It is filled with many normal human beings who are equally filled with sexual desires, lusts and human failings. With time, churches are engulfed with lots of immoralities, fornication and adultery. The Corinthian church is a perfect example of this trend. There was immorality, adultery, and fornication in the Corinthian church and this problem sought to engulf and overwhelm the ministry.
> Today, there are churches that have problems of deep-seated adultery, fornication, immorality and sexual perversions in the leadership. A failure to pass judgment on these things causes the church to be destroyed.
> Paul reacted strongly to the fornication and adultery in the Corinthian church. He passed judgment and asked that the people involved be delivered to satan for the destruction of their flesh. The apostle Paul went on to warn the Christians to flee fornication. Paul taught the Corinthians to flee fornication, then he taught them to avoid fornication by getting married. Paul was well aware that the Corinthian church had no future if it was overwhelmed by immorality. Indeed today, the Corinthian church does not exist.

## [[Books/064 Bema Judgment And Justice/03 CHAPTER 2 - Formula for Judgment|CHAPTER 2 - Formula for Judgment]]

> It is a rare and bizarre aberration for a son to sleep with his father’s wife. It is an abnormality par excellence! It is even more bizarre that a son should sleep with a number of his father’s wives and precious women. It is a complete and unfortunate deviation from normality that a man should do such things in the view of servants and the general public. By doing these things Absalom opened himself up to receive a judgment. An eye for an eye! A tooth for a tooth! A stripe for a stripe!
> Absalom received a rare and bizarre accident in which he was caught by his hair in a tree. He hanged with his hair. It was a very unusual and bizarre accident. Bizarre for bizarre! Aberration for aberration! Be careful when you do these unusual things.
> Absalom embarrassed his father and openly defiled his father’s wives. These women were the precious chosen women whom David had selected from among the population of Israel, throughout the years that he had been king. Absalom reduced them to nothing by having sex with them openly in front of the whole of Israel.
> Everyone pointed to the tent on the roof and said, “Absalom is in the tent having sex with his father’s wives.” One after the other, Absalom had sex with David’s precious brides. Absalom humbled, humiliated, embarrassed and shamed his father, David, by stomping from woman to woman in the sight of all David’s servants.
> The women had to humbly give themselves to Absalom in the same way that they had been giving themselves to his father, David. Indeed, openly sleeping with his father’s wives and concubines was the greatest embarrassment for King David.

> But if thou hast gone aside to another instead of thy husband, and if thou be defiled, and some man have lain with thee beside thine husband: Then the priest shall charge the woman with an oath of cursing, and the priest shall say unto the woman, The LORD make thee a curse and an oath among thy people, when the LORD doth make thy thigh to rot, and thy belly to swell; And this water that causeth the curse shall go into thy bowels, TO MAKE THY BELLY TO SWELL, AND THY THIGH TO ROT: And the woman shall say, Amen, amen.
> Numbers 5:20-22
> The woman who is unfaithful to her husband shall reap a swollen belly and a rotten thigh. Belly for belly! And thigh for thigh! Instead of a belly full of a baby, she is getting a belly full of water. Instead of thighs that are full of delight and deliciousness, her thighs become rotten and ugly. “Belly-full” for “Belly-full”! Lovely thighs for ugly thighs!

## [[Books/064 Bema Judgment And Justice/04 CHAPTER 3 - Sin and Judgment|CHAPTER 3 - Sin and Judgment]]

> Satan is the one who committed the first sin in the universe. The first sin in the universe was pride. Satan is not called a fornicator. There is no scripture which says that satan is a father of fornication. Fornication is a sin of human fleshly weakness but lying and deception are demonic in origin. You may fall into a sin of human weakness but enter into satanic sins when you start telling lies and involving yourself in deception.

## [[Books/064 Bema Judgment And Justice/06 CHAPTER 5 - Bema Judgment|CHAPTER 5 - Bema Judgment]]

> Many Christians are steeped in stealing, lying, fornication, idolatry and adultery. Do you think there will be a difference between those who practiced all these things and those who did not at the judgment seat? Of course there will be! We will be held accountable for our lives at the judgment seat of Christ. Those who do not bother to take God seriously will regret it when they stand at the judgment seat of Christ.

> Our motives for doing things are also not clear. Remember that motive is very important in deciding whether something is a crime or not. A woman who is raped is not considered to be a fornicator, even though she might have undergone the act of sexual intercourse. Her motive for being in that situation is clearly not because she desires to have a sexual or romantic experience with a rapist. Her lack of motive and desire for sex exonerates her completely from the sin of fornication.

> It is also a great mistake to judge people based on your personal standards. Many things are actually grey areas and people have different opinions about different things. For instance, all through the Old Testament, polygamy was practiced even as an act of goodness and kindness to care for widows and women who were without husbands.
> When Jesus was asked about a woman who kept marrying because her successive husbands were dying, He did not make any comment about it being wrong for the woman to be an additional wife to anyone. Today, polygamy is frowned upon but the question many people ask is: “When did polygamy become a sin and why are widows not taken care of by surviving brothers as was done in the Old Testament?”

> It is important to see yourself as an average person. If you are a man, see yourself as an average man! If you are a girl, see yourself as an average girl! What is an average man like? An average man is someone who likes girls. An average man is someone who will not go through this life without fornication, adultery or immorality.

King David was an example of someone who refused to apply judgment to himself. He was allowed to have many wives; and indeed he did!
> Polygamy does not prevent fornication. Polygamy does not prevent adultery, per se. When David saw Bathsheba, he thought he could add her to his group of wives. But he should have judged himself, restrained himself and restricted himself to the women that he was legally allowed to have. If he had restricted himself and judged himself, he would not have entered into the trouble that he had in the latter part of his reign.

> 2\. The Earthly Judgment of David for the sin of adultery: family troubles.
> King David suffered dearly for committing adultery with Bathsheba. His judgment involved the death of his child, experiencing continuous conflict in his family and having his son sleep with his wives.
> Indeed, it is frightening to think of the judgment that King David experienced for his adultery. Considering that so many people commit adultery and fornication, judgment according to the “eye for an eye” principle is scary to even contemplate if it is to be carried through for everybody. Although David committed his sin secretly, his punishment was done openly. Indeed, David paid for his adultery.
> I am sure that many people on earth do not pay in the same way for the adulteries and fornications they have committed. Whether it is better to go to heaven and receive the recompense for such sins or to receive a judgment on this earth for these sins, I cannot tell! I know a pastor who was a longstanding adulterer who lived happily beyond the age of eighty while he successfully pastored his church.
> I do not know of any judgment comparable to what happened to King David that happened to this pastor. His church declined towards the end of his life but he survived and seemed to live longer than most other people. It is possible that he had his judgment on earth. But it is also possible that a fiery judgment awaits him in heaven.

> 9\. The Earthly Judgment of Onan for not giving his brother’s wife a child :
> And Judah said unto Onan, Go in unto thy brother’s wife, and marry her, and raise up seed to thy brother. And Onan knew that the seed should not be his; and it came to pass, when he went in unto his brother’s wife, that he spilled it on the ground, lest that he should give seed to his brother. And the thing which he did displeased the Lord: wherefore he slew him also.
> Genesis 38:8-10
> Onan suffered greatly because he was unwilling to marry his brother’s wife and refused to impregnate her. In those days, it was an important duty to marry your brother’s widow. His failure to show this kindness to a widow cost him his life. God was unhappy with Onan and his mistake was recorded to serve as a reminder for us all. If all those who do not show kindness to widows were judged in this way, there would be many more sudden and unexplained deaths in the world today.

> Jezebel received a terrible judgment because she influenced her husband in the wrong way. There are many strong women who are manipulating their husbands into doing things the husbands would never have done on their own.
> One day, I heard a pastor make a comment. He said, “I have never met a pastor from ‘such and such a country’ who is not controlled by his wife.” I thought to myself, “I know a number of people who are pastors from that country and I think I agree with him.”
> Jezebel was a dangerous woman. Her husband did much evil and she was credited with stirring him up. You must be careful what you stir up your husband to do.
> But there was none like unto Ahab, which did sell himself to work wickedness in the sight of the LORD, WHOM JEZEBEL HIS WIFE STIRRED UP.

> If all women who stir up their husbands to do evil and to disobey God were to be judged on earth, we may have much fewer wives alive today. As you can see, not all judgment happens on the earth. Some people’s sins are dealt with immediately. Others simply pass on and are judged in eternity.
> Eve also stirred up Adam to disobey God. Through Eve’s influence, Adam became a sinner and rejected God’s company and God’s direction. Even though Adam was the head of the house, he received the influence to do evil from his wife.
> You will notice that Eve did not escape judgment, even though she was not the head of the family. In God’s judgment, there is equity. Judgment is and was shared fairly to everyone who had a part to play in the fall of man. Eve could not hide behind her excuse that she was deceived. Adam could not hide behind the fact that his wife had stirred him up.

## [[Books/064 Bema Judgment And Justice/09 CHAPTER 8 - Apostolic Judgment|CHAPTER 8 - Apostolic Judgment]]

> Every church can be overwhelmed with immorality, fornication and adultery as it grows larger and advances into the future. Indeed, many churches are overwhelmed with immorality, fornication, sexual perversions and adultery. The Corinthian church was overrun by these very sins. Apostle Paul spent large sections of his letter addressing these issues that were very important for the life of the Corinthian church. In his letter, he spoke judgment against the immorality and perversions that were rampant in the church.
> Human beings are weak and God simply does not accept fornication and adultery to be part of the church. Many large denominations are overwhelmed with adulterous, fornicating or sexually pervert priests and pastors. The manifestation of evil at this level spells doom for the church. Evil and immorality in the church open the doors to demons and devils. Many groups of pastors have become the habitation of devils because of their immoral lifestyle. Immorality has caused them to fall to the level where demon activity is heightened.

> Apostle Paul passed judgments on several people who were involved in immorality in the Corinthian church. His judgments serve as great examples of what to do when such problems befall the church.
> Judgment is necessary to establish righteousness and order in the church. “I speak to your shame. Is it so, that there is not a wise man among you? no, not one that shall be able to judge between his brethren?” (1 Corinthians 6:5). We need to judge between the brethren with the wisdom of God.
> 1. Immorality, fornication, sexual perversions and adultery are leavens that spread within a congregation. Judgment and discipline is important in the church because evil spreads mysteriously within a congregation. Evil is like leaven which mysteriously influences the rest of the dough. Evil is perpetuated by evil spirits who are behind the wickedness that is being manifested in the church.

> Immorality, fornication, sexual perversions and adultery are leavens. They spread mysteriously in the congregation. Without discussing with each other, fornicators and adulterers mysteriously multiply in a congregation. When a scandal hits a church, several people will be found to be living similar immoral and unacceptable lives. This is why it is important to root out the leaven of immorality from the church.
> 2. Immorality, fornication, sexual perversions and adultery spread naturally from the head downwards. Apart from the leaven that spreads from member to member within the church, evils at the level of the leadership spread downwards to the congregation.

> Below are some rules for apostolic judgments. When it is time to pass apostolic judgments, your failure to do so will spell the end of your ministry. Today, many churches are riddled with immorality. Today many churches are powerless against immorality because the highest level of ministers are deeply involved in scandalous secret sexual activity.

> 2. Apostolic judgments must be delivered in matters of immorality. When immoral cases arise in the church, apostolic judgments similar to the ones which Apostle Paul passed on the church must be enacted.
