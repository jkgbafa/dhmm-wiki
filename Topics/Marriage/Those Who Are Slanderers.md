# Those Who Are Slanderers — on Marriage


## [[Books/093 Those Who Are Slanderers/10 Chapter 8 - The Slander of Your Character|Chapter 8 - The Slander of Your Character]]

> IF A MAN TAKES A WIFE AND, AFTER SLEEPING WITH HER, DISLIKES HER AND SLANDERS HER AND GIVES HER A BAD NAME, SAYING, “I MARRIED THIS WOMAN, BUT WHEN I APPROACHED HER, I DID NOT FIND PROOF OF HER VIRGINITY,” then the young woman’s father and mother shall bring to the town elders at the gate proof that she was a virgin. Her father will say to the elders, “I gave my daughter in marriage to this man, but he dislikes her. NOW HE HAS SLANDERED HER AND SAID, ‘I DID NOT FIND YOUR DAUGHTER TO BE A VIRGIN.’ But here is the proof of my daughter’s virginity.” Then her parents shall display the cloth before the elders of the town, and the elders shall take the man and punish him. THEY SHALL FINE HIM A HUNDRED SHEKELS OF SILVER AND GIVE THEM TO THE YOUNG WOMAN’S FATHER, BECAUSE THIS MAN HAS GIVEN AN ISRAELITE VIRGIN A BAD NAME. She shall continue to be his wife; he must not divorce her as long as he lives.
> Deuteronomy 22:13-19 (NIV)
> Here in this story, we see an irreparable slander against a virgin. In the scripture above, a man had sex with his new bride and announced afterwards that he felt his wife was not a virgin when he married her. This gave a bad name to the young bride, making her unable to recover from her husband’s depiction of her as a shameful slut.
> The man who brought this slander did this because he did not like his bride after sleeping with her and wanted to change her. It is unfortunate when people who have a change of mind about a relationship do not just leave quietly. They want to slander the person they are breaking away from and cause irreparable damage. Can you imagine how difficult it would be for this young lady to recover from the slander of this man?
