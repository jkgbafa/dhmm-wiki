# Am I Good For Nothing — on Marriage


## [[Books/113 Am I Good For Nothing/03 CHAPTER 2|CHAPTER 2]]

> 4\. You must spread the knowledge of God about sex in order to prevent the spread of corrupted sexual perversions. God has a lot to say about sex and sexual sin. The word of God is not silent about sexual corruption. God has given rules for human beings to live by. As we teach God’s word about sex and sexuality, it will prevent the spread of abnormal variants and sexual perversions.

## [[Books/113 Am I Good For Nothing/11 CHAPTER 10|CHAPTER 10]]

> Salt is quiet and effective and you can be just like that. Work effectively and quietly for the Lord by being a good pastor’s wife. A wife who is helpful is indeed a quiet and effective backbone to any minister. Many ministers depend on their wives for the quiet and effective help that they give. Not all pastors’ wives are helpful. Some pastors’ wives are ill-natured. Some pastors’ wives are stubborn and argumentative. Indeed, behind every great minister there is not always a quiet and effective wife. Sometimes, a great man of God flourishes in spite of (and not because of) having a great helper as his wife.
