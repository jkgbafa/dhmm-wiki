# Be Faithful Unto Death — on Marriage


## [[Books/076 Be Faithful Unto Death/03 CHAPTER 1 - Why “Faithful unto Death”|CHAPTER 1 - Why “Faithful unto Death”]]

> 3\. “Be thou Faithful Unto Death” is the covenant of marriage.
> For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall be joined unto his wife, and they two shall be one flesh.
> Ephesians 5: 31
> Faithfulness unto death is the expectation of every married couple. Marriage is a covenant unto death. It is the coming together of two people to become one flesh. When you separate from your spouse, you are wrenching your flesh out of your spouse’s flesh.
> All marriages are based on the concept of faithfulness unto death. All couples vow to be faithful unto death. Those famous words, “Until death us do part” are the promises we make to be faithful unto death. Are you not disappointed when a couple breaks up their marriage before death parts them? Marriage is a covenant that we enter into with a mind that, “I will be faithful unto death.” Many spouses are faithful to each other for a period of time. Sometimes, their season of faithfulness is short and sometimes it is long. When a spouse is faithful for his entire life then he is faithful unto death.

> You do want a church that will be faithful to you unto death! You do want a friend that will be faithful to you unto death! You do want a wife that will be faithful to you unto death! You do want a husband that will be faithful to you unto death. You do want a God that will be faithful to you unto death!

## [[Books/076 Be Faithful Unto Death/04 CHAPTER 2 - A Faithful Man Who Can Find|CHAPTER 2 - A Faithful Man Who Can Find]]

> Being faithful unto death means being constant and unwavering all through your youthful years before you are married and after, until the day you fall down and die. Plan to be faithful unto death! Thank God for your faithfulness before you get married. God wants far more from you than faithfulness before marriage. God wants faithfulness, loyalty, unflinching and unwavering commitment before you are married and unto death.
> 2\. Be Faithful before and after marriage
> Being faithful unto death means being loyal and unchanging before and after marriage, all the way into your grave. One day, I interacted with a man who was lamenting about the poor performance of a manager of a legal firm. He said that he belonged to a board that had mistakenly employed a lady who was unstable. He described how this lady was unstable because she was unmarried. Being unmarried had affected her performance and stability.
> Sometimes marriage stabilizes a person and at other times it causes instability. Will you be faithful after you are married? Will you be the same all the way through marriage and unto death?
> 3\. Be Faithful before and after having children
> Be faithful unto death means that you must be faithful even after you have children. There are jobs that require you to be married with children. Children cause a lot of instability and bring changes to our lives. Will you be constant after you have children?
> 4\. Be Faithful before and after your mid-life
> Being faithful unto death means you can be faithful until you are in your forties. Many people experience a mid-life crisis when they are in their forties. Midlife crisis usually occurs between the ages of thirty-seven to fifty-five and peaks in the mid-forties. When people are in their mid-life crisis, they do strange things. During a mid-life crisis, a person may quit a job impulsively, spend a lot of money or even divorce their spouse. People in their mid-life crisis are often depressed.

> Be faithful unto death means that you must be faithful through all the different changing scenes of life until you finally die. Indeed, there are many changing scenes of life. There is faithfulness in the season of marriage. There is also faithfulness in the season after your spouse passes away.

## [[Books/076 Be Faithful Unto Death/06 CHAPTER 4 - The Evil Stick Skandalon|CHAPTER 4 - The Evil Stick Skandalon]]

> As you can see from this scripture, offence, Skandalon, comes through somebody. That is why he says WOE TO HIM THROUGH WHOM OFFENCES COME. If you have a friend who is a dangerous person, you will be infected with his evil. There are people who will connect you to immorality. They will sin with you or they will cause you to sin.
> There are men who are disloyal and they will infect you with their evil way of speaking. By associating with and befriending disloyal people, you will be affected and infected with the evil of disloyalty, unfaithfulness and treachery. You will not be able to be faithful unto death because of your evil associations and friendships.
> That is why it is important to find the right marriage partner. I remember a sister who desperately wanted to be close to me in the ministry. She had been in the church since she was a baby. All that she wanted to do was to serve the Lord with me.
> When she finished university, she met and married an erratic personality. As she was entering into this relationship, I thought to myself, “I hope my daughter will be able to continue serving the Lord as she desires.” But it was not to be so. Within two years of her marriage, she was sitting in front of me with her husband informing me that they were leaving the church. I marvelled as I watched this young lady who was so desirous to serve in the ministry being taken away by this erratic man.
