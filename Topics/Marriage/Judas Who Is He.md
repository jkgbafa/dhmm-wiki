# Judas Who Is He — on Marriage


## [[Books/099 Judas Who Is He/03 CHAPTER 1 - Judas is Someone Who Should Not Have Been Born|CHAPTER 1 - Judas is Someone Who Should Not Have Been Born]]

> Jesus met many evil people in His lifetime. He met the adulterous woman who was caught in the very act. He met the woman of Samaria who had had five husbands. He encountered thieves like Zacchaeus. He encountered two robbers on the cross. He encountered wicked soldiers who whipped Him and punished Him for nothing. Yet He never said to any of them, “It would have been better that you were not born.” According to this statement, Judas was more evil than all the murderers, liars, adulterers, and thieves Jesus had encountered in His ministry. Jesus never said that these people should not have been born.

## [[Books/099 Judas Who Is He/08 CHAPTER 6 - Judas is Someone Who Will Leave|CHAPTER 6 - Judas is Someone Who Will Leave]]

> Judas Wives
> I have encountered a number of ministers of the gospel whose wives turned around and betrayed their husbands. I personally know a number of pastors’ wives who divorced their husbands despite their husbands’ pleas for them not to leave and walked straight into desolation. They were not faithful to their calling and covenant to submit themselves to their husbands all the days of their lives. One wife said to her husband, “You know that I can destroy you.” Today, each and every single one of these wives is begging for money and living off the handouts and compassion of others.
> I know a number of divorced pastors’ wives who have tried starting churches on their own but the churches ended up closing down after a few months. One pastor said to me, ‘My former wife is struggling to live so I support her and send her money every month through a friend of mine. He added, “She does not even know that I am the one sending the money to her.”
> Why do these prominent women do this to themselves? There was no need to leave! There was no need to fight their husbands in the public domain! There was no need to attempt to start churches on their own.

## [[Books/099 Judas Who Is He/10 CHAPTER 8 - Judas is Someone Who Pretends|CHAPTER 8 - Judas is Someone Who Pretends]]

> Many pretenders present themselves publicly as perfect family men. Publicly, they will say, “In this family, we marry only once.” But in private, they have multiple partnerships without rules!
> A very nice personality indeed. Notice people who are too nice. How can it be that you are always smiling and grinning in every circumstance? Are you never sad? Are you never unhappy? Are you always elated and excited? That cannot be the case. Such people have a picture of goodness, righteousness, perfection, orderliness and holiness. Such people have a good picture of their marriage. Often, this is a façade for hidden things and dark secrets. A Judas is often too nice and too good!

> 5\. Watch unfaithful adulterers.
> A Judas is an unfaithful man. He is disloyal. Judas is wicked! Judas is two-faced! Judas is a dual character! Judas is somebody who does the unthinkable, the unspeakable and the unimaginable. You need all the characteristics of a Judas to have extramarital affairs. That is why most Judases have extramarital affairs. Having an extramarital affair is the easiest thing for a Judas to do.

## [[Books/099 Judas Who Is He/14 CHAPTER 12 - Judas is a Betrayer|CHAPTER 12 - Judas is a Betrayer]]

> Have we not all one father? hath not one God created us? why do WE DEAL TREACHEROUSLY every man against his brother, by profaning the covenant of our fathers? JUDAH HATH DEALT TREACHEROUSLY, and an abomination is committed in Israel and in Jerusalem; for Judah hath profaned the holiness of the Lord which he loved, and hath married the daughter of a strange god. The Lord will cut off the man that doeth this, the master and the scholar, out of the tabernacles of Jacob, and him that offereth an offering unto the Lord of hosts. And this have ye done again, covering the altar of the Lord with tears, with weeping, and with crying out, insomuch that he regardeth not the offering any more, or receiveth it with good will at your hand. Yet ye say, Wherefore? Because the LORD hath been witness between thee and the wife of thy youth, against whom THOU HAST DEALT TREACHEROUSLY: yet is she thy companion, and the wife of thy covenant. And did not he make one? Yet had he the residue of the spirit. And wherefore one? That he might seek a godly seed. Therefore take heed to your spirit, and LET NONE DEAL TREACHEROUSLY against the wife of his youth. For the LORD, the God of Israel, saith that he hateth putting away: for one covereth violence with his garment, saith the LORD of hosts: therefore take heed to your spirit, that ye DEAL NOT TREACHEROUSLY.
