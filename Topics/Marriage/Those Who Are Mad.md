## [[Books/112 Those Who Are Mad/18 CHAPTER 16 - Those Who Are Offended PostTraumatic Stress Disorder|CHAPTER 16 - Those Who Are Offended PostTraumatic Stress Disorder]]

> But Herod the tetrarch, being reproved by him for Herodias his brother Philip’s wife, and for all the evils which Herod had done, Added yet this above all, that he shut up John in prison.
> Luke 3:19-20
> Herod, the king turned into a mad man because he was rebuked by God’s prophet. Herod went as far as attacking God’s prophet and imprisoning him because he was rebuked. Herod metamorphosed into a killer because he was rebuked about his marriage. Jesus said that John the Baptist was the greatest person to be born of a woman. Herod became the killer of the greatest person ever born of a woman! That is true madness!

> 2\. Herod’s wife is offended and develops a Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder
> For Herod had laid hold on John, and bound him, and put him in prison for Herodias’ sake, his brother Philip’s wife. For John said unto him, It is not lawful for thee to have her. And when he would have put him to death, he feared the multitude, because they counted him as a prophet. But when Herod’s birthday was kept, the daughter of Herodias danced before them, and pleased Herod. Whereupon he promised with an oath to give her whatsoever she would ask. And she, being before instructed of her mother, said, Give me here John Baptist’s head in a charger.
> Matthew 14:3-8
> Herod’s wife was a regular woman having a regular affair. There are many women that have love affairs. However, when Herod’s wife was rebuked by John the Baptist, she was really offended and turned into a monstruous killer.
> This over-reaction to a rebuke is a strong reminder of the reality of the Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder. A Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder can affect an individual until he becomes mentally ill. A soldier suffering from Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder can become depressed, suicidal and harmful to others. Herod’s wife became harmful to herself and to John the Baptist, just because he had spoken the truth to her.
> Four Symptoms of Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder in Herod’s Wife
> First of all, Herod’s wife went through nightmares, flashbacks and fearful thoughts of John the Baptist’s strong preaching which highlighted her evil ways. She had recurrent, unwanted distressing memories of the church service in which her sin was mentioned. Each time Herod’s wife thought of John the Baptist, she relived the fear that came into her heart when the fact that she was a husband-thief was brought out.
> Then, Herod’s wife went through what is called avoidance symptoms. She refused to think or even discuss what John the Baptist had said. She would now avoid John the Baptist at all costs or anything that reminded her of him.
> The third set of symptoms affected Herod’s wife’s mood. She became negative, hopeless, detached, estranged, emotionally numb, guilty, depressed and anxious about the existence of this young preacher in town. This is probably when she asked her husband to arrest him.
> The fourth set of symptoms of Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder was reactionary and this is when Herod’s wife initiated the execution and cutting off of the head of John the Baptist. Aggression is a symptom of Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder.
