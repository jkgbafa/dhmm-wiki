# Opportunities — on Marriage


## [[Books/132 Opportunities/04 Wasteful Spirits|Wasteful Spirits]]

> You can have prodigal sons, prodigal businessmen, prodigal wives, prodigal Christians and even prodigal pastors. The list is endless.The “wasteful” tag can be fixed on anyone who misuses money, opportunities and resources, as though they were in endless supply.In all cases, wasteful people do not seem to realize what they have. They seem to walk away from very good opportunities, and simply waste the blessings of life.Sometimes when people are near something great, they don’t value it.You may have a good church, a good pastor, a good mother, a good father, or a good job. Do you realize how blessed you are?Someone may be in a good church, with a good pastor who feeds him with the Word of God. The pastor trains him to pray, sing, and generally brings him up in ministry.However, such a person may turn against the very people who helped him. And he walks away from golden opportunities.What makes people behave in this way? Why did the prodigal boy walk away from his golden position, opportunities, and riches?Why did he walk away from a bright future? Why?I believe he was influenced by the devil.I believe evil spirits influenced him to waste away his life.Demons are real, and they make people do evil things.A man who was driving at 120 miles an hour towards a cliff was advised to stop. He rather decided to go faster towards his own death. As a bystander the only reason that I could give for his behaviour, was that he was under the influence of something — perhaps alcohol or drugs.In the same way, when someone is under the influence of evil spirits, he often does not heed advice but will continue in his sin.I believe this boy was influenced by evil spirits to do the things he did.This is the only explanation to his almost senseless behaviour.In medicine, there are many diseases which we call idiopathic. This means that they have an unknown cause. Many of these diseases with unknown causes are actually caused by evil spirits.I believe there are at least seven evil spirits which influence people to behave in a prodigal way.They may be sons, businessmen, church members, pastors, wives or husbands. But they can all be exposed to these spirits.Join me now as we begin a detailed study of the seven prodigal spirits.

## [[Books/132 Opportunities/05 The Spirit of Beleyahal|The Spirit of Beleyahal]]

> This is an evil spirit in the world which makes you good-for-nothing, valueless and cheap.By the time the devil finished with the prodigal son, he was destitute. He had to feed with pigs — animals which were considered unclean in Israel.Sometimes God elevates a man and blesses him with a good business and lots of money, but when he becomes successful in life, he decides not to serve the Lord. He rather spends his time and resources on women, food and extravagant living. In the end, he loses all his wealth and becomes worthless!The Beleyahal spirit leads you to squander all opportunities to do well and leaves you useless and worthless.I spoke to someone who had once been rich, but had wasted his wealth. He told me he would be unable to account for the money God had blessed him with.He said, “I earned a lot of money some years ago, but I spent it all on restaurants, discos and girls.” As he was speaking, I realised how penniless he had become. The devil had led him on a journey to waste his riches.Oh, how he wished he could have that opportunity to earn so much money again!It is this same spirit of Beleyahal that leads a young lady to have sexual affairs with so many men.After she has slept with Kuku, Yoofi, Fiifi, and all the other men, no “proper” person would want to marry her. She has now become cheap and valueless. Her dreams to marry and settle down may never be realized.Nobody wants a cheap thing, which everybody has played around with.It is the spirit of Beleyahal which makes you go to bed with a pot-bellied unbeliever after he has bought a pair of shoes for you!It is this same spirit of uselessness that causes you to exchange your purity for a Chinese dinner. Dear sister, you are worth more than a portion of Chinese spring rolls. Do not be cheap! Stay pure! The right moment and the right person will arrive!Do not allow the spirit of Beleyahal to make you cheap and valueless!

## [[Books/132 Opportunities/08 The Spirit of Foolishness|The Spirit of Foolishness]]

> Being a pastor, I have learnt not to advice certain people in my church. Any attempt to counsel them will be misinterpreted.There are also many parents who can no longer counsel their children, because the spirit of foolishness is embedded in the children.I would not be surprised if many people spoke to the prodigal boy. Perhaps, his father spoke to him. His mother and brother may also have counselled him. But he was foolish and refused to listen.Uncles and aunties were called in. Pastors spoke. Bishops added their voice. But to no avail!The spirit of foolishness makes you deaf to advice and counsel.A married woman became extremely familiar with another man. Her husband and another relative advised her not to get too close to this other man.She got so angry with this relative, and has hated this person to this day. Fools get angry when they are advised.She insisted that she was not having any extra-marital affairs. But her husband suspected foul play.When she was confronted again concerning the paternity of one of her children, she got even more outraged.You see, what was happening was that, someone was making the mistake of advising a fool.After several years however, she admitted that she had unfortunately had an affair with this man. She went on to confess that one of her children was actually the child of this other man.Everyone was shocked! Remember, this woman became very angry when she was advised.

## [[Books/132 Opportunities/09 The Spirit of Selfishness|The Spirit of Selfishness]]

> You have to think about other people and not just yourself.One day I spoke to a Christian brother and sister who were living together. I asked them if they were married. They told me they were not. Now, they may not have been committing fornication (which is highly improbable). But what would their next door neighbour think, seeing them living together but unmarried?If you are not married but live together, people will still see you attending ‘Goobley-gob’ Charismatic Church International. And they will say, “All these Charismatic churches are full of hypocrites.”You are giving your church a bad name. And you are giving other churches a bad name.The selfish person is not concerned about trampling upon the testimony of Christ and the church. He will make up every possible excuse. You must care what people think, especially if you are giving the wrong impression.It is a spirit of selfishness when we don’t care what happens to the next person.Do you have a spirit of selfishness?

## [[Books/132 Opportunities/10 The Spirit of a Wild Goose Chase|The Spirit of a Wild Goose Chase]]

> What are you searching for?Students think that if only they can pass their exams, they will be alright. They can then have good jobs with fat salaries. Those who are not married are thinking of how to get a fiancée or “beloved” and how to prepare for their wedding.Those who are working are also burdened with getting more money, buying a car, building a house — the list is endless.At every stage in life, people are not satisfied with what they have. So they think that reaching another level in life is the only way to satisfaction, but the goal post keeps shifting. The Bible teaches us that the goal posts of life keep shifting!

## [[Books/132 Opportunities/11 The Spirit of Ungratefulness|The Spirit of Ungratefulness]]

> They do not value God. They do not value the church, the pastors, or their teachers.They do not value anybody who helps them.It’s only a few people who remember, and are grateful for what was done for them.A pastor can sacrifice the best part of his life for his congregation. He can take time off to pray for them, counsel them, and nurture them with the Word. He will give all of his time to them — at the expense of his family.However, it is amazing that many people do not value what the pastor does for them. They easily forget the sacrifice he made on their behalf.Jesus knew that we could easily forget the price he paid on the cross, so he instituted “the Lord’s Supper”.The Lord’s Supper is intended to remind us of the true founder of the Christian church.Other people could easily overshadow Christ if we allow it. The pastor is not the hero of the church, Christ is!At weddings, I administer communion to the bride and groom. Why do I do this? In that moment of their great joy, I want us all to remember that the marriage was made possible by Christ our Saviour.I want us to remember Christ and be grateful to him!When you listen to the response to the toast by some bridegrooms, you realize they are more grateful to other people for their marriage than to God.The bridegroom is grateful to the one who connected him to the bride, but friend, the greatest connector is Jesus.Let’s be grateful to Him! One result of ungratefulness is that it opens the door to all sorts of plagues.
