# The Determinants — on Marriage


## [[Books/052 The Determinants/03 Chapter 2 - The First Determinant Speed|Chapter 2 - The First Determinant Speed]]

> Speed is necessary to avert many evils determined in your life.  Abigail’s speed saved her family’s life and hers. Many times, evil comes into your life because you are too slow to prevent it.  Abigail was fast! She realised that her husband had made a big mistake.  She acted quickly, humbly and wisely.  This turned away David’s anger.  She won his heart with her humility, her wisdom and her offering.
> When Abigail’s husband died, David offered to marry her.  Many times marriage is an act of pity and compassion.  David had compassion and pity on this widow.  Some modern women may not accept that being married is an act of God’s compassion towards them. But it is!
> Once again, Abigail was quick to act.  With speed, she agreed to the new marriage. She said, “Yes” to King David.  Some people would have said that she needed to have time to mourn her husband.  If she had delayed in any way she may have remained a lonely widow for the rest of her life.  Abigail’s speed of thought determined her marriage to the king.  Abigail’s speed in taking decisions determined her status for the rest of her life.
> And when David heard that Nabal was dead, he said, Blessed be the Lord, that hath pleaded the cause of my reproach from the hand of Nabal, and hath kept his servant from evil: for the Lord hath returned the wickedness of Nabal upon his own head. AND DAVID SENT AND COMMUNED WITH ABIGAIL, TO TAKE HER TO HIM TO WIFE. And when the servants of David were come to Abigail to Carmel, they spake unto her, saying, DAVID SENT US UNTO THEE, TO TAKE THEE TO HIM TO WIFE. And she arose, and bowed herself on her face to the earth, and said, Behold, let thine handmaid be a servant to wash the feet of the servants of my lord. AND ABIGAIL HASTED, AND AROSE, and rode upon an ass, with five damsels of hers that went after her; and she went after the messengers of David, AND BECAME HIS WIFE.

And while he lingered, the men laid hold upon his hand, and upon the hand of his wife, and upon the hand of his two daughters; the Lord being merciful unto him: and they brought him forth, and set him without the city.

## [[Books/052 The Determinants/12 Chapter 11 - What Understanding Determines|Chapter 11 - What Understanding Determines]]

> **2. Your understanding determines your ability to get a husband. ** Abigail was able to have two husbands in quick succession.  Most women are fortunate to have one husband.  Abigail had two husbands because she was a woman of good understanding**.  **
> Now the name of the man was Nabal; and the name of his wife Abigail: and she was A WOMAN OF GOOD UNDERSTANDING, and of a beautiful countenance: but the man was churlish and evil in his doings; and he was of the house of Caleb.

## [[Books/052 The Determinants/13 Chapter 12 - What Happens to People Who Lack Understanding|Chapter 12 - What Happens to People Who Lack Understanding]]

> Vashti was someone who did not understand her great position.  She had gotten used to the glory of the throne. She did not understand that her greatness was only because she was married to the king.  She did not understand that the king had a choice. It was her privilege to be called his wife.  People without understanding must not be put into high up positions. They will simply mess things up.

> This scripture clearly teaches us that having a good understanding will cause you to receive favour.  A man was over fifty years old and had to choose a new wife because his first wife had gone to be with the Lord. He was not interested in any of the pretty young girls who were all around him.  The people in his life tried to get him to marry but he simply did not want to marry any of the pretty fresh virgins who were dying for him to choose then.
> One day, he chose an older, weather-beaten lady who no one expected him to choose. Everyone was shocked.  “Why did you choose this woman with a big stomach when there are all these fresh-looking ladies in the church?”
> He simply answered, “It is about understanding. These young ones will not understand my life. They do not understand a man and they will not understand an older man. I want someone who understands me and understands what I want.”  Indeed, the lady he married was like a weather-beaten eagle. But with time, it was clear that he had chosen well because the new weather-beaten lady with the big stomach understood the needs of the man she had married.
