## [[Books/074 Ready At 20/14 CHAPTER 13 - Ready@20 to Flee Youthful Lusts|CHAPTER 13 - Ready@20 to Flee Youthful Lusts]]

> 2\. “Youthful lusts” are sexual desires that you must respect.
> Unto the woman he said, I will greatly multiply thy sorrow and thy conception; in sorrow thou shalt bring forth children; and thy desire shall be to thy husband, and he shall rule over thee.

> Your sexuality is something you must respect. Any young person who comes into the service of God must have a proper respect for his or her sexuality and desire for the opposite sex.
> A proper respect for youthful lusts, rather than a denial of them will save you from them. Desires are real forces that guide the events of life. Desires are so powerful that they can be used to guide human beings to their destiny. God punished Eve by giving her a desire for men. A woman helplessly desires a man to marry her without knowing that she is being disciplined and guided by God into her due punishment.

> Do not trifle with this subject of immorality. It is true that there are ministers who are involved with sin and immorality and they seem to be getting away with it. However, Paul was emphatic that it brings about the wrath of God. Immorality will cause the wrath of God to be directed towards you.

> Joseph would have been robbed of his destiny to become the Prime Minister if he had slept with Potiphar’s wife. If Joseph had slept with Potiphar’s wife, he would have ended up as a secret and deceptive adulterer living permanently in Potiphar’s house. Joseph would have gone to hell and darkness for being a wicked and treacherous man for many years.
> He would never have gone to prison and had the opportunity to interpret dreams for the butler and the baker. Therefore he would never have been introduced to Pharaoh and he would never have become the Prime Minister of Egypt.
> Having sex with Potiphar’s wife would have robbed him of the future that God had planned for him. Having sex with Potiphar’s wife would have changed his destiny. His childhood dreams in which the sun and moon and stars had bowed down to him would never have been fulfilled. Many visions and dreams that you have for your life are not fulfilled because of youthful lusts. What has God called you to do? Beware of youthful lusts! Youthful lusts can abort your divine calling. What has God put in your heart to accomplish in the ministry? Do not let youthful lusts end it all.
> 5\. “Youthful lusts” in a girl are also desires for marriage and children.
> Unto the woman he said, I will greatly multiply thy sorrow and thy conception; in sorrow thou shalt bring forth children; and thy desire shall be to thy husband, and he shall rule over thee.
> Genesis 3:16
> Many young ladies do not realise that they have strong desires and cravings for marriage and children. There are many ladies who serve the Lord well but when it comes to marriage and children, they will give up everything so that they can marry.
> I was once conducting a camp meeting with the most zealous and ardent Christians in my church. These were Christians I had earmarked for missionary work, outreach, prayer and fasting. I had earmarked them for these things because they were so zealous and committed to the Lord. In the middle of the camp meeting, I asked how many of them would be prepared to marry a prince from a pagan nation. “Would you be ready to marry a prince who is not a Christian and go into his palace with him?” They all said they were ready for that. About six ladies from this group gave up their Christianity and volunteered to marry the pagan prince. I was stunned beyond measure! Indeed, I realised that their strong desires and cravings for marriage had gotten the better of them. Every young woman must respect that need in herself.

You must not be under the influence of lust. When you sense that you are experiencing strong desires for a woman or a man you must run away from it. Nowhere in the Bible are you taught to fight lust. If you do not respect these animal instincts they will overwhelm you, override you and lead you away from God’s plan for your life.

## [[Books/074 Ready At 20/22 CHAPTER 21 - Ready@20 to Be Submitted|CHAPTER 21 - Ready@20 to Be Submitted]]

> There is nothing wrong with the concept of submission. In these last days, many young people are going to flow with the idea of being submitted. Satan has stirred up wives to become rebellious against their husbands. Many Christian wives are in rebellion to their husbands’ ideas and ways of doing things. Many assistant and associate pastors are stirred up by satan to rise up against the authority of their senior pastor. The inability to submit is the reason for the disloyalty and confusion in many churches.

> In a home where the parents do not agree on issues, it is because there is no submission. The presence of opposing arguments and disagreeing partners is evidence that submission does not exist. Most Christian wives will say that they are submissive but this is how to catch them out. The fact that there is disagreement all the time is the greatest evidence that there is a wife who is not submissive. As the years go by, the topics that are discussed in a home where there is no submission become fewer and fewer because there are fewer and fewer topics where there is agreement.

> Then the king of Israel called all the elders of the land, and said, Mark, I pray you, and see how this man seeketh mischief: for he sent unto me for my wives, and for my children, and for my silver, and for my gold; AND I DENIED HIM NOT.”
> 1 Kings 20:7
> Saying, “Yes” is a great sign of submission. When King Benhadad of Syria came to Israel, he requested to have the king of Israel’s wives, children, silver and gold. The king of Israel said “Yes” to all the requests of Benhadad, king of Syria. By not denying the requests of Benhadad, the king of Israel was submitting to the king of Syria. There should have been no war because the king of Israel had submitted by not saying “No.” Saying “Yes” is always a sign of submission. A proud person will say “No” and it will open the door for a war to determine who is stronger and who is greater.

> “No” to having sex once!
> “No” to having sex twice!

> Marriage brings you closest to the concept of acceptance. Through marriage, you will be brought to your knees and made to accept the temperaments, ways, behaviour and lifestyle of your partner.

> In the last days, many will be ready to bend over so that they can do the will of God. Proud people cannot bend. Young people are going to be happy in their marriages because they have flexible wives who bend and bow with ease.

## [[Books/074 Ready At 20/25 CHAPTER 24 - Ready@20 to Avoid Foolishness|CHAPTER 24 - Ready@20 to Avoid Foolishness]]

He then proceeded to tell me how he had struggled in the early stages of his marriage. He described to me how he had only two chairs in the living room. He described how he and my mother would play musical chairs any time they had a visitor. Any time my mother came around, he would get up and go out to do something. When he came around, she would get up and go out to do something else. In this way, the two chairs were just enough to handle each visitor in a way that the visitor would not notice that there were only two chairs in the house.

The sexuality of a young person can lead him or her into destruction. Today, you are delivered from the foolishness of riotous living that happens when you are a young man. Even Apostle Paul admitted that he had lived an evil life following the desires of his flesh and his mind.

I told him that he was creating a situation for himself in which he would be unable as a married man to live with one girl. When you develop a taste for having a different girl every other day, it becomes difficult to live happily ever after with one woman. Today marks the end of riotous living in your life! You will no longer take pride in the number of girls you have slept with! You will no longer boast about the number of ladies you have deflowered!
