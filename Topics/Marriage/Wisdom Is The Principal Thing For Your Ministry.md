> Another pastor he met in heaven told him about his marriage. The pastor described his marriage and said, “Everyone thought we had a good marriage but I did not love my wife at all. Neither did she love me. In fact, after just a few years of marriage, we did not even like each other. But we both needed each other so we found a way to work together. Our marriage was not a yoke of love but of bondage. The more successful we became, the more unhappy we became, and the more deception we used to fool those who followed us. We were empty wretches by the end of our lives.”

## [[Books/070 Wisdom Is The Principal Thing For Your Ministry/28 CHAPTER 27 - Thinking about Death Increases Wisdom|CHAPTER 27 - Thinking about Death Increases Wisdom]]

> Thinking about death always makes you wise. It makes you live your life better. You must be thankful for your house, your cars, your husband or your wife, your children and all the physical things you possess.
> However, you must remember that all these things will not fit into your coffin when it is time to go. You must therefore seek to enjoy them as much as you can. Storing everything for the future that never comes is not wisdom.
> 6\. Thinking about death makes you have a good marriage.
> Live joyfully with the wife whom thou lovest all the days of the life of thy vanity, which he hath given thee under the sun, all the days of thy vanity: for that is thy portion in this life, and in thy labour which thou takest under the sun.
> Ecclesiastes 9:9
> After death there will be no marriage. It takes wisdom to enjoy the blessings of marriage and the partner you have.
> Many people are full of complaints and perpetually discontented because they do not have the wisdom that comes from thinking about death.
> The thought of your dead spouse may spur you on to do everything you should be doing. When the person is dead, you will no longer have the opportunity to be a good husband or a good wife. It is usually the loss of something good that reveals its worth.
> We suddenly become aware of how great a spouse we had when that spouse dies. I have seen people become frightened when they suspected that their spouse was dying. Even before the spouse died, they began to panic. Just the thought of death can make you wise.
