# Everything By Prayer Nothing Without Prayer — on Marriage


## [[Books/051 Everything By Prayer Nothing Without Prayer/02 CHAPTER 1 - Everything by Prayer|CHAPTER 1 - Everything by Prayer]]

> Pray about your sexual life. Pray about your character. Pray about your temperaments. Pray about your quarrels. Pray about your failings. Pray about your promises. Pray about your finances!
> Pray about your marriage. Pray about your needs.  Pray about your work. Pray for your boss.  Pray for a partner. Pray for a lover. Pray for help.

## [[Books/051 Everything By Prayer Nothing Without Prayer/03 CHAPTER 2 - How to Pray for the Will of God|CHAPTER 2 - How to Pray for the Will of God]]

> All through your life, human beings will be taking decisions that will affect you. If you do not pray about it, you will not see the will of God happening in your life.  The will of capricious and unreasonable men will be implemented in your life when you do not pray. Think about it! Your boss may be taking a decision about you right now!   Your teacher may be taking a decision about you right now! Your husband may be taking a decision to leave you!  Your wife may be taking a decision treat you in a particular way! A young man may be taking a decision to become your beloved. A young lady may be taking a decision to hunt for your life. All these decisions by these people around you can affect your life. This is why you must rise up in prayer like Jesus did.

## [[Books/051 Everything By Prayer Nothing Without Prayer/07 CHAPTER 6 - How to Pray with Forgiveness|CHAPTER 6 - How to Pray with Forgiveness]]

> Your prayers can be hindered when you have someone that you frequently quarrel with. This is why spouses are admonished to forgive each other lest their prayers are hindered. Spouses can have serious arguments, offences and hurts between themselves.  Therefore, marriage can become an obstacle to your spirituality.  When you get married, your prayers may be hindered and blocked because of your frequent quarrels. Your marriage may actually bring you to the end of your relationship with God.  Watch out!   \
> Likewise, ye husbands, dwell with them according to knowledge, giving honour unto the wife, as unto the weaker vessel, and as being heirs together of the grace of life; THAT YOUR PRAYERS BE NOT HINDERED.\

## [[Books/051 Everything By Prayer Nothing Without Prayer/11 CHAPTER 10 - How to Pray in the Closet|CHAPTER 10 - How to Pray in the Closet]]

> We watched the royal wedding on television. There was great joy, great happiness and much celebration all over the world to see a prince getting married in style. However, the public celebration we all witnessed was the product of secret times and private moments that the couple had together.   Without those secret times and private moments there would be no public celebration.  \

## [[Books/051 Everything By Prayer Nothing Without Prayer/17 CHAPTER 16 - How to Pray and Do Greater Works|CHAPTER 16 - How to Pray and Do Greater Works]]

> I realize that I don’t have much to say to people who I don’t work with. How are you? How are the children? How is your husband? How is your wife? How is your nation? How is your job? How is the weather? I hear that so-and-so has died? And that is all. This is the conversation for someone I do not work with. Once I start working with you, I have many more things to discuss with you. How much more Almighty God? God has many things to say but He doesn’t have much to say to people He doesn’t work with. \
