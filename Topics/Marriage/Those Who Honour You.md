> **3. Vashti did not adjust herself to her husband, the king.  **

> Vashti was an older wife who was tired of adjusting herself and adapting to her husband.  She was saying to herself, “This nonsense must stop! How long will I be asked to do such silly things?”  She simply refused to adjust her life and obey her husband.
