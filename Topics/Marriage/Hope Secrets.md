## [[Books/090 Hope Secrets/06 Chapter 5|Chapter 5]]

> It is amazing that Joseph had to meet a woman like Potiphar’s wife in order to go to the next step of his ministry. Most people would have considered meeting Potiphar’s wife as a curse. However, without Potiphar’s wife, Joseph would not have gone to prison for twelve years. It was in prison that he found the next step to his destiny.
> Do not be discouraged that you are in prison.
> Do not be disheartened because you met a woman like Potiphar’s wife.
> Do not be discouraged because you met a woman who behaves like Potiphar’s wife and tries to take advantage of your youthfulness.
> Joseph was about sixteen years old when he encountered Potiphar’s wife. He may have considered it the greatest curse of his life to be abused, and taken advantage of by a mature, grown-up woman. However, these bad things are leading you to your next step. Notice forty divine steps that led to Joseph’s fruitfulness.

> 14\. Potiphar’s marriage is in trouble.
> 15\. Potiphar’s wife falls in love with Joseph.
> 16\. Joseph encounters Potiphar’s wife.
> 17\. Potiphar’s wife brings up an accusation of rape.

## [[Books/090 Hope Secrets/13 Chapter 12 - Maintain the Victory Attitude|Chapter 12 - Maintain the Victory Attitude]]

> It is important that you enjoy the good things that God brings to you. He satisfies your desires with good things. Satisfying your desires helps you to remain young. “Who satisfies your desires with good things, so that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s” (Psalm 103:5 (NIV)). Every man has desires for food, rest, sex, play, happiness and friendship.
> According to the Bible, when the desires for good things are satisfied, it increases a person’s youthfulness. It is important to have a youthful, cheerful and hopeful attitude because young people have cheerful and hopeful attitudes. To renew your youth is therefore to renew yourself to a higher level of cheerfulness and hopefulness. To renew your youth is to allow yourself to be satisfied with food, relaxation, rest, sex, play, happiness and socialisation.
> God is going to satisfy your mouth with good things like food, rest, sex, play and happiness. Accept these blessings in your life to help you relax so that you can grow even more youthful.

## [[Books/090 Hope Secrets/14 Chapter 13 - God Will Use Your Night Season|Chapter 13 - God Will Use Your Night Season]]

> 3\. Ruth had a night season when her husband died young. God used it to bring her to her new husband and to bring forth King David.

> I remember a brother whose wife died after a short illness. After the funeral, he had the opportunity to marry a nice lady friend who also liked him. Everyone waited for him to propose to the lady, but he never did until he himself developed a serious illness. The lady also developed a serious illness. He then decided to propose to the lady, but it was too late. They were both too ill to marry and cohabitate. It had taken him several years to accept the loss of his wife and accept that he needed to move on with his life.

## [[Books/090 Hope Secrets/16 Chapter 15 - God Will Right all the Wrongs|Chapter 15 - God Will Right all the Wrongs]]

> God knows what is happening to you because you are as helpless as a widow. God plans to correct them like a judge who brings justice. God knows how helpless you are. God sees that you are just like a widow. You may not be called a widow but you are just like a widow because you do not have anyone to help you. It is not right that you should be alone. God is going to right all the wrongs! Perhaps you were supposed to marry someone, but they ended up not marrying you. Perhaps you were abandoned by your lover and now you are all alone, just like a widow. God sees the wrong things that have been done to you. God knows how you feel and He has decided to right all the wrongs.

## [[Books/090 Hope Secrets/18 Chapter 17 - You Will Experience Wound Healing|Chapter 17 - You Will Experience Wound Healing]]

> If you are part of this life, you will one day need healing of your wounds. Confess your faults one to another and pray for one another so that you may be healed. If you are part of this life, expect to be hurt and expect to be wounded. Many things will hurt you. If you are in the ministry, expect to be hurt and wounded by fellow pastors. If you are married, expect to be wounded by your spouse. If you are in business, expect to be hurt and wounded in the business world.

> God will give you much more than you have now if you can overcome your difficulty with a good attitude. Job’s good attitude is revealed in that he stopped his wife and friends from speaking in the wrong way. Job was on his way to a greater life with more abundance and a greater and better family. He needed to maintain a good attitude throughout his crises. He needed to fight bad attitudes in all the people around him.
> Job Stops His Wife from Speaking Nonsense

BUT HE SAID UNTO HER, THOU SPEAKEST AS ONE OF THE FOOLISH WOMEN SPEAKETH. What? shall we receive good at the hand of God, and shall we not receive evil? In all this did not Job sin with his lips.
