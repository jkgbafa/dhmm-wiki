In an interview with the state news agency IRNA, Baghai firmly denied reports of the alleged meeting and expressed surprise at the American media's coverage of the matter. The Iranian media had a mixed reaction to the reported meeting between Tehran's UN ambassador and Tech billionaire Elon Musk. While some outlets described it as positive, others labeled it as treason. The reformist-leaning Iranian media outlets largely portrayed the meeting in a positive light. The Hamihan Daily noted that Iran's diplomatic strategy had elicited a reciprocal and positive response from the American side.

Similarly, the Sazan Deghi outlet interpreted the encounter as an indication of Iran engaging in secret diplomacy with the United States, though it cautioned against exaggerating the significance. In contrast, the ultra-conservative Kahan Daily criticized the reported meeting as naivety or treason against Iran and demanded that Trump pay damages for withdrawing from the 2015 Iran nuclear deal. The conservative Jamhuri Islami newspaper stated that the interaction can be viewed as the beginning of a new path in Iran's foreign policy, though it did not name Musk, referring to him instead as Trump's representative.
